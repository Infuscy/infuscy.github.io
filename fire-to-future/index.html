Begin reading Download PDF · 8.1 MB

A dependency-aware field manual for moving from reliable fire to a resilient industrial civilization. It teaches what to build, how to verify it, what can fail, and what institutional capability the work requires. Historical pathways are evidence and context—not a universal critical path.

The core path is chapters 1–47; chapters 48–51 are an optional defense annex. Hazardous procedures are gated behind trained, supervised competence.
