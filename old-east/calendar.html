The calendar of power

Old magic is timed magic: most rites below work only on their night. The table follows the year from New Year’s Eve to St Andrew’s Eve, 29 November.

Date Night or feast What is worked Where
1 Jan (eve 31 Dec)Sf. Vasile, New YearCounting fence stakes for a husband; the onion calendar for the year’s rain; Plugușorul and Sorcova blessings; survakaneRomania, Bulgaria
24 Dec – 6 JanThe Twelve DaysKarakondzhuli and kallikantzaroi roam; Russian svyatki divination with mirrors and candlesBulgaria, Greece, Russia
5–6 JanBobotează, EpiphanyThe priest’s basil under the pillow to dream of the destined oneRomania, Bulgaria, Serbia
1–9 MarMărțișor, Baba Dochia, Baba MartaRed-and-white thread against sun-burn and the eye; pick your “Babă” day to forecast your yearRomania, Moldova, Bulgaria, North Macedonia
23 Apr (eve 22)Sângeorz, Gergyovden, ĐurđevdanWitches steal the mană of milk and fields through the dew; green branches and turf on gates; buried treasure burns with blue flameRomania, Bulgaria, Serbia

Serbia and Russia keep the old Julian calendar, so their fixed feasts fall 13 days later: Đurđevdan on 6 May, Kupala on 7 July.
