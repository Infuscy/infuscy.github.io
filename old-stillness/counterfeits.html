Appendix A

Counterfeits

Many methods sold as ancient were designed in the last 150 years. Most are not bad practices; they are simply not old, and several have a genuine old relative in this book.

Sold as What it actually is Old relative here
“Ancient vipassana” noting (“rising, falling”)The “New Burmese Method”, developed by U Nārada (1868–1955) and spread by Mahāsi Sayadaw (1904–1982) (overview)Mindfulness of Breathing (15)
Mindfulness, as taught in apps and coursesJon Kabat-Zinn’s eight-week Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (1979) and its offshootsSpells 5 and 15
The “body scan”Draws on 20th-century Burmese body “sweeping” (U Ba Khin, S. N. Goenka) and MBSRHakuin’s soft butter (9)
Transcendental MeditationTaught by Maharishi Mahesh Yogi from the mid-1950s; trademarked, with mantras assigned in privateThe Sacred Syllable (19)
Centering PrayerDeveloped in the 1970s by the Trappist monks Thomas Keating, Basil Pennington and William MeningerThe One Word (30)
Yoga Nidra guided relaxationSystematised by Swami Satyananda Saraswati in the 1960s–70s; the term is old, the guided method is notThe edge of sleep (20)
Seven rainbow-coloured chakrasA Western synthesis. Indian texts give the centres colours but no rainbow, and their number varies; the rainbow scheme dates from the 1970s (Leland)The Locked Spells (Chapter III)
The Taoist “Inner Smile”Part of Mantak Chia’s Healing Tao system, founded in New York in 1979 (background)Spells 1 and 4
“Tibetan” singing-bowl meditationNo credible evidence of Tibetan monastic use; the bowls first appear in print on a 1972 American album (Tricycle)Listening to the Inner Sound (22)
Kundalini Yoga (3HO)Introduced in the United States by Yogi Bhajan from 1969The Locked Spells (Chapter III)
“112 meditations” of the Vijñāna BhairavaLoose poetic rewrites, from Paul Reps (1957) onwardDoorways in the Gaps (20)

A modern date does not make a method useless: the trial evidence in “Before you cast” comes almost entirely from modern programmes. It only means that “ancient” is a sales word worth checking.
