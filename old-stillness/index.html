The Spellbook of Old Stillness

Thirty-four meditation techniques, each traced to a named text or teacher from before 1800.

They come from seven traditions: China, Japan, Tibet, India, Greece and Rome, the Christian desert and cloister, and the Sufis.

Buse Florin-Emilian · 24 November 2026

A spell gets into this book only if its method is written down in a source older than 1800. Where the date or the author is uncertain, its seal says so.
