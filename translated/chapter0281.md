## Chapter 281: Who Can Make Their Master Happier

"Jagin?! Jagin, you bastard, are you even alive?"

Jace shouted toward the door and hammered it with his fist — except the blow landed without much force and just pushed the door open. It hadn't been latched at all.

A cloying, dizzying fragrance immediately rolled out of Jagin's pitch-black room, undercut by a thick reek of shit and blood so strong the perfume couldn't even cover it.

Jace's anger drained by more than half in an instant. The sudden assault hit his stomach like a fist, and a violent wave of nausea followed.

Sulenna walked in with a frown, smoothly pulling a dagger off the doorframe by feel. She held it with both hands aimed at the dark, her eyes wide as saucers, not daring to blink.

"I'm casting Light," Jace said.

A glow of arcane radiance lit up Jagin's room, and Sulenna couldn't help squinting.

Jace saw at a glance that the walls were covered in dried, darkened blood, plus brownish stuff that looked like feces, and other unidentifiable liquids scrawled into a bunch of symbols he didn't recognize at all.

Sulenna finally couldn't take it anymore. She leaned against the wall and dry-heaved. Jace glanced at her, then walked toward the hallway deeper in the house. Every variety of foul smell grew even more intense, and he caught the one scent he wanted least of all — the smell of blood.

When he reached the bedroom doorway, the Light swept across the threshold. He took one look at what was inside, and that was all it took — he couldn't concentrate enough to keep the spell going anymore.

Darkness closed in again. He leaned against the wall and closed his eyes, though he wasn't sure what closing his eyes in a place this dark was supposed to accomplish. Maybe it gave him a tiny, meaningless sliver of comfort. He just needed to catch his breath.

Because that image was a little hard to stomach.

After calming himself down a bit, he walked back in and reignited the Light.

Jagin was lying on the bed — if the thing on the bed was still Jagin.

His eyelids had been peeled off. Bloodshot eyeballs stared fixedly toward the upper corner. His mouth was frozen in a somewhat strained smile, the gaps between his teeth packed with shit that overflowed and ran all the way down his neck, his chest, and the bed.

His skin was gone. Yellow fat and dark red muscle lay exposed to the air. His arms and legs were tied up and hung from the walls and ceiling with what looked like intestines of every thickness and color dragged out of his belly, the other ends nailed in place.

His nether regions were still standing at attention, swollen to a size that looked like they'd never return to normal.

On the wall at the head of the bed, someone had used blood-mixed shit to write a passage in Demonic. Jace recognized a few of the characters but didn't know the overall meaning. He really needed to pick up the pace on learning Demonic.

Sulenna only glanced inside once before breaking into sobs, her legs giving out as she slumped in the hallway, face in her hands, sobbing uncontrollably. Jace didn't want to look again either. He could only go by the general build and hair color to confirm that this really was Jagin.

What was this, the end of the dark arts road?

The two of them walked back into the hallway. Jace pulled the door shut and took several deep breaths in a row.

"Did Jagin summon this monster? Did the monster kill him?" Sulenna asked through tears.

"What else?" Jace said. "No time for grieving. We need to clean this place up, Sulenna. Otherwise, when the Wizard Sanctum people show up later, the whole Dagger Society is screwed."

"Let me handle it, Jace." Sulenna forced herself steady.

"You sure?" Jace asked. "You sure you can take care of everything? Everything connected to us?"

"I know everything Jagin had in this place better than anyone, don't I?" Sulenna said. "Thank you for helping me… thank you for helping me get rid of that monster. You've already done enough. Leave the rest to me. I'll go find Erland tonight… he knows better how to deal with this kind of situation."

Jace nodded. Even if Sulenna wasn't exactly a qualified warlock right now, she was right about this. And when it came to this sort of mess, Erland was definitely the expert.

"I know people among the guards," Sulenna said. "If anything goes wrong, I'll let you know."

"If there's any word from the Wizard Sanctum, I'll tell you all as soon as I can," Jace said.

"Thank you. And, tonight…" Sulenna wiped the tear tracks from her face and nodded. "I don't know what else to say, Jace."

Jace nodded back. He honestly wasn't sure what this woman's feelings for Jagin had been.

Sulenna locked the door and disappeared at the end of the street. When Jace left Old Town and reached the canal, he couldn't help it — he pulled off his shirt and jumped straight into the black, cold water.

He scrubbed every inch of his body until his skin and hair squeaked before he was finally willing to sit back down on the embankment.

After getting home, he threw the dirty shirt into the corner. The clothes hadn't picked up any of the smell, but he didn't want them anymore. He'd find a place to burn them tomorrow and buy a new one.

Morophil picked up the shirt, sniffed it, then looked over.

"Master, where have you been?"

Jace glanced at her. His mind was full of the image of the monster he'd stabbed to death.

"None of your business!"

"Oh? None of my business?" Morophil moved closer.

That sickly-sweet scent coming off her made him want to vomit. Jace couldn't help frowning. He thought of Jagin again.

"Let me cool down for a bit."

"What, you met some other sisters? They know how to please you better than I do, so you're sick of me now?" Morophil pressed.

Jace suddenly caught something off in her tone.

He grabbed her wrist. "What did you say? Sisters?"

"You know better than I do," Morophil said, a note of complaint in her voice. "You went to see other succubi… and you can't even remember how many?"

"I only saw one," Jace said. "I already pierced her heart. You're telling me there's another?"

"I can smell two scents," Morophil said.

"Damn it. Come with me!"

Jace threw on his robe, slung his staff over his shoulder, and ran all the way back to Jagin's place. He shoved hard at the locked door. Morophil watched from the shadows deep in the hallway and said, "The other one probably fled before you first got here, master. His scent is already fading!"

"So Jagin summoned two succubi," Jace said.

Morophil sniffed the air and clicked her tongue. "Looks like Mr. Jagin got what he wanted."

"You mean he deserved this?"

"That's not what I meant, master."

"Summon a second succubus and you get killed? Out of some kind of jealousy?"

"Maybe," Morophil said. "But to me, this looks more like a competition."

Jace frowned. "What kind of competition?"

"Who can make their master happier." Morophil smiled.

"Fucking lunatics. Every last one of them."

Jace cursed, but thinking back to what the succubus he'd killed had said, he had to admit Morophil had a point.

"All right, the problem now is the other one. Aren't you supposed to hunt demons? Where did he go?"

"It's a 'he,'" Morophil said. "The other one is male."

"He's still in the city?"

"I'm not sure."

"We find him and kill him," Jace said. "Tonight. Otherwise I won't be able to sleep."
