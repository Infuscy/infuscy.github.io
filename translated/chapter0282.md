## Chapter 282: The Hunter

Night had grown deep. The streets were empty of people. Stormwind didn't strictly have a curfew at the moment, but anyone wandering the streets without good reason would still be questioned by the Watch. If that happened, you could only hope the Wizard Sanctum's ring would be of some help.

"Where would a freed succubus go first?" Jace asked.

Morophil materialized from the shadow of a building and said in a low voice, "With or without a master, we're always drawn to all kinds of things. Emotions, desire, release, magic… even simple curiosity…"

"Same as anyone, then," Jace said.

The succubus smiled and melted back into the darkness. Her steps were light but purposeful. Jace looked at the stunted wing bones on her back and thought of the succubus from earlier with two wings sprouting from hers — instead, the wings now seemed like a symbol of weakness and powerlessness.

Compared to Morophil, that succubus Jagin had summoned was far behind, whether in terms of seductive magic or raw fighting ability.

If it came to a pure physical fight with Morophil, Jace wouldn't dare guarantee he'd win, let alone walk away unscathed.

"I can smell his anxiety. His urgency." Morophil spoke, then raised her head and gazed at the high tower on the city wall. "I think he's hungry, master."

"Hungry? So he'll look for a mage? Do any mages live in Old Town?" Jace looked at the jumbled houses layered over one another below, the windows still glowing among them. "There should be some, but I can't keep track. Can you track one down?"

"A mage. Let me see." Morophil walked off into the distance, her shadow blending with the shadow of the city wall.

Relying on her sensitivity to demons and magic, she pushed deep into an old residential quarter. Jace watched her climb up one of the buildings and vanish. The second-floor window there was open.

He climbed up to the second-floor window and peeked inside. A man in his twenties or thirties was lying naked on the bed, his whole body trembling, eyes rolled back to whites, and his lower half still standing at attention.

This had to be the place.

He was indeed a mage. Jace could see quite a few books piled up in his room, and a robe hanging on a rack by the window. Judging by his outer robe, he looked like an apprentice mage.

"Sir?"

Jace called out twice. The guy didn't react at all. He'd basically lost consciousness — or he'd already been magically hypnotized and was completely unaware of anything happening around him.

Morophil had already slipped inside. Jace climbed in, took one last look at the man, and followed quickly — but when he reached the end of the hallway and went downstairs, the succubus male had already darted out the building's door.

The soul bond let him feel that Morophil had crossed the street and gone far ahead. His own pace couldn't quite keep up.

But he couldn't exactly order the succubus to wait for him, not with the prey getting closer by the minute. He ran in the direction his soul-sense told her she was heading, and eventually lost his bearings in a run-down Old Town alley.

Those two must have been running across the rooftops. He'd gotten himself boxed into a dead end.

Jace found a lower roof and clambered up using assorted debris.

A succubus could turn invisible and flee across rooftops. He couldn't. If he got spotted by the night patrol guards, he'd be in real trouble.

He climbed out of the dead end and dropped back down, trusting his instincts to keep tracking. That's when rapid barking came from the direction he was heading — then cut off halfway through, breaking into a choked whine.

Jace reached the alley where the barking had come from and glanced both ways. A dog was lying on the ground, faint shadow energy coiling around its whole body. It had been hit by the succubus's sleep spell.

The scent the succubus was giving off was already thick and strong — coming from behind the door right past this dog.

Jace pulled himself up the steps and yanked open the unlatched door, when suddenly an inexplicable aura of evil crashed into his face!

A demon appeared out of thin air. His body was purple-red, his pupil-less eyes a violet-blue. His features were sharp and aggressive, yet at the same time possessed a kind of androgynous beauty.

The two long horns on his head shot straight up without any curve. The lines of his muscles were polished to a shine that didn't look real. Seeing Jace in front of him, the demon gave a cold smile and reached out with his left hand — unlike the female succubus, his hands weren't delicate human ones. They were black claws!

Jace nimbly stepped back and dodged the swipe. He had a feeling one rake from those things would shred his face.

The succubus was fast. Seeing Jace dodge, he whipped out a metal whip with one hand. Jace blocked it with his staff. The whip coiled around the staff with a clanging crash, the tip whipping around toward his face and nearly smacking him on the nose.

Jace grabbed the staff and yanked backward, trying to hold him in place. The succubus also pulled hard on the whip. This guy's strength was clearly far beyond that of the previous succubus. Jace used all his strength just barely to keep the other from tearing free.

Seeing he couldn't win a contest of strength, the succubus grabbed his own whip, spread his black wings, and took to the air, kicking at Jace's arm and staff with black hooves. The kick was vicious and fast, catching Jace before he could react and knocking the staff right out of his hands.

The opponent leaped into midair, beat his wings once more, and bounded away into the distance. Jace immediately cast Curse of Weakness — but the instant before the spell completed, the target vanished back into the shadows and was lost.

"Damn it!" Jace snatched up his staff and was about to give chase when a black sword came spinning from behind him and stabbed into something in midair. The male succubus appeared again with a howl and crashed into a pile of debris!

"Thank you, master." Morophil chuckled softly in his ear. Like a black panther, she bolted toward the spot where the male succubus had fallen. She leaped over several crates in her path and charged at the collapsed pile of junk. The male succubus had already scrambled to his feet and was fleeing into the distance.

Jace chased hard behind them. He rounded a corner and followed them into a broken-down, filthy corner of the city, the air thick with the stench of rotting garbage.

Seeing there was no escape left, the male succubus turned back. His features had turned savage, driven to desperation, completely losing the beauty from before.

With a sword in one hand and a whip in the other, he lashed out with the whip first, but Morophil caught it with one hand. His other hand thrust at her, but she wrapped her tail around his arm.

She leaped up and landed a clean kick to his face, slamming the male succubus to the ground.

The whole thing took no more than an instant. Her movements were so agile and eerie they didn't seem like something a humanoid creature could pull off.

By the time he clambered back up from the debris, the male succubus was already pinned under Morophil, his long horns in her grip and a sword aimed at his chest. He didn't dare move.

Noticing Jace's slightly surprised expression, Morophil said softly, "How about that, master? After all, the demons I've hunted most are probably my own kind. Well, except for the felhounds."
