Chapter 283: Jagin's Wish

The male succubus was genuinely gorgeous — gorgeous enough that looking at him threw Jace a little off balance. Though the most awkward part, really, was that thing dangling naked between his legs, swaying as he moved, weirdly shaped to the point of being impossible to ignore. The demon spat out a mouthful of purple blood and looked at Jace. "The huntress who doesn't serve mortals — she'll betray you sooner or later. Let me go, and I'll serve you, human. Together we'll kill her and rid you of that future threat!" "Serve me? I think you're overestimating the diversity of my sexual orientation a little," Jace said. The smile on the male succubus's face vanished. "What method did you use to resist mental magic?" "No idea. Maybe you're just too weak," Jace said. Morophil laughed and said in a low voice, "Master, kill him. I really can't stand his bullshit anymore!" "I still have questions to ask," Jace said. The male succubus looked up at Morophil then and said something in Demonic in a low voice, smiling. When Morophil finished listening, she cleanly sliced off the thing with her sword. The male succubus's once-beautiful face suddenly twisted out of shape. His eyes went blank in an instant. He coughed and swore a few things. "And now?" she asked with a laugh, switching to Common. The male succubus growled hoarsely, his claws digging into the dirt on the ground, eyes squeezed shut in pain, even forgetting how to breathe. That one strike from Morophil had undoubtedly wounded two people. Jace witnessed the whole thing and felt a chill run through his own lower half. "Why did you kill Jagin?" Jace asked. The male succubus gasped for a long while before lifting his head. "You mean my previous master?" "I don't even know his name… Maybe I don't even count as a servant. More like a guest who showed up halfway through the party." He drew a deep breath. "Clearly, he got wrong what he actually wanted." "He wanted?" Jace said. "Didn't you seduce him and get him killed?" "Mortals always like to make us responsible for their own ideas." The male succubus gave a soft laugh. "Why would we interfere with his decisions? Was he planning to kill us and send us back? No. So what benefit was there in killing him? Just for soul shards you can find anywhere in the Twisting Nether?" "Sajeda and I both wanted to satisfy his desires as much as possible. After all, who'd want to leave Azeroth? Well, at least I kept a bit of cool-headedness. That senseless bitch Sajeda even thought she could bring him back to his senses, so she refused to leave with me — but I'd caught on from the start, so I wasn't surprised in the least. I suppose you've already killed her? She deserved it anyway…" Could it really have been Jagin's choice? Jace felt he still didn't know this colleague nearly well enough. He just couldn't imagine — if things had gone that far, why hadn't he ordered the succubus to stop? Didn't he know having his belly cut open would kill him? Then again, he remembered that night. Even though his own skin had been sliced open and seared by flame, he hadn't felt any pain. It was only deep into the night that more and more neglected pains, more and more suspicions about his own abnormalities, had gradually let him feel a panic at losing control. That panic, like ice water, had extinguished his shadow fervor and pulled him slowly back to normal. And Jagin? Maybe that fervor was exactly what he'd been chasing. The feeling of being fully embraced by the Shadow. Of course, Jace would never know the truth now. The male succubus eyed the staff in Jace's hand. "I sensed your power earlier, warlock. You took this black staff from an Eredar, didn't you?" He raised his eyes weakly and looked at the succubus gripping his horns. "Is that why you're willing to serve him? But in the eyes of a shadow manipulator like him, you're nothing more than an expendable pawn that can be discarded at any moment. You really think you'll get some kind of return?" "Oh dear, oh dear." Morophil feigned surprise. "You're not trying to toy with me with that pitiful magic, are you? Or do you genuinely mean what you're saying? Your magical weakness is so profound I can't quite tell!" "No — it's a shadow-magic fluctuation. Truly impressively stupid." As she spoke, she yanked hard on the male succubus's horn. The latter let out a resentful growl and swore a few things in Demonic. "Send him back to the Twisting Nether," Jace said. Morophil drove her sword into the succubus's chest, then slashed upward with force, splitting his muscular torso straight down the middle. The demon convulsed and collapsed to the ground. The torn demon-heart leaped out of his body, pulsing. His body mumbled a few things and curled up, like a worm that had just been stomped flat on one side. "We need to deal with this demon's corpse," Jace said. "This place may be remote even by Old Town standards, but if we just leave it here, it'll definitely draw attention within a few days." "I could help you move this demon back to that young mage's apartment," Morophil suggested. "That way when he wakes up tomorrow morning, he'll have a perfectly reasonable explanation for his mysteriously exposed body and all those stains in the middle of the night." "He's got a reasonable explanation now," Jace said. "Namely that he had an incredibly terrifying wet dream in the middle of the night. All right, stop making trouble for me." Morophil smiled. Jace looked at the demon's corpse. "If I burn him with shadow fire, what'll be left?" "Nothing!" Morophil said. "He's not a living being of Azeroth, master. He's just a lump of flesh condensed from dark energy." Jace nodded, spoke the incantation, and set the demon's body alight. Purple flames danced wildly in the night. Jace watched that body — which could almost have been called a work of art — shrivel, blacken, and curl in the fire, slowly turning into a black stain that no longer resembled anything at all, until finally it vanished completely in a cloud of black smoke. Jace stared at the scorched demon blood left on the ground in silence for a moment, then said, "I just remembered — he asked you a question in Demonic earlier." Morophil glanced over. "You're still hung up on that?" "Just curious." "He thought he was stronger than you!" "Stronger than me?" Jace said. "But then why did you cut off his…" Morophil said, "I needed to give him a clear enough answer, didn't I?" "Ah, that kind of stronger." Jace nodded and asked again, "So if you hadn't cut it, the answer wouldn't have been clear enough?" "You really want to compare lengths with a male succubus?" "Forget it." Jace waved a hand. "Forget I asked."
