Chapter 285: A Good Teacher

After everyone had left, only Jace and Ursula remained in the basement. Today was Saturday — no classes all day. Jace didn't really want to go back home and face Morophil either. He wasn't the only one with psychological scars from last night. "I thought you didn't care much about Jagin," Ursula said, holding her wine cup. Jace glanced at her. He honestly couldn't tell if he was sad right now or feeling something else entirely. But a person he'd known was dead, so he just nodded. "He taught me a lot!" Ursula nodded. "Jagin always wanted to teach me incantations too, even though it was just a waste of time. At first I felt it was a shame I couldn't learn any magic. Now that I think about it, not touching something this dangerous isn't the worst outcome." Jace said, "The Shadow is genuinely dangerous. It can influence a person's emotions, making them impulsive, irritable, suspicious… the more you use it, the stronger these negative emotions become. As a caster, I think we should find ways to resist that infectiousness. Jagin clearly mistook those Shadow-induced influences for his own thoughts." "Is that your own conclusion?" Ursula asked. "Didn't Galders say something about it?" Jace said. "I always thought you didn't want to learn magic because you couldn't be bothered. Now it seems you actually tried?" "Of course I tried," Ursula said. "Maybe I'm the exact opposite of you. You seem to have an exceptionally strong talent, but as for me… Galders once told me that the Shadow refuses no one, but that line just doesn't work on me." Jace said, "Try casting Curse of Weakness on me. The one Jagin brought up during the meeting last time." "On me?" "It's not like it'll do any harm." "All right…" Ursula nodded, sat up straight, and intoned, "Anun'jii, Dur'zala, Kana…" She pronounced it quite standardly and skillfully, but Jace genuinely couldn't sense any movement of shadow magic. Given Jace's current sensitivity to shadow magic, let alone a completed incantation — the moment a warlock began chanting an incantation, or a demon suddenly moved, causing a sudden shift in shadow energy, he would feel a subtle fluctuation. Could there really be someone who actively embraced the Shadow, and the Shadow couldn't be bothered to deal with them? Jace asked, "You have been practicing seriously, right? Otherwise you wouldn't know the incantation this clearly." "Of course. I want to participate too." "Have you ever considered that your inability to learn shadow magic might have something to do with your own personality?" "My personality?" Ursula laughed. "What's wrong with my personality? It sounds strange that personality would affect casting." "I would have thought it was ridiculous too." Jace's mind flashed to that night with Morophil, and he took a deep breath. "Do you have any potions that can influence emotions? Something that makes a person excited, or depressed, or angry…" "Yes." Ursula raised a finger. "A Rage Potion — or Fury Potion, if you prefer. It completely ignites a person's fury…" "Drink one and try again," Jace said. "Will that work?" "I don't know," Jace said. "No harm in trying." The two of them went into the alchemy lab. The alchemist buried herself in the cabinets rummaging around, finally pulling out a reddish potion from the very back. "This one's perfect. If I don't get rid of it soon it'll expire and go to waste. I honestly forgot why I made this in the first place. Probably something Jagin wanted." She looked at Jace. "I drink it now?" Jace gave an "mm." Ursula closed her eyes and took a small sip, then set the bottle aside. After a while, she couldn't help laughing. "Damn it, there's nothing to get angry about either. Or maybe this potion didn't work?" "You're very annoying," Jace said flatly. "What?" Ursula looked like she'd been suddenly slapped. She asked unhily, "Why would you say that out of nowhere?" "I felt like it." "Are you looking for a fight?" she snapped. "And what the hell are you?!" "Right now," Jace said. "Curse of Weakness!" Ursula caught on immediately. She raised her hand and, using the casting stance she'd described to Jagin earlier, spoke the Curse of Weakness incantation at Jace. Jace suddenly felt a faint dizziness, like a blood-sugar drop. He didn't feel his legs go weak or anything, but he could already feel the shadow energy taking effect — faint, but clear enough. He took a couple of hard breaths to fight off the dizziness. Was this the Curse of Weakness? It should be. The power wasn't that strong, and the duration was only two or three seconds, but at least it had been cast successfully! "I think you did it!" He looked over. Ursula was still in a state of confusion. "I did it? But I didn't see anything at all… Although I did feel like something was off, wasn't that just a placebo effect?" Jace said, "All right, do you know any other incantations? Something with a more obvious effect. Like… Curse of Agony, for instance. Sulenna knows that one." "I'm not about to cast Curse of Agony on you, Jace." Ursula leaned against the cabinet beside her. "Maybe next time. I feel a bit tired." "Look," Jace said. "Your energy was noticeably drained when you channeled the shadow energy, but you definitely didn't do anything else physical, right? That's important evidence the spell took effect." "I actually forgot about that," Ursula said. "It's true, Jace. You say it and I can cast magic that easily, when I tried so many times with them and it never worked!" Jace lay back in the wooden chair where Ursula normally slept. "This is something I figured out from your own experience, but you can't rely on drinking potions to pump yourself up and cast every time in the future, can you?" "Why not?" Ursula picked up the Rage Potion and glanced at it. "Drinking this doesn't have any obvious side effects, does it? With my temper, I probably wouldn't reach the effect from just now without the potion." She hesitated for a moment, then asked, "Do you really find me that annoying?" "That's a strange question to ask," Jace sat up. "Would I be lying here if I hated you? Otherwise, what was I supposed to say to make you angry — tell you you're a wonderful person with great alchemy skills and tons of knowledge? Then that almost-expired Rage Potion would've gone to waste." "Fine." Ursula gave an awkward laugh, then asked, "You're going to sleep here?" Jace glanced at the doorway. "If I'm in your way I can go out to the hall. It's just that in the main room I don't feel comfortable. No sense of security. I'm always worried Erland's going to slit my throat." "Of course not. Go ahead and sleep." Ursula busied herself tidying up bottles and jars as she spoke. "Actually, Erland doesn't dislike you that much. He just thought you were getting too close to Jagin." "Well. We're distant now." As he said this, Jace was already unable to fight off his sleepiness. He hadn't slept a wink last night, and this chair was just too comfortable. Ursula was still talking to herself about something, but he couldn't make out the words anymore.
