Chapter 36: Touching Darkness

The potion's effects wore off after an hour, and sprinting from here to Old Town alone might take half an hour.

Climbing slopes, taking stairs, crossing the canal, advancing along crooked little alleys — by the time he saw the gate to Old Town, Jace felt like he was about to collapse.

It was already dark by the time he could read what was on the note.

"Limited Protection, across the street, second floor, door on the left."

He wandered around for about ten minutes before spotting the Limited Protection shop. The sky had darkened, and reading the sign wasn't easy.

Across the street stood an unremarkable little building. Jace had no watch, so he could only guess how much time he had left by feel.

When the potion started wearing off, his face would grow hot and his eyes would water. If those signs appeared, it meant less than twenty minutes of potency remained.

He hadn't felt anything like that yet, so there was still time.

He climbed to the second floor and came to the door on the left. Through the window on the far side, he could see a light on inside — someone was home.

Jace knocked on the door. The potion wearing off any moment made him frantic, and he forgot all about being polite.

"Sulenna, open up. I'm Jace Sesso — remember me? A week ago—"

Before he could finish, the door was flung open and a middle-aged man appeared.

The man was wearing underdrawers and nothing but a white tank top on top. He looked at Jace with barely suppressed fury, like he was staring at an enemy.

He turned his head toward the room. "Sulenna Keldom, who is this?"

"How should I know?"

Sulenna approached the door clutching a robe over her bare upper body, glanced at Jace, and said, "Some brat who makes coin testing spells for us."

"Is that so?" the man asked coldly.

"Gain… darling, you doubt me?" Sulenna leaned against him seductively, fingers tracing his lower belly. She couldn't even spare a hand to hold her robe, and the falling fabric left little to hide. Jace awkwardly lowered his head.

"What's some shy little brat to me? Would I betray you for some fledgling? Gain, you're the strongest among us — only you can lead us down the right path. I know that better than anyone."

With that, Sulenna turned her head and said coldly, "Do yourself a favor and leave. I genuinely don't understand why you'd come this late."

Jace thought about his potion about to expire, heard these two talking about him like that, and was already boiling with rage. Before he could blow up, the man called Gain suddenly said, "Hold on, boy. You want to earn coin, don't you? I've got a new spell here — help me test its effect, and I'll give you two silver."

He let out an unfriendly laugh.

Jace knew the man was going to hit him with something nasty. He must have thought Jace was Sulenna's secret lover and wanted to teach him a lesson.

Even if not, it'd serve as a warning to Sulenna on the side.

Then Jace finally remembered who these people were.

In the game, this Gain was one of the leaders of the warlocks in the Slaughtered Lamb's basement, but in the game he looked much older.

This Sulenna Keldom was his student and also his secret lover, but in the end she'd eloped with a petty thief and made off with a precious Bloodstone pendant.

In the game, Gain would have warlock players go to a farm and kill Sulenna and her lover to reclaim the Bloodstone pendant.

The guy was a born cuckold — he'd been getting cheated on for over a decade starting from this point, and he still hadn't figured it out after all these years?

Since he had a Shadow Protection Potion on him, might as well not waste it. This warlock wanted to hit him with something big — perfect. If he was going to learn, he'd learn the strongest spell these people had.

"Into the street," Gain said. "So this little wretch won't be afraid I'll kill him."

"Aren't you afraid of being seen?" Sulenna hesitated.

"You come too," Gain told her. "Watch how my new spell deals with your little darling."

"How many times do I have to say it before you believe me?" Sulenna said, shaking with anger. She glared at Jace and turned back inside to change.

Gain threw on a shirt and headed out without waiting for Sulenna, going straight downstairs.

The three of them got to the street. There weren't many people around — a few passersby, and some workers sitting outside drinking in the distance.

Jace's expression was grim, worried that Gain's power might exceed his estimates and burn through the potion's effects too quickly.

Gain laughed. "Barely any nerve at all. I don't need to get a manslaughter charge over this. Just be ready."

"Hurry up, stop talking," Jace said. "Don't forget the two silver."

"As you wish." Gain dropped his smile. He had no staff, no casting focus of any kind — just made a simple gesture with his hand and muttered a short incantation.

Short, but Jace still remembered it clearly — roughly, "Fekt."

He repeated it over and over to avoid forgetting, while keeping his eyes locked on Gain, carefully feeling for any pain, injury, or whatever else might come.

Before long, he felt disturbed and uneasy. Watching Gain's confident expression, he actually started worrying more and more that the man might kill him.

Jace glanced back at the men drinking around the corner, confirming they could see what was happening here, and prepared to run over and call for help if he couldn't take the damage.

But he quickly realized that even with all his preparations, he still couldn't set his mind at ease.

By now Jace's palms were drenched in sweat — yet there was no pain, no dizziness or nausea.

Had the spell even taken effect? Why was he so nervous?

The sounds around him grew quieter and quieter. He just kept repeating "Fekt, Fekt…" — afraid he'd forget, afraid he'd come all this way for nothing, afraid the Shadow Protection Potion would go to waste.

Then he felt his face grow hot and flushed, his eyes watering — that was the feeling of the Shadow Protection Potion about to wear off, he remembered.

He started to fear that once the potion wore off, the spell's damage might hit him all at once. He had no idea how much shadow energy the potion had actually absorbed.

Jace gradually realized he was irrationally afraid of everything there was to be afraid of. What was going on?

Fekt… Fekt…

Then Gain's confident expression froze.

He muttered something under his breath that caught Jace's attention. Jace watched him take a few steps back, his whole body starting to tremble for some reason.

Sulenna noticed something was wrong and ran over to grab his arm.

"Gain, darling, what's wrong?"

"No…" Gain muttered. "No… I don't want to be here, I… no!"

He suddenly lost control and screamed, sobbing as he bolted away into the distance. The front of his trousers was soaked through — had he… pissed himself?

Sulenna went pale with fright at the scene. She glared at Jace and shouted, "If anything happens to him, I'll make you pay, you little shit!"

With that, she ran off after Gain. The men drinking glanced over curiously, but only for a moment before going back to their own conversations.

Now Jace was left standing there alone. He more or less figured out what had happened.

The other man had cast Fear. Without the Shadow Protection Potion, the one pissing himself in terror would have been Jace.

Jace would have lost all face in the middle of the street. Gain must have figured that if Jace really was involved with Sulenna, this would make the biggest spectacle.

Though Jace didn't know how he'd channeled shadow magic to cast the same spell, regardless — this counted as the first spell he'd ever successfully cast.

Amid a surge of irrepressible excitement, he felt like he was missing something that kept the excitement from reaching its peak.

Right — where was the money?

He looked toward the head of the alley. They were long gone. Those two silver were probably a lost cause.
