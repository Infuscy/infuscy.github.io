Chapter 378: The Day Job Still Has to Go On

The next morning, Jace arrived at Malin's study. The door was open. When he pushed it, he found Malin inside talking with an apprentice.

"Mr. Sesso, you've returned at just the right time," Malin said to the apprentice beside him, who looked no older than fifteen or sixteen. "You may go to class now, Mr. Magelt."

"Yes, Master." The apprentice bowed to Malin and left the room.

Malin sat behind his desk, pulling open a drawer and taking out a stack of documents as he spoke. "I have a document here that needs to be delivered to Moonbrook. The school there was just rebuilt this year. The mayor picked out a few children who might have arcane talent and plans to send them to the Wizard's Sanctum for evaluation. Master Andromath has put me in charge of this — this is the Wizard's Sanctum's reply letter."

"I was going to ask Mr. Magelt to go, but as you know, the road from here to Moonbrook isn't exactly safe. I'm a bit concerned about that boy's well-being. If you go, I'll rest much easier. Besides, ever since the King gave you that horse, you haven't had a chance to take her out for a ride around town, have you?"

"No problem, Master." Jace took the document Malin handed him. "When does it need to arrive?"

Malin said, "Today's the 22nd. I think getting it there before the end of June will do. The mayor wants the children to be ready before the Harvest Festival. Three months should be plenty of time."

"You just got back from Menethil Harbor, didn't you? Take a few days to rest. No need to rush."

Jace said, "I'd still rather set out as soon as possible, Master. After all, those children will be leaving home at such a young age to travel far and live on their own in Stormwind — they'll need quite a while to adjust."

"It reminds you of yourself, doesn't it?" Malin said with a smile.

Jace nodded, then asked, "By the way, where's Amy? I didn't see her this morning."

"She's at Nethergarde Keep," Malin said, pushing the drawer shut and looking up. "She's been gone for about two weeks now."

"Is the situation at the Dark Portal getting difficult?" Jace asked.

Malin said, "Nothing critical yet, but she insisted on going, and Nethergarde is short-handed, so Master Andromath approved her request. From what I can see, the Expedition's progress has still been fairly smooth. After breaking through the Horde's defenses at the Dark Portal, they haven't run into much fierce resistance along the way. But the further the army pushes in, the more strained the supply lines are getting."

"The Expedition's campaign is still primarily Lordaeron's affair — I'm only keeping up on the side." The old mage tapped his fingers on the desk. "There's something I've been meaning to ask. When you were in Menethil Harbor, did you run into Master Morganth and the others?"

"No, but I met a Kirin Tor mage," Jace said. "He was tracking our progress too. He told me a lot of orcs are holed up in the mountains of Khaz Modan, waiting for a chance to retake Dun Algaz and cut off the link between Ironforge and Menethil Harbor."

"The Kirin Tor has indeed been very concerned about that. Those orcs might even include members of the Dragonmaw clan." Malin rolled the quill in his hand. "King Wrynn is very worried about the Blackrock orcs and the Dragonmaw clan joining forces. If they find an opportunity, they could very well invade the south again."

"Red dragons... black dragons..." the old mage said with a deep sigh. "At this rate, they might be even more troublesome than the Dark Portal."

"How's your Counterspell practice going?"

"Ah?" Jace blinked. "Not much to show for it..."

Malin said, "I think you should find a mage of roughly your level to practice with. Don't try to interrupt their casting right away — start from a normal state and get a feel for it. This kind of magic is still quite dangerous."

Jace admitted, "I don't really know any other mages in the Mage Quarter. And Amy's at Nethergarde."

"Make more friends, Jace." Malin stood up. "I have a class to teach. If you've got nothing to do, brew me a pot of tea — I'll be back for it warm in a bit."

"Oh, Master — would I have a chance to go to Nethergarde?" Jace asked.

Malin leaned against the door and looked back at him. "You're hoping to use a trip to Nethergarde to dodge your work here, Mr. Sesso? Forget it. You keep an eye on the Redridge situation for me first."

"Understood, Master."

After Malin left, Jace thought about practicing Counterspell. Since there were no other mages around, he'd just have to use the two demons for target practice.

The imp should work well — that thing could cast Firebolt.

Coming out of Malin's study, Jace retrieved the vial of spores from Ursula and headed to Alchemy Needs. He walked in and set the vial on the counter.

Hearing a customer, the shopkeeper Ruth Lumere came out from the back, eyeing the vial with curiosity. "bog beast spores?"

"Could you help me raise it, ma'am?" Jace asked, leaning on the counter.

"To raise it... it might end up destroying my entire garden," Lumere said with a shake of her head.

"I don't need to grow it into one of those giant bog beasts from the Wetlands," Jace said. "I need a drought-resistant variety — a Bramble Beast that grows in arid regions."

Lumere looked at Jace, then at the jar. "You're sure you can grow something like that?"

"An herbalist in Thelsamar told me it could be done," Jace said.

"I've actually heard of this method of growing different bog beasts by controlling the environment," she said. "Are you a mage from the Wizard's Sanctum? Show me your ring. If it's part of a research project, I'll certainly help you."

Jace handed her his ring to look at. Lumere narrowed her eyes. "You're Sesso... Jace Sesso?"

"That's right," Jace said. "I've sold you herbs a few times before, ma'am."

Lumere leaned in. "I heard you killed a Horde death knight. Is that true or not?"

"It's true," Jace said. "In Dalaran."

"No, that's not right," Lumere said. "I heard it was in Redridge... with that sister of the Expedition leader, Lady Alleria Windrunner. What was her name... that elven lady."

"Vereesa! It's Vereesa Windrunner!"

From the doorway of a nearby room, her daughter Maria Lumere was carrying a small box past, glancing over. When she suddenly spotted Jace, her expression immediately changed.

"Jace Sesso? Is that you? My goodness, I'm actually looking at the mage who killed Teron Gorefiend!"

"The death knight you killed was Teron Gorefiend?" Lumere said in surprise, looking at her daughter. "You didn't mention it was that... fellow."

"I heard it from Uncle Carter. He told me that even inside Stormwind Keep, not many people know the whole story," Maria said.

"I imagine there are still quite a few Horde stragglers hiding in the corners of the Kingdom of Stormwind, ladies," Jace explained. "The King doesn't want too much publicity, lest they develop ideas about revenge. And I was only part of the hunt — please don't put all the credit for killing that thing on me."

"You're far too humble, sir," Maria said. "If I'd seen that thing, I'd have fainted on the spot. I heard even his warhorse was an undead monster."

Lumere said, "Mr. Sesso, rest easy. Leave these spores with me. I'll write to a colleague in Dalaran and have them look into how to cultivate a Bramble Beast for me."

"Thank you so much, ma'am," Jace said. "I'll leave it in your hands."
