## Chapter 392: The Dark Master's Legacy

Friday morning, Jace set out from Stormwind with the two demons. They spent the night in Goldshire, and the next day crossed the Nafeltari River to the south bank. It was dusk by the time they arrived.

The dim light naturally carried a gloomy atmosphere, especially once they entered Duskwood.

After crossing to the uninhabited lands south of the Nafeltari River, the imp started chattering away at Jace the whole way, telling him how it had discovered this incantation. Jace carried Gorefiend's staff on his back, and whenever the imp spoke the incantation words he'd guessed at, he always felt as though that shadow gem was responding from time to time.

This incantation was definitely no ordinary thing.

Savia, Avarus, Anthea, Minuelle, Wilma.

This was definitely demonic. With Jace's current vocabulary, he could even roughly sense what this incantation was trying to express.

But it had to be said — the feeling when speaking it was even stranger than the demonic he'd heard before, or rather, far less strange than other demonic speech.

Perhaps this was what the imp had described — the calm, intelligent evil of Eredar speech.

Spinning... darkness... annihilation... summoning...

Feeling the deep meaning contained within these incantation words, Jace even felt himself getting a little nervous.

They walked deep into the night. Even in summer, Duskwood was deathly quiet — they didn't even hear the faint, elusive beast calls in the forest that he'd felt the last time he'd come here with Vereesa.

Because the two demons were here, especially Morophil, even the shadow-corrupted beasts knew they shouldn't provoke this crimson otherworldly killer.

Jace looked at the half-dead old trees all around, the withered, lifeless weeds and mud covering the ground, and said, "I think this is far enough."

He cast Arcane Intellect on himself. "If I pass out from mana exhaustion, remember to carry me somewhere safe, Morophil."

The succubus nodded. "I will protect you with my soul, master."

"If some kind of shadow loss of control happens, remind me — don't let me get blown up by magic flying from who-knows-where for no reason."

"Protect me, Sazeno. If something truly unavoidable happens, use your Blood Pact on me or Morophil."

"No problem, master!" the imp shouted. "I can't wait to see my master put this magic into practice. How exciting!"

Jace popped two cherries into his mouth, savoring the sweetness of the fruit, giving his spirits a slight boost that had been tainted by this dark forest.

"Morophil, one more thing to watch for. I mean, just in case — if my will gets eroded by shadow power to the point of losing control of my emotions, try to stop me with mental magic."

"I can't," Morophil said bluntly. "It's not that I don't want to — it's that I'm powerless. I can't charm you, master. Even when I was first summoned, I could only charm you for a few seconds when you were completely off guard. Stopping you from doing something now is completely beyond me."

"Trust your own will, master. After all, it's because of that that I was drawn to this world."

Jace looked at Morophil in silence for a moment. "I understand."

He looked at the Elixir of Wisdom Ursula had given him, but in the end put it back in his potion bag. He'd use it after he'd gotten a feel for things — after all, the potion's effect only lasted a few hours.

He took a few deep breaths, fixing his eyes on an oak tree up ahead that had broken off for some reason. That would be his target, whatever the incantation did.

He raised Gorefiend's staff, lifting the gem into midair.

No casting gestures, no other cues — only the incantation. He had to find a suitable casting motion on his own.

This was Jace's first time exploring such a difficult incantation entirely on his own.

Spinning... darkness... annihilation... summoning...

Savia... Avarus.

As he spoke the words, Jace felt the area around him grow dark, as if the shadow power had already heard his call.

Last time, in Elwynn Forest, the sensation hadn't been this obvious. Perhaps it was because he was mentally prepared for a shadow ritual of this kind.

Anthea, Minuelle, Wilma...

This feeling rose as he finished the incantation, lifted by his rapidly depleting energy, and then vanished among the treetops.

Jace took a few breaths. At this point, he actually felt a little tired.

And the two demons' expressions weren't as relaxed as before either. They both raised their heads and looked up high — right where that feeling of his had disappeared.

"You sensed it too?" he asked.

"Quite obviously, master," Morophil said with a shake of her head. "Although I've fought countless demons and seen all sorts of strange demonic warlocks cast all sorts of strange magic, I've never encountered a power like this. But strangely, I can sense a hint of... emotion from this magic."

"What emotion?" Jace asked.

"Hatred... loneliness." Morophil smiled slightly.

Sensing emotion from dark magic wasn't strange, given how strongly shadow magic correlated with emotion. And a succubus, being such an emotional demon, was even more sensitive to feelings.

Jace also sensed that deep in his heart, he'd felt a hint of anger while speaking the incantation. He'd assumed it was frustration from failing to cast successfully, but with Morophil's reminder, he realized it was indeed the influence of the energy brought by the incantation.

He touched his potion bag, but in the end let his hand drop. He couldn't exactly drink a mana potion on his first attempt, could he? He still had the whole night ahead.

Jace rested nearby for a while, eating the snacks he'd brought while recalling the sensations from just now. When he felt his energy had mostly recovered, he tried again.

But this time the feeling was similar — no improvement. He had to change his approach.

Hatred. Loneliness.

Could it be that he needed to experience these two emotions to help release the incantation?

Hatred — he couldn't quite think of anything off the top of his head. But loneliness...

He thought back to the period just after he'd first arrived in this world. There had never been a time lonelier than that.

He'd felt completely out of place in Azeroth, a total outsider. Even his own birth parents had been difficult for him to accept for a long time.

He'd had so much he wanted to say but no one to say it to. So many emotions he wanted to express but could only keep locked in his heart.

Not a single person in this world could understand what he felt, could appreciate his difficulties. And of course, he couldn't tell them. If he did, he'd die a miserable death.

It wasn't until he grew older, until he could finally leave home and go out into the world to play with people his own age, that he was finally able to slowly let go of those years of suppressed emotions, bury them in his heart, and try to live like a normal Lordaeronian, grow up like a normal child, and accept everything about this world. Now, thinking back on those memories, it already felt like something from hundreds of years ago.

After resting for a few hours, once his energy had mostly recovered, he took out the green bottle and drank a little Elixir of Wisdom, then walked to the center of the clearing again.

Recalling the thoughts and feelings from just now, he spoke in a low voice: "Savia, Avarus, Anthea, Minuelle, Wilma."

That familiar feeling appeared again. Jace's energy lifted the shadow power, gathering it from all directions. Right then, he saw countless purple stars in the sky begin to spin like long serpents — but just as he was stunned by the spectacle, his attention wavered, and those serpents scattered like sand dropped into water, vanishing in an instant...

Jace collapsed onto the ground from exhaustion, panting heavily, eyes wide open at the now-empty sky. The imp's excited shrieks grew more and more distant.

At that moment, the murmuring voice from Gorefiend's memories surfaced in his mind once more.

Jace realized — that wasn't Gorefiend's voice. That was... another orc's voice. The voice of an orc warlock whom Gorefiend had worshipped and admired!

Gul'dan... In that moment, Gorefiend had wanted to remember Gul'dan's incantation. That's why, even as his soul dissipated, he couldn't forget it — and instead imprinted it into Jace's mind!

This was Gul'dan's magic!
