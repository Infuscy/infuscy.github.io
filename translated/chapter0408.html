Chapter 408: Another Chance

In the days since arriving at Stonard, aside from following the paladins out on that first day, Jace had spent the next couple of days helping the priests.

That evening, a group of wounded under escort by Lordaeron cavalry bearing white-shield-emblem banners arrived at Stonard. Jace joined a crowd of laboring soldiers here in rushing over to help carry the wounded to the open ground outside the priests' den. Before long it started raining, and he helped move the wounded who couldn't fit under the tents into the orc den.

After a busy afternoon like that, Jace hadn't even gotten a meal. He changed clothes and sat down to rest in a shelter near the orc den. A few soldiers who seemed to be from Stormwind were sitting nearby, talking about the condition of the wounded.

One soldier asked worriedly, "Why are so many injured suddenly being sent here? Has the situation up ahead gotten worse?"

Another soldier answered, "No. These soldiers are probably ones who can't fight for now and have been sent to the rear to rest. According to the soldiers at the front, the demons around Nethergarde Keep have been mostly driven back. General Mograine is preparing to break the siege of the Giant Hammer Fortress. If the Giant Hammer Fortress falls to the demons, retaking the Altar of Storms will be even harder, and even Nethergarde Keep will face a direct threat from the demon army."

Jace knew the Giant Hammer Fortress was the first stronghold the orcs had established on Azeroth, considerably larger in scale than Stonard, which had started out as little more than a watchpost. In a decade and a half, this place would be occupied by ogres — perhaps because the fall of Lordaeron and the destruction of Dalaran had left the Alliance unable to maintain control of this region. The soldier went on, "Reinforcements have been arriving too. More gryphon riders from Ironforge have come. Patrolling this swamp and the mountains around Nethergarde Keep will be easier from here on."

A soldier sitting behind him said, "I wonder when this will end… Demons keep pouring out of the Dark Portal without pause, but we can't keep going like that. This isn't like fighting orcs."

"What about the situation on the other side of the Dark Portal?" Jace couldn't help asking.

Several soldiers looked over. One of them shook his head. "No news at all."

A soldier with a thick beard said, "The earliest reports the Wildhammer riders brought back said demons hold the Dark Portal on the other side too. And I don't know if you've seen the situation over there — on this side, the Dark Portal stands all by itself in the middle of nowhere, looking like a little green window from a distance. But the Dark Portal on the other side is like a massive castle. Once the demons hold it, taking it back would be like conquering Nethergarde Keep all over again."

"You've been to the other side?"

"I was in the transport corps. Went twice."

"As long as we win here again and push through the Dark Portal, linking up with the Expedition on the other side, victory is certain."

Jace knew the people here had no idea that Draenor was on the verge of collapse — it was just that world's destruction wasn't unfolding as quickly as he'd expected. He wondered whether, in these few days, the Expedition could make it back from across all of Draenor to the Dark Portal?

Just thinking about it, it seemed nearly impossible. Draenor was a sufficiently massive planet too — not as large as Azeroth, sure, but it was still an entire world.

Now that Ner'zhul had decided to use the three holy artifacts to open portals, it meant he'd been driven to the end of his rope, holed up somewhere in a corner of Draenor.

He might be trapped right now in the Karabor Temple in Shadowmoon Valley, while the Alliance Expedition had already reached the foot of the temple.

The distance between that temple and the Dark Portal… judging by the situation on this side, it was unimaginable. After all, the Alliance Expedition had fought their way almost unimpeded after defeating the remnants of the Horde army at the Dark Portal, and it had still taken them the better part of a year to reach the temple.

Getting back would mean facing demons manifesting everywhere amid Draenor's spatial rifts, the obstruction of Horde remnants, and walking atop ground that was slowly splitting open beneath a murky sky.

Jace could hardly imagine the immense catastrophe unfolding on the other side. Compared to that, he'd been downright lucky in this swamp.

The next morning, the portal mages gathered again on the open ground near Stonard's main gate. Another round of teleportations was about to begin.

Unlike at the Wizard Sanctum, where they opened portals relying on that grand teleportation gate, the mages here waited for the portal channels to open before using their power to stabilize the entry and exit on this side. Even so, Jace could still feel the surging arcane energy tearing at the fabric of space — the intensity was no less than what he'd felt at the Wizard Sanctum.

The mages were exhausting themselves to keep these gates stable. Even with several mages from the School of Transmutation who'd arrived earlier pitching in to help maintain them, it was clear how difficult it was for every single one of them.

Before long, one brilliantly-robed mage after another stepped out of the portal. Among them was Magino Dumas, master of the Wizard Sanctum.

After speaking with Barry Fred for a while, he returned to the group of mages who'd come with him. Fred gathered the mages who'd been stationed here for the past several days.

"Starting now, Master Dumas will take over Stonard's magical defense operations. More mages specializing in demon hunting will be arriving in succession. Colleagues of the Wizard Sanctum — Nethergarde Keep now needs more mages to assist in the counterattack. Those willing to go, please come to me and give me your names."

At last they were mustering mages for Nethergarde Keep again. But this time, fewer mages stepped forward eagerly. Even those who intended to sign up didn't have the same fiery look on their faces as the full mages who'd been on the verge of battle that first day.

Perhaps the days of fighting and exhaustion had worn down their will. And of course, the news from the front had given most people a taste of the demons' cruelty.

"Jace Sesso." Jace came before Fred and gave his name. "I feel the magic I've mastered is… enough to be of help on the front lines, Mr. Fred."

Barry Fred nodded. "Of course, Mr. Sesso. I'm actually quite surprised Master Marin didn't put you on the support roster from the very beginning."

Watching Fred write his name on the thin scroll with his pen, Jace finally set his mind at ease.

Before setting out, Jace went over to Ferby Golobo's side. Ferby asked, "They agreed to let you go to Nethergarde Keep, Jace Sesso?"

"Yeah," Jace said. "Maybe they figure there's no need for too many combat-only mages here?"

Ferby said, "Jace, I think Nethergarde Keep is the right place for you. I might have to stay here for now — the wound on my leg doesn't even let me walk properly yet."

"The work in the rear is just as important," Jace said. "Take good care of the wounded here, Mr. Golobo."

"Of course," Ferby said. "When you get back, be sure to tell me how many demons you killed with Fireball!"

"Probably hard to count, Ferby."

Both of them laughed.
