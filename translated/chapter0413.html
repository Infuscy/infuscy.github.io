Chapter 413: Demon Onslaught

A blue-skinned monster over two meters tall leaped straight out of the green flames. Clad in heavy armor, its steps were clumsy, but it closed the distance in huge strides and swung a massive polearm in a crushing arc at a shield-bearing soldier!

The soldier was blasted clear through his shield, sent flying into a pile of stone blocks, fate unknown. The charging demon was immediately snared by a Slow spell and crashed to the ground. Two crossbow bolts hit him square in the shoulder and back, but they didn't stop him from climbing back to his feet — until a pikeman stepped in from the front and drove a spear up through his jaw, and another soldier thrust a spear into his chest. It took several men working together to push him back down before they finally put an end to him.

Another fast-moving Felguard had already charged into the pike formation. Though pierced through in multiple spots and pinned in place, unable to advance, the creature kept driving its massive hooves against the ground and swinging the two long blades in its hands, howling like a madman at the soldiers before it. Despite the soldiers' best efforts to hold him back, they got splattered head to toe with the fel blood spewing from his mouth.

"Pierce their hearts! Stab the demons in the heart!"

An officer shouted the reminder. By now the air was thick with the stench of filth and the acrid smell of fel fire. Jace was reeling from it, barely able to focus on chanting Fireballs and hurling them at every demon that tried to close in on the formation.

Shouts, howls, prayers, and curses rose and fell in waves — it was nearly impossible to tell which voices were demons and which were human. A bloated, corpulent Mo'arg demon staggered forward, swinging its arms wildly. Its hands had been chopped off and replaced with two flails, and it smashed its way straight into the soldiers. A mage promptly hit the Mo'arg in the face with a Fireball, blowing the thing to a bloody pulp — but the blast knocked over a shieldman nearby who'd been trying to engage it.

"Damn... sorry!" the young mage's voice trembled as he called out. But the soldier below brushed the flames off his cloak and shouted up, "Don't you dare go soft on me, mage — I'm still alive!"

In the distance, more Felguards of roughly the same height came charging with giant swords and polearms. They strode over the shattered remains of the Infernal, some still wreathed in flames as they ran. The knight captain roared at the soldiers to regroup, but it was clear — after being scattered by the Infernal's debris and the spreading fel fire, getting the soldiers back into their original formation was going to be extremely difficult.

The mages each unleashed Slow spells or frost magic at the charging Felguards. Jace chanted Fireball after Fireball while watching Amy's Frostbolt punch straight through a Felguard's torso and abdomen. Ice spread rapidly across its waist and thighs, hampering its movement. The surrounding soldiers saw the Felguard go down and swarmed it, stabbing its chest.

Jace's Fireball struck the ground at a Felguard's feet, blasting it back several steps. As it tried to regain its balance, several crossbow bolts tore through its abdomen and thighs, and two spearmen closed in to drive their weapons into its chest. The fel fire in its eyes guttered out.

"Keep at it, mages! They're done for!" a shout rang out from somewhere. After the chaos of the Infernal smashing through, the soldiers in the front had finally found their rhythm again with the mages' support.

The first few Felguards had been surrounded and cut down by squads of soldiers. The rest fell under a barrage of crossbow bolts and magic. The Felguards who charged in behind them now slammed into the soldiers' regrouped shields and were picked off one by one by long spears. These monsters, who'd relied purely on brute force to charge, quickly lost the advantage once cut off from magical support.

Jace gathered his focus again, trying to pick out demonic silhouettes through the flames — when he noticed some faint shadows circling toward the darkened areas around the mine.

But those shadows... were completely invisible to the naked eye!

"There are invisible demons!" he tried to shout to the knight captain not far away. "Demons have infiltrated behind us! I can smell Succubi!"

The knight captain glanced over with a look of utter confusion — either he didn't believe Jace, or more likely, he simply couldn't hear him over the din.

Then a mage nearby, in the middle of chanting a Frostbolt, suddenly broke off with a shriek. A faint purple phantom materialized behind him, a bloodied dagger freshly drawn from his back!

Jace cursed under his breath. They'd spotted it too late.

The Succubus was trying to flee, but before she could vanish again, she took a blast of Arcane Missiles to the body. Her slight frame was blown apart and she crashed to the ground — but she'd already created enough chaos. The mages could no longer cast in peace, each one now worried about a Succubus at their back.

Jace tried to focus and Detect Demons to scan the area near their group. The moment he concentrated, he saw a Succubus creeping up behind a mage in front of him who was mid-incantation. He lunged forward and seized the female demon by the throat before she could strike!

The Succubus let out a sharp cry and became visible. Her face instantly shifted from that of an innocent young woman to a savage beast. She raised her dagger and slashed down hard — but Jace caught her wrist.

The demon's expression flashed with shock. She realized she had no hope of matching Jace's strength. At that moment, the mage who'd barely avoided being stabbed pulled out a dagger of his own and drove it up under the Succubus's ribs, straight into her heart. The fel light in her eyes immediately dimmed.

The two exchanged a glance without a word. Jace raised his eyes and caught sight of another shadow charging straight at him. He swung his staff and struck the Succubus square in the face out of thin air, knocking her out of the shadows and sideways to the ground. Before she could scramble up, he drove the butt of his staff through her back, piercing her chest.

He was getting well-practiced at killing Succubi.

Then he sensed another shadow flickering behind a battle mage standing beside him.

"Behind you! Behind you!"

Jace shouted at him. The mage heard and immediately spoke a short incantation. A burst of white smoke erupted from his body, sending a layer of frost outward in all directions. The Succubus behind him was blasted out of hiding, her feet already sheathed in two thick layers of ice, immobilized.

The mage strode over and ran her through with his sword. The Succubus collapsed to the ground like a soulless husk.

After killing that Succubus, the battle mage nodded in Jace's direction. Jace started to relax — when shouts rang out from the other side.

The mages on the opposite height seemed to be dealing with Succubus-caused chaos of their own. But through the thick smoke of the Infernal's burning, Jace couldn't make out what was happening over there at all.

Relying on his Detect Demons ability alone... at best he could only protect this one group of mages around him.

Then he noticed several full mages behind the formation chanting the same incantation in unison. Within seconds, Jace clearly felt the temperature around him plummet.

The scorching, oppressive wasteland was suddenly swept by a blizzard. Snowflakes and ice shards stung against his face. In moments, Jace was already dusted with a layer of snow.

The wind grew stronger, quickly suppressing the spread of fel fire across the battlefield. And the effect of the Blizzard was immediate — vague shapes scraping through the snow became visible in places behind the mages, fleeing toward the edge of the storm.

Two snow-covered shadows moved rapidly toward several of the mages, only to be blown apart one by one by Arcane Missiles flying in from both sides. Shattered flesh and the Succubi's piercing screams were quickly swallowed by the blizzard.

"We won... the demons are retreating!" a soldier below roared.

An officer immediately drowned him out with an even more hysterical shout, "Stay alert! Hold formation! The demons could counterattack at any moment! Hold your positions!"

Jace looked again at the front line where the soldiers were making their stand. The ground was littered with the bodies of Felhounds, Felguards, and Mo'arg vanguards. Only two Felguards were still putting up resistance in the formation, and they were nearly dead too.

Not many demons could be seen near the blasted gate anymore. They'd pulled back after all.

Jace knew there had to be an Eredar warlock among this group of demons. If not, who had summoned that Infernal?

Before long, the soldiers had started cheering. Amy ran over and grabbed Jace's arm tightly. "You far exceeded anything I imagined, Jace. Those Succubi looked at you like they'd met their nemesis — they couldn't do a thing to you... If it weren't for you, I don't know how many of our mages would have died..."

Her voice trembled a little as she spoke. Jace knew she was still pretty wound up — or rather, she hadn't yet come down from the feverish excitement after battle.

But this wasn't the time to let his guard down. He looked toward the passage leading to the mine behind them, wondering how many Succubi or Felhounds had slipped inside.

That was when he noticed an officer staring at the mine behind them too. Clearly, he'd had the same thought.
