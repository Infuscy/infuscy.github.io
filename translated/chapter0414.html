Chapter 414: The Mine

The fierce battle had only just ended, and Jace was just realizing his hands were still trembling slightly. He couldn't tell whether it was from the shock of facing that swarm of Felguards, or the frenzy of close-quarters combat that still hadn't fully left his system.

The soldiers below were clearing the battlefield, searching for the bodies of comrades among the demon corpses, or wounded companions who'd passed out but weren't yet dead.

The Blizzard that had helped slow the demons' advance and revealed the Succubi had now become an obstacle to the rescue effort. A thick layer of snow covered the entire battlefield. Everyone's trouser legs and boots were soaked through.

There didn't seem to be too many dead, but of the several hundred — nearly a thousand — men here, the vast majority had been wounded to one degree or another. Even though Jace hadn't been directly harmed by any demon, he could feel the skin on his face stinging from the burns. Beside him, Amy breathed heavily, gazing at the gate destroyed by the Infernal, her face showing faint burns of its own.

And the consequences of being burned by fel fire were far more severe than an ordinary burn. It could cause all sorts of complications — skin ulceration, necrosis, even more serious internal ailments.

But compared to death or disability, most people here were in no mood to care about any of that.

"Jace, are you hurt?" Amy asked.

Jace wiped the dust and snow off his shoulder. "How could I be? A death knight couldn't kill me!"

Amy said, "Thinking about it, just last night I was going on about how I'd protect you!"

"We protected each other," Jace said. "I wasn't about to let anything happen to you either, Amy."

Amy smiled, her gaze drifting upward. Jace followed her gaze.

Up along the path to the higher ground, a few officers who hadn't been badly hurt were leading a group of soldiers and miners toward the mine entrance. Several mages followed behind them.

"You think there are Succubi inside the mine?" Amy asked.

Jace nodded. "Let's go up and take a look."

The two of them headed up the slope. Even though the battle had taken a lot out of him and he was feeling the fatigue, the thought of demons still lurking around kept him from relaxing, from letting his guard down.

Amy asked curiously, "How did you see those demons? I saw you swing your staff at empty air and thought you'd lost your bearings — then that Succubus got knocked right out of the shadows by you, as if their invisibility was useless against you."

"You smell that sweetish scent in the air? Like some kind of weird perfume," Jace said.

Amy tried sniffing, then said, "Maybe? I can't really tell."

"That's the Succubus's scent," Jace said. "As for hitting them so accurately... I was guessing on instinct. I didn't expect to nail one on the first try either."

Amy nodded and followed Jace to the area near the mine entrance, where a hundred or so people had already gathered. A Lordaeron lieutenant holding his helmet looked back at a foreman behind him and asked, "Is anyone still inside?"

"There are a few tool-keepers in there," the foreman said.

"Tool-keepers?" the lieutenant shook his head. "At a time like this, they're still watching the tools... Find a few mages and shield-bearing infantry to watch the entrance. We're going in. I can practically smell the stink of demons from here!"

"Any mages willing to come in with me?" he called over.

Jace, Amy, and several other mages answered one after another.

The lieutenant looked at Jace. "I noticed you earlier, mage. You're a proper hand at dealing with demons. From your performance, I imagine you've got a way to track them. If you can guarantee you'll sense it if a demon slips past you, then you'll go with Captain MacLeare's squad."

Jace nodded. "I can do that."

The lieutenant said immediately, "Sergeant Major MacLeare, you take this mage. Mr. Dreann, you're with me. Sergeant Major Norris, you take Mr. Thane here — he's a student of Master Dumas, and he's quite good at tracking demons too. We'll split into three teams, follow the minecart tracks forward, and regroup at the deep rest area at the very back of the mine."

The squad leaders nodded confirmation. The infantrymen following them drew their swords one after another.

The lieutenant turned to face the soldiers at the entrance. "Seven or eight men per team. The rest of you watch this place — don't let any of these damned things slip out. We can't bring too many in, because the most important thing is still guarding against a demon counterattack. They could come at us again at any moment."

Once the arrangements were made, Jace followed Captain MacLeare into the mine. Amy walked ahead, holding up a Light spell supported by a sapphire-gemmed staff. Its glow was warm and steady — a far cry from the Light Jace produced, which was about as reliable as a cheap candle flickering on and off.

Still, as they followed the long tunnels deeper in, the absolute darkness at the deepest stretches still left some people unable to see.

Amy's pace was remarkably firm, her rhythm steady as she moved forward. It was the soldiers behind, shields and swords in hand, who hesitated now and then, tensing against the darkness ahead as if they could stare something into revealing itself.

Jace kept his focus locked ahead, ready to blast any Succubus that might lunge at them with a Scorch, to keep them from getting to Amy.

But after walking a considerable distance without spotting a single demon, the only thing that filled the passage was the ever-present scent of Succubi.

After carefully advancing for maybe ten to twenty minutes, a figure suddenly appeared at the edge of the Light spell's reach. It was a middle-aged man, drenched in sweat, shielding his eyes with his arm. "Finally, someone's here... there are demons inside!"

"You saw the demons?!" Captain MacLeare stepped forward. "Did they go deeper, or where?"

"I... I don't know. They might be hiding, lying in wait around here," the middle-aged miner said. "I was in a storage room at the back of a rest area further in, which is how they didn't find me."

MacLeare grabbed him by the neck and pulled him close for a look, then asked the three mages behind him, "We don't have a priest. Any of you have a way to tell if this fellow's been charmed?"

"Charmed?" The miner didn't seem to understand what that meant. Jace stepped forward. "Let me try."

Jace came up close and examined the miner's eyes. He noticed some murky shadow traces in them, and by now he could smell the scent of Succubus coming off him too — far stronger than the faint trace lingering in the air.

"He's been charmed," Jace said. "At the very least, a Succubus has already worked on him, Captain MacLeare."
