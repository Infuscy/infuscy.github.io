Chapter 415: The Demoness

"I haven't been charmed, I really did hide from them and get away, mage!" the miner said helplessly. "Why won't you believe me?"

Jace knew he was telling the truth — but it was only the truth as he believed it. A Succubus using a charm spell to scramble someone's memories wasn't hard at all, especially on someone without a particularly strong will.

"I don't understand," MacLeare said. "If he's been charmed, why would the Succubus let him tell us there might be an ambush around here?"

"That means the Succubi want us to slow down," Amy said. "To buy themselves more time."

"What's in the mine?" MacLeare asked.

"The question is what they're trying to bring into the mine, Captain!" Jace said. "Amy's right — these Succubi definitely want this miner to slow us down. They might already be summoning demons in there. We either rush in now and disrupt their plans, or go back for more reinforcements. Your call, Captain."

MacLeare said, "We should push forward immediately. After all, those Succubi haven't been inside that long..."

"If we charge straight in and run into a Succubus ambush, what then?" another mage asked.

"I can guarantee I'll detect any Succubi lying in wait around us," Jace said. "Trust me on this."

"Then we do it." MacLeare clapped two of the men behind him on the shoulders. "You two head back the way we came and call for reinforcements. Everyone else, charge in! You lead the way, mage!"

Jace nodded and plunged into the dark passage. Amy immediately matched his pace, and the captain and the other soldiers followed close behind.

Jace pushed forward, and it didn't take long for his suspicions to be confirmed — the shadow energy ahead was growing stronger. He was starting to feel the same power as the void rift the warlocks in the basement of the Slaughtered Lamb opened when they summoned demons.

"I can feel the shadow energy, Jace. There must be some kind of evil ritual going on in here..." Amy panted in a low voice.

Jace nodded and kept moving forward. So these Succubi were already summoning other demons... What were they trying to summon? To bring that Eredar warlock inside?

A roar suddenly echoed from the other side of the mine, followed by the chaotic clash of steel. MacLeare said, "The other squads must have run into demons."

"We're nearly at the deepest point, Captain," a miner behind them panted.

The way he said it, Jace could feel the shadow magic growing stronger too.

Passing through a timber-reinforced opening, several Succubi suddenly came into view — and right in the middle, a number of imps were channeling shadow magic!

Jace wasted no time chanting a Scorch and blew one of the imps to pieces. Amy murmured an incantation and guided a stream of Arcane Missiles to bombard the area around the summoning ritual. The Succubi scattered into the shadows. MacLeare shouted in frustration, "The Succubi are hiding again, damn it! Watch your backs, don't let your guard down!"

"Amy, keep using magic to disrupt those imps' casting! I'll handle the Succubi!"

"Be careful, Jace!"

The two mages kept up a relentless barrage of Arcane Missiles on the imps, blasting them into a panic. A Succubus shadow closed in fast on Amy — Jace drew his sword and cleanly lopped off her head.

Several soldiers with shields pressed together to deal with the Succubi. Whenever one emerged from the shadows to attack, she was quickly swarmed by multiple men. Jace actively hunted the Succubus shadows, using Scorch to force them into visibility.

With Jace coordinating with the soldiers to intercept and surround them, three or four Succubi with nowhere to run were quickly run through one by one. The imps were precisely blasted apart by the mages as well.

"Wait..." Amy said. "The imps are all dead, but the summoning circle is still active, Jace!"

Hearing Amy's words, Jace knew the summoning ritual was already complete. They'd still arrived a step too late.

Then the squad from the passage on the opposite side arrived. At the head of it were Sergeant Major Norris and the mage Thane. Seeing the scene, Thane said immediately, "The summoning circle is complete!"

"The demon hasn't come out?" a soldier asked.

"No... wait..." Jace watched a chaotic shadow streak along the wall and suddenly rush to the front of them in a flash of light.

Blink?!

"Look here — see the surprise I've prepared for you!"

A deeply magnetic female voice rang out nearby. A female demon over two meters tall materialized in a glow of arcane light!

Her skin was dark purple. She was half-naked, wearing nothing but a loose crimson breastplate and faulds. Six slender arms grew from her body, each gripping a saber with a yellow blade. The bronze crown on her head blazed with surging fel fire, bathing the entire cave ceiling in green light in an instant.

"Seen enough, little thing?"

Even as she asked, she raised her blades. Jace immediately backed away to flee — but the demoness was tall and fast. A few strides and she was right behind him, before anyone else had even reacted.

In his haste, Jace drew Song of the Sky and cast Conjure Water on the ground. Several ice spikes shot up from the floor, blocking the demoness's path. She casually kicked them apart, but it bought Jace a little time to escape.

This was a Shivarra — or a Shattered Hand demoness, some called them. Some said they were the leaders of the Succubi; others said they were a more mysterious demon race. But there was no question that these demons fought even more ferociously than Felguards, the blades whirling across their bodies like a flying threshing machine.

The Shivarra's rhythm was thrown off for just a moment — and two Frostbolts immediately streaked in toward her. The Shivarra casually sliced through the Frostbolts, shaking the spreading frost off her arms. She saw Jace had already run far away.

She laughed and immediately charged toward Captain MacLeare not far off. MacLeare was clearly unprepared for the demoness's speed. He raised his shield and took a wild flurry of cuts from the Shivarra. After a crisp clatter of metal on metal, he was knocked to the ground with ease.

The demoness raised her blade to stab straight into his chest — but a Frostbolt from a nearby mage froze that arm solid.

"Annoying mages!"

The Shivarra shook her arm and shattered the ice. On a whim, she hurled a blade at the mage. The blade was deflected by an invisible barrier — the mage was surrounded by a faint arcane aura, some kind of mana shield.

"You react fast!"

The Shivarra laughed and charged straight at the mage. Mid-charge, without any warning, she vanished in a flash of light. The soldiers who rushed after her all lunged at empty air.

Just when everyone thought the mage was done for, the next instant the Shivarra suddenly reappeared behind the soldiers who'd been chasing her, her blade severing a soldier's arm in one swing. She was so fast Jace couldn't even track her shadow.

"We play by my rules!"

The other soldiers immediately raised their shields to block — but the Shivarra didn't swing. With an agility absurd for her frame, she spun around behind them and suddenly drove her blade through a soldier's back!

Jace immediately cast a Scorch. This Scorch carried a fair amount of shadow power with it — but given the room was already saturated with shadow energy, and even the other mages' fire magic had taken on a bloody tinge, his spell didn't stand out that much.

The shadow fire instantly blew off one of the Shivarra's arms, blasting her several steps sideways and breaking her rhythm before she could press her attack on the next target.

The Shivarra cried out in surprise and looked over — seemingly a little shocked that a human mage would use magic like this. By then another wave of reinforcements had arrived at the cave entrance, and more infantry were closing in, trying to trap the Shivarra in the middle. She smashed through a soldier and flashed toward the exit.

Jace saw her shadow dart toward the cave mouth and immediately shouted, "She's running — stop her!"

If they let this demoness leave the open cavern chamber and slip into the mine tunnels, using the shadows and her absurd speed to hide and strike at will, then all their numerical advantage would be completely nullified.
