## Chapter 435: Relic of the Order of Tirisfal

The next morning, Jace sat up in bed.

After a night's rest, he could clearly feel his body had improved some more.

At least getting out of bed and walking back and forth no longer required him to find something to prop himself up.

He got down on the floor and tried to do a push-up, only for the muscles he engaged to feel like they'd been torn, and he collapsed in pain — just as someone knocked on the door.

"Jace?"

It was Amy's voice.

Jace scrambled up quickly, dusted the dirt off his clothes, and opened the door.

"Amy. Good morning."

"It's nearly noon," Amy said with a smile. "How are you feeling?"

"Better today," Jace said. He hopped lightly once, then kept himself from showing any sign of pain on his face. "At least much better than yesterday."

"I knew you'd recover fast," Amy said. "I came to tell you there's a mage from Dalaran here to see you. But don't be nervous — I'm quite sure it's nothing bad."

Jace said, "Oh? I heard the Kirin Tor wanted to give me something. I'll come down right now. Let me put on my coat…"

"Give you something?" Amy was taken aback for a moment, then said, "Ah, he did bring something. What is it?"

Jace pulled his outer robe from the wardrobe. "I don't know either. Let's go find out!"

The two of them went downstairs together to a hall on the lower level. The Dalaran envoy was already there, and sure enough, sitting on the table before him was an exquisite box.

"Master." Jace nodded to the golden-haired mage.

"You are Mr. Jace Sesso?"

"Yes."

"Mr. Sesso." The mage drew himself up. "My name is Avaris, a full mage of Dalaran."

Jace nodded. "Mr. Avaris."

The mage Avaris continued, "I heard about everything that happened at Nethergarde Keep a month ago. First, I must express my respect to you. My wife was also among the ranks fighting the Burning Legion's demons. You may not have heard her name, but you are unquestionably the one who saved her life."

"So when the Kirin Tor needed a courier to carry this gift here, I immediately volunteered for the task."

"The item in this box is over a thousand years old, Mr. Sesso," Avaris said, taking a deep breath. "Now, the Kirin Tor entrusts it to you."

The Dalaran mage opened the box. Jace saw a crystal sitting inside, like a block of ice.

What is this?

Jace's mind searched back and forth through loot tables. Of course, no matter how he searched, he couldn't think of any famous treasure connected to the Order of Tirisfal.

After all, that Order had existed in name only for many years now. With Medivh's betrayal, the few remaining mages who could still call themselves members of the Order of Tirisfal had been murdered one after another. By the time the game's timeline began, the organization had practically vanished into thin air.

"This is the Frostflow Crystal," Avaris said. "You may have heard of the Dragon Hunt War, eight hundred years ago, when the Guardian Aegwynn defeated the demon lord who attempted to invade Northrend…"

"This is a part of the magic ornament she wore at the time."

"A part?" Jace asked.

"The demon lord shattered Aegwynn's ice gem, but the largest fragment was recovered and re-fashioned into this amulet."

"After Aegwynn betrayed the Order of Tirisfal, she stole many precious treasures. Some of them were tracked down by the Order's hunters — this is one of them."

"Good god," Amy couldn't help but exclaim.

Jace wanted to exclaim even more.

Because he'd already frozen in shock.

That demon lord was none other than the Avatar of Sargeras. This was something shattered by the Dark Titan Sargeras?

Aside from the Order of Tirisfal and the Kirin Tor, not many people in the world knew that the demon king who had walked out of the dark gate of Northrend was actually the avatar of the Dark Titan. Of course, even Aegwynn herself had been completely unaware of this when she faced him — otherwise she probably wouldn't have charged in to fight him with such confidence.

Avaris continued, "You defeated the demon lord Azgoth, Mr. Sesso. The demons remaining in this world must hate you beyond measure… The frost power within this amulet can purify the spirit, enabling its wearer to resist the shadow magic and mental interference of demons. Though it has suffered heavy damage, much of its core magical power has not been lost."

Jace knew the Kirin Tor was more worried about him being manipulated by demons and doing something unimaginable.

"Can… I take it now?"

"Of course," the mage said. "It's yours now."

Jace nodded, took the amulet out of the box, and held it in his hand. He could immediately feel the gem's icy energy seep into his palm, little by little calming his mind. Even the wounds left by the tearing of shadow power on his body didn't hurt as much anymore.

"Where are the rest of the gem's pieces now?" Amy asked curiously. "Are they still with Lady Aegwynn?"

"No one knows," the mage said. "Logically, they should have been scattered into the frozen soil of Northrend. As far as I know, Aegwynn wasn't able to recover the other pieces at the time either. Of course, perhaps that was just a lie on her part. After all, this was never originally her treasure — it was entrusted to her by the Order of Tirisfal to aid her in going north to hunt demons. Perhaps even then she already had the intention of keeping these treasures for herself. Getting lost in Northrend was merely an excuse."

Jace said, "This is truly a very precious treasure, Master. I hope you'll convey my gratitude to the Kirin Tor for their kindness. And thank you for bringing it all this way."

"You're too kind," the mage said. "Now that the item has been delivered, I still need to head to the Giant Hammer Fortress next, so I won't keep you from your rest. I hope you recover soon, Mr. Sesso."

"Thank you…" Jace and Amy nodded. The mage gave a light bow and hurried back downstairs, leaving the two of them standing there in a daze, staring at the object.

"So Aegwynn really did live for eight hundred years," Amy said.

Jace glanced at her. "You were still doubting that?"

"All right, I'm not doubting anymore," Amy said.

"I've also learned that many mages of great power live far longer than ordinary people. Aegwynn took the power of so many archmages of the Order of Tirisfal from centuries past and became the Guardian. Her living for a few hundred years… isn't actually that hard to grasp."

"It's really hard to imagine this gem was once used by her, and eight hundred years ago at that." She asked carefully, "May I take a look?"

Jace handed the amulet to Amy. The moment she held it, she shivered slightly.

"Keep it safe, Jace," she said, handing the ice crystal back. "There are definitely mages who study frost magic who'd give anything to get their hands on this…"
