Chapter 438: Partings Are Inevitable

After bringing Vereesa back to the Quel'Thalas elven soldiers, Jace and Grede sat on the slope outside for a while.

A squad of guards holding longswords and tower shields marched past the two of them. Grede watched the elves gradually disappear into the distance, then asked, "Jace, where are you planning to go after this?"

"Back to Stormwind first, naturally," Jace said. "Then I'm planning to make a trip back to Lordaeron. I don't know if my parents… have heard about any of this."

Grede looked over as if he'd suddenly sobered up by half. "If they heard about what happened down here…"

Jace rubbed his face. "Exactly. So I need to get moving as soon as possible. With this gem, I figure I'll be leaving Nethergarde Keep tomorrow or the day after at the latest. After all, getting from here back to Lordaeron still takes about a month."

"You should talk to that elf," Grede said. "Have her find a mage to go to Lordaeron first and let your parents know. Isn't her sister the Ranger-General? She'll definitely help you."

"Ask a mage to teleport over?" Jace scratched his head. "That seems like too much trouble. But I suppose she could help me teleport a letter over first."

Grede nodded. "That's right. Ugh, really shouldn't have been in such a hurry to drink her under the table tonight."

"I'll come find her tomorrow," Jace said.

"What about you? What are you planning to do after this?"

"I'm planning to stay here and keep fighting demons, Jace," the dwarf said. "It's not often you get a chance to live with such a huge flock of gryphon riders and knights. I feel like I've found that feeling again — those days battling the forest trolls. And I can get some real training in. The only downside is there's no pay, but at least food and board are covered…"

Jace said, "Then when you get back to Stormwind, remember to find me. I'll probably be back in about a month. Take care of yourself, Grede."

"What kind of joke is that?" Grede glared and pounded his chest. "Who in this rotten land could still lay a finger on me?! Who?!"

After finally extricating himself from Grede's drunken, endless rambling and making it back home, Jace didn't sleep as soundly that night as he had the previous few days.

The past few days, when his injuries had been so agonizing, Jace hadn't had the means to leave Nethergarde Keep even if he'd wanted to. But now that the gem had helped him recover considerably, he could no longer stay put in the Blasted Lands with a peaceful mind. All he could think about was finding a chance to get moving as soon as possible.

At the very least, he needed to let his parents know he was still alive as early as possible.

Early that morning, Jace arrived at the gate of the Quel'Thalas encampment. He walked straight up to the spear-bearing guard with the expressionless face and said, "Hello, I'm looking for Lady Vereesa Windrunner…"

The elven guard stepped aside and pointed to a small door at the foot of the wall, speaking in heavily accented Common. "Lady Vereesa is in the small building on the western side."

Jace gave him a strange look, wondering why he didn't even ask a single question before just letting him in.

"May you recover your health soon, Mr. Jace Sesso," the elf added.

Hearing that, Jace paused, then said, "Thank you."

According to Marin, he'd spent a long time here with the Quel'Thalas botanists while receiving treatment, so it was quite possible that quite a few of the elves here recognized him.

Jace followed the guard's directions toward the small building. The whole Quel'Thalas compound was full of elves, which made him feel a bit uncomfortable. But the elves didn't seem to mind a human walking around here at all.

The door to the small building was ajar. Jace went in and found it was an alchemy lab filled with magical potions. Vereesa was sitting by the window, holding a transparent potion bottle that was half empty.

Hearing the door, the elf turned around and saw Jace, her long eyebrows rising in surprise.

"Why are you here? Are you all right?"

"At least I could make it here," Jace said. He glanced at the potion bottle Vereesa was holding. "What is this?"

"A potion brewed from blackmouth oil and thorn algae. It sobers you up," Vereesa said. "I shouldn't have drunk like that last night, but that dwarf was far too cocky. Though, to be fair, he did have the capital to be cocky."

Jace said, "Honestly, he was about at his limit too. If you'd held on a little longer, he probably would've been done for."

Vereesa took another sip of the potion and said quietly, "The outcome of a contest often comes down to the smallest margin. I concede this time."

"If that guy heard you say that directly, he'd be over the moon."

"Let him be happy then."

Jace smiled, then said, "Actually, I came because there's something I wanted to ask for your help with, Vereesa."

Vereesa nodded. "Go ahead, Jace."

Jace said, "It's been a long time since I've been back to Lordaeron. I'm worried my parents might have gotten word that something happened to me. So could you help me ask a mage to send a letter over by magic first? After all, even if I actually found a gryphon to fly me back to Lordaeron, it would still take many days."

Vereesa said, "Your father is Mr. Vic Sesso, right?"

Jace nodded. "How do you know?"

"Don't worry, Jace," Vereesa said. "Sylvanas already asked the Quel'dorei mages in Lordaeron to go speak with your father. How could we not notify them about something like this?"

Hearing this, Jace let out a huge sigh of relief.

"Thank you so much. This really helps. And remember to thank the Ranger-General for me too. Still, I need to go back myself — probably tomorrow… I'll be leaving Nethergarde Keep. But at least now I don't need to rush quite so much."

"Ah, I'll be returning to Quel'Thalas soon too," Vereesa said. "I must go back to Eversong Woods to help defend against the trolls who might try to take advantage of the situation. I need to make sure they don't dare act rashly."

Jace said, "After all, you're one of the most powerful rangers among the Farstriders."

"Just wait. Soon it won't be 'one of' anymore." Vereesa twirled the square bottle in her hand. "I will surpass Sylvanas."

As she said this, she let out a soft sigh. "It's just… we'll have to part ways again."

Jace smiled. "We'll definitely meet again."

"We will!" Vereesa rapped the table excitedly. "And when I get back I'm going to practice my drinking. Next time we meet, I definitely won't let that gryphon rider be so smug, that damn… oh, don't tell him. Next time, let's go to Thelsamar, Jace — that place you mentioned last time, in Loch Modan!"
