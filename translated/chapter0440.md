## Chapter 440: Who Did You Kill?

Early the next morning, Jace caught a carriage to Goldshire, spent the night there, and set out again before dawn. He reached Stormwind City just as morning broke.

Since the Dark Portal had closed, the perpetually gloomy sky had cleared, and the faint, lingering taint of evil that had drifted everywhere was swept away at last.

The thing that made Jace miserable, though, was how brutally Stormwind baked in August and September.

After spending so long in the overcast, slightly chill Blightlands, plus several weeks laid up in bed, his body — already ravaged by a gauntlet of shadow and fel torment — had grown unaccustomed to such intense sunlight.

That said, coming back to Stormwind still felt pretty good.

Passing through the gates, he noticed quite a few craftsmen had erected scaffolding and were refurbishing the bridge.

So this place was about to officially be called the Valley of Heroes, and they'd be putting up statues of the Sons of Lothar here?

He thought back to standing here over a year ago, watching the expeditionary force march out of Stormwind. Never in a million years would he have imagined that a year later, things would have turned out like this.

He had personally fought in the battle of the Dark Portal — and they had even left his statue back at Nethergarde Keep.

All the way home, Jace pulled out his key and opened the door. The place was completely empty.

"Morfell?"

"Sazeno?"

The whole room contained nothing but furniture. His clothes and some daily necessities were still there, but the money, the demonic-language notes — everything had been cleaned out, spotless.

What the hell?

There was not even a trace of demonic aura left in the room.

Had the Wizard Sanctum raided his place?

Damn it. Where were those two demons? Had they been caught? Or just killed outright?

Jace dropped his luggage and immediately headed for the Mage Quarter, making straight for the Slaughtered Lamb.

At the tavern entrance, he poked his head in cautiously. The place was empty in the morning. Tharle Mor stood alone, wiping down tables.

Hearing someone come in, Tharle said without looking up, "Whatever you need, just say it."

Jace cleared his throat. Tharle lifted his eyes and glanced over.

The moment he saw Jace, he visibly froze.

"Jace? Is that you?"

"It's me." Jace glanced toward the basement. "Is Morfell here?"

The bartender nodded, and Jace finally breathed easier.

"We thought you were dead," Tharle said.

"Hardly," Jace said. "Morfell's right there, isn't she? If I'd died, she definitely would have felt it."

"She insisted you weren't dead, but Stormwind... at least Galders got word that you were dead." Tharle said. "He said you'd been killed by a demon lord."

"Backwards," Jace said. "I killed that demon lord."

"So it was true?" Tharle asked, startled. "So you really are that Jace Sesso?"

"Hey," Jace said. "If you breathe a word, SI:7 will be at your throat by nightfall."

Tharle blinked, then nodded. "I understand... Jace."

"We'll catch up later." Jace smiled and headed down to the basement.

Through the iron door and into the hall, he swept the area with Detect Demons — the first time he had tried using that ability since waking up at Nethergarde Keep.

But aside from the hellhound eyeing him warily, he did not see a thing.

Where was Morfell?

Suddenly, a surge of dark energy behind him—

Jace spun around and seized the demon by the throat. Morfell was yanked out of the shadows, gripping his wrist, staring straight into his eyes.

Jace frowned, let go, and said, "What the hell are you doing, ambushing me?"

"Master made me worry, so I wanted to give you a little punishment..."

Jace knew the succubus was only joking. Otherwise, given her lethality, there was no way he could have disarmed her this easily.

"I didn't die, so what's there to worry about?"

Morfell leaned against the wall, peevish. "For a while... I could barely feel your existence. But I knew you weren't dead, because of our pact! So I knew you must have been injured — so far away that even the soul link could not reach you..."

"Good thing there's no soul link," Jace said. "Otherwise, trust me, you'd have died a horrible death, Morfell."

"Hmm, I seriously doubt that... but is Master worried about me?" Morfell asked with a smile.

"Something like that," Jace said. "You watched over my thirty-some gold. If you'd died, was I supposed to leave that to the imp? Speaking of which, where is that little guy?"

Just then, a golden imp came bounding out of the alchemy lab tunnel, shouting as he ran, "Master's back! Master's back!"

Jace said, "So what happened exactly? Why aren't you living at the house anymore?"

"A group of mages came to our room, Master," Morfell said. "They approached from a distance, with a clear purpose... The imp sensed something was off, so we took your demonic-language materials and spell scrolls and left."

"And of course... the gold that matters most to you!" the imp shouted. "I hid it and carried it away!"

Morfell shot the imp a look. "What were those mages there for? Did you expose yourself as a demon worshipper at Nethergarde Keep?"

"Correction — I'm not a demon worshipper," Jace said, looking at her.

"Of course. The demons worship you." Morfell leaned against Jace. "Master, quick, tell me I did well."

Jace nodded. "Fine, you did good. Those mages were there to investigate me. I killed a demon commander in public at Nethergarde Keep, so I gave a few things away. But they let me go, since I'd also done my part, right?"

"Well then, the Holy Light has not completely scrambled humanity's brains after all." Morfell leaned in closer. "So, what kind of commander did you kill? A Mo'arg overseer?"

"You don't know his name? Though I guess it doesn't matter which demon you kill," Jace said. "They'll all resurrect in the Twisting Nether eventually."

"That's not the same at all, Master!" Sazeno said. "True, for lesser demons it makes no difference — live, die, come back, nobody cares! But for powerful demons, the process of reforming a body in the depths of the Twisting Nether can mean losing their original power... even a fall in status. And getting killed near the Dark Portal, in a place soaked in fel fire, might even mean a grievously wounded soul!"

Morfell nodded. "He's right. So I'm curious, Master — who exactly did you kill?"

"Azgoth," Jace said. "Ever heard of him?"

Morfell's smile instantly froze. "You killed Azgoth?"
