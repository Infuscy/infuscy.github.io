## Chapter 441: Pit Lord Slayer

"Impossible," Morfell said, stunned. "Azgoth is Kil'jaeden's adjutant and jailer. He has his own domain in the Twisting Nether, holds a massive fortress, and guards countless demon criminals who defy the Legion's orders... He's a high-ranking officer of the Burning Legion."

"Remember that spell we trained with last time?" Jace said. "That spell that calls down a meteor from the sky — I used it to crush him to death."

"That shadow magic is indeed powerful, but..." Morfell frowned. "How is that possible?!"

"That demon didn't really wound me much," Jace said. "I was nearly killed by the backlash of the shadow power I'd channeled myself. It took a whole group of people a long time to pull me back."

Morfell nodded. "So that's why Galders said you'd become a hero — you helped the humans defending that castle and killed an Annihilan lord invading this world?"

"And... not just any one. Azgoth." Morfell frowned. "Kil'jaeden and Archimonde rarely send war-chiefs like this to invade a world, Master — unless they consider that world strong enough to warrant it. And you went and killed one."

"You might have just drawn Kil'jaeden's wrath directly!"

"And yet, he is powerless... Ha! With the Dark Portal gone... he simply cannot launch an invasion of this world."

Morfell wrapped her arms tightly around Jace. "Quick, Master, I want to taste what an Annihilan-slayer smells like. I can't wait... Master!"

Jace caught her arm just before it wandered lower. "Can you dial it back for a second? I've got things to do... damn it."

Morfell seized the moment and ran her long tongue across the sweat on Jace's neck, murmuring, "Even this little bit is good..."

He shoved her away and wiped the slick mucus from his neck, muttering, "I swear, you are unbelievable..."

Morfell licked her lips. "I've never been with anyone who could kill a demon like that — or anything else, for that matter! Anything at all! Just kill me now, Master, I beg you — kill me with your demon sword. Cut open my chest, pull out my soft heart, and let me watch you eat it before I leave this world... mmm... ah... just thinking about it is about to finish me..."

As she spoke, the succubus's eyes had grown hazy all on their own...

"Cut it out for now," Jace said. "Tell me — where's Ursula?"

Morfell slowly drew her fingers from between her lips, then glanced sideways. "She's in the room. She's sleeping, though."

"How can she sleep through that?" Jace said. "I thought she wasn't here — we were talking this loud and it didn't wake her up?"

"Because I put her to sleep." Morfell lifted a finger. "She's a friend of Master's. It's a small favor I can do, isn't it?"

Jace nodded. "Fine. Stay out here and keep watch. I'm going in to check on her."

"As you wish." Morfell smiled.

Jace entered the alchemy lab and found Ursula lying quietly on a mat, fast asleep.

Jace hadn't slept much the night before in Goldshire either, and watching Ursula sleep so peacefully, he was actually getting drowsy himself.

"Jace?"

A voice from the side snapped him awake again.

Jace sat up and looked over at Ursula, and found her eyes open, staring this way.

"Ursula, I didn't mean to wake you."

Ursula didn't answer. She stood up, sighed, walked over to a cabinet, rummaged around for a while, and found a small vial, which she drank.

She leaned against the cabinet and waited a moment. When she turned back and saw Jace, she immediately frowned and let out a quiet sigh.

Jace understood right away what was going on.

"I really am... Ursula," Jace said.

The alchemist walked over suspiciously and touched Jace's face. The suspicion in her eyes instantly turned to recognition.

Jace said, "I reached Stormwind this morning. From Nethergarde Keep to here, it was roughly two weeks on the road..."

Ursula pulled Jace's head into her chest and murmured, "You're back at last, Jace. I thought the sleeping draught I made the other day was so poorly brewed that it gave me hallucinations."

Jace, smothered against her, caught only the familiar, overwhelming scent of herbs. He looked around the little alchemy lab and thought about everything he'd been through these past two months — it all felt unreal.

"Your money, those books and papers — they're all with me, Jace," Ursula said softly. "Galders said you were probably dead... If Morfell hadn't told me you were definitely fine, I don't know what I would have done."

"I told you I'd be fine, didn't I?" Jace patted her waist. "Don't worry, Ursula. Wizard Sanctum mages have probably already searched my room. If it weren't for this place, those two demons wouldn't have had anywhere to run. Thank you for taking them in."

"Could've been Dalaran's, too," Ursula said. "Galders said there've been a lot of Dalaran mages showing up in Stormwind lately. At first we thought they were here to help keep watch, make sure no demons slipped behind the walls. Might've been that group that went into your room."

Jace nodded. "So from now on, coming to the Slaughtered Lamb, I'll have to be even more careful than before."

"Now that you're back, you should be able to stay in Stormwind for a good long while, right, Jace?" Ursula said, leaning against his chair.

Jace said, "Tomorrow I'm setting sail for Lordaeron to visit my parents. I'll be gone about a month, Ursula. Sorry..."

Ursula sighed. "All right. Don't make your parents worry. But since you're going to Lordaeron, could you deliver a letter to Ambermill for me?"

"Of course," Jace said. "I'm not planning to go to Dalaran this time — I'll be passing right through there."

"That would be wonderful. That way it won't be too much trouble for you." Ursula rummaged through the storage chest behind her.

Jace said, "Did you go ask Lumere how that brush beast of hers is doing?"

"I went to see," Ursula said. "Still too young to tell much, but I do think it looks different from the swamp beasts in the Wetlands — the color's lighter, more yellow. She set up a dry, desert-like environment the way you described, but I don't know if it'll work out in the end."

"Then all we can do is wait patiently."

As he spoke, Jace stood down and took his pack down from the cabinet. He removed the scroll containing Gorefiend's memories, then flipped through and sorted out the demonic-language materials the imp had written, setting them aside. "Ursula, while I'm gone, you can show these materials to the others in the Dagger Society. These are all demonic-language study materials Sazeno wrote."

"No problem." Ursula handed him the letter. "Before you go, let me take a look at your injuries."

"Huh?" Jace said. "I should be fine..."

"You're skin and bones, and you still reek of demonic and shadow corruption. I won't rest easy until I check."

"That's... that's the Nethergarde Keep uniform..."

"What are you getting shy about with me?"
