## Chapter 451: The Kirin Tor's Warning

Early the next morning, Jace accompanied his mother to the Cathedral to pray to the Holy Light. The plaza outside was already packed, and the cathedral doors were a crush of people coming and going. Quite a few priests had to station themselves there constantly just to keep order.

The old cathedral was said to be older than Lordaeron itself. Viewed from the front, it stood like a towering pale stone monument.

The interior of the cathedral was much cooler than outside. A plainly dressed but high-ranking bishop stood in the corridor by the entrance, reciting blessings to every citizen and traveler who entered.

Jace didn't know whether the man could sense the shadow power within his body, but when it was Jace's turn to walk up with the crowd, he delivered the Holy Light's blessing just the same — no different from anyone else.

Coming to a row of pews, Jace leaned close to his mother's ear. "Mom, I won't go up to the front with you."

"Why?" Hennie tilted her head. "You've come all this way — just come pray to the Holy Light together with me..."

"But a lot of people here know me," Jace said, pressing his lips together helplessly. "I'm taller than I used to be, and a lot thinner. With this messy hair as a bit of a disguise, they might probably not recognize me. But if I stand right next to you, I'll be easy to spot."

Hennie nodded. "Then sit in the back and pray with me? The Holy Light will understand, Jace."

"The Holy Light's always understood me, Mom," Jace said.

Hennie moved on with the crowd. Jace sat in an empty seat to the side, watching his mother glance back three steps with loving concern, smiling at him from time to time. He smiled back.

He looked up at the cathedral's pale, night-sky-like vaulted ceiling, the ornately decorated stone pillars, the sculptures of sword-bearing figures gazing down at the people in the center of the cathedral, the golden chandeliers, the stained glass that was almost blinding in the sunlight...

"Hello, Mr. Sesso."

Hearing the greeting, Jace lowered his head and looked to his side. A middle-aged man dressed like a nobleman was standing there, but Jace noticed this fellow was wearing a variety of rings on his fingers — among them a Kirin Tor ring, a Lordaeron archmage's ring, and a few sigils Jace didn't recognize at all.

"Hello, Archmage," Jace said.

"My name is Biso Ershad." The man extended a hand. "I am a member of the Kirin Tor, and also a magical advisor to the royal house of Lordaeron."

"I've heard of your name, Archmage," Jace said, standing to shake his hand.

"You've heard of me?" Biso Ershad said with a smile. "That is a surprise."

Of course Jace knew his name — but not in this world. In his previous life.

Biso Ershad, who later died during the Scourge invasion, was resurrected by a death knight into a powerful undead mage. But later, because the Lich King and the Frozen Throne were grievously wounded, he regained his own will and so joined the Forsaken under the rule of the Dark Lady Sylvanas, becoming the head of the magical district in the Lordaeron underground city — what would later be known as the Undercity.

Of course, the reason he could be put to use by both the Scourge and the Forsaken was that in life he had been a high-ranking member of the Kirin Tor.

"I'll be honest — out on the plaza earlier, I was taking bets with yourself on whether you'd be willing to enter the cathedral. I was even a little curious what would happen if you did walk into this church..." Ershad said, then glanced sideways at the chair beside him. "Sit down quickly, Mr. Sesso."

Jace nodded and sat. Biso Ershad smoothed his robes and sat down too, continuing, "I was also curious whether you'd be harmed by the Holy Light if you entered this cathedral."

"Would there really be a problem like that?" Jace asked.

Ershad shook his head. "I imagine even members of the Kirin Tor can have their share of strange superstitions. For instance, virtually every heretic we know of who secretly studies shadow magic — even those unforgivable criminals who attempt to research necromancy — never sets foot inside any house of the Holy Light. So I occasionally pay attention — if they really did go in, what the Holy Light's reaction would be."

"And then you walked in just like that — completely open, without the slightest hesitation. And the Holy Light didn't do a thing to you. It's cleared up a little question I've had for a long time."

"Or perhaps you're just a special case." Ershad smiled. "I think if you were going to be in trouble here, it would be the Holy Light that had the problem."

Jace smiled slightly but said nothing.

"Of course, I didn't come here just to chat." The smile on the mage's face faded bit by bit. He went on, "From the moment you set foot in the royal city, the Kirin Tor has taken notice of you, Mr. Sesso. Frankly, we'd rather you didn't show up here so openly. Far too many people in the royal city know you — never mind that the last place you should appear is at the Sessos' home."

"I'm sorry, but I had to come back," Jace said.

Ershad said, "General Sylvanas Windrunner has already sent an envoy to explain the situation to your parents. I believe Lady Vereesa must have told you this as well. You really didn't need to make a special trip back."

"Of course I understand your feelings, but... you should also understand the Kirin Tor's difficulty. Telling the entire Alliance that you killed a Pit Lord with a single fire meteor was already quite a stretch. The rumor that you executed that demon lord with shadow magic will spread rapidly once the soldiers who reinforced Nethergarde Keep return home one by one — and will soon swallow our version whole."

"But as long as you stay dead, in a few years everyone will have forgotten about it completely. Everything is controllable. So we'd rather you didn't come back to life — not even a rumor of you coming back to life. Because those rumors are a wildfire. They'll ignite that earlier rumor about shadow magic and burn it into a panic... You're a warlock — I imagine you understand fear better than anyone."

"The Kirin Tor doesn't want to blame you for the impulse to see your parents, so they've asked me to communicate with you further. I imagine Archmage Marin may not have made some things clear enough because of the teacher-student bond between you."

"Of course, I can't be too blunt either. I can only do my best to convey the Kirin Tor's meaning. After all, I truly admire the feat you performed at Nethergarde Keep — this is no polite empty talk. This is my sincere feeling."

"Thank you for the warning, Archmage." Jace paused a moment. "So I should leave the royal city right away, correct?"

Ershad looked at Jace, turned his head to the side with evident difficulty, and said, "Like I said, the Kirin Tor doesn't blame you for coming back here to see your parents. Of course you can stay a few days — just don't go out and draw attention. But after that, try not to come back anymore."

With that, he glanced toward the front of the cathedral. "Well, I won't disturb you and your mother's worship any further, Mr. Jace Sesso. Despite everything I said earlier — welcome back to Lordaeron. I hope you have a pleasant time here."

Biso Ershad finished speaking and quickly disappeared into the crowd, leaving Jace sitting alone on the pew with a quiet sigh.
