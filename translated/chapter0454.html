Chapter 454: Another “New Member”

After meeting Jalane, Jace came over to the alchemy lab. Ursula was carefully bottling medicines one by one. Hearing footsteps behind her, she glanced back, saw it was Jace, and smiled. “You’re back, Jace. Give me a moment…”

Jace nodded and sat to the side, waiting for Ursula to finish her work. Once the bottles were put away, he said, “What kind of medicine is this?”

“This isn’t medicine. It’s a shadow-magic dye.” Ursula turned the dark-colored bottles. “Our new arrival hopes I can help her put together a new robe — to prove her abilities to us.”

“She’s pretty eager,” Jace said under his breath. “She shouldn’t be able to hear us from here, right…”

“She can’t,” Ursula said. “She’s no felhound.”

Hearing that, Jace couldn’t help but smile. But he could also tell that Ursula wasn’t too fond of this Jalane Ayrole.

“What’s her relationship with Zardeth?” Jace asked.

“You mean lovers, right?” Ursula smiled. “I’m not interested. Frankly, she looks down on people a little. I really don’t care for that feeling.”

“I spoke a few words with her just now,” Jace said, sitting backwards in his chair and hugging the backrest. “Probably just first-meeting impressions — I didn’t feel much. I thought she was just a pretty ordinary person, if a bit sharp-looking, or cold, like you said.”

“She knows who you are, Jace.” Ursula sighed. “How could she possibly join us and immediately get on your bad side? Zardeth told her all about you long ago.”

“Fine,” Jace said. “Don’t take it too much to heart. It’s just a collaboration, after all. As long as she’s useful in her own way and can help us master shadow magic more smoothly, everything else is secondary — as long as she follows the rules.”

Ursula nodded, not entirely willingly.

“By the way, I delivered your letter to Ambermill for you. That alchemy lab — I completely forgot the name.” Jace said.

“Ouldric,” Ursula said.

“Is that Ouldric a partner of yours?” Jace asked casually. “I got the feeling he knows you pretty well.”

Ursula sighed and fixed Jace with a stare. “Are you sure you want to ask this much, Jace Sesso? If you keep asking, I’ll tell you — but you should know I’m saying it reluctantly.”

“Never mind. I was just curious.” Jace raised his eyebrows. “Did Zardeth get a chance to look at those demonic-language materials I left here last time? What did he think?”

“A godsend.” Ursula raised her eyebrows. “Zardeth was even a little annoyed. He thinks you always like to hoard these good things, leaving everyone stumbling around like headless chickens and bleeding all over the place before you’re willing to bring them out.”

“That’s really not how it is. I’ll admit I did hold back the succubus incantations for a while, but these demonic-language materials — I only just got them properly organized not long ago,” Jace sighed. “Zardeth is always suspecting this and that… I’ll go see him in a bit and clear all this up.”

“But it really is a big help, Jace.” Ursula glanced at the felhound that had walked in step by step, then continued, “Just from looking at those demonic-language materials and with the help of Zardeth’s succubus, Miss Senlora, we’ve figured out a few more incantations. Zardeth learned them quickly, and I’m practicing them too. Want to hear?”

“In just this one month?” Jace asked.

“In just this one month.” Ursula smiled.

“Then you two are making really fast progress,” Jace said. “I’ll just go ask Zardeth directly, otherwise he’ll suspect the two of us of forming some kind of clique. Don’t want him getting suspicious again.”

“It’s not that bad — he’s not seriously blaming you,” Ursula said with a smile. Then her expression suddenly went cold. “Oh, right — Surena Caledon has summoned a succubus too, Jace.”

“That’s great,” Jace nodded. “It must have been done with the help of Senlora and Basadim, right?”

“Yes. This time we were prepared, and with the help of the Shadow Protection Potion, everything went much more smoothly.”

At this, Ursula turned her head away and frowned, as if there was something she hesitated to say.

Jace said, “So what’s the problem?”

“The succubus Surena summoned is… a man,” Ursula said.

Jace looked at the alchemist and could already more or less imagine what would happen between Surena Caledon and an incubus.

Ursula said, “Those few days, every time I saw Surena, she looked like she had only half a life left… walking leaning against the wall, couldn’t sit still, her voice weak and breathless.”

Jace laughed. “Erlan must have been furious.”

“Of course. So he’s eager to summon his own succubus too, Jace.” Ursula said coldly. “It’s foreseeable that this basement of ours is going to turn into a full-blown den of debauchery. And I mean even more of a den of debauchery than it already is.”

“You’re part of it too, Jace Sesso. Don’t look at me with that innocent face, you ringleader… Surena’s always trying to get me to summon one too. Damn it, I’d rather find some giant demon to swallow me whole than have a demon’s cock waving in front of me every day, never mind letting it inside me… Absolutely not. That incubus’s ever-smiling face makes me sick. I’ve already warned Surena not to bring him into the alchemy lab anymore. I should prepare a big pair of shears to keep here, damn it… the ones I use for cutting herbs are clearly not big enough.”

Hearing her say this, Jace shifted his legs uncomfortably. “Why does it have to be an incubus? You could summon a female one.”

“What do you mean?” Ursula asked. “Isn’t it males summoning females and females summoning males?”

“Gakin summoned an incubus too, remember?” Jace said. “I think it’s not fixed — or it depends on the casting materials. We haven’t figured it out yet.”

“Ah… you’re right.” Ursula nodded, then shook her head. “No need. Basadim is very obedient, and the imp is a big help too. That’s enough for me — I’m not interested in summoning a succubus of my own. If I ever really need a succubus to do something for me, I’ll just ask Miss Morophil for help. You’ll send her over, right?”

“Of course,” Jace nodded. “She likes you too, Ursula.”

“She doesn’t like me. She helps me because I’m your friend, that’s all.” Ursula raised her eyebrows. “She makes a point of emphasizing that every time she helps, and she loves to say, ‘If it weren’t for him, I’d hang every idiot in this basement up and whip them all…’”

Hearing this, Jace laughed. “She’s just joking. She doesn’t even have a whip.”
