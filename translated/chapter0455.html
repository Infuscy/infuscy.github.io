Chapter 455: Shadow Council Recipe

Leaving the Slaughtered Lamb, Jace pulled his cloak tight, keeping an eye out for anyone who might be watching him.

He figured that right now, instead of Detect Demon, he could use a more advanced Detect Humanoid spell — to figure out exactly who was paying attention to him. What Byssa Eschad in Lordaeron had told him had turned his earlier suspicions into reality, and now it was making him a bit paranoid too.

No wonder Galders was always suspicious of this and that. The man was running an underground dark magic ring hidden deep in the Mage Quarter — it was only natural for him to be constantly on edge.

Following the address Tharle had provided, Jace arrived at the door of a small building by the canal. Behind him, across the canal, lay the plaza of the Cathedral of Light. Jace was quite sure this wasn't Galders's only residence. It was hard to imagine that guy content to live in such a shabby little dump forever.

He climbed the steps and knocked, asking softly, "Galders?"

Before long the door was pulled open. It was Galders himself. Seeing Jace, he immediately looked up and said, "Jace... you've finally come to see me. Tharle told you, didn't he? Last time you came back, why didn't you say anything and just leave?"

"I was in a hurry to get back to Lordaeron, Galders. I'm sorry," Jace said. "And at the time I didn't know where to find you. I'd forgotten that Tharle knew where we live."

Galders nodded. "That's on me too. I haven't been going to the Slaughtered Lamb much lately either. After all, Bosadim — who doesn't talk much — is helping us watch over the basement there now. I feel a lot more at ease than before."

He tilted his head. "Come in."

Jace nodded and walked into the room, immediately hit by the thick scent of succubus. It made him uncomfortable. He'd already gotten used to Molophil's smell, and was always weirdly wary of the strange tastes other succubi carried — probably because he'd run into too many succubi in the Blasted Lands who'd wanted to slit his throat.

In the dimly lit room, record scrolls and boxes of casting materials in every size were scattered everywhere. Senlura sat completely naked on a chair in the corner, lit by the firelight on one side that illuminated half her face and body, her skin and full breasts glowing a ruddy red.

She stood up, smiled this way, and said, "Jace, we meet again."

"It's still Mr. Sesso, Miss Senlura," Jace said coldly.

"As you wish, Mr. Sesso," the succubus said softly.

Galders smiled. "Senlura is afraid of you, Jace. Make yourself at home."

"More like she's afraid of Molophil," Jace said, sitting down to one side. "What's scary about me?"

At the mention of Molophil's name, the smile on Senlura's face instantly deflated.

She couldn't help asking, "I've always been curious, Mr. Sesso — how did you and Miss Molophil meet? She's a high-ranking shayateen, a witherer... They're followers of the Pit Lords, assassins employed by the Eredar... they shouldn't have any dealings with mortals at all."

"That's none of your business, Senlura," Galders said, displeased.

"It's fine," Jace said. "You may have heard, Galders — I was involved in the operation to hunt down the leader of the death knights. As a reward, the shadow gem from Teron Gorefiend's staff was left in my keeping, and I used that gem to summon Molophil. After all, at the time I wasn't sure what to use as casting material for the summoning ritual. I suppose I just got lucky."

Galders sighed lightly. "Senlura just speaks without thinking. But I'm still glad you're being candid with me. I did hear about that... You really are a capable spellcaster, whether as a mage or as one of us, Jace. At first I even wondered whether the Jace Sesso in those rumors about Gorefiend's death was actually the same person as you."

At this, he said thoughtfully, "So... different casting materials can attract different demons..."

Senlura furrowed her brow and asked in an urgent tone, "Master, are you planning to abandon me... I've never done anything to wrong you. I've never betrayed you."

"What are you talking about, Senlura?" Galders said. "We and Jace are friends and partners now. We're cooperating well — that's what I value most."

"Thank you, Master. I feel much better," Senlura said with a smile, narrowing her eyes. "I'll love and serve you even more."

With that, Senlura slowly leaned in and kissed the dark-haired warlock's neck. In the dim light, the intimacy between the two carried a peculiar, eerie quality.

Jace awkwardly cleared his throat. "Galders, how did those Demonic materials turn out? Were they any help?"

"You mean the ones you left with Ursula," Galders said, wrapping an arm around the succubus and nodding. "Jace, if you'd brought them out earlier, we might have resolved a lot of problems sooner. I used your Demonic materials to fix the blockages that had been stuck in several spells, including the incantations for Corruption and Fel Breath... We originally found these two spells from the orcs, but they were always hard to pull off. After adjusting them against the Demonic text, casting them went much more smoothly. Ursula and Sulenna have been practicing too. The progress is quite encouraging."

Jace explained, somewhat helplessly, "I brought them out as quickly as I could. Translating those materials took me and my demon servants a lot of time, Galders, and you know succubi aren't intimately familiar with every Demonic incantation either."

"I'm well aware of that," Galders nodded. "And I'm not letting you hand over all that hard work for nothing. Your contribution will definitely be rewarded. You know that tailor I found — Galene Ailuo? She has a method for crafting shadow robes that she got from the Shadow Council. You know the Shadow Council?"

"Of course I know it," Jace said. "The orcs' warlock council."

"Right, and it's a very high-level group — an elite band that answers directly to their Warchief," Galders said. "The robe's crafting process and materials are written in Orcish, and right now we're still looking for someone who can translate Orcish to render that recipe. The plan is, once we finish Galene's first robe, the second one will be an attempt at crafting that Shadow Council robe... Of course, with appropriate modifications to better suit the human frame."

Jace said, "I studied some Orcish at the Wizard Sanctum, Galders. My vocabulary's a bit limited, but I could go to the library to—"

At this, he frowned. His ring had been confiscated — he probably couldn't get into the library anymore.

Right — Marin had said he could have a new ring made under a new name. He didn't need some apprentice mage's ring; just an assistant's or apprentice's badge would do, and he could consult the Orcish materials in the library.

"You really can?" Galders sighed. "When are you going to tell me everything you know, Jace?"

"I didn't know Orcish when I joined the Dagger Society. I've been picking it up slowly over the past year, Galders," Jace said, scratching his head. "But getting into the Wizard Sanctum's mage tower is a bit of a problem for me right now. If you really can't find an Orcish translator, give it to me first and I'll see what I can do."

Galders said, "There aren't many other options right now, Jace. People who know Orcish are hard to find, but not impossible. It's just that someone both trustworthy and skilled enough — I don't see any hope of finding that. There's no way I'd hand a recipe that Galene Ailuo obtained to warlocks elsewhere — dwarves especially — for translation. This is the Dagger Society's property."

Jace nodded. "I understand."

Galders said, "Then, next time I run into Galene, I'll tell her to hand it over to you. For now, help me look at this first — it's one of the reasons I had Tharle call you here."

With that, he gently stroked Senlura's breast. "Fetch me that incantation I showed you last time and bring it to Jace."
