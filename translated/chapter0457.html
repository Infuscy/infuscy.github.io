Chapter 457: Names and Their Masters

Early morning, and it was still raining. Jace stepped outside and held out his hand — the rain was a bit cold.

He threw on his cloak and headed toward the Mage Quarter. Probably because of the rain, there weren't many people around — mostly eager apprentices waiting for their instructors to arrive.

Jace glanced toward Marin's study and saw the door open briefly. Was that Marin? He should have returned from Nethergarde Keep by now — after all, the various soldiers and mages coming to reinforce the front had already been making their way back home one after another before Jace left.

If he wanted into the Wizard Sanctum, he'd better go get a ring sooner rather than later.

He arrived at the study door and knocked. The familiar voice inside said, "Come in!"

He pushed the door open and saw Marin sitting behind his desk. He glanced up at the figure in the doorway, then smiled. "You're back from Lordaeron, Mr. Sesso? When did you arrive in Stormwind?"

"About two or three days ago. Though it's Mr. York now, Master," Jace said with a polite smile, sitting properly in the chair to the side and setting his soaking-wet cloak aside.

"York... lilac arcane flame... I know it. It's a name favored by Dark Iron dwarves." A hint of amusement showed on Marin's face. "I hate to say it, but it sounds exactly like the name of every dark-magic practitioner who ever got caught."

"Is the name really that unlucky?" Jace asked.

"It's a Dwarvish problem," Marin explained. "Dark magic researchers think it's a great idea to pick some language other than Common, find a pretty word, and ideally tie it a little to demons or darkness — then boom, mission accomplished. Unique and with a touch of exotic flair. But in the end, every single one of their colleagues thinks the same way. And most of these people don't know any language besides Common and Dwarvish, so Dwarvish gets to be the popular choice."

"That said, I can make you a ring under the name York to certify your identity, Mr. Sesso. It's no big deal — after all, don't forget that Archmage Khadgar's name is also Dwarvish. Before him, every Khadgar gave people the impression of an uncultured brute, but now Khadgar has become quite a refined name. So in the end, what a name really comes down to is the person who bears it."

Jace rubbed his eyes. He was starting to regret letting Grede pick his name for him, but since it was done, so be it.

"Thank you, Master," he nodded.

"Oh, right — where's Amy?"

"Amy's already gone to Dalaran," Marin said, peering at him over his glasses. "Something you need her for? She originally wanted to wait for you to come back, but the Kirin Tor didn't give her much leeway on time, so she left."

"Nothing important. I can't take up her time," Jace said. "Is she a full mage now?"

"Of course. What exam could be more valuable than the Battle of Nethergarde Keep? And her other grades had long since reached the required standard." Marin sighed softly. "Honestly, at first I wasn't too keen on her going to Dalaran. After all, the Kirin Tor keeps absorbing the most talented magical minds from the other six kingdoms — at this rate, when will we ever be able to stand shoulder to shoulder with them? But on the other hand, I'm happy for her too. Her teacher Amansa is one of the Kirin Tor's top frost mages."

"I'm happy for her too, Master," Jace said.

"If Amy heard you say that, she'd be very happy too," Marin said with a smile. "But then — you came back from the north and came straight to me for a ring so quickly. Is there some urgent need?"

Jace nodded. "I have to keep studying Orcish and Draconic, so I need to consult materials in the Wizard Sanctum's library. Languages get rusty if you don't use them for a while — it's been months since I touched either. Getting a ring from order to completion should take a few days, so I thought I'd come early. Thank you for the help, Master."

"Don't mention it," Marin said. "If you ever run into difficulties I can help with, come find me anytime, Mr. Sesso. Though try to come in the morning if you can — it gets crowded at noon or in the afternoon, and quite a few people still remember your face."

"I'll remember, Master."

Jace nodded and was about to head out when he thought of something. "Master, there's one more thing I wanted to ask."

"Go ahead."

"I'd like to write a letter to Quel'Thalas — to Lady Vereesa Windrunner. But given my current situation... I need to find an elf mage who knows my circumstances to deliver it. Is there anyone in Stormwind I can trust?"

Marin thought for a moment. "There is a Lady Aysalin of Quel'Thalas still in the Mage Quarter. She's a high arcanist of the Magisterium and participated in maintaining the arcane crystals during the final battle, so she knows your circumstances."

"Where does she live?" Jace asked.

Marin said, "Go through the passage due west of the Wizard Sanctum gardens, into the deepest part of that community. There's a small building there for the elf mages who fought in the Battle of the Dark Portal to rest in. There shouldn't be many elf mages still there."

"Thank you, Master." Jace nodded.

He stepped outside, looked up at the sky, and headed for the Slaughtered Lamb. He'd already arranged with Galders to try out the new incantation today.

When he entered the main hall, Galders and Senlura were already waiting behind the bonfire.

Ursula was sitting off to one side too, with the hellhound Bosadim lying at her feet. The alchemist even had her hand resting on the hellhound's back, covered in those weird black wisps of fur and scales.

These two had really gotten thick.

But Jace felt a bit more at ease too. With Bosadim standing guard, even if something went wrong with a succubus summoning at the Slaughtered Lamb, at least Ursula would be a lot safer.

Succubi might be good at combat, but against a hellhound's relentless pressure they'd have a hard time using their strengths — after all, even attempting to charm required mana. Galene Ailuo sat off by herself, and Jace noticed she was hugging a staff wrapped in cloth. There was probably another shadow-energy-infused gemstone head hidden inside.

"Sorry, everyone," Jace said. "I had some things to take care of, so I'm a bit late."

Galders stood up. "Tharle's lamb is already tied up in the practice room, Jace. We can start practicing the new incantation anytime."

Jace nodded. He was pretty curious about this new magic too.

The group headed to the practice room. Ursula handed Jace a Shadow Protection Potion. After he drank it, Galders said, "We've all tried it, including Galene — no effect."

"But there is definitely a sense of shadow power being stirred up. I think we're just not doing something right," Galene said.

Jace looked at the newcomer. "Then let me give it a try."

"Remember what I said before about shadow magic being influenced by emotion? Galders, I think it's hard for us humans to perceive the emotion inside the magic through spellcasting alone — but a succubus can. This time, have Senlura focus on sensing the shadow power I gather when I cast. Let's see if she notices anything."

"You hear that, Senlura?" Galders asked.

"Of course. At your service, Master," Senlura said with a smile. "And Mr. Sesso's."
