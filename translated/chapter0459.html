Chapter 459: The Role of Emotion

Galders was brimming with confidence, and he had reason to be. Jace noticed that by the time he walked to the front of the group, shadow power was already creeping along in his wake.

He was already trying to adjust his emotions... and quite intensely at that.

Galders was right — he'd long ago detected the influence of emotion on dark magic, he just hadn't been able to convince himself that the two were genuinely connected before.

So when Jace personally proved that this method truly worked, he'd quickly recovered the feelings he'd once cast aside.

Galders chanted the incantation skillfully. At first he didn't use a staff to cast, instead trying to gather shadow power with both hands — but clearly this attempt was far more difficult than expected.

After a few tries, he too had to pick up the long staff leaning against the wall and try again, imitating Jace's gestures...

This time the channeling succeeded on the very first attempt, though he couldn't manage to hurl the Shadow Bolt out smoothly. The second time, he completed the entire spell successfully. When the Shadow Bolt struck the wall, though not as powerful as Jace's, it was still plenty devastating.

Galders looked at the spreading dark energy and smiled. "You were right, Jace. Once you master the emotional trick, everything changes. I imagine the dwarves know this too — they just refuse to give away even a scrap of the knack, those bastards..."

"Maybe they don't know either, and are just going by feel," Jace said. "I once met a dwarf warlock who thought he was really strong, but in reality he was just mediocre."

"Only because he ran into you," Galders said with a smile.

He went on, "Actually, I originally tried to channel the energy without relying on the staff's power to guide it, but it was completely futile. Shadow Bolt looks simple, but it really does presuppose that your preparations are solid."

"I think I need a shadow staff to help me cast too, Galders," Ursula said.

Galders nodded. "The orc warlocks left behind a considerable number of staffs in this world, Ursula. Don't worry — I'll find you one suited for casting shadow magic soon. Sulenna and Erland don't have their own staffs either. I've been working on this."

"Controlling your own emotions... sounds interesting," Galene Ailuo said softly. "I'd like to be the next to try, Galders."

"Go ahead, Galene Ailuo," Galders said, stepping aside.

Galene unwrapped the cloth from her staff, revealing a small, exquisite shadow stone inside.

She walked to the front of the group, glanced briefly at Jace, then closed her eyes as if mentally reviewing everything Jace had just done.

Then she raised her staff with focus and attempted to chant the incantation. The effect wasn't good, though — while she tried hard to gather shadow power to the staff head with her hands, and shadow power did continuously gather along with the incantation's guidance, it couldn't form an energetic mass with any force.

Galene pushed herself for all she was worth, and in the end could only sigh in exhaustion.

"Pushing too hard isn't good either," Jace said.

"You're right," Galene said. "I have a hard time maintaining the patience to guide magic like threading a needle. When I studied magic in Alterac, my teacher pointed that out as my problem."

"Everyone has their strengths," Galders said. "Here you can relax and practice at your own pace, Galene. There are no exams, no strict targets. Think of us as family and companions, not your superiors or teachers."

"I know. Thank you, Galders," Galene nodded. "Maybe I need more time to adjust. I feel like I'm still quite a ways from success."

"Then that just leaves me," Ursula said. "I just need to make my mood as depressed and low as Senlura described, then speak the incantation and use the gesture to guide shadow power on the staff, right? Can I try with your staff, Jace?"

Jace handed her the staff. "Of course."

Ursula walked to the front of the group with Gorefiend's staff, facing the opposite wall.

She glanced this way, then looked at the staff in her hands. Jace clearly felt her mood sink rapidly.

The alchemist stared fixedly at the wall, quiet for a long while — brewing something — then began chanting the incantation.

They'd clearly practiced this spell a few times before, so Ursula had the incantation down quite fluently. Her slender fingers guided over the Gorefiend gem, and Jace felt shadow power gathering with incredible smoothness... even more smoothly than Galders.

By the common understanding of the day, Ursula counted as a half-elf, so she might have some innate advantage with magic — though her elven blood was already so diluted it was practically negligible. Rather than half-elf, she was more like a human with elven ancestors. Could she really surpass Galders on the strength of that racial edge alone?

Jace was skeptical.

Maybe it was the staff, but...

Was it the emotional factor? What was she thinking right now? What was Ursula recalling to access this emotion?

She'd mentioned before that her father had died early, after which she'd left the Kingdom of Lordaeron and come to Stormwind.

Right — in that regard, she'd have a much easier time summoning up this magic than he did.

After a few attempts, Ursula drank a mana potion and finally managed to hurl the shadow orb she'd gathered out at the wall.

For her, releasing the shadow orb was far harder than gathering it — but that was just a matter of experience, since she rarely cast spells.

"I did it... Jace," Ursula said, glancing at the black shockwave where the shadow struck the wall. By now her forehead was already beaded with sweat.

Jace even felt that the power of her Shadow Bolt was indeed a bit stronger than Galders's — and Galders noticed too, involuntarily gasping in surprise.

Ursula took the towel Jace handed her and wiped her neck. "It's exhausting, Jace, but... I feel a real sense of accomplishment. I can't wait for Sulenna to come back and see my new trick."

She looked again at the spot her Shadow Bolt had struck and couldn't help but smile.

Galene Ailuo stood to one side, brows tightly locked — clearly anxious.

Jace could guess what she was thinking: even Ursula, widely considered the least talented one here, had succeeded. As an enchanter and robe tailor who'd studied magic, she really ought not to do any worse.

They'd figured out this incantation in one go, and within an afternoon three members had mastered it. Galders was clearly thrilled too.

He looked at the new member and said, "When Erland and Sulenna get back, we'll still need to teach them. Don't get hung up on this moment, Galene. It's foreseeable that for those two, finding the right negative emotions to release a Shadow Bolt won't be simple at all."
