## Chapter 462: Human and Elf

The next morning, Jace went to the place Marin had told him the elven mages were staying, carrying the letter for Vereesa.

The residence the Wizard Sanctum had prepared for the guests from Quel'Thalas was an unremarkable-looking building from the outside — not nearly as grand as the places where Marin and the other senior mages lived. But right now, "unremarkable" only applied to the exterior.

The elven mages had added their own touch. They'd placed a small arcane crystal at the very top of the building, constantly radiating arcane energy. Living there meant bathing in an arcane glow around the clock. The moment you turned into the alley, you could see it clear as day.

No wonder Marin hadn't been more specific with the address. You really could tell which building it was the instant you arrived.

But when he got to the entrance, he saw a battle mage standing guard in front of the building.

Jace didn't have a Wizard Sanctum mage ring — no way to prove his identity. The guard probably wouldn't let him in, or go call someone for him...

Jace figured he should just go back and wait for his teacher to get his new ring made before coming back. He was just about to turn around when a female elf in a pale purple robe came hurrying over from the direction of the Wizard Sanctum's high tower.

Jace glanced at her. The elf noticed Jace and involuntarily raised her eyebrows.

Did she know him?

"Excuse me..." Jace asked immediately as they passed each other. "Are you Master Aysalin?"

"You know me?" the elven mage asked, surprised.

"I don't," Jace said with a polite smile. "I just heard from my teacher that you still live in the Wizard Sanctum, and that you're one of the few Quel'dorei mages still remaining in the Mage Quarter."

"You're Jace Sesso." Aysalin glanced off into the distance, then looked back. "I'm not mistaken, is I? So your teacher is Master Marin, senior mage of the School of Abjuration."

"That's right, I am," Jace nodded.

"How can I help you, Mr. Sesso?" the elf asked with a smile.

"I'd like to deliver a letter to Lady Vereesa." Jace took the letter from his bag and held it out to the mage. "This one, right here."

Aysalin took the letter, hefted it, and looked it over. "What's written in this letter?"

"That shouldn't matter, should it?" Jace gave an awkward laugh. "Do mages need to know the contents of a letter before they'll send it for you?"

"Not exactly," Aysalin said. "Captain Alleria Windrunner took a human as her partner. General Sylvanas even brought a human into Quel'Thalas. And now their younger sister, Lady Vereesa, is getting a little too close to a human... Frankly, if there's anything questionable in this letter, the Sunstrider's envoys are likely to intervene."

"I'm not trying to pry into nobles' private lives or get involved in your romantic affairs. I'm trying to keep you out of trouble, Mr. Sesso. If the letter doesn't touch on those matters, then of course there's no problem. But if it does, then even if I go through the trouble of helping you, there's still a chance it never reaches Lady Vereesa. I just hope you won't come looking to blame me when that happens."

"It's only about the Reliquary," Jace said. "I contacted the Reliquary through Vereesa's help before, sold them some magic-related items. I've got new leads this time, so I wanted to consult her."

"Since you say so, I'll take your word for it, Mr. Sesso." The elf smiled. "This letter will reach Quel'Lithien by this afternoon. To get it into Quel'Thalas and into Lady Vereesa's hands will probably take another two days, so if you're waiting for a reply, give it at least a week. Be patient."

"That's unbelievably fast. Thank you, Master."

Jace nodded and watched Aysalin tuck the letter into her robe and walk through the courtyard gate guarded by the battle mage.

When he got home at noon, Jace pushed open the door and said, "Sazeno, you've got new work."

"New work?!" The imp's jaw dropped. "You're not making me translate some terrifying language again, are you? I don't know that many languages, master! At best I only know a little Nathrezim and Annihilan! Void Shadow language? I don't know a single word of it!"

Jace took the materials Galders had given him — the ones they'd found going through Jagin's room — and set them on the table. "This time you won't have to copy or translate. I just need you to reorganize this material. In demonic, of course."

"What a headache, master." The imp tilted its head. "Those two remaining sections on the scroll you wrote from Teron Gorefiend's memories — I still haven't made any headway on those at all, and now you're piling more work on me. Which one should I do first?"

"This is the simpler one," Jace said. "I trust you can finish it in a few days. The stuff on that scroll isn't urgent."

With that, he glanced sideways at the succubus. "You could help look at that scroll too, Molophil. If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have learned that Shadow Shield so quickly."

The succubus hesitated and made a couple of noncommittal sounds. Jace said, "The imp Galders summoned — Senlura — she's always been very enthusiastic about helping her master organize spell materials. Look at you — the moment it comes to this, you shrink back."

"Galders is more than happy to satisfy Miss Senlura," Molophil lowered her eyes, then looked back up into Jace's. "But my master always finds all sorts of reasons to push me aside, unwilling to satisfy his poor demon servant. How am I supposed to have the energy to serve my master properly like this?"

"How do you know what Galders and Senlura are like on a daily basis?" Jace asked.

"I ask, and Miss Senlura answers." Molophil curled the corner of her mouth and said softly, "Look — other masters pour their hearts into serving their demon servants every day. And me? Listening to their stories, I can barely hold my head up in front of Senlura. At this rate, even the succubi summoned by the other humans will look down on me. Humiliating."

Jace sighed. "Are you even in the same league as those other succubi, Molophil? You say Senlura and Grede pour their hearts into it every day, but when I went to Galders's place, I didn't see him too weak to stand up."

As Molophil grabbed Jace's wrist and said softly, "My master and those warlocks are in completely different leagues..."

Reverse PUA, damn it.

Jace sighed. "Alright, alright. Let me rest a day first. I've been practicing that Shadowbolt incantation I just learned. Burned through a lot of mana."

The succubus reached into Jace's potion pouch and pulled out a Mana Potion. "Congratulations, master. Just drink this one, darling."

"Why don't you drink it?" Jace asked.

Molophil snorted coldly and put the potion back. Sazeno had been covering his face with paper, pretending to read, and at this he couldn't help but start shaking.

She immediately snapped in his direction, "The hell are you laughing at?!"
