## Chapter 463: The Orcish Robes

Another two days passed, and Jace figured his ring should be engraved by now. He took advantage of a gloomy, rain-threatening morning when few people were about and headed to the Mage Quarter to ask Marin.

The Wizard Sanctum had engraved him an apprentice mage sigil. This time it wasn't just the initials of his surname and given name — it was a full "Uurk."

With this ring, Jace could enter and leave the Wizard Sanctum again. It felt pretty good.

Still, he'd harbored a small hope — that maybe the Wizard Sanctum would give him a full mage ring... After all, he was "the young and fearless battle mage Jace Sesso," wasn't he?

But then again, that was Jace Sesso. What did that have to do with Uurk...

If he actually got a full mage ring, it might draw more attention. And more trouble.

Thinking it that way, he let it go.

After getting the ring, Jace brought the robe-making manual Galene Ailuo had found and headed into the Wizard Sanctum.

The battle mage at the gate gave his ring a quick inspection and waved him through. Stepping into the library, breathing in that familiar scent of old books mingled with the magical aura flowing from the arcane wards — it was like the old feeling had come back.

Jace made his way to the language section without missing a beat, found the area with the Orcish texts, located the dictionaries, the Common-Orcish glossaries, and so on. He pulled down a few books, set a pot of coffee and a small pack of dried bread slices beside him, and started translating right there.

Thinking about it, the last time he'd sat down this seriously to translate and copy a document was back when he'd first gotten that order from Gorefiend.

Of course, this manual had far more content than that letter, but Jace's Orcish had come a long way since then.

He could understand most of it. He just needed to track down some of the less common, more formal Orcish usages and specialized terms.

Jace could even pick out a few demonic words mixed in. Those he'd have to go back and ask Sazeno and Molophil about — his demonic still wasn't at the level of translating technical texts.

After reading it carefully, Jace realized that most of the Orcish booklet was teaching the reader how to sew special patterns and runes with special magical materials to enhance the garment's power. The part about actually constructing the robe itself was comparatively casual, but that made the translation a lot harder.

By looking up quite a few Orcish magic-related terms, he figured out that crafting these magical runes required the power of souls and elemental flame.

Considering this was a crafting method from Draenor, did that mean it needed Draenor's Essence of Fire and Essence of Undeath? The sort of "Primordial Fire" things from the game?

Did Draenor even have "Primordial Undeath"? Jace couldn't quite remember the details from his past life anymore, especially these fine points.

In any case, he wrote everything down exactly as the book described it. If that didn't work, he'd drag someone with the relevant knowledge over to ask later.

Then there was a large quantity of demonic thread, which had to be sourced from the armor and garments worn by Mo'arg demons. Jace had a feeling this was what the game called "Felcloth" — in the game, you had a chance of getting it from killing high-level demons.

But here, you had to find demon corpses, strip their clothes, and pull out the raw materials.

And then the book wrote about needing demon skin — specifically, skin segments inscribed with fel markings. You had to cut these pieces of skin off and transfer the power of the fel markings onto the runes of the garment.

Jace managed to translate this section, but he didn't quite understand what the actual process was. Sew the demon skin onto the garment?

Maybe this part would make more sense to a Shadow Council tailor.

Fortunately, even though the booklet looked thick, a lot of the space was taken up by hand-drawn illustrations, and each sheet of paper was quite heavy, so there wasn't actually that much to translate. Jace spent about two days working through all the unfamiliar terms and produced a Common-language version.

In the afternoon, after the streets had emptied out, Jace went to the Slaughtered Lamb.

He'd barely stepped into the main hall of the basement when he heard the reverberations of shadow magic echoing from deep in the tunnel.

Galene was still practicing Shadowbolt.

Jace walked down the tunnel. As he got closer to the practice room, the concentric waves of shadow energy already made him feel uncomfortable.

When he reached the doorway, Galene was channeling her next Shadowbolt. Three empty Mana Potion bottles were already lined up beside her, plus one that was half full.

When she noticed Jace, she put down her staff in an uncharacteristic show of self-consciousness.

"Jace Sesso."

Jace nodded. "I've more or less finished translating that robe recipe you gave me, Galene. I saw you were here, so I thought I'd come let you know."

There was a flicker of surprise in Galene's expression.

"I didn't expect you to finish so fast."

"Fast, yes. But even though I've translated most of it, there are still parts I don't fully understand. I did a straight translation." Jace took out his Common-language notes. "It says here that it needs a kind of elemental essence — the sort of elemental energy the orcs found on Draenor. Can you find the same thing on Azeroth?"

"I don't know if it's exactly the same, since I've never been to the other side of the Dark Portal," Galene said. "But I know Azeroth's elemental essences can serve pretty much the same purpose."

"Making this robe requires elemental essences of both fire and undeath," Jace said. "Neither of those sounds like the kind of thing that's easy to get your hands on."

Hearing about these materials, Galene frowned too.

"Maybe check Booty Bay... see if the goblins have any for sale?"

Jace nodded. "Before that, I'll go ask Ursula and see if she has any ideas. Is Ursula here?"

"She left not long ago," Galene said.

"Well, looks like my timing's off," Jace said.

That evening he got home and set down the section of his translation about the fel markings and demon skin on the table. "Molophil, take a look at this for me. What does it mean, 'transfer the power of the fel markings'?"

Molophil came over and wrapped her arms around Jace's waist, lowered her eyes to glance at what Jace had written, then said, "Master's handwriting is really pretty."

"Skip the useless stuff," Jace said.

"Mm..." The succubus gave a soft laugh. "A lot of demons in the Burning Legion are tattooed with magical sigils. These fel markings have many uses — maybe to strengthen their bodies, or boost their mana, or make them more loyal and obedient, or even turn them into machines that follow orders without question... like those on the Felguard."

Molophile's words reminded Jace of the demon hunters.
