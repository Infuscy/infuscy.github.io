Chapter 464: Fel Markings

Demon hunters weren't scavengers like Erland, or self-proclaimed demon hunters. They were a very special group.

They wielded demonic power in mortal bodies, fel blood running through their veins, and in extreme cases could even transform into demonic forms to fight demons. Many demon hunters had even forgotten whether they were mortal or demon.

The world's first demon hunter, Illidan Stormrage, later codified the process of becoming a demon hunter into a systematic method for training recruits. He churned out batch after batch of apprentices, forging an army of demon hunters loyal to himself — the Illidari.

And a crucial method these demon hunters used to gain new power was the demonic tattoos on their bodies.

Obtaining these demonic sigils wasn't easy. The first few batches of demon hunters either went mad or died.

Perhaps the orcs had made similar attempts, wanting to use these fel markings to gain demonic power for themselves. But if they really had tried, they would have failed even more spectacularly than Illidan Stormrage.

But clearly, they'd found another, lower-tier method — copying these fel markings onto garments, letting the clothing take the place of their own skin to gain similar demonic power and strengthen themselves.

Molophil leaned against Jace's shoulder and continued, "And these tattoos require the most powerful demon lords to infuse them with power to take effect — Annihilan, for instance, the same race as the Azgoth you killed, or Nathrezim, or Eredar lords. It's very difficult for mortals to empower these tattoos."

"But with this power attached to a demon's skin... you can cut the skin off directly and stitch it onto your own skin... and gain the same power, master."

As she spoke, her fingers traced across Jace's chest. Of course, Jace didn't feel any affection or seduction from it at all — the mere thought of that scene in his head was enough to make him want to crawl out of his skin.

"Of course," she went on, "the orc who wrote this book was clearly much smarter. They could use a ritual to transfer the sigils from the demon skin onto matching sigils on a garment, and that works too. Demonic script can carry out that kind of transfer."

With Molophil's explanation, Jace finally understood a good deal of it. This was the process of using demonic runes to fully enchant a garment at the end, giving the clothing power.

At that moment, Jace glanced at the succubus's bare shoulders and midriff. "Why don't you have any of these fel markings on you?"

"I just told you, master," Molophil smiled. "Only the most powerful demons can inscribe such runes on their subordinates to strengthen them. The reason the Burning Legion can put fel markings even on their cannon fodder is precisely because they have no shortage of demon lords. They wanted their legion to be unstoppable... so they armed them to the teeth."

"I, naturally, never had that opportunity. When I served Brutallus, he hadn't joined the Legion yet. And I seriously doubt that idiot even knew this kind of magic. There are clever beings among the Annihilan — their destroyer leader, Mannoroth, for instance — but clearly, Brutallus wasn't one of them."

"Ha!" Sazeno suddenly jumped out. "Didn't think the day would come, Sayaad!"

Molophil narrowed her eyes at the imp. "What do you want?"

Sazeno grinned. "You don't have a lord who inscribed you... but I'll have you know, I do!"

Jace looked at him curiously. He didn't see any sign of fel markings on him.

"Where?" he asked.

"Right here."

Sazeno opened his mouth wide, and Jace actually did see a swirling fel rune on the imp's upper palate.

Sazeno grinned. "Well?..."

"Who tattooed you?" Molophil asked, displeased.

"Why, my former master, the Ghost King Yarozebar, of course!" Sazeno said. "Transferred it from a Demon Inquisitor onto me, forced me to learn all kinds of demonic incantations, trying to make me summon a true Burning Legion emissary to Nashtoi's world so the Burning Legion would accept his allegiance. He made my life a living hell!"

"Look at me, still suffering under my master's torment, just because I learned so much... If I'd known, I'd have kept it to myself. But what choice do I have, if I want my master to grow stronger and stronger?"

Listening to the imp say this, Molophil beside him had already gone dark in the face.

"I want a demonic tattoo like that too..." she said coldly.

"Hahaha!" Sazeno said excitedly. "This female Sayaad is jealous! She's jealous!"

"No." Molophile glared at Jace through clenched teeth. "You're getting me one too."

"Where the hell am I supposed to get one of those?" Jace said helplessly.

"I don't care, master..." Molophil's eyes suddenly darted to the side, as if an idea had just struck her. She grabbed Sazeno and lifted him up. "You just said your former master, the Ghost King Yarozebar, could transfer a demonic rune from an Inquisitor demon onto you. So, do the imps from your world know this ritual?!"

"Maybe, but I... I don't," Sazeno said.

"Don't you dare lie, Sazeno," Jace commanded.

"Ugh, alright, why... damn it, I... fine, alright, I do!" Sazeno said miserably. "But I don't want to transfer any fel marking power for this Sayaad, no!"

Molophil smiled. "Look, master, look. This little snot is still hiding things from you."

"Well observed, Sazeno," Jace said. "I don't know if the new warlock in the basement knows this ritual. If she doesn't, when we've gathered all the materials and it's time to enchant this robe, you'll be the one chosen to perform it."

The imp wailed, "Why is it always me?! I haven't even finished organizing the dead Jagin's incantations for you, master!"

"There's a reward if you finish," Jace said. "Molophil, get him a few soul shards when the time comes."

Molophil had been wearing a wicked grin, but when she heard Jace say this, her face immediately dropped and she muttered, "Why... that lazy good-for-nothing! Without his master pushing him, he can't sort out a few simple incantations in two or three days. All he does here is put on an act..."

"Who says I can't? At the very least I finished Curse of Recklessness," Sazeno said. "The Curse of Recklessness written in here has at least two misheard syllables. If that Jagin could actually cast it, he'd have to be one hell of a dark magic master."

"Gathering the materials will probably take a long time still, Sazeno," Jace said. "In a little while, you two might be coming out with me to join the demon hunt, Molophil. That'll be your time to shine."

"Hah!" Molophil said excitedly. "Finally, a chance to serve my master. All this time, bottled up. Near the Dark Portal, those demons must have done quite a number on you. I'm going to pay them back, one hit at a time..."

Jace thought of Grede, who was probably still in the Blasted Lands helping the Nethergarde Keep soldiers clean out the demons near the Dark Portal. He wondered how things were going in the Black Morass.
