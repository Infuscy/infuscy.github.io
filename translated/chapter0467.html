Chapter 467: The True Shadow Shield

Galders stepped forward eagerly and chanted the incantation using the method Jace had written down.

He closed his eyes for a moment, then channeled the shadow power. Jace had expected it to go poorly, but Galders only needed a second attempt before successfully conjuring the Shadow Shield.

Though it only lasted a few brief seconds, it was clearly much stronger than Jace's earliest attempts.

Next up was Ursula. She tried several times and couldn't even summon the Shadow Shield at all. By the time she was nearly drained of energy, she could only give up voluntarily and yield her turn to Galene.

Jace looked at Ursula gazing at him with a somewhat apologetic expression, and he already had a feeling.

Shadow magic really did have something to do with a person's personality. Some people were just naturally better at accessing certain emotions — coldness, for instance.

By now Galene had already begun chanting. She held the incantation Jace had recorded and attempted the spell. On her very first try she channeled out a shadow silhouette. She looked up and observed this dark afterimage that quickly vanished, and in that motion Jace detected something far more seriously wrong.

Her Shadow Shield... wasn't being channeled at all.

Jace was stunned. On her first attempt, Galene had achieved an effect with this incantation that he himself had never managed.

His own Shadow Shield had never once existed independently of his magical channeling!

Galene Ailuo quickly made a second attempt. This time her expression was blank. She chanted the incantation while facing the cold wall behind the group, and the Shadow Shield emerged from her surroundings with a slight wave of her hand. But she didn't continue channeling — she stopped the motion of her hands, stopped the chanting, and the shield just stayed there on its own for five or six seconds before slowly fading.

"This isn't a channeling incantation..." Jace said. "Looks like I never managed to cast its full effect before."

He couldn't help asking, "Can you tell me, Galene — what were you thinking when you tried to cast this spell?"

"I thought I should be able to surpass you," Galene said.

"That... seems pretty normal too," Jace said with a laugh. "How would this thought bring about an emotion that helps cast this spell? I..."

"Don't you think I'm pretty arrogant?" Galene asked again.

"This spell I've never been able to grasp. I've been using it in a very strange way from the start," Jace sighed. "So I wanted to borrow everyone's help to find its true method of use. Clearly, I think you've found the real approach."

"But you killed a demon general — Azgoth. You're the hero they're talking about everywhere," Galene said.

The smile on Jace's face slowly froze.

Right. If he'd never killed some powerful demon, and one day a basement full of warlocks saw the arrival of a terrifying figure rumored to have slain a Pit Lord...

Would he have been confident he was better at shadow magic than that person?

Definitely not.

Perhaps it was precisely Galene's confidence — one could even say arrogance, and her contempt for those stronger than her — that amplified the effect of this defensive magic, or rather, truly brought out its original effect.

Maybe he still hadn't found the right emotion. But he also didn't believe that personality alone determined whether someone could cast a given incantation. Otherwise, the day an incantation requiring self-pity showed up, would Gul'dan cast it stronger than Kil'jaeden?

Arrogance... contempt.

Who had Jace ever looked down on?

At that moment, he suddenly recalled that instant.

The moment he'd torn open Azgoth's chest with the Hand of Gul'dan.

In his heart, a thought had genuinely surfaced.

——A Pit Lord... is that all?

Though that thought had been extinguished in an instant by Azgoth's wild laughter...

He had, indeed, felt that fully released smugness.

By now Galders was still trying his hand at it with relish — after all, none of them had tried it many times, and the afternoon was still long. After he'd practiced to the point of exhaustion, Jace stood up. "I want to give it another try too."

Galders nodded. Jace walked to the front of the group and slowly closed his eyes, savoring that feeling from back then...

"Tuojiesuogasi, Weiholisia, Wasidaang..."

Jace spoke the incantation fluently. This time, when he spread open the shield, even the casting sensation was completely different from the past.

This time it was much more stable. It didn't need continuous channeling with mana at all... it just floated up directly and protected the surrounding area.

That's right... when Teron Gorefiend had released this shadow barrier, this was exactly how the shadow energy had radiated out. He still remembered that when Teron Gorefiend released the Shadow Shield, he'd even been thinking about using a Shadow Bolt to attack the rangers in the distance... This spell was supposed to be like this.

The shield lasted about two minutes before slowly fading. This time even Galene was astonished, her lips slightly parted.

"I'm still... far from you, Jace," she said quietly.

"You'd been practicing this incantation for a long, long time before you, Galene," Jace said. "Without you bringing up your own trick, I might never have found this feeling."

Galders laughed. "This is why we need a group to progress together — to summarize experience and fill in each other's gaps."

Galene nodded too.

"Why can't I do it," Ursula said, displeased.

"But you mastered Shadow Bolt very easily, Ursula," Jace comforted her. "Every shadow magic is different. You can work on it slowly in the future."

After everyone had practiced a while longer, it was about dinner time. While waiting for Tharle to make bread, Jace found Galders and asked, "When will Erland and Sulenna be back, roughly? Or rather, when will they be ready for the next operation?"

Galders thought for a moment. "If they're coming back, it'll take a week or two. After all, you know the journey from here to the Black Morass takes at least a week on the road, Jace. You have to cross the entire Everstill valley to reach that primitive forest full of gnolls. Coming back here, organizing what they found, finding channels to sell it through, resting for a while, and then setting out again — that should be a month from now, November. When the time comes I'll contact him and have him notify you."

"I'm planning a trip to Booty Bay," Jace said. "The round trip should take about three weeks. If they come back, remember to have them wait for me a bit."

"Three weeks is no problem," Galders said. "Is it about that robe?"

"Right," Jace said. "I'm planning to go ask the goblins."

"Then I'll leave it to you, Jace," Galders said.

Jace smiled. "Don't put it that way — I'm also helping myself find materials to craft a robe."

"With the experience from the first one, the rest will be much easier," Galders said.
