## Chapter 468: Necrotic Essence

After saying goodbye to Ursula, Jace boarded a goblin merchant ship bound for Booty Bay on his own.

Considering that this trip was to meet the elven mages of the Reliquary, there were probably no mages in this world better at tracking demonic traces than them.

So he decided not to bring his two demons along for now.

Still, he was going to Booty Bay, that chaotic place — but this time his state of mind was completely different from last time.

Last time, he'd still been carrying the death curse left behind by Teron Gorefiend, a shadow always hanging over his heart.

This time, he didn't have much time to linger in Booty Bay, so he picked the fastest, earliest ship.

He wouldn't waste time looking for an inn either — once he got there, he'd head straight for his destination.

Aside from consulting the Trade Station about the prices and availability of elemental essences, he also needed to find that goblin alchemist and ask him about the warlock Dasmora Darkeye and her collection.

Eight days later, on an early morning, Jace arrived at Booty Bay without incident. The last time he'd been here was about half a year ago, just as spring was turning. Now it was late autumn, on the cusp of winter. Booty Bay didn't look any different — still just as lively. Only the wind off the sea seemed a tiny bit cooler than before.

Walking all the way from this dock to the Trade Station on the other side, he passed by that human shantytown he'd seen before and deliberately looked inside.

The number of people had visibly dwindled, but if you looked carefully toward the deepest part, you could still notice a few people sitting around a clergyman next to that monastery.

Probably the king couldn't ship everyone away all at once either — that was his only guess.

He could come back and ask after he finished his business.

Jace hadn't imagined that by reporting the situation around the king's circle and to the Church of the Holy Light, he could solve all the refugees' difficulties at once.

Arriving at the Trade Station on the other side, Jace saw the Station's cargo ship docked at the freight pier behind it, the Trade Cartel's claw-and-gear flag hanging from the ship and fluttering in the sea wind.

Just then, an enormous monster carrying a stack of boxes taller than itself walked down off the ship step by step onto the pier.

When it set the boxes down and moved to the side, revealing its upper body, Jace realized it was an ogre...

He'd heard so many stories about ogres roaming Azeroth, and especially after encountering one himself in Duskwood, he'd instinctively tensed up at the sight of these monsters who'd come here from Draenor. But this ogre just wiped its sweat and sat down to one side, while a goblin on the ship shrieked at him nonstop. Jace couldn't understand what language it was either, but the ogre just put on a long face, nodded, and stood back up.

As for the goblins and others on the pier, they completely ignored the whole thing — including the tauren guard who'd been standing next to the Trade Station master the whole time.

That ogre counted as tall and strong even for an ogre. At a glance, it was nearly four meters high. When it walked back onto the merchant ship, the whole vessel seemed to rock slightly.

"Never seen an ogre before, human? That thing could smash your little face into something thinner than crab jam with one punch," a goblin beside him said with a grin. "Welcome to the Trade Station. Would you like to inquire about the auction schedule, or are you looking to buy something?"

"I'm looking to buy something," Jace shifted his gaze from the ogre to the goblin standing on the crate. "I don't know if the Trade Station has Fire Essence and Necrotic Essence for sale."

The goblin raised his eyebrows. "Fire Essence, yes. But no Necrotic Essence, young sir. After the Orc Wars ended, Trade Prince Revilgaz reached an agreement with the Kirin Tor and the Alliance — several casting materials used extensively in necromantic magic can't be traded at the Trade Station in Booty Bay. Necrotic Essence is unfortunately one of them. But no worries — those sneaky necromancers are mostly broke anyway! Hah!"

"Of course, I won't report you just because you're a necromancer. The agreement doesn't include us driving necromancers out of Booty Bay!"

"I'm not a necromancer," Jace said.

"Then what good is that?" the goblin asked.

Jace said, "My employer wants to use Necrotic Essence for experiments, to deal with the undead plague problem in Duskwood."

The goblin nodded. "Sounds pretty interesting, but unfortunately, what's sold here isn't up to me. It's not even something the Station supervisor Razlovitz has any say in."

"Then what about Fire Essence?" Jace asked this way, even though he neither knew the price nor how such a thing was even packaged.

"Three gold and thirty silver a vial, sir," the goblin said. "The Fire Essence shipped here from Kalimdor is quite plentiful, and that includes the cost of the elemental vials and transport. If you use up the Fire Essence inside, you could haggle your way to more than a gold for the empty vial in Dalaran. Really a great deal."

Hearing that price, Jace felt his vision go dark.

If Fire Essence was already selling for this price, he wondered how much Necrotic Essence — which wasn't even allowed to be sold here — would cost.

At noon he bought some grilled prawns at the inn to tide him over, and in the afternoon Jace followed the route from before up to the highest point of Booty Bay to find that Bacchus Mithershield.

He wondered how this fellow's business had been doing over the past half year.

He'd prepared for all sorts of scenarios — him running away to dodge taxes, or getting assassinated by Noggenfogger over modified formulas and stolen clients... or some other bizarre situation. But when he arrived, Mithershield was still lying in a chair, face turned up to bask in the sun, exactly identical to the first time Jace had come here.

"Mr. Mithershield," Jace called.

The goblin took off his glasses and looked over, gave a surprised yelp, then said, "You're still not dead?"

"Why would I be dead?" Jace asked.

"I figured a reckless guy like you would've gotten himself killed in a few days, for sure," the goblin said with a laugh.

"Reckless? What did I do?" Jace asked again.

"Oh, don't tell me you lost your memory?" The goblin walked over and knocked on Jace's belt buckle. "That business you did at Syliaa's — she told me everything, down to the last detail."

Hearing this, Jace took a pained breath. "Just pretend you never heard it. Besides, wasn't that for your new formula?"

"I know, I understand! We have a saying where I come from — what happens in Booty Bay stays in Booty Bay, hey!" The goblin laughed. "But isn't that exactly why you came back?"

"Let's talk business," Jace said coldly.

"Business, business!" The goblin laughed, then his face suddenly darkened. "You're not here to ask me for money, are you? My Mithershield Potion hasn't officially hit the market yet. You don't think the Trade Station would just approve a new potion that easily, do you?"

"Say whatever you want. I never had any expectations to begin with," Jace said. "I'm not here for money. I have a few things I want to ask you."

Mithershield let out a relieved breath. "Ask away, ask away. I love questions! Asking questions, answering questions — all good."

"Since you're an alchemist..."

"The best alchemist in all of Booty Bay!"

"Right, the best alchemist in all of Booty Bay. Do you know where I can buy Necrotic Essence?"

Hearing Jace ask this, the goblin's still-interested expression immediately cooled.

"Necrotic Essence hasn't been allowed for trade in Booty Bay since the Orc Wars, young man. Since you came to me asking, you ought to know about that rule, right?"

Jace nodded.

"Then aren't you looking for trouble for me?" the goblin said.

"I won't tell anyone I bought it from here," Jace said.

"Did I say I have any for sale?!" the goblin said, displeased. "And I can't trust you. The moment you lot get caught by the Kirin Tor, you spill everything. I still owe Revilgaz money — if they fine me again on top of that..."

After a moment, he lowered his voice. "But well, I do happen to have some dark hearts of undead creatures. You can take them and extract from them yourself."

The goblin glanced sideways and held out three fingers, excluding his pinky. "I only sell them for thirty silver each. But how much you can extract depends on your alchemist friend's skill. What I'm trading isn't necromantic materials — it's body parts of undead creatures. That should be fine, right?"

"Hearts taken from what kind of undead creature?" Jace asked.

"Uh." The goblin pursed his lips. "Naturally... my experimental subjects. They die and come back, die and come back... the leftover body parts can't go to waste, right?"

"How much Necrotic Essence can you even extract from that?!" Jace said. "Don't try to mess with me. To extract Necrotic Essence you'd need powerful undead monsters. You're not a necromancer — aren't your experimental subjects all just gnolls and the like?"

The goblin said, "The Necrotic Essence other necromancers use is extracted the same way... Just be content. At least the hearts I give you are carefully selected — they'll definitely yield a tiny bit."

Jace hesitated for a moment. "Sounds like you know quite a bit about it. Then do you know where to get a lot of Necrotic Essence?"

Mithershield stuck out his tongue. "You looking for a challenge?"

"Tell me."

"You know about... the ghosts of Karazhan? I have a Trade Cartel honor product here that can extract Necrotic Essence from ghosts."

"You want me to go to Karazhan?! No way. That's purely asking for death — no, worse than death."

"You don't need to go into Karazhan tower. It's still far from Karazhan — just the valley over there." Mithershield leaned in close. "The rewards are abundant, young sir..."

"I'll buy a few undead hearts first," Jace said. "And there's another thing I wanted to ask you, about a warlock..."
