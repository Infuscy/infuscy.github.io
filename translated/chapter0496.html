Chapter 496: What Dwarves and Demons Have in Common

"Don't tell me what I can and can't do, demon!" Grede shouted at once. "I'm warning you—"

"I am the master's friend." Morophil, standing behind Jace, spoke in an even tone. "So I won't hurt you. But you'd better not act so hostile either, making things pointlessly difficult for both of us."

Grede's gaze flicked quickly to Jace, then snapped back to the succubus. "How am I supposed to not be on guard facing a demon?! And a succubus at that."

"Jace, you know what those succubi who came through the Dark Portal are like — you've killed enough of them. You know how terrifying they are, how many of our people those monsters slaughtered!"

"I know, Grede." Jace nodded.

The dwarf wrinkled his nose, then said, "Damn it, that's the smell all over again. Makes me feel like I'm back at Nethergarde Keep. You lot really do stink something awful…"

Morophil smiled. "You'd best not lump me in with those brainless mongrels who grovel before the Burning Legion, Mr. Thunderfist."

"How the hell am I supposed to tell the difference?" Grede demanded. "Looks the same damn thing to me!"

"All right, then — to accommodate your limited powers of judgment, let me show you the most obvious difference." Morophil turned around. "See? No wings."

"Huh? No wings?" Grede, who'd been scowling a moment ago, blinked in surprise.

Jace figured that throwing a Wildhammer dwarf and a succubus together like this out of nowhere had been a bit too abrupt. He put a hand on Grede's shoulder and steered him toward the door. "All right, all right. I'll take you out for a walk. Don't be angry… Grede."

Grede wore a dazed expression as Jace hauled him out of the alley. A good while later, he glanced at Jace and pointed back toward the house. "That succubus really didn't have wings. Did you notice?"

"Did I notice?" Jace frowned. "You think I could've missed that? Her previous master tore them off. He must have been too powerful — damaged the integrity of her soul. So even after being resurrected several times since, the wings never grew back."

"Soul integrity? Resurrection?" Grede said. "What are you talking about…"

"I'll explain it to you slowly later."

Jace led Grede to the canal. Grede sat down on the ledge by the water and said, "I've never seen a wingless succubus. That monster was right — that does make them pretty easy to tell apart."

"Why do you care so much about her wings?" Jace said.

"Found it weird," Grede said. "I always thought all demons looked basically the same, just carrying different weapons or growing differently shaped horns. Who's got the time to tell demons apart on a battlefield? But the presence or absence of wings — that's a pretty obvious distinction."

Jace clearly sensed that Grede's attitude had shifted a lot after Morophil mentioned her severed wings. Could it be that it reminded him of his own eye?

Then again, a Wildhammer dwarf sympathizing with a demon was frankly too absurd, and Jace immediately shook the thought out of his head.

"I never expected you to accept this demon right off the bat, Grede. But she's helped me a lot, and she's never hurt me. I just felt it wasn't right to keep hiding it from you all this time — I just never found the right moment to tell you."

"You should have told me from the start! Otherwise if the demon you summoned had done me in, I'd never have known! Succubi aren't imps!" the dwarf said. "But hey, Jace Sesso — no wonder you always looked so nonchalant whenever we went out, no matter what inn we stayed at. Turns out you were already set for food and drink! And that succubus is really something, isn't she? No wonder you couldn't be bothered with the girls out there, you bastard."

"You find a demon attractive? Have you been charmed?" Jace asked.

"Now that you mention it, maybe…" Grede rubbed his face. "Have I been charmed? How do I even tell if I've been charmed?"

"Rubbing your face definitely ain't gonna tell you shit," Jace said. "But don't worry — she probably didn't charm you. She follows my orders. Even her charm magic works that way."

"Everything?" Grede asked. "Just like that imp?"

Jace said, "Most things. The vast majority."

Grede nodded meaningfully, then let out a laugh. "I think I'm straying further and further from the teachings of the Wise Ones, Jace. I'm actually a little jealous of your demon."

Jace said, "Then you could head to Shadowforge City this time. They'd gladly take you in."

"No thanks," Grede said. "I prefer my rich and colorful single life. I've already been through that kind of thing once — I don't need a second round. You think some elf is any better than a succubus?"

"I'll admit, there was definitely that angle to keeping a succubus around…" Jace said. "But the benefits Morophil brings me go far beyond that. For example — if not for her, I might never have learned the spell that killed Azgoth."

"You mean that black meteor spell," Grede said.

"Right," Jace said. "She knows shadow inside and out… If I want to master shadow magic any further, doing it without her advice and guidance would be a lot harder."

"Right now, she'd be a huge help on this Kalimdor trip. The Reliquary's mission for me is tracking down an elven warlock who's been alive for ages, and based on my earlier investigation… this person is very likely a demonology expert. Morophil is extremely good at dealing with demons — trust me, Grede, extremely good. If we bring her to Kalimdor, our chances of earning a lot more bounty go way up."

"I don't doubt that," Grede said. "Back at the Dark Portal, those succubi were all sneaky killers lurking in the shadows — nothing like the succubi those Dalaran mages gossip about. I could tell your companion's got real power too. Just from those two exchanges, I could tell. Someday I should have a proper fair duel with her…"

"Look — as long as you can guarantee this demon won't crawl into my tent in the middle of the night and cut my kidneys out like the rest of her kind, I can accept her helping us make money, Jace. But what about that elf traveling with us? Have you thought that through?"

"Of course I have," Jace said. "So before heading to Deadwind Pass, I plan to write to Vereesa first and find out what's going on with that ranger."
