Chapter 50: Joining Up

The date for officially joining the Slaughtered Lamb underground crew was set for the evening of the next day. When Jace arrived at the tavern this time, the bartender Jarel came out from behind the counter the moment he saw him.

"Gakin's waiting for you."

Jace watched Jarel head for the door — the meaning was clear: he was to head down into the basement alone.

He'd already drunk a Shadow Protection Potion, just in case. His throat still felt raw and sour. He'd also hidden the confiscated dagger behind his back, worried Gakin might try some other bullshit on him.

In a place like this, it was hard not to be paranoid. Gakin was waiting at the entrance to that cell-like labyrinth. When he saw Jace, he unlocked the door and said, "Tonight you'll meet many talented people, Jace. Most of them are just as passionate about the dark arts as you are, and just as full of curiosity about another world."

"Another world?"

"Draenor. On the other side of the Dark Portal. It must be a paradise for dark magic." Gakin asked, "Don't you long for it?"

Honestly, he didn't long for it much, but Jace still nodded along out of obligation.

Gakin led him through the labyrinthine basement, twisting and turning all over the place, before finally entering a broad hall through a hidden passage. A bonfire was burning in the center, and every person's face in the room was lit by the flames with an eerie glow.

Just as Jace was worrying whether everyone here was going to collectively succumb to carbon monoxide poisoning, he noticed there was actually a vent in the ceiling. But at this hour, when it was still fairly light outside, that vent was black too — definitely not open to the sky. It probably led to the tavern's back kitchen chimney.

Come to think of it, did this world even have carbon monoxide?

Watching his thoughts start to drift off in every direction, Gakin suddenly called out loudly, "Welcome our newest member — Jace Sesso."

"Jace Sesso."

Everyone intoned his name in unison. Combined with the many unfamiliar faces flickering in the bonfire's light, it sent a chill down Jace's spine. He had a reflexive aversion to this kind of pyramid-scheme weirdness.

At that moment, Gakin walked up to a woman. Jace looked carefully and recognized her as Surena.

Surena looked at Jace, then at Gakin, her expression somewhat complicated. Gakin said matter-of-factly, "This is Surena Caledon. You two have already met."

Then he walked up to a red-haired woman and said, "This is Ursula Deline, our member most skilled in alchemy. She's also from Lordaeron."

Ursula Deline looked to be in her early twenties. Her red hair was tangled like weeds, making her look a bit unkempt. She nodded slightly, and Jace nodded back.

But as Jace passed by her, Ursula's brow furrowed slightly, as if she'd caught some kind of scent. Jace tensed up — she hadn't smelled the Shadow Protection Potion he'd drunk, had she?

Hopefully this so-called alchemy expert was just put off by the fact that he hadn't had time to bathe and was covered in sweat, rather than actually detecting anything.

But in the end she didn't say anything. By then Gakin had already walked up to a black-haired man.

This man had a head of smooth, flowing hair that reached his shoulders, with something vaguely Eastern about his features.

He was in his early thirties, wearing a blue-and-black shirt and dark-gray, nearly black silk trousers. There was a hint of arrogance in the way he regarded Jace.

"Our most talented member. I believe his strength is no less than my own," Gakin said with a proud expression. "Zardeth of the Black Claw."

Zardeth stepped forward and offered his hand to Jace first. "Welcome."

This guy's attitude was a bit warmer than his appearance suggested. Judging by the way the others looked at him, his status seemed to be no lower than Gakin's.

It's just that this "Black Claw" moniker was a little edgy-teenager. None of the others had a title like that.

"Erlan Drudgemoor — you've met before too." Gakin walked up to the next person. Jace looked at this guy and realized just how tall and burly he was compared to the others.

"Jace, lad — the first time I saw you, I knew you had what it takes to join us." Erlan smiled, his face creasing. "Gakin, you take good care of him now."

"Of course." Gakin looked toward Surena, who was staring blankly from not far off, and said, "We treat all our members the same."

Jace looked at these three people and a whole love triangle was already playing out in his head. Gakin couldn't possibly know about the affair between Surena and Erlan, could he? So what did that make this? This was about to exceed his ability to comprehend.

With that, he led Jace to the bonfire and said, "Let's carry out the initiation ceremony now."

As he spoke, Jarel suddenly appeared, dragging a half-grown lamb bleating loudly behind him.

"End its life," Gakin said.

So it really was the Slaughtered Lamb — did they have to be this on-the-nose about it?

"This is to give you a rudimentary experience of the meaning of sacrifice, newcomer," Zardeth said from the side. "To help you try to understand one truth — that there is little joy or delight on the path of the dark arts."

Gakin looked at Zardeth and nodded in agreement. "Use the dagger I gave you."

For Jace, who'd already killed quite a few wolves, slaughtering a lamb shouldn't be a problem. So as he walked over, he didn't find it difficult at all.

But when he actually lowered his head and looked at the lamb, he hesitated.

A lamb was completely different from a wolf. After all, when facing a wolf, if you didn't kill it, the one who'd get hurt — or lose their life — would be you.

But this lamb was being held in place, bleating, its cries full of fear and despair. Add in the fact that the little lamb was actually kind of cute, and at this moment Jace found that he couldn't bring himself to do it.

"Is this necessary?" he asked, looking back.

He noticed Zardeth smile, while Erlan said, "This is a necessary step."

Jace didn't hesitate any longer. He drove the dagger into the lamb's throat, and when he pulled it out a spray of hot blood splashed onto the web of his hand.

The lamb's struggles were feeble and panicked. Jarel held it down even harder, making it seem even more stiff and helpless than before.

It was quickly dragged out of the basement, leaving behind only a patch of blood reflecting the firelight. Jace looked at the dagger in his hand, stained with lamb's blood, and didn't know what to say.

Was this some kind of test? Or some kind of obedience test?

"Congratulations — you've successfully completed the ceremony." Gakin walked to the bonfire, looked around the room, and announced loudly, "Jace Sesso felt compassion for this lamb. That's only human nature. Walking together on the path of the dark arts, what we need are partners we can trust, not a monster devoid of humanity. But at the same time we must understand that some sacrifices are necessary. We can suffer, we can struggle, we can even regret — but in the moment of decision, we must decide."

The people in the room responded in unison. Jace responded along with them, but he seriously doubted whether these people were partners worth trusting — after all, he knew full well that he was the least trustworthy one here.

Gakin said all this with such solemn sincerity that it was genuinely hard to connect him with the thug who'd flown into a rage over suspecting Surena of an affair and ambushed Jace with a knife by the alley.

Although Jace didn't know whether the death of this one lamb could really express all that much meaning, he figured a reclusive crew in their current circumstances probably couldn't find a more dignified initiation ceremony.

Dinner was that very lamb, made into grilled lamb chops, sprinkled with some unidentifiable spices — actually pretty tasty.

A bunch of warlocks chatting and laughing — they didn't look like some secret organization at all. More like friends eating and drinking together, just like Gakin had said.

Late at night, the ladies living in Stormwind left early. Erlan went back to his quarters in the basement. Gakin said he occasionally stayed the night here when it got too late, since it was a long walk back to Goldshire.

Just as Jace was about to leave, Zardeth suddenly called out to him.

"Newcomer — Gakin has business with you. He says he's here to fulfill his promise."

"Where's Gakin?"

"He's already gone to our practice room. Just follow me."
