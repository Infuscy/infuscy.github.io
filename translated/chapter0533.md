## Chapter 533: Cooperative Hunting

Seeing the thunder lizard about to spit lightning at him, Grede immediately turned and ran, seemingly looking for a rock to hide behind.

Jace seized the moment while the thunder lizard's attention was fixed on Grede and began chanting Fireball. His left hand traced casting gestures through the dry air, drawing fire elements toward the tip of his staff, while roughly calculating the distance between himself and the lizard — whether he could hurl the gathered fireball far enough.

But even as he was thinking it, before the fireball had grown to any real size, the thunder lizard seemed to sense the mage's incantation.

The rapid gathering of elements clearly drew its fury even more. It whipped its head around and hurled its gathered electricity straight at him!

The moment Jace locked eyes with the thunder lizard, he felt his blood suddenly boil, heat surging through his entire body.

His arm was already covered in crimson runes — the Blood Pact. The imp still remembered he could do magic?!

With the imp's magical protection, Jace had nothing to fear. Not to mention, he could feel Molofel's shadow already sliding into position beside the thunder lizard…

Molofel suddenly appeared above the thunder lizard's head. Her black blade drove diagonally into the throat from which it was channeling lightning. The thunder lizard convulsed with a violent cough. The electrical energy that would have blasted toward Jace was thrown into chaos by the cough, discharging in every direction as scattered flashes of lightning and bursts of white explosions across the ground.

Some of it struck Jace's position, but it didn't deal any real damage.

Through all of this, Jace never stopped chanting his Fireball. By now he'd finished the incantation, and gathered at the tip of his staff was a blazing sphere of pure flame, untainted by any shadow magic.

He would never contaminate the group's food with shadow magic, of course.

The brilliant fireball streaked toward the thunder lizard, trailing splatters of scorched black earth before slamming into the lizard's back and detonating. Several bony plates on its back were blown apart, and scales and flesh peeled back, blackened. The thunder lizard, which had been trying to bite the succubus, was staggered by the blast, coughing several times, nearly losing its footing.

An agile succubus wasn't about to be caught so easily by a giant beast like that — she'd already vanished without a trace.

Still, the thunder lizard was massively built. A few shattered plates on its back and a gash in its hide didn't seem to slow it down in the slightest.

Grede came charging out of who-knew-what patch of grass, his body swirling with the winds of nature magic. He howled and swung his stone axe, burying it in the thunder lizard's neck. The gash tore open, flaps of flesh dangling, and the thunder lizard vomited a mouthful of blood.

The thunder lizard had just turned its head to bite the dwarf when another arcane arrow came flying in — and pierced the same eye again!

The second arrow had gone in deep, seemingly piercing the brain. The thunder lizard was clearly dazed by the shot. It struggled to stay upright but kept listing, again and again, on the verge of collapse.

"It's done!" Wenresa stepped out of the grass with her bow, aiming at the thunder lizard's remaining eye — though by now she was slowly easing the string.

Grede backed off to the side. Musa moved a few meters away too. The group watched the thunder lizard swaying where it stood, clearly no longer able to attack.

Just as Wenresa had said, the thunder lizard finally couldn't hold on. Before long its legs gave out and it collapsed on the slope. The whole ground shuddered with the impact.

Seeing the giant beast fall, Musa let out a few hearty laughs, then said something in an excited rush.

Wenresa explained to the dwarf beside her, "Musa says this was an elderly thunder lizard. In a tauren tribe, the hunting parties powerful enough to slay a beast this formidable… are made up of the finest warriors. If we were tauren, we'd definitely earn the respect of our people when we got back."

"Hah, and that earns us the tauren's respect? Compared to that demon at Nethergarde Keep, this thing's got nothing on it, whether you're talking size or strength!" Grede pounded his chest. "I didn't even use my real power and it went down!"

Hearing the dwarf mention the pit lord, Wenresa glanced over at Jace, who was catching his breath and resting. Her smile faded slightly.

Musa walked over to her, excitedly asking Wenresa to translate the dwarf's words. Wenresa gave her a brief summary — probably mentioning the battle with the pit lord Azgorsh — and the tauren's expression shifted from excitement to shock in an instant.

Judging by the worshipful look on her face, she probably wanted to start learning Dwarvish on the spot.

Clearly, Wenresa hadn't mentioned how Jace had finished off that pit lord.

Because if she had, she'd have to describe that magic — that terrifying method of fusing soul and dark power. That wouldn't be easy to explain to a tauren.

But Jace didn't particularly care anymore.

Grede slapped the thick scales on the thunder lizard's carcass. "Now we've got enough food reserves to cross the whole savanna, right?! I bet the strong Musa would be happy to help us carry some extra roast meat!"

Wenresa relayed the dwarf's words to Musa, who was leaning against the beast's corpse. Musa nodded vigorously. Wenresa looked over, "Getting a fire going for the roast is on us two."

"No problem," Jace said.

He couldn't conjure food, but whether it was conjuring water or starting a fire, what he'd learned at the Wizard Sanctum still made him a perfectly serviceable logistics man for the group.

At that moment, Molofel materialized behind Jace and placed into his hand a pale purple shard containing a powerful soul within. "The soul of this elemental beast, master!"

"That's mine, that's mine, right?!" Sazeno stared at the soul shard.

"Of course." Jace handed it to Sazeno. "If nothing else, you cast a Blood Pact on me in the middle of that emergency. Take this soul shard and put the energy inside to good use."

The succubus said unhappily, "Even if that beast had actually hurt the master, my Soul Link would have been enough to keep him from being badly injured…"

"Damn right, your skills weren't needed anyway, since the master never would've let that idiot touch him!" the imp muttered. "Anyway, all that patience finally paid off! This big brute's soul is fierce, master — I can feel it in there, straining to break out! Ah, the master's getting stronger, and I've finally earned a better reward. But if we could get our hands on the soul of that pit lord Azgorsh and swallow it down, that'd be…"

"You're insane," Molofel said with disgust.

Grede and Musa were already eagerly cutting into the thunder lizard's flesh with Wenresa's knife. Grede sighed, "What a shame. A beast this huge, and we can't eat most of it. The rest'll go to waste."

Wenresa said, "It won't go to waste. Look up."

Jace raised his head and saw a flock of red-headed, black-tailed scavenger birds circling high in the yellowed sky. They'd probably been following this old thunder lizard for a long time.
