## Chapter 571: Scion of Cenarius

The atmosphere was tense. Mosa said something to the dryad in the tauren tongue, gesturing toward Jace as she explained.

The two sides conversed in the tauren language for a while, and the dryad ahead seemed to let her guard down a little.

The dryad leaped lightly down the stone slope and came before the group. She wasn't tall at all — in fact, as the descendant of a demigod, she seemed almost excessively small. Even walking on her lower deer body, she didn't quite reach the height of Jace standing upright, let alone the tauren.

Up close like this, Jace seemed to have some faint impression of this white dryad, but couldn't remember exactly where he'd seen her before.

Could it be in Maraudon, in the game?

Before he could think it through, the dryad spoke. "I am Cavindra, but I am not the daughter of the forest god Cenarius, travelers. I am the child of Keeper Remulos. Still, I am surprised you recognized the source of my blood. I have never seen a human before. I have only seen your kind in the Dream, so have humans learned of the Dream's existence?"

As she spoke, her lips and voice were out of sync, as if she were using magic to produce the sound.

Cavindra studied Jace for a while, and Jace got a good look at the dryad up close.

She had a pair of long, elf-like ears. Thick, blue-green hair cascaded over her shoulders and lower limbs. Thin, curling vines wound around her arms, waist, and chest like the arcane tattoos of some nature magic.

The dryad's features, half-hidden by her hair, were somewhat similar to an elf's, but lacked expressiveness. Calling them stiff like wood might be over the top, but Jace felt she was genuinely lacking many mortal emotions. You could tell just from looking at her.

Vereesa said, "Many humans and Quel'dorei mages have already learned of the Dream's existence, my lady."

"That does not surprise me, child of the Sunwell," Cavindra said.

Hearing the dryad address her that way, Vereesa visibly relaxed. She seemed quite pleased with the title.

The other party looked at Jace again. "That student of nature magic over there wants to convince me you are a good human. Still, I would like you to explain the source of the demonic aura on you, to avoid any possible misunderstanding."

"I learned some magic from another world — from the orcs on the other side of the Dark Portal," Jace explained. "And my robes are made of felcloth. Not only is felcloth durable, it also aids in channeling this kind of magic."

In any case, he certainly couldn't tell her he had a Soul Link with a succubus, could he?

But Cavindra didn't seem convinced by Jace's explanation. She fell silent for a moment.

Then Jace suddenly thought of Azuresong.

He unslung the magic sword at his hip and raised it in front of him, drawing it out slightly. The arcane light that spilled forth illuminated the faces of everyone around him, accompanied by a faint, pleasant hum.

"I think this is enough to prove…" He glanced at Vereesa beside him. Vereesa pressed her lips together and gave a slight nod.

Jace went on, "Enough to prove we mean no harm. I think you should recognize what this is."

"I… have never seen it."

Though Cavindra didn't recognize the sword, she had clearly sensed the purity of its power, and hesitated. Just then, another voice came from above.

"This is a treasure of the Blue Dragonflight, sister. Its significance is profound. Allowing a human to wield it is proof of the trust the guardians of magic have placed in him."

A tall, half-deer, half-human figure appeared among the shade of the trees, walking forward step by step with a long, branch-like staff in hand.

When he appeared, all the dryads looked up to him and bowed their heads respectfully in turn.

Cavindra looked up at him. "So that is what it is. Forgive me, Celebras. I never considered that possibility."

The keeper of the grove named Celebras nodded slowly. "Then, since the brood of Malygos has already given their approval, we need not worry further."

He came before the group and looked at the demon head Vereesa was carrying. "Hmm… this is Vyletongue. I know this demon. He has finally met his end. So the demons are indeed attempting to corrupt all of Maraudon. The centaur aren't losing their minds and seeing things."

"Travelers, Cavindra and I were sent by our father to find the soul of a noble ancient. He has become lost in this place. We hope he can return home, to be reunited with my father and his offspring as soon as possible. But no matter how I call out here, I cannot hear his voice. If you have returned from the depths of Maraudon, I hope you can describe to me what happened inside, and how I might enter."

Jace remembered him. He was the one from the game who led a host of dryads near the Poison Falls, preventing players from entering Earth Song Falls — the corrupted keeper of the grove.

He had never been able to find Zaetar's soul, and instead was corrupted by Vyletongue's magic, losing himself within the cave.

It seemed that at this point in time, he still hadn't found a way into Maraudon, let alone been corrupted by demons.

But Jace didn't think this fellow would have any way of dealing with Theradras if he charged in recklessly.

Since Vyletongue, who had corrupted him in the game, was already dead, Celebras would be able to push far deeper than he had in the game — and would likely be killed outright by an enraged Theradras.

So he thought for a moment and said, "If the soul you're speaking of is Zaetar, son of Cenarius, I sensed his sorrow in the deepest part of Maraudon."

When Jace said this, Vereesa and Grede both looked over at the same time. Especially Grede — his face clearly said, "How the hell do you know this too?"

Celebras took a step forward. Though Jace could sense the keeper's urgency, he still maintained a measured pace. "How did you sense the sorrow in his soul, mortal?"

Jace looked up at the half-deer, half-man with a pair of enormous grey-brown antlers. His face bore the look of a stern, displeased night elf, his eyes gleaming with moonlight-like silver, his hair a cascade of green vines covering his entire body.

He explained, "Perhaps it is precisely because I learned the dark magic of the orcs that I can sense the emotions of souls. I can feel that Zaetar's soul does not wish to leave Maraudon. He wants to stay here. And until Theradras dies, his soul cannot be freed, and all of Desolace can never return to what it once was."

Of course, all of this was what Jace knew from the game. Standing before Zaetar's grave, he could certainly sense where the soul was — but what that actually meant, he definitely couldn't explain clearly.

But since he couldn't explain it clearly himself, no one else could point out where he'd gone wrong either.
