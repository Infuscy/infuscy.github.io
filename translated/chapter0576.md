## Chapter 576: Fishing

"You give it a try, Jace Sesso!"

Grede's voice came from a distance. Jace, who was setting up a roasting rack over here, looked over. The dwarf was wiping water from his face, bare-chested except for a pair of small shorts, walking over with a sharpened wooden spear in hand.

His hair was soaked through, and his whole body looked as if he'd been pulled out of the water. Jace looked at him and said, "Weren't you going fishing? Why do you look like you just took a bath?"

"The water's a bit deep," Grede said, shaking water from his head and beard. "So I'm letting you give it a try."

"I can do it," Musa said.

"Don't," Grede said. "He's the worst cook. Let him go, otherwise he'll just sit to the side waiting to eat every time."

"I still have to build the fire," Jace shot back.

"How much effort does building a fire take?" Grede said. "Speak an incantation, wiggle your fingers..."

Wenresa glanced at Jace and her hand moved toward the buttons of her coat. "I can go."

Jace stopped her. "I'll go. You stay here and keep watch. Don't let the Kaldorei sentinels spot us again."

Grede handed the wooden spear to Jace and sat down, watching him. "Are you sure it was the roast meat that gave us away before? And not the stench of demons on you?"

Jace looked at the dwarf as he stripped off his outer robe and shirt, tossing them to the side, and rolled his shoulders. He didn't respond.

"He's got a guilty conscience," Grede said with a laugh. "Maybe those elves followed the smell of demons on him right to us."

"Anyway, I've ordered Morophil to stay invisible this whole time," Jace said, glancing at a crooked tree not far off. "She's over there keeping watch for us right now. If we still get found by the Kaldorei like this, then it can only mean every corner of this enormous forest is locked down tight by them, and we never had a chance of fooling them to begin with."

"The Kaldorei are few in number," Wenresa said. "I don't believe they can manage that. Don't forget, their real homeland lies thousands of miles to the north, in Ashenvale and Mount Hyjal. Do they have that many hands to spare watching this place?"

"Still, if anything really happens, I'll call you, Jace."

"It's not far anyway." Jace nodded, took the wooden spear, and headed toward the riverbank.

When he was a kid in Tirisfal, his father would sometimes take him fishing on the Aliwas River, so he had a fair amount of experience.

He still remembered how, back then, he would sometimes see bears on the opposite bank watching them. His father was never afraid of those beasts. Sometimes he would simply stand his ground against a bear until it turned and left.

Unlike the broad, flat Aliwas and the Nafeltari River, the rivers of Feralas were narrow and turbulent, the channels uneven, flowing down from the heights with little cliffs everywhere forming small waterfalls.

Jace stood in the water and waited, listening to the rush of the current and, faintly, the sound of Grede talking from over there.

Before he'd even spotted a fish's shadow, he took a deep breath and felt the forest — the natural scent of the river — and finally felt himself break free a little from the constant tension.

Soon he noticed the shadow of a fish. Remembering his father's teaching, a fish's shadow wasn't quite where the fish actually was — you had to aim a bit lower...

Jace gripped the wooden spear, about to thrust it down, when he suddenly felt as though something was watching him.

He raised his head and looked across the river. Right below a small waterfall stood an enormous brown bear, looking straight at him.

The goddamn thing was absurdly huge — and it was standing upright beneath the low waterfall, with more than half its body still sticking out above the water.

Jace was fairly certain that bear hadn't been there when he'd arrived.

Of course he wasn't afraid of an ordinary beast. If it wanted to pick a fight, he had several spells that could send it packing — something as simple as a Fear, for instance.

A giant bear?

No sooner had a rather uneasy thought surfaced in Jace's mind and he was about to shout for the others than a explosive splash of water sounded behind him, and he felt as if he'd been slammed by a cart straight into the river!

Dizzy and disoriented, he felt something grab his leg and drag him underwater. He didn't even get a Fel Breath incantation out. His arms and back slammed against the rocks on the riverbed several times.

He'd already forgotten about the pain. He forced his head down and opened his eyes toward the direction he was being dragged, and saw a burly black shadow in the silt-churned water pulling him downward...

Jace had nothing on him — no sword, no staff, not even clothes. The wooden spear in his hand had been knocked away in the collision.

He opened his mouth to shout a Fear incantation, but the moment he struggled to lift his head and open his mouth, river water rushed into his nose and mouth. Jace choked on the water and roared out the spell. What came out was far from complete, let alone precise, but he clearly felt the shadow power answer his call... The enormous shadow dragging him still let out a muffled roar, and the hand gripping him loosened at once.

The water here was clearly much deeper than before. Jace tried to surface and swim toward the bank. Before he'd swum far at all, he felt something behind him churning up waves. He was just about to turn his head and speak another spell when he felt himself hurled out of the water by a strange force and slammed heavily onto the bank. Before he could even register the pain all over his body, rapid footsteps suddenly closed in!

"Fektar!"

Jace spun around and pointed his finger at the black shadow shrouded in the dense treetops ahead, casting Fear once more. The shadow roared and clenched its fists, its form stalling for a moment. Seizing the opportunity, Jace immediately scrambled to his feet and backed away — only to see the shadow pull an axe from its waist and hurl it without hesitation. Jace reacted extremely fast, twisting his body to the side to dodge, but the still high-speed spinning axe grazed his shoulder and opened a cut.

The Soul Link was clearly sharing the blade's damage — but where was Morophil?

He'd been ambushed. This thing was terrifyingly huge, already about as tall as Musa or even taller, yet this good at hiding?

The axe flew out and buried itself in a tree trunk, shaking the whole tree. Jace used Bloodgrasp to snatch the axe into his hand, and seeing the giant charging straight at him, he shouted, "Wait! Rexx—"

The shadow showed no hesitation at all. It swung a fist and drove it straight at him on the momentum. Jace backed up several steps but had no hope of matching the speed bearing down on him. He took the punch full-on and was sent flying to the ground.

That punch knocked the soul right out of Jace — but he already knew who the other party was. Considering his strength, the force of this hit was actually a lot lighter than expected.

Of course, that was definitely also because of the Soul Link.

Rexxar grabbed Jace and lifted him into the air. Raising his fist with the other hand, he roared in Common, "Speak, warlock! Whose summons did you answer?!"
