Chapter 577: The Mantis Stalking the Cicada

Before Jace could answer, Morophil suddenly leaped out of a shadow, wrapping her tail around the giant's neck and raising her right hand. The giant's eyes darted to the side and he reached back, catching the succubus's black blade mid-thrust!

Purple blood was already streaming from the corner of the succubus's mouth. She unleashed her full power, fighting Rexxar fiercely, trying to free Jace from his grip.

But the demonic energy radiating from her had weakened considerably compared to before... probably because she'd used the Soul Link to absorb the hits Jace had taken moments ago.

Or perhaps her strength was just recovering too slowly.

By now, Jace had come back to his senses after that punch and got a clear look at the monster before him.

No mistake — this was Rexxar. A body far bulkier than a normal orc's, skin the color of brown mud. That iconic black wolf-head mask he always wore in the game was currently soaking wet and tied around his neck — most likely to make diving easier — which let Jace see his face directly.

It's just that his appearance was nothing like what Jace had imagined. If he'd had to guess, both his body and his skull looked far more like an ogre's than an orc's.

Of course, this fellow was a lot more agile than any ogre. The way he fought really was more like an orc's style.

Jace grabbed his arm and said in Orcish, "Rexxar! I've heard of you. There's no need for us to fight!"

The succubus dodged Rexxar's grasp and turned invisible again. Rexxar glanced back at Jace, but his expression only grew more furious.

"You speak our language. Are you that traitor's student? Or a follower of Cho'gall's?!"

Oh no — how did this make the misunderstanding worse?!

"Not..."

Before Jace could get the word out, Rexxar tightened his grip, and he felt like his body was about to be snapped in half!

Under the Soul Link's influence, Morophil, who was just about to strike from behind again, also changed expression. Her feet gave way and she lost her balance, getting swatted aside by a palm strike from Rexxar.

But the moment Rexxar turned around, Jace piled Curse of Weakness and Curse of Agony onto him all at once. Rexxar growled, released his hand, and retreated to the side. Seizing the window while the sudden pain had the half-orc reeling, Jace threw up a Dark Shield...

Rexxar steeled his mind and, with a berserker's fury, pulled another axe from his back and hacked it hard into the shield. A burst of shadow exploded, blasting him back several steps. The surrounding leaves, caught in the shockwave, withered and rained down.

The shield had already been shattered by that blow. Just as Jace saw Rexxar raise his axe to hurl it over here, a Quel'Thalas arrow struck him square in the shoulder.

Rexxar swore under his breath, yanked the arrow out, and glanced to the side. From a high slope nearby, Grede came sliding down, shouting, "Stop, monster!" The half-orc looked that way and blew a series of whistles. Jace shouted, "Watch out — he has backup!"

The words had barely left his mouth when the giant bear plunged down from the high cliff and pounced on the dwarf. The two tumbled straight into the river. The bear had just raised its body to press him under when it was lifted by some strange force and thrown to the side. Grede burst up out of the water, roaring madly, "Looking for death, you mad she-bear?!"

The dwarf and the giant bear grappled. Not far off, the howls of wolves rang out. Gray wolf shadows came pouring out of the forest from every direction, blocking Wenresa, who was rushing over.

Musa, in the form of a brown lion, charged into the pack to break the encirclement for Wenresa. The wolves could only dodge the attacks of the two great cats and didn't dare press forward. Rexxar glanced that way too and felt the situation reversing fast.

"Your friends care about you a great deal. We'll meet again, warlock," Rexxar spat out the words, then turned and ran toward the forest. He hadn't gone more than a few steps before he stopped dead, staring fixedly into the undergrowth — and backed up again.

He blew several whistles. The pack of wolves blocking Wenresa and Musa immediately scattered in every direction into the deep forest. The giant bear also stopped fighting, took a few steps back, and looked over. Rexxar turned his head and roared in Orcish, "Run, Misha!"

But the bear simply stood up on her hind legs, roared back at Rexxar, and showed no intention of obeying his order to leave.

Rexxar sighed. Three night elves appeared from the undergrowth, each with a wooden bow aimed at the half-orc, who stood like a brown earthen wall.

Jace heard movement behind him. He turned and came face to face with a night elf drawing a bead on him with an arrow nocked.

She wore silver outer armor and a dark cloak, almost perfectly camouflaged in the shadows of the forest. If not for her silver-glowing eyes, Jace wouldn't even have been able to make out where her face was — he could only see a vague silhouette.

Not far off, on the riverbank, several more night elves were closing in step by step on the dwarf. Grede backed up a few paces and said, "I'm not putting down my hammer. Don't force me..."

Wenresa also had three sentinels aiming bows at her. She glared resentfully at the half-orc over here and cursed at him in Thalassian.

They'd been surrounded by the sentinels. Night elf figures were everywhere he looked — all told, probably ten or twenty of them.

These things had come in such numbers and so fast, they must have been lying in ambush around here for a while.

Either these night elves had been tailing Rexxar, or they'd been tailing the group of them. The only question was when they'd been spotted — before running into those night elves earlier, or after?

Of course, thinking about it now didn't mean much anymore.

Jace used Sense Demons and noticed that Morophil had already hidden herself away in the undergrowth some time ago, watching the scene.

He warned her softly in the demonic tongue. "Get out of here. As far as you can."

"Stop, human." The sentinel behind him warned in Common. "Don't try to use any more evil spells."

Jace nodded. "We can prove this was all a mistake from the start, ma'am."

The sentinel just looked at him without speaking. Then a sentinel captain riding a great black cat emerged from the jungle. She looked over the group surrounded by her sentinels and said something in elvish to the sentinels nearby.

Musa held up Celebras's antler staff and said something in elvish to the captain. The captain dismounted the great cat, came before Musa, and took the staff, examining it from every angle. She said a few words to the sentinels beside her, then shook her head.

Wenresa exploded into furious curses. The captain frowned. Three sentinels rushed forward to restrain her. She punched one of them down, and it took a struggle before they finally pinned her to the ground and tied her up good and tight.

"Why didn't it work?" Grede asked, bewildered.

"This idiot doesn't even recognize the antler!" Wenresa shouted. "Get your druids over here."

"The druids have more important duties right now than authenticating this object, Quel'dorei," the captain said in Common. When she said "Quel'dorei," her tone carried unmistakable coldness.

She pocketed the antler, said something in elvish to the sentinels around her, and the sentinels began tying up each of their prisoners' hands one by one — not even sparing the she-bear, who got a hood and a muzzle.

"I won't deny it may be the token of the son of Keeper Remulos, but even if it's genuine, I can't make the decision to let you leave on my own. From here on, you'll follow me back to the stronghold. I'll show this to the general and ask for her orders. Don't resist again, or you won't leave this forest alive."
