Chapter 592: Dire Maul East

"The magical trail — is it the demon we've been looking for all along?" Grede asked.

Vereesa didn't answer right away. She stared at the tightly shut door, which looked like it hadn't been opened in decades, maybe centuries. It was covered in withered, thriving layers of vegetation — plants that had grown and died back who knows how many times over. Clearly, she wasn't a hundred percent certain either.

In the game, Dire Maul East was home to a pack of satyr demons, and a blood elf named Rathildis who'd come to Eldre'Thalas to explore arcane ruins with her imp.

Rathildis had clearly arrived only after the Scourge invasion, because at this point the Sunwell hadn't been destroyed yet, and there were no high elves calling themselves blood elves.

Based on the string of signs so far, there were no hidden satyrs in Dire Maul either — though that still needed to be confirmed further.

In any case, the state of Dire Maul East must be very different from what it would be a decade or more from now. Jace couldn't say for sure whether it was currently occupied by ogres or still inhabited by a group of Highborne — Kaldorei.

Jace cast Sense Demons toward the interior through the wall. He couldn't see a thing — not a single demon's shadow. On the contrary, the demonic aura coming off Morophil beside him was especially glaring. Her power really had grown a lot stronger than before, particularly when she wasn't deliberately hiding it.

But Jace knew his Sense Demons had a limited effective range. He asked the succubus, "Can you sense any demonic presence, Morophil? In that direction."

"I sense nothing at all, master," Morophil said.

"Are you sure?" Vereesa cut in. "You're saying my perception is wrong? The demon Dathmora summoned isn't in there?"

"I didn't say that, Quel'dorei," the succubus said.

Jace said, "I still need to go ask that oguard guard. Even if there really is the demon we're looking for in the East wing, we can't just barge straight in."

Vereesa nodded. Jace glanced at the elf and the demon, who'd nearly come to blows not long ago, confirmed there was no follow-up, then looked toward the two ogre guards not far off.

By now, one of the ogres was already staring their way, curious about what this group was talking about.

Jace walked over and greeted him in Common, but the ogre gave no response at all.

"Throm'ka," Jace said in a fairly formal Orcish greeting. The ogre lowered his head and said in Common, "You speak the Horde's tongue, pink-skin. Very funny. Very novel."

"Since I was going to compete in your arena, I learned a bit in advance to prepare. Now it's come in handy," Jace said. "Since you understand Common, let's talk in Common."

"King Gordok tell me no answer questions," the ogre said. "Any language, no."

Jace scratched his head. Morophil came over.

"You will answer," she said.

"Ah, right, I will answer," the ogre nodded.

"No you won't!" the other ogre guard punched him. "You break king's command."

Morophil looked at the other soldier and said softly, "Standing around being a nuisance here — don't you have something even dumber to do?"

"You right, lady. I need to take a shit." The ogre guard who'd been flying into a rage immediately deflated, tilted his head, and walked off in another direction.

Jace looked over at the dozing warlock in the distance, and the two-headed ogre mage watching the arena walls from above, confirming they weren't paying attention to what was happening here.

"I'm curious why you don't like letting me help in situations like this, master," Morophil said.

"Because it wastes mana," Jace said. "And in the end it gets spent on me anyway."

"That's true," the succubus smiled, raising an eyebrow. "Ask away, master. He's more honest with you than Vereesa Windrunner right now."

Jace shot her a look, then turned to the ogre and pointed at the enormous wooden door not far off. "What's behind that?"

"East half of city. Very big. Great architects and gardeners," the ogre guard said with glassy, vacant eyes. "Pointy-ears guard inside, but they almost can't hold on. When tree inside sleep, we make them dead. Then more Gorduni can move into city. Very nice."

"How many pointy-ears inside?" Jace asked.

"Don't know," the ogre said. "Maybe ten. Maybe fewer. Soldiers who went in before got eaten by plants. Pointy-ears and plants allied. Plants listen to them. We not stupid. Can't keep dying. Need wait for more Gorduni to arrive, then attack."

"Are there demons in the East?" Jace asked.

"Demons?" the ogre said. "No demons seen. East no demons."

"Satyrs?" Jace said.

"What that?"

"A kind of creature with long horns, covered in fur, about this tall," Jace gestured.

"Never heard of such a monster," the ogre said.

Jace nodded. "How do I get into the East?"

"Side door over there," the ogre pointed to an upward ramp behind him. "Soldiers guard there..."

Jace was about to ask about Dathmora when Morophil quietly reminded him, "The two-headed mage is looking this way, master."

He nodded. "Put him back to normal."

The succubus acknowledged with a sound, flicked her fingers, and with a soft crackle of shadow energy the ogre blinked and looked at Jace. "Stop staring, pink-skin. King Gordok's advisor very smart. He say answering questions bad, then bad. If I answer questions I get punished, so I no answer your questions. Next time I hit you."

Jace couldn't be bothered with him. He went back to Grede's group and relayed what the ogre guard had said. Vereesa said, "I just tried using the incantation the Reliquary taught me again. Damn it — I swear I really did pick up Dathmora's magical trace from behind that eastern door. A hundred percent, crystal clear — at least a lot clearer than the trace I detected at the entrance to Maraudon."

"But what the ogre said matches what the succubus said, Vereesa," Grede said. "No demons inside."

"Isn't it possible you're both right?" Jace said. "There's a trace in there, but it has nothing to do with demons?"

"We'll know once we're inside," Grede said.

Vereesa said nothing. Jace looked at her. "Anyway, let's go in and take a look first. The ogre said there are still elves left inside — we'll see if they're willing to talk to us. The ogre mentioned there are ancients in there too. We need to be careful."
