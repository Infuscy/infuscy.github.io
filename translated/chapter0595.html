Chapter 595: A Difficult Conversation

Vereesa chatted with the Kaldorei woman for a while. During the conversation, Jace noticed she kept glancing over at the rest of their group.

Grede was running his hands along the walls nearby, probably looking for other hidden passages in — or checking for magical traps.

Mosa sat to one side watching the dwarf fuss about, then looked back toward the hole leading out to the forest, unclear what she was thinking.

This frail Kaldorei had never left Eldre'Thalas, Jace thought to herself.

Perhaps in her centuries — or millennia — of life, everything she'd ever seen and heard had all been within these layers of towering walls.

The thought alone was terrifying. To the people inside, Eldre'Thalas was like a prison nested within a prison.

You could tell as much from the Kaldorei's demeanor. Her eyes darted, her hands and feet trembled from time to time. Even though Jace couldn't understand a word she was saying, he could sense the stammering, the uneven shifts in tone and emotion.

And then there was the sealed door.

Even with Jace's perception of arcane energy, he could tell the difference between this door's seal and the small one outside was enormous. This door, which imprisoned the demons, must have been completed through a meticulately complex incantation and casting process.

At that moment Vereesa said, "Unfortunately, she doesn't know all that much either."

"Doesn't she live in here?" Grede turned back to ask.

"She came here from the West. She was originally the keeper of an arcane tower. After the ogres invaded, the magical array in the Eastern district destabilized, and she received orders to come here and assist a Highborne noble in maintaining the magical seal. So how many demons are imprisoned inside, how many have been released now... she doesn't know the details clearly either."

"This dead Kaldorei probably knew more. He was her teacher," Vereesa said, looking at the male elf's corpse on the ground.

"Did you ask about Dathmora?" Jace asked.

"I did," Vereesa said. "She had no idea about that name. I tried translating the family name 'Darkeye' into ancient elvish every way I could think of. If she'd even heard a little of the legends surrounding Dathmora Darkeye, she should have understood what I was getting at."

"Dathmora Darkeye," Grede turned back. "A Highborne warlock wielding the Eldre'Thalas dagger, and nobody in Eldre'Thalas has heard of her?"

"The Reliquary only pieced those clues together as a guess, Grede," Jace said.

"But Shandris Feathermoon also thought she was one of the Shen'dralar," Grede said.

Hearing the word "Shen'dralar" come out of the dwarf's mouth, the Kaldorei woman looked up in their direction.

"Because the place with the most surviving Highborne right now is Eldre'Thalas," Jace said.

The dwarf pressed again: "You mean there are Highborne surviving elsewhere to this day?"

"I don't know," Jace said.

The dwarf pointed at the frail woman and said, "This Highborne has been surviving in this city on the power of those demons, and she's this weak. I flat-out don't believe Highborne outside could have survived this long. Don't forget what that sentinel general said — leaving Eldre'Thalas means giving up immortality."

"She's not some so-called Highborne, and she's not an ancient mage who's lived since ten thousand years ago," Vereesa shook her head. "She's just a young Kaldorei commoner, Grede. Not every resident of Eldre'Thalas is a noble. Only the members of the Shen'dralar are."

"Fine, fine. I don't have time for a history lesson or an elven culture class, Vereesa," Grede smoothed his beard. "I just have a bad feeling — that we probably came all this way for nothing, relying on a pile of stuff the satyrs left behind."

"But the fact that she doesn't recognize Dathmora doesn't prove anything either," Jace said. "First, not everyone in this city necessarily knows each other. If she's only a commoner mage, she'd have even fewer chances to interact with nobles like the Shen'dralar — not knowing them is perfectly normal. Second, more than one person has told me that Dathmora sounds like an alias. Does that sound like an elven name to you, Grede?"

Grede pressed his lips together and swished his tongue around inside. He rested a hand on his waist and looked at the Kaldorei woman sitting in the corner. She'd clearly grown a little dazed from an excess of fear, tension, anguish, grief, and other emotions.

That was perfectly normal. Locked in a cold hall like this, with plants that could kill you at any moment on one side, ogres on the other, and demon prisoners who could break through the seal at any time on yet another — while the only Kaldorei who could save her either hid behind a gate on the other side of the garden, shut away behind closed doors, or were blocked by the ogres on the far side of the city, miles away.

She'd been abandoned by the entire world as she knew it. The last person keeping her company had died here for unknown reasons. And the ones who finally showed up at her door, giving her a sliver of hope, were a bizarre bunch of foreign invaders.

"What about her real name, then?" Grede asked.

Jace looked at Vereesa. "What we need to find is a warlock who once lived in Eldre'Thalas, then escaped and never came back. She summoned many demons, wrote many works on demons. Oh, and she's a woman."

Vereesa nodded and tried explaining to the Kaldorei. The Kaldorei shook her head, then said something.

The moment she shook her head, Grede and Jace both involuntarily looked away — one leaning against the wall, the other sighing.

"We're here for that demon, aren't we, Jace," Grede urged. "Hurry up and ask about the doomguard."

Vereesa hesitated. "Don't rush it. She's only a low-level caster who was sent here. She doesn't know the details of the demons inside clearly either, Grede — I just told you that. And she's in an unstable state. She doesn't fully trust us yet."

"The most important question, and the last one," Jace said, pointing at the magical seal. "Can she open that door."

After Vereesa asked, the Kaldorei said a word firmly, then explained a few sentences. Grede said, "Doesn't take a genius to see she won't do it."

"Opening this door would release the demons inside and throw the whole city into chaos," Vereesa said. "She's been guarding this door for a long time. She doesn't want to just give up."

"Isn't this city chaotic enough already? And she's clearly a pawn who was thrown here to be sacrificed at any moment, all to protect those nobles hiding behind the gate!" Grede said heatedly. "So what if we let them out? Either we kill a bunch of ogres, or we kill a bunch of Shen'dralar, or we get killed by those two bastards. Either way it sounds like a good thing to me."

"Or run off into Kalimdor," Jace said.

"Then it depends on how many we can kill," Grede said.

In the game's lore, there was no mention of what happened to this group of demons imprisoned in Eldre'Thalas. A decade or more later, the satyrs occupied the place, and the demons inside completely vanished — and this Kaldorei mage's fate was unknown.

So in the end, this seal must have failed one way or another — either the demons inside found a way to break it open, or the ogres eventually fought their way in.

Grede actually had a point. If they could kill some of them here, there was no downside. Vereesa talked with the elf woman for a long time, and at some point the other party's eyes seemed to light up a little. Not long after, she agreed.

Both Grede and Jace had been sitting to the side listening, feeling like this trip might end right here — they might even have to try breaking the seal themselves by force. Seeing the elf suddenly give in, they were both a little taken aback.

"Why'd she agree again?" Grede asked. "What did you tell her?"

"I told her we killed an Annihilan," Vereesa shrugged.

"That's not exactly a lie, is it?" Jace said.

"Not even a little," Grede said.

Flags

- Dathmora Darkeye: 达斯莫拉·暗眼 — the warlock they're tracking; the Kaldorei doesn't recognize the name or the family name "Darkeye" (暗眼). - Shen'dralar: Canonical Highborne faction (辛德拉) — only Shen'dralar members are true nobles/Highborne; the Kaldorei woman is a commoner. - Shandris Feathermoon: Canonical night elf general (珊蒂斯·羽月) — previously identified Dathmora as Shen'dralar. - Reliquary: 遗物学会 — the organization that pieced together the Dathmora clues. - Annihilan: Canonical pit lord race (安尼赫兰) — Vereesa's persuasion: she claims they killed one. - Doomguard: 末日守卫 — the imprisoned demon they're after. - "run off into Kalimdor": Jace's dry addition to Grede's "kill them all" enthusiasm. - POV: Third person throughout.
