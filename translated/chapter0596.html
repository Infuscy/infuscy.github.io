Chapter 596: The Demon Corridor

The mage came to the stone gate and began casting. Even though she'd said she'd been guarding this gate for a long time, she showed no hesitation as she worked the dispelling ritual.

Jace guessed she'd already reached her breaking point. If there was a reason that could let her set down the burden and free herself — even a flimsy one — she'd grab it without a second thought.

Whether this group had really killed a pit lord didn't matter much at this point.

While the mage performed the unsealing ritual, the group checked what they'd brought with them. They left all the materials they'd carried here, taking only potions and other combat-related items.

After all, if they didn't make it out, this luggage would be meaningless. And bringing it inside might only slow them down.

Once the casting was complete, the magic on the stone gate rapidly disintegrated — but the gate didn't immediately open. The Kaldorei mage looked at them and pointed at the gate. Everyone understood at once.

The magical dispelling was done. Now it needed a physical push.

Grede sighed and, together with Vereesa and Jace beside him, walked up and pushed against the gate. The gate was absurdly heavy. As he pushed, Grede muttered, "Don't tell me I have to go into my Avatar just to push a door..."

Mosa, who'd just set down several food packs she'd been carrying, walked over and reached over everyone's heads with both hands to shove the gate. This time the stone gate rumbled and shifted a little.

"That should be about enough. On three, two, one," Jace said.

The group nodded and chanted together: "Three, two, one — push!"

Everyone threw their weight into it. The stone gate surged open with a boom, dust showering down. Jace closed his eyes, gritted his teeth, and pushed forward a few steps, finally forcing open a gap wide enough for one person to pass through.

Grede immediately drew his warhammer and aimed it at the gap. Jace also took a step back and gripped the sword hilt at his waist. They waited a moment at the doorway, then peered inside. There was nothing but a long, deep tunnel, lit by arcane light of unknown origin — bright for a stretch, then dark, stretching all the way to a bend they couldn't see.

Jace caught a thick, pungent demon stench almost immediately. He tried sweeping the area with Sense Demons — at least there were no demons in the corridor right in front of them.

After the group filed in one by one, he looked back at the elf who'd stayed behind in the gap and asked, "She's not coming?"

Vereesa turned to ask. The elf hesitated and shook her head. Grede said, "Forget her."

Mosa squeezed through the gap with considerable effort. Morophil materialized at Jace's side.

"Can you sense how many demons are inside?" Jace asked.

Morophil stared deep into the corridor for a good while before answering. "I'm not certain, but there really aren't many, master."

"There are spellcasters, master — demon warlocks!" Sazeno said. "And there's a powerful arcane energy source in here, connected to the city's other energy sources, all the way to the West!"

"It's one of the arcane towers the mage was guarding," Jace said. "This city relies on these towers to supply power to its residents, just like..."

"Just like the Sunwell," Vereesa said.

Jace nodded.

He took out a soul shard he'd retrieved from Maraudon and began chanting a summoning incantation.

"What's this for?" Grede asked.

"The master's summoning a felhound," the imp said. "Don't forget who saved your skin last time by that tower, fatso!"

Vereesa asked, "What tower?"

"Last time Jace and I went to Karazhan — well, not inside Karazhan, just scouting around the outside," Grede said in a low voice. "Made a pile off the dead!"

"Why didn't you call me?" Vereesa asked.

"You want to make money too?" Grede asked.

Vereesa pressed her lips together. "Maybe. But more importantly, it sounds like it'd be a lot of fun. I know the Reliquary has always been very interested in Karazhan too."

"I'd know those folks would be interested even without you telling me," Grede said. "But we didn't deliberately leave you out, little elf. That was not long after we came back through the Dark Portal. You'd just returned to Quel'Thalas and were under orders to garrison the Amani border, if I remember right."

"Ah, that's when it was. All right," Vereesa said, relaxing her shoulders.

By now Jace had finished chanting. A massive felhound crawled out of the shadowy corner of the corridor.

Before the felhound had fully materialized from the void, Jace had already finished naming it in the Annihilan tongue. So the hound obediently came to Jace's side the moment it landed, showing no aggression. The three tentacles sprouting from its head sniffed and probed everyone present.

"Dazarmu," Jace let out a breath. "Our new friend."

When the felhound sniffed its way to Mosa, her eyes went wide and she said something in the tauren tongue that sounded like pure, meaningless cursing, shoving the felhound hard away from her.

Jace said in demonic, "Keep away from the tall one."

The felhound let out a low growl in response and immediately ran over to Grede's side.

"I remember the last felhound had a pretty similar name. So is it the same one?" Grede said, dodging the demon hound as it approached.

"No, just the same name. Naming things is hard, Grede — especially in a demonic language," Jace explained.

Even though he knew the felhound was Jace's minion — and considerably more obedient than the succubus — Vereesa still couldn't fully relax watching this demon.

No matter how powerful a mage you were, it was hard to keep your composure around a felhound, let alone these children nurtured on arcane energy.

To deal with any demon spellcasters they might encounter, Jace carefully cast a Protection from Evil and a Mage Armor. The brilliant glow dimmed as the incantation completed, and then he cast an Arcane Intellect on both himself and Mosa.

"I need an Arcane Intellect too, mage Jace," Vereesa said.

Jace gave her one as well. After casting all these incantations one by one, his mana was badly depleted. He had to take out the leather pouch of Kingsblood broth the tauren had prepared and drink it down.

Even if its effect couldn't match a mana potion brewed by Ursula, at least it was plentiful and cheap. Gulping down the whole pouch of purple-tinged broth perked him right back up.

The corridor was deep and long. Jace wasn't sure if it was because they were being especially cautious, but it felt a lot longer than the passage in the game.

The corridor curved and climbed higher as they went. Braziers along the sides burned with shadowfire, and after a while the dark fumes they gave off made everyone in the passage feel rather uncomfortable.

Rounding a bend, Jace caught a scraping, scuffling sound from a side passage.

Vereesa keenly nocked an arrow and aimed at the source. The scraping grew closer, and suddenly a felhound lunged out of the shadows, pouncing straight at them. It was terrifyingly fast — as if it had been tracking their group all the way here.

But Vereesa's arrow was faster. One shot pierced straight through its skull, burying deep. The felhound dropped to the ground motionless on the spot. That arrow had definitely pierced its demon heart.

The first one had barely hit the ground when a second felhound leaped high over the corpse. Vereesa was just reaching for another arrow when Morophil's black dagger flew across and struck it precisely in the chest. The felhound crashed into the wall, rolled a few times, and lay still.

"They've noticed us," Vereesa lowered her bow a little. "Might be my fault. These felhounds are extremely sensitive to arcane energy."

"Might be because of my casting," Jace said. "At least we only ran into these two felhounds on the stretch we've covered. Maybe it's only because they were close enough to sense us."

Vereesa nodded. Morophil went to the felhound corpses to retrieve her dagger, but seemed to notice something and looked up in the direction the two demons had come from.

"What is it?" Jace asked.

The words had barely left his mouth when he caught a familiar yet strange, faint scent. It was the scent of a succubus — but absolutely not one Morophil was giving off.

And right now Morophil was carefully avoiding giving off too much of her presence to keep from being detected by other demons hiding inside. A scent this obvious couldn't be coming from her either. Grede walked past the two felhound corpses a little way into the depths and said, "Here!"

Jace went over and saw him pointing at something dark on the ground ahead, like a fallen chunk of rubble. "What's that?"

The group took a look. Only when they got close did they realize it was the dried-out, hardened, curled-up corpse of a succubus.

The felhound Dazarmu paid the corpse not the slightest attention, not even showing the least desire to go sniff it. It seemed thoroughly impatient at being led into this corridor, only wanting to press on in the direction they'd originally been heading — because the magical energy was stronger over there, more attractive to a felhound.

"This sayaad has been completely drained. Not a trace of mana left," Morophil said.

"Did the felhounds do it?" Jace asked.

The succubus examined the corpse up and down again. "I don't know..."

Jace crouched down and tried to look the demon corpse over carefully, then attempted to turn it over. A weak sayaad was already small to begin with; drained dry, it was even tinier. He felt like he was flipping over a wild dog that had died of disease or starvation by the roadside.

Once he turned it over, a wave of thick, rotten stench hit him in the face. Grede turned away like he was about to vomit. Vereesa frowned too. Mosa simply stood far back and watched.

Jace carefully examined this side and found that the demon's corpse had no wounds at all.

"If a felhound drains mana, it should suck with its tentacles — there ought to be puncture wounds."

"Ah, yes, master."

The moment Jace said it, the succubus noticed it too.

"So this was drained by another demon, using magic," she said.

Translator Notes

Flags

- Dazarmu: 达扎尔姆 — the name Jace gives the summoned felhound (Annihilan tongue); Grede notes it's the same name as a previous one. - Annihilan tongue: 安尼赫兰语 — the demonic language Jace uses to name the felhound at summoning. - Protection from Evil / Mage Armor / Arcane Intellect: Canonical mage spells (防护邪恶 / 法师护甲 / 奥术智慧) — Jace buffs the group before advancing. - Kingsblood broth: 皇血草熬汤 — a mana-restoring brew the tauren prepared; Jace compares it to Ursula's potions (厄苏拉 = Ursula, the alchemist). - Karazhan: Canonical location (卡拉赞) — Grede and Jace previously scouted its outskirts for loot. - Dark Portal: Canonical gateway (黑暗之门) — referenced as the time marker for when Vereesa was stationed on the Amani border. - Sayaad: 萨亚德 — the drained succubus corpse; Morophil notes it was magically drained, not killed by felhound tentacles (no puncture wounds). - Demon heart: 魔心 — Vereesa's arrow pierces the felhound's heart; a felhound's mana drain would leave puncture wounds, which the sayaad corpse lacks. - Reliquary: 遗物学会 — Vereesa notes their interest in Karazhan. - POV: Third person throughout.
