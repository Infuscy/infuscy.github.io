## Chapter 597: The Fel-Blood Chamber

The depths of the Eldre'Thalas palaces were bitterly cold. Especially once they reached this last stretch, the final traces of life force were banished entirely by the shadow braziers that were everywhere, and all manner of dark emotions stirred within — loneliness, anxiety. Even Jace, a warlock already accustomed to dealing with negative feelings, felt his mood sinking under the magic's influence, let alone the others ahead and behind him.

That demon corpse from earlier especially had the whole group thinking hard. If it wasn't a felhound, then what kind of demon had drained that succubus, and with what magic? Some spell like Morophil's?

Pushing forward through the corridor saturated with dark magic, Jace even felt like his mind was just spinning uselessly, arriving at no possible answer.

Just when he felt like he was about to lose the ability to think altogether, the group arrived at a doorway, and before them lay a deep, cavernous hall. Broader than the chamber with the pool where the Kaldorei mage had been, massive stone pillars supported the floor and the ceiling that rose so high it vanished into shadow. Stairs along the sides led up to the high terraces.

This had probably once been a hall for important councils. The residents of Eldre'Thalas would gather here, watching performances from different levels, feasting together, conducting rituals and so on. But now it was empty — not a soul, not a demon. Jace's Detect Demons couldn't sense the faintest trace of demonic activity.

Where were the demons? If it was just two felhounds, why go to so much trouble... Just then, Jace noticed a demon lying at the base of a pillar ahead.

His face was withered, his body emaciated. From this distance it was hard to tell whether he was Eredar or Mo'arg. He was tall — at least two meters — drained as though nothing but a skeleton had been left to slump here.

"A Mo'arg warrior," Morophil said. "Hard to say whether he's a Burning Legion guard. He's not wearing Legion armor, and the fel tattoos on his skin are too faded to make out."

"Could just be an ordinary prisoner," Grede said.

"There," Mosa said, pointing at another pillar. At the base of the one she indicated, a dark shape was curled up.

The group walked over to take a look. This was another Mo'arg roughly like the first, but shorter and thinner, wearing a tattered robe.

Though the robe had been torn by some force, the material itself didn't seem nearly as decayed as the corpse appeared — as if it had only been ruined recently.

"There are still traces of enchantment on it," Vereesa said. "I can't say exactly what enchantment it is, but... I can feel it, Jace. The last time this robe's wearer channeled mana might have been less than a year ago."

"So what the hell happened here?" Grede asked.

"No idea," Jace said.

"Could they have been fighting over an energy source?" Vereesa said. "Battling each other for the arcane power released by that magic tower, until only the last victor remained to claim all the energy for themselves."

"Sounds like a candidate for the ogre arena," Grede said. "An underground colosseum beneath Eldre'Thalas... a bloody, brutal contest to crown the strongest."

Some battle royale... Jace thought to himself.

But the more he thought about it, the more he felt they might actually have a point.

"So where's the final victor?" he said. "Can you sense any trace of Das'mora here, Vereesa?"

"Honestly, Jace," Vereesa said. "The mana here is chaos. By my senses, Das'mora's magical traces are everywhere, but faint — very similar to the aura on that demon statue we found."

"You think maybe that doomguard killed these demons?" Grede asked.

"Eredun drain demons to this state?" Sazeno said, clutching the edge of the soul pouch. "If you told me the master did it, I'd be a lot more inclined to believe that!"

"A demon's body is full of fel energy," Jace said. "If I sucked that into myself, wouldn't that be awful? Only a demon could do something like that."

As he spoke, he noticed the staff in the demon's hand had already become a dead stick. Every gem, enchantment, anything magical on it had completely dried up.

Whoever killed this demon was definitely a ruthless bastard who left nothing behind — sucked the bone dry.

The group found a staircase leading up to the second-floor terrace. Up there, besides a demon remnant so far gone that its features were unrecognizable, there was even an elf's corpse.

It was a male elf's corpse, dressed quite splendidly, even wearing a jeweled crown. Most likely a Highborne.

But like the other demons, his body had been completely drained. Though his face had withered to little more than a skull, it was still clear that his final experience had not been a pleasant one — his expression twisted into something out of a nightmare.

Grede reached over and picked up his crown. The moment he applied pressure, the dim gem set in it shattered, leaving only a finely crafted silver circlet.

Even the energy in that mana gem had been drained dry.

Looked like there wasn't anything else of interest up here besides the corpse. "There's a door over there," Grede said, pointing at a half-open stone door on the other side, below.

"Let's check it out," Vereesa said, and started down the stairs. Jace followed right away.

When they reached the doorway, a wave of thick, putrid stench hit Jace so hard it nearly knocked him off his feet. He was already the weirdo in the group best equipped to tolerate all kinds of foul smells, and yet the stench in there almost made him throw up the two pieces of dried mushroom he'd eaten in the outer hall before coming in.

What the hell was in there?

It was hard to say pure Shadow could produce a stench like this. The smell of Shadow was less a simple foulness and more a nauseating fishiness that disgusted you from spirit to flesh. Fel energy stank too, but it was an explosive foulness that drove you back. Neither, though, was anything like this smell that made you want to vomit on the spot.

Vereesa was already leaning against the stone door, dry-heaving. Her face was waxen, her legs too weak to hold her. Grede was beside her cursing up a storm, gasping for air. Mosa hadn't vomited, but she clearly had no desire to get any closer either.

Morophil was the first to push through the stench and step into the doorway, but before she fully entered, she hesitated right in front of Jace — then vanished in an instant into a blur of light.

Someone inside?

Jace signaled the felhound to hide behind a distant pillar first, glanced inside, then covered his nose with his arm and stepped forward in.

A few steps down the stairs, he found himself in a rather cramped dark chamber, the floor soaked with viscous liquid.

All over the floor and walls were demons dead in gruesome ways — mostly Mo'arg and succubi, even an Eredar head piled to one side. The viscous liquid was, beyond doubt, demon blood that would never coagulate.

Seeing those bloated demon organs, Jace finally couldn't take it and vomited to the side. After a few heavy breaths cleared his head a little, he realized that while most of the corpses here were withered like the ones elsewhere, a good number of them were still quite plump — as if they'd just been killed.

The others gradually overcame their physical revulsion and came in too. The moment Grede saw the scene, he doubled over and dry-heaved. Vereesa seemed a bit weak-legged and dizzy too — she nearly tripped over some demon's thigh on the floor as she walked over.

Worried there might still be a living demon in here, Jace used Detect Demons to peer past the demon remains into the depths. Soon, he spotted a gaunt figure sitting in the black blood at the lowest point of the chamber.

This figure wasn't a demonic shadow at all — it was a void, a silhouette surrounded by demonic aura that was everywhere, as if some power had shielded the demonic shadow from the body...

Jace tried to relax his mind and look with his naked eye. The figure had its head bowed, features unrecognizable. Was it another elf's corpse? Just as Jace stepped closer, the figure suddenly raised its head.

It was a Kaldorei woman, her skin a pale silver. When she opened her eyes, they burned with a silver-white arcane light.

She wore a pale purple robe stained with too much demon blood, holding a wooden-shafted staff. Seeming to notice someone approaching, she tried to lift her leg and stand, but the blood underfoot was too slippery — her whole body slipped and she half-knelt back down.

"Invading Eldre'Thalas is a grave crime, outsiders."

After she finished speaking, Jace was dazed for a moment before realizing this elf's Common had not a trace of an accent.
