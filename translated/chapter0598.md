## Chapter 598: The Prisoner

Grede looked around at the demon corpses and rubbed his nose hard. "Your Eldre'Thalas has been invaded to hell and back. How many cages do you even have to hold all us invaders?"

Vereesa glanced at Grede and gave a slight shake of her head, signaling him not to run his mouth. The dwarf seemed a little pissed off about the whole "grave crime" business, but he caught Vereesa's warning and kept quiet.

The Kaldorei looked at Grede expressionlessly, then at Vereesa. There seemed to be some confusion in her eyes.

Jace couldn't sense much powerful mana radiating off her, but anyone who could take down this many demons was no simple opponent — maybe she was just too weak. After all, she hadn't even been able to stand up a moment ago.

"Who are you?" Jace asked.

"I am Reyhidian," the elf woman answered. "I was sent by Tortheldrin's orders to cleanse the demon infestation here, but I underestimated the demons' strength and was badly wounded. The situation here has long since spiraled out of control..."

"If you're not the demons' accomplices, then take me out of here. Prove your good faith, outsiders, and perhaps I can put in a word for you with the Shen'dralar and lessen your crime of invasion."

Jace studied this elf who called herself Reyhidian. She was noticeably taller than that Kaldorei mage outside. Though her robes were stained with demon blood and she looked somewhat ragged, her skin was far smoother, her hair sleeker, her bearing more composed, her emotions steadier.

"The mages sealed off this whole district to stop the demons from entering the city," she continued. "Since you were able to get here, does that mean the seal no longer exists?"

"The wardens outside released it for us," Jace said.

Reyhidian laughed, as if she didn't believe that for a second. "Take me out and everything will become clear," she said.

Grede looked at her. "Hold on a moment — do you know Das'mora?"

Reyhidian raised her eyes and fixed the dwarf with a stare for a moment. "What do you want with her?"

"You know her?" Jace asked.

"She lost contact with Eldre'Thalas a long time ago," Reyhidian said. "She tried to get Eldre'Thalas to summon a second demon to sustain the entire city's survival. The Shen'dralar refused her, so she planned to summon a demon on her own. Once her intentions were exposed, she fled."

"Relying on a single demon for energy has already brought endless disaster," Vereesa said. "Two demons, and the whole city would sink into an abyss beyond saving."

"You who lived beside the Sunwell will never understand Eldre'Thalas's despair," Reyhidian said with a faint smile.

Vereesa was taken aback for a moment. Jace asked, "How long have you been in there?"

"Two years," Reyhidian said. "Ever since the magical defense array was destroyed..."

"Who destroyed the magical defense array?" Jace asked.

Grede looked at Jace with a face full of question. The elves outside had said the defense array was destroyed by the ogres' assault — so why ask again?

But of course he didn't say anything, because it only took him a second to catch on to what Jace was really doing.

Reyhidian didn't answer. She just stared at Jace and said, "This city is no longer whole."

Of course the city was no longer whole. Every elf inside it could feel that. But a mage sent into this district to hunt the rogue demons after the array was destroyed — and she wasn't going to mention the ogres?

Maybe this elf simply didn't know about the ogres.

Jace had a feeling this one was one of the prisoners — and the most powerful one at that. Which brought him back to that battle royale Grede had mentioned...

But why did Detect Demons show nothing at all when he looked at her? Could she be a warlock imprisoned here? Was she Das'mora?

After Reyhidian finished speaking, she set off toward the half-open stone door, each step looking like it cost her considerably, though she still fought to maintain her elegance.

Jace exchanged glances with Grede, Vereesa, and Mosa. He knew they all felt the same thing — this elf was not as simple as she appeared.

Back in the broad hall they'd been in before, Jace said, "Lady Reyhidian, once we get you out, we'd like you to help us find Das'mora."

Reyhidian leaned against a pillar to catch her breath. The flickering light of the shadow braziers cast her silhouette in shifting shades, making her as statuesque as the sculptures on Eldre'Thalas's outer walls.

"Eldre'Thalas does not permit anyone to leave, but once I report back to Tortheldrin, I can offer you leads that only the Shen'dralar would know."

"You're a member of the Shen'dralar?" Vereesa asked.

"Yes. A 'kinswoman,'" Reyhidian said.

"You seem quite familiar with the other continent, Lady," Jace said. "You know about Quel'Thalas and the Sunwell. And most importantly, you speak Common."

"I have existed in this world since before the Well of Eternity was destroyed, human," Reyhidian said coldly.

"But there was no Quel'Thalas, no Sunwell, and no Common before the Well of Eternity was destroyed," Jace said, his tone just as cold. "If Eldre'Thalas doesn't permit anyone to leave, how do you know all these things?"

Reyhidian stared at Jace expressionlessly. After a moment, her lips trembled into something like a smile.

"I will take you to Eldre'Thalas's Great Library. You will marvel at the richness of its knowledge. You will be astonished by the incredible ways the astrologers of Eldre'Thalas acquire knowledge and understand the world..."

With that, she turned and walked step by step toward the entrance of the passage. She had left Eldre'Thalas — that much was beyond doubt. A Highborne warlock who had left Eldre'Thalas, locked away at the very bottom of a demon prison...

Jace looked at Vereesa and said quietly, "Check for magical traces, Wen."

The ranger nodded, raised her right hand, and murmured an incantation. Reyhidian heard it and turned back to look at her, her expression seeming to shift.

"What magic is this?" she asked.

The Reliquary's incantation for confirming magical traces — the ranger had recited it so many times these past few months that Jace felt like he was about to learn it himself. After she finished, Vereesa slowly opened her eyes and fixed her gaze on the Highborne before her.

"You are Das'mora," she said.

"What?" Reyhidian let out a dry laugh, as if this were some sort of absurd joke.

"Oh, I see — you're looking for magical traces, aren't you? Fine. I killed Das'mora's minions, those abominations. My body, my very being is soaked in their demon blood. How could I not be tainted by her magical traces?"

"You need to stay here, Lady," Jace said. "Until I confirm your identity with the wardens guarding this prison."

"I do not need to confirm my identity with you lowly outsiders."

Reyhidian turned and rapidly spoke an incantation. Just from the power in the spell alone, Jace could tell the amount of magical energy she could command was staggering. Everyone present, including Mosa, was quickly bound by arcs of arcane energy, barely able to move. Even forcing themselves to act, they could only move at maybe a third of their normal speed, if that.

"Thank you for persuading those foolish elves outside to open the seal. As your reward, you may now fully immerse yourselves in the torment I have endured all these years..."

Reyhidian straightened the collar of her rune-inscribed robe and looked at the arrow Vereesa already had nocked. "Fast. But not fast enough."

She raised an eyebrow and smiled, heading toward the passage — only to see a dark shadow shift out from behind a pillar ahead. It was Dazhalmu!

The felhound let out a snarl and lunged. Reyhidian hastily loosed a volley of Arcane Missiles, but the felhound swallowed them in one bite...

Reyhidian shrieked, grabbed the felhound, and hurled it aside. Jace and the others used the opening to struggle against their bonds. The arcane bindings gradually faded, their movements growing smoother by the second.

The felhound shook free of Reyhidian and pounced again from the side. The weakened Kaldorei hadn't finished her incantation before the tentacles wrapped around her neck and body, and then the felhound's massive frame pinned her to the ground entirely.

"Beast! Abomination!"

Reyhidian's voice completely lost control as she howled in rage — and Jace realized with a shock that she was screaming in demonic tongue.

The felhound bit hard into Reyhidian's face, tearing away a strip of skin. Jace watched, stunned, as beneath that strip of skin was exposed a bestial eye burning with fel energy.

"Enough! Enough!" Reyhidian's voice had become completely distorted, a jumble of sounds as if several demons and a woman were howling at once. She hurled the felhound away in fury and stood back up, her bloody wounds revealing rough demon skin and bristling fur beneath.

Her body rapidly swelled to three or four meters tall. Demon claws tore through the outer disguise entirely. Her muscular pair of wings spread into the air. Thick, curved horns thrust from her head. A pair of black hooves stepped forward and cracked the floor with a boom.

An Eredun covered in complex fel tattoos. A demon of the Burning Legion.

Those satyrs hadn't been trying to summon Das'mora's servants. They'd been trying to summon Das'mora herself.
