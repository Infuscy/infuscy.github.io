## Chapter 600: The Legion's Loyal Servant

"Sazeno, can you sense the location of that mana source? That magic tower!"

"Sazeno!"

"Master... master, why are you calling me out now!"

"Because this is exactly when I need you... That's an order!"

"Order!" Sazeno stammered. "Say it, master."

"Find that magic tower!" Jace said. "And destroy it. Cut off this demon's energy source... damn it, how are you supposed to destroy that tower."

"I'll do it." Mosa appeared at Jace's side at some point. "The demon leads the way. I'll destroy the magic tower."

"Don't hesitate. I'm fast — faster than anyone, Jace Sesso."

Jace knew he didn't have the luxury of hesitation.

He drew Azuresong and said, "Take this sword. Watch out for Arcane Anomalies that might show up — this sword is more than enough to hurt them."

"Anomalies?" Mosa asked.

"Arcane monsters," Jace said. "Like walking elementals."

Mosa understood immediately. She took the sword from Jace's hand, strapped it to her waist, and crouched down, transforming into the great cat. She nodded at Sazeno. Jace tied the imp to the binding rope on Mosa's back. Sazeno pointed at a distant passage. "That way, that way, quick!" Mosa shot out like an arrow. Without the healer, now they could only rely on themselves.

Jace took the Rimecrystal out of its case and placed it in a pouch over his chest as an emergency measure. Holding his staff in both hands, he walked out from behind the pillar. Up ahead, Grede and Morophil had been locked in combat with the demon for a while. Vereesa kept looking for an opening to shoot, but the demon's arcane shield was shattered again and again, only to be raised once more.

The felhound's head had already been twisted off by the demon, writhing in pain on the ground. After fighting side by side for even this short a while, Jace already felt sick looking at the scene.

He was destined never to be like Jagin, treating demons as nothing but tools.

He had no idea what Mosa and Sazeno would run into. The demon before them was powerful beyond any clear limit. Grede, Vereesa, and Morophil were still fighting desperately. A worry — and a bitter taste — rose up in Jace's heart.

He rode that bitterness, that faint hint of despair. This was exactly what he wanted.

He channeled the force of those intense, tangled emotions into a thread of Shadow power and once again chanted Curse of Weakness. The demon, who was in the middle of grappling with Grede, suddenly went soft in both arms and was abruptly overpowered by Grede. Her black hooves buckled and she nearly fell.

Grede swung his war hammer with a roar and brought it crashing down. The burst of lightning even sent the rubble around them flying a distance — and the hammer smashed straight into the demon's shield. Jace even felt the demonic aura and arcane energy surrounding the entire hall thrown into chaos by the blow.

The demon wasn't cowed by the blow, and Grede's upper hand didn't last long. The demon spat a blast of blue flame that drove him back a few steps, then pounced and seized his shoulder again.

While the dwarf was being suppressed, Morophil leaped onto the demon's shoulder from behind and, using the opening while she grappled with Grede, drove her knife into the demon's skull. The demon howled in pain and released the dwarf — then suddenly grabbed the succubus, who was about to leap away, yanked her back, and slammed her hard into the ground.

Even Grede in his Avatar form could only barely suppress this fel mage empowered by the magic tower. How could Morophil possibly match her strength?!

"Morophil! Damn it!" Vereesa shouted, firing another powerful Arcane Strike that detonated against the arcane shield on the demon's body, blasting her back to the side and releasing the succubus.

Jace watched the succubus get thrown, one arm twisted out of shape, and his heart lurched. A Curse of Agony incantation followed that sudden pang in his chest and landed on the Eredun. The demon, who had been staring at Vereesa, broke into a violent cough. Her whole body pitched forward a few steps. She turned back and flung a Shadow Bolt straight at Jace.

She chanted her Shadow Bolts so fast there was no time to react. Jace didn't even have the chance to speak a Dark Shield to protect himself. He had to rely on the Mage Armor and the robe alone to tank the hit...

The Mage Armor was on the verge of collapse. Jace was slammed to the ground by the powerful Shadow Bolt. The dark force searing into him from every inch of skin tormented him on both a physical and spiritual level...

He had no control over his body, but he could at least fight back on the spiritual level. Jace forced himself to stand up again. At the very least, this round of fighting had taken the pressure off Grede, who'd been bearing the brunt.

After that far too intense battle, the dwarf's Avatar was no longer so stable. His stone-gray skin had already begun to fade. This demon's mana was simply too vast — and not just demonic power. By now Jace could clearly feel her being endlessly sustained by some arcane source.

After that chaotic bout, Grede had been knocked aside, Morophil had been thrown into the distance and was hiding behind a pillar using mana to recover her damaged body, and Jace had been squarely hit by a Shadow Bolt and was still struggling to get up. The demon suddenly Blinked behind Vereesa and swept her claws across. Vereesa sprang lightly into the air to dodge the blow, and the moment she landed she jumped again, farther this time, pulling three magical arrows in midair.

She released. The three arrows struck the demon's shield in succession and turned to ash — yet that triple shot had visibly weakened the shield's protection.

Her lips had already turned white, though she still forced herself to fight at her best. Jace's mana was also severely drained. He had no mana potions left on him.

Even so, he immediately fired off another Shadow Bolt, continuing to pound the demon's shield.

Grede had caught his breath and was about to rest for a bit, but seeing the shield being stripped away by Arcane Arrows and Shadow Bolts in succession, weakened to the very brink of shattering, he scrambled to grab his war hammer and charged in again!

The dwarf swung. The demon seemed to sense this blow was about to break the shield for this round. She thrust her arm out to meet the war hammer head-on. The demon's arm was instantly shattered and split open, green demon blood flying everywhere. Grede was also shaken and rolled to the side, watching his Earthen form rapidly fade...

"Don't think that without my Avatar I'm just a useless dwarf!" Grede spat, pulled himself back up, and raised his war hammer to charge.

It was then that Jace suddenly thought: if the demon was being sustained by arcane energy, then draining her vitality would mean... He stepped forward, staff raised in both hands, and immediately began chanting Life Drain.

The demon, who was about to clash with the dwarf again, was just raising her shield when she felt her body go soft — the mana supporting her was draining away at terrifying speed!

The mana shield she tried to raise also failed to cast from the sudden loss of mana. In that moment of hesitation, Grede had already charged in front of her. One swing of the hammer smashed into her face and knocked out several demon fangs. But the demon immediately stretched out both arms and seized Grede's neck, trying to tear his head clean off. Grede gritted his teeth and matched her strength, but it was clear he was about to give out...

And then the demon's arms had already fully recovered, even gleaming with arcane light! She'd been using arcane energy to repair her body all along...

"Grede!" Vereesa, seeing Grede caught, drew her blade and charged in to hack at the demon's freshly repaired arm — only to be casually caught by the demon's tail, lifted into the air, and flung away!

After sucking in enough arcane energy, Jace tossed three centaur Soul Shards onto the ground and chanted an incantation...

The Shadow-corroded miasma filling the hall swiftly gathered from around the pillars into the air at his summons. The demon flicked one eye toward the dark meteor coalescing there and froze for a fraction of a second.

Hand of Gul'dan was already second nature to Jace. He swept his staff and guided the dark meteor to slam straight into the demon's chest and belly. The demon panicked for a moment. Grede seized the opening, called back his war hammer, and smashed the demon's arms apart, breaking free to the side.

The demon could no longer bother with catching the dwarf. She immediately raised the arcane shield manifesting on her skin once more. The dark meteor smashed into Reyhidian's chest and shattered her shield directly.

After the purple demonic fire from the Shadow explosion burned through, the dark figure slowly rose again.

Reyhidian steadied herself. The cracks and wounds blown open in her body were rapidly healed by the nourishing arcane energy. She looked at the ragged group before her and smiled. "You are strong. But I am invincible! As long as—"

Without warning, the hall shook. Walls shed stone. Rubble collapsed. The demon stood in the center of the hall, looking around in every direction. Jace could clearly feel the arcane energy that had been overflowing completely snap, turning into free-floating particles that dissipated and drained away at an incalculable rate...

Had they succeeded? Had Mosa and Sazeno destroyed the magic tower?

"Bastard!" Reyhidian screamed at the top of her lungs. "My mana... my magic!"

An arrow devoid of any mana energy pierced straight through her lips, then through her neck. Reyhidian furiously tore the two arrows away and used her demon wing to block the steady stream of arrows from Vereesa. Under cover of the wing, she once again chanted a Shadow Bolt volley.

Vereesa saw it and pulled her bow back, leaping off the pillar to dodge. Her movements were already growing disjointed.

Grede had also fought himself to exhaustion. He tried to stand and escape, only to trip and fall to the ground. Jace spoke a Dark Shield incantation and stood in front of him, taking all the Shadow Bolts head-on. The Mage Armor shattered. The collar of his outer robe was torn by Shadow power. But Jace still didn't fall.

Reyhidian's Shadow Bolts were already far less powerful than they had been at the start.

The demon extended her right hand, trembling, toward Jace. She chanted a familiar incantation — Life Drain, too!

In a nightmarish shimmer of green and purple tangled together, Jace felt his body rapidly collapsing. The demon laughed wildly as she continued to chant, walking toward him.

"What a delicious force. No wonder that succubus..."

Before she could finish, the Rimecrystal on Jace's chest suddenly radiated a massive golden light, accompanied by an ethereal boom. The demon was shaken back several steps by the force, and the Life Drain came to an abrupt halt.

"What is this?"

While the demon was still reeling, Grede leaped in from the side and buried his axe in the demon's head. The demon's massive body swayed several steps and steadied itself again — only for one of her demon horns to have been chopped clean off.

Grede seemed to have trouble seeing clearly. He shook his head, searching for the demon's silhouette to launch another attack, only to be casually grabbed by the demon and lifted into the air.

"I'll tear all of you to pieces... Aaah!"

She suddenly shrieked and turned her head. Morophil was channeling Drain Mana! "A huntress-wraith is nothing before—"

Reyhidian's voice grew weaker by the second. She turned and pointed at the succubus, muttering an incantation. Gritting his teeth, Jace forced himself up with the last of his strength, raised his staff high, and once again chanted Life Drain, draining with all his might from the demon who could barely stay standing...

The Eredun's incantation was halfway through when it turned into a helpless, wretched howl. The draining magic pulled her in different directions. The demon had no strength to resist the forces from both sides, and could only claw painfully at her own chest and waist. Her powerful body visibly withered.

Without the magic tower's support, and after a continuous string of fierce battles and blows, she could no longer resist. She dropped to her knees with a thud. Her arm, not yet fully repaired, shattered and split open once more. Her fur fell away in patches. She moaned weakly.

She turned her head and said in demonic tongue, "Do it. Send my soul back to the Twisting Nether. Lord Archimonde will reward my efforts."

"Your soul belongs to me," Jace said.

"What?"

The demon's ugly face suddenly lost all color. Morophil understood her master's intent and began channeling Soul Drain. Reyhidian roared, "The fall of Eldre'Thalas means nothing! Lord Archimonde will descend in earnest, and all of you will burn in agony in his magic!"

A brilliant Soul Shard took form in the succubus's hands. The demon's eyes instantly went dark and lifeless, and she collapsed to the ground, turning into an empty gray shell.

Grede held his stomach and shuffled over to Jace's side, taking off his armor and tossing it to the side — it was already covered in claw marks. He glanced at the armor, then at the demon. "So... that counts as a win, right?"

"Should be a win," Jace said.

As he spoke, he reached out with hands that wouldn't stop shaking from the exhaustion and took the Soul Shard from Morophil's hands, placing it in his pack. Inside, Vilethane's Soul Shard still gave off a faint, ghostly glow.

"This time the Reliquary's not getting away with less than two hundred gold," Grede said.

"Two hundred gold," Jace muttered under his breath.

Vereesa walked over slowly and crouched beside them. "Did this demon want to use Eldre'Thalas's mana to summon... Archimonde?"

"I don't know," Jace said, sitting to one side. "Maybe thousands of years ago it could have. But now, the city's mana might not be enough."

"What kind of monster is Archimonde?" Grede asked, astonished. "Even the mana of this entire city isn't enough to summon him?"

Jace swallowed. He had no idea how to explain Archimonde's power to Grede. After all, the World Tree was still intact. Dalaran hadn't been destroyed by a single spell yet. The combined armies of the Alliance and the Horde hadn't been casually crushed by Archimonde on Mount Hyjal...

"The tauren!" Sazeno suddenly came bolting from a distance, trailing a streak of fel fire, and shouted, "The tauren's dying! Get over here, quick!" Jace felt his brain ring. Grede, who'd been weak as hell a moment ago, suddenly sprang to his feet. Vereesa immediately ran in the direction Sazeno had come from.

Mosa... Mosa...
