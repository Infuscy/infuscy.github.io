Chapter 621: Vilethorn's Shadow Sigils

In the dim basement, Molphis lay on the table with her lips slightly parted, staring fixedly at her master. Half her chest was coated in fel blood, radiating an intoxicatingly thick demonic fragrance. She no longer had the strength to deliberately mask her aura.

If Jace hadn't shut the door tight, anyone who wasn't used to succubi lingering near the entrance might quickly lose their mind steeped in that scent.

Jace braced himself against the table's edge, watching the demonic power sigil held up by the imp, his throat bobbing slightly. He felt his own consciousness getting a bit fuzzy — he had to slap his cheeks and force himself to focus. With one hand he steadied the demon's collarbone, and with the other he gripped the sharp blade, laying it against Molphis's right breast.

This time he hesitated far less than when he'd carved the first sigil. After all, the succubus clearly didn't care much about pain — and if she didn't care, he had no reason to hold back either.

The blade tip gradually sank into her skin, slicing open a clean, shallow cut. Purple blood welled up around the edge once more. Molphis bit her lip and smiled, looking at Jace. "I wish every single moment from now on could be spent like this… my master."

"Much longer and I might lose my mind, Molphis. So let's hurry this up."

Jace muttered the words, but his focus was entirely on the work beneath his hands. He glanced at the sigil's strokes once more and kept going.

The succubus grabbed hold of the arm Jace was steadying her with, letting out a soft moan each time he pressed the blade down. By the time the sigil was more than halfway done, Jace finally couldn't take it anymore. He lowered his head and said, "And — I'll say it one more time — don't make noises, Molphis. It breaks my concentration."

Molphis wiped a streak of blood from beneath her chest and slipped her finger into her mouth. "I'll try…"

Just like Sazeno had said, carving demonic sigils was an enormously draining process. Jace wasn't reciting incantations, wasn't deliberately channeling his mana — but merely performing the act, carefully tracing the sigils from Vilethorn's body onto the demon, required extraordinary willpower.

In the process, his mana was being consumed at an alarming rate. By the time he finished the second sigil, he realized his mana was nearly bottomed out.

Jace sat down beside him and rubbed his temples, closing his eyes to recover. Sazeno examined the sigil closely. "Master, the second one is also excellent… really excellent. Your incredibly diligent study of demonic runes must have paid off handsomely here!"

"Of course, the master's language talent deserves no small credit either! With your runic augmentations, the sayaad already looks like a proper minion of the Burning Legion now, hah!"

Listening to the imp, Jace also looked over Molphis's body as a whole.

Her appearance reminded him of the dark-skinned blood elf female demon hunters from the game, rather than a succubus summoned by a warlock.

It hadn't been obvious before, but Molphis had never had wings on her back to begin with — already a major difference from a typical succubus. Now, adorned with these sigils oozing purple-red blood, she looked even more like a demon hunter.

"Third sigil, master."

Sazeno picked up the largest piece of satyr skin — it even still had Vilethorn's pale purple hair on it. Jace popped a big chunk of cheese into his mouth and asked, "What's this one?"

Molphis said, "Shadow Enhancement Sigil. This one's quite common on other demons — like on that Ered'ruin corpse we ran into in the swamp before."

The imp said, "You can tell this one's more complicated too, sayaad, and the mana's much stronger. This time I'm certain — the Burning Legion must have placed great importance on the satyr demons converted from the local inhabitants, otherwise they wouldn't have bestowed such powerful sigils…"

"Shadow Enhancement?" Jace asked. "What does that mean — boosting shadow magic, or…"

Sazeno said, "Satyr demons have a powerful command over shadow, and Vilethorn was far stronger than all his underlings! I won't say for certain, but I think this is the main reason the master's shadow magic had such a hard time affecting that satyr."

"Then put it on her," Jace said. "Next time we run into a tough bastard like that, I'm counting on you to hold the line, Molphis."

"I would die for you, darling," Molphis tilted her head to look over. "Just say the word…"

"Let's skip the dying. Even though dying in Azeroth isn't anything to be afraid of for you, try to stay alive and useful as much as possible."

As he spoke, Jace stood up and downed a mana potion in one gulp. He did a few warm-up stretches — the kind you'd do before gym class — loosened up his joints, then glanced at the succubus. "But there's no more space left on her chest. Her waist is thin as it is — how's a sigil this big supposed to fit?"

"We can flip her over," the imp said. "Turn around yourself, sayaad. Don't waste the master's precious energy." Jace twirled his finger, and the succubus nodded and turned around, lying flat on the table.

He tried not to look at the two broken bones, pressing down on the succubus's shoulders. "All right, I'm starting."

"I can't wait any longer, master," Molphis whispered.

Jace confirmed the complex sigil in his mind, found a suitable starting stroke, and carefully pressed the blade down.

Honestly, this felt less like learning the demonic tongue or some other runic language and more like copying out a Chinese character he didn't recognize — except this "character" was far more complicated, with far messier strokes. Still, he felt he was picking it up fairly easily…

Molphis gripped the edge of the table tightly, trembling from time to time. At those moments Jace would feel a faint stinging pain across his back, and he'd slow down a bit, take a rest, then continue.

At times like that, the succubus would wrap her tail around his waist again and again, even leaving scratches and cuts across his back and waist. He didn't pay it much mind either — he was just glad he hadn't worn anything expensive today.

By the time he finished the last sigil, Jace felt like if he'd pushed even a few more minutes he would have burned through his mana and passed out. This beat-up old table had been clawed to pieces by the succubus, wood shavings curling up everywhere with deep gouges all across its surface, and stained a dark purple by fel blood that gave off a dizzying, cloying stench.

He should probably compensate Erland for the wooden table, he thought to himself as he caught his breath and recovered.

Jace pulled out his pocket watch and glanced at it — it was nearly midnight. He looked at the imp and asked, "Are there any more sigils?"
