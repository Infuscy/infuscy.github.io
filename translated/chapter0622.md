## Chapter 622: For Love, or for Power

Sazeno gathered up Vilethorn's demon hide and mulled it over before answering. "There are two more… or three… I'm not sure what they do, master. If we don't know their effects, I think we shouldn't go carving them on willy-nilly! Better safe than sorry! Who knows what the sayaad'll turn into once we carve them in!"

"Fair enough," Jace said. "If you're ready, we can start transferring the power."

"Pour the mana potion into the bowl for me, master!" Sazeno said. "Finally, it's my turn. I know you hate me, sayaad, but remember — don't resist my magic on instinct. Otherwise we'll have to waste the master's time tracking down powerful casting materials to forcibly transfer the mana against your will. That wastes time and money!"

"Just for tonight," Molphis smiled faintly. "Consider it for the master's gold."

"For the master's gold!"

The imp sniffed the mana bowl Jace had poured, then dipped a finger in and tasted it. Jace watched a dark taint streak across the surface of the mana potion wherever the little creature touched it.

"Very good. Very good."

It raised its high hands and began chanting the incantation. The sigils on Vilethorn's hide released an increasingly bright purple-blue glow as the imp sang, bathing the entire room in the same color!

The incantation sounded similar to the one the imp had chanted last time when transferring the power of the Void Robe, but also somewhat different. Jace had only heard it that once, so he couldn't remember the exact details — but this one seemed longer, and the imp was chanting it with even greater fervor.

A few lines in, the pitch climbed higher and higher, until it was practically screaming. Jace remembered that last time the imp had passed out the moment the spell finished. It wasn't going to fail halfway through this time, was it?

Mana in the bowl gave off a dark blue flame, and Vilethorn's hide caught fire along with it. Molphis shuddered and braced herself against the table — but the table had begun giving off black smoke too…

Flames — purple and green intertwined — poured from Sazeno's eyes, horns atop his head, and both his hands. His voice had grown as deep and thick as a true demon of the Twisting Nether. Jace remembered the last time he'd heard the imp make a sound like that was when he'd used the summoning spell to call out Jace's name…

This little imp, barely bigger than a cat, had its shadow cast by the flames from the mana bowl onto the wall behind it — a massive, almost all-consuming phantom, swaying and cackling, while Molphis's burning sigils made her look like a helpless witch about to be burned alive beneath that ghostly shadow… capable of nothing but struggling and wailing.

And soon even that wailing was swallowed by the demon's chanting echoing through the narrow practice room…

The roar made Jace cover his ears. When he opened his eyes again and looked toward the center of the room, the table had been completely burned away. Molphis stood in the ashes on her black demonic hooves, supporting a body that was clearly a good deal taller. Set against the purple-blue fel light radiating from her entire body, Jace felt she was nearly his own height.

After the flames on her sigils died down, Jace noticed her horns had grown thicker, longer, and more curved, with sharper forks — but her face and body seemed thinner than before, or rather more slender, though the lines of taut muscle stood out even more.

The sigils had stopped burning, but the purple-blue fel light still flickered across them. Though dimmer now, it was still quite eye-catching in the dark room.

"Ah…" The sayaad stroked her sigils and let out a long breath. "This brings back memories of serving under Overlord Brutallus. But rather than having my soul taken captive by some bloated fool, I'd much rather entrust it to you, my master, Jace Sesso. The moment I first saw you, I knew I'd chosen right. Only you can help me reclaim the power the Twisting Nether stole from me, help me fill the gaps in my soul left by death after death… something countless self-important Eredar fanatics could never do."

Jace could feel that even her voice seemed to have changed somewhat — the immense void mana made her speech heavier. He let out a slight breath and smiled. "I thought you were really willing to be my servant out of love."

Molphis smiled at Jace. "My pursuit of you… and my desire for you to help me reclaim my power aren't in conflict, master. Besides, becoming powerful is part of the pursuit itself. Isn't a powerful servant more likely to win her master's favor?"

"A useful servant wins her master's favor, you stupid succubus!" Sazeno laughed. "Otherwise wouldn't the master have tossed me back into the Twisting Nether long ago? Huh? That alone proves how important I am!"

"Molphis is certainly important too," Jace said, sitting back in his chair against the wall. "But your wings still haven't grown back. No matter how much mana you absorb, there's no sign of recovery at all."

"That has nothing to do with the master's shadow mana," Molphis said. "Those demonic wings can never return to my body. Reckless as he was, Brutallus is still one of the most terrifying demon lords in the Twisting Nether — his savage strength is no less than that of Mannoroth, Lord of the Annihilan. The fact that my soul wasn't completely annihilated when he tore apart his fortress is already more than I could ask for."

As she spoke, the succubus turned her body. Jace was surprised to find that the two broken bones on her back had healed — they looked like two bulging horn buds.

"Not to mention, dear, this is already perfect."

Jace nodded and didn't pursue the topic further. He fiddled with the Blade of Eternal Darkness in his hands for a bit, then turned his gaze to the imp.

"Speaking of which, you didn't pass out this time. Is enchanting Molphis's sigils even easier than enchanting my robe?"

"Give me more soul shards, master, and I'll wield mana even more freely! I've told you this before!" the imp said.

"You did well this time. When we get back, I'll reward you with a centaur hero's soul shard." Jace said.

"Wonderful! Then when do we carve the rest of the sigils on her?"

"Not worried about the danger this time?"

"What's danger compared to the importance of a soul shard…"

"Figure out what those sigils actually do first."

As he spoke, Jace stood up and looked at Molphis. "Does this count as me keeping my promise?"

"Of course, master." Molphis vanished into a swirl of shadow, then reappeared at the doorway, leaning over to pull the door open for Jace. "Then I'll take you at your word." Jace reached the door and held it. "Thanks, Molphis."
