## Chapter 625: The Timeless Pact

No matter what he'd experienced on the road, Elwynn Forest was always exactly as it had been when Jace first arrived — almost completely unchanged.

The afternoon sun shone through the trees. Jace quietly listened to the distant sounds, confirming there were no hunters or random wanderers — or worse, mages from the Wizard Sanctum — around before pulling a soul shard from his pack.

This time he'd brought Gorefiend's Staff, hoping to use the staff to bolster his shadow magic and summon as powerful a felhound as possible. After all, he was testing both the shadow control spell and the demon enslavement spell today — so the stronger the opponent, the better.

"I don't need me to help hold the felhound this time, do I, master?" Molphis asked.

"If you see I can't hold on, step in and kill whatever demon I summon," Jace reminded her.

The succubus nodded. Jace tossed the soul shard onto the grass, then closed his eyes and raised his right hand, chanting the felhound summoning incantation.

The soul shard evaporated in an instant and a rift in the void tore open. He gripped the dark staff in his hand, guiding the fissure wider as a felhound struggled its way out and crashed to the ground…

This felhound was enormous, its body lashing out with four or five terrifying tentacles. An ordinary felhound had only two, but this one flailed the claws on each tentacle like an octopus lying on its back.

Even Molphis frowned at the sight of this demon. Jace had no time to think about anything else. "Duk, Heduniloyinmosi!"

He thrust out his hand and chanted the incantation. A Shadow Claw materialized following his hand shape and slammed the felhound's head into the ground — but he could clearly feel the brute force of the demon's thrashing being transmitted back through it.

Very soon he was about to lose his grip on it. The demon's four thick legs scraped against the ground nonstop, trying to break free of the shadow's control. Jace instinctively tried to yank it off the ground — and to his surprise, the Shadow Claw seemed to respond to his intent, lifting the felhound bit by bit into midair…

Was this the Death Knight's Strangulate from the game?

Even if it wasn't the same spell, it at least served roughly the same purpose — using shadow power to hoist an enemy into the air to incapacitate and control them.

But just as Jace was about to follow the satyr scroll's instructions and cast the Enslave Demon incantation while the creature was restrained, the Shadow Claw clamping down on the felhound suddenly gave out and vanished. The felhound hit the ground and immediately thrashed back to life, snarling and lunging straight at Jace…

Wait — was this a channeled spell?

Molphis had already gripped her black blade, ready to strike. Jace used the Shadow Claw again to check the demon's movement. The felhound was yanked back through the air a short distance, then lifted again by Jace's shadow energy. These two bouts of struggle had already cost Jace roughly a third of his energy to watch drain away — add in the mana spent summoning the felhound, and he was already past two-thirds spent. The felhound he'd summoned this time really was vastly stronger than the little runt he'd casually summoned in the Eldre'Thalas catacombs…

And this was just the beginning. If he kept this up, two mana potions definitely wouldn't be enough for today's experiment.

He focused his mind, maintaining this Shadow Claw — or rather, this Strangulate — keeping the gesture with his right hand while his left followed the Enslave Demon casting motions described by the satyr… and spoke the incantation from his lips.

"Vita, Tahotusiqi, Miloen, Tahotuotani…"

Jace clearly felt that casting Enslave Demon here was a bit harder than in Eldre'Thalas, saturated as it was with arcane and shadow power. Shadow still answered every shift of his lips, his wrists, even his fingers, letting out a low murmur… but it wasn't as fervent as it had been over there.

Still, this time the Strangulate didn't dissipate as quickly. The felhound thrashed with signs of breaking free — only to suddenly be bound by another shadow sigil, carved straight into its skull…

The shadow sigil quickly sank into its cranium. The felhound's savage roars turned to whimpers, its struggles growing weaker and weaker.

Jace could feel its emotions. It was no longer some otherworldly demon trying to kill him in a frenzy — it was more like a servant, a hunting hound, confused about why its master wanted it dead.

As the felhound submitted, he felt that inner fire again. A wisp of green light flickered at his fingertips — it burned for barely a moment before winking out, but Jace caught it.

He reined in his emotions, resisting the urge to unleash his bloodlust, doing his best to steady his agitated heart. Until the Strangulate's effect faded, his right hand lowered bit by bit, and his left slowly unclenched.

The Flowing Ice Crystal couldn't completely purge the fel energy that had merged into his blood — he might have to wait for it to fade naturally, just like the orcs did. But at least now Jace could control it well enough that it didn't interfere with his spellcasting. That was something, at least.

This was exactly why Jace had always been reluctant to use Life Drain carelessly. You never knew what kind of strange things your target might be carrying.

The black dragon blood brought plenty of benefits. Its downsides, apart from the occasional bizarre dream connected to Northrend, were few enough.

The necrotic filth in Teron Gorefiend's body had been vomited out long ago, then instantly purified by the waters of the Sunwell — luckily without leaving any serious aftereffects.

Hopefully this extraction of the Ered'ruin blood from Dath'mora would turn out well too. For now, the felhound was clearly successfully enslaved. It slowly crept to his side, looking remarkably docile.

Jace didn't even know what to call it — since he hadn't used the Annihilan naming ritual, the Enslave Demon spell should have worked.

"How long does the Enslave Demon incantation last?" Jace asked. "Five minutes? An hour?"

"How long does it last?"

Molphis turned her head, wearing an expression as if Jace had asked something utterly absurd. "Does the master really think the bond between him and his demon would fade away on its own that easily?"

"What do you mean?" Jace asked.

Molphis tossed her dagger into the air and caught it. "Naturally — until one of them dies. Either you or it. Or until you're no longer able to maintain the enslavement — say, losing your mana and willpower due to some terrible calamity, or having the connection severed by another powerful caster… Of course, all those scenarios are extremely rare. After all, you haven't enslaved some day laborer, master."
