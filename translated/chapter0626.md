## Chapter 626: The Demon Skull

So the Enslave Demon incantation here was permanent?

Jace was a little surprised. Of course, the satyr scroll hadn't mentioned anything about a time limit either — he'd assumed it had something to do with mana, but he hadn't expected it wasn't even worth mentioning.

Still, Jace wasn't planning on maintaining a master-servant relationship with this powerful felhound for very long. After all, he had a second spell to test.

Kormakien — Death Coil.

Ever since he'd learned this incantation on the ship on the way to Kalimdor, he hadn't tried it even once.

After all, practicing such an evil spell in front of Grede and Vereesa — especially with Mosar there too — would only make the already somewhat awkward party dynamics even more strained.

Korili, Ferathos, Pakumili.

A very short incantation, but the pronunciation was bizarrely tricky — constant trilling, constant lip-biting, like it required several layers of lips and teeth producing sound at the same time. Every time Jace tried to practice the exact pronunciation of this incantation, he found it endlessly frustrating.

But even just silently reciting this incantation in his mind made Jace feel as though all the shadow power he'd summoned with his previous spells was turning its gaze toward him at once.

The succubus frowned. "What is the master thinking?"

"That counts as telepathy, right?" Jace asked.

"No," Molphis said. "I can feel the shadow energy being wielded by you, master. But it makes me uneasy."

"I'm trying to familiarize myself with the Death Coil incantation," Jace said.

"That figures," the succubus said softly.

The felhound lay crouched beside them motionless, its tentacles draped over its head, no longer swaying about. The imp hadn't said a word the entire time — as if making the slightest sound might get him chosen as the Death Coil's casting material.

Death Coil was an aggressively invasive shadow magic that drained the target's life force. So Jace needed to pick a good target first.

He'd just use the tree of moderate size beside them.

He recalled the tone in Teron Gorefiend's voice when he'd shouted those words in the memory fragment, the look on his hand, the motion of his hands…

Jace couldn't be sure whether the scene in his memory was still clear after all this time, but he had nothing else to reference.

Considering that when he'd tried dark magic before without gesture references, the shadow power itself had guided him toward the correct casting motions when he released the spell…

The most important thing was to get a grip on his emotions and give the spell a powerful driving force. Greedy desire. A craving to seize everything…

This was no time for mercy.

The felhound seemed to sense the danger and began making low, uneasy growling sounds. But Jace had already touched its soul from within his consciousness…

Korili, Ferathos, Pakumili…

Jace spoke the incantation in a rush, and the spell took effect quickly. The felhound burst into flames, struggling amid a burst of expanding green fire as it burned to ashes. A demon skull wreathed in a churning mass of dark and green fel fire rose from the charred remains into the air, baring its fangs and claws as it screamed toward him — as if furious at being killed so suddenly…

"— Hear my orders, you savage demon soul, and stop snarling at me!"

Jace pointed at the tree he'd marked as the target. The demon skull immediately seemed to find an outlet for its rage, howling as it dove at the tree and began gnawing at it furiously. Its form grew fainter with each rip and tear, gradually merging entirely with the surrounding flames and setting the tree ablaze.

The evening sun was already somewhat dim, and the green-fel-lit tree illuminated the view unnaturally, making everything around it appear even more gloomy.

The poor lone tree was tormented by shadow, withering rapidly… Jace felt his whole body filled with a scorching life force, and the exhaustion from all that spellcasting invigorated him considerably.

He stared at the tree, yet couldn't describe what he was feeling inside. The joy of a successful first cast? Not at all.

He'd actually expected to go smoothly, because the shadow magic reaction this incantation provoked was very different from the Hand of Gul'dan. The memorization and study of the incantation along the way — whether it was the shadow power hanging in the air, the shadow within his body, or the fel fire… all had seemed to be eagerly waiting for him to perform this spell, to devour the demon's soul… and that actually made him feel quite uncomfortable.

"Erdemensamalus…"

Jace hesitated no longer. He drew Azure Song and chanted the incantation, using the arcane power of the magical sword to slowly gather an extraordinarily potent pure fireball at the blade's tip.

The gathered, dancing fire element not only swept away the chaotic shadow and disordered fel energy radiating from the tree, but also helped Jace burn away the unease in his heart bit by bit.

He hurled the fireball out in one go, finally blowing apart the blackened tree that had already been on the verge of collapse.

The nearly spent fel embers were devoured by the suddenly arrived high-energy fireball, while the roots completely corrupted by chaotic and dark magic were blasted to ashes — even blowing a crater in the ground.

Molphis's black hair was thrown back by the fiery heat wave. After the scorching wind had completely dissipated, she looked over. Jace was still staring blankly at the crackling remnants of the flames.

He raised his head and gazed at the sky, which had already darkened. "Let's head back early. Don't let anyone find out there was some kind of demon gathering here."

Whatever Death Coil had ultimately made him feel, today's incantation training had gone reasonably smoothly on the whole — especially managing to successfully reproduce the two incantations he'd practiced back in Kalimdor.

Walking along the canal late at night, his mood was fairly relaxed, so Jace observed the buildings along the bank as he went.

He was planning to buy the house in the Trade District close to the Cathedral of Light — the northern part — because that would make it more convenient for his mother to attend services.

Though it was quite far from the Mage Quarter, and as a warlock he certainly didn't want to be too close to the Cathedral of Light, the house was ultimately for his parents, not for him. Their feelings had to come first.

But in the end it would depend on which houses were actually on the market.

Jace didn't want to wait around for some perfect house anymore. He'd already saved up enough money, and the longer he waited, the more could go wrong. The sooner he got his parents settled, the sooner he could rest easy.

Lying in bed at night, he thought about how tomorrow he'd be going with the agent to pick out a house, and couldn't help but get excited.

He glanced at the Quel'Thalas chest at the foot of his bed. Originally Vereesa had used it to hold Azure Song; now he was using it using it to store his money. He wished he could cast some sealing spell to lock it up tight.

"Watch this chest well, Molphis," he muttered, heard the succubus's acknowledgment, and drifted off to sleep.
