Chapter 646: Viscous Darkness

Anyone who'd been in a battle knew that when you faced an opponent you had to read their intentions and anticipate their moves. But a rusty sword emerging from a pile of rotten muck rising out of the water gave Jace absolutely no clue what would happen one second from now.

Of course, he at least knew that a sword being pointed at him by something he couldn't see could only mean him harm. He instinctively stepped back — bracing for the thing to suddenly swing the blade at him — but instead the sword went whistling past him in a throw.

If he hadn't taken that step back, it would have opened a hole clean through his gut!

"Ambush?!" Grede jumped to his feet, barefoot, and snatched up his axe. But after the sword was hurled, the surface of the water went calm again. The dead face from before had vanished too.

"What happened?" Vereesa had already drawn her twin blades. She stared toward Jace. "Murlocs? Or what?"

Before Jace could work out an answer, Vereesa suddenly seemed to get yanked by the leg and was dragged straight into the water weeds beside her. Grede leaped off the slab and lunged over, grabbing Vereesa's arm. He stared into the weeds and shouted, "The water's moving! The water's moving!"

After a brief tug-of-war, whatever was in the weeds finally let go. With a great splash it sent a spray of black, unidentifiable garbage and filth everywhere. The dwarf and the elf were thrown off balance by the sudden release and stumbled onto the dry ground. Jace was just about to chant an incandescence spell to burn those weeds when he felt his own leg get grabbed and dragged backward…

A swamp beast? No…

He forced a step forward to fight back against the pull, turned around, and saw a broken sword hurtling straight at his face. He ducked in fright. The hilt grazed his hair and sailed past him — but his feet lost their balance and he got dragged down into the muck…

"Master!"

Morophil vanished from a distance and reappeared beside Jace, seizing his arms. The imp scrambled out of the pack and leaped onto Jace, screaming an incantation toward whatever was behind him…

"Burn!"

He watched the "living sewage" jutting up from the creek behind him erupt in fel-green demon-flame under the imp's Immolate, letting out a piercing screech. Jace felt his feet loosen and was dragged back onto solid ground by the succubus.

A thought had just surfaced in Jace's mind when the next scene confirmed his guess.

From the pool behind the slab, a mass of thick black shadow spread outward, dragging countless shattered bones, tattered clothing and armor, hauling a tangle of blades and unidentifiable weapons through the muck as it climbed onto the slab. A quivering, rotting head was lifted to the highest point by the mass of filth, its jaw clacking away as if trying to say something — but in the end only producing a clack-clack-clack of bones knocking together.

Grede buried his axe into the black sludge and sewage, but it had absolutely no effect. Instead, the muck grabbed hold of his arm and weapon. He finally wrenched himself free, but his arm was already covered in bloody scratches from the filth inside.

"Slime!" the dwarf shouted.

Vereesa nocked an arrow and blew the head at the highest point apart — but the black muck was completely unaffected. Its body only swelled larger, until finally it swallowed the entire slab and came flowing rapidly in their direction. The head that had been shot away was replaced by another, lifted back to the highest point, still clacking away with that same eerie sound.

And it wasn't just that one head — the other heads, the ones lower down, deep in the body, even the ones being trampled underfoot by the giant slime… just how many dead had this thing devoured?

Not just humans — far more were animals!

Since the imp's fire could hurt it, there was no reason Jace's couldn't either.

While the slime was still trying to chase down Grede, Jace planted his staff and began chanting a Fireball. Gathering fire elements in a wetland environment wasn't easy. He'd only just started casting when the slime seemed to sense what Jace was doing — several heads came dragging their garbage and waste around with a splash, turning toward him, even hurling a few weapons as they came.

The giant slime raised a blob of sewage the size of an arm, dragging a badly rusted hooked shortsword, swinging it wildly. Jace used Fel Grasp to yank it straight into his hand, then slashed through the raised mass of filth in one motion, churning it into a puddle of mud that splashed to his feet.

With his other hand he swung his staff and hurled the freshly gathered fireball forward before the entire slime creature could crash into him — right into his target!

The blazing explosion sent a shockwave that pushed Jace back a step. Black, burning filth sprayed high into the air. The slime was blown apart, dissolving into sewage that flowed into the pond beside them.

Jace had only just felt a flash of surprise that he could guide a Fireball with one hand while using Fel Grasp with the other when he noticed, with a start, that the blob of sludge chasing Grede in the distance was still there — just a little smaller!

So the slime had split into two when it branched off to chase him and Grede!

The one chasing Grede had gotten smaller, but it was also faster. Grede, who'd only just managed to break free, had his foot seized again. It took a good while before he managed to escape with Vereesa's help.

Vereesa fired an Arcane Arrow at the head of the larger slime in the distance. The arcane flame pierced through the thick, viscous mucus and drilled a little further toward the skull inside — then quickly guttered out, its light becoming part of the slime. She cursed under her breath and looked over.

"Jace, we're getting out of here!"

"We're fighting the entire swamp, master," Morophil warned too.

Jace agreed with her. He'd only meant to help relieve the pressure on Grede and Vereesa's side, but he hadn't expected that using magic would barely even slow these slimes down. This didn't feel like fighting a few monsters anymore — it was about to become a fight for their lives against the very creeks and muck beneath their feet. "Go."

He stepped over a puddle at his feet and ran toward Vereesa and Grede. Morophil followed nimbly. The three of them picked their way between the black slime creatures rising out of the water all across the swamp, searching for solid footing. Anyone who accidentally sank into a muck pit was pulled out by the others. Bit by bit, with difficulty, they drew together and fled in the same direction.

The sun sank lower and lower, until only a sliver of dull red light remained, staining the swamp like a pool of sickly, diseased blood. In those bubbling, ominous pools of water, one slime after another rose and fell, clacking out the sound of bones, trying to catch the dwarf, the human, the elf, and the demon leaping overhead — wanting to turn them all into the same indistinguishable chaos as itself…
