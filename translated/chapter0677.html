Chapter 677: The Master's Power Is Always the Best

Back at the rental house that night, Jace changed his clothes and looked over to see the succubus sitting on the edge of the bed watching him. Once, this had been an utterly familiar sight — but seeing it now, he really could tell she'd gotten noticeably taller than before.

"There's something I want to ask you two."

"Go ahead, Master." Sazeno was the first to poke his head out from under the bed and scramble nimbly onto the table. "I'll tell you everything I know!"

"How do you subdue a Voidwalker?" Jace asked.

"Voidwalker," the imp said. "Master can just use the Enslave Demon spell to forcibly control it!"

"I mean binding one with a contract, like you two," Jace said.

"You need to defeat it with Shadow magic, Master," Morophil said. "Powerful Shadow magic can disrupt a Voidwalker's existence, making it submit to the summoner's power. Soon it will be drawn to the summoner's dark side and become its servant… obeying its orders."

"That shouldn't be hard for Master," the imp said. Simple-sounding enough, but how about in practice?

At the very least, this shouldn't have been an easy target for his Dagger Society colleagues — otherwise they wouldn't have kept that Voidwalker sealed inside for so long.

Jace had a feeling the Chain of Will's power probably needed a stronger target to properly test. A Felguard, for example. But that would probably have to wait until after he came back from Quel'Thalas.

By next year, once everything had settled, he planned to set out for Moonbrook again, see Mosh'aru, and have a talk with him about that greater demon — find out what exactly he was trying to summon.

And then there was the doomguard. If he wanted to get to the bottom of the Ulthalesh leads, mastering the Ered'ruin language was a must.

From the whispers drifting around Stormwind to the latest news passed around the basement of the Slaughtered Lamb, Jace had the feeling Mosh'aru still hadn't achieved his goal. It had been dragging on for over a year now. Mosh'aru should have been even more anxious than before, and Jace himself had far stronger chips to bargain with than back then — it'd be easier to take control of the situation.

Compared to summoning a Felguard, a brute of a soldier, summoning an Ered'ruin — a terrifying Shadow manipulator — was several times more dangerous. Lost in thought, Morophil had already crawled onto the bed and was gently draping herself across Jace's chest.

Yes, at least he still had Morophil's help…

"Master, isn't it time to satisfy your loyal servant?" the succubus asked.

"One more question," Jace said. "Do you know that Shadowstep the satyrs use? The kind where you slip into shadow and use Shadow magic to move quickly?"

"Give me your… Master, and I'll tell you." She made a little walking motion with her fingers toward Jace's lower belly, slowly dipping into his waistband, and whispered by his ear, "I'm nearly withered away from waiting, my good Master. Let your power nourish poor little me…"

"You're sure the Dragonqueen's blood won't hurt you?" Jace asked.

"So what if it does?" Morophil tilted her head and stared into Jace's eyes. "You think I was joking before, Master? You think I… wanted to die by your power, wanted to be tormented by you, abused by you… was just the sweet-talking lies a succubus is used to telling?"

"If I burn you to death here today, my soul will be happy in the Twisting Nether. I'll patiently reshape this body and wait for Master to summon me again. And now Master's life has been extended a great deal — you can certainly wait for me to come back to your side again, can't you?"

"So let me serve you properly…"

With that, she suddenly yanked down Jace's trousers, turned, and ducked down.

Jace felt like something had viciously lashed his whole body… and that one stroke nearly made him lose control. "Are you trying to kill me…"

Morophil didn't respond, just kept sucking in muffled rhythm — once… twice… until Jace felt his heart about to hammer out of his chest, his mouth dry, a fierce ache and tingle surging downward… The rate of mana draining was far faster than when he kept casting Hand of Gul'dan without stopping.

But even so, Jace didn't feel any sign of overdrawing.

Morophil caught her breath, let out a soft laugh. "Master… you're incredible."

She ducked back down. The demon's prominently-ridged spine shuddered rhythmically with each draw, letting out the occasional moan — as if enduring extreme agony, though from her expression just now it was hard to say whether she was enjoying it…

Jace couldn't help but sigh, staring at her rune-covered back — except the runes were actually giving off a faint fiery glow now.

"The succubus has had it, hah!" the imp laughed from the side.

By the end, even the succubus's black hair was burning, both her forked horns gleaming with flame. Jace reached out and grabbed her horns. "Damn it… stop, Morophil, this house is going to catch fire."

"A little longer… Master, don't worry."

Morophil said this while wrapping herself around Jace's leg and refusing to let go, until at last Jace felt his mind go completely blank… He gripped the succubus's shoulders, and only then did she finally lift her head, savoringly.

She turned back, licked her lips, and smiled with satisfaction.

The arcane demon-flames in her eyes had been completely replaced by brilliant blazing fire. The hair and horns all over her body glittered with flames, only fading bit by bit after quite a while.

But her eyes never returned to blue — they had turned into searing sparks of fire, permanently.

"You're so good to me, Master. This is exactly why the Life-Binder didn't give me anything as thanks — she knew I had my own way to claim my reward." Morophil smiled, her voice barely containing her excitement.

"But how could you possibly withstand life force like that?!" the imp asked in shock.

"I'm not you." Morophil flicked her tail sharply, shaking off the flame at its tip. "A succubus takes everything of Master's power and turns it to her own use. Even if the conversion process is agonizing, anything that is Master's power is still thrilling beyond words… If you took that kind of power, you'd be slag in the first instant, you little snot."

"Pah!" the imp cursed.

Morophil flicked a glob of flame with her finger and smeared it across her black Nathrezim blade. The knife immediately erupted in layers of deep crimson fire. Seeing this, the imp shrieked and dove under the bed…

"So are you going to tell me about Shadowstep now?" Jace asked.

The succubus looked at Jace, tilted her head, and vanished into a puff of shadow — reappearing by the table not far away. "Master, there's no incantation, no way to cast it. You need to merge yourself with Shadow. Feel the shadow power drifting around you, and become one of them…"

Listening to Morophil's words, Jace closed his eyes and felt for the shadow power in the room.

Thanks to the two demons' presence, his room wasn't lacking in Shadow. But without an incantation, without the urge to fight, Jace felt like he had no point of leverage to draw that Shadow to his side.

Still, as he pondered this, no small amount of shadow energy was gradually gathering in this direction — it was just that there was pitifully little of it, and Jace couldn't find any possibility of "merging as one" with it.

"Can't do it," Jace said. "But without an incantation to assist, summoning with just my thoughts alone doesn't work well."

"Try communicating in Demonic, Master," Sazeno poked his head out from under the bed.

"Channel your mana into the words," Morophil said. "I almost forgot — your mana was just…"

"Not to the point of overdrawing," Jace said. He slowly closed his eyes and said in Demonic, "Shadow, gather to me…"

This time the effect was much stronger, just as he'd known it would. Shadow would respond to his call quickly, as long as he expressed his intent accurately.

When he opened his eyes again, he felt the room had darkened a great deal. So what next — how did he merge as one with the Shadow?

He tried to feel the nature radiating from the surrounding Shadow — chaotic, disordered… full of doubt and unease. Jace tried to find that emotion and let his body rise and fall with those energies.

This groping, combined with the chanting in Demonic, was burning through his mana badly, but he felt he was getting close to the critical point of harmonizing with it — and he didn't want to give up…

Suddenly Jace felt a darkness suddenly seize his body — but it vanished in an instant, leaving only a tremor.

The already-ethereal Shadow billowed out a wave of black mist in the wake of that tremor, and the flame of the candle before him was snuffed out in one blow.

"I knew Master could do it," Morophil said.

"Still a long way from truly doing it!" Sazeno said. "Getting into Shadow is one thing — moving within Shadow is another!" Morophil was silent a moment, then said, "That's true."

Jace took a few deep breaths to steady himself. "Let's not try anymore tonight. I need a good night's sleep, and I'll practice more later."
