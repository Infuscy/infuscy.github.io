## Chapter 678: News from Ironforge

A few days later, one morning, Jace heard that Galders would be coming to the basement that day, so he headed to the Mage Quarter intending to go to the Slaughtered Lamb and see about that Voidwalker. Before leaving Stormwind, he'd best get this matter resolved — and if he could sort out the Felguard problem too, even better.

Jace figured the Felguard people like them summoned couldn't possibly compare to the one that Blood Ring orc warlock had summoned. As long as Morophil was at his side, there was no need to worry the Felguard would cause any havoc.

What's more, the Morophil of now was far stronger than she'd been back when they were scavenging in the Black Morass.

Lost in these thoughts as he hurried to the garden beneath the Wizard Sanctum, he suddenly heard someone call out. "Mr. Sesso, wait a moment."

Jace turned around. Marin had already caught up.

Marin said, "Archmage Andormas wishes to see you. He learned at yesterday's meeting that you had already returned to Stormwind and is eager to find you. If you have time, you'd best go see him. He doesn't know where you live now."

"I understand." Jace glanced up toward the top of the mage tower. "Then I'll go now."

"That would be for the best," Marin said. "He should be in his office right now. I have a class on Transmutation magic, so I can't accompany you."

"I more or less understand what you've been doing, Mr. Sesso. You really met Deathwing?"

"I met him. He's dead," Jace said.

The old mage looked at Jace. "King Magni Bronzebeard's letter said that were it not for you, and Grede Thunderfist of Aerie Peak, and Vereesa Windrunner of Quel'Thalas… Deathwing might have destroyed all of Khaz Modan."

"From what I saw, that's not an exaggeration," Jace said.

The old mage said, "Many people don't understand what a terrifying being Deathwing is — including many mages. They think he's just a larger black dragon. But I know who he is, what he is. Merely facing him requires unimaginable courage and will, never mind fighting him. I imagine even those who feared you because of the Nethergarde Keep incident won't question anything anymore."

"Go on up. I'd really like to sit down and talk with you at length when you have time."

"I'll come find you, Master," Jace said.

"Anytime. Good luck, Mr. Sesso."

After parting with his teacher, Jace walked up the stairs into the mage tower, all the way to the highest floor, through the rotating corridor lined with portraits of the greatest mages in the history of the Wizard Sanctum, and into the final great door.

Sunlight from the high windows hit him, making his eyes — long accustomed to the dark stairwells and corridors — flare white for a moment.

"Mr. Jace Sesso." A middle-aged mage in blue with brown hair called from a distance. That was Magino Dumas, the Wizard Sanctum's overseer specializing in investigating necromantic, demonic, and dark magic cases. Every time Jace passed him, he couldn't help but feel tense.

Hearing his name, many of the mages in the hall turned to look. The entire space went quiet in an instant.

Standing beside him were several mages, including the mage instructor Jenya Cannon, and of course the Archmage Andormas, master of the Wizard Sanctum.

Andormas beckoned. "Over here, Jace. We have something we'd like to ask you."

Jace walked over and heard the people beside him talking about the orcs of Grim Batol, the red dragons, and the black dragons, their words tinged with shock, disbelief…

Reaching the table, the black-haired Archmage stared at him and said in a low voice, "Jace Sesso, you helped free the Dragonqueen from the orcs' captivity, and after returning to Stormwind you've just been wandering around? At the meeting a few days ago, Marin said you still wanted to learn a few fire spells… You're not planning to report to the Wizard Sanctum at all? To me, or your teacher? You resolved Grim Batol — the fortress of the Dragonmaw orcs. Without taking that fortress, all of Khaz Modan could never know peace, and the lines of communication between Stromgarde, Stormwind, and Ironforge would never flow smoothly. Do you have any idea how great a contribution you've made to the Alliance? And then you… just say nothing?! I've had to go looking for you everywhere."

"Calm down, Archmage Andormas," Jenya Cannon beside him reminded.

"My apologies," Andormas said. "I am… rather overwrought."

"Actually," Jace explained, "the Kirin Tor hasn't given me any response yet, Master. I don't know how they intend to evaluate the results of my mission… After all, the Kirin Tor wouldn't let me reveal anything to anyone."

He'd actually been expecting Krasus to take the initiative and find him, but for some reason, these past few days neither Krasus nor any other Kirin Tor mage had shown up.

Andormas said, "The Kirin Tor got that part right, at least. There are more hidden cultists who worship the black dragons in the Kingdom of Stormwind than we imagined. Colluding with the orcs, they could very well expose the plan to the black dragons."

Magino Dumas said, "Still, since Ironforge has notified us the crisis at Grim Batol has been resolved, we can at least breathe easier for a while."

"Well said, Master." Andormas looked at Jace. "The King wishes to invite you and Mr. Grede Thunderfist to Stormwind Keep. Within a week, you may pick a time, or discuss it with Mr. Thunderfist. At that time, you may request entry into the Royal Mages, Jace. Isn't that what you've always wanted?"

"Really?" Jace asked.

Andormas sighed. "You entered the orcs' fortress and rescued the Dragonqueen, and emerged victorious in the battle against Deathwing…"

"…If King Terenas II's mind is still sound, he should be offering to marry his daughter to you on his own initiative."

At that moment Jace heard two mages whispering nearby.

"I hear the Menethil princess has fallen in love with an Alterac nobleman."

"…It's Lord Daval Prestor."

"Does he hold the line of succession to the Kingdom of Alterac?"

"I hear he's both talented and handsome."

"A rising political star. I can't remember the last time an idol like this appeared among the Seven Kingdoms."

"I'm not the only one," Jace said.

"Of course. I'm sure the King will consider everything carefully," Andormas said. "But why would the Kirin Tor bypass us and come directly to you? Who approached you?"

"It was Archmage Krasus," Jace said.

"You mean Archmage Krasus of the Council of Six?" Magino asked in surprise.

Several nearby mages gasped at the sound of Krasus's name. One mage said, "I've been to Dalaran so many times and never once seen Krasus in person. Plenty of people in the Kirin Tor suspect he doesn't actually exist."

Jace explained, "He promised me that if I completed this mission, the Kirin Tor would restore my name."

Andormas sighed. "The Kirin Tor knows you've been desperate to have your true name accepted by the Alliance, to stop living as a dead man, so they were certain you'd accept this perilous mission. They guessed right. If they'd come to me first, I'd have had a hard time raising this with you on their behalf — but I couldn't find a second person in the entire Wizard Sanctum better suited for this mission, so I would have refused outright."

Jenya Cannon said, "But no matter how dangerous it was, you truly did it, Jace Sesso."

Andormas said, "The Kirin Tor pilfered the Wizard Sanctum's investigation findings on Grim Batol from the Wetlands and Redridge, the arrogant bastards. And in the end they still had a Wizard Sanctum person take the final step — and do it under their name, no less. Fortunately, the Dragonqueen knows exactly who gave everything and who simply sat back and watched. No matter how the Kirin Tor treats you, the King will not treat you poorly, Jace. Go to Stormwind Keep, and you'll see."
