## Chapter 683: For the Dagger Society

This wasn't the first time Jace had encountered the Shadowfury incantation.

When he'd run into Teron Gorefiend in Dalaran, Gorefiend had used Shadowfury against Kafal and Vereesa — two Farstriders — to disrupt their rhythm, and very nearly killed Kafal.

This was no simple spell. The Shadowfury Galders had just unleashed obviously couldn't compare to Teron Gorefiend's in raw power, but at least it had produced results.

Quite a few people in the basement had been shaken up by that blast of Shadowfury and were sitting off to the side resting. Galders clearly felt he'd been reckless too — he apologized to Jace and Sulenna, who were closest, and sprinkled a potion that purified shadow corruption. A bitter smell wafted through the basement.

Jace could tell that Galders was angry at his inability to enslave the void walker he'd summoned. It was rare to see Galders this emotional.

"Have all of you learned this incantation?" Jace asked.

Erland shook his head. The others stayed silent. Ursula said, "I can successfully cast the spell using a Rage Potion, but the effect isn't as good as Galders's either."

Galene, sitting to the side coughing, said, "It's because this spell causes too much destruction in an enclosed space. None of us get many chances to practice it regularly."

"The Rage Potion is crucial, Ursula," Galders looked over and said. "I've said it before — the reason the orcs call this magic Shadowfury is exactly that. Shadow itself is dark and gloomy, but that doesn't mean rage can't stir its power. Shadowfury is a quintessential rage-fueled incantation. But because the rage suddenly releases the gathered shadow, while it produces a severe disruptive effect, it doesn't have much follow-through in terms of shadow corruption."

"Because the shadow that could cause corruption is detonated in advance," Ursula said.

"That's exactly right," Galders said, looking at Jace. "What do you think of this incantation?"

"It can help us seize control of a situation quickly, or create chaos to find a way out. Important for a warlock," Jace said.

"Especially when Fear is hard to land," Galders added. "For instance, when facing undead... the disruptive effect of Shadowfury really stands out. I'll give you the incantation in a bit. Find an empty place to practice on your own — just like Galene said, the basement isn't suitable for unleashing Shadowfury."

"I'll remember, Galders," Jace said. "You've taken too many risks gathering these incantations for us. Don't put too much pressure on yourself."

Galders shook his head and said quietly, "It's really nothing. Only when everyone in our Dagger Society can contribute their strength can we uncover the potential of shadow magic and demons faster."

Jace nodded. He could see that the resentment the others had been carrying on their faces had mostly smoothed over. "What's your name?" Galders asked in Demonic of the void walker lingering in the corridor.

"Kumosh," the void walker answered in a hollow voice.

"Kumosh. Go back to that smaller practice room. We need to do the next summoning over here."

"Yes, master."

The void walker drifted away like a ghost, and the corridor and main hall slowly brightened. Only then did everyone realize that this fellow's presence had darkened the basement by more than a few shades.

"You're remarkable, Galders," Sulenna said. "Just looking at that creature made my nerves scream, let alone ordering it around."

"Then you'd better prepare yourself, because next we're summoning a doomguard," Galders said, producing a tattered scroll. "I bought this from another mercenary out of the Black Swamp. If I didn't know Demonic, I probably wouldn't have been able to figure out its value at all."

"Everyone's in rough shape today, Galders," Ursula said, pressing her forehead. "I think dealing with that void walker demon is already a solid result. If we summon something stronger — especially a doomguard... shouldn't we pick another time?"

"None of us are familiar with the doomguard incantation," Erland said. "If we want to attempt the summoning, it'd have to be you or Jace. Are you sure about this, Galders?"

Galders hesitated, then looked around at the others. It seemed no one was keen on going through another trial. He looked over and said, "I heard from Ursula that you're heading north soon, is that right?"

"About two or three months," Jace said.

"Not too long a stretch, then. Let's leave it for next time," Galders said. "In the meantime, we'll learn as much as we can about void walkers as a demon type. A doomguard really is dangerous."

Everyone breathed a small sigh of relief. After Galders announced the gathering dismissed, he hurried off to the small room where the void walker was hiding, his succubus following close behind.

Galene returned to her own room. Erland and Sulenna pulled out some wine, planning to eat and drink a bit in the main hall. Jace went to Ursula's alchemy lab.

Jace glanced out the door, confirming there was no one in the entire corridor leading to the lab, then asked in a low voice, "Has Galders been acting unusual lately?"

"Recently? I don't know, but today he was off the whole time," Ursula said. "Other than that, I don't know. Lately he's either in Ironforge or in Duskwood — he's not in Stormwind much. He's been doing everything he can to gather more incantations and casting materials."

As she spoke, she straightened her bookshelf and continued, "After Jagin died, these tasks have fallen to him alone. Fortunately, Erland doesn't clash with him the way he did with Jagin — he's been a big help."

"In that case, I won't be bringing Morophil on this Lordaeron trip," Jace said. "I'll leave her here with you, Ursula."

"Why?" Ursula asked. "I have Basadim."

"I think Galders..." Jace frowned. "Anyway, just do me a favor and keep an eye on this succubus for me."

"Also," he said, "this is another incantation I found in Kalimdor — a spell that can enslave demons. It's not easy to learn, but I've already mastered it. Enslaving an ordinary demon is no problem."

He spread out the scroll the imp had copied out over the last couple of days on the table and said, "If Galders really does try to summon a doomguard without me here, and you don't have any good way to bind a demon pact, you can try using this spell to forcibly enslave the demon first."

"A demon-enslaving incantation? My... you really are worried about Galders," Ursula said. "But I think he's just jealous, for the moment. When that void demon was first summoned, trapped in the circle it absolutely refused to obey Galders's orders — but the moment you appeared, that void walker showed submission... If I were him, I'd feel unbalanced too."

"But Morophil can stay here, Jace. They've all been busy lately — only Galene comes by regularly to craft the shadow robes. It'd be strange for just the two of us in the whole basement. Let your succubus keep me company here."

"Maybe. But with Morophil here, plus this demon-enslaving incantation, things should be fine even without me," Jace said. He took three silver coins from his pack and set them on the table. "The scene was a bit awkward earlier, so I didn't say anything to Erland. Remember to give these to him for the table compensation."

Ursula pocketed the silver and smiled. "No problem. I'll give them to him, Jace. You can head off with peace of mind."

Back at his rented room that evening, Morophil materialized from the shadows the moment they walked in, and said with a grievance, "You say you want me to protect the basement, but really you just don't want me ruining your sweet little trip with Vereesa Windrunner, master."

"What if I am?" Jace said. "And were you planning to walk right into Quel'Thalas's magical defenses with me? Want to get incinerated by all that mana? And then have Quel'Thalas's guards catch me red-handed as a demon-summoner..."

"At least I'd get to die with master," Morophil said with a smile.

"Go to hell," Jace said. "Who'd want to die with you. You keep an eye on Galders — I think something's off about him."

"He resents you for being favored by Shadow, and you resent him for growing ever stronger at wielding Shadow," the succubus said, half-joking. "You're afraid he'll surpass you soon, and you envy the fact he can effortlessly pull off a powerful shadow incantation like Shadowfury... Am I wrong? After all, it's not the sort of magic an ordinary warlock can master easily."

"I hope I'm only suspecting him out of envy or jealousy," Jace said. "I know what I went through to resist Shadow's mental interference. I hope Galders has a stronger will than mine."

"Hard to say," Morophil said.

Jace said, "Whatever happens, don't let anything go wrong in the Slaughtered Lamb basement, Morophil."

The succubus nodded. "Beyond obeying my master's orders, I don't have much choice. So tonight..."

"You're low on mana?" Jace said. "No can do. I've got to write my master's assignment. Don't come distracting me while I'm grinding. If I don't finish this, I'll be too ashamed to face my teacher."

A laugh came from beside the desk. Morophil's tail was just about to flick over when a scroll suddenly materialized in front of her. The tip of the succubus's tail screeched to a halt.

"I'm working hard too, Sathayde," the imp said. "I'm going to see if there are any problems or flaws in Galders's incantation..."

"...If you dare hurt me and slow down master's progress, you're done for!"

Jace said, as he took out fresh new paper he'd just bought, "That's for sure."

Morophil gritted her teeth, hopped onto the bed, and curled up furiously. She'd grown considerably larger than before — the impact made the bed creak and groan.

## Flags

- **Shadowfury** (暗影之怒): Jace recognizes this spell from Teron Gorefiend's use against Kafal and Vereesa in Dalaran. Confirms the name and nature established in chapter 682.
- **Kafal** (凯法尔): A Farstrider Jace and Vereesa encountered in Dalaran. Not in the glossary; rendering established in earlier chapters.
- **Galene** (嘉莱恩): A warlock member of the Dagger Society who crafts shadow robes. Rendering established in earlier chapters (Galene Ailuo).
- **Kumosh** (库尔莫斯): The void walker's name. Not in the glossary; rendering tentative (OC demon).
- **Doomguard** (恶魔卫士): The powerful demon type Galders wants to summon next. Canonical — the Ered'ruin.
- **Rage Potion** (愤怒药水): A potion that helps fuel the Shadowfury incantation by stoking rage. Not in the glossary.
- **Jagin** (加金): A deceased warlock whose death left Galders handling incantation-gathering alone. From the glossary.
- **Sathayde** (萨亚德): The name the imp uses when addressing Morophil in this scene ("I'm working hard too, Sathayde"). Chapter 688's translation treats Sathayde as the demon Vereesa is jealous of — i.e., Morophil (the succubus). So Sathayde appears to be another name for Morophil. The glossary lists the imp as Sazeno/Sazenilaz, distinct from Sathayde. Flagged for name reconciliation.
- **Black Swamp** (黑色沼泽): Region south of the Wetlands; source of the doomguard scroll. Canonical (the Blasted Lands/Swamp of Sorrows area).
