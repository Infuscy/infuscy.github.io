## Chapter 685: The Violet Citadel

Last time he'd stayed in Dalaran for a while, but the closest Jace had come to the Violet Citadel was the Orcish-language conference hall.

This time, standing before the statue fountain in the small square in front of the citadel, Jace glanced at that hall again, thinking of how Kafal Mingwing had stood guard there last time, and Kel'Thuzad had been waiting for him inside.

He wondered whether Kel'Thuzad was in the Violet Citadel at this moment.

Following a Dalaran mage up the steps and through the citadel's gate, stepping into the dim lobby — the purple carpet, the glass windows suffused with a dim yellow glow, wrapped in a thick, almost oppressive fragrance of flowers.

The lobby was quiet enough that you could hear the footsteps of the mage leading him and his own, muffled by the carpet.

The Violet Citadel was far more complex than its game counterpart. Jace remembered that in the game, the citadel had only this one lobby, with a staircase behind it leading to the second floor — but the second floor had nothing on it except a few bookshelves.

Here, though, when you looked up, you could see many levels — white spiral staircases stacked one on top of another, dizzying to look at. Following the Dalaran mage to the second floor, turning right into a corridor, walking all the way to the end, they arrived at an utterly ordinary-looking door.

Jace sneaked a curious glance inside. It was a rather small room, but exquisitely furnished. The entire floor was covered in purplish-red carpet. The bookshelves on either side held a variety of heavy tomes and scrolls. The ornate carvings on the chairs beside the bookshelves looked like the sort of thing you couldn't afford just by glancing at them — to say nothing of the desk at the far end, covered in scrolls, expensive inks, and quills made from the feathers of unidentified animals.

Even just the arcane-patterned tablecloth spread across it made Jace feel like it wasn't something a man of his station ought to own.

A floating magic lamp lit the whole room with a somewhat dreamlike glow. Jace felt a study like this ought to be a bit brighter, but perhaps the Kirin Tor mages just loved that mysterious atmosphere.

He eyed the room, thinking he could use it as a reference for the layout of things — to prepare the study in his future mage tower. Just then, the mage leading him said, "The court mage of the Stormwind Kingdom, Jace Sesso, has arrived, Archmage Antonidas."

The moment he spoke, the coat rack behind the desk actually moved.

Lit by the glow of arcane magic, Jace had genuinely thought it was a coat rack — but it was actually an old mage who had been staring at a mural on the wall, lost in thought.

He was simply too thin. When he turned around, his bulging eyes, wrinkled face, white beard hanging all the way to his waist, bald pate with only a few white hairs left, and the trembling of his eyelids as he looked at Jace all made it seem as though he hadn't long to live.

But Jace also knew that the old man before him was one of the most powerful mages in the entire Alliance — the master of Dalaran's mages and its "king," the leader of the Kirin Tor, Antonidas.

He was Jaina Proudmoore's teacher. A decade and a half later, during the Scourge invasion, to protect Dalaran's artifacts from being stolen by the Scourge to summon a fearsome Burning Legion lord, he would be personally cut down by Arthas, who had by then become a death knight.

Last time he'd come to Dalaran, Jace never would have dreamed he'd get to meet this old mage. And this time, it was the old mage who had invited him.

"I apologize — I didn't notice you had arrived." Antonidas braced himself on the table and gestured to a chair beside him. "Please, have a seat here, Mr. Sesso."

"Yes, Archmage Antonidas."

After Jace sat down, Antonidas straightened his blue robe and slowly sat. "Master Biso Eshad — do you still remember him?"

"I remember, Master," Jace said.

"I was the one who sent him, Mr. Sesso," Antonidas said. "Many people say I'm getting on in years, inflexible, clinging to dogma. But I know there are plenty of people across the Seven Kingdoms more conservative than I am — people who couldn't accept a hero capable of using that kind of dark magic."

"For most people, the red dragons of Grim Batol were a visible threat, not some far-off thing like the Dark Portal. You, Mr. Grede Thunderfist, and Lady Vereesa Windrunner tore out the red dragon prison of Khaz Modan — and the obstacles to clearing your name were considerably reduced. At the very least, that's how it stands in Ironforge, Lordaeron, and Dalaran."

"I understand the Kirin Tor's intentions, Master," Jace said. Though it doesn't mean I like it.

In truth, Jace still harbored a measure of resentment toward the Kirin Tor. Antonidas's voluntary explanation had dampened that fire a little, but he still felt a flicker of displeasure.

Antonidas looked at Jace. "The red dragon emissary says that you and your companions destroyed Deathwing."

"It was the Dragonqueen and her children fighting Deathwing from start to finish. I saw the whole thing clearly from the ground," Jace admitted plainly. "I only helped my companions disrupt Deathwing's plans and throw him off balance, making him lose that battle."

"But the Dragonqueen is crediting you with the achievement," Antonidas said. "Do you know what it means to defeat Deathwing?"

Jace felt he understood this question better than Antonidas did.

In another timeline, Deathwing hadn't died at Grim Batol. After the Demon Soul was destroyed, he'd been badly wounded by the combined assault of the other four Aspects at the height of their power and fled deep into the mountains of Grim Batol.

From that point on, he'd hidden in the deepest corners of the world, waiting for a chance to strike back — until the terrible power of the Old Gods drove him completely mad, and two or three decades later he wrought a cataclysm that swept across all of Azeroth.

By then, countless forces loyal to the Old Gods — including the remnants of the Black Dragonflight, the Twilight's Hammer cultists, the elementals, even the insectoid aqir, the mad Old God cultists — had all seized the chance to emerge into the sunlight and wreak havoc everywhere, nearly bringing about the end of the world.

Deathwing dying in the Barthor Mountains meant none of that would come to pass.

Antonidas said, "Compared to the other dragonflights, the Black Dragonflight has always been the most evil and savage — and Deathwing is the source of all that evil and savagery. I know you were here in Dalaran when the black dragons raided it, fighting alongside the Farstriders against the death knight, so you should understand: when the Horde invaded Azeroth, the black dragons firmly stood at the side of those otherworldly enemies, as though their hatred of this world could bridge every prejudice and distance."

"So it's only natural that the Dragonqueen would want me to restore your name — that was what the Kirin Tor promised from the very beginning. And I intend to invite you to come to Dalaran to study arcane magic. Would you be interested?"

"Forgive me, Master," Jace said. "King Varian hopes I can give him more counsel on shadow magic in Stormwind. Though I've always longed to come to Dalaran to study... let me think it over."

## Flags

- **The Violet Citadel** (紫罗兰城堡): The seat of the Kirin Tor in Dalaran. Canonical.
- **Antonidas** (安东尼达斯): Archmage and leader of the Kirin Tor. From the glossary. Canonical.
- **Kel'Thuzad** (克尔苏加德): The archmage Jace suspects is still in the citadel. Canonical — later a founding member of the Cult of the Damned and a lich in the Scourge.
- **Kafal Mingwing** (凯法尔·明翼): The Farstrider who guarded the Orcish conference hall on Jace's last visit. Not in the glossary; rendering established in earlier chapters.
- **Biso Eshad** (比索·埃斯沙德): The mage Antonidas sent (on a prior occasion). Not in the glossary; rendering tentative.
- **Jaina Proudmoore** (吉安娜·普罗德摩尔): Antonidas's student. Canonical.
- **Arthas** (阿尔萨斯): Canonical — by the time of the Scourge invasion he is a death knight who slays Antonidas.
- **The Demon Soul** (恶魔之魂): The artifact that enslaved the dragons' power. From the glossary.
- **The Barthor Mountains** (巴托山脉): Where Deathwing falls in this altered timeline. Not in the glossary; rendering tentative (possibly a variant of a canonical range).
- **Old Gods** (上古之神): The ancient dark entities (N'Zoth, etc.). Canonical.
- **Twilight's Hammer** (暮光教徒): The cult serving the Old Gods. Canonical.
- **Aqir** (虫族): The ancient insectoid race serving the Old Gods. Canonical.
- **Skybreaker's Song / staff**: Not directly referenced; Jace's lack of his former weapons is the subtext of his unease earlier in the chapter arc.
- **Kirin Tor Council of Six** (肯瑞托六人委员会): The ruling body of the Kirin Tor. From the glossary.
