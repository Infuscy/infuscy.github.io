## Chapter 691: Visiting the Lady Mage

Outside the Quel'Thalas embassy district, Jace glanced at his pocket watch. At that moment, Vereesa came hurrying out carrying a small bag. "How did it go?" Jace asked.

"Come with me — I found out where she lives, near the college." Vereesa handed him a small slice of cake with a raspberry on top, pointed north up the street, and asked, "Are you sure Amy won't report this to the Kirin Tor?"

Jace took the cake, shoved the whole thing into his mouth, and spoke while chewing. "She might refuse to help me, but she definitely won't report me."

"That's good, then," Vereesa said. "But, not that I doubt you, Jace — I'm still curious why you've suddenly become so interested in what Dar'Khan is up to."

Of course Jace couldn't possibly explain to Vereesa what he was really thinking. After all, Dar'Khan was, for now, a respected magister. And as for the Scourge... he didn't even know whether the plague of undeath had appeared in Northrend yet.

And now that he was here, he even harbored a sliver of suspicion about another layer to it.

Dar'Khan was a Quel'Thalas scholar of the Twisting Nether. Not only that, but he'd also been privately researching dark magic all along, and the subjects of his research were quite forbidden. And Kel'Thuzad was a senior archmage who'd been secretly researching the death knight's fall into dark magic in Dalaran — could there be some connection between the two of them? Was that the reason Dar'Khan had appeared in Dalaran?

Maybe. But more likely, he was letting Kel'Thuzad get to his head and was overthinking things. In any case, visiting Amy Marin was a must.

"Nothing better to do right now," Jace said, glancing at his pocket watch. "Almost three o'clock. We've still got a few hours before our ship leaves for Lordaeron. Don't you want to find something to do with that time?"

"Find something to do?" Vereesa walked along, slowly nodding. "You mean simultaneously breaking the laws of both Quel'Thalas and Dalaran?"

"Exactly. What do you think?" Jace asked.

"Quite interesting," Vereesa said. "But if Amy doesn't agree, you can't force her. You saved her life — I'm worried she'll be unwilling to refuse you out of a desire to repay the debt."

"No problem," Jace said.

"Tell me — does this have something to do with Kel'Thuzad?" Vereesa asked in a low voice.

"I don't know. Right now I can't be sure of anything."

"All right."

The two of them walked to a garden path beside the college. The Kirin Tor mages' dormitories were just ahead.

Following the address Vereesa had gotten from an elven professor, they entered a pretty little building with purple roof tiles, and found a wooden door on the ground floor facing the garden's shaded walkway. On it Jace saw the surname Marin, plus Amy's initials in both Common and elven, as well as a symbol of the Eye of the Arcane.

"This is it." Jace raised his hand. "Should we use some kind of spell or cantrip to knock?" Vereesa sighed helplessly and watched Jace obediently rap on the door several times.

"Amy?"

"I'm home. Who is it?"

A familiar voice came from inside, and it immediately brought back memories of his days doing chores for old Marin — it had been just like this, a wooden door with a garden outside too.

He glanced sideways at the elf beside him. Vereesa was looking at him too. The difference was, this time he wasn't coming alone.

"Jace Sesso and Vereesa Windrunner are here to visit, Amy." Jace called out. "Jace?"

The door was flung open with a bang. Amy still looked the same, but she seemed a bit more worn out than before. She stared at Jace, clearly sizing up her height, her eyes full of surprise.

"Why are you here? You couldn't even write ahead... and Lady Vereesa Windrunner..."

"We're taking a trip to the royal city," Jace said. "Passing through Dalaran, so we thought we'd come see you, Amy."

"The two of you?" Amy's eyes went even wider. "You two are..."

"That's right," Jace said. "We're together now."

"My." Amy pressed a hand to her forehead. "Hold on, I..."

"Not going to let us in, Amy?" Vereesa asked with a smile. "Ah, right." Amy hurriedly stepped aside. "Please, come in."

Walking into Amy's room, Jace immediately got the sense that despite the frequent disagreements and the seemingly distant relationship between father and daughter, the style of how they arranged their rooms and the decorations they favored were practically identical — especially when two cups of goldthorn tea were set before them.

"When did the two of you make it official?" Amy asked, sitting to the side.

"Less than a month," Vereesa said.

"But people have been saying for ages that the two of you would end up together," the mage said, cradling her steaming teacup. "I heard rumors about it back at Nethergarde Keep, and it seems that over in Quel'Thalas..."

"That doesn't matter anymore," Jace said. Vereesa beside him nodded. "What about you?" Jace asked.

Amy sighed. "I've got no mind for that right now, Jace. I didn't come to the Kirin Tor to find a boyfriend. What's more, it wasn't until I came here that I realized my knowledge and skills in arcane and frost magic are downright childish. I'm not young anymore. If I don't push myself hard, I'll soon become just another mediocre mage in a crowd of them. I don't want that."

Looking at the massive stacks of densely written scrolls piled on the desk, the bookshelves, and even the floor beside them, along with the spent, depleted magic crystal shards, Jace could tell Amy wasn't just saying empty words.

"I mean, even when you're busy, write your old man a letter, Amy," Jace raised an eyebrow. "The master's been complaining to me that you haven't been in touch for ages."

"Damn it, I really did forget." Amy rubbed her forehead, then suddenly said, "Sorry — I didn't mean to swear, Lady Vereesa Windrunner."

"Relax, Amy. I'm far cruder than you could ever imagine. Jace knows that perfectly well," Vereesa said.

"I'd never have guessed," Amy said with an awkward smile. "The Kirin Tor really is overwhelming. So, what about the two of you? I haven't seen Jace since Nethergarde Keep — what have you been up to this past year?"

Jace and Vereesa exchanged a glance, momentarily unsure where to begin. It seemed Amy still hadn't heard about Grim Batol — she really had thrown herself entirely into her studies.

"All in all, we earned enough to buy a house," Jace said.

"Stormwind?!" Amy said, startled. "You bought a house in Stormwind?"

"The Leskoval house, by the canal," Jace said, unable to hide a hint of pride. "I'm going back to Lordaeron this time to discuss moving with my parents... So, Amy — I kept my word, didn't I?"

"You really are something..." Amy looked at Vereesa and narrowed her eyes. "You didn't have Lady Vereesa Windrunner subsidize you a little, did you?"

"I would have liked to, but the Windrunner family's assets are completely out of my reach unless my sister speaks up," Vereesa said.

"Your sister," Amy said. "That would be General Sylvanas of Silvermoon?"

"You can imagine how hard it is to get anything out of her," Vereesa said. "But to say I gave no support at all wouldn't be quite accurate."

"Support in action," Jace said. "We went to Kalimdor together..."

Jace and Vereesa gave her a rough account of the Kalimdor adventure, and Amy talked about her studies at the Kirin Tor — including those powerful senior mages. She even mentioned Kel'Thuzad.

"Do you attend his classes often?" Jace asked. "I mean, Archmage Kel'Thuzad."

- **Amy Marin** (艾米·马林): Apprentice mage, daughter of Master Marin. From the glossary.

- **Kel'Thuzad** (克尔苏加德): Archmage of the Kirin Tor. Canonical.
