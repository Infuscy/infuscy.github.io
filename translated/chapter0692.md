## Chapter 692: An Incantation Best Not Learned

"Of course," Amy said. "Archmage Kel'Thuzad isn't just an Orcish-language professor, Jace — he's a remarkably skilled frost magic master. He frequently holds large lectures or delivers speeches on frost magic. But it's been a while since I've heard news of him giving a lecture or a speech."

"He's not in Dalaran anymore?" Jace asked.

"He should still be here," Amy said. "I heard just the other day that someone went to consult him on a question, but I don't know the specifics. After all, he's not exactly my teacher."

Jace glanced again at Amy's crowded shelves of incantation books, the various casting materials and short wands, and her visibly fatigued appearance. He felt that asking her to help track a Quel'Thalas magister was really pushing it.

To say nothing of the fact that all of this was built on his own wild speculation.

He looked at Vereesa. She'd probably read his thoughts, and likely felt the same way.

After everything they'd been through together, Jace could guess what Vereesa was trying to convey from a single glance.

"Our ship will be setting sail soon, Amy. We're heading to the port now," Jace said, standing up. "We won't take up any more of your time," Vereesa said.

Amy shook her head. "You're not taking up anything at all. I'm really happy you came — studying alone here all the time is quite dull. Still, I imagine you didn't come find me purely to catch up, did you?"

She saw Jace hesitate and say nothing, and continued, "Now that you've found me, what is there you can't say?" Jace thought it over, then asked, "Do you know what sympathy is?"

"Of course," Amy said. "When a person touches an object, they leave a part of their magical aura upon it. Each person's aura is different. The strength of the aura is related to the number of times and the degree of mental concentration during use. Relying on this principle, one can find out a great many things... things we're not supposed to know."

The moment this subject came up, she seemed to become a different person — brimming with confidence.

"I understand this much, but I haven't been granted the qualification to learn this incantation, Jace. Since you didn't raise it with me directly, you must know that using sympathy to trace another person's trail without permission is illegal. So learning the relevant tracking incantations is strictly restricted in Dalaran. It's not that I don't want to teach you — I'm simply unable."

"You're not teaching him, Amy. He's an idiot — he couldn't learn it even if you did," Vereesa said.

Jace glanced at her unhappily. The elf stared back at him with an expression that said "isn't that right?" Jace helplessly looked away again.

"Actually, I'm the one teaching you," Vereesa said.

"Teach me?" Amy was taken aback, then seemed to catch on. "That's right — you're a senior member of the Farstriders, and a Windrunner. You ought to have mastered this incantation already. So you want me to help trace someone's trail."

"That's right," Jace nodded.

Vereesa said, "I'll write down the relevant incantations and the essentials of casting them on a scroll, Amy."

Amy immediately rummaged through a big pile of scrolls in a flurry, pulled out a blank one, and set it on the desk, then produced a quill and ink. The scroll was of good material — probably expensive — enough to show that Amy was quite eager to learn this incantation too.

Vereesa didn't take the pen. Instead she picked up the scroll, placed her other hand on top of it, closed her eyes, and silently recited an incantation in unintelligible Thalassian. Soon, complex script glowing with arcane light began to appear on the scroll of its own accord.

Not only was she a Farstrider — she was also an enchanter?

In the span of just ten-odd minutes, dense paragraphs of text had been arcane-inscribed onto the scroll. Vereesa let out a breath and set the scroll down. Amy sighed and said, "I don't understand, Vereesa — why don't you just use this incantation yourself to trace the person you're looking for? You're a Quel'dorei, and your enchanting skill..."

Vereesa said, "Now that you're already a member of the Kirin Tor, Amy, you should know that while enchanting and spellcasting may look similar, they're almost two entirely different fields of study. Using sympathy, I definitely couldn't match you. You'd better keep this scroll hidden — if someone finds it, both of us will have to answer to investigations from both sides."

"I'll remember," Amy said with a smile. "Thank you both. But the casting method for this incantation isn't that simple — I might not get the hang of it for a while. Give me some time to practice, Jace. When will the two of you be back in Dalaran?"

"About a week," Jace said. "I'll do my best," Amy said.

"Will it interfere with your normal studies?" Vereesa asked.

"A proper balance of work and rest is more conducive to progress, isn't it?" Amy said. "Does this count as rest?" Jace asked.

"It does to me," Amy said, glancing at the scroll. "This really is the best Winter Veil gift I've ever received, you two."

"Then — a happy Winter Veil to you in advance, Amy," Jace said.

"And to you. Happy Winter Veil," Amy said with a smile.

Coming out of the college dormitory grounds and back onto the street, Vereesa glanced at the mages hurrying to and fro, then said, "Don't forget — if you want Amy to trace Dar'Khan, you'll need at least one object he's touched, Jace."

"Can you help with that?" Jace asked. "Like getting him to cast a small spell, or enchant something."

"Quite risky," Vereesa said. "If it's too deliberate, he might get suspicious. Mages are very careful about that sort of thing — especially when there's no suitable reason."

Jace frowned. "Can Dar'Khan enter the Sunwell?"

"Of course," Vereesa said. "He's a senior member of the Magisters' Platform, even angling for the position of Grand Magister... though the chances are slim, he does have the right to enter the Sunwell's inner sanctum."

"So," Jace asked, "how many Quel'dorei can enter both the Sunwell and the Order of Tirisfal's archives?"

Vereesa said, "Magisters rarely come to Dalaran... Wait, you mean to trace the Sunwell?!"

"Is it feasible?" Jace asked.

Vereesa thought it over. "Now that you mention it, it might actually work. I never even thought of that."

"Let Amy give it a try when the time comes," Jace said. "We might need a vial of Sunwell water to serve as..."

"I'll figure it out. When we come back, I'll try to borrow a vial," Vereesa said. "Actually, I'm a bit surprised. I'd have thought a daughter raised by a great mage like Marin would be quite by-the-book."

"Marin himself is anything but by-the-book," Jace said. "He once turned me into a sheep on the spot just to teach me the Counterspell incantation — probably wanted to give me some kind of crisis feeling to stimulate my learning potential... although in the end I didn't learn it very well either."

Vereesa burst out laughing.

Jace asked in a low voice, "If I were cursed by Archmage Kel'Thuzad with an unbreakable hex and turned into a sheep forever, would you still date me, Lady Vereesa Windrunner?"

"Of course," Vereesa said, looking toward a distant street corner. "As long as that sheep still remembered its name was Jace Sesso."

## Flags

- **Sympathy** (交感): The magical principle of tracing residual aura left on objects. Jace wants Amy to use it to track Dar'Khan. Canonical-adjacent concept (sympathetic magic).
- **Quel'dorei** (奎尔多雷): "High elves" in Thalassian. Canonical.
- **Thalassian** (萨拉斯语): The elven language. From the glossary.
- **Sunwell** (太阳之井): The source of the high elves' power. Canonical. Vereesa suggests tracing the Sunwell's aura as a way to track Dar'Khan.
- **Magisters' Platform** (魔导师平台): The seat of Quel'Thalas's magister leadership in Silvermoon. Canonical (also a raid dungeon).
- **Grand Magister** (大魔导师): The highest rank among Quel'Thalas's magisters. Canonical.
- **Winter Veil** (冬幕节): The WoW winter festival. Established in earlier chapters.
- **Counterspell** (法术反制): The mage interrupt spell. From the glossary.
- **Polymorph** (变形术): The spell Master Marin used to turn Jace into a sheep. From the glossary.
