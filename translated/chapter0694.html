Chapter 694: A Mother's Wish

A sudden sheet of flame engulfed his entire body, filling the room with blistering heat.

When the fire and black smoke cleared, a tall orc — its head nearly hitting the ceiling — stood before the several of them. Vereesa sighed and covered her eyes.

The orc thumped its chest and said to the guard captain before it, "Well now, Vik Sesso — for all your loyal vigilance guarding the walls of Tirisfal, you still couldn't stop an orc from entering this city..."

Vik laughed. "How's the Orcish?"

"Golkosh!" the orc growled.

The smile on Vik's face vanished in an instant. His eyes went wide with shock. Jace said. "Believe me now?"

Vik nodded. "Now I truly believe you, Jace."

Jace chanted the incantation again and returned to his original form. He took a sip of wine, set down his glass, and said, "That's how I got into Grim Batol. Every last one of those orcs was completely taken in by me, just like you were."

"If I put on that cloak and chant the spell, would I turn into a female orc?" Hennie asked curiously. "Er... you'd better not..." Jace hesitated. Vik looked over in surprise too.

Hennie immediately laughed. "I'm only joking..."

The family chatted lightly about what had happened inside Grim Batol — of course, without saying much about the black dragons, especially everything that had happened before entering Grim Batol.

Come evening, Vereesa went to the Farstrider station in Lordaeron to report in. Jace was alone in the bedroom writing his death knight report when his mother knocked on the door.

"Jace?"

"Come in, Mom." Jace set down his pen and turned around. Hennie stood in the doorway, looking over at him. "The Grim Batol business... it wasn't as simple as you two made it sound, was it?"

"Of course it wasn't that simple," Jace said with a smile, raising a hand. "Otherwise the king wouldn't have given me this ring."

Hennie sat down to the side. "The king of Stormwind also knows this was no simple matter. Vik and I can both see it — you must have been through a great deal of hardship, Jace."

Jace was taken aback. His nose suddenly stung.

His mother pulled him into her arms. Jace stared hard at the candle before him, watching its light grow increasingly blurred.

He forced himself not to show any expression, not to make a single sound — wanted to be more of a man, more like a hero who'd saved the whole Alliance. But he couldn't do it.

"Mom... I missed you so much."

"I missed you too," Hennie said softly. "You've grown up. Your mother is prouder of you with each passing day."

"But selfishly, I'd much rather you lived a good, happy life, instead of bearing all these terrible things alone, Jace."

"As long as you and Vik are both well, nothing is frightening," Jace said quietly.

Even Deathwing himself was no exception.

"We will be, Jace," Hennie said. "We're truly delighted you're back this time. Vik and I both like Vereesa very much. She's beautiful, and I can tell she's nothing like those haughty elves — kind and gentle, and yet a formidable warrior. Your father especially likes that about her. I've long since heard of the reputations of the three sisters in her family, Jace. Treasure her well..."

Jace nodded.

Lying in his mother's embrace, Jace felt as though he'd become that lost child once more.

He no longer wanted to think about complicated things, no longer wanted to consider too much. He just wanted to sleep well, and that was all...

Tirisfal's winter was bleak and merciless. The cold wind swept across the pine groves and came to the cemetery in Brill. Vereesa wrapped her dress a little tighter. Even this Quel'dorei, who had been nurtured by the Sunwell since childhood, found the chill a bit hard to bear.

Jace draped that cloak over her and pulled her close. The ranger slowly leaned against his shoulder.

"I thought you were really going to die back then, Jace," Vereesa said, looking at the statue bearing a longsword on the headstone. "I even felt that I should have died under that pit lord's blade too, rather than holding you there, unable to do anything."

"If either of you had died, then my death would have been worth nothing, Wen," Jace said. Vereesa said nothing. The two of them stared at the name "Jace Sesso" carved on the headstone in silence for a long while.

Just then, a Brill local bundled in a thick padded jacket passed behind the two of them, heading toward the other side of the cemetery. The footsteps broke the quiet of the moment.

"Tell me — what's the reason for King Terenas II's illness?" Vereesa asked. "Surely it's not really just on account of the winter?"

Jace said, "Archmage Antonidas told me that Deathwing had long been operating in the Lordaeron court under the identity of Lord Daval Prestor. He sought to claim the inheritance of the leaderless Kingdom of Alterac, but apart from the King of Lordaeron, essentially every other ruler in the Alliance opposed his claim to the inheritance."

"The royal house of Alterac — the Perenolde — isn't entirely without heirs, and King Terenas II had previously been supporting another descendant of their royal house. For him to suddenly start backing Prestor... I think Deathwing must have cast some dark magic to control his mind, just as he did to me. Deathwing's death caused the magic to fail suddenly. King Terenas II had been deeply affected by shadow — for an old man his age, being unable to bear the shock would be normal."

"Seen that way, the black dragons thought you'd stand on their side too, just like the King of Lordaeron or Kel'Thuzad," Vereesa said.

"And the leader of the Horde, Ner'zhul," Jace said. "He and Teron Gorefiend were responsible for protecting and transporting the black dragon eggs across the Dark Portal, and providing a portion of Draenor's land as the black dragons' rookery, in exchange for the black dragons' help in holding back the Alliance Expedition and assisting in seizing those three holy artifacts. In short — no one could refuse the black dragons' terms."

"Only you," Vereesa said.

"I was just lucky to have a chance to fight back against him," Jace said. "By the way — has Quel'Thalas had any news about Northrend lately?"

"Northrend?" Vereesa asked. "What about it?"

Jace said, "The black dragons have their own rookery in Northrend. I want to see whether those black dragons will cause some sort of turmoil because of their father's death... Do you have any mages stationed in Northrend to keep watch?"

Vereesa said, "We do, but I honestly don't know the details. When we get to Quel'Thalas, we can go ask together."

Just then, a figure in white entered the cemetery. Jace recognized him as a messenger, or more likely a herald, from the royal city, with a Brill guard beside him.

"Sir. Madam." The herald came before them. "You are Archmage Jace Sesso and Lady Vereesa Windrunner?"

"That's right, it's us," Jace said.

The herald bowed. "His Majesty King Terenas II sends his greetings to you both. His Majesty has already received word of the victory at Grim Batol, and sincerely hopes that the heroes of the Battle of Grim Batol might come to the royal palace. Will the two of you accept His Majesty's invitation?"
