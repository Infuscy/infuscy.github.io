## Chapter 699: Another Visit to the Library

"That's wonderful," Vereesa said, lightly tapping her forehead. "I can't believe I overlooked that. Then again, I suppose only Kirin Tor mages who study demons or dark magic would hold clearance for that section after applying for it. Do you have an interest in that area too, Amy?"

Amy explained, "After I came back from Nethergarde Keep, I wanted to help Jace restore his reputation. So I wrote quite a bit, drawing on my experience fighting demons there, and used those records to apply for clearance to that section. I don't need it now, of course — but even then I knew in my heart that Jace didn't need my help. He'd prove himself again on his own, sure as anything."

"Thank you for still looking out for me, Amy," Jace said.

"Your saying that is thanks enough," Amy smiled. "And looking at it now, my efforts back then weren't wasted either, were they? That said, to perform magical tracking you need a magical item connected to the target. May I know that person's name?"

"It's Dar'Khan Drathir," Vereesa said.

Amy said, "I've heard his name. If I'm not mistaken, he's a high magister. Are you two planning to…"

"Tracking a magister that dangerous is no simple matter," Vereesa explained. "Which is why Jace means to try tracking the trace of the Sunwell instead."

"So that's the idea. I understand," Amy said.

"Amy, can you go to the Great Library tonight?" Jace asked.

"I can," Amy said.

"Then I'll head to the embassy district right now and borrow a vial of Sunwell water," Vereesa said.

"A vial of Sunwell water?!" Amy said in surprise. "I thought you meant to have me rely on the energy inside your body…"

Vereesa shook her head. "I may have grown up in Quel'Thalas, but as a Farstrider, the Sunwell's aura within me can't hold a candle to a magister's. If I walked into that library full of Quel'dorei with only my own aura to track by, it would be harder by I don't know how many times. Dar'Khan is no ordinary magister — he holds a high enough station to have had access to the Sunwell Highlands. The energy signature he carries is more distinctive as well. Right now I'll head back to the embassy district and ask around. You two wait for me at the entrance to the Great Library."

"You're sure you can borrow one that easily?" Jace asked.

"Any magister residing long-term in Dalaran should have little trouble keeping a vial on hand."

"Then we're counting on you, Wen," Jace said.

As evening fell and the sky slowly darkened, the Great Library of Dalaran — fortunately — stayed open even in the dead of night, and the number of elves coming and going after dark was noticeably greater than during the day.

Jace couldn't help wondering what on earth Dar'Khan came here to read. He thought of many possibilities, the most likely being something related to necromancy.

When he had casually browsed the shelves here before, Jace had noticed that the Order of Tirisfal had clearly taken a keen interest in necrotic power as well. No small number of demons had wielded necromantic magic on Azeroth — it had even taken root and developed right here in Dalaran.

Thinking along these lines, he felt he might actually be able to trace Kel'Thuzad through Dar'Khan.

Before the sky had fully darkened, Vereesa came hurrying over. She pulled the two of them into an alcove off to the side where no one was about, produced an ordinary-looking water flask from inside her robe, and broke the seal.

The half-flask of water within sloshed, and the sheer volume of energy it gave off made Jace's head swim.

He recalled the time in Dalaran when he had first taken the vial of Sunwell water Vereesa handed him and simply drunk it down — he hadn't felt anything unusual about it at all. He didn't know whether it was because his battle with Teron Gorefiend had nearly stripped him of all perception, or because his sensitivity to the arcane had grown by leaps and bounds since then.

"Here, Amy," Vereesa said.

Amy rubbed her fingers and took the flask. Jace could tell that even on this cold street, she was nearly breaking into a sweat. "This thing is weighing on me so hard I can barely breathe," the mage said.

"It's the same for you and me both, but you need to memorize the signature of its power here, Amy," Vereesa said. "Using the method I wrote down on that scroll. I can't let you take it into the library and compare it openly in front of everyone… it's far too conspicuous in a room full of mages. It would draw attention we don't want."

"I understand," Amy said. She held the vial of Sunwell water up before her face, slowly closed her eyes, and moved her lips in silent incantation. After a while, she exhaled sharply. "Seal it back up quickly, Vereesa."

The ranger immediately took the flask and resealed it. All three of them breathed a sigh of relief.

Vereesa looked around to be certain no one else had been drawn over, then tucked the flask into her bag. "This thing's quite precious. I only just managed to borrow it — I need to return it right away. Amy, now it's your show."

Entering the Great Library, Jace had been escorted by a guard the last time he came. Returning after a week, he no longer recognized the way — but Amy was completely at home.

She walked at a brisk pace with Jace in tow, leading him along until the guards stationed at this section inspected the two of them's rings and let them through.

What Amy produced was a golden ring — quite heavy and beautiful, covered in runes, with a violet-gemstone set in the middle that brimmed with radiant arcane energy.

She didn't seem to wear this ring on her finger ordinarily. She only brought it out when she needed to present it.

Amy glanced at the silver Kirin Tor trust ring on Jace's hand, and once they were in the corridor she asked, "Has Archmaster Antonidas shown any intention of bringing you into the Kirin Tor?"

"What?" Jace asked.

"This ring is given to those who are trusted by the Kirin Tor but are not yet members of it," Amy said.

"It may be that I truly lack any talent for the arcane," Jace said, touching his ring. "I'm a misfit here, Amy. I'd think you of all people would know that."

"Jace… that was only a simple test. And even setting that aside, not everyone in the Kirin Tor possesses so-called talent either — I only came to realize that after coming here," Amy said, stopping beside several towering bookshelves. "I truly cannot understand why a man who stood against Deathwing with his own magic should have any reason to be excluded from the Kirin Tor. Perhaps Archmaster Antonidas has his own considerations."

"But this is no place for that sort of talk. Keep an eye on our surroundings for me, Jace. I'd rather not be seen by outsiders while I'm concentrating on tracing magical residues…"
