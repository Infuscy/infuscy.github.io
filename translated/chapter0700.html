Chapter 700: The Magister's Objective

Amy secretly worked her incantation inside, searching for the Sunwell's trace, while Jace kept watch at the junction between several bookshelf rows. Just as Vereesa had predicted, few magisters or ordinary arcanists from Quel'Thalas came to this section to consult works on dark magic. After waiting there for ages, Jace only saw an elderly human mage slowly pass by a distant bookshelf. Not a single elf appeared. Dar'Khan was an outlier — and that greatly improved their chances of finding a lead. Some time passed, and the sky grew ever darker, when Amy hurried over. "I found it, Jace — the Sunwell's magical trace. Quite strong." The moment Jace heard that, his whole body tingled. It felt as though he had somehow leapt over God-only-knew-how-many steps and seized Kel'Thuzad by the tail. "Where?" "Follow me." Jace followed Amy across bookshelf after bookshelf, gradually arriving at a place that felt more and more familiar. Wasn't this where he had come last time? The language section. Demon-language research. It was getting more and more familiar. "Right here," Amy said, scanning the shelves before her. She pulled down two rather thin books and leafed through them. "These books bear the strongest trace. The elf magister carrying the Sunwell's power must have concentrated on reading them for quite some time — and not long ago at all." How could that be? Jace stared at the book in Amy's hand. Wasn't that the very volume on the linguistic traits of the Ered'ruin demons he had pored over himself last time? The Order of Tirisfal's records on this demon language were scarce as it was. The books on this shelf were already among the few in this entire section dealing with the language of the doomguards. There was no mistaking it. Could it be that Amy had tracked him instead? Could it be that because he had drunk the power of the Sunwell vial, tracing the Sunwell had led straight back to his own front door? "Are you certain, Amy?" Jace said. "Are you sure you tracked a Quel'dorei magister and not… me?" "You? Why would you think that?" Amy said. "I'm familiar with your magical aura, Jace. Ah — you mean that you drank Sunwell water. No, absolutely not. I'm not so hopeless that I can't tell a human from an elf. This is most definitely not a magical trace you left behind." So was it some manner of coincidence? Let's suppose Dar'Khan wanted to look into the Ered'ruin language. What was he planning? Did he want to summon a doomguard too? No — summoning a doomguard didn't require understanding their language. You only needed to speak the incantation in demonic. For all he knew, the low-tier doomguards of today probably couldn't even speak their own ancient tongue clearly anymore. "Had you come to this spot before and read this book?" Amy asked. "I did," Jace nodded. "Then…" Hearing that, Amy looked doubtful too. She thought for a moment. "What did you come here for?" "To see if the Order of Tirisfal had any way to translate a particular demon language, naturally. But in truth there wasn't much…" Halfway through speaking, Jace froze. Could Dar'Khan have come here for the exact same reason? Could he have gotten hold of that scroll too? That magical scroll recovered from Dath'mor, which very likely recorded the trail of Ursolus and its previous master… Its original should be in the Reliquary's possession by now. As a magister of considerable stature in Quel'Thalas, Dar'Khan shouldn't have had much difficulty using his connections to access the Reliquary and view that scroll. Let alone the fact that he was a scholar with a keen interest in dark magic, demons, and even necrotic forces… There might well be someone in the Reliquary who would curry favor by presenting all of that material to him. If he learned that the indecipherable portions of the scroll were in the Ered'ruin language, then coming to Dalaran to look up the relevant material would be perfectly reasonable. Was he searching for Ursolus? Even if he had no such intention originally, after seeing Dath'mor's scroll, he would certainly have ideas. After all, this was an ancient demonic artifact capable of reaping souls and brimming with unimaginably vast dark power. Once it was in his hands, let alone a senior magister… Dar'Khan probably wouldn't even care about the position of senior magister of Quel'Thalas by then. Still, Jace knew he couldn't jump to absolute conclusions about exactly how much Dar'Khan knew. "How about it, Jace?" Amy asked. "Did you think of anything?" "A whole mess of things," Jace said. "Thank you, Amy — truly. The lead you helped me find is important. Are you certain there are no other traces of comparable strength anywhere else in this area?" Amy said, "There are some in the Tirisfal summoning records over there, but they're nowhere near as distinct and strong as here. Then again, I've only just learned this incantation — I can't say with absolute certainty that they're truly connected to a magister." "Let's go take a look together." Coming to this section of shelves, Jace examined a few of the books Amy pointed out, flipping back and forth, but couldn't see anything out of place. If these traces had also been left by Dar'Khan, it might suggest he really did intend to summon a doomguard to help him decipher the Ered'ruin language. Still, if they wanted to know more clearly and pin down Dar'Khan's intentions, they would have to go to Quel'Thalas and make inquiries. Relying on the fact that he and Vereesa had once done work for the Reliquary, plus Vereesa's current standing as a Ranger-Lord and her connection as the sister of the Silvermoon Ranger-General, someone inside should be willing to reveal a little information about who had consulted those files. Let's hope so. Even in the dead of night, the Great Library still held no small number of people. Leaving the library and coming outside, Vereesa was waiting for them. The moment she saw them emerge, she walked over, unable to contain herself. "Well? Did any of you find anything?" "We found it," Amy nodded. "The Sunwell trace was distinct enough, all right." "Amy, you truly are a gifted mage," Vereesa sighed with admiration. "Just now I tried sensing the Sunwell's energy outside on my own, and it felt as though all of Dalaran was packed full of the arcane — I couldn't tell one trace from another at all." "Being praised for arcane talent by a Quel'dorei is no small thing," Amy said with a smile. "In any case, I'm just glad I could be of help. So you two are heading to Quel'Thalas next, aren't you?" "Yes," Vereesa said. "I want to take Jace to meet my sister. They haven't been formally introduced yet." "Then I wish you both the best of luck — especially you, Jace," Amy said. "Now that you can come and go from Dalaran freely, remember to come visit me often. You're welcome here anytime. If you ever need help with anything else, come find me." "I will," Jace said. After seeing Amy back to her quarters, they confirmed that this attempt at magical tracking hadn't drawn any unwanted attention. Jace and Vereesa came to the outside of the garden. Jace said, "Dar'Khan may truly have nothing to do with Kel'Thuzad. At the very least, the leads found in the library aren't enough to prove that." Vereesa asked, "So this lead is a dead end too?" Jace said, "But I noticed something else. Dar'Khan may be searching for Ursolus — that demonic weapon. Remember? We need to get to the bottom of this when we go to Quel'Thalas."
