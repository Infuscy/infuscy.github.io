Chapter 727: Demonic Profanity

Kazzrez raised both hands high, and a mass of Shadow energy rapidly gathered into his palms. Jace knew this spell well — the demon wanted to wipe out everyone here in a single barrage of Shadow Bolts.

The other warlocks saw the demon casting and immediately guessed what it was. They rushed to hide behind the stone pillars and statues around the altar. Jace and the other two summoners were too close to the demon — there was nowhere to hide.

Trying to stop a demon like this with a felhunter or a Counterspell was unrealistic. Still, he glanced at the sphere of darkness the doomlord had conjured and gauged just how much energy that mass of shadow contained.

If the whole thing landed directly on him, he probably wouldn't be able to take it. But scattered across the area in the form of a Shadow Bolt barrage, Jace figured he could weather it.

So he swiftly raised a dark shield, then swiftly drank a mouthful of Shadow Protection Potion.

As expected, the Shadow sphere in the demon's hands unleashed a massive volley of Shadow Bolts, wave after wave striking the ground, the stone slabs, the distant hillside, the stone walls…

Mosur also drank a Shadow Protection Potion, trying to dodge the scattering Shadow Bolts while sprinting toward the edge of the site — but he was eventually struck by one, crashing to the ground in a fit of violent coughing.

Eli chanted a Shadow protection ward and barely held on. Regis'var, who had been maintaining the binding circle, had already fainted from the effort. By now the Shadow Bolts that hit him had blown him into a blackened, charred corpse. The Bronzebeard dwarf at the edge behind a statue was blasted by a Shadow Bolt, lost his footing, and fell into the lava beneath the altar.

Jace blocked several waves of Shadow Bolts with his dark shield, and in the process even managed to throw up a Mage Armor warded with warding against evil. When the Shadow Bolt barrage finally ended, the dark shield had been shattered — but the effect of the Shadow Protection Potion was still active!

The doomlord was clearly enraged by the mortal who stood right in front of him, shrugging off his magic without even trying to dodge. He immediately vomited a mass of fel fire straight at Jace. Jace raised his dark shield again. The fel fire broke through the shield and blasted fully against Jace's skin. Jace's skin grew redder, brighter — some places were burned — but this was definitely not the damage the doomlord had been hoping for…

"Who's more immune to fire, demon?" Jace said in a low voice.

The demon stepped forward furiously and brought his massive hoof stomping down. Jace couldn't possibly tank that — but the creature's movements had become considerably sluggish, and he dodged it easily.

Kazzrez's enormous wing swept sideways in a wide arc, covering a huge range. This time no matter how agile Jace was, there was absolutely no dodging it — but Jace Shadowstepped again, appearing behind a distant demon and slipping past the sweep.

The demon turned and locked his eyes on Jace in the distance. He clearly realized this warlock was completely off compared to the others. He immediately conjured a powerful Shadow Bolt, preparing to single out and annihilate this maggot who had deflected his attacks one after another.

But while the demon lord was busy chasing Jace, the warlocks who had been hiding behind the stone pillars rushed out again, chanting curses to keep pressing the demon. Kazzrez's Shadow Bolt had only been halfway conjured before the magic of the remaining ten warlocks staggered him a step and interrupted his casting.

Seizing the opportunity, Jace chanted the Fireball incantation — but to his surprise, the demon actually reached out and gathered the Shadow energy scattered across the altar once more, lowering his head and muttering something.

The second Fireball blasted toward the demon lord's chest from afar. The demon threw up his arm and blocked the Fireball. The metal vambrace on the arm he'd raised was blown apart with a bang, falling to the ground with a tremendous clanging.

But at the same time, Kazzrez's incantation had completed. Two small portals tore open behind him, and felhounds lunged out, snapping and clawing, throwing themselves at the nearest warlocks!

The warlocks' minions reacted quickly, turning around one after another to shield their masters from these suddenly appearing lesser demons — but a warlock was still caught by the fastest felhound before he could turn. Tobias, who had been maintaining the binding circle, got his spell Counterspelled by a felhound before he could turn around, then had his throat seized and torn open.

Luther Peaceman was surrounded by several felhounds and only avoided being killed immediately thanks to his succubus's protection and the blade in his hand to fight back. The most troublesome part was that the dead warlocks' demon minions, having lost their masters, immediately defected to the doomlord right in front of them. The scene on the Storm Altar — twenty-plus demons and warlocks ganging up on a single demon lord — had nearly become an even fight in terms of numbers!

Morfiel and Jace, one wielding a short blade and the other the Song of the Blue Sky, protected each other. After Jace drank a bottle of Demonslaying Potion, channeling the magic sword's power to cut through the felhounds rushing at them was easier than slicing tofu. Head, legs, body — anything the blue light touched was cleaved clean through, flesh, bone and skin in one stroke. Morfiel's flame-engulfed dagger stabbed into the felhounds and the demons would erupt entirely in flames, fleeing in agony.

For a moment the two of them carved out a gap in the middle, but the portals kept releasing more felhounds. The warlocks were being suppressed by the felhounds and couldn't even cast spells to attack those two gates!

The demon lord propped the two portals open and dropped to one knee in the center of the altar, too weak to straighten up — but still mustering his last strength to laugh.

Jace suddenly realized that by this point in the fight, the demon lord's will had been weakened to an unbearable degree. Of course, to an ordinary mortal warlock, his will was still a towering peak impossible to scale.

But Jace was no ordinary warlock.

"Protect me, Morfiel!"

"From the very beginning, master," Morfiel replied.

Jace ignored the felhounds around him and the succubi attacking after losing their masters. As long as Morfiel's darting shadow was there, he didn't need to worry about all those messy lesser things.

He only needed to grip the chain of will around his neck and keep his eyes on the demon before him.

"Veta, Tahotoschi, Miloen, Tahototani! Hear my command, demon!"

The doomlord coughed violently, raised his eyes, and locked onto the Shadow sigil that had suddenly appeared between his brows. Instinctively he raised his thick hands to his forehead, trying to claw at the sigil — but it was completely useless.

This giant demon's power and will had been weakened to the extreme by the crowd of warlocks, making it difficult for him to resist Jace's enslavement. And that was without even counting the boost from the chain of will around Jace's neck!

When that Shadow sigil sank into the demon's skull, the demon couldn't hold himself up and dropped to one knee, roaring: "You think you can enslave me forever, mortal! I will remember your name forever, and the Legion will remember your name forever — Jace Sesso! The Burning Legion will hunt your soul for a thousand years, ten thousand years!"

Jace ignored his threat and barked: "Close the portals! Now! That's an order!"

"What a stupid command! You control my will just to make me close these gates? Just to make me stop the killing? My power could easily crush every idiot around you!"

The demon lord roared furiously — but still waved his hand and closed the two portals.

Losing their endless reinforcements, the remaining felhounds were killed by the warlocks and their own demon minions. The traitorous demons were dealt with as well. In the center of the Storm Altar, only six or seven warlocks could still stand.

Covered in wounds and blood, Mosur sat on the ground, staring blankly at Jace. "You actually enslaved… Lord Kazzrez?"

Jace didn't have the energy to respond. He glanced at the other areas and immediately pulled his attention back to the demon lord.

Kazzrez coughed and said slowly: "Your willpower is astonishingly strong. But I will eventually recover my strength, and you can't stay like this forever. Think about what disaster I'll bring you when that time comes… enjoy this brief satisfaction while it lasts."

The demon laughed. Truth be told, Jace didn't know whether he could keep enslaving this demon lord after the Refined Wisdom flask's effects wore off.

So while he still had the time, he needed to issue the most important order.

Jace said in Demonic: "It really is dangerous. So second order — confess your true name to me, right now!"

"True name?!"

The demon heard the order and furiously slammed his heavy pauldron. His lips trembled, wanting to hurl insults but unable to get them out — and yet unable to stop himself from wanting to shout something…

Just then, Jace suddenly heard a thunderous, nearly unbearable roar in his mind: "K'ALY'AK'ROTH MOLOTH!"

The demon transmitted his spell-like true name into Jace's brain. This really was a powerful demon — just remembering this name, Jace felt like he was about to burn through his energy all over again.

He took a deep, ragged breath and steadied himself from the chaotic force. The demon muttered again: "Now that's a proper command, Jace Sesso. Unfortunately, so noble a name will only fester inside your rotting meat of a body for a few years, a few decades… such a fleeting moment."

Morfiel said in Demonic: "The unfortunate thing is your new master really does live longer than other mortals. You can wait and see."

Kazzrez seemed to tell the succaudus wasn't bluffing. He looked at Jace and said: "Don't get too cocky. My loyal servant Dath'mora will come to kill you, my… master."

When he said "master," he gave a contemptuous laugh, then continued: "She might not be good at much else, but she's absolutely brilliant at fooling the eyes of weaklings like you. You'd better wait for her, you clever little bastard."

Jace pulled the Soul Shard of Dath'mora from the demon pack and asked: "Is she also good at disguising herself as this?" Seeing the doomguard's Soul Shard, the warlocks present were too shocked by the scene to even know what to say.

Dath'mora's Soul Shard. A human who'd enslaved a doomlord… either reality was something they couldn't immediately accept.

The demon stared at the Soul Shard emitting a faint blue-purple glow, went rigid for a moment, then erupted into a screaming string of Demonic profanity. Morfiel couldn't help but laugh. Jace glanced at her, feeling a little grateful he hadn't gone and studied that scroll full of Demonic swear words first.
