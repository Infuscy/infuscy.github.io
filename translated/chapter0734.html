Chapter 734: The Assault

The forest east of Stonewatch connected to the vast gnoll forests further east. Jace still remembered traveling through here with Wynethra to the Badlands — he'd even been able to catch a corner of Ilgara Tower from the ridgeline of the Redridge Mountains.

February had arrived. There was still snow between the trees, but new buds had sprouted on some of the higher branches.

Most of the old leaves and twigs still clung to the trees. Sunlight spilled through the forest, the withered old growth and tender green reflecting off each other in the light, giving a faintly desolate feeling.

Stonewatch's cavalry had already entered the forest from the north. Their objective was a feint against the gnoll tribe's base — a collection of caves and canyons hidden at the foot of the Redridge Mountains to the north. Following Tordman's orders, an apprentice mage would accompany them close to the gnoll encampment and set fires, drawing as much attention as possible so that the gnolls around Ilgara Tower would receive the news and rush north to reinforce in droves.

Even with reinforcements from Stormwind and Lakeshire, Stonewatch's garrison still fell short of the mage tower's defenders and the surrounding gnolls combined. Launching a head-on assault like this, even with Jace's help, would likely result in heavy casualties.

So Captain Tordman planned to use his smaller cavalry force to draw the gnolls' attention first. This simple, direct tactic worked like a charm against gnolls time and again, because the gnolls valued the safety of their tribe and the altars in their encampment, and their brains really did work in a straight line.

The soldiers following Captain Tordman and Jace numbered perhaps fifty or sixty. They all wore at least simple breastplates and helmets, carrying either long spears or longswords paired with shields. Gnolls favored crossbow bolts, and when attacking a mage tower like this, protection was an absolute must — after all, the tower housed not just warlocks but gnoll guards as well.

Jace looked over the soldiers' equipment, most of which was battered and riddled with dents. The helmets in particular were covered with dents from orcish blows, and probably weren't very comfortable to wear.

At noon, a scout came from a distance to Tordman's side and reported something. Tordman looked back at the soldiers crouching behind him and said in a low voice, "The gnolls around the mage tower have headed north to reinforce. We move now. Our cavalry can't pin them for too long or they'll be surrounded by gnolls! The mage tower still has a number of elite gnoll guards protecting it, not just warlocks. Now is our moment, lads!"

The soldiers nodded in response and followed Tordman out of the dense woods, pressing deeper into the forest.

Crossing a high slope, Ilgara Tower came into view. It sat atop another rise in the distance, with a low ground between covered in various camps and debris. There were five or six gnolls lingering there at the moment, but it was clear this camp had originally housed at least twenty or thirty gnolls. On the far side of the mage tower, there was another camp where only a single gnoll crossbow sentry could be seen on watch.

Before long, that gnoll crossman lowered his head to the side, as if detecting some sound, then turned to look up high — and suddenly clutched his neck and collapsed. The other squad must have already moved, taking out that sentry with an arrow. At this distance, Jace couldn't make out the bolt.

Tordman gestured for the soldiers to move quickly. Several bow-armed soldiers swiftly moved forward and fired at the remaining gnolls in the hollow. After dealing with the few standing outside, the infantry rapidly seized the camp.

The camp was littered with the gnawed bones of various beasts, not excluding shattered human skulls. The soldiers who saw this spat curses and clashed with the gnoll patrolmen who came rushing over, smashing up the gnolls' camps and roasting racks. The scene nearly spiraled out of control for a moment.

After all, among the soldiers stationed at Stonewatch long-term, there were likely comrades or even family members who had died at the hands of the Darkhide tribe. Tordman had to shout at all the soldiers to calm down, since the important objective right now was Ilgara Tower. As long as they seized and held the tower, the gnolls would have no choice but to withdraw.

After the two groups of soldiers dealt with the two gnoll patrols outside, they swiftly crept up the rise where Ilgara Tower stood. The gnolls atop the tower seemed to sense something was amiss. A gnoll head poked out from a window above. Now it was Jace's time to shine.

He gathered a Shadow Bolt and fired it straight into the window. The shadow corrosion from the Shadow Bolt's detonation immediately covered the window. The gnolls near the window howled in unison, and none of them dared poke their heads out to look anymore.

Right then, Jace noticed the top of the tower beginning to emit a faint shadow glow, exactly as that captain had described.

The other squad leaders noticed it too. One of them said, "Soon the gnolls will realize our main target is this mage tower, Captain Tordman. We need to move fast."

"Leave men to watch the gnoll movements outside. Those assigned to assault this mage tower, follow me!"

Tordman beckoned, leading Jace and a group of soldiers in noticeably better equipment up the steps in front of the tower door. But just as Tordman was about to lead the charge inside, a violet-black curtain of light suddenly appeared at the doorway.

"A magic seal?" Tordman cursed under his breath. "Didn't expect these gnolls to figure out something this complex. They must have learned it from the orcs. Master Sesso, can you deal with this door?"

"Let me try."

Jace approached the tower. He could feel the direction of the energy flowing through the entire structure. Right now there was a focal point of magical power at the doorway, maintaining the existence of the curtain.

It could be said that the shadow energy supplied by that voidwalker was the lifeblood powering the entire mage tower's operation at the moment.

He drew Azuresong, recited an incantation, and channeled Life Drain toward the shadow energy above the doorway. In an instant, he felt himself connect with the entire tower's magical power, and his body became like a black hole that instantly drew all the shadow energy toward him. The curtain's shadow rapidly dimmed, and the energy of the entire tower began to destabilize.

Before long, the gnolls above let out an angry roar. The curtain vanished instantly — the gnolls had voluntarily dropped the seal. Because they knew that if they let this strange warlock downstairs keep draining, even the demon powering the tower would probably be sucked dry.

After this mass of chaotic shadow was drawn into Jace's body, he couldn't digest it immediately, and for a moment it produced some rather uncomfortable reactions. He took a deep breath and gave himself a moment to recover.

"The seal is broken!" Tordman waved his hand. "Charge in, now."

"Wait, Captain," Jace said. "I can feel quite a few gnoll warlocks hiding inside. I have a more efficient and safer method that doesn't require our soldiers to charge in first."
