Chapter 735: Fighting Darkness with Darkness

"You've got a better idea?" Tordman asked.

"Though you might all need to brace yourselves a bit..."

As he spoke, Jace took a centaur soul shard from his pack, closed his eyes, and recited a summoning incantation.

Waves of dark power drifted up from his body. The surrounding trees were buffeted by a rush of air from the spatial disturbance. Jace pointed ahead, and a rift in the Twisting Nether was torn open in midair. A blood-red fel hound tumbled out, hit the ground, scrambled to its feet, and Jace immediately recited the Annihilan naming incantation.

"There. You're Dazar'm too."

Aside from "Dazar'm," Jace had already forgotten every other name on the naming chart Mosur had given him. He'd decided that every fel hound he summoned from now on would be called Dazar'm, to save time.

The soldiers surrounding them all raised their weapons in shock at this demon that had been summoned so openly, but they didn't know whether to aim at the demon or at Jace.

"This... this is... Master Sesso..." Tordman clearly didn't know what to say.

Jace said, "I don't expect you to accept this demon right away, gentlemen. But he is genuinely useful. You can think of him as a bomb that walks itself. If he charges into the enemy ranks, he'll hurt the enemy. If you get too close to him, he might hurt you too."

Tordman said, "My creed is simple, master. If you're certain he'll obey your commands completely, efficiently kill the enemy, and not harm our own men, then I can accept it."

"No problem." Jace finished speaking and said to the fel hound in Demonic, "Charge in and clear the way. Kill those warlocks, Dazar'm. Their mana is all yours."

This rather terrifyingly sized fel hound roared excitedly and plunged ferociously through the door. Jace looked at Tordman and said, "Let us follow!"

Several tall gnoll guards were hiding inside the doorway of Ilgara Tower, with gnoll crossbowmen and warlocks positioned above, watching below, ready to unleash all manner of attacks the moment an intruder poked his head through the door. But the last thing they'd expected to come plunging in was a savage demon!

A shield-bearing gnoll in the lead was directly bowled over, shield and all, by the powerful demon hound, its throat instantly torn to shreds. The crossbowmen and warlocks up high fired in panic and recited Shadow Bolt incantations. The bolts that struck the fel hound were completely useless, and Shadow Bolts that hit the fel hound were easily resisted.

Fel hordes had appeared in swarms on the Dark Portal front lines, only to be blocked and destroyed by the Alliance's armies like cannon fodder, because they'd faced the Alliance Expedition's finest — short of the forces that had entered Draenor — along with the Kirin Tor's battle mages and Ironforge's axe-wielding defenders.

When a fel hound — especially a powerful one summoned by Jace — plunged into these gnolls' formation, it was practically a starving wolf charging into a flock of sheep. And not some weak forest wolf, either, but a warg that had broken free from the Horde in Elwynn Forest!

Of course, despite being far more ferocious and powerful than gnolls, a fel hound still couldn't handle several elite gnoll warriors in armor armed with spiked clubs. These gnolls tasked with defending the mage tower were far larger and sturdier than ordinary gnolls. After being thrown into disarray by the sudden intrusion of the demon hound, several gnolls reacted and immediately rushed over, trying to rescue their trampled comrade.

Jace's eyes burned with flame. Wreathed in a Dark Shield, he cast Fear on the nearest gnoll, sending it fleeing in terror out of his sight, then followed up with a Shadow Bolt that struck directly into the cluster of gnolls surrounding the fel hound.

The shadow explosion immediately tore apart the flesh of the gnoll at the center of the blast. The gnoll crossbowmen up high immediately shifted their aim and fired at Jace. A warlock also hurled a Curse of Agony at him. The curse wasn't blocked by the Dark Shield and took direct effect on his body.

But protected by the Mage Armor's warding against evil, this level of pain was nothing to Jace.

Actually, even without the Mage Armor it wouldn't have affected him much, because Jace could feel that this Curse of Agony wasn't even as intense as the ones Sulenna cast.

The gnoll warlocks up high poured spell after spell at him without the slightest regard for their mana, and with the support of this mage tower, these warlocks' magic was far more powerful than Jace had expected. But he still pressed forward through all this dark power, driving the fel blood force within his body, raising his hand toward the several gnoll crossbowmen above who were nocking bolts for a second volley.

A crimson force erupted from his palm and seized three crossbowmen up high. One of the warlocks seemed to sense that Jace's magic was off and managed to step back in time, bracing against the platform beneath his feet to block the crimson force and save his life.

But Jace didn't care about one or two getting away. He yanked his arm hard. The crimson threads snapped taut, and the other warlock and crossbowmen were violently torn from above, smashing through railings and tumbling down with howling cries.

One of the crossbowmen had his body ripped apart by the Blood Grip. By the time he hit the ground, he was already headless. The other two weren't much better off. Even if they hadn't died, a fall like that from up high was more than enough to make them regret being alive.

Just then, a gnoll warrior who had survived Jace's Shadow Bolt barrage swung his club furiously and struck the Dark Shield squarely. After enduring a sustained assault of crossbow bolts and Shadow Bolts, followed by this final club strike, the Dark Shield couldn't hold together and shattered, releasing a powerful shadow shockwave that blasted this warrior away and swept the surrounding area clean...

Several gnolls who had already been tormented into weakness by the shadow were knocked unconscious by this blast. Shadow corrosion had burned through their clothes and blackened their skin. They howled and rolled into the walls and other corners and crevices.

The fel hound struggled free from the gnoll corpses and, without hesitation, bolted up the stairs, continuing to carry out Jace's orders by pursuing the gnoll warlocks above.

From the moment Dazar'm charged in to the entire ground floor being swept clean had taken less than half a minute. Tordman and the soldiers who had just followed in stared at the wretched state of the seven or eight gnolls strewn across the floor, along with the shadow corrosion permeating the air. Some fell silent; others drew sharp breaths.

Jace also deeply felt that after so much tempering through combat against all manner of terrifying foes, his magical power had risen to a level he himself could no longer gauge.

Marin had said that the intensity of one's magic and the upper limit of one's stamina — or mana — had to be raised bit by bit through the continuous use of magic and growing familiarity with the casting process.

Compared to practicing in the Wizard Sanctum's mage tower or in the gardens of the Mage Quarter, the efficiency of improvement through real combat was definitely far greater.

Judging by what the Stonewatch soldiers had said earlier, the Darkhide gnolls around Stoneware were more ferocious than the Riverpaw gnolls of Elwynn. Recalling the time he'd fought through a pack of gnolls in the forests of Westfall hunting Hogger, Jace felt he was now completely in a different league compared to his old self.
