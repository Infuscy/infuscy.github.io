## Chapter 742: The Demonologist's Incantations

"Then, Mr. Sesso." Demetra smiled with some nervousness. "That Nethergarde Keep veteran told me you were the one who killed the demon lord using dark magic, not a fire meteor like Dalaran claimed. Is that true?"

"It's true," Jace said.

"So that's why they claim you're already dead. You accomplished great deeds using dark magic, but they felt it was simply heretical, something the Alliance couldn't accept..."

Demetra's tone was quite indignant, as if she wanted to speak up for Jace's grievances.

Afterward she sighed, then asked as naturally as she could, "What kind of magic was it? With your power, were you able to gather a Shadow Bolt to the point where it could kill a pit lord?"

Jace said, "That wasn't a Shadow Bolt. It's a magic I learned from an orc." He paused for a moment, then asked, "Do you want to learn it?"

"Of course." Demetra's eyes lit up.

"It's a very powerful magic, Lady Kraus," Jace said. "Both learning and casting it are difficult, and once mastered, it can cause extremely enormous destruction. Of course, that goes without saying — I already demonstrated it in front of Nethergarde Keep. Your friend has already witnessed it."

"I'm well aware of that," Demetra said eagerly. "So could you..."

"This kind of magic..." Jace raised an eyebrow. "I can't teach it to you for free."

Demetra glanced to the side at Morophil. The succubus kept wearing a strange smile aimed at her, as if she could see right through whatever she was scheming.

The female warlock swallowed and said, "Jace Sesso, then..."

As she spoke, she pulled at her collar, revealing half of Jace's gaze drifted downward, then he said, "I think you've misunderstood, lady. I already have someone."

Demetra cleared her throat. "That doesn't interfere with anything. I'll keep my mouth shut tight."

"You want to trade your body for your master teaching you an incantation?" Morophil asked, propping her chin in her hand, her tone carrying clear contempt.

"You have no right to interfere with your master's decisions, succubus."

Demetra said this as sternly as she could, then locked eyes with the succubus. Jace could see that she was also a bit afraid of Morophil, covered in fel patterns and with burning flames in her eyes, but the impulse to learn Hand of Gul'dan helped her overcome her fear of facing such a succubus.

"Of course I can't make decisions for my master," Morophil said, leaning against Jace's shoulder. "But what makes you think that spending a night with Jace Sesso is a 'sacrifice'?"

The female warlock was completely stunned. Jace folded his arms and leaned back against the wooden post behind him, chuckling. "Never mind what she says, Lady Kraus, but this really won't work. What I want is far simpler than that. Fifteen gold, and I can teach you the incantation for that magic."

"Fifteen gold?!" Demetra nearly choked. "I've never seen that much money in my life."

Jace said, "Fifteen gold for the power to kill a demon king — I don't think that's any kind of losing bargain. If you knew how much I paid to obtain this incantation, what kind of opponent I killed, you wouldn't question this price."

Morophil glanced sideways at her master, as if she found the notion of "fifteen gold for the power to kill a demon king" somewhat amusing, but it couldn't exactly be called a lie.

Demetra frowned. "Isn't there any other way?"

"Of course there is," Jace said. "For example, trading me an incantation I don't know, or using it to offset part of the fee."

"Curse of Weakness," Demetra said immediately. "An incantation I learned from Ironforge for this demon summoning. In the end I was responsible for maintaining the binding circle and didn't get to use it, but this is definitely a powerful magic against those savage opponents."

Jace couldn't help but feel a little disappointed. It seemed these demonologists' mastery of shadow magic outside of demonic knowledge really wasn't as strong as he'd imagined.

Noticing his expression, Demetra added, "This incantation wasn't even learned from orcs, Mr. Sesso. I'm certain the number of warlocks who've mastered it is absolutely not large..."

"Anything else?" Jace asked bluntly.

Demetra thought anxiously for a while. Jace could tell it wasn't that she didn't know any high-value incantations — she was considering whether to speak up.

In the end she said, "Fel Empowerment."

"Fel Empowerment?" Jace's interest was immediately piqued.

In the game, this was a spell that could temporarily make a demon under your control grow powerful and even break free from many forms of magical control. Jace was genuinely curious what Sazeno or Morophil would look like after being imbued with Fel Empowerment.

"This incantation can strengthen your minions' will and enhance their power and speed," Demetra glanced at Morophil beside her, then said, "Though many warlocks don't use this incantation in combat, I believe it would definitely be effective in battle as well."

"That's Annihilan magic," Morophil said. "Pit lords would stir their own powerful fel blood into a boil, influencing the contracted demons closely linked to them, sending them into a frenzied bloodlust..."

Demetra said, "I don't know what language that incantation is in. I only know it's effective enough that even humans are no exception." Jace asked, "Other than you, do the other warlocks here not know it, Lady Kraus? They're all demonologists."

"We all have our own channels for acquiring knowledge, Mr. Sesso," Demetra said seriously. "The channel through which I obtained this incantation is also quite unusual, and I paid a great deal to get it..."

"But I haven't witnessed the power of this incantation," Jace said. "So I can't judge whether it can truly be exchanged for my magic. Don't forget — the power of my magic was witnessed by the entire Alliance."

"That's true," Demetra said. "My home is in Lakeshire. When I go back I'll bring you the booklet where I recorded this incantation right away. The incantation and the casting method are recorded on it. If I were to dictate it verbally, I'm worried there would be recording deviations that affect the accuracy of the incantation. Mr. Sesso, I believe you understand that many powerful incantations have grown weaker and weaker through this kind of inaccurate transmission, again and again."

"After you teach me to learn it, I'll then consider how much value it holds," Jace said.

"Then I'll set out immediately..." Demetra stood up. "I hope to return before Mosur gets back. Lakeshire isn't far from here."
