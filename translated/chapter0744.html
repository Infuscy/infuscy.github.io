Chapter 744: Turning a Curse into an Enhancement

He had to admit, being able to produce incantations with a demonic voice was yet another boost to his casting of dark magic.

Curse of Tongues corroded the throat with demonic power, making it hoarse and rough, producing a voice that sounded as if it had been soaked in searingly hot fel blood and dragged low. But it also came with two enormous drawbacks.

One was that after Jace forcibly resisted the oppression of Curse of Tongues and practiced for most of the day, he could only produce two or three syllables before feeling somewhat drained.

But that was still the better problem, since Jace could see a glimmer of hope for withstanding Curse of Tongues and forcibly casting. The second issue was the real headache: he couldn't very well have someone cast Curse of Tongues on him every time he entered battle, could he?

He didn't trust these warlocks, and he especially didn't trust this orc. He had no desire to go adventuring with these people, and even less desire to make his future casting ability dependent on one of them.

So how to solve this problem?

Jace thought of the orc warlord he'd encountered in Grim Batol — Nekros.

He'd actually been able to transform into a demon. Could someone in the Shadow Council demonize themselves? That was terrifying. But it was indeed a method of becoming more adept at manipulating fel and shadow magic.

But becoming a demon wasn't a direction Jace wanted to pursue. He still preferred his current appearance. And if you became a demon, wouldn't you run the risk of being enslaved by someone else?

There's always a bigger fish — Jace understood this principle well. He couldn't act as he pleased just because he relied on the power of the Chain of Will, since the Chain of Will wasn't omnipotent either. All its previous wielders had failed.

So he could only learn the demons' incantations as safely as possible using the conditions currently available to him.

After being tormented by the orc's Curse of Tongues all day, he felt as though his throat was about to be scorched to a crisp by demonic power. The intense pain made him restless and irritable.

Actually, the obstacles to learning demonic incantations irritated him even more. At that moment, he looked toward the succubus beside him.

"Could you learn Curse of Tongues?"

"Master," Morophil said. "I can certainly try to learn Curse of Tongues for you, but how should I put it... you don't seem to enjoy bringing me along very often, after all. I'm not that... convenient."

She tapped the demon pack beside her with her finger. Sazeno immediately poked his head out. "What are you doing, sa'ard?"

"Let it learn," Morophil said.

"Learn what, Curse of Tongues?" The imp looked at Jace and the succubus beside him. "I know the incantation for Curse of Tongues. It's not some profound magic. But I don't have enough mana, my master!"

Jace asked, "You can cast several Firebolts and channel the Blood Pact — isn't that enough mana to release a single Curse of Tongues?"

"You see, those fire magics and the Blood Pact..." The imp spread his hands. "Those are the magic I'm good at. All imps know them! I've never used Curse of Tongues, and I'm not proficient at casting it. This is a strange incantation invented by the Eredar. You know the Eredar's magical characteristics, my master — they pay absolutely no attention to conserving mana..."

Jace scratched his chin and leaned to the side with a frown.

If there was absolutely no way to learn Cripple, then so be it. But now it seemed he'd touched the edge of it without being able to fully grasp it.

Or perhaps he could see hope of learning it, but it would be hard to put to practical use in real combat. In the end, turning such a powerful control magic into something like a party trick for showing off or entertainment — that was truly frustrating.

"Not entirely without a way," the imp said, jumping out of the demon pack and holding the bag of soul shards. "As long as my master finds a way to make me stronger... perhaps I'll have enough mana to cast Curse of Tongues! How about it?"

Jace asked, "I've already given you several soul shards, haven't you? Have you gotten stronger now?"

The imp said immediately, "Of course! I'm much more powerful now than when I first came to Azeroth. But still not strong enough. Oh, my master..."

"But... that's right, that's right!" it said suddenly, getting excited. "We've got a Burning Legion lord in our hands now, so we might be able to get that thing — that... the kind of demon stone refined from soul shards!"

"What the hell is that?" Jace asked, puzzled.

"Some demons refine soul shards into strange stones that can restore a demon's physical strength. My former superior, the Ghost King Yarozbal of Nastoy, ate this stuff to slowly grow into such a powerful demon. You should know he started out just like us — a brood mother's son. But then he took control of the forge and refined the disobedient imps, ones like me, into soul shards. You think he was going to use these soul shards for what... Some he used as ritual materials, and the rest he refined into those fel stones, my master!"

"Healthstone?" Jace asked.

"Healthstone?" The imp shook its head. "I don't know this name."

The imp's description made Jace think of the Healthstone from the game — the kind of stone warlocks made from soul shards to restore health, colloquially known by players as "sugar."

But obviously, demons didn't call this thing a "Healthstone." This name was probably coined by human warlocks or orc warlocks.

"Never mind. I mean, is it a stone that can be used for healing? You said it can restore a demon's physical body," Jace added.

"It can heal demons, but I don't know if it can heal you, ha!" the imp said immediately. "How about it, my master?"

"Can you refine it?" Jace asked. "Since you were a close guard of the Ghost King Yarozbal, you should know something about this technique, right?"

"No." The imp shook its head. "I wasn't a worker in the forge. I have absolutely no idea how that stuff is made! Go ask that big fellow tomorrow. He's got far more imps under his command than Yarozbal did. He might know something."

After saying this, it looked expectantly toward the outside of the tent, then added, "Why not go ask right now!"

"No, I'm tired now." Jace spread out his cloak and lay down. "We'll talk about it tomorrow."

Despite saying this, Jace was quite hopeful that Sazeno could grow stronger, at least enough to cast more demonic spells.

If its mana wasn't strong enough to continuously launch attacks, it could at least provide enough enhancement for himself or others fighting alongside him.

Of course, Curse of Tongues was still a debuff right now. A person afflicted with this incantation couldn't chant incantations at all. But if Jace really practiced to the point of completely suppressing the disruption this curse caused to his language functions, then it would actually be equivalent to an enhancement spell that turned his own vocal organs into a demon's.
