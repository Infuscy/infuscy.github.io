Chapter 751: The Advisor's Duties

Having spent a week at the Storm Altar, Jace obtained Demisette Crowlass's demonic enhancement magic and immediately returned to Stormwind City with his demon servants — though of course, Kallez wasn't coming along.

After all, he still needed to report back to the king about taking Ylgalag Tower. Having gone missing for a week or two after leaving Stonewatch could be explained by saying he'd stayed in Lakeshire or Goldshire for some leisure, but disappearing too long would be hard to account for.

Especially given his current identity as a warlock.

Although the royal court had publicly expressed their trust in him, if he acted suspiciously, it would probably still stir up some private discussion.

So on the early morning of his return to Stormwind, he dropped his things at home, changed out of his robes, and hurried straight to Stormwind Keep. When he arrived, the guards were changing shifts.

The moment they saw him, the guards immediately saluted. Jace explained his purpose to the guard captain, who promptly led him into the palace.

Walking up the long ramp, hearing each announcement shouting his name and the title "Court Mage Advisor," Jace straightened his collar, trying as hard as he could to grow into that title a little more.

The guard captain brought him to a side hall. Jace remembered this was the place where the king had met with him after dealing with Teron Gorefiend.

Back then, Varian Wrynn had still been quite green, seemingly still working hard to craft his image. Now he'd made further progress toward looking like a real king. After all, nearly five years had passed since Stormwind's liberation.

Standing beside the king was an older woman, but Jace found it hard to bring himself to describe her with words like "old woman" or "crone." Though her age was evident from her deeply wrinkled face and nearly all-white hair, the way she stood and the expression on her face made people feel she was still full of vigor — still a long way from the concept of "aged."

"I heard about Ylgalag Tower, Jace," Varian said, glancing at the old woman before looking back. "Not a single soldier sacrificed. Do you know how many years it's been since we've had an operation go this smoothly… dark magic or not, those people will shut their mouths before ironclad facts."

"So handing Ylgalag Tower over to you faced no obstacles at all. Your performance was several times better than we expected. I knew a champion who could rescue the Dragonqueen would have nothing to fear from a bunch of gnolls."

"If you have time, head over to the Wizard Sanctum. Master Andormas will give you the deed, along with some other paperwork and documents. You'll need to sign some things before you can officially become the owner of that tower. I think it's hard for you to understand how much it means to me to have someone I can trust in such an important place, Jace."

"Much appreciated, Your Majesty," Jace said.

Varian smiled and said, "And I think it's time now for you to meet the true guardian of this kingdom." This woman?

When Varian used the word "guardian" to address her, he used the feminine form, which made Jace almost think this was Medivh's mother — the female mage Aegwynn, who had occupied the position of Tirisfal Guardian for eight hundred years.

But even though she had married the royal court mage of Stormwind, Nielas Aran, she could in no way be considered the guardian here.

And most importantly, he remembered that Aegwynn had used magic to preserve her youthful appearance. Though her hair was all white… she shouldn't have looked this old.

"Lady Pasonia Shaul, founder and leader of SI:7," Varian introduced. Pasonia Shaul?

Jace had never heard of this person, but her surname was quite striking — especially when connected to SI:7.

Because in the game, the leader of SI:7 was Mathias Shaul. What was the relationship between these two? Mother and son?

Considering there were still over ten years until the game's timeline, Mathias Shaul wouldn't have been very old at that time, while this woman already looked to be in her sixties or seventies. Given her condition, her actual age might even be older.

Could she be… the grandmother?

"Soon I won't be, Your Majesty," Pasonia Shaul said with a smile. "My age and body are no longer sufficient to support me in continuing this heavy responsibility. The war is over, and I feel I'm falling behind the times of today' s young people."

"At least help the kingdom cultivate an excellent successor who can take over, my lady," Varian said, his tone full of respect.

"This is Jace Sesso, the young man who recently joined the court mages. I imagine I don't need to introduce him at length."

"Of course," Pasonia looked at Jace and nodded. "Very pleased to meet you here, Mr. Sesso." Jace lowered his head. "It's an honor to meet you, my lady."

The king looked at Jace and said, "Although SI:7 doesn't officially exist, I imagine you've already learned about this organization through various channels, Jace?"

Jace nodded, though he always felt that admitting this might expose something in front of Lady Pasonia Shaul. Still, he seemed to have heard about SI:7 from either Andormas or Marin, so he was probably just overthinking it.

"Then I won't disturb your conversation any further, Your Majesty," Pasonia said with a slight bow. "Be sure to remind Regel."

"Of course, I'm not senile enough for that yet, Your Majesty."

As she spoke, Pasonia politely nodded to Jace once more, then left the hall. The moment her shadow disappeared through the doorway, the respectful smile on Varian's face vanished without a trace.

He glanced at Jace and said, "I heard about what happened in Lordaeron, Jace. King Terenas II's illness, how Lord Daval Prestor is actually the legendary Deathwing — now the courts of every kingdom in the Alliance are uneasy. Who knows what kind of colored dragon the person next to you might be?"

"Especially since you mentioned before that black dragons might transform into mortal forms to infiltrate among us and cause trouble — how prophetic. Except back then you told me that black dragons would always return to their lairs and expose their tracks… but clearly Daval Prestor used his Alterac estate as cover. He actually seized a real noble title for it! And SI:7's response was still too slow. If the Kirin Tor hadn't uncovered the problem, Lordaeron — including us — might still be in the dark."

"That is Deathwing, after all," Jace said.

Varian nodded. "Of course, so I'm not blaming anyone. And that's Lordaeron, not our territory — the work is very hard to carry out. Still, I intend to conduct a purge of the court. Who knows if those damn black dragons have set their sights on us. As the court mages' dark magic advisor, I hope you can assist Lady Shaul's work, Jace."

"As you command, Your Majesty," Jace said.
