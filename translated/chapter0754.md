## Chapter 754: Future Itinerary

Returning to the small building where the elven mages lived, it had started to drizzle by this time.

Walking into Vereesa's room and seeing the purple and red decorations and furniture, the cushions — if not for the misty green treetops, pale purple eaves, and stone paths outside the window, Jace almost felt as though he'd returned to Windrunner Spire.

The two of them tossed their cloaks aside and leaned against the wall in each other's arms. The elf glanced sideways at the rain beating against the window and said, "There are no other mages here, Jace. Were you planning to talk about that scythe, or… first…"

Jace threw Vereesa onto the bed behind him, pulled off her soaked long boots, unlaced the corset carved with tree patterns and elven runes from behind her, burrowed underneath, and buried his head between her thighs…

He tasted her greedily, not stopping no matter how she begged, until the elf was trembling all over, gripping the headboard stiffly, curling up… only able to emit broken, intermittent moans from her throat.

"Wait… hah… slow down… slow down, bastard," Vereesa forced herself to turn over, raised her narrow, thin, ice-cold sole, and kicked Jace's face to the side. She said weakly, "Let me rest a bit."

Jace held her foot and sat to the side, licking his lips. "So, how much do you know about Mount Hyjal? I know the Reliquary once hired mages to head north on Kalimdor to search for lost Highborne relics. It should be somewhere around Mount Hyjal, right?"

"There is something to that," Vereesa exhaled, tidying up her disheveled silver hair that was now covering her face. "But you know, that's the Reliquary, not the Farstriders. I… haven't looked too closely into what they're doing up north on Kalimdor. What exactly is written on that scroll? Did that demon tell you?"

"The content goes something like this," Jace said. "Beneath a mountain demons cannot approach, there is a place with elven traces where ghosts murmur. There are great green walls there, and perhaps something like a labyrinth. Das'mora believes this place hides a necromancer's artifact — hidden there by a mage."

"Elven traces, demons unable to approach — that does sound like it fits Mount Hyjal," Vereesa said. Jace thought for a moment and asked, "Have you heard of a place called Darkwhisper Gulch?"

The elf frowned and thought for a long time. "Darkwhisper Gulch, I don't know. Maybe it has another name in Thalassian? But as far as that Common name goes, I've never heard of it. Is this a place around Mount Hyjal?"

Jace said, "This is just a place I happened to hear about from some traveler or other in Booty Bay. It might just be a legend."

Vereesa sighed. "You're guessing this place's name based on whispers of souls? Mount Hyjal is too big. Have you considered the other clues? A labyrinth — could it refer to the ruins of the Kaldorei empire? A place where ghosts murmur — you know those ancient Kaldorei. Many of them still exist as specters today because of the magic surging through the cities, right?"

What Vereesa said actually made Jace waver in his fixation on Darkwhisper Gulch.

She was right. Because of the ancient Kaldorei empire's worship of the arcane, most cities had been saturated with arcane abuse — not just Dire Maul, Zin-Azshari, Suramar, and a few other big cities.

Therefore, after the explosion of the Well of Eternity, countless Highborne had died in that catastrophic magical apocalypse. Their souls had all transformed into magical energy drifting through the cities, filled with hatred and madness, unable to dissipate for thousands of years. To this day, they remained a nuisance for the night elves living around Mount Hyjal.

Vereesa's guess made Jace think of another place… the Dark Abyss.

The Dark Abyss was an ancient temple sunken into the sea on the western coast of Ashenvale, at the foot of Mount Hyjal — that fit. Were there specters existing within it? Judging by the characteristics of other elven ruins, there very likely were. A labyrinth… it could indeed be counted as one.

Great green walls — if the Ashenvale forest was likened to great walls, then the Dark Abyss on Ashenvale's edge certainly fit this description too.

But the Dark Abyss had already been occupied by cultists who served the Old Gods. In the game, it was one of the Twilight's Hammer cultists' main strongholds. Whether the Twilight's Hammer had truly been established yet, Jace couldn't even be certain of that. But he could be sure that the Dark Abyss, that ancient elven temple, had at the very least been occupied by naga. They were unquestionably servants of the Old Gods.

Would Guardian Scaravel have hidden Ursaeus there?

Jace expressed doubt. As a leading figure of the Tirisfal Guardians, he must know that naga were by no means good folk. He wouldn't hand over such a terrifying weapon right to the naga's doorstep.

"Should I help you ask the Reliquary about Darkwhisper Gulch after I return to Quel'Thalas?" Vereesa asked.

"Better not," Jace said. "Don't reveal any clues we've found to the Quel'Thalas side. Of course, I mean people like Dar'Khan. By the way, has Dar'Khan been making any moves lately?"

"At least the last piece of news I got before coming to Stormwind was that he's still in Dalaran," Vereesa said. "Unlike Prince Kael'thas or the Sunreavers, he has no official-level relationship with Dalaran. What I mean is, as a senior member of the Magisters, him staying in Dalaran for so long really doesn't make sense. But I don't think he's made any particular progress, otherwise there would have been other movements before now."

Jace asked, "If I need to make a trip to Kalimdor in the future, can you come?"

Vereesa lowered her brows. "I'm not sure. When are you planning to go?"

"I haven't even figured out the clues yet," Jace said with a light sigh. "For now, the plan to go to Kalimdor is just a thought. No specific target, no preparation either."

Vereesa thought about it. "If it's not urgent, I can try to apply for a Reliquary mission to Kalimdor, Jace. Like you said, they never refuse Farstrider mercenaries willing to help them investigate ancient artifacts on Kalimdor. And this would count as serving the Sun King too. As long as Zul'Aman doesn't make any moves, I believe my sister would also agree to my request."

"Then I'll wait for your news, Wen," Jace said. "So, shall we continue?"

"Always letting me be on the losing end — isn't that a bit unfair?" Vereesa grinned mischievously, flipped over, and lay on top of Jace, glancing down.

"Let me taste you too…"

Her hand — that right hand with enough strength to draw a bow and shoot an arrow through a black dragon's scales… had already tightly gripped him. Did Jace have any choice at all?
