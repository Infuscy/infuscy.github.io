## Chapter 755: A Viable, Dangerous Idea

That evening, Jace brought Vereesa to the house he'd bought for his parents by the canal on the north side of the Trade District.

Vereesa looked up and sized up the spacious front hall, traced the patterns on the fireplace, and said, "I've always wanted to come see this place, Jace. Look at the fruits of your hard work… it's a beautiful place."

She looked through the window toward the distant harbor and said, "When I was little, I always wanted to have a house by the sea too. I have to say, Windrunner Spire is still too far from the coast, and from up there you can barely see the ocean. By the way, that mage tower — when can you take me to see it? I barely get a chance to come here."

Jace said, "There'll be a chance. In the next couple days I might need to meet with people from SI:7, Wen. When are you heading back to Quel'Thalas?"

"Then next time, after I find Sylvanas and secure a long-term outside assignment," Vereesa said with some regret. "If you don't have anything else, I'll head back tomorrow, since what I wanted to know… I already know."

"Then are you sleeping here tonight?" Jace asked. "Or back at your place in the Mage Quarter."

"This is the house you prepared for your parents. How could I move in first?" Vereesa said. "Can't I just come see your rented room?"

"Morophil lives there. You sure?" Jace asked.

"We've lived under the same roof before," Vereesa said, folding her arms and leaning against the wall. "You think I'm scared of her?"

"Forget it," she smiled. "I know what kind of demon a Sayad is. I won't provoke her. I'm just visiting, wanting to see where you've been living all this time."

The two of them walked west along the canal all the way to Jace's rented room in the narrow alley. Pushing open the door, Morophil smiled when she saw Jace, then noticed the elf coming in behind him and was taken aback.

Seeing this scene, Sazeno burst out laughing.

"Ha, I knew it! I knew it! Sayad, how about that, surprised? I told you just now you were done for, ahahaha! I could sense an elf's arcane power from two streets away!"

Morophil licked her sharp demon teeth and said nothing. Vereesa looked at her and said, "Good evening, Miss Morophil. Good evening, Sazeno."

"Good evening, my lady Windrunner!" the imp jumped onto the table, then bounced onto the window.

Jace pointed at it. "Get down. Do you want the soldiers on night patrol to see a ball of green fel fire bouncing around on my window? You're a hell of a lot brighter than before now. I really regret feeding you those stones."

"Ah, sorry, master! Regret anything but that…" Sazeno obediently jumped back onto the table.

Vereesa sized up the single-room dwelling. Morophil, sitting on the bed, asked, "Are you staying the night here, Quel'dorei?"

"Just visiting. No need to worry about your territory being encroached upon, demon," the elf said.

"This isn't my territory, it's the master's territory. Everything is decided by the master. My opinion doesn't matter in the slightest," the succubus said this, but her tone was obviously dripping with sarcasm, and her gaze toward Jace was icy.

"Morophil…" Jace sighed. "I haven't seen Vereesa in a while. Letting her see my room is no big deal, right?"

Morophil tilted her head. "Master doesn't need to consider my feelings. After all, I'm just a lump of dark flesh summoned from the Twisting Nether. I have no feelings whatsoever. I'm no different from that pack of felhounds that came out of the portal summoned by that Doom Lord. Once used, I can be discarded at any time…"

"I've never said I'd discard you, Morophil," Jace cut her off. "Then I'll take you at your word, master."

The succubus said this, smiling as she glanced sideways at Vereesa. But right now, the elf's attention seemed completely elsewhere. She was lowering her head, lost in thought.

"Doom Lord?" Vereesa looked up. "The one you summoned was a Doom Lord? A leader of the Doomguard?"

"That's right, that's right, my lady Windrunner!" the imp shouted. "Lord Kallez, a mighty demon! He's the one who gave me the chance to taste fel fire stones!"

"I'd like to know, Morophil," Vereesa said, looking at the succubus. "What did you mean just now with those portals? Can a Doom Lord really open portals connected to the Twisting Nether?"

When Vereesa asked this, Jace realized what she wanted to do.

"His portal can only let a pack of felhounds through," he warned.

"Isn't this the kind of small portal I mentioned to you back in Kalimdor, Jace?" Vereesa said with some urgency. "Maybe I shouldn't bring this up again, but…"

"The reason he can only summon a pack of felhounds is that he was severely weakened and struck when he came to Azeroth, master," Sazeno explained. "If he's allowed to fully recover his strength, I wouldn't be surprised at all if he made a whole squad of Doomguard walk out through the portal!"

Vereesa asked again, "Then does he have the ability to open a small portal to Draenor for us to go through?"

"There's a price, Quel'dorei," Morophil said. "This requires consuming enormous magical power. Even a small portal is the same. Coming here from the Twisting Nether and going from Azeroth to Draenor are not comparable in difficulty. Besides, it would also need the coordinates of Draenor to fix the portal's target, and that's not something easy to obtain either."

"But that Doom Lord has this ability, correct?" the elf asked.

"Based on my understanding of the Burning Legion, yes," Morophil said. "The Burning Legion sends Doomguards to maintain those massive Legion portals precisely because they excel at this."

"Yeah, yeah, how could I have forgotten!" the imp pointed at the succubus. "Isn't this a mage who can maintain a portal? We don't need to ask the Kirin Tor for help, and we don't need to resurrect Medivh who opened the Dark Portal… we have such a mage ourselves! And there's no one more professional!"

The elf looked at Jace eagerly, as if just waiting for his approval. Jace stared at her and said, "Calm down… Wen. I hadn't thought of this before either. This matter is too important, and too dangerous. It needs time to think through carefully. At the very least, I can't let this Doom Lord recover to his original strength right now. I'm afraid the situation would spiral out of control."

Vereesa was stunned. She lowered her head and swallowed. "Yes, that's… I feel like I'm not myself, Jace. Sorry."

Jace said, "Have patience. Trust your sister — I mean, Captain Alleria Windrunner. Give me time to consider this matter… Don't be too hasty. Let's continue with our original lives for now. Remember to keep this secret. Don't tell anyone besides Grede."

"I'll remember," Vereesa nodded.
