Chapter 756: Galders' Trust

Morning, at the main gates of Stormwind.

Though the weather was still a bit cool, spring had arrived. Tiny yellow flowers swayed in the breeze by the pool beneath the bridge. Birds flew across the sky from time to time. Passersby had shed their heavy robes. There wasn't much of winter's atmosphere left.

Jace helped Vereesa put on her cloak. She looked back at the statue of Alleria Windrunner and said, "I miss her. But I wouldn't risk your lives just to save her, or even just to see her once, Jace. About last night… I apologize to you. I feel like I was a headless fly. The moment I knew there was that sliver of hope, I lost my reason."

"I mean, don't do anything dangerous because of my requests. After all, knowing even a little bit of news about her is already more than I or Sylvanas could dare hope for."

"I understand," Jace said, holding her shoulders.

Just then, a squad of Stormwind cavalry passed by from the side. After the clatter of hooves faded, he lowered his voice and said, "I'll use discretion, Wen. If the timing isn't right, or the risk is too great, I won't make any decision lightly. But you need to understand too — since you have the intention of finding Alleria, you'll definitely have to take risks in the end. And I can't let you bear that alone."

The ranger nodded in his arms. "Then I'm off. Eat more, Jace. You've lost a lot of weight recently." Jace thought, the best thing I ate at the Storm Altar was that bizarre roasted scorpion crap — how could I not lose weight?

Still, he nodded, watching Vereesa leap onto her horse's back and take the reins toward the city gates, finally disappearing between several fluttering blue lion banners. He felt that hollow feeling in his chest again.

Just then, a patrolling Stormwind soldier came up beside him. He seemed to recognize Jace, or perhaps the ring on his hand. He immediately stood at attention and placed a hand to his chest. "Master Jace Sesso."

"Good morning, sir," Jace smiled, then suddenly remembered something. "Oh right, is there any news from Stonewatch recently?"

The soldier replied, "The most recent news is that with your help, Ylgalag Tower was captured. Everything's fine there now. The gnolls have left the forests around Stonewatch. As long as they have half a brain, they should know there's no way they can stay in those forests without controlling that tower. Will Redridge be back in our hands soon, Master? Maybe by then we won't have to worry about gnoll tribes coming from the east anymore."

"Let's hope so," Jace said honestly. "But I think it's still too early to be optimistic."

"We've survived the catastrophe of the Dark Portal," the soldier said. "And also with your… help, Master Sesso."

At this point, the guard seemed to feel he'd said something wrong. He lowered his head and said, "Those gnoll warlocks only dared to be so brazen with the help of orcs and demons, isn't that right?"

Jace nodded. It seemed more and more people knew he'd used dark magic to kill that Demon Lord at Nethergarde Keep.

After eating at the Gilded Rose at noon, Jace came to the Slaughtered Lamb.

Tharle was sweeping at the entrance. Seeing Jace, he straightened up and stretched. "Good afternoon, Court Mage Master Jace Sesso."

"So you all know," Jace said.

"Of course," Tharle said, leaning his broom to the side and stretching. "Galders even held a meeting to discuss how to deal with your problem. Now that you're the king's man, he doesn't really want to take the risk."

"It seems like the result of your discussion isn't to 'deal with' me?" Jace asked.

"You mean kill you?" Tharle Mor let out a laugh, a bunch of wrinkles creasing his dark face.

"How could we? Even Mosul — when he left here, we never actually planned to do him in. But Galders really did have preparations to relocate to Ironforge, in case you'd expose us to clear your name with Varian Wrynn."

"So," Jace glanced toward the door, "can I still go in?"

"Of course," Tharle stepped aside. "But Ursula Delin isn't downstairs."

"I'm not here to see her. Is Galders around?" Jace asked.

"He's at his room," Tharle said. "Right now there's only Jaline Aro and Ursula's Basadim downstairs."

"Thanks, Tharle." Jace turned and walked down the steps, then asked, "Is Ursula daring to go out now?"

Tharle said, "It seems her troubles have eased quite a bit, but I'm not sure about the specifics. You can ask her yourself next time she comes."

"All right." Jace nodded.

"One more thing, Jace," Tharle said. "I heard you bought a house over by the Trade District, didn't you? Does that mean the room you rented can be vacated? I've got someone else who wants to rent it, so I'm asking on the side."

Hearing Tharle Mor say this, Jace felt a bit uncomfortable.

He didn't really want to mix his parents in with these people from the Slaughtered Lamb. It would be best if, when they lived here in the future, they had absolutely nothing to do with the things he did on the outside.

But he also knew this was simply an impossible wish.

Jace said, "That house hasn't been renovated yet, so I'll still be living in that rented room for a while. When I plan to leave, I'll definitely come tell you in advance."

"You're renovating the house?" Tharle said. "Aren't you going to go ask Erland Dudderdale?"

Jace thought that although he didn't want to entrust the renovation of his newly bought house to Erland, having the Stonemasons' guild help out with the newly acquired mage tower should be fine.

If he saved up enough money before hiring people for the renovation, and didn't default on wages, there shouldn't be a problem… right? "Is he around, at that house in Goldshire?"

Tharle tossed his thick braid behind him. "Anyway, he's been staying put recently, Jace. He should either be at Sulenna Kayldon's place or in Goldshire."

Back at the rented room, Jace glanced at Morophil, who was sitting in a chair reading a scroll. Right now she was leaning an arm on the chair back, looking sideways at him. Bathed in the sunlight from outside, the fel markings all over her body were as dim as dried bloodstains, and the horns on her head were somewhat dry too. Under the clear contrast of light and shadow, she looked less real than a demon from the Twisting Nether.

"Master's planning to go to Kalimdor?" Morophil asked.

"Maybe. I still need to verify my guesses," Jace changed into a low-key shirt and jacket, swapped Azure Song for an unobtrusive scabbard and hung it at his waist. He was about to visit Galders.

He looked up at the succubus. "What is it?"

"This time, are you planning to take me?" Morophil asked.

"Of course I'm taking you," Jace said, widening his eyes. "What are you talking about? If we really are going to find Ursaeus… you know what that is, Morophil. There's a demon's soul hidden inside that even Sargeras found troublesome. I must make thorough preparations. What do you mean by that question? You're not planning to come with me? You scared?"

Morophil smiled. "Master, ever since we met, only you have ditched me. I have never once abandoned you, no matter what we faced. I'm just confirming."

With that, the succubus stretched out her arms and threw herself straight at him. Jace grabbed her arms. "I'm going out, damn it… Can't you see I just pulled out my sword?"

"It's still early, isn't it? Why not take this opportunity for a round?" Morophil brought her lips close to Jace's ear. "That night at the Storm Altar, I couldn't forget it even if I went back to the Twisting Nether. How about it — does Vereesa Windrunner feel the same way? Did she praise master's skills? She must know this is all thanks to me…"

"What the hell do you mean, thanks to you?" Jace pushed her to the side.

The succubus lightly tumbled onto the bed, looking up and smiling at him from there. Jace knew this demon's strength was by no means something he could brush off so easily. After that battle in the valley outside the Storm Altar last time, Jace's impression was unforgettable.

She was acting every single second.

But thinking this way, Jace felt that going to Galders' place, he still needed to bring her along. He remembered Galders' room — dark, narrow, and also inhabited by a succubus.

Considering the wariness Tharle Mor had mentioned Galders felt toward him, plus what Morophil had mentioned before about Galders' shadow energy seeming a bit off, Jace always felt that going to talk to him about this matter like this was a bit rash.

"Come with me, Morophil."

"Where?"

"To Galders' place."

"Master's actually willing to take me for a walk in Stormwind? Now that you're a dark mage trusted by the human king, can I stop being invisible…"

"No!"

"Forget it… heh."

Heading south along the canal, crossing a bridge, they arrived at Galders' residence.

Jace didn't know why he remembered this place he'd only been to two or three times so clearly. He walked up and knocked on the door. Galders' voice came from inside.

"Who is it?"

"Jace Sesso."

Not long after, the door was opened bit by bit. Galders looked at him from inside.

His already pale skin looked even paler right now, practically like a vampire. He looked at him and said, "Come in."

After closing the door, he looked at Jace and asked, "What capacity are you here in? Court mage, SI:7, or a friend?"

"At least not SI:7," Jace said.

Morophil materialized in the chair nearby. The flames burning in her eyes even outshone the candle burning beside Galders, illuminating the shadow of the succubus who was also looking over from inside the room.
