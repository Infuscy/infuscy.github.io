## Chapter 758: Fighting Fire with Fire

Jace actually somewhat admired Zardeth's willpower.

As a transmigrator, even after surviving several encounters with the temptations of the shadows, and now with the blessing of the Chain of Will, he still had to know how to avoid touching things he shouldn't. The Old Gods were the most dangerous among them.

If he were a native of Azeroth, he didn't think he could do better than Zardeth. Facing an artifact that could strengthen his shadow power, sensing something wrong and immediately refusing it.

But now, how to destroy this thing?

In another practice room, Zardeth placed the obsidian fragment on the table in front of him. He glanced at Jace and said, "I'll go to Erlan's room and take a look."

After a while, he came back carrying a hammer. Jace seriously doubted that these magic stones, so closely connected to the Old Gods, could be smashed so easily.

Zardeth swung the hammer hard, banging the table with a clang.

However, the fragment wasn't as hard as imagined — at least far inferior to that "goddess" statue stone he and Grede had found in Uldaman. Under this one hammer blow, it split into two halves with a crack. But each half… still didn't look quite right.

The shadow left behind by the Old Gods within still hadn't dissipated.

The two of them exchanged a glance. They knew that unless it was completely destroyed with magic, even if it was just smashed, someone would still be corrupted by these fragments. The people most likely to be corrupted would be the others in the Slaughtered Lamb.

"Purification Potion?" Jace suggested.

"Ursula's not here," Zardeth said.

"I know where she keeps it," Jace said. "I'll go get it."

Zardeth nodded. Jace went to the alchemy lab and retrieved the Purification Potion, but honestly he didn't hold out much hope.

Sure enough, after pouring the Purification Potion over the obsidian fragments, although for a moment you couldn't feel the dark power emanating from them at all, once the potion's effect wore off, it was almost no different from before it was poured.

Perhaps Purification Potion would eventually completely destroy the power within this stone, but the amount of Purification Potion required would be staggering.

"Let's try fire magic," Jace said.

Zardeth stepped aside. Jace placed the fragment on the ground and recited the Scorch incantation.

"Kibimofulosas!"

A ball of scorching fire instantly swallowed the small stone. The entire room heated up in the firelight. Even Zardeth, a few steps away, had to raise an arm to block the wave of heat. The power of the fire far exceeded expectations. Jace even forgot about the red dragon essence flowing through his veins… his bangs were curled by the blast of heat.

After the explosion, the obsidian fragment was blown to the back of the room. The larger piece had shattered into three more fragments, and the smaller one had a sharp corner chipped off from the impact. Yet the power contained within hadn't dissipated.

The two of them took the fragments that had been blown scattered and collected them back together. "It's not afraid of fire elemental power?" Zardeth asked, staring at these fragments.

"Remember what I said before?" Jace said. "The Old Gods' servants use them to summon elemental power. In Silithus, these stones are called 'Wind Stones' precisely because they call upon the aid of wind elementals there. Many of Azeroth's elementals obey those Old Gods. Perhaps for this reason, its power is also highly resistant to fire. That's my best guess."

"I've heard similar things from the dwarves, but the idea that all the elementals around us obey the commands of some ancient fiend… it's really hard to imagine," Zardeth said in a low voice.

"Maybe we should hand this thing over to the Church for handling," Jace said.

"If there really is no other way… then we'll go to the Cathedral of Light and ask those priests for help," Zardeth said, shaking his head. "If they realize how extraordinary this thing is, they might investigate it further. I'm worried it would implicate us, and my friend."

"If we had a doomguard servant here, destroying this thing would probably be effortless, master."

Morophil said this from the side, giving Jace a meaningful look. Jace knew what she was hinting at. She wanted to mention Kallrez. Of course, she also understood that bringing up the doom lord Jace and Mor'zul had summoned together in front of Zardeth right now was definitely inappropriate.

Senlora nodded and said, "Miss Morophil is right. The Burning Legion uses fel fire to burn everything that tries to resist them to ashes…"

"…the kin hiding in the shadowy abyss, even including the parasites themselves."

"You mean demonic power," Zardeth asked. "Fel?"

"Yes," Morophil nodded.

Jace was just about to call Sazeno's name and try having it burn away this stone with its Scorch magic, but Zardeth spoke first. "Let me try."

He stared at those fragments, recited an incantation in Demonic. Flames laced with bright green fel energy ignited at his fingertips. The entire room was immediately filled with the stench of fel. Even the charred smell left behind by Jace's fire magic combustion was completely replaced — no, transformed.

It was obvious Zardeth didn't like this smell either. But he still furrowed his brow and finished the incantation, clenched his hand, and pointed at those fragments. With a sharp, brief "crack," the fel fire produced a clean explosion, and the fragments were blown scattered once again.

But when they picked up these fragments stained with the smell of fel energy, they could clearly feel that these remaining small fragments had completely lost their eerie magical power within… they'd turned into ordinary black glass-like things.

"What incantation was that?" Jace asked.

"The dwarves call it Searing Pain," Zardeth said. "Demonic power drills into the enemy's body, affecting their mind, making them irritable and volatile. Add Curse of Recklessness and it immediately drives these people mad. I thought that this fel-affected fire might also be able to damage the power within these fragments, so I wanted to give it a try. I didn't expect it to work so well."

"You haven't shared this fel incantation with the others in the basement," Jace said.

"It's only you I haven't shared it with. The rest of us have discussed this magic before, Jace," Zardeth said. "Take Erlan Drudgemoor, for example. Though he has no gift in shadow magic, he learned this fel fire magic very quickly."

As Zardeth said this, Jace seemed to suddenly realize why warlocks had been studying shadow magic all along, yet hadn't worshipped the Old Gods like the Twilight's Hammer believers, or certain priests who'd come into contact with the shadows…

Warlocks learned shadow magic from demons. They not only contacted the shadows but also gained a deep understanding of fel power.

Demons with fel blood flowing through their bodies naturally had resistance to the corruption and temptation of the Old Gods. This was why, although warlocks were crazy and loved courting death, very few of them followed in the footsteps of the Old Gods.
