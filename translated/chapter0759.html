Chapter 759: The Kirin Tor's Investigation

Having obtained the incantation for summoning a Voidwalker from Galders, Jace stored the scroll in a box as one of the spell scrolls he planned to bring back to his mage tower.

In the future, if he wanted to summon Kallez from the Storm Altar into the mage tower and lacked the energy, the Voidwalker summoning magic could serve a critical purpose.

Remembering how Galders had summoned fel power in the basement to annihilate the evil within the fragment, Jace recalled that his fire magic hadn't progressed in a long time.

Embarrassingly speaking, the book Master Marin had given him, The First Burning — he'd already been stumped by just the Fire Ward incantation. No matter how he tried, he couldn't cast it perfectly.

Actually, after being embraced by Alexstrasza and blessed with the essence of the Life-Binder, Jace felt that even casting Mage Armor wasn't as smooth as before.

Of course, he was all too familiar with this spell. He'd cast it countless times in all kinds of dangerous environments over the past two years. He believed that if he could achieve that same level of proficiency with Fire Ward, he could definitely pull it off. So the lack of fluidity was only slightly perceptible and hadn't gotten to the point of interfering with his spellcasting.

The specific sensation… was that manipulating the arcane to have fire elements protect himself had become much more difficult. The fire elements grew increasingly wild and fervent as they approached him.

But the Dragonqueen had clearly said that he must be cautious when using fire magic from now on. That meant his fire elemental affinity had actually grown stronger — so why had fire-related spells become less easy to cast?

He should go ask Marin again. Though his Fire Ward practice had been quite a failure, this wasn't the time to worry about saving face.

The next morning, Jace took The First Burning and came to Marin's study. He knocked and pushed the door open, and saw a young mage apprentice standing beside Marin, looking over.

Jace had actually forgotten that the Wizard Sanctum's new term had started, and his teacher had students to teach… He'd come straight in without asking ahead of time.

"Sorry, Master."

Jace lowered his head and closed the door to leave. Then he heard the mage apprentice inside say in a voice that could be clearly heard through the door, "That was Master Jace Sesso! The Master Jace Sesso who killed that Demon Lord at Nethergarde Keep!"

Then came a burst of Marin's voice that couldn't be made out clearly. After Marin finished speaking, the apprentice's voice became much smaller, but could still be heard as very excited.

After a while, the apprentice ran out, looking at Jace excitedly. "I'm so glad you're still alive, Master Jace Sesso! Many people slandered you, saying you used dark magic to kill the Demon Lord Azgoth. That's just a rumor made out of jealousy to attack you… I won't disturb your conversation with the grand mage. Anyway, it's an honor to meet you, Master."

Entering the room, Marin looked at him with a complicated expression. He also closed the door with a complicated mood.

"Maybe I should have just told him directly that I killed that demon with dark magic," Jace said.

"He might refuse to believe you are Jace Sesso because of it," Marin said, leaning back in his chair. "Sit anywhere, Mr. Sesso." Jace nodded helplessly and sat in the chair beside him.

"I have something rather important to tell you." Marin took a document from his drawer, unfolded it, and said, "Do you know about the Blackrock Spire operation? A Kirin Tor mage went to Blackrock Spire and rescued Council of Six member Master Krasus, who had been imprisoned by the black dragons. The red dragons seemed to have been involved as well."

"I heard about it," Jace said.

"Of course," Marin said. "This matter caused quite a sensation in Dalaran, and the news quickly spread throughout Lordaeron and Stormwind. To Dalaran, a real mage, a member of the Kirin Tor, was definitely more worth publicizing energetically."

"This matter doesn't seem to have much to do with me," Jace said.

"It has to do with you," Marin said, turning to another page. "Apparently Master Krasus obtained some evidence of dealings between the Kirin Tor and Lord Daval Prestor… His escape from the hands of the orcs and black dragons clearly exposed certain people within the Kirin Tor. Now Kel'Thuzad has been listed as a key investigation target. As far as I know, the investigation against him has already begun. I and Master Andormas intend to provide a document to the Kirin Tor explaining the inconsistencies between the Kirin Tor's report and your account regarding the Eye of the Arcane theft incident. I remember that report was also released by Kel'Thuzad to other magical institutions within the Alliance. This document will be magically transmitted to the north. When the time comes, you might need to go to Dalaran to testify."

"I'm fine with that," Jace said.

"Then I'll tell Andormas as much," Marin said with a sigh. "A Council of Six member falling into this kind of situation seems to be the first time since the Kirin Tor's founding. Things have come to this point. Although I don't like the Kirin Tor, it's hard to gloat. After all, it's hard to imagine what conditions the black dragons used to make such a high-ranking mage betray them. The full picture of the matter might be much more terrifying than we imagine. Of course, this isn't set in stone yet, and I'm just overthinking things, Mr. Sesso."

He glanced at Jace, observing him briefly, then said, "You don't seem surprised. When you went to Dalaran for the first time with Master Dushan, you should have met Kel'Thuzad. I remember he spoke highly of you."

"After all, the Kirin Tor Council of Six only has six members. No matter who it ends up being in the end, I wouldn't be surprised, Master," Jace said. "That's true," Marin agreed.

Actually, the fact that the Kirin Tor and Antonidas had taken notice of Kel'Thuzad this quickly was quite a surprise to Jace — a pleasant surprise.

Thinking back to Antonidas's indifference toward him at the time, it didn't quite seem like the mutual protection within the Kirin Tor that Grede had imagined. It was more like… a kind of discouragement, or rather, disappointment.

As the leader of the Kirin Tor, the master of Dalaran, perhaps he had already sensed something off about Kel'Thuzad long ago. And having a complete outsider like Jace hint at such a thing might have touched Antonidas's pride.

But at the same time, it also confirmed his conjecture, making it hard for him to accept the fact that Kel'Thuzad, his close friend and student, was actually a traitor.

Of course, Jace could no longer determine what this old mage was really thinking. The only important thing was that he was currently leading the investigation into Kel'Thuzad, and Jace himself might even participate in it.

"Oh right, what did you come to me for?" Marin asked.
