## Chapter 766: An Agreement with the Dagger Society

In the morning, Jace brought Morofell to Galders' residence, but no one was there, so he went straight to the Slaughtered Lamb.

Tharle Mor was wiping down tables. The tavern was completely empty. After asking, sure enough, Galders was in the basement — which was unusual. Ever since Jagin had died, Galders rarely spent much time down there.

But thinking about it, Galders had been traveling back and forth between Ironforge and Stormwind and rarely went to Duskwood. That was probably connected to that strange Silithus obsidian. Now that the matter with that stone had been settled, he'd likely cut off contact with his associates in Ironforge for the time being.

After all, that person was very likely connected to the Twilight's Hammer. Jace wasn't entirely sure whether the Twilight's Hammer had officially been founded at this point, but according to the half-orc Rexxar's account, at least the group of warlocks led by Cho'gall — the future leader of the Twilight's Hammer — had already arrived in Kalimdor. Which meant that regardless, the Twilight's Hammer had already taken initial shape.

When Jace arrived at the great hall in the basement, Galders was there memorizing something, and Senrola was copying scrolls right beside him. Seeing Jace, Galders set down the papers in his hand and said, "You've come for that Searing Pain incantation, haven't you, Jace?"

"That's not the only thing," Jace said as he sat down. Morofell materialized from a nearby chair, lay down lazily, and stared straight at her master.

"I met with someone from SI:7 yesterday."

"What do you want to tell me that for?"

Galders' expression visibly wavered. Jace said, "What do you have to worry about? You know people in SI:7 too, don't you?"

"Sorry," Galders said. "It's probably because I'm still a little uneasy about your current position. So, what does this have to do with me?"

"It has to do with the entire Dagger Society," Jace said. "Stormwind is currently facing a threat from the black dragons. Some black dragonkin have taken human form and infiltrated the city — these are the ones among the dragonkin who are skilled at magic, so their disguises are extremely well hidden and hard to see through. So I proposed cooperating with our Dagger Society to him."

"You didn't expose our location, did you?" Galders asked.

"Of course not," Jace said.

"But," Galders said, leaning to one side of his chair, "just because you can identify dragonkin doesn't mean all of us can. I've heard of black dragons disguising themselves as mortals. There are people in Lakeshire who worship the black dragons — warlocks who learn dark magic from the dragons…

"…Before the organization there was taken out by SI:7, I attended one of their gatherings. I was told the leader of that gathering was a dragonkin, but I couldn't sense it at all."

Jace asked curiously, "You actually managed to attend their gatherings? What did you go for? Where'd you hear about it?"

"You just said it yourself — I know people in SI:7," Galders said. "I went to those gatherings of course to find more magic. The dragons' incantations are shadow magic too. But they were very tight-lipped with newcomers like us, unwilling to leak anything. They just preached stuff about the end of mortals, that the black dragons would rule the world, and I lost interest."

Jace nodded. Grede had really exhausted every avenue to find more incantations — though he most likely had no idea about the black dragons' connection to the Old Gods, otherwise he probably wouldn't have gone at all.

Considering that dragonkin often used psychic magic to enslave those dragon worshippers, the fact that Galders had pulled out in time was a stroke of luck.

"Actually, I don't have the ability to see through dragonkin disguises either," Jace said. "But it seems the felhounds can identify them."

"Ah… so that's how it is. I should have figured that out earlier," Galders said with a laugh. "But cooperating with SI:7… aren't you afraid they'll figure out all our information and then wipe us out in one stroke?"

"At least they can't arrest me along with them, can they?" Jace spread his hands and showed him the Court Mage's ring on his finger. "Unless SI:7 and the Kingdom of Stormwind decide to make a complete break with me someday, they probably won't go that far. At the very least, their people are considering the matter right now. When the time comes, we help them deal with the black dragon problem, and they'll turn a blind eye to our activities — as long as we don't do anything that harms others."

Grede smiled slightly. "Frankly, I'm surprised you'd fight this hard for the Dagger Society, Jace. In your eyes, is the Dagger Society more important than the Kingdom of Stormwind?"

Jace smacked his lips. "How am I supposed to answer that? All I can say is that everyone in the Dagger Society is my friend. Even if Jaline Aylo doesn't talk to me much, I have to admit that without her robes, I might not have survived this long so easily."

"Sorry, I didn't mean to put you on the spot like that," Galders said. "I'll gather everyone in the Dagger Society for a meeting to discuss this and decide whether to cooperate with SI:7. Bring that summoning circle incantation when the time comes, and we'll study it together. I'll also give you the incantations I've gathered over this period."

"All right," Jace said. "I'm still waiting for word from SI:7. I'll let you know once they respond."

With that settled, Galders took Senrola back to his room. Jace went to the alchemy lab and saw Ursula sorting herbs, so absorbed in her work that she hadn't noticed someone had already appeared at the door.

"Ursula."

"Ugh?" Ursula looked up, saw Jace, and said, "How are things, Jace? Those potions I gave you?" Jace glanced outside the hall and said, "They worked wonderfully."

"Good. So you're with that Windrunner noble, aren't you?" Ursula asked.

"Yes," Jace said. "Did Erland tell you?"

"The whole of Stormwind's talking about it," Ursula said. "At first I thought it was just a rumor, but then Vereesa Windrunner came to the Mage Quarter, and a lot of people saw you with her at the Blue Heron… kissing her right there in public."

"Isn't that kind of thing perfectly normal at the Blue Heron?" Jace said, lying back in his chair with a smile. "Young mages of both sexes date there all the time."

"But a Ranger-Lord and a Court Mage dating there is anything but normal," Ursula said, glaring. "Tell me the truth, you little bastard — that night… right after we killed that satyr, in my quarters — were you already with her?"
