Chapter 769: A Long-Awaited Gathering

Jace couldn't remember the last time he'd attended a Dagger Society gathering.

It was rare for so many people to fill the great hall beneath the Slaughtered Lamb — or rather, it was rare for Jace to see so many people here.

Jaline Aylo, Ursula Dering, Sulenna Keldoan, and Erland Dudemoore were all seated around the fire chatting and laughing. The great hall, which was cold and quiet most of the time, suddenly felt so lively that it made him deeply uncomfortable.

Sulenna was wearing a dark red robe, and from the arcane runes on the robe one could sense it held considerable shadow power.

She looked much more relaxed than the last time Jace had seen her. She'd cut her hair short, and her black legs, reflecting the firelight, were crossed lazily to one side as she chatted warmly with Jaline about something.

Jaline, wearing a lilac-colored outer robe, was smiling and chatting with her — the two of them seemed to get along quite well.

Jaline had by now integrated herself into the Dagger Society considerably. Not only did she have plenty to talk about with Sulenna, but she could even occasionally exchange a few words with Ursula. Jace remembered that before he'd left for Kalimdor, Ursula had probably been the person in the Dagger Society who liked Jaline the least.

But after a year of getting along together, Jace even felt that within the Dagger Society, Jaline got along with the others more harmoniously than he did. At moments like this, he was the one who felt more like an outsider.

Erland was hugging his succubus, the diminutive Sathris, exchanging saliva with her ravenously. Jace was so far away he could practically smell the demonic stench.

Ursula was absorbed in reading her book at the moment, as if the messy commotion around her didn't exist at all. The whole hall seemed to have only Jace sitting there in a daze, with nothing to do.

"Master, do you feel like you've been isolated?" Morofell slowly wrapped her arms around Jace from behind.

Jace said nothing. Morofell continued, "What do you want to talk about? I'll be right here beside you. Whatever you want to say, I'll say it with you."

"Thank you, Morofell," Jace said, and he meant it sincerely.

"Thank? How about…" Morofell leaned against Jace's shoulder, glanced sideways in Erland's direction, and said in a voice so quiet it could barely be heard unless pressed to his lips, "Look at those two. How about we pass the time like that too? I want that as well, master. You won't let me taste you down below — then at least let me taste the top…"

"What's so great about tasting that." Jace's smile instantly stiffened.

"To me, anything of my master's… no matter what it is, is utterly delicious."

Morofell pressed her voice so low that Jace could almost hear the friction of shadow energy, and he even wondered if he'd misheard. She seemed about to say something else, but her gaze suddenly darted toward the door — Grede was here.

Everyone greeted Grede one after another. Erland also stopped and moved his succubus to the side. At this point, Galders' minion Senrola had taken on something of the bearing of a demon leader in the Slaughtered Lamb's basement — even the succubus Gakres, summoned by Sulenna, lowered its head to her.

After all, a demon's power was tied to its master, and Galders' strength in shadow magic would make Senrola stronger and stronger. The shadow flames in her eyes were already quite fierce.

Of course, in the end, what demons valued most was strength. When Senrola caught sight of Morofell, she still lowered her head in acknowledgment. Morofell simply returned a smile.

"I apologize, everyone," Galders said. "The Mage Quarter was crowded tonight, so I took a detour to bring Senrola in, which is why I'm a little late."

He walked up to the fire and continued, "This gathering should be the most important one we've had in a year, everyone. Perhaps tonight we'll decide the future of the Dagger Society."

Ursula put down her book and glanced at Jace — she'd probably guessed what Galders was going to say.

"You've probably all heard about the servants of the black dragons appearing within the kingdom, attempting to carry out sabotage. As a member of the Court Mages, Jace is currently cooperating with SI:7 to root out the hidden black dragons. Now SI:7 is hoping to cooperate with us… The felhounds can do what even the mages of the Wizard Sanctum struggle to do — see through the disguises of dragonkin. Jace and I wanted to hear everyone's thoughts."

At this point, Jace looked around at the others' reactions. Ursula had naturally already accepted it, since he'd mentioned it to her before. But Erland and Sulenna were clearly a little hesitant. Jaline simply wore an indifferent expression, showing no reaction.

"Is there no benefit?" Erland asked. "You want us to work for free, with no pay at all?"

Galders said, "In exchange, SI:7 is willing to turn a blind eye to us — that is, to the members of the Dagger Society — when we engage in 'harmless' dark magic activities within the kingdom."

"What counts as 'harmless'?" Erland said. "Is summoning a demon harmless?"

"SI:7's Mathias Shaw put it more concretely — no harming others," Jace said. "Considering he's already seen me summon a felhound and had no objections, that means as long as your demon doesn't hurt anyone, SI:7 won't interfere."

"Is that only for us in the Dagger Society?" Erland asked again.

"Of course," Jace said. "I specifically mentioned it — people I've personally designated."

"That sounds good," Erland said with a nod.

"I'm willing to join," Ursula said.

"Frankly, I have serious reservations about that group," Sulenna said. "As it happens, I'm not particularly keen on summoning felhounds either. Count me out, would you?"

"We're the same group, Su," Erland said. "We can't have half joining and half not — that's unfair either way."

Jaline said, "I have no objections."

"Then don't take what I said seriously," Sulenna said, glancing at Jaline with a resigned expression. "But I can't help with this matter, and then I'll get to enjoy the benefits you've fought for — just don't blame me for freeloading."

"It's fine, Sulenna," Galders said. "I've always hoped that we wouldn't have to hide away beneath the Slaughtered Lamb all the time, and that we could start showing ourselves a little, letting the kingdom slowly accept us. I think the opportunity Jace has fought for us this time… is an important step forward."

"But it's best not to let SI:7 know where we gather, Galders," Jaline cautioned. "I'd feel very unsafe. We should go to their people to cooperate."

"Of course," Galders said. "Jace and I have considered that too."

Jaline nodded, and Erland said, "I also think it's time to try making contact with them. Every time I hear about a warlock getting caught, I think we might be the next batch. This would at least put my mind a little more at ease."

"Then it's decided for now," Galders said. "Next, Jace has a very special incantation to share with us."

"What incantation?" Erland asked.

"A summoning circle," Jace said.

"A summoning circle for what demon?" Jaline asked curiously.

Jace said, "It's a circle that can summon other people. It can also summon demons, to be precise. It designates a specific target and requires a token closely tied to the other party's essence."

"Good heavens," Jaline said in surprise. "I've heard of this kind of summoning incantation. I thought it was just something made up by some dark-magic-worshipping warlocks. Can it summon someone no matter how far away they are?"

"It can even summon someone here from the Twisting Nether," explained Gakres, Sulenna's male succubus minion. "I know this magic. Using it to summon a mortal from the Twisting Nether to Azeroth would be extremely dangerous."

"That doesn't sound like it has anything to do with us," Erland said. "We're not demons — why would we go to the Twisting Nether." Jace glanced at Morofell, and Morofell was looking at him at the same time.

Both of them knew what the other was thinking — Draenor.

If they really wanted Karaleth to open a portal for them into Draenor, it would almost certainly involve the Twisting Nether.

"At least I know there's no problem using this magic to teleport someone from Stormwind to Lakeshire," Jace said.

"That distance is already astonishing," Jaline said. "Teleporting that far — even in Dalaran there aren't many mages who can do it."

"But this magic requires three people to work together," Jace said. "One person to lead, and two to assist. The person leading needs to be quite familiar with shadow magic or summoning magic. I think our Dagger Society needs at least two people who can lead this summoning magic — that way, even if one of the leaders isn't in the basement, the other person can use the incantation to summon them back."

"After all, Jace, you're basically never here. Even if you learn it…" Ursula said, raising an eyebrow slightly, and the others all laughed.

Jace said, "Actually, I can't do it either. I just brought this incantation here — I haven't started learning it yet. This is an extremely complex magic. I think it would be very difficult to learn in a short time."

"Then how about this — today we'll copy down this incantation and the casting method first. Everyone go back and practice, and we'll find a chance to practice together?" Grede proposed.

The warlocks' minions all took out pens and scrolls to help their masters copy down the summoning incantation. Erland looked at them and said, "I might be the first among us to learn it…"

"All that demon magic has given you quite the confidence, Erland," Sulenna said from the side.

"A summoning circle isn't demon magic too?" Erland said with a smile.

At this point, Galders handed a stack of scrolls to Jace and said, "These are two spells we've organized, Jace. One is Searing Pain. The other is the 'Immolate' incantation — a fearsome magic that burns an enemy's soul with fel energy. Remember not to practice it inside Stormwind. Fel flames are really hard to control — Erland almost set his own house on fire before. And the fel stench isn't like shadow; it's extremely easy to identify. After what happened at the Dark Portal… every resident of Stormwind knows what fel fire smells like."

"I'll remember," Jace said with a nod.

At this moment, Tharle shouted down from above, telling the people in the basement to come to the kitchen to get the pumpkin pies and mutton soup. Erland and Sulenna went together to move the ale. Jace set the table together with the succubi. Tharle had even prepared plenty of fruit, assorted cheeses, and boiled potatoes. After pushing the tables together and setting out the meal, it really did look like a sumptuous dinner.

Especially with male and female succubi attending on all sides, the firelight flickering in the shadows, a group of warlocks chatting and laughing…

Dark and just a little eerie — but as Jace looked at these familiar faces, he felt this place was nothing like the Altar of Storms.

By midnight, the plates had been swept clean. Sulenna was sitting on Erland's lap with a cup in her hand. Galders had pulled Senrola into his arms, and his hand had already slipped beneath her skirt.

Ursula had gotten a little drunk as well, secretly pulling a small bottle of medicine from her pocket and gulping it down. She blinked hard and seemed to sober up a bit. The male succubus Gakres was saying something to Jaline — judging by the expressions on their faces, it certainly wasn't anything decent.

Jace drained the ale from his cup and said, "Come with me, Morofell."

"Ah, master, you've finally come to your senses," Morofell said with a smile. "What shall we play this time? How about… I use my tail?"

"To the practice room," Jace said in a low voice. "Try that Empowerment incantation with me. Remember? I haven't used it even once yet."

"Fine, at least it's another kind of fun," Morofell said, tilting her head.
