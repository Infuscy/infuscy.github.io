## Chapter 770: Hot Blood

"Why… so shy, Jace? Why not just have some fun here with Lady Morofell?"

Sulenna asked, hugging Erland's head. Though her body kept trembling to Erland's rhythm, she still craned her neck to look over here and continued, "I'm saying, Jace, don't… uh… in the middle of everyone having a good time at the gathering… go off to the side and practice some incantation, huh… You're really putting me under pressure here."

Ursula looked up at Jace and smiled. "Don't mind her, Jace."

Jace said, "I might be leaving Stormwind again before long, Sulenna. So I want to take this time to test out all the incantations I haven't had a chance to verify yet, and then share them with you all."

Jaline Aylo, hearing this, ignored Gakres' flirtation beside her and glanced over here with curiosity — she seemed interested in the incantations Jace had mentioned.

Sulenna also nodded busily, but she was clearly already in the zone and didn't have the energy to say anything more to Jace. Arriving at the practice room with Morofell, Jace took out the scroll of Demiseth Kraus that he'd brought.

This was an Annihilan incantation, with phonetic notation and explanations in demonic, along with annotations in Common and Orcish.

The language of the pit lords was crude and straightforward. As long as you learned the pronunciation of each rune, you could correctly recite their words and sentences, because their language had almost no pronunciation rules that were even slightly complicated. And Jace had essentially already learned the pronunciation of these runes when he'd been practicing the felhound-naming incantation earlier.

Jace recalled what Demiseth Kraus had said. This Demon Empowerment incantation was how pit lords ignited their own demonic blood, driving the minions connected to their power into an enhanced state — but she hadn't said what the effect would be if a human used it this way.

But considering the power link between Erland and his little succubus, and between Galders and Senrola, Jace figured that while the effect on humans probably wouldn't be as direct as with a pit lord, it should be more or less the same.

Demon Empowerment magic was a spell that existed in the game, of course. Each type of demon minion had a different effect. For a succubus, it was a forced invisibility that dodged damage and broke bonds. For other demons, it was either breaking bonds, a period of increased damage, or increased resistance.

After finishing the incantation, Jace closed his eyes and recited it silently, then looked over the casting instructions.

The target of this incantation was his own minion. He glanced at Morofell off to the side. She was already looking a little restless standing there, her long tail swishing back and forth nonstop, her tongue licking her lips, the flames in her eyes flickering.

"Let's try the first time," Jace murmured. "Miyoni, Zharuonu, Morokaerio…"

Before he'd even finished, he felt his whole body grow hot. Morofell looked down at her own chest — her skin was slowly flushing red, even showing hints of purple.

Jace felt his emotions shift slightly from the surging power within him. He felt restless, eager to learn this magic as quickly as possible, and at the same time he began to lose control over doubting how effective this magic actually was — of course, he clearly recognized that this was fire elementals and shadow energy rapidly burning through his mana at the same time. Was this the process Demiseth had described as "igniting one's own blood"?

A smell that made the back of Jace's neck prickle spread through the air… the scent of black dragons, or to be precise, something extremely close to black dragonfire.

He quickly darted a glance at Morofell and realized the aura was coming from her! Shadowflame?

Jace hadn't tried using shadow power to ignite his own fire magic in a long time. When dealing with Karaleth, because he'd felt the intense fire affinity brought by the Dragonqueen's essence, and because the doom lord's shadow resistance was extremely high, he'd simply released a pure Fireball.

Which meant the last time he'd smelled this kind of flame's aura was in Grim Batol.

But this time, Morofell had somehow been ignited by this incantation into a hybrid demon of shadow and flame?

However, the Shadowflame on her quickly faded. Immediately after, she groaned in pain and slumped against the wall, her throat moving as though she were swallowing something.

This incantation hadn't succeeded, because Jace had been distracted by Morofell's reaction and failed to concentrate on finishing the spell.

"Sorry," Jace said. "Let me try again."

"No need to apologize, master," Morofell said, lowering her head. "I even hope you fail once more. The torment you give me is more intoxicating than pleasure. As long as it's you, my dear."

Jace glanced at her. Even though Morofell openly flew the banner of wanting him to fall in love with her, not concealing her intentions in the slightest, after being spoken to this way so many times, her words were becoming more and more potent.

"Let me concentrate, Morofell."

"Yes, master."

Based on what Demiseth had said, and his own analysis of these powers, Jace could more or less guess why this incantation had caused Morofell to be suddenly enhanced by both shadow and flame at the same time.

His own shadow affinity had actually been suppressed ever since he'd been embraced by the Dragonqueen — after all, Alexstrasza's essence was simply too powerful.

So from then on, when Morofell drew on his mana, she'd drawn more from the essence's power.

But using this incantation to boil his blood and awaken the resonance with his minion, Jace could feel his shadow power being roused once again. That darkness hidden deep within his heart rose to the surface once more, reaching out a hand to extend an invitation to him — and all he had to do was simply take that hand…

Jace hadn't expected that Demiseth Kraus' incantation would make him discover so much about the relationship between the powers within his own body, and the relationship between him and his minion.

If not for the struggle between these different forces, he probably wouldn't have been able to figure all this out so clearly.

For a while, he'd even worried that Alexstrasza's life flame had cleansed away part of his shadow affinity. Now it seemed it had merely grown a little dormant beneath the fiery power.

After all, he was just an ordinary mortal. He couldn't stand up to the Red Dragon Aspect's blessing head-on.

Thinking of this, Jace even laughed at himself. Why was he thinking about going up against this blessing? This was the power the Dragonqueen had granted him, and it had already fused with his body.

Perhaps letting his shadow power and the Red Dragon's fire cooperate with each other — or even fuse, like what had happened to Morofell just now — was the perfect path forward.

This was different from the black dragons' Shadowflame. The black dragons' shadow was corruption tied to the Old Gods. He had to walk another path of Shadowflame — one of his own making — rather than following the black dragons' road to ruin.

At this point, Jace thought of Marin's words again. Skip the arcane… and speak directly to fire. He had to admit, the shadow had already been doting on him quite enough, and Jace was grateful for that doting.

But his other power — the fire elementals — though willing to dance with him, was still wild, waiting for him to tame it. It looked like making contact with the Thorium Brotherhood was unavoidable.

But first, let him finish the matter at hand.

Jace looked down at the scroll in his hands, tossed all these random thoughts to the back of his mind, and recited the incantation once more. "Miyoni, Zharuonu, Morokaerio."

He roused the shadow and fire powers within himself and waved a hand toward the succubus beside him. Morofell let out a low moan, the bright flames in her eyes were instantly replaced by dark red flames, and she gritted her sharp teeth in pain, the grinding of her fangs squealing. The bristles on her arms and hooves all caught fire, and her hair instantly ignited into crimson flames!

In that instant, her body even swelled in size, like a true demoness who'd walked out of a burning hell that didn't even exist in this world — Morfessalithor, a name so long and grand only her lengthy true name could match the visage of such a mighty demon lord.

Bright red blood trickled from the corner of her mouth. She barely managed to steady herself on her hooves, which were now glowing a purplish-red, observed the taut muscles on her arms, and stroked her chest and neck, slowly closing her eyes as though savoring this state immensely.

Amidst the fire and shadow, Jace even felt for a moment that he saw the shiv-maw he'd encountered in that mine in the Blasted Lands.

But while Morofell's expression slowly shifted from pain to enjoyment, Jace was the first to give out. He felt his mana burning away even faster than dry kindling in a sudden blaze — it had been exhausted in just one go.

Of course, this didn't mean the Demon Empowerment magic was a more mana-intensive incantation than the Hand of Gul'dan. Jace guessed that he was currently still unskilled at casting this magic, and had been too earnest about it, while Morofell's reaction on the other end had been too intense — resulting in excessive consumption.

So next, he'd need to get a little more familiar with it, turning it into a spell he could cast normally, rather than a trump card as draining as the Hand of Gul'dan.

He released his focus, and Morofell's body rapidly shrank back down, quickly returning to a form a little shorter than Jace, and she collapsed onto the ground. Lowering her head with a smile, she rubbed her face against the ground and asked, "Heh… when are we doing that again, master?"

Jace caught his breath, picked up a cup beside him, and took a big swig of ale to soothe his throat. By now, scalded by all sorts of dark flames, even the wine had turned hot.

"We'll see when there's a chance," he said.

"In that state just now, I'm confident I could take down Vereesa Windrunner," Morofell said softly.

"What?" Jace turned his head sharply. "Take her down for what?"

"A duel," Morofell said.

"You still remember that?" Jace rubbed his eyes in exasperation and sat down on a stool nearby. "That was all just a joke, wasn't it?"

"I love a challenge, master, and Vereesa Windrunner is a suitable opponent," Morofell said, bracing herself against the wall and standing up. "I want to cross swords with her… it has nothing to do with fighting over you. It's purely for the sake of battle."

"Fight over… me?" Jace shook his head. "Anyway, if you insist on finding a chance to fight her, I won't use this incantation to help you."

"Of course, that was just an estimate," Morofell said, a hint of arrogance creeping into her tone. And it was in this moment that Jace was reminded, just a little, that she was a real demon after all.
