Chapter 781: Completing the Pact Alone

Jace chanted again, reinforcing the Suffocation spell, making the shadow chains tighten more and more. The powerful succubus let out another low moan, all the while staring at Jace and curling her lips into an evil, supremely confident smile.

If a man was into this type of woman — strong, all lean muscle and curves, with a tomboyish edge — he wouldn't be able to resist the temptation before him.

"Order her to give up her true name and complete the pact, Ruige. That's how you become her real master."

"True name?" Ruige kept rubbing his fingers together. "What's that?"

"A demon's lifeline. Mastering a true name means earning the demon's absolute trust. You can use a true name to make a demon submit to you without limit. It's also the foundation for controlling a demon and establishing a solid pact," Jace explained. Then he turned to the succubus and said in Eredun, "Tell him your true name, demon. Complete the pact!"

The succubus strained against his dark magic, lifting her head, and replied in Eredun, "Forget it. I won't submit to a weakling!"

"He's your summoner, you clueless thing," Jace said.

The succubus coughed under the pressure of the shadow energy and said, "I would never let this ugly black lump of flesh command me…" Jace snorted coldly. "Tell him your true name now, or I'll send you back with a dismissal incantation!"

"A dismissal incantation?" The succubus laughed. "That's just a return ticket. Go ahead and chant it now. Why would I be afraid of going back where I came from? Anyway, I can't for the life of me figure out what deal you and this dwarf have going on, putting on this ridiculous summoning ritual. I will never obey a weakling like that!"

"What are you two talking about?" the dwarf asked blankly. "Is that the language between demons? Don't keep me in the dark — I'm the one who summoned her!"

"I'm emphasizing that very point, Mr. Ruige," Jace said, glancing at him. "But it seems this succubus has no intention of obeying you. I have a few ways to force her to reveal her true name. Want to consider it?"

"Why?" Ruige said, displeased. "Why did I summon her here if she won't obey me, just because I don't speak her language?! I can learn! Learning another language is no problem at all for me. How complicated can a demon's language be? Is it more complicated than the elemental tongue?"

The succubus couldn't help but burst out laughing — whether at Ruige's naivete, or because she'd compared the complexity of Eredun and the elemental tongue and found it amusing.

Jace shot the Dark Iron dwarf a look. Whatever else this trip had become — and it was getting more absurd by the minute — at least he'd found the right person. Ruige really did understand the elemental tongue, the language of Kalimdor.

"She thinks you're a weakling, Ruige. Maybe you should prove your strength to her," Jace said. "If you're willing."

"A weakling?!" The Dark Iron dwarf's red beard seemed to bristle and swell. He cursed out a long string of words nobody could understand, looked around, and said, "Wait here. I'll be right back. I'll show this bitch who the master around here is, damn it!"

Jace blinked. What was this guy going to do?

A while later, the Dark Iron dwarf came back into the room. He'd changed into a heavy, brownish-red robe, and in his hands was an iron rod, blackened and charred. The head of the rod had a claw-like bracket holding a round ruby.

This ruby was quite extraordinary. Now that Jace had an affinity for flame, he could sense a remarkably primal fire power surging within it.

"You're sure you want to subdue her yourself?" Jace asked.

The Dark Iron dwarf muttered something under his breath, and the tip of the iron staff immediately burst into flame.

"Remember what I told you, Sesso? A long time ago, I was a fire-tender apprentice in Shadowforge City. I never became a true fire-tender among the Dark Iron clan, but that doesn't mean I threw all that away after I left Shadowforge City and joined the Thorium Brotherhood.

"Now let her go, and let her see who the real master is!"

Jace knew that dwarves generally cared deeply about face and honor, that they yearned for battle — and hot-tempered Dark Iron dwarves were basically the dark, amped-up version of every one of those stereotypes.

So he nodded and withdrew his magic. The succubus grinned and vanished from the shadows in an instant. Jace frowned and tried to lock onto her position with Detect Demons — sure enough, she charged straight at Ruige!

The succubus still had that hooked battle-lance in her hand! If she drove it into the dwarf's flesh, then all the effort of coming to Ironforge, finding the Thorium Brotherhood… would have been for nothing. And there was a good chance Ironforge and the Thorium Brotherhood would come after him for it…

Although, according to Galders, there were already plenty of warlocks hiding in Ironforge, and no small number of Dark Iron among them. But from the looks of it, they hadn't been exposed yet.

Still, Jace kept his hand clenched, ready to trust the dwarf. The demon's shadow swooped in front of the dwarf, and the staff suddenly blazed with light. The already-warm air seemed to have the heat ripped away from it in an instant. A great shell of flame gathered around the dwarf's body. The demon materialized behind him and swung at the shell, the impact sending a burst of fire outward. The high heat struck Jace's face with a wave of searing force — the demon had actually been thrown back by the power of that flame!

The Dark Iron dwarf roared and swung the iron staff. The demon raised a wing to block but was knocked back a step, barely melting back into the shadows and vanishing again. Ruige muttered something else, and a scorching shockwave suddenly exploded from under his feet!

The room wasn't small, but it wasn't so big that you had room to dodge after a spell like that hit. Jace hadn't anticipated it — he stumbled back a few steps until he hit the wall, taking the shockwave full force. The sleeves and hem of his robe caught fire!

If he'd known he was going to run into this kind of trouble in Ironforge, he'd have worn Jaleen's void robe…

After he finally managed to pat out the flames, Ruige and the succubus he'd summoned had already traded several close-quarters blows. The demon seemed to be fighting herself into a frenzy, too — after all, she'd mocked her summoner as a waste of space several times over. If she got beaten by that waste of space… wouldn't that be the ultimate humiliation?

But Ruige clearly wasn't as weak as Jace had thought. Probably because when he'd gone out to buy bread earlier, he hadn't expected to come back and have to fight the most famous dark mage in the entire Alliance, so he hadn't made any preparations.

Now flame followed his shouts, coiling around the staff, enchanting and charging it, forming barrier after barrier of fire to block the succubus's attacks — even jetting out to scorch her hooves and wings and disrupt her movement. Soon the demon was on the back foot, retreating step by step. Even when she tried to turn invisible, she was immediately blasted back into view by wide-area fire.

And of course, Jace, watching from not far away, got caught in the crossfire from time to time. He had to chant a simple Mage Armor spell, just to keep from having too thrilling a time as a spectator.

The succubus might have sensed that Ruige wasn't a seasoned warlock, but she definitely hadn't expected that this dwarf who barely knew the first thing about summoning magic was actually a seasoned flame shaman!

By now she'd realized something was wrong, but it was too late. After several failed attempts at a strong assault, she tried to turn invisible again and slip away to find an opening — but Ruige, already familiar with her stealth rhythm, swept the staff across. The ruby blazed with light, and a shock hit the succubus right in the back just as as she was vanishing, knocking her to the ground.

Ruige seized the chance, tossed the staff aside, and pounced. He grabbed the succubus with his bare hands and pinned her to the ground. Flames had erupted in the Dark Iron dwarf's eyes. The dark red hair on his head was more than half singed black. He looked like a raging fire demon that had leapt straight out of a lava pit.

"Tell me your true name, you damn demon! I summoned you! I defeated you! Now you're pinned under me and can't break free — what more do you have to say for yourself?! Even a crazed fire elemental would acknowledge me as its master by now!"

"I… I yield, Master," the succubus said, looking up. "I've seen your strength. I'm willing to serve you. My true name is…"

The demon spoke her true name in a magical voice, low enough that only the dwarf could hear clearly. Jace watched it all unfold, recalling the way Ruige had manipulated magic just now — strengthening himself, protecting himself, suppressing his enemy.

He thought that if he could truly wield flame the way these Thorium Brotherhood members did, or those powerful Dark Iron fire-tenders, he could make use of the blessing the Dragonqueen had granted him and complement the shadow magic he was already skilled at — magic that just wasn't as viscerally destructive.

Too many demons in the Burning Legion possessed extremely high shadow resistance. Every time Jace ran into an opponent like that, he struggled. If he could make up for that shortcoming with fire power, then whether it was venturing into Outland or facing an invasion by the Burning Legion, he'd be far more at ease.

Not to mention, if the Scourge came inevitably, those skeletons, undead, and other creatures of death had far weaker resistance to fire than to shadow.

Jace realized he was getting ahead of himself. He pulled his attention back to reality. Before him, Ruige was grinning dopily, licking his lips. His heavy hands were already roaming back and forth over the succubus's full chest and firm abs, swallowing again and again.

"Good, good. Submit to me, Gaiada. Look at these solid thighs. Is this sweat? Do demons even sweat? Enticing! This is exactly my type. Can I trust you now? Can I trust you now? Answer me, dear."

The dwarf's hand slowly moved lower, groping at his new minion's waist. The succubus's tall, powerful frame made for a stark contrast with this little fellow who stood maybe a meter thirty or forty, making Ruige's fumbling hands look somewhat comical.

But Jace had to admit — at this point, the pact was probably truly complete.

"You can trust her, as long as you don't go creating danger on your own, and you'll be fine," he warned.

"Ah, I almost forgot you were still here, Sesso." Ruige looked up. "What do you mean, 'creating danger on your own'…?"
