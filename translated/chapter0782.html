Chapter 782: The Path of Flame

"Don't try to summon a second succubus," Jace said. "The last guy I know who did that got hanged by his own intestines at home."

"What… what." The Dark Iron dwarf blinked. "Is that some kind of metaphor?"

"Literal," Jace said. "He wanted to summon two succubi and make them fight for his affection. It didn't go very well — or rather, it went a little too well."

Ruige swallowed and looked down at the succubus beneath him.

"He's right, Master. Promise me you'll never fall in love with another sayaad." The succubus gripped the collar of her master's robe as she said it. What sounded like flirtation coming from this ferocious sayaad came out more like an ultimatum.

Ruige said immediately, "I promise I won't. I definitely won't fall in love with any other demon. As long as I have you, that's enough… I'm sure of it!"

"So, when can you teach me the techniques for communicating with fire elementals?" Jace asked.

"Why the rush?" Ruige said, annoyed. "Now that I've got my sayaad, I'll definitely teach you. Can't you let me have my fun first? Get out… give me some time!"

"I'm short on time. I don't have any to spare," Jace said. "You'll have plenty of time to canoodle with this succubus later. Either take me to meet your other Thorium Brotherhood members — the ones who know more about flame — right now, or teach me yourself."

Ruige held the succubus close. "Take me to meet my colleagues? How am I supposed to explain why I brought you there — because you helped me summon a succubus? That's absolutely out of the question! I'll teach you how to wield flame, but not now! I just went through that fight. The least you can do is let me rest a while. Give me two or three days."

Jace's expression slowly cooled, because he could feel the other man was planning to stall as long as possible.

A Dark Iron dwarf was still a Dark Iron dwarf, Thorium Brotherhood or not. Get the benefits, but don't want to pay the price?

"Your name is Gaiada, right?" Jace looked at the succubus.

"What about it?" The demon raised an eyebrow.

"Earlier you said you'd rather be my servant than this Dark Iron dwarf's. Was that the truth, or just part of your seduction magic?" Jace asked.

The succubus glanced hesitantly at her master, then back at Jace. Her gaze softened considerably. Ruige's eyes went wide at once. "What the hell do you think you're doing?!"

Jace lifted his head slightly and flicked a glimmer of shadow light from his fingers. "I can help summon this demon, sure — but I also have plenty of ways to claim her for myself. Ever heard of the Enslave Demon incantation?"

At those words, the succubus sat up as if she'd suddenly become very interested, completely disregarding her master's feelings.

Ruige immediately panicked and said, "You said a warlock can't control two succubi! You already have your own succubus, don't you?"

Jace said, "I'm sure my lady companion would much rather I punish you first, you self-important, promise-breaking piece of work."

"What promise-breaking, self-important?! Don't you talk about me like that!" Ruige scrambled off the succubus at once. "Ugh, don't be like this, Sesso! I said two or three days! I just wanted to be lazy for a bit. Who knew you'd get angry so easily. You seemed like a pretty easygoing guy, too. Besides, communicating with elementals takes patience. Elementals aren't going to respond to you just because you snap your fingers…"

"Patience?" Jace snorted. "You want me to believe your people have any such thing as patience?"

Ruige frowned. "Don't drag the whole clan into it. Fine. You're right. We'll start on the Path of Flame tonight. Of course, if you don't have any talent, or if you're just not a good match for fire elementals, that's not my problem."

He kissed the succubus's dark lips. "Sorry, Gaiada. Your master can't satisfy you right away. If you want to blame someone, blame this demon master here! He won't give me any time to be with you!"

"Should I wait here for you, Master?" the succubus asked.

"Yes! Yes!" the dwarf said excitedly. "You're so considerate! I've never met a woman who's been this good to me, this obedient!"

"Demon woman," Jace corrected.

"Demon woman — what's the difference?" Ruige swallowed. "You think a dwarf woman wouldn't stab you in the middle of the night, skin you alive, or use some strange power to turn you into something else? Ha… who knows which one's more evil? I bet you've never heard the tale of the witch Modgud."

With that, he stood up, dusted himself off, picked up the iron staff, and took a deep breath. "Follow me, Sesso." In truth, Jace breathed a sigh of relief too.

As much as he'd threatened, he hadn't really intended to go through with it. Even if it came to the worst, the most he'd do was kill the succubus — just enough to make this Dark Iron dwarf understand that things weren't entirely under his control.

Gaiada… just the smell of her in the air made Jace extremely uncomfortable, let alone making any kind of intimate contact. The fact that Ruige had caved so easily had made everything a lot simpler.

Then again, having your succubus taken from you was still a devastating blow to any warlock — especially when the succubus herself was wavering.

Leaving that cramped room and heading toward the end of the corridor, the temperature climbed steadily. There weren't even any braziers here, but it was still bright as day.

Coming to the chamber at the very end, there was a narrow window facing outside. Jace looked through it and saw a river of lava.

It reminded Jace of the Dark Iron city corridors in Blackrock Depths from the game. The deepest parts of Ironforge were full of magma, too. The clanging of hammers on anvils from the heights echoed through the canyon, only to be drowned out by the bubbling gurgle of the lava.

"Come meet the big fellow."

Ruige pointed to the corner of the room, where a black metal golem over two meters tall squatted. If you didn't look closely, you'd think it was just a pile of metal crates stacked up.

He went on, "For a long time, he was the Thorium Brotherhood's helper here. But the one who could activate the golem isn't around anymore. Nobody can operate him, so he'll have to serve as our practice target."

With that, the Dark Iron dwarf raised the iron staff and waved it, muttering something Jace couldn't understand. The golem was immediately wreathed in a ring of flame.

The fire didn't last long, though. Driven by elemental fire power, it burned for a while and then quickly went out — after all, the golem's body was nothing but bare iron plates. There wasn't much to burn.

"Like that." Ruige stepped aside. "Let me see what you can do, Sesso. Since you want to learn fire magic, I'm guessing you've already got something up your sleeve."

Jace didn't actually know any techniques for communicating with elementals. All he knew were the incantations the wizards of the Wizard Sanctum had taught him — the Scorch spell and Fireball.

And of course there were Immolate and Searing Pain. Jace had those incantations memorized, but those fel-tainted fire spells… were clearly not suited for communicating with fire elementals.

"Kimibomflosath!" Jace chanted the Scorch incantation, and a ball of flame exploded on the golem's shoulder!

The whole golem shuddered with a boom, and a plate of iron clanged to the ground. Ruige raised his eyebrows in surprise. "That… caught me off guard."

"What?" Jace asked.

Ruige said, "That's the Scorch incantation. I know that one too. Of course, I've got no talent for the arcane — I only learned it out of curiosity from those mages, and it took a hell of a lot of effort. But clearly, when you called on the arcane to guide the flame for you, the fire obeyed more than just the arcane's direction. I could feel the fire elementals willing to answer your call… how is that?"

"Maybe I've got a talent for being a fire-tender," Jace said with a smile.

"Then there was a reason you came looking for us." Ruige nodded. "It's just that the fire elementals can't figure out your motives. They just sensed that you wanted them to appear, so they appeared — which is why that Scorch spell hit harder than expected! But they need to understand your intentions better. They need to understand what you're saying!"

"Come on, listen to me, Sesso," Ruige said. "Repeat after me: Endur, vendal, milg, frag!"

"Endur, vendal, milg, frag," Jace repeated after the Dark Iron dwarf, but nothing happened.

"You're not talking to me, you're talking to the flame." Ruige handed the iron staff to Jace. "Feel the fire drifting in the air. Here in Ironforge, you should be able to sense it easily. You have to talk to it. Don't think it doesn't exist, and don't think you're performing some kind of fake ritual. Talk to it. Let it feel your sincerity!"

Jace looked toward that narrow window, watched the flickering glow of the firelight, and repeated it again. Still nothing.

Ruige frowned. "That shouldn't be. I can feel you really do have some kind of flame affinity. This isn't a complicated request… there should be at least some response."

"What does this phrase mean?" Jace asked.

"It makes your weapon burn with flame and imbues it with fire's power," Ruige explained. "I mean, what does the phrase itself mean, literally."

"Ah, right, I should have told you… It means — 'Flame, bless what I hold. Burn!'" Ruige clicked his tongue after he said it. "Saying it in Common really is embarrassing."

"I understand," Jace nodded.

He looked toward the window again, gazed at the firelight, and said with full sincerity, "Endur, vendal, milg, frag!"

Suddenly he felt a violent surge of heat rush up from his waist. Skysong's scabbard was instantly incinerated, and the sword clattered to the ground. Jace dropped the iron staff and pulled the burning Skysong to him with a spell, slowly lifting it.

The blue sword was now wreathed in crimson fire — and not just a flash, but burning steadily. The contrast was so striking that it felt like something out of a dream.

"I almost forgot — this is my real weapon."

Simply speaking the elemental language wasn't nearly enough. You had to understand what you were saying. More importantly, you had to make the elementals grasp what you were trying to express… This wasn't a matter of lip movements or vocalizations. It was true mutual understanding.

The Dark Iron dwarf beside him was already staring, transfixed. He stared at the sword and said, "Wait here while I find someone to borrow that elemental language tome, Sesso."
