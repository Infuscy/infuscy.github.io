## Chapter 791: Attempting the Summoning Ritual

That afternoon Jace arrived at the Slaughtered Lamb, exchanged greetings with Jarel, and headed down to the basement. The moment he entered the hall he caught a whiff of an unusual shadowy aura.

It wasn't the sort voidwalkers gave off, nor was it black dragon — it felt more like the hollow, echoing remnant of shadow left behind after countless void summonings. You'd catch a scent like that occasionally during demon summoning, but down here in the basement the aura was especially pronounced.

Jace felt a vague unease creep over him, until he spotted Ursula's Basadim lying in the passageway watching him, and he relaxed.

Coming to the passage that led to the alchemy lab, he found Ursula lying there reading a scroll. She glanced over at the sound of footsteps. "Jace?"

"It's me," Jace said. "There's a strange smell in the basement."

Ursula sat up, sniffed the calm cauldron, turned to sniff the robe hanging to the side, then lifted her arm and sniffed herself. "Is it me? I can't smell a thing."

Jace said, exasperated, "Even if it's not you, you sit still down here every day without moving — you wouldn't notice if the basement filled with a strange smell."

"What nonsense," Ursula said, displeased. "I do go for walks by the canal to exercise."

"It's not the smell of potions, and it's not sweat. It's the smell of shadow magic."

"Ah, right, yes," Ursula said. "Jace, learning the summoning ritual has gone more smoothly than expected. We've already managed to successfully perform the summoning ritual. The incantation works, the circle works — everything is exactly as recorded in that grimoire you gave us. Granted, we only summoned Mr. Basadim from the alchemy lab into the practice room. We haven't actually tried summoning a person yet. Zardeth wants to wait until you're back so we can try together."

So that was it. The void aura in the basement was the result of their repeated attempts at the summoning circle — that explained it. Granted, they'd only moved a felhound a short distance, but it was still a breakthrough from zero to one.

"That's already exceeded my expectations. Did Zardeth lead the summoning ritual?" Jace asked.

Ursula gave a mysterious smile. "Not him — surprising, right? We were all surprised. The first person to successfully lead the summoning ritual was Jalane."

"Her?" Jace said. "I never even imagined she'd be this enthusiastic about this magic."

Ursula said, "She had a real talent for spatial magic back in magic school. You might not know this, but she was quite skilled with Blink — she just never cast it after joining the Dagger Society. Ever since you brought the summoning ritual incantation here, she's been studying it carefully in the basement every day, even more keenly than Zardeth. I think it's brought her back some of the joy she'd lost outside of enchanting and tailoring."

"And who assisted with the summoning?" Jace asked.

"Zardeth, of course," Ursula said. "And Surena. She's quite enthusiastic about magic that doesn't involve fighting demons, and she cooperates well with Zardeth and Jalane. I think if Surena Caledon weren't so lazy, she really could become an excellent warlock."

"I don't doubt that for a second," Jace said. "The problem is she's terrified of every demon except Gakres. I thought Gakres would help her overcome that a bit, but it hasn't helped much."

"At least Gakres cured her interest in ordinary men," Ursula said. "She won't get played for a fool by someone like Gakin anymore. That's not bad — good for her, and good for the whole Dagger Society."

"Does Erlan not mind?" Jace asked.

"You talk as if this is the first day you've met our Mr. Drudgemoor down here," Ursula said with a wry smile. "Didn't you see Surena and Jalane when you came down? They're both in the practice room over there."

"Really? I came straight here when I came down," Jace said.

"Go have a look. They know more about the summoning ritual than I do. I still need to memorize this recipe…" Ursula said, shaking the scroll in her hand.

Jace nodded. He'd actually been a little worried earlier that he'd stepped on a psychological landmine of Ursula's — the issue of her half-elven blood. But after chatting a bit, it seemed Ursula hadn't dwelled too much on that last conversation, so he felt a little more at ease.

Coming to the other side of the basement, sure enough he could hear Surena chatting with Jalane before he even got close. Arriving at the doorway of one of the practice rooms, Jace noticed it was already covered in a complete summoning circle — identical to the one he'd seen on the Burning Steppes. Every assistant's position, every rune, was exactly as he remembered it.

Even though the summoning ritual grimoire that Mor'zul had brought contained fairly detailed instructions for drawing the circle, reproducing it this accurately was quite a feat — especially for a circle this complex. This had to be Zardeth's handiwork.

Back then, the summoner Ely had used this very circle to summon Morophil from Stormwind to the orc camp on the Burning Steppes. He wondered if, someday, this group could use the same circle to summon Kallrez to his own mage tower?

"Jace?" Jalane, who'd been looking this way, spotted Jace first and said eagerly, "You're finally back! We're all prepared — we've just been waiting for you to witness the first attempt at the summoning ritual."

There really was something different in her eyes. She genuinely seemed interested in this summoning ritual. Jace said, "I can't wait either. I actually practiced half a day myself — I thought I might be able to help."

Surena smiled and said, "I'm quite important in the summoning ritual, so just you watch. I'll go get Zardeth. We'll try the summoning ritual tonight — how does that sound, Jace? You should be free tonight, right?"

Jace had originally planned to go check on Grede's place tonight, but since everyone in the Dagger Society was this eager to try the summoning ritual, and he was looking forward to them being able to perform a summoning independently, he agreed.

Normally the Dagger Society's gatherings involved a proper big meal, but tonight nobody had the mood to eat slowly — after all, everyone was thinking about trying to summon a person with the summoning ritual as soon as possible.

After a simple meal, the Dagger Society members gathered in the practice room with their demons. Zardeth looked over those present and said, "That should be about everyone. Jalane, we can begin."

"Why isn't Erlan here tonight?" Ursula asked Surena. "Didn't you find him?"

"We're summoning him tonight, did you forget, Ursula… He's in Goldshire right now, waiting for our call."

"Oh… my God, my head is full of the recipe I memorized today." Ursula shook her head. "Forget I said anything."

Zardeth said, "Anything else we should watch out for, Jace?"

Jace said, "You all have more experience than me, honestly. After all, I've only assisted in a summoning once so far."

"Then let's try starting," Zardeth said, looking toward Jalane.

Jalane Ayrole took a deep breath, nodded, and walked to the center of the circle. Surena and Zardeth also moved to their respective positions. The air suddenly grew still.

Jalane glanced at the scroll recording the incantation, closed her eyes, and began channeling the magic. Jace listened to her chant — it was almost identical to what he'd memorized, including the pronunciation, which was quite good. It looked like she'd studied the Demonic language materials he'd left behind seriously.

When the circle began to flicker, Jace felt the transmission of void energy. Zardeth and Surena then joined in, assisting Jalane with perfecting the ritual. Jace fixed his eyes on the tailor in the center and felt her mana was more than sufficient — performing this summoning ritual wasn't taxing her at all. Truly a student from the Alterac magic school; her foundation was much sounder than that of the second-rate warlocks here.

A shadow slowly appeared in the middle of the ritual — Erlan must have already felt the summoning magic and was responding to the summons.

But right then, Surena frowned. Jace keenly noticed something was off. She seemed to be burning through her mana too fast!

Damn.

What was going on? They'd summoned a felhound successfully — if Surena's mana was stretched thin, why had they decided to summon a person?

Still, Jace remembered that when he'd assisted in summoning Morophil, he'd also felt his mana being severely drained. It had been quite uncomfortable at the time. The assistant's role wasn't as complex as leading, but it wasn't easy work either.

Surena gritted her teeth and kept chanting. The surrounding silence was deathly — nobody dared make a sound that might disrupt the ritual, especially at this most critical moment.

Erlan…

Kallrez's summoning had been forcibly cut off by Mor'zul, and a doom lord that powerful had nearly been crushed to death by the void gate. How could Erlan withstand that kind of mishap?

Jace glanced at Morophil. Morophil had clearly sensed the problem too.

She looked toward Gakres in the distance. The pleasure demon glanced at Morophil, then looked at his master, as if understanding something.

He came up behind Surena, spread his arms, and chanted some incantation. Jace felt a stream of mana from his body pour into Surena's. The slightly weakened Surena brightened again and her posture steadied.

In the center of the summoning circle, Erlan's figure grew clearer and clearer. When he truly arrived here, he vomited a mouthful of blood on the spot.

"I'm sorry… I'm sorry…"

Surena broke free from the summoning ritual and apologized in a panic. Ursula sprinted from the alchemy lab with a potion powerful enough to drag someone back from death's door and handed it to Jace. Jace immediately helped Erlan up and got him to drink it.

Erlan trembled as he downed the whole bottle, then finally let out the breath he'd been holding in. "This time I've really… sacrificed myself for the Dagger Society," Erlan said between coughs.

Jalane, who'd been on the verge of grinning from the success of the summoning ritual, had her face drained of color by Erlan's condition upon arrival. She stood rooted to the spot.

She looked at Surena, then at Zardeth. Zardeth said, "This was too dangerous. Without Gakres, it's unimaginable. Summoning a person requires far more mana than summoning a felhound. By no means can we call this a success. We must continue to refine the summoning ritual."

"Glad to be of service," Gakres said with a smile, as if nothing had happened — a stark contrast to his master, who sat on the ground overwhelmed with guilt and fear.

"If that fellow can transfer mana, why didn't he do it sooner? He had to wait for you to prompt him?" Jace asked Morophil in a low voice.

"He cares about his master's safety, but he doesn't give a damn whether Erlan Drudgemoor lives or dies," Morophil replied.
