## Chapter 814: The Marsh in Flames

The airship scraped against tree trunks and branches, flames dragging through the forest. Even the sturdiest structure, after being burned for so long and struck by a black dragon... could no longer survive this kind of scraping and dragging.

The envelope and gondola quickly broke apart and collapsed. Jace had no choice but to let go and plummet down before the branches continuously smacking his body could knock him unconscious. The last thing he saw before hitting the ground was the airship crashing into the depths of the marsh with a plume of smoky fire.

After bouncing off several branches in the trees, Jace finally fell by a small stream. Every part of his body hurt so much he could barely get up. He just wanted to lie down and go to sleep right there.

A burst of birdsong in the branches sobered him up quite a bit. Birds that dared linger at a disaster site like this had some real nerve. They wouldn't be raptors, would they? Vultures that had followed them from the Barrens?

No, vultures' calls shouldn't be this bright and clear.

He raised his head and observed the treetops, thinking that even if they weren't vultures, birds lingering around an injured person were never anything good. He couldn't just fall asleep and let them have their way with him.

Not to mention, there might be even more black dragons flying this way. And even if there weren't black dragons... Dustwallow Marsh itself was an extremely dangerous place.

He forced himself to his feet, dusted off the dirt, wet mud, and rotting leaves from his body, and looked at his arm that had been exposed during the fight on the envelope.

The skin seemed to have some burns, as well as marks of shadow corrosion — but it wasn't serious. The black dragon blood he'd drained must have helped him recover somewhat. But that last blow from the tail bone still made it painful to breathe, and all the tumbling and battering on the way down from the sky...

Jace checked his potion pouch. The things Ursula had given him were unexpectedly reliable — the vials inside were all intact. He pulled out a Healing Potion and was about to open it and drink it to relieve his injuries when he stopped.

Right now there was only this one Healing Potion in his pouch, because most of the Healing Potions had been with Grede. He and Vereesa had each only brought one for emergencies.

Only now Vereesa didn't know where she had ended up. Could she have fallen off the envelope? Or had she come down with the airship? Was she hurt? Had her potion been destroyed?

Jace looked toward the pitch-black forest ahead and the burning debris scattered everywhere, then thought of Grede too... He'd been gripping those ropes on the airship. Could he have lost his luggage during the violent shaking?

After hesitating for a moment, Jace still pushed the stopper back in.

He felt his injuries weren't serious — at least not to the point of affecting his ability to keep moving. If he ran into some wild animal — like the ferocious crocodiles lurking in Dustwallow Marsh trying to ambush him — he could just use Life Drain to patch himself up.

But if the two of them had lost their potions and gotten hurt, and he drank this guaranteed full Healing Potion himself, that would be a lot more trouble.

Jace tried to stand, nearly slipped on the slick mud, steadied himself, and after standing up, patted the bag at his waist and said, "Sazeno, come out."

The imp cautiously poked his head out and asked, "Is he dead?"

"I don't know. Probably dead."

Jace said this as he looked toward the forest behind him. The airship had left a long trail of fire. It would certainly be quite striking seen from the dark of night. The longer they stayed here, the more dangerous it would be.

He went on, "Your task right now is to help me find the elf's arcane trail. Help me find Vereesa."

"Arcane, arcane... I understand!" The imp looked around and said, "But I can't feel anything, Master. Miss Vereesa Windrunner must have found a way to hide her presence. I can't feel a thing!"

Jace stared at the imp and swallowed hard against the lump in his throat, unable to stop himself from recalling how hard he'd shoved her on the airship. He didn't want to think about it in a bad way, but he couldn't stop his mind from running wild.

If something really had happened because of that shove, he'd probably be in agony for the rest of his life. "Then be careful of other black dragons approaching."

"Understood, Master, understood!" Sazeno said. "But there are no black dragons around. At least not right now!"

Jace nodded. He looked ahead toward where the airship had crashed and figured he should at least go over there first and see if Vereesa had come down with the airship. At the very least, Grede was very likely to be there.

He just wasn't too worried about Grede, because he genuinely couldn't imagine how the crash of an airship could possibly harm a dwarf warrior who had awakened his Avatar.

He observed his surroundings, scanning back and forth. By now the birds overhead had vanished. But a faint shadow had appeared in the woods not far off.

When that shadow noticed Jace, two blue lights flickered on. "Jace?"

"Jace!"

Vereesa came sprinting across the stream and the low slope as if she was about to throw herself hard into his arms. But right in front of him she stopped again, hesitating for a moment.

She seemed to sense that Jace's legs and feet weren't in great shape yet and worried that her rough movements might aggravate his injuries. And so the elf just stood there, frozen, staring at him in a daze.

Jace couldn't help but look at her a few more times. Her originally clean face was now covered in soot and ash, streaked with tear tracks. The arcane glow in her eyes seemed to be moistened too, like the reflection of the moon in a lake.

Is she really mine?

Jace couldn't help but think.

"How are you? Are you hurt?" Vereesa asked anxiously.

"I'm fine. Those birds... were they you calling them to find my trail?"

"Yes."

"That explains it."

Jace said this as he looked her over, only then noticing that one of her trouser legs had been dyed a dark color, gleaming wetly in the firelight.

"Blood?"

Vereesa noticed Jace's gaze and lowered her head to glance. She wiped her eye and murmured, "Don't worry, it's nothing."

"What happened?"

"Just now when I fell off the envelope, I got slashed by a piece of metal up there."

"Same here, though I got off a bit better than you." Jace pulled out the Healing Potion and handed it over. "This is for you too."

"I haven't drunk mine yet." Vereesa smiled and shook the Healing Potion vial in her hand. "I thought you might need it more."

"What, you think I couldn't take that dragon?" Jace asked.

"I certainly don't doubt your strength." Vereesa drank half the potion and said in a joking tone, "It's just that watching you try to keep your balance on the envelope, I had a feeling if you'd fallen with me, you would've had a pretty nasty crash."

Thinking of how pathetic he must have looked tumbling from the envelope into the treetops, Jace had no room to argue and could only sigh softly.

"Let's go to the airship. I hope Grede and Morofir are all right," Vereesa said. Jace nodded and followed the ranger's footsteps.
