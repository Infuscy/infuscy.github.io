Chapter 817: Dragonkin News

In the morning, the sun shone through the thick humidity, illuminating the entire marsh in a dull yellow.

The mist drifting through the marsh moved slowly. Jace looked back toward where the airship had crashed and found he could no longer see the firelight from there.

The succubus's shadow always followed at a short distance behind the group. Although she couldn't be seen with the naked eye, Jace could clearly sense her presence and condition through the Soul Link.

Morofir had never given a signal warning the group of enemies following them. Perhaps by morning, the black dragons had lost interest in pursuing them anymore.

However, Jace only relaxed for a moment. Vereesa, who had been leading the way up front, suddenly raised her hand for them to stop. Grede and Jace immediately found a large tree to use as cover. Vereesa's figure nimbly leaped up a tree and vanished without a trace.

Jace picked up the necklace on Grede's neck and took a look. A full night had passed since Grede's last transformation. By now the necklace had dimmed to the point where no light was visible at all.

Of course, if the necklace were still as brilliant as when Grede summoned the earth elementals, the dragonkin searching for them in the surroundings would have long since pinpointed the group's location with precision.

Grede pointed at his stone axe and mouthed silently: "If worst comes to worst, we fight." Jace gave him an exasperated white glare.

Not long after, the sound of clanking armor came from the distance, growing from faint to clear. At first, Jace thought it was some monster living in the marsh making a rhythmic, hacking roar.

Between the shadows of the trees, an enormously tall black dragonkin warrior swaggered closer on four legs. He was covered in heavy dark-red armor, layer upon layer. Every step made the armor shudder and chafe against each other, giving off a strange noise.

His armor was extraordinarily ornate. The dark-red plates even had golden decorative patterns around the edges, setting off the black skin they covered, appearing quite majestic. And flowing over it was a dark enchantment Jace could clearly sense.

The power of that dark enchantment was extremely strong, stirring up not-very-pleasant memories in Jace. He couldn't help but pull up his sleeve and look at the puncture wounds on his arm that had nearly healed.

Had Onyxia personally enchanted this dragonkin's armor with magic?

If so, then even if he wasn't a powerful dragonkin chieftain, he was definitely one of the dragonkin guards living in the black dragon princess's lair.

But would a lair guard come this far away?

This was truly a blood feud for a murdered father.

Behind this dragonkin followed several much smaller dragonkin. Some held spears, some held cleavers. Bringing up the rear was a dragonkin mage draped in a red cloak. Altogether there were roughly seven or eight of them. But the ones following were all careful. Only the leading dragonkin chieftain's noise could be heard from very far away.

This party of dragonkin was simply too numerous. Although they weren't impossible to deal with, if these dragonkin got a signal out and let the Black Dragonflight know the group was nearby, the road ahead would get a lot harder.

So Jace and Grede exchanged a glance and decided to let them pass. Vereesa up ahead had been quiet the whole time. She wouldn't take the initiative to attack without the two of them here making a move either.

However, that dragonkin chieftain stopped by a puddle of water not far ahead. At this point the dragonkin party was sitting right between Vereesa and the two of them.

Morofir, sensing something wrong on this side, had also pressed closer. She was now crouching beside a tree to the south. Which meant the group of dragonkin were being surrounded by the four of them in a semicircle. So they shouldn't have sensed the presence of outsiders here — otherwise they never would have swaggered so openly into the middle of the encirclement.

Grede gripped his stone axe tightly, as if he was itching to charge out and start fighting. But at the same time he tried to keep his breathing even and undetected. Jace also prepared to strike at any moment. But then a voice suddenly came from the dragonkin's direction.

"Turn around," the dragonkin captain said.

Dragonkin Draconic actually sounded a lot simpler. Jace guessed it wasn't because they enunciated clearly or spoke more standardly, but most likely because the Draconic he'd learned at the Wizard Sanctum had been learned from dragonkin in the first place.

"Maybe they've left," said the one who spoke this line — the dragonkin mage. Although the voice was very hoarse, it could be told to be a woman's voice.

"We must keep searching. Carry out the Devourer's orders!" the dragonkin captain's roar was extremely heavy. Jace almost didn't catch it — especially that word "Devourer."

"Theramore has already paid the price for his impulsiveness, chieftain," the dragonkin mage said. "Our master left orders that we must guard the dragon eggs no matter what. Theramore defied our master's wishes!"

"They are already close. This is an unprecedented threat to the dragon eggs. Theramore's judgment is no different from our master's wishes. Keep searching," the dragonkin captain said through gritted teeth, turning around with the glaive in hand to head back east. The other dragonkin had no choice but to follow his footsteps.

Jace crouched behind the tree, replaying the conversation between these dragonkin in his mind.

His lips moved, silently repeating what they had said, trying his best not to misunderstand. After all, Draconic wasn't as familiar to him as Orcish or Demonic.

But what he was certain of was that the "our master left orders" the dragonkin mage had mentioned was definitely, absolutely correct.

The "master" in their mouths was a feminine pronoun. Considering this was Dustwallow Marsh, then it was almost one hundred percent referring to Onyxia.

The master was Onyxia. She had left orders to protect the dragon eggs. Then did that mean she wasn't in her lair right now?

By now the dragonkin had walked far away. Vereesa came to his side and whispered, "What were they saying, Jace? I think I heard something about a master and such. I know a little Draconic... but it's about the same as not knowing it at all."

"There's a piece of bad news and a piece of good news," Jace whispered. "The bad news is that this is the territory of Deathwing's daughter, Onyxia. The good news is that she very likely isn't here right now. These dragonkin are probably subordinates of the black dragon that attacked us on the airship. They know we were involved in what happened at Grim Batol, so they want to settle accounts with us. But I get the feeling Onyxia's left orders that don't quite approve of them leaving the lair like this to come after us."

"Those dragonkin were only standing there talking for two or three sentences, and you managed to read all that from it?" Grede looked at Morofir beside them, then back over. "What do you actually know — Draconic, or mind-reading?"

"Onyxia," Vereesa said. "I've never heard that name."

Jace suddenly realized he'd accidentally said too much. After all, although Deathwing was famous enough these days, his children still kept as low a profile as possible. Besides the dragons themselves, who had lived for thousands of years and were familiar with them, mortals probably hadn't heard of them.

Still, it was a good thing he was in front of these two people. So he said offhandedly, "That name was told to me by those red dragons. Use your imagination, you know, Grede? Although the dragonkin didn't say much, connecting it with what I already know provided enough information."

"Let's just say you understood what they said. If Onyxia isn't here, then where did she go?" Grede asked.

Vereesa thought for a moment and said, "Could she have disguised herself as another human noble, like another member of the Prestor family? That would actually make a lot of sense. Though it's been a while since I've heard anything from the Prestor side. Ever since Lord Daval went missing, that family hasn't been valued by the Alliance either."

Jace looked at Vereesa and felt she was already groping her way toward the answer on her own.

He organized his words and tried hinting: "I heard the name of a female member of the Prestor family in Andorhal — Katrana Prestor, or something close to that. Have you heard of her?"

"Not really," Vereesa said. "I'm not from Alterac or a Lordaeron native like you. How could I possibly know the noble names over there so clearly. How about you, Grede?"

"I know even less," Grede spread his hands. "I've done work for the Trollbane family in the east, and I still can't quite keep straight the names of all the people in that family, let alone the nobles in the west."

"When you return to Quel'dorei, you can try investigating, Ve," Jace said.

Vereesa nodded and mused for a moment. "Could she have disguised herself as some other mortal, or even disguised herself as a Quel'dorei and infiltrated Quel'Thalas? But I don't think her disguise could quietly fool Ban'dinoriel..."

Grede frowned and said, "What do you mean? You think you're not mortals?"

"That's not what I meant..." Vereesa said helplessly.

"Shh." Jace pressed a finger against his lips. "Your voices are too loud. There are other dragonkin around. We need to get out of here fast. We're already very far from their lair. Maybe if we walk a bit further, they won't pursue us anymore."

Vereesa glanced around, exchanged a look with Morofir to confirm there were no dragonkin following from behind, then picked up her bow and continued forward.

"These arrogant elves look down on us." Grede pointed at the ranger's back and lowered his voice. "See? Your little sweetheart's no different. Her everyday kindness is all fake. One careless moment and the mask slips!"

Jace slapped him on the shoulder. "Stop the crap and move!"

"Going, going!" Grede pulled the helmet he'd been carrying on his back down over his head and ran after them.
