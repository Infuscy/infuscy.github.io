## Chapter 839: Evil in the Snowy Mountains

So this green dragon was searching for the Scythe of Elune alongside Musa Thornmane?

It all clicked for Jace in an instant. The "ally from another world" Musa had mentioned — they were worgen!

Thousands of years ago, the night elf druids had used the power of that staff to grant numerous night elf druids the ability to transform into savage worgen, in order to fight against the satyr's demon legions. But these transformed druids grew ever more frenzied. Even after winning the War of the Satyr, they left behind a grave menace.

The great druid Malfurion Stormrage had been forced to banish the worgen into the Emerald Dream, relying on the Dream's power to calm them. They lived in peace alongside everything else in the Dream, until they fell into slumber.

A decade or so later, during the Battle of Mount Hyjal, the demon legions came to the mountains. Druids found the scythe and awakened the slumbering worgen from the Dream, briefly driving back the demon invasion — but once again brought about the worgen running amok.

It was a weapon where curse and power coexisted. This was precisely why, after the War of the Satyr, the night elf druids refused to speak of it again.

So how did Musa know about this thing? She was a tauren… and had no contact with the night elves at all. "You know that's an extremely dangerous object, don't you, Lady Sola?" Jace said.

His words seemed to land squarely on the green dragon's raw nerve. Jace could even sense the guilt she failed to conceal.

"I know. But I have to find it," Sola said, doing her best to keep her tone level. "That has nothing to do with the mission that brought us to Musa, does it? I don't wish to discuss this matter at length — not even with you, Jace Sesso."

"I understand," Jace said, nodding slowly.

"You freed the Dragonqueen. You defeated Deathwing, Jace Sesso," Sola said, fixing her gaze on him. "I never dreamed I'd encounter you here. With your help, Musa is certainly saved. But how did you come to know her?"

"About a year ago, I'd say," Grede said. "We came to this continent and ran into her. She'd accidentally fallen into a centaur trap and was in serious danger, so we pulled her out. She'd lost her tribe and family by then. When she heard we were going off to hunt demons, she tagged along with us, all the way until we left Kalimdor."

Sola said softly, "So that's how it was. She's mentioned that story to me, but she never gave me your names. I assumed they were just a band of ordinary mercenaries. But what kind of mercenary survives an encounter with a daughter of Therazane?"

Listening to her, Jace figured Musa probably hadn't wanted to talk about them precisely because of him.

Green dragons hated demons. Musa had likely been afraid that saying too much would expose her identity as a demon summoner — and Morophil's existence along with it.

"This time it wasn't carelessness. She got trapped trying to save me," Sola said.

"What exactly happened?" Grede tapped his fingers on the table in quick succession, impatient. "Can you just spit it out all at once? You're killing me here."

The green dragon nodded, though her pace remained unhurried. "That staff has been lost for several thousand years. Only recently did word surface that it had appeared in Winterspring, discovered by the owlbears in the mountains and worshipped as a divine relic. After hearing the news, Musa and I set out to find it. But after we located it, I was caught completely off guard by a magical trap and plunged into sleep. When I woke, I was already in an unfamiliar snow valley."

"She must have been captured by whoever set the trap, trying to save me — because I can still sense her connection to the Dream. She's alive. But when I went back to that mountain afterward, I couldn't find anything at all, and I even got attacked by owlbears. They were completely maddened, full of hostility toward me. I couldn't match their arcane magic alone, so I had no choice but to withdraw and regroup."

"There are plenty of mercenaries in Everlook, but they're either not strong enough or not trustworthy. If I hadn't run into you, I truly wouldn't know what to do."

"Then we set out right now and deal with those owlbears. Damn it," Grede said. Windrunner nodded and reached for her bow.

"Wait." Jace looked at the green dragon. "Do you think the owlbears are behind this? You mentioned evil — what did you mean?"

Sola said, "That trap was woven from shadow magic. I didn't sense that kind of power from the owlbears."

She glanced at Morophil, then continued, "Which is also why I don't want to harm them. I suspect the owlbears' frenzy is very likely connected to the power of that trap. They despise evil, they despise darkness — and they've probably been thrown into agitation by the intrusion of those foul presences. They're likely victims too."

Windrunner asked, "But if we want to figure out where Musa was taken, we have to go back there too, don't we? And we'll very likely run into owlbear attacks again."

"That's true," Sola said. "But it's a risk we have to take. When necessary, we'll simply have to defend ourselves." From the green dragon's words, Jace could tell there was a lot more she wasn't saying.

Grede looked at Jace apologetically and ventured, "Jace… are you still willing to go? Sounds like things might not be that simple."

"What do you mean?" Jace said. "You're asking if I'm willing to save Musa? Have you lost your mind? Musa isn't just your friend."

"Sounds like it'll take a while," Grede said. "I'm worried you'll—"

"You think I came here for what?" Jace rapped the table. "I came here precisely so that a lot of people — especially the ones close to me — wouldn't have to worry about some horrible disaster suddenly appearing in the future. But now my good friend, my brother's future wife is in trouble, and it's staring us right in the face. If I don't go save her, instead I—"

He glanced at the green dragon beside them, realizing he shouldn't say too much about their original plan regarding Ursulus. He pressed his lips together. "You know what I mean, Grede. Don't say things that hurt feelings. You're honestly baffling me."

"He's my brother, all right. What about you, little elf?" Grede asked.

Windrunner shot him a sidelong look. "Get this straight — my bond with Musa isn't any shallower than yours. Ask her yourself if you don't believe me." Grede nodded. "Then… that's settled, that's settled!"

"Thank you for being willing to help," Sola said, her expression relaxing considerably. "If in the end I'm forced to go to Mount Hyjal for help and expose our purpose, Musa might be imprisoned by them, and as for me… I don't know. So one more thing — no matter how it turns out, please keep this a secret."

Jace nodded.

He had no idea where the Scythe of Elune was at this point. After all, in the end it was the night elf druids who found it after the Battle of Hyjal. Even Medivh's treasure hunters didn't know its location.

If Musa really did end up finding this thing and used it to summon worgen to fight the centaurs, it was hard to say whether that would be a good thing or a bad thing. But Musa, in order to resist the centaurs' invasion and protect her dying tribe, probably couldn't afford to think that far ahead.

Then what was this green dragon's reason for wanting to help Musa find the Scythe of Elune?

That seemed to have nothing to do with Musa. The green dragon was clearly hiding something too, but Jace had no opening to press further.

"Even though Musa is our friend, Sola," Jace said, "when it all comes down to it, we're doing you a favor here. The commission is the commission — not a single copper less. And on top of that, there's hush money. After all, you don't want Queen Ysera to learn about your whereabouts and your purpose, do you?"

Sola said, "I have a total of twenty-nine gold coins. That's all the money I have right now. And since this whole thing started because of me, I won't sit on the sidelines either. I'll help however I can."

Jace's eyes went wide. "Why does a dragon have so much money?"

"Aren't dragons supposed to be rich?" Grede said. "Otherwise why are there so many legends about great dragons guarding hoards of treasure?" Jace muttered, "Those aren't the same thing…"

Sola explained, "I sold some of my scales to goblins. Green dragon scales seem to fetch quite a price."

"By the Light…" Windrunner exclaimed. "I've only ever seen two green dragon scales, and those were in Silvermoon's collection hall. A lot of people even questioned whether they might be magically forged, or the scales of green-scaled dragonkin."

"That precious?" Grede whispered.

"One more condition." Jace pointed at Morophil beside him. "If you want to come with us, you'll have to accept working with her — and all manner of dark magic."

At the mention of the succubus, the other two suddenly remembered there was a demon right beside them.

Morophil had been silently observing the green dragon's attitude, saying nothing off to the side, slowly almost making people forget she was even there.

"There's no shortage of spellcasters who enslave demons here in Everlook," Sola said calmly. "Since I came here seeking help, I've already prepared myself mentally. What's more, even Queen Ysera herself is aware that the dark mage Jace Sesso used demonic magic to deceive Deathwing, and thereby managed to free the great Alexstrasza… So I imagine that sometimes it takes extraordinary means to achieve extraordinary ends, doesn't it?"

Morophil smiled. Jace nodded as well. "Then I have nothing more to say."
