## Chapter 868: The Demonic Sword

"Duk, Heduonilomus!"

Jace stood up almost simultaneously and cast Asphyxiate. Powered by the Distilled Wisdom elixir's formidable mana, shadow chains spun rapidly into existence and bound the black knight leader Eredin, slamming him to the ground.

Eredin growled. The several black knights around him turned toward them in unison. Beneath their dark hoods, sickly yellow necrotic glimmers flickered.

Almost at the same time, the other side of the room suddenly exploded with a burst of lightning. A battle hammer trailing arcs of electricity was hurled violently into the midst of the group. The flash it emitted lit up the entire hall in an instant.

The earthen, wreathed in crackling lightning, charged in straight after. He caught the returning battle hammer and crushed one black knight's torso with a single blow. The other black knights drew their blades and pole hammers to meet the dwarf's swung hammer again — and actually held their ground against the strike!

Thunder elements and shadow force howled and scattered throughout the hall. Even the Dream was affected — the forest that had been as heavy as ink began to stir faintly.

The black knights had barely dispersed their formation when a white furbolg suddenly pounced out of the darkness, thrusting a short spear into one of them and pinning him against the wall. But this black knight couldn't care less about being run through. He swung the war hammer in his hand and brought it down on the furbolg's shoulder.

The furbolg howled in pain and staggered backward. The black knight roared, stepping forward to pursue and smash again — but was struck through the armpit by an arrow trailing arcane light. He stumbled and fell into a pile of rubble, struggling to get back up.

In the span of a few seconds, Jace watched Eredin nearly break free from the Asphyxiate boosted by his elixir. "Inkakweila…"

The shadow chains shattered one after another. Jace immediately cast Cripple on Eredin. Eredin had just raised Apocalypse. His eyes and the runes on the sword's body began to flicker with necrotic dark light. But as the fel power guided by the Eredun incantation wrapped around them, that dark light instantly went out again.

"Warlock…" Eredin spat out the word with difficulty, then gritted his teeth and let out a strained "Mmm… ngh…" His entire suit of armor and cloak was trembling. He was using all his strength to break free of Jace's magic, and Jace immediately felt his power.

Or rather, Apocalypse's power.

The resistance that sword brought was bizarre and terrifying. Right now Jace even had the feeling that it was devouring his mana and consciousness through his own magic.

Jace called upon the power of the fire elementals. One hand wreathed in surging flames, the other gathering a shrieking mass of shadow — at this moment both flame and shadow howled in response to his call, intertwining in his incantations into a blood-red burning core…

The wrappings covering Apocalypse had already been torn apart by shadow force. Jace caught a glimpse of the runes flickering with malevolent green soul-shadows — all ancient Nathrezim script. He couldn't make heads or tails of it.

Before he could finish channeling, the black knight leader had completely shattered the shadow chains. He even forced himself to his feet under the weight of the Cripple.

In the next instant, a bolt of shadow flame accompanied by a scorching whirlwind was hurled by Jace, striking Eredin, who had just stood up, dead-on. The explosion of flames shook the entire room. A nearby black knight who had just been driven back by Grede was even blasted a step off-balance.

But as the blood-red smoke cleared, the first thing Jace saw was the blade of Apocalypse pointing straight at him.

Apocalypse lit up another rune. Eredin stared over here, muttering an incantation. Despite how difficult it was for him to speak, every syllable gritted through his teeth under Cripple's restraint.

Jace immediately hurled another Asphyxiate, trying to choke off the enemy's spellcasting. But this time Eredin seemed to be enhanced by Apocalypse. Even though shadow choked his throat again and his incantation turned into a rasping, croaking cry, a halo of soul-darkness still spun around this black knight whose entire body was engulfed in blood-red flames, gradually solidifying into another Apocalypse!

The moment this Echo of Apocalypse appeared, it spun and sliced straight over here — practically a soul-blade flying through the air. Jace reacted extremely quickly, chanting the spell for Dark Shield. But Apocalypse shattered the shield with one strike. The second blow was deflected by Jace barely parrying with Azuresong, using the recoil of the broken Dark Shield.

The golden radiance of the Consecrated Oil and the blue light of arcane energy interwove into a brilliant gold-green — only to be devoured whole by the soul-darkness of the Echo of Apocalypse.

The darkness of the Echo of Apocalypse swallowed his vision. The conspicuous blade rose up right before his eyes, clearly preparing to deliver a third strike with full force. Jace knew all too well that given the speed at which the opponent was bringing the blade down, he wouldn't be able to dodge it. He could only dissolve into a shadow and retreat backward to the wall. But this runic weapon didn't pursue. Instead, it spun and flew back, straightening its trajectory toward Grede, who was fighting two opponents at once!

Grede had just endured a round of attacks from two black knights. He'd found an opening to counterattack when he saw the Echo of Apocalypse coming straight at him. He had no choice but to raise his shield to block. The Echo of Apocalypse slammed hard into the Dragonkin Shield with a nauseating, grating noise, pushing Grede — even in his earthen form — back several steps before dimming and dissolving into wisps of soul-fragments that scattered in every direction.

Seizing the opening created by Grede's loss of balance, the black knights stepped forward and raised their weapons again. One of them was pierced through the head by a volley of arcane arrows and stumbled, unable to keep up. Another engaged Grede in close combat once more. These black knight subordinates possessed considerable strength too.

Jace had no energy to spare for what was happening over at Grede's side. Seizing the window while Cripple was slowing Eredin and making his spellcasting extremely difficult, he chanted the incantations for Curse of Agony and Corruption in quick succession, piling every spell that could inflict torment and suffering onto this black knight.

Eredin's entire body was wreathed in green fel light. Cripple restricted him from joining the offensive. Under the tearing force of the warlock's dark magic, his skin rolled and churned as if boiling, and purple dark blood seeped from his mouth as he coughed, again and again.

But even so, he still raised Apocalypse. Jace watched his movements and felt as though the hands holding the sword had become disconnected from his body.

The runes on Apocalypse's blade lit up one after another. In the center of the hall, Eredin tilted his head — his skin already beginning to break apart — and chanted an incantation once more. His cloak and armor were burning, making his head, which flickered with necrotic light, look like a skull floating amidst flames.

He raised Apocalypse again. Another Echo of Apocalypse spun into existence in the air, this time flying toward Grede once more. Grede was being attacked in rotation by three black knights. He'd just blocked a chop from a black knight wielding a pole axe and taken a step back when he saw the Echo of Apocalypse flying straight at him.

Grede's expression tightened. It was too late to block — or so it seemed. But then he saw it caught mid-air by a thread of pale blue soul drain, slowing its pace. Only then did the dwarf barely manage to dodge.

The one who'd grabbed the Echo of Apocalypse was Molofel!

She had originally been with Vereesa, blocking two of the three black knights who were trying to attack Jace. One of them had already been stabbed into a honeycombed, rotting corpse by the two of them and collapsed to the side. When the succubus saw a shadowy blur fly past, she gave chase and caught the spinning Echo of Apocalypse with a soul drain, pulling at its flight.

She chanted the incantation of Sayath in her mouth. She could see the power of that demonic sword declining rapidly. The Echo of Apocalypse clearly didn't want to let her have its way. Having failed to hit Grede, it turned and flew straight at her.

The succubus had no choice but to interrupt her spellcasting and slip into the shadows. Apocalypse hacked down hard into the wall behind her, leaving a deep cyan-blue soul-scar before vanishing.

This chop shook the entire hall. Vereesa, who was locked in combat with a black knight wielding a longsword, along with her opponent, swayed a few steps to steady themselves. Here on Jace's side, the wall that had already been torn by magic shed its surface. Jace dodged a collapsing pillar. Ahead, Eredin was once again trying to raise the demonic sword with effort.

Jace chanted the Asphyxiate incantation once more, suppressing Eredin, who was trying to channel power through Apocalypse again. Eredin was seized by the shadow chains by the throat and coughed, dropping to one knee. He propped himself up with the sword and cursed furiously in demonic tongue.

Even though he'd succeeded again, this time Jace felt that his mana was barely enough to suppress Apocalypse's power.

That's right — he wasn't fighting Eredin at all. He could clearly sense that his true opponent was that sword. That demonic blade forged in the Twisting Nether.

That demonic sword seemed to have a will of its own. Even Eredin couldn't control it. Eredin was coughing incessantly from Jace's magical suppression, looking powerless and weak. But Apocalypse shone with unbridled ferocity, the runes on its blade flickering with wave after wave of light.

As the light manifested, shadowy ghosts gathered from every corner of the hall. One of them extended its claws and darted straight at Jace!

Vereesa was drawing a bow and aiming at Eredin when she saw a soul suddenly pounce from the side. She quickly turned and pierced it with a magic arrow. Although the soul had been considerably weakened by arcane energy, it still flew right up to her. The ranger simply drew the longsword at her waist and swept it through with holy light, flattening the specter.

On Jace's side, he used Dark Shield to hold off the mad clawing of the specter. The moment Dark Shield shattered and the shadow light burst outward, startling the soul, he ran it through with a single sword strike, destroying the specter's form with the Consecrated Oil.

But the specters in the hall were gathering in growing numbers. Not a few of them were furbolg spirits. There were even satyr demon-souls among them. Some darted in through the doorway. Others even seeped forth from the cracks in the walls and floor.

Kenluk was instantly surrounded by several soul-shadows. Vereesa wanted to go to his aid but was immediately blocked by a number of soul-shadows. Jace burned to death one of the specters chasing Vereesa with a wall of flame — then felt a chill behind him. He turned and cleaved through this specter attacking from behind with a single slash, only to have a black knight charging at him head-on with empty hands.

The black knight's eyes had already lost their necrotic glimmer, becoming dim and hollow. He growled and grabbed Jace's sword. Jace yanked it free and cut off his legs and feet in a single strike.

This black knight's dark hood had already fallen off. His originally human-like face now looked twisted, like a feral ghoul — full of fangs and drool. Had Apocalypse raised him?

That's right — it wasn't just this black knight who had been reanimated. All those who had been struck down by Grede and Vereesa earlier were getting back to their feet, one by one!

Jace was besieged by souls and reanimated black knights. The magic of Cripple on Eredin was consequently becoming much weaker too. Still, the reanimated black knights were clearly not as powerful as before, and they couldn't withstand the burning of Quel'Thalas's Consecrated Oil at all.

Seeing the light of the Consecrated Oil, Eredin clearly became agitated and impatient. His cloak had already been burned away, and his armor, scorched by shadow flame, was falling off in pieces, tattered beyond recognition. His already withered hair had completely disappeared, exposing a head covered in burn marks and scalds. Dragging his body, restricted by Cripple, he walked step by step with difficulty toward Grede, who was nearby.

Jace wanted to cast on Eredin, but another soul had already latched onto him. The extreme cold right now even felt scalding hot. His skin felt as if it had been suddenly burned, and his whole body shuddered. In that instant, dread and fear gripped his heart.

But he gritted his teeth and forcibly dispelled the torment of these shadows with sheer willpower. He turned and hurled a wall of flame at the soul that had just darted past him. The soul burned, shrieked, and dove into the shadows. Only then did he get the chance to glance at Eredin from the corner of his eye — just in time to see Grede, who had knocked to the ground the last black knight he'd been grappling with, look up to meet Eredin, who raised Apocalypse and brought it down on his shield with a single chop!

The shield was immediately split open by a burning cyan-blue gash. Grede yelled — whether out of surprise or anger was unclear. Erenin's next strike he had to block with his battle hammer — only to have the hammer cleaved clean apart by that single blow!

Even the soul power burned onto the hand holding the hammer. Jace saw his originally metallic arm and fist rapidly regress to flesh-and-blood skin, even beginning to swell and fester.

Grede threw aside the bent hammer shaft and roared, raising his shield with one thick arm and one thin to block Eredin's next strike. That strike cleaved the shield in two as well. Grede hurled the shield aside and called upon lightning with both hands, charging forward to grab Eredin's arms and grapple with him.

The Neltharion necklace at his throat flickered with golden light, and the lightning on his body began to run wild. His hand, which had regained its flesh, once again gleamed with metallic luster, and even the swelling and festering showed signs of receding. Who could have expected that with every flicker of Apocalypse's runes, Eredin's entire body became more swollen, even growing in size. His armor expanded along with him, allowing him to fight Grede one-on-one without falling behind!

"Death, death, death!"

Eredin chanted this word in demonic tongue incessantly, like an apocalypse-bringer who knew nothing but killing. Grede could only cough and roar, "Help! Help me! Goddamn it!"

"Master, I'll deal with these ghosts… go help the dwarf!"

Molofel stood before him, the blades in both her hands blazing with the brilliant flames of the Red Dragonflight. For a moment, the ghosts surrounding them were actually frightened into retreating.

Jace nodded and quickly cast Curse of Weakness on Eredin. This time Grede gained the upper hand again, pushing the black knight back a few steps. Then Jace cast Bloodfiend's Grasp, catching Eredin's arm and neck and dragging him backward along a thread of blood.

But the moment he pulled, he immediately felt Apocalypse's power firsthand — and exactly what kind of pressure Grede was under.

A terrifying soul force had devoured the power of Bloodfiend's Grasp almost in the blink of an eye and ignited both of Jace's hands. He felt his own eyes erupt in malevolent green flames. agony instantly consumed all other sensation.

In his blurred vision, Grede had already picked up the stone axe on his back and was hacking away at Eredin in a frenzy. The furbolg's roar followed close behind.

Two massive monsters unleashed a relentless barrage of attacks on a single ravaged, emaciated black knight. In Jace's hazy vision, he could barely make out Eredin's shadow. All he could see was the near-mad Apocalypse, like a deranged dancer, resisting the pincer attack of the furbolg and Grede.

Although the furbolg was enormous in size, his movements at this moment were remarkably nimble. But the weapon in his hands was, after all, just an ordinary short spear.

He finally found an opportunity and tried to thrust the spearpoint into the black knight's chest — only to have Apocalypse sweep over and chop off both his hand and the spear together. He howled and backed off several steps.

Grede's stone axe had already lit up with arcs of lightning. Under the flashes of white light, it was like a blade forged from crystal. But Apocalypse still hacked crack after crack into it, one sword strike at a time. The dwarf roared until his voice was completely hoarse.

The shrieking of souls in the hall had already dissipated. The hazy canopy high above swayed violently. The moonlight in the dead of night was extremely dim. Vereesa and Molofel had already killed all the reanimated black knights and wandering souls. In the hall, only Eredin remained.

And… Apocalypse.
