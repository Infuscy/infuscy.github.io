Chapter 9: Bloodblade, Soul

Vereesa's silver hair had been soaked through with black blood, and her face was smeared with gore. Her eyes erupted with arcane light. She roared and raised a pair of blades high, thrusting straight in from the side. But even as Apocalypse knocked Grede back, it hacked over in this direction at a speed that was nearly impossible to follow.

The ranger's reaction was terrifyingly fast too. She raised one long and one short blade to block. The shorter blade immediately shattered, bursting apart in flames. "Miyoni, Zaparoni, Molokerio…"

Jace raised his right hand, its skin already burned cyan-blue by soul power, and chanted the Demonic Empowerment incantation. Molofel charged over from the other side, her entire body engulfed in violent flames, like a furious she-demon. She slammed straight into Eredin and knocked him aside.

The remaining black knight let out a savage howl and hacked over wildly. The demon also went berserk to meet the attack. Flame and soul tore at each other before Jace's eyes, but he could barely tell anymore whether this was the howl of Apocalypse or Eredin's voice.

He had already entered a state of severe mana exhaustion. Although he hadn't cast that many spells, the enemy was simply too powerful, multiplying the difficulty of his spellcasting several times over.

What's more, Apocalypse kept striking back at his magic with soul power, leaving him on the verge of losing consciousness by now.

Jace groped around everywhere but couldn't find his potion bag. He could only grit his teeth and force himself to stand up, barely keeping himself from collapsing. He had to use Soul Siphon again. He had no time to look for his pack.

The incantation was completed. Purple-red shadow force suddenly gathered within the hall and was seized into Jace's hands.

The mana he'd recovered drove away his exhaustion. He felt his consciousness suddenly become clear again. His legs and hands both had strength once more.

Bracing himself against a collapsed pillar beside him, Jace wiped the sticky blood from his eyes and stared toward the light ahead. Then his heart sank.

The dwarf had fallen. A horrifying gash had opened across his face. Purple dark blood trickled from the corner of his mouth as he slumped against the wall. One hand tried to grab the wall to pull himself up, but each time he slipped and fell back into his own blood.

The furbolg had collapsed across the wreckage of the arcane golem. His one remaining intact hand was bracing hard against the stones beneath him, as if he still had the strength to fight.

Eredin was locked in a deadly duel with Vereesa and Molofel. The elf seemed to have inexhaustible energy, but she could only rely on her nimble movements to dodge Eredin's attacks. Her short blade had already shattered. The other longsword in her hand didn't dare meet Apocalypse head-on. So she was completely on the defensive.

However, Apocalypse's runic power seemed to have dimmed somewhat too. It pulled at Eredin's arms to block, to attack. And the black knight's arms had already been torn to nothing but bones draped with flesh, continuing to function only on the soul power that Apocalypse pumped into them…

At least, pressed hard by the succubus and the elf, it no longer had any room at all to cast spells. It could only counter wildly, swinging, chopping, blocking.

Seizing this opportunity, Jace raised Azuresong and, with a Shadowstep, closed the distance behind Eredin. He hacked down hard at the back of his neck. Azuresong sank into the flesh. Arcane power scorched the corrupted, grey skin and rotten meat. Yet the black knight's neck was nearly severed more than halfway through, and soul power still held his head and body together.

Not only that — Eredin seemed completely unaffected. Apocalypse swung over furiously. Jace raised Azuresong with all his strength to meet it head-on. After a piercing shriek of clashing magic, Jace was knocked to the ground. He turned his head and looked at Azuresong, lying to the side — the blade had been hacked with a gash too.

Azuresong's light dimmed considerably. Arcane magic quickly condensed at the notch in the scale of Malygos, as if trying to repair the damage. But Jace's own hands had already been burned to the bone by soul power. The flames of the Red Dragon's blood burned at the edges of the wounds, as if trying to heal them — but they couldn't resist the erosion of Apocalypse's dark power, only bringing him more severe pain.

Every enemy before him was undead. He couldn't use Life Drain to draw away necrotic power — that would be no different from stabbing himself.

Eredin let out a cold laugh, as if it came from the abyss. Vereesa's furious roar was muffled as if through countless layers of walls, impossible to make out.

Not far away, Molofel could no longer continue fighting either. She collapsed weakly to the ground.

The succubus's hands had also been corroded by soul power. But Jace knew she hadn't been wounded by Apocalypse — she'd only taken a share of that blow through Soul Link and ended up like this.

Her entire body burned with flames, repairing the damage of soul power. But that single shared strike had left her almost unable to move. Without her, Jace would probably have been killed on the spot by that one swing of Apocalypse.

Then Jace noticed that, in the hazy Dream, the staff on the pedestal had somehow already disappeared.

A slender green figure holding a long staff suddenly appeared at the doorway. Eredin, who had been pursuing Vereesa, was immediately wrapped layer upon layer by tree roots and vines that seeped down from the cracks in the walls and ceiling!

Eredin shouted and struggled within. The vines were quickly set ablaze by soul power, breaking off and falling away in sheets.

But seizing this opportunity, Sora cried out — a cry no different from a green dragon's roar. Following her call, the entire hall and the world within the Dream were instantly enveloped in waves of starlight and moonbeams.

Jace felt his hands rapidly regain sensation. His mutilated flesh also grew at a visible speed under the nourishment of wave after wave of natural power. Grede, sitting against the wall, began to cough violently. Even the fallen furbolg stirred, pawing at the rubble and bricks…

Such powerful healing magic — this had to be Tranquility, the ultimate nature magic of restoration druids, and likewise the signature move of green dragons.

But Jace wasn't the only one who realized how powerful this healing spell was.

Eredin transformed into a specter and broke free from the vines' restraints, charging straight at the green dragon who had just appeared. Sora was startled. The others even went pale. How could she possibly withstand a single strike from Apocalypse?!

But it was too late to react. Apocalypse slashed over ferociously. The green dragon had no choice but to raise the staff in her hand to block. A dull thud rang out. Sora groaned and fell to the ground. The staff in her hand flew from her grip. The Tranquility spell was interrupted along with it.

"Ursalias, Ursa… lias…"

Eredin stared tightly at the flying skeleton, as if he had only now finally regained a sliver of consciousness and retaken control of his body, which had been enslaved by Apocalypse.

Jace saw the staff fall to the ground — completely unscathed.

It was just a staff… and yet it had withstood a chop from Apocalypse!

Jace forced himself to turn over, ignoring the wounds and pain burned into him by soul power. He reached out and used Bloodfiend's Grasp, snatching it into his hands before Eredin could get to it.

The moment he saw this staff suddenly yanked away by a thread of red, Eredin's expression twisted. He screamed hysterically, "Return Sargeras's treasure! Return Karazhan's treasure!"

He charged over wildly, raising Apocalypse with its flickering runes. Jace chanted the Soul Siphon incantation once more… "Luhravankta, Luarakipta… damn it…"

Shadow gathered and drove away the soul-green flames still burning on his body. Jace used the power from this Soul Siphon to support himself upright. His vision had turned purple-red. Purple-red shadow blood also flowed from the exposed wounds in his hands, burning with fel fire the moment it dripped to the ground.

The black knight's metal boots slammed against the floor as he bore down. Jace steadied himself, then called upon the aid of the fire elementals in Kalimandoran and the aid of shadow in demonic tongue.

Any aid. All aid…

The horned demon's head instantly spewed forth a blood-like substance, condensing into a slender, curved blade of purple-red flame. Jace stared blankly at the staff in his hand — it had already transformed into a sickle with a bone handle and a blood blade!

Eredin raised Apocalypse — or rather, Apocalypse had already dragged the body behind it straight up, lifting its blade. Jace simply swung this sickle with all his might, cutting straight at it!

Although Apocalypse was a legendary demonic sword, it was slightly shorter than the staff. Before his eyes, the purple-red light of bloodfire and the soul blade crossed.

Jace felt his hands go empty. He staggered weakly forward a step… Clang.

The broken blade of Apocalypse fell to the ground. Its runes instantly dimmed. All the souls shrieked and fled. Even the forest in the Emerald Dream was blown by a dark demon wind, swaying wildly.

Apocalypse… was broken?

Jace stared blankly at the demonic blade that had, moments ago, bared its fangs and claws with an air of destroying everything… broken. Eredin, holding the severed hilt, took two steps back, sobbed twice, and said, "Father…"

But very quickly he screamed hysterically again. He drew a long black sword from his waist, trying to stab over once more. Jace swung the sickle without hesitation, cleanly slicing him — sword and all — in two!

The black knight's upper half tumbled to the ground behind Jace by momentum. His lower half knelt in place on the ground, trembling and writhing a few times.

Eredin dragged his half-body, coughing, trying to crawl toward the broken blade of Apocalypse. But he only managed a few feeble inches before tilting his head. His fingers ended up less than half a meter from Apocalypse. The soul support on his arms and neck quickly dissipated with Apocalypse's death. Black blood gushed out. His body tore, broke apart, and collapsed into a pile of rotten flesh.

Jace stared at the black corrupted corpse, but felt a she-demon's whisper in his ear.

It was an incantation in demonic tongue, except he had never heard demonic tongue chanted with this kind of accent — it was almost like a solemn hymn.

Jace could barely understand what these incantations were chanting, but he could almost guess who it was — the former owner of Ursalias, the Eredar necromancer Sathael.

When Sathael was defeated by Skavel, the sickle had harvested its master's soul.

Jace slumped weakly to the ground. The dark mana brought by Soul Siphon had also been exhausted.

The blood-red blade turned into a ball of purple-red flame and burned away. Within seconds, the staff had transformed back into that spine-like shape from before, complete with a hollow, horned demon's head.

The voices of souls slowly faded, until silence returned once more.
