Chapter 870: Will Breaks the Labyrinth

"Jace, Jace, are you all right? Eredin is dead. What on earth happened…"

Vereesa's face was streaked with tear tracks cutting through the bloodstains. She lowered her head and inspected every part of Jace's body, saying as calmly as she could, "Your hands, your, legs… they're all injured."

She'd found Jace's pack from somewhere and taken out a healing potion from within. Jace could see the pack still bore the scorch marks of soul fel fire — the straps had been burned through.

Still, all things considered, the pack was more or less intact.

"Don't waste healing potions on me, Wyn," Jace grabbed her wrist. "Save the potions for…"

"Me," Grede said from a distance, lowering his head and groaning as he suppressed the urge to cough. "He doesn't want his, but I'll take mine. I'm in a bit of pain right now, no… I'm in fucking agony. And it's not just my body that hurts. My heart hurts too. That was my father's hammer. Curse these goddamn undead black bastards, give me back my hammer…"

As he spoke, the dwarf actually started sobbing.

In the gloomy hall, his shoulders heaved. He still seemed to have some strength in him at least, so Jace wasn't as worried. "Don't give him any," Jace said.

"What?!" Grede cursed. "You fucking wait until I've rested up…"

The dwarf tried to stand up. Jace stared at him and said to Vereesa, "Go check on Sora. See how she is. Save our green dragon first. Once she's up, we'll all live."

"Ah… yes, right." Grede lowered his head again dejectedly and sat himself firmly back down.

Vereesa nodded, stood up abruptly, then her legs gave out and she stumbled a few steps before running toward the direction where Sora had fallen by the doorway.

Jace watched her back. Her green cloak had been burned away by a good section. Gaping wounds left by blades were everywhere — from her waist and legs down to her ankles.

If he'd sustained that many injuries, he definitely wouldn't be able to stand. But this Ranger-Lord's movements were still light and powerful.

"Gotta hand it to Quel'Thalas, huh?" Grede said, looking at Jace. "Even if our little elf isn't a real Quel'dorei, at least she's got the blood of those hateful bastards in her veins. She really is a perfect killing machine."

Jace nodded. But listening to Grede's words, he suddenly felt as if he'd missed something. Quel'Thalas, a real Quel'dorei, hateful bastards…

Right — what about Dar'Khan Drathir?

From beginning to end, this bastard Dar'Khan hadn't shown up at all. But if Dar'Khan hadn't connected his clues to Karazhan, how would this group of black knights have come here?

But now that Ursalias had fallen into his hands, even if Dar'Khan showed up here now, there was no way he could deal with their group.

Although Jace had no idea how to use Ursalias's power at all. Right now, to him, this spine-like staff wasn't much different from a long-handled sickle.

Sure, it would be extremely efficient for chopping people, but when Sargeras had personally forged this weapon tens of thousands of years ago, it definitely hadn't been for the purpose of hacking people to death.

Of course, he wasn't hoping for Dar'Khan to show up either. They'd just been through a brutal battle. Injured and maimed all over — and now they had to immediately deal with a powerful archmage?

"Sora, Sora!"

Vereesa's shouts interrupted Jace's train of thought.

"Sora's dead?! Don't you die! If you die…" Sazeno's voice drifted out from a corner.

"Shut up!" Jace barked. The imp shrank back in fright. Jace didn't even know when this little devil had run out of the pack. At this moment, he was poking his head out from behind an inconspicuous pile of rubble.

"What happened to Sora?" he asked.

"She's… covered in wounds. I don't know how badly she's hurt." Vereesa held the collapsed green dragon in her elven form, so anxious that she couldn't speak coherently for a moment.

"Everfrost Crystal," Jace raised his hand. "Try using that."

Vereesa immediately took out that ice-like gem from her bosom and pressed it into Sora's palm. She murmured an incantation. The Everfrost Crystal radiated a golden light that was extremely dazzling in this underground dark chamber. This was followed by a string of Sora's coughing.

"The green dragon isn't dead!"

The imp also excitedly jumped out and ran toward Vereesa. Why was this guy suddenly so concerned about Sora?

But hearing her movements, Jace's mind was finally set at ease. He braced himself against the rubble and cracked walls beside him, standing up step by step, and moved closer to Vereesa and Sora's side. Sora still had her eyes tightly shut, her expression one of great pain.

Her green outer garment had had its sleeves burned away by the soul fire released when Apocalypse chopped down on it. It was covered in damage and tears. And beneath the clothing, her skin was also covered in claw marks left by some unknown thing — some as deep as knife cuts.

Her already pale complexion now had even less color. And her brows furrowed from time to time, as if she was still enduring severe pain.

"Her neck…"

Vereesa lifted the green dragon's collar as she spoke. Only then did Jace see the thing that was causing her pain at this moment.

The already-reddened Will-Chain continued to scorch the delicate skin of her neck. This chain had already left several burn marks on her neck. Although its heat was fading, the residual heat was still enough to burn its weakened wearer.

Jace tried to help her take off this chain. Sora suddenly gasped violently, as if someone suffocating had suddenly regained her breath. Then she coughed and opened her eyes.

"She's alive! She's alive!" Sazeno said excitedly. "She's alive!"

Sora stared blankly at the people before her, then looked at Sazeno. "Thank you, demon."

"You see, Sayath? Even a green dragon knows I'm a demon. What more nonsense do you have to say?!" the imp said excitedly.

"Why are you thanking it?" Jace asked.

"When that black knight hacked at me with that terrifying demonic sword, it protected me with its foul magic," Sora said.

"Ha, don't mention it," the imp said proudly. Then, realizing something, he put on a cold face. "Why use the adjective 'foul'? You could have used 'evil,' 'dark,' 'gloomy,' 'terrifying' magic, and so on…"

The imp chattered away with complaints. Vereesa also let out a breath, smiling as she looked at him.

So this guy had only been afraid of Sora dying so that Sora would say how capable he was. Jace had really thought he was being genuine for a moment.

When Sora had charged in from the doorway holding the staff, Jace had just taken a blow from Eredin. That blow had nearly knocked him unconscious, and his vision had been blurred. So he hadn't been able to see what protective magic had been on Sora when she was hit.

He hadn't expected Sazeno to have seized that moment to cast the most crucial Blood Pact Seal… Turns out he even understood the need to protect the healer.

Vereesa fed Sora a bottle of potent healing potion, then asked, "How did you get these injuries? Were they all caused by that sword?"

"No." Sora shook her head. "After I entered that forest, I quickly realized I'd fallen into a trap… a magical trap left by Guardian Skavel. He must have manipulated the trees in the dream fragment to weave a living, self-moving 'labyrinth' to block anything trying to enter."

Sora continued, "At first I tried to force my way in relying on my knowledge of forests. But those trees relied on the power of the Dream. Without Guardian-level magic, they could barely be defeated. I was still too weak. Even in the Dream, even using all my strength, I couldn't push deeper. These wounds were left there."

So this was the labyrinth Das Mora had mentioned? It seemed she really had almost made it to the last step. Could it be that her previous departure from Eldre'Thalas had been to search for Ursalias?

It seemed to match the time when the old Frostmace furbolg described the plague beginning to spread — roughly several decades to a hundred years ago.

That time, she had undoubtedly failed. But she had probably more or less figured out the method for obtaining Ursalias. That was why Viltaan had been in such a hurry to summon her out of Eldre'Thalas — probably to send her to search for the Ursalias sickle once again.

Then why had she been captured after returning to Eldre'Thalas?

Perhaps to obtain even greater magical power to attempt entering Skavel's dream fragment again. Or perhaps to search for a treasure that could break the Dream — after all, Eldre'Thalas was Azshara's treasury.

Thinking here definitely wouldn't yield any clues.

Sora continued, "After wasting some time, I discovered that they actually responded to my calls. I called for them to leave just as I would dispel the ordinary trees and plants blocking my path in the Dream. But I knew this wasn't my own power."

She stared at the primal chain Jace was carrying in his hand. "It gave me the willpower to resist Skavel's command. I could feel it. What exactly is this?"

"The iron chain that binds the souls of dragons," Jace said honestly.

The green dragon's eyes, fixed on the Will-Chain, trembled slightly. Her lips moved faintly. Then she said, "I… I actually touched the Will-Chain. This is Deathwing's thing."

"It's mine now," Jace said.

Sora nodded. "It should be yours, Jace Sesso."
