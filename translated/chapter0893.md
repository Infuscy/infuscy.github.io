## Chapter 893: Moonbrook's "Guide"

After buying some pig blood from a Hillsbrad hunter, Jace and his two companions headed into the forest far south of town, avoiding the main road, and drew the circle according to Kallrez's modified scroll.

The circle wasn't particularly complex. Jace was more than practiced at drawing these kinds of demonic circles by now, and with Morophil and Sazeno's help, he finished it quickly.

But once it was done, the looks on Sola and Grede's faces behind him were strange.

"Sorry." Jace gave them an apologetic expression. "If you really can't stand watching, you can look away."

"No can do," Grede said. "I'm responsible for the little elf. I have to keep an eye on you and make sure you don't do anything shady."

"Grede's right," Sola nodded. Though Jace still wasn't quite used to her human appearance.

As a young dragon, she'd naturally taken the form of a girl of sixteen or seventeen — but she was wearing a rather old-fashioned long robe with a leaf pattern. Combined with the earrings and the crescent forehead ornament, these accessories hadn't seemed out of place on an elf, but on a human they looked a little odd.

"Fine, then watch. Just don't say I didn't warn you if it's too much."

Jace exchanged a glance with Morophil, then turned to face the circle, raised his hands to channel void energy, and began chanting a demonic incantation.

Soon he felt as though he'd linked the circle to something far away. After chanting the name of the doom lord Kallrez, the blood-red lines and characters on the circle slowly ignited with a crimson glow.

The blood symbol at the very center burst into shadowy flame. Within the flickering fire, a deep, rumbling voice echoed continuously. The circle was working.

"Is that you, Kallrez? Answer me."

"Mm. Jace Sesso, my master. It seems you've learned how to control this circle."

"How are things on your end — the Altar of Storms?" Jace asked.

"No different from when you left," the flame flickered. "But I'm getting impatient."

The demon had responded, and Jace set his mind at ease. The demonic disturbance that had drawn Dalaran's attention had nothing to do with him.

"I'll find a way to move you somewhere else, Kallrez. Be a little more patient with me," Jace said.

The flame snorted a laugh. "The way you say it, it sounds like some grand favor, lad. You don't think a half-hearted promise like that's going to make me grateful, do you?"

Jace said offhandedly, "If it means that little to you, then never mind. You can stay in that binding circle. Sounds like you're still not someone I can trust."

"I'm not someone you can trust?" the doom lord's voice was practically a roar. "I've told you my most important secrets, you feathered little freak — hurry up and find a way to get me out of this godforsaken place!"

Hearing the circle's flame rage like that, Morophil couldn't help but laugh.

"Getting you out of there isn't simple." Jace shot the succubus a sidelong glance, then continued addressing the demon in the flame. "For a feathered little freak like me to pull that off, I'd need your full cooperation."

"Tell me what to do," the demon grumbled. "Tell me how to break free of this binding circle!"

"When I get back," Jace said. "Now tell me — how are the warlocks guarding you doing?"

"They…" the flame flickered. "I don't give a damn about those weak little scraps. Besides, I can't leave this altar, and nobody tells me anything about the outside world. How am I supposed to tell you where they've gone?"

Jace turned the demon lord's words over in his mind, then asked, "What do you mean, the warlocks aren't in front of you anymore?"

"There's only one orc and one woman left. The others don't come around much anymore, master," the demon replied.

"Do you know anything about Moonbrook?" Jace asked.

"Moonbrook, Moonbrook…" the demon in the flame murmured the name repeatedly. After a moment he said, "I remember now. One of the human warlocks mentioned Moonbrook. He said the warlocks in Moonbrook have found a new guide…"

"What kind of person?" Jace immediately tensed. "Have they summoned another doom lord?"

"It doesn't sound that simple. From their description, that guide seems to have come to them of their own accord," the deep rumble in the flame said slowly. "Naturally, whoever it is can't hold a candle to me. If you hadn't forbidden me from teaching them too many spells and too much magic, I could have used my knowledge of the dark arts to bring them to their knees — instead of running off to find some other 'guide.'"

So Kallrez's knowledge of the matter was limited too.

Who would go to them of their own accord? A dreadlord?

Jace couldn't rule out that possibility.

Given Jace's presence, the dark magic forces in the Kingdom of Stormwind on this timeline were far more powerful than on the other. They'd learned all kinds of magic — various demon summonings, like succubi, and even some orcish magic — far earlier on this timeline, thanks to Jace's early discoveries.

So drawing the attention of the Nathrezim, or even summoning a dreadlord into the world, was entirely possible. Could word of the pit lord Azgoth's death at the Dark Portal have finally drawn the Burning Legion's revenge?

No point spinning theories here. Jace needed to get to Moonbrook as soon as possible and see for himself what was going on.

At that moment, Morophil looked toward the circle and said, "The flame is getting smaller, master. Say what you need to say quickly. Pig blood is a terribly weak fuel."

"I've got nothing more to say to him." Jace waved a hand. "Let's clean up the circle together. We're not far from Dalaran, and Southshore has plenty of mages. Do a thorough job."

Morophil acknowledged the order and, together with Sazeno, helped Jace dismantle and bury the circle. Sazeno grinned. "We should let that self-important bastard see master's new toy!"

"Toy?" Morophil shot him a look. "That's Ursolus, forged by the Dark Titan's own hands, you reckless little bastard."

"Though I am looking forward to seeing the look on his face," Morophil said, turning to Jace, her gaze suddenly growing soft and alluring.

"After all, his attitude toward master just now was far too arrogant. He needs to learn what a real doom lord looks like."

"I'm afraid he'll think I'm lying to him through a circle," Jace said with a smile. "Wait until he sees this staff with his own eyes — and then let him know he was the one who helped me find Ursolus. That way he won't dream of going back to Kil'jaeden."

From start to finish, Jace, his two demon servants, and Kallrez had all been speaking in Demonic. Behind them, the green dragon and the dwarf watched, both feeling a chill run down their spines.

Grede coughed. "All right, if this demonic gathering is over, let's get out of here. I can already feel the Kirin Tor's magic carpets floating toward us. I don't want to end up in the Violet Hold."

Jace nodded and looked at the green dragon. "Sorry to ask this of you, Sola — take us back to Stormwind."

"But I don't know where Stormwind is," Sola said.

"Don't worry. Just fly along the coast. When we reach Stormwind, you'll know it right away," the dwarf said, pointing toward the southern coastline.
