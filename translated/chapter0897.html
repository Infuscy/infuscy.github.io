Chapter 897: Cooperation Hits a Snag

"Morophil, hide here. Keep an eye on those patrolling Stormwind guards and any mages who might pass by. I'll go into SI:7 myself and ask about the situation. If you spot any familiar faces or anyone suspicious following me, let me know afterward." Jace rubbed the Court Mage's Signet on his finger and stared at the gateway opening in the distant staircase.

After sensing the demon soul's acknowledgment, Jace cleared his throat and walked through the gateway. Once inside, he couldn't help but glance toward the corners at the base of the city walls or other inconspicuous spots, wondering whether SI:7 might have someone lying in wait to rush out and arrest him.

But of course things weren't that bad. Jace walked straight into SI:7's compound outside the city walls without anyone stopping him. A male servant sweeping fallen leaves along the way even gave him a slight nod of greeting upon seeing his ring.

The weather had turned cold. A heavy, gray sky pressed down overhead. The air was a bit stifling and damp — it looked like rain.

Jace took a deep breath and walked up the gravel path toward the front of the building. His mood felt as though something were weighing on it — probably just the weather.

In the hall after entering, Jace hadn't even called out — he just looked around — when Mathias Shaw, seemingly having heard something or picked up some signal from outside, came out on his own.

"Master Sesso. I hadn't even heard you'd returned from Kalimdor. Did the mages teleport you back?" Mathias asked.

Jace looked at the would-be head of SI:7 and felt he seemed a bit thinner and darker than when they'd last met. His previously clean-shaven face now had stubble. Jace wondered what he'd been through these past weeks.

"Very funny, Shaw," Jace said.

Mathias said, "I'm not joking. I didn't receive word of your arrival at the harbor. But please, have a seat, Master Sesso."

"I won't sit. I want to know about the demon summancers you've been cooperating with," Jace said point-blank.

He didn't know how far Galders's cooperation with SI:7 had gone. Whether they'd told SI:7 about the Dagger Society's activities in the Slaughtered Lamb basement, the full roster of members, or even whether they'd told them the name "Dagger Society."

SI:7 might have already gotten all of that information — or they might not have. Given that Galders knew people within SI:7, and the Mage Quarter was under the Wizard Sanctum's tight control — which didn't exactly get along with SI:7 — it wasn't impossible that Galders had found openings to keep things hidden from everyone.

In any case, for now he could only use "demon summancers" to refer to those people.

Mathias nodded. "I imagine you've already heard something about the demon trouble in Moonbrook."

"I've heard a little," Jace said.

Mathias said, his tone slightly hesitant, "This matter is being investigated by the Wizard Sanctum. SI:7 is only cooperating with the investigation of criminal organizations related to those demon worshippers. After all, we're not experts in magical investigations. Even when we find clues related to magic, all we can do is cooperate with the Wizard Sanctum as much as possible. The king also trusts you court mages and Master Magino Dumas more than us when it comes to dark magic investigations…"

"Get to the point, Shaw," Jace cut him off.

"This distrust is mainly because of the cooperation we reached in searching for the dragonkin, Master Sesso," Mathias said. "Our use of felhounds to track the dragonkin was remarkably effective. Within a few months we'd dealt a heavy blow to the dragonkin's activities in Stormwind and uncovered the manipulators behind many previously unsolved cases — all thanks to Mr. 'Black Claw' Galders and Ms. Ursula Dering. But no matter how you look at it, all great power comes at a cost, doesn't it? Using demons to hunt criminals… also brought SI:7 a rather unsavory reputation. This Moonbrook incident is very likely that cost."

"When news of the demon plague in Moonbrook first broke, I planned to deepen cooperation with your friends — working with the Wizard Sanctum to investigate warlocks with warlocks, investigate demons with demons… After all, I know, Master Sesso, that you're probably the best person in Stormwind at dealing with demons, which is inseparable from your deep understanding of them. But my thinking was still too naive. Word soon came from the palace ordering us to cut off cooperation with the warlocks. I'm afraid Stormwind Keep can't afford the outside gossip right now either."

The demon plague in Moonbrook had forced SI:7 to cut off cooperation with the warlocks?

In that case, what about the black dragons still lurking within the Kingdom of Stormwind's borders — was there no one left to deal with them? How convenient for them.

But was it really that convenient?

Just listening to this, Jace had the feeling that the black dragons were behind it. From what he could see so far, the black dragons were the ones benefiting the most from this whole affair.

But did black dragons and demons… actually have some kind of cooperative relationship?

He parted his lips, wanting to share his suspicions with Mathias, but swallowed the words at the last moment.

Mathias was a smart man. The possibility that the black dragons were stirring up trouble had surely occurred to him too. Saying it out loud would most likely just be stating the obvious.

And considering his current position — publicly the foremost warlock in the Kingdom of Stormwind, or at the very least the chief "culprit" — if he now shifted the blame onto the black dragons, wouldn't it look like he was trying to absolve himself, and all the warlocks along with him? More importantly, if this really was the work of the black dragons, were the black dragonkin behind it?

Was there a possibility it was Onyxia?

Had Onyxia already taken on the alias Lady Katrana Prestor and entered the court?

Although Krasus had said that with Deathwing already dead, the other black dragons shouldn't dare make their move so hastily.

But who could predict the black dragons' logic? These fellows were a bunch of deluded lunatics through and through. Jace had to consider every possibility.

"Did you take the initiative to cut ties with the warlocks, or was this the king's order?" Jace asked.

"We don't need a king's direct order to read the king's intentions," Mathias said helplessly. "I'm asking — is this the king's own idea?" Jace pressed.

"Ah…" Mathias laughed. "If you're asking my guess, it's most likely Bolvar Fordragon's doing. But whoever's reasoning it is, it isn't wrong, Master Sesso. In this situation, everything tainted by demons is dangerous — and that includes you."

The words Jace had been forming hung in his mouth. He realized that regardless of whether Onyxia was behind this, he couldn't mention anything touching on Onyxia herself to the man in front of him.

Otherwise, he'd very likely become the focus of suspicion for knowing too much.

Better to head to Stormwind Keep and speak with the king first — under the guise of offering a confession. After all, a matter this closely tied to demons had erupted within the kingdom, and he, as a dark magic advisor, had been absent on another continent.

At the same time, he could sound out the king's attitude and see whether Onyxia had come to Stormwind.

Of course, he was also going there to resolve the dark magic crisis — that absolutely didn't count as dereliction of duty. And now that he had Ursolus in his hands, he'd arguably nipped the disaster in the bud.

"So after you cut off cooperation with Galders and Ursula Dering, you have no idea where they went?" Jace asked. "You want us to send people to tail those warlocks, Master Sesso?" Mathias asked.

Jace ran his tongue over his teeth, thinking this wasn't something he could just decide because he wanted to. Still, he nodded. "Then I won't take up any more of your time, Shaw."

Mathias said, "Oh right, Sesso — there's also Erland Dudmoor…"

"What about him?"

"He's in prison. I'm sorry. There's nothing I can do about this. It was the Wizard Sanctum's decision." Jace frowned. "Did he take part in summoning that demon?"

"That demon?" Mathias said, visibly surprised. "Quite a few demons, Master Sesso — many demons. At first, when I heard the report from the agents returning from Moonbrook, I thought those warlocks were trying to form a demon army to launch a rebellion. But Mr. Dudmoor insisted he hadn't been involved in any of it. Still, he is a warlock, and he'd been involved in demon material trade with Moonbrook for a long time. In any case, he can't escape implication for now."

"Can I go see him?" Jace asked.

"You can, but it's best not to," Mathias said. "Given your current position, and your relationship with him, Master Sesso — if you went, it would stir up trouble."

"I understand," Jace said. "About those demons that were summoned — is there anything you can tell me?"

"The specific summoning rituals, which people summoned which demons, how powerful the demons are… you'd be better off asking the Wizard Sanctum mages than me, Jace," Mathias said.

"Regardless, thank you," Jace nodded.

When he came out, it had already started to rain. He guessed the Dagger Society people had probably decided to relocate because Erland Dudmoor had been arrested.

Galders had never fully trusted Erland — possibly because of Jagin's influence, or possibly because Erland was also a member of the Stonemasons. So with him arrested, the Slaughtered Lamb probably wasn't safe anymore.

Since SI:7 was located in Old Town, he took the chance to swing by the old house where Jagin's fire had happened, and also stopped by Sulenna Keldoan's residence. Of course, no one was at either place. Galders and the others wouldn't have moved from the Slaughtered Lamb to somewhere even more dangerous. In fact, probably nowhere inside Stormwind proper.

Then what about outside Stormwind?

Goldshire was probably out of the question, seeing as Erland lived in Goldshire. Galders would probably hole up somewhere farther away. Lakeshire?

Jace remembered that Galders had attended a Dragon Cult gathering in Lakeshire at some point. From his tone at the time, he didn't seem very familiar with Lakeshire. If that was the case, the odds of him relocating the entire Dagger Society to Lakeshire weren't high either.

So besides Stormwind, where in the kingdom did he know best?

Probably Duskwood.

Galders had found Galene Aylo in Duskwood to craft magic robes, although Jace didn't know how the two of them were connected. Now that the Dagger Society couldn't stay in Stormwind, Moonbrook was ground zero for the conflict, Lakeshire was unfamiliar, and Goldshire was tied to Erland — it wasn't impossible they were in Duskwood right now.

However, Jace couldn't set out for Duskwood just yet. After all, the round trip on horseback would take at least a week. If he was going, he'd need to visit the king first, then head over to Marin's to ask about the investigation into Moonbrook.

Considering he'd been back in Stormwind less than a day, the Dagger Society people probably hadn't even heard the news that he'd returned. If they found out, they'd probably try to track him down.

By now it was already late, and the rain was clearly getting heavier. Going to the palace today wasn't appropriate either. After grabbing some bread at the Pig and Whistle Inn to quiet his stomach, Jace hurried home. After Morophil shut the door, Jace asked, "Well? Was anyone following me?"

"At least I didn't spot anyone," Morophil said. "I don't think master needs to worry too much. If I were them, I wouldn't risk tailing a mage who could single-handedly kill a young dragon."

Jace asked again, "Then — have you sensed any demonic presence in Stormwind?"

"I haven't sensed any from Old Town all the way here," Morophil replied.

Hearing this, Jace both breathed a sigh of relief and felt a little disappointed.

The relief was that the demon plague in Westfall hadn't spread to Stormwind at least. The disappointment was that it seemed the people from the Slaughtered Lamb had really left Stormwind.

He hoped they were all right.

"Don't worry, my master."

As she spoke, Morophil climbed onto the bed and slowly leaned against Jace's chest, wrapping her tail around his ankle. At this moment he could feel the barbs on her tail lightly scraping his skin — a sensation that made you worry about pain while being maddeningly alluring. It was genuinely itchy in a way that drove you crazy.

"I'm tired today, Morophil. And with everything that's happened," Jace said helplessly.

"Then master don't move. Let me serve you." Morophil pinched the waistband of Jace's trousers and lifted them slightly. "I've been craving your taste, going nearly out of my mind… Master made me watch you and Vereesa Windrunner have your fun by the river that day. Such a deep sting — aren't you planning to make it up to me properly? My heart back then was colder than the river water."

"Your demonic heart contains nothing but fel energy and life flame. How could it be cold?" Jace muttered.

"At least it's not hot enough yet. It needs… master's power."

As she spoke, Morophil wet her lips with her long tongue.
