Chapter 898: Caught Between Two Fires

The next morning, outside the gates of Stormwind Keep.

Jace had just drunk a mana potion to replenish the severe deficit from the previous night. Morophil was growing more and more powerful, and his mana was starting to feel a little insufficient.

Considering what he might face today, he was starting to regret letting Morophil have her way with him last night. He didn't know whether his mana would be enough to handle whatever came his way.

But it shouldn't come to that — it wasn't as if he'd actually have to fight Onyxia herself in the middle of Stormwind Keep, right?

To be honest, after that exchange with Azuregos, he wasn't even that worried about a duel with a real adult black dragon anymore. Win or not, at least he had a bit of nerve to fall back on.

Basically, he'd gotten cocky.

On the other hand, Jace felt he might be putting too much stock in Lady Katrana Prestor standing beside the throne from the game.

After all, he still had no concrete evidence that any of this was the black dragons' doing behind the scenes. Maybe they'd just freeloaded off those reckless demon summoners?

He glanced at the mirror stone in his pocket, thinking about how to covertly use this little thing to detect any black dragons who might appear. The stone wasn't big, but it wasn't small either — there was no way to make it into a ring that he could check at any moment.

Maybe it could be mounted on a staff. The mirror stone would be a bit small for a staff head, but it wasn't inappropriate. And a mage carrying two staffs wasn't unusual — this staff could even serve as cover for the other wrapped-up staff.

"Please follow me, Master Sesso. The king is waiting for you."

A royal guard stepped forward and saluted. Jace nodded and followed him into the palace.

Walking down the long pathway, Jace looked back and forth at the rooms on either side. Even as someone skilled at emotional manipulation who could easily suppress his nerves, he had to admit — he was genuinely a little worried that the guards here might suddenly all transform into terrifying heavy-armored black dragonkin and rush at him, hacking him to the ground with blades, then crowd around hacking away while shouting "our dragon father is avenged at last" and the like.

Of course, Deathwing's blood debt wasn't the only one anymore — there was now also Teremus the Devourer. From what the black dragons had said back in Dustwallow Marsh, Teremus the Devourer might very well be Onyxia's child.

Having killed someone's father and then also done in someone's son — the grudge was getting more and more solid.

The royal guard led Jace to a study off to the side of the gardens. Varian Wrynn was waiting inside.

Seeing Jace appear, Varian looked up, his eyes visibly brightening.

"You're back at last, Jace. When you left Stormwind for Kalimdor, who could have expected such a serious problem would crop up."

"Your Majesty." Jace bowed slightly. "I came to Stormwind Keep precisely to offer my apologies."

"There's nothing to apologize for," Varian waved a hand. "Now that you're here, I expect the problem can be resolved quickly. After all, not even that doom lord at Nethergarde Keep could withstand your magic. To hunt down those demons roaming everywhere, I had to pull troops from Lakeshire and Westbrook Garrison and send them to Moonbrook and Sentinel Hill."

"That'll only make the Redridge gnolls think they've found their chance, damn it," Varian said, pressing his hand to his forehead. "After the demons fleeing the Dark Portal were largely dealt with by the Alliance, the gnolls had already grown considerably bolder. Now with the troops around Lakeshire reduced, they're going to become even more rampant. So the Moonbrook demon problem has to be resolved quickly."

Jace frowned. "When I was in Dalaran, I heard the situation in Moonbrook had already been stabilized. But hearing it from you, it sounds like it's still…"

"I suspect the Wizard's Sanctum has been putting the best spin on things so the Kirin Tor wouldn't stick their noses in," Varian sighed. "I don't think they've fully controlled the situation yet. They probably haven't even completely resolved the problems inside Moonbrook — otherwise they wouldn't need me to send in reinforcements."

"If Lakeshire is short on troops, the threat won't just be gnolls, Your Majesty," Jace warned. "Blackrock Spire to the north is still eyeing us hungrily, and I have ample evidence that those orcs are in league with the black dragons. They could move south at any time to try to retake Stonewatch…"

"I'm well aware of that," Varian said. "I've already sent men to monitor the orcs' movements near Blackrock Mountain. At least for now they haven't made a move. But right now the demons in Moonbrook are a tangible threat, aren't they, Jace? The problem is the kingdom doesn't have the spare manpower to protect both Stonewatch and the Tower of Ilgalar."

"I didn't say that because I want the soldiers protecting the Tower of Ilgalar, Your Majesty," Jace said immediately.

"I'm sorry. I'm not singling you out — that was just a place name," Varian said. "So I need you to solve this problem, Jace. If you can help those Wizard's Sanctum fellows put the Moonbrook matter to rest once and for all, then the whole of the west and Lakeshire can rest easy. I don't have many people I can rely on right now, Jace — you're one of them."

Jace nodded. "I understand, Your Majesty. I'll head to the Wizard's Sanctum immediately."

Onyxia wasn't in Stormwind — or at least, she wasn't in the palace.

Judging from King Varian's words, things in the palace seemed much the same as when he'd left. There was no special advisor, no outside noble offering crucial counsel.

And judging from the reflection in the mirror stone Jace was holding, there were no dragonkin among the royal guards either. Dragonkin shapeshifting couldn't possibly be more advanced than Somaelir's — they definitely couldn't fool this Highborne-forged artifact.

Could Jace have been overthinking it after all?

Still, he didn't want to let go of this possibility so easily.

After all, from every angle, the current situation favored the black dragons infiltrating Stormwind. Now it was time to head to the Wizard's Sanctum and learn more about the Moonbrook situation.

Then there was Erlan Drudgemoor. If he really hadn't been involved in the Moonbrook affair, then a way had to be found to get him out. That would be a great help in finding the Dagger Society and tracking down the warlocks who'd taken part in the demon summonings.

After all, there was still that "guide" in Moonbrook — Kallrez's words couldn't be ignored either. Right, Kallrez.

Before the Moonbrook demon problem was resolved, there was no way he could move Kallrez into the Tower of Ilgalar. By any measure right now, that would be pure trouble.

But keeping him at the Altar of Storms wasn't safe either. After all, there were fewer warlocks guarding him than before, and the situation was getting tenser by the day.

If outsiders entered the Altar of Storms — Blackrock Spire orcs, say, or Wizard's Sanctum mages searching for demonic clues — and discovered this demon, the bound Kallrez would be completely helpless, and Jace would be dragged down with him.

He couldn't let him rot in prison anymore. A more suitable hiding place needed to be arranged.
