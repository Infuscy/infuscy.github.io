## Chapter 904: A Complete Work

Just as Jace had expected, the ogres took to their new king quite well. Naturally, Jace also issued this new king's first decree on his behalf: under no circumstances were they to tell anyone the true identity of the ruler of this gray valley, or he'd have their teeth knocked out.

"He's a giant ogre with especially long horns on his head," Kogert, one of the tribe's only two-headed ogre mages, described it. "And he has brown skin, just like a mighty gronn."

Deep in the black forest, just as the former king of this ogre tribe, Golkrash, had described, there was a dark valley shrouded in dead trees. Karlez could move about freely here, patrolling his territory back and forth.

But Jace also reminded him not to wander out on his own, to guard this place well, and to carefully draw on the mana leaking from Karazhan. After leaving with Morophil, looking back from a distance, there was nothing unusual to be seen.

A couple of ogres dragged the unconscious former king Golkrash back into the forest, and the others followed him back deep into the valley. Apart for the occasional ogre laugh echoing from afar, it was quite peaceful.

No one would ever suspect there was a doom lord hiding in here.

"When is Lord Karlez going to realize he's just been moved from one prison to another?" Morophil asked lazily, one hand draped over Jace's shoulder.

"At least this time there's no binding circle restraining him," Jace said. "Let's go. From here we head north to the main road, then follow it to Duskwood. I'm going to ask around and see if there's any news of the Dagger Society people."

"If they left Stormwind to lie low… I don't imagine they'd be found so easily, master," Morophil reminded him.

"We'll see," Jace said.

Still, he had the same guess in his heart — he had a feeling this trip to Duskwood would very likely come up empty.

For all he knew, the Dagger Society members had holed up in some farm, or even the basement or warehouse of an abandoned farm, somewhere outside the city. He had no idea.

It was even possible that Erland, sitting in prison, didn't know either. Knowing Galders's personality, Jace doubted he'd pick a location that a fellow member in jail knew about, just waiting for him to crack and expose the whole operation.

Although Karlez looked slow flapping his flesh wings across the sky, he had in fact flown a long way into Deadwind Pass. It took Jace an entire afternoon to walk to the abandoned little hamlet of three or four houses he'd caught a glimpse of when they'd first come in.

At this rate, he probably wouldn't see the main road from Stonard to Duskwood until tomorrow night.

"Let's rest here for the night," Jace said, rummaging through his bag and looking at the few leftover salted fish chunks he'd brought from Lakeshire, sighing to himself. "Traveling with companions who don't need to eat is really something."

"I don't need to eat, master?" Morophil leaned against the already-crumbling doorframe in front of him. "You'd better eat your fill, and then feed me well tonight…"

As she said it, she gently stroked the lower curve of her breast, covered in fel runes, and tilted her head. Jace sighed. "You're not short on mana."

The succubus flicked her tail and raised her voice. "Who says? If we're heading to Moonbrook next to face those unknown demons, the terrifying monsters that could make the entire Kingdom of Stormwind tremble — I'm truly worried I'll find myself in a mana shortage. Just like in Azshara fighting that blue dragon. So I need to make ample preparations, and pour out everything I have to protect my most precious… you."

"Pour out everything I have," Jace muttered.

"Would it be so bad to just store it in me?" Morophil's gaze followed Jace as she walked through the door, speaking softly.

In the middle of the night, after eating, Jace grabbed the succubus who'd been rubbing herself against him with hooves for half the night, shoved her against the wall, and pinned her arms hard to the surface. Morophil immediately furrowed her brows, trembling all over, and whispered, "Hurry, master…"

But Jace didn't go any further. He just stared into the succubus's eyes. "I want to ask you something, Morophil."

"Why… right now…" The succubus wrapped her arms around Jace's neck, her voice seemingly not yet cooled from excitement. "Why didn't you tell Karlez about your past service to Brutallus?"

"You think I'm lying to you," Morophil's voice cooled slightly.

Jace said nothing. The succubus continued, "You told me not to lie to you, and of course I haven't lied to you, master."

"Then tell me why you kept it from Karlez, Morophil," Jace said.

Morophil smiled. "Nothing special — just a bit of pride, that's all. I had no obligation to tell him, did I? Karlez has probably heard of me. As a senior officer of the Burning Legion, if he knows Brutallus's past, then he might know of my existence. And I'd rather he didn't know who I am."

"Why?!" Sazeno looked over from the side. "I don't don't understand — isn't knowing a senior Burning Legion officer like Lord Karlez something to brag about?"

"It's not hard to understand," Morophil buried her head in Jace's neck and said softly. "I just don't want those demons to know what I look like now that I've finally been revived…"

"What's wrong with how you look now?" Jace said. "I think it's not bad."

"Really?" Morophil slanted her slowly burning demon eyes and locked onto Jace's gaze. "You truly think so?" Jace raised his hand and scratched the ridges on one of his demon horns with a fingernail. "After all, you're half my work, aren't you?"

"Fel runes, power…"

"Of course, it's not bad. Just compared to when I served Brutallus…" Morophil smiled.

"And it seems a bit too weak, is that it," Jace said. "So you don't want those Burning Legion officers to know that the once-terrifying demon huntress Morophil has become what she is now."

"That wasn't my name back then," the succubus said. "Morophil is just a name I picked on a whim, master. I told you — if you'd like, you can give me a different one."

"The current name is fine. But how can you become as powerful as you were?" Jace asked.

"Whenever you're willing, someday, my master," Morophil gently kissed Jace's lower lip and murmured. "Just let me become your work… a complete work."
