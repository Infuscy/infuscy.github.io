## Chapter 906: Temporary Hideout

The Dagger Society's chosen location wasn't actually inside Darkshire, but in a small detached house on the southern edge of town.

The house looked quite crude, like the kind of place a lone hunter or ranger would cobble together to live in, but the basement was sizable. Of course it couldn't compare to the basement beneath the Slaughtered Lamb, but it had a corridor and three rooms — compared to the little house above, the scale of this basement had exceeded Jace's expectations.

"The previous owner of this house moved to Stormwind, so we rented it for a very cheap price," Ursula said. "Because it's too close to the Tranquil Gardens Cemetery — so it's very cheap."

Tranquil Gardens was Darkshire's cemetery, located to the south of town. From here Jace could see the spire of the monastery beyond the high ground in that direction — he guessed that was where the burial ground was.

Although living next to a cemetery wasn't exactly a taboo in Azeroth, it was a completely different matter during a time when dark power and the undead plague pervaded all of Duskwood. Aside from doctors like Thomas Waller or clergy from the church, nobody liked making trips to Tranquil Gardens for no reason.

For the Dagger Society, this counted as a kind of cover too.

In the basement corridor, Jalane Ayrole was holding an old book reading. When she saw Jace appear, she immediately put down the book and looked up.

"It really is you, Jace. I was worried it was a Wizard's Sanctum trap. And Miss Morophil, good to see you again."

Morophil smiled and nodded. Jace asked, "Does the Wizard's Sanctum know I'm with the Dagger Society? Did SI:7 tell them?"

"Probably not — I'm just worried they might find out," Jalane shook her head. "As far as I know, SI:7 probably hasn't shared it with anyone yet, but since Zardeth has people on the inside feeding him information… I'd worry the mages would find some way to dig up intelligence in there too."

"SI:7 has been treating us quite well, Jace," Ursula said. "Master Shaw helped us relocate out of Stormwind and covered our tracks, while also destroying some of the evidence linking us to demons that we'd helped them preserve…"

"That's just them making an effort to help themselves shrug off suspicion, Ursula," Jalane said coldly. "But it has to be said it objectively helped us too."

"That's true," Ursula said.

Ursula had mentioned before that she really didn't like Jalane Ayrole, but seeing the two of them converse so naturally now, they'd probably grown familiar with each other after going through this string of difficulties.

"Still, I'm very glad to see you, Jace," Jalane said this, though her expression didn't exactly look glad. "Since you were willing to show yourself, at least it means our troubles haven't grown beyond saving — at least the palace or the Wizard's Sanctum doesn't intend to wipe out everyone connected to demons or dark magic."

"I imagine their focus right now is still on Moonbrook," Jace said.

"How's your Robe of the Void, Jace?" Jalane glanced at his cloak. "Has something gone wrong with it? You're not wearing it."

Jace explained, "I'm still worried that robe would draw too much attention. There's no occasion to deal with powerful opponents right now, so I'm not wearing it all the time."

Jalane said, "I'm planning to improve on the orcish crafting method. You were in Kalimdor for quite a while — you must have run into some trouble?"

Jace thought about it. "As things stand, this robe seems to have a hard time blocking mages' frost magic — at least not as effectively as it does against demons and dark magic."

"You fought mages in Kalimdor?" Jalane asked.

"A fairly powerful mage," Jace said.

Jalane thought for a moment, then nodded. "Bring the robe to me when you get a chance, Jace. Let me help you repair it and also research a better enchanting method. I honestly hadn't thought about countering mages' evocation frost magic. Given that we might have to deal with all kinds of mages looking for trouble in the future… we might really need to pay attention to this area."

"Thank you, Jalane," Jace said.

"It's nothing. In the entire Dagger Society, you are unquestionably the best subject for testing my magical tailoring skills." Jalane's tone even carried a hint of pride. "I'm quite proud that a powerful robe of mine took part in the Battle of Grim Batol… But can you tell me who the mage was that tried to attack you with frost magic? Also from the Wizard's Sanctum, or the Kirin Tor?"

"An adult blue dragon," Jace said.

"What? A blue dragon?! You ran into a blue dragon in Kalimdor?!" Ursula nearly shrieked. Jalane was stunned too — the tailor, who was imperturbable toward everything, now showed a look of surprise on her face.

"Why did it attack you?" the alchemist asked.

"Do dragons need a reason to attack people?" Jace explained offhandedly. "Probably we intruded on his territory. It was chaotic, but the misunderstanding was cleared up afterward — he doesn't intend to wipe us out either."

Jalane said, "I want to see that robe damaged by blue dragon magic as soon as possible, Jace."

"I'll come by and give it to you after I get back to the tavern," Jace said.

"Leaving something that valuable in that crummy tavern? Especially in a place as unstable as Duskwood." Jalane's tone held a hint of blame.

"I've got my imp keeping an eye on it. If anything goes wrong he'll call me," Jace said.

"As long as your imp is reliable," Jalane said. "I'm really worried about missing a good chance to see how a robe holds up against an adult blue dragon's magic…"

After thanking Jalane, Ursula brought Jace to a basement room at the end of the corridor with a small skylight.

Jace noticed that although this place was much smaller than the alchemy lab beneath the Slaughtered Lamb, it had everything — cauldrons, storage racks, all sorts of tools, and what seemed to be a small storehouse in the back. Some of the things here even looked quite familiar.

Ursula had arranged things here roughly the way they'd been set up beneath the Slaughtered Lamb, which gave Jace an immediate sense of familiarity.

The imp she'd summoned was helping sort medicines — these seemed to be freshly gathered herbs from not long ago, still fresh as if just pulled from the soil.

"Though the conditions are far worse, Zardeth still did his best to find everything for me," Ursula looked around. "After all, our situation is special right now, and we're doing our best to avoid contact with anyone we can't trust. And with Erlan arrested, I've practically become the Dagger Society's sole source of income. Though doing business with Mr. Thomas Waller carries its own risks — he's been watched by the Wizard's Sanctum for a long time too… but for now I don't have any other good partners in Duskwood."

"How did you know we were here?" Ursula asked.

"Guessed, then searched one by one," Jace said. "Erlan was just arrested, so I figured you wouldn't be in Goldshire. I'd been to Lakeshire, and I didn't think Zardeth liked that place."

"All right." The alchemist breathed a slight sigh of relief. "As long as none of us exposed our own trail. About Erlan — have you been to see him?"

"Mathias Shaw doesn't want me to visit him, worried it'd bring me trouble," Jace said. "And I don't think he'd know where you moved to either, right? Zardeth wouldn't allow it."

Ursula laughed. "You know Zardeth too well. He definitely wouldn't like you figuring him out so clearly."

"Right, Jace," she said, suddenly remembering something. "Though asking this might be a bit much — would the Wizard's Sanctum people follow you to Duskwood?"

Jace said, "My master told me the Wizard's Sanctum doesn't have plans to monitor me right now. I'm willing to trust him, and my path to Duskwood wasn't exactly 'normal' — they shouldn't be able to track me here."

"With me around, don't worry, my dear Ursula," Morophil leaned against the wall. "I've been watching him."

The alchemist glanced at the succubus. "Jace really ought to thank you for taking care of him all this time, Morophil. He's never been one to pay much attention to his own safety. But given the recent situation, I'd assumed Jace wouldn't dare take you out on the road."

That reminded Jace — the felhound seemed to be gone. "Where's Master Basadim?"

Ursula said, a bit dejectedly, "He can't turn invisible, and there's no way his size could escape Stormwind. I could only banish him, Jace. I shouldn't grow attached to a demon, but I'm a warlock and also a human being. After sending him back to the Twisting Nether I felt awful for days — after all, he'd been protecting us there for a long time."

"So the other demons…"

"Yes — the ones we couldn't take with us were all banished. Voidwalkers, felguards…"

"Felguards?" Jace asked. "You managed to summon a felguard?"

"Yes. Relying on the mind control magic of the incubus Gakres that Surena summoned, we managed to enslave a felguard, but not long after that the Moonbrook incident happened." Ursula paused for a moment. "Back then we all thought that explosion in Moonbrook… had something to do with our summoning the felguard. We were on edge for days — the Twisting Nether is so chaotic, after all."

"I thought you'd wait for me to come back before attempting the summoning," Jace said.

Ursula said, "Previously, Mathias had asked Zardeth and Erlan to take part in an operation to hunt a demon worshipper by Lake Everstill. That summoner barely knew any dark magic. He was less a warlock and more of a boxer. Erlan suspected the fellow must have gone to the underground boxing matches in Stormwind, otherwise how would he have had that much money to prepare a summoning ritual? After all, the guy smashed several of Erlan's teeth with a single punch while under Erlan's Fear spell."

"He knocked out Erlan's teeth?" Jace asked in surprise.

Ursula nodded. "Yeah. Don't forget — Erlan's from the Stonemasons, and a proper tough guy too… I figured a man that good at fighting wouldn't have time to study dark magic, so he gave us quite a bit of confidence. Plus your demon binding circle — after a few attempts, it went smoothly enough."

Though Jace had already seen several "warlocks" at the Altar of Storms summoning ceremony who were well-versed in demon knowledge but barely knew any magic, a guy who barely knew incantations but could summon a felguard all by himself still caught him a little off guard.

"So how did he do it?" Jace asked.

"Not just you — we were all quite interested," Ursula said. "SI:7 was grateful we'd helped them deal with that demon summoner, so they gave us some incantations, materials, and a few medicine bottles they'd collected from his residence. Of course we didn't expect him to be any kind of demon scholar. Later we found his motives were even more outlandish than we'd expected — it seemed he just wanted to summon a demon to spar with in boxing. Only later did he realize a demon could do far more than be a sparring partner, and he started trying to pull off criminal schemes."

Jace said, somewhat skeptically, "Just for that? Isn't that a bit too…"

"There might be other reasons — there were some things he left behind that we still haven't been able to figure out," Ursula said. "Zardeth said he wants to wait for you to come back and take a look. It wouldn't be right to ask you for help with problems the moment you arrive here. I was originally going to treat you to something first…"

"It's fine — I'm a bit curious myself," Jace said.

"Then come with me," Ursula said.
