Chapter 92: Critical Data Missing

He opened his eyes. Outside was still night.

What time was it now? Had he slept the whole day, or only an hour?

Jace felt around beside him, then felt a wave of intense emptiness.

Of course he didn't have a phone anymore.

"Sazeno… Sazeno?"

Calling out twice in a drowsy haze, Jace suddenly realized the imp wasn't in the granary anymore.

This fact made him wake up all at once, his whole body tensing instantly.

Not only was the imp gone, his pack was gone too — those naga scales, the pearl strand on his hand…

What happened?

Jace felt himself over — his pants were still on, his shirt was just underneath him, he wasn't missing any limbs, his hair was still there, no injuries… He searched all around where he'd been sleeping, and finally found that broken knife. But the biggest problem was, the stick was gone.

What the hell? Who had come in… or had the imp betrayed him and stolen everything valuable he had?

He immediately recited the strangling incantation he'd already memorized cold. If this guy had fled without his orders, there was no need for him to exist anymore. But after finishing the incantation, nothing happened. Was the imp dead?

Something was wrong. Jace didn't feel any Shadow energy dancing along with his incantation at all — the way he did when he summoned the imp, or used Fear.

Was this incantation fake?

Impossible. He'd seen Gagin use this incantation with his own eyes in the basement to kill a freshly summoned demon.

What on earth happened? Could it be the naga's companions coming for revenge?

He gripped the knife tightly, moved to the door, and peered out.

The night was terrifyingly dark — even the twin moons were gone. Was it going to rain?

In the air he couldn't smell any moisture, couldn't feel the soil, couldn't feel any of nature's fresh scents. Here, it was as if everything had died.

Then he heard someone calling his name.

Jace turned toward the source of the sound and saw what seemed to be a blurry figure in the tangled weeds not far away on the farm.

Wait — that name wasn't the name of this lifetime of his!

This was wrong. This wasn't real at all.

It must be that naga's magic playing tricks. He must have been hit by Fear at some point without knowing it, and was now experiencing the aftereffects of Fear.

Jace hardened his heart and cut his finger with the knife, wanting to wake up quickly — but after a burst of intense pain, he watched in despair as blood flowed down his finger to his palm. Completely useless!

He gritted his teeth against the pain. What now?

Looking over there again, suddenly a familiar face was right up against his, so close it made his whole body jolt and he nearly fell.

This… was a girl's face. He couldn't tell who it was, but felt he'd seen her somewhere.

But this face was tightly shut, lifeless, purple-red blood streaming from the corners of her eyes, her lips trembling incessantly. This distortion made it impossible for him to connect it to anyone he'd ever known.

Behind her face was a giant serpent's body. Unlike the dead-like cheeks, this snake's body constantly writhed and twisted, its scales rubbing together in a death-like warning, its tail tip shaking frantically — as if mocking Jace's fear.

Jace roared and swung his knife, slashing that eerie cheek. The serpent let out a hissing screech and darted into the weeds.

"Come on! Come here! Let's see what else Shadow can do — what other freaky things can you come up with?"

"Then what do you expect?"

Hearing those words, Jace looked behind the source of the sound. The girl was lying there, her lower half still a snake, but her upper half had become human — lying there completely exposed.

Jace swallowed to wet his dry throat. Had to say, that was quite a shock.

Just as he was standing there dazed, the naga-like monster's lower body muscles suddenly snapped, and the whole thing lunged right up to Jace's face. Before Jace could even react, he was grabbed by the neck and lifted into the air.

Wet, slippery skin pressed against his arm, his chest, and his cheek. He felt less like he was being lifted up and more like being embraced.

The serpent's long tongue slid from his neck toward his chest and lower abdomen. Unexpectedly, Jace didn't feel any danger at all — instead his mind was already filled with all kinds of strange thoughts.

This was a dream. Whatever he wanted to do, he could do.

After a bout of heart palpitations, Jace felt that earlier fierce will to fight to the end rapidly melting, like it had been dropped into an extremely corrosive solution. His gradually clouding mind was losing the ability to think actively, bit by bit.

Right now all he thought was, since there's no price anyway, if he waited a little longer, the lower half should transform too, right?

Wait — was there really no price?

This was a Shadow dream. Deep in his subconscious he felt he couldn't yield to… couldn't yield to these things. But he felt his whole body being swallowed by desire, all his senses reduced to nothing but ethereal, unreal bioelectric impulses rhythmically assaulting his brain. Absolutely not… He struggled and grabbed the giant serpent coiling around him, trying to tear it off. The girl's face suddenly split in two along the slash mark of his knife! Like a watermelon that had just cracked open — flesh and bone clearly visible, two eyes far apart looking over at the same time.

A violent scream pierced through Jace's brain. He shouted and sat bolt upright — it was already morning.

"Master?" Sazeno was still sitting over by the granary, watching. "Why're you yelling so loud?"

Jace wiped the sweat off his neck with his fingers and said, "Just had a dream."

Recalling the dream, he actually felt a bit of regret that it hadn't continued.

Was it because he lacked the experience, so the critical data was missing and it crashed? But doing it with a snake was pretty weird, huh.

Actually, it wasn't that weird.

In a wave of emptiness, he hummed the tune of "A Thousand Years of Waiting" out of habit, packed up his things, ate a bit of dry bread, and prepared to leave this abandoned farm.

The autumn air was clear and cool. When Jace walked to slightly higher ground, he could see the scattered ruins of houses beyond a distant mountain hollow.

That was an abandoned town. According to what the Westfall Keep soldiers had said, that place shouldn't be the so-called West County — it should be the mining town outside Jangolode Mine.

The very place Furbloom had said he and his wife had hidden during the war.

Gazing out over the endless slopes and wilderness, Jace let out a deep sigh. Felweed… how was he supposed to find this?

He hadn't even found a single bundle of Silverleaf along the way. Westfall had so many farms, seemingly especially fertile, yet after walking half a day he couldn't find any herbs at all.

Could it be that this place lacked a ley line nexus, so not many magical plants grew?

But Ursula Deline had sworn up and down that there was lots of Felweed on these slopes. Since Furbloom said hardly anyone came here anymore, no one should be competing with him for herbs.

Along the way Jace hadn't seen any sign of gnolls either — no camps, no living gnolls. So there shouldn't be any gnoll herbalists competing with him either.
