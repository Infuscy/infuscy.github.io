Chapter 93: The Secret Alchemy Lab

Jace followed the high slope all the way toward the mine. When he reached the mining town, he observed the settlement from higher ground for quite a while.

Calling it a mining town, it actually wasn't much bigger than that fishing village — only a few houses and a main road leading to the mine.

After confirming there were no living things inside, he walked into the village, searching every room to see if there was anything useful.

"Stop following me around all the time." He looked down at the imp at his feet. "Search everywhere. See anything unusual — metal… money, paper, anything usable — gather it up and bring it to me."

The imp shouted a "Got it!" and darted out. In the sunlight, this little guy wasn't too conspicuous among the overgrown weeds.

After saying that, Jace felt like he was here scavenging. But what else could he do right now? If he couldn't find Felweed, then he'd try his best to find something useful to trade for money back in town, buy some materials to make mana potions.

Since Ursula was willing to help him make them for free, just buying the materials shouldn't be that expensive either.

From afternoon until evening, the most valuable thing Jace and Sazeno found was a broken pocket watch — the hands didn't move anymore, and the surface glass had cracks.

Jace didn't know how much pocket watches sold for in Stormwind, because the people who owned pocket watches were either nobles, merchants, or those scholars — felt like a really expensive thing. He didn't know if this pocket watch could be fixed. He wiped the dust off it and stuffed it in his bag.

The sky was getting darker and darker. Jace gazed at the lonely cave not far away and asked Sazeno, "Wanna go in and take a look?"

"Wanna go in and take a look?" the imp repeated.

"I'm asking you!"

"Awaiting the master's orders!"

Jace sighed in exasperation and said, "Alright, you go in invisible and scout around. Come out and tell me if you spot anything suspicious, useful, or dangerous."

"Understood!"

With that, the imp dashed toward the cave entrance, turning into a ball of arcane light and disappearing halfway there.

Jace sat on the doorstep nearby, watching the cave entrance, waiting for the imp to come out. While he waited, he recalled last night's battle.

That first spell the naga had cast… Jace still remembered it.

Anuniji, Durzala, Kana.

He hadn't caught the first one, but the second time, the impression was extremely vivid.

He still remembered the effect of this spell — it made his whole body weak, almost unable to stand, even doing very simple movements was quite strenuous. Was this Curse of Weakness? It didn't sound like demonic language — it sounded more like the elven tongue.

Could it be magic modified by those naga? This was Shadow magic without a doubt, after all it was possible to reduce the aftereffects with Shadow Protection Potion.

Jace murmured it softly: "Anuniji, Durzala, Kana."

Nothing happened, of course — he had no target and didn't make any casting gestures.

He could imitate that naga's pronunciation, but he couldn't imitate the naga's casting gestures.

Because naga had four arms, and each arm had a movement when casting.

Jace silently recited this incantation. He had no paper or pen right now, so he could only rely on his brain to memorize this incantation.

No matter what, this incantation was too important. If he could master the original text of the incantation, perhaps he could deal with some powerful warrior enemies who could resist Fear.

Reciting and reciting, the sky had already started to get dark.

Why hadn't the imp come out yet? Did it die inside?

Jace didn't remember what the imp's summoning incantation was, because it was way too long. He rummaged through his bag and found that slip of paper on which he'd written the summoning incantation and the strangling incantation, and looked at the cave entrance again.

Jace took the stick off his back and held it steady, preparing to try summoning. After all, he didn't plan on going deep into that cave himself — he didn't have Phase Shift, and if something was blocking him inside, that'd be death.

Just as he was about to start reciting, he suddenly saw a spark of fire in the cave.

The spark got brighter and brighter… it was Sazeno!

Sazeno bounced out, dragging a torn burlap sack.

"Master! Master! Help!"

"Help?!"

Jace ran over, grabbed the imp, and took the torn sack in his hand. He only heard a burst of wild howling from inside the cave… this howling, he was way too familiar with it.

Gnolls!

He stuffed the imp into his warlock's satchel and turned to run. Before long, firelight flickered from the cave, and a gnoll came charging out, its whole body ablaze.

"Don't run… little thing!"

The gnoll howled in badly-accented Common. Jace took off running toward the distance.

Those gnolls' stubby little legs naturally couldn't compare to a corporate drone who'd been training with long-distance running in Stormwind City for years. Even carrying several bags he was faster than them, and quickly pulled away.

Although those gnolls couldn't run, they were quite persistent. They chased him for a good ten minutes, and only finally disappeared from sight after Jace had run until he was about to pull a muscle. Jace didn't even know where he'd run to. He found a random rock to lie down on, put his pack to the side, and only then did the imp pop its head out.

"What… did you grab?" he asked.

"Look yourself!" the imp excitedly jumped out of the bag. "Master will definitely be satisfied!"

Jace opened the torn sack — it was actually a big bundle of already-dried herbs!

Some unidentified herbs, and underneath were several bundles of Felweed, almost identical to that pot of plants Furbloom's wife had been growing.

No wonder everyone said there were lots of herbs around, yet he couldn't find a single one — it turned out they'd all been scooped up by the gnolls hiding inside.

But why were there clearly gnolls in this mine, yet Furbloom said he'd never seen any here?

"How many gnolls are inside?" Jace asked the imp. "Did you search carefully?"

"At least a dozen or so! All holed up in the deepest part. They've got an alchemy lab in there, master!" Sazeno added. "A really crude alchemy lab. Those gnolls are constantly brewing medicine inside. There are also lots and lots of murloc corpses!"

Murloc corpses?

Jace hadn't had time to think it through when the imp excitedly continued: "Several gnolls using several pots to brew medicine. It felt like those gnolls were about to suffocate from the stuffiness! I snuck into the room where they kept the herbs, grabbed a light bag, and ran. You don't know how much they had — piled up together… it all smelled rotten!"

This torn sack did indeed have a faint smell like urine, extremely foul. After Jace opened the sack, the smell inside was far worse than outside the sack.

This wasn't the gnolls' filth — it was the smell emitted by one of the herbs.

He had a faint feeling that the gnolls in this cave might be secretly manufacturing something, which was why they never went out and never harassed the surrounding farms — all to avoid drawing the attention of Sentinel Hill or even Westfall Keep.

Those corpses… could it mean they were testing some kind of poison? Were the gnolls at war with the murlocs, or did they have another agenda?

Jace alone definitely couldn't destroy this nest. The herbs in this sack had to be identified by someone as soon as possible — find out what potions these herbs could be made into.
