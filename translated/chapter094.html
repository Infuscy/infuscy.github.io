Chapter 94: The Unlucky Veteran

That evening when he passed Westfall post station, Jace went over to take a look at the military camp.

Compared to when he'd come with Grede to deliver grain a month ago, the camp's scale had even shrunk a bit. He heard it was because some soldiers had gone to Redridge to help clear out gnolls.

He observed for a while, especially looking toward that lone tent area — that strange soldier hadn't appeared.

"What're you looking at here?" A soldier walking over from the side, holding a wet soldier's shirt in his hand, asked. "You're lurking around — what do you want?"

"I'm looking for the commander here," Jace said. "I have… a fairly important matter I'd like to report."

The soldier said, "What is it? Tell me. The captain went south on a scouting mission. I can tell him when he gets back."

This soldier was shirtless, looked about Jace's age or not much older, and had a righteous face with a bit of anger — probably having mistaken Jace for a thief or something.

"You know that guy in your camp who sells scrap iron?" Jace asked.

"I know him," the soldier replied. "Agmond. He's the most senior fighter here — he's been a soldier longer than the captain. He was injured before, so he doesn't go to the front lines anymore."

"He said he was a deserter," Jace said.

"He told you that?" The soldier scratched his head. "What's wrong with him? I'd say if you have some conflict with him, don't come here looking for trouble, brother. No one's gonna help you."

"I don't have a conflict with him," Jace said. "Do you know about his wife?"

The soldier suddenly grabbed Jace's collar and yanked him close. "You trying to mock Agmond? Who the hell do you think you are?"

Jace grabbed the soldier's wrist and squeezed hard. The soldier frowned, looking a bit surprised that Jace's grip was this strong.

"I'm from the Wizard Sanctum, soldier. Calm down," Jace warned.

The soldier noticed his ring and the stick on his back, then glanced at the pouch at his waist. His previously somewhat angry expression dissipated quite a bit.

"Sorry." The soldier let go. "Agmond takes good care of us younger guys. As for his wife… he doesn't have a wife. If you're really asking, that's just something he dreamed up. Did he tell you he had a wife at the fishing village waiting for him to go back? Would even say his wife was more beautiful than Queen Taria…"

Saying this, he paused. "You know those sea mermaid rumors, right? Those mermaids can drain a person's soul. I don't know if Agmond is like that, but based on his description, we all think so. Why are you so interested in this?"

"I'm investigating the murloc migration situation, and their relationship with the gnolls," Jace said. "Earlier, your Mr. Agmond told me he had a wife living in that fishing village. I planned to consult them as coastal residents, but after going there I came up empty, so I planned to come back and ask."

"So that's how it is." The soldier said. "Sorry, because some people at the post station like to mock Agmond and his wife's story, so I misunderstood you. As for Agmond… we think it's precisely because he was bewitched by those mermaids that he made a mistake on the battlefield. You think there's a possibility of that, sir? You mages definitely know way more than we do about this."

"Mermaids can indeed use their voices to bewitch people's minds," Jace said matter-of-factly. "As for whether a soldier would be bewitched into cowardice… I wouldn't dare say. But I guess a soldier who's been bewitched by a mermaid would definitely become a different person."

"That's what I thought," the soldier said.

After the young soldier left, Jace gazed at the fires of the camp. Right now, that strange old soldier with the limp had just come out of the tent, slowly walking toward that lone tent storing battered weapons, seemingly talking to himself along the way, but completely inaudible.

Jace understood in his heart that the odds of the old soldier having been bewitched by a naga into deserting weren't high. If he'd been harassed by a naga on his way to the Hillsbrad Foothills, would the naga have followed him all the way across Baladin Bay to Westfall?

Could it be true love?

If the young soldier's guess was correct — that the old soldier had encountered a naga before deserting — then maybe the naga sea witch in the fishing village had nothing to do with this old soldier at all. Either way, going to find that old soldier again wasn't necessary. Either his "wife" had already died tragically, or this old soldier was just an unfortunate poor wretch who'd coincidentally led Jace to an unfortunate place.

The next morning, Jace returned to Stormwind. He dropped his things and took a bath in the canal, then brought a few scales and the torn sack of herbs straight to the basement of The Slaughtered Lamb.

He hadn't slept very well last night either, because he'd considered that those gnolls were very likely manufacturing some kind of poison. Although it seemed like it was aimed at murlocs, what if they also planned to target Stormwind?

Especially considering the gnoll uprisings at Redridge, the Crossroads, and Westfall Keep — recently the gnolls across the entire Kingdom of Stormwind seemed to be planning to join forces and make trouble.

Was all this related to Hogger? Was Hogger the overall leader of all the gnoll groups now?

Hogger was a powerful warrior, alright, but to say he had enough intelligence to unite all the gnoll tribes to fight humanity again after the Orc Wars?

After greeting Tharle, Jace walked into the back room, gazing at the deep, dark iron gate leading to the underground sanctuary that could be called a "catacomb," recalling how Stormwind dealt with these troubles in the game.

At the World of Warcraft game's timeline — that is, eighteen or nineteen years later — the gnolls' power was still rampant, but they still hadn't reached humanity's major settlements or directly threatened Stormwind's rule. There hadn't been any large-scale poisoning incidents either. Could it be that the gnolls' unification had been thwarted somewhere in between?

Or was that alchemy lab just an ordinary alchemy lab, providing potions to treat diseases for the gnoll tribe, with no other poison-making purposes?

Was Jace overthinking it?

No — there was a massive turning point here that couldn't be ignored: Onyxia.

Ever since Onyxia's arrival, the internal instability within the Kingdom of Stormwind had been rapidly brought under control, effectively unified beneath her wings.

Maybe the gnolls' threat had been eliminated by Onyxia as her political capital in Stormwind…

Of course, all of this was just Jace's speculation. Maybe the gnolls themselves had internal problems, or maybe the hidden backer behind the gnolls had run into trouble.

But right now, the threat was real.

Entering the alchemy lab, Ursula was sitting in the corner reading a book. The crucible wasn't lit, and the air carried a faint medicinal smell — much clearer than when he'd come before.

Ursula looked up. "You're back so soon?"

"Staying longer wouldn't have done much good," Jace said. He opened the bag and handed it to Ursula. "Can you help me figure out what herbs these are?"

Ursula smelled it and frowned. "Deathweed. It's been sitting a while, otherwise it wouldn't have this kind of smell."
