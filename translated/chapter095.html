Chapter 95: Deathweed

"You wouldn't know it normally — even ordinary herbalists might not understand this thing's value," Ursula explained. "From the name alone you should know how dangerous Deathweed is. Don't touch it with your hands. Let me take it out for you."

While searching for the right tool in the box, Ursula said, "Deathweed… it might be in high demand by certain forces in Stormwind, but my suggestion is, don't get involved with them. Don't let them think you can help them find Deathweed. Otherwise, your movements in Stormwind will be much more troublesome than they are now. Jace, you're already one of us — don't take on another burden."

"SI:7 isn't particularly adept at dealing with groups like ours, because of their distrust of magic, so they lack the relevant talent. But for rogues, assassins, killers — roles like that… Anyway, burn these herbs."

She held a long-handled pair of tongs, grabbed the edge of the torn sack, and looked at Jace. "This isn't your own bag, is it?"

"This is a gnoll's bag," Jace said. "That's the problem, Ms. Ursula Deline."

Ursula asked, "You ran into gnolls?"

Jace nodded. "Not only did I run into gnolls, I found their alchemy lab. In those alchemy labs they're conducting some kind of experiment. There are lots of murloc corpses. They're experimenting on murlocs. I don't think the gnoll tribe's ultimate goal is just to poison those murlocs to death."

Ursula asked, "You mean the gnolls want to poison Sentinel Hill or Moonbrook?"

"Not sure yet," Jace said. "Can you tell from the herbs in this bag what they're planning to make?"

Ursula used the tongs to poke through the herbs inside and shrugged. "There are many possibilities. These herbs can be made into a drug that's extremely lethal to spellcasters but does little harm to ordinary people. They can also be made into a poison to contaminate water sources. In any case, it seems like something's still missing. There's Deathweed here, a large amount of Deathweed, plus Briarthorn, quite a bit of weeds, Mageroyal… I can help you pick out all the herbs related to making Mana Potions."

"Thanks." Jace handed the bag to Ursula. "Should I hand this bag over to the Wizard Sanctum so they can report it to the king?"

"Even if you let the king know, what can that little imp do?" Ursula sighed. "The war in Redridge is intense right now. I heard from the grapevine that a gnoll warlock attacked Stonewatch with dark magic just a few days ago. Gagin was just planning to go over there and see if he could get clues to some new incantations. In any case, in this situation, where would the kingdom find the troops to send to Westfall to hunt down a group of gnoll alchemists? The troops guarding Moonbrook, Sentinel Hill, and the Westfall Keep trade route are already stretched thin."

"You know about the new gnoll leader near Westfall? Word is they've already wiped out two mercenary squads. But other than mercenaries, what does the king have? If I had to guess, he's almost lost the ability to even hire outsiders to resolve crises. Right now Hogger's bounty is only thirty silver — and that's after the raise. You think thirty silver can attract what kind of talent?"

Jace felt a bit tempted when he heard thirty silver, but after thinking about the strength of the Hogger he'd encountered that day, that thought immediately died.

Based on the battles he'd been through, Hogger's pure strength far exceeded that of any ordinary gnoll, far exceeded that elven ranger. By Grede's reckoning, Hogger might only be slightly weaker than that Redridge shaman when using wind elements to boost his own strength.

And judging by Hogger's level of ferocity and savagery, if that shaman went up against Hogger, they probably wouldn't have much of a chance either.

After all, an orc shaman using that kind of force required constant communication with the elements, which was extremely draining. But Hogger's stamina… as the leader of a group like the gnolls that worshipped absolute violence, it could be described as bottomless.

Thinking it this way, even someone like him would give up on these thirty silver because of Hogger's strength, to say nothing of the stronger mercenaries. This bounty really was a bit low.

He remembered when he was at Stonewatch, the king's secretary Ailian had told him that the national treasury was in a very tight spot — they couldn't even come up with ten gold to reward the heroes of Stonewatch. Maybe these thirty silver were handed out quite reluctantly.

"Short some Silverleaf." Ursula pressed her waist. "You planning to buy from me, or buy outside?"

"I'll buy from you."

"4 silver a pound."

Jace remembered the outside purchase price was 2 silver 50 copper and immediately asked, "You're screwing even your own people?"

Ursula smiled and said, "I'm guessing you've sold herbs to that Lumere woman outside? Go buy from her — the price is even higher than mine."

Jace rubbed his forehead. He didn't have much money left right now. Buying some Silverleaf outright would really hurt.

"You make it for me first. However much Silverleaf you use, I'll go pick more to make up to you after. How's that?"

"Don't take too long."

"Deal."

"Oh right," Ursula said. "How'd that thing I asked you to do go? Don't tell me you didn't even see a murloc and ran back as soon as you ran into gnolls."

"Didn't see any murlocs." Jace pulled out the largest of the three scales he'd brought and said, "But I picked this up."

"Naga scale!" Ursula narrowed her eyes. "This… it's gorgeous."

"Found it on the beach."

Jace of course wouldn't tell her he had a whole bag of these things at home.

But looking at Ursula's expression right now, he kind of regretted not bringing the naga's head back. But at the time, the way that head looked at night — he felt his sanity was about to bottom out.

Ursula held the scale and said, "This kind of scale is an excellent Frost magic enchantment material, Jace. A robe made from it can increase Frost resistance and strengthen affinity with water elements, boosting the caster's water magic power. How many did you find?"

"How many scales are there to find?" Jace asked. "How much can this one sell for?"

"This one's in quite good shape," Ursula said. "You give this to me, and I won't charge you for the Silverleaf. How's that?"

Jace frowned. "You're not screwing me, are you?"

Ursula smiled and said, "Although this thing is rare, the demand isn't big. Only those Frost magic practitioners at the Wizard Sanctum can use it, or for making poisons — yes, this thing also has some toxicity. Even if you sold it outside, it'd be hard to get a price of ten silver. I just… need it for some formula experiments I'm working on right now. You just give it to me, and I'll make you six Mana Potions. Whatever Silverleaf is lacking, I'll cover it for you. Any extra materials I'll give back to you. How about it?"

"Fine."

"Come get your potions tomorrow morning, Jace."

Coming out of The Slaughtered Lamb, Jace had already planned to go ask at the tailor shop in the Mage Quarter. He had so many scales — although there weren't many as large or intact as this one… even if each could only sell for one silver, that'd be thirty or forty silver! A fortune!
