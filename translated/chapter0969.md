## Chapter 969: An Unintended Clue

"Then I'll begin," Molus said.

Jace stepped back to Sola's side. The dwarf warlock raised both hands and began to chant.

The dwarf began with a sacrifice spell. Jace knew this incantation but almost never used it. For him, the shadow fire released by combining his manipulation of fire elements with his own shadow power was far more powerful than the fel flames of a sacrifice. But this time it looked like he'd have to dust off this demon incantation he'd left forgotten in a corner.

When the chanting was complete, the blood runes in the circle ignited all at once, belching fel-green flames. But the shadow in the blood was no less aggressive — it clashed with the flames like two rabid dogs tearing at each other, letting out piercing shrieks.

But soon the two sides seemed to find some way to coexist, intertwining and leaping together over the blood-light. It reminded Jace of the Death Coil condensed from sacrificed demon souls — perhaps this was the true process of simulating "demon blood."

"Velilinki, Toxintani..."

The dwarf continued casting, this time beginning the summoning incantation. Jace cross-referenced the casting gestures and incantation recorded in the book, and observed the two soul shards the imp had placed around the perimeter, seeing the soul energy within them rapidly dissipate.

"...Tosesta, Maalimasta."

As Molus chanted, Jace silently memorized the incantation at the same time.

Not long after the incantation was complete, the blood runes on the circle dried up and the flames died down. A void rift unfolded from the center, and Molus's succubus servant walked out.

The succubus looked around, then asked, "Why did you summon me, master? Is something the matter? I haven't found Mr. Fremal yet..."

"If you haven't found him by now, he's most likely gone to the barracks. Don't waste any more time. And I imagine our guests aren't in the mood for dinner right now either." Molus turned to look at Jace, then asked eagerly, "Well, Master Slovakia?"

Jace nodded. "I'd like to give it a try. Can I do it right here?"

"Of course." Molus glanced at his pocket watch. "Redraw the circle, Kipjip!"

"Yes, master!" The imp immediately began erasing the circle that had already been damaged by the magic.

"Who are you planning to summon, master?" Moruphil asked.

Jace thought about the demon servants stationed far away. The two felhounds guarding Ilgath Tower couldn't be moved — with only one left on watch, he couldn't be sure it could hold off an attack by gnoll warriors and warlocks.

The other one was in the SI:7 prison helping Mathias track down dragonkin. That one was best left alone too, to avoid SI:7 mistakenly thinking something had gone wrong.

The biggest problem was that he hadn't given these felhounds any proper names at all — they were all "Dazarm." To avoid problems with the summoning incantation, he couldn't use them as summoning targets either.

Kalez was even more out of the circle — even if the summoning succeeded, this stone hall couldn't fit him. "I'll summon one now," Jace said.

Molus handed over a soul shard. Jace said thank you, took the shard, and chanted the magic, soon summoning a small-bodied felhound.

"Gahun." Jace named the felhound using a word from the Annihilan language he'd picked from his list of names, then said in demonic, "Leave the hall and wait at the end of the corridor."

The felhound let out a string of puttering responses and scampered out of the stone hall, the sound of its claws scraping the flagstones growing more distant. Jace sat on a stone bench nearby and looked at Moruphil. "Help me cut my wrist for the blood."

"Yes, master."

Though Moruphil agreed, she frowned with hesitation.

Still, she eventually drew a dagger and braced Jace's arm. The moment the blade tip touched Jace's skin, the demon fire in her eyes guttered by more than half.

"What is it?" Jace asked.

"I don't know... probably because of the command you placed on me not to harm you," Moruphil said softly. Jace looked up at her. "You usually have no problem using your tail to cut me..."

The succubus seemed to grit her teeth in irritation. Seeing her react that way, Jace took the dagger back. "I'll do it myself."

Moruphil nodded and stepped aside. The succubus belonging to Molus, watching from not far away, had a faint smile on her face — whether it was the habitual false smile of a succubus or a smile with some purpose, at least Moruphil didn't look pleased when she looked at her.

After drawing roughly a Lesser Mana Potion's worth of blood, Molus nodded. "That's about enough. This should be enough for three or four attempts. Your blood is very powerful — it burns as fiercely as shadow fire itself, hard to detect beneath your skin..."

"Could I keep a little of your blood, Master Slovakia?" the dwarf asked tentatively.

"No," Jace said.

"What a shame." The dwarf shrugged.

Jace had originally intended to call on fire elements to cauterize the wound, but glancing at the Dark Iron dwarf ahead, he decided not to reveal too much.

He wrapped his wrist with clean linen bandages the dwarf handed him, drank a small vial of healing potion, caught his breath, and stepped up to the circle.

After writing Gahun's Annihilan name in his own blood, Molus confirmed the script was compatible with the circle, then nodded and stepped aside.

Jace chanted the sacrifice incantation, calling on fel fire to ignite his blood — but clearly the power of this fel fire fell a good deal short of the dwarf warlock's magic.

He hadn't cast sacrifice fel fire much, so lack of practice might have been part of it, but clearly the weakness of the fel fire couldn't support the union of shadow power and fel power either, and naturally couldn't bring about the formation of simulated demon blood.

After a bout of boiling and churning, the script in the center of the circle was damaged — that harmonious demon fire never formed at all.

"It seems before learning the servant-summoning spell, you still need to strengthen your fel magic, Master Slovakia," Molus said with a helpless expression.

Jace truly was lacking in his study of fel magic. The most powerful demon spell he'd learned so far was still the Cripple that Kalez had taught him.

Wait a moment...

Jace wiped away the scorch marks, rewrote Gahun's name, and said, "Sazeno, Curse of Tongues."

"Curse of Tongues?" The Dark Iron dwarf looked at Jace in confusion. "What are you planning to do?"

Jace didn't answer. Sazeno had already begun chanting the Curse of Tongues incantation. When the incantation was complete, a searing sensation quickly crawled up from his neck, choking Jace into a couple of coughs — but each cough sounded more like a demon's cough than the last.

Molus and his succubus's expressions grew more and more shocked. He seemed to guess what Jace was planning, but was unwilling to believe it. Moruphil was already sitting to the side smiling, thoroughly enjoying the looks on this master-and-servant pair's faces.

Jace rasped the word out in demonic. Molus flinched as if he'd been struck. "How can you control what you want to say under the torment of a Curse of Tongues?"

Jace looked at him and laughed — a laugh that really did sound like Kazel's waves of mocking, sinister laughter, dull enough to make one's scalp tingle.

He wasn't going to waste energy answering the dwarf's question. Instead he pointed straight at his own blood and chanted the sacrifice incantation. That line of runes immediately shot up a tongue of green flame, then triggered the frenzied resistance of the shadow blood. The two intertwined and wrapped around each other, like a ghostly dancer laced with purple-red and emerald fel light.

"Now, master," Moruphil said softly.

Jace nodded, gave the incantation in the grimoire one last look, closed his eyes, raised both hands, and channeled the summoning power. "Velilinki, Toxintani, Tosesta, Maalimasta."

The incantation complete, the flame surged one final time, and a burning void gate opened at the top. Gahun leaped out of it, looked around in bewilderment, then let out a series of steam-engine puttering sounds at Jace.

Jace stared at the felhound, forcing down a swallow against the searing pain in his throat. His expression was hard to look at, but inside he was barely containing his excitement. It worked...

In any case, this was the first time he'd successfully summoned a servant from far away entirely on his own.

Severe mana depletion beaded his forehead with sweat, but he knew this was just because he was still unfamiliar with the incantation, the casting, and everything else. As long as he kept optimizing from here...

Seeing this, Molus couldn't help but start clapping. He said in amazement, "I've never seen a way to compensate for insufficient fel fire power like this... Master Slovakia, I don't even know how to describe it... Curse of Tongues, by the Light. It makes sense that you'd be able to attract a Sayaad demon like this."

"I... need water," Jace said in demonic.

"Bring me my cellared Frostwine, dear," Molus called out.

Taking the wine poured by the succubus into a Dark Iron goblet, Jace drained it in one gulp. The wine was ice-cold, as if it had just been pulled from an ice blue dragon's lair. Downing it in one go in the stuffy heat of the Blackrock Mountain underground seemed to dispel even the power of the Curse of Tongues by more than half.

Jace rolled his tongue around. "Th... thank you very much."

"Is there a method for resisting the Curse of Tongues... could you possibly teach it to me too?" Molus asked, lowering his head.

"Relatively speaking, that's considerably more difficult than the Demonic Empowerment spell, Mr. Molus." Jace set the goblet aside and coughed twice. "Frankly, though there are a few tricks, they aren't decisive. I basically managed this through willpower and long training. I do have some shortcomings in fel magic — that may be a flaw in my own character, or... simply a lack of talent."

"A living person who can resist the Curse of Tongues standing here... that already says more than anything, Master Slovakia!" Molus finished, laughed heartily twice, and gave Jace a fierce hug. "Think about it — chanting demon magic with a demon's throat. The demon who invented the Curse of Tongues probably never imagined anyone would do this!"

"Now I'll teach you Demonic Empowerment," Jace said. "This spell was stolen from the orcs, so there are no records in Dwarvish or Common. My friend and I can help you translate a Dwarvish version."

"Thank you very much," the dwarf said. "My Orcish isn't particularly fluent yet... being able to read a Dwarvish version directly would be best."

Jace flipped his cloak aside, took the demon satchel from beneath it, and flipped through his spellbook, looking for the record of the Demonic Empowerment spell that Demisette had given him, tucked inside.

Molus glanced at the cloak and complimented him, "Made of black dragon hide — extremely expensive and beautiful, master. Even in Shadowforge City it's a rare sight."

"And it's also earned me the black dragons' enmity several times, nearly costing me my life. I'm not sure whether it was worth it," Jace said offhandedly. "One of the reasons I came to Shadowforge City is that I heard there's a way to counter black dragon magic here."

"Hmm..." Molus stared at the booklet in Jace's hand. "I once tried to ask the pyromancers about information on this incantation, but it didn't go well. My relationship with the pyromancers isn't very good."

Sola glanced at Jace. Jace licked his lips and nodded at the flowing script written by Demisette Crowlas.
