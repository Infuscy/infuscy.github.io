Chapter 987: The Dagger's Edge

After bidding farewell to Windsor and his soldiers, Jace and Sola headed east. Once they'd left the soldiers' sight and entered a charred forest, Sola returned to her natural green dragon form and carried Jace flying northeast.

Reaching Grim Batol required traveling north across the entire Blasted Lands and into Khaz Modan — an almost impossible task for an ordinary traveler, but for a green dragon like Solamere, it counted for nothing.

Flying along the eastern coast of the continent, although there was nothing but Goblins' Gulch as far as the eye could see, at least there were directional references so they wouldn't get lost in the desert. After roughly two days' time, rivers and forests covered the ground once more.

Although along the way they encountered not only no black dragons or orcs but hardly any living creatures at all, Solamere remained tense the entire time, kept asking Jace what to pay attention to when meeting the Dragonqueen. "Is Queen Alexstrasza's temper good or bad?" was asked five times across two days.

It was only as they approached the Bathor Mountains that Jace, seeing the familiar landscape, felt his heart grow slightly tense too.

And it wasn't because of meeting the Dragonqueen. It was because of that hellish scene, and that colossal collapsed carcass at the edge of his vision.

However, as he flew over the Bathor Mountains, he didn't sense that terrifying dark power that had lingered even long after they'd flown away from Grim Batol a year ago. He even heard faint, indistinct voices in the empty valley — possibly Dragonmaw orcs, or perhaps dwarves who'd come here to hunt.

But no matter who it was, it meant people dared to return here. The apocalyptic influence the black dragons had brought was already considerably weaker than before.

By evening, Jace felt that the closer they got to Grim Batol, the denser and more lush the forest vegetation of the entire region seemed to become. This place didn't look like it had endured a devastating hellfire just a year ago — if anything, it more resembled the best-preserved stretch of the entire mountain range.

The surging power of life drifted formlessly through the forest. Even Solamere, who had still been anxious, calmed down involuntarily.

Clearly, over this past year, the red dragons' healing power had already produced remarkable results. Perhaps in the future the Wildhammer dwarves would truly be able to return here and rebuild their homeland.

As Solamere pressed forward, several dragon silhouettes nearby also drew closer in the light of the setting sun. Of course they didn't launch any attacks, nor did they try to ask anything — they just silently accompanied them, helping the green dragon guide the way.

By now it was already winter. Beneath the mountains of Khaz Modan, which should have been bitterly cold, the air wasn't so piercing. Countless birds flew over the mountains, flocking in groups around a lowland in the valley filled with flowers of every color. No guessing was needed to know who was there.

At that moment, a enormous blood-red head lifted from among the treetops and looked over — but that wasn't Queen Alexstrasza. It was an ancient giant dragon who seemed somewhat unfamiliar, yet not entirely unfamiliar.

"Welcome."

The giant red dragon gently parted the canopy with its massive claws, folded its red-glowing enormous wings, and walked with slow steps before the green dragon who had landed on the flower-covered meadow. Lowering its head to look at the green dragon, who resembled a tiny pet in comparison, it said: "Solamere, Jace Sesso… why have you come here on your own?"

"Your Excellency Prince Korialstrasz." Solamere lowered her serpent-like head and bowed respectfully. Only then did Jace confirm that this red dragon with golden pendants hanging from its horns was Krasus. One of his eyes was visibly somewhat clouded — the curse of Deathwing seemed already beyond repair.

"Have you come here seeking Queen Alexstrasza?" Korialstrasz said. "Unfortunately, the Queen is sleeping in the Dream. I am here to guard her and Grim Batol."

"The Queen is sleeping?" Jace asked. Upon hearing that they wouldn't have to meet the Queen, the green dragon beside him breathed a huge sigh of relief.

Korialstrasz said, "Indeed. She is helping Queen Ysera purify the fallen corruption. It was precisely because of your timely report that the threat of the Nightmare was kept from reaching a point of no return."

"Truly? The Queen was really willing to help?" Solamere asked, lifting her head in surprise.

"Regarding Queen Ysera's difficulties, the Queen has never had the slightest hesitation," the red dragon said with a slight nod.

"Thank you, Your Excellency. I don't know what to say," Sola said nervously. The red dragon slowly lowered its head and said, "There's no need for such words. The Queen is grateful to you. After all, it has been a long time since she last heard Queen Ysera's voice. Although for the sleeping Dream-Queen there is nothing particularly unusual about this, it is precisely for this reason that it's easy for other companions to overlook the latent crisis of the Emerald Dragonflight."

Hearing this, Jace felt that the green dragon beside him not only instantly cast aside all her nervousness — the excitement and joy in her heart were practically seeping out from between her scales.

"If it's not regarding Dream matters," Korialstrasz looked toward Jace and asked, "then what other difficulties are there?"

"Archmage Krasus." Jace looked up and said, "Do you remember the demon incident in Moonbrook I mentioned to you before? There are now sufficient indications that… it's Deathwang's children causing trouble within the borders of the Stormwind Kingdom. I suspect they're trying to replicate what their father did in Lordaeron all over again here in Stormwind."

"I haven't been following Alliance affairs lately," the red dragon said. "Who do you suspect?"

"It's Katrana Prestor," Jace said.

"I met that Prestor in Lordaeron," the red dragon said. "I didn't see anything unusual about her, Jace. Can you be certain?"

"I can only be certain she is not the original Prestor, Archmage," Jace said.

"You're a master of manipulating shadow, Jace. Perhaps you can see things about black dragons that I cannot — I'm not surprised by that," Korialstrasz thought for a moment and said. "Ever since the business with Daval Prestor, I'm not sure I have the ability to recognize a dragon's disguise either. After all, Nefarian and his siblings are no less powerful than I am, so there's no need to treat my judgment as authoritative. Is there anything I can help with?"

"Actually," Jace said, "I've now obtained something that many people believe can break the magic of black dragons — at least their disguises."

"Break their magic?" Korialstrasz was taken aback. He glanced at Solamere beside him — clearly, he rather doubted there existed anything in this world that could strip a dragon's shapeshifting.

Jace took out the package containing the stone tablets from his pack, which the Rune-Smiths had given him. The red dragon raised its head and began to burn all over, rapidly shrinking in a blaze of scorching flames. Eventually, an arm of a High Elf reached out from the flames and took the package Jace handed over. The flames then receded just as quickly.

Krasus shook off the last wisp of fire from his golden hair, unfolded the package, took out the stone tablets, and looked at the runes on them while his lips moved along silently.

Jace handed the black ring he'd gotten from Windsor to Krasus as well and said: "The one who made this stone tablet used this ring to encrypt the incantation. He told me this ring must be used to activate the incantation."

Krasus took the ring, closed his eyes, and sensed it for a while. His expression grew increasingly grave. "What was this written with?" he asked.

"The black dragons intended to combine the bloodlines of all dragonkind to create a new species of dragon — chromatic dragons," Jace said. "This is the blood of that new species."

"So those orc rumors were true," Krasus recalled slightly. "Rhonin and I heard those orcs mention this kind of experiment in Blackrock Spire, but I've never seen the new dragon-creature with my own eyes."

Jace said, "The one who crafted this incantation participated in the experiment. According to him, the experiment hasn't been very successful."

"I don't understand." Krasus examined the ring carefully and said, "I can sense that the magic on it seems to have something in common with the encryption magic in the Kirin Tor's vault — exquisite and compact. How is it that the creator participated in Nefarian's experiments?"

"The creator is a Kirin Tor alchemist who Nefarian forcibly 'ascended,'" Jace said.

Krasus's brow furrowed slightly. "I cannot imagine what kind of pain and torment he went through. Perhaps this is also what drove him to complete this thing. This incantation is indeed sufficient to deal a heavy blow to a dragon and force them to reveal their true form. Not only that, it's designed very deliberately. The reason it's so long and complicated… is because its energy is limited, so it deliberately avoids any possibility of mistake or waste of power, targeting only the most powerful black dragon within its range. It's like an assassination tool specifically designed to target Deathwing's children."

Listening to Krasus say this, Jace seemed to understand what the orcs had meant by a dagger stabbed into the heart of a black dragon.

Jace recalled that in the game, after Windsor recited the incantation in the royal palace, indeed only Onyxia was forced to reveal her true form, while the other soldiers disguised as dragons revealed themselves in response to the mother dragon's call — very likely because of this.

Solamere asked: "So what you're saying is, if there are other weaker dragons or drake-men around its target, this incantation can ensure all its power is aimed at their leader?"

"Well said." Krasus handed the ring and stone tablets back to Jace. "Considering that the one who made it was an ascendant who wanted to rebel against the black dragons, and was inside the Blackwing Lair, this precision was a necessity. So he really went to great lengths to ensure he could leave a deep impression on his true master."

"Then that means it has to succeed in one shot," Jace hesitated and said. "I'm wondering, Archmage — could you come back to Stormwind with me… I want to be absolutely certain and not give that disguised black dragon a chance to escape."

Krasus said without the slightest hesitation: "No problem, Jace."

"I need to summon reliable kin first and arrange the work of protecting the Queen and Grim Batol. Once everything is in order, I'll come find you in Stormwind."
