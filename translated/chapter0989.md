## Chapter 989: An Anomaly in the Palace

After sending Sola back to her apartment, Jace hurried straight to The Slaughtered Lamb.

Hopefully those people had already returned — otherwise he'd have to spend two days running down to Duskwood. And if he'd already arranged with Ursula to brew some kind of potion, he'd have to make another trip just to pick it up, which would be way too much trouble.

But right now, with the threat of Onyxia hanging over everything, Jace couldn't help thinking — there might be a chance they'd be forced into a big fight in the Park. Would The Slaughtered Lamb get dragged into it too? And she was a black dragon princess…

Even if they evacuated the warlocks in advance, what about the entire Mage Quarter? The Trade District?

They had to find a way to lure her outside the city. This point was practically non-negotiable. They'd barely managed to avoid Deathwing coming to wreck the place — couldn't let his daughter make up for that "regret" out of nowhere.

Arriving at the door of The Slaughtered Lamb, Jace saw an unfamiliar customer glance around the entrance and then walk in. Was Tharle Mor open for business again?

He followed in for a look. A fellow showing only one black arm was hauling a crate toward the back kitchen. Catching sight of Jace at the door from the corner of his eye, he turned his head.

"Oh, it's you." Tharle rolled his eyes, the whites glaring starkly against his pitch-black skin. "Haven't seen you in ages. What've you been up to?" Jace asked.

Tharle set the crate on the counter and said quietly: "Spent some time in Lakeshire. Later, Dudemoor found me and said I could come back, so I reopened the shop. This period's losses are being covered by the Dagger Society, so I didn't really lose anything."

"Have they all moved back?" Jace asked.

"Not completely yet, but close." Tharle raised an eyebrow. "What about you? Lemme guess — been avoiding the hassle of moving, found some excuse to hide?"

"Compared to the trouble I'm dealing with right now, I'd honestly rather it was just a matter of moving house." Jace leaned on the counter and glanced toward the back. "Is anyone in the basement right now? I need to see Ursula about something."

"Nah, like I said, they're all still moving. Bad timing." Tharle said: "Tomorrow or the day after — Ursula's got the most stuff to move, but then again she makes the most money."

Jace nodded and asked: "Anything new going on in Stormwind?"

"New? Yes and no." Tharle said: "It's all just that business about the Alterac princess. Have you heard?"

"Of course I've heard. I don't live in a cave," Jace said.

Tharle leaned on the counter, thinking. "Other than that… oh, the Queen's pregnant."

"Tell me something I don't know," Jace said.

Tharle smiled. "Drinks on you? It's not busy right now, and you're not in a rush to go to the basement."

"Sure, why not," Jace said.

The tall bartender hauled out a jug of wine and glasses from the back, poured, and handed one to Jace. "Not as good as what you drink in Dalaran, but definitely not bad."

Jace took a sip and asked: "About that Alterac princess — has she made any other moves recently? I haven't been in Stormwind these past few days."

Tharle shook his head and said: "No. She's been staying in the Park the whole time, occasionally going to the palace to see the King, or other nobles coming to pay their respects. I hear she's been social with quite a few noble families — Lescovar and Elgadin especially. A lot of folks in the Mage Quarter say she's angling for the Moonbrook lands. I figure with Daval Prestor's whereabouts unknown, and her reputation up north already halfway to witch territory, she probably wants to find a chance to settle down here."

"Why would the King give Moonbrook to a northerner?" Jace asked.

"Originally, pretty unlikely." Tharle said: "But now — well, don't forget you're a northerner too… A lot of people say that under your influence, the King's been favoring northerners more and more."

"What's that got to do with me?" Jace put down his glass, eyes widening. "I only handle dark magic and demon stuff. I never get involved in politics."

"The way you talk…" Tharle sighed softly. "Magic can kill people easily, change the weather — and you say you're not involved? Fine. I don't care what other people think, I believe you. But you are in the palace, and there are some things I really don't know whether I should tell you."

"Just say it, Tharle. How long have we known each other?" Jace said.

Tharle hesitated for a good while, unconsciously drinking several mouthfuls of wine before saying: "A lot of people think there's something going on between the King and that princess."

"You believe that too?" Jace asked. "This isn't exactly a new rumor, is there something new? Did you see something?"

"Those nobles messing around in private — we wouldn't catch that. Much less His Majesty." Tharle smacked his lips. "Nobody's actually seen anything with their own eyes, but there seems to be some… how shall I put it… a crack between the Queen and the King now. Queen Tiffen is pregnant right now, think about it — the King's still young, he's only in his twenties, and that princess is stunning beyond human, throwing herself at him… If it were me, I wouldn't be able to resist. I'm not an upright man, I admit it."

Jace pondered this. "But I know Varian…"

"There you go." Tharle swirled his glass. "You're already on the King's side — even if I tell you, you won't believe it. But what's certain is that Queen Tiffen is definitely in a bad mood. If this goes on, it might affect the health of the future Prince or Princess."

Jace drank his wine in silence, then asked: "She's in a bad mood — how do you know that?"

Tharle cleared his throat and lowered his voice: "The King and Queen used to go to the Cathedral to pray to the Holy Light and distribute food to refugees — you know that. The last time the Queen made an appearance, something already felt off between her and the King. A few days ago she didn't show up at all — only Varian Wrynn came."

"And then? That Lady Katrana Prestor was the star of the Cathedral, wasn't she? She definitely doesn't have the nerve to stand next to the King when the Queen's not there, but the contrast is striking enough, isn't it? It's true — plenty of people in Stormwind like her. But I don't like the feeling of this. If Dudemound were messing around, nobody would care. But the person who rules this country shouldn't be like this. That's my view."

"Maybe it's just the pregnancy making it inconvenient for her to go out," Jace said offhandedly.

"We're still far from the stage where her belly's too big to walk, Jace." Tharle leaned in and said quietly: "Of course, you can say it's all speculation, no proof, and I can't argue."

Jace took another sip of wine. He had to admit he seemed to be using this motion to cover his unease inside. Was that female dragon really planning to use such a crude method to replace the Queen and control the King?

He couldn't act rashly before Krasus arrived. He needed to find Mathias as soon as possible and confirm exactly what was going on.
