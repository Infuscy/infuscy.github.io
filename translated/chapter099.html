Chapter 99: Andormas's Office

The Arcane Intellect incantation wasn't difficult. Relying on his sensitivity to incantations, Jace memorized it after hearing it just once.

"Misthis, Eupori, Hininge." After Marin finished reciting it, Jace could even hear the connection between this incantation and modern Common. The phrase roughly meant: grant the eyes to see deeper things.

But the truly difficult part of this spell was casting it on himself.

This was Jace's first time learning a spell that he released on himself. His previous magic had either been hurling a blast of shadow energy straight at someone to wreck their sanity and terrorize them, or gathering a tiny orb of energy at his fingertip. Self-casting was a whole new experience.

Through this process, he also understood Marin's intention: learn a relatively simple incantation first to practice self-casting, and later summoning other, more complex arcane forms to enchant himself would be safer and more practiced.

After Marin performed a demonstration, Jace began trying to mimic his gestures to self-cast.

Unsurprisingly, the first attempt ended in miserable failure. He gathered a mass of indescribable arcane energy that coalesced in the air into a set of disordered runes, then scattered — nearly singeing his hair in the process.

"Connect the mana weave to yourself, channel the arcane energy in your blood into yourself, and shape that arcane energy according to the incantation — that's far more subtle than simply letting it cause destruction," Marin said. "So this is a completely different experience from simply gathering an arcane light, Mr. Sesso. Don't rush it. You can practice this one on your own at home. I need to head to the university library in a bit, and right now I'm going to eat something — I haven't taken a single bite out of the bread I bought."

Despite agreeing verbally, Jace was still a bit discouraged.

Clearly, having learned and mastered Light didn't mean he had any particular talent for other arcane magic. Even from Marin's expression, he could tell he wasn't performing any better than Marin's other students.

After bidding Marin farewell, Jace climbed to the top of the Wizard Sanctum to find Archmage Andormas and select his courses, just as Marin had told him.

Although he'd heard many people mention the leader of the Wizard Sanctum, High Mage Andormas, he'd never actually laid eyes on the man.

Walking to the very top of the Wizard Sanctum, he turned left from the corridor leading to the library, passed through a busy office area, then went through a circular, ascending carpeted corridor lined with portraits of the Wizard Sanctum's great mages throughout history.

Here, Jace saw the portrait of Nielas Aran — the very father Marin had just mentioned, the poor man Medivh had killed by mistake while asleep.

A remarkably handsome man, with a scholar's-style mustache on his upper lip, long hair braided into several plaits that hung over his chest, and a lean, resolute face staring straight ahead. He didn't look like a stubborn, conservative mage — more like some kingdom's prince.

In the portrait he was probably not yet forty. Any younger, and he would have been even more dashing.

No wonder Aegwynn, the eight-hundred-year-old Guardian, had ultimately chosen this man as her "twilight years" consort and been willing to bear a child for him.

At the end of the corridor was a dark-red door, old enough to plausibly be around the same age as Aegwynn.

The door was ajar. Jace pushed it open. Behind it was a fairly large polygonal office, with quite a few people still working inside, some conversing in hushed voices. Above their heads, each wall held a massive window — these were the spires of the Wizard Sanctum.

Jace had never been here in the game, so he was a bit captivated by this novel hall. Just then, a middle-aged man asked, "Can I help you, apprentice?"

"I'm Master Marin's student. I'm here to see Archmage Andormas," Jace said.

"I am Andormas. If I'm not mistaken, you'd be Jace Sesso?"

Jace looked at the speaker. He was an extremely robust man — even wearing the heavy purple robes and golden pectoral, his powerful build was unmistakable.

You could even see the marks of training on his face; distinct muscles were visible on his neck.

Somewhat at odds with his warrior-like physique, his expression was composed and calm, and his gaze when looking at people carried a hint of detachment, like an outsider observing the scene from the sidelines. It made Jace feel dizzy for a moment, as though the words he'd just heard hadn't been spoken to him at all. He was younger than Marin, but clearly held a much higher position. And on top of studying magic, he clearly still had time to train his physical conditioning — perhaps this was the very thing the Wizard Sanctum had always emphasized as magical talent.

Jace wondered how many people like Andormas there were in Dalaran.

"I am indeed Jace Sesso." Jace bowed slightly. "Master Marin sent me here to select my courses."

"You came quickly," Andormas said. "Though if you're selecting courses now, formal classes probably won't start until after Winter Veil. Did Master Marin tell you that?"

"He did," Jace replied.

"Come over to my desk."

Andormas led Jace to a corner, rummaging through the desk.

Jace glanced at the bookshelves behind him and could practically feel the arcane energy brimming from those books. Without question, they were all magic-related — nothing like the jumbled encyclopedias, natural history tomes, and novels in Marin's study. The difference was like that between the Wizard Sanctum's library and the university library.

"Every apprentice chooses one specialized school, one incantation language, and one auxiliary course."

As he spoke, Andormas laid several booklets out on the desk. "Have you looked into the arcane schools before?"

"Yes," Jace said. "Conjuration, Divination, Illusion, Enchantment, Transmutation, Evocation, Abjuration, Necromancy."

"It seems you've read Archmage Ansirem Runeweaver's series," Andormas said. "For well-known reasons, the school of Necromancy isn't within our academy's curriculum, so you'll have to pick from the other schools."

He smiled, then continued: "The grimoire for each school in the Wizard Sanctum is right here. Since you were willing to make entering here the wish you pledged to the king, I imagine you know more about the academy than most new apprentices do. I don't need to give you the full introduction. Sign your name in the roster at the back of the grimoire, and you can officially become a new student of that school."

Jace nodded, then placed his hand on the abjuration grimoire.

"Abjuration," Andormas said. "A pity — the most accomplished abjuration mages are all in Lordaeron and Dalaran. Still, laying a foundation here at the Wizard Sanctum is good. Next, choose your incantation language. You can pick one among draconic, giant runes, and Thalassian. After passing the graduation exam you can choose another. I'd say, for you, at most a year — so no need to agonize over it."

Jace had just picked up the language selection booklet when he saw there was even a Common course listed.

"Common?"

"Ah, that's for the foreign peoples within and beyond the Alliance," Andormas said. "You might not imagine that some dwarves or gnomes would be willing to come study magic at the Wizard Sanctum — but it does happen."

"I understand." Jace remembered Marin had said that Stormwind's Thalassian courses were, frankly, a disaster — they'd basically become a crash-course training ground for the royal family's diplomatic personnel.

"Draconic. I'll take draconic."

"Your ambition is high-level incantations?" Andormas nodded with a trace of approval. "Draconic is a very difficult language. Be prepared for a tough grind."

"Master, which abjuration spells are in draconic?" Jace asked.

Andormas thought for a moment. "Quite a few. Many abjuration spells are extremely ancient and fundamental, and naturally stem from ancient draconic. Legend has it that the dragonkind created many abjuration spells to protect their precious treasures — such as the Ice Barrier incantation, the Mage Armor incantation, the Frost Armor incantation, and so on."

Hearing Mage Armor, Jace's resolve hardened.

"Now then, the elective course," Andormas said. "You can choose one from the following list."

Jace pulled down the list. The curriculum included a long string of liberal arts courses — history, culture, geography, and so on — and also included Inscription and Alchemy, schools that had appeared in the game. As for Enchantment, it was probably a branch within the school of Enchantment.

Inscription and Alchemy were likely two of the Wizard Sanctum's major sources of income — especially Inscription… magical scrolls, inscribed glyphs, magical ink and paper production, and so on, all fell under it.

He'd already found a teacher for Alchemy — Ursula Deline. He didn't know how skilled she was, but she sounded impressive enough. So why not sign up for Inscription? He wondered if getting into the Inscription course came with a chance to score free ink and paper, or to learn some simple magic-powered ink-making methods. That would save him some money.

After signing his name in the Inscription grimoire, Andormas collected the grimoires and said, "After Winter Veil, before January 18 of next year, be sure to go to Master Marin to collect your course schedule. Before then, you can start doing some abjuration-related preparatory study under Master Marin. He's one of the best abjuration instructors we have here. Study hard under him for two months, and by then you might already be ahead of many apprentices."

After organizing the grimoires, Andormas looked Jace up and down with an appraising smile and said, "By the way, Jace Sesso — now that you're in the Wizard Sanctum, couldn't you change into a different set of clothes?"

"I'm used to it, Master." Jace rubbed his head, too embarrassed to say it outright. "Would dressing like this outside reflect badly on the Wizard Sanctum?"

Andormas smiled. "Not at all. It's just that nobody would ever take you for someone from the Wizard Sanctum."

Jace went "ah" and asked, "Then if I wanted to get a set of clothes befitting a mage, where should I go?"

Andormas glanced around at the people nearby. "Go outside the Wizard Sanctum to Larson's Tailor Shop. The Larsons have an agreement with the Wizard Sanctum — they tailor clothes for our mages. Remember to go there to get fitted."

Jace was a bit short on coin right now, and getting a new set of clothes wasn't the most urgent thing on his plate — after all, he still had plenty to do.

So the moment he came out of the Wizard Sanctum, he hurried straight home to continue studying orcish, and started using the demon-to-Common translation notes the imp had produced to cross-reference and translate that orcish-demon bilingual text.

There was less than a month left until November 12. He had to seize every moment.
