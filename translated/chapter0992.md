## Chapter 992: Better Safe Than Sorry

"Your blood?" Ursula was dazed for a moment before asking. "What do you need so much blood for? Drawing that much at once is going to be a problem."

Jace pointed to his wrist. "The single most important casting material for the summoning-minion spell is the master's blood."

"Ah, right, I see — casting material, that makes sense." Ursula nodded. "The power of blood strengthens the bond between master and minion. I've got the bottle you want, but given its size... if you're planning to fill it all in one go, you'd better have a Lesser Healing Potion on hand."

"I know," Jace nodded.

"Then I'll prepare you something to prevent the blood from clotting — it's extracted from the venom of the blood-draining spiders in the Wetlands." Ursula snapped her fingers.

"Will that interfere with the casting?" Jace asked.

Ursula rummaged through a cabinet. "Not to my knowledge. I once heard about a blood magic from an Arathi client — supposedly it could heal a warlock's demon minions. You probably know our healing potions don't work on demons for the most part."

"Sacrificing one's own blood to heal the injuries done to demon minions." She pulled out a medium-sized, coal-gray jar, scooped in some water and washed it as she spoke. "He called this spell Life Channel. Honestly, it's pretty fitting. Those warlocks stored their own blood using this special component from the spider venom."

"Did you ask him if he knew the incantation?" Jace asked.

"He was just an herb merchant — knew blood, knew spider venom, but didn't know spells." Ursula set the bottle and the anti-clotting potion on the counter. "Though I'm not expecting you to do any advertising for Miss Alkhaana's Mystar Alchemy, Jace, my clients have been getting more numerous, and higher quality too. Otherwise, I wouldn't have been able to brew the Ursoc's Strength tonic."

Jace couldn't recall ever mentioning to anyone outside the city the advertisement Ursula had commissioned him for, so he could only manage an awkward smile. "Is there any way you could help me draw the blood?" he asked.

Ursula shook her head. "No, go ask your Miss Morophil to help. I'm sure the shivarrin are far more professional at this than I am."

"A succubus can't harm her own master," Jace said.

"Doesn't work that way." Ursula said. "From what I know, the warlocks who've mastered Life Channel all draw their blood this way."

Jace stared at Ursula, turning her words over in his mind, his tongue rolling around his mouth. He couldn't help but recall that tall dwarf's succubus back in Shadowforge City, and the faintly mocking glance she'd thrown Morophil's way.

"Then I'll go back and ask her," he said.

"Good luck, then. Oh, and the Greater Fire Protection Potions should be ready by tomorrow evening." Ursula reminded him. "Come pick them up here, Jace. If you don't show, I'll come to your place — don't want you passing out from drawing your own blood."

"It's a deal, Ursula."

Jace hurried back to his rented room with the bottle, and before he even reached the door he could hear shouting inside.

"Clearly this rune originates from the Ered'ruin tongue, which makes perfect sense — the Ered'ruin are, after all, the mage lineage most skilled at opening void channels and summoning demons. So it should be pronounced... Dosasosta, Dosazta — blend those two syllables together, do you understand what I mean, she-demon? I genuinely don't understand why you know absolutely nothing about incantations and still lecture me!"

He glanced toward the neighbor's door at the other end of the hallway, then slammed his palm against his own door with a bang. The noise inside immediately stopped.

When he opened the door, Sazeno and Morophil both stared over at him, wide-eyed. "Are you two out of your minds?! What the hell are you fighting about?!" Jace hissed.

"No one's home, Master," Morophil said with a smile. "Your neighbor — that old carpenter's apprentice — moved out."

"Fine. I've barely seen him since I moved in." Jace walked to the table and glanced at the scroll pinned under Sazeno's hand — the summoning incantation they'd gotten from that Dark Iron dwarf warlock. Lately, whenever Sazeno had free time, he'd been puzzling over what was off about it.

"Good work. Diligent." He nodded in satisfaction, set the bottle on the table, and said to Morophil, "Help me draw blood. I need it for casting material for the summoning spell."

"I can't do that," Morophil said matter-of-factly.

"You hold the bottle. I'll do it myself." Jace picked up the letter opener, eyed his wrist, and shot Sazeno a look. "Grab me a healing potion from my pack."

Morophil picked up the bottle and weighed it in her hand. "Master, why are you drawing so much blood?"

The imp hopped onto the table holding a healing potion, grinning. "With a bottle this big full of blood, I could summon this succubus myself! Haha!"

"You think I'd answer a summon from the likes of you?" Morophil said coldly. "I'd rather die and be thrown into Kil'jaeden's prison than accept that kind of humiliation!"

"Bah! Every summoning incantation I've come across in Nastoi was linked to some big shot in the Burning Legion. You think I'd waste my energy summoning the likes of you?!"

"All right, all right — even if the neighbor's gone, keep it down." Jace flicked the imp on the head, sending it yelping and sitting down hard on the table.

"This blood is mainly for preparation," he explained. "What, wait until the moment we actually need it to draw some? I'm not Zubel."

"Don't tell me you're planning to summon our Lord Karlez to deal with that black dragon." Morophil crouched down beside Jace's arm with a smile, watching the blade press into the skin.

Jace paused, thought for a moment, then said, "I've definitely thought about it, but I'd rather not have to use him. If he showed up anywhere near Elwynn Forest or the city walls, I'd have absolutely no way to explain it. Even Sola flying this close would be easy to spot, let alone that guy. But harder to explain is still better than being unable to fight back at all. Better safe than sorry. Even if we don't need him this time, I can use him to summon you two later. Once I get the hang of it, I won't have to keep dragging you through Stormwind — I can head to the destination first, then summon you there."

"What a pity." Morophil watched the blood drip down. "I rather enjoyed Master walking me along the canal. The sky was so pure and flawless, the river water so enticingly scarlet..."

Her long tongue couldn't help but dart out and lick her lips. Jace glared at her. Morophil immediately let out a small gasp. "Ah, it was so blue, so blue..."
