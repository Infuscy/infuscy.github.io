Chapter 993: Setting the Stage

After drawing the blood, Jace headed to the Gilded Rose at noon for a proper meal to recover, then made his way to SI:7's Old Town garrison that afternoon.

The SI:7 agents said Mathias had gone to Stormwind Keep, so Jace waited in the parlor for a while. By the time the sun looked ready to set, Mathias came hurrying back, apologetic from the moment they crossed paths.

"Sorry to keep you waiting, Master Sesso." Mathias tossed his cloak aside and, rubbing his hands together, asked eagerly, "Any results from the Prestor investigation?"

"Some progress, but I'll still need to verify certain clues with Lady Prestor herself. What I have on my own isn't enough to prove anything," Jace said. "Has there been any movement in Stormwind lately? I mean..."

"Dragonkin." Mathias frowned. "At least around the princess, we haven't detected any trace of dragonkin. And she's been behaving herself — aside from public appearances, like banquets or prayers at the Cathedral, she hasn't made any extra moves."

"After attending her first prayer service at the Cathedral of Light, she even announced that the House of Prestor would contribute eight hundred gold in thanks for Stormwind's sacrifices against the orcish invasion. The funds would go toward paying the Stonemasons' outstanding wages — mainly those for the Cathedral and the walls."

Jace sat stunned. "She actually did that?"

Mathias looked up with a smile. "I'm guessing the North isn't short of rich families besides the Barovs, hmm? That said, I wouldn't call her especially charitable. She's clearly angling for enough support down south — after all, nobody in the Alliance can or would dare criticize her donation at this point, and our king has no choice but to accept the money. Otherwise, the Stonemasons might well rise up on the spot. But once the gold's in hand, the ones who should keep quiet will keep quiet."

He shook his head. "Our surveillance work is running into more and more obstacles, and even some of our own agents are getting antsy. Looks like you and I are being isolated in Stormwind, Master Sesso. Times like this, I want to go to my grandmother's place and ask what to do next, but I know she'd just be disappointed."

"She's not in Stormwind?" Jace asked.

Mathias said, "Actually, ever since we dealt with the dragonkin problem, she's rarely involved herself in Stormwind affairs. Now she's living a relaxed retirement in Lakeshire — fishing, gardening, leaving the rest of us with any stamina left to run around. Even when I visit, she doesn't like me talking intelligence business."

"Best not to mention her — makes me sound like a kid who never grew up."

He fiddled with a tea cup he'd produced from somewhere. "It's obvious that in this day and age, a pretty face solves a lot of problems, a warehouse full of gold solves even more, and when you combine the two, the power is terrifying. Right now our opponent has both, and we have nothing."

He glanced at Jace and tilted his head with a smile. "Of course, Master, I'm not saying we're just a couple of..."

"Ahem." Jace cleared his throat and nodded. "I know what you mean. I heard her ambitions don't stop at winning Stormwind's support — her use of that pretty face doesn't seem limited to the people of Stormwind either."

"You've heard the rumors, then. About the king and her." Mathias wagged a finger. "I can tell you responsibly, Master Sesso — our young King Varian's loyalty to the queen is witnessed by the Holy Light and our agents alike. Of course, that doesn't mean I've had the king followed."

"Of course," Jace nodded. "So what's going on between the king and queen?"

"Ah." Mathias slowly narrowed his eyes. "Isn't some friction between a young couple perfectly normal? Though I probably shouldn't say this, but women tend to feel insecure when they's carrying a child — especially when her husband is a king, and a wealthy, beautiful, nearly flawless foreign princess has suddenly dropped into his domain. I think that's only human. Though King Varian has done his best to reduce contact with the princess to set the queen's mind at ease, the queen has every right not to trust him. That doesn't mean there's an unbridgeable rift between them..."

"I didn't come here to gossip about scandal or relationships, Mathias. I'm only concerned with my actual job." Jace drummed his fingers lightly on the armrest. "What if that Princess Prestor used a curse to get the king? Shadow magic can control minds — or it can exert influence even more discreetly... Simply planting a thought in the queen's mind out of nowhere would be dangerous enough."

Mathias thought for a moment. "But so far, the queen and Prestor haven't met privately at all. To cast dark magic openly on Queen Tiffin in the square of the Cathedral of Light, under the gaze of the Holy Light, the sun, the Archbishop, the king, the citizens, and the nobility? Frankly, even I find that a stretch."

"Maybe Prestor hasn't had the chance — but what about other dragonkin?" Jace asked.

"You mean dragonkin in the palace secretly cursing the queen?" Mathias's first instinct was to shake his head, but he stopped himself, resting his elbow on the armrest and falling silent in thought.

"After all, we're dealing with a bunch of giant reptiles wielding dark magic. On this, I don't think I should let my own judgment override yours. So tell me — what do you intend to do, Master Sesso?"

"I want to meet the queen," Jace said.

Mathias gave a bitter laugh. "Do you know how many rumors there are about you and that princess? No fewer than the gossip about the king and her. I've even heard both sides going at it in a Lakeshire tavern — one side saying that princess from Alterac rose to power through her connection with the king, since you're a Lordaeronian too, and the other side saying the king was seduced by her beauty and means to grant Moonbrook to her and her family. Given all that, imagine what would happen if I arranged a meeting between you and the queen. Or are you trying to fight fire with fire — spread a rumor about a court mage and the beautiful queen to launch an all-out assault on every other piece of gossip, and single-handedly destroy Stormwind's entire rumor mill with one tornado?"

"I can see you're stressed, Mathias," Jace said, resting his chin on his hand. "But since you trust the king and queen's relationship so much, why not consider that the queen's worries might have an external cause? And I've heard the queen's distress has been affecting her health. She's carrying the future of the kingdom — doesn't that warrant attention? I only need to see the queen once. A public setting is fine. A single glance is enough for me to judge whether she's under the influence of dark magic. It doesn't have to be some specially arranged meeting."

Mathias couldn't quite hide the anguish in his eyes, but after hearing Jace out, his brows relaxed slightly.

"But the queen has stopped attending almost all public events now, including some of the less important banquets. And there aren't any particularly major palace banquets coming up..." He leaned back and sat thinking for a moment. "I know the king takes Queen Tiffin for a walk in the Park every evening. If a single look is all you need, you could head to the Park district around that time and you'd probably run straight into the queen. Would that count as the simple 'glance' you mentioned?"

"That works." Jace checked his watch. "Roughly when?"

"Three or four hours from now," Mathias said. "Every day without fail. You want to go today?"

Jace said, "Of course. The sooner we get to the bottom of this, the more trouble we avoid. If the queen really is under the influence of dark magic, I need to find a solution as early as possible."

Mathias murmured, "If the queen really is under dark magic's influence... would that be a hundred percent confirmation that Katrana Prestor is an impostor?"

"I'd say so," Jace said. "At that point I wouldn't have any more reservations, and we could find a way to break it as quickly as possible. Oh — have you mentioned to the king..."

"I've dropped him a hint," Mathias said. "If he shares our thinking, he'll understand, Sesso. Judging by how he's acted these past few weeks, he should have caught on."

Jace nodded, then said, "Of course, this ties into the other thing I wanted to discuss today. I'd like to borrow a place from you to have a proper chat with Prestor about my findings — outside Stormwind City."

"Outside the city?" A trace of surprise flickered across Mathias's face. "You're going to..."

"Yes," Jace said. "I'm worried the intensity of her resistance could exceed what Stormwind can handle, Mathias. I'm not gambling with ordinary citizens' lives."

Mathias laughed. "How intense? Blowing up the Park? I honestly wouldn't mind."

Jace narrowed his eyes. "How would you guarantee you blow up only the Park? It's separated from the Trade District and the Mage Quarter by a single canal."

"Just a joke," Mathias said with a smile. "Compared to arranging a private meeting with the queen, this is no trouble at all, Master Sesso. Between Stormwind and Goldshire, near the eastern shore of Mirror Lake, there's a property belonging to SI:7 — a detached red-tiled house."

"Much appreciated," Jace said.

His expression shifted. "That doesn't mean the place is going to end up worse off than the lighthouse, does it?"

Jace raised an eyebrow in silence. Mathias said helplessly, "Do what you want. If it really gets destroyed because of a black dragon mess, I suppose it won't be my responsibility to bear. Besides, that Princess Prestor already paid compensation in advance, didn't she?"

Both of them couldn't help but laugh.
