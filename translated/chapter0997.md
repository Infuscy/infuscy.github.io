## Chapter 997: The Source of Shadow

The rain hadn't let up, drumming coldly against the walls, the eaves, the windows.

If they were fighting a blue dragon, Jace might have suspected the enemy was deliberately making things difficult for them.

But there wasn't much arcane energy in this rain — just like the rest of the forest, which held nothing beyond the life essence of the trees themselves. Not much else at all.

Yes, including the area shrouded by Ysera's mist — it was so clean it made Jace's skin crawl.

This was his first time peering into the dream-shrouded zone from the outside. Now he understood how those dragonkin and whelps in Dustwallow Marsh felt, going round and round in circles.

And yet even so, Aegwynn had still been able to tell something was off. It was hard to imagine just what kind of power that woman possessed.

Hearing the legends was one thing; experiencing the gap firsthand was another.

Over on the other side, a figure just as clean as a blank sheet of paper appeared by the lake. Katrana Prestor had come as agreed — her height and gait made that clear.

But what surprised Jace slightly was that she'd brought someone with her. The maid had come too.

From the perspective of an unmarried noblewoman, this was perfectly natural enough — after all, a man and a woman meeting alone in the wilderness — but it made things a bit more complicated.

If a real fight broke out and the maid really was an innocent, how would they handle her?

Still, Jace found it genuinely hard to believe Onyxia would keep an unnecessary, uncontrollable variable by her side. So she probably had a problem too.

Maybe a dragon cultist, maybe something else.

If she really was an ordinary mortal, the most Jace or the others could do was try to throw her as far away as possible in the chaos.

Katrana Prestor arrived at the door, removed her rain-soaked cloak, and handed it to the maid beside her, smiling as she looked outside. Jace said with a trace of apology, "I didn't expect it to rain this hard today."

"It's all right, Master Sesso." Katrana waved a hand. "I actually felt everything had been going a little too smoothly ever since I came to Stormwind. The people here are so warm, and His Majesty the king and the queen have been so kind to you, and Master Sesso, you found clues so quickly. Compared to everything I went through up North, it's all been like a dream. This rain is a bit of a reminder that things don't always go smoothly — there are occasional setbacks. It's actually set my mind slightly at ease."

Jace nodded. "Please, sit wherever you like."

Katrana sat in a small chair by the table. She glanced around the room with an expression that was hard to call at ease.

"Then I'll come straight to the point," Jace said. "Based on the information I've gathered so far, Lord Daval Prestor is probably no longer in the black dragons' hands."

"No longer in the black dragons' hands?" Katrana looked somewhat surprised. "Master Sesso, can you be certain? Or have you already been inside Blackrock Spire..."

"Of course I couldn't risk searching the entire Blackrock Spire," Jace said, thinking to himself that if I'd followed your plan I'd have ended up in your dear brother's hands — no chance of coming back to chat with you.

"Truth be told, getting this intelligence involved a bit of luck," he said as he took out the Dark Iron dwarf's stone tablet and held it in his hand.

"This is intelligence I obtained from a group of Dark Iron dwarves, mentioning a group of orcish rebels' plans and location. As I understand it, a group of orcs, in order to fight against the black dragon's rule, kidnapped Lord Daval Prestor from the black dragons' prison, using him as leverage to negotiate peace with the Alliance..."

At this, Katrana Prestor's eyes widened in shock, as if she couldn't believe it.

Jace continued, "This is also why I had to tell you about this in such a secret location. If the black dragons learned of the orcish rebels' plans and location, not only would your father's life be threatened, those rebels would quickly be hunted down, and the Alliance would lose a crucial ally in their assault on Blackrock Spire."

"Let me translate the original text on here for you in detail, my lady." Jace placed his ring-wearing finger on the stone tablet. "The language on this is an encrypted script that requires special magic to reveal. You know Dark Iron dwarves are quite different from other dwarves — they're very fond of using arcane magic..."

Katrana stared at the stone tablet. It was hard to say what emotions were churning inside her, since Jace couldn't sense them.

When the ring touched the tablet, before Jace even tried to activate the magic item, the text on the tablet suddenly seemed to shed a layer of some kind of isolating film, and a faint fishy odor wafted up.

Jace couldn't hesitate any longer. He knew he could smell the chromatic dragon blood, and Onyxia would realize very soon too. So he immediately began reciting the incantations Sola had taught him earlier, accurately and quickly.

"Voytraz, Sangjeli, Danuloy, Kaolorain..."

By the time he got here, Jace already sensed from the corner of his eye Katrana Prestor's expression beginning to change. He'd anticipated this — if Krasus could decipher the contents of the incantation, then naturally Onyxia would be able to make it out too.

So he paid no mind to her reaction. The incantation was already half done. The most important thing now was to concentrate and finish it. "Patiwa, Puteli..."

"Wait, Master Sesso." Katrana suddenly said in a low voice. "Something doesn't feel right..."

"Razbonali, Razbonali!" Jace finished the incantation, then fixed his eyes on the woman before him. The dark script on the tablet that had been dim now showed a vivid red. The incantation seemed to have activated.

Katrana Prestor's appearance hadn't changed at all, but after the incantation was complete, she couldn't say a word anymore.

Soon, as if struck by something heavy, she coughed without stopping, even spitting a mouthful of blood onto the table. She clutched her stomach and whimpered softly in pain. The maid beside her turned pale with fright and dropped to her knees beside her mistress.

"Master, Lady Katrana... what's happened to you? What's going on?"

She looked at Jace, tears streaming, and shouted, "What kind of dark magic is this?! You lured her here just to murder her in this vicious way?!"

The maid shouted as she pulled a dagger from her boot and pointed it at Jace, but she didn't seem to have the courage to lunge forward either, tears pouring down her face.

And Jace stood rooted to the spot too.

The largest stone tablet seemed to have developed a crack from all that commotion, but the power of the script on it didn't appear to have been expended much — it still emitted a fairly strong fishy odor. What was going on? Had it worked or not? Was the Katrana Prestor before him actually Onyxia?

Was she actually a black dragon?!

Krasus pushed the door open at once, his red staff already glowing with fire. He clearly knew the incantation had been released.

Behind him, Grede, Vereesa, and Sola also filed in, all fully armed and holding various weapons, ready for a decisive battle. But it seemed...

They'd struck nothing.

Katrana was kneeling on the ground, completely devoid of the vitality she'd had before, sobbing in bursts, even tears of blood streaming from her eyes. Her emotions were now clearly perceptible — clear as an ordinary mortal's.

The maid, terrified nearly out of her mind, had given up any thought of fighting Jace and could only hold her mistress and keep asking what was wrong.

"What happened?" Grede asked. "I'd like to know too," Jace said.

Vereesa frowned, looking down at Katrana. "The smell of black dragon's blood."

With Vereesa saying it, Jace realized it at once — that's right, this was the smell of black dragon's blood. He'd been so tangled up in what was going on with the incantation that he'd overlooked this.

"The incantation worked," Krasus picked up a stone tablet from Jace's hand. "But it has no definite target — because there's no black dragon here... Jace! It merely broke a black dragon's spell!"

"Damn it, I already drank the potion," Grede muttered.

"Father..." Katrana Prestor suddenly murmured in a low voice. "Father, I... why..."

"Stand back!" Vereesa shoved the petrified maid aside in one motion. After the others had charged in, she no longer seemed to be wrestling with whether Jace was trying to harm anyone.

Vereesa grabbed Katrana and shook her. "Who used magic to control you? Was it Onyxia? Where is she?"

Katrana swallowed something, then broke down crying — crying more and more grievously, more and more wildly, her voice turning now shrill, now hoarse, until even Vereesa's hands gripping her collar began to tremble...

Krasus thought for a moment. "I think she knows, Jace. I mean Onyxia. Such a meticulously complex spell being broken — she must know everything, and... she may already know about this incantation."

Jace had felt this too; he just hadn't been able to accept it psychologically yet.

With that, the red dragon looked at Sola. "Let her rest a while, Solamyer. Her reaction clearly shows she's been under the black dragon's control for God knows how long and has only just been freed — there's no way we'll get anything out of her like this. Pushing her further could even cause irreversible damage to her mind."

Sola nodded and cast a spell to put Katrana to sleep. She stiffened and collapsed, and Vereesa carried her to a nearby bench.

A deathly silence filled the room. Jace had practically collapsed into a seat himself, but his mind was racing as fast as it could, searching for every piece of information he'd overlooked.

Black dragon, disguise, control, shadow... the woman high up in the Blackrock Spire arena, the arrogant one who made Nefarian himself wary. Undoubtedly, that was Onyxia's confirmed mortal guise.

But beyond that, the orc hadn't revealed anything more. A woman...

Could it really be Ponasia Shaw — this woman who'd ostensibly retired but was actually pulling all the strings in secret?

But according to Shaw's account, she didn't seem to have influenced anything either — no dark magic controlling minds, no verbal orders directing SI:7, so...

Those unnatural doubts, shadow, darkness, Vereesa...

When had Vereesa started feeling uneasy? Entering Stormwind, attending noble banquets, meeting Katrana — and he himself had started feeling something was off after meeting Katrana too, doubt, suspicion...

No... wait, Katrana was right in front of him.

Hold on. Before becoming emotional and full of suspicion, he and Vereesa had both met someone else — and the other person too, after meeting her, or rather after spending time with her, had grown suspicious, pained... King Varian.

Tiffin Ellerian Wrynn. Was it the queen?

The moment this possibility occurred to him, Jace felt a chill run down his back. He looked up at Vereesa, recalling the shadow that had permeated the palace when he'd met the queen yesterday.

That wasn't shadow magic, wasn't a black dragon's spell or curse. Though it really was intense, it hadn't reached the level an ancient black dragon would project — it was merely shadow wrought by emotions. Set against the queen's suffering, it wasn't incomprehensible...

But there was indeed something very strange about it.

Why did the suffering of one queen feel like the negative emotions released by a huge crowd?

Could it be the child in her womb? The queen, the king, the guard, plus the child in the queen's womb — four people in total. But how much negative emotion could they add up to?

And though the king was downcast, he didn't have that intense anxiety, unease...

Yes, that was clearly anxiety, unease — not like jealousy, not like anger, in short, not like an emotion that wanted to blame Varian for being too close to Katrana Prestor.

Who would feel anxious, uneasy? Where exactly was it coming from?

Wait, could it be...

The child in the womb?

Jace felt his hair stand on end.

Could what he'd sensed be the emotions emanating from the countless dragon eggs within Onyxia's body? At that moment, he recalled the look Queen Tiffin had given him last night.

Indifferent, cold, hollow.

"The palace..." Jace looked up at Krasus. "The king is in danger, Master. It's the queen!" Krasus didn't say another word, instantly transforming into a burst of arcane light and vanishing among them.

Jace slammed the table. "Sola, take us to Stormwind Keep — now!"
