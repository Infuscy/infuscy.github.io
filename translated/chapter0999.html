Chapter 999: Between Worlds

Jace wasn't much better off than Vereesa — he couldn't get anywhere near the rift at all. And Krasus was clearly already at his limit just holding back the black dragon who'd entered the rift; at this point he couldn't even hear a sound from outside...

That was... the Firelands.

Had the black dragon used a flame teleportation spell? Like Greston the Arbiter back in the Order of the Circle.

It should be the same in essence. Jace remembered Sola saying that black dragons loved using the power of fire elementals to cross between planes, showing off their power while intimidating their enemies at the same time.

Jace somewhat regretted not having a proper talk with Rokhtus Darkpact about flame teleportation before leaving Shadowforge City. But after all, they'd only just learned the news then, and who knew they'd need this to fight a black dragon?

Now it seemed his affinity for fire was useless, and he didn't know the flame teleportation incantation! Right — the incantation.

Ironforge's Ruige had said that fire communicators didn't actually communicate with flames through incantations. Those so-called incantations were merely commonly used, passed-down phrases that let elementals understand quickly.

If you wanted the fire's help, you could simply shout out your need directly. Then let's shout out the need.

"Take me in." Jace stared at the rift and shouted in the elemental tongue. "Fire, take me in!"

Clearly, his mastery over fire might not be enough to open a portal from Azeroth to the Firelands — but the portal was already here. He just needed to...

"Take me into this flame gate, fire! I make my request!"

Jace shouted in the elemental tongue. Suddenly, the hot winds and flames swirling in the hall gathered down and enveloped him, hurling him into the gap between the chains at a terrifying speed he couldn't adapt to. As Jace felt as if some scorching beast had clamped its jaws around his head and swallowed him whole, everything around him became completely different.

All the familiar auras and magic had vanished. The imp screamed and tumbled off to who-knew-where. There was nothing but endless flame... flame... and Ursaal in his hand.

In the distance, on a scorched, fire-riddled black ground covered in black stones like monster horns, a terrifying giant dragon bound by fire chains by its tail was continuously breathing fire at a tiny silver figure.

And though that figure hadn't been destroyed... it also didn't seem able to mount an effective counterattack. With Grede pinning her down, the black dragon also found it difficult to turn around and break free of the chains behind her at once.

The black dragon spread its massive wings with purple membranes, swaying its plump head covered in ram-like curved horns, like a twisted behemoth straight out of a nightmare. It had to be said that Onyxia's size wasn't as enormous as Jace had imagined — she might even be a bit smaller than Korialstrasz. But at this moment, the dark power fully unleashed by this bound, enraged evil dragon far surpassed that of the red dragon prince. Even Azuregos of Azshara was overshadowed.

"Lightning, bring back my warhammer! Bring back my warhammer!"

Grede shouted in Dwarvish, but the distant glimmer showed no reaction. This was the fire elementals' domain — how could it accommodate the existence of storm elementals? The black dragon merely swept a wing casually, and the warhammer was knocked even further away.

"You're an outright insult to my father's treasure, you stupid clod..."

"I've never seen a bitch as vicious and despicable as you in my whole life. Shut your mouth!"

Grede had no choice but to roar in fury. He was quickly swallowed by another blast of fire, and his shouting grew muffled and indistinct.

Jace realized over here that his Mage Armor spell had already been stripped away by the intense flames brought on by crossing planes just now. Not only that — his whole body was covered in burns, but at this point those wounds hardly counted for anything.

He cast another Mage Armor, then fumbled through his potion pouch, wanting to drink a Mana Potion to replenish himself in preparation for dealing with the giant dragon before him. But to his surprise, the entire pouch had vanished along with everything else.

What the hell had he even prepared for? Aside from the Fire Protection Potion he'd drunk in advance, what else was of any use?!

Jace cursed under his breath in anger. Whether it was because of this insult or the Mage Armor incantation just now, the black dragon noticed the sudden disturbance over here.

"I knew we'd meet again, Jace Sesso — I just didn't expect it to be this soon."

Onyxia let out a cold laugh, her voice making the entire region unstable. Lava rivers shot up terrifying jets of magma and bubbles.

She didn't cast any fancy spells, didn't breathe fire. Instead, she raised her wings and suddenly flapped, raising claws several times larger than Jace's entire body and sweeping them horizontally...

A wisp of shadow slipped through the black dragon's fingers, but the dragon immediately lifted her claw and smashed down hard on where the shadow landed. Another burst of shadow exploded beneath her palm, pushing her hand open. Onyxia's whole body drew back slightly, then she lunged forward again and swung a claw.

Jace, who had just urgently cast a Shadowstep and a Dark Shield spell, gasped and spat out a mouthful of blood. Seeing the black dragon strike again without a moment's pause, he simply raised Ursaal, already glowing with blood-light.

Seeing this blood-light, Onyxia's eyes clearly hesitated — but this claw carried tremendous force and a momentum that would destroy everything, and it was already too late to pull back. Jace swung the scythe to meet it head-on. A blinding flash of blood-light and metal collision, accompanied by a grinding noise that made one's whole body uncomfortable, Onyxia was yanked backward several steps by the burning chain behind her, a deep blood-colored mark already left in her palm.

Seizing the opportunity while Onyxia was injured, Jace immediately cast a Curse of Weakness on her, adding a layer of Curse of Agony on top.

As the incantation was recited, Jace could practically hear Ursaal's scream with his naked ears. It wanted to devour the soul of the giant creature before it more than anything — but clearly it hadn't given much consideration to its master's strength or survival.

The opponent wasn't some dragonkin, wasn't some Black Knight... she was Deathwing's daughter.

Still, Ursaal's shadow empowerment made each shadow spell take obvious effect. Onyxia clearly gritted her teeth and let out a pained, low growl. Even a black dragon — a black-winged dragon with powerful fire and shadow resistance — felt the might of this shadow magic.

"Damn you, Korialstrasz!"

Onyxia turned furiously and seized that chain, pulling wildly. Jace realized he absolutely could not let the red dragon's magic be destroyed — otherwise he and Grede would be in serious trouble.

The imp was nowhere to be seen. He couldn't use Curse of Tongues to cast a Cripple, so he could only try using Asphyxiate to hinder Onyxia's movements. It was just that... strand after strand of shadow chain wrapped around the black dragon's wrist, only to be shredded by a single exertion of her strength. Magic that could easily pin demons firmly to the ground was achieving almost nothing now.

Jace could only raise Ursaal and chant Shadow Bolts, continuously bombarding Onyxia's swaying giant wings and back. Cracks and sprays of blood bloomed between scales and wing membranes. Onyxia grew more and more enraged, more and more frenzied, yet showed no sign of fatigue. "Die, beast!"

Grede, at some point, had retrieved his warhammer that had fallen into the lava. Gripping the warhammer, he scrambled and climbed his way up the black dragon's back, then up to her head, raising the hammer and smashing it down again and again on the black dragon's skull and horns, pounding Onyxia into a frenzy of cursing in the dragon tongue that Jace couldn't understand.

At the same time, Jace seized this opportunity to continuously fire Shadow Bolts at the areas far from Grede. Seeing that the power of Krasus's chain was already far weaker than when he'd first entered, he had to weaken Onyxia as much as possible, so that when she regained her freedom, she wouldn't...

Instantly destroy both him and Grede completely.

Recalling the force of Onyxia's claw that he'd just blocked with Dark Shield, Jace had already lost much hope of defeating this she-dragon deep down. Grede was also being restricted of too much power in this strange elemental plane. If he could call upon the power of the storm now, this continuous hammering would already have inflicted extremely serious damage on the black dragon — even if she was Onyxia.

But just then, Jace felt that while the opponent's power had barely been depleted at all, the chain binding Onyxia's tail suddenly dimmed, shattering like a cloud of dust. Onyxia laughed wildly and whipped her long tail around, shaking her head to fling Grede away. She stared at Jace and said, "Game on!"

This crazed she-dragon charged like a locomotive. A Shadow Bolt was still forming in Jace's mouth when Onyxia was already in his face — at a glance, it looked like a small black-purple mountain toppling toward him!

He instinctively activated Shadowstep, but whether it was because the emergency mana expenditure had been too great or because he hadn't dodged completely, in an instant he felt as if something had smashed him squarely on the head. He blacked out, collapsing to the ground barely able to tell direction.

Onyxia laughed wildly and flew high into the sky, a ball of flame held in her mouth, spewing it straight at Grede over there. This flame, swollen up in an instant from within her belly, exploded on impact with the ground, raising a huge cloud of gray smoke and expanding a dandelion-like blast that sent even Jace, over here, tumbling head over heels.

"Grede..."

Jace's mana was nearly bottoming out by now, but Onyxia was hovering in midair like she was admiring her handiwork — which also gave him another chance to cast Life Tap.

"Luhrawenkta..."

But he'd only gotten halfway through the incantation when he noticed Onyxia's expression change slightly. Hesitation, vexation, impatience, disbelief...

Grede was still alive, still trying to hurl the Skyshatter upward.

Onyxia kicked the warhammer away with a clawed foot and looked toward Jace. "A shame, gentlemen, but I don't want to waste any more time. I'd thought you might refuse, but you're more stubborn and more blind to right and wrong than even my worst-case scenario. Leaving you alive would be a menace."

The black dragon flew higher, pausing for a moment beneath the crimson sky. She raised her head and drew a deep breath.

The dust and embers drifting in the air all poured into her nostrils and mouth. The entire plane seemed to grow much quieter, leaving only tinnitus and the rumbling of the distant earth...

Jace glanced at Grede in the distance, coughing and trying to stand, still gripping his warhammer and staring at the sky. He knew what Onyxia was about to do.

A deep breath, a devastating breath capable of destroying everything. Before, Alexstrasza's magic and the walls of Grim Batol had together absorbed Deathwing's fury for them. This time, he could only face Onyxia's wrath on his own.

Jace sprinted toward Grede, who was crawling with difficulty toward his warhammer, while at the same time gauging the mana he'd accumulated from Life Tap, preparing to gamble everything on a Dark Shield.

"Get behind me, Grede."

"Maybe I shouldn't have charged in here without asking anything first. Sorry." Grede lay on his back clutching his warhammer. After taking that blast, he had almost no strength left either.

"Save the apologies." Jace raised his head and stared at the black dragon's silhouette. "Just don't go saying you save my life every other time from now on."

"Sola told you?"

"Yeah."

"Damn traitor, hack..."

Tomsogas, Veholisha, Wastadaung.

It felt like ever since he'd learned Dark Shield, Jace had never recited this spell so carefully. A film of darkness rose before his eyes, blurring even the silhouette of the black dragon in the sky.

The next second, blood-red swallowed everything. Black smoke devoured everything in sight — the spiked black stones, the sky...

Frankly, when that powerful shockwave struck the Dark Shield, it was weaker than Jace had expected. He didn't know why — perhaps because Ursaal's power had truly brought a qualitative change to the Dark Shield's strength, or perhaps Onyxia's power wasn't as formidable as he'd imagined.

After all, she was only Deathwing's daughter.

The hellish flames wrapped in black smoke dispersed, and Jace was still standing there.

The Dark Shield had already been destroyed. The blood-light on Ursaal flickered on and off like breathing. "Hey, what's that."

Grede asked from behind him.

Before them lay a dark brown colossal form. A hideous, flame-burning giant wing was spread open, facing the silhouette of the black dragon above.

"So it was for this." Karlez flapped his fleshy wing, blowing away the ashes left by the flames. He turned his head and fixed those dull little green eyes on Jace, muttering in the demon tongue, "Dragging me to this weak plane fragment... I haven't even fully savored the void power of Karazhan yet. I haven't had my fill. Too hasty, my master. Did you order this little wretch to do this?"

"Deal with her, quick!" Sazeno stood on the demon lord's horn and shouted. "Hurry, Lord Karlez! This is your moment to show your strength!"
