Chapter 1000: A Soul on Loan

"So that's the..." Grede muttered.

Jace coughed. He was a bit unclear about the situation himself right now. "Well, help us out here, Karlez."

"Ah, no need to be so polite — it's not as though I have any way to refuse."

The Doomguard's hoof took a step forward. The Onyxia in the sky, who still didn't quite understand what was happening, was suddenly enveloped in a mass of green fel light. Watching the frequency of her wingbeats grow slower and slower, she descended uncontrollably.

"How did you bring this ugly alien bastard here?!" Onyxia demanded. Karlez stared back at her. "Alien bastard?"

The demon raised both arms, the fel fire in his hands burning more and more fiercely, even igniting above his head, while the Cripple on Onyxia high above grew stronger and stronger.

"Get down here, big lizard, and show me how much you weigh for having the nerve to say something that stupid to a noble Ered'ruin!"

The imp yelped and fell from a height, slamming into the ground in front of Jace — probably blasted off by the surging force of the demon's spell.

Jace immediately grabbed him. "Where's my pouch? Where are my potions?"

"Not much left, Master."

The imp seemed considerably larger than before. He could barely lift the mouth of half a Mana Potion bottle with one hand. "Look — I saved you some."

"Where did the other Mana Potions go?"

"I used them."

"The Refined Elixir of Intellect?"

"Same. Summoning Lord Karlez... takes a ton and a ton of energy! That bottle of yours I used too, look — I... couldn't help it! Worst case I just go back to the Twisting Nether. But if the summoning failed, then Master would have been done for. I had to do everything possible to make sure..."

"All right, I know, I know."

Jace nodded. He really couldn't blame the little wretch — he hadn't expected this bastard to actually manage to bring Karlez over.

Though Karlez was a considerably massive demon, before Onyxia he didn't appear all that impressively large.

The black dragon held a mouthful of flame and, bracing against the Crushle, descended from the high sky and crashed into the demon, knocking him to the ground. She breathed a great gout of fire straight at his head — but a demon lord's fire resistance was no joke either. While enduring the black dragon's flames, he struggled to his feet on wings and hooves, grabbing the dragon and trying to wrestle her down.

But clearly, Onyxia's strength wasn't something a Doomguard could overpower either. The two stalemated right there, neither Shadow Bolts nor gouts of flame dealing much damage.

The two colossal forms grappled on the black ground wreathed in flames, but Jace could clearly feel that Karlez couldn't hold on much longer.

He wasn't a being of this plane to begin with, and he was far from having recovered the full force he'd projected when he first established contact with the warlocks at the Altar of Storms. Relying only on the brute strength from having just been summoned to fight Onyxia, he'd likely fall into a disadvantaged position very quickly, let alone win.

This was the last chance. Jace glanced at Grede beside him, then asked the imp, "My Soul Shards?!"

"Gone, all used up. Ow, I had to make sure..."

"Damn..." Jace scratched his head in distress. He drank what little Mana Potion remained, then raised Ursaal — and suddenly thought of the last card he had left.

"I need a bit of soul, Ursaal. I need... the power of a soul. I know you have plenty."

"If I die here, you won't get to enjoy a soul feast again for God knows how long, isn't that right? So give up a little, in exchange for a bigger payoff."

Ursaal said nothing, just flowing with blood-light.

In the distance came Onyxia's mad laughter. She gripped Karlez's horns and wrenched them with all her might, another mouthful of flame welling in her mouth — just as Jace had expected, Karlez was losing!

No time to wait for Ursaal to give some response. Perhaps this staff never had any real consciousness to begin with — just a brainless glutton.

Jace raised the scythe and chanted, "Saviyal, Avalus..."

He fixed his eyes on Onyxia's swaying form, trying his best to ignore Karlez...

"Antiel, Minuelle!"

He swung the scythe. The blood-red sky suddenly flashed with dark purple.

Strands of what looked like ignited shadow — or rather, serpentine bolts of Shadowflame — rolled and coiled together.

Ursaal's power flickered with the same frequency as the serpents, pulsing with blood-red energy. Jace knew it had surrendered a portion of its own soul, substituting for Soul Shards to complete the spell. Whether it had understood his meaning or whether it was simply supposed to work this way, either way...

"Wilmar!"

Jace swung Ursaal. The gathered purple-red magical serpents let out a shrieking hiss and hurtled toward Onyxia and Karlez, who were locked together. But by now Onyxia had gained the upper hand. When the blood-red-eyed black dragon suddenly noticed the black serpent streaking toward her, she froze for an instant, then used all her strength to grab the demon and drag him in front of her...

Damn it.

Jace poured in nearly all his energy and instinctively tried to alter the trajectory of that serpentine bolt the way he'd manipulate a Shadow Bolt. To his surprise, it actually bent slightly, grazing the demon's massive wing and releasing a string of sharp, shriking shadows, slamming straight into Onyxia's body again.

Karlez, realizing Jace's magic was bearing down on them, also used all his strength to yank Onyxia in the opposite direction. The black dragon tried desperately to dodge but ultimately couldn't avoid it, struck hard on a giant wing and an arm by the serpent...

A shadow explosion mixed with the black dragon's fel blood erupted, forming a terrible vision like a purple rose. Karlez was blown to the ground, while the dragon howled and tumbled away.

One of Onyxia's wings was blown to tatters, nearly severed. An arm had already exposed bare bone. "Arrogant lesser being, Jace Sesso..."

She shrieked hysterically, "Arrogant, despicable bastard!"

Jace was nearly spent. He sat down heavily on the ground. Karlez, in front of him, propped himself up on his arms. "The plane fragment is collapsing, Jace Sesso. This is the molten plane of fire and earth that this black dragon propped open to pass through, and her power is no longer enough to sustain its continued existence..."

"Molten plane?"

Jace repeated weakly. So this wasn't the true Firelands.

Still, this wasn't entirely beyond his understanding either. After all, beneath the Wyrmrest Temple in the game, there was also a portal leading to the Obsidian Sanctum ruled by the black dragons, and the scenery there was extremely similar to here — perhaps that place was connected to this world.

Wait — a plane of flame and earth?

In the distance, Onyxia growled low, like a powerless whimper. The ground had already cracked. The sky was covered in cracks like shattered black glass.

This whole world was becoming unstable. Smoke and dust came rushing in from unseen places, gradually filling the field of vision. Jace struggled to keep his balance and made his way to Grede's side, who was also kneeling on the ground, lowering his head and coughing.

"Hey, Grede." He grabbed Grede's nearly shattered breastplate. "Your necklace!"

"My necklace?" Grede looked back, holding up the golden Earth-Force gem. "What about it?"

"I don't know how you activate it. Quick, get it to help us — this whole world is going done." Jace said. "Help how?" Grede asked. "I..."

Jace grabbed his beard in agitation. "Didn't you read that elemental tongue book I gave you?! Damn it, just say 'earth, help us, don't let us die along with this world!'"

"All right, all right, hold on, damn it, let me try." Grede nodded frantically, then gripped the necklace and began chanting in the elemental tongue.

Jace secured Ursaal on his back, stuffed the imp into his coat, and prepared to face whatever unknown disaster came.

Looking into the distance, Onyxia was already nowhere to be seen. Karlez braced himself against the ground. The fight with Onyxia just now had clearly consumed most of his strength.

"If I survive, I'll try summoning you again, Karlez," Jace said.

Karlez seemed not to hear. Soon, earth and stone flew, the ground shattered, and noise drowned out Jace's voice. From the corner of his eye, golden energy radiated from Grede's fingertips...

When consciousness returned, Jace felt as if he'd been teleported from the Obsidian plane to the plane of air elementals. Fierce winds sent him flying in every direction. The pitch-black sky was impossible to see clearly.

"What the hell is this now?! Grede! Grede?"

He shouted several times, then suddenly saw a mountain in the distance... no, a mountain below, wait — Stormwind was down there! "Hey... hey! This can't be right."

Jace flailed wildly in midair. Ursaal, strapped to his back, battered his head ceaselessly in the hurricane like a big bone. The imp in his coat screamed at the top of its lungs, "Let's go! I can't die from a fall!!!"

"You little..." Jace was so furious he wanted to tear this wretch's mouth off. Just then, a familiar dark shadow suddenly swept past beside him.

He craned his head upward and saw a silver gem atop that shadow's head gleaming in the moonlight. "Sola! Sola! Grede — where's Grede?!"

"Jace is below, Sola, the hell, didn't you see him?!" Grede's roar came from above. Hearing the dwarf's voice, Jace felt his eyelids grow heavy, and even the howling of the wind became indistinct.
