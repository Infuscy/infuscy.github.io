Chapter 1001: The Flame Fades

"Onyxia..."

Jace sat up with a start, reaching out his hand. A streak of blood-light tore through the air. Ursaal and the Song of Azure Sky, in the corner, flew over together and smashed hard into his face and chest, making him hastily cover his head.

The pastries and plates on the table, the Peacebloom aromatherapy bottle — everything was torn back and forth by the power of the Blood Grip, clattering and scattering across the floor.

Jace grabbed the scabbard of the Song of Azure Sky and swallowed, glancing at the Ursaal staff that had rolled off the bed. The wrapping cloth that had been torn open by the blood-colored scythe blade had been re-wrapped. But this time it wasn't the silk cloth from Jace's memory — it was a high-end elven fabric with a green tree-grain pattern that looked quite expensive.

Was this Vereesa's cloak?

Jace sighed, then a tearing pain came from his chest.

This was the aftereffect of blocking Onyxia's claw with Dark Shield.

In hindsight, he shouldn't have been that confident — but faced with the arrogance and conceit of that she-black dragon back then, he'd wanted to take her down a notch or two.

Of course, the more important reason was that he'd lost all his Mana Potions. He really didn't have the confidence to use Shadowstep repeatedly in a short time. Daylight was already streaming through the window, brilliant sunshine filtering in, fine dust drifting in his line of sight.

The air carried a faint, not-too-obvious scorching smell.

Jace could sense Morophil's Soul Link. Though she wasn't nearby, her emotions were stable — she should be fine.

What surprised him more was that he could still sense Karlez's presence occupying his mind. In other words, this bastard hadn't died in that plane fragment at all.

As for Sazeno, he swept with Detect Demon the nearby demon-cloth bag tied tightly at the mouth. He could faintly make out a demon's shadow inside — it should still be in there.

But knowing its usual behavior, it should have come out and run its mouth by now. Why was it hiding inside and refusing to come out? "Sazeno, Sazeno!"

"Mmm..."

A mumble came from inside the bag.

Jace tried to get out of bed. The moment his head swayed, a sharp pain like an ice block pressing on his head seeped in, followed by a wave of dizziness. It took a while before he steadied himself.

When Onyxia had charged over, he remembered dodging in advance and not taking a direct hit from her body — so this was probably just the force of the flames she'd brought along slamming into him.

He walked slowly to the corner, opened the bag, and the imp poked its head out, glancing around. "So, Master, have we left the Cathedral square yet?"

Jace took a deep breath, eased the dizziness, stood up, and walked to the table, leaning on it to look out the window. Right next to him was the Cathedral of Light, its white walls dazzling in the sunlight. Before him was the Cathedral square. "Not yet."

"Then why'd you call me out?!" The imp shrank back in at once.

Jace yanked it out. "Stop hiding. Tell me what happened."

"How should I know?!" The imp said innocently. "All I know is after you stuffed me in the bag, everything went haywire, Master. Don't know how many planes we crossed, don't know... ohh, then spinning around and around, that fat guy yelling and screaming..."

"The black dragon?!" Jace asked.

"Mmm." The imp spread his hands. "I didn't see anything..."

"Karlez?" Jace asked.

"I... I don't know either." Sazeno spread his hands. "If even the Master doesn't know, how would I know? But I reckon, he..."

"...He's an Ered'ruin. A high-ranking demon who guards the Legion's gates for Sargeras himself! I'm sure he has his own profound understanding of planes... Master."

The imp was right. Compared to him and Grede, two fellows who could only rely on luck to shuttle between planes, that Doomguard was indeed far more reliable — otherwise he wouldn't have been able to tell at a glance exactly what kind of passage Onyxia had torn open.

Jace picked up the pastry on the ground, blew off the dust, and ate it. The sweet flavor eased quite a bit of the fatigue from last night to now.

He sat on the stool by the window and looked out again. Looking north from here, he could only see the tip of Stormwind Keep belching thick smoke.

Not just there — looking from here toward the harbor, though the buildings around the Cathedral blocked the specific view, thick smoke filled the high skies. The whole city looked as if it had just been bombed.

Just as he wanted to see further, an armored gryphon suddenly flew across the sky, carrying a fully armed Bronzebeard dwarf on its back.

What was going on — had Onyxia still found a chance to attack the entire city? Watching the gryphon disappear behind the Cathedral, Jace felt his heart tighten.

Recalling the final moments in that Obsidian realm, Onyxia had vanished before everything collapsed.

Knock knock. The wooden door rapped gently twice.

"Jace? Are you awake?" Sola's voice came from outside.

Hearing her voice, Jace's tensed nerves relaxed considerably. "Yeah, Sola. I just woke up."

Sola pushed the door open, picked up the aromatherapy bottle from the corner, and set it on the table. "You haven't been asleep long, Jace. Feeling any better?"

"At least I'm still alive," Jace said. "That black dragon?"

Sola said, "She escaped. But the whole city... is covered in her scales and blood."

"How did that happen?" Jace asked.

Sola recalled for a moment, then said, "After Onyxia broke free of Prince Korialstrasz's magic, the plane rift lost its support. But not long after, a rain of blood and fire fell from the sky. The prince judged that she must have attempted to teleport and failed, suffering grave wounds in the plane's interstitial space for this to happen. So the prince had me go up to the source of the fire rain in the sky to find you, hoping you could escape too."

"Thank you, Sola." Jace sighed. "And thank Master Krasus — without you two, my ending would probably have been falling to my death for no reason... It's just a shame we still let her get away."

"Even for her, recovering from that kind of wound will take a very long time." Sola sat on the edge of the bed. "At least she didn't have time to bring more terrible disasters to Stormwind. King Varian is very grateful to you and Grede."

"The king recovered?" Jace asked. "Does that mean Onyxia might already be dead?"

"Hard to say, Jace." Sola nodded. "Not long after you entered the plane, many of the soldiers began slowly recovering, and King Varian was the same. I reckon that to break free of the prince's magic and fight you, Onyxia had to abandon her energy on the other side of the rift."

"Well, I was a little worried I was being held here under house arrest as a traitor." Jace asked again, "What about the others? Grede..."

"..."

Sola said, "Grede is over at Stormwind Keep helping dig survivors out of the rubble. Vereesa is doing the same."

"I should've gotten myself a bottle of Ursoc's Strength tonic too." Jace rubbed his temple as he said it.

"I'm curious, Jace." Sola asked. "How did you figure out the queen was Onyxia in disguise? The prince wants to know too. Though he never intended to doubt your judgment, he couldn't quite work it out..."

Jace cleared his throat, somewhat sheepishly. "It's complicated. I'll explain slowly some other time. The queen?"

Sola shook her head.

"Dead?" Jace asked.

"Not that I've heard anything."

"What did the king say?"

"He hasn't mentioned it. Maybe I don't know... I only saw him organizing soldiers and mages to put out the fires throughout the city, coordinating with the Archbishop to tend to the wounded. He hasn't slept all night."

"Where is he now? I'd like to check on him."

"In the Cathedral, with the wounded soldiers, Jace. But you'd best rest a little longer for now," Sola reminded him.

"I can walk and run — I'm fine, Sola." Jace stood up, leaning on Ursaal. "Let the clergymen keep this room for other wounded."

"All right, then I'll go let Vereesa and the others know," Sola said. "I'll leave it to you, Sola," Jace said.
