Chapter 1003: Each to Their Duties

It wasn't long before Mathias Shaw hurried over. "Your Majesty, I'm here."

When he saw Jace, he drew a sharp breath. "Master Sesso. It seems I've witnessed at least something of it." Jace was sitting to one side — his head was still swimming a little, so all he could do was grip the armrest and nod.

"I just finished discussing it with Jace, Mathias," Varian said. "Judging by that black dragon's behavior over this period, she must have prepared a double for herself here, so that when she returned to her lair, the double could carry on the act in her place… and that double is very likely Tiffin. I mean, the real her."

"Could it be another disguise?" Mathias said. "For instance, a dragonkin transformed into the Queen's likeness…"

"That's your problem," Varian said, fixing him with a stare. "I remember you gave me your word that there were no hidden dragonkin left anywhere in Stormwind. The daughter of Deathwing is an immensely powerful sorceress, and her disguise would be meticulously crafted — I won't hold it against you if you can't find its flaws. But a dragonkin…"

"Forgive me, Your Majesty. It really can't be a dragonkin," Mathias said, while stealing a guilty glance at Jace.

"Even if it's not a dragonkin, you could still try using the felhounds," Jace said. "Get the warlocks involved in searching for any traces the black dragon left behind in the city, or in the palace. See if they can find any clues related to the Queen."

"But right now the whole of Stormwind is drenched in that black dragon's blood and flame," Mathias said helplessly. "Even the felhounds we keep in the SI:7 basement are agitated. They're used to helping us track dragonkin scents. I imagine right now, for them, it must feel like the entire city is a target."

Varian said, "But if Tiffin really is being held somewhere, now that Onyxia has fled, there's no one left to look after whether she lives or dies. If she goes several days without food or water…"

The three of them in the room fell silent for a moment. Mathias suddenly said, "If Onyxia intended to use the real Queen as her double, she wouldn't personally go to tend to her. She very likely has accomplices in the palace."

"What about Katrana Prestor? Could she provide any information?" Jace asked. "She's still unconscious," Mathias said.

Varian pinched the bridge of his nose. "Turn Stormwind Keep upside down and search it. I don't care how you do it — demons, or cooperate with the mages of the Wizard Sanctum… you cannot overlook any possible corner, including the residences of the Privy Council members. I'll sign a royal warrant for you, Mathias. Go all out."

"Yes, Your Majesty."

Mathias bowed to the King and to Jace, then quickly left the room. "I can help too, Your Majesty," Jace said.

"Your current state is worse than mine after a sleepless night, Jace." Varian's emotions had calmed down somewhat by now. "You and Lord Grede Thunderfist charged into that rift… my guards had no way of getting anywhere near that place. And inside that rift, the two of you faced that giant dragon alone, fought a battle I can't even imagine — and in the end, you even defeated her. The Holy Light's blessing alone couldn't have accomplished that!"

"Although I want the Queen alive more than anyone, right now you need to recover as quickly as possible. Let Mathias send people to search — after all, when it comes to finding people, no matter how capable you are, you're still just one pair of eyes and two hands. What's more important is, we don't know if that monster will suddenly go mad and double back for revenge, or whether the other black dragons will seize this moment of weakness to launch another attack. When that time comes, this city will still need to rely on every force that can be mobilized to protect it. Don't waste your energy on this."

"I understand." Jace nodded, leaning on his staff as he stood. "Then I'll take my leave, Your Majesty."

"Oh, one more thing, Jace," Varian said. "I just discussed it with the bishop. A portion of the funds that Princess Katrana donated to the Cathedral of Light will be used to help you cover the repair costs of Ilgalar Tower. Don't worry that this black dragon's rampage will render all your previous efforts wasted. Ilgalar Tower itself is also an important part of the kingdom's defense system. If it weren't for the kingdom's current situation not being optimistic, the crown really should have borne a portion of it to begin with."

"Thank you so much, Your Majesty. This really helps me out of a huge bind," Jace said. After walking out of the room, he'd half been convinced Varian could read minds.

While sitting in the room waiting for Mathias, there'd been a moment when he'd been worrying about how he was going to come up with the money for that portion of Ilgalar Tower's repairs.

After all, Katrana's commission had been "find Lord Darval" — and now there wasn't a single hair of Lord Darval to be found. And Katrana had reached that agreement with him while under Onyxia's control. He'd originally assumed he'd really have to fill the remaining hole through his own hard labor.

He hadn't expected Varian to seemingly know exactly what he was thinking, and with a single stroke of the pen, settle it for him.

With this, all those potions the imp had swiped and drunk last night, that whole bag of soul shards that had vanished into thin air… they didn't sting quite so much anymore.

After bidding farewell to Krasus and coming out of the cathedral, the first thing Jace wanted to do was head over to his parents' house and check on it. Last night the fire rain had fallen from the sky, and so many people had been injured… it was really hard to rest easy about how things were there.

With Onyxia badly wounded and fled, it meant Stormwind shouldn't see any danger in the short term. The next step would be figuring out how to bring his parents over from Lordaeron.

But looking at the thick smoke still drifting up into the sky from the city — even though they'd just survived a disaster, almost no one's face showed any relief or joy. Just as Varian had said, everyone was worried that the black dragons might make a comeback in short order, or that her kin would seize this opportunity to launch another attack.

At a time like this, having his parents move over might come across as a bit heartless in other people's eyes. Before he'd even taken two steps onto the church square, Jace heard a shout from not far off.

"Jace!" It was Vereesa!

Jace looked up and waved in the elf's direction. She immediately excitedly weaved through a group of soldiers and charged straight over, throwing herself into Jace's arms… but the impact nearly knocked Jace unconscious again. He had to steady himself against the edge of a fountain to keep from falling over on the spot.

"Ah, it hurts a little…"

He reminded her in a low voice, sucking in a breath through his teeth from the pain all over his body. Only then did Vereesa seem to notice Jace's condition and quickly support him.

"Sorry, Jace. I thought you were…"

"It's fine." Jace shook his head — he supposed he bore some of the responsibility too.

After coming out of the cathedral, he'd tried leaning on his cane a little less. Especially when he saw Vereesa, he'd even puffed out his chest a bit, striking a heroic pose as if she'd come through the battle with Onyxia completely unscathed. One bump and he'd been exposed for what he was.

Then again, looking at it, Vereesa didn't seem to be in much better shape either.

Her cloak was already gone, her ranger's tabard was covered in scorch marks and burns, and even her silver hair was stained with no small amount of ash. Quite a few strands had even been singed into curls, and her neck was covered in burn marks.

He wondered just how many times she'd tried to force her way into that rift…

To be honest, before he'd realized he might be able to get in with the fire elemental, Jace had simply stared at that planar rift, feeling the power spewing out of it, and hadn't had the slightest thought of forcing his way in again.

He was someone who carried Alexstrasza's blessing, and Vereesa was merely a High Elf — she might possess some innate magic resistance, and her physical constitution might be many times stronger than an ordinary person's, but in the face of that elemental planar rift, she was still a mortal body of flesh and blood.

"Jace, I'm sorry… I couldn't help you and Grede. I'm useless." Vereesa said with her head lowered.

"Don't say that, Wen." Jace placed his hand on her ash-dusted cheek. "At least we all survived, and we defeated the enemy, didn't we? There's no need to keep score over who contributed how much. Of course, unless you came to Stormwind purely to compete with Grede rather than to help — then there's really nothing to be done."

Vereesa couldn't help but let out a laugh, wiping the tears from the corners of her eyes. "I admit it, that dwarf won."

Jace smiled too, then asked, "By the way, did you see my parents' house when you came?"

"That place is fine," Vereesa said. "Some dragon scales might have fallen on it — I saw a bit of damage to the back eave. But there was no fire, and no signs of burning. The rain lasted until the middle of the night last night, so those falling flames didn't cause much damage. The palace was probably the worst hit, but aside from the throne hall, the other areas aren't too bad either."

Jace finally let out a sigh of relief and sat down heavily on the outer edge of the fountain.

He'd been worried that his decision to tear off the mask with Onyxia would bring a disaster upon Stormwind even worse than the one in the other timeline. When he'd woken up that morning and looked out at the sky, that feeling had tightened around his throat even more.

Now, at least, the situation was much better than he'd imagined.

The Stonemasons hadn't fallen out with the nobility, so the entire Kingdom of Stormwind probably wouldn't face the Defias Brotherhood's rebellion either… Moonbrook and all of Westfall had suffered the demons' corruption, but at least it wouldn't become a barren wasteland like in the other timeline, and by now things had basically returned to normal.

Jace just had a feeling that Varian would never let this go easily. "Where's Grede?" Jace asked.

Vereesa said, "He's asleep in the palace. The effects of that potion you gave him must have completely worn off. Sola is looking after him. No need to worry."

"Once he wakes up and order in the city is restored… let's all go to the Pig and Whistle together, Wen," Jace suggested. "All right." Vereesa said with a smile.
