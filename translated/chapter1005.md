## Chapter 1005: Where Souls Come From

"The black dragon princess Onyxia has returned to Dustwallow Marsh. If you don't know where Dustwallow Marsh is, I can tell you: from here to Booty Bay is a two-week boat ride, then from Steamwheedle Port a two-month voyage across the Great Sea… and by the time you reach Dustwallow Marsh, half a year will have passed."

"And in that black dragon lair lie countless powerful dragonkin, whelps, dragonnest guards, and possibly even other races they've tamed — ogres, kobolds, for instance. I don't know the exact situation, but you're asking me to face an entire legion."

"I know I failed to fulfill our agreement, but you can't put all the blame on me either. First I need to make sure I survive before I have the ability to repay. If I die, how am I supposed to honor my promise? You can't get anything from a corpse."

"And my soul is certainly nowhere near as powerful as Onyxia's. Killing me would be a pure loss for you. After all, as long as I'm alive, I can continuously offer you souls."

Jace sat in his chair, a demonic-language dictionary in his hands, staring at Ursolus placed on the desk before him. A string of ancient demonic tongue loaded with honorifics and proper grammar had his head in a tangle and his tongue nearly tied — but Ursolus gave no response whatsoever.

Ever since leaving the inn and all the way back to his rented room, after uttering that single word "debt," it hadn't made another sound, and naturally it gave Jace no response either.

Right now, it was basically a big bone club — and not even the kind you could make a rich broth from.

Mophile sat to one side with her folded arms, watching Jace with an amused look. Jace glanced at her, then lowered his head to flip through the dictionary again.

After flipping through it for a while, Jace looked up again. "It's impossible for me to chase her to a dragon's lair alone. That would be pure suicide, not to mention that you're refusing to help me fight right now. And I can't possibly drag my friends and companions into a dragon's den just to use her soul to fill your belly."

Mophile smacked her lips. "Ursolus probably wouldn't consider any of this at all, master."

Jace thought about it for a moment, then said, "Then let's find a substitute — find souls that can satisfy it. They might not be as powerful as Onyxia's, but if the quality isn't enough, we'll make up for it with quantity. How about that?"

Ursolus remained silent, but Jace knew it could understand. This staff knew everything.

"Sounds like the master is planning a massacre," Mophile said with a tilt of her head and a smile. "That's completely different from the Jace Sesso I know."

"Who knows," Sazeno said, hopping onto the table. "He could be planning to go killing anywhere. Maybe the master wants to make another trip to the Dark Portal and kill a few more demons entrenched there. After all, for the master, killing demons is just killing demons — he never hesitates!"

"I didn't say the master had to kill people in Stormwind," Mophile said. "Maybe he's planning another trip to Shadowforge City. There's an arena there, after all… Speaking of which, my dear sweet master, if you'd be willing to visit that ring of slaughter again, I'd be more than happy to accompany you."

Jace shook his head. "I can't reveal Ursolus's power in Shadowforge City. If you want to go in and have fun, I'll find you an opportunity next time I visit Shadowforge City, but I can't go in and expose myself. Once those Dark Iron dwarves see what I have in my hands, they'll never let me leave."

"Then Blackrock Spire!" the imp said, stepping onto the windowsill and pointing north. "The master can go to Blackrock Mountain in the King's name, sweep away the orcish threat, and single-handedly assassinate the legendary Prince Nefarian of the Black Dragonflight! Personally harvesting his noble yet filthy soul…"

Jace said with a cold snort, "You really do think highly of me."

"Then where are there souls powerful enough to replenish Ursolus's strength?" Mophile asked.

"Karazhan!" the imp suddenly shouted. "The legendary tower of Medivh, home to countless wandering souls from different planes and timelines." Jace murmured, "Don't quote the library's original texts back at me. You want me to go to Karazhan to collect souls, or to donate mine for free? And there are members of the Violet Eye active around Karazhan — bringing Ursolus there would be far too risky."

"It looks like every viable path has been blocked by your courage, master," the imp said with a grin. "You're not really planning to start butchering the humans in Stormwind, are you!"

"Hunting demons at the Dark Portal is indeed a viable idea," Jace muttered as he thought. "But the demons there have more or less been hunted clean by the Nethergarde Keep army, mercenaries, warlocks, and Kirin Tor mages. Searching for roaming demons would be a waste of time."

At that moment, Jace thought of a place not particularly far from Nethergarde Keep.

Sola had always hoped to go to the Pool of Tears deep in the Blackmire and find out what was going on with the green dragons there.

He'd promised her ever since Kalimdor that he'd go take a look, but after returning he'd run into the black dragon's mess. Even now, two or three months had passed and he still hadn't made good on it.

That corrupted green dragon hiding deep in the Blackmire — judging by her size, she was also an adult dragon. Ever since Jace had barely escaped with his life from that mercenary party, he hadn't heard any news during this time of the Nethergarde Keep army or the mercenaries who'd been there successfully slaying a dragon.

So, she might still be entrenched in that lair guarded by dragonkin.

It's just that looking at her appearance, she seemed like a moving corpse. He didn't know how much soul she still had left, or whether it would be enough to satisfy Ursolus's appetite.

But that was, after all, the soul of a green dragon. Even a corrupted one was still Sola's kin.

Killing her was one thing — freeing her from corruption, and Sola or the other green dragons would probably support that. But devouring even her soul down to the dregs was a completely different matter.

If this corrupted dragon wouldn't work either, then… what about deeper in? The Temple of Atal'Hakkar?

The Temple of Atal'Hakkar, submerged by the Pool of Tears, was where the "Atal'ai," worshippers of the ancient god Hakkar, had hidden after being exiled by the Gurubashi troll empire.

A thousand years ago, the Gurubashi trolls, unable to halt their decline, had called upon this blood god for protection, but the worship of Hakkar soon turned into a blood corruption of the entire empire. After a long struggle, the resisters had only managed to exile the Atal'ai — the most extreme and fanatical of the cults.

The Atal'ai hadn't abandoned their worship of Hakkar because of the exile. These trolls had still attempted to summon the god in this temple, eventually catching the attention of Queen Ysera, who sank this temple to the bottom of the lake.

However, the temple's interior was deep and complex, and the lake water hadn't completely submerged the trolls inside — on the contrary, it had given them a relatively secluded and hidden environment. The trolls hadn't given up their summoning because of this setback; instead, they placed even more hope on summoning Hakkar to save them from their plight.

Sensing the problem, Eranikus, a prince of the green dragonflight, led the green dragonflight deep into the temple and stopped the summoning ritual that had nearly succeeded, but was himself corrupted by Hakkar's dark powers and became lost within the temple.

Previously, when mercenaries had wanted to go deep into the Blackmire, or even approach the vicinity of the Pool of Tears, to search for traces of demons, Jace had been apprehensive and reluctant — precisely because he feared encountering the dark powers left behind by Hakkar near the Pool of Tears, and the green dragon lost here. He hadn't expected that in the end, he still couldn't avoid it.

Thinking of this, Jace realized that wasn't this temple itself a massive repository of souls?

Setting aside the souls of the corrupted Atal'ai trolls, Hakkar's other title was "the Soulflayer." From ancient times until now, summoning Hakkar to this world required the sacrifice of countless souls.

If the Atal'ai really intended to summon Hakkar again, they must have stored countless dark souls in the temple by every means possible… to use in the summoning ritual, or to await Hakkar's true form's arrival so he could enjoy them.

Jace looked at the demon skull with its hollow eye sockets on Ursolus before him, thinking to himself that if this still wasn't enough to fill this big bone club's appetite, he really didn't know where else to look.

But, just like Shadowforge City, that place was by no means as simple as a dungeon full of monsters and quest objectives.

The Temple of Atal'Hakkar was also a massive, intricate "labyrinth" in the game — it could even be called an "underwater town." In reality, it would only be easier to get lost in.

If he really planned to go, he'd definitely need to do his research and preparation in advance.
