Chapter 1006: Making the Most of Everything

Early the next morning, Jace got out of bed and came to the canal bank. He gazed at the prison tower in the distance and stretched with all his might. There weren't many people on the street yet, but there were already guards — regular patrols had probably resumed.

Every squad of guards was accompanied by a Church of the Holy Light cleric. Right now, several guards on the far side of the canal were conversing with their clerics about something.

Judging by their expressions, although they were still a little tense, the fear that had been on almost everyone's face the morning after that night — the fear that the black dragons might strike again at any moment — had vanished.

Although the air still carried a faint, barely perceptible smell of shadow-scorched stench, it was far less thick and pungent than it had been a few days ago. Everything was recovering. At this rate, it probably wouldn't be long before Stormwind could return to its original order.

Jace glanced in the direction of Stormwind Keep. From here, only the top of the keep was visible. The billowing thick smoke was gone, and on the surface it looked no different from before.

He wondered if King Varian and Mathias had found any leads on the Queen.

Setting aside the last day when they'd been forced into a fight, the Queen had used almost no shadow magic or curses in the palace. Without the involvement of these dark magics, Jace was naturally no match for SI:7 when it came to finding people, so for now he could only wait for news.

Today he was going to the Mage Quarter, and he had quite a few things to do. First, he'd visit Master Marin and see if he'd been injured in the chaos of the past few days. And of course, ask about those dragon scales.

Onyxia's dragon scales — these were no ordinary dragon scales.

According to Sola, after Onyxia fled, the whole city had been covered in her blood and scales. But these past two days, whether in Old Town, the church, or the Trade District, including the roof of his parents' house, Jace hadn't seen any trace of the so-called dragon scales. They'd probably already been cleaned up by the citizens. After all, the fact that black dragon scales were especially valuable had been known to the people of Lakeshire for years now.

Second, he'd visit the Slaughtered Lamb and give Ursula some feedback on the effects of that titanic elixir called Ursoc's Strength.

Third, he'd go see Sola and discuss with her going to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar to find Eranikus — see if she was mentally prepared.

Thinking it over as he crossed the canal bridge, Jace flicked a silver coin engraved with the bald-headed King Thorbein's likeness into the air and caught it, thinking to himself: he'd just gotten the King to help him fill the hole for Ilgalar Tower, and now he'd immediately taken on another debt.

Although what he owed wasn't actual gold coins, these souls… were indeed something he'd have to personally risk his life to find. But if he didn't fill the soul debt, this big bone club would just go on strike and play dead. What else could he do?

If only this thing could be as obedient as the game's legendary weapon, the Reaper of the Accursed, doing whatever it was told — how great would that be.

Still, thinking about how the Hand of Gul'dan summoned by Ursolus had sent Onyxia, who'd been laughing wildly and rampaging just seconds earlier, rolling all over the ground with a single strike — it wasn't quite so hard to accept anymore.

He went to the Gilded Rose for breakfast first, and specifically asked for an extra boiled egg to nourish his body — after all, the King had ordered him to rest and recover properly.

After eating, Jace entered the Mage Quarter through the gate on the Trade District side. He looked up at the towering buildings and trees on either side — they didn't seem to have suffered any damage. After all, the Mage Quarter was the district of Stormwind farthest from the palace, so it was normal that it hadn't been affected.

After entering the Park, Jace immediately spotted the burly High Elf mage sitting on a chair, wearing a plain brownish-red outer robe.

Obviously, the mage apprentices coming and going here had no idea of his true identity. "Master Krasus?" Jace asked in a low voice.

"Hmm?" Krasus turned his head, saw Jace, and said, "What brings you here?"

"I've come to visit Master Marin, Master," Jace said with a smile. "I thought you'd be over with Grede."

The red dragon mage smiled. When the wrinkles on his face squeezed the scars left by Deathwing's curse until they were barely visible, he still looked kind and approachable despite his High Elf appearance.

"I really do like that dwarf, I truly do. But his snoring seems to never stop, and he wakes up too late. Today I still need to go to the cathedral to help treat the wounded, so I thought I'd come to this garden first to quiet my mind a little."

Jace couldn't help but laugh out loud. He hadn't expected that even a red dragon couldn't handle Grede's snoring.

"But Jace, it's a good thing I ran into you. There's something I need to tell you." The archmage's expression grew serious. "After you fell unconscious, Somaeril handed those incantation stone tablets over to me for safekeeping. The magic in those stone tablets was meant to deal a heavy blow to an adult giant dragon, Jace. Breaking a black dragon's control magic… was far from enough to exhaust its power."

"You mean it can still be used?" Jace asked.

"Yes," Krasus said. "Perhaps in the future, when facing Nefarian, or Deathwing's other offspring — for instance, Sabelian, who's been missing for a long time now — this thing could still come in handy."

Sabelian had once been Deathwing's most loyal child. Unlike Nefarian and Onyxia, who coordinated Deathwing's plans in other places, he served as an aide by his father's side at all times.

Krasus didn't know this, but Jace did: at present, he should be stranded in Draenor, protecting the large number of dragon eggs that Deathwing and the Horde had smuggled over, and hadn't returned to Azeroth with his father.

With the Dark Portal destroyed by the expeditionary force, he probably wouldn't have a chance to return either.

Moreover, Sabelian was the most clear-headed of the three siblings. Being left behind in Draenor for a long time had made him increasingly doubtful of his loyalty to his father. So the most threatening black dragon right now was Nefarian.

Jace thought for a moment, then said, "When I brought this stone tablet out of Shadowforge City, I promised those Dark Iron dwarves I'd help them figure out how to use it, and then return it. I'm afraid I can't go back on my word, Master. This stone tablet is very important to the Dark Iron dwarves. If I break my promise, it could have a negative impact on the Alliance's future assault on Blackrock Spire… So, is it possible to first work out the method for crafting this incantation stone tablet?"

Krasus nodded. "The material of this stone tablet isn't rare. The key lies in the method for refining the dragon's blood used to inscribe the incantations. Moreover, the material can't even properly bear the incantations written on it, which is why it shattered into so many pieces. I believe using a sturdier medium would work even better. In that case, I'll start researching it as soon as possible."

"I appreciate it, Master," Jace said.

"Don't mention it. After all, this was originally my mission," Krasus said.

After leaving the small garden, when he arrived at the study door, a group of little apprentices who looked only ten or so were discussing the black dragon from the night before.

"You know, that black dragon… was badly wounded. When she left, one of her wings was almost completely shattered, and one of her arms was nearly nothing but bare bone!"

"With a wing that shattered, how could she even fly?"

"Did you sleep through all your dragon-speech classes? The most powerful dragons all rely on magic to assist their flight. Only the weak whelps and drakes rely solely on their wings to fly."

"Your dragon-speech class even teaches this? Who's your dragon-speech teacher?"

"Miss Cannon."

"Ah, well… Miss Cannon. You're lucky."

"Speaking of which, who was it that beat that black dragon to such a state? What kind of magic…"

"Damn it, that night I slept more soundly than a pig. When I woke up, the whole city was wrong."

"Nobody saw it with their own eyes — even if you'd been awake it wouldn't have helped. Besides, that night some of the guards in the streets went crazy and attacked other soldiers. It was terrifying."

Passing by this group of apprentices and arriving in front of Marin's study, Jace knocked on the door. "Come in."

After pushing open the door, Marin squinted over, then immediately widened his eyes. "Was that you?"

"It wasn't just me, Master," Jace said, closing the door. "Master Krasus restrained that black dragon at the critical moment. If it weren't for him, we might not have even gotten the chance to fight her head-on."

"What, Master Krasus of the Kirin Tor is here?!" Marin's eyes grew even wider.

Only then did Jace realize that Marin had no idea about any of this. He had no choice but to say, "He probably returned to the Kirin Tor long ago, Master. You know very well that very few people can find out where he goes."

"What a pity." Marin shook his head. "Ever since helping deal with the out-of-control city guards that night, I've been running around these past few days trying to track down news of black dragon scales and flesh. I've been far too busy to care about anything else."

"Black dragon scales and flesh?" Jace asked. "Is the Wizard Sanctum buying these from the citizens?"

"Yes, partly for the magic materials, and partly because the pollution caused by those things scattered across the city is hard to estimate." Marin looked up at the skylight. "That black dragon… if she weren't Deathwing's daughter, I doubt she could have pulled through. The weight and quantity of black dragon scales and flesh tissue the Wizard Sanctum has acquired so far are terrifying. It's hard to imagine a dragon beaten to such a state could still survive."

"So this was your doing, wasn't it, with that meteor magic…" he said, fixing Jace with a stare. "Don't forget I witnessed you cast that dark magic with my own eyes at Nethergarde Keep, Mister Sesso. And the flesh and scales scattered throughout Stormwind… almost every single one still carries the residue of that familiar aura."

"That's right," Jace had to admit. "But I had no choice, Master. If I hadn't done it, I'd have lost my own life too. And if I'd let her escape like that, who knows if she'd come back soon with some even more vicious plan."

"What are you explaining for… no one's blaming you now," Marin said with a smile. "I'm just very curious about that incantation."

Jace nervously pressed his lips together. The old mage immediately waved his hand. "I understand, I understand. As a mage, I of course know what secrets like this mean to you all. Rest assured, I won't try to research any shadow magic, nor will I help Andormas and the others pry into anything. After all, I'm already this old — my brain can barely turn anymore."

"Still, that was truly remarkable," he added with a shrug.

"I'm very curious about that flesh, Master," Jace said. "Is there any… tendon material in there?"

"Tendon? It seems… let me look. Don't get your hopes up too much." Marin raised an eyebrow as he spoke, flipping through a ledger to one side, while asking in a joking tone: "Asking so specifically — are you planning to use black dragon tendons to make a bow stave?"

You really did guess it in one try, Master, Jace thought to himself.
