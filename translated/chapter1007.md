## Chapter 1007: A Gift for the Ranger

In the game, there was an extremely powerful Ancient Longbow known as Rhok'delar, Longbow of the Ancient, whose bowstring was made from adult black dragon tendon that was nearly impossible to destroy.

He'd been adventuring with Vereesa for so long that she'd broken quite a few bows. Although she rarely complained about the weapons Grede-style, making do with whatever was available as much as possible, Jace still wanted to properly prepare a gift for her.

After all, ever since she'd truly become a Ranger-Lord, he hadn't properly given her anything.

Of course, in the past, Jace hadn't been able to produce anything that a Quel'Thalas noble would consider precious. But now a ready-made treasure was right before his eyes… and he also knew how to turn it into a legendary artifact that every hunter in Azeroth dreamed of — so he couldn't hesitate any longer.

As for the other half of the bow, the Ancient's staff used as the bow stave, it came from the maker of this bow, the ancient treant Watrous.

In another timeline, it was a treant spirit lingering in the corrupted Felwood. When a player found its shed leaf fossil in the collection of the Executor in Molten Core and traveled to Felwood, they could earn his trust and, with his help, craft that Ancient Longbow.

But at this time, the Burning Legion hadn't invaded Mount Hyjal yet, so this ancient treant Watrous should still be alive. Naturally, no one would be carrying his shed leaves all over the world.

He wondered if, after Queen Alexstrasza finished dealing with the corruption of the Emerald Dream, she'd be willing to help him get in touch with the green dragon queen, Ysera.

With Queen Ysera's help, would that ancient treant be unwilling to help him craft that Ancient Longbow?

Jace watched Master Marin beside him flipping through the ledger's records while his mind kept running over these thoughts, and he couldn't help but smile.

"Ah, found it." Marin looked at the spot he was pointing to with his finger. "From the description, it seems to be the wrist area… a section of what looks like tendon, Mister Sesso. Come with me."

"Yes, Master." Jace wiped the strange smile off his face and followed Marin out of the study.

Given Onyxia's size, even a very small section of tendon would be more than enough to make a bow for a High Elf.

Marin led Jace to another storeroom in the basement of the Wizard Sanctum's mage tower. After opening the door, there were several mages wearing apprentice rings inside organizing things. After Marin exchanged a few words with them, one of the apprentice mages brought out something wrapped in an enchanted cloth. After unwrapping it, inside was a piece of slightly blackened, whitish meat that had been simply treated with cleaning and purification potions.

Although this apprentice Marin called it a "small" piece, it was quite a bit longer than Jace's arm, looking like a slightly curved staff.

"How much for this?" Jace asked.

"Strictly speaking, this is your trophy, Mister Sesso," Marin said.

The mage carrying the bundle glanced at Jace with some surprise upon hearing the surname.

"Then just take it," Marin continued. "I imagine the Wizard Sanctum is probably only keeping this as a souvenir."

"I won't need it for now, Master," Jace said. "So could I entrust the Wizard Sanctum to hold onto it for me for the time being?"

"If you really want it, just take it away, Mister Sesso," Marin said in a low voice. "The Wizard Sanctum has lost treasures more than once or twice. Even things related to Medivh have been stolen. Reluctant as I am to say it, when it comes to items from the Black Dragon Princess's body, I really can't guarantee anything to you."

"All right then." Jace took the bundle. "Thanks for the reminder, Master."

Marin raised his eyebrow slightly. After having the apprentice mages return to their work stations, he said in a low voice, "I heard that SI:7 seems to have made some progress."

"The Queen?" Jace asked.

"Yes," Marin said. "Come this way."

The two came out of the treasury. Marin glanced down the empty corridor at the end, then said: "Because it seems to involve a fairly high-ranking member of the Wizard Sanctum, Master Andormas and Master Maginor Dumas are both involved in this matter. Of course, for now only a few of us know about it. You must absolutely not mention this to anyone who isn't involved — and I mean anyone unrelated who didn't participate in the palace battle, Mister Sesso. I'm telling you this because I want you to be more on your guard."

"Who is it related to?" Jace asked.

"Do you remember the mage who collaborated with me on the Dragonmaw orc investigation project?" Marin said. "He was also deeply involved in the Grim Batol investigation in the Wetlands that followed, cooperating with the Kirin Tor and achieving considerable results. It seems his research into black dragons has been a little too in-depth."

"Master Morgas?" Jace asked.

Marin let out a breath. "For now, Morgas is still in the Wizard Sanctum. He probably doesn't know yet that he's already under SI:7's surveillance. Because they're worried he still holds information related to the Queen, and once that card is on the table, it could jeopardize the Queen's personal safety, so all sides are being very cautious."

"I understand, Master," Jace said.

Just as expected — Morgas, the lord of Ilgalar Tower in the game, had eventually found a way to defect to the black dragons after all.

Jace had originally thought that since Stonewatch hadn't fallen to the orcs like in the other timeline, and Onyxia hadn't had enough time to lay the groundwork step by step in Stormwind, there shouldn't be that many people defecting to the black dragons. But in truth, he had underestimated the allure of the black dragons' power to mortals — especially people like Morgas, or Kel'Thuzad, who researched shadow magic. And, of course, Jace himself.

If the Dragonqueen hadn't bestowed her blessing on him, allowing him to live longer and granting him more powerful fire affinity, then even if he'd defeated Deathwing and survived Grim Batol, Jace could hardly guarantee he wouldn't have regretted refusing Deathwing's offer at the time.

Jace knew he wasn't a particularly noble person, and he also knew that agreeing to Deathwing would mean utter, irreversible damnation. But once matters of life and death, strength and weakness were involved, a lot of people's heads became muddled — himself included.

After leaving the Wizard Sanctum tower, Jace headed straight for Sola's residence.

When he arrived at the door, a small, creamy-white flower had inexplicably bloomed on the wooden door. He knocked. "Jace Sesso."

After quite a while, the door slowly opened. Sola said with a smile, "Jace."

Jace observed her for a moment, then snapped his fingers right in front of her face with a click. Sola started with a jolt, shook her head, and only then made out what was in front of her. "Ah, Jace, sorry…"

"I need to discuss something important, Sola," Jace said with a smile. "I needed to make sure you were truly awake."

Sola let out a soft sigh. "I hypnotized too many people the other day, and for days on end I've been helping Master Krasus treat the wounded, expending too much mana. So once I relaxed, I just wanted to sleep a little longer."

"Ah, I see. I should have come later." Jace said. "It's fine, come in," Sola said.

This room had already been decorated by Sola to look like the green dragon treehouse Jace had always imagined in the Emerald Dream. The walls were covered in tree vines whose roots were nowhere to be seen but whose vitality was remarkably vigorous, hung with all sorts of dazzlingly beautiful insect specimens Sola had collected from who-knows-where, as well as samples of plants and herbs.

Not only that, but the entire room was permeated with a faint, completely non-irritating natural fragrance.

Even so, this natural vitality was remarkably restrained — it filled the room but hardly revealed itself outwardly. It had been a long time, and there was only that one small cluster of white flowers on the door.

As Jace looked around in amazement, Sola suddenly fixed her gaze on the strange object in Jace's hand. "What's this?" she asked.

Jace snapped back to his senses, looked up at it, and said, "Hmm, one of Onyxia's tendons. Should be a tendon."

"What… what." Sola's expression immediately turned slightly panicked.

Jace looked at her and said a little sheepishly, "My cloak is also made of black dragon skin… I thought you all didn't care about this kind of thing."

"Skin and scales are one thing, but a piece of meat clinging to the bone…" Sola said with a distressed sigh, pinching her wrist. "Fine, fine, it doesn't matter. After all, it's an evil black dragon… evil…"

"I think if it were skin it'd probably be even scarier," Jace said, sucking in a breath and placing the bundle behind his back. "Anyway, I find human skin very frightening, but you're dragons — I won't impose my own views on you."

"Let's just not talk about this topic," Sola said with an awkward smile.

"Right," Jace said. "The main subject. I came this time to talk to you about the Temple of Atal'Hakkar."
