## Chapter 1008: Another Possible Method

"The Temple of Atal'Hakkar." Sola slowly nodded. "I've also mentally prepared myself to go there, Jace. It's just because of the black dragon business… and you took such a heavy beating from Onyxia that I really felt too embarrassed to bring it up."

"With your help and the Church of the Holy Light's, my injuries have mostly healed." Jace moved his lips and pinched a flame from his fingers, then clenched his fist and snuffed it out. "It's just that my mana hasn't fully recovered yet, and we're not setting out immediately either. If you're willing to go, I still need to discuss it with Grede and Vereesa. Fortunately, Vereesa is in Stormwind right now, which makes things much more convenient."

"I'm really embarrassed to have you all help me," Sola said.

"Don't say that," Jace said. "That pool is located not far from Nethergarde Keep and the Kingdom of Stormwind's borders. If there are green dragons entrenched there who've fallen to dark magic, the Alliance won't be able to rest easy no matter what, so this isn't just helping you."

Hearing this, Sola seemed to relax a little. Of course, Jace ultimately couldn't bring himself to say the words "besides, I also want to let Ursolus have its fill at the bottom of that temple."

"Oh, by the way, if you plan to go back to Shadowforge City to get that staff, you can tell me anytime, Jace," Sola said with a smile. "Now the King and the entire palace have witnessed with their own eyes that you have a green dragon friend, and Onyxia is no longer in Stormwind — we don't need to be so careful anymore. At least I can fly you there from Stormwind to the Burning Steppes."

"That's right, of course!"

Jace had been thinking that going to Shadowforge City and back would take several weeks of hard travel, which was why he'd never committed to going. It wasn't until Sola reminded him that he realized.

"Master Krasus is researching that stone tablet, trying to find a way to replicate one. Once he has results, we'll set out. I've already told him that I need to return this stone tablet to Shadowforge City as soon as possible, so it shouldn't be too long."

Sola acknowledged this as she tucked a strand of golden hair behind her ear, her gaze still stealing an involuntary glance toward Jace's hand. "By the way, Jace, what are you going to use this for?"

As soon as she asked, Jace immediately thought: wasn't this a green dragon right in front of him? She might know something about that ancient treant.

"A gift for Vereesa," Jace said.

The green dragon's eyes widened slightly. Jace explained, "I'm thinking of using it to make a bow. I don't suppose you know any Kaldorei hunters who could work with this kind of material to craft a longbow?"

"So that is it," Sola said with a helpless smile. "Even if there were Kaldorei with such skill, they probably wouldn't tell a dragon like me… But now that you mention it, I do recall there are legends about this, Jace. Many stories mention that the Kaldorei have always hated black dragons — they once crafted extremely powerful longbows using black dragon tendons and the tough branches of war ancients. But beyond what's recorded in these poems and ballads, I don't know much else."

Sola thought for a moment, then continued: "Queen Ysera would definitely know, but right now even I can't get in touch with her. Oh — if we go to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, we might encounter the Dreamlord Eranikus, Queen Ysera's consort. Ysondre told me that he was the one responsible for stopping the summoning of the evil god in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar. Regarding this matter, he should know far more than I do."

It seemed that neither Sola nor Ysondre knew what had actually happened to Eranikus in that temple. They still thought he was fighting those troll cultists.

Of course, Jace shouldn't have known about this either, so he just nodded. "Jace, when are you planning to… attempt to go to Draenor?" Sola asked. "This needs to stay secret," Jace said.

"I know," Sola said. "I won't betray your trust."

Jace said, "Wait until things in Stormwind settle down and they don't need my help here. Wait until the summoning materials needed to summon the doomlord have been gathered again, and then wait until the repair work on Ilgalar Tower is finished — that'll be summer, that's the date VanCleef gave me. In other words, half a year from now. Why do you ask?"

"Can I come?" Sola asked.

"You want to come?" Jace said. "If you know about the Dark Portal, you should know that Draenor is probably an apocalyptic landscape, Sola. The orcs tore that world apart with magic."

Although he didn't agree right away, Jace knew very well in his heart that having a willing dragon to carry them along to Draenor would be an enormous help in that shattered world full of collapsing land, fissures, and floating islands.

"I've heard Archmage Marin mention it," Sola said.

Jace smiled. "Then you two have certainly talked enough."

"Shouldn't I discuss the topic of Draenor with him?" Sola asked. "That's fine, but don't mention our plan," Jace said. "Of course, of course," Sola said immediately.

Jace said, "I still hope you'll think it over carefully, Sola. If you ultimately decide to come, we'll be very happy too."

Sola nodded. "I will, Jace."

After coming out, it was already past noon.

Jace had lunch at the Blue Heron. Quite a few mages who weren't in class were discussing the black dragon's magic and speculating on the Queen's whereabouts. Although Onyxia hadn't caused any damage to the Mage Quarter, she had brought an atmosphere of considerable fear to the place.

Quite a few mages mentioned that terrifying spiritual enslavement, and the fire rain that was clearly the result of a shattered plane, all with grave expressions.

After all, even today, in most humans' eyes, black dragons were just flying monsters that could breathe fire. Even for the young mages whose knowledge base wasn't broad enough yet, it wasn't much different.

But when they witnessed her abilities with their own eyes, even if this magic hadn't acted directly on them, it was shocking enough. Of course, Jace just boredly watched them, listening to them ramble on with the same awe and panic he'd already grown tired of hearing these past few days.

After all, he'd seen the black dragon father who could blow half of Grim Batol away with a single spit, and in the blink of an eye turned the lush Batol mountain range into a lava hell… Compared to that, the current situation really didn't amount to much.

After eating, in the afternoon Jace went to the Slaughtered Lamb. He exchanged greetings with Tharle and went downstairs. He hadn't expected that the place would also be permeated with the aura of a black dragon — and far more thickly than in the Mage Quarter, or even the Trade District outside. He'd almost suspected that Onyxia hadn't fled to Kalimdor at all, but was hiding here.

He had just entered the main hall when a shout suddenly came from the direction of the practice rooms: "Who's there?!"

Of course, this wasn't Onyxia's or the Queen's voice — it was a howl from Erland Dulmore.

"It's me, Erland," Jace called back, while glancing toward the alchemy lab. Hearing the voice, Ursula also came running out, still holding several vials of unidentified potions in her hands.

When she saw Jace, her first expression was one of relief, but she didn't say anything right away — just glanced over in Erland's direction.

"Jace Sesso!" Erland roared. "Didn't you say that as long as we didn't go to Mirror Lake, we'd be fine!"

"Uh." Jace looked at Ursula and mouthed, "What's wrong?"

Ursula shrugged. Erland shouted again: "I didn't go to Mirror Lake… and I still got burned this badly!"

All right, so he'd been injured. Jace smacked his lips with some embarrassment. But judging by how vigorously Erland was shouting, he probably hadn't suffered any serious injuries.

"Who told you to go snatching scales," Ursula shouted. "Why blame Jace?"

Erland roared, "Don't defend him, Ursula Dearing! He said as long as we didn't go to Mirror Lake we'd be fine — that's why I felt safe going out to watch the excitement. Who knew that dragon would just fly right over our heads!"

Ursula immediately shouted back: "Your burn salves and healing potions were all made by me, Erland. Think about that!" The moment these words came out, the other side immediately fell silent — not a single retort came back.

Jace glanced over. "Sorry, it ended up deviating a little from what I expected… some unexpected situations came up."

"Was this black dragon business related to you, Jace?" Ursula asked. "It's only been a few days, and we still haven't figured out exactly what happened."

But right after she finished speaking, she seemed to connect something. "Flame Protection potions… wait, those potions you asked me for that day — they weren't for dealing with this black dragon, were you? You already knew she was going to attack Stormwind?!"

"It's not that simple, Ursula," Jace said. "But those potions were indeed used against her. The result wasn't exactly perfect, but we won in the end."

"Damn it," Ursula said. "I really didn't expect a dragon to be that powerful. I originally thought those dragons would never dare show up in a place like Stormwind. Well, did you end up using that new potion?"

"I came here specifically to report the effects of that elixir to you," Jace said.

"Come this way, Jace." Ursula beckoned, and the two returned to the alchemy lab together. She immediately pulled out her own notebook.

"A friend of mine, a Wildhammer dwarf warrior, drank that Ursoc's Strength elixir and was even able to go head-to-head with that black dragon," Jace said, leaning against the wall, recalling Grede's state when he'd fought Azuregos in Azshara. "Before that, he could barely handle a dragon's strength. I have to say, the improvement was truly enormous. And the duration of the effects was also much longer than what you said before. Based on his condition, it lasted until the next morning… I estimate at least ten hours."

"Go head-to-head? What exactly happened? I don't recall anyone saying that black dragon landed," Ursula said, looking Jace over with surprise. "But it doesn't look like you were injured…"

"Uh… like I just said, it's hard to explain all at once. Overall, it was a close call," Jace said.

"All right, if anything feels off, make sure to tell me as soon as possible." Ursula looked at Jace's neck and ears before relaxing slightly, then said: "Based on what you said, I'm guessing the effects might be related to the strength of the person who drinks it. If it were an ordinary person, even if they drank it, there wouldn't be much improvement. Of course, I'm not implying Erland, who helped me test the medicine before… Although he was injured, he really did snag quite a lot of things — dragon scales and shed dragon skin. Jalene has been completely unlike herself these last couple of days, contacting people outside nonstop, purchasing materials to craft new robes."

"New robes?" Jace asked. "I wonder when they'll be ready…"

"The Dagger Society's financial situation is much better than before, Jace," Ursula said with a smile. "I imagine the crafting of the new robes will also go much more smoothly than before. Once Sulenna returns from Booty Bay with enough elemental essences, I think they can begin."

"I'm already looking forward to it," Jace said with a smile. "This Void Robe — of course I'm not saying it isn't powerful. Even when it took that black dragon's flame, it wasn't seriously damaged… but it's always nice to have a new one."

"So, what about the money for the Arcane Elixir?" the alchemist said, smiling and reaching out her hand.

Jace's smile was swept away by a few coughs. Ursula pointed her thumb behind her and said: "I have medicine for lung ailments here too, Jace. It has Living Root in it, and it's not that expensive — a half-month's course only costs seven silver. I can add it to your tab too."

"No need, no need." Jace swallowed and waved his hand. "There's a whole group of dwarves in Ironforge who still owe me more than ten gold. I'll go collect in a few days and definitely pay you back."

Ursula folded her arms with a skeptical look, but in the end she helplessly nodded.
