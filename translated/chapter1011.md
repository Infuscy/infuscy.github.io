## Chapter 1011: Only One Dark Iron Anvil

By now it was already evening. Although Ruige looked thoroughly unhappy, he still brought out ale, hard bread slices, and smoked sausage to host Jace. Although Jace had already eaten a little at an inn, after being tossed around on the tram for a full day and night, he'd barely been able to keep anything down during that time. Now he was starving, so he didn't put on airs by refusing.

Of course, Ruige's hospitality wasn't without reason. The moment Jace sat down and started eating, he dragged an iron chair over and sat down across from him. "Every so often, the watchtower would send news that Rokktus might already be dead, Jace Sesso. A lot of people think Dagran Thaurissan would find a way to have him killed. But I figured even if that young Thaurissan is as full of dirty tricks as his ancestors, he wouldn't go so far as to move against Master Rokktus, who served alongside his ancestors. How is he now?"

Jace answered while eating: "His living conditions aren't great. He's staying in the Ironforge Tavern right now. And not in a guest room — in a storeroom."

"The Ironforge Tavern — you mean 'Coldshot'?" Ruige thought it over. "Well, staying there is still better than being thrown into the Ring of Order. Compared to Master Roktus, my familiarity with fire elements is… frankly, nonexistent. You saw him — didn't you bother to properly learn a few things from him? Hmm? Tell me about it…"

"No, the main reason I went looking for him certainly wasn't to study fire magic, although I'm already regretting not asking a few more questions," Jace said with a slight sigh, dipping a bread slice in the paper wrapping of the greasy sausage and taking a bite. "But he did mention something to me about a warhammer."

"What warhammer?" Ruige asked.

"The Sulfuron Hammer," Jace said. "Do you know about this thing?"

Ruige licked his lips. "He even told you about that? This warhammer hasn't even been forged yet, and just the matter of what it's used for has already gotten a lot of people killed, Sesso."

"Because of the Dark Iron Anvil?" Jace asked.

"What else." The Dark Iron dwarf said. "If the Dark Iron Anvil were guarded by Dagran Thaurissan, or some other Dark Iron dwarf… that would be much easier to deal with. But it just so happens to be guarded by Incendius. He's the Firelord's most trusted lieutenant, and the watcher of Shadowforge City. A weapon capable of standing against Ragnaros, yet it must be forged right under Ragnaros's nose — it sounds like some cunning devil deliberately spread this news just to toy with us."

Jace asked, "Is the Dark Iron Anvil only found in Shadowforge City?"

"Dark Iron Anvil — just listen to the name," Ruige said with a cold snort. "If there were one in Ironforge too, then it should be called a Bronzebeard Anvil!"

"Don't joke around," Jace said.

"I'm not joking," Ruige said, his face turning cold. "You think Sorcerer King Thaurisman and his pack of magic-wielding lackeys summoned the Firelord from the Redridge Mountains… for nothing? From the moment Blackrock Mountain rose from the ground, the terrifying abyss beneath it — what is now Shadowforge City — had already become a chaotic place where the world of fire elements and our world intersect and merge. Master Rokktus didn't tell you that only a place like this could give birth to dark iron? Of course, I mean the mineral, not the dwarves."

As the Dark Iron dwarf said this, the magma deep within Ironforge seemed to understand what he was saying and gurgled, and the entire room seemed to grow a little more stifling.

"The Thorium Brotherhood hasn't failed in their attempts to replicate a Dark Iron Anvil from the outside, but they haven't succeeded in decades," he continued. "I suppose lacking that incomparable explosive force from when the Flame King descended, something is always missing. Of course, I haven't participated in their attempts, so I don't know the exact reasons."

"So the way to produce a second Dark Iron Anvil would be to summon Ragnaros one more time, somewhere else?" Jace asked. "What?! Are you crazy!" Ruige bristled.

Jace quickly explained: "Don't panic, I'm just speaking metaphorically…"

Ruige stared at him, seeming to sense that something was off. "Why are you asking this? You didn't hear some rumor and immediately want to forge yourself a Sulfuron Hammer, did you?"

"Of course not," Jace explained immediately. "My strength is probably far from enough to swing a warhammer like a dwarf warrior, and I don't understand at all how that kind of weapon is balanced. I suppose it's just the curiosity of a mage toward rare and peculiar knowledge."

"Sounds like it has nothing to do with a mage's curiosity, and more like the mad scheme of some evil warlock," Ruige muttered. Jace noncommittally pressed his lips together. "After the Thorium Brotherhood members finish examining it, can I borrow that book again?"

"No." Ruige said it almost instantly. "You don't think you kept this book in very good condition, do you? It's only because of our relationship that I haven't asked you for compensation!"

"Sorry," Jace said. "But I feel like I still haven't learned enough about the elemental tongue…"

"You've even met Master Rokktus, and you're still pestering me?" Ruige said, his eyes wide. "Or are you not planning to return to Shadowforge City in the future? I'd understand that, after all, I've heard the war at Blackrock Mountain… Shadowforge City is already at a complete disadvantage, and getting in is becoming more and more difficult."

"Does Master Rokktus also have an elemental-language dictionary?" Jace asked.

Ruige stood up and roared: "A dictionary? He's one of the greatest fire controllers the Dark Iron dwarves have ever produced! If he hadn't refrained from participating in summoning the Firelord back then out of fear of causing an irreparable disaster, he'd already be entombed in the Tomb of the Seven — no, the eighth!"

"Let me put it this way. Anyone familiar with Shadowforge City's history knows that there wasn't a single true fire controller among those seven — it was just later fire controllers who exaggerated their powers to what they are now. Most of those people were sorcerers and lords under the Sorcerer King's command, and Sithrel was even a fairly powerful ice mage. As the leader of the Thorium Brotherhood in those days, a renowned blacksmith far and wide, Rokktus's ability to communicate with fire elements… was probably stronger than any of them!"

He was right. Jace had also fought the Spirits of the Seven many times. Those ancient Dark Iron dwarf souls wielded a real mishmash of powers — there was even among them a priest who used Holy Light divine magic.

The Dark Iron dwarf stroked his beard. "Stop obsessing over the dictionary and parsing every word. Why is this human even more rigid than a dwarf? It's truly baffling."

"I'm only just starting to scratch the surface of manipulating fire elements…" Jace said. "So it's still better to be cautious. But I understand now. Next time I go to Shadowforge City, I'll definitely ask Master Rokktus more about techniques for communicating with fire elements."

"Let me know when you achieve anything noteworthy, Sesso!" Ruige nodded, then suddenly said: "Oh, right — if you plan to go back to Shadowforge City to find Master Rokktus, bring this for me."

He darted out the door, and shortly after came back carrying a small pouch. From it he pulled out an exquisite, pale green dagger. "Take a look at this, Sesso. This is my work. Bring it to Master Rokktus and let him evaluate my skills. Don't think that just because I'm guarding the door for the Brotherhood in Ironforge that I've stopped honing my craft — this piece is one of my proudest achievements."

This dagger looked like it was made of thorium, though Jace couldn't be entirely sure.

After all, he'd only read about it in books. Since coming to this world, he hadn't really seen many thorium creations with his own eyes, or if he had, he wouldn't have recognized them. After all, this material was far too expensive, so he couldn't help but steal a few more glances.

Seeing his gaze, Ruige's eyes widened. "Don't get any ideas. If you dare sell it for money… you'll never get a single word out of any member of the Thorium Brotherhood again, warlock!"

Jace immediately wrapped the dagger back up. "Got it, got it."
