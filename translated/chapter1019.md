## Chapter 1019: A Strange Collection

Coming onto the beach, Jace caught sight of Pucidiaz's sailboat from a distance. Sola looked around the sandy ground and said, "There shouldn't be any other defense robots around here, should there?"

"This goblin is very rich." Jace said. "He shouldn't buy something that unreliable."

Jace gave Sola a brief introduction to this Pucidiaz fellow. Just then, a goblin walked over from not far away. He looked Jace over and said, "You look a little familiar."

"Jack Slovak." Jace said. "I'm a friend of your boss. I've come to visit him on business."

"Ah… Mr. Slovak, yes, indeed. Mr. Pucidiaz mentions you often." The goblin nodded. "Please, follow me."

Following the goblin aboard the ship, it wasn't long before Pucidiaz came running up onto the deck, looking quite excited.

"Ha, Mr. Slovak!" Pucidiaz spread his arms and walked over, then took in Jace's height and realized a hug probably wouldn't work out very well either way, so he awkwardly lowered his arms.

"I've gotten my hands on a rather suspicious orcish journal. The contents aren't in Orcish — there's some Demonic, but the warlocks I've found can't read all of it. So I thought of you. It's just that I never had a good way to reach you, and then you came looking for me yourself. So this is perfect — could you come take a look for me?"

"Of course, Mr. Pucidiaz." Jace said with a nod.

"Wonderful." The collector glanced at Sola beside him. "Uh, and this lady is…"

"Sola Shade, from the Wizard Sanctum," Sola introduced herself.

"My partner and friend as well," Jace added. "We were sent by King Varian to investigate the Stormwind incident together."

"Stormwind?" Pucidiaz asked. "That black dragon?"

"Yes." Jace said.

At the mention of the black dragon, a wave of discomfort clearly rippled through Pucidiaz's emotions.

But he didn't show anything on the surface. He led the two of them below deck, walking through the hallway as he spoke. "Stormwind isn't that far from Booty Bay. Those black dragons could probably fly here in two or three days. So even though I'm not human, hearing about this disaster makes me feel quite uncomfortable. Does your coming to my ship have something to do with the investigation too?"

"It does indeed." Jace admitted.

"I'm truly sorry, you two." Pucidiaz said. "There are indeed many things related to demons on this ship, but black dragons… there isn't much. Probably can't help. You're not going to refuse to look at that journal for me because of this, are you?"

"I wouldn't do that." Jade said.

"Ha, now that you say that, I actually feel a little guilty." The goblin gave a dry laugh and kept walking.

The journal Pucidiaz had found wasn't any spellbook — merely a record of Shadow Council activities, documenting their early contact and communication between demons and Azeroth's magic during their invasion through the Dark Portal.

While it might be of some help for learning Demonic or tracking down surviving Shadow Council members, it wasn't much good for studying dark magic.

Even so, Pucidiaz was quite excited. After all, he was a collector, not a warlock. Everything related to demons and dark magic — whether spells or mere firsthand accounts, documents, and other items — he was willing to keep.

While discussing the journal with Pucidiaz, Jace kept trying to find an opportunity to steer the topic naturally toward chromatic dragons, but in the end, he never managed to find the right opening.

At most, he got as far as mentioning that the death knights created by the Shadow Council had cooperated with black dragons to invade the Kingdom of Alterac and steal an artifact belonging to Dalaran — barely adjacent to the topic of chromatic dragons.

Watching the goblin put the journal back in its box, Jace could only ask in a casual tone: "So, Mr. Pucidiaz, is there really no way to counter black dragons, or magical items that could help?"

"Counter black dragons?" The goblin laughed. "Even someone like you is at a loss, Mr. Slovak. This ship has plenty of curious little novelties, and some magic-related items too, but don't overestimate the power of these things. Never mind black dragons — any dragon at all could fly over and burn my ship to ashes with a single breath of fire. I'd have no power to fight back at all."

"Only the breath of red dragons and black dragons is flame, Mr. Pucidiaz, and even their flames differ," Sola corrected. "Other dragons command different powers."

"Is that so?" Pucidiaz looked at Sola. "Besides red dragons and black dragons… I don't know much about other dragons either. I've probably only seen some pretty scales and horn fragments of different colors at the trade station, and some of those might even be from chimeras or thunder lizards… Since both of those creatures can breathe fire, I assumed all dragons were the same too. It sounds like this Lady Sola is an expert on dragons?"

"Indeed she is — she's probably the top expert on dragon research in Stormwind, Mr. Pucidiaz," Jace said. "Otherwise the Wizard Sanctum wouldn't have paired me with her for the investigation."

"Really?" The collector seemed to become interested at once. He came up to Sola and asked, "Then have you seen dragons? I mean, besides black dragons and red dragons."

Sola glanced at Jace. Pucidiaz noticed her gaze and said immediately, "I'm no outsider, am I? Tell me, Mr. Sesso — actually, I still find it more natural to call you Slovak. Don't hide things from me! We're old friends!"

"It's fine, Sola." Jace said. "Mr. Pucidiaz and I hide nothing from each other. Tell him what you know as a dragon researcher of the Wizard Sanctum…"

Sola caught on to what Jace meant at once. She nodded and said, "I know many dragons."

"You know dragons?" This actually seemed to startle the goblin collector.

Jace felt he might have overshot it a bit, so he gave Sola a look, signaling her to tone it down.

After all, in an era when many people didn't even know dragons existed beyond red and black, claiming to know actual dragons would be quite startling even to a dragon scholar like Marin.

But Pucidiaz's emotions seemed to go beyond surprise. He looked at Jace, then at Sola, and said, "Lady Sola, could you, uh, could you introduce me to a real dragon?"

Jace was stunned for a moment, but then he realized this might be a perfect opening.

"Impossible." he said. "Even Sola rarely gets to see dragons — how could we ask those dragons to come see you?"

"Still infinitely better than me not being able to see any dragon at all. Or when she gets a chance to meet a dragon, she could let me secretly peek from the side and strike up a conversation somehow, by any means…"

This normally self-assured goblin richster was suddenly reduced to a humble, pleading manner, as if he'd do anything for a chance to get to know a dragon.

Seeing Sola hesitate, the goblin hurriedly added, "All right, what I said before… that I have nothing related to black dragons on me — that was because I was afraid of causing trouble. Since you are enemies of the black dragons, frankly, I'm also not afraid you'd leak the news, right? I have some black dragonkin scales. Legend has it that black dragonkin scales can resist the flames of black dragons. There isn't much, but if they were set into a robe and their power bound with magic… it should provide no small amount of protection. This would be of tremendous help in countering black dragons!"

After the goblin said this excitedly, he seemed to sense the two weren't interested. He sighed, looking like he wanted to say something but couldn't bring himself to open his mouth.

Sola didn't dare say much more from the side either. Jace felt the timing was about right, so he said, "We've heard of a special kind of dragon, Mr. Pucidiaz. Its origins are hard to trace, but it's said the power contained within its body can counter all kinds of dragons — like a… terminator of dragons. Even against black dragons. If there's any information about this kind of dragon, it might be worth taking a risk to try."

Pucidiaz was taken aback, then said, "A dragon that terminates all dragons? I, uh, I've never heard of such a thing."

At this point, Jace felt this trip to Booty Bay might truly have been a waste. This Pucidiaz might be very rich and might indeed have connections to goblins serving the black dragons, but perhaps even those goblins couldn't steal a bit of Nefarian's most prized experimental results.

Just as he was about to tell Sola to leave, Pucidiaz added: "But I do know of a special kind of dragon. Earlier, I received some things through a special channel. Frankly, I've never seen scales like this on any other dragon anywhere."

"Could you show me?" Jace asked.

"All right, wait a moment." Pucidiaz went back into the cabin. Jace and Sola exchanged a glance — both of them had a premonition.

Not long after, the goblin walked out carrying an enchanted treasure chest. He took a small scroll from his clothes, unfolded it, and read an incantation.

The chest banged open — and there was another chest inside, giving Jace, who'd been looking forward to seeing something, a bit of a jolt.

The goblin took out a small key from his person and opened the inner small chest. Then, something resembling a piece of cloudy, turbid glass revealed itself before the three of them.

Sola looked at it as if she'd been struck dumb on the spot. Jace couldn't help but suck in a breath.

This fragment, no bigger than a fingertip, clearly showed blue, copper, red, and several other colors when viewed from different angles. Step back a little and it looked blurry, revealing a faint purple.

Without a doubt, this was a chromatic whelp's scale. This fellow really did have something. But scales alone weren't enough.
