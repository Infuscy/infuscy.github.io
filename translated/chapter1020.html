Chapter 1020: Pucidiaz's Worth

"This is indeed the scale of the dragon we're looking for, Mr. Pucidiaz. Your collection truly amazes me." Jace stared at the tiny scale. "Frankly, before coming to Booty Bay, I had almost no expectation of actually finding traces of this kind of dragon here. I didn't actually expect to find evidence of this dragon's existence anywhere in Azeroth…"

He glanced back at Sola. Sola nodded and said, "A strange sixth color."

"A purple dragon, is it?" Pucidiaz, having been praised by these two dragon experts, looked like his grin was about to split his face. He shook his large pale-green head and said, "You have no idea how much I spent on this little scale!"

"But," Jace continued, "our ultimate goal in searching for this new species of dragon isn't to make armor — it's to find suitable casting materials. This bit of scale won't help much."

Pucidiaz said immediately, "Scales can also serve as casting materials. For example, I know some goblin mages use murloc scales to cast underwater breathing incantations. A scale from such a precious dragon could certainly be used for an extremely powerful, uh, dragon-restraining spell!"

"Indeed." Sola nodded. "Blue dragon scales can be used for arcane divination…" Jace gave her a look. Sola tactfully cleared her throat and shut her mouth.

"I'm truly sorry, Mr. Pucidiaz." Jace looked back, raising his eyebrows in a helpless expression. "But what we need really isn't scales, especially not such a small whelp's scale. But that doesn't mean this trip was a waste — after all, we've seen evidence of this dragon's existence, which means… the investigation now has a direction."

"Tell you what. If you can tell us where you found this material, or who sold it to you, perhaps after Sola and I return, we could actually try to contact a dragon to come meet you in Booty Bay."

"Uh, well." The goblin looked at the two of them hesitantly. He seemed unwilling to let this opportunity slip, but also found it hard to say.

Jace understood. After all, the person who sold this scale could be discovered by their black dragon masters at any moment and suffer a fate worse than death. Under those circumstances, keeping it secret was naturally the most important thing of all.

Pucidiaz paced back and forth in the room for a good while. Jace was already considering going back to Mitherserd's alchemy lab to see if there were any other leads.

But this one scale was already enough to prove too much. The goblins might truly dare not steal things directly related to black dragons, but nicking a bit of other stuff seemed to be no problem.

Considering the failure rate of the chromatic dragons, this dragon blood and the broken scales were most likely taken from those imperfect, deformed corpses. Jace guessed these discarded bodies were either used to stitch together abominations, or simply thrown away to feed other chromatic whelps or black dragons. Stealing a bit of material from these waste products would be very hard to detect.

"Dragons are extremely irritable and proud creatures." Jace said. "I'm sorry, but without sufficiently clear results, we have no reason to persuade them to make a special trip to Booty Bay to meet you. As for secretly hiding nearby during our meeting… do you think the keen senses of these magical beasts wouldn't detect a goblin lurking to peek at them? The consequences would be very dire."

"What my partner says is true, it really is so," Sola agreed.

"All right, all right, all right." The goblin raised his hands high and waved them. "It's Franmecki of 'Best Permit.' His uncle Kreel seems to have some channels, but Kreel has been missing for a very, very long time. Go look for him. As for the chance to meet a dragon, I think we can forget it. Consider this my repayment for your help in investigating the secret of Dasmora Darkeye before. 'Best Permit' isn't far from the trade station. He sells some explosives and firearms there. The quality… I can only say they're a bit more powerful than what the dwarves use."

As Pucidiaz said all this, he appeared relaxed on the surface, but that undercurrent of unease had clearly intensified.

He'd already told himself about this chromatic dragon scale fragment and even revealed the source of the goods. This was basically showing all his cards. So what was he still uneasy about? Was he afraid Jace was a spy sent by the black dragons?

Or was there still something he hadn't fully revealed?

"Is there anything else I should know, Mr. Pucidiaz?" Jace asked.

"What else could there be?" The goblin spread his hands and said with a joking tone, "Next time you come looking for my help, Slovak, you definitely won't get to see my treasures and learn such important information for free like this!"

"Then we'll go take a look first." Jace said. "Good luck to you both." Pucidiaz said.

Coming onto the deck, Jace looked over the goblin sailors around them, then at Sola. He always felt something was off about this collector's emotions, but couldn't put his finger on what exactly.

Maybe finding this Franmecki fellow would reveal what was really going on. "Wait!"

A loud shout from Pucidiaz suddenly came from the cabin behind them. He ran all the way onto the deck, panting a few breaths, and looked at Sola. "How deep are your connections with those dragons, exactly?"

Sola hesitated and looked at Jace. Jace answered, "Hard to say."

"Let me be frank — Franmecki can't give you anything, you two." Pucidiaz leaned over, resting on his knees and lowering his head. "Because everything he had related to this kind of dragon, I've already bought it all."

"Are you sure?" Jace asked.

"I have my methods." The collector glanced at a passing sailor, then continued. "Let's not beat around the bush with each other. What is it you really want?"

"Blood." Jace said directly this time. "We're hoping to find some blood."

"Ah, I knew it." Pucidiaz nodded exaggeratedly. "Blood… blood, blood. The eternal theme of dark magic — and even more so for your kind, Mr. Slovak."

"Come with me, come with me." Pucidiaz beckoned.

There really was blood? Jace hardly dared believe what the collector was implying.

Following Pucidiaz into the depths of this massive sailboat, they entered a corridor guarded by fully armed goblin warriors and mages. Jace could even smell a hint of fel energy here. Before long, he realized exactly where that fel energy was coming from.

A floating Eye of Kilrogg slowly drifted into view, passing between the guards, drifting past Pucidiaz, Jace, and Sola, floating slowly toward the other end of the corridor. Jace couldn't even determine which warlock on the ship had summoned this Eye of Kilrogg.

Coming to the deepest part of the corridor, the door was guarded by a jungle troll warrior. He wore blood-colored exquisite armor enchanted with a highly effective life enhancement, and carried a short battle axe surrounded by a malevolently glowing green curse orb. His thick fingers were covered in magical troll rings whose effects couldn't even be determined at a glance.

God knows what it would feel like to fight one of these Gurubashi axe-fighters built with money. At least the petty thieves in Booty Bay or the pirates prowling the surrounding waters certainly wouldn't have the strength to come here and walk away unscathed.

After pushing the door open, inside was a narrow "gallery" packed with all sorts of strange novelties. Jace didn't recognize most of them, nor could he judge their value. He could only sense that more than half were related to demons.

But since they were mostly sealed in bottles and jars that shielded magical auras, or in glass cases, Jace couldn't feel much energy and had no way to trace their exact origins.

But just then, Jace spotted a thick claw placed in a massive jar — like a demon's foot. It even looked somewhat familiar to him.

"What is this?" he asked.

"Ah." Pucidiaz smiled. "If my investigation is correct, you should have seen this thing before."

"I can't recall." Jace said.

Pucidiaz stroked the wall of the bottle and smiled. "This is a claw of the Pit Lord Azgoth. I paid a fortune for it. Actually, the harder thing to get was this jar. To prevent the terrible fel energy carried by this foot from wreaking havoc on my entire ship, I put in no small amount of effort. The mage who sold me this claw absolutely refused to sell me his box along with it — truly doesn't understand business."

Jace was stunned into silence. This fellow really would buy anything unusual, as long as it was related to dark magic — nothing he could get his hands on would he let slip.

But precisely because of this, it meant the two of them hadn't come to the wrong place.

"However, no matter how much this claw cost, no matter how much effort it took, it's nothing compared to the thing you want to see, Mr. Slovak." Pucidiaz said mysteriously.

He took an enchanted box from the shelf beside him, pulled out yet another scroll from his pocket, read a different incantation to undo the seal on it, then muttered, "Just the act of opening this box is worth four gold pieces, damn it."

After opening it, he took out a bottle the size of an inkwell and handed it to Jace. "Careful now."

Needless to say, Jace was extremely careful, but even holding it in his hand, he could hardly believe this was actually chromatic dragon blood.

"Can I smell it?" he asked.

Pucidiaz stared at him painedly for a moment, then nodded helplessly.

Jace gently pulled out the stopper. Before he even brought it close to his nose, he immediately caught a rich, fishy stench and quickly stoppered it back up.

This was simply the ultimate version of the smell of the runes on that slab. By now, Sola's expression beside him had also gone quite wrong. Seeing Jace look at her, she also nodded, confirming Jace's judgment.

This time, there was no mistake.
