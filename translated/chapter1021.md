## Chapter 1021: A Dark Collector's Dream

"I get the feeling you've seen this blood somewhere else?" Seeing their reactions, Pucidiaz looked at them suspiciously. "Could it be that someone else in this world is selling it? Have I been swindled?"

"No, Mr. Pucidiaz, that's not it at all." Jace held the bottle and said. "Those dragons us that the blood of this special kind of dragon carries the signatures of many different powers within its scent. Just now I confirmed their suspicions."

"That is indeed so." Sola chimed in at once. She even glanced at Jace, her eyes seeming to marvel at how fast Jace could spin a yarn.

"In that case." Jace stuffed the bottle into his pocket. "We'll arrange for that dragon we know to meet you as soon as we get back. I'm certain that after such a tremendous help, he'd be more than happy to make your acquaintance."

"Hold on!" Pucidiaz grabbed Jace's arm. "Give that back!"

"What?" Jace said. "Didn't we just agree?"

"What we agreed on was the scale…" Pucidiaz said. "If you let me meet a dragon, I was willing to give you that fragment. But this bottle is dragon's blood! Everyone knows the value of these two things is completely different!"

"Then how do you plan to sell this?" Jace grabbed the goblin's wrist and asked.

"Give it back first!" Pucidiaz glared, reaching out as if he wanted to claw Jace's face. By now, the troll warrior outside the door had already peeked in to check on the situation.

Jace had no choice but to give the bottle back, lest it get knocked over in the struggle. The goblin got the blood back and immediately stuffed the bottle back into the cushioned lining of the box, closing the lid and clutching it tight.

"Name your price." Jace asked.

He was already planning to pluck a few scales off Krasus and trade them. Ancient dragon scales like that probably weren't something many in the Kirin Tor had ever seen either. For a collector like this, the value should be quite extraordinary. If that wasn't enough, he'd pluck a few more, or throw in some blood as a bonus.

"You think I'm short on money, Mr. Slovak?" The goblin caught his breath and smiled.

"Since you were willing to bring us to see this bottle of blood, that means you've already decided on a price." Jace said.

"That's not wrong." Pucidiaz glanced at Sola, then at Jace. "It's very simple. I want to ride a dragon once."

"Forget it." Sola said almost immediately, and anger was clearly written all over her face.

Jace hadn't even reacted yet when Pucidiaz said directly, "Then there's nothing to discuss. Please leave, you two. And not just in Booty Bay — not in Gadgetzan, not in Everlook, not in the entire Trade Cartel will you find a second bottle. Or you could try searching for the habitat of this kind of dragon. Good luck with that."

"No dragon is willing to be ridden by someone, Mr. Pucidiaz." Jace said.

"If this were something easily done… or something money could solve, would I need your help?" Pucidiaz looked at Jace. "But I know it's not entirely impossible. Those orcs rode dragons into Hillsbrad, didn't they? And in whole flights of dragon-riders at that!"

Sola said with some agitation, "That's because they enslaved the red dragons with dark magic — it doesn't count as evidence at all!"

Jace nodded. "And that kind of dark magic is already completely useless now. The Dragonmaw orcs paid a tremendous price for it as well."

"I've made up my mind to pay a tremendous price too, Mr. Slovak." Pucidiaz patted the box holding the blood and looked at the two of them. "Just once. Just letting me ride a dragon once in this lifetime — is that too much to ask? Judging by what you said earlier, it sounds like that dragon you know… needs this blood quite badly too. I really don't understand, Lady Sola — what's so hard about carrying a goblin for a spin in the sky in exchange for something you could never find no matter how hard you tried…"

Seeing Sola's emotions about to boil over, Jace said immediately, "Allow us to discuss this privately and see how to bring this up with them. How about that, Mr. Pucidiaz?"

"That's perfectly reasonable." Pucidiaz said.

Coming into a guest room and confirming no one was eavesdropping around them, Sola said at once, "I don't want that goblin riding on my back."

"It doesn't have to be you, Sola. We could ask Krasus."

Of course, the moment he said it, Jace himself didn't believe it. He'd sooner believe Krasus would fly over and sink this ship with a mouthful of spit.

"In any case, this at least proves that the goblins really do have a way to steal dragon blood out. We should have a chance to get some from other places."

After saying that, he sat to the side and sighed. He hadn't expected the goblin to want something like this, but it was indeed another demand that sounded simple yet was actually quite complicated.

By now, Sola's wave of anger seemed to have passed, her emotions slowly settling, but the expression on her face remained complicated.

"If we could really get it from somewhere else, would this goblin still spend so much money buying it and protecting it, Jace?" She shook her head. "Fine. If the blood he's giving us can truly help us replicate the slab, then our chances of defeating Nefarian and completely ending the Blackwing flight will increase by no small amount. That would be of tremendous help to us too. So it's really not a big deal."

Jace looked at her torn expression, lowered his head in thought, then suddenly rubbed his fingers together and asked, "Would you mind if I rode?"

"Of course not." Sola looked over, puzzled. "Haven't I been carrying you everywhere all this time?"

"Then if I bring an extra large piece of luggage, would you mind?" Jace asked.

"Why would you…" Sola hadn't even finished when she suddenly froze, then furrowed her brows. "You're not going to…"

Jace spread his hands. "What do you think, Sola?"

"This." Sola's expression became even more complicated at once. She said uncomfortably, "It's fine, Jace. I'll just carry him for one spin. You don't need to…"

"I don't mind this. I'm not a dragon." Jace smiled. "And he's not as heavy as Grede. Carrying him shouldn't be difficult. The question is whether he'd be willing."

"All right, Jace." Sola lowered her head.

"Since you're fine with it, I'll go ask him." Jace said.

Coming out onto the deck, Pucidiaz was standing at the bow with his arms spread wide, gazing into the distance, as if already savoring the feeling of flight.

"If all you want is to fly, you could ride a gryphon — it wouldn't cost more than a few silver, Mr. Pucidiaz." Jace walked over. "No, completely different." The collector turned his head. "Have you figured out how to bring it up?"

"There's a viable plan now." Jace said. "I can take you to ride a dragon for a spin. What do you think?"

"What do you mean." The goblin asked. "Why do you have to come along?"

"No reason." Jace said. "All I can say is that not only can we get a dragon to carry you to fly, but it would be a green dragon that few mages in the Kirin Tor have ever seen — provided I come along. Miss this opportunity, and you'll never get another chance to ride a dragon in your life, Mr. Pucidiaz. Because by then, word of your refusal to help will reach the ears of every dragon."

"What, what?" The goblin said, a little flustered. "I didn't refuse to help. I just wanted a little compensation. That's not too much to ask, is it? I can't just give it away for free, can I?"

"The thoughts of dragons are always hard to fathom. You tell me." Jace said.

Pucidiaz frowned, paced back and forth on the spot a few times, then asked, "So what you mean is, I'll be riding on you, and you'll be riding on the dragon — is that it?"

Jace leaned against the mast beside him. "That's right."

The goblin slapped his own face. "Ugh, that way I won't even be able to touch the dragon's scales at all! It completely lacks any real feel!"

"That's easy to solve." Jace pulled at his own cloak. "This cloak is made from a young dragon's hide. I'll wear it, and you sit on my shoulders — there'd be no difference at all."

The goblin stared in surprise at the hem of Jace's cloak. "This… is a young dragon's hide?"

But before he'd been stunned for even half a second, he shook his head. "No, no, wait! I have a chair made with icewind chimera fur — does that mean riding on it equals riding a chimera? That's completely not the same thing!"

"So under that chair of yours, there's a real icewind chimera flying, Mr. Pucidiaz?" Jace asked.

Pucidiaz was stunned for a moment, then his large head drooped. "Argh, damn it. A green dragon. A green one. Oh, Lord, I really can't refuse. All right, all right, Slovak — Jace Sesso — you've convinced me, you've won! Tell me, when can we fly? Best not drag it out too long."

"Give me that bottle of blood first." Jace glanced toward the cabin behind him. "Lady Sola will take this bottle of blood back to report to them, and we'll meet at the agreed spot. It can't be inside Booty Bay — it would cause a commotion. I'll stay here as collateral, how about that? Have that jungle troll bodyguard of yours keep an eye on me."

"Then I have one more request." The goblin said. "Let's hear it." Jace said.

Pucidiaz lowered his voice. "I want to fly a lap over those Bloodsail pirates up north. No need to breathe anything on them — just scare them a little. I want to hear their screams…"

"I can agree to that for you." Jace said.

"No problem, no problem." The goblin said. "Come with me to get the thing!"
