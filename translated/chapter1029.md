## Chapter 1029: Delivering a Message

This was definitely no illusion on Jace's part, because Amy also glanced warily into her own wine glass—she must have noticed it too.

"You mean the Tide Duke," she looked over and asked. "Where did you come across that name?"

Jace nodded. "Do me a favor. Is there any way a mage could get in touch with this Duke Hydaxis?"

"How could there be?" Amy couldn't help but glance at her wine glass again. "This is… a name that only appears in books. A legendary water elemental lord. There's absolutely no way I could just summon it right here in front of me."

Jace said, "I'm not asking you to summon the lord himself. What I mean is, could you have the water elements you summon help get in touch with that lord?"

"Uh, I…" Amy thought for a moment. "I've never tried making that kind of request. Why do you want to contact him?"

Jace tapped his fingers on the table, pondering for a moment how to explain it to her. Amy noticed his fidgeting and narrowed her eyes slightly. "Forget it if you can't say, Jace. I'm sure there are plenty of things you can't tell me."

"It's related to Shadowforge City. You know about the Dark Iron dwarves and their fire faith," Jace said.

"Water and fire cannot coexist," Amy said slowly, nodding. "I more or less understand. Do you have time this afternoon? We could try at Mirror Lake. Summoning water elements would be easier by the lake."

"Today works?" Jace asked.

Amy said, "I'm heading back to Dalaran in a couple of days, Jace. And I'm curious myself whether a water element could get in touch with a legendary being. Let me head back to the Mage Quarter first to drop these things off with my father, and this afternoon…"

She glanced at her pocket watch. "Two o'clock, meet at the shores of Mirror Lake. How's that?"

"Good," Jace said.

That afternoon, Jace arrived at Mirror Lake early to wait.

Finding Duke Hydaxis was the key to breaking the fire-sealing runes of the Molten Core. Without the Essence of Water, there probably wouldn't even be a chance to lay eyes on Ragnaros, let alone destroy him and lift the curse on all of Shadowforge City.

He'd been thinking about making a trip to Azshara, or to the water elemental shrine on the coast of the Barrens, to find a tauren shaman to have a chat with the sea.

He'd even considered going to Kul Tiras to speak with the Tidesages there about the possibility of contacting Hydaxis. Turning it over and over in his mind, he'd almost completely overlooked the Kirin Tor.

Not long after, Amy appeared in the forest not far off. It wasn't even one o'clock yet. She saw Jace from a distance and said, "Didn't think you'd be here even earlier than me."

Jace glanced at the lake. "Couldn't wait to see your magic display, of course."

"Don't get your hopes too high. I haven't heard of many mages summoning water elements for this purpose…" Amy said as she approached the lake, taking a deep breath.

She stepped back, raised her hands, and chanted. Soon a cluster of water splashed together between her palms.

Finishing the incantation, she clenched her right hand, and a small water elemental creature burst out of the lake, murmuring something in the elemental tongue.

Jace could tell it was the elemental tongue, and he could tell it was responding to Amy's summons, but there were a few parts he only half understood, not quite confident.

Clearly, although fire elements and water elements spoke the same language, there might be some differences between them too. "Whatever you want to know, Jace, you can ask it now," Amy said.

Jace stepped forward and said in the elemental tongue, "I wish to make the acquaintance of the Tide Duke Hydaxis."

"Hydaxis… Lord… will not be interested in you," the water elemental grumbled. "Where is he?" Jace asked.

"I… cannot know. Hydaxis… capability… perception… insufficient," the water elemental replied. "Futile… futile effort, human."

"A group of dwarves intends to overthrow the Firelord Ragnaros's rule on Blackrock Mountain, water elemental," Jace said directly in Common. "Does Duke Hydaxis have no interest in this at all? I know you understand."

Hearing Jace say this, Amy's eyes widened in surprise. She murmured, "Firelord?"

The water elemental splashed out a great sheet of foam. Jace couldn't tell whether its emotion was excitement or anger.

After a while it spoke in the elemental tongue: "The exalted Hydaxis… dwells upon Azeroth. And I… cannot leave the arcane… to dwell upon Azeroth. I am not… powerful enough… to help you deliver a message, but… water and fire, Blackrock Mountain, Ragnaros… war. Duke Hydaxis will wish to make your acquaintance, human, but I cannot… be your messenger."

"I understand," Jace said.

"I… may be released… my lady?" the water elemental asked.

Amy nodded. The water elemental suddenly splashed into a vast sheet of water and vanished into the lake, leaving only two dim bracers that plopped into the water.

"It seems this water elemental's existence here couldn't last long enough to make contact with Hydaxis," Jace said, watching the two magically shaped elemental binding bracers gradually crack and eventually disappear without a trace. "Amy, could you help me find… your teacher, Archmage Amansa? I wonder if he'd be willing to help."

Amy took a deep breath, cast Arcane Intellect on herself, and said with a hint of reluctance: "Let me try again. Let's see if I can summon one powerful enough."

"All right," Jace nodded.

She didn't drink any mana potion, but instead cast again directly facing the lake. A faint arcane circle spun beneath her feet.

This time Jace felt the power she drew from the ley lines churn the very surface of the lake before her. Quite a few water droplets were blown onto his own face by the magical wind.

When had this female mage, who'd even struggled with her promotion to full mage, become so powerful?

Perhaps she'd simply lacked a platform like the Kirin Tor to unleash her potential. Perhaps she'd never properly recognized herself.

Jace was still marveling when she finished chanting. Though her voice was not loud, under the arcane enhancement it struck the eardrums of Jace, a bystander, with heavy force.

This time, a water elemental far taller than Jace rose up, wreathed in a misty haze.

Amy caught her breath, looking proud of having completed this summoning ritual.

She certainly had reason to be proud. Jace couldn't even sense any over-expenditure of her mana… especially considering she'd summoned one small and one large water elemental in succession.

The water elemental raised its head and said in rumbling elemental tongue: "I see no need for… your help, mage. Is there some enemy here worth drowning?"

"I need the help, elemental," Jace said.

"Hmm… your voice is familiar, human," the water elemental lowered its head. "Speak your name."

"I am Jace Sesso," Jace said.

The water elemental lowered its head. "Hmm, a favored one of the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza. So it is you. I can feel the fire and shadow boiling within me… tell me, is this summoning ritual some kind of provocation?"

"No," Jace said. "Duke Hydaxis and I share a common enemy—the Firelord. I'm looking for allies to deal with him together."

"Ragnaros…" the water elemental fell silent for a moment. "I will deliver the message for you, Jace Sesso. If the Tide Duke is willing to trust you and interested in an alliance, you will know."

"Thank you," Jace said with a nod.

It looked at the female mage beside it and asked, "Is this the entire purpose of this summoning, my lady?"

"Yes," Amy said.

"Then, release me…" the water elemental rumbled.
