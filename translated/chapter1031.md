## Chapter 1031: The Void, One Step Further

The nine gold coins brought back from Ironforge, plus the "cut" from Misersheld, were more than enough for Jace to pay Ursula for the Refined Wisdom Potion and still have a few gold left over to get by on.

Ursula often stayed up late into the night and then slept straight through until the afternoon. This showed that the elven blood in her veins was running pretty low now. The half-elves of Stormwind couldn't match their elven elders' sleep efficiency—going a full week without closing their eyes—but they could still handle staying up a night or two.

In any case, looking for Ursula in the middle of the night was far better than looking for her in the morning. So Jace took the tablet and enchanted metal plate that Krasus had given him, brought them home, handed them to the two demons to keep an eye on, and immediately set off for the Mage Quarter.

The Slaughtered Lamb was still open at night. Jace exchanged a greeting with Tharle and headed down to the basement, only to find Galders sitting in the main hall reading a book.

"Jace?" he looked up.

Jace was taken aback. "Galders, where have you been this whole time?"

"Don't even ask. I've been racking my brains trying to find ways to perfect the summoning circle, Jace," Galders said with a sigh. "The demon incident at Moonbrook a while back implicated too many warlocks. A few warlocks I'd finally found who could guide me on the summoning circle either went silent… or got thrown in jail. I only managed to get back to Stormwind thanks to Mr. Mathias Shaw's help. But all in all it wasn't a waste—I've figured out some of the things about that summoning circle incantation we didn't understand before. I'm planning to try again. Interested in joining?"

Jace said helplessly, "I still need to head to Shadowforge City to return some things. If I don't want to get surrounded and stabbed in an alley by a bunch of black dwarves one of these nights, I'd better not drag this out too long."

"Those Dark Iron dwarves have enough trouble just trying to get out," Galders said with a cold laugh. "It doesn't matter. We'll try it first once Galen Ailro gets back. Oh right—Ursula said you got a minion-summoning incantation from the warlock in Shadowforge City, didn't you?"

"Yes." Jace took out the scroll that the imp had copied from his bag and handed it to Galders. "Here it is. I also summarized some casting pointers beyond the incantation itself, plus some material knowledge and the like, all on there. You can take a look, or have Lady Sensora make a copy."

The succubus in the dark corner heard her name and nodded respectfully toward Jace. Galders took the scroll and unrolled it. Jace asked, "Is Ursula inside?"

"She's in there, of course," Galders nodded. "But there's one more thing I want to discuss with you, Jace. This is something I can probably only talk over with you within the Dagger Circle."

He rolled up the scroll and continued, "I won't take up too much of your time, and I'm not trying to do everything at once. After all, this magic is a serious matter—there may be some hard-to-control consequences."

Jace glanced down the passage toward the alchemy lab. He had to admit, Galders's words had piqued his curiosity.

Galders said, "You once faced Teron Gorefiend in Dalaran during the black dragon's attack on the city… so I imagine the Kirin Tor must have disclosed part of the situation regarding Kel'Thuzad's betrayal of Dalaran to you."

Jace hadn't expected Galders to bring this up at all, but he just nodded. "Yes. But wasn't Kel'Thuzad locked in the Violet Hold? Did he escape?"

"How could he?" Galders said. "No one can escape the Violet Hold—not even members of the Kirin Tor Council of Six. But you should know, the Kirin Tor has been searching for concrete evidence of Kel'Thuzad's betrayal this whole time, right? The reason he's still locked up now is only 'researching necromantic magic'—it's not even betraying the Alliance. By tradition he'd be exiled, not kept locked up indefinitely."

Jace nodded.

Galders said, "Magino Dumas and the Kirin Tor dug up some leads related to Kel'Thuzad from those warlocks in the Stormwind prison. Did the Wizard Sanctum people tell you?"

Jace shook his head. "No. I haven't seen them this whole time. I've been dealing with the Lady Katrana Prestor problem."

"Lady Katrana Prestor—I was even excited enough to forget about that," Galders said, leaning in closer. "It's normal that you don't know. Even Mathias Shaw doesn't seem to know, because that prison is tightly controlled by the Wizard Sanctum people."

Though there was no one else in the basement, he lowered his voice and continued, "I heard it from my own informant, and he only found out after the news reached the Kirin Tor. There are some northern demon summoners—possibly from Eastweald, possibly Lordaeronians—who have been secretly serving the black dragons and death knights. It was this group who helped Kel'Thuzad hide the evidence. The reason the Kirin Tor investigators have found nothing all this time… is because they hid the crucial incantations and necromantic knowledge the black dragons gave Kel'Thuzad inside the Twisting Nether!"

"The Twisting Nether?" Jace was truly stunned now.

"Of course, this is only those warlocks' testimony, Jace," Galders said. "The Kirin Tor people haven't found the 'entrance' to that Twisting Nether yet. Actually, it's not that the Kirin Tor lacks mages who can do this—there have been mages in history who tried to open rifts to the Twisting Nether and used a small piece of that space as a kind of magical warehouse… but those warlocks didn't achieve the effect through similar arcane magic. They did it through something more secretive. So the investigators, using the methods they used to track down those Dalaran mage criminals from back then, have gotten nowhere."

Jace thought for a moment and asked, "Demonic magic?"

"Exactly," Galders said with a smile. "What demon comes to mind?"

"Without question, a Doomguard," Jace said.

"I knew I should come to you with this, Jace," Galders said. "The Ered'ruin are powerful planar demons. Those Doomguards can easily tear open a rift in the Void, but clearly, the Doomguard serving Kel'Thuzad is no ordinary character… every warlock I know who can summon a demon guard—their minions can't create a stable void gate leading to a defined space."

"My god," Jace said. "You're not also trying to get yourself a 'void warehouse' too?"

"I'm thinking even further," Galders said. "If one day we could carve out a hiding spot for ourselves in the Twisting Nether, wouldn't that be incomparably stronger than this basement, or that place in Darkshire? I know it sounds like a pipe dream to you—after all, we haven't even successfully summoned a Doomguard yet, but… at least it offers a possibility, doesn't it? I have to admit, those mad black dragons and mages really have ideas. We warlocks, dealing with leftover orc junk all day, are still far too timid."

Actually, Jace certainly wasn't someone who'd never thought about it—after all, the "class hall" that warlocks would later build in the game was a shattered piece of space located in the Twisting Nether.

But to be planning something like this now—wasn't that a bit too far ahead of the curve for the warlocks in the Dagger Circle? "This does involve the Twisting Nether, after all, Galders," Jace said.

Galders immediately said, "That's exactly why I said I'm not trying to do everything at once. It can't be accomplished in one go. You're right—magic involving the Twisting Nether is inherently dangerous and unstable. But I have to say, it's a direction worth striving for."

"I won't deny that," Jace said with a nod.

Galders smiled. "If you have time, let's research summoning demons together. I think the time is right now. Actually, I know there are already warlocks trying this. Once Galen Ailro is back, the Dagger Circle had better find time to gather again. I believe with your power, Jace, you'll definitely be the first to summon a Doomguard powerful enough."

Seeing Jace's thoughtful expression, Galders said, "You can think about it, Jace. Of course, we're still playing it safe as the priority. Maybe I'm just a little too excited after hearing this news. I won't keep you from going to find Ursula. Oh, and by the way, I brought back a batch of quite rare and expensive herbs from Ironforge too—you can go take a look and see if there's anything you can use."
