Chapter 1035: A Deserved Reward

The red dragon led them away from the corridor, passing without obstruction across the bridge guarded by Shattered Hand orcs, and arrived at a spot low down, very close to the bridge surface that the Dark Iron dwarves had blown apart.

Vaelastrasz looked up at the smoke-filled vaulted ceiling and said, "Going down here can avoid the surveillance of the little bastard up above. When you leave, if you can't time it right, wait here for a while — if I sense you, I'll come help."

"Thanks, Vaelastrasz," Jace said.

"Don't mention it," Vaelastrasz said, clapping Jace on the shoulder. "Get going — if we drag this out any longer, orc patrols might come this way and that'll be hard to explain."

Jace nodded. He and Sola climbed over the bridge railing and dropped onto the leg of a dwarven statue whose upper half had been blown away. From here, the entrance into Shadowforge City was already very close — just the opposite direction from last time they'd come here. The rest of the road would be much easier.

This time, they'd really run into Vaelastrasz at just the right moment. If they'd had to fight near Blackrock Spire, any delay — or being noticed by other orcs — would have been way too much trouble.

Once they approached Shadowforge City, Sola shifted into the appearance of a Dark Iron dwarf. This time when entering Shadowforge City, Jace informed the soldiers that he had an appointment with the Flamebenders at the Thor'San Shrine, and the Dark Iron soldiers escorted him straight to the shrine.

Arriving at the lava bridge before the shrine, Jace saw the Anvilmaster approaching from a distance.

"By the Firelord, Mr. Slovakia, I thought you weren't planning to come back!" Sorgaden called from far off.

"How could I not," Jace said. "I take Shadowforge City's friendship very seriously."

"Of course."

Sorgaden smiled and led Jace and Sola into the shrine. Arriving before the door to the vault, he dismissed the lower-ranked Flamebender apprentices and servants milling about, and called for the door to be opened.

"How about it, Mr. Slovakia — did you figure out how to use those stone tablets?" he asked as he led the two of them inside.

There were two Flamebenders in the vault. When they saw Sorgaden, they saluted and shouted dwarven slogans of reverence to the Firelord. Jace glanced at them, set the fragmented stone tablet on the table, then looked over the furnishings in the vault. "I wouldn't have had the face to come back to you otherwise, Lord Sorgaden. Bring my staff out first — let me confirm it's still intact."

"Bring out Master Slovakia's gemstone staff!" Sorgaden barked.

"Yes, my lord!"

A response came from the corner of the vault. Jace noticed two more Flamebenders over there. One of them walked inside, and before long came out carrying the Mirror Stone Staff.

Later on, he could hand this Mirror Stone over to Vereesa and have her bring it back to the Reliquary to fulfill the contract — no telling how much of the final payment he'd get.

The dwarven Flamebearer carried the staff up to Sorgaden's side. Just as Jace reached out to take it, Sorgaden raised a hand to block him. "Help me confirm how to use this stone tablet first."

Jace nodded and took out the dispel ring. "The key is this, Lord Sorgaden. As long as you use this ring to lift the sealing magic on the stone tablet, then read it aloud normally, it will deal damage to black dragons."

"Hmm." Sorgaden took the ring and looked it over, then lifted the stone tablet and placed the ring on top. "Like this?"

"Yes, just touching the tablet is enough," Jace said.

Sorgaden, half believing and half doubtful, set the ring on the tablet. When he saw the tablet seem to react and give off a dark aura, he raised his eyes in surprise.

Then he picked up the tablet and chanted the incantation upon it. The blood seemed to have some magical reaction, but was quickly suppressed by the incantation itself.

"What's going on here?" Sorgaden asked.

"This incantation only works on powerful black dragons, my lord. If there are no black dragons nearby, it won't activate — this is to avoid wasting the precious mana inscribed upon it," Jace explained. "You can try it again the next time you face a black dragon."

"Sounds like some kind of trick," Sorgaden said. "I need to confirm it works before I return your staff to you, Mr. Slovakia."

"The staff was left here as collateral for the stone tablet," Sola said. "Now that the tablet has been returned to you, you ought to return the staff accordingly."

Sorgaden looked at Sola. "Are you standing on Shadowforge City's side, or the human's? Get your position straight! Who knows what you two might have done to this stone tablet? I need to confirm it's perfectly intact."

"Don't make a decision you'll regret, Master Sorgaden," Jace said.

Sorgaden asked angrily, "Are you threatening me?"

With that, he immediately transformed into a ball of flame and teleported to a high platform.

Jace was just about to use Grasp of the Blood to drag him down when he felt as though a powerful electric current had pierced through his body, choking off all flow of mana. After a spell of dizziness, he found himself fixed in place by something like a lightning cage — he wanted to move, but his body simply wouldn't obey.

So the two massive golems responsible for opening the door had done their job. Both of them aimed their arms at him simultaneously, channeling some kind of anti-magic electric arc. Jace could barely even open his mouth to speak.

Several Flamebenders moved in, surrounding Sola and Jace in the center, their staves and scepters already blazing with flame.

"So this is the warlock who can make cloaks out of black dragon hide?" Sorgaden laughed from above. "I won't hurt you, Master Slovakia. I just need to confirm the effect of this incantation. It won't take long — just wait until we next see a black…"

He broke off mid-sentence, slowly raising his head. A black blade was already pressed against the top of his skull.

"This knife is black too, dear Lord Sorgaden," Morophil leaned down, pressing close to his forehead and murmuring. "Ah, I forgot — you can't see the top of your own head. No matter, wait until it stabs down into your eye from above…"

Before Sorgaden could say anything, the Flamebenders began dropping one after another. With every thud, Sorgaden's whole body shuddered.

"What kind of lethal magic is this?" he asked in surprise. "I can't sense any… trace of magic at all."

"They're not dead, just asleep," Sola said, her eyes already showing the light of the moon. "However, if you don't order the golems to stop, they'll suffocate to death within two minutes."

Sorgaden immediately commanded the golems to halt in Dwarvish. Once Jace broke free of the lightning's grip, he scratched his head, moved his limbs a bit, and picked up the Mirror Stone Staff from the ground beside that Flamebender.

He looked up. "You dwarves are a real headache, Master Sorgaden. I'm planning to stay in Shadowforge City for a while yet, so you'd better pray to the Firelord that nothing happens to me — otherwise this demon friend of mine will…"

"Lover," Morophil corrected unhappily. "A succubus lover."

"Then lover." Jace glanced at Morophil and smiled. "She can give you a very tragic ending any time, Master Sorgaden. Understood?"

"Understood, Master Slovakia." Sorgaden nodded vigorously. "I know… a banshee huntress."

"Good, then I won't need to explain further." Jace swept his gaze across the hall until it landed on a red staff.

The head of this staff was like a bronze ornamental claw, with a purple gem set in the middle, faintly releasing lightning-like mana. Below it, in Dwarvish, was written "Dominance."

Was this the Staff of Dominance that dropped from the Molten Core in the game? From the outside it certainly looked like it.

Jace picked up the staff and tossed it to Sola beside him. "Consider this my reward for helping Shadowforge City fight black dragons — how about it, Master Sorgaden?"

"That's the staff Sage Vallerel himself used!" Sorgaden shouted.

"Then that's enough to appease my anger." Jace looked at the four or five Flamebenders lying on the ground. "You have one minute to decide." Sorgaden deflated at once and said helplessly, "Just… this one item."

"Much appreciated." Jace gave a slight bow and said to Sola, "Let's go."
