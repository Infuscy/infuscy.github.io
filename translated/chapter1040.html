Chapter 1040: Arkesius

They arrived in Stormwind the next morning. Although he'd been riding on a dragon's back, thanks to the green dragon's magic Jace had slept extremely comfortably. So there was no travel fatigue at all — if anything, he felt full of energy.

Sola was a different story. Before they even reached home, she was so sleepy she felt like she could barely walk straight.

After seeing her home, since they were both in the Mage Quarter, Jace figured he'd stop by Ursula's on the way and see how the potion-making was coming along — though of course they couldn't set out until her Refined Wisdom Elixir was ready.

Arriving at the basement of the Slaughtered Lamb, there weren't many people around at this hour. Erland, Galders, and the others were all out. Only a felhound was watching Jace from the shadows.

He had to say, even though Jace was more than confident he could handle this felhound, and even though he knew they were on the same side, for a caster like him, the gaze of such a magical hunter still sent a chill through him.

Over in the alchemy lab, there seemed to be the faint sound of something brewing. But right then, a string of footsteps sounded behind him — Ursula was running down the stairs carrying a bundle, and she was a little startled to see Jace in the hall.

"Oh, Jace, you're a little early. I haven't finished making the Refined Wisdom Elixir yet. You know, some ingredients can't be produced in this alchemy lab — I need to contact suppliers elsewhere, but the day after tomorrow at the latest."

Then, a little embarrassed, she held up the paper bundle in her hand and asked, "Want some bread together?"

"You definitely didn't buy my share, right? I'm not hungry anyway," Jace said. "Then I'll come back the day after tomorrow. But there's something I want to tell you in advance — the green dragon blood… I might not be able to get it. If it really doesn't work out in the end, I'll compensate you in gold."

"Safety first. Everything else is secondary." Ursula smiled as she finished. "But I've already finished the anti-venom potions — ten bottles total. Come pack them up here."

After leaving the Slaughtered Lamb, Jace took one bottle out and opened the lid to sniff. Sure enough, he caught a scent similar to the Restorative Potion — that aroma unique to expensive Elemental Earth.

This wasn't ordinary anti-venom medicine — this was a fairly potent Greater Nature Protection Potion.

By their previous agreement, Ursula hadn't charged him a single copper for this kind of potion — a complete freebie. This time it was Jace who felt he was getting the better end of the deal.

On his way out of the Mage Quarter, Jace noticed someone who seemed to be dressed as a priest talking with a full-fledged mage at the base of the mage tower.

Because of the Onyxia affair, the Church of the Holy Light and the Wizard Sanctum had been in close contact lately, so there was nothing strange about a priest showing up here — the mages were used to it.

What surprised Jace was that the priest was no other than Morgan Ladimore, who'd moved to Stormwind from Raven Hill not long ago.

When Jace looked over in that direction, Morgan noticed Jace too. He quickly wrapped up his conversation with the mage and walked over.

"Jace… Master Sesso."

Even without needing to carefully sense shadow, Jace could feel the tension radiating off him. "Don't call me that, Morgan. Sounds like you're making fun of me."

Morgan gave an embarrassed laugh. "I've been asking around the church about you these past days, Jace. Light above, you're not just 'working for the king' in any simple sense, are you."

"I was in a hurry to find the king that day — didn't have time to explain everything to you," Jace glanced toward the Blue Heron behind him. "Have you eaten lunch yet?"

"Not yet," Morgan said. "I was just heading back to the Cathedral."

"To the Blue Heron?" Jace said. "The Pig and Whistle's too far — I'd probably starve to death on the streets of the Trade District before I got there."

Morgan smiled. "I've never been to the mages' part of town."

"Then today's your chance to experience it. Remember Grede? Even he's been in there," Jace said.

"Grede!" Morgan said softly. "Of course I remember. I've also heard a lot about him these days — even back then I could tell that gryphon rider was no ordinary fellow."

The two of them arrived at the Blue Heron, chatting briefly along the way about their experiences since parting at Raven Hill. Once seated, Morgan still couldn't help saying, "So you and that elf are still together, right? But I never expected it to be Lady Vereesa Windrunner!"

Jace smiled proudly. "Shh, keep it down — don't let these mages hear." Morgan stole a couple of quiet laughs himself, but the smiles faded quickly.

Jace could tell — he had something on his mind.

After chatting a bit more, Morgan seemed to have about finished eating. His eyes began drifting back and forth between every plate and cup, occasionally darting toward the little menu board by the bar — probably calculating the prices. Compared to the Pig and Whistle, the Blue Heron really wasn't a cheap place.

Jace took a sip of his ale and looked at him. "We've known each other a long time now, Morgan. You helped me and Vereesa out — if you're in trouble, I won't just look the other way."

"We're living quite well in Stormwind, Jace. Nothing to complain about," Morgan said with a smile.

Seeing Jace keep staring at him, he probably knew his cards had already been read, so he sighed and said, "All right. Before, I did want to ask you and Grede for help, but after hearing about you two from the church, I didn't know how to bring it up. I've been agonizing over this ever since moving to Stormwind."

Jace was taken aback. "You're not looking to borrow money to buy a house? No way, Morgan. Don't think I'm being rude, but forget about me — even the king's broke."

"Of course not," Morgan said quickly. "I haven't even been to the registry. Liz and I haven't seriously considered buying a house yet. It's something that belonged to my grandfather — a sword."

Arkesius.

This was practically the first name that came to mind for Jace — the paladin's sword that would make all of Duskwood pale at its mention a decade and a half from now, wielded by the "Moradin" that roamed the land.

That massive sword, brimming with the Holy Light, looked so jarringly out of place in the hands of that evil skeleton prowling the great cemetery north of Raven Hill. Every player who'd seen it remembered it vividly.

"That sword had always been kept in my grandfather's tomb. But you know the great cemetery has already been overrun by undead monsters, and my grandfather's burial chamber is at the very deepest part." Here Morgan hesitated. He shook his head. "My father doesn't want me to take up that sword. He's always believed it was because of that sword that my grandfather died. But I can't just leave it in that desecrated hole forever. I tried going to find it myself, but several times I nearly died at the hands of ghouls. The only sword I had left was destroyed by those monsters, and Liz absolutely forbids me from going back."

Jace looked at him. "Are you sure you want to get it back?"

Morgan looked like he hadn't expected Jace to ask that. He looked up, stunned for a moment. But in the end, he still nodded firmly.

"I've got some business these next few days, Morgan. In a couple of days I'll need to make a trip to Booty Bay," Jace said. "Wait until I'm back, and then I'll go with you to Raven Hill to find it. In the meantime, don't you dare go adventuring again on your own. Take care of Liz and the child."

"Thank you, Jace. Thank you," Morgan said.
