## Chapter 1042: Far Away, Right Before Your Eyes

That afternoon, Jace arrived at the Slaughtered Lamb and heard the clinking and clatter of bottles coming from the passage toward the alchemy lab. Ursula must have been packing her things up — good, so she hadn't been napping.

But just as he was about to head over, he caught sight of Galene Aylo poking her head out from the passage on the other side, as if she'd heard him coming.

"Good afternoon, Jace," she said. "I heard from Ursula that you'd probably stop by again in the next day or two, so I've been waiting here for you. I won't take up too much of your time — there's something I wanted to ask you."

"I'm not exactly an expert on tailoring problems, Galene," Jace said with a smile. "If this has to do with what Galders brought up last time — making robes in the Twisting Nether and all that — I probably don't know any more than you do."

"It's related, but not entirely," Galene said. "You've been to Kalimdor a few times. I don't know if you've had any contact with the elves over there — the ones called the Kaldorei? I've heard those elves are extremely xenophobic and very difficult to talk to."

Jace nodded. "Some, but honestly not much conversation."

The moment Jace said that, Galene's eyes lit up.

"That's incredible. Sulenna Kel'don hasn't met a single goblin in Booty Bay — or any other traveler, for that matter — who's had contact with those Kaldorei. I've read some of Dalaran's records, and they mention that the Kaldorei once used a very special method to purify precious fabrics that had been fel-corrupted by Burning Legion warlocks. Since you've had contact with the Kaldorei, have you ever seen that kind of cloth? I imagine only the highest-ranking priestesses would be qualified to wear garments made from that material."

Jace knew exactly what she was talking about. It was a rare fabric called Mooncloth. In the game, a skilled tailor could cleanse Felcloth in a Kaldorei Moonwell to produce it.

A decade and a half from now, after the Third War ended, the night elves would establish friendly ties with humanity. A number of them would move to Stormwind's Park district and build a Moonwell at its center, so tailors here would be able to cleanse Felcloth nearby.

The problem was that Jace had no idea whether there were any Moonwells anywhere on the Eastern Kingdoms right now, because the night elves hadn't made contact with this part of the world yet and hadn't established any outposts or footholds here. That raised the difficulty by an order of magnitude.

"If you're making robes related to shadow magic, wouldn't Felcloth be better to begin with?" Jace asked.

"Once purified, it's far more versatile, Jace," Galene said. "If we keep using cloth stripped straight from demons, then our work amounts to little more than patching things up… even if we use the orcs' methods. If we want to take it further and produce top-tier goods rivaling Eredar-quality robes, we need to start from the base as much as possible."

"All right, I think I follow," Jace said with a nod. "I haven't actually seen that cloth, but I have a pretty good idea how they purify it. When I was in Kalimdor, I stayed in a Kaldorei town for a couple of days, and I noticed they liked to rest near a ritual structure called a Moonwell. The water in those wells contains incredibly pure arcane energy — really just like moonlight. So maybe…"

"Special water," Galene cut in, nearly grabbing the words out of his mouth. "I've definitely come across that theory, Jace. Good lord, you've actually seen one! I was about to contact a goblin about a trip to Kalimdor. Do you know how to make contact with those Kaldorei?"

"Don't go alone, Galene," Jace said. "When I went, my party had a dwarf fighter, a High Elf ranger, and even a local tauren guide, and we nearly got wiped out on the way. You have no idea how dangerous Kalimdor is."

After hearing that, the light on Galene's face visibly dimmed. She leaned against the wall and shook her head. "Then I should hire a goblin expedition team."

"You'd probably die even faster," Jace said. "The Kaldorei and goblins especially can't stand each other."

"Help me out here, Jace. The first new robe I make is yours," Galene said. "I've summarized some of the problems with the previous void robes — things like lacking elemental resistances beyond shadow and fire, not supporting fel magic very well, and so on — and combined that with some experience I gained from Eredar robe fragments I acquired from a few colleagues… Right now, the only thing missing is a perfect, highly magically versatile material. I want to pursue the best quality, Sesso…"

Although Jace's first instinct was that this sounded like a hassle, he had to admit this approach worked on him. The moment she reminded him that this new robe would be handed to him for free, Jace felt like he'd already boarded a pirate ship with no way off.

"I do know a friend who's fairly familiar with the Kaldorei, Galene, but I can't give you an answer in the short term," Jace said.

"I know. Ursula told me you're about to leave Stormwind," Galene said. "Actually, there are still a few other problems with this new robe design that need solving, but in any case… it's still far more reliable than me heading off to an unfamiliar Kalimdor to look for Kaldorei."

The Kaldorei expert Jace had in mind was, of course, Sola.

He wasn't counting on finding a Moonwell anywhere on the Eastern Kingdoms, but there was a chance Sola herself could cleanse Felcloth into the legendary Mooncloth.

He wondered whether the Tears of the Green Dragon or Moonwell water were more powerful. Logically, it should be the Tears of the Green Dragon — after all, they were precious enough as an alchemical material in the eyes of a troll witch doctor.

Was he really going to make Sola cry again just for the sake of a robe material?

Jace shook off his increasingly strange thoughts and headed over to the alchemy lab. Ursula was tidying up her cabinet and the moment she saw Jace she said, "It's done, Jace — the Refined Wisdom Elixir. Good thing I pulled an all-nighter last night. I knew you wouldn't be able to help coming by again today."

"Thanks, Ursula," Jace said with a smile, taking the small pouch she handed him. Then he asked, "Does the Dagger Society have any extra Soul Shards?"

"No," Ursula said, shaking her head. "We used up a chunk of them trying to summon a Felguard earlier, and the rest has gone into the collaboration with SI:7 these past few weeks. Galders might have a few extras, but… he's gone out again. I remember you used to have a lot of Soul Shards?"

"Gone," Jace said. "I haven't had many chances to let Morophil drain souls for me lately, and beast souls aren't enough to fuel powerful spells, so…"

"All right," Ursula said. "Sorry I can't be of more help."

"This is plenty," Jace said, giving the pouch a shake. "Well, good luck with everything," Ursula said.

Jace had originally wanted to scrounge some Soul Shards from the Dagger Society to see if he could make up for the mana shortfall in summoning Karlez, but now that was a dead end too.

Galders had been toying with the idea of summoning a Doomguard to sort out the Twisting Nether situation, so getting Soul Shards out of him was probably wishful thinking. And of course, Jace wasn't ready to let the Dagger Society crowd know he'd already summoned a Doomlord — so naturally he couldn't use that as an excuse to snatch Galders's Soul Shards either.

He'd put this matter aside for now. Helping Sola check out the temple came first.

At noon the next day, the group had agreed to meet near the gate where the Trade District opened into the Valley of Heroes. Jace arrived early and found Sola already waiting there.

She sat outside the little tavern where he and Grede used to frequent, the Staff of Domination cradled in her arms, already wrapped up early to look like a long, straight pole for carrying luggage. After all, the gem at the top of the Staff of Domination crackled with arcs of light — far too conspicuous. Jace had spotted it at a glance even in the Pyromancer's vault, let alone on the streets of Stormwind.

"Afternoon, Jace," Sola said with a wave.

"You're here early," Jace said.

"I didn't know what to bring," Sola said with a laugh. "I probably don't need to prepare anything, so I came a little quickly."

"What you need to carry is the rest of us — the living luggage," Jace said, patting the demon bag at his waist. "Plus two more I'm not sure count as 'alive.'"

"And most importantly, this time you're doing this for me," Sola said. "How could I possibly make you all wait?"

"Hah, who knows, Grede might have secretly taken a swamp job from the Fighters' Guild," Jace said.

Sola smiled. Jace cleared his throat, waited for a passerby to walk past and move on, then said, "There's something I wanted to ask you, Sola. Did you know the Kaldorei can use their Moonwells to purify cloth taken from demons?"

The green dragon's eyes went wide in apparent shock. "How do you know about that?"

"I… uh, a tailor told me," Jace covered quickly.

Sola nodded. "Humans seem to know more about the Kaldorei than I thought. What about it?"

"I was wondering — could your power achieve a similar effect?" Jace asked.

"You mean like a Moonwell, or just cleansing Felcloth?" Sola thought for a moment. "I don't think so. I have no experience with anything like that… and to me it's more like a legend too, Jace. Healing mortals and performing that kind of purification aren't the same thing. Cleansing Felcloth is practically removing the fel corruption from it. Even for the Kaldorei, it requires an extremely skilled craftswoman or a priestess of Elune. At least from what the stories say, Moonwell water is essential — even a green dragon would need it."

"Then we'd have to make a trip to Kalimdor?" Jace scratched his head. "Looks like this needs more long-term planning."

"There's a Moonwell on this continent too, Jace," Sola said. "You didn't know?"

"Where?" Jace asked.

"If I remember correctly, it should be near the Gurubashi Empire — an old volcanic crater left behind by a dead volcano, suffused with incredibly powerful natural magic," Sola said after a moment's thought. "Thousands of years ago, Kaldorei druids planted a seedling of Andrassil there, hoping to use the natural magic to grow the tree and nourish the region. But when Andrassil shattered, it affected the younger tree that hadn't yet grown. To repair the damage the druids had caused, my kin took possession of the place and linked it to the Emerald Dream to heal the wounds Andrassil had left behind. There should be a Moonwell the Kaldorei left there — at least as far as I know."

"I know the place you're talking about," Jace said. "It hasn't had anything to do with the Gurubashi Empire for centuries. The Kingdom of Stormwind took it over hundreds of years ago and drove the trolls out."

Damn it — there was a Moonwell this close? And Jace hadn't had the faintest idea…
