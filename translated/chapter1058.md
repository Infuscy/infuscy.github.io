## Chapter 1058: Scion of the Blood God

A deep tunnel. From the corridors outside, the troll howls were mixed with words like "Hakkar, Hakkari, Atal'ai" that Jace could make out.

Without needing to understand the troll tongue, Jace could guess these were surely fanatical believers howling their loyalty to the Blood God.

This time he ran at the front, following the spiral stairway all the way up. The sound of troll footsteps and thick claws striking the floor below and behind grew closer and closer.

After passing through a fractured, water-seeping wall, Sora waved her staff and summoned thorns that jostled each other as they extended, blocking the stairway solidly. They even extended downward to meet the trolls chasing up.

Before long, those furious howls turned into shrieks of pain, their words no longer making any sense.

Sora still basically had an elf's face at this point, but her pointed ears had drooped slightly, and her mouth had widened a bit compared to before. Her eyes darted back and forth like a giant lizard's as she observed the situation within the stairway.

The fingernails on the fingers gripping her staff had turned into brown-black claws. The areas on her arms that had just been corroded by shadow were now exposed as scales. A thick tail stretched out from under her robe, maintaining her balance as she ran.

She was no longer going to waste energy maintaining a flawless mortal appearance. After all, this wasn't Stormwind City, and it wasn't Everlook either.

Jace could even feel that if it weren't for this passage being genuinely difficult for a whelp to pass through with ease, she would have long since returned to her green dragon form to tear these Atal'ai apart with her own claws and vent her fury for her trapped kin. And this somewhat off-key current form at least helped release some of the foul mood she had nowhere to vent.

After drinking the Refined Wisdom Elixir, Sora's mana had clearly grown much stronger. She layered thick patches of thorns along the way, basically cutting off the possibility of trolls catching up from behind, allowing Jace's group to advance with peace of mind.

But when they encountered dream barriers blocking the way, it was still clear that dispelling these magics, sustained and channeled by God-knows-how-many Atal'ai priestesses, cost her quite a bit of effort.

So they had to break the guidance of the Atal'ai priestesses as soon as possible. After all, even these small barriers in the corridors were this powerful, and aside from Sora, the others were completely helpless — who knew how powerful the barrier magic protecting Jammal'an could be?

Jace had no desire to finally enter the Temple of Atal'Hakkar only to be helpless against the entire Atal'ai, forced to flee, or even trapped inside, draining away who knew how long.

At a fork in the road, Jace stole a glance inside, wanting to confirm whether this led to the ritual terraces where the priestesses were channeling. He hadn't expected that with this glance, a series of scale-rubbing sounds would come from the stairway higher up.

The sound of scales rubbing and colliding had almost reached him by the time it appeared — there was no process of it approaching from far to near. It was as if it had suddenly flashed right before his eyes, catching him completely off guard.

Jace tensed all over. He'd just turned his head when he found himself staring straight at a white cobra with a head even larger than a whelp's, stretching its neck toward him with its mouth wide open!

Vereesa reacted the fastest, firing an arrow straight through its sail-like flared neck. But perhaps she'd shot too hastily, not having time to empower the arrow's destructive force with arcane energy. Though it burrowed into the scales and sank in, it didn't cause much serious damage.

Seeing that the serpent was about to spew out who-knew-whether poisonous fog or acid, the moment Vereesa's arrow flew, Jace immediately pointed at its head and began chanting the Fear incantation.

This was the spell he was most familiar with and could chant the fastest, an effective way to control enemies. But this serpent's resistance to fear was far stronger than expected. It even withstood the Fear incantation empowered by Ulthalesh, letting out a hoarse hiss as if going mad, while spraying a massive mouthful of venom at them all at once...

This venom not only caused nerve-searing agony the moment it sprayed onto exposed skin and eyes, but the force of its spray was terrifyingly powerful too. It blasted Jace, who was closest, clean off his feet.

Jace felt his feet leave the ground. Instinctively he called upon shadow power to catch him in midair, using Shadowstep to shift sideways several meters and steady his footing. Though this avoided smashing hard into the wall or crashing to the ground, he still couldn't open his eyes once he steadied himself.

A string of giant serpent hisses and Morophil's Demonic cursing erupted in his ears. Then another spray of foul-smelling venom. Followed by Grede's roar. From the sound of boots he could tell he'd stepped forward several paces, then a heavy hammer striking something with a muffled bang.

Accompanied by a crackle of lightning, after smelling the scent of roasted meat, a surging current of air carrying the fragrance of grass and flowers washed across Jace's face. The things before his eyes also rapidly became clear.

In that instant, under the combined effect of the temple's oppressive nightmare power and the lingering pain of the venom, the fragrance of grass and flowers even gave Jace the illusion that he'd returned to Quel'Thalas for a moment.

A powerful hand grabbed his jaw. Vereesa shook his head, shouting, "Jace, Jace?!"

Jace was shaken back to reality in an instant. He coughed. The hot blood throughout his body grew even more feverish. This was the Dragonqueen's blessing power doing its best to repair him, burning away the pain, and the illusions and drowsiness that had regained the upper hand due to his body's weakness.

He spat out a mouthful of bloody saliva and looked ahead. The giant serpent had been crushed by Grede, its skull smashed as it lay crooked on the ground, but its upper body was still twisting incessantly.

Sora had already dispelled the toxicity of the venom on Jace and Vereesa's side. The venom staining their clothes and skin, as well as splattered on the ground, had all lost their frightening yellow-green color, turning into a grayish, dried, pale-colored mucus.

Not far off, Morophil seemed to have been hit by something too. She leaned against the wall, somewhat weakly, watching this side.

Though there wasn't much resentment in her eyes, Jace knew this definitely wasn't because she'd been clumsy and hurt herself. She'd been at the very back of the group with Sora, far away from him just now. This was definitely because the Soul Link had helped him share part of the damage.

"It's been a long time since we last drank the potion. Shouldn't we drink again?" Vereesa supported Jace's eyes with her hand as she said, "Including the Nature Protection Potion. Damn it... Jace, there's blood in your eyes. You all right?"

Had to be said, the force with which this serpent had just sprayed its venom was practically comparable to a high-pressure water gun. Setting aside the toxicity, just being blasted head-on like that made Jace feel like he'd taken a punch from Grede. What the hell kind of freakish thing was this?

But Vereesa did have a point. Wearing the Chain of Will for a long time had indeed made him somewhat neglectful of mental pollution, illusion erosion, and the like. Once he let his guard down, he could feel something was wrong.

Seeing that Jace was fine, Vereesa raised her bow and walked down several steps, guarding against anyone breaking through the thorns from behind at this moment. Jace drank his potion again, pulling out Ursula's Nature Protection Potion. He looked over toward Grede's side as he did.

"Hey, you lot come look at this."

In the darkness, Grede, illuminated by the afterimage of the lightning-ignited wall, waved. "This damn thing... isn't a normal serpent."

Vereesa looked back. Sora also walked over. Jace forced his eyes to blink once, looked carefully, and only then discovered that the back of the serpent's skull, smashed by Grede, had actually grown many white-based, colorful feathers far thicker than a man's arm.

When it had suddenly appeared just now, the skin it had spread open like a cobra's wasn't its neck, but... a pair of broad wings. This was a wind serpent — a Son of Hakkar.

Jace understood why he'd heard absolutely nothing before this "serpent" pounced right before his eyes. Because it hadn't crawled over at all — it had flown!
