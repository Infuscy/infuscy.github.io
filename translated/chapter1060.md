## Chapter 1060: The Awakened Legion

"I've never felt it this strongly... his powerlessness," Sora said in a low voice, following behind Grede. "Those dragonkin, those dragon-blooded descendants who should have been at his command, and even the green dragons — not a single member of the Emerald dragonflight is willing to obey his orders anymore. And his voice, his call, should have been heard by every single one of my kin throughout all of Azeroth through the Emerald Dream. But it never has... It's been a very, very long time since I last heard his voice from outside."

"At least this means Eranikus can still be saved, Sora," Jace said, keeping a wary eye on a tunnel branching off to the side.

A sharp cry of a whelp tore through Jace's words. The sound rapidly pulled from one end of a wall to the other, and everyone's attention swept across the wall along with it. Clearly, the whelps already knew roughly where they were.

They couldn't jump directly off the terrace. From the terrace to the Sacrificial Pool was a drop of about three stories. Even for Vereesa, jumping down directly wouldn't be much of a problem. But once down, it meant being directly surrounded by countless dragonkin taking orders from the trolls, while also facing aerial attacks from green dragons appearing at any moment...

They had to turn back into the corridors to avoid the fury of the green dragonflight before seeking another opportunity to hunt down the Atal'ai leader — none other than the Prophet Jammal'an.

But now, Jace could no longer hear that voice which had been commanding the trolls all along as if chanting incantations. His voice had long since been drowned out by the howls of dragons, trolls, and other monsters nearby.

"Why?" Sora asked. "Prince Eranikus has clearly already..."

"You can understand some of the Atal'ai trolls' language, Sora," Jace said. "You just told us — those trolls believe the dragons are about to lose control... This means at least they haven't completely brought them under control yet. As for the other dragonkin, the green dragons, they've basically become the trolls' puppets. This is precisely why they don't obey Eranikus's orders. And you... you're at least still under his influence. He can awaken the dragon blood in you, Sora. This means he still holds some power over the Emerald Dream."

This wasn't something Jace had made up specifically to comfort Sora. Eranikus really might not have fallen beyond saving.

At that moment, a troll's howl suddenly came from ahead. Looking forward, at the end of the corridor, a troll axeman holding a torch was pointing at them and shouting something.

"Damn it." Vereesa cursed under her breath, raising her bow to nock an arrow. But before the arrow could fly, a tall golden figure stomped the axeman underfoot.

The arrow flew out and bounced off layer after layer of golden plate armor. The giant figure turned its head, its green lizard visage staring fixedly at them.

This tall dragonkin guardian gripped a golden war blade. Its proud bearing and gleaming armor made it hard to see it as a protector of some terrifying evil god.

"You have... awakened... the Sleeper."

The dragonkin murmured in Draconic. Before long, several slightly shorter dragonkin materialized from beside it.

"Stand aside!" Sora suddenly stepped forward and shouted sternly. "Clear the way! You traitors, scum... who gave you the nerve to speak to me like that!"

Hearing Sora's voice, those dragonkin were clearly startled. Even the dragonkin guardian in the center hesitated. Grede said skeptically, "They're still willing to listen to you?"

Sora didn't respond, only glaring at those dragonkin. Even Jace hadn't expected her words to actually work. Perhaps their blood made them unable to disobey the commands of a dragon master, just as Sora couldn't help but submit to Eranikus's voice...

The dragonkin didn't respond immediately. Though they didn't clear the way, they didn't make any further moves either.

Sora gestured for everyone to quickly pass by from the side. Who would've thought before they'd gone far, the dragonkin guardian suddenly said in Draconic, "I can't sense your connection to the Emerald Dream, you cunning little thing. You're nothing but a shapeshifter disguised as an Emerald dragon."

"What?!" These words gave Sora a shock. Anger, suspicion, and surprise all surged into her heart at once. But before she could retort, the leading dragonkin guardian suddenly raised its war blade, lifted its forelegs, and leaped fiercely toward them!

Even Vereesa couldn't match the speed of these four-legged beasts, even wearing heavy armor. Watching the dragonkin guardian and its several followers catch up in just a few strides, the people here had no choice but to turn and fight.

"Demon!"

The dragonkin guardian shouted and chopped down. Morophil, at the very back, dodged the strike sideways, leaped up, and drove her dagger hard into the guardian's shoulder, making the whole creature's body sway.

Seizing this opportunity, Grede — without even transforming — leaped up and smashed the fellow's head hard. This strike dented the dragonkin's helmet, and blood poured forth immediately. But this tall fellow didn't fall immediately. Instead, he grabbed Grede, trying to lift him up.

But though Grede was short, he clearly wasn't as light as the fellow had imagined. Instead, he grabbed the fellow's hand, and the two of them inexplicably started a contest of strength.

Jace had just finished chanting the Shadow Bolt incantation and accurately hurled this shadow energy onto a dragonkin warrior who'd charged to the front. He wasn't wearing any armor. Struck in the chest by the Shadow Bolt, his ribs were corroded away. He coughed, tumbling into the wall with intermittent, piercing screams.

The remaining smaller dragonkin warriors were trapped in place by thorns Sora had summoned, breaking through the old wall. Two of them had already been shot through by several arrows and lay dead on the ground.

Taking advantage of the moment Grede and the dragonkin were locked in their tug-of-war, Morophil snatched one of the dragonkin war blades that had fallen to the ground, jumped onto the tall dragonkin guardian's head, and hacked down several times until she'd chopped the fellow's head clean off. Only then did this creature finally collapse obediently.

"Damn it." Grede pried open the dragonkin's fingers. "This fellow's strength is way too much."

"This isn't the time to rest, Grede!" Vereesa shouted. It was because another crowd of dragonkin had charged out from the corridor ahead.

The dragonkin in front hurled the short spear in their hand. Vereesa raised her hand and shot it mid-flight with an arrow, sending it flying askew into the wall with a clanging, grating noise.

The elf reached into her quiver for another arrow, felt something was off, and glanced at it — only to realize she'd already used more than half her arrows.

If it were simply to kill this crowd of dragonkin, or if these dragonkin had been chasing from behind, Jace could have summoned Shadowflame and burned straight over at this point. Even if it didn't kill them all at once, it wouldn't leave them much life.

But right now the dragonkin were charging toward the direction they needed to enter the Sacrificial Pool. In such a narrow corridor, Jace couldn't summon violent Shadowflame — the others would inevitably be caught in the blast too.

And Shadowflame that could ignite corpses would probably burn for a long time. Wanting to pass through this corridor, for Vereesa and Sora, it might be no better than just dealing with a few dragonkin.

Sora also faced the same awkward situation as him. It wasn't that she didn't command offensive magic. Jace clearly remembered that in Winterspring, to deal with the demons summoned by satyrs in those caves, she'd directly spewed a mass of toxic acid that burned those demons into crawling all over the ground.

But ever since getting familiar with them, Sora had stopped using that ability. After all, last time she'd nearly accidentally injured Grede and Jace.

And so, Vereesa drew the two sharp blades at her waist and charged up together with Grede and Morophil. Jace only used a few Corruption spells and Curses of Weakness to help disrupt those dragonkin's movements and strength, assisting them in quickly resolving the trouble.

But after these few dragonkin fell, the corridor once again rang with dragon claws scraping and metal armor plates colliding, rising higher and crowd after crowd. More and more powerful dragonkin guardians and warriors were coming. They'd clearly been summoned by the cries of their companions here.

Grede stepped on one of the dragonkin's corpses and sighed, glancing at the corridor entrance. The sounds of fighting ahead also rose and fell. Troll speech and Draconic howls mixed together.
