## Chapter 1063: Blood, Bones, Sacrifice

Jace rubbed his arm. "Their high priest isn't here. Let's move."

"Where is Prince Eranikus?" Sora asked.

"At least not here. Let's find him outside," Jace said.

The group nodded and left the troll corpses behind. What surprised Jace was that not a single one of the green dragonkin who'd been guarding this passage had come in to help Ogom.

Had they rushed off to protect Jammal'an?

But the moment they left the corridor and saw what had become of the Sacrificial Pool, Jace killed that line of thought.

Every last dragonkin had fallen. Even the whelps circling above the pool had dropped from the sky.

Piles upon piles of fresh corpses of every size lay strewn among the ancient bones — a sight so bizarre it made the blood run cold. Compared to the hulking bodies of the whelps and dragonkin guards, the little hatchling corpses seemed like a swath of dead insects, tossed aside without a second thought.

Sora froze at the sight, both hands trembling. And it wasn't just her — Jace couldn't help but swallow hard. Even if those dragonkin had been hunting them like mad just moments ago, this sudden, wholesale slaughter was enough to stop your heart.

And they shared Sora's blood.

"Those trolls…" Grede muttered through gritted teeth.

"They were sacrificed," Vereesa said. "Some kind of ritual… a summoning ritual."

"We have to find that high priest right now!" Jace looked around. "Any of you able to tell where the gong is coming from?" By now, more trolls had gathered on the terraces, but they made no move to pursue — just sat there staring down from above.

"I… seem to…"

Vereesa hesitated, as if reluctant to admit she couldn't quite place it. The troll howls swelled again. "A' Talak, Kagar, Dath, Zul, Dorg, Hakkari!"

The empty temple echoed with the droning chant, cut short by another gong blast.

Vereesa turned her head sharply — but toward the direction they'd come from, the great hall guarded by the Deathwalkers, the Atal'ai's former "sanctuary."

In truth, the gong seemed to come from every direction at once. Forget Jace — even Vereesa and Grede couldn't pin it down.

But a summoning ritual…

Jace remembered where it had started. He should remember.

He swept his eyes across the Sacrificial Pool and spotted the entrance to a tall, broad gallery in the distance. "Let's check inside there."

"Blood…"

Sora's murmur pulled Jace's stepping foot back. "What blood?" Jace asked.

Vereesa turned as well, following Sora's gaze downward — to the floor, to the ground of the entire Sacrificial Pool. "The blood. It's all gone."

Yes — Jace saw it now. The thick blood that had covered the floor of the entire pool, that had been flowing in lazy streams… all of it, gone.

Boom.

A gong blast shuddered through the silent hall once more, and Jace's attention snapped to the circular hole at the very center of the pool.

"Below. It's coming from below!" Jace shouted. "The gong is coming from below!"

The group immediately ran toward the dried basin of the Sacrificial Pool, vaulting over piles of bones and corpses. Just as they neared the hole, another gong struck.

The air seemed to congeal. Jace felt himself running harder and harder, his chest tightening, his ears filled with nothing but the trolls' chanting.

"Hakkari, Hakkari, Hakkari…"

Then, without warning, a hideous hum erupted before him. Bones speared straight up out of the hole. The Shadow shockwave it rode on sent Grede, who was closest, flying backward; Jace and Vereesa were blasted off their feet and slammed to the ground.

A chain of bones soared into the air above the pool, unfurling into the shape of a bone dragon's vast wings. Every green dragon corpse in the hall exploded in rapid succession at the appearance of this abomination.

A mist of blood, shards of bone, and waves of Shadow energy rippled through the Sacrificial Pool. The ground quaked and split. Everything thrown up by the exploding bodies rained upward, converging on the skeletal horror circling overhead.

"Attack it now!" Jace shouted back to his feet, though his voice probably carried nowhere in the chaos at the heart of the pool.

He didn't need to tell Vereesa twice. She'd already loosed the first arrow — but the shaft, trailing arcs of arcane light, shattered the moment it touched the gray-white bones. The arcane glow was swallowed by a dark barrier in an instant, as if it had done nothing at all.

Amid the storm of spinning bone fragments, Jace raised Ursales and hurled a immensely powerful Shadowfire orb. When the blast struck the serpentine horror looming over everything in the pool, it didn't even detonate properly.

It wasn't like hitting something with high Shadow resistance. If Jace had to describe it, it was more like… he'd missed. As if some strange plane had devoured it, dissolving the force of his spell entirely.

Before Jace could figure out what had happened to his magic, the arcs of lightning crackling from Grede's war hammer sank into the thing like stones into water — completely useless. Even worse, once the lightning drained away, the hammer lost the power to fly back to its owner, forcing Grede to sprint over and catch the falling weapon as best he could.

"In my temple… fighting over my souls…"

Jace felt a heavy, unfamiliar male voice settle into his heart, spreading wave after wave of rippling dread.

The abomination, now woven from every dragonkin corpse and all their blood, had become a flying blood serpent. Its enormous serpent head was nearly indistinguishable from a darkened, corrupted dragon — that blank, vacant gaze sweeping slowly over everything below, jaw trembling as though speaking, yet producing no sound at all.

The hollow hum it gave off in flight swallowed the trolls' chanting whole. Another heavy blow struck Jace in the chest.

How… dare…

Wind serpents of every size poured from the terraces and galleries overhead, screeching and howling. Vereesa raised her bow and shot down the lowest flyer, then drew a second — but the nearly immovable horror at the center let out a savage shriek, and Jace felt as though someone had ripped a string out of his brain. The pain nearly dropped him.

Vereesa cried out and hit the ground. Jace watched three or four wind serpents, venom dripping from their mouths, hurtle straight for her. He raised Ursales through the pain and tried to incinerate the creatures with a burst of flame — when a shadow struck from afar and swallowed his every sense.

That blood-soaked, hideous serpent head was suddenly right in his face, nose to nose. No matter which way he turned, no matter how he dodged, he couldn't escape it.

I will take everything from you.

The voice repeated, cold and flat: "I will take everything from you."

Just as Jace clenched his teeth against a psychic pressure so immense it made his heart quake, an icy cyclone came howling from nowhere and blew the serpent's phantom apart. Then a green shadow swept over Jace's head.

The young green dragon smashed through several of Hakkar's brood in the air, then dove to Vereesa's side and tore apart the nearest giant wind serpent with claw and fang.

Somaleer had reverted to her true form at some point. She flicked a glance at the colossal, winged bone horror hovering at the center but did not attack it, instead continuing to strike at the wind serpents scattering in every direction.

Under the protection of her nature magic, Vereesa and Grede shook off the serpents' harassment and joined the hunt, which made Somaleer the focus of nearly every wind serpent's assault.

Which meant now was the perfect moment to channel something powerful. Hand of Gul'dan… should he use the Hand of Gul'dan?

No. Impossible.

Jace thought of the souls that had been drained away without a sound. The terrifying evil before him possessed an absolutely unchallengeable power over souls. Not only could he never summon the dark star from gathered souls in its presence — even if he'd drawn on Ursales' soul power to bring it forth, it probably wouldn't have done much more than that last Shadowfire blast.

Because just as it had said — this was its temple, its domain… the avatar of Hakkar…
