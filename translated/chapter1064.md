## Chapter 1064: Soul Snatcher

Jace had fought him in the game before. To force it into manifesting, you had to shatter its domain first — you had to destroy the things in this temple that sheltered the avatar, namely the flames in several soul braziers.

But in the game, it had never been this terrifying. It hadn't even been summoned from the center of the Sacrificial Pool, but from a small side room.

Those braziers had been tucked into the four corners of that room… and you only needed to douse them with the blood of Hakkar's brood, the Blood Guardians, to force Hakkar's avatar into the real world.

Now Hakkar's brood filled the sky, and no shortage of powerful guardians among them — but there were no braziers to be seen anywhere. Could the "soul flames" be hidden in some deep corner of the temple?

No. Jace could feel it through his connection to Shadow and soul power — this avatar had an intensely strong link to something within the Sacrificial Pool.

"How the hell do we fight it?! These serpents just keep coming!"

Grede roared, his voice carrying an oppression and darkness Jace had never heard from him before — pain and torment. Jace had never heard those in the dwarf's voice.

Though Hakkar's avatar merely hovered at the center of the hall, it was using psychic magic to torture everything within it every single moment. Not just the four of them — even the trolls up on the terraces occasionally howled and plummeted over the edge…

Morophil walked up to Jace, her blade in hand. She was drenched in wind serpent blood.

"They won't last much longer, master. We have to figure something out — or… find a way to run."

"Run? Run where?! Can you not feel the domain of this blood avatar, succubus?!" Sazeno shrieked. "Blood…"

Jace murmured the word under his breath, forcing himself to stay calm. Then he suddenly recalled a scene he'd deliberately pushed out of his mind. When they'd entered the Sacrificial Pool — the strange ghostly flames he'd witnessed inside Ysera's earring.

Could those ghostly flames be real? Merely hidden by the dream?

No — those were just phantoms born of the dream's distortion. There was no way Hakkar would wield green dragon power to hide the soul flames that sustained his undead form within the plane of the Emerald Dream.

Wait — this avatar had been fully summoned by draining the green dragons' blood, called into being by Jammal'an. It wielding the Emerald dragonflight's dream power was no surprise at all. Even the trolls had been able to weave dreams from green dragon blood to block their path…

And even setting all that aside, there was no other option left right now.

"Give me a knife, Morophil." Jace held out his hand. "A bloody one."

The succubus handed one over without a question. Jace immediately ran toward Vereesa. "I need your earring, Vere! Give me the earring!"

"What?" Vereesa turned, at first looking as though she'd misheard. But she broke free from a Hakkar broodling's entanglement, unhooked the earring, and tossed it over.

The moment Jace clutched it in his hand, he channeled his power into it. The dream's haze swallowed everything in an instant — the hiss of wind serpents, the piercing hum in his mind, Hakkar's murmuring, all of it vanished. Only the empty, echoing Sacrificial Pool remained, and…

Six drifting ghostly flames ringing the entire pool. "Six… six…"

Jace immediately grabbed the knife and ran for one of them. He glanced at the blade to make sure blood would still drip from it — and his heart lurched.

The blood on the knife had vanished entirely with the coming of the dream!

In fact, throughout the Sacrificial Pool, the wind serpent corpses, the blood that had flowed down — all of it blurred and indistinct beneath the dream's haze. Jace could feel the sticky wetness, but he couldn't see the blood staining his fingers in this twisted dream.

The only blood he could see was the blood still seeping from the torn skin on his own arm, half-transformed earlier. Only his own blood could enter this layer of dream along with the earring. The rest… seemed unable to.

But his blood, though blessed by the Red Dragonqueen, wasn't Hakkar's blood either. How could it douse these soul flames? He ran to a floating flame, dipped his own blood, and flicked it over — nothing happened.

No… there was still one way.

Jace dropped the dream's veil and returned to reality. A wind serpent suddenly swooped over his head. The green dragon was still battling serpents in the air. Somaleer, bound by the Chain of Will, was clearly more at ease than Grede and Vereesa — even relying on a whelp's raw strength and venomous breath, she could hold her own against several wind serpents without falling behind.

But Jace hadn't come back to help her.

He locked onto a dark-blue wind serpent barreling toward him from afar and hit it with a Suffocation spell, shadow chains seizing its wing and yanking it crashing to the ground.

"Elaman, Silondor!"

Jace shouted the Life Drain incantation and, wielding Ursales, drained the poor creature down to a pale, shriveled husk in seconds.

A violent wave of nausea rolled through him from the inside out — but Jace was far too practiced with Life Drain to sneeze blood or vomit from it.

He caught his breath, then channeled the veil once more. Ysera's dream power was just absurdly strong — short back-and-forth trips barely degraded the spell's quality at all. The soul flame, which had replaced the clamorous pool, appeared clearly before him again.

Jace pulled out a knife, slit his wrist, and let his blood drip onto the soul flame. To his surprise, the purple fire was snuffed out like something doused in water, leaving only a wisp of shadowy black smoke.

"Good… good."

Jace couldn't help but grin, then glanced toward the other flames.

At that moment, the blurry colossal shadow at the center shifted. Its dark-brown, hazy skull fixed its gaze this way. Those originally muddled, vacant eyes suddenly blazed with a dizzying green light.

"You see me. In your domain, think of a way, Hakkar…" Jace murmured.

He had to admit — he'd already forgotten the cut on his arm, forgotten the vicious ringing in his ears, the agony like nails being driven through his skull. A thrill of having grabbed the bastard's tail surged through him.

But he'd barely taken a few steps before a massive, hazy green shadow crashed down before him, followed by countless wind serpents writhing like maggots as they swarmed over something, tearing at flesh…

"Jace, help… where did you go?!"

The green dragon's desperate howl echoed through the entire dream. Jace drew a long breath. Damn real, that was.

But he only glanced up at those hazy green lights overhead and kept running forward, reaching the second flame and dousing it with blood…

"Then the third." Jace frowned, shrugging off some Shadow magic that was pressing down on and gnawing at him. By now, the Sacrificial Pool no longer looked like the Sacrificial Pool. He was starting to not recognize this place.

After dousing the fourth flame, he caught sight of Grede's corpse lying not far off, torn to shreds by wind serpents. Vereesa had a bloody hole punched through her chest, half-kneeling at the edge of that central hole, blood streaming down into its depths to merge with the green dragons' blood.

"Very interesting." Jace nodded, then doused the fifth flame. That was when he saw, at the center of the grounds, the one who'd executed Vereesa Windrunner.

A demon. A blood-red devil spreading its wings like a Nathrezim, gripping a crimson trident as it hovered at the center of the grounds, staring straight at Jace step by step, eyes burning with emerald fel fire.

"Jace Sesso, don't keep going down the wrong path. You've already…"

She chanted in Demonic, her voice so familiar it sounded like his own.

"You know I'm Jace Sesso." Jace looked up. "So don't waste your time with this trick. Stop… prying into my mind, Soul Snatcher. And besides, you're not the real you."

The female devil let out a savage roar. Jace splashed his blood into the flame without hesitation. With a sharp hiss, the last flame was snuffed out. The entire dream began to spin violently, twisting and warping further.

Jace seized the earring and wrenched himself out of the veil. The skeletal horror before him thrashed wildly, its enormous tail suddenly whipping around and sending Jace flying several meters into a wall, where he crashed and tumbled down.

He coughed up a mouthful of blood, watching the very tail that had struck him shatter and spray a storm of bone fragments… "Kill it!" Jace screamed. "Now! Kill it!"

Hearing his voice, the elf, who'd been stabbing a wind serpent in the distance, snapped back to her senses. "How'd you get over there…"

Of course, she only muttered the words under her breath before looking where Jace was pointing.

Hakkar's avatar was no longer simply hovering at the center — it had joined the fight personally, lunging to bite at the green dragon in the sky. That enormous head, stitched together from bone fragments and wrapped in blood, was even larger than Somaleer's body. Jace's heart stopped a beat at the thought of those jaws closing — but Somaleer pulled away just in time.

Vereesa raised her bow without hesitation, nocked one of her last few arrows, and loosed it with an arcane incantation. The arrow struck that seemingly immovable skeletal body — and the whole thing shuddered. Bone fragments and blood showered from the sky. It didn't even have the strength left to chase the dragon!

"Grab me! Quick, grab me!"

Grede clearly sensed the shift in the tide. He shouted at the green dragon nonstop until Somaleer dove down and snatched him into the air. The dwarf, lightning hammer in hand, launched himself from the dragon's claws and came crashing down onto Hakkar's skull. A white flash swallowed the entire hall in an instant.

The sound of shattering, of splitting, a mist of blood, a storm of bones… Jace felt as though an entire building had collapsed before him. Bone fragments flew like shrapnel — one grazing his body was enough to make him curl up and clutch his head, unable to cry out.

Inside the dream's veil, going head-to-head against Hakkar's avatar with nothing but his own will — without the Chain of Will's protection — Jace admitted he'd been talking big.

He hadn't lost his mind entirely, but the psychic torment and Shadow corrosion had left him barely able to stand. He could only huddle against the wall, gasping.

Shouting that one warning to everyone had been the last of his strength. "Jace!"

Vereesa slid through the blood and bone dust on her knees, reaching him and grabbing his shoulder to keep him from slumping over. "What happened? How did you… where did you go just now?" she asked.

"Saved your lives, that's what." Jace managed a pained laugh, handed her the blood-soaked earring, then glanced down at her chest — at the spot where, inside the veil, a bloody hole had been punched through.

For a moment, he was disappointed in himself. He'd actually worried, even just a little, that it might be real. Shaking his head, Jace asked, "Is it dead?"

"At least… it looks dead." Vereesa smiled. "We won."

Grede was whooping in the distance, smashing the head off a dying Hakkar broodling with one swing of his hammer.

Somaleer was still circling high above, screeching at the Atal'ai trolls who hadn't yet fled — but her excitement was about to overflow.

Morophil stood not far off, leaning against the wall. She'd absorbed no small share of Jace's suffering, and her expression was clearly strained. Jace met her eyes; she glared at him with displeasure. Jace pressed his lips together and nodded slowly. Only then did some of the tension leave her face, and the corner of her mouth curled up.

But none of them was the most excited.

The most excited, the most satisfied, was the staff at Jace's side. The empty Temple of Atal'Hakkar no longer held the faintest whisper of a soul. The soul essence sacrificed to awaken Hakkar's avatar had all been gobbled up by this demon bone club when no one was looking — after all, this time there was nothing to compete with it.

Jace could even hear its unhinged, quiet giggling, like an idiot cackling behind someone's back.
