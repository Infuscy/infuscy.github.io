## Chapter 1066: The Essence Pool

The deeper they walked into this broad gallery, the more the thrill of having just crushed a terrifying god-avatar faded.

Grede gripped his war hammer with both hands, eyes wide, staring hard at the snake-shaped reliefs rearing across the pitch-black walls. From where they'd entered — the Sacrificial Pool — the light of the braziers had more or less reached here, but its glow was all but spent.

Vereesa carried her bow with an arrow already nocked — her fourth-to-last — her steps growing ever more cautious.

Jace held his belly and brought up the rear. Even after downing one of Grede's Strong Healing Potions, he still didn't feel like the ordeal in the Sacrificial Pool had fully mended. Maybe damage to the mind and spirit wasn't something that healed so easily.

"You're sure that Soulflayer's summoning ritual is broken — the green dragon leading this place will…" Grede muttered. Vereesa picked up his thought: "Prince Eranikus."

"Right, Eranikus." Grede said. "Will he regain his senses? Judging by what he was screaming at us earlier…"

"You're only thinking about that now?" Jace said. "I thought you didn't care much a minute ago."

"Mainly you lot gave me the impression that once we dealt with the summoning ritual, everything would be fine," Grede said.

Vereesa walked as she spoke, her voice soft. "If Hakkar's power corrupted the green dragons, then as that power vanishes entirely, if Eranikus is still alive, he should regain his senses, right?"

She glanced at Sora beside her as if seeking agreement, but Sora said nothing — she only shook her head slowly, unsure whether she was disagreeing with the guess or simply didn't know.

More likely, she just couldn't bring herself to believe Eranikus was still alive.

Defeating Hakkar's avatar was exhilarating, but that victory hadn't erased a single green dragon corpse from the temple.

The blood, the shattered bones, the thick stench of it throughout the temple, and that faint, unaggressive floral scent unique to green dragon scales.

Frankly, in a temple this corrupted, a fragrance that should have been refreshing smelled instead like the weird, gut-twisting stink of crushed locusts.

Jace could hardly imagine what it was like for Sora to walk through all this.

"I need to top up that troll witch-doctor potion, make sure I'm still sane," Grede muttered, pulling out his potion pouch.

"That potion is for countering the Hakkar priests' magic, Grede," Jace reminded him. "What about countering green dragon magic?"

"Nature Protection Potion," Jace answered.

"Damn it." Grede coughed. "I'm guessing this green dragon lord's magic isn't limited to spitting poison, huh?"

"Shh." Vereesa suddenly raised a finger to her lips.

"See the light?" She squinted ahead. Jace peered forward too, but he saw nothing at all.

"That's…"

Sora's eyes widened in surprise, and she broke into a run.

The others followed her. Rounding a corner, an enormous, grand chamber appeared before them. It was unquestionably part of the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, but in terms of overall atmosphere, it didn't feel like a troll temple.

Jace couldn't point to anything specific — maybe the arcane ambiance here was very different from outside.

The chamber held the skeletons of many dead green dragons. Deep within stood a rather beautiful jade watershrine, wildly out of place among the dark, sinister troll-and-serpent reliefs on the surrounding walls.

And what lit this hall was the water in that jade watershrine. It wasn't all that bright, but even this faint source of light was enough to give shape and form to a space that should have been pitch black.

"Essence Pool!" Sora said excitedly. "Even with an evil god's power dominating the entire temple, the Essence Pool has kept its true form!"

She hurried down the steps toward the watershrine. Jace watched her back and remembered where this was.

This was the hall guarded by the Shadow of Eranikus in the game. Jace felt it differed quite a bit from the game — not this complex, no steps here — but the green dragon skeletons, and that watershrine… it was enough to confirm things.

But the Shadow of Eranikus wasn't here. Where had it gone?

Watching Sora draw closer and closer to the watershrine, Jace felt something was off. That watershrine… wait.

"Sora!" Jace shouted. "Stop — don't touch it!"

Sora had nearly reached out to touch the water's surface. She jerked her hand back at Jace's yell and turned around. "Trust me — the water inside isn't as pure as you think," Jace said.

He remembered that in the game, Eranikus had stored a portion of his uncorrupted mind inside a gem. He'd asked the adventurer who retrieved it to purify the gem using the water in this watershrine to save him — but the water, too, had long been corrupted, and Eranikus had been swallowed deeper by the Nightmare as a result.

But when Jace reached the watershrine and looked closely at the water, he couldn't see anything wrong with it. Depart from the holy spring, demon worshipper!

An explosive roar thundered through the hall. Jace spun around. The light of the Essence Pool was suddenly swallowed by a layer of shadow. Enormous black veils fanned across the ceiling, like vast…

Dragon wings.

"Get away from there, Jace!" Vereesa shouted, running this way. At the same time, Jace felt every shadow in the chamber converging on him, rapidly coalescing into a savage, indistinct maw, flanked by two silver wicked eyes trailing sharp light as they lunged at him —

His weakened body couldn't even manage a quick dash, let alone a Shadow-step to dodge. At the last instant, Vereesa reached him, wrapped her arms around him, and tackled him to one side. Shadow flooded his vision. The dark mass made the dying glimmer of the Essence Pool flicker in and out of sight.

"Father!" Sora stood in front of Vereesa and Jace, shouting at the top of her lungs. "You're hurting allies who came to help the green dragonflight! They crushed the Atal'ai's ambition to summon Hakkar once more!"

—No… it was me… it was me! I destroyed the Atal'ai's ritual. My legion tore apart Hakkar's bones. I devoured Hakkar's blood. It was I who shattered Hakkar's dream of returning to this world!

—Don't try to deceive me, you lowly worms who twist minds… release my child!

The roar of shadow nearly burst Jace's chest. A layer of darkness swept past, snuffing the faint light on every wall once more… and with it came a blast of magic that sent Sora and the succubus running toward Jace flying backward. Vereesa and Jace were blown into a corner of the hall. There was nothing any of them could do to stop it — after all, how do you hurt a shadow?!

"Stop cowering!"

Grede bellowed, trying to track where that layer of shadow had flown. The lightning blazing from his war hammer suddenly lit the entire chamber.

The lightning and the shadow wrestled over the border between light and dark — but it was precisely in that instant, when light and shadow were so sharply divided, that Jace and the others finally saw the nightmare's emerald shadow for what it was…

"Look at this, Prince Eranikus!"

Vereesa scrambled to her feet, holding high the bloodstained earring.

The moment she spoke, the murderous shadow that had been bearing down on them vanished without a trace. The light from Skybreaker seized the upper hand in an instant, illuminating the hall so brightly it hurt the eyes.

But the light faded quickly enough — Grede had spent no small amount of energy in an unbroken string of battles against trolls and Hakkar's avatar, let alone holding his own against a giant dragon's shadow. By now he seemed spent too.

The hall fell dark again, returning to silence, leaving only the faint glow of the Essence Pool.

Vereesa simply held the earring high, alert for anything that might appear.

That was when Jace, sitting by the watershrine, noticed a layer of shadow on the ground slowly shifting. The darkness crept along bit by bit, gradually taking the shape of a great dragon.

Its shadow on the ground inched toward Vereesa's shadow — or more precisely, toward the shadow of the emerald earring her shadow held aloft.

Soon Vereesa noticed the change on the ground too, lowering her head carefully.

Grede moved closer as well. Jace shook his head, signaling that they shouldn't try anything just yet. He could feel that the emotions radiating from this shadow had shifted considerably — most importantly, they'd grown much calmer.

When the shadow's claw on the ground touched the earring, Vereesa's arm trembled. Then the entire hall brightened once more, as though a heavy, gloomy curtain had been drawn back.

A long while passed with no sign of Eranikus's voice. Jace, Vereesa, and even Sora, who'd just managed to sit up, all waited in silence for whatever reaction might come — but in the end, nothing appeared.

"What's that?" Grede suddenly asked.

Everyone followed his gaze. At some point, a gem had appeared in the center of the hall — a green gem about the size of a fist.
