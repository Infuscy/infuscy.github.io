## Chapter 1081: A Necessary Test

"Indeed," Jace said. "I actually went to take a look at the Blackmoor this time, since there were rumors of dragons deep in the marsh before. I stopped at Stonard along the way — the Alliance commander there knows about this."

"Captain Waldemar," Varian said the name without a moment's hesitation, then asked, "So what exactly is going on there?"

Jace was a little surprised that Varian knew the situation at Stonard so well — but then again, that was perfectly normal.

Before the Dark Portal opened, even though the Blackmoor hadn't been fully explored or controlled, at least nominally it counted as part of the Stormwind Kingdom's territory. Now, although nominally it was Alliance-controlled territory, in a way it had become a Lordaeronian enclave. He hadn't expected this young king to still care so much about the matter.

"I did find some dragon bones already dead and tainted by dark magic," Jace said. "But the place was steeped in evil magic. I suspect it may be connected to troll corruption rituals or the demons of the Dark Portal. In any case, the Stonard patrol's decision not to venture deep and explore was wise enough."

Jace had no intention of telling Varian everything that had happened in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar — mainly because the fact that Uthales had devoured all the troll evil spirits inside made him feel guilty.

If Varian and the Wizard Sanctum learned about the situation in the Atal'Hakkar, the Kirin Tor absolutely wouldn't pass up the chance to rush into the temple for a "salvage excavation," and their speed would definitely be faster than the Wizard Sanctum or any other mage organization.

Considering Uthales's reputation with the Kirin Tor and even the Order of Tirisfal, he was a little worried the Kirin Tor's in-depth research might expose something.

Once Eranikus regained his physical form and returned to the temple for purification, and the green dragons reoccupied the place, it wouldn't be too late to explain the situation — by then the Kirin Tor wouldn't be able to insert themselves at all. Anyway, those corrupted green dragons and the Atal'ai could no longer pose any threat to Stonard or Duskwood.

Varian lowered his head. "I thought Lordaeron occupying that place would at least deal with the troubles there properly. But in any case, it's good news — hearing that a hostile dragon is dead is better than anything."

"How is the queen, Your Majesty?" Jace asked.

At the mention of Queen Tiffin, Varian visibly brightened. "Your guess was completely accurate, Jace — that black dragon must have been too wary of Stormwind's ability to detect disguisers, so she didn't dare risk using magic to teleport Tiffeny off to some god-knows-where, and instead relied on those…"

He glanced toward the entrance to the Park and lowered his voice. "Right now it looks like the Lescovar family's people, plus Elgadin, and even the Allian family were all involved in this abominable kidnapping. But SI:7 is still investigating further — because too many families are involved, plus some foreigners, and now that the investigation's progress has been exposed, the difficulty of the investigation has gone up as well."

"So it really is connected to dark magic?" Jace said. "If the black dragon did use mental magic to control them, maybe I can help with something."

"Damn the dark magic!" Varian moved closer and said in a low, clenched voice. "These traitors did it purely for money, or for the Kingdom of Alterac's interests… damn it, I hate to admit that some of them simply wanted to curry favor with that Lady Katrana Prestor!"

He took a breath, then said coldly, "Look at it — a shape-shifting black dragon wormed her way into this city, and without even using any magic had the entire royal council and the lordships spinning in circles, myself included — I'm one of the idiots too."

Jace gave a soft sigh. He'd been tricked in circles just as thoroughly. Because of the memories of his past life, right up to the end he'd firmly believed that Katrana Prestor was the real black dragon princess — completely forgetting that even for a juvenile dragon like Sola, changing appearance was a trivial matter.

"Anyway, Tiffeny is back," Varian said. "How is the queen's condition?"

"She's gotten a lot thinner," Varian said. "But overall much better than I expected. Those kidnappers lost their backing and didn't dare mistreat her too badly. Thank God the black dragon was forced to reveal herself — otherwise, if that evil dragon had been given enough time to arrange an escape, she definitely wouldn't have survived."

After saying that, the king thought for a moment and fell silent.

Jace looked at his expression and could more or less guess what he was thinking — whether the recovered Queen Tiffin might also be fake. The two of them looked at each other for a while, and in the end it was Varian who couldn't help but say quietly, "So, could she really… be?"

"I don't think so," Jace shook his head. "For one thing, that black dragon is already badly injured, Your Majesty. If she came back the same way, her control over all of Stormwind would be far less than before, and our vigilance is much stronger than it was — the cost-benefit ratio of doing that is too low."

"What about the other black dragons?" Varian asked.

"If you're really worried," Jace said, "I've kept an enchanted metal plate here that can expose black dragon magic, Your Majesty — crafted by Master Krasus. Lady Katrana Prestor was freed from the black dragon's control by the same spell.

"As long as you use the spell on this plate in front of the queen, whether she's being controlled by a black dragon or transformed by one, it'll be exposed."

"What if there's no problem — what effect would the spell have?" Varian asked.

"No effect. This spell is specifically targeted at black dragons and won't affect ordinary people." Jace said. The king looked at a guard passing by in the corridor and asked quietly, "Then… when can we arrange it?"

"Very soon — the plate is right at my place, Your Majesty," Jace said.

"Tonight then." Varian nodded. "It'd be best if you could bring that friend of yours who can conjure that powerful sleep cloud, Jace. I don't want her to have barely escaped death with her body still not recovered, only for me to test her with this powerful spell… but you understand it's still necessary."

"I understand," Jace said.

In the afternoon, Jace went to the Mage Quarter to wake Sola and brought her to Stormwind Keep.

Actually, seeing the queen, Jace was still a bit nervous — after all, the last time they'd met, Queen Tiffin had been like a demon queen walking straight out of hell, and the triumphant look on her face when she nearly crushed Krasus to pieces was still burned into his memory.

Arriving at the queen's resting quarters, waiting in the corridor outside, Jace carefully sensed every trace of emotion bringing shadow aura into this place, wanting to see if there was anything similar to the feeling he'd had last time he'd stumbled upon Varian and the queen strolling in the garden.

Recalling that countless anxious, chaotic emotions wanting to break free of their cages, and considering their origin — clutches of unhatched black hatchlings, right there in that seemingly frail queen's belly — it really made his scalp tingle.

Fortunately, this time he didn't sense any clear negative emotions — only a trace of tension from King Varian, and more obvious, the nervousness and unease from Sola beside him. She looked extremely calm on the surface, but inside she was nearly scared to death.

"So this is…" Varian looked at Sola. "The Lady Green Dragon."

"Yes." Jace nodded.

"A pleasure to meet you again, my lady." Varian said respectfully. "The pleasure is mine, Your Majesty." Sola bowed slightly.

In Jace's eyes, the two of them looked like they were politely greeting each other, but in reality they were both relying on meaningless small talk to steady their nerves.

Varian gently pushed open the half-closed door. The queen was resting inside.

In Jace's impression, the queen had always been a quite healthy, vigorous figure — more like someone raised in a farmer's household than a frail noblewoman.

But at the moment her face was gaunt, her hair dry and withered — clearly she'd been through no small amount of torment.

Actually, compared to physical pain, mental torture was far more terrifying. At least Jace could hardly imagine this situation — being locked up, while outside there was a terrifying hag who'd taken your place, occupying your husband, your family, everything…

Varian looked at Sola, signaling that she could begin. Sora reached out and softly recited a spell. After the green dragon's magic took effect, the queen's tightly locked brows gradually relaxed, her whole person looking much more at ease, and Varian's worried expression eased as well.

Jace took out the metal plate inscribed by Krasus with chromatic dragon blood and was just about to begin reading the spell when Varian stopped him. "Are you certain this spell won't harm ordinary people, Jace? This sounds like a rather powerful magic… and Lady Katrana Prestor still hasn't fully recovered…"

"That's the after-effects of the black dragon's magical torment, not caused by this spell, Your Majesty," Jace said. "Rest assured — the creator of this spell is a senior scholar of the Kirin Tor. To achieve his revenge against his nemesis, the black dragons, he made this spell extremely precise and ingenious."

Varian nodded slowly, and Jace began to chant the spell.

Frankly, by this point his heart was already in his throat — he'd even considered whether he should have called a few more people to stand around ready to fight at any moment, even though no way the queen in front of him showed any sign of black dragon magic contamination…

Still, the spell was completed. A wave of magic fluctuation seeped out from the stone plate, but brought no reaction at all. The queen remained deep in sleep, and the room was deathly quiet.

After a moment, Varian looked at Jace and raised an eyebrow. "Done?"

"That's it," Jace whispered. "No problems at all, Your Majesty."

Varian clenched a fist, let out a long breath, walked around the bed, and threw his arms around Jace in a fierce hug, punching him hard on the back — so hard that Jace suddenly realized the injuries he'd sustained in the temple didn't seem to have fully healed yet.

"Thank you, Jace. Thank you."
