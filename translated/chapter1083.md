## Chapter 1083: Demon Armor, But Another Way

That afternoon, on his way home, as he reached the mouth of the alley Jace suddenly felt that something was off — as if that succubus scent from the Cathedral of Light had never really dissipated.

Actually, traces of this feeling had appeared the moment he set foot in the Trade District, but because those priests that morning had been able to smell something on him, he hadn't paid it much mind.

This time, though, he carefully sniffed his clothes, then sniffed the air, and confirmed that this smell wasn't Molophil's — a cloying sweetness with a tinge that turned Jace's stomach.

He scanned around with Detect Demon and suddenly caught sight of a shadow closing in slowly from behind. An unfamiliar succubus. What, had the warlocks from the Lighthouse come looking for revenge?

He pretended not to notice and kept walking, while his other hand fumbled at his waist, seizing the green dragon-claw dagger he carried on him, thinking that once the stranger got close he'd send her a one-way ticket back to the Twisting Nether. But just as he was figuring out how to deal with a succubus's whip, a figure suddenly stepped into his path ahead.

"Jace, hold on, don't..." It was Jalen Airuo.

"What's going on?" Jace asked.

Jalen glanced behind Jace and said, "That's my succubus minion, Jace. She was just... helping me wait for you here. When she saw you coming home, she was supposed to use Soul Link to let me know to come find you."

"How did you know I was back?" Jace asked.

"Ursula told me this morning — she said you'd been to the palace," Jalen said. "So I figured I'd come see you as soon as I could."

"So you had a succubus tail me?" Jace shoved the dagger back. "If you'd shown up a few minutes later, you'd have been looking at re-summoning a whole new one."

"Sorry, but... how are you able to spot a succubus's trail?" Jalen asked curiously. "I know some warlocks can use some kind of incantation to detect demons in the vicinity. Galders was actually trying to track down a spell like that, but hadn't had any luck yet — yet you seem able to sense her just with the naked eye..."

"Probably just subconscious instinct," Jace said offhandedly. "I've killed enough succubi, after all."

If Detect Demon were something he could teach, Jace would have told the Dagger Society bunch a long time ago.

Jace had actually considered telling Galders the whole story of how Teron Gorefiend had tried to replace his soul and seize his body, only to get devoured by Jace instead — and been honest about the powers it had brought. Whether it was Gorefiend's Grasp, or detecting invisibility, not to mention the further affinity for shadow power it had granted him...

But the dark magic involved — especially its connection to Gul'dan's soul-and-flesh rituals — made him feel completely out of his depth.

After all, this was far more dangerous and evil than summoning a succubus. And unlike summoning a succubus, the entire process hadn't even been something he could control.

Not to mention that for this he'd even contracted the "death shadow" curse that the Church of the Holy Light itself had been powerless to handle, and only managed to escape Teron Gorefiend's torment through one near-death ordeal after another, trading his way into these abilities in exchange.

How the hell was he supposed to tell them all that?

Jace pushed the thoughts aside. "Honestly, there was no need for you to come find me. I've still got something to hand over to Ursula — I'll stop by The Slaughtered Lamb in the next day or two."

Jalen said, "Mainly I need to make another trip to Tarren Mill soon — evening ship. I thought I might not be able to catch you. The moment I heard you were back, I asked Tharle about where you're staying."

"You wanted to talk about purifying the demon cloth, didn't you?" Jace asked.

"Right." Jalen said. "How about The Gilded Rose? My treat, Jace."

"All right." Jace nodded.

The two of them came to The Gilded Rose. Jalen ordered a flagon of mead and poured a cup for Jace.

This magic tailor who normally didn't talk much and rarely took the initiative to approach anyone was suddenly being this enthusiastic, and it left Jace a little unsettled. Of course, it was also obvious she wasn't used to treating anyone to a meal — she was a bit nervous and slightly at a loss.

"My friend told me there indeed is a moonwell in these parts, in south Elwynn," Jace said. "Could it be in that 'Dawnwood'?"

"It is," Jace said.

"You've been inside?" Jaren asked. "Dalaran mages used to suspect that place was an ancient Kaldorei magical relic from the old times, hidden behind mysterious illusions that concealed everything within."

Jace said, "Actually, it's a place opened up by an ancient druid. Only, the moonwell inside can't be used either. The one guarding it needs its power — there's no way they'd let us interfere with its purity using demon cloth."

"What guardian?" Jalen asked. "Is there any way to take it out?"

Jace, mid-sip, choked on his drink and said hastily, "That's not a moonwell guardian, Jalen — it's a guardian protecting the Emerald Dream. You can't just solve it with something that crude, it'll bring serious trouble. If you or the Dagger Society get any ideas about him, then forget about the whole moonwell thing."

"The Emerald Dream... you're serious?" Jalen's mouth fell open slightly, then she took a drink. "Sorry, I misunderstood. I didn't... mean to think like that."

"I didn't make it clear enough." Jace set down his cup. "But there is another solution for now. That friend of mine is heading to Kalimdor soon, and he might get a chance to make contact with the Kaldorei. If you've got demon cloth you want purified on hand, I can have him do it for you."

"Really?!" Jalen drew a sharp breath. "That's wonderful! Right now I have several demonic sigils, plus a Demon Armor incantation. I've already found a way to enchant it onto garments, but even Runecloth from Dalaran can't withstand its shadow power. Like I said before — the powerful cloth harvested from demons is high quality, but the magic is muddled, the interference is too strong... if it can actually be purified, and I can get my hands on that cloth, I can make a new robe right away."

"Demon Armor?" Jace asked.

"Yes — a very powerful, very mana-intensive incantation, but once enchanted onto cloth it makes it harder than a demon guard's armor, like the thick hide of a pit lord," Jalen said excitedly. "Of course, under ideal conditions... and the ideal condition is this purified cloth."

"The Demon Armor incantation you mentioned — can't it be attached to a person's skin?" Jace asked. "Like Mage Armor?"

"Mage Armor isn't attached to the skin, Jace," Jalen said. "It's actually a barrier of arcane energy tugging at different elemental forces around the body, more like an elemental resistance shield. Demon Armor is the magic those powerful demons use to strengthen themselves — mortal skin can barely handle it. Of course, you're not the first to think of it, but just about everyone who's actually tried it ended up being tormented by the changes to their own skin until they died, even driven mad, before they ever found a way to undo the magic. But we've always known this incantation exists among the Horde's warlocks — especially the Shadow Council."

Jalen glanced around at the nearby patrons, confirmed no one was eavesdropping, and lowered her voice before continuing: "At first we thought it was that orc skin could withstand this power. Only recently did we figure out that they attached this 'demon curse' onto demon hide first, then combined it with cloth to stitch together powerful outer garments — just like our Voidcloth robes, Jace. But that method is, after all, a compromise... what we're seeking now is a complete solution. Which is — the moonwell."

"I more or less understand," Jace said.

Demon Armor, in the game, was a warlock self-buff that strengthened their own power — but evidently here, no one had yet figured out how to make it act safely on the warlock himself. Probably, over the next decade or so, it would be those warlocks who'd tortured their own skin with Demon Armor who'd finally found a way to coexist with the Demon Armor incantation and forge the version from the game.

A garment harder than a demon guard's armor... Jace felt his anticipation was no less than Jalen's sitting right in front of him.
