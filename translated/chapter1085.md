## Chapter 1085: Burn What?

Hakkar's blood was utterly foul and carried a terrible blood plague — a foul pestilence that had already shattered the troll empire more than once, stirring up storms of blood and rain.

The trolls themselves were a people thoroughly versed in all manner of evil incantations and bizarre sorcery. Even they had to pay a tremendous cost to contain its spread — never mind humans.

If it were to somehow spread through Stormwind, Jace would be the greatest villain in history.

So although this bottle of blood had been extracted from the body of a green dragon that appeared more or less fine, Jace didn't dare treat it with anything less than total seriousness. Hearing Jace say this, Ursula was naturally on full one-hundred-and-twenty-percent alert. She pulled out a whole pile of bottles and jars from the cabinet — including various antidotes, Shadow Protection Potions, purification potions, and Restorative Potions — then put on the alchemical robe Jalen had made, and finally didn't forget to put on her enchanted face mask.

Once she was fully armored and had downed all sorts of protective potions, Jace swallowed and suddenly felt, by comparison, like he was running around naked.

Ursula pulled the stopper and a rotten stench wafted out. In the environment of the temple, this smell hadn't counted for anything next to Hakkar's fishy reek, but in this alchemy lab it was extremely pungent.

The alchemist immediately furrowed her brow. "I thought green dragons lived in forests... why does their blood stink this much?"

Jace sniffed carefully. "It was like that in that temple. Don't worry, this probably isn't contamination from the evil ritual."

Ursula nodded, poured out the blood, and dripped in a bit of blood-purifying potion. She watched the viscous blood stir along with the medicinal liquid, and the vitality within it even recovered somewhat.

"At least it's not as stubborn as black dragon's blood," Ursula said with a long exhale, setting her mind at ease, then took off her mask. "Thanks for the hard work, Jace. Mathias Shaw promised me that if the truth potion works, he'd help promote my alchemy lab and drum up clients for me. I've already gathered everything else — I was just counting on this one bottle of blood from you."

"But I can't guarantee I'll be able to keep supplying you in the future, Ursula," Jace said. "It was hard enough getting this one bottle."

"Shaw didn't say I have to keep supplying him with truth potion nonstop to get his promotion," Ursula raised an eyebrow. "I'll remind him to use it sparingly."

As she spoke, she took a bottle of Refined Arcane Elixir out of an elegant box and handed it to Jace. "The second bottle, as promised." Jace took the elixir. "You're not going to wait until you've actually made that truth potion before giving me the reward?"

Ursula lightly patted Jace's hand holding the bottle. "Our agreement was that you'd help me find green dragon's blood, not help me make the potion. Nothing wrong with that."

Jace's mouth curled up slightly. "Then I'll accept it."

The day after tomorrow was Monday — the agreed time to head for Raven Hill with Morgan Ladimore. With two days in between, Jace planned to start collecting his own blood, to prepare for summoning Karlez.

Of course, he wasn't in a rush to start the summoning right away, but it was best to collect a bit of blood every day. That way he could prepare more, so that in case Karlez got himself stuck in some far-off plane, there wouldn't be enough energy to summon him here.

After getting some anticoagulant potion from Ursula, he left the alchemy lab and got ready to head home.

Just as he was debating whether to stop by Marin's to pay his respects to his teacher, a shriek in demonic suddenly came from the alchemy lab.

The hall had been quite peaceful just moments ago — the sudden outburst made Jace jump out of his skin. Still, the demonic was extremely nonstandard, sounding exactly like it was being spoken in Common. One listen and he knew it was Erlan Dudemore.

Jace went over for a look and saw Erlan holding a scroll in one hand and, in the other, a slim black metal staff of quite decent quality — the mana gem set in it even Revealed a faint trace of shadow radiance.

By Jace's understanding of staffs, this one staff's gem alone was worth several gold.

Sensing someone at the doorway, Erlan glanced over and his eyes immediately lit up. "Jace Sesso, you're finally here — come help me with something. You promised before to teach me a couple of incantations."

"Of course," Jace said. "Did you just get here? I didn't see you earlier."

"I've been here a while," Erlan said. "But I was carefully studying this line of the incantation just now... you might not have noticed me. I didn't hear you come in either, otherwise I'd have called out long ago."

"Unusual," Jace said. "You actually taking the initiative to study an incantation."

Erlan sighed. "Ursula's busy making money, plus that thing with SI:7. Sulenna's the same. Jalen's new robe is close to bearing fruit — her mind's completely elsewhere. And I remember you promised to help her out, right? So right now I'm the only one studying incantations, and I can barely believe it either."

"Didn't Galders help you?" Jace asked.

Erlan handed Jace the scroll he was holding, plus a battered notebook beside it. "Galders has been spending all his time gathering casting materials for summoning demons. He's become utterly obsessed with matters related to the Void plane. He thinks that once we can enter the Twisting Nether, we'll be able to rely on the shadow power within to easily break through some incantations we haven't been able to figure out before — so investing too much energy right now is a waste of time. But I think there's an incantation in here — this one right here."

He pointed to a relatively short line and continued, "I feel like I'm already getting the edge of it. It doesn't need any powerful shadow environment to succeed."

"Menath, kathale, shukile..."

Jace softly finished reading the incantation. He could feel that shadow was indeed being stirred by the incantation, and the feeling was even somewhat familiar. Erlan was right — this incantation didn't need a powerful shadow environment, and it wasn't that complicated either — not like a summoning spell, or some kind of immolation ritual.

He asked, "Do you know roughly what this incantation is supposed to do? For example, some kind of curse, or a direct attack..."

"It should be some kind of shadow flame," Erlan said. "The method of action might be similar to Immolate, but not fel fire — I'm sure of that."

Jace knew that although Erlan didn't have much particular talent for summoning demons or shadow magic, he was quite good at learning fel magic, so his judgment shouldn't be too far off.

And this demonic incantation was indeed related to burning and destruction — the phrasing was extremely vicious, even carrying a "if I can't have it, neither can you" kind of resentment.

That said, the meanings of many ancient incantations weren't as precise as everyday conversation, and didn't necessarily relate directly to the spell's effect — but they more or less illustrated something. For example, most summoning incantations had something to do with pacts and exchange.

But shadow flame... if it truly was some kind of shadow fire, it should be easy for Jace to use this incantation to ignite something.

Erlan had already placed some old clothes on the iron rack up ahead. Jace said, "Let me try."

"Go on, I've been waiting for those words," Erlan said.

Jace cleared his throat, stared at the old clothes before him, then moved his lips and summoned some shadow power to gather around him — it made it easier to cast shadow magic.

Although this was already a fairly routine operation for Jace, Erlan beside him still widened his eyes in shock when he sensed that this warlock's shadow had already started gathering before he'd even spoken the incantation.

"Menath, kathale, shukile..."

Jace pointed at the metal rack with a fairly standard offensive shadow magic gesture. The shadow power was stirred, and he could indeed feel the violent desire within this shadow to ignite something pouring out — but the incantation had clearly failed.

Nothing was ignited. Neither the clothes nor the metal.

The shadow power seemed to have struck at thin air. Jace even felt some of his mana get consumed, but there was no effect whatsoever. The strangest part was, he felt like this wasn't the first time he'd encountered this incantation — he'd definitely come across it before.

"What's going on?" Erlan glanced at the rack. "I almost thought you'd succeed on the first try."

Jace pondered for a moment. "Let's try a different target."
