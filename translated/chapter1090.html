Chapter 1090: An Old Acquaintance

"It's been a very long time since I last saw this sword, Jace." Morgan carefully scrubbed the blade with holy water. "I truly can't believe I was actually able to find it again." "It's a beautiful sword," Jace nodded. "No wonder you've been thinking about it all this time. But best not to let your guard down — we're not out yet, and I'm worried the commotion that monster made will draw more walking corpses." Morgan lifted up the radiant, exquisite longsword. "Holding it in my hands, I feel as if I truly possess the courage to stand against countless undead. Of course, I know I shouldn't let the power brought by this courage cloud my judgment." Jace smiled and watched Morgan wrap the sword in the gray linen he'd prepared in advance and tie it to his body. The two of them returned to the passage entrance. Jace had assumed that after fighting that reanimated brute just now, all the undead throughout the passage might wake up and launch a big siege, forcing him and Morgan to flee in a panic just like he and Grede had before. However, things didn't seem to be as bad as he'd imagined. The wind hummed as it blew through the tunnel. The entire underground crypt was quiet, just like... ...an ordinary underground crypt. Molophil followed some distance behind the two of them, watching for any ghouls or other walking corpses that might be quietly tailing them, covering their retreat. Although Jace very much doubted whether there existed such a thing as a "quiet" ghoul anywhere in this world. Once the atmosphere seemed a bit safer, Jace still couldn't help but ask, "Who was that fellow just now... you really have no clue?" Morgan found the hammer that had smashed into his shield, lifted it by the dirty cloth, and looked at it. "Is this a blacksmith's hammer?" "I noticed that too," Jace said, sitting to the side. "So is it possible that your grandfather commissioned someone to forge 'Achaeus' but didn't pay them?" Morgan turned to look at Jace. The expression on his face was... hard to describe — a somewhat awkward feeling bottled up inside. "I..." "Sorry, forget I said anything," Jace coughed. "I just meant that after we get back to Stormwind, I could try to have someone investigate that suspicious 'blacksmith.' See if there are still any relatives or friends of his in the kingdom, or at least people who knew him." "I agree," Morgan said in a small voice. "If it really is as you said, I'd be willing to make it right on my grandfather's behalf." The two walked to the tomb entrance. The sound of rain outside grew even more clamorous. Jace glanced at this priest and said nothing. Morgan walked in front and said, "Thank you so much for helping me get this sword out, Jace. To you it might not count as dangerous, but every time I've come here..." As he spoke, he glanced toward the outside and suddenly froze. Jace noticed something off about his expression, followed his gaze, and looked outside the tomb gate. In the pitch-black curtain of rain, in the distance was the gloomy forest swaying in the cold wind. Several shadows stood among the grave markers. Those weren't undead — they were living, breathing people. A faint arcane glow lit up bit by bit from the shadows, like will-o'-the-wisps floating over this graveyard. "Mr. Jace Sesso." The shadow standing in the middle called out loudly. "It wasn't easy finding you. Even knowing you live in Stormwind, we couldn't catch a good opportunity to meet with you." The one calling out was draped in a gorgeous blue-and-red long cloak, the gold-thread patterns on his hood clearly visible even in the shadows of Duskwood. Jace was very familiar with his voice — although he hadn't heard it many times, the impression was already deep enough. "Lord Dar'Khan Drathir," Jace said. "It's been a long time." "It's 'Magister,'" the elven woman beside him corrected, wearing a long robe of gold background with blood-colored patterns. "No need to quibble over that — we're old friends, Ressaticis," Dar'Khan said. "And Mr. Sesso here is the Court Mage advisor to His Majesty King Varian Wrynn of the High Kingdom — there's no need to fuss over titles." Ressaticis? Jace had some impression of the name. She looked like an arcanist, but in an instant he couldn't recall much else, because... Dar'Khan Drathir had come after him after all — and at a time when Grede, Vereesa, and Suola were all nowhere around. At the same time, he had Stormwind's protection no longer, and beside him was only an obscure brother... He must have deliberately chosen this opportunity. How long had he been waiting for a chance like this? Jace observed the two people beside him. Besides the mage Ressaticis, the other was clearly a Farstrider — there was no doubt from her attire and bearing, even though the other party wore a hood and Jace couldn't make out her face. However, although three people were standing before him, the other side didn't only number three. There was another person lurking in the shadows. Jace didn't know where. The reason he could sense it was because Molophil had detected it, and was using the power of Soul Link to hint at it to him. It's just that the information Soul Link could convey was also extremely limited. Jace could understand that Molophil was hinting to him that there were other people, but he didn't know what kind of existence the other party specifically was, nor whether Molophil had found that person. "Actually, it hasn't been that long," Dar'Khan said at this point. "It might indeed be a while for you humans. I hope you haven't forgotten what I mentioned the last time we met." Morgan still didn't know what was going on. He looked at Jace as if wanting to ask what exactly was happening, but in the end didn't open his mouth. "I really don't remember," Jace replied. "You seem to carry a very strong air of rejection, Mr. Sesso." So saying, Dar'Khan leaned forward against a grave marker. The curtain of rain falling on him was like leaving a halo-like outline. Jace made out his reddish-brown long hair, and the exquisite long staff of silver and crystal-blue intertwined, seemingly made of metal, held in his hand. He raised his voice a little. "I just want to consult you on a few questions. Once I've asked them I'll leave — no need to be so tense." "As you can see, the weather isn't great right now," Jace raised his head. "Could we talk back at Raven Hill first? There's an inn there — we can drink while we chat." "I'm not accustomed to lingering in human settlements." Dar'Khan raised his head, letting the rain strike his palm. "This is just fine, Mr. Sesso. And I think you should quite like this sort of place — full of strange shadow power, and death." "Then let this brother go back first," Jace lowered his voice. "No need." The Magister smiled. "Our conversation will be open and honest. Besides, this brother should be a friend you trust — I see no reason to keep anything from him." Open and honest? So you chose to meet in this deserted graveyard of the dead, in the middle of a rainy night? Jace wasn't surprised that they'd been able to follow him all the way here without him noticing — after all, Dar'Khan was also a Magister of considerable standing among Quel'Thalas's mages, not to mention they had a Farstrider among them. What truly made him uncomfortable was that, actually, he should have caught on to some clues before this. There were many elves in Stormwind who'd come to celebrate Stormwind casting off the shadow of the black dragon and the Dar'Khan group had definitely slipped in among them. Under normal circumstances, Jace might have sensed something amiss and been on guard against the possibility that Dar'Khan had sent spies to follow him and so on... But having just come back from the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, with his mind on purifying Illanicus, Hakkar's avatar, Grede and Suola going to Kalimdor, and the Dark Portal in the future... and because of the celebratory atmosphere throughout the kingdom from top to bottom, he'd let his guard down. At this point, Morgan had also more or less sensed that the atmosphere was off. The information being passed between these people seemed even darker and more shadowy than this big graveyard itself — cold to the bone. "Let's go back together, Jace," the priest said in a low voice. Although Jace didn't show anything, hearing him say this, he slowly let out a breath in helplessness. Morgan didn't seem afraid of trouble at all — but this wasn't what Jace wanted. Dar'Khan was definitely going to ask about Ulthalesh. He clearly wanted this priest standing there to witness the entire process of him "exposing" Jace. If things reached a point where they couldn't be taken back, this probationary Church brother who couldn't even handle a few ghouls could be casually taken out by these high elves before him at any time. Then he and Molophil would be facing multiple opponents — how could they still look out for him? What's more, even if they completely ignored him, he and Molophil weren't necessarily a match for these people. "Then what questions do you have? Ask away," Jace said loudly. "I'll answer as best I can."
