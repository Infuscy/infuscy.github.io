Chapter 1092: The Duskwood Siege

The rain seemed to freeze in place. Jace gripped Ulthalesh behind him, the words of Fear already forming on his lips, the shadow power needed for a mass fear taking shape. But he wasn't faster than his opponent. Halfway through the incantation, he watched the same mage who'd just polymorphed Morgan point straight at him. Arcane energy shifted in a visible tide under her command — Counterspell. This mage had been ready for a Counterspell all along. Dar'Khan had already raised his hands, and Jace had no idea what spell he was about to cast, but one thing was certain: Fear wasn't going to land in time. Once his Fear got countered, even a warlock as practiced in shadow as he was could find his connection to the Shadow severed. That was the brutal nature of the Weave. In the instant his consciousness shifted, the gathered shadow power began to slip from his control. He felt a savage arcane force stab straight into his skull — a flash of searing pain, but bearable. After all, he'd only caught a Counterspell with nothing behind it. This Counterspell... wasn't much stronger than Marin's. The rain and air around him turned abruptly cold. Jace channeled the shadow that hadn't yet dissipated to Shadowstep out of that pocket of air, flashing several meters to the side. Where he'd been standing, a mass of frost clamped down and a pillar of ice erupted from the ground, sprouting countless needle-sharp spikes. He only caught a glimpse — no way to tell whether that was a spell that could kill instantly, or a Bindings of Ice that had simply missed its target. Two mages at once... Damn it. He should have summoned a felhound ahead of time, but there was no point dwelling on that now. How the hell was he supposed to summon a felhound right in front of Morgan when they hadn't found a single mage in the crypt? A bowstring's hiss tore through his thoughts. The Shadow Veil was still carrying him when he caught the sound of several high elves calling to each other in the distance. Right now his only helper was Morfile, and Morfile couldn't just show up and pin down these three opponents, because there was still someone lurking in the shadows. Who? It's up to you, Morfile... keep an eye on that one. I can't afford to be distracted right now. He drifted behind a headstone and steadied himself. The shadows faded. Dar'Khan was right — the shadow suffusing the Great Graveyard and all of Duskwood was his ally. But that advantage wasn't much of an edge, because Dar'Khan was a master of Shadow too... He felt a soothing sensation drift over through the soul link. Maybe Morfile had understood. Maybe she hadn't. Jace didn't have the mental bandwidth to figure it out. Suddenly, a burst of light flared in the distance behind the headstone. An arcane orb drifted toward the rear flank, illuminating a wide stretch of mausoleum foundations nearby. This was a good moment... to summon a felhound. But a proper summoning ritual took too long. Those two mangers and that Farstrider would never give him the opening. Rely on Sazeno? Impossible. Onyxia might overlook an imp, but a mage like Dar'Khan... The arcane orb drowned against the distant graveyard wall. Rapid footsteps closed in fast — the Farstrider. Damn it, she didn't even need to see him to know where he was. No time. Jace pulled a small vial of his own blood from his bag — about half a bottle he'd collected a few days ago. "It has to be Zabel," the Dark Iron warlock Morulus's words echoed in his head. "He's a demon magic genius. He can summon demon minions by burning the blood-writing right in his palm, like an orcs do. But on my end, I'm not skilled enough yet, and the cost is enormous..." "Write the summoner's title, name, nickname, and so on, at the key point." "Your demon... your minion." "Flame..." Jace drew his dagger and murmured in the tongue of fire elements, eyes closed. The cold rain struck his face, but it turned scalding fast under his words... A column of flame suddenly spiraled outward through the rain, blasting an entire curtain of raindrops into a shockwave of steam. A stifled grunt came from nearby — the Farstrider Maela clearly hadn't expected that and got blasted aside by the fire... "Surrender, Jace Sesso!" Dar'Khan shouted from a distance in Thalassian. Jace could even feel the arcane distortions detonating one after another behind the cry. But he ignored all of it. He had to finish the summoning. He gathered shadow power again, using the blazing firestorm as cover to retreat several meters. At the same time, he carved the name "Dazarm" into his palm with the dagger. There were currently three felhounds within the borders of the Kingdom of Stormwind — one with SI:7, one near Iylogara Tower. So summoning any one of those three to his side wouldn't cost too much mana. He scribbled an Anihelan numeral four after Dazarm. Things were too urgent; he hadn't controlled the force properly, and the last stroke felt like it had cut a little deep. But this pain... was nothing he couldn't ignore right now. Jace clenched his fist, gritted his teeth, and intoned: "Velirinki, Toxintani, Toxesta, Maalimasta!" The blood vial shattered. The rune burning in his palm ignited in shadowflame that lit the splashing blood midair. The moment the incantation finished, an arcane figure suddenly flashed through the flames right before Jace's eyes. Golden-red robes whipping in the wind, Ressathia raised her arm high and brought it down hard. Before the arcane energy of her Blink had even dispersed, she seized countless fire elements Jace had summoned, gathering them at a speed that even surprised Jace into a palm-shaped blast of flame that slammed toward him. Having just finished the summoning incantation, Jace didn't have nearly enough time to raise a Dark Shield to block the attack. But he had the blessing of red dragon blood — maybe he could... Just as he thought he was going to have to take the hit with his own body, a burly black shadow suddenly lunged from behind him and swallowed the entire "flame palm." The rest of the force was absorbed by thick hide. Dazarm IV, wreathed in flames still lurching on its tendrils, charged straight at Ressathia. The elf's confident look collapsed in an instant. She raised her hand and tried to cast what looked like a Slow spell on the felhound closing in at impossible speed — but the incantation was cut short by an ear-splitting roar that nearly deafened Jace. The countered mage turned and tried to flee on foot, but Dazarm pounced and clamped down on her neck, pinning her against a shattered headstone and tearing into her like a spotted hyena ripping apart a helpless kitten. But Jace didn't even have room in his heart for the satisfaction of getting Dazarm here. Instinctively, on the other side of the flames the rain hadn't yet put out, he sensed a gathering arcane light flickering. Not good... A sharp crack — an arrow of arcane energy shot straight at him. Jace was used to the speed and force of Vereesa's arcane arrows, so this one didn't terrify him too much. But it was still a magic arrow loosed by a Farstrider. Even though he'd thrown himself sideways ahead of time, the arrow still grazed his cheek, leaving a deep, charred gash at the corner of his mouth. Jace didn't even have the strength to deal with the Farstrider calmly loosing arrows at him from a distance, because the moment that arrow came, another arcane hum rose from behind him. An ethereal incantation began — a Draconic spell. Frost, or arcane? He couldn't worry about it anymore. He turned and hit the casting, resplendent mage-shadow with Asphyxiate, choking it into the air. Dar'Khan dissolved into a cloud of blue-white mist, laughing wildly. Mirror Image... Another Dar'Khan suddenly Blinked to the headstone in front of him, shouting an incantation at the top of his lungs. Dazarm abandoned Ressathia's corpse and howled as it lunged at Dar'Khan — but only swallowed another arcane afterimage. "Follow that magic," Jace said in Demonic. "Hunt down the mage's true body, Dazarm!" The words had barely left his mouth before Dazarm charged into the smoke and dark rain on his command. But immediately another rush of wind struck his face. Jace suddenly felt his arm go limp. An arrow fletched with Quel'Thalas silver had punched into his shoulder, knocking him back two stumbling steps and nearly sending him to the ground. Seemingly realizing that magic arrows drew this warlock's attention too easily, that Farstrider who'd been hiding in the shadows... damn it, she'd just used a plain arrow for the ambush. After chaining multiple Shadowsteps and summoning Dazarm, Jace was starting to feel the strain. He needed to drink a mana potion while the ranger was still trying to locate him. That was when Sazeno grabbed his collar and shoved a Refined Wisdom Elixir into his hand. Right, almost forgot. "Master..." the imp whispered. "Drink it, quick." Jace nodded and downed it. He instantly felt his depleted mana shoved back by a surge of power so fierce it was nearly nauseating. But precisely because of that, his senses sharpened abruptly — and the sting in his shoulder became a lot clearer too. "Go find a chance to help, anything..." Jace said. "Don't stay on me." "Yes, Master." Sazeno nodded. Jace added, "Oh, and Curse of Tongues. Now." From the other side of a large mausoleum came a muffled boom of arcane energy, followed by a felhound's furious howl. A wave of scalding flame crawled up his throat. As his panting drew closer and more demonic by the second, he felt the fel fire within him, the churning shadow in his heart, beginning to climb into his skull and infect his emotions...
