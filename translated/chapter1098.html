Chapter 1098: The Duskwood Meeting

Varian had been resting on the second floor of the inn watched over by guards these past two days. The townsfolk knew some lord had arrived, but they hadn't realized it was the king himself.

Still, when several high elf magisters suddenly appeared near the watchtower to the north of town, everyone sensed something unusual was going on.

Even though the Archmagister didn't want this matter spreading beyond Duskwood, the sudden arrival of a group of Quel'Thalas emissaries in this town, combined with the "lord" who'd shown up earlier and the soldiers escorting a prisoner from Raven Hill, meant all sorts of rumors were bound to spread.

That day, Jace changed into some clean clothes the king had brought and headed toward the town center. He could already see quite a few high elves along the way.

These normally aloof, cold-eyed aristocrats of a race were being remarkably friendly and approachable toward the curious townsfolk. Even children who wandered up and pestered them with questions got enthusiastic answers.

Jace could tell that at least for now, Archmagister Belor'al valued Alliance relations very highly.

When he reached the entrance to the Town Hall, Jace asked an elven guard standing duty there, "Has Lady Vereesa Windrunner arrived?"

"Not to my knowledge, Master Sesso," the elf said with a slight bow.

Jace nodded his thanks. It had only been a little over a week since Vereesa left. Could she still be on her way, completely unaware of what had happened here?

Just the thought of Vereesa possibly knowing about this gave him a headache.

He'd promised to be careful, and yet he'd let a guy he'd been watching all along catch him off guard and ambush him. Forced to fight outnumbered, he'd ended up in this pathetic state.

Still, he noticed there was indeed an unfamiliar Farstrider standing inside the Town Hall, talking with a town clerk. After all, there had been a Farstrider ranger among the four. Logically, the Farstrider corps should send a representative to get the full picture.

Just then, Morgan walked out of the Town Hall.

"His Majesty the king and the Archmagister of Quel'Thalas are already inside, Jace."

"That's who I'm here to see," Jace said.

Morgan pressed a hand to his brow and gave an awkward grimace. "To this day I still can't believe all of this started because I wanted you to help me get Acherus back."

"Then you'd better keep that sword safe," Jace said, glancing toward the door. "Otherwise what was the point of all this fuss?"

Morgan nodded. "Don't worry. No matter what happens next, I'll never let it out of my hands again."

Jace clapped Ladimore on the shoulder and walked through the Town Hall doors. The elven guards, the town watch, and the Stormwind guards all nodded to him. Past them, a guard pushed open the inner chamber door, and the assembled humans and elves all turned toward the entrance.

A high elf mage with auburn hair tied in a braid, wearing a deep purple robe, had been speaking with King Varian. She now looked this way.

The spell-robes this mage wore were covered in intricate, orderly spell-script, containing a power so immensely strong it was visible to the naked eye — yet so restrained that it didn't leak the slightest influence into the surroundings. Even with Jace's senses, it was hard to detect.

By human standards, she looked to be under forty. But Jace knew that any high elf who could be read as "not young" was likely over a thousand years old.

"Your Majesty," Jace greeted the king.

"Jace, you're here." Varian nodded, then turned his gaze to the mage beside him.

"Mr. Jace Sesso." The high elf inclined her in greeting. "Archmagister Belor'al Saronna, leader and overseer of the Magisters' Platform. Magister Dar'Khan Drathir was sent to the Kingdom of Stormwind to clear out the undead here at my behest. This staff was entrusted to him by me."

Archmagister Belor'al — in another timeline, a decade and a half from now, she would sacrifice herself defending Quel'Danas and the Sunwell. Wounded by Arthas wielding Frostmourne, she would ultimately be torn apart by the undead horde.

Looking at her calm expression right now, it was hard to immediately connect her with that "already-happened" fate.

"Archmagister," Jace returned the greeting.

"Upon receiving King Varian's letter, my first reaction was utter shock." Belor'al swept her gaze across everyone in the hall. "Though Dar'Khan had long been conducting research into the Twisting Nether and Shadow, and his character and conduct were controversial even among the magisters, the research he and his colleagues produced saved many soldiers and mages in Quel'Thalas from the dark magic of the trolls. This is why I trusted he could contribute to purifying the necromantic darkness of South Elwynn, and so entrusted the Staff of the Torrent to him."

At that point, another magister beside her said, "Even the respected Magister Dar'Khan couldn't escape the fate of so many Quel'Thalas Shadow researchers — having his heart devoured by the darkness of power."

Belor'al shot him a look and offered no response. Then a flurry of footsteps came from the hall entrance.

"Don't you dare shift the blame onto shadow magic, Passarion!"

A Farstrider in a pale green cloak stormed in, throwing back her hood and letting loose a cascade of silver-white hair. She fixed a cold stare on the one who'd just spoken. The magister saw her and lowered his head, not daring to answer.

Hearing the voice, Jace knew it was Vereesa. The ranger spotted him too.

"Jace..."

Vereesa walked over quickly and embraced Jace. He could still catch the faint, iron-rust scent of the arcane clinging to her — the residue of having just stepped through a portal.

"I should have paid more attention to the Quel'dorei presence in Stormwind when I was in the city. If I'd known Dar'Khan was here, I never would have returned to Quel'Thalas so quickly... This is my fault. I should have been at your side then."

"It's fine, Wen." Jace stroked her long, pointed ear. "I'm not hurt. Everything's fine."

Vereesa nodded, then turned back to face the group of magisters.

"No magister can shift the blame elsewhere, Lady Vereesa Windrunner," Belor'al said in a very formal tone of Common. "Here, I apologize to Mr. Jace Sesso, to His Majesty King Varian Wrynn, to the residents of Elwynn Forest, and indeed to the entire kingdom, for the folly of Dar'Khan Drathir and my own grave oversight. I also apologize to you and to General Sylvanas Windrunner, and to the Farstrider corps. Dar'Khan deceived a Farstrider ranger into participating out of personal greed, inflicting damage on both Quel'Thalas internally and the Lordaeron Alliance that will be equally difficult to repair."

The Archmagister continued. "Based on our preliminary investigation these past few days, including inquiries into Dar'Khan and some of his associates, he had recently become obsessed with a fearsome artifact said to be the handiwork of a demon of the Twisting Nether. It was the immense power of this artifact that drew him on, leading him to commit this unexpected and unforgivable crime. Since this matter is connected to Mr. Jace Sesso's prior cooperation with the Reliquary, it's very likely he targeted him for exactly this reason."

"To get to the bottom of this whole absurd affair," Vereesa stepped forward, "Jace and I must be involved in the investigation, Archmagister."

"A reasonable request, my lady — and one we should have offered proactively as a gesture of apology." Belor'al nodded. "The magisters were already planning to invite the Farstriders and the Kingdom of Stormwind into the investigation of this vicious incident. We intend to investigate the mage towers of Dar'Khan Drathir and his apprentices. As the person who first discovered clues about that artifact, I believe Mr. Sesso would be more likely to notice traces we might easily miss. Of course, this is pending Mr. Sesso's full recovery."

After the Archmagister agreed, Vereesa glanced Jace's way.

Her eyes were of course full of guilt and concern, sizing up Jace's condition. But Jace still caught a hint of barely concealed smugness in them.

Clearly, this ranger had the same idea he did.

She'd prodded the Archmagister on purpose, definitely wanting to use this chance to let Jace run over to that shadow mage's tower and see if there was anything useful to be had.

Even if the investigation ended up being a joint effort with the Magisters' Platform, that was already a big enough concession.

But this immediately put Jace in an awkward spot. This was a precious opportunity he'd planned to trade the Archmagister's Staff of the Torrent for — and now the Archmagister had just agreed outright. If he handed the staff over now, wouldn't it feel like he was giving it away for nothing?
