Chapter 1099: The Same Model

"Thank you, Archmagister," Jace said. "I never suspected that Dar'Khan's actions had anything to do with Silvermoon or the Magisters' Platform. At this critical moment, with the shadow of the Dark Portal still hanging over us and the black dragon scourge spreading across the kingdoms, everyone walking on eggshells... no one would willingly sow discord within the Alliance or deliberately create a rift." Belor'al nodded. "Quel'Thalas has always stood with the entire Alliance, Mr. Sesso. During the Expedition, during the battle for Nethergarde Keep. So it was, so it is now, and so it will remain." "Rumors of discord between humans and the Quel'dorei have been thoroughly debunked," Varian said with a smile. "I'm glad to hear that Quel'Thalas will always stand on our side, Archmagister. After all, no one wants to see the alliance we paid so dearly for teeter on the brink of collapse because of one foolish mage's personal actions." Jace had deliberately tried to steer the topic up to the level of the entire Alliance. Judging from Varian's reaction, he seemed quite satisfied. Jonder whether he could use Belor'al's promise to push the high elves into marching on Blackrock Spire together someday. But with things having been said to this extent, even if the high elves chose to stand aside and wash their hands of it later, they'd be breaking a promise — and that meant losing the moral high ground. Seeing the atmosphere between the king and the leader in the middle had eased, the guards around them relaxed quite a bit. But Jace could tell the elven magisters, Archmagister Belor'al included, still hadn't let their guard down. Their willingness to concede to this extent was partly because they didn't want this affecting relations with the Kingdom of Stormwind. And partly... it was definitely because they wanted Jace to willingly hand over that staff. "The damage the graveyard sustained in this incident is severe, Archmagister," Varian said, walking up to the Archmagister. "Large sections of mausoleums and headstones were destroyed by magical force, and clearly under the influence of Dar'Khan's shadow magic, even more undead in the surrounding area were stirred awake. After some of the fencing was knocked down, a number of undead wandered out as well. Relying on Raven Hill's guards alone is no longer enough to keep the road between Moonbrook and Duskwood Town safe." "Rest assured, Your Majesty," Belor'al said. "Dar'Khan originally came to Elwynn to deal with the undead problem. We certainly wouldn't abandon the matter just because he committed a crime. The Magisters' Platform will certainly re-cooperate with the Kingdom of Stormwind on this front, and will conduct more thorough vetting of every magister involved. There is a senior magister who has cooperated with the Kirin Tor's Eye of the Arcane before — I'm certain he won't fail this mission." "Does General Sylvanas know about this?" he asked, turning to Vereesa beside him. "We'd like to continue the interrogation of that ranger together with you." "She already knows. And Maela is no longer a Farstrider," Vereesa said in a low voice. "We've already made our decision." Vereesa and Varian exchanged information about Duskwood's dark curse and the next steps for cooperation. With the opening this created, Belor'al came over to Jace. "Mr. Jace Sesso." She lowered her head slightly. "The Staff of the Torrent is of immense importance to the Magisters' Platform and the guarding of the Sunwell. My willingness to hand over this staff at the time was also in hopes of using its power to strengthen our relationship with the Kingdom of Stormwind. Moreover, once it leaves the Sunwell, its power will gradually wane as time passes, and the Sunwell's power will become increasingly difficult to control, adding to the difficulty of our guarding and monitoring." Though the game never explicitly stated the Staff of the Torrent's power level, Jace knew full well that this staff was indeed extraordinary — but certainly not to the extent Belor'al claimed. If it were truly that powerful, Jace wouldn't believe for a second that a staff capable of affecting the Sunwell and all of Quel'Thalas would be lent out so casually by the Archmagister to Dar'Khan to go kill undead. So this was definitely bluster — meant to make Jace consider the consequences and think twice before refusing to hand it over. Of course, Jace had never actually planned to keep the staff. It was just that the conditions he'd carefully prepared had been blurted out by Vereesa right in front of him. So what was he supposed to ask for in exchange now? Seeing Jace hesitate, the Archmagister paused, then continued. "Dar'Khan Drathir wielded this staff to recklessly cast dark magic, which may already have inflicted negative effects on it. Had he continued to hold this staff, his depraved magic, cast without any regard for consequences, might even have affected the Sunwell itself. Before everything became irreversible, you seized it from the hands of a magister who'd already lost his mind. That undoubtedly set the entire kingdom at ease, and for this we are willing to offer our thanks." Gold. At least a thousand gold coins, just to hear the sound of it. Though Jace had no idea how much this Staff of the Torrent was actually worth right now. Considering that Azuresong had fetched a high price of over a thousand gold in Booty Bay before, what would this staff be worth? But honestly, after defeating Onyxia, the king had used the funds Katrana Prestor donated to Stormwind to cover the repair costs for Iylogara Tower, so he wasn't in desperate need of money right now. If it was to provide for renovating and furnishing a place after moving his parents here, there was no need to trade the Staff of the Torrent for gold just for that. Faced with this Archmagister, he'd be better off trading for something money couldn't buy — something he needed even more than gold. Like souls. Yes, souls. He needed an infinite number of souls to fill the emptiness and hunger of tens of thousands of years. You hear that, Jace Sesso? Ask Archmagister Belor'al for souls. Use the souls of the dead Zul'Aman trolls. For thousands of years you had no idea how many Amani souls these high elves of Quel'Thalas had seized. Even Zareth doesn't know, and neither do I... Jace frowned. Ulthalesh was in Morfile's hands right now, lying obediently in the inn room. Jace obviously couldn't bring it before Belor'al. Who knew how much connection Archmagister Belor'al had to the Order of Tirisfal? What if she actually recognized this staff somehow? Then everything would fall apart. But even from this distance, it could still influence its master's mind, speaking its own thoughts in the master's voice inside the master's head — especially when the emotion of greed began to rise... After devouring the blood god Hakkar's soul, Ulthalesh had clearly grown more active. Shut up. Jace gave his response in his mind, and it actually seemed to quiet down. But Ulthalesh's point wasn't entirely without merit. Jace did have something he needed right now — the thing Morfile had raised when discussing the void pathways to Outland. Something that could stabilize the Weave and a doomlord's mana. "After the orcs occupied and destroyed Iylogara Tower, that mage tower's mana stone has been missing, Archmagister," Jace said in a low voice. "Iylogara Tower is an indispensable part of the Kingdom of Stormwind's entire defense system. The Shadowhide tribe of the gnoll forest is still biding its time, having inherited the dark magic of the orc warlocks. Not only that — the shores of Lake Everstill now face the threat of the Blackrock orcs and the Dark Iron dwarves from the north." "If Quel'Thalas could provide a mana stone powerful enough to replace the one that was depleted and damaged... something like..." At that, Jace recalled the Quel'Thalas mana stone that had sustained a barrier even eredar sorcerers couldn't break, and said, "Something like the one at Nethergarde Keep." Hearing Jace bring up the mana stone on that mage tower, Vereesa looked over too. Jace met her eyes, wondering if he was being a bit too much. But Vereesa just shifted her gaze curiously back to Belor'al, wanting to see the Archmagister's reaction. "There are many types of mana stones used to stabilize mage towers in Quel'Thalas," Belor'al thought for a moment. "I don't yet know which type Iylogara Tower needs most. If you're willing, when you come over, I can take you to have a look before we decide." So he could actually get one of those same-model stones? Jace had figured he'd have to do some haggling, maybe lower his demands and settle for a less powerful mana stone as a compromise. He hadn't expected the other side to just agree. Could that staff really be as significant as Belor'al claimed? If so, Dar'Khan had really wasted a terrifying treasure. Or was the reason he'd been able to withstand that staff's bombardment because he possessed the dark power of Ulthalesh? Right now, the look of utter disbelief on Dar'Khan's face as he channeled the Staff of the Torrent to summon a mighty arcane barrage, only for it to be completely deflected by the Dark Shield, was still fresh in Jace's mind. Jace pushed these thoughts aside and said softly, "Then it's settled, Archmagister."
