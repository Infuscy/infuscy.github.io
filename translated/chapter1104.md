## Chapter 1104: The Examiner's Advice

Back in Stormwind, Vereesa went straight to the Quel'Thalas embassy near the Stormwind Keep gardens with the letter Jace had written to his parents. Jace headed to the church to check on Morgan Ladimore, who'd returned with the king.

Ever since the skirmish with those uninvited guests in the great graveyard, Jace had been able to sense that Morgan seemed to possess a fiercely passionate fighting heart.

Even though he'd almost never fought a living person before, even though his power might have been nothing more than an unsustainable burst brought on by a sudden favor of the Holy Light, there was no question that his courage, and his remarkable will capable of holding himself back even while completely unleashing his strength, made Jace feel... that if they ran into some really big trouble in the future, he could even call on him to help — so long as Morgan could accept Ursulus, accept the fact that a whole pack of demons was being kept in his own home, including an end boss-level doom lord.

A paladin favored by the Holy Light could possess unimaginable power.

But after arriving at the church, Jace saw his wife Liss bringing their daughter to bring him food. So he hid behind a not-too-distant pillar, thinking he'd wait until they were finished before going over. He wasn't busy with anything right now anyway.

The assassination attempt at the great graveyard wasn't known to many people in Stormwind, but the Church had clearly overlooked Morgan's contribution in this "battle against a rogue dark warlock." They'd promoted his rank, and the new necklace he wore around his neck — bearing the insignia of an unfamiliar Holy Light devotee — was one Jace no longer recognized.

Liss was clearly very happy, because this might mean her husband had a chance to realize his dream and join the Knights of the Silver Hand. Morgan was of course happy too, but even from this distance Jace could sense that his joy and satisfaction weren't nearly as intense as his wife's and daughter's.

He knew Morgan had always felt guilty. He felt it was because he'd asked Jace for help that he'd dragged Jace into this mess.

If Morgan learned that Jace's mind was currently filled with that magic stone that could be placed atop Ilgala Tower, he'd probably feel a lot more at ease.

But watching Morgan smile at his daughter Sara, Jace knew that if they ran into trouble like the Temple of Atal'Hakkar again, it would be hard for him to come here and call on him.

He'd finally moved his whole family to Stormwind, instead of staying in Raven Hill like in that other timeline until the necrotic plague spiraled out of control. Better not to invite further trouble. Let him take good care of and spend time with his family, help the citizens of Stormwind, and serve his faith.

After exchanging a few words with Morgan and confirming that he and Archeus were doing fine, Jace prepared to head over to the Mage Quarter. The Reliquary's examiner was said to be staying at the elven residence in the Mage Quarter.

With the Winter Veil and the Elder's Moon festivals behind them, temperatures had risen quickly. Wearing the same outfit he'd worn to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar was already a bit hot by now.

The apprentices in the Mage Quarter had all switched to loose long robes, chatting about the new spells their teachers had taught this semester, plus gossip from other countries. In general, not many people had heard about the assassination that had shaken King Wrynn and the Silvermoon Grand Magister in the lands south of the Nafeltari River.

Though it had already been over a week since the incident, Jace still felt a lingering dread. Winning against overwhelming odds had been exhilarating, but he had absolutely no desire to go through it again.

"Jace Sesso? Master Sesso?"

Lost in thought, walking along the cobblestone path, he suddenly heard a somewhat odd-sounding Common tongue from behind him. Jace turned around, and several young apprentice passersby who'd heard the name also looked over.

The one calling him was an elf wearing a gold-trimmed blue robe, hurrying toward him. Though he wore something far more lavish and eye-catching than most of the archmages at the Wizard Sanctum, after seeing that group of archmages at Duskwood — and Belvar — Jace actually found this elf's outfit a touch understated.

"That's me," Jace replied, while stealing a glance at the curious apprentices who'd heard the name. They immediately lowered their heads, mumbled something like "Master Sesso," and shoved each other away as they fled.

It seemed the vast majority of people at the Wizard Sanctum already knew he was a "hexer" who wielded shadow magic. That was fine, actually. Before this, many people had mistaken him for a young archmage with extraordinary arcane talent, and he'd been quite anxious on the inside. At least now he didn't have to pretend anymore.

"Master Sesso." The elf caught his breath and came to a stop. "I am Oren Sunrest, an examiner of the Reliquary. I was sent here by the Order on account of the head of the black dragon beast lord Leshrethal."

"Actually, I was looking for you too," Jace said.

Examiner Oren nodded. "Though it's unrelated to my purpose here, I still have to say... regarding the matter of South Elwynn, I'm truly sorry, Master. I hope you won't take that incident as a reflection on the entire Order of the Reliquary."

"I won't," Jace said. "It's only natural that an archmage of considerable standing would want to examine the important clues gathered by the Reliquary."

"That puts my mind at ease." The elf smiled. "Now, may I see the head?"

"Of course. Follow me."

Jace led the examiner out of the Mage Quarter all the way to a standalone house beside the canal.

Ever since Ilgala Tower had begun renovations, he'd hidden the sealed iron box containing Leshrethal's head in the cellar of this house.

He took out his booklet and recited the sealing incantation given to him by the Wizard Sanctum. With a bang, the iron box cracked open a seam, and the distinct shadow-tinged bloody smell unique to black dragons immediately seeped out. These dead black dragons and their draconic bloodline, owing to their own terrifying dark energy, hardly decayed at all in nature. A simple magical seal was enough to preserve them for a very long time.

The examiner's eyes lit up at the scent. He leaned down and carefully observed that big, crooked-eyed dragon head. "Pure-blooded... breathtaking, Master Sesso. Leshrethal is an extremely ancient draconic bloodline of the black dragons. It's said that long, long ago, he was a member of the guardian legion that watched over the Demon Soul for Deathwing. After the Demon Soul was lost once more, only a handful of the most powerful guardians of that legion seem to have survived — and he was one of them."

"Do you know about the Demon Soul?" he asked. "A dish," Jace said.

Jace realized that not all elves knew exactly what he'd done in Grim Batol.

"Yes." The other man couldn't help but smile, then continued. "That thing was of paramount importance to Deathwing, so the guardians watching it must have been formidable. Leshrethal... judging by the quality of this head's preservation and the story behind it, two hundred gold really isn't high."

With that, he turned around and said, "Recently I've heard at the Wizard Sanctum that the dragonblood gem carried by Leshrethal is right here in Stormwind. I wonder if you know where it ended up, Master?"

"I know," Jace said. "But it's not in Stormwind anymore. By now it should be..."

He looked up and rolled his eyes. "In the Great Sea, or Northrend."

"What?" Oren Sunrest was taken aback.

"On Lord Grede Thunderfist's person," Jace said. "And he's currently flying toward Kalimdor — he may have already arrived. Go ask him."

"All right. Thank you very much for letting us know this important information."

Though his words were grateful, Oren was visibly deflated. Jace even felt that his main objective in coming here hadn't been this head at all, but that enormous gem.

After all, no matter how extraordinary the head's owner had been, it was still just a head. Quel'Thalas couldn't use troll sorcery to make it open its mouth and talk, and Jace very much doubted this head knew anything useful. So to the Order of the Reliquary, this was at best a keepsake that could demonstrate the Order's strength.

But that gem was a different matter entirely. A fearsome artifact that truly contained the essence of dragon blood, a powerful relic capable of enabling a dwarf warrior to hold out against a black dragon princess Onyxia's relentless assault.

"Take this token, Master." Oren produced a green-eyed stone ring imbued with a strange, faint magical aura and handed it to Jace. "Stormwind Bank, Vault 45. Inside is a chest containing two hundred Stromgarde gold coins. This ring will open it."

"Understood." Jace pocketed the ring. "There's one more thing I'd like to consult you about. Do you know about magic stones?"

"You've changed your mind — you want to trade for a magic stone?" Oren asked.

"Not that kind of magic stone," Jace said. "The ones on mage towers. Connected to the ley lines, energy storage, mana stabilization, power discharge — that sort of thing."

"Ah... you mean that." Oren said with a touch of pride. "I graduated from Dasviser and participated in the mana-stone tending work there, Master, so I know a thing or two."

"Is there one that can help stabilize shadow, arcane, and fel forces?" Jace asked point-blank.

Oren was taken aback. Jace explained, "I'm trying to find a kind of gem capable of digesting various dark powers, Oren. Because Ilgala Tower won't just be dealing with gnolls who wield shadow incantations — it also has black dragons and orc neighbors to the north. They can summon demons, unleash powerful Shadowflame. I have to prepare for every possibility."

The examiner before him didn't seem entirely fooled by Jace's explanation, but he nodded anyway. "Then what you're looking for is a prismatic mana crystal. I recommend a type known as a Deep Shadow crystal."

"Quel'Thalas's Deep Shadow crystals, drawing upon the Sunwell, already contain powerful and stable arcane energy within them. But what's special is that their fragments can be used to aid in crafting artifacts, garments, and so on that resist the forces of shadow. More complete Deep Shadow crystals can be used to neutralize the dark Amani sorcery. I know that several years ago, many shadow mages used this kind of crystal to devour the demonic magic of Gul'dan and his warlocks..."
