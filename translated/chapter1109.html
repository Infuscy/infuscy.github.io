Chapter 1109: Sailing for the Eldarath River

In the morning, the two of them followed several archmages down the slope to the northeast. The smooth cobblestone path was an absolute pleasure to walk on. Facing the pink dawn glow, and with last night's hangover probably still lingering, he felt the meadows and seawater before his eyes were all somewhat dreamlike.

It's just that his lower body still ached a bit, his legs and feet felt like they'd just recovered from being wrenched out of joint, and his neck hurt too, so walking wasn't particularly comfortable.

He could barely remember what had happened last night. All he knew was that when he'd woken up in the morning the entire room was in shambles, and even Vereesa had been sleeping like the dead beside him, her arms and legs black and blue.

All in all, the two of them didn't feel like they'd "battled" through the night — they felt like they'd actually battled through the night.

Vereesa noticed his slightly off-kilter gait and immediately turned her face sideways, glaring with an expression that clearly said, "You still unconvinced?" She glanced back and forth at the archmages and attendants before and after them, her meaning being that Jace had better not act so conspicuously strange.

Jace seemed to remember something, so he smacked his lips and said in a low voice, "You clearly didn't outdrink Grede."

"What?" Vereesa asked with a look of complete bewilderment.

"I said." Jace cleared his throat, covering his mouth with his fist. "At Nethergarde Keep you didn't outdrink Grede. You were the one who passed out first."

"It must be you who's remembering wrong, you idiot who has to flip through a spellbook just to cast Light." Vereesa said in a low voice, giving him a sidelong glare.

Jace pinched the tip of her ear and tugged hard. Vereesa sucked in a breath. One of the archmages walking in front, hearing the rustling from behind, turned his head curiously.

Jace immediately pulled his hand back and straightened his collar. Vereesa also casually brushed her ear back and straightened her posture.

Following the sloping path down the northeast face of the village, they passed through a sparse forest and arrived at the harbor of Sunstrider Isle.

Jace didn't remember there being a harbor here in the game. But considering that later Sunstrider Isle was also ravaged by the Scourge, and that countless towers and courtyards that still existed now were destroyed — and that the Scourge had marched from this very direction along the path of ice paved by Frostmourne toward Quel'Danas Isle — it would be perfectly normal for this harbor to have been leveled entirely.

A crowd of high elves had already gathered at the harbor. Most were mages, wearing gorgeous long robes and holding tall staffs. From here, Jace couldn't make out the exact appearance and clothing details of the mages waiting at the harbor, but he knew in his heart that Grand Magister Belvar was among them. Because here, he could already feel the power of the Torrent Staff.

In the Elwynn Forest far from the Sunwell, though the arcane power contained within the Torrent Staff had already been the most surging Jace had ever seen. But only after arriving at Sunstrider Isle could Jace deeply feel why the archmages would pay any price to bring it back.

The invisible mana it released in wave after wave made the golden phoenix-banner sailboat moored to the side look a full head shorter, echoing the sunrise-like radiance of the Sunwell in the distance over the sea.

When the group arrived beside the harbor, Grand Magister Belvar stepped out from among the archmages and said, "We meet again, Master Jace Sesso, Lady Vereesa Windrunner."

"Good morning, Grand Magister." Jace and Vereesa greeted him together.

Belvar pointed at the sailboat. "As agreed, the Archmage Platform will present a mage-tower magic crystal as a token of gratitude. Today I will show you the various types of magic crystals, Master Jace Sesso, and you may choose one suited for Ilgala Tower. Please board."

Jace thanked him and followed several shield-bearing guards onto the sailboat. A high elven officer relayed orders to the crew in Thalassian. With a series of creaking timber sounds, the sails rose, and the hull swayed gently.

When the sailboat began sailing north, it was so stable that it didn't feel like traveling on the water at all. Jace, accustomed to the rocking of human merchant ships, felt something was off.

After the sailboat passed the northern tip of Sunstrider Isle, it turned to sail south. Before long, the tall golden-red spire of the Dath'Remar Shrine could be seen poking up among the distant trees.

Though this overall white, ornately decorated pagoda was quite eye-catching, Jace certainly hadn't come here to pay homage to the first Sun King.

"Where are we headed?" he asked, a little unable to contain himself.

The Grand Magister smiled and pointed at the lush Eversong Woods on the shore ahead. "We'll be entering the Eldarath River shortly, where we can see several of the most powerful mage towers in Quel'Thalas. Since those magic crystals aren't convenient to transport back and forth, and it's difficult to bring them all in front of us to view at once, we might as well go see the finished products on the towers directly."

So that was how it worked. Jace nodded. He'd actually been curious about this all along.

Transporting the magic crystal for the Nethergarde Keep mage tower had required the Quel'Thalas military to use an enormous transport wagon all the way to the Blasted Lands. Yet when the archmages had told Jace to come to Quel'Thalas to select a magic crystal, they'd made it sound as casual as asking him to pick out a staff from a shop counter.

This had kept him on edge, feeling like these archmages were going to hand him a few downscaled versions to get by. Now that they were going directly to see the mage towers, that sounded a great deal more reliable.

"These mage towers were all built to resist the Amani trolls' attacks," Belvar said, walking to the bow. "Before the Farstriders were established, those mage towers were just as important as the Ban'dinoriel."

With that, he bowed slightly to Vereesa. Hearing the Grand Magister praise the Farstrider corps like this, Vereesa looked quite pleased as well.

The Grand Magister continued, "Although the borders of Quel'Thalas have now been pushed forward by hundreds of miles, right to the foot of the Amani Mountains, these mage towers have continued to play an important role. Several years ago, when Orgrim Doomhammer and Zul'jin's evil coalition attacked Quel'Thalas, the outermost magical barrier was destroyed by the red dragons, and those ancient mage towers stepped forward once more, forming the last line of defense protecting Silvermoon together with the runestones. Thanks to them, Quel'Thalas was spared the fate of being completely burned to the ground."

"There are many types of magic crystals. Some can help mages channel destructive evocation magic, sustain powerful arcane barriers — like the one you're familiar with that supported the Dark Portal campaign. Some can channel the power of the Sunwell and the ley lines to take the initiative against invading enemies. Some are better at sustaining teleportation magic. And some are capable of devouring and transforming dark forces, resisting the corrupted energy summoned by those demons and orcs."

Yes, it was the last kind. Jace rubbed his fingers together, thinking to himself. This was exactly what he needed.
