Chapter 1110: The Three Moons

Jace knew exactly what he needed. Ilgala Tower itself sat atop a powerful energy source — a ley line node of Azeroth. Once he summoned Karlez, the demon's fel blood would also be able to supply fel and shadow forces powerful enough. All he needed was a mana stabilizer to make these energies coexist in harmony — he didn't need a "cannon" or another super-battery that would only make the energy more chaotic.

Right now, the most important thing was stabilizing the teleportation magic to Draenor, so they wouldn't be lost forever in the Twisting Nether.

Leaving Sunstrider Isle, they continued south toward the shore of the Eversong Woods. From here, quite a few distant islands were still visible, and the mage towers standing like lighthouses on them released a pale blue arcane glow clearly visible even in daylight.

Those islands varied in size. Some looked as large as Sunstrider Isle, but Jace didn't recognize a single one.

In his memory, he couldn't recall any other large islands in all of Quel'Thalas besides Sunstrider Isle and Quel'Danas Isle where the Sunwell was located — but clearly, the distant islands visible at sea right now were everywhere to be seen.

Those islands weren't uninhabited and barren. Some had mage towers perched on high cliffs and mountains, and some even had harbors built on them, with villages or estates large and small that should exist above. Perhaps this was the reason why, after the Scourge ravaged Quel'Thalas over a decade later, the surviving high elves could still rebuild their homeland.

Right now, the archmages on the boat were chatting about amusing anecdotes on Quel'Danas Isle. From what they were saying, it was clear that the Sunwell and the Archmage Platform were only a very small part of Quel'Danas Isle, located at the island's southwest corner — and that Sunwell Plateau didn't refer to the name of the palace guarding the Sunwell, but an actual plateau.

Judging from what one of the archmages said, traveling on foot from Sunport and the Morningstar Village near the Sunwell heading north, it took about two or three days of hiking to reach the northern cape of Quel'Danas Isle. This surprised Jace greatly, because in his memory, Quel'Danas was a small island, and the Sunwell and Morningstar Village were already at the island's northern end — further north was nothing but the harbor and open sea.

Judged this way, this island seemed to be nearly half the size of all of Quel'Thalas. Not only was it much larger, it even seemed to be the complete opposite direction from the game.

Previously he'd encountered situations where the game and reality were quite different, but this was the first time the directions were completely reversed.

But then these archmages went on to mention that there were naga prowling near Quel'Danas, looking for ways to break through the Ban'dinoriel's protection and land at the Greenscale Coast on the eastern and southern sides of Quel'Danas — and this allowed Jace to roughly piece together the general shape of this island in his mind by cross-referencing the game.

In truth, Quel'Danas here wasn't the opposite of the game — it had simply gained a large stretch of wilderness and forest that didn't exist in the game. This area was under the jurisdiction of the Archmage Platform, and out of reverence for the sacred status of Quel'Danas Isle and the Sunwell, no elves had been permitted to settle or live there, so it had largely remained in its primitive state. Perhaps that was why the game had simply omitted this part.

At twilight, the sailboat reached a river mouth. Jace thought this was the mouth of the Eldarath River, but the sailboat showed no sign of turning — it continued sailing south.

Grand Magister Belvar had just been communicating with a crew member about something and was now finally at leisure. Jace walked over and asked, "Isn't this the Eldarath River?"

"No. This river is the Three Moons River, Master Sesso." Belvar explained. "The Eldarath River is further south. We should arrive there tonight. We'll rest for the night at Goldmist Village, then set out east into the river channel tomorrow."

The Three Moons didn't refer to the third month of the year, but to the three Moon Temples — Andulos, Anoven, and Anthelas.

These three temples were hidden in the forests and meadows south of the Eldarath River, protected by magical barriers and runestones, and additionally guarded by heavy troops. All of Quel'Thalas's residents knew these three temples were of paramount importance, but very few people knew what they actually did — even the temple guards were the same.

In reality, these three Moon Temples were built upon Quel'Thalas's ley line nodes, closely linked with the Sunwell, and together they wove the Ban'dinoriel itself. If the energy cores of the three temples — the "Moon Crystals" — were deactivated, then the Ban'dinoriel would collapse along with them. Therefore, these three Moon Crystals were also known as the "Three Moon Keys."

This was quite possibly the most important secret in all of Quel'Thalas, known only to the topmost quel'dorei leaders. Jace looked toward Vereesa not far away. She was gazing at the Eversong Woods on the distant shore bathed in a layer of golden light. Jace suspected that even she might not be clear on this matter.

Unfortunately, Dar'Khan Drathir, who had been deeply involved in the defense of southern Quel'Thalas, not only knew this secret but even knew how to destroy this barrier. It was precisely for this reason that Arthas and his Scourge had easily accomplished something even Gul'dan couldn't — completely destroying the Ban'dinoriel and striking straight at Silvermoon.

Noticing Jace's thoughtful expression, Belvar asked, "What is it, Master Sesso?"

Jace absolutely couldn't reveal even a trace that he knew about this matter. If the archmages here learned he knew all this, they probably wouldn't let him leave Quel'Thalas alive — no matter what kind of hero he was, or what kind of dark warlock.

"It's a very poetic name," he said.

"I think so too," Belvar said in a low voice.

Jace could sense a flicker of worry pass through the Grand Magister's heart, perhaps related to the Three Moons Temples.

Dar'Khan Drathir — this senior archmage who had nearly destroyed the trust between Stormwind and Silvermoon out of greed for dark power — actually knew a vital secret that endangered all of Quel'Thalas. If Jace were in his position, he couldn't help but worry about it either.

The sailboat slowly passed Sunsail Anchorage at the mouth of the Eldarath River in the afterglow, and both of their attention was drawn over.

The radiance of the Sunwell made this river mouth and the forest on the shore extraordinarily magnificent and beautiful. The harbor was also enormous, with layers upon layers of colorful, gorgeous sails stacked one on top of another. Compared to it, that little harbor on Sunstrider Isle seemed far too crude.

Besides the Sunstrider phoenix banners at the harbor, there were even quite a few Lordaeron Alliance blue and white flags fluttering about.

The royal crest of Lordaeron looked somewhat out of place and odd on this golden-white, curvaceously elegant elven harbor — but considering it more or less represented that Quel'Thalas was still willing to remain within the Alliance, it wasn't all that jarring.
