Chapter 1111: Controlling the Chaos

Night fell from the south. When the darkness spread close, it was held at bay by the red glow from the north, unable to advance even an inch further.

Goldmist Village was right before their eyes. Though it was a village, every settlement in Quel'Thalas was like a meticulously dressed-up garden. The nearby dock was also a beautiful work of art, a perfect embellishment extending this village to the coast.

The archmages' sailboat drew a great deal of attention as it docked at Goldmist Village harbor. After all, archmages held very high status in Silvermoon and all of Quel'Thalas — let alone the Grand Magister himself.

Jace followed Belvar's group off the boat and through the square under the gaze of many residents, travelers, and dock workers. There wasn't a single human here — not even a single half-elf. Jace felt like he was being tracked by countless cameras flickering with arcane light, his whole body so uncomfortable that he couldn't help but pull his hood down lower.

Last time he came to Quel'Thalas, at least he'd had Grede by his side. A cheerful dwarf who'd drawn off too much "radar illumination" for him. Now he was taking the full brunt of it alone.

What's more, back then most of the elves attending the Ranger Lord promotion ceremony were nobles of Silvermoon. Even if everyone was curious or whatever about an outsider being present, they at least put on an air of politeness. The residents of Goldmisc Village didn't bother with any of that. A human had swaggered right in — they had to get a good, close look at what this fellow was up to.

Jace and Vereesa, led by the archmages, came to a white palace by the harbor to rest. Spending the night in this palace, one could sit on the terrace outside, drink, and enjoy the sea view bathed in a layer of gold in the distance. There were even wine attendants who proactively served crystal-clear fruits Jace had never seen before, fresh-caught and freshly prepared delicious seafood. If one wasn't satisfied, they could even pick from an entire menu written in Thalassian — and Jace didn't even recognize the names of most of the dishes.

Of course, Jace wasn't quite used to being waited on like this either. After picking up a bit of knowledge from the menu, he declined the palace's services. Being able to sprawl out on a lounge chair on the terrace, facing the deep red sea, and sleep soundly was already unimaginably comfortable for him.

Just then came the sound of bare footsteps from behind. Jace turned his head to see Vereesa walking over with a wicked grin. She sat down by Jace's legs and said, "Mm, you're about the same, Jace."

"What?" Jace asked.

Vereesa leaned over, reached out, picked up a small fruit, and tossed it into her mouth, chewing as she spoke. "Remember when I went to Stormwind to find you about the death knight? That was my first time entering a human city alone. I was just a little shy, and that look of disdain on your face — you thought I couldn't tell? I'll never forget it for the rest of my life."

"And yet you're the same, hiding and skittish like a little kitten. Or is this a warlock thing, hmm?"

"Hmph." Jace snorted, picked up a small reddish-orange fruit, and took a bite. Vereesa laughed triumphantly for a while before catching her breath.

"How is this?" she asked, staring at Jace's lips, mouth slightly open. "Is it good?"

"Very sweet, and the sourness is just right." Jace ate the remaining half of the fruit. "What's this called?"

"Sithanpondivelos," Vereesa said.

What the hell? Jace choked on the juice and mumbled, "I can't understand that. Fire something?"

"Next time I'll give you a proper lesson on the culture and cuisine in Thalassian. Just learning a bunch of random dirty words won't do." Vereesa brushed the brown hair by Jace's ear. "If this were translated into Common, it'd be something like Flame Grape. But I think this is more like some kind of cherry affected by the Sunwell's mana."

"Scary name." Jace nodded and replied in Thalassian, then swallowed another one.

"It's worth mentioning," Vereesa leaned in close. "This grape comes from Dar'Khan Drathir's birthplace — the eastern shore of Lake Eldarath at the foot of the Amani Mountains. A particularly beautiful place, and also the final stop of our journey — Morningstar Tower."

"Morningstar Tower belongs to Dar'Khan Drathir?" Jace asked in surprise.

"Of course not. It belongs to Morningstar now too." Vereesa picked up a crispy biscuit sprinkled with salt and crumbled cheese by her hand, eating as she spoke. "His mage tower is not far from there, up on the mountain. It's called Drathir's Terrace — he bought it from the Morningstar family a long time ago. That tower was originally a watchtower for observing Amani troll movements below the mountain. As the Farstriders and archmages together pushed the front line further south to Zeb'tela, and Lake Eldarath stabilized, Dar'Khan converted it into a laboratory for studying the Twisting Nether and troll sorcery. Because it's far from the heartland of Quel'Thalas, the Sun King didn't interfere too much."

"So when the time comes, we'll be going to that tower to investigate Dar'Khan's situation," Jace said.

"Not just there. Morningstar Tower also preserves some scrolls and records gathered from that tower." Vereesa lay down beside Jace. "The investigation had already begun before we came. If what Dar'Khan hid extends beyond those two towers..."

"So the place has already been searched once before us?" Jace thought about it. "I'm worried someone's already hidden everything they don't want us to know."

"Don't worry too much, Jace," Vereesa said. "We have to thank Mara for being such a fool as to be deceived by Dar'Khan — it gave the Farstrider corps ample reason to step in. And a Farstrider garrison is located on the southern shore of the lake, very close by. Besides, right now within the archmage ranks, nobody wants to be associated with Dar'Khan and Reshtadis. The Farstriders and archmages who helped Dar'Khan hunt the black knight in Winterspring are also under guard and investigation. I think it would still be quite difficult to secretly hoard the search results from that tower at this point."

She tilted her head and stared into Jace's eyes. "What do you think we'll find?"

"Knowledge related to Twisting Nether magic," Jace said. "Dar'Khan easily tore apart my shadow magic with some incantations I'd never seen before — it might be connected to his research. Also, I'm quite curious about what he found in Karazhan with the Violet Eye — for instance, how he knew about the connection between Medivh's black knight and Ursulus..."

He turned his head and asked, "Can we reach Morningstar Tower by tomorrow evening?"

"If it were a Farstrider boat, I think it'd be hard. After all, this means crossing half of Quel'Thalas, passing numerous mage towers and runestone sites." Vereesa leaned on her arm and lazily flicked her fingers, counting off the Thalassian names of those runestones one by one. "But if it's the Grand Magister's sailboat, everything's different. Just wait and see — I've never traveled on his ship either."

The ranger gave Jace a light kiss on the lips. "Tonight, you just need to rest well..."

The next day, the sea was still veiled in a faint blue shadow. Though it was around six in the morning, Goldmist Village harbor was still bustling. After all, elves were a race that didn't need much sleep — quite the complete opposite of Ironforge and Shadowforge City.

Jace crossed the square and hurried all the way to the boat, with Vereesa following behind. From the corner of his eye, he sensed a group of young elven nobles huddled together at the harbor, muttering something as they stared at the two of them. The displeasure radiating from them could be felt even from such a distance.

But Jace just glanced back at them, and those elves immediately avoided eye contact, quickly changing the subject and pretending to discuss other matters.

He hadn't expected every elf to bless his being with Vereesa. As long as Sylvanas Windrunner permitted it, other people's opinions didn't matter much.

Vereesa hadn't paid those elves any mind at all. She discussed the remainder of the journey with the archmages, then walked over happily and said, "The Grand Magister says we should reach our destination tonight. Rest assured, Jace."

"Good," Jace nodded.

The archmages didn't delay long. Once Jace and Vereesa were in position, they immediately set sail.

Last time, traveling from the Windrunner Village to the Farstrider sanctuary near Silvermoon, Jace had felt the Eldarath River was much broader than he remembered.

In another timeline over a decade later, when Arthas's Scourge army reached this river, Sylvanas had ordered the Farstriders to destroy that bridge, halting the death army's advance. Judging by the scale of that river in the game, Jace felt it would still be somewhat difficult to stop those undead soldiers from forcing their way across.

Only by looking at this river — broader even than the Nafeltari River — and gazing at the lush golden forests on both banks, could he somewhat appreciate Arthas's helplessness.

Not far along, a temple-like tall tower revealed itself from among the forest. The pale blue magic crystal floating above a golden base flickered in and out of sight among the swaying branches.

"The Endatost Crystal — also called the Mana Rain Crystal." Grand Magister Belvar said this as he walked to the edge of the deck, facing that mage tower. One hand held the Torrent Staff, the other gently raised, channeling a surge of arcane force in his palm.

Jace watched the slowly rotating crystal gradually decelerate, then suddenly release a brilliant arcane light with the Grand Magister's mana. A magical wind swept through the surrounding trees and plants, making them come alive, eagerly stretching their branches and leaves toward that crystal as if determined to soak up a bit more arcane energy no matter what.

Belvar relaxed his arm. The flickering radiance dimmed accordingly, and the magical wind eased.

He introduced it: "After the Orc Wars, this crystal has been stationed here all along, assisting the Sunwell and the ley lines in repairing the devastation wrought by the red dragons' flames."

"I can't see any trace of burning at all, Grand Magister," Jace said. "That's the power of the Endatost Crystal," Belvar said.

Jace nodded, though he didn't show much interest. After all, the gnoll forest and Stonewatch didn't need the repair of magical forces.

The sailboat continued forward. Rounding a lush forest, another tall, slender, and magnificent mage tower appeared ahead.

The crystal on this tower was dark blue — quite matching the "Deep Shadow crystal" the examiner had mentioned to him earlier. Jace immediately perked up and walked to the bow, craning his neck for a clearer look.

As the sailboat approached this mage tower, Belvar cast again to activate it. The moment the dark-blue arcane light released, Jace immediately felt a strange craving within it — an attempt to suck in all mana, whether shadow or arcane, and make it its own.

Could this be the one?

"The Vatiazkia Crystal — the Deep Shadow." Belvar asked as if reciting poetry. "Perhaps you're hoping to rely on this crystal to control the balance between shadow and arcane, Master Jace Sesso? This mage tower can help mages purify land corrupted by dark magic, cleanse tainted mana, and keep the ley lines and the Sunwell unaffected by the dark magic of trolls and orcs."

So this was the Deep Shadow crystal, Jace thought to himself.

But after seeing this magic crystal with his own eyes, he felt more and more that something was off. He needed a mana stabilizer, not a conversion stone that swallowed all mana into itself. And while the Deep Shadow crystal could indeed accommodate shadow forces — what about the fel energy within a doom lord's body?

"Arcane, shadow — the power of this crystal is astonishing, Grand Magister." Jace turned his head. "But what I'd prefer is a mana crystal capable of controlling these magical forces, while also... controlling demonic power."

"Controlling demonic power, fel..." Belvar pondered for a moment.

Jace explained further: "Ilgala Tower is very close to the Dark Portal. Before the Dark Portal was closed, mages sensitive to magic could almost directly feel the terrifying mana surging within that gate from there. And even now, there are still many demons roaming near the borders of the Kingdom of Stormwind."

"I see," Belvar said. "The Taralfsin Crystal."

"What's that used for?" Jace asked.

"It's hard to explain all at once, Master Sesso." Belvar raised his head slightly. "To put it simply, Dalaran has a Taralfsin Crystal that was gifted to us, and to this day it's still stabilizing the chaotic energies of the entire Violet Hold. Do you know the Violet Hold? I hope my Common pronunciation isn't off."
