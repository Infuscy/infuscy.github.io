## Chapter 1116: A Flower's Scent

Jace hadn't sensed anything off in the basement. This many arcanists, Magisters, and Ranger Farstriders had all searched down here — if there had been any magic barrier, it would have revealed itself during these past few days of investigation. It wouldn't wait for him to show up before making an appearance.

Actually, thinking it over, he was already feeling somewhat pessimistic. Most people with something to hide liked to stash sensitive things in the basement. That was true in the world of his past life, and it was about the same in Azeroth.

Of course, Dar'kan Drahelis was probably different. But Jace figured that was just him consoling himself.

He gave the basement one more careful sweep, checked the floor for hidden compartments and the walls for mechanisms. Truly sensing nothing, he followed Alionnis and Halduron Brightwing to the stairs and headed up toward the top of the tower.

"No need to rush, Mr. Jace Sesso," Halduron said, looking back. "The investigation has been going on for less than a week. A tower of this size — even if a dozen mages and Farstriders search it thoroughly, there are likely still places we've missed. Dar'kan is already dead, his assistants, servants, and students have all been taken into custody. We can afford to be patient and tear this place apart inch by inch. I'm quite optimistic, personally."

"That's true, Jace," Vereesa agreed with a nod. "If you want to find what a mage deliberately hid — especially a senior Magister like Dar'kan — you have to have patience. The Farstriders aren't handling a place like this for the first time either."

As they climbed the stairs, Jace nodded slowly.

Despite Halduron's reassurance, Jace couldn't feel cheered up in the slightest. If the investigation into Dar'kan really dragged on, he probably couldn't stay in Quel'Thalas waiting indefinitely. Which meant that if some sensitive results were actually uncovered while he wasn't around, the Magisters might very well have time to hide certain things.

It wasn't that he was unwilling to trust Vereesa's keen instincts — it was just that if the Magisters wanted to deceive them on a magical level, even the Farstrider forces would have a hard time detecting it. What's more, not everyone among the Farstriders was necessarily on his side either.

All the way up to the top of Drahelis's Tower, Jace observed the layout of each floor as he went. Partly this was to get a sense of the spatial distribution in preparation for searching floor by floor. Partly, he also wanted to see if there were any secret mechanisms.

But then, every floor had several elven mages and their assistants investigating. After this many days, there probably wasn't much chance of him being the one to spot a magic hidden door.

He even suspected that some things had already been moved away by the Magisters during the past few days, which was why he glanced at Vereesa at his side, planning to have her help dig up some internal information from the Magisters after they left this tower.

At least Sylvanas would still be willing to stand on his side — perhaps he could use the pretext of reopening the Draenor portal to ask her to help… With a Ranger-General of Silvermoon overseeing things, it wouldn't be so easy for some Magister to hide the results of Dar'kan's investigation.

With these thoughts in mind, the four of them had already reached the top of the tower — the magic stone pedestal of Drahelis's Tower. The wind howled here, offering a bird's-eye view of most of the Elrendar River. The Sunstar Tower not far off was also laid out clearly below, while the courtyard, manor, flowerbeds, and garden plots from before had all shrunk into little colored squares.

The magic stone on the pedestal beside them had vanished. Jace asked, "Where did this magic crystal go?"

Alionnis pointed toward the Sunstar Tower down the mountain. "Inside there. Dar'kan's magic crystal was closely tied to the energy operation of the entire tower. We had to move it far away from this tower to ensure a complete severing of the magical link before we could safely conduct investigation work inside, Mr. Jace Sesso. After all, every mage tower in Quel'Thalas is influenced by the Sunwell, the runestones, and Bandinoriel — it's difficult to shut them all down completely by simple means."

The Magister had that lecturing teacher tone, as if the people on this side knew nothing at all.

Jace glanced at him and looked back toward the distance. The morning breeze over the lake swept through the mountain forests, stirring up layers of waves — just watching it made one feel relaxed, forgetting that one was standing in the residence of a dark Magister.

But in any case, there was nothing here that Jace was looking for. Aside from two Farstriding standing guard at the top, there was nothing else. That empty magic stone pedestal, plus Alionnis's attitude, gave Jace an even stronger feeling — this tower was already a picked-over ruin. The useful things had probably all been taken away.

"Let's look inside the tower, Mr. Jace Sesso?" Halduron suggested.

Jace nodded. In a normal mage tower, the top section might be more important than the more spacious lower floors. Though he somewhat doubted a Shadow researcher would be so brazen as to keep his materials in the highest room of the entire tower.

Leaving the magic stone pedestal and descending the stairs, Jace pushed open the door to the top-floor hall and caught a familiar scent.

This was by no means the miasma of Shadow — it was a floral fragrance that made him feel profoundly at ease, even comforting. Because to him, this scent represented more than just a flower or an herb — it represented…

Jace took a small journal from his waist pouch, opened it, and pinched up a dried purple flower that had been pressed inside. Vereesa glanced over casually and started upon seeing the flower in Jace's hand.

"You've been… keeping it?"

"Of course," Jace said. He looked at Vereesa, gave it a gentle sniff, and confirmed it — this was the very same scent.

This room carried the fragrance of the "Flower of Sorrow" — the Plaguebloom.

But it wasn't purely the scent of Plaguebloom.

"Purification Potion has been used here," Jace said. "And quite high-quality at that."

Vereesa blinked, then looked at Alionnis. The Magister and Halduron exchanged a glance, then looked toward two elven mages nearby who were examining items inside the room.

"We've had the same suspicion, Magister," one of the mages said in Thalassian. "But we haven't found any item in this room that would require the use of Purification Potion."

"The thing that was purified may have been taken away," Halduron said.

"If it could be taken away, then why bother using Purification Potion?" Vereesa asked. "Are you certain every one of Dar'kan's accomplices and subordinates has been taken into custody?"

"At least according to the relationship information we've been able to find, yes, Lady Vereesa," Alionnis said. "The Magisters take this matter very seriously."

"Perhaps to avoid leaving too many foul traces when moving the thing out of this place," Halduron said, stroking the fine stubble on his cheek. "It's easy to understand — no matter what it is, purifying it first makes it much easier to slip past inspections."

Jace sniffed around a few more times, then lowered himself and sniffed at several of the stone floor tiles. He wanted to find where the Purification Potion had been applied — it seemed to be on the floor, but it was hard to be certain.

The scent of Plaguebloom was already difficult to detect to begin with. Combined with being brewed into Purification Potion and all the days that had passed, the scent trail was already extremely faint.

He looked at Halduron before him, then glanced at the Magister at his side. "Could I call in one of my own helpers?"

"Uh," Alionnis said hesitantly. "If it were a Sin'dorei, certainly. But if it were an outsider, it would involve an extremely complicated process of procedures and approvals — unimaginably complicated."

Vereesa asked, "You're not thinking of…"

"I mean summoning a felhound," Jace said bluntly. "Whatever's here…"

"No, no, no," Halduron cut him off immediately. "I understand, Master Sesso. You're quite skilled at using demons to help, but that is absolutely not possible here. I don't care what Alionnis thinks — at least I have no issue cooperating with a well-meaning demon. The problem is Bandinoriel."

"Setting all else aside, Master Sesso, Captain Halduron is right," Alionnis said with a summoning a living demon from the Twisting Nether within the protective range of Bandinoriel is fundamentally impossible. You'd be going up against the power of the Sunwell itself. And along the banks of the Elrendar River, near this lake, there are several runestones. The density of the magic barriers here isn't much weaker than up north."

Vereesa ran her fingers along the floor for a while, then stood up, rubbed her fingers together, and sniffed them. "Then we could get a botanist to help."

"Ah, that would be much simpler," Halduron said with a nod. "There's a botanist at the Sunstar Tower — we can bring him here."

He asked the Magister, "I think I saw him in the garden just now, Tanionrel. I'm not very familiar with him — how's your relationship with him?"

"About the same as yours," Alionnis pressed his lips together and said softly. "But the investigation of Drahelis's Tower was ordered jointly by Archmage Belovarl Saronar and Ranger-General Sylvanas Windrunner. There is nothing in Quel'Thalas more important than this right now. If we request his presence in the Archmage's name, Captain Halduron, he'll certainly agree."

Halduron chuckled. "As long as you're willing to take responsibility, then I'll go take a look." With that, he nodded quickly to Jace and Vereesa and walked out of the room.

Jace listened to the sound of Halduron's quick footsteps running down the stairs, then looked at Magister Alionnis.

He could sense that this Magister seemed quite enthusiastic about this new lead. Judging from this, his suspicion that the Magisters intended to hide information about Dar'kan's secrets didn't seem all that solid after all.
