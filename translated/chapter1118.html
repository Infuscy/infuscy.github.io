Chapter 1118: Remnants of the Orc Wars

The runestone of Caer Darrow?

Jace seemed to recall hearing someone mention that there was indeed an elven runestone in the Weald region, and that it had been destroyed by Gul'dan's army during the Orc Wars. But he hadn't realized there was one as far away as Caer Darrow, so distant from Quel'Thalas.

After all, Caer Darrow… wasn't far from Hillsbrad or Durnholde at all. When the Horde's armies reached the continent of Lordaeron, Caer Darrow was one of the earlier strongholds to come under attack.

"But, Lady Vereesa," Alionnis said. "The runestone of Caer Darrow was already transformed by Gul'dan into a Stormsummoning Altar used to summon demons and create evil creatures. Now only the ruins of that altar remain near Caer Darrow. So whether that runestone can even still function is questionable. This is also why the Archmage and the Sun King haven't paid much attention to that evil runestone."

"But there's a teleportation circle here, Alionnis — or rather, a receiving circle," Halduron said, glancing at Jace with a slight nod. "This shows that, at least in the eyes of whoever made it, summoning with Shadow magic within Bandinoriel was still feasible."

"Maybe he simply doesn't understand the power of Bandinoriel," Alionnis said flatly.

Halduron said, "A person who doesn't understand the power of Bandinoriel snuck into Dar'kan Drahelis's mage tower after Dar'kan's death, before you and I investigators did, and climbed all the way to the most crucial top floor to teleport something using dark magic? Don't forget what dangers we had to deal with when we broke in. He's either someone whose understanding of Arcane and Shadow is no less than Prince Kael'thas's or the Archmage's, or he's Dar'kan's closest subordinate or friend."

Alionnis pondered for a moment, then looked back at Jace. "Can you be certain, Master Sesso? I mean, about the analysis of this suspected circle."

"Besides this, I can't think of any other possibility," Jace said. "This does indeed resemble the orcs' Summoned Circle. Look at these large areas smeared with Purification Potion, Master Alionnis. Though we can't confirm whether these are the positions of runes, this does very much resemble the orcs' habit of placing demonic runes."

Halduron continued, "At the very least, neither you nor I have the knowledge to figure out what other use it might have. What's more, it was very likely made with demon blood or some other material bizarre enough to require wiping away with Purification Potion. In this regard, Mr. Jace Sesso — as His Majesty King Wrynn's advisor on dark magic — is probably more reliable than us grasping at straws here. Or should we wait a few days for the Archmage to come back and take another look?"

"In a few more days, the lingering scent of the Sorrowful Flower will have vanished as well, everyone," Tanionrel said, sniffing his fingers.

"More than that," Alionnis said. "If this really is a teleportation circle, all the important materials and evidence may have been moved away days ago. The longer we delay, the lower our chances of finding those things. Who knows who Dar'kan Drahelis was in contact with on the outside…"

"Then Jace and I will head to Caer Darrow together," Vereesa said. "Alionnis, you and the Magisters stay here and search through these books and scrolls. Master Halduron, it would be best to find a trustworthy Shadow mage to come look at this suspected circle. I recall there are Shadow mages living at the Moon Temple south of Tranquilliin and in Corona Village."

"If you really are heading to Caer Darrow, Lady Vereesa," Alionnis said, "you'd best bring an additional helper along. Because if this truly is a circle used to respond to a summoning ritual, who knows what might be hidden at the site where the summoning ritual was initiated. Don't forget what Dar'kan was researching…"

"Yes, and preferably a Magister," Halduron added immediately.

Vereesa glanced at Jace. Jace said, "Indeed, having a mage to help would be best."

"Then please recommend one, Magister," Vereesa said with a nod.

Alionnis thought for a moment and said, "I have a quite talented student and enchanter — Aeradhnia. She has been responsible at the Sunstar Tower lately for helping to break the Arcane wards and magic locks on items found in this tower. If there are more magical troubles at Caer Darrow, she might be even more useful than if I went along."

Vereesa said, "Aeradhnia — I think I've heard of this enchanter. Since she's someone you trust, Magister, then please have her come help. I hope she's willing to lend us her aid."

"Don't forget she's already a member of this investigation team, my lady," Alionnis said.

The Magister and Halduron gave instructions for the work at Drahelis's Tower, then returned to the Sunstar Tower with Jace and Vereesa, heading to the floor where the investigators were temporarily quartered.

Alionnis called out to a female mage. "I have an assignment for you, Lady Aeradhnia."

She was an elf of modest height with dark blue curly hair and skin with a faintly reddish tinge, wearing a pale yellow robe of a rather simple style among Magisters.

Just from watching her walk and work, one could tell she was a remarkably capable and energetic woman of action.

"What is your order, teacher?" She walked up to the group and saluted the two to the side. "Lady Vereesa, Captain Halduron."

Then she noticed the human in the black cloak beside the Magister. "You must be Mr. Jace Sesso. Welcome to Lake Elrendar."

"Thank you," Jace said with a slight nod. "Lady Aeradhnia."

"Then we needn't introduce each other," Alionnis said. "At present, we suspect that Dar'kan Drahelis moved some of the tower's important evidence to Caer Darrow. Lady Vereesa and Master Sesso intend to go investigate in person. They need a Magister who understands runestones and Bandinoriel to assist, which is why I thought of you."

"Truly an honor, teacher," Aeradhnia said with a puzzled expression. "But do you mean Dar'kan might have transferred that evidence using teleportation magic? The runestone at Caer Darrow has already been dismantled and tainted by Shadow magic — it may hardly function like a normal runestone…"

"That is precisely the problem you would help us investigate," Alionnis said in a low voice. "After all, based on my knowledge of runestone history, this is not entirely impossible. If Dar'kan and his accomplices really did exploit a damaged runestone to create some kind of vulnerability, then this isn't just some dark magic and evil dealings by one or two individuals — it becomes a potential threat to all of Quel'Thalas."

"I understand," the female Magister said with a nod. "I will do everything in my power to help you complete the investigation, Lady Vereesa, Master Sesso."

"I'm delighted to work with you, Lady Aeradhnia," Vereesa said with a smile.

"We should set out as soon as possible — there's no time to waste," Jace said, glancing at his pocket watch. It was just past midday. "Could we arrange a teleport to Caer Darrow?" Vereesa asked.

"We could, but it would take a long time," Alionnis said, glancing at Halduron beside him. "We'd need to contact the royal mages of Lordaeron first and communicate the matter. By the time that's done, it might take long enough for you to have already flown to Khaz Modan on a dragonhawk. So unless it's for deploying troops, best not to consider it."

Vereesa frowned. "Haven't the Farstrider forces been in Lordaeron helping them hunt orcs all this time? And there still isn't enough trust built up?"

Halduron shrugged, folding his arms. "Given how cautious we are about teleportation magic within our own borders at present, it's hard to criticize the Lordaerons for their prudence."

Jace asked, "Can we leave on dragonhawks right now, then?"

Alionnis said, "I can at least arrange to have you teleported to the Gate of Quel'Thalas. From there you can go to Quel'Lithien and ask the dragonhawk riders for help getting to Caer Darrow."

Vereesa rubbed her forehead. "Damn it, I can't mobilize the dragonhawk riders of Quel'Lithien on my own."

Halduron said, "Send word to your sister. By the time you reach Quel'Lithien, it should be arranged."

"We don't need to go through all that trouble," Alionnis said, pointing upward. "You forgot — this is the Sunstar Tower. A dragonhawk rider commander is right at the top."

Arranged by Ista Sunstar, Jace, Vereesa, and Magister Aeradhnia left Quel'Thalas and the next morning flew directly to Caer Darrow on dragonhawks.

Leaving the perpetually spring-like elven kingdom, the further south they went, the more bitterly cold it became. The Eastern Weald region, where spring had only just started, still seemed to be locked in a deep freeze — never mind flying on the back of a dragonhawk against the wind.

Jace wrapped his cloak tightly around himself, relying on the circulation of the red dragon's blood and the lingering thread of Arcane magic he still carried from having just left the Sunwell to keep from freezing solid.

Along the way, the thick-clothed travelers below and the vast stretches of dark green pine forests flying past only intensified the psychological chill.

At this point he couldn't help but recall how comfortable it had been when Sola carried him, protected by the power of Nature. Never mind flying over the great pine forests of eastern Lordaeron — even flying across the tundra of Northrend wouldn't have been a problem.

Thinking of this, he couldn't help but wonder where Grede and Sola were now.

By now they should have reached Ashenvale already. Perhaps the essence of Eranikus had already been cleansed successfully by the night elf druids in Moonglade… He wondered if they'd be willing to help cleanse that Felcloth while they were at it?

And of course, the black dragon sinew. If those green dragons or night elves were willing to help craft that legendary bow for Vereesa, he wondered what her reaction would be.

He glanced at the Ranger-Lord riding a dragonhawk not far ahead. She was also turning her head to glance this way at the moment, and when she saw Jace looking right back at her, she narrowed her eyes and smiled.

In a flash, the forest beneath them suddenly seemed to be pulled away, replaced by a dark green lake.

In the distance, on the lake-island occupied by walls and fortifications, the white royal banners of Lordaeron fluttered in the lake breeze. Two Gryphon Riders seemed to have noticed the three dragonhawks from this direction — they slowed their flight and came toward them.

They had arrived at Caer Darrow.
