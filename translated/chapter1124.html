Chapter 1124: If Not Here, Then Where?

Walking deep into the woods, Jace lifted Ursaleth off his back and into his hand, speaking low in Demonic. "I don't have any soul shards right now, so… it shouldn't be a problem for you to contribute a little from the souls you drained in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar."

Ursaleth gave no response at all, as silent as if it had died, but Jace wasn't surprised.

He glanced at the figures outside the woods — right now the two elves and the Barov people over there couldn't see clearly.

Jace took out his spellbook and reviewed the succubus-summoning incantation once more, then said, "Yinhatet, Tulioksenoni, Tayatansayidini, Binoduquis."

In a flash, a void rift tore open, and a succubus with fluttering red wings stepped out.

This succubus had been all smiles of curiosity at the moment she appeared, but when she saw the staff in Jace's hand oozing dark red blood, her legs immediately began to tremble, and she turned to melt into the shadows and flee.

Though this succubus's magic was far stronger than an ordinary succubus's, there was no way she could escape Jace's eyes. The succubus fleeing through the shadows was effortlessly pinned to the ground by a single Asphyxiate from him.

The succubus immediately became visible again, and with a tearful voice she looked up at Jace approaching and said in Demonic, "Spare me, demon-soul master! I was wrong to greedily accept the summoning, drunk on your mana… please let me go!"

"Shut up." Jace pressed his voice low and vicious.

The succubus immediately bit her lip shut. Jace raised his head and observed the outside of the woods, making sure no one had heard the succubus's crying.

"Help me influence a mortal's mind with psychic magic, Sayad," Jace pressed the succubus's neck and said in Demonic. "Don't actually control his mind. I know you can subtly influence a mortal's mind while staying invisible — I only need you to do that much. As long as I get the intelligence I want, you can get out of here."

"Remember, don't let anyone else discover you, and don't complicate this. If anyone else becomes aware of your existence, your soul had better forget about ever returning to the Twisting Nether."

The succubus nodded vigorously. Jace let go and snapped his fingers. "Vanish."

The demon immediately hid herself in the shadows. She was so focused that even the shadows Jace could see were less distinct than before. Certain she was well hidden, Jace walked back out of the woods and glanced over toward Sylvanas's direction.

Sylvanas and Aeridania were looking at the Storm Altar and discussing something, unaware that Jace had come out. Summoning a second succubus right in front of Sylvanas… honestly, it was a bit thrilling.

But the circumstances demanded it, and Jace didn't have time to draw blood on the spot to drag Morofir all the way over from Stormwind. Besides, she had to watch over his more than two hundred gold coins — relying on just Sazeno the imp, Jace couldn't exactly rest easy.

After coming out of the woods, Jace straightened his clothes and strolled over toward Alexei with a casual air.

As he walked, he even put on a show of observing the island's forest, the orcish remnants of war — as if he were trying to find something more in these ruins that had been picked over who knew how many times.

Young Alexei noticed Jace approaching and gave a slight nod. "Is anything unusual about this altar, Magister Jace Sesso? I hope I can be of help to you and the two elven ladies."

Jace said, "I heard that Caer Darrow experienced some plagues and panics connected to dark magic before. It seems to have something to do with these stones."

"That's right," young Alexei said. "But the Kirin Tor already helped us resolve that crisis, magister. Nothing similar has happened in the four or five years since."

"Didn't the Kirin Tor come to inspect this island?" Jace said. "There might still be some bricks and stones in this pile contaminated by the orcs. If left alone, they'll pollute this land further, and might even pollute the lake water. The consequences are hard to estimate."

A trace of wariness brushed through young Alexei's mind, and his impatience dissipated along with it.

He said, "The Kirin Tor has already inspected this island and dealt with the remaining dark magic-related materials on it — and it was overseen by the Kirin Tor's Archmage Kel'Thuzad. You could go to Dalaran and ask him for the specifics."

"Kel'Thuzad has already been locked in the Violet Hold, Mr. Alexei Barov," Jace said. "And I'm not that familiar with Dalaran either."

Young Alexei gave an awkward laugh. "I'm sorry, magister, I had no knowledge of his misfortune. But whatever crimes he committed certainly have nothing to do with our family. The dark magic on this island would bring pollution to Caer Darrow and the surrounding lands. If left unchecked, the nearby residents would all move away, and then attract demons and more orcs — and Caer Darrow would be finished. That's something none of us want to see. The Barov family has been actively cooperating with the Kirin Tor to protect Darrowmere Lake and the surrounding areas."

Jace narrowed his eyes and stared at the broken statue on the Storm Altar. "I could help too, sir — do my part to purify the orc contamination on this island."

"Uh, thank you very much," young Alexei said with a strained laugh. "But I'm not sure what you're looking to…"

"If there are still stones laced with shadow magic on this altar, I'm willing to trade with whatever I can offer for them, Mr. Alexei Barov," Jace said. "You know Stormwind Kingdom sits right beside the Dark Portal. King Varyan Wrynn is harassed day and night by orc warlocks, demon magic, and black dragons. Research on Storm Altars would help us find the weaknesses of the orc warlocks and aid us in hunting the enemies roaming Elwynn and Redridge."

Young Alexei was slightly taken aback by Jace's speech, then said hesitantly, "I'm not very clear on this, magister."

He glanced at the two high elves in the distance. Jace could clearly feel his doubt, his caution — but whatever the emotion, it had already been severely shaken by another emotion: greed, and not just one kind of greed.

Jace could even feel the dark power brought by this emotion within arm's reach, almost enough that he could have wrenched it out and rolled it into a Shadowbolt.

"Don't mind them, Alexei," he leaned in a little and said quietly. "They're here to investigate a case. I'm here for King Varian's interests. I couldn't care less about whatever trouble Lordaeron or Quel'Thalas is in."

Young Alexei swallowed lightly, and seemed to let his guard down bit by bit.

At least Jace's lousy reputation as a black wizard could occasionally come in useful — making people believe he used shadow magic for good deeds might not be so easy, but making people believe he was actually using good deeds to cover up his true dark desires was relatively a lot simpler.

"I understand, Magister Sesso," young Alexei said with a hint of helplessness. "But this Storm Altar really doesn't have many stones imbued with magical power left. I'm not hiding anything. If we found anything else worth looking at left behind by the orc warlocks or demons, we'd certainly find a way to contact you."

As he spoke, he glanced toward the woods not far off — exactly the direction those people dismantling the camp had left in last night.

This was entirely within Jace's expectations. After all, Aeridania had already confirmed that nearly all the rune-carved stones on this altar had been carried away.

He'd actually been counting on young Alexei saying exactly this, so he could lead into the next question — because what he cared about wasn't whether he could buy stones from the Barov family at all, but tracking down where those special stones had all gone.

"Were all those stones taken by the Kirin Tor to be destroyed?" Jace asked.

Young Alexei took a deep breath, as if he didn't really want to answer this question. So this was the moment.

Jace swept his demon-detecting gaze toward the succubus hiding to the side. The succubus felt Jace's look and confirmed the time had come.

Young Alexei heaved a sigh and pinched the bridge of his nose hard, his expression rather unpleasant. A force of shadow clamped around his neck and brain like a pair of pliers — this succubus was really putting in the effort to be of help to Jace.

Jace was even a little worried whether this shadow power might leave some permanent damage on the young Barov's mind. If it really left a trace, it wouldn't be much fun when the Kirin Tor came investigating later.

Dath'remar, dark magic, the Void… no matter what it was, once it got tangled up with the Kirin Tor, things became extremely troublesome.

Alexei scratched his hair and suddenly said in a low voice, "Not all of the stones, Magister Sesso. There were warlocks from Stratholme who… bought several of them. They paid a lot, a lot of money, and they could keep it confidential. Perhaps I shouldn't be telling you this, but… I know Father has always been willing to make friends with a great mage like you, I know, so…"

Jace stared at a few Barov servants who kept glancing over from nearby. "I need a name, sir."

He asked a little urgently, after all — they couldn't drag this on too long. Young Alexei wasn't a Nekros-type orc warlord who controlled dark magic. Pushing it like this for too long could lead to an accident.

"Marysia," Alexei said. "Marysia… she's also an elf, a magister, I know."

"Thank you very much." Jace spoke as he gave the succubus's direction a look, signaling her she could wrap it up.
