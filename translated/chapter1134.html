Chapter 1134: Shadow Hideout

The fact that this gate resembled the architectural style of the Scourge citadels didn't mean this place had anything to do with the undead Scourge, because those citadels were all magical fortresses built by the nerubians of Northrend, modeled after the underground kingdom of Azjol-Nerub.

There were no skeletons here, or death-related ornamentation, so this was probably simply an ancient ruin left behind by the nerubians. In other words — could there be an ancient spider kingdom buried beneath Stratholme?

Could those dark kin, abandoned by the Old Gods, have once lived beneath the city that most fervently believed in the Holy Light across all of Lordaeron?

Wait — or was it actually the other way around. Could it be that the humans who'd settled here in ancient times had turned to the path of the Holy Light precisely to combat the dark shadow magic of these nerubians lurking beneath their new home?

Jace had always been too preoccupied with the Holy Light faith to bother studying much about Stratholme's founding history, and besides, that wasn't the point right now.

Judging by the size of this gate, it didn't look like the entrance to some vast underground metropolis either. More than likely, long ago this had just been a hidden underground chamber of the spider kingdom, discovered by whoever built the villa and quietly put to their own use.

"How are we supposed to open this gate?"

Vereesa spoke softly and stepped closer. Suddenly, the clustered eyes on the insect-beast face of the gate lit up like gems, emitting a faint green glow.

"Seeker of darkness... seeking... what?" the face asked in labored Thalassian. Vereesa glanced back at Aradnia, who shook her head.

"Telogrus," Jace pronounced in Thalassian.

After he said it, he stared tensely at the gate's reaction, ready to resort to brute force if necessary.

Telogrus was the birthplace of the void elves as a race in the game — the name of the void rift that Dar'Khan's research had always sought to explore.

Jace didn't know whether this was the answer the face was looking for, but since it had asked in Thalassian rather than the Sha'tharik spoken by the Old Gods' servants, that was enough to confirm the face was addressing elves.

"Telogrus... indeed." The face murmured, and the great gate rumbled open.

So that was it. Jace's anxious heart settled slightly.

Both elves stared at Jace in shock. Jace glanced back and forth between them. "We'll explain outside."

Vereesa nodded. Aradnia suddenly looked deeper inside and said, "I feel it again — the runestone's power."

"Then this is the place," Vereesa said softly.

Passing through the strange gate, they stepped into an ancient temple. Jace really did feel like he'd walked into a miniature Naxxramas.

Beneath the pale blue arcane braziers on either side, dark grey brick walls, reliefs and doorframes shaped like bones or spider legs, stone pillars covered in strange runes and spider motifs.

"You've finally come around? We must find time to return to Kalimor with the things he left behind, Theraldri." A hollow female voice came from within, speaking in Thalassian. "Neither Lordaeron nor Khaz Modan has a suitable place for us anymore. Belovar Sarona will find this place sooner or later, and that Brill man probably doesn't know much more than we do."

"Besides, the runestone is useless now. Let go of your Bandinorel sentimentality and stop wasting energy maintaining it. Let Alonsus Faol figure it out himself."

The sound of boots clicking on stone floor came closer, and a female High Elf in a grey robe with silver hair appeared smiling in the doorway ahead.

When she saw Jace, Vereesa, and Aradnia standing before her, the smile vanished from her face. "How did you get in here? Did that idiot Theraldri bring you?"

"In a manner of speaking," Jace said. "Jace Sesso..." The silver-haired elf took a step back.

"Surrender, Malicia," Aradnia said. "Hand over the runestone and what you stole from the Dawning Star Tower, and return with us to Quel'Thalas to stand trial. Don't end up with the same tragic fate as Dar'Khan Drathir."

Malicia gave a cold laugh and turned, vanishing through the gate in an instant. Aradnia Blinked to the doorway in a flash. She was about to rush in after Malicia when she abruptly stopped — from the shadows on both sides came the sound of bones and flesh cracking and grinding...

A thick stench of necromantic rot poured out. One after another, rotting orcs in blackened armor, wielding badly rusted war axes and blades, charged out of the shadows and surrounded Aradnia!

Vereesa was already charging in with Dragon's Call, her sword cutting one orc clean in half at the waist, then swinging again to chop the head off another that tried to attack the magister.

After the ranger helped the magister take down the closest rotting orcs, the magister seized the moment to chant the Arcane Missiles incantation. Purple missiles flew one after another from the tip of her short staff, striking the remaining orcs. Jace summoned a Fireball that hit a resurrected troll axeflinger, the ferocious fire element blasting the troll into nothing but a charred, broken torso.

After clearing the gate, the three of them left the eight or so broken corpses and pressed forward.

Through several fang-like gates they entered a circular hall. Malicia was standing at the far end, unfurling a scroll and chanting to channel some kind of ritual.

Vereesa didn't hesitate — she nocked, drew, and loosed. The arrow pierced Malicia's back shoulder with unerring accuracy, the powerful arcane force knocking her unconscious to the ground. But it seemed it was already a little too late.

The stone gate at the far end of the hall had already been opened by the incantation, slowly swinging wide. A dark shadow stepped out through it with heavy, solid footfalls, growing clearer with each "thud... thud..."

It was a stone construct — a half-human monster, its body a purple-black color, possessing a golden-brown head that was both feline and insectoid, flashing a mesmerizing, venomous green light that closely resembled the face on the gate.

Its upper body was humanoid, but its lower body resembled a lean, low-slung lion — structured like a centaur with an oversized upper half.

Of course, this thing was much larger than an ordinary centaur. Its body was probably over three meters tall, nearing four. When it fully revealed itself in the light of the arcane braziers, it arrogantly spread a pair of black stone bird wings from its back, making its already fearsome appearance all the more bizarre.

This was an Obsidian Destroyer — a guardian creature originating from the Titans' creations. But untold tens of thousands of years had passed, and most of these obsidian guardians had long since been corrupted by dark forces into fallen, bloodthirsty shadow monsters.

The moment the Obsidian Destroyer revealed itself, Vereesa immediately loosed an arcane arrow at it. But this creature, which seemed slow and clumsy, swung its arcane-glowing scepter with an impossibly incredible speed, shattering the arrow.

"The runestone!" Aradnia shouted. "The runestone is right up there!"

Jace looked carefully and realized that the scepter the Obsidian Destroyer was wielding had, as its head, a white magic stone — inscribed with part of the characters from a Quel'Thalas runestone!
