## Chapter 1136: Dar'Khan's Search

"Are any of you hurt?" Vereesa asked.

"I'm fine, thanks to you both," Aradnia said.

"I almost got smashed just then, but it's nothing," Jace said. "And you?"

"I... should be alright, at least it looks that way." Vereesa walked over to the collapsed Obsidian Destroyer, then craned her neck to look up at the pitch-black dome of the circular hall. "How did Malicia, or Dar'Khan, or Theraldri even get hold of this thing?"

"It probably has something to do with this ancient temple." Aradnia took a drink from a rejuvenation potion. "It looks very much like the underground crypts I've seen before in Northrend, Lady Vereesa. Those crypts were all excavated by arachnid beings who call themselves the 'nerubians,' who believe their ancestors were living on that snowy continent tens of thousands of years before humans and elves ever set foot there."

"I know of those nerubians," Vereesa said. "But I've never actually set foot inside one of their ancient structures."

Aradnia said, "These animated statues are probably the guardians or sentinels of these nerubians, much like arcane golems."

Vereesa glanced at the broken stone head and muttered under her breath, "Guardians of spider-folk, with the head of a mountain lion."

"Using the power of this fallen statue to continuously corrupt the runestone was a clever idea," Aradnia said. "After all, Dar'Khan doesn't have an entire Shadow Council backing him up like Gul'dan did, maintaining something like the Altar of Storms to seize the Sunwell's power. Here, relying on just a few magisters, the runestone fragment's energy could easily slip beyond Dar'Khan's control at any time."

"What about the runestone fragment?" Vereesa asked.

"Burned," the magister said. "But it's hard to say whether it was burned by Mr. Sesso's magic or whether it collapsed along with the power it was anchored to... It had already been corrupted twice — once by Gul'dan and once by this dark statue — and was thoroughly depraved. And yet even in that state it could still fool the magic of Bandinorel... This orc war truly has given the magisters and our kingdom much to learn and reflect upon."

She glanced back at the passage they'd come through, and at the orc corpses lying hacked to pieces on the floor that Vereesa had cut down. "I wonder about the lords, mages, and the Church of Stratholme..."

While Aradnia and Vereesa discussed this, Jace said nothing, because he was busy rewrapping his staff with the linen bandages from his pack that he hadn't had time to use yet.

The magister noticed Jace's movements from the corner of her eye and looked over. "Your staff is extremely powerful, Mr. Sesso. While traveling together these past two days, I couldn't sense its power at all, so I assumed it was just an ordinary shadow staff. May I know what it's called?"

Hearing the magister's words, Vereesa looked over. She glanced at Aradnia first, then at Jace, her eyes full of unease.

"I don't know its name either," Jace shook his head.

"So where did you get it?" Aradnia asked.

The magister probably wasn't hiding any ulterior motive — she was simply curious, because she had no reason to recognize this staff.

On the current Azeroth, aside from Jace's group and a few ancient demons that might be in hiding, anyone who knew exactly what Ursulas looked like should have been dead for ages.

Even Medivh and Aegwynn had never laid eyes on the thing — after all, when Aegwynn's mentor Skavil defeated Sathael and seized Ursulas, Aegwynn hadn't yet come of age. After Skavil relinquished the position of Guardian, Ursulas never saw the light of day again.

"From a demon sorcerer," Jace said. "I realized at the time that this staff was extremely powerful, so I took it from that demon. I just never got around to giving it a name."

"The Eredar are exceptionally skilled at forging all manner of magical artifacts — they always have been." Aradnia nodded with a hint of envy. "There may be other undead in this ancient underground palace, like those orcs... We can't let our guard down, you two."

She looked at Malicia, who had been shot in the shoulder and knocked unconscious by arcane force. "Since she hasn't left this place, that means Dar'Khan's evidence is probably still hidden in this cellar. We need to hurry up and search for it."

"You're right." Jace looked toward the pile of Obsidian Destroyer rubble. "Looks like Archbishop Alonsus Faol won't need to trouble himself with this thing. He ought to give us some kind of reward — Honorary Citizen of Stratholme, or something."

Aradnia gave an easy laugh. Jace could tell that all the tension and pressure the magister had been accumulating along the way had been released the moment Malicia was captured and the Obsidian Destroyer was toppled. As for her curiosity about his staff, she'd already shoved that to the back of her mind.

Seeing Jace finish tying up the staff and sling it over his back, Aradnia's attention shifted to the walls and colonnades around them. Vereesa watched from the corner of her eye, her nerves finally relaxing a little, though traces of lingering fear remained.

Jace looked up at her and said in a joking tone, "What are you looking at me like that for? Shocked by the Mana Burn spell that decided the fight just now?"

"Very shocked." Vereesa smiled. "Anyway — thank you, Jace." Jace watched the magister's back; he knew what Vereesa was thanking him for.

"It's nothing, Vere. Let's figure out how to restrain Lady Malicia here first."

This circular hall reminded Jace of the chamber in the Scourge stronghold Naxxramas where they'd fought the Lich King's Kel'Thuzad.

Of course, it was nowhere near as spectacular in scale, nor did it have so many ghastly skull decorations. But structurally — the circular tile pattern in the center, the arched alcoves shaped like spider legs on all sides — it was similar.

What sat in these alcoves wasn't portals raised by the lich, or endless abominations, revenants, and skeleton soldiers — it was rows upon rows of bookshelves, storage cabinets of various sizes, medicine bottles, alchemy lab tools, and more.

Jace noticed that the floor of one of the alcoves was inscribed with an extremely complex ritual circle that could strengthen nearly all shadow magic, and on top of it there were even some traces of demon blood that looked like summon circles.

This was probably where Malicia had performed her summoning ritual — or more likely, where Theraldri and their other accomplices had done it. After all, someone had had to go into Quel'Thalas, to Dar'Khan's mage tower, to collect the evidence.

He looked at the magical materials arranged in the cabinets around them — most were related to demons and undead. There was also a small soul crystal inside, imprisoning a speck of soul debris that shimmered with a faint pale-blue spirit-light.

This small soul crystal could probably serve the function of a soul shard. Was the debris inside from the dream-soul of a faerie dragon? Jace couldn't make a call on that just from this alone — at the very least, there was no sign of that faerie dragon anywhere in this underground palace.

Then again, thinking back to the strange shriek the Obsidian Destroyer had let out when it was incinerated by the Mana Burn spell — it had immediately reminded Jace of the roar of that resurrected faerie dragon near the dream shard where Ursulas had been sealed.

It had probably already been drained dry by that statue.

On the desk nearby were some scrolls and papers tied together. The papers were correspondence between these magisters over the past period, documenting the process by which Dar'Khan, Malicia, and Leth'tis had searched for Ursulas.

From these messages, it was clear they'd been in quite close contact with the Eye of the Arcane of the Kirin Tor, and had been keeping their focus firmly on Karazhan.

Although it wasn't spelled out explicitly in these letters, between the lines one could tell that Dar'Khan had found some channel in Karazhan to cooperate with Medivh's Dark Riders in searching for Ursulas. By offering what they knew — primarily what they'd obtained from the Reliquary — in exchange for access to Karazhan's depths and Medivh's knowledge.

Of course, that was only the agreement on the surface. Dar'Khan's ultimate goal had always been to stab these Dark Riders in the back — otherwise he wouldn't have left that powerful anti-evil ward on the ice bridge outside Frosthammer Valley, waiting for the Dark Riders to walk right into it.

In the end, though, not only did the Dark Riders fail to find Ursulas — they didn't even make it out of Frosthammer Valley alive. Dar'Khan had never anticipated that Jace's group would find it faster than they did. He'd probably been confident that Jace couldn't possibly translate that report of Dath'Mora's written in Eredun.

Indeed, if not for summoning Karlez at the Altar of Storms in the Burning Steppes, Dar'Khan very well might have beaten the Dark Riders to Ursulas.

So, if not for Jace as that wild card, the original timeline should have been: the Dark Riders of Karazhan delving deep into the demon-ravaged prison of Eldre'Thalas in Dire Maul, destroying the magic crystal in that prison, killing Dath'Mora, and searching her body for that report.

When Jace's group had traveled to Kalimor for the first time, those few undead Dark Riders they'd seen in Eldre'Thalas must have been there for exactly that objective. Which meant these two groups had barely missed each other by a matter of steps.

In that timeline, the Dark Riders would have returned to Karazhan and used the Eredun language to decipher Dath'Mora's assessment of Skavil's vault location, eventually making their way to Winterspring, using the power of the Apocalypse to destroy that dream shard, seizing Ursulas and bringing it back to Karazhan — fulfilling Medivh's vague and ambiguous command.

If Jace hadn't managed to summon Karlez, if he hadn't guessed faster than Dar'Khan what that ambiguous demon scroll said — once Dar'Khan obtained Ursulas, which direction would everything have gone then?

That probably only the Bronze Dragonflight knew.

In any case, after Ursulas was snatched away by Jace, Dar'Khan, having hit a wall in Kalimor, saw his investigation grow murky. Several members of their group suspected that Ursulas had actually been hidden by the Guardian somewhere near Karazhan, so they'd invested considerable effort in Deadwind Pass and Duskwood.

During that time, they'd been operating within the Kingdom of Stormwind under the pretext of hunting undead and demons, without arousing suspicion. Until... Onyxia appeared.

Judging from the correspondence of that period, on the night Onyxia had been forced into her true form by Krasus, Leth'tis had been right there in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind. She had personally witnessed Onyxia being gravely wounded by shadow magic and fleeing, and concluded that Jace was the one who'd done it.

That wasn't hard to deduce. In the Stormwind Kingdom of that time, aside from "the Brill man" Jace Sesso, there probably wasn't a second person who could have pulled it off.

After she'd communicated with Dar'Khan, Dar'Khan had agreed with her assessment. But Dar'Khan firmly believed that Jace must have already found Ursulas — otherwise he absolutely could not have defeated Onyxia.

Others, including Malicia, believed that with the help of Vereesa Windrunner and Grede Thunderfist, plus the pit lord that had been executed with a single strike at the Dark Portal, inflicting such heavy wounds on Deathwing's daughter wasn't beyond the realm of possibility.

In the end, Dar'Khan appeared to compromise on the surface, but he clearly hadn't truly been convinced, and insisted on finding an opportunity to meet Jace again.

After some Dark Riders' tokens of unknown origin had been discovered among the goblins of Kalimor, the others had also reluctantly agreed that Jace might know some clues they didn't, and so consented to Dar'Khan's plan to approach Jace again. Though they seemed to think Jace's intelligence might have been found among the dead Dark Riders.

In these letters, the tone of these people did genuinely sound like they wanted to find an opportunity to have a proper talk with Jace, rather than come to blows. How it ultimately escalated to threatening Jace when he was alone in Stormwind — that wasn't spelled out in the letters either.

Judging at least from the preparations Dar'Khan had made, even the Torrent Staff he'd borrowed from the Archmage, he might have been heading for a confrontation with Jace from the moment he left Quel'Thalas.

After going through this stack of letters back and forth, confirming there was no definitive evidence that Ursulas was in Jace's possession, Jace called over to the magister who was inspecting the alchemy chamber: "Come take a look at this, Magister Aradnia. The letters here contain the names of several other Quel'dorei besides Dar'Khan, Malicia, and Leth'tis — several of them are addressed as magisters, so they're probably all connected to this group."

Aradnia came over and flipped through them for a while, frowning. "Damn it — there's one in here I'm quite familiar with. This way, even if Malicia refuses to reveal the names of the other participants when the time comes, we'll be able to round them all up in one net. What surprises me is that the names of several other magisters who also specialize in shadow research aren't on here. For instance, Umbric of Silvermoon — it's said he quite agreed with some of Dar'Khan's theories, and this incident affected his research too. But now it seems he's probably innocent."

"Magister Aradnia," Vereesa said, "right now you'd best take those letters and notify the magisters still in Stratholme, and have them rush here to reinforce us as soon as possible. We don't know when Theraldri will realize this underground palace has been discovered. Jace and I will stay here to keep watch over Malicia and the things in this place."

"I'll leave at once, my lady," Aradnia said.

"Be careful," Vereesa said.
