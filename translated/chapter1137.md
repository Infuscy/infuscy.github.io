## Chapter 1137: The Elder

Aradnia hurried out of the circular hall. After her footsteps faded completely into the passage on the other side, Jace asked, "Did you deliberately send her away?"

"Send her away?" Vereesa said. "So you, evil warlock, can secretly pocket any clues in here? She knows the Quel'dorei mages in Stratholme, and she knows some of the Farstriders too. We two don't even know this city, let alone know who to ask for help. We're better off standing guard here."

"Fair point," Jace said.

Vereesa widened her eyes. "Stop wasting time and hurry up and look for whatever you don't want to hand over to Belovar."

Jace smiled slightly, nodded, and walked into another alcove.

The bookshelves here were mostly encyclopedias on the Twisting Nether and demonic knowledge. While many were quite detailed, they also contained plenty of scholarly speculation.

What Jace wanted to find was the path through which Dar'Khan had learned void magic — flipping through these things definitely wouldn't help.

Then Vereesa suddenly called out, "Come here, Jace. There are some orcish scrolls here, and the strap binding them is labeled in Thalassian with 'the Possibilities of Telogrus' — isn't that the name you answered earlier?"

She handed the scroll over. "What exactly is Telogrus?"

Jace answered while looking through it. "A shattered world in the Twisting Nether, Vere. Some void creatures — voidwalkers who serve the Burning Legion, for instance — know about that place. I know there are researchers of the Twisting Nether on Azeroth who've been trying to pinpoint its coordinates and harness the void power within."

"So does this contain the method for reaching Telogrus?" Vereesa asked.

Jace hesitated, not answering immediately, because Telogrus was actually far less well-known than he'd made it sound — so he was also puzzled as to why an orc who'd written this scroll would know about that place.

But Jace hadn't read much before he realized things weren't what they seemed.

"This isn't the method for reaching Telogrus, Vere — this is the method for reaching Draenor!" Jace skimmed through the entire scroll quickly. "Although this letter isn't signed, from his tone and what he's describing, it's very likely a letter written to a Dalaran mage."

Hearing the words "method for reaching Draenor," both of Vereesa's eyes lit up instantly. "Then does this contain the teleportation coordinates for Draenor?" she asked.

Jace flipped through the scroll. "It mentions that a Draenei crystal storing the coordinates will be sent along with a black dragon to..."

Upon seeing the words "black dragon," Jace's heart sank. He asked, "Could this be written to Kel'Thuzad? Does Kel'Thuzad want to go to Draenor too?"

Vereesa said, "If Kel'Thuzad is in league with Deathwing, then it's not impossible that he'd want to leave himself an escape route — fleeing to Draenor to seek the protection of those black dragons — if the theft of the Eye of Dalaran were exposed. But the key question is why this letter would be in Dar'Khan's possession? And why is it labeled Telogrus?"

That genuinely stumped Jace. He was getting a little confused too.

"But didn't you just mention a Draenei crystal storing Draenor's teleportation coordinates?" Vereesa asked.

"Yes." Jace looked up and glanced at the shelves around him, and at the magical materials and storage cabinets in the other alcoves. "Let's find... a magic crystal."

"Good." With that, the ranger picked up the pace of their search.

During this time, Jace also came across some research on Telogrus and the Twisting Nether, records of conversations held across the void with some unknown entity within the Telogrus Rift, and tests conducted on that plane's power.

He simply stuffed all these scrolls and materials into his pack as well — after all, it was nearly the middle of the night by now, and there was no time to study them here page by page.

However, no matter where they looked, they couldn't find any magic crystal, let alone a Draenei crystal.

"Jace, I found an orcish iron box." Vereesa dragged out a black metal box from under the cabinet where she'd found the "Possibilities of Telogrus" scrolls, inscribed with runes in both the orcish and demonic languages.

After Jace took the box, he discovered it was sealed by some kind of shadow rune.

"Take it." Jace stuffed the box into Vereesa's satchel. "And these scrolls too — we'll take our time figuring out these records once we're back."

Before they had a chance to search the last two alcoves, came the sound of footsteps from far off in the passage outside. "Is that Aradnia back?" Vereesa asked. "How long has she been gone?"

"I don't know," Jace said. "An hour, two hours... after we took down that statue I forgot to check the time."

Before Vereesa could respond, a rather tall male High Elf had already appeared in the doorway.

He was dressed splendidly, holding a sword enchanted with arcane energy that, from a distance, even shone brighter than Azure Song. By his side followed quite a retinue of High Elves and half-elves armed with blades and staves — mages, and warriors wearing enchanted armor — seven or eight in total, all fully armed and geared up.

Jace noticed that the staves in the hands of two of the mages radiated a purple-red shadow power that looked rather fearsome.

"Honorable Lady Vereesa Windrunner — what are you doing here?" The leading High Elf swept his gaze across the circular hall in surprise, then at the pile of Obsidian Destroyer wreckage, and asked, "I am Theraldri Dawnrose, who resides nearby. You may have heard my name. My servants told me there was an unusual magical disturbance here, so I came as quickly as possible. What is this bizarre and terrifying place?"

Even without reading his emotions, one could tell this fellow was acting — after all, one could guess who he was from those two shadow mages alone. He had to be Theraldri Dawnrose.

"Take us out of here first, Theraldri." Vereesa raised her voice. "We stumbled upon this dark lair by accident, and barely managed to defeat the fallen statue guarding it. And Malicia was clearly hiding here, carrying out some sinister plot... We must get out and report what's happened here to the Kingdom of Lordaeron."

"Understood, my lady, but..." Theraldri replied with some hesitation, but his gaze had already slid sidelong toward the alcove with the shadow ritual circle — clearly noticing that the letters on the desk had vanished without a trace.

"But you already know everything."

After he said this, his expression and demeanor turned dark at the same time.

Just as Jace thought they'd have to fight this whole crowd to the death, and even Vereesa was reaching for her bow, Theraldri suddenly turned his head to look behind him.

Another group of footsteps came from the distant passage, and someone called out in a loud voice: "Yes, Theraldri Dawnrose — we already know."

An elderly white-haired High Elf magister in a gold-and-black robe arrived behind the Dawnrose retinue with two mages — one of them was Aradnia.

"Master Vorethal?" The fierceness Theraldri had barely managed to muster evaporated without a trace. He even murmured helplessly, "I'm not quite sure what's happened..."

"The Archmage has been investigating the crimes of Dar'Khan Drathir." The magister named Vorethal held a letter in his hand. "You and Magister Malicia are suspected of involvement in these crimes. By the highest decree of the Sun King, the Silvermoon Council, and the Magisters' Assembly, you and your accomplices — including all members of the Dawnrose family in Stratholme — must return to Tranquillien immediately to face investigation."

"Lady Vereesa Windrunner." Vorethal looked toward Vereesa and inclined his head slightly. Vereesa also respectfully bowed in return. "Honored Master Vorethal."

"Mr. Jace Sesso — I'm pleased to have seen you recover." He looked at Jace as well. "Thank you for your concern, Master Vorethal."

As he spoke, Jace slowly loosened his hand, which had been reaching for his staff. Theraldri and his retinue, who'd seemed to have the nerve to fight Vereesa and Jace moments ago, were like frostbitten plants upon seeing this magister — they'd completely lost the will to resist.

Because this Vorethal was precisely the leader of the Astromancers — the group of mages who would later follow Kael'thas to Outland — and also Kael'thas's most trusted magister for a time.

Not only that, he was also one of the oldest High Elves in the Magisters' Assembly, and indeed in all of Quel'Thalas.

Considering that many of the mages by Vorethal's side worried about his physical frailty due to his age, Jace wouldn't have been the least bit surprised if he was actually older than the Sun King himself.
