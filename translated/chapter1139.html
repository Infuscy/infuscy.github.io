Chapter 1139: Broken in Two

On the sailboat back to Quel'Thalas, Jace couldn't help but fall asleep. When he woke, the sky was dim and overcast — it could have been morning or evening.

Gazing at the forests on the distant shore, Jace knew the ship had already arrived in Quel'Thalas. Vereesa was outside the cabin, gazing at the mage towers in the far distance. Noticing Jace was awake, she turned back.

"Good evening, Jace. You slept long enough — I was starting to think you were injured."

"Haven't had a proper sleep in a while. From Kel'dathron to Stratholme..." Jace clicked his tongue and said quietly, "Remember to have the Farstriders investigate, Vere — see if there's anything we missed in that cellar."

"Apparently a lot of it is related to necromancy," Vereesa said. "Research into Gul'dan's death knights, and black dragon necromancy. Considering those undead orcs that attacked us, and that Dar'Khan wanted to get information from you yet wasn't above trying to kill you... that may not be contradictory."

"He wanted to make me an obedient corpse — ask me whatever he wanted. I've suspected that before," Jace said. Vereesa swallowed slightly and fell silent, unwilling to discuss it further.

Jace knew that even in Quel'Thalas, which was slightly more tolerant of shadow research, necromancy was an absolute taboo.

Even after receiving the pile of demons of every color that Jace had summoned, Vereesa, just like Grede, had absolutely no desire for Jace to touch necromantic magic. So her words were full of caution and reluctance, probably also hinting that Jace shouldn't interfere with the rest of it.

"If there are any other leads related to Kel'Thuzad, remember to let me know," Jace said. "You don't need me to tell you that." the elf said quietly.

"No one searched your pack, did they?" Jace asked, keeping his voice low too.

"Search a Ranger-Lord's pack?" Vereesa cleared her throat in a deliberately pompous tone. "Kael'thas can come do it himself. Master Vorethal doesn't quite cut it."

Jace snickered, and Vereesa couldn't help but laugh too, sneaking a glance at Jace as she laughed.

Maybe it was the relief of finally having this whole affair behind them, or maybe it was that Jace wasn't pressing the necromancy issue anymore that put her mind at ease — or maybe both.

"Once we get Theraldri and his lot delivered back to Quel'Thalas, I'll find a ship to take us to Lordamere, Jace," Vereesa said with considerable enthusiasm.

"You're that eager — and I doubt it's just because you want to see my parents," Jace said.

"Of course I want to see Mr. and Mrs. Sesso again too." Vereesa clasped her hands behind her back. "But..."

"My friends in Stormwind — those few demons, anyway — someone will have a way to safely open that thing, Vere," Jace said.

"I believe you." Vereesa nodded.

At dusk the next day, the golden sailboat docked at Sunstrider Port. By the time they arrived, quite a number of High Elf soldiers and mages had already gathered at the harbor, waiting to receive the High Elf suspects captured from Stratholme.

On the dock, Aradnia came over to Jace and Vereesa and bowed. "I must return to the Dawning Star Tower as soon as possible to report to Master Aloniss, so I must say goodbye. This journey has been truly memorable, Lady Vereesa. Before this, I didn't have much concrete impression of the name Jace Sesso, but now I understand why you chose him as your partner. Truly enviable."

"Get in line and take a number, Magister Aradnia." Vereesa raised her long eyebrows. "It's a little late for that now."

Aradnia sighed. "Alright — actually, I'd still hoped to find a lover who doesn't spend half every day sleeping like the dead."

The two elves immediately burst into laughter together. Jace managed a perfunctory laugh along with them, but honestly he didn't quite get what was so funny.

Before he could think it through, a male High Elf in a bright red outer robe pushed hurriedly through the crowd, his eyes fixed on Jace as if he was making a beeline for him.

"I've finally caught up with you, Jace Sesso." The High Elf's tone wasn't exactly polite — though he might have just been anxious.

He lowered his head and pulled out a pair of weapons in scabbards from his pack. "These are yours. The Prince entrusted me to deliver them. I went to Stormwind, and the elves there told me you'd come to Quel'Thalas."

"Ah, I'm sorry." Jace took the items in his hands, a little confused. "But who..."

"I am Aquen, emissary of the Queen. You may also call me Aquenastarz." the High Elf said.

So this was a red dragon. He explained in a low voice: "For some time now, the Queen and the Prince have attempted to repair the Apocalypse and cleanse the demonic corruption within it, but each time they failed. Later, the Prince realized that as long as the sword remained in its broken state, the death will within it was extremely weak. So the Prince decided to reforge the two halves of the sword into two new warblades, and successfully transformed its magic with the flame of life."

Jace stared blankly at the weapons in his hands. "This is actually..."

"In this process, the Cadros Anvil deep within Grim Batol also played an enormous role," Aquenastarz said. "So the Prince also asked me to convey his invitation — he hopes you'll visit Grim Batol and see that anvil left behind by the Wildhammer dwarf. He said you've always had that idea."

"Yes." Jace nodded. "Though I might not be able to get there in the short term. If you see the Prince, thank him for me. And thank you very much for making the trip."

"It's nothing, Jace Sesso." The red dragon bowed deeply, glanced at Vereesa approaching from the side, and said, "The Red Dragonflight will never forget what you, Vereesa Windrunner, and Grede Thunderfist did in Grim Batol."

He raised his head. "But forgive me — I must leave. I have other missions to attend to."

"Of course." Jace nodded and watched the red-robed elf turn and disappear into the crowd.

"So this is the demon sword that was hacked in two?" Vereesa asked. "You want it?" Jace asked.

"Hah — I'd never use something that depraved. Didn't you hear what the emissary just said? Even the Dragonqueen herself couldn't cleanse it completely." Vereesa pursed her lips, then glanced at the two scabbards again. "They're clearly better suited for that Sayad."

These two warblades weren't entirely identical — one was fairly straight, the other more like a curved saber.

Jace drew the curved one. A burst of golden flame leaped from the blade and startled several nearby High Elves — and scared him so much he immediately shoved the sword back into its scabbard.

He examined the two clearly enchanted scabbards carefully and began to wonder what they were even made of. "Are you sure you want to give this to her?" Jace asked.

"Give? I have a proper dragon-forged magic sword myself, Jace — not to mention you still owe me a bow." Vereesa said with an expression of utter disdain.

"Then I'll pass along your kind intentions to her," Jace said with a smile.
