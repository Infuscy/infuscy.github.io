## Chapter 1140: All for Quel'Thalas

Theraldri, Malicia, and several other High Elf warlocks who had studied shadow magic and participated in the summoning ritual after Dar'Khan's death were all sent to Tranquillien for interrogation.

Per the conditions Vereesa and the Archmage had agreed upon earlier, Jace naturally could participate in the questioning. He was quite curious about the cooperation between Dar'Khan's group and Kel'Thuzad, and also wanted to probe into the process of Dar'Khan's "communication" with the void.

Malicia had been shot and wounded by Vereesa, and though she'd regained consciousness after treatment, she still seemed rather groggy.

When she saw Jace enter the room, she raised her eyes to glance at him, then lowered her head again without saying a word, as if she had absolutely no interest in Jace.

If not for the fact that the person before her was a prisoner fixed to her seat by an arcane spell, anyone walking in here would never take this room — decorated as beautifully as some princess's boudoir — for an interrogation chamber.

Jace set his staff aside, leaned it against the wall, walked over and ran his hand over the wooden chair carved with bird-feather motifs, then said, "Good morning, Lady Malicia."

Malicia kept her head down and said softly, "Ask what you want to know, Jace Sesso."

"Why did you want to obtain that artifact — Ursulas?" Jace asked.

"You have no right to know, human," Malicia said calmly.

"Kel'Thuzad is human too," Jace said. "Does he have the right?"

"He doesn't either." Malicia raised her head.

"Then do you think you have the right to know?" Jace sat down. "What do you mean?" Malicia asked.

"There's only one Ursulas," Jace said. "And Dar'Khan seemed determined to have it. Whatever you elves are really planning, would Dar'Khan necessarily use that staff to help you achieve your goal?"

"His research has been going on for over a hundred years." Malicia frowned. "And you, a brat who knows nothing and has only lived twenty years — you think you can pass judgment on him?"

"So you think Dar'Khan wanting to obtain Ursulas is connected to his research," Jace said in a flat tone. Malicia gritted her teeth, a flame of anger quietly kindling in her heart, and the dejected air from moments ago swept clean away.

Perhaps it was because Jace had caught the thread in her words, or perhaps it was that Jace's flat, slightly indifferent tone had provoked her.

She said in a low voice in Thalassian, "You don't even know what Ursulas is, do you, Jace Sesso? Sitting here so self-assured — aren't you the least bit ashamed?"

"Then tell me — what is Ursulas?" Jace asked.

Malicia said in a low voice, "It's one of the most fearsome artifacts ever forged by the ruler of the entire Twisting Nether with his own hands. The souls it has devoured could destroy tens of thousands of worlds. The hatred and agony it brings could make the leaders of the Burning Legion tremble with fright!"

"Sounds terrifying indeed," Jace nodded.

Malicia couldn't help but let out a laugh. "I know that before Deathwing attacked Dalaran, you were in Dalaran participating in an orcish-language exchange meeting. At that meeting, you mentioned an artifact that one of the three death knight leaders, Teron Gorefiend, wanted to find — one that, like Ursulas, had been believed destroyed by the Guardians but in fact still existed in the world: the Staff of Sargeras. Don't forget, it was your little sweetheart who reported this intelligence to Quel'Thalas — she was even promoted to Ranger-Captain for it."

"If you had any understanding of Aegwynn's and the Tirisfal Council's research, you'd know that during a previous Burning Legion invasion, it was by the power of the Staff of Sargeras that a teleportation channel was opened, allowing the demon army to descend upon Northrend. Such is the might of Sargeras — the demonic artifacts he forged are the Legion's reliance for traveling between worlds. And Ursulas is the same. The eredar sorceress Sathael intended to use this staff to guide the Burning Legion to descend upon Azeroth, only to be stopped by the Guardian of the time..."

Jace listened, propping his chin in his hand. Though he very much wanted to remind Malicia that although the Staff of Sargeras bore the Dark Titan's name, it hadn't actually been forged by Sargeras himself — but that wasn't the point either.

It was just that when Malicia got to the part about Ursulas being, like the Staff of Sargeras, a tool for opening Legion teleportation gates, Jace could barely hold it in.

"Is this what Dar'Khan believes?" he asked. "That Ursulas is an artifact for opening Legion teleportation gates."

"This is the conclusion reached by many researchers! And the Eye of the Arcane, as well as records from the Tirisfal Council and previous Guardians, all support this!" Malicia said. "To put it simply, Sathael came to Azeroth under the orders of the Legion's master, Sargeras. If she wasn't there to summon more demon armies to descend and complete the conquest that Archimonde failed to accomplish ten thousand years ago, what could she possibly do alone?"

Although Ursulas was a powerful shadow artifact and would certainly provide some enhancement to summoning and teleportation magic of the Twisting Nether, it was still different from something like the Staff of Sargeras, which was truly used by the Legion for void projection.

But listening to Malicia's explanation, the inference she and those she'd mentioned had drawn wasn't groundless nonsense either.

The Burning Legion's standard playbook was always to first send a small force to land, then use various means to channel local mana sources to teleport more Legion soldiers, or even greater leaders, to descend upon a world to carry out destruction. These means included specific artifacts, summoning rituals, and so on. So it was quite normal to misunderstand that Ursulas's previous owner Sathael had brought this staff to Azeroth to pave the way for a Legion invasion.

It was just that even Sargeras himself hadn't realized that Sathael, upon mastering this staff, would become ambitious on her own — wanting to seize Azeroth's magic to defy him and the entire Legion.

Since Malicia had said this much, her motive for helping Dar'Khan find Ursulas could be guessed. "You want to use Ursulas to travel to Telogrus, don't you?" Jace asked.

Malicia was silent for a long while before asking, "Did Dar'Khan tell you the name Telogrus — in the second before you executed him?"

"I'm the one asking the questions, my lady," Jace said. "Frankly, I think you've chosen the wrong collaborator. Dar'Khan was blinded by the powerful strength Ursulas could bring, acting impulsively and without regard for consequences. The moment his collaborator, companion, or friend so much as doubted his purpose or hindered his actions, he'd threaten to kill them — or just kill them."

"If he were still alive, the moment he got his hands on it, he'd immediately wipe out all of you potential competitors and monopolize that artifact. So you don't need to speak for him anymore."

Malicia gave a cold laugh. "Don't measure others by your own dark heart, Jace Sesso. Not everyone is a filthy sorcerer with no bottom line who's willing to consort with demons, like you."

Jace frowned when he heard that. Vereesa and quite a few magisters were still outside.

The female magister said in a low voice, "I know how a Sayad demon draws power from its master, and the Sayad serving you is unusually powerful — even Vileth Deepshadow couldn't handle her. So the fact that you can still stand here today already surprises me."

"Me, Dar'Khan, and several other void researchers — Lady Leth'tis, for instance — all act for the sake of all Quel'Thalas." As she spoke, she struggled against the magical restraints and raised one hand with effort, rubbing her fingers together. "Bandinorel is nothing but a laughable illusion, and the Sunwell that powers it is like a fragile cradle — a single accidentally toppled candle could burn it to ashes! Countless Quel'dorei living in this beautiful illusion woven by Dath'Remar are as ignorant and pitiful as infants staring dumbly at the wind chime above their heads and giggling — and we've been foolishly wasting our time like this for seven thousand years."

"Over-reliance on the Sunwell was still a critically important issue thousands of years ago, but as time has passed, the sages who witnessed the explosion of the Well of Eternity have died one by one. The second and third generations have long forgotten how their ancestors struggled to survive the crisis of annihilation. They increasingly view the Sunwell as father, mother — as an eternal supreme deity standing on the Isle of Quel'Danas, a holy idol imbued with divine power! And this so-called deity... can't even restore the immortality we lost."

"I thought Gul'dan's magic and the death knights' invasion would awaken our 'great kingdom.'" she murmured. "But that was nothing more than wishful thinking."

"Perhaps Dar'Khan was right — only the true destruction of the Sunwell can shatter this dream. Only the true destruction of the Sunwell... can make our propositions and goals receive the attention of all Quel'dorei, can make that arrogant old stubborn Belovar lower his head and carefully consider where he went wrong."

"That will be all, Mr. Sesso." A magister walked in at this point.

Malicia looked at the male magister. "You know everything I've said is true too, Kanthris. It's just that you and the other magisters, and Silvermoon and the Sunwalkers alike, avoid the problem and refuse to face the crisis head-on."

The magister bowed slightly to Jace and made no response to Malicia's words. Jace also nodded and stood up.

Malicia pointed at Jace. "In the endless dark void, Quel'Thalas, Azeroth... are all nothing but helpless islands, tiny fragments between the stars. Even the Quel'dorei are nothing but a pack of arrogant wretches — and you humans even more so."

"You want me to tell you the clues to Ursulas? Forget it — haven't you already occupied that underground palace? Look harder!" After Jace left the room with Ursulas and returned to the corridor, Malicia was still inside, agitated and pouring out a torrent of words, hurling abuse and mockery at the magister before her.

The crowd of Quel'dorei outside was silent — they'd probably all heard what Malicia had just said. Jace and Vereesa left the corridor and came to the garden outside. Vereesa's expression wasn't looking too good.

Jace knew that she, like Malicia, had thought about the destruction of the Sunwell before. Malicia's words must have struck a chord with her.

"Let's leave Quel'Thalas first with the things we found, Jace. Don't waste any more time with them." Vereesa said in a low voice. "Leave Dar'Khan's accomplices to the magisters and Farstriders to interrogate... if there's any truly valuable information, both I and Sylvanas will know immediately and inform you."

"Alright. Remember to thank your sister for me," Jace said. "It's a pity Malicia didn't reveal anything about that orc and Kel'Thuzad, or the Draenor coordinates. Actually, I didn't expect her to get this emotionally agitated either. So we still need to make a trip to the Alterac Mountains, find the orc tribes there, and see if they've preserved Draenor's teleportation coordinates."

"She believed she could save Quel'Thalas together with Dar'Khan." Vereesa said. "Now that this dream is gone — it's not surprising she ended up like this. Next I'll arrange a ship to Lordamere, and then we'll take a wind hawk to Alterac City. How does that sound?"

"Fine," Jace said.

"Oh — if there really are orcs there, do you think we could capture one who knows shadow magic and force him to open that box using orcish methods?" Vereesa asked.

Jace smiled. "I think we should try talking first, and only resort to force if that doesn't work..."

"Sure, sure, whatever you say." Vereesa waved her hand lazily.

Jace had been wondering how a bunch of shamans and wolf riders were supposed to open a warlock's box. But then he remembered the orc warlock Karol he'd met at the Altar of Storms — after all this time, he might have already arrived in the Alterac Mountains.

If he could meet him, he might actually be able to help.
