## Chapter 1156: A Big Otherworldly Creature

Explaining Karlez's situation clearly to a Kirin Tor mage still felt unnatural to Jace.

But after all, Amy wasn't an ordinary Kirin Tor member. He thought it over briefly, then said: "After the Karazhan Cataclysm, the Ley Lines across the entire Kingdom of Stormwind became extremely unstable. Ilgalar Tower also lost most of its magical power for this reason. The other mage towers are in roughly the same state. The Wizard Sanctum is looking for a way to fix it."

Amy nodded, and Jace continued: "My solution is to find a demon to provide a stable power supply for the mage tower."

"So that's what the Thalarax crystal is for — coordinating demonic power with the unstable Ley Line energy." Amy understood almost immediately. She said, "You remind me of a senior mage from a while back. After the Eye of Dalaran was lost, he proposed using a demon lord imprisoned in the Violet Hold for over a thousand years to continue powering Dalaran's defensive shields, so as to free up Archmage Antonidas…"

"If you get the chance, thank that senior mage for me. I suddenly don't feel so guilty," Jace said. Amy smiled and said, "What kind of demon is it? A doomguard, or…"

"A doomlord," Jace said.

"You mean an Ered'ruin." Amy's gaze bounced between Jace and Vereesa, then she nodded. "That kind of demon does possess abundant magical power, and they're skilled at manipulating arcane, shadow, and fel energy. If it can be controlled, it really would be well suited as a mage tower's energy source."

"You really learned a lot in Dalaran," Jace said.

"Don't forget the very important motivation I had for joining the Kirin Tor in the first place, Jace." Amy smiled. "Not to mention, one of the Kirin Tor's vital missions is to keep our world from being ravaged by otherworldly creatures. After the Order of Tirisfal disbanded, this duty became all the more important."

"But that's not a doomguard. It's a doomlord," Jace reminded her. The trace of a smile on Amy's face vanished in an instant.

"There's a doomlord in the Kingdom of Stormwind?"

"More or less," Jace said.

"No, no, no." Amy narrowed her eyes. "You can't try to control a demon that big, Jace. That's way too dangerous. You should know that kind of demon is a high-ranking general the Burning Legion uses to deploy armies. Last time you came to Dalaran and asked me to help find Ered'ruin language materials — was that related to this demon? Don't tell me you summoned a demon like that just to decipher Ered'ruin…"

"Not entirely for this…" Jace said.

"Fine." Amy shook her head. "Does my father know? He does know about this, doesn't he? You just don't want to tell me."

"He shouldn't know. I didn't tell him," Jace said.

"You didn't tell Master Marin how you defeated Onyxia?" Vereesa asked.

"I did, but I couldn't have spelled it out that clearly," Jace said.

"Didn't you tell me last time that you used that shadow magic to seriously wound Onyxia?" Amy asked.

"Yes, but you know that magic isn't that easy to cast. It wasn't Grede who bought me time." Jace said. "It was…"

"This demon you mentioned." Amy said. "I see. Well, I suppose only a demon like a duke of doom could go head-to-head with a powerful black dragon like Onyxia. Actually… I had my suspicions back then."

"That's exactly it," Jace said. "If we hadn't gotten lucky and successfully summoned that doomlord into the dragon's plane, Grede and I would probably both be dead in there, and Stormwind might have gone down with us. Very few people know about this — including the King, and your father."

"Of course they can't know." Amy asked. "So besides the three of us, who else knows?"

"Grede Thunderfist — he witnessed the whole thing with his own eyes," Vereesa said.

"And Sola," Jace added. "She's a green dragon."

"A green dragon?" Amy said, as if remembering something. "Wait, my father wrote quite a bit of research on dragons recently, with many new ideas and summaries related to green dragons. Even some Kirin Tor scholars who study dragons here mentioned it to me. Is that related to the green dragon you mentioned?"

"Probably. She did help out quite a bit," Jace said.

Amy supported her forehead with her hand. "I even told other people it was probably because he's getting older, doesn't have to teach so many classes or instruct so many apprentices, and has more time to do research — so he's been more productive."

She was silent for a moment, as if struggling internally, then said: "Could it be put this way — that demon saved the several of your lives when Onyxia attacked Stormwind."

"I wasn't in there," Vereesa said. "I mean, I was in Stormwind, but I didn't go into the elemental plane Onyxia used to escape. So that demon helped Grede and Jace."

Then she added: "But strictly speaking, if those two had died in there, Stormwind probably would have gone down too. It was the middle of the night — many people hadn't even reacted yet. Even if the Wizard Sanctum's mages and the army in the city had fought heroically together and driven off the black dragon, the losses would have been hard to bear… so it counts as saving my life too."

"Then my father's the same," Amy said. "Let me go to Ilgalar Tower and have a look, see if there's a way to conceal his existence through arcane means and replace it with some other more normal image. But don't get your hopes up, Jace — this isn't easy work. If there weren't a mage tower supplying the power… I can't even imagine how this could be done."

"Thank you so much, Amy." Jace nodded. "Actually, it's not just to keep my parents in the dark. In the future, soldiers from Stonewatch, hunters, lost passersby — I don't want them taking one peek into the tower and seeing a giant demon. This… is for their own good too."

"I understand," Amy said. "So how tall is he, roughly?"

Jace recalled — about four or five meters — and said: "I'd say… fifteen, sixteen, seventeen feet tall?" The woman mage coughed so hard she nearly choked.

"All right… I'll need to gather materials and spells, find a suitable way to deal with an otherworldly creature of this size, so I can't set out right away. And I still have some work to do here. If I remember correctly, isn't the Ilgalar Tower project still two or three months from completion?"

"Yes," Jace said. "So we're not in a hurry either."

Amy clapped her hands. "How long are you staying in Dalaran? Want to have a meal together and talk while we eat?"

"No thanks," Jace said. "The ship is still waiting at the harbor. We need to set out for Stormwind as soon as possible."

"All right then." Amy nodded. "It's a deal, then. If there are any other questions, I'll write to you."

"Don't make it too obvious in the letter. Don't let the Kirin Tor catch on," Jace reminded her.

"I'll be careful," Amy said.

After saying goodbye to Amy and coming out to the courtyard outside the building, making sure no one was around, Vereesa said in a low voice: "I thought you were going to tell Amy our plan."

Jace said, "Let's wait until she gets to Ilgalar Tower and see how things go. I'm not confident she could take it all in at once."
