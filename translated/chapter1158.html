Chapter 1158: The Favorite

The joy of cleaning and settling into the new home passed quickly, and the sky darkened.

Jace needed to make a trip back to his rental place first to put down the things he'd brought back from Quel'Thalas and Stratholme — spell materials, spellbooks, and so on, plus the materials he'd scavenged from Dar'Khan definitely couldn't be left at his parents' house.

After saying goodbye to his parents, he and Vereesa came outside. Looking at the lights coming on in the house behind them, Jace let out a long breath.

A layer of golden brilliance was spread over the somewhat dim river water. Although the evening wind still felt a bit chilly, Jace increasingly felt that Stormwind was becoming more and more like home.

At this moment, he still couldn't quite believe he'd actually accomplished all this — bought a house in Stormwind, brought his parents over, and settled them in.

Vereesa seemed to sense the thick emotions in his heart right now, slowly leaning against Jace's shoulder.

"I need to go to the embassy district tonight, Jace. About Dar'Khan, I have to report the details to the Farstriders here in person."

"All right," Jace said. "Tomorrow I'm going to the palace too — I need to tell the King about Dar'Khan, and meet that Ironforge envoy. After that, I'll be summoning that demon. The materials needed for the summoning ritual are all ready."

Vereesa said softly, "I hope everything goes smoothly."

"Mm." Jace nodded.

On the way back, Jace went over the spell materials, incantations, and summoning procedure for summoning Karlez in his mind. He'd still need Sazeno to walk him through it again at the time — after all, although this little gremlin had burned through all their stock of soul shards and mana potions, plus a big bottle of Jace's blood, it was the only one on the team who had successfully summoned Karlez so far.

By the time he got back to the rental place it was already dark. Jace came to the door, knocked, and opened it — and saw Morofiel tilting her head, leaning on the table, looking this way.

"This wasn't the longest you've ever been away, dear master, probably counts as…"

"I think it's the third longest," Sazeno's shrill voice came from under the bed. It bounced its way up onto the table, raised its arms, and shouted, "Welcome back to your dark castle, master! I actually missed you a bit!"

"I can tell when a demon is lying," Jace said.

The little gremlin said at once, "I missed you with all my heart, master. You can't imagine the vicious, torturous methods this damned succubus came up with to kill time. I think my soul has suffered irreversible damage!"

Morofiel said in a bored tone, "Your soul shard contains less energy than an apprentice's Arcane Missile. A sneeze from a patrolling guard could deal it immeasurable devastation…"

"I've repeated it over and over — I'm not afraid of that patrolling guard!" the gremlin yelled. Morofiel snorted coldly. Jace sat on the bed and said, "Skip the nonsense. Look at this."

He took out the crystal recording the Draenor teleportation coordinates and placed it on the table. Sazeno immediately picked it up and examined it back and forth. "What is this, an Eredar artifact? I thought it was a gift for me."

"Can't you see what's inside?" Jace asked.

"Teleportation coordinates. Are these Draenor teleportation coordinates?" Morofiel asked.

"You can recognize them?" Jace asked.

"I've had quite a bit of contact with the Eredar. This is indeed the Eredar's customary method of recording teleportation coordinates," Morofiel said with some pride. "It's perfectly normal that this little booger that leaked out of some creature's cloaca in a corner of the Twisting Nether I don't even know wouldn't recognize it."

"I know this is what the Eredar use to open portals, damn it!" Sazeno roared.

"All right, all right." Jace poked the little gremlin with a finger, making it yelp, then said: "This is something an orc left behind. He said these are Draenor's teleportation coordinates. Since you can both confirm these are teleportation coordinates, that should settle it."

"It'd be even more reliable if we could ask our Lord Karlez," Morofiel said.

"Yes." Jace nodded, then suddenly remembered something. "I have something else to give you, Morofiel. To be precise… two things."

"A gift?" Morofiel scoffed. "Though I'm delighted, our relationship doesn't need gifts to be maintained, master. You're actually kind of cute…"

"Strictly speaking, this is a gift from Vereesa Windrunner."

As he spoke, Jace took out the two objects wrapped in layer upon layer of linen and placed them on the table with a heavy clunk.

At the same time, Sazeno immediately scooted over. The moment it got close and touched the bit of scabbard poking out from under the linen, it yanked its hand back as if it had been burned.

It looked up at Jace in a daze, then looked at Morofiel, unable to utter a single word.

"She? What for — thanking me for not slitting her throat in the forest?" Morofiel chuckled and tried stroking the surface of the cloth bundle. The blazing flames in her eyes immediately dimmed quite a bit.

"This… no, that's not right…"

Morofiel pinched the corner of the cloth with her fingers. "May I open it?"

"Of course. They're yours," Jace said.

The demon carefully unwrapped the linen, gripped the scabbard of one of the scimitars, and slowly drew the blade.

A warm golden-red flame spread gently, and her eyes burned brighter and brighter along with it. The ominous life force all over her body grew hotter and redder, even glowing, and the demon runes all over her body flickered as well.

Jace could even see the demonic blood vessels on her skin, and those hideous spikes were glowing as if they were red-hot metal.

"Akaul, shekai!" Morofiel cursed in demonic, then curled the corners of her mouth. "This is the Apocalypse!"

"The Apocalypse?!" the gremlin exclaimed in shock.

Morofiel continued in demonic: "Those red dragons really have a way about them. If the Nathrezim saw their weapons infused with the Life-Flame like this, they'd be driven mad… and the sight of a Nathrezim going mad is unquestionably the second most beautiful scene in this Twisting Nether!"

"What's the first?" Jace asked in demonic, propping his cheek.

"You, of course, my master. Every time I get close to you in your trance, it fills me with uncontrollable excitement!" The succubus kissed Jace on the cheek. "I think this is the best gift I've ever received, darling. Thank Lady Vereesa Windrunner for me tomorrow — if you can still stand up, that is…"
