## Chapter 1160: A Tavern Reunion

After bidding farewell to Lord Kurthalos and leaving the palace, the sun was already directly overhead.

Jace planned to go to the Pig and Whistle first for lunch, then head to his parents' house to find his Dad and go visit Arna in the Mage Quarter together.

But walking along the road, Jace kept thinking — if he moved Arna from the Mage Quarter stables to his own house, could the crown still cover her expenses? After all, all of Arna's upkeep in the Mage Quarter stables was covered by Varian.

The thing was, Varian hadn't brought this up just now. Had he forgotten, or would there be no change — or was he deliberately not mentioning it?

After all, although Arna wasn't a proper warhorse, the standard of her care and feeding had always been higher than a warhorse's. If that money were dumped on his parents, it absolutely wouldn't count as a small sum.

The midday Pig and Whistle was far more lively than Jace had imagined, probably because the crowd that had been blocking the gate at Stormwind Keep that morning had all run over here to drink. From the conversations of some people near the door, you could tell many were discussing the kobolds causing trouble in the gold mines again — some wanted the King to quickly find someone to resolve the issue.

Jace wasn't planning on any big meal. He ordered a plate of beans and sliced smoked sausage and was eating propped against the bar when suddenly someone lightly poked his shoulder from behind with a finger.

"Who—" Jace turned his head sharply and saw a green-clothed young woman with pale golden long hair standing behind him. "Sola? You're back?"

"Jace…" Sola looked down and smiled. "I saw you come in from outside and thought you might not recognize me."

"What kind of nonsense is that? I was distracted thinking about something just now," Jace patted his head. "I'm not a stupid lamb. What do you want to eat? My treat."

"Then I won't be polite." Sola stared at the menu hanging beside them.

Mushroom soup, candied venison, blood boar offal, and finally banana bread. Listening to Sola order one item after another, Jace couldn't help sweeping his gaze across the whole tavern hall, wanting to see if Grede had secretly called her here to fleece him.

But in the end it was just Sola — one dragon. Jace couldn't help asking, "How many days have you been back?"

"Five." Sola said. The two found a seat by the window.

"You haven't had a single meal since you got back, have you?" Jace asked as he sat down.

"No, but I haven't enjoyed a single meal either," Sola said. "When are you going to take me out to get a lamb again, Jace? The beasts in Kalimdor are all skin and bones…"

A Warden of the Forest, huh — sounded like the kind of guest who treated the forest like an all-you-can-eat buffet. But green dragons were also part of nature's cycle, so no problem.

Jace took a small pouch of silver coins from his pocket and counted them, confirming he'd brought enough pocket money to cover the bill, then said: "Wait until I get my parents settled, and I'll take you to ask the gryphon master."

"Your parents came to Stormwind?" Sola asked.

"Yeah, we only got back to Stormwind yesterday. They've already moved into the new house," Jace said with some pride.

"I'm really happy for you, Jace." Sola said earnestly. "This has been your wish all along, hasn't it?"

"Yeah, it really hasn't been easy." Jace let out a long breath. "Even now I still feel like all of this is too beautiful to be real."

"Once they're settled, I should go pay them a visit," Sola said.

"You definitely should, and often," Jace said in a low voice. "Spending time with a green dragon is good for their health — so this meal's money won't be wasted."

Sola couldn't help lowering her head and laughing, then said: "I'll remember. But I heard from Master Marin that you went to Quel'Thalas with Vereesa? Or did you go straight from Quel'Thalas to Lordaeron's capital to pick up the Sessos?"

"Yes," Jace said. "Didn't your teacher tell you why?"

"No." Sola's smile stiffened bit by bit. She asked carefully, "Was it… because of something bad?"

"It definitely can't be called good news, but it's in the past," Jace said. "After you all left, the leader of that group of elves we ran into in Winterspring found me in Raven Hill. He wanted to get to the bottom of the Ursales situation. He tried to kill me, forcing me to kill him instead. Looks like I still can't do without you, Sola. The moment I go out alone, there's always some strange person looking to make trouble for me. Damn it."

"Were you hurt?" Sola asked.

"Don't worry, with the priests of Duskwood looking after me, I recovered pretty quickly. No problem now," Jace said with a smile. "Oh, and don't tell my parents about this. They don't need to know."

"All right." Sola nodded. "If there's still anything off about you, remember to tell me, Jace."

"I will." Jace spooned a mouthful of beans and stuffed them in, chewing as he said: "Let's talk about your side instead — you and Lord Grede's trip to Kalimdor."

"Overall it went fairly smoothly, though there was some trouble at the start," Sola said. "We initially decided to land on the eastern coast of Azshara, where we'd set out before. But this time we ran into a storm and got blown off our original route, and then we ran into a naga witch. She and her followers had been trying to track us the whole time. I guessed she was after the treasures on us, but we finally shook them off, fortunately without incident."

"With the help of some Kaldorei sentinels, we met High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind in Astranaar. She took us to Moonglade, and together with the moon priests and druids there, we used the power of that place to cleanse Prince Ilanicus's essence. His soul is now recovering in Moonglade, and he asked me to bring back his thanks to you and Vereesa."

"Father is truly grateful, and also deeply regretful that he can't leave the grove in his condition. So he also said that once he's recovered he wants to come to Stormwind to see you in person, though it might take quite a long time."

Jace nodded. Ilanicus's smooth recovery could mean a great many things. It could be said that the Emerald Dream's severe corruption, plus the fall of the four Dream Wardens, had a great deal to do with his soul essence being trapped in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar.

Now the nightmare corruption in the Emerald Dream had the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza's help in cleansing it, plus the Dreamlord's rebirth. If the Nightmare wanted to corrupt the entire Emerald Dream again, it wouldn't be so simple.

But to him, another matter right now was more important than all these things that hadn't yet arrived. "So, the bow?"

"I knew this was what you cared about most, Jace." Sola smiled, then said: "Father told me that if you want to use black dragon sinew as the bowstring, then the other parts of the bow must also be of matching quality… such as the branches of a war ancient."

Jace knew that in the game, this ancient bow's body was made from the wood of an ancient tree — the Staff of the Ancient Guardians.

Sola continued: "After learning about Onyxia, he gave me his own staff — a wooden staff he'd been carrying since before the War of the Ancients. Is Vereesa in Stormwind? If she's willing, I can take you back south to the Dawnwood Forest at any time, and from there summon the Dream Wardens to craft this longbow personally."

"She's in Stormwind," Jace said at once. "I really can't wait to tell her this…" A green dragon willing to personally help craft this bow — wasn't that way more impressive than having an ancient tree do it?

Jace couldn't help pressing his lips together, but soon realized he'd overlooked something too. "Right, where's Grede? He didn't come to the tavern?"

"He took a job clearing kobolds out of a mine. He went with a group of mercenaries to the Eastvale Logging Camp area — might be two or three days before he's back," Sola said.

Jace stopped what he was doing and asked: "Is something bothering him?"

"You really know him well, Jace." Sola said. "Nothing too serious, actually. He met Mosa, and the two of them spent some time together in Moonglade. But we still had to leave Kalimdor."

Jace said, "Let him find a place to blow off some steam for a while. Those kobolds who only dare harass miners couldn't threaten him even with his bad eye. No need to worry."

"Hmm." Sola nodded. "There's one more thing — what you told me to remember, those bolts of demon cloth. The Kaldorei cleansed all of them for you, and even gifted us some cloth they'd been keeping. They call this cloth 'Mooncloth.'"

"Shame I can't thank them in person," Jace said. "With this cloth, I wonder if my friend can craft a robe powerful enough for me — after all, next we'll be…"

Jace glanced at the guests beside them and still couldn't bring himself to say the words "heading to Draenor." "Is everything ready?" Sola asked.

Jace said, "Once we can bring our friend back, then we just wait for the tower to be finished. Judging by the date Edwin VanCleef gave me, two months at most."

Sola took a deep breath. Undoubtedly, heading to another world was also a decision that required courage for her.

"Before that, though, I still need to make a trip to Grim Batol," Jace said. "Archmage Krasus told me they've found the Cadros Anvil. Whether Grede wants to take on this responsibility or not, I have to help Roktus Darkanvil get in touch with the members of the Thorium Brotherhood and have them go take a look at that anvil. I'll need to trouble you again then, Sola — take me flying around there."

"Don't mention it, Jace. I'm happy carrying you all over the place," Sola said, holding a piece of venison dripping with honey glaze. "As long as before takeoff you can…"

"I remember, I remember — one fat lamb," Jace nodded vigorously twice.
