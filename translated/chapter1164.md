## Chapter 1164: From the Deeper Void?

It was still before noon when Jace arrived at the Slaughtered Lamb carrying a bolt of mooncloth and a pack full of scrolls. This little tavern was always deserted at midday, with no one about. Tharle had so little to do he'd polished those glasses over and over again — even his greetings came out listless.

Entering the basement hall, Jace spotted Erland sitting on the sofa, lost in thought.

He wasn't wearing the leather jacket he usually had on. Instead, he'd properly draped a robe over himself. Judging by the material, it seemed to be stitched from felcloth as well — not exactly handsome, but it did provide a considerable boost to shadow magic power. Still, he didn't look much like Erland Dudmoor right now. With that long hair draped down and his solid, muscular build, he actually bore a bit of a resemblance to Andormas of the Wizard Sanctum.

But right now he was staring grimly at the dim fire in the middle, clearly not in the best of moods. When he noticed Jace coming down, he only looked up and gave a brief greeting.

"Good morning, Jace. If you're here for potions, you're a bit out of luck — Ursula went out to buy herbs."

"What about Jaylen?" Jace asked.

"Mm, she's over at the Wizard Sanctum." Erland said.

"What?"

"At that tailor's in the Wizard Sanctum — Larson. You know the family?"

"Fine." Jace felt Erland was being rather distracted, so he asked, "Is something on your mind? Is Sulenna having an affair?"

"Heh." Erland propped his head up, his whole body shaking once. "There are no messy rules like that between the two of us. It has nothing to do with feelings."

"The Dagger Society's business?" Jace asked.

Erland nodded. "I think Sulenna is being a bit too hasty about entering the Void. Actually, I'd say she's even more impatient than Galders — at least Galders isn't the one coming down to the basement every day to force us to draw magic circles."

Before Jace could say anything, a "clap-clap-clap" of boots on the floor came from the training room.

"Jace?" Sulenna appeared in the passageway. She glanced at Erland. "Don't you think our research into shadow magic has hit a bottleneck? Perhaps a Void passage is the solution — otherwise the orcs wouldn't have mentioned it in all those books."

Erland raised his hand. "Take a look at Jace Sesso here. I feel that given your mastery of shadow magic, you haven't reached the point where you must venture into a Void rift to explore! If you ask me, you've just been nagged incessantly by that voidwalker you summoned until your mind's been messed up."

"Ditoktu hasn't said anything unnecessary to me, Erland," Sulenna said.

"You've summoned a voidwalker too?" Jace asked. "At your place in Old Town, or in the basement?"

"At my place," Sulenna said. "That bastard picked up some girl from Alterac these past two days and rarely goes to my side anymore. Then he fantasizes every day about me getting my mind corrupted by some voidwalker's whispers. I'd like to know whether your suspicious, cautious personality these days was molded by your succubus whispering in your ear at pillow-side every day."

"That fits a warlock's corrupt image even better, doesn't it?" Erland shot back. "Back when I wasn't interested in these shadow incantations, you, Jagin, and Galders would take turns lecturing me at every meeting, implying I was an idiot. Now that I've learned a bit, you think I'm going further and further down the 'dark arts road'?"

"All right, all right." Jace raised both hands. "I've got some new material related to Twisting Nether rifts here. Interested?"

"Yes," Sulenna said.

"No." Erland stood up irritably, then realized his attitude had been too aggressive. He couldn't help but sigh and lower his head to Jace. "Sorry, Jace. I'm not taking it out on you. It's just that these past two days I've been hounded by her and Galders, both taking turns to pressure me into 'communicating with the Void' in this basement, so I'm a bit mentally fatigued. You know that trying to summon demons continuously in the Twisting Nether can get your brain corroded into black water. I need some time to rest too — take a stroll to the market or something."

"I understand." Jace nodded.

Sulenna asked, "You're going to go find that Alterac man again, aren't you?"

"You can come along. I don't mind a threesome, Su," Erland said.

"Go to hell," Sulena said. "I hope your lover is a black dragon too."

"That wouldn't be bad either." Erland sneered. "Then I'd have ridden a dragon — even more impressive than Varian." With that, Erland headed toward the basement entrance. Jace looked at Sulenna and asked, "You sure he's all right?"

"In what regard?" Sulenna asked.

"What you mentioned — the succubus interfering with his mind," Jace said.

"Don't take it so seriously, dear Jace." Sulenna sighed with a smile. "I was just teasing him. All right, I admit I've been pushing him a bit hard lately. But if there's really a chance to enter the Twisting Nether, wouldn't you want to find a breakthrough as soon as possible? You should understand us, after all — that's the source of our power."

"I know," Jace said. "Which is why I've been scrounging up materials here and there these days, looking through these scrolls."

Sulenna nodded, took a scroll from Jace's hand, and opened it to look. Jace pointed at the mysterious pattern on it and asked, "Do you know what this is?"

The female warlock was taken aback when she saw the pattern, then bellowed, "Erland Dudmoor!"

After a moment, a few footsteps came from the passageway entrance. Erland pushed the iron door open. "Why yell so loud? I haven't gone that far."

"Look at this." Sulenna said. "Doesn't it look similar to the pattern on that scroll you got from Booty Bay the other day? The one Galders thought was some kind of Void passage circle."

Erland took the scroll and looked it over carefully, frowning. "It does… it does look alike." He looked up at Jace and asked, "What kind of magic circle is this?"

Sulenna looked over too, her expression full of anticipation. Jace said, "I don't know either, but my demon minions and I suspect it's an unknown system of writing, not a magic circle diagram."

"Related to the Void?" Sulenna said.

"Probably," Jace said. "At the very least, the person who originally owned these materials studied the Twisting Nether."

"Should we ask your voidwalker?" Erland asked, looking at Sulenna.

"What about Galders's?" Jace asked. "Isn't that one right here in the basement?"

"It… got snuffed out," Erland said. "Because of Galders's attempt at summoning. If you ask me, it most likely took the chance to escape back into the Twisting Nether, but whatever Galders says goes."

"Voidwalkers are completely different from other demons, in any case," Sulenna said. "No true name, no conditional exchange, and their very existence is extremely unstable. As things stand, we also can't successfully summon them using the method you brought… If there's no good way to bring my Ditoktu over to this basement, I'm planning to snuff mine out too."

Jace nodded and asked, "Then at least we can ask him about this pattern."

"Let's hope he knows." Sulenna said.

"Then let's head out now." Erland glanced at the bolt of cloth Jace had set to the side. "This is…"

"For Jaylen," Jace said.

"Is this felcloth purified by elves from another continent?" Erland asked.

"Yes," Jace said.

"I can already imagine how excited our tailor is going to be when she sees this." Erland took off his robe. "I'll help you carry it over to her room."

"All right," Jace said. "Then let's hurry over to Sulenna's place."

"Let's go." Sulenna picked up the pack from the table.

The three of them told Tharle to lock up, then hurried all the way to Old Town. None of them brought up lunch — they all wanted to find out as soon as possible what this pattern actually was.

Jace knew it wasn't just him. Sulenna and Erland seemed to have a premonition too — that the voidwalker very likely knew what these patterns represented.

Arriving at the small building Jace had visited a few times before, Sulenna went into the ground-floor hallway and prepared to open the door. Jace asked, "I thought you lived on the upper floor."

"I rented this ground-floor room too, to make it convenient to use the basement here," Sulenna said.

Jace nodded. The three of them entered the room together and went down into the basement. In the pitch-black room, a pair of purple flames floated over there.

Although in the game, the voidwalkers summoned by warlocks and those serving the Burning Legion were classified as demons, in the real world, voidwalkers really weren't the same thing as demons.

The most obvious proof was that even when Jace used Detect Demons, he couldn't make out its shadow — even though it was standing right there, not using any means to shield its presence.

"Hello, Ditoktu." Sulenna glanced at the two beside her. "Let's get straight to the point. I have something I want you to look at. Answer our questions honestly."

Ditoktu stretched out both arms, then slowly nodded.

Erland held up one of the scrolls for the voidwalker to see. "Look at this, Ditoktu. What is it?"

The voidwalker let out a hollow, sighing sound, then brought its head close to the scroll. After muttering for a while, it said in Demonic, "Abyssal."

"Is it Shaith'yar?" Jace asked.

"No. It has nothing to do with them." The voidwalker switched to Common to answer. "This is pure… shadow… given voice, and then recorded."
