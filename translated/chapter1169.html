Chapter 1169: Everyone Wants the High King to Return

After flying into Deadwind Pass, Somaelir dropped her altitude a bit. After all, this gloomy valley was far too dark — if Duskwood's heavy shadows still more or less let a little light through during the day, then this place was practically steeped in pitch-black night from dawn to dusk.

Occasionally some suspicious light source would be seen casting shadows of distant scenery, trees, mountains, or the broken walls and ruins of villages and towns. There was no telling where the magical light was seeping through from.

Doom Lord Kalez could fly straight in and observe the entire valley with his demonic sight. Somaelir could manage some perception relying on draconic vision. But for gryphon or other flying beasts that didn't possess much magical power, Deadwind Pass was pretty much an absolute forbidden zone.

Somalir flew southward along the forest canopy for a distance, moving away from the road that ran from Duskwood to Stonard, plunging fully into the deep darkness of Deadwind Pass.

Jace lay on her neck, observing the ahead, searching for a place where shadow power was quite stable — a suitable location he could use to commune with the Twisting Nether.

Of course, shadow was a chaotic force. Although it wasn't as violent and frenzied as fel energy, trying to find relatively "stable" shadow was still a rather difficult endeavor.

But as long as they were near Karazhan, all types of shadow energy could be found. Ahead, in a silent, hollow stretch of forest, the free-floating dark energy was much calmer than the shadow that permeated those abandoned towns and villages they'd passed through earlier. Probably because of the emotions spread by the agonizing souls who'd perished in Medivh's death.

Not only Jace could sense these terrifying emotions. The others, although they couldn't analyze these emotions as specifically as he could, were still affected by their negative influence.

The two demons couldn't care less, and Grede had been to this area with Jace before, so he had some mental preparation and was fine too. But as for Vereesa, she'd never gotten this close to Karazhan before, so ever since leaving that road up north, her expression had grown worse and worse.

And Somaelir was about the same. As a young green dragon, and one who'd survived the corruption of the Nightmare and now gained the recognition of her kin, she'd grown less susceptible to shadow's influence. Still, Jace kept a constant watch on her emotional fluctuations.

A heavy, cold wind blew from some indeterminate direction, swaying the gray-blue forest below back and forth. In the dark vision, these dead, dark branches writhed like a swarm of arthropods struggling in the agony of death. And Jace had to try to find an opening among these "limbs" — a place suitable for performing a summoning ritual.

But just as he'd focused most of his attention on the fluctuations of shadow energy, he suddenly felt Somaelir's emotions tighten violently.

"What?"

Before this short question was finished, the green dragon suddenly raised her wings and veered her body to the side, shaking the group on her back so badly they nearly all got flung off.

Jace barely managed to hug Somaelir's neck tightly enough not to be thrown down. Then he saw the green dragon at this moment catch and hurl a long spear into the forest…

"Damn, who threw that?" Grede cursed loudly.

"Dammit, it's ogres — I saw their shadows hiding in the forest," Vereesa said. At the same time she stood up, took Belanur, and, on the now-stabilizing green dragon's back, chanted the incantation and aimed at the forest below. Arcane light had already gathered on the bowstring.

Jace looked in the direction she was aiming, but couldn't see anything at all.

He patted the green dragon's neck. "Let's land, Somaelir. Flying up here we'll just be target practice."

"Won't we get swarmed by a whole pack of ogres once we go down?" Somaelir asked.

"That would be fantastic." Grede laughed. "Isn't that exactly what I came for? You can't expect me to lend a hand at a demon summoning ritual, can you?"

"It's not like that," Jace said. "Our friend here has a somewhat special relationship with the ogres."

"Strictly speaking, Lord Kalez is also the High King of the ogres here!" Sazeno poked his head out of Jace's pack and said. "Maybe those ogres will even willingly offer up some souls to contribute to the summoning ritual!"

"Ogre king?" Grede was taken aback. Vereesa also loosened her bowstring in confusion. "It's complicated — I'll explain when there's time," Jace said. "For now let's just go down."

Somalir landed in a sparse stretch of forest. A massive magical beast coming down naturally couldn't be too stealthy. Soon Jace could hear footsteps coming from all directions in the forest.

Grede jumped down from the dragon's back, raising the warhammer in his hand. "So do we fight or not?"

"Wait — let me talk to them." Jace walked to the front, raised both hands high, and shouted toward the forest, "I come on behalf of the ogre High King, bearing his message!"

After saying this, he repeated it in Orcish, then fell silent for a moment to observe the situation in the forest.

The shouting clearly had an effect. By now even the chaotic footsteps in the forest had vanished.

Morophil jumped down from the dragon's back, scanning the dense forest all around. Vereesa held her long bow, warily aiming in front of Jace. Somaelir, on the other hand, was in no hurry to resume human form — after all, when dealing with big fellows like ogres, it was much more convenient to use her true appearance.

Grede stood with his warhammer beneath Somaelir's head. Although he was watching the forest intently, he would occasionally brush aside the golden chain ornaments that kept slapping him in the face as her neck moved.

"High King has abandoned his crown!" a roar came from the forest.

"Though he has been the longest-ruling king since the Horde's defeat…" another voice said.

"The greatest and most powerful king!"

"But he abandoned the king's throne! Our king now is the Cursed One Mashvol — he will lead us to devour the Black Tower's magical power, surpassing all ogres and humans."

"And orcs!"

"The orcs are all dead — no need to surpass them!"

"Only cowards take orcs as rivals!"

"Are humans even more powerful than orcs, Kagwugg?"

"But there are more humans!"

The several people beside the green dragon looked at each other. None of them expected the ogres lying in ambush in the forest to start arguing among themselves first.

"Then what if the High King announces he's coming back?" Jace shouted loudly.

"You claim you can bring back the High King?" an angry roar came from the forest. Jace saw a bare-chested ogre carrying a spear that was quite short by his standards walk out of the forest.

Compared to those abandoned Stone Fists in the Alterac Mountains, this ogre was truly solid and powerfully built.

"No one could make contact with the High King — not even when the magician Nagiva was alive. You think you can do it?"

At this moment, the several ogre soldiers following behind him also walked out. One of the followers said in Orcish, "I recognize that wingless succubus. When the High King came to our clan before, she was right by his side!"

"Being remembered by an idiot like this is truly an honor." Morophil smiled.

"You understand Orcish?" Grede asked.

"I'm a demon, not a dwarf." The succubus tilted her head.

Hearing his own retainer say this, the powerfully built ogre seemed to hesitate for a moment. Jace asked, "Are you the Cursed One Mashvol?"

"No." The ogre said. "We are loyal to the Cursed One Mashvol. If you can summon back the High King, King Mashvol will definitely be willing to see you. Before the magician Nagiva died, the King was helping him try to bring back the High King. He built altars and magic circles according to the orc methods, because as long as the High King returns, he can destroy those traitors."

"The Headwind clan?" Jace asked.

"Yes." The ogre raised the club in his hand high. "They betrayed us who wanted to bring back the High King. They believed the magician Nagiva was performing rituals to use the Black Tower's power to contact the Burning Legion — how foolish! But many ogres believed their claims. They call themselves the Headwind clan, hiding in the forest to the southeast…"

"Looks like we don't need to find a suitable place to perform the summoning after all, Master." Morophil said softly. "These ogres aren't complete idiots either."

"But do you think their magic is reliable, Morophil?" Vereesa asked.

"We can take a look first," Jace said.

"All right," Vereesa said.

Jace nodded. "Take me to your altar, and before your current king. I will attempt to summon back your High King."

"I think you've got a big mouth, little human," the ogre said.

Jace pursed his lips and clenched his right hand. A dead tree beside the ogre was immediately wreathed in a mass of purple-red demonic flame. The howling Shadowflame spiraled upward, and in the blink of an eye burned the tree to ashes.

The ogres who saw this immediately roared loudly. The ogre chief leading them, holding his club, was also startled, his mouth slightly agape as he stared at that tree.

"I'll take you to find King Mashvol!"
