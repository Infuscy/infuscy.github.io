Chapter 1171: Dangerous Coordinates

Kalez's appearance finally made the altar the ogres had built hold up no longer — it was cracking severely everywhere. The demon set his black hoof directly onto the ground and crushed the bricks and stones to powder, pressing out a hoof print. Now Jace had no choice but to step down from this crude stone platform along with the others. The Doom Lord's armor was already battered and tattered, but his physical condition looked quite good. His mane drifted like brown flames in the night sky. The fel energy in his two small eyes glinted like sparks. His arms and legs were sturdy and powerful too — you could barely see the exhaustion and wounds all over his body from when he'd fought Onyxia to the bitter end. Over this period of time, he'd clearly gotten respite and recovery in the Twisting Nether. The ogres around the altar roared like a tidal wave in all sorts of languages, hollering all kinds of slogans expressing their loyalty. The entire forest echoed with their intense dark emotions, brimming with panic and dread. Vereesa raised her bow slightly and drew the bowstring with her other hand. Grede stood not far away, having just dodged a large scattering of rubble blown out by the air currents when the Twisting Nether rift closed. Seeing that demon appear, he clearly wasn't thinking of fighting — after all, this counted as a "comrade" who'd fought together before Onyxia. But seeing Vereesa's wary reaction, he also felt a bit uneasy and hefted his warhammer. Solia looked at Vereesa, then glanced at Grede, then looked back toward Jace's side. Although she seemed to want to believe Jace could still keep the situation under control, her expression was still involuntarily tense, her brow gradually furrowing tight. "Welcome back to reality, Kalez." Morophil sheathed the twin blades at her waist and looked up to say in Demonic. "I imagine the chaotic time in the Twisting Nether has left you a bit unclear on the situation." "There are no enemies here worth my dealing with, sayard." The great demon slowly spread his enormous, sail-like brown-black leathery wings. "If not for dealing with that black dragon, why would Jace Sesso be in such a hurry to call me back?" With that, he turned his head to gaze in Karazhan's direction. Although the tall tower couldn't be seen with the naked eye from here, Jace knew he could see it crystal clear with his demonic eyes. "Though I have to admit, the shadow power obtained near this tower is even more abundant than in that corner of the Twisting Nether." The Doom Lord clenched his fist. "Because Master has an even grander goal!" Sazeno looked up. "Lord Kalez, we're practically old acquaintances by now, hmm? Compared to the goal this time, repelling a great black dragon is nothing! And besides you, there's not a second person in the whole world who could do it!" "Is that so?" Kalez snorted, stroking the tentacle hanging down from beside his neck with a thick finger. "Sounds quite interesting." "Are you certain he's still on our side, Jace?" Vereesa asked. The Doom Lord's small eyes flicked over the long bow in Vereesa's hand, then switched to Common. "What, you'd prefer I immediately magic away all you little bugs?" The elf glared at the demon and gritted her teeth. Jace immediately said, "Don't mind his words, Wen. As long as I'm still alive, he can't defy the contract and attack us." "Regrettably, that is indeed true." The great demon admitted it frankly. Instead, Vereesa was the one who couldn't adjust right away — her expression, fixed on the demon, stiffened slightly. "But don't forget your condition, Jace Sesso." The Doom Lord pointed at Jace with his sharp black fingernail. "I want Kazak's death. I think you're very well aware that destroying a demon outside the Twisting Nether hardly counts as death." "I haven't forgotten, and I'm actively working toward this goal," Jace smiled. "And in this process, I need your active cooperation too." "I don't see where your active effort is." Kalez folded his giant wings behind him. "You executed Dasmora and seized the demonic artifact that Kil'jaeden had always wanted to take from Sathael — that made me quite optimistic that you'd be able to put Kazak to death one day. But clearly, enraging that black dragon who's lived over ten thousand years had absolutely nothing to do with the promise of hunting Kazak. I just didn't want you to lose Ulthalesh so easily, so I answered that imp's summoning…" "You'll see my effort soon." Jace glanced at Vereesa, who had also relaxed quite a bit by now and slowly nodded. She handed the Draenei crystal recording the Draenor teleportation coordinates to Jace. Jace raised the crystal high. Kalez reached out a hand, and the crystal was immediately pulled by a mass of shadow energy, floating before the great demon's eyes under his guidance. "Mm, this is something the draenei made." Kalez snorted. "What coordinates are recorded on this — Draenor?" "You don't know either?" Jace asked. "The supreme commander in charge of attacking Draenor was Magtheridon." The great demon said. "Besides him, and Lord Kazak, the King of the Eredaru, and Lord Kil'jaeden, no one holds Draenor's teleportation coordinates. Kazak doesn't trust me — he wouldn't divulge these things to me. Are you planning to go to Draenor?" "Yes," Vereesa asked. "Can you help?" Kalez observed this Draenei crystal floating in the air a bit further, then after a while said: "Judging from the energy radiating from these teleportation coordinates, it does indeed resemble a world like Draenor." The demon took a step forward, drew out his chest, and felt the shadow power of this valley. He continued, "If there's a sufficiently powerful energy source providing me with a steady stream of mana, I can maintain a teleportation rift to send you over. Back in the Burning Legion, this kind of mana was maintained by fel crystals forged by the draenei and the souls of countless pitiful mortals. But here, I can't stabilize the power released from this Black Tower." "I know," Jace said. "I'll handle that part — no need for you to worry." "Since you put it that way… but I still have one concern." The great demon added, "Although Kazak is the leader of all the Eredaru, that doesn't mean he's currently in Draenor." "At the very least, he's definitely not in Azeroth," Jace said. "You can treat that as the starting point of my hunting journey." "There's some sense in that." Kalez hummed, then asked slowly, "Can you be certain these coordinates lead to what specific location?" "From what I can see, this looks like something inscribed by magic by an Eredar sorcerer, or a student of one. It would very likely send you straight into Magtheridon's fortress. I wouldn't want Ulthalesh to just fall into his hands like that." "So relying on this coordinate alone, you can't help us determine what's on the other side either," Jace said. "Obviously." Kalez said in a low rumble. Vereesa couldn't help but sigh. Just as Jace had been worried about all along, a teleportation coordinate held by an orc warlock was truly hard to trust. Relying on just this one coordinate, they couldn't rashly set out on the journey into Draenor either. Still, at least Kalez claimed he could send them as far as Draenor relying on the teleportation coordinates and sufficiently powerful mana support. That had already put quite a few of their concerns to rest. "Let us discuss this for a moment," Jace said. Kalez said no more. He slowly raised his head. As his entire body's center of gravity shifted slightly, the already cracked altar beneath his hooves was ground to even finer pieces by his hooves. The entire forest clearing echoed with the noise of gravel churning. Jace had nearly forgotten about the ogre chief Kigu and his surrounding kin — after all, they'd already been so frightened they were prostrate on the ground, not daring to breathe. Now that the Doom Lord had moved his body slightly, the ogres grew even more terrified all at once, several of them even throwing themselves flat on the ground, trembling. But not all the ogres had been scared into a stupor. Seeing that things had quieted on the altar, one rather goofy-looking ogre struggled to raise his head. He shouted in halting Common toward Kalez: "The great High King has come here once again! This human warlock did not deceive us… High King, there is a group of self-righteous traitors who left your throne and stronghold, fleeing into the forest to the southeast to set up their own stronghold. Please, lead us to destroy that 'Headwind' clan!" "But our king now is a Sayard — she just defeated the former king, the Cursed One Mashvol, with your own eyes!" "Long live the new Queen Sayard! I must worship Queen Sayard!" "But the High King was brought back by the Queen's master from the Twisting Nether!" "The one who brought back the High King is the Queen's master!" "What kind of king is the Queen's master?" Several ogres with some guts, one after another, actually started arguing about this topic down below. But aside from themselves, neither Kalez nor Morophil cared in the slightest about who was king. They couldn't even be bothered to look at them. Jace also had no mood to deal with the ogres' succession struggle at this moment. Clearly the more important matter right now was the problem of the teleportation coordinates. "What do we do now?" Grede asked. "There's one more possible avenue." Jace looked at Vereesa. "Remember those Lost Ones I mentioned to you before?" Grede jabbed his thumb toward the east. "You mean those monsters we ran into east of Stonard?" "Yes." Vereesa frowned. "But even the Frostwolf orc chieftain didn't have Draenor's coordinates, Jace. Would those creatures who've already lost their minds have preserved those things?" "We won't know until we ask," Jace said. "Remember what we agreed on, Wen — if there's only this one coordinate, we can't just rush over there so recklessly. I believe even Sylvanas wouldn't agree to us being this rash." "Alleria even less so," Vereesa murmured. "Then let's go take a look around Stonard first," Jace said. Vereesa and Solia nodded. Grede agreed too: "Not like I've got anything better to do anyway."
