Chapter 1174: A Reasonable Exchange

Noburu, his head wrapped in tree roots, nodded vigorously. Only then did Sola extend a finger and loosen the roots from his mouth. "Did the Bloodmaul orcs send you?" Noburu said with a cough. "If you mean no harm, then you've been tricked by them. They won't help you use this teleportation coordinate — they'll kill you and take your results for themselves... That's always how those orcs 'cooperate' with outsiders!" "We have nothing to do with orcs," Wynneza said. "Why would you think orcs sent us?" "Because only the Bloodmaul know the teleportation coordinate is in our possession." Noburu glanced at several of his tightly bound companions. "The reason they wanted to attack is that they know some humans come to this forest not to hunt orcs and demons, but to search for treasure and wealth. Some of them are even hired by orcs..." "So you actually have the teleportation coordinate?" Jace asked. Noburu narrowed his crystal-blue eyes and asked suspiciously, "You mean you weren't even sure whether it existed before you dared come here?" "Our plan was to search out everyone who came through the Dark Portal, looking for a way to Draenor," Wynneza said. "Whether it's orcs, demons, or you draenei — whatever form the draenei take. We're not looking for a teleportation coordinate to help the orcs. We want to find the Azerothian Expedition that's lost on the other side of the Dark Portal!" "If you know about the Dark Portal campaign from a few years ago, you should know what happened to the Expedition that fought the orcs," Jace said. "We've encountered some Lost Ones who clearly also know about the Dark Portal's closure and the disaster that happened in Draenor. I imagine they learned that from you as well." Noburu visibly hesitated. He lowered his head and thought for a moment before finally nodding and saying, "So that's how it is. Yes, those Lost Ones learned about the Dark Portal from us." The other Broken also calmed down considerably after learning this and stopped trying to struggle. Jace said, "Let them down, Sola." Sola nodded, and the layers of coiled tree roots loosened their grip and returned to the ground, setting the Broken back where they stood. Noburu rotated his thick wrists and said, "I'm sorry, but although the teleportation coordinate is in our possession, it's already been tainted by dark energy. The information recorded within has likely been partially affected as well." "What happened?" Wynneza asked. "Our kin — those Lost Ones — have been trying to seize it all along," Noburu explained. "Even though they have no ability to activate teleportation magic at all, and this teleportation coordinate is of no use to them, they attack us and seize everything related to magical energy purely on raw impulse and instinct. The most important target is that teleportation coordinate crystal." He coughed violently and continued, "Since we fled here, we've been forced to relocate again and again. But once, along the way, we were intercepted by a group of Lost Ones. It was during that conflict that the record crystal holding the teleportation coordinate was tainted by dark magic by a maddened Lost One warlock." "If they also want to go back, why would they destroy the teleportation coordinate?" Grede asked. "We can't understand their logic any more than you can, even though they are our kin," Noburu said. "Perhaps it's because they believe they've been cursed by shadow and can't successfully activate the teleportation coordinate — that the teleportation coordinate must also be influenced by shadow before it can be used by them... Beyond that, I can't think of any other explanation that makes sense." "After that battle we lost many — killed in battle, died of grave wounds... and many others gradually lost themselves to anger and grief and joined them. Now, fewer and fewer of us can maintain our reason. As the crystals here slowly lose their power, as our leader grows weaker, every person here could collapse and fall in with them at any moment." "The longing for home was originally the courage our kind struggled to survive on. Now it's become a curse. Before long, perhaps even I will become one of those repulsive creatures, lost forever in this great swamp." "Perhaps you could leave here," Sola said, pointing into the distance. "Head north, or to another continent, far from the Dark Portal's corrupting influence, and you could walk out of this shadow." Noburu said, "We aren't trusted by anyone. Whether orcs, humans, or trolls, they all see us as enemies. Perhaps a few years ago it would've been possible, but now there are only a handful of us left. We no longer have the strength for a long migration. Your arrival is probably fate's last shred of mercy for us. After you leave here, you can carry our story to the outside world, so everyone knows that those crazed beast-men deep in the swamp once struggled, once resisted..." The group fell silent for a moment. Noburu shook his head and said, "I'm sorry we couldn't help you. You should know — we want to return to Draenor more than anyone." "Is there no way to repair the teleportation coordinate?" Jace asked. "The mage among us who could repair it was already killed," Noburu said. "At present, unless there's another intact Draenor teleportation coordinate crystal to serve as reference, and a caster skilled in teleportation magic to mend it, it can't be reactivated. There's no one here who can do that." The dwarf, the elf, and the green dragon all looked toward Jace at the same time. The words "Isn't that exactly what our teleportation coordinate is?" were practically visible on their faces. But everyone tacitly chose not to speak. After all, the Broken had already shown intense hostility toward orcs. And the teleportation coordinate in Jace's possession wasn't just connected to orcs — it was inextricably linked to an orc warlock and an eredar demon. If they revealed that coordinate here, it would probably mean a complete breakdown of relations. These Broken had already admitted they had no ability to repair the coordinate. Showing them their own wouldn't accomplish anything. If they could get their hands on the Broken's coordinate, they didn't know whether Kallrez could repair it. "Can we take a look at the damaged coordinate?" Jace said. "Perhaps we can find someone who can repair it. Do you know the Kirin Tor?" "The Arcane Eye sigil on that castle," Noburu said. "We know this magical organization. They possess many powerful arcanists. Can you really find a member of the Kirin Tor to try to repair this coordinate?" "I can't guarantee a hundred percent," Jace showed the sigil on his Kirin Tor trust ring, "but at present, you seem to be out of options." "That is indeed the case." Noburu glanced at the sigil, then looked at the other Broken companions and said in Eredar, "Even if Magtheridon were to wake up, he probably wouldn't have the ability to repair this teleportation coordinate. We must seek help from these otherworlders." "Can they take us back to Draenor?" one of the Broken asked. Noburu looked at Jace and said, "We have a condition." "Go ahead," Jace said, pretending not to understand. "If you're willing to hand over the teleportation coordinate to us, we're naturally willing to help you as much as we can." "Perhaps you've already guessed, Jace of Stormwind," Noburu said. "It's this: if the teleportation coordinate can be made to work, I hope you can take us back to the other side. This isn't without benefit to you. We know Draenor well, and once we arrive there we can serve as your guides — even if it may have become something quite different by then." "No problem," Jace said. Several of the Broken seemed unable to contain their excitement. After all, they were on the verge of losing themselves in this alien world, and suddenly a solution had come knocking on its own. But not all the Broken were equally excited. the female Broken asked in Eredar, "Aren't you going to ask for Magtheridon's opinion, Commander Noburu?" "If he were still able to give it, of course," Noburu said. "But he's in a coma now, and there's no telling how long it will be before he wakes and can take charge. So I am the commander here. But do you think that even if he woke up, he would stop us from exploring a way back to Draenor?" "No." The female Broken lowered her head. "Then there's nothing more to say, my lady." Noburu finished speaking, then beckoned and switched back to Common. "Follow me, everyone."
