## Chapter 1179: Void Hunt

After dinner, the warlocks drank their potent Shadow Protection potions and mana potions and came to the practice room — it was time to summon the voidwalker.

Various runes and magic lines had already been drawn on the practice room floor. For tonight's gathering, Galders and Erland had drawn a special Void Binding ward for hunting and capturing voidwalkers.

Still, Jace had no intention of trying to summon an unprecedented voidwalker just to show off that he had more "talent" than Galders or anyone else in the Dagger Circle.

Although Ursulus wasn't a staff specifically for summoning powerful beings, he had no plans to use its power to cast the spell either — after all, such a powerful shadow amplifier could bring unknown results.

He just needed to follow the voidwalker summoning methodology that Galders and the other warlocks had compiled, step by step.

After all, this time was just about finding a "consultant" for the Dagger Circle who could answer questions about the Void rifts — not summoning a master over.

The Dagger Circle had already summoned two voidwalkers. Galders' was quite powerful, and despite being bound, its dark energy still made the entire basement feel somewhat wrong. Sulenna's was weaker, but it was also a genuine voidwalker.

Probably because they were already quite experienced in dealing with voidwalkers, the other members of the Dagger Circle's attitude was different from Jace's this time — they weren't as tense about this summoning ritual as they'd been during the first succubus summoning.

Jace walked to the front of the group and came to the edge of the binding ward, taking one last look at the summoning incantation.

Although it wasn't summoning a demon, the incantation was still written in Eredar — probably because the spell had been invented by the eredar.

The eredar had likely used their dark power to manipulate the Twisting Nether to forcibly drag voidwalkers over to be enslaved by them — rather than summoning them the way demons were summoned, it was more like luring them over with a powerful, edible energy.

"Xa'athosian..."

Jace raised his right hand and closed his eyes, chanting earnestly. "Karoth, Olithayara..."

When he reached this point, he followed what was recorded on the scroll, sensing his connection to shadow and even to the Twisting Nether that existed on another plane.

For him, this was already a well-trodden path — no difficulty at all — and this incantation quickly brought him to some kind of "edge" of the Twisting Nether. It felt as if taking one more step forward would pull him into that boundless, endless cold darkness.

Jace carefully groped along the edge of the Void, feeling for something he could "capture." During this period, the first half of the incantation needed to be chanted continuously to maintain this state.

Shadow power constantly swirled around him, to the point where the purple light emanating from it was visible to the naked eye.

The warlocks of the Dagger Circle had only looked on with somewhat solemn expressions at first. But when the shadow magic beneath Jace's feet also corroded a ward-like pattern into the ground, Galders slightly furrowed his brow.

"Why is it taking so long?" Erland couldn't help asking.

"I don't know." Galders glanced at his pocket watch and said quietly, "I only went through about three or four rounds of chanting myself. What about you, Sulenna?"

"I don't remember — it was a long time, but I don't remember having..." Sulenna stared at the shadow magical light beneath Jace's hands and feet and didn't continue, perhaps worried about disturbing his casting.

Although Jace was already completely immersed in that darkness, he could still hear the faint sound of the people around him talking — it just felt like his brain was about to be unable to comprehend Common under these circumstances.

"This is his first attempt at summoning, Galders — that's perfectly normal," Ursula said quietly. "Don't forget that Jaylene and I still can't successfully summon a voidwalker to this day."

"Nor can I," Erland muttered.

"Mm," Galders responded softly.

"Maybe we could rest for a moment and then try again, Jace," Ursula called. "Your summoning ritual has already lasted much longer than when Galders and Sulenna succeeded... going on like this, it might get harder and harder to concentrate, which would actually make it less favorable to completing the ritual successfully."

Although Jace heard Ursula's words, he didn't immediately give a physical or verbal response. Because his consciousness was now immersed in the distant Twisting Nether, controlling his own body had become somewhat sluggish.

But that didn't mean Jace disagreed with Ursula. He also felt this wasn't the time to show off.

Thinking this, he stopped chanting, and his consciousness began to withdraw from the Void, gradually returning to reality... when suddenly he felt a powerful hand grab his consciousness!

It was as if something had suddenly yanked hard on a string in his brain. Jace manipulated the shadow in the Void and tried to break free forcibly, but it was as if his consciousness had been stretched out a section — he couldn't completely escape this binding in the Void.

Jace felt a surge of despair and helplessness rise in his heart along with the force's grip. But this wasn't any emotion born of being powerless and trapped in another plane — it was more like this mass of Void power was forcibly implanting emotions into him to make him give up resisting.

Jace, however, was already thoroughly accustomed to this kind of forced influence.

He decisively turned his emotions around, recalling the scene of Deathwing bursting apart in the burning sky, recalling the desperate roar of Hakkar's avatar, recalling Onyxia's shocked expression when she was grievously wounded by the Hand of Gul'dan...

In an instant, a fierce arrogance and pride seized his consciousness like a beast, tearing apart the restraint that had been clinging tightly to him... When he'd pulled some distance away, the appearance of that thing reflected in Jace's mind from afar.

That was a sturdy, tenacious mass of Void energy, glaring at him furiously at this moment. One of its arms had just been deflected by that shadow power, its body leaning backward, appearing somewhat deformed.

This was a Void being — a voidwalker. But unlike the ones Galders and Sulenna had summoned before, its body was much more solid, like a living, breathing purple demon. In the instant Jace broke free, he could even clearly sense its muscular lines, its angry bloody maw, and the purple flames burning all over its body through his consciousness.

Still, Jace couldn't possibly be driven by the arrogance and pride he'd deliberately summoned to duel this voidwalker within the plane of the Twisting Nether. After all, in the Twisting Nether he was just a mass of consciousness reaching over, while the other party was a genuinely powerful force. So Jace decisively retreated while the other party had been shaken free, disengaging from the Void...

What Jace didn't expect was that his escape just now seemed to have angered the other party, and it was unwilling to let him go so easily.

This mass of Void energy seemed to follow him as he disengaged from the Void, becoming more and more tangible, drilling out of a passage in the Twisting Nether and forcefully charging after him.

When Jace opened his eyes again, arcs of shadow energy like lightning actually flickered back and forth in the practice room several times. Jace clutched his head, which had developed a splitting ache from exploring the Twisting Nether, and said through gritted teeth: "Be careful — something followed me!"

Before the warlocks could raise their implements, in the flickering of shadow lightning, a powerful shadow hand tore a rift in the air. This hand grabbed the chaotic shadow energy drifting around it, preventing the rift from closing, and a low roar staggered everyone in the practice room a step.

The Void Binding ward on the rift suddenly lit up. A struggling head emerged from the rift. It opened its giant mouth, like a torn chunk of sticky gore, and roared, then frantically tried to squeeze in through the rapidly closing rift.

The armor on its body was pressed out of shape, cracks appearing, fragments erupting like crystal shards. The warlocks hastily conjured their respective defenses and barely managed to withstand it.

"Anuniji, Durazala, Kana!"

Sulenna bit her lip and chanted a Curse of Weakness, but it was obvious this Void being didn't fear shadow magic of this level — it only trembled slightly and became angrier.

By now its entire body had already emerged from the rift. It swayed back and forth like an expanding cloud of smoke and flame, tearing at the power of the Void Binding ward. The lines and runes of the ward were visibly dimming...

A high shout of an incantation from the side — a burst of green fire suddenly exploded on the voidwalker's body. Chaotic fel fire ignited half its body at extreme speed.

This was Erland's Immolate magic. This spell finally weakened the voidwalker's momentum somewhat, allowing the ward and Jace's incantation to regain the upper hand.

Galders, Jaylene, and Ursula had all raised their staves and magical implements, casting spells at this towering Void being.

The warlocks of the Dagger Circle understood shadow most thoroughly, and the spells they were familiar with were naturally all shadow power. It's just that although this Void being wasn't completely immune to shadow, it possessed extremely strong shadow resistance. Whether it was Shadowbolt, Corruption, or various other shadow control magic, none could achieve much effect.

Most importantly, this voidwalker's power seemed to be bottomless as it resisted furiously. The practice room wasn't that spacious, and by now various fel and shadow magic had filled the place. Without the potent Shadow Protection potions, they probably would have already been torn to grave injuries by the dark magic here.

This wasn't the time for hesitation. Going on like this, the entire practice room — and even the Mage Quarter outside — could be in trouble. So they had to use some special measures.

Jace gripped Ursulus and seized the shadow power gathered in the room, then ignited it with flame elements, conjuring a brilliant orb of Shadowflame from his hand.

He put his arm into a full swing and hurled this orb of flame into the body of the voidwalker before him. The voidwalker's body was instantly flooded with blood-red from its pure purple.

The flames emanating from its body became uncontrolled and chaotic, and in an instant lost the savage energy from before that had wanted to completely devour everyone present.

The armor on the voidwalker's chest was burned by the flames until it twisted, blackened, and fell off, leaving only the armor on its right shoulder still intact.

After struggling for a while, it finally used its own body to endure the torment of this Shadowflame, but it no longer had the strength to resist further.

Its form shrank by at least half. Even so, it still stood a full head taller than the voidwalkers summoned by Galders and Sulenna.

Jace stood before the voidwalker, a second Shadowflame already conjured in his hand. By now his upper clothing had been completely burned away by the purple-red magic orb in his hand. The magic-bound cloth wrapped around Ursulus could barely hold on either, already soaked through with blood.

"If you still plan to keep struggling, I don't mind throwing another one into your body," he warned in Eredar.

"Enough," the Void being before him said weakly. "I have recognized your power... now I must pay the price. I am yours, master."
