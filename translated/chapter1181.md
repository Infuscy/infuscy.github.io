## Chapter 1181: Devourer of Shadow

Jace hadn't rested well at all that night, spending most of it inspecting the damage in the tavern basement together with Galders, Ursula, and Jaylene.

By the wee hours, Erland returned from SI:7, but things hadn't gone as smoothly as expected. Mathias Shaw had gone to Lordaeron, and the others couldn't communicate the complicated situation regarding the Slaughtered Lamb to the Wizard Sanctum. The Wizard Sanctum had indeed detected the commotion from the tavern and didn't want SI:7 meddling in the affairs of the Mage Quarter.

In the end, Jace had to go to the mage tower himself in the middle of the night and explain the situation to Maginot Dumar, taking responsibility for this incident, before finally putting the Wizard Sanctum's concerns to rest.

Back at the Slaughtered Lamb, everyone was leaning against the corridor outside the practice room waiting for news. The entire basement still echoed with the ethereal rumbling emanating from the Voidlord. Considering the complicated situation in the Mage Quarter, and this Void creature that was far stronger than expected...

...nobody could rest easy. Only after delivering the response from the Wizard Sanctum did everyone relax a little.

After sending Sulenna, who had been more seriously injured in the battle, to rest in a room in the tavern's back kitchen, and seeing the sun that was almost overhead outside the door, Jace had no mood for sleep either.

This unexpected conflict had brought a considerable degree of shadow corruption to many parts of the basement. Even in the tavern — the room where the corridor entrance to the basement was located — had been affected. But the cleanup process went surprisingly smoothly, as if someone were helping them with the purification.

At first Galders found it somewhat strange, but he quickly realized that the one "purifying" for them was no one else — it was this Voidlord.

Unlike ordinary voidwalkers, this special kind of Void being would devour all uncontrolled magical energy and convert it entirely into part of its shadow body. The most powerful Voidlords would even devour different planes — like a walking cosmic destroyer in the Void.

Returning to the basement once more, the "Drowsy" Gaothoros was even brighter than when it was first subdued. After taking back some of the power it had released over the course of the night, it actually seemed to have grown a bit larger than last night.

The Void flames quietly burning on this fellow's head were already touching the ceiling of the basement. Fortunately, these swaying dark lights weren't real flames that could burn things — just a manifestation of the power on a Void being's body. Otherwise, who knows whether this room could keep it bound here for long.

Although this Voidlord had already been subdued by his shadow power and acknowledged him as its master, Jace didn't feel the kind of mental strain that came with enslaving a demon.

Although being unfamiliar with the process of enslaving a Voidlord made Jace feel this wasn't very secure, this also had its good side for him.

What he worried about most was that before long he might need to go to Draenor. If he was still controlling such an extraordinary and powerful minion here, it would bring extra pressure to his mind and very likely affect his combat performance.

By now, Kallrez alone already occupied a considerable portion of his mind. And as Morophil's power grew stronger, the pressure brought by her contract was also gradually becoming significant.

If Gaothoros were a demon, and the other members of the Dagger Circle had no way to take over the enslavement of this Voidlord for him, then Jace would have no choice but to send him back to the Twisting Nether and be rid of this trouble.

Coming before the Voidlord, this creature's two eyes drifted down warmly like flickering ghostly will-o'-wisps, fixing on his master, who was a full head shorter than it.

"You call yourself the Lord of the Biraatu Shards." Jace asked in Eredar, "Tell me — what kind of place is that?"

"I was born in Biraatu, but Biraatu was destroyed by the demons of the Burning Legion." Gaothoros raised one arm and answered in halting Eredar. "Since the destruction of my homeland, I and my minions wandered... until I met you, mortal. You wanted to take my minions — I had to protect, had to devour you, feed... transcend! But now, I myself must submit, to not be completely annihilated."

Gaothoros made no attempt to conceal his resentment and unwillingness, yet at the same time his tone was humble — practically the complete opposite of the demons Jace had caught before. It made him a bit unaccustomed.

Upon hearing that Biraatu had already been destroyed, Galders was visibly disappointed, probably because he'd still been hoping to have one more option.

Kaylane Arrow, who had also been awake all night long, on the other hand, seemed even more energized. She kept scribbling in a small notebook with a pen, occasionally looking up to observe this Voidlord. She'd probably already calculated in her head how to use this Voidlord's power to strengthen the shadow properties of her robe.

"Thank you for your honesty, Gaothoros." Jace rubbed his temples and nodded. "Have you ever heard of a place called the Telogus Rift?"

"Countless different Void rifts fill the Twisting Nether, and countless of them have no names," Gaothoros answered. "Telogus... hmm, haven't been there, haven't heard of it, don't know its master, don't know when it was born or destroyed — just like the thousands of rifts I don't know, Void..."

"Ask him, Jace — if we find the exact location of a Void rift," Galders said, walking over in Eredar. "As a Void being possessing this kind of power, can he take all of us to that rift?"

"Answer his question," Jace ordered.

Gaothoros stared at Jace and said, "I cannot transport mortals, unless... voided."

"Voided?" Galders looked at Jace and the warlocks behind him. "What's that?"

Kaylane looked up and put the tip of her pen to her lips. "Voiding is actually the 'digestion' method of these powerful voidwalkers, Galders. I came across it when I was looking up information on voidwalkers in Dalaran. These special voidwalkers cross different planes and convert all things into shadow to eat them and grow. To put it simply, he plans to eat us and then flee into the Twisting Nether — wouldn't that count as 'taking' us in?"

Erland looked at Kaylene and said, "Isn't that the same as a mage planning to take his friend on a travel trip by first cutting him into pieces and eating him, then casting a teleportation spell?"

"Seems like there's really no difference," Kaylene said.

"Then he's really quite honest," Grede said with a laugh.

Kaylene was partially correct, because Jace knew that between being voided and being eaten, there was actually a process.

The blood elves taken into the Telogus Rift by Magister Umbric were captured by the Voidlord there and subjected to "voiding."

The shadow beings had attempted to convert them into shadow beings to enslave or devour them, but those blood elves were fortunate enough to be rescued during the process, so they remained in that half-mortal, half-Void state.

Still, Jace had no intention of revealing things that hadn't happened yet in advance.

The reason void elves were ultimately able to gain their freedom was that they learned from Alleria — who had already learned the secrets of the Void in the Twisting Nether — the method of maintaining sanity in the Void state. But Jace knew nothing of that secret, so if they were voided now, all that awaited them would be getting eaten.

"Leaving teleportation aside — can you help interpret these runes, Gaothoros?" Jace handed one of the scrolls to the Voidlord.

"Thalassian." Gaothoros lowered his head, reached out and unrolled the scroll, lifting it into the air with shadow power, then slowly pressed his other hand upon it.

At first Jace had hoped that, as a "lord," he could interpret faster than the voidwalker in Sulenna's basement. But in the end, he also started slowly "reading" out the complex patterns on these runes in exactly the same way.

Perhaps this was just the general way these strange runes were interpreted.

Here at the Slaughtered Lamb, because the basement was much larger and protected by the magical runes Galders had set up, it wasn't so simple for the commotion to spread outside, so he could carry out the interpretation here with peace of mind. But Jace couldn't stay here watching him explain that thick stack of scrolls and materials all day.

"In a few days I'll need to leave Stormwind again and head north, Galders," Jace said. "So I'll need to leave these scrolls to you all to study. You understand voidwalkers better than I do — I don't think there should be a problem."

Galders nodded, and Kaylene also stood up and said, "Leave them to us, Jace."
