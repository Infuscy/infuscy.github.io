Chapter 1182: News from Amy

Time quickly rolled into May. Back in Lordaeron it might still have been a bit chilly, but here in Stormwind, the first hints of summer heat were already creeping in.

Once May arrived, it meant Jace and Grede would soon be heading to Searing Gorge to make contact with the Thorium Brotherhood. Of course, before that, he had to take Sola out for a proper meal — eat well before hitting the road, after all.

Truth be told, getting to Searing Gorge quickly pretty much required Sola to carry them. Ordinary gryphons had a hard time flying with any composure over the Burning Steppes and Searing Gorge.

Near Blackrock Mountain, the scorching ash and heat were enough to overwhelm even the strongest gryphon. It was partly for this reason that Orgrim Doomhammer had been able to make his stand here against the Alliance army led by Lord Anduin Lothar with nothing more than a ragged remnant of troops.

Going on foot would've meant God knows how much time wasted on the road.

Having arranged to set out with Sola today for a meal, Jace made his way to the Mage Quarter, and along the way he noticed that the flower beds and pathways here had once again been filled with a fresh crop of young, unfamiliar faces.

Children just over ten years old, sent by wealthy families from all over Stormwind and the outlying towns to study magic at the Wizard Sanctum. Before the Dark Portal, this would have been hard to imagine.

After all, back then magic and spellcasters still carried a certain aura of fear in the eyes of common folk — even the Arcane.

But now magic apprentices were growing more numerous by the day, which meant talent was becoming more uneven. Jace wondered if it would cost Master Marin what little hair he had left.

Even now, Jace still remembered clearly those early days of coming to the Mage Quarter to study. Whenever he walked to the door of Archmage Marin's study, he'd hear sighs drifting out from within, along with sarcastic grumbling and reproach.

It was almost like some kind of strange summoning spell. Jace had barely reached the path between the archmages' residences, and Marin's face had barely begun to swirl through his mind, when Marin's voice suddenly rang out from behind him.

"Mr. Sesso, slow down a moment!"

Jace turned his head and saw Marin hurrying over. "Master?"

"It's me." Marin waved a hand and came up to him. "I heard from Archmage Andormas the other day that you spoke with Magino Dumas about a magical incident to the north of the Mage Quarter?"

As he spoke, he twitched his nose, as if trying to sniff out whether Jace was carrying any shadow energy on him.

Jace, for his part, was quite confident on that front. After all, even the residual shadow energy in the basement had been drained clean by Gaothoros — never mind his person right now.

"Yes," Jace said. "I'm sorry for the trouble I caused the Wizard Sanctum, Master."

"I was only worried you might have been hurt." Marin looked Jace up and down. "I haven't heard any reports of damage from the incident, and you look fine, so all is well. And I didn't come here to talk about that anyway. Amy told me she's coming back to Stormwind next week. She mentioned in her letter that she'd like to treat you to a meal, and asked me to let you know if I saw you. I don't suppose you'd have the time?"

Amy was finally coming? She'd told her father about her return but hadn't mentioned a word about the demon in the Tower of Ilgalar. It seemed that on this matter, at least, she was willing to stand on the same side as Jace and the others.

"Uh, I might not be in Stormwind next week," Jace said. "I'm heading north."

"North?" Marin asked.

Jace raised a hand and waggled his fingers. "Khaz Modan."

Marin nodded, as if wanting to say something but holding back. Jace tilted his head slightly and studied his expression, then said, "Dun Morogh."

"I wasn't asking where exactly you plan to go." Marin coughed. "I was asking how you plan to get there. By ship? By gryphon? Or will Lady Sola be taking you?"

Jace unconsciously bit his lower lip with his teeth, thinking to himself — did Sola just blab about everything she was asked? "Yes. Because it's cheaper." Jace smiled.

"I'll cover the gryphon rental fee, Mr. Sesso." The archmage's tone was sincere. "Lady Sola Shade has been away from Stormwind for several months and only returned recently. I've prepared quite a number of questions for her, and a good many of them we haven't even had the chance to begin discussing…"

"This won't take too long, Master," Jace said. "And Sola isn't simply giving us a ride — she's connected to the matter we're handling herself. You're a scholar who studies dragons, Master. You should know that dragons won't consent to carrying mortals without a sufficiently compelling reason."

"Based on my previous research, there are no exceptions to dragons refusing to carry mortals," Marin said seriously. "Of course, ever since the Dark Portal opened, a great many academic papers from both the Wizard Sanctum and Dalaran have become laughingstocks, so my earlier misjudgment isn't particularly conspicuous among them."

"Very well." The old mage sighed. "Since that's the case, I won't dwell on it further. But roughly how long before Lady Sola returns to the Mage Quarter?"

"About two weeks," Jace said. "So I hope you can explain things to Amy for me, Master."

"I'll tell her." Marin said, "Amy mentioned she might be staying in Stormwind for quite a long time this time, so there's no rush. She has a research project to conduct here, something to do with the ley lines in this area."

At that, the old mage lowered his head in thought, then said, "Regarding this matter… I should give you a slight warning too. I suspect that when she invites you to a meal, she's probably got her eye on the Tower of Ilgalar. I mentioned to her earlier that the Tower of Ilgalar's reconstruction should be completed by early this summer, so the fact that she's rushing back to Stormwind at this time is probably not mainly to visit and look after her old father."

Jace smiled slightly. "If Amy wants to borrow the power of the Tower of Ilgalar, I wouldn't refuse her."

"You'd be willing?" Marin said, somewhat surprised. "Then I really must thank you on her behalf, Mr. Sesso. But when she sees you, don't mention that I shared these guesses with you."

"I'll remember, Master," Jace said.

"Then I have nothing else to say. May your journey to Khaz Modan go smoothly." Marin said.

Jace watched the old mage turn and hurry away, feeling a bit pleased with himself. Well, that saved him the trouble of making a special trip to explain to the Wizard Sanctum why Kirin Tor members would be conducting activities at the Tower of Ilgalar in the future.
