Chapter 1188: Surviving the Dragon-Father's Flames

"It might be a brief while to you, Master, but for me it's already been too long." Jace called out with a laugh. "I didn't expect you to help clear out this dangerous cavern — the situation here is much better than I'd imagined."

Jace's voice rose and fell amid the rumbling underground echoes. The group descended the steps formed by solidified black lava, stepped onto the cracked floor, and the Kadros Anvil was right before them.

"During this period the Queen has been unable to oversee matters at Grim Batol, requiring me to assist with the guarding here. Helping the whelps quickly cleanse the evil magic buried here is my duty." Krasus turned and placed his hand on the chains surrounding the Kadros Anvil. "Did you receive the two battle blades I asked Aquestrastrz to deliver to you? I was unable to completely destroy the demonic essence within them, after all — the weapon itself was forged from demonic power."

"So I gave them to a demon to use," Jace said.

"Lady Morophil," Krasus said with a smile. "When I came up with the idea of separating the two weapons to sever the demonic will within them, I felt no one was more suited to wielding them than her."

The red dragon-mage's gaze drifted to the green dragon standing behind them with her head lowered. "I heard you found Prince Eranikus in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar?"

"Yes." Sola lowered her head. "Actually, it was because of my willfulness… Your Highness Korialstrasz. I heard my kin seemed to have run into trouble there, so I kept urging Jace and the others to take me to have a look. I never expected the situation inside to be even more horrifying than I'd imagined. If not for their help, I probably would have ended up trapped in there just like my father."

Krasus said, "Fortunately you sensed something was amiss and went to rescue Eranikus. Otherwise the corruption would have used that place as a crucial anchor to continuously seep into the Dream, dragging us all into the abyss. I dislike passing judgment on others, especially an elder of my own kin, dear Solamriel — but Eranikus… ever since I've known him, he has rather overestimated his own strength. I hope this time he comes to realize that our enemies likewise possess no small measure of cunning."

"Yes, Your Highness. I believe he has already recognized his mistake," Sola said.

"The price was far too heavy." Krasus said, "For hundreds of years we all believed that to be a major stronghold of the Emerald Dragonflight, after all it had once been garrisoned by vast numbers of young green dragons. Who would have thought it had long since been corrupted by Hakkar's worshipers? It's terrifying to think about."

He looked at Grede and said, "This time you were there, but it won't be this lucky every time."

It was clear Krasus was truly worried over the matter of Eranikus. If he'd seen the scene of corpses lying everywhere deep within the temple at the time, he probably would have had an even harder time keeping his temper in check.

The twisting of the Emerald Dream was a catastrophe that could never be taken lightly. Krasus was likely also concerned that the lingering threat left by Eranikus would lead to even more trouble for the Dragonqueen within the Dream, which was why he took it so seriously.

"You didn't come here to listen to me complain either." He let out a breath, smiled, then looked toward the Dark Iron dwarf craftsmen who were peering around not far off. "Dwarves of the Thorium Brotherhood, come here please."

"Sire of the noble red dragons." Master Smith Burnhart bowed deeply to Krasus, his voice trembling slightly as he recited, "My name is Burnhart, Chief Smith of the Thorium Brotherhood. I come here by the order of Overseer Ofester, and bearing the hopes of all freedom-loving Dark Iron dwarves. It is a great honor to meet you!"

Grede stood to the side, watching him finish this string of pleasantries with a face full of displeasure. For a Wildhammer dwarf, the combination of the Dark Iron and Grim Batol was always enough to stir up certain emotions.

"Jace informed me in advance that you've come here to see whether this anvil can forge the special weapon you've always wanted to create," Krasus said. "The anvil is right here. It was once buried in the dark lava spewed by Deathwing, but we have purified the corruption covering it. The rest of the work is up to you."

"Thank you immensely, great dragon!" The Master Smith's eyes were fixed on the anvil. He nodded to Krasus, then beckoned to his companions. They mounted the steps and approached this object that was slightly tall for a Dark Iron dwarf, running their hands over its dark surface.

The other smiths couldn't wait to crowd in as well. The moment each of them touched the anvil with their hands, they couldn't help but draw a sharp breath.

"It's hard to imagine anything in this world capable of unleashing flames more powerful than the master of the Molten Core himself," Burnhart muttered in Dwarvish. "But the black dragon who destroyed Grim Batol managed it, my brothers — he was the father of all black dragons!"

The Dark Iron dwarves bustled about, taking out the tools they'd brought from Thorium Watch. Among them were also tools and weapons of Dark Iron material. They lit a furnace with the fire from the lava that had yet to cool deep within the city.

When the heat in that furnace expanded, it even reminded Jace of the ferocious flames the black dragons had spewed at him.

The smiths placed the glowing Dark Iron weapons on the anvil and struck them blow after blow. Amid the clanging ring of hammer on metal, one could almost make out a low, rumbling roar — perhaps the echo of this hall, like the growl of some abyssal beast.

The more they hammered, the more fired up they seemed, as if they could never get enough. But after the initial excitement, the expressions on their faces grew increasingly grave and solemn.

During this time, the smiths would occasionally exchange a few quiet words, then involuntarily glance at the anvil — or the part that had been severely damaged. Their voices gradually grew softer, until finally they fell silent.

"Well?" Jace asked.

"Even more formidable than we anticipated." Burnhart turned around and said in Common. "To put it simply — the Kadros Anvil can absolutely be used to forge tools and weapons of Dark Iron material now, Master Jace Sesso. Although I can't remember the last time I touched the Dark Anvil of Shadowforge City, I imagine its performance is far superior to the Dark Anvil."

Jace wasn't actually surprised. In another timeline, before Deathwing, who had descended upon Azeroth with the Cataclysm, even Ragnaros, ruler of the Fire Elemental Plane, would probably have been trembling with fear.

Not to mention that the Dark Anvil of Shadowforge City had been forged by the dwarves with the flames of the Molten Core — while the Kadros Anvil had withstood the full fury of Deathwing's wrath and been reborn from these ruins…

If the Sulfuron Hammer forged on the Dark Anvil could truly deal the Firelord a heavy blow as Roktus had described, then a Sulfuron Hammer created here would certainly possess even more terrifying power.

"So this place can definitely forge that warhammer?" Grede said.

"That's right," Burnhart said. "If it can't be done here, I can't imagine any corner of this world that could manage it, Lord Grede Thunderfist."

Some of the other Dark Iron dwarves nodded in agreement. Others lowered their heads, looking rather displeased. At first, Jace didn't quite understand what the problem was — why would finding a place capable of forging the Sulfuron Hammer make anyone unhappy? But when he noticed Grede's excited expression as he stared at the anvil, he understood immediately.

Yes — no matter how powerful it was, this uniquely mighty anvil was the Wildhammer's, not the Dark Iron's.

"I think I should return to the watchtower immediately and report this news to the Overseer," Burnhart said, looking at Grede. "At the same time, we must inform Thorium Brotherhood members across the world of this matter, so they can bring the materials they've gathered from every region back to the watchtower and together complete this Sulfuron Hammer! And this time, the Firelord and his lackeys won't be able to destroy our property or hunt down our brothers!"

With that, the Master Smith jumped down the steps and came before Jace, agitated. "Thank you so much, Master Jace Sesso. The Thorium Brotherhood and the countless Dark Iron who yearn to throw off their chains will never forget you and the aid of the great red dragons. After our last operation in Shadowforge City ended in failure, I thought I would never live to see the birth of this warhammer. But now… now…"

As he spoke, this Dark Iron dwarf who'd kept a straight face the entire journey actually shed tears. And Grede, who loved mocking the Dark Iron more than anything, stood not far off in silence as well.
