Chapter 1190: The Shadow Stone and the Black Robe

Flying from Stormwind to Thorium Watch, then making the round trip to Grim Batol and back to Stormwind, even carried by a green dragon approaching adulthood flying at what could literally be called "dream speed," still ate up eleven days.

Still, this was already much faster than Jace had expected, considering the communication with the Thorium Brotherhood in between, plus meeting with the red dragons, and incidentally persuading Grede…

Of course, this couldn't really be called persuading Grede. It was more like helping him find the choice he truly wanted to make deep down.

Actually, Jace had long since noticed that this Wildhammer dwarf loved to show off, loved to flaunt. The primary motivation for pulling off grand deeds, besides wanting to save his loved ones and friends, was equally about padding his credentials for swaggering into a tavern to boast.

In a dwarf's mind, the most worthy things to show off and brag about were defeating an insufferable enemy and defending the homeland and kin of the dwarven people — what many called fighting for glory.

Grede naturally felt this way too. He just wasn't accustomed to the heavy burden and responsibility that came along with glory and renown.

As someone who had drifted along with the current in his previous life and spent this life running wild in the streets, drinking and eating with friends and traveling, Jace understood this mentality all too well.

Still, Kalimoth was undoubtedly a perfect way out. The Sulfuron Hammer had been forged to defeat Ragnaros. If someone wielding it defeated Ragnaros, then the hammer would have fulfilled its purpose. So what if it suddenly disappeared from everyone's sight?

Besides, they weren't even sure they could defeat Ragnaros yet. Why think so far ahead?

The Thorium Brotherhood wasn't optimistic about gathering all the materials to forge the Sulfuron Hammer in the short term. Just contacting their Brotherhood members stationed across the world was no small matter — Winterspring, Gadgetzan…

In other words, although they had found a legendary anvil capable of forging the Sulfuron Hammer, it had only given the entire Brotherhood a clear direction for action all at once.

For Jace and Grede, the most pressing matter at hand was still Draenor.

But from then on, all the way back to Stormwind, Grede was noticeably more relaxed than when he'd left Thorium Watch. He'd restored his old chatty self from the journey there and kept regretting that he'd forgotten to search for broken black dragon eggs in Grim Batol.

Of course, by the time they returned to Stormwind, the Garden Festival had already passed. The egg-painting contests held across the districts of Stormwind had concluded, leaving only some withered festival greenery hanging on storefronts and houses.

In the evening, Jace had a simple meal with Grede and Sola at the Gilded Rose. After such a long flight, they hadn't had anything decent to eat along the way either. After eating a bit of bread and some pastries, Sola slipped straight into that half-sleep state, her speech reduced to simple responses like "mm-hmm, all right, I hear you."

After the meal, Grede was dead tired and went straight back to the Dwarf District. Jace happened to need to go to the Mage Quarter to find his teacher Marin, so he walked over with Sola.

Sola had her eyes closed the whole way home, and crossing the canal was a bit nerve-wracking. Jace couldn't help imagining that if she slipped and fell into the river, she'd either struggle in panic or suddenly shift into her dragon form and fly out.

Of course, a green dragon was no ordinary mortal. Even asleep she could walk, fly, and even carry on simple conversation just fine. Getting home was naturally no problem either.

She even remembered to say goodbye to people.

After coming out of the apartment building, while the sun hadn't fully set yet, Jace hurried straight to Marin's study — after all, Marin had been thinking of his consultant all this time. Of course, Jace also wanted to ask whether Amy had arrived in Stormwind.

But the teacher wasn't in his study at the moment. Clearly, at Master Marin's age, it was no longer suitable to stay up this late in the study agonizing over things with students every day. Jace waited in the garden next to the mage tower for a while. He didn't see the old mage come out of the tower, and he didn't spot Amy at the Blue Heron either, so he decided to come back again tomorrow morning.

Since he was already in the Mage Quarter and the teacher wasn't around, Jace went to the Slaughtered Lamb. After all, he was still quite interested in what the Voidlord Gaothoros had deciphered from Dar'Khan, and wanted to see if Galders and the others had produced any results these past few days.

Crossing the rather lively tavern to reach the basement, the gatekeeper hellhound tilted its head from the shadows. Upon seeing Jace, it backed up a few steps and shrank back into the darkness.

The basement was quiet, with no sound of conversation, only the bubbling from the alchemy lab. It was probably getting late and the warlocks had all gone home.

Just as Jace was about to go to the alchemy lab and ask about the situation, Galene suddenly walked out from the passage on the left.

"Jace, so you're back. If it weren't for you, our guardian wouldn't have been scared out of his wits." Galene waved him over. "Quick, come look at my new piece… I only finished her the day before yesterday. I think this must be the finest work of my life!"

"You mean that new robe?" Jace asked.

"Yes, what else?" Galene nodded. "Originally I planned to find Galders to contact you and arrange another gathering time, but damn it… I simply can't hold back any longer — I have to introduce her to you. The mooncloth you brought back from Kalimoth has a compatibility with magical energy that far exceeded mine and my friends' expectations. But that makes sense — after all, those elves were the first race in our world to learn to harness the power of arcane magic!"

Realizing she might be rambling, Galene gave an embarrassed smile. "I won't say anything else — come look with me."

Entering Galene's room, she immediately dragged a treasure chest out from under the cabinet and opened it, taking out an outer garment folded with meticulous care.

After shaking out the garment, Jace felt the room being touched by a hidden dark force — but not very strongly.

Just as Galene had said before, this was a short robe. Its main color was like dried blood, and it bore the rather ominous fine textures from the purified fel cloth that had once been used.

In addition, the inner lining of this short robe was stitched with countless demonic runes. And this time Galene's craft was clearly a great deal more advanced than the previous robe — the runes this time conformed much more naturally to the garment itself, like part of the texture, unlike the robe Jace was wearing now, which would reveal a hint of obtrusive magic if he didn't carefully conceal it.

To Jace's naked eye, it indeed possessed very extraordinary dark power. But through his senses — at least through Jace's perception of shadow — you wouldn't easily detect it unless you got very close.

"You're probably thinking this garment looks rather unremarkable, at least at first glance?" Galene said proudly, staring at the outer garment. "I learned some shadow and arcane shielding runes from my colleagues at the Kirin Tor, Jace, used to restrain the power released by the robe's enchantments. This helps conceal our presence… Because of this mooncloth, I was able to attempt fusing arcane enchantments with demonic power perfectly, without worrying about them fighting and clashing with each other and destroying the garment."

Galene stared at the garment less like her own work and more like a lover. Jace could even feel an intense, unnatural affection from her emotions.

"Try it on, Jace," Galene said. "I made it specifically to your measurements. Apart from Erland, no one else in the basement quite fits, and there's no way I'd let that fellow Erland try on this robe. So to date, no one has actually worn this robe."

"Then I'll try it." Jace nodded.

He took off his own robe, and as soon as he put on this blood-brown robe — before he'd even put it on properly — he felt a dark power swiftly envelop his whole body along with the garment. The feeling was just like being shrouded in Mage Armor.

Jace felt a certain rune on the robe at work, constantly sustaining a potent dark protective incantation, shielding the wearer like a layer of tough skin. And if he hadn't been wearing it, Jace wouldn't have sensed there was a spell maintaining itself right under his nose.

"Mage Armor?" he asked.

Galene clenched her hands tightly, like a teacher who had finally guided a student to the right answer. "That's right, Jace! This is the Mage Armor I told you about before… Though it pains me to say it, you should find an opponent to test her power. It could be a mage, it could be a demon holding a blade!"

Jace immediately thought of Morophil — but he had no intention of letting that sayaad swing the Apocalypse twin blades at him. Still, he could at least use the Blade of Endless Darkness, or that black sword of the death knight, to test it.

After all, with Galene talking it up like that, his own curiosity had been piqued. He might as well call Morophil out to the woods tonight when he got home and see what this new robe could really do.

"As for the amplification of shadow and demonic magic…" Galene looked at Jace. "I originally planned to use a high-quality Shadowstone as an amplifier for the demonic runes on the robe. But then I thought about it — perhaps you have a better idea, Jace."

She lifted the metal chains hanging from the sides of the robe. "These are chains made from enchanted mithril. Lock a powerful dark energy gem to this position, link all the chains together, hang this gem at the position of your chest, and this robe would be truly complete."

A robe that could "socket gems"? Jace had never imagined Galene Ailuo could achieve something like this.

But right now, he did indeed have a shadow gem hidden at home — the power stone from Teron Gorefiend's staff.

Ever since Gorefiend's staff had been destroyed in Grim Batol, Jace no longer had a gem socket on his staff, so that gem hadn't found a suitable use. Fortunately, he hadn't sold it for money when he was short on cash.

"Do you have any ideas?" Galene asked. "If you don't have a suitable spell gem on hand, I can find a sorcery crystal to hang on it for now."

"I have one," Jace said. "I'll go back and bring it for you to take a look."

"All right." Galene nodded.
