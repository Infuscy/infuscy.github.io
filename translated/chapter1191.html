Chapter 1191: Loud and Full of Meaning

Jace hurried all the way home, buying a few pastries along the way to fill his stomach on the road. After all, with such a powerful new robe to wear, he couldn't wait to place the gem from Teron Gorefiend's staff on it and see just how much power it could provide.

Back home, Morophil was clutching her tail, inspecting the spikes and bristles on it. When she caught sight of Jace, the barbs on her tail slowly stood up.

"Oh, master, you're back so early this time?" She smiled. "You seem to be in a good mood. What's the matter — another succubus confessed her feelings to you?"

"We're not like you, wearing nothing but a single piece of who-knows-what leather that reeks of blood and guts all year round, demon." Jace snorted a laugh as he pulled out the storage chest from under his bed. "Humans are creatures who naturally feel joy when they have new clothes to wear."

"So it's Galene Ailuo's new robe…" Morophil nodded. "I thought it was something truly remarkable."

"What are you looking for, master? I can help!" the imp poked his head out of the demon bag.

"No need." Jace took out the blood-red gem. "I still remember where this is kept."

"Miss Ailuo plans to use this gem to power that robe, master?" Morophil asked, leaning against the bed and propping her head on her hand.

"Yes," Jace said. "You want to come take a walk with me? Come along — you can help me test just how powerful the enchanted Mage Armor on this new robe really is."

"Not particularly enthusiastic," Morophil said. "After all, it's merely a mortal creation. I've seen countless powerful robes and magical armor during my travels through the Twisting Nether."

"Don't those mages in heavy armor interfere with their casting rituals? Their movements become clumsy, and their gestures are hard to make precise," Jace asked.

"They're demons," Morophil said with a smile, hopping off the bed. "You can't measure their abilities by your standards."

She had now reached roughly the same height as a high elf, yet this simple, brisk movement still appeared lithe and fluid in a way no humanoid should manage — really just like a gaunt, light-red big cat.

She raised an eyebrow. "Actually, I'm more interested in that Voidlord you summoned. Gaothoros, right? It's always particularly interesting dealing with creatures that simple and straightforward."

"You'll meet him," Jace said, placing the gem in a small box and picking up the demon bag. "Let's go." Morophil leaned close to Jace's face and sniffed at his collar. "What about your old robe?"

"Give it to Galene, of course," Jace said. "She has plenty of research and testing to do. It'll help her make even more powerful and refined new works in the future. Aren't you the one who doesn't care about mortal creations? This robe certainly can't compare to the one I'm getting today."

"But you've worn it for so long — it's saturated with your scent, my dear master. It's truly hard for me to let go…" Morophil narrowed her eyes.

The imp grabbed the outer edge of the demon bag and made a gagging motion, then got his head whacked hard by the tip of the succubus's tail. Yet Morophil's face still wore that infatuated expression as she looked at Jace.

"I'm sure the new clothes will feel the same way before long," Jace said.

"Mm, I suppose we'll see." With that, Morophil melted into the shadows and followed Jace out the door.

Back in the basement of the Slaughtered Lamb, Galene immediately couldn't wait to take the gem Jace had brought and socket it onto the robe's magical chains with arcane force.

By now, Ursula, who'd been working in the alchemy lab, had also come out early to the main hall, waiting to see just what caliber this new robe was.

After Galene finished her spell, binding the gem to the mithril chains and adjusting the position, she had Jace put it on again. This time, Jace seemed to feel once more what it had been like to hold Gorefiend's staff in the past.

Although Jace's ability to read emotions from shadow wasn't as sharp back then as it was now, what he felt this time still resonated with the past, bringing back many memories and details from those days.

"You look very handsome, Jace." Ursula leaned in. "You know I've never been one to say things I don't mean to flatter people, especially in matters of appearance — but this short robe really does suit you well."

"Don't doubt my taste." Galene wagged a finger, then looked at Jace. "A long robe wouldn't quite suit a man of your skills, Jace. Don't you think?"

"Maybe." Jace smiled, a little embarrassed. After being looked down on by Grede, Vereesa, and Morophil for so long, he really had a hard time associating himself with the phrase "a man of your skills."

He glanced guiltily at Morophil — but this time the succubus wasn't in a hurry to mock her master. Interestingly, she sat there watching Jace try on the new outer garment, and actually dropped her indifferent attitude from the journey, growing serious.

"This isn't just orcish robe-crafting technique, Lady Galene Ailuo," she said. "Many of the enchantments here… the arrangement of the runes… they're very much in the style of the Eredar."

"You know tailoring too?" Jace asked.

"I don't know how to make robes," Morophil raised a finger. "But I've fought against the power of many a robe, my master."

"What Lady Morophil says is correct, Jace," Galene said. "Many of the crafting methods and techniques of this robe were reverse-engineered from the Eredar sorcerers on the other side of the Dark Portal… Of course, the foundation still comes from the Shadow Council, but it's already very different from the original Shadow Council robe recipes we first found."

Ursula laughed. "So that's why you've been making so many trips to Dalaran lately? I heard the things that died on the battlefield — the Eredar — were all snatched up early by the Kirin Tor. We warlocks and the mercenaries Erland found didn't exactly have legitimate reasons to go to the front lines."

"That is indeed part of the reason," Galene nodded.

Morophil stood up and walked over, running her fingers over the collar of the robe. She narrowed her eyes and said in a low voice, "I take back what I said at home, master. This is unquestionably a robe beyond my imagination. The fel cloth purified by the Kaldorei through the Moonwell has a compatibility with various magics that even surpasses the raw materials used by the Eredar."

Jace thought to himself, well of course — otherwise the Burning Legion wouldn't have tried so desperately to conquer this world. The Moonwell, though small — like a little bathing pool — drew its power directly from Azeroth itself. It absolutely could not be underestimated.

The succubus drew the Blade of Endless Darkness and gripped it tightly. She asked in Demonic, "So, how should we test the power of this robe?"

"I should explain to you first," Galene said, lifting the chains of the robe. "I don't know how familiar you are with this shadow stone, Jace. Although this robe is always maintained by runes at a stable level, as long as you try to trigger its magical link with this garment, you can further enhance the strength of the entire robe."

"Let me try." Jace said this as he gripped the Gorefiend gem at his chest. He was of course intimately familiar with this thing, after all — he'd spent day and night with it in the past.

And most importantly, he had absorbed a portion of Teron Gorefiend's power, so his affinity with this gem went without saying. The demon patterns on the robe's shoulders ignited with flame from the sudden surge of power, and the hem and collar fluttered from the dark magic wind.

Both Ursula and Galene couldn't help but take a step back. Galene couldn't refrain from warning, "Jace… I didn't design an energy-concealing scheme for a magic gem of this power level. Remember — once you do this, don't expect any nearby demons or mages to not notice you!"

"I know," Jace said, watching as Morophil took a half-step back, adjusting her grip on the short blade as if preparing to attack.

Jace nodded. Morophil immediately launched forward at a bewildering speed, driving the short blade straight at Jace's shoulder…

Given that this succubus had previously hesitated even to cut herself, her sudden ferocity caught Jace a bit off guard. And at this distance, he didn't even have time to react.

As the succubus's short blade approached the outer garment, it suddenly scraped against the demonic energy with a harsh, grating sound that made both warlocks frown.

The sound was like a blade chopping against a demon guard's armor — but clearly this force wasn't truly as hard as metal. The short blade still pierced through this magical barrier and reached the surface of the robe, though its momentum had been considerably weakened.

At that moment, the Blade of Endless Darkness's mana-drain effect activated. Jace felt the mana he'd poured into the Gorefiend gem being seized by an external force, trying to rip it away — but he could resist this external force with his own control over shadow. After all, this external force was itself shadow, a surge tinged with sorrow and self-destructive emotion…

Amid the churning of multiple forces, Morophil's blade grazed the enchanted mooncloth, but didn't leave any visible damage.

She stepped back a few paces and frowned. The magical clash focused on the point where the blade had met the garment dissipated, Jace's mind calmed along with it, and he released his right hand, which had been clutching the gem tightly.

"Incredible! I knew this was the level of robe I could achieve — damn it, I can't tell Dalaran about this…" Galene muttered, unable to contain herself. Beside her, Ursula had already stared herself stupid and could only nod along.

The succubus looked at Galene with a hint of displeasure. "I certainly didn't expect it to be this difficult to break through this Mage Armor spell — but that has more to do with my master's shadow power, Lady Galene Ailuo."

Galene's survival instinct overcame her pride in her own robe. Clearly she had no desire to argue with Morophil, who'd just been frustrated and was in a bad mood.

"Don't forget who made this robe, Morophil," Jace said.

Morophil looked at Jace and lowered her head slightly. "I haven't denied that, master. Besides, it's meant to protect your safety. Of course I hope it's as reliable as possible."

The Mage Armor enchantment on this robe was indeed satisfying. Being able to withstand a thrust from Morophil… was already enough to handle close-quarters combat from the vast majority of enemies.

That way, even if he fought an enemy to the bitter end, with neither Shadow Step nor Dark Shield left to muster, Jace wouldn't be left completely at their mercy in one stroke. At the very least, he could still rely on Ursolus's scythe and the Mage Armor of this robe for a final resistance.

Not to mention that with this Gorefiend gem supporting the robe, the wearer's efficiency in casting shadow magic itself improved somewhat, and mana consumption was further conserved.

At this point, Galene seemed to suddenly remember something. "However, this robe still has one shortcoming."

"What?" Jace asked.

"It doesn't have a name," Galene said. "While making this robe, I thought up several names for it — like Shadow Council Robes, or more dramatic ones… Robe of Darkness, Demonic Witchcraft, and so on — but none of them felt quite right. Especially now that it's paired with this precious gem, Jace. So it's more fitting for you to think of one."

"Let me think of a name?" Jace sighed. "I'm really not good at this. Ursula, do you have any good ideas?"

"Good heavens — I'm neither a tailor, and my skill at manipulating shadow magic pales in comparison to yours. Why should I be responsible for naming such a precious treasure?" Ursula said, waving her hands in fright. "Spare me, Jace."

"In any case, it deserves a name that's loud enough and full of meaning, my master," Morophil said with a smile.

"Since none of you plan to take on this responsibility, then I will!" the imp said, putting his hands on his waist and hopping onto the table nearby. "I've already thought of a very suitable name."

"Go ahead and tell us, Mr. Sazeno," Galene nodded.

"It's called Morophil's Defeat!"

The imp finished with a provocative laugh, and Jace choked on his own saliva.

The imp looked at the succubus. "As you said — a name that's absolutely loud and full of meaning, sayaad! Think of it — a robe beginning its legendary journey under the name of a triumphant demon hunter! I could even add a supplement — its power once made the most terrifying wingless sayaad in the Twisting Nether wail in despair…"

"Uh… that." Both warlocks froze. Neither of them dared glance at Morophil's reaction.

And Morophil's expression had stiffened at the end of her last sentence. Her gaze slowly shifted toward the imp, and the demon patterns on her body gradually ignited with flames.

"Seems this is the only name we could come up with," Jace said, spreading his hands.

The moment the words left his mouth, the succubus's gaze shifted back to Jace. Baring her fangs, she said word by word in Demonic, "A wonderful name, master. I am deeply honored…"
