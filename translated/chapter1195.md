## Chapter 1195: The Warlock's Trap

With that, VanCleef headed downstairs. Jace watched him walk all the way to the bottom and leave the tower, while Amy kept staring at the rather conspicuous empty space in the middle of the tower's ground floor for quite some time.

"So that's the spot, Jace," she said, looking at it. "You're planning to put that demon there."

"Yes," Jace said. "I'm hoping the power of the Taralfsin Crystal can stabilize its connection to the ley lines. What do you think — using illusion magic to conceal this tower, plus that demon in the middle? That demon is the most critical part."

"Until I see that demon, I can't give you a hundred percent guarantee of what I can achieve," Amy said, looking at the booklet in her hands. "But before coming, I did quite a bit of research on both doom lords and Ilgara's Tower itself. Let's just say I'm still confident."

As she spoke, Amy surveyed the tower's condition while carefully writing notes with a pen held between her fingers — a "quill" made from the antennae of an emerald whelping.

"Is this the pen you gave me?" Jace asked, surprised.

Amy looked at the pen in her hand and smiled. "The very one, Jace. Honestly, at first I was reluctant to use it… but it turned out to be far more durable than any pen I've ever used, and it writes so smoothly. I slowly got used to it. I had someone specially enchant it for me — I didn't want to ruin your gift, after all."

"Amy, have you ever thought about why I wanted a doom lord stuffed into this tower?" Jace asked.

The smile on the female mage's face slowly faded. She nodded. "The ereduin are the demons in the Burning Legion most skilled in Void magic. When it comes to controlling spatial magic, even most eredar can't compare to them. And the way they help the Burning Legion project troops is by relying on a powerful mana source… you want to use that demon to achieve long-distance teleportation, don't you."

Jace said nothing, nor did it deny it. She closed her eyes. "Are you trying to get to Quel'Thalas quickly, or Kalimdor?"

"If it were for Quel'Thalas, I wouldn't advise it — even forming a landing point for Void magic within the Kingdom of Lordaeron would easily attract the Kirin Tor's attention, let alone within the protective range of Ban'dinoriel."

"It's Draenor," Jace said.

"Draenor." Amy murmured the name, as if pondering it carefully for a while — then her eyes suddenly widened. "You mean… Draenor."

"Yes," Jace said.

"No, no." Amy frowned. "You're trying to open another Dark Portal?"

"Clearly I couldn't build a Dark Portal like that with my own strength, Amy," Jace said. "We just want to use that doom lord's power to fling ourselves to Draenor."

"You?" Amy said. "Who else? Does Wynneza know about this? Wait… is she the mastermind behind this?"

"You could say that, in a sense," Jace said. "And she's been hoping you'd help us."

"We're going to Draenor to retrieve the Sons of Lothar, Amy — not like Medivh, who only listened to demons' temptations to bring the orcs here in the first place. What's more, given the scale of energy in this mage tower, it couldn't possibly support an invasion of that magnitude."

Amy raised her eyes to the ceiling of Ilgara's Tower, then slowly nodded. She rubbed her eyes, sighed, and said nothing.

"Are you going to stop us, Amy?" Jace asked.

"I am a member of the Kirin Tor, but first and foremost I'm a son of Elwynn, Jace," Amy said. "I may not miss her the way Wynneza misses her sister, but I want the Sons of Lothar to come home just as much. But in Dalaran, this is… fundamentally a matter that cannot be brought up. Some time ago, a few mages proposed to the Kirin Tor Council that they could use the teleportation coordinates brought back from Draenor to go back and search for surviving members of the Expedition. But now, most of those mages have either kept their mouths tightly shut or been expelled from the Kirin Tor. That's how seriously Archmage Antonidas takes this matter."

Amy closed her eyes and agonized for a while before asking, "If I refuse to help, aren't you afraid I'll leak this to the Kirin Tor, Jace?"

"Not especially," Jace said. "Our crew is made up of either demons who'll never die, elves who'll practically never die, dwarves who can live for hundreds of years, and then there's just one human — me — and I've been blessed by the Dragonqueen. According to her, I should live far longer than normal people too."

"What are you getting at?" Amy asked.

"What I'm getting at is that we can definitely serve out our full sentences," Jace said seriously. "I did some simple research. Conspiring to open a spatial gate to the Twisting Nether doesn't warrant a very long sentence — it's not considered a particularly serious crime in Dalaran. There was a guy who tried to use the Twisting Nether as a bottomless storage locker; even after he grievously injured several innocent people, he only got thirty-some years. And we haven't even actually done anything yet… so for our crew, this is really no big deal at all."

Amy burst out laughing. Jace could clearly feel the dark cloud hanging over her mood get blown away in an instant.

The female mage pressed her lips together and asked, "So do you have Draenor teleportation coordinates? I can't very well break into Dalaran's secret vault to steal them for you the way Kel'Thuzad did."

"So you're planning to join us?" Jace asked.

"What choice do I have?" Amy said, leaning against the railing. "Just the attempt to help you conceal a living doom lord is enough to land me in prison for a while."

Jace grinned and pulled two small pouches from his demon-hide bag and handed them over. "Here are two Draenor coordinates, Amy. One was recorded by an eredar — or an orc warlock. The other was recorded by a draenei exile. You've heard of the draenei, right?"

Amy nodded. "I know… but how do you have so many teleportation coordinates?"

"I'll tell you the rest later," Jace said, pointing at the small black pouch. "The problem right now is that the orc warlock's coordinates were most likely given to Kel'Thuzad on Deathwing's orders, so he could flee to Draenor to avoid capture by the Kirin Tor after everything fell apart."

"If we use those coordinates, could we end up flying straight into a black dragon or orc stronghold?" Amy asked.

"Yes," Jace said. "So we got the other one. The bad news is that it may have been damaged in several battles, and we need a seasoned arcanist like you to help repair it. The good news is that we know this orc warlock's coordinates should serve as a useful reference for the repair work."

The female mage held the two coordinates in her hand, her lips moving unconsciously as she silently repeated what Jace had said.

After a while, she looked up. "I feel like I've fallen into a trap you carefully laid out…"

"It wasn't exactly carefully laid," Jace said. "We only realized a few weeks ago that it would be hard to move forward without a professional mage…"

"Fine." Amy pressed her palm to her brow. "Give me some time, Jace. Let me take a good look at these two things."

She'd barely lowered her eyes when she suddenly remembered something and held up a finger. "You absolutely cannot tell my father about this. Absolutely, absolutely cannot, Jace. If you don't promise, I won't help you."

"I promise I won't say a word," Jace said, raising a hand.
