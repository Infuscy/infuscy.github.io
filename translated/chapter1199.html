Chapter 1199: Crystal, Activate

"Goddamn it, get out here! Morophil, stop hiding — you know the moment I see you I'm going to bash your head in, don't you?" "Hey, is this any fun?" "Ooh! Damn it, don't you dare run! This is a one-on-one duel, not hide-and-seek! You treacherous demon!" Grede's furious cursing came drifting through the forest outside the tower. He'd come looking for Jace yesterday after getting back from Booty Bay, having heard that Jace's mage tower had been fully renovated. After arriving and being bored for a spell, he'd been insisting on a one-on-one with Morophil, and Jace had agreed. Morophil naturally had no interest in going toe-to-toe with this mountain king head-on. Slowly turning the duel into a game of hide-and-seek was inevitable. Jace certainly wasn't as idle as those two. He was currently working with the imp to draw up blueprints for the modified summoning circle. He'd been at this for the past several days, and still needed to embed a shadow-binding circle around the outside of the summoning circle. This wasn't meant to restrain Kallrez like at the Storm Altar. That demon's behavior over this period had basically earned Jace's trust. The fact that there'd been no news from the Wizard Sanctum or the Kirin Tor about a powerful demon lord appearing near Duskwood or the Black Morass basically confirmed the situation. So there was no need to use a demon-binding circle to shackle him tightly inside the tower the way they'd originally envisioned. This shadow-binding circle was to prevent the demon's power from swelling too rapidly and corrupting the entire tower into a black demon tower in short order. This circle was something Galders had gotten from somewhere — unknown origin, but remarkably effective. Galders had used it extensively in many rooms of the basement. Otherwise, given the number of demons the Dagger Society summoned, the patch of grass outside the Slaughtered Lamb tavern would've long since yellowed and rotted its way into becoming another Hellfire Peninsula. Even though Sola had agreed to come bless the timber in this tower to minimize the influence of demonic power as much as possible, Jace had to have his own means to restrict the leakage of evil energy at the source. Because from this point on, the most important thing was to ensure that the Void energy within Ilgara's Tower didn't flow into the Stonewatch Forest and corrupt the environment here. And of course, this wasn't purely to protect nature or maintain the beautiful scenery around the tower. If they wanted Amy to cover up everything they were doing, if they wanted Varian Wrynn to help keep their crew's secret in the future, if they wanted their plan to go undetected by the Wizard Sanctum and the Kirin Tor — this was the most fundamental work. Just as he was concentrating on finding anything unsuitable about the circle on the scroll, Sazeno, standing beside him, suddenly turned his head toward the window. Morophil had also crept up to Jace's side at some point. Grede was still cursing below. Jace looked at the succubus. "What's happening outside?" "A group of elves is coming, master," Morophil said, sitting in a chair beside him. "I believe they've come looking for you." "The Magisters are here." Jace immediately set down what he was in his hands and leaned over the railing, shouting in Demonic at the two felhounds below: "Hide!" The two Dazar'm split up and ducked into the dark rooms on either side of the library. Morophil and Sazeno both obediently turned invisible. Jace threw on his cloak and ran all the way downstairs to the entrance. Over there, Grede had also set down his weapon, casually combed his hair and beard, put on his coat, and darted over. By this time, the elves had already approached from the deep forest. To transport this Taralfsin Crystal, the Magisters had really gone all out. Four red and green striders pulled the crystal transport wagon loaded with the magic crystal, emerging from the forest under the escort of several dragonhawk riders on foot. Five elven magisters flanked the transport wagon on both sides. The magister at the head of the group was an elf with a black hair braid. He wore a rather form-fitting crimson robe and held a golden staff — he clearly held considerable status. Jace felt he looked somewhat familiar. He seemed to have met him at the ceremony for Wynneza's promotion to ranger-lord, but couldn't recall who he was. "Welcome to Ilgara's Tower, friends of Quel'Thalas," Jace called out, walking forward. "Master Jace Sesso, Lord Grede Thunderfist," the leading magister said with a slight bow. "I am Rommath, deputy to the Grand Magister. I come here also representing His Majesty the Sun King and Prince Kael'thas, to bring their sincere regards to you and Ilgara's Tower." "Good morning, Master Rommath," Grede walked over and shook his hand. Jace nodded respectfully as well. This Rommath was precisely the one who would take over the position of Grand Magister after Quel'Thalas was destroyed by the Scourge and Belvar fell defending the city, in another timeline. Of course, long before he succeeded the Grand Magister, he'd already become quite famous in Dalaran and had earned the honorific of "Archmage" even among human mages. Jace hadn't expected Quel'Thalas to send such an important figure to transport the magic crystal, and he'd even claimed to represent the Sunstrider dynasty. It seemed Wynneza had been right — the Dar'Khan affair had really caused an enormous uproar in Quel'Thalas this time. "I didn't expect you to arrive so quickly, Archmage. I'm grateful to the Grand Magister for still keeping my affairs here in mind," Jace said. "After all, this time the fault lies on our side." Rommath spoke as he looked up to survey Ilgara's Tower. "The Grand Magister is very grateful to you for recovering the Torrent Staff of the Sunwell for us. Judging by the information obtained from our subsequent interrogation of Marisia and her accomplices, it's hard to imagine what a devastating disaster it would've brought upon all of Quel'Thalas if they had continued to wield that staff. I'm afraid his target wasn't only directed at you, Master Sesso." Jace nodded. Rommath continued, "So if there is any other information regarding Dar'Khan and his secret plans, I hope you will contact the Magisters or the Farstriders as soon as possible. This is very important to us." "I'll keep an eye out," Jace promised. By this time, the dragonhawk riders had already moved the Taralfsin Crystal out of the wagon together. Right now, under the sunlight, the dormant magic crystal looked quite dim and gray. "How are we supposed to carry this crystal up to the tower's peak?" Grede asked, eyeing the riders' movements. "We only need to install the crystal base," Rommath said. "After that, the Magisters and I can send the crystal to the very top from outside the tower." With that, the elven knights carried the base into the tower together. Jace followed in with some unease, making sure they didn't detect any demons as they carried it all the way up. But judging by the way Rommath kept staring into the windows of the uppermost floor, Jace figured he'd most likely already noticed that something was off about this tower. After all, even the gatekeeper mages of Quel'Thalas could detect the invisible imp in Jace's bag in one glance. Although Sazeno and Morophil both possessed far greater power now, detecting their presence shouldn't be difficult for a magister of Rommath's caliber. Grede followed the dragonhawk riders all the way to the tower top and helped fix the base onto the tower. By this time, the Magisters had already taken their positions below, surrounding the Taralfsin Crystal. At Rommath's command, the Magisters guided the ritual together. The magic crystal slowly stood upright under the pull of arcane energy, floating gently into the air. This so-called crystal fragment was truly a massive piece. It was hard to imagine what kind of power it must have possessed when it was once part of the same complete crystal as the one in the Violet Hold. The crystal was slowly lifted to the highest point, and finally stood upright on the base, supported by the elven knights. When it settled fully into the base and stabilized, Rommath handed Jace a scroll and said, "You can activate the Taralfsin Crystal through the incantation on this scroll, Master Sesso. The Grand Magister said that given your connection with this crystal, you should be able to complete this ritual quite smoothly." Jace took the scroll and nodded at the Thalassian runes written on it. The incantation was long, but not obscure. Jace raised a hand and pointed at the Taralfsin Crystal, doing his best to feel his connection to the power within it, recalling the sensation he'd had when making contact with this crystal at Tranquillien, and began to chant. When he was halfway through the chant, as his consciousness intertwined with the power within the crystal, Jace clearly felt that the ley lines connected to the mage tower were still vibrating unstably, like some kind of aftershock, slightly interfering with his ritual. But as the crystal was gradually touched by the power of the incantation, this trembling aftershock slowly quieted as well. Before long, Jace could even sense the mana aura of the demons within the tower from inside the Taralfsin Crystal. Had they all already established a connection with this crystal at this very moment? When the incantation was complete, the Taralfsin Crystal lifted itself off the base and began to rotate slowly, its magic glow becoming increasingly visible. Right now, under the midday sunlight, its magic glow wasn't yet blinding, but it had fully recaptured the brilliance it had displayed at Tranquillien — even more dazzling, if anything. The sight of it hovering above the tower left Jace stunned for quite some time. Grede and the dragonhawk riders near the base stared even more transfixed, their eyes glued to the crystal. A trace of irrepressible smile surfaced on Rommath's face. He clenched his fist and said, "Perfect, Master Sesso."
