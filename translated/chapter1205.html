Chapter 1205: Possibly a Slight Deviation

After receiving the king's approval, Jace felt immensely encouraged. Just as Vereesa had said, even though Jace held the deed to Ilgalar Tower and controlled the mage tower's ownership, Varian was still his liege lord. This was the Kingdom of Stormwind's territory, under his dominion and protection. Without the king's approval, a plan like this would've felt like floating in the air — impossible to rest easy about. For something as major as exploring Draenor, they needed to secure safeguards on as many levels as possible before they could proceed with confidence. After returning to Ilgalar Tower, the forest surrounding the mage tower had grown increasingly lush and vibrant under Sola's care. She hadn't just blessed the wood inside the tower — she'd also used the life force of the surrounding forest to counter the Shadow energy that occasionally leaked out of Ilgalar Tower, completely preventing the demons' power from spreading. The green dragons really had their own methods, guardians of the Emerald Dream for tens of thousands of years. You could only blame the opponent for being too powerful. But being in this environment only made Jace more and more restless. Watching Amy grow more excited and more certain with each passing day, he knew that day was drawing closer and closer. The night before the day Amy expected to finish the repairs, Grede and Vereesa arrived at the mage tower together. Vereesa ran out from the forest and threw her arms tightly around Jace, not speaking for a long time. Jace could tell she was even more nervous than he was — when she finally let go, her lips were even trembling. "What did King Varian say?" Grede asked eagerly. "You guessed right. He's fully supportive. I even got the feeling that when he handed Ilgalar Tower over to me, he was just waiting for me to throw myself into this plan," Jace said with a wry laugh. "He's not a Dark Iron dwarf!" Grede laughed. "I set out for here the moment I got your letter, Jace," Vereesa said. Jace smiled and touched Vereesa's face. "You didn't need to rush off like that, Wen. We need to test whether that teleportation coordinate can actually open a viable portal first. Once we've confirmed what's on the other side and made sure there's really no problem, that's when we contact those Broken." "I can't wait that long," Vereesa said, looking at Sola behind Jace. "I have to watch it open with my own eyes, or I'll lose my mind right then and there. Not even the Sunwell could save me…" "I understand," Jace nodded. That night, everyone had originally planned to get a good night's sleep, but in reality, no one could sleep except Grede. Amy simply got back up and dove straight into her repair work. The moment the arcane lights in the library level came on, there was no way Jace could sleep either. He tossed and turned in bed, occasionally glancing at Vereesa by the railing, staring fixedly below without blinking. In the end, he beckoned Morophil over and had herself put him to sleep. Nervousness and anticipation were one thing, but he had to have enough energy… Because no matter what, the first one through the portal would have to be him — the one most familiar with demons and Void forces. When he woke up the next morning, the sun was already high in the sky. Morophil's sleep spell really was potent, no question about it. Vereesa was no longer on this level, and the library level had gone quiet too. Sazeno stood on the bedside table and said, "You're awake, master! They're all waiting for you downstairs!" Jace walked down the steps. The two felhounds, Vereesa, Morophil, Grede, Sola, and Amy — everyone was sitting or standing at the foot of the doom lord, lifting their heads one by one at the sound of footsteps on the stairs. He looked at Kallrez and noticed the big demon was already holding the teleportation coordinate in his hand — a crystal fragment that, compared to his palm, was no different from a tiny shard. "Everything's ready, Jace," Amy clenched her hands together, then quickly hid them behind her back. "Damn it, I've never been this nervous in my life." "Anyone opening a Dark Portal in front of them would be nervous!" Grede said. "You slept like a log last night," Vereesa said. "How could I have the energy to tremble if I didn't sleep?" Grede said, pointing at Jace. "And he slept even heavier than me." Jace spread his hands. "I have to maintain enough energy to guard against various Void magic that could spiral out of control. Unless you know how to manipulate Shadow forces better than I do…" "All right, all right." Grede raised his hands in surrender. The mention of Void magic inevitably silenced everyone again. Jace walked all the way to the center of the ground-floor hall. Kallrez said in a low voice, "Losing control? You underestimate a Legion lord a bit much, Jace Sesso." "This teleportation coordinate is good to go, right?" Jace asked. "No problem," the doom lord said. "A very typical arcane teleportation coordinate. But this mortal repaired it flawlessly." Though still wary of this big demon, hearing his praise couldn't help but make Amy smile. Kallrez hadn't called her a maggot or a little creature — he'd actually called her a mortal. That caught Jace off guard too. It seemed he really did approve of Amy's work. "So, can we begin?" Jace looked around at everyone. All of them nodded, some taking deep breaths, some rubbing their hands together. Jace turned to Kallrez. "Then let's begin." "Then let's begin," Kallrez replied in Eredun. He wasn't particularly invested. After all, after serving in the Legion all these years, this teleportation portal was probably one of the smallest and most boring ones, if not the very smallest. The big demon clutched the teleportation coordinate in one hand and channeled a purple-black rune with the other. Jace felt the entire mage tower suddenly seized by a powerful force of dark magic. The overflowing purple light radiating from the Taralfsin Crystal at the top of the tower dimmed considerably all at once. A current of magical air spun and surged forth from nowhere where the demon lord pointed, gradually forming a whirlwind that lashed at everyone's cloaks and hair. The current grew stronger and stronger, to the point that Grede glanced over uneasily. But for Vereesa and Jace — people who'd already passed through teleportation portals several times — this level of wind current was really nothing to speak of. At least the tower's mana was still stable. Then Kallrez began chanting an incantation in Eredun. He enunciated each syllable with great care, his voice so low that Jace could barely make out most of the syllables — it was as muffled and indistinct as distant rumbling from deep within the earth. But so long as the Twisting Nether understood his meaning, that was enough. As Kallrez finished chanting that lengthy and mysterious incantation, the space where he pointed suddenly began to distort and warp, tearing open a black rift. Shadow hands, like demon claws, pushed and squeezed their way out, reaching back and forth to grope around. Amy's eyes widened slightly as she looked at the demon, then at that patch of space, as if she couldn't believe this kind of magic was possible. Of course, for the others who were already accustomed to Jace summoning felhounds, this had become rather routine. It was just that unlike summoning felhounds, where the demon had to squeeze through such a narrow opening, this portal tore wider and wider, eventually forming a floating rift over two meters tall and more than a meter wide — enough for a person to easily jump through. Even Vereesa and Sola were stunned by this. The clawing hands gradually stopped exerting force, allowing the portal to stabilize at this size. The whirlwind that had been spinning just moments ago gradually died down, and a harsh Shadow rumbling emerged from where the wind had fallen silent… Though everything seemed to have settled, Jace could feel the mana of the entire mage tower trembling. The Taralfsin Crystal wasn't stabilizing things effortlessly either. But it was much better than the worst-case scenario he'd envisioned — at least there was no need to shut the portal down immediately. Kallrez fell silent for a moment, then said, "Draenor… is clearly no longer an intact world. Its energy is extremely unstable, Jace Sesso. So maintaining this portal was a bit more difficult than I expected. If you don't want the tower's power to ravage this forest and the nearby keep, or cause the ley line nexus here to collapse and tear apart, you'd best keep the portal at this size." "Then we'll keep it at that," Jace nodded. "It's done?" Grede hiked up his pants as if he was about to dive right in. "I'll go in first," Vereesa gripped the sword at her waist and stepped forward. Jace immediately grabbed her shoulder to stop her, then looked at the felhound beside him and said in Eredun, "Go in and come back, Dazarm II." The felhound immediately dove into the portal without hesitation, then bounced back out a moment later, claws and toes covered in a bit of gray dust. Everyone fell silent, watching the dust drifting in the air. So this was Draenor's dust? Although the orcs had brought all sorts of junk over from that side, the Dark Portal had been closed for several years now… Jace reached out and touched the dust, then said, "I'll go in first. I'm more familiar with the Twisting Nether." Vereesa took a hard breath and stepped aside. Jace nodded, gripped Ursales, and stepped through. He felt the tower's energy shudder distinctly as he crossed the threshold, as if he'd stepped into a sticky river of the abyss… Pushing his body in was as difficult as burrowing into boiling tar. He'd never actually jumped into tar before, but he imagined it couldn't be far off. Getting in or out felt like some kind of agonizing struggle, but he suddenly found solid ground. The second step he took brought him to a pitch-black place where he couldn't see his hand in front of his face. After everything settled down, he looked around. He couldn't see anything at all. Wait — weren't the coordinates supposed to be a village near Auchindoun? Jace had seen the dust the felhound had tracked in and thought that once he went over, he'd immediately find himself on a vast, uninhabited gray plain — just like the Bone Wastes near the Auchindoun sanctum in the game. But this was clearly a dark room. No living things, completely silent, total darkness. Jace tried observing through Detect Demon sight — also nothing. He drew the Song of the Blue Sky and used Arcane Brilliance to illuminate the area. A badly damaged wall suddenly appeared before him. On the wall were some damaged relief inscriptions, parts of which had flaked off. After studying them carefully, Jace realized this was likely Eredar script. One section read: Losing direction, seek… instruction, remember… Had the draenei written this? Jace realized he'd been staying in a completely unknown place for too long. He sheathed his sword, turned around, and immediately dove back through the portal. The light all at once was blindingly uncomfortable. A chaotic Shadow howl swept past, and then cries of "Jace!" "Jace!" sounded hazily in his ears. "I'm fine, I'm fine!" Jace fumbled around for a moment, grabbed Vereesa's hand, and made out Sola, Vereesa, and Amy beside him. "What's it like in there?" Grede leaned in eagerly. "I… saw draenei architecture," Jace said. "It should be Draenor, and there are no demons. But…" He glanced back at the portal, an uneasy premonition rising in his mind — they probably weren't outside Auchindoun at all. They were deep inside the sanctum.
