Chapter 1207: To Go Home

After determining what kind of trouble they might face, everyone set about making their own preparations.

Grede took Vereesa on Stonewatch's gryphon and flew back to Stormwind. Vereesa headed to the elven embassy to borrow enchanted oils and medicines against undead creatures from the farstriders and mages who'd been hunting roving zombies in Duskwood.

Sola could fly fast, and she was acquainted with Sylia, the Zul'Gurub witch doctor in Booty Bay. So Jace gave her a small pouch of gold coins and had her go to that witch doctor to purchase Ghost Detection Potions.

Given that this Sylia frequently collaborated with mercenaries looking for necrotic essence, Ghost Detection Potions should have been standard stock for her — not something that needed to be brewed fresh like the medicine against Hakkar's nightmare corruption. So it shouldn't take too long.

Jace was responsible for heading south to the Black Marsh to find the Broken. After all, besides him, no one else in the group could guarantee they'd be able to track down their sanctuary by following magical traces.

Actually, Ilgalar Tower was quite close to the Broken's sanctuary as far as locations in the Kingdom of Stormwind went. Only a primeval forest occupied by gnolls separated the two. Even setting out from Duskwood to reach where the Broken were hiding, you'd have to traverse the dangerous Deadwind Pass in between.

But for Jace now, opponents like gnolls posed hardly any threat. Those gnoll warriors who couldn't cast Shadow magic — Ursales wasn't even interested in devouring their souls.

There were many Broken, and even with Jace, there was no way Sola could carry them all on her back, let alone a gryphon. What's more, there were wounded among them.

So they'd have to trek north on foot through the forest to reach Ilgalar Tower. After the green dragon carried Jace to the vicinity of that forest and dropped him off, she flew farther south toward Booty Bay to find the troll witch doctor.

After seeing Sola off, Jace observed the surrounding forest to make sure no orc hunters or gnoll trackers were following, and was just about to summon a felhound to guide the way.

Before he'd even started chanting, a rather clumsy figure flashed ahead. The figure's movements were distinctive enough that Jace immediately recognized it as most likely a Broken or a Lost One.

He warily raised his staff toward the figure. "I see you. Show yourself."

The cloaked figure stepped out from behind a tree. "Jace Sesso?"

"It's me," Jace said. "Noburu? I remember your voice, but I'm sorry — telling you alien visitors apart by appearance is just too difficult."

The figure pulled back its hood and let out a choking laugh, as if coughing up water. "I am Noburu, Jace Sesso. Put down your staff — I have no intention of attacking you. Our sentries noticed that familiar dragon fly overhead and land here, so I guessed it might be you and came looking for you on my own."

"Glad to see you've come through this time safely," Jace said.

"I've been fortunate," Noburu said. "But my comrades haven't been so lucky. A month ago, a group of Lost Ones ambushed our hunters while they were out hunting. Now, counting myself and Sage Magtur, there are only five of us left in our sanctuary."

Jace nodded.

"So why have you come here again, Jace Sesso? Did you run into trouble repairing that teleportation coordinate?" Noburu asked.

"You're underestimating me and my friends," Jace said with a smile, then gave a slightly guilty cough.

Strictly speaking, that Kul Tiras princess really couldn't be counted as his friend.

Though Noburu's stiff, strange face showed no change in expression, Jace noticed the violent tremor of emotion — a powerful surge of anticipation and longing for home.

Still, he asked cautiously, "So you have a way to make use of that teleportation coordinate?"

"Has your leader — Magtur — woken up?" Jace asked.

"By the grace of the Light, he has. That counts as one of our few pieces of good news," Noburu said.

"If possible, I'd like to speak with him," Jace said.

Noburu raised his crude spear. "Please, follow me."

Following Noburu and one of his comrades who'd been lying in ambush nearby back to the Broken's sanctuary, Jace noticed the arcane crystals stored in the dwelling had grown even weaker compared to last time.

Just as Noburu had said, the aged Broken leader Magtur had awoken. He sat slumped in exhaustion beside that repaired bed, and when a strange human like Jace entered, he just tilted his head slightly.

If not for that movement, he could easily have been mistaken for a corpse propped casually to the side. He actually looked even weaker than when he'd been lying in bed.

Jace knew this was because there were no more arcane crystals to power the magic that repaired their bodies. These people were doing everything they could to conserve the crystals' mana, to delay their degeneration into Lost Ones.

Noburu walked up to Magtur and whispered something. Only then did Magtur turn his head toward the doorway — rather than looking at Jace, who'd already entered the room. His vision seemed to be severely impaired as well.

"Greetings, Jace Sesso," Magtur said softly in Eredar. "I'm sorry I wasn't in a conscious state the last time you came here. And I'm sorry I must speak to you in my native tongue — my condition is poor, so I don't have the energy or time to learn your language like my comrades do. Noburu can serve as our interpreter."

"Actually," Jace admitted in Eredar, "I've studied your language. I understand what you mean."

Noburu raised his head in surprise. Magtur also seemed taken aback. After a moment of silence, he said, "Then this will be much more convenient. Noburu just told me that you and our comrades have repaired our teleportation coordinate?"

"Yes. So the next step is to proceed with the teleportation," Jace said. "As agreed, I'll take you to Draenor. You can be our guides."

Though Magtur couldn't see clearly, he seemed to hang on every word Jace said. Still, after listening carefully with a grave expression, he simply nodded and said, "Thank you for coming back to honor your agreement."

"But there's something you might care about that I have to explain in advance," Jace said.

"What?" Noburu asked warily.

Jace observed the positioning of the Broken around them, bracing himself for the worst possible reaction. Just as he was about to speak, Magtur beat him to it.

"I suspect it's a high-ranking Eredun helping you," Magtur said.

Jace stared. The other Broken were also startled by his words.

"Eredun?" Noburu looked at Magtur, then back at Jace. "What do you mean?"

"How did you know?" Jace asked.

Magtur coughed, lowering his head. "Despite the careful cover, Jace Sesso, I can still tell you learned Eredar from a demon."

"Yes," Jace admitted.

"It's not hard to guess," Magtur continued. "The only ones who could master the use of that teleportation coordinate in such a short time are draenei arcanists, or our Argus brethren who are familiar with our magic, or high-ranking Eredun who command the Void planes."

"So, hard to believe as it is, unless you're a powerful enough warlock to make a high-ranking Eredun serve you, there's no way you could have succeeded so quickly…"

"You're planning to have a great demon take us back to Draenor?" Noburu asked angrily.

"We don't have a choice if we want to go to Draenor," Jace said, looking at the officer. "Unless you want the Dark Portal to continue bringing deeper pollution and corruption to this world… and the reality is, we don't even have any way to reopen it. Or are you planning to beg the Eredar demons for help?"

"Don't call those people 'Eredar'!" Noburu said through gritted teeth. "They're nothing but 'Man'ari' who betrayed Argus. We are the true Eredar, and we would never work alongside the Burning Legion!"

"A demon, yes. But not of the Burning Legion," Jace said.

Noburu raised his spear. "Demons are all liars, just like you."

"Stand down," Magtur said, laboriously leaning over to grab Noburu's arm. "Haven't there been enough killing and death, Noburu?"

"You're going to listen to this warlock?" Noburu asked in surprise.

"Think about how our former friends and kin view us sick ones," Magtur said, panting. "They accuse us of being running dogs of the Burning Legion, accomplices of the Horde, slaves of the Man'ari… exactly as you're accusing him right now."

Hearing this, Noburu seemed to lose his spirit all at once. He couldn't even hold his short spear steady.

"You were willing to be honest about everything, so I'm willing to trust you too, Jace Sesso," Magtur said, then looked around at the other Broken. "What about you? Are you willing to trust this human as I do?"

The Broken looked at each other, then expressed their agreement in Eredar. Finally, Noburu stood his spear upright and slowly nodded.
