## Chapter 1208: A Hard-to-Shake Problem

This group had always been somewhat apprehensive about heading north into the forest, since the north was completely unknown to them. But if they didn't go this way, they'd have to detour around Deadwind Pass, which would take them through Duskwood, across half of Elwynn and Lakeshire — and they'd inevitably be spotted.

Jace had to explain to them that the vast majority of people on Azeroth right now didn't want another teleportation passage to Draenor opened. The whole affair couldn't be revealed to any outsider, nor could it be detected by anyone outside. Only then did they finally set aside all reservations and follow Jace northward.

Although Magtur had woken up, his physical condition didn't allow him to walk like a normal person. So after leaving the sanctuary, he had to be carried on a makeshift stretcher by two of his comrades.

Even though these Broken had withered physically and mentally from demonic corruption, they still possessed stamina far beyond what any ordinary human could match. Even carrying a sick man, their marching pace was quite fast.

Jace figured it should take about three or four days of walking to cross the gnoll forest and reach the Stonewatch River.

Fortunately, Jace had explored this area for a while with that group of mercenaries from all over the world that Erland had found, so he was fairly familiar with navigation methods in this forest and wouldn't get lost easily. The Stonewatch River was connected by tributaries to various rivers throughout the Black Marsh, even all the way to the Pool of Tears. As long as they followed these rivers north, they wouldn't lose their way.

After two days and two nights of walking, the group reached an unnamed river to the north. They hadn't encountered any danger along the way. The Broken gradually relaxed and occasionally chatted about their past experiences on Draenor — and not the miserable suffering after contracting this "demon disease," but the time when they still possessed powerful bodies and minds.

These ugly, deformed Broken spoke Eredar with an accent so steady and elegant it gave Jace goosebumps.

But the longer he listened, the stranger the sounds from their bodies and the rhythms of their speech became. It felt like a pack of low-level beasts gathered together speaking human language.

Thinking back to what Magtur had said earlier about how the draenei viewed them, Jace really couldn't imagine what these people had actually endured among their own kin.

"Magtur," Jace said, looking at the Broken leader who'd been silent the whole time. "When I got that crystal containing the teleportation coordinates from you before, Noburu told me this crystal could teleport us to a village next to Auchindoun."

"Yes," Magtur replied.

The other Broken, hearing their conversation, also quieted down and looked over.

"Would this coordinates bring the teleporter to a basement?" Jace asked.

"No," Noburu explained. "It would bring us to a plaza. This is a Shattar garrison teleportation coordinate, Jace Sesso. It was prepared so that if Auchindoun came under attack, the army of Shattrath City could use these coordinates to provide reinforcements. So the teleportation destination had to be spacious enough to accommodate at least several hundred people standing at once."

At this he looked up at Jace and said, "Could it be that the other side of the portal is a basement?"

"Yes," Jace said. "I didn't explore deeply, but the other side is clearly an underground palace, and a large one. I came across a wall at least four or five meters high — I could barely even see the ceiling. There were also some runes on it, with words like 'follow instruction' and 'seek help from' something or other."

Magtur and Noburu beside him exchanged a glance. Both their expressions were complicated. "The teleportation coordinates have drifted, Sage. What does that mean?" Noburu said.

"I don't know," Magtur shook his head. "Draenor's situation…" Magtur fell silent. He really didn't dare make any more guesses.

Jace asked, "Does that small town next to Auchindoun have an underground palace of this scale?"

"Near there, the only thing matching your description would be the palace deep within Auchindoun, Jace Sesso," Magtur said. "The teleportation coordinates rely on the powerful magical ley lines near Auchindoun for positioning. I imagine the great catastrophe has distorted those ley lines, causing the teleportation coordinates to be pulled in by Auchindoun's arcane… or soul energy."

He shook his head. "This is just my guess. There's no point speculating. Based on your description, I think that place resembles an Auchenai lecture hall. I've entered there before with a few Auchenai priests. The runes on the wall…"

"So, can you get us in contact with those Auchenai priests?" Jace asked.

"I know a few Auchenai priests, but I'm not sure if they're still alive," Magtur said. "Before we crossed the Dark Portal, Auchindoun had already been destroyed and the Horde had taken the place. But I have no way of knowing the situation there. All I know is that the orcs have been trying to kill every last draenei soldier inside."

Jace nodded. "But once we encounter the Auchenai, you should be able to help us establish trust with them."

"Let's hope it goes that smoothly," Magtur said.

The Broken didn't seem particularly excited about making contact with the Auchenai — perhaps because they weren't sure if there were still any living people inside, and were worried about what had befallen the Auchenai priests.

Still, at least Jace felt much more at ease after hearing Magtur's words. He sounded even more important than he appeared, and had quite deep connections with Auchindoun's defenders. This would make the Auchindoun expedition much easier.

Now, Jace was really thanking his lucky stars he hadn't recklessly charged straight to Draenor.

Noburu said, "I hope those Auchenai can set aside their previous prejudices and understand that in this situation we must stand together to face this unprecedented calamity…"

At this he suddenly turned his head to look at the forest to the south and said, "We can't rest, everyone."

"What?" Jace asked.

"Someone's chasing us," a Broken soldier said, grabbing the bag of draenei crystals from the ground. "We need to leave, Sage Magtur."

The group immediately packed up and set out. Jace kept looking back at the situation in the forest, but he couldn't sense anything, of course. He definitely couldn't compare to these Broken, who'd been fighting their own vicious kin for years.

"These monsters who've forsaken the Light!" one Broken cursed under his breath.

Jace noticed Magtur glance at him weakly, as if somewhat displeased with him calling their kin that. But he didn't say anything — probably knowing this wasn't the time for compassion, not to mention that he was currently holding back the entire group.

Although the whole group had set out quickly, before long, some noisy, unnatural sounds still appeared in the forest.

The sound of claws scraping against leaves and branches, growling and snarling — Jace almost thought a gnoll tribe was surrounding them.

But a bizarre shadow appearing from the side dispelled Jace's suspicion. A pair of weirdly long legs like an ostrich's, stubby arms, and flea-like jumping movements were more than enough to identify these pursuers. It was indeed a group of Lost Ones.

"Kolli! Kolli! Katra, Sukir!"

These Lost Ones cursed in the filthiest Eredar in the forest, swearing and cursing at these people to die in agony. In this strange otherworld, there wasn't the slightest shred of mercy for their own kin.

Soon these awkward-looking but incredibly fast freaks surrounded Jace's group. They emerged from between the trees, appearing in the treetops — like a swarm of brownish-black dwarf locust-men.

"By the Light…"

A thick wave of despair immediately spread among the Broken. Neither Noburu nor Magtur had any intention of communicating with these Lost Ones at all.

The Lost Ones closed in step by step, holding short spears, sickles, and crude spiked clubs. The reason they hadn't immediately pounced and torn these Broken to shreds wasn't mercy — it was the human standing in the middle. They still couldn't figure out exactly how capable this human was.

There were roughly twenty of them in total, with probably more left back at their dwelling.

In the past, they'd all been Broken like Noburu and Magtur. If they hadn't been powerless to resist the worsening of demonic corruption, they really could've established a fairly proper settlement here.

"In the end, we still couldn't make it out of this forest," Magtur said in a low voice. "If you have any way to escape — teleport, invisibility… you'd best use it now, Jace Sesso. We won't blame you for it. Perhaps our tragic fate is meant to end here. All struggle is meaningless."

"Don't give up hope just yet, Sage," Noburu said. "I'll fight these vicious bastards to the last." Magtur gripped his short staff tightly and nodded. Every Broken prepared to fight to the death.

Jace looked at him. "You wouldn't mind if I hurt your kin, would you?"

"Don't take such risks for us, human…" Noburu looked at him. "Leave. Go back to Draenor. Tell the draenei there our story. Tell them that until the very last moment, we never forgot the teachings of the Light."

The Lost Ones pressed closer. Among them were even some holding light crossbows they'd gotten from who-knows-where, already aimed at Jace. Jace took several purple bottles of Shadow Protection Potion from his pack. "Drink up. Everyone needs to drink. Quickly."

Magtur and Noburu took the potions. Seeing Jace's resolute gaze, they didn't hesitate and passed them around to drink.

Just as the last Broken who'd drunk the potion swallowed, the Lost Ones seemed to realize these people were preparing to fight. They immediately began howling one after another.

"Fekkt!" Jace raised both hands and roared. In an instant, the entire forest was overwhelmed by a shriek of Shadow. All the howls of the Lost Ones, the wind, the footsteps — everything was drowned out and distorted…

A crossbow bolt whistled and struck Jace's shoulder, only to be easily deflected by the tough black dragonhide. Jace just felt as though someone had patted him on the shoulder.

He'd actually been somewhat looking forward to "Morophil's Bane" being put to use in real combat for the first time, but it looked like that opportunity was gone. The Lost Ones hit by this Shadow roar had all screamed out of control. They couldn't even stand steady, couldn't even hold their weapons — they could only clutch their ears and eyes, crying, howling, and stumbling away in flight.

A few even had dark blood-tears streaming from their eyes, convulsing in agony on the ground.

Not just them — even the Broken who'd drunk the Shadow Protection Potion were so terrified by this spell that they stood rooted to the spot, unable to move. Even when a Lost One charged up to them in a frenzy wielding a weapon, they couldn't mount a counterattack.

Jace casually muttered an incantation and pointed at this Lost One. He immediately burst into purple-red flames. Before he'd taken five steps, he dropped to the ground and was incinerated by the Shadowflame into an unrecognizable black clump of residue. Not even his ravaged, hole-riddled soul had managed to escape the sinister bright fire.

In the distance, the Lost Ones who hadn't yet been terrified into mental collapse by the mass Fear finally couldn't hold out any longer. They turned and fled without hesitation into the forest, not even caring about the things they'd left behind.
