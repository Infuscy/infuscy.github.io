## Chapter 1209: Gradually Coming to Terms

The air was already thick with the fishy stench of dark magic. The Shadow shriek dissipated, leaving only scattered wails in the forest.

Jace hadn't expected these vicious hunters to be scared off so easily. Logically speaking, creatures corrupted and fallen to Shadow magic should have at least some Shadow resistance.

But it had also been a long time since he'd used a Fear spell of this scale with the staff. He wasn't entirely clear on just how far his Shadow power had reached under Ursales's amplification.

These monsters fleeing on their own was a good thing too. Hopefully they'd come to understand that the northern part of this forest wasn't a place they could just come and go as they pleased. Besides, Jace didn't want to use too much dark magic here — he had no desire to casually destroy Sola's hard work.

After the surroundings quieted down, Jace grabbed Noburu beside him and shook his thick shoulders hard. "Leave before they can organize another attack. I don't want to tangle with them any further. Too much dark magic would seriously pollute this environment. This forest isn't far from my tower."

"Uh… ah…"

Noburu had just barely snapped out of his daze. He squeezed his eyes shut and let out a sound like a painful wail before gasping a few breaths.

He looked at Jace, then at the Lost Ones who'd already fled, at the corpse on the ground that had been torn apart by the powerful Shadow force, then at the other Lost Ones.

Magtur sat slumped on the stretcher, clearly too shocked by the Shadow magic just now to say a word.

Jace couldn't sense any clear emotion from any of the Broken except fear. "I've already been upfront with you all about using an Eredun to take us to Draenor. So I think you should have had some idea of what kind of magic I command."

Magtur forced a cough from his throat and said, "I… only gratitude."

"Now I understand how you found a powerful Eredun to cooperate with," Noburu said, lowering his head. "Even among the orcs, warlocks as powerful as you are rare. I must thank you for saving our lives."

"Don't mention it," Jace said, one arm cradling his staff and the other pointing north. "I still need you to guide the way on the other side, so not a single person here can die. Now let's find a safe place to rest."

The rest of the journey presented no further danger. Once they reached the Stonewatch River, it basically meant they were safe. Even gnolls rarely showed up here after the Dark Portal campaign.

By the time they reached Ilgalar Tower, it was already late. Noburu walked at the front of the group, gazing from a distance at the mage tower surrounded by lush, healthy forest, with the magic crystal slowly rotating above it. His eyes darted everywhere in disbelief.

Jace noticed something was off about his expression. "What's wrong, Noburu?"

"So this is your tower, Master Sesso?" Noburu pointed at the tall tower still glowing in the distance ahead. "Where's that demon? I can't sense any demonic aura here at all."

"If you could sense a demonic aura here, then all my efforts up to this point would've been meaningless," Jace said. "You'll see him once you're inside the tower."

Noburu nodded half-believingly. Magtur, who'd been sleeping for quite a while, seemed to hear the conversation and slowly opened his eyes from the stretcher.

Amy had long since come out of the tower, looking this way. With the support of the Taralfsin Crystal's power, it was only natural that she could detect someone approaching from this far away.

"Jace!"

"Amy!" Jace called out from a distance. "I've brought them."

Amy already knew these people shared the same roots as the Eredar warlocks in the Burning Legion. She also knew these people possessed a highly advanced magical civilization in their homeland, and that they were the ones who'd created that teleportation coordinate. But when she actually saw their appearance, that visceral sense of revulsion at seeing an alien was still quite pronounced.

After all, even though the Broken looked much better than the Lost Ones, they were still bizarre and frightening. Even for Jace — a player who'd long been familiar with them in the game — seeing them in the flesh was still somewhat unsettling. For Grede and Vereesa, who'd grown accustomed to all sorts of strange and ugly demons, it was hard to accept too, let alone Amy.

Still, Amy was the daughter of an archmage, after all. She'd had close contact with upper-class society from all over the world in both the Mage Quarter and the Kirin Tor. No matter what she was feeling, maintaining surface-level politeness wasn't difficult.

"Pleased to meet you," she said, nodding proactively. "I've already heard about you from Jace."

"The honor is esteemed, honored arcanist," Noburu said.

Though polite on the surface, the Broken were still somewhat wary of making contact with a human mage. Still, both sides maintained their reserve and exchanged courtesies. Magtur didn't speak Common, but he understood the simplest greetings, which kept the situation from becoming too stiff.

"The demon responsible for teleporting us is right inside the tower," Jace explained to Magtur in Eredar. "My friends haven't returned yet — we'll have to wait until they're all back before opening the portal. For now, you'd best meet that demon in advance and mentally prepare yourselves."

"Very well," Magtur replied.

Despite the advance warning, the moment Noburu entered the tower and caught his first glimpse of the doom lord, he was so frightened he froze in the doorway. Kallrez lowered his head and looked at these Broken — and actually couldn't help but burst out laughing.

The demon's laughter made the tower's energy tremble. The purple-red of the Taralfsin Crystal also began to show traces of fel-green. Magtur coughed violently. The situation quickly descended into chaos, and Jace had no choice but to lead the Broken out of the tower first.

Kallrez said with a laugh, "If the Eredar could see their people twisted into these ridiculous things by fel magic, their reactions would be quite interesting, Jace Sesso."

The demon's voice rumbled through the tower. Jace turned back and pointed at him. "Shut up."

"If that's your command," Kallrez said with a cold snort.

Out in the open ground outside the tower, Noburu said helplessly, "Before we set out, we'll just camp outside the tower for now. Sleeping rough is no great hardship for us, and besides, this place is much safer than the marsh."

Jace nodded. Aside from Magtur and Noburu, the other three Broken had already been frightened into a daze.

Watching them tidy up a low spot that had once been a gnoll dwelling, Jace said to Amy beside him, "Before we set out, I need to make another trip to Stormwind to pick up some medicines we'll need for Draenor from my friends, and also check how Vereesa and the others are doing with their preparations. So I'll leave them in your care."

"Anything I should watch out for?" Amy asked, lacking confidence.

Jace thought for a moment and said in a low voice, "Don't mention their appearance. Don't mention their relationship with their kin… aside with that, these people aren't that hard to get along with. Their impression of the Kirin Tor is actually quite good."

Amy touched the Eye of the Arcane ring on her finger and nodded reluctantly.
