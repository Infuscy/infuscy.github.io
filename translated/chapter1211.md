## Chapter 1211: Unable to Rest Easy

The next day, Jace met up with Grede and Vereesa to organize everything they'd need for the journey. On the day they set out, Jace went to his parents' place in the morning for breakfast and said his goodbyes.

He'd originally planned to keep this farewell from feeling too significant — just like the other times he'd come over for a meal, eat, and say see-you-later. But his mother seemed to sense something. She ended up accompanying him along the canal for a long, long way.

This stuck with him all the way to the gryphon roost by the city walls, and even as he flew into Elwynn Forest, he still couldn't shake off the feelings from his parents' place.

Vereesa and Grede, on the other hand, were quite excited the whole way. Vereesa didn't need to be asked — she'd always been the most eager to go to Draenor, whether it was for the mysterious Twisting Nether, the extremely risky interplanar teleportation, or the threat of the Burning Legion… none of it could slow her steps.

Grede wasn't far behind either. A new adventure, new battles, the Wildhammer grand lord Kurdran Wildhammer waiting in another world — to him, it was all as appealing as Karanos's Thunderbrew Ale.

Of course, when the gryphons landed at Stonewatch and the group made their way through the forest to Ilgalar Tower, Jace had already completely shaken off the emotions he'd carried out of Stormwind. After all, what lay ahead wouldn't allow him to keep carrying these complicated feelings.

The five Broken had put up a simple shelter against rain and wind outside the tower. The trees around the mage tower seemed even more lush than when Jace had left. Jace could tell at a glance that Sola had already returned to the tower.

Sure enough, before the group had even reached the tower, Sola and Amy had already come out and were looking over.

"Sola!" Vereesa ran over and hugged Sola, then asked, "How'd it go? Did that troll witch doctor give you any trouble?"

"No," Sola said. "She was very willing to help Jace. She gave me many bags of Ghost Detection Potion… and didn't even charge me."

"What the hell!" Grede asked, looking at Jace. "Are you sure that's a forest troll from the Hinterlands?" Jace also frowned and asked, "She was that kind? Didn't ask for anything at all?"

"Not exactly nothing," Sola said. "She had me use magic to nourish the growth of a pot of bloodvine. That was no difficulty for me."

"God knows what kind of crazy mutant bloodvine that is," Jace scratched his head. "Forget it, it's none of our business anyway. If she tries to cause trouble, Revilgaz will deal with her."

"Never mind the trolls," Vereesa gave Jace a sidelong glance, pursed her lips slightly, and looked over at the Broken walking up from the low ground. "When do we set out?"

"I don't think there's anything else to prepare," Jace said.

"At least I'm ready," Grede said, slapping his palm with Cadros Skybreaker. "Dive straight into that portal any time!"

"What about you?" Jace asked Noburu, who'd just walked up nearby.

"We're ready too, Master Sesso," Noburu said. "Sage Magtur has recovered quite a bit. Getting some distance from the Dark Portal really is better for his condition."

Jace nodded and looked up at the mana crystal floating above the tower. "Then let's set out."

Everyone gathered together in the ground-floor hall of the tower. As the Taralfsin Crystal flickered with light once more and the entire mage tower's energy began to tremble, the Void rift tore open before them. The stiff faces of the Broken actually began to relax and shift — excitement written all over their faces. Their fear of the doom lord before them was quickly overwhelmed by the thrill of going home.

"Nobody's getting in before me this time." Grede shouldered his pack, raised his warhammer, and charged straight into the rift. Vereesa glanced at Jace and followed him in.

The Broken filed in one after another behind the elf, then Sola. With each person who passed through, Jace felt the tower's power and the ley lines being violently wrenched, as if by some great force. Just as Amy had said, establishing a stable teleportation passage from Azeroth to the shattered Draenor wasn't as simple as it looked.

Jace looked at the young mage beside him. "Amy, hold down the fort for us. This portal needs a normal person watching over it. I'm leaving the two felhounds here to assist you. They'll listen to you."

"Don't worry, Jace," Amy said. "I've got a good idea of how to use illusion magic to conceal the tower's interior. No need to worry about anyone unrelated catching wind of what's going on here."

Jace nodded and gave his instructions to Dazarm II and III in Eredun. He gave his clothes and everything he was carrying a final check, patted the demon bag at his waist containing the imp, and stepped into the Void rift together with Morophil.

After another sticky, struggling passage, he stepped once more into that pitch-black place.

The wind, the rustling of leaves, the ethereal echoes within the tower — all vanished in an instant. Silence swallowed everything… leaving only the faintest tremor of Shadow power from the rift behind him.

A beam of blue light illuminated the entire hall — that was the Light spell Vereesa had sent up high.

Under the illumination, that familiar tall wall covered in shattered Eredar runes stood right before them. But higher up there were also collapsed stone pillars, broken walls, and collapsed sections of wall and ceiling — things Jace hadn't noticed at all before.

Vereesa tried lifting the Dream Bow Bella'nur and drawing it. As a radiance of dream-illusion gathered on the bow, confirming that the Staff of Eranikus still held powerful mana even on this side, she slowly lowered the bow.

Grede pinched the Earth-Force gem at his neck. A faint golden light flickered within.

Jace glanced sidelong at the gem. Honestly, he wasn't the least bit worried about the Earth-Force gem losing its power. After all, even in the shattered Draenor, elemental forces still played an enormous role.

"This really is Auchindoun… we're home." Noburu said excitedly to his Broken companions in Eredar. "I can't believe it. We actually made it back."

"Can you confirm where we are, Sage?" he asked Magtur, who was leaning against a nearby stone pillar, panting. The latter seemed to have yet to recover from the struggle of passing through the portal.

After a moment, Magtur raised his head and surveyed the hall. "The Auchenai have many halls like this inside, Noburu. The collapse here is too severe. I can't tell which one we're in."

"So we should explore the surroundings first?" Jace asked. "I think so," Magtur said.

Grede looked over and asked, "So what do we do next?"

"Leave this hall first and move forward," Jace said. "We need to figure out what part of Auchindoun we're in."

"Let's drink the Ghost Detection Potion right here," Vereesa said.

Grede took the medicine pouch Sola handed him. "Here's hoping the potion that detects Azeroth's ghosts works on Draenor too."

Noburu said, "Considering the Shadow Council once used the souls here to summon demons, and the Shadow Council has done the same on Azeroth, that means our souls should be similar. A potion that detects Azeroth's souls should have the same effect here."

"Sounds like good news," Grede nodded.

Jace took a sip of potion, then asked Noburu, "Do you need any?"

"No need," Noburu said flatly. "We can sense the souls here."

"Like demons?" Grede asked.

Jace glared at the dwarf, hinting him to watch his words. Only then did Grede realize the problem and gave a guilty cough. Unexpectedly, Noburu nodded frankly and said, "Indeed, just like demons, Lord Thunderfist."

Jace stuffed the half-drunk potion pouch back in his pack. He noticed Sola beside him with her eyes closed, her complexion not looking too good. "What's wrong?"

"I can no longer call upon the Dream's power, Jace," Sola opened her eyes and said. "I feel as though I've lost contact with all my kin, lost my connection to the entire Emerald Dream…"

"Try wearing this." Vereesa took off her emerald earring and personally parted the green dragon's golden hair to put it on her ears. "This probably should have been on you all along, since it was your mother's."

Sola flusteredly supported Vereesa's hand. "This is a gift from Queen Alexstrasza to you, Wen…"

"Then treat it as me lending it to you," Vereesa insisted.

Only then did Sola nod. She pinched the earring and closed her eyes again. The tense expression on her face eased considerably.

"Ah, I feel it. Though there are no voices of other kin, it's as though I'm back by my mother's side. My God, I never imagined what it would be like to be completely cut off from the Dream and then find it again…"

"Comforting," Jace said.

"Yes." Sola said with a smile. "Comforting…" Ooooh—

Suddenly, a rumble from somewhere shook the entire hall, cutting Sola's voice off mid-sentence.

The hollow, cavernous blast rang out without warning, like a ship's foghorn at sea. Passing God-knows-how-many high walls and unknown halls and corridors, it still reached here dizzy-makingly loud. Everyone had to clutch their ears tightly just to barely endure it.

Grede swore in Dwarvish. Though it was obvious he'd been shouting at the top of his lungs, his voice still sounded pale, dry, and feeble after that blast just now.

"M'muru." Noburu held up Magtur, who'd nearly been knocked unconscious by that last blast. "The monster that destroyed all of Auchindoun is still entrenched here."
