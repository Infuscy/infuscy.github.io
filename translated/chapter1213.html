Chapter 1213: Who Is the Traitor

The draenei ghosts walked at an unhurried pace, much as they had on the way in — exactly as though they were strolling through the halls they'd once lived in, chatting away with perfect ease.

Right now, their relaxed, cheerful expressions and the cadence of their voices formed a stark, almost violent contrast against the gray, shattered old halls all around them.

Jace's group and the Broken followed at a wary distance. Every so often, Morophil would warn them to watch the heights — every now and then, ghosts would poke their heads down from above to peer around, as if watching Jace's group, or maybe not seeing them at all.

The ghosts gazing down from above seemed just like the ones walking ahead: completely unaware of what had become of this temple, as though they were still living in the past.

Some of the spirits nearby would be persuaded by the walking draenei ghosts to join them; occasionally, some would leave. Those who broke away were often dressed in fine robes, some even wearing crowns — almost certainly people of very high standing in life.

Jace didn't quite understand what made them join, or what made them leave, or where this whole group of ghosts intended to go. After all, although the spirits were presumably speaking Eredun, the boundary between life and death had blurred their voices to the point that even the Broken couldn't understand what they were saying.

Mag'tour kept looking up at the hall around them, but he too seemed quite unfamiliar with this place. That was no surprise — Auchindoun was one of the largest cities the draenei had ever built on Draenor. Unless you were one of the highest-ranking priests here, you'd be hard-pressed to have mapped out every last corner and corridor.

After passing a toppled stone pillar and rounding a bend, the ghosts picked up their pace. Jace's group gradually sped up too, careful not to lose them.

But after weaving through a heap of rubble and broken bricks, Jace noticed a faint, flickering magical glow in the dark, hazy hall ahead.

Vereesa slowly crouched down and raised a hand for the others to halt. Everyone took cover behind the rubble pile. Jace edged forward bit by bit until he was beside Vereesa, peering ahead.

Out there, his eyesight could make out nothing at all — just what looked like will-o'-the-wisps drifting in the darkness. The souls were walking straight toward those ghost-light flames, gradually vanishing entirely into the dark.

Just then, something shifted in Mag'tour's expression. Gazing at the hall shimmering with faint arcane light ahead, he seemed to recognize certain features of this place.

But just as Jace was about to whisper a question to him, Vereesa gripped his shoulder tightly and pointed toward the source of the magical glow.

Jace caught sight of a humanoid figure passing in front of the lights — a draenei in heavy silver robes. A real, living, breathing draenei, not a ghost.

He carried a long staff, his pace carrying him closer and closer until he stopped before a flight of steps and glanced in their direction. The arcane blue light in his eyes flickered faintly, then he turned to look back the way he'd come. For a few moments, Jace could even catch the pale blue sheen reflecting off the smooth skin of his horns.

"Auchenai," Noboru said in Eredun. "That is an Auchenai priest, Sage Mag'tour."

Mag'tour nodded. "It seems our luck has held — we've found an Auchenai stronghold. I knew these spirits would guide us true."

Noboru beckoned to Jace and Vereesa beside him. "Come with us." Vereesa asked, "Are you certain they aren't Eredar demons?"

"They are not demons," Jace said. "They're draenei, plain and simple."

That said, Vereesa and Grede still gripped their weapons, unable to relax. After all, the powerful Eredar warlocks near the Dark Portal had left a deep impression on them and their respective comrades. It was hard to put their faith so quickly in a group of aliens who looked exactly like those demons.

Jace had his own doubts, though not because he was unwilling to trust the draenei — more because something felt off.

These ghosts had come here as if with a purpose. And right now, two more spirits had paired off and were heading back in the direction the living draenei had come from. Why were they gathering here?

But considering that the Auchenai were an order of priests who served the dead, rounding up the lost spirits wandering the depths of Auchindoun into one place was perfectly reasonable. Besides, these Broken certainly knew the depths of Auchindoun better than he did — there was no need to second-guess them further.

Jace told Morophil to turn invisible and lie low, then revealed himself from the rubble along with the others.

The draenei heading back heard movement behind him and turned around. Noboru walked up to him and said in Eredun, "The Light preserve you! We've finally found you."

The draenei looked over the Broken, then at Vereesa, and raised the staff in his hand. "Stop right there." To Jace's surprise, he was speaking Common — these people had probably already made contact with the Alliance Expedition.

Hearing the draenei speak Common, Vereesa and Grede relaxed considerably. Vereesa said quickly, "We're reinforcements just teleported over from Azeroth, Auchenai priest. We're searching for the Alliance Expedition..."

"Why are you with these things?" the draeneai priest cut her off. He looked at Vereesa, but the staff in his hand was pointed squarely at the Broken.

Just then, another figure appeared from the direction of the magical glow — also an Auchenai priest. "Weren't they with you to begin with?" Sola asked.

"Naive Alliance soldiers. This isn't the first time you've been deceived by these corrupted wretches," the draenei priest said. "Come over to our side. We'll deal with these traitors."

"Traitors?" Noboru raised his spear and roared in Eredun. "On what grounds do you make that accusation?!"

"I am Mag'tour, venerable priest," Mag'tour said with difficulty. "I led a group of Broken who were driven to Azeroth by the orcs to serve as... soul fodder. We escaped from the orcs' prison in the chaos, and with the aid of a magical portal coordinate given to us by a Sha'tar Vindicator, and the help of these residents of Azeroth, we endured untold suffering to finally return. We have no connection whatsoever to the traitors of Auchindoun."

"The Exarch has issued his orders," the priest said in Eredun, utterly devoid of emotion. "Every corrupted wretch in Auchindoun must be arrested."

Vereesa caught Jace's eye. Jace glanced where she was pointing and, after studying it carefully, noticed a large pile of iron cages tucked beneath the shadow of a high wall.

Inside the cages, sitting one to a cell, were the corpses and skeletons of numerous unidentified humanoids. Judging by the distinctive bone crests on some of the skulls, these were very likely Broken — or even other draenei.

"Damn it, necromancers," Grede muttered, raising his war hammer. "We can't trust them, Jace!" Jace had considered that the draenei here might have long since lost their minds, but seeing those naked corpses still came as a shock.

Those souls must have been lured here by the Auchenai priests' identities. Only the wisest spirits would have sensed something amiss and chosen to leave midway...

The draenei priest noticed the whispered conference. He looked over, caught Grede and Vereesa's expressions, then glanced back at the cages.

Before he could say anything, the draenei priest behind him took a step forward, raised his staff toward Jace and Grede, and a formidable surge of shadow energy rapidly gathered at the staff's tip...

A beam of many-colored light pierced through the shadow energy before it could fully coalesce, scattering it. The strange soul-shield protecting the second draenei priest was immediately shredded by Vereesa's dream arrow. A hole punched through his chest and he cried out in pain, tumbling off the platform.

The priest in front reacted by raising his staff at once, but before he could utter an incantation, Jace had already choked him with shadow chains using a Suffocate spell.

Jace was just about to ask a question when the priest's entire body was suddenly engulfed in shadow, transforming into a black wraith that broke free from the chains!

A savage, demonic roar shook the entire hall. The Broken were staggered by the dark shout — some clutched their heads and fled, others stood frozen or collapsed where they stood.

Jace was just gathering a ball of Shadowflame to blast the wraith when his hand had barely risen before a lightning-wreathed war hammer tore through the shadow obscuring his vision and slammed into the priest's dark form!

Sola raised his staff, dispelling the darkness and the shock spell the Broken had endured. Seeing no more enemies, Vereesa lowered her bow, which had already gathered a second dream arrow. Jace blew out the flame in his palm and crushed it.

Grede hauled his war hammer back. The draenei priest had already been smashed in two at the waist, his blood and bizarre viscera splattered everywhere.

Still, both of them looked pretty relaxed about it — as if these blue-skinned aliens with tendrils and hooves were exactly the sort of thing you dealt with this way.

Though the darkness had been dispelled, the Broken still looked dazed and shaken — whether from the draenei's attitude, or from the skeletons and necromantic magic, it was hard to say.

"Thank you for being willing to trust us," Noboru said, looking over at the group. "Nothing to thank us for," Grede said with a wave.

Jace gazed at the gathering soul-shadows in the distance and said, "We need to move. The noise here may draw their allies before long."
