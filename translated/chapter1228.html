Chapter 1228: Shadows in the Forest

Just as the Shadow Council orcs had said, the sky over the Bone Wastes had been all but ruled by black dragons. The group spotted black dragons several more times over the next few days, but fortunately none came close. Keeping a low profile and sheltering behind the remnants of draenei and orc wartime wreckage under the guidance of the Alliance soldiers, they managed to avoid detection by those flying devils.

Jace had no desire to fight a pack of black dragons head-on in a vast wasteland with almost no cover — especially considering their numbers might be alarmingly large. Once they called for help, who knew how many reinforcements would come rushing in. Once things got tangled up, it would be nearly impossible for the group to get out without casualties.

Thinking about it, it was a good thing they hadn't impulsively decided to have Solia fly across the Bone Wastes — that truly would've been a narrow escape from death.

Between now and the day the Dark Portal was reopened by the Burning Legion twenty years from now, most of the adult black dragons here would die at the hands of the Legion's demons and the gronn of Blade's Edge Mountains. By the time players arrived on this shattered wasteland, there wouldn't be many adult black dragons left.

Their leader in Draon, the black dragon prince Saberion, had always wanted to take out a few gronn to ease the pressure on the black dragons' survival in Blade's Edge, but it was hard for him to manage alone.

Although the gronn king Gruul and his brood couldn't do a thing about Deathwing himself, they weren't something Saberion could deal with easily either.

The Bone Wastes were far vaster than Jace had imagined. The group pushed themselves as hard as they could, and it wasn't until the fourth day that they finally began to see the remains of fallen trees, one after another.

Thanks to the remnants of draenei and orc structures left on the wastes, and the guidance of the expeditionary soldiers and draenei, they hadn't lost their way inside.

Looking back toward Auchindoun, there was nothing to be seen anymore.

At that moment, Solia clutched the emerald earring of Queen Ysera in her hand and gazed at the remains of the trees in the distance. "I can feel the power of Life here."

"It might not be very clear from here," Livius pointed ahead. "But once we pass the Tomb of Light, we won't be far from that hillside. Terokkar Forest is right behind it."

"The expeditionary fortress isn't far either," Vereesa said.

Walking through the stretches of toppled, half-dead trees, the higher they went, the more solid the ground beneath their feet finally became, and the smell of death and dust in the air lightened considerably.

After dark, the group reached a cliff. Looking north from here, mysterious glimmers of light could occasionally be seen through the hazy, gloomy darkness — that should be Terokkar Forest below.

"Let's rest here tonight," Jace said. "We've finally made it out of this wasteland. I think everyone's exhausted. We'll sort things out, then head into the forest tomorrow."

The Broken nodded and crouched down one after another. Livius also acknowledged with a voice and turned to check the luggage with Nitlin. The two Alliance soldiers plopped down on a rock beside them. The corporal simply flopped onto the ground, let out a long sigh, and muttered, "I never thought I'd survive."

Jace looked in his bag. The dried black mushrooms from Azeroth were all eaten up. The raisin he'd lifted from the Shadow Council was down to a small handful, and the bread was nothing but some dry, crumbly scraps.

Traveling on edge across the desert, the food had been consumed far faster than Jace had estimated back in Auchindoun. Jace licked his lips, drank a mouthful of conjured spring water, and looked toward Vereesa. The ranger hitched up her little food bag and shrugged.

"Grede." Vereesa looked toward the dwarf. "Let's go up ahead and see if there are any beasts or fruit in the forest. Scrounge up something to eat."

"Solia," she said to the green dragon sitting behind Jace. "You stay here with Jace. Keep him and Morophil on their toes for any demons. If there's any danger, you can use the earring to hide everyone."

Solia nodded, then smiled at Jace. Grede stretched and got to his feet. "Looks like it's time for the veteran hunter of the Hinterlands to make his appearance again. I don't think Draenor's beasts are any tougher than back home — the scorpions here haven't even grown a second stinger!"

He'd barely taken a few steps when he suddenly glanced warily at Vereesa and Solia, then said, "Ah, right, I remembered. Every time I think things are going to be easy, we all have a streak of bad luck, right? This time, to avoid causing you trouble, I'll stand guard at our 'camp'!"

Vereesa put her hands on her hips and looked down at him with disdain. "You think I can't bring back prey without you, dwarf?"

"Hah!" Grede shook his head. "Then prove yourself, great Lord Ranger!"

Vereesa was just about to turn and walk off when Jace stood up immediately. "I'll go with you, Ven. We don't know if there are demons in the forest either. I can help."

Seeing Jace come over, Vereesa's brows immediately relaxed and she smiled. "You're actually in the mood to go hunting with me?"

"Don't underestimate me." Jace pounded his chest. "Son of a veteran Tirisfal hunter."

Vereesa glanced at Grede, who was standing to the side with his arms folded, deliberately facing away, and wanted to snort — but couldn't hold back a laugh.

"I can see how a Wildhammer dwarf and a Lordaeron man ended up together," Jace said.

Jace picked up the demon bag carrying the imp. "Morophil, stay here and help keep watch. Be careful — demons might come close."

"Fine, master." Morophil sighed deeply. Vereesa cleared her throat, rose on her toes, and headed happily down the mountain with her bow.

A thick fog hung over Terokkar Forest. Vereesa and Jace advanced slowly and carefully. Though it was night, it wasn't pitch black — Draenor's moonlight at night was practically nonexistent, but a gentle silver-blue radiance still shone down through the dense, light-blocking canopy.

Jace knew what that was. It was the glow of a species of giant tree unique to Terokkar Forest. Though he couldn't see clearly from here, its shape should be somewhat like a pinecone.

"It's beautiful here." Vereesa looked up at the hazy glow of those seeds and said softly. "I really can't imagine this is where the orcs live. Actually, that wasteland fits my imagination of this world far better."

Jace thought to herself: you haven't seen Nagrand yet. As he mused, he casually dug out some small fruits from the bushes under the trees that looked like sour dates and popped them into his mouth — and immediately screwed up his face in bitterness.

"Are you crazy?" Vereesa hissed. "Aren't you afraid they're poisonous?"

Jace pulled out the big bottle of Restorative Potion that Ursula had given him. "Can anything be more poisonous than Teron Gorefiend's curse?"

Vereesa pressed her lips together helplessly, waved a hand, and kept walking. Jace followed behind her. "If it comes down to it, we still have Solia..."

"Shh." Vereesa pressed a finger to her lips, drew a short dagger from her boot, and crept forward slowly.

Jace crouched slightly and followed her. After a while, Vereesa raised a hand for him to stop here, then went on ahead by himself.

Jace looked in the direction she was staring and observed carefully for a while before he noticed something pale-colored clinging to a big tree in the shifting light and shadow in the distance.

A haze of dark blue seemed to emanate from its body, and a pair of enormous antennae trembled slightly. Just as Vereesa was about to hurl the dagger in her hand, the thing suddenly spread its giant wings and flew higher.

It was a bizarre-looking giant moth, its body looking considerably larger than a person. It fluttered into the air excitedly and quickly burrowed into the dense canopy, vanishing as if frightened by something.

Vereesa was a bit frustrated too. She slowly straightened up and stared into the heights. "I was as quiet as I possibly could... How did it still detect me? Do the beasts here have some special sense?"

"Stop!" a human-like shout suddenly came from above. Jace looked up. A dark shadow was sweeping through the trees in a very strange trajectory.

Jace immediately locked onto the shadow with his demon-detecting sight. He could confirm it wasn't a demon, at least — but that flight path was almost certainly not something a gryphon or a hawkstrider could produce...

"Who's there?" Vereesa raised the bow in her hand.

Just as Jace was still guessing the other party's identity, another dark shadow had silently drifted to the tree trunk behind him.

Jace immediately raised his staff and turned around. In the pitch-black mass, a cluster of lightbulb-like eyes suddenly lit up — grouped together, they even looked a bit like a spider's eyes. It flapped the scimitar-like "wings" on both sides of its body, barely managing to hover in place, like a strange fish swimming through the air.

A void ray!

Jace knew that in Draenor, only the draenei knights of the Sha'tari Skyguard had mastered the art of training void rays as mounts. So this should be a friendly.

On the back of the void ray, a Farstrider removed his goggles and leveled his spear at Jace's staff. "Put down your weapons and submit to inspection, mage, ranger."

"Unless you can't understand Common and only understand the language of demons." Another void ray descended from the other side. Hearing the voice, Vereesa immediately turned to stare at it.

Judging by the sound, the one sitting on it was a female ranger in a green cloak.

"We're not enemies." Jace raised a hand and looked back and forth between the two. "We came from Azeroth. I'm Jace Sesso, from Stormwind. This is Vereesa Windrunner, Ranger-General of Quel'Thalas. If you're a member of the Farstriders, you should recognize her."

The ranger frowned in disbelief, narrowed her eyes, and carefully studied the female elf before her, then hesitated and looked toward her companion on the void ray across.

At that moment, the ranger on the other void ray pushed back her hood, revealing golden hair. She removed her goggles and asked in an almost trembling voice: "Ve... Vereesa?"
