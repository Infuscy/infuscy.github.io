Chapter 1229: Who Is Jace Sesso

The golden-haired ranger leaped off the void ray and rushed toward Vereesa. Vereesa could no longer hold back her emotions and threw herself into the ranger's arms. The two hugged each other tightly, and tears spilled from the golden elf's eyes, glimmering faintly in the misty light of Terokkar Forest at night.

"This isn't a dream..." she murmured in Thalassian.

The unfamiliar female ranger's appearance, to Jace, was practically a copy of Sylvanas. So even without any introduction, there was no need to guess who she was.

"Sister, I... I missed you so much." Vereesa buried her head against Alleria's shoulder. "I knew I'd see you again. Both of us knew we'd see you again!"

"I didn't know, my little moon." Alleria choked on a sob. "I never dared hope for this day. I thought I could hold on here, for you, but now... are you all right? Is everything all right over there?"

"Most people are still alive, if that's what you're asking." There was a hint of complaint in Vereesa's words.

Alleria burst into laughter through her tears and wiped her eyes. Vereesa looked into her ice-blue eyes and said, "Without you, we'll never be all right."

The two elves embraced again. Jace stood to the side, breathing deeply and slowly, steadying his emotions a little. It wasn't just them who felt this moment was unbelievably dreamlike — so did he.

Before coming to Draenor, he'd already steeled himself for the possibility that he'd have to drag Vereesa across the whole of Outland and return empty-handed. Even after hearing that Alleria Windrunner and Turalyon were still alive and might still be fighting the demons alongside the expeditionary force, he'd felt the search might still be a tortuous road. He hadn't expected that in the end, it would be Alleria leading her people who caught them on the spot.

But for this day — to find the Sons of Lothar — Jace and his companions had put in too much effort and taken too many risks. Maybe it was time they got their reward.

"But..." Alleria reached out and wiped the tears from the corners of Vereesa's eyes, cupping her face. "How did you find your way here?"

"We found Alliance soldiers and draenei priests in Auchindoun. They brought us here." Vereesa pointed behind her. "They're resting on the slope over there right now. I'll take you to them. Our friends are all there waiting for us to come back."

"Good... but, Auchindoun?" Alleria looked toward Jace. "What I meant was, how did you come to Draenor? Khadgar said it's nearly impossible to establish any contact with Azeroth right now, let alone portal magic."

"Um."

Vereesa hemmed and hawed for a moment, then glanced toward Jace. Probably because they'd run into one twist after another along the way with barely a chance to breathe, and the encounter had been too sudden, she hadn't prepared how to explain it either.

Jace wasn't much better off. Meeting Vereesa's gaze, he just swallowed.

Seeing the state they were in, Alleria asked anxiously, "So Ner'zhul's dark ritual did reach Azeroth after all? How is the Sunwell?"

"No," Vereesa said. "Azeroth wasn't affected much. Why would you think that?"

"You didn't come through those rifts?" Alleria asked.

"What rifts?" Vereesa asked.

"Draenor was torn apart by dimensional rifts." Alleria explained, "Those rifts could lead anywhere — perhaps the Twisting Nether, perhaps an unknown world, and of course possibly Azeroth. Some of those rifts still exist to this day. The demons came through those rifts."

Jace and Vereesa exchanged a glance. In the end, he decided to tell the truth. Rather than covering things up and getting exposed for certain later, it was better to lay everything out clearly from the start.

After all, bringing the Sons of Lothar back to Azeroth would be an enormously difficult task. The slightest mistake or suspicion could lead to catastrophic failure. Jace didn't want to be constantly worrying about how to deceive the most capable elite warriors of the entire Alliance while carrying out such a dangerous plan.

"Azeroth truly wasn't affected by the destruction of Draenor," Jace said in Thalassian. "But we did indeed come here with the help of a demon."

"I don't understand. A demon's help?" Alleria looked back and forth between the two. Vereesa's eyes widened slightly, as if surprised Jace had just come right out and said it.

Jace looked at the Farstrider knight beside him. "Yes. Through a demon under my control."

Alleria drew a sharp breath. She looked at her younger sister. "Don't tell me you're only finding out about this now, Vereesa Windrunner."

"No, I..." Vereesa hesitated. Alleria looked at her. "Who is he? Jace Sesso... that name sounds somewhat familiar. What is he, a demon summoner? And you're willing to trust that he brought you to this demon-infested place to find us?"

As she said this, the ranger captain's hand was already reaching for her quiver. Vereesa immediately grabbed her hand. "He's my fiancé, sister!"

Jace had already gripped his staff tight, ready to catch an arrow with magic — but Vereesa's words gave the emotion he'd been preparing to resonate with the Shadow a jolt.

Fiancé...

Of course that was what he thought too. And at the ceremony where Vereesa became Ranger-General, he'd felt all of Quel'Thalas probably saw it that way too.

But the Alleria Windrunner before him was, after all, the true matriarch both Sylvanas and Vereesa recognized. And hearing the word come from Vereesa's own lips made it feel very different.

Compared to Jace's slight inner stir, Alleria on the other side was practically stunned by Vereesa's words. She stared wide-eyed into Vereesa's eyes and asked word by word: "What did you say..."

"Think carefully before you kill him like an orc warlock." Vereesa held her tight. "He is the savior personally acknowledged by the Sun King, and the most trusted man of King Varian Wrynn."

Hearing the title "savior," Alleria hesitated slightly.

"He entered Grim Batol and, under the terrifying threat of Deathwing, rescued the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza, completely lifting the red shadow that hung over the continent of Lordaeron... and Deathwing ultimately died there too."

"He killed Deathwing?" Alleria asked immediately.

"I won't tell you he slew that devil with his own hands, but that devil truly died because of him," Vereesa said.

The dark blue forest was utterly silent, even the fog seeming to have frozen. Alleria's intense emotions, which had just been knotting up inside her, slowly melted away, and the suspicion in her gaze toward Jace gradually dissipated.

"He also killed Teron Gorefiend and destroyed his wicked soul!" Vereesa grew more agitated as she spoke, her voice beginning to tremble slightly. "He avenged Lirath for us. He accomplished what neither you nor Sylvanas nor I could do!"

Alleria looked at her younger sister in surprise, her eyes flickering. She lowered her head and thought for a moment. "Yes... I remember. Both Turalyon and I heard about it, right before the catastrophe happened. But I hadn't had time to remember that name before this whole world became completely different. It was Jace Sesso..."

"And if not for him, maybe I and Sylvanas would have accepted your departure like the other Quel'dorei."

"Accepted reality!"

At this, Vereesa took a hard breath, forced the sobbing out of her voice as much as she could, then continued seriously, "And none of this is the reason I love him, Alleria. None of this is the reason I love him... You'll understand. You'll understand very soon."

"Jace Sesso." Alleria murmured the name and nodded.

She let a long breath out, slung her bow, and gave a Farstrider salute. In Common with a faint Lordaeron capital accent, she said, "I am Alleria Windrunner, Ranger-Captain of Quel'Thalas and scouting commander of the Sons of Lothar. It is a true honor to finally meet you."

Jace smiled, cradled his staff, and bowed slightly. "Jace Sesso, Magical Advisor to His Majesty Varian Wrynn. The honor is mine, Captain Alleria Windrunner."
