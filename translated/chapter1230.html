Chapter 1230: Knowing Friends from Foes

"Honestly, I really didn't expect you'd end up with a human too."

"Why?"

"When I decided to marry Turalyon, remember who screamed like a crazy wildcat in the courtyard?"

"Who? Kael'thas?"

"Hey!"

"Wasn't me, anyway."

Vereesa turned her face to the side, sneaking a glance at Jace not far behind them. Alleria squeezed her shoulder hard, and the two burst into happy laughter again.

For some reason, Jace always felt like Sylvanas was more of an older sister, while Alleria and Vereesa were more like her two younger sisters.

Maybe that was also why Alleria had handed the heavy responsibility of Ranger-General over to Sylvanas without hesitation, then followed Turalyon and the Sons of Lothar through the Dark Portal.

"You should call me Lord Ranger now, respected Captain Alleria. To your superior..."

"Lord Ranger?! My Light, that's the Mantle of Franal — it's real."

"Hurry up and call me superior, your Moon superior."

"Little Moon Superior."

"No 'little'!"

Right then, the two rangers were chattering away together, talking as if they'd never run out of things to say. Jace and the Farstrider ranger following behind, meanwhile, were silent.

But Jace wasn't eavesdropping on their conversation. He was carefully thinking about one thing — what was he going to do about Morophil?

"Jace."

Alleria turned her head. "May I call you that?"

"Of course, Captain." Jace quickly looked up and smiled in response.

"Through what demon?" Alleria asked.

"A, um..." Jace cleared his throat. "An Eredar. We found an Auchindoun portal coordinate from the Broken, and then used void magic to have him send us here. The whole process went fairly successful, considering... the current state of Draenor."

Alleria nodded slowly, then looked at Vereesa again. "I imagine the Alliance doesn't know about this?"

"At least King Varian knows about it," Vereesa said. "And it was me who reminded Jace to go to him and confirm first. Did you think I was still that little girl sneaking out of the house, Sun Lady?"

Alleria smiled — a smile full of complicated emotions that even Jace found hard to read. But on the whole, she was quite happy.

"In the last moment before the Dark Portal was closed... we managed to send back several portal coordinates for Draenor, along with several artifacts that must never fall into the hands of the Burning Legion." Alleria said, "From the moment I heard you had to find the portal coordinate from the Broken... I knew the Kirin Tor definitely wasn't planning to come looking for us."

"I'm sorry, sister." Vereesa said softly.

"I understand their decision." Alleria nodded. "Every portal opened means the destructive energy of Draenor could reach the other side. The Kirin Tor knows nothing about the situation here — they certainly wouldn't dare risk opening another Dark Portal. Still, how did you decide to find a way to come here?"

Vereesa gave Jace a meaningful look. Jace understood — she didn't want him to say she'd been the one forcing everyone to come. Alleria immediately glared at her, startling Vereesa into biting her lip and turning her head away.

Alleria looked at Jace again. "You weren't simply tricked here by her, were you?"

"How is it tricking?" Vereesa said, displeased.

"No." Jace raised an eyebrow at Vereesa, signaling for him to do the talking, and explained, "The Eredar I summoned once served the Burning Legion. From him I learned that Magtheridon had brought the Legion's demons to invade here again after the great explosion of Draenor, aiming to wipe out all draenei and Alliance forces here and take control of the Horde once more..."

"On Azeroth, everyone thought Draenor had met its doom, including many orcs. But if the Burning Legion needed to send a great army here, I figured the expeditionary force must still have the ability to resist — meaning this world truly wasn't completely destroyed yet."

"I told her about it right away," Jace said. "From that moment on, we made up our minds to find an opportunity, find a way to take a look here."

"Yes, that's how it was." Vereesa nodded vigorously.

After hearing him out, Alleria clearly wasn't in the mood to banter with Vereesa anymore.

"I wonder what Turalyon would think if he learned that the one who told Azeroth we were still alive... was actually a demon of the Burning Legion," she murmured, as if to herself.

"Then, do you have a way to get back?"

"Yes," Jace said. "But we need to return to Auchindoun, because there's only one portal coordinate on the other side, leading to a collapsed hall deep in Auchindoun. I don't know if the expeditionary force has any other portal coordinates. That way I could bring a coordinate back to Auchindoun and hand it over to the other side, and we wouldn't have to risk discovery by the black dragons or the Shadow Council."

"The Kirin Tor... it's still hard to say they trust me. If Archmage Khadgar is willing to go back first and convince Dalaran, then with the full power of the Kirin Tor coordinating with this side, they would certainly find a way to bring everyone back."

Listening to Jace lay out this plan, Alleria was clearly moved too, nodding along continuously.

"Khadgar is currently in Shadowmoon Valley fighting the demons with Turalyon and Kurdran. Once we reach the fortress, I'll take you to see him."

By now they'd already walked up the slope. The place where Grede and Solia were resting wasn't far off. Seeing Morophil at any moment, Jace couldn't help but glance ahead unnaturally.

Sensing that Jace seemed to have some reservation, Alleria said, "Don't worry about Turalyon, Jace. I was just sighing a moment ago. He may be a member of the Silver Hand, but after these past few years, he's far more tolerant than you think — better at knowing who the enemy is and who our allies are. If he'd gone by the education and views he'd accepted in Lordaeron before the Dark Portal, he never should have been with me at all, let alone become friends with a mage like Khadgar... I am honestly surprised Varian accepted this. His own father died at the hands of orcs."

She asked Vereesa, "Just now you said you rescued several prisoners from Auchindoun. You went to the Shadow Council's prison?"

"Yes," Jace said.

"How did you get in?" Alleria asked.

"I learned quite a bit of magic from the Shadow Council, Captain," Jace said. "So getting in wasn't particularly difficult for me."

"If only you'd been there too," Vereesa said. "I really would have loved to see you watch Jace turn those demons and orcs against each other."

Alleria looked at Jace with considerable surprise. "Now that you mention it, I really do regret not being inside Auchindoun at the time. Those orcs now call themselves 'the Cabal,' and they're even more insane and cruel than when Gul'dan and Teron Gorefiend ruled over them. Whatever miserable end they get, it's no more than they deserve... Still, I'm guessing there are no other survivors left in that prison."

"Yes," Jace said.

"Then at least we don't have to worry about anything else." The ranger captain said softly.

"Hey, Jace! Little elf!" Grede appeared on the slope ahead. He stood on high ground and looked this way. "Let me see what kind of good food you can scrounge up without me!"

When he saw Alleria walking at the front, he froze and called out: "A... Lady Alleria?"

"You know me?" the elf asked in surprise. Vereesa was quite taken aback too.

"Of course!" Grede ran over and looked at Jace. "When you set out from Stormwind, me and Jace were on that bridge in the Valley of Heroes watching you! I am Grede Thunderfist, of Aerie Peak. A pleasure to meet you, my lady!"

Hearing Grede was a Wildhammer dwarf, Alleria immediately smiled and nodded. "A pleasure to meet you, Mr. Thunderfist. Though the Valley of Heroes..."

"Ah, that's the bridge at the Stormwind gates," Jace said quickly. "It's called the Valley of Heroes now, because..."

"Because they put up a few statues of you there as a memorial." Vereesa shrugged. "Sylvanas even wrote you a eulogy. Heaven knows what expression the people who built those statues will be wearing when you return to Stormwind."

Alleria's eyes flew open in surprise, and for a moment she didn't know what to say.

Just then, she suddenly stared upward warily and drew a blade with a speed nobody had time to react to. The Farstrider behind her also raised his spear. The two void rays drifted into the air at once, letting out sharp, savage cries toward the heights.

Morophil, standing there, raised her hands. "What's wrong, everyone? No need to be so tense, is there?"

Sazeno, in Jace's satchel, couldn't help but cover his mouth and snicker. Catching the glance from the elf ranger behind him, he coughed and shrank back.

"That..." Vereesa raised a hand. Jace immediately picked up the thread. "Morophil, my... ah, succubus. She's a friend, not an enemy."

Alleria relaxed slightly and sheathed her sword. But at the same time, her gaze jumped back and forth between Vereesa and Jace once more.
