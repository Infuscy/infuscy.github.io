Chapter 1242: The Wildhammer's Welcome

After waking up, Jace glanced at his pocket watch and couldn't tell whether it was seven in the evening or seven in the morning.

Since coming to Azeroth — or rather, since meeting Grede, Vereesa, and the others — living in places where you couldn't tell day from night had become perfectly normal for Jace. There were plenty of faint, indistinct voices drifting from beyond the walls, more than enough to signal that it was a fine time to get up.

Sola's room was already empty, and the snoring from Grede's side had gone quiet too. Jace stepped outside, feeling the faintly glaring fel light in the distant darkness and even the violently surging dark power filling all of Shadowmoon Valley. He actually felt rather invigorated.

On the wall, a few warriors in bright-colored armor seemed to notice Jace's appearance and looked over. Just as he was wondering whether those men were paladins who'd come to arrest him, a Wildhammer warrior walked over and saluted.

"Master Sesso, Marshal Turalyon and everyone are waiting for you in the great hall. Lord Kurdran says we're holding a banquet tonight to welcome you to Shadowmoon Valley!"

The dwarf pointed toward the largest of the fortress gates, the one set into the mountainside. "The Wildhammer brothers have prepared a feast and fine ale for you, Lady Vereesa, Lady Sola, and Lord Grede. You won't want to miss it."

Jace glanced down at his coat. "Have they all gone over already, Sergeant?"

"Yes, you're the only one missing, Master. But the banquet hasn't started yet — you're not late!" the dwarf nodded.

"Thank you very much. I'll head over right away." Jace smoothed out his sleep-wrinkled coat, watching the distant line of dwarves hauling barrels of ale and food as he walked briskly toward the gate. The dwarf caught up to him again.

"You must be sick of being asked… but I'd still like to ask one more time, Master. Has Grim Batol really been retaken? Can people live there now?"

"I can't say about people living there," Jace said. "But I know the red dragons are purging the dark magic the witch Modgud laid down there, to make amends for the damage done to your homeland during the Orc Wars. At least the red dragons have assured us it won't be long before the Wildhammer can return to Grim Batol and rebuild their home. I imagine the timing will be just right by the time you head back."

Hearing this, the dwarf wiped his nose vigorously. "I see… Master, I have nothing more to ask."

"Grim Batol!" he turned and shouted to a dwarf guard standing by a nearby dwarf cellar door. "Did you hear that? We can go home! Our real home!"

The dwarf guard in the heavy helmet couldn't be made out clearly, but he lowered his head all the same, then steadied the poleaxe in his hands and straightened up.

"Go on ahead, Master, we'll be right there!" the dwarf sergeant called out.

Jace nodded and walked through the fortress's main gate. The noise from inside was already audible from here.

The red-clad dwarf guards inside the gate all straightened up when they saw him come in, calling out "Master Sesso," and one dwarf warrior bellowed at the top of his lungs deeper inside: "Master Jace Sesso is here!"

At that shout, the chaotic noise from deep within the fortress seemed to quiet down all at once.

Guided by a dwarf guard, Jace followed the scent of smoked meat and ale through the corridor and into the great hall within. Between the long tables laden with strangely colored fruits, pastries, black and brown roasts of every size, and wine cups, a great many soldiers, officers, dwarves, humans, and elves were all staring in his direction, quite a few raising their cups and calling out Jace's name in salute.

"Jace, we've been waiting for you."

Vereesa was standing off to the side. She wasn't wearing a cloak at the moment, and her hair was down — her silver hair just casually draped over her shoulders. At first glance she actually looked a little unfamiliar.

"Vereesa," Jace replied softly.

Then, beside Khadgar, a tall man in a blue surcoat with golden short hair and a touch of stubble pushed through the crowd toward them.

From the respectful way the people around him regarded him — and especially the look in Alleria's eyes as she watched him — Jace was fairly certain this was Turalyon himself.

As he approached, he said, "The journey all the way from Auchindoun must have been exhausting."

"Sorry I'm late," Jace said.

"Not late at all. It's an honor that this fortress could give you a place to rest easy." The man touched his chest. "By the way, I haven't introduced myself. I am Turalyon, one of the Sons of Lothar. I've already heard a great deal about you from Vereesa and Lord Grede Thunderfist, Jace."

Turalyon's introduction was dead simple, but Jace knew full well that he was not only the leader of the Sons of Lothar and commander of the expeditionary force, but also one of the founders of the Order of the Silver Hand. In other words, the man before him was quite possibly the most powerful paladin in the entire Alliance right now, an absolute bane of evil. Standing before him, Jace could hardly claim he wasn't the least bit uneasy. After all, in another timeline, Teron Gorefiend had barely made his way back to Shadowmoon Valley, only to be met head-on by him in a skirmish and smashed then and there, clean and quick.

So, upon hearing that Vereesa and Grede had already "said a great deal," he almost instinctively glanced over toward Vereesa and Sola. Grede, off to the side, was nursing a wine cup, and when he noticed Jace glancing at him, quickly set his cup back down.

"…Including, of course, how you came to be here." Turalyon had seen through Jace's concern almost immediately. He said in a low voice, "There's no need to worry, Jace. Here, everyone cares only about how to destroy the Burning Legion and the orcs as thoroughly as possible. As for the methods used, that's secondary. Even if one day Ner'zhul suddenly appeared before me and told me he had a way to send every soldier of the expeditionary force back to Azeroth, I'd let him finish his proposal before considering raising my hand — let alone a hero who sacrificed himself at the Dark Portal to save countless Alliance soldiers."

"Turalyon!" Kurdran Wildhammer shouted unhappily from his seat up high. "I can hear the heroes of Grim Batol's stomachs growling from right here! Is there anything more important right now than eating? Or do you want every brother in this great hall to stand around waiting for you Lordaeronians to slowly stumble through every last one of your ridiculous court formalities?"

Khadgar gave Turalyon a helpless look, and before the two could even react, the Wildhammer host bellowed at the top of his lungs: "The Great Lord of the fortress now declares the banquet open! Everyone, eat!"

"For the Alliance!"

"By the Light, in the name of Marshal Lothar!"

More and more dwarves and soldiers poured into the spacious hall, the crowd shouting slogans, laughter, cheers, and the clinking of cups all mingling together.

We can go home. For Jace's group, who had only just arrived on Draenor, they probably couldn't possibly understand what it felt like for these soldiers who had garrisoned a desperate alien world for years on end to hear those words.

"A toast to you, my dear brother-in-law." Alleria handed him a wine cup, then clinked cups with Jace. "To Azeroth."

Vereesa looked back and forth, then said quietly, "We're not married yet, sister."

"To Azeroth." Jace ignored Vereesa beside him and readily accepted the ranger-captain's toast. Vereesa couldn't help but laugh in spite of herself, and Turalyon laughed along with her.

"Try that one, Jace." Sola leaned in then, pointing at a strange food in the distance that was puffed up and steaming, like some kind of fruit. "You'll never guess what that is…"

Jace picked one up, about the size of a fist, and after biting it open, a golden, crab-roe-like scalding liquid suddenly gushed out and ran down his hand. He hurriedly licked it up, and to his surprise it had a quite delicious steamed-fish flavor to it too.

After licking the thick juice inside clean, he thought for a moment and said, "Is this some kind of sea cucumber, or…"

Sola shook her head, and at some point Khadgar had come up beside them and said softly, "It's a species of mushroom that grows along the Zangarmarsh coast."

"Mushroom?" Jace blinked, and looked back and forth curiously. He'd always thought the mushrooms in the Zangarmarsh were bigger than buildings. A mushroom this small… never mind that it didn't look like one, the taste was so strange it was genuinely hard to recognize.

"I heard from Vereesa that you caught quite a bit of grief from King Terenas II and his nobles," Khadgar said.

"Uh." Jace hadn't expected Khadgar to bring that up suddenly. He said, "I understand those nobles' concerns, Master."

Khadgar looked at Jace's expression and smiled. "I think whatever they said, at least one thing the King said was quite right."

"What's that?" Jace asked, licking the remaining juice from his hand.

"When you get back, you really should visit the Silver Hand sometime," Khadgar said in a serious tone. "Only I don't know if they take mages these days. Perhaps Turalyon could change their minds."
