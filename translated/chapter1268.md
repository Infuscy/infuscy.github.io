## Chapter 1268: A Disturbance Loud Enough

"You're sure the Demonicon is right beside that demon?" Jace asked.

Just now he'd tried using demon detection to observe the number of demons inside, but combined with the intensity of the magical light brought by Khadgar's potion, it had instead made him overlook everything inside besides the arcane and the demons.

"I saw it too, master," Molofier said.

"So we'll have to fight the lord of this castle," Sola said.

"We can't even break through that arcane shield." Jace looked at Yolin and asked in a low voice, "Can that staff break this barrier?"

"It should be able to. That barrier is anchored to Highmaul's mana source," Yolin said. "But I'll need time to try. You can't fight the demons of the entire castle and that eredar sorcerer at the same time while also protecting me as I use the staff to manipulate the arcane energy here…"

"Then, think of a way to lure him away?" Jace said. "He seems to care a lot about Karabor. Maybe we can think of a crisis worrying enough to make him rush over — like Magtheridon plotting a rebellion or something — and leave us enough of an opening. By the time he comes back, we should have everything sorted out."

"If he'd believe us, of course we could, Jace Sesso," Yolin said, looking at Jace. "But are you really planning to talk to that demon lord face to face? The felguard outside couldn't tell anything was wrong with the Mark of Sargeras, but whether he can see through it — I couldn't say."

"That's a high-ranking eredar lord, master," Molofier said. "Since that demonic tome is right there, it means Kil'jaeden trusts him with the qualifications to monitor Magtheridon. So he should be able to see through my disguise easily. We can't trick him into leaving. So we might as well just…"

She gripped the hilt of one of the Apocalypse Twinblades at her waist. "Don't forget — we still have the advantage of being in the shadows."

Molofier was right. But once they tore off the mask and started fighting, there would be no other options left — it could only be a last resort.

Jace looked toward the corridor entrance behind them, then out at the ogre city far below, its darkness lit by Nagrand's night to a dark purple, and the demonic shadows of the doomguards standing on the towers.

"Can we get those two to help," he said.

"You mean having them hunt demons outside to lure this eredar out?" Yolin said. "He's so focused on his magical ritual, I'm not optimistic he'll take the bait."

"You have a better idea?" Jace said. "Even if he's unwilling to go out, he should still send some demons out to search. That would at least divert some pressure from our operation."

"The earring is on Vereesa," Sola said, looking at the Bleeding Hollow orc. "The natural life around this castle has been pretty much corroded by fel energy, Jace. The earring won't be much use. So it's more useful to me if I leave it with Vereesa — it can help her evade the demons' pursuit and buy more time."

Yolin thought about it for a moment, then nodded. "Indeed, I don't have a better idea, you two."

Jace patted his demon bag. "Time to put you on messenger duty again, Sazeno. Your Phase Shift is less conspicuous than Molofier's. Tell them the situation here — about that eredar's ritual — and then have them make a little commotion for us."

Sazeno jumped out of the bag onto the ground. "It's a real shame I can't take part in the thrilling battle to kill the eredar. But trust me, master, I'll definitely complete the mission!"

With that, the little fellow eagerly bounded off the steps and vanished into another plane mid-leap, not looking the slightest bit regretful.

"Now we wait," Jace said with a deep breath.

Not long after, a patrolling doomguard walked up nearby. He glanced sideways at them, observed the silent group for a while, and asked in Demonic, "Is there a problem here?"

Molofier gave him a sidelong look and asked coldly, "And who might you be?"

When the doomguard saw Molofier's burning eyes, he immediately lowered his head, and his tone dropped as well. "My lady, Lord Batas's ritual is at a very critical stage. I must—"

"Yes." Molofier gave a false smile and said in a rather sharp voice in Demonic, "So we are waiting for Lord Batas to complete this phase, then deliver an urgent message from the Black Temple. Now you may continue with your duties, Ered'ruin."

The doomguard exhaled a breath of scorched black air, turned, and strode away with broad steps. When his shadow disappeared around the corner, Molofier turned back and tilted her head. "We'd better find a place more suitable for Shadow Council members to wait, master."

Following Molofier away from the terrace, they came to a ruined garden below the Spellbreaker's Hall. Quite a few Shadow Council orcs were sitting there discussing something in low voices.

The three orcs squatted at the railing's edge. From here, they could look out from beside a massive watchtower.

Jace could no longer see any trace of the imp. He hoped it would find Vereesa and Grede smoothly, and hoped that eredar would actually be willing to go out in person to deal with the problem, buying them time and space.

But just thinking about it, Jace also felt the chances were slim. The eredar wasn't a gronn lord who could be lured out so easily. He also didn't think the lives of the demons guarding outside would make him itch to act.

That night was an excruciatingly long one — not just worrying about Vereesa and Grede, but also calculating how to deal with this eredar.

In a corner, Jace went over the potions and elixirs in the pack with Yolin and Sola, including that bottle of Elixir of Distilled Wisdom brought from Azeroth. If it really came to a fight with that eredar, this potion was absolutely not something to be stingy with.

Just as Jace was starting to feel a little drowsy, Sola beside him patted him and pointed outside. "Look, Jace."

"I don't see anything." Jace rubbed his eyes. Yolin and Molofier also looked around blankly.

"It's the radiance of the Dream," Sola said. "It has to be Vereesa making her move."

Everyone leaned over the railing and observed outside for a while. A demon's wail sliced through the silence of the early morning, and then green fel fire blazed up on several watchtowers high above.

The orcs beside them grew agitated. Several Dead Shadow soldiers grabbed the axes at their sides and came to the railing to look out. Two doomguards with spears flew up high and swooped down toward the lower gates of Highmaul. Jace watched those two demonic shadows and took a deep breath, instinctively praying to the Holy Light in his heart that everything would go smoothly.

Just as Jace was watching intently, a blast of demonic light struck the watchtower before their eyes without warning. Dreamlike phantom light exploded before their eyes, sending a large spray of rubble everywhere and frightening the nearby Shadow Council orcs into curses.

A doomguard up high stuck his head out and roared at the demons farther away, telling them to be on guard. The doomguards over there also rumbled back in response.

Far away, near that enormous rift, fel fireballs streaked across the sky above the gloomy city ruins and bombed into a building. A doomguard then swept over from above. Jace frowned with some worry, but there was still no movement from inside the Spellbreaker's Hall.

Suddenly, the doomguard flying over the city let out a savage shriek and plummeted into the ruins. Then a burst of white light erupted from its body. The orc warlocks near the group saw this and began whispering and discussing among themselves.

In the shadows beneath Highmaul, many Dead Shadow soldiers filed down the steps in squads toward where the doomguard had fallen. One after another, two more doomguards left the watchtowers and flew over. Jace noticed one flying demon fly in the opposite direction into the Spellbreaker's Hall — probably to report the situation outside to Batas.

The city outside had already descended into chaos. The demons clearly hadn't caught Grede and Vereesa yet and were still sweeping the districts with fel fire outside.

But that demon had been inside the great hall for quite a while now, and the eredar lord still showed no reaction. Just as Jace and Yolin had expected, he had absolutely no intention of going out to inspect in person. All his focus was on that ley line nexus.

Yolin was watching the movements of the Spellbreaker's Hall just as he was, but he'd also grown a bit discouraged. After all, even the Ered'ruin at his side showed no intention of going out at all.

Actually, this put Jace a little more at ease. Rather than having Batas go out to deal with Vereesa and Grede, he'd prefer to leave him here and let Molofier and himself — wielding Ursolus — handle it.

He was already calculating: when that eredar sorcerer intended to show his face to deal with the little DeadEye breaking the shield, he'd summon a Hand of Gul'dan in advance…

Just as he was mentally considering how to deal with Batas, the entire ground suddenly shook. The nearby orcs all looked bewildered and panicked, grabbing onto the railings and stone walls beside them.

"What's going on?" Sola asked.

A low voice sounded from within the Spellbreaker's Hall. Jace raised his head and only then noticed that a long crack had appeared, he knew not when, on the outer wall of this palace.

The ground continued to shake. The outer walls of the watchtowers beside them kept shedding stones from time to time. The Spellbreaker's Hall also began to tremble incessantly. One of the stone pillars had even collapsed.

Not only the temple — and most critically, in the ley line vision provided by Khadgar's potion, the mana being guided by the eredar had also become unstable!

"The elements." Yolin looked toward the Elemental Throne. "This is the power of the earth. Could the Mag'har shamans be guiding a ritual to help us? But…"

"Not the Mag'har shamans, but it is indeed the help of the elements." Jace understood in a flash. "It's Grede. That fellow is using Deathwing's necklace — it has to be!"

Jace couldn't help but let out a soft laugh, raised his head, and said, "He really does understand how to 'make a commotion,' DeadEye!" The sound of heavy footsteps came from the front. Everyone looked toward the Spellbreaker's Hall in unison.

A dark-blue figure appeared from the faintly trembling palace corridor, striding downward toward the main gate of Highmaul below with a fury blazing in his heart.

In the morning light, Jace could see from here the glow of his golden enchanted armor and the fel fire-runes drifting on his dark-blue forehead.

"Mark of Sargeras," Molofier said with considerable disdain. "He's displaying that thing as if it were some kind of honor? What a loyal, devoted dog."

"The dog's out of the cage, so it's time for us to move." Jace grabbed Sola's leather shoulder guard. "Right now!"
