## Chapter 1269: The Fel Barrier

Perhaps the arcane spell he'd studied most seriously and practiced the most times since coming to this world, Jace had the incantation of Mage Armor down cold, even though this spell was a bit more complicated than the Hand of Gul'dan.

"Lobpotori, Arkan…"

As he followed Yolin Deadeye and Molofier's footsteps, he cast an anti-evil version of Mage Armor on himself, because there was still one more demon to deal with inside the Spellbreaker's Hall.

They needed to blast that doomguard into oblivion as fast as possible, lift every protection on the Spellbreaker's Hall and the Demonicon — at the very least, they couldn't be slower than Grede's one hammer strike.

Speaking of Grede, by now the ground around the Spellbreaker's Hall had already stopped shaking, and the murmuring voices of the elements released by the familiar power of the earth had also faded away.

Those two had probably realized the master of this castle intended to deal with them personally, so they were letting go and fleeing. From here on out, it was up to the rest of them.

The succubi and felguards on the spiraling corridor outside the Spellbreaker's Hall were shouting at the Dead Shadow soldiers and warlocks to stand on guard. No one had time to pay any attention to their small group slipping through the chaos.

By the time they smoothly reached the entrance of the Spellbreaker's Hall's great hall, to their surprise that barrier had already been lowered and hadn't been raised again in time.

It seemed that with that barrier in place, the eredar himself couldn't enter or exit either — which had conveniently saved Jace and the others a good deal of time.

Right now two orc warlocks were holding mana crystals and channeling mana to help that Ered'ruin demon restart the shield. The arcane magic on the door was still just a thin magical film!

"Don't let them succeed!"

The words had barely left Jace's lips when the Molofier in front of him suddenly vanished into a mass of reddened black mist, reappearing in the blink of an eye behind one of the orc warlocks dozens of yards away.

The succubus drove one of the Apocalypse Twinblades through the orc's back, and the other she hurled straight through the forehead of the second orc. The two orc warlocks, caught completely off guard, were killed almost simultaneously, their corpses bursting into flame.

Molofier vanished again, reappearing beside the second orc, pulling out her dagger and stabbing straight at the chest of the tall doomguard before her — but this time the demon had already reacted.

Although the doomguard was tall and powerfully built, he still flapped his wings and retreated a step, dodging the succubus's thrust. The holy fire of the Apocalypse Twinblades erupted, blowing away the dispersing arcane mist, and the Ered'ruin demon's face twisted in fury.

"This is Nathrezim martial…"

The doomguard's roar turned into a ferocious blast of fel breath. Molofier tilted her side-stepped and slashed out with a blade.

The doomguard sucked in a breath and instinctively blocked with his demonic wings, but was cleaved straight through the wing membrane. The Apocalypse Twinblades drove into his black shoulder guard with full force, hacking several metal plates of the armor to pieces.

Forged by a red dragon, the intense explosive energy ignited by the Apocalypse was so powerful that even the fel chains binding the shoulder guard were burned through. The entire shoulder guard crashed to the ground as well.

However, although this doomguard was shocked by the strength of the weapon in the sayaad's hands, he didn't lose his head completely.

He wasn't a doom lord like Kazzak, but judging from the dark-brown heavy armor and complex magical runes covering his body, this should at least be an Ered'ruin officer — probably Batas's deputy ruling over this Highmaul.

It was no wonder the doomguard had a hard time holding up. Molofier's speed was just too fast. These two exchanges had taken place in barely a few seconds. Before he'd even recovered his balance from the strike that had shattered his shoulder guard, Molofier had already flashed behind him and cleaved through that battered demonic wing with a single sword!

But just as Molofier moved in to strike a second slash, the doomguard summoned a long whip of flame despite the pain and lashed it viciously toward her.

The whip splattered a vast cloud of dark-green black smoke. The crack of the lash set off a deafening blast that sent a wave of scorching shock force outward, forcing Molofier to step back and dodge the strike. But immediately after, a streak of dark-green magical energy had already bound her hands and feet tightly…

She used Shadowstep to throw off the Cripple and dove into the shadows. Jace charged to the front, bracing through the shock wave with the Black Dragon cloak, and released Suffocation, seizing control of the doomguard's hand, which held the flaming whip.

The doomguard shook his arm, realizing he couldn't break free. Those bovine eyes stared in shock at Jace, who had appeared here. He knew he was finished.

Molofier seized the opportunity to reappear and drive a blade into the demon's seemingly impossibly thick adamantine chest plate, cutting through it as smoothly as slicing through tofu.

The demonic heart was pierced. The demon's burning eyes were extinguished in an instant. It struggled, burning into a ball of fel-green fire in midair, leaving behind only a few chunks of charred flesh and blackened armor. Its demonic soul howled as it was drawn into Ursolus.

The Demonicon was floating on a stone platform burning with fel fire behind where the doomguard had stood. The group came before it and looked carefully. It was a thick demonic tome covered in dark-purple magical runes, wrapped in layers of burning green fel flames.

Jace could tell that what looked like burning flames was actually an incredibly powerful fel barrier.

Looking at this ball of fel fire, Molofier said, "The magic Batas laid down… this eredar lord might be considerably more powerful than I thought, master."

Yolin looked at the fel crystal in the center of the Spellbreaker's Hall connected to the ley line nexus, then at the Demonicon, and said, "Of course he'd have abundant mana with an energy source like that to support him. But it's all right. I can try to sever the connection between this pedestal and Highmaul's mana source."

"Are you sure you can pull that off with just this Withered Staff?" Jace asked.

"My father was better at it." Yolin walked up to the pedestal. "But now I am the chief of the Bleeding Hollow, Jace Sesso." What Yolin said seemed to be directed at the few people around him, but it sounded more like he was talking to himself.

Jace glanced at the orc raising the staff, then out at the exterior of the Spellbreaker's Hall. At this point, he could only trust Yolin.

Thanks to Batas not allowing anyone to disturb his ritual, and thanks to Molofier being fast enough, the outside of the Spellbreaker's Hall seemed to have not yet noticed anything amiss, even though all of Highmaul was already crying out for blood.

Outside the door, the sky was just getting light, but the glow of fel fire still flickered from time to time.

Yolin raised the staff, which glowed with a dark-yellow light, and chanted a spell in the High Arak language that Jace couldn't understand.

Although he'd had his doubts about DeadEye's claims at first, when Jace noticed the fel crystal floating in the center of the Spellbreaker's Hall beginning to slowly rotate along with his chanting, the credibility of what he'd said seemed to jump up several notches all at once.

As the crystal began to turn slowly, the sound of arcane energy rose again inside the Spellbreaker's Hall's great hall. A buzzing sound assaulted their eardrums and transmitted through the walls to the outside.

Sola gripped her staff tightly. Molofier also began to pace back and forth restlessly, the sound of her hooves striking the ground drowned out by the crystal's hum.

Jace kept glancing back at DeadEye, who was guiding the magic. He appeared to have devoted all his energy to the demonic tome on the pedestal before him, but the fel fire around the tome still hadn't vanished. The dark-yellow gem on the Withered Staff was visibly growing greener and greener.

"Are you sure there's no problem, DeadEye?" Jace tried asking.

Yolin Deadeye gritted his teeth. "My mana can't quite keep up."

"I don't know if this works on orcs…" Sola muttered under her breath. Jace said, "I know what you want to do. Quick, Sola, give it to him directly."

Sola nodded, raised her staff, and with a strain of laboriously drawn natural force, wrapped Yolin in vines. A lightning-like energy shot from his hands straight into his brain. Yolin took a deep breath and glared his eyes wide open, as if he'd been revitalized several times over. Even his waist straightened up considerably.

This was the druid's Innervate. Although it wouldn't fill a person up with energy all at once like the Elixir of Distilled Wisdom and boost their mana by a large margin, it could strengthen a person's mental intensity for a short time by vitalizing life force, giving a freakishly large boost to the rate of energy recovery.

Yolin also seemed very clear that Innervate wouldn't last long. Without time to thank her, he raised the staff again and channeled mana into the pedestal. The crystal's rotation slowed slightly, then started up again — and this time it spun faster and faster. This time Jace watched the fel barrier burning on the Demonicon begin to waver, its energy weakening noticeably.

But at the same time, the color of the Withered Staff was also growing more and more fel-green. The fel crystal could not only corrupt the Highmaul ley line nexus it was in contact with — it could also corrupt every mana conduit connected to it, including this staff.

The sounds of shouting and commands seemed to come from outside the door, which had already gone quiet. Jace felt the Burning Legion's demons had probably noticed something amiss here.

He had just turned his head to glance at the state of the Demonicon when a demon's furious roar suddenly came from the doorway. "Lord Batas! Lord Batas…"

Jace's Suffocation had already sealed the doomguard's neck. The doomguard struggled, retreating two steps and tearing at this chain of Shadow with his bare hands. But shadowflame instantly crawled all over his body, gnawing at his skin and flesh.

But Jace knew it was already too late. The news of the Spellbreaker's Hall being attacked by intruders had definitely reached the demons.
