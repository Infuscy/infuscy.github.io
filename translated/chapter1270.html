Chapter 1270: The Felfire Hall

Amid the savage howling of felhounds, imps with wings crawled in through the doorway and hurled fireballs at them the moment they showed themselves. Jace had just blasted apart the fireballs with a storm of black flame and burned the imps back when another doomguard dropped in through a window high above the Chamber of Nullification.

The demon landed on the ground and cast Cripple at Deadeye. Morophil charged forward immediately and chopped off one of his arms to stop his magic, then tangled with this doomguard.

Black flame spread recklessly among the demon corpses, already turning the doorway into an extremely hot oven. The temperature of the entire Chamber of Nullification was also climbing higher and higher, becoming unbearable.

Fortunately, none of Jace’s group were normal people. Even Jace’s blood had been blessed by a red dragon, so enduring this kind of heat wasn’t difficult. On the contrary, quite a few orcs and imps who’d tried to charge in were frightened back by this terrifying flame. Only doomguards and felhounds dared to force their way straight in.

Morophil cleanly pierced the heart of one doomguard. Another doomguard then spat a fel fireball at her. The violent blast forced her to dodge into the shadows to temporarily avoid the brunt and look for another opening to attack.

She and Jace did their best to protect Jorin as he dismantled the fel barrier, but there were more and more demons. After Jace set the fire-spewing doomguard ablaze with Shadowflame, another doomguard that had broken free of the flames at the great door charged straight at Deadeye with felhounds in tow.

Black smoke was already pouring from his mouth. A fel fireball was already building in his throat…

Suddenly, a giant figure burst out of the flames, dragging a mass of hissing venomous acid, and slammed into the doomguard and the two felhounds following him — directly detonating the fireball building in his mouth. Demon blood splattered across the walls and floor from the explosion.

The green dragon used her powerful claws to tear open the doomguard’s chest, smashed the surrounding felhounds to pulp with her flail of a tail, then spewed her venomous acid at the demons descending on demonic wings from above.

The moment the demon landed, it fell to the ground. Its hooves had already been corroded by the acid down to the bone and could barely stand. It was then blasted through the chest by another strike from the dragon’s tail.

Sola’s strength and the intensity of her breath had already advanced by a notch compared to when they’d been in the Temple of Atal’Hakkar, after her meeting with the Dream Queen. An ordinary doomguard could barely withstand her full-force charge and tail sweep, never mind a felhound.

She raised her body and forcefully whipped up a whirlwind that blew all the fel fire and Shadowflame to the doorway, igniting the corpses outside. Then she made a fierce charge toward the high window, smashing through the rubble blocking her bulk, and roared as she fought with more demons outside trying to crawl in through the window.

Jace stared blankly at the smashed window. The shadows of dragon wings and a dragon tail swayed back and forth, but he knew that no matter how battle-hungry Sola was, she couldn’t hold out much longer. Deadeye…

“Done yet?! Hurry, Jorin!”

He turned his head. Deadeye was already holding that thick tome in his hands. His hands had already been scorched black by the power of dark magic.

“I have it…”

“You’re still a long way from having it, mortal.”

A deep voice came from the doorway. A figure several meters tall stepped through the entrance, accompanied by a doomguard attendant. His hoof crushed the charred head of an Ered’ruin corpse underfoot. His lips moved slightly and Jorin was blasted back several steps, pinned to the wall by a fel chain together with the Book of Fel Names.

“Take back the Book of Fel Names.”

As the eredar gave the order, a doomguard moved toward Deadeye. Jace was just about to make his move, but the eredar’s magic was even faster than him. Countless star-like arcane energy gathered from all corners of the Chamber of Nullification and bore down on him violently.

All the white light struck a single point and triggered a violent explosion. Even the fel crystal in midair was blasted high into the air by the shock wave, cracking with discordant shrieks.

But after the white light, the thing in the middle had vanished. Deadeye had manipulated the staff to use the arcane energy of the ley line nexus to tear apart the fel chain before the doomguard could grab him. With one hand holding the Book of Fel Names and the other gripping his ritual dagger, he was prepared to fight the doomguard head-on.

The eredar was just about to cast some spell at Deadeye when Morophil, her magical runes all blazing with fire, emerged from the burning shadows at the sorcerer’s side. Empowered by demonic enhancement, she’d grown a good deal taller. Wielding her blazing red twin blades, she was like a meteor wrapped in flames, charging straight at the eredar.

Batas’s fel-green eyes dimmed slightly. Just as Morophil’s blades were about to pierce his chest, a broad flaming sword flashed into existence in his hand and he swung it violently at the attack!

Three crimson blades of different sizes collided in succession. The eredar’s war sword, already gouged with several notches, fell from his hand to the ground. He frowned slightly, as if unable to believe what was in this succubus’s hands.

But the sayaad was much faster than him. A follow-up ferocious thrust was already at his face. The flaming demon blade pierced straight through the sorcerer’s forehead… an illusion! Three identical eredar swung blue arcane blades forged from arcane magic simultaneously, attacking Morophil in the center from three different directions at different angles — some thrusting, some slashing. Morophil decisively vanished in a mass of burning shadow mist to avoid the pincer attack.

The illusions struck empty air, then saw the demonic serpents converging from the window and doorway, already gathering into a black-purple meteor in the air of the Chamber of Nullification.

“Gul’dan?”

The three eredar exclaimed in surprise at the same time, simultaneously raising magical shields and scattering to evacuate. But in Jace’s demon vision, the strength of his illusions was crystal clear. He flung down his staff and smashed this shadow meteor at the sorcerer farthest away. A roaring explosion of purple fire overturned a vast section of wall, and the surging shock wave also shattered a nearby illusion at the same time.

Morophil seized the opportunity to pierce the last surviving illusion, then charged toward the doomguard fighting with Deadeye. The illusions hadn’t vanished on their own — enough to prove that eredar was still alive. Jace wasn’t surprised either. The time Morophil had bought him hadn’t been enough to channel a sufficiently powerful Hand of Gul’dan.

A doomguard whose body had been corroded by venomous acid until it had lost a leg howled as it crashed down from the window. Jorin picked up a felhound and hurled it straight onto the doomguard. The felhound, corroded by the acid, immediately screamed and thrashed about in struggle.

Jace was busy reaching into his potion pouch, planning to take the opportunity to drink the Flask of Distilled Wisdom while using demon detection to concentrate on observing the enemy’s traces.

By now, aside from the chaotic shadows of doomguards fighting with the green dragon on top of the Chamber of Nullification, several demonic shadows had actually appeared, darting into the great doorway.

Before the potion was fully down, four powerfully built felhounds — their size far beyond what the patrolling soldiers outside Highmaul could compare to — pounced at Jace’s position from the settling dust of the purple-fire explosion. Jace decisively enslaved one and ordered it to tear into its remaining companions.

“Korili, Feraos…”

Taking advantage of three-against-one, Jace immediately began chanting the Death Coil incantation, planning to detonate the felhound’s demonic soul to kill the other felhounds. But when the chant was half done, his consciousness sensed the arcane energy in the air being gathered in a very familiar and dangerous way in the blink of an eye.

Counterspell!
