## Chapter 1271: The Eredar's Struggle

That fellow hadn't just been blasted with grave injuries — he'd even come right back immediately!

Batas seized the opening as Jace was casting and unleashed a Counterspell. Although Jace's reaction was already fast enough that even Marin would have marveled at it — he immediately stopped the incantation and released the shadow energy gathering in his hand — he was still blasted until his head felt like it was splitting open. The eredar's Counterspell itself possessed absurd power.

Batas, reappearing from the flames, was already covered in blood. The golden armor on his chest had been shattered and blasted off by the Hand of Gul'dan, and massive laceration wounds from Shadow covered his entire body, but his gaze was still resolute.

Seeing Jace silenced by arcane magic, Molofier immediately shook off the doomguard she was tangled with and went after the eredar.

However, after fighting several doomguards who'd dashed into the great hall one after another while dodging the green dragon, the succubus was clearly starting to tire. Her movements had slowed somewhat.

The eredar sorcerer shouted, spread his palms, and released a Mana Detonation that blasted across half the Spellbreaker's Hall. Unable to dodge in time, she was shoved back several meters. After she was caught up to by a doomguard, she barely dodged the demonic war axe he swung. The demonic enhancement strengthening her was visibly fading.

The felhound Jace had just enslaved had already been torn to pieces. The remaining felhounds, brimming with the remnants of the Mana Detonation, charged desperately at Jace.

Perhaps it was the power of the Elixir of Distilled Wisdom already taking effect — the ringing in Jace's ears from the Counterspell didn't last long. Seeing the felhounds converging on him, Jace immediately raised a Dark Shield. Two felhounds bit into the black membrane simultaneously but failed to break through the defense. Instead, they were pushed to the sides and thrown down by the dark force bouncing back out.

Jace caught one of them with a fel chain, stepped forward, and swung the Blood Scythe in his hand, cleaving the felhound's body clean in two. The demonic heart was split precisely open and its devouring demonic soul consumed.

Suddenly, the shadow protection before his eyes was covered in cracks like shattered glass. The eredar had used a spell extremely similar to Dar'Khan's to dismantle his dark shield.

Jace immediately turned back and cast a Suffocation at Batas. Batas was choked by the neck, but still summoned a fel chain that bound Jace's hands and feet tightly.

"Jace Sesso!"

A felhound was drained by blood magic and collapsed to the ground. Then streaks of arcane starlight surrounded these chains. Not far off, Yolin raised his staff high, seemingly trying to use Highmaul's mana to dismantle Jace's bonds.

But as Yolin channeled his magic, he had no defense against two felhounds darting out from the side and interrupting his casting. Fortunately, he was a sufficiently sturdy orc, not a fragile scholar-mage. Even with his magic interrupted, he could still swing his dagger and pierce a felhound's demonic heart on the spot, then crash into the other felhound and tumble to the ground together.

But if he wanted to break free of these chains, Jace could only rely on himself.

At this moment, the demon sorcerer tore apart the Suffocation binding his own neck with his bare hands, then pointed both hands at Jace and chanted a spell. His eyes now held only the most troublesome warlock before him, and this warlock seemed to have already surrendered…

"Eraram, Xiliodor!"

A searing Life Drain seized Jace's mind straight on. While his own strength was still intact, Jace immediately drove Shadowstep. Although this Shadowstep hadn't carried him more than a few steps, it could rely on the shadow force it had gathered to tear apart the fel fire's bonds!

The blood magic gem on his chest emitted a sinister light amid the billowing of the Black Dragon cloak. Under the dual resistance of "Molofier's Bane" and the anti-evil Mage Armor, the fel chains' effect on Jace was far less than their effect on Yolin. And under the Shadowstep empowered by Ursolus, they shattered at a single touch…

"Eraram, Xiliodor!"

As the life energy throughout his body rapidly weakened, Jace turned around and cast Life Drain at the eredar in return!

The demonic souls of the demons killed nearby by the green dragon Solamiel and Molofier were continuously devoured by Ursolus. The power of Jace's dark magic did not weaken as the fighting wore on. On the contrary, its momentum grew stronger and stronger. The cold laughter of an ancient demon was even faintly audible.

"Woo-woo-woo…" Accompanied by the eerie sound of foul magic rubbing against the air, two beams of life-draining energy twisted and tore at each other. Jace clenched his teeth and grew increasingly fervent as he followed the magic's guidance.

On the opposite side, Batas stared into the hollow eyes of the ancient Nathrezim king at the head of Jace's staff, and the fear and panic in his heart sharply intensified…

"No… this can't be Utha—"

"Batas, you have seen the fate of your own soul!" Jace laughed wildly as he drained the eredar's life and mana, while feeling his own power had grown almost even stronger than before entering the Spellbreaker's Hall. Even if every demon in all of Highmaul came to besiege you next, Jace Sesso, no one could be your opponent. Soon you would devour all their demonic souls and the power of the Demonicon to truly become the "Lord of Outland"!

Dark energy swirled about his body. Shadow power had completely torn apart his orcish form. Jace's eyes were already burning with a terrifying purple flame. His deathly pale face was practically a skeleton.

Although Batas was tall and large, his momentum had been thoroughly slaughtered. His magic could barely hold. He retreated two steps, glanced at the fel crystal beside him, now covered in cracks under bombardment of arcane energy, and suddenly released the guidance of Life Drain.

Jace felt the abrupt change in the growth of power, but was also made considerably more calm by the other party's sudden, bizarre move. Batas used his robust eredar body to drag Jace's Life Drain spell, raised his right arm toward the crystal, and murmured something.

The doomguards fighting with Molofier almost simultaneously looked toward the crystal. Molofier also followed their gazes to look over. The ley line…

An almost unstoppable shock wave struck Jace's consciousness. Under the violent shaking, he had to grab hold of everything he could — staff, stone pillar, wall…

The wall facing west was completely torn open, revealing a massive breach. The ground had already split, held fast by chaotic beams of fel and arcane energy devouring each other, but it could barely still be called an intact great hall.

Beneath the high tower, the panicked shouting of orcs and demons came one after another.

This goddamn eredar — he had definitely recognized Ursolus, and rather than be devoured soul and all, he'd simply detonated the ley line nexus, choosing to perish together with them and destroy his physical body to flee back to the Twisting Nether!

He'd gotten his way. The Batas who had been standing there just moments ago had already vanished.

Countless felhounds and doomguards were flying toward the enormous vortex high in the sky amid the rubble and roof tiles. The remaining demons were also fleeing wildly, putting as much distance as possible between themselves and the Spellbreaker's Hall.

In the center of the great hall, an orc was raising his staff high, struggling to hold on. Between the shattered floor tiles and giant stones beneath his feet, the energy of the ley line nexus was almost completely beyond control…

"Yolin!"

Jace shouted. The orc, his face deathly white from the arcane light, glanced over this way weakly with effort.

"Come here, quick, take it and go!" The orc's other hand held up the demonic tome. "The hall is done for. I have to stay here, or everyone will die with it!"

With that, he hurled the Demonicon over with all his might. Seeing the tome land and nearly fly high into the air, Jace immediately pounced and grabbed it tightly. The book seemed possessed, struggling several more times before it was finally brought under control.

"What about you?!"

"Don't worry about me." DeadEye stared at him. "Today is not my day to die."

"What…" Jace was stunned for a moment.

"What more can you do? I've grown used to scenes like this these past few years!" the orc roared. Jace nodded, gripped Ursolus, clutched the Demonicon to his chest, and leaped out of the tilting great door.
