## Chapter 1272: The Hunting Queen

The corridor ahead had fallen apart — cracks and sheer drops everywhere. Every time Jace leaped a gap, he felt like the Void's pull was going to drag him in whole, and the suction was only getting stronger. More than a few walls and pillars had given up the fight and been sucked into the Void.

"Morophil! Sola!"

Jace shouted, but his voice was pathetically small against a roar that dwarfed any earthquake. He could still feel the thread of the soul-link, which meant Morophil was somewhere close. She was probably fighting the Void's pull too. But Sola?

Right now he could barely keep himself alive, let alone spare the focus to go searching.

Within the thunderous crashing, the nearby walls and floors could give way at any moment. Highspire and the city beneath it were shaking themselves to pieces. Beyond the torn-away ceiling, the sky had turned a terrifying violet-red, a rolling vortex devouring everything without mercy.

Even through that apocalyptic din, Jace could still hear the demons' indistinct whispers — but it wasn't Uthales' voice, nor the frenzy in his own mind. It was the other thing he was carrying.

The Book of Demon Names, its surface flickering with fel light, seemed to be urging him to let go, gnawing away at his mind. The book even seemed to be pushing back against Jace, trying to shove him straight into the Void.

So now Jace wasn't just fighting the vortex's pull — he was fighting the mad struggling of this demonic tome...

A wall ahead collapsed with a roar, rubble and broken stone hurtling straight at him. Jace was about to dodge aside when his footing gave way — and in that instant he felt the drag behind him surge. The demonic ledger seemed to seize the moment and redouble its struggles. His feet left the ground in a stumble...

A sound of shattering stone, then a powerful claw clamped hard around his midsection and waist. A massive green shadow appeared from the side, lowering its head and smashing its golden horns through the wall of debris bearing down on them. With its remaining three talons it clawed frantically at walls and cracks, lunging forward.

"Sola..."

"Jace, I heard your voice, Jace..."

The green dragon was gashed all over with wounds from the doomguards' blades. Her wings were torn to ribbons. She had no time for healing magic, and didn't dare try to fly in that terrifying Void suction. She could only claw her way forward along the broken walls.

After dodging a spinning, collapsing tower, Sola succeeded in carrying Jace out of Highspire and toward the city. She soared over churned-up slopes and cliffs, and when the suction began to ease, she was about to spread her wings and take flight — when a dull, heavy boom suddenly came from behind.

Jace turned his head. A blinding white light swallowed his vision in an instant...

The green dragon's screech, the howls of countless demons — all of it vanished in a flash. Jace felt himself hurled by an irresistible force, his consciousness nearly snapping...

Highspire couldn't hold. In the white light, the shadows, the towers and castle, the ramparts — all of it melted into the Void and came apart at once. When Jace came back to himself, he felt himself tumbling powerless into the sky, the cliff face shrinking below him...

A wind rushed past his ear — a floating island, heavy enough that it had fallen behind the rest.

Jace seized the moment and dashed toward the island with Shadow Step, covering some distance before landing on it, throwing himself at a bent tree trunk and gripping it tight.

The ley line nexus had collapsed. The exposed ley lines had taken physical form, but the local currents had been corrupted by fel energy, showing a ghastly emerald green.

Highspire shattered completely in its wake. The remains of the fortress ahead, the walls, a whole section of the city — all collapsing, breaking apart, shearing off the land and falling into the Void. Deatheye probably couldn't hold on much longer. The magic Sola had used to awaken him wouldn't last forever — not even for a green dragon.

The Book of Demon Names had gotten its wish, finally breaking free of Jace's grip and vanishing. But given the doom of the current situation, that didn't matter anymore.

He had to survive.

Jace made his way to the other side of the island, picked out a floating island that wasn't too far off, backed up a few steps and leaped. When his momentum started to fade he immediately Shadow Stepped, flinging himself forward and grabbing dangling tree roots, hauling himself up onto the cliff edge.

After barely managing to leap across several islands, he felt like he was wasting his effort — he didn't seem to be getting much closer to the cliff ahead.

But he couldn't give up... Alleria had said that some islands, heavy enough, would reach equilibrium with the Void's pull. If he could just find a big enough one...

As he stared ahead desperately looking for the next foothold, the ground beneath his feet suddenly split open — the island was breaking apart! "You motherf—!"

Jace cursed and immediately leaped toward whichever island he could see. But this jump fell woefully short. He immediately used Blood Grasp to seize a large enough boulder on the island above — but his own intense emotion, paired with terrifying shadow power, wrenched the boulder clean out of the ground and sent it flying into the air...

The recoil sent him tumbling head over tail several times before he could figure out which way was up. But the surrounding islands were all too far. Jace still had the energy for Shadow Step, but where the hell was he supposed to go?

Right then, he saw something strange, flickering with lightning, flying straight toward him... wait...

Grede?! Grede!

Jace almost laughed out loud — but the hammer wasn't flying straight at him. He felt himself drifting further and further away, about to lose his grip on it...

A crimson thread pierced the sky instantly, linking the warhammer and the warlock. The lightning-flickering hammer was snatched into his hand.

He drifted toward the vortex a bit further, then the hammer in his hand suddenly erupted with lightning again. Jace gritted his teeth and bore the blast of elemental power, hanging on to the hammer's hilt for dear life — then he was hauled back toward Nagrand by an absurdly powerful force of elemental energy!

Past countless floating islands and debris, past orcs and demons struggling to fly free — Jace felt the pull behind him growing weaker and weaker, until finally he and the hammer crashed into a wide stretch of grassland, rolling several times before coming to a stop.

"Jace!"

Sola turned her head and called out, ecstatic. Grede immediately ran over and pulled Jace to his feet.

"I'm fine, Sola, I'm fine..." Jace raised his head. They were on a not-very-large island. Sola was manipulating countless roots to lash this island tightly to another one, keeping it from drifting further away.

"You goddamn..." Grede shed his silver-gray form, shrinking down, and hugged Jace hard. "I thought you were gonna die!"

"Almost..." Jace asked, "Where's Vereesa?"

"She's down in the city below. Don't worry. She and the imp ran off leading those demons gods-know-where to buy me time to shake that castle apart. They're a lot safer than us right now! But damn... that eredar didn't take the bait. He came out and then went back in!"

"The situation up there... it's too complicated. It's not your fault." Jace gripped the dwarf's shoulder and stood up. "But right now I have to..."

Jace frowned. A pain he could barely endure surged through his whole body — it had to be Morophil being dragged toward the Twisting Nether. Violent Void energy was squeezing and tearing at her body, transmitting through the soul-link into his own.

He didn't hesitate any longer. He drew Azuresong and cut a line across his arm, clutched the blood welling up in his hand and ignited it with shadow fire, then rapidly spoke the demonic incantation.

"Velinthi, Toxintani, Tostasta, Molessfil, Salitolar, Malimasta!"

A Void rift tore open in front of him. Morophil burst out of it and collapsed on the ground, her body covered in hideous cracks and demon blood. One of her four horns had even snapped off.

Seeing she was alive, Jace let out a breath and sat down on the ground, panting hard.

"We have to leave this island and get down into the city to find Vereesa. That city won't hold much longer either — it'll collapse along with everything else soon." Sola asked, "The book, Jace?"

"I lost it. Sorry." Jace shook his head.

"Don't apologize. You being alive is what matters, Jace," Sola said.

"She's right. Lost is lost," Grede waved a hand. "We can just pretend we've still got it. The castle's already smashed to shit — who the hell would know..."

Morophil suddenly retched. Everyone looked over at her in unison. She propped herself up on the grass, arched her back, opened her mouth and let out a low, choked sound.

"What's wrong?" Jace asked.

The demon vomited out a mass of mucus-wrapped violet-red grimoire, slapping it onto the ground, then broke into violent coughing.

She raised her head and smiled. "Well, my master? Nothing escapes Lady Morophil's hunt. Books included."
