Chapter 1279: Return to Shadowmoon Valley

Three days later, Sola carried the group into Shadowmoon Valley. Without stopping to rest, she flew straight for Wildhammer Stronghold. When the massive black walls and ramparts, far away, were just beginning to blend into the earth of Shadowmoon Valley and weren't yet very clear, Jace noticed a rather eye-catching arcane radiance on top of that wall, guiding them in.

That had to be Archmage Khadgar's magic. Wildhammer Stronghold had already detected the return of this very special squad.

The green dragon landed in the open ground at the center of Wildhammer Stronghold. Set against the dwarven fortifications and steam tanks around it, she finally didn't look quite so enormous. But right now, the group Sola was carrying had become the focus of the entire stronghold. The archmage and the Wildhammer overlord couldn't wait to come down from the wall and hurry over.

"You're back faster than I expected, everyone," Khadgar said, looking up and sizing the group over. "I'm glad to see none of you too badly hurt."

Vereesa and Grede exchanged a glance and smiled without saying anything. Jace jumped down from the dragon's back last, pulling the Book of Demon Names from his pack. "We got it, Archmage."

"So you really did get that grimoire," Khadgar said, glancing at the dwar beside him. "Yesterday Turalyon said the demon officer ruling over Auchindoun, 'Unscarred' Omor, had withdrawn from the ruins of Auchindoun, and I said this had to be unusual. Maybe his true name is recorded in here..."

"I think so too, Khadgar. That ugly creature stayed there for so long without any intention of leaving — how could it be a coincidence?" Kurdran Wildhammer said as he stepped closer to the Book of Demon Names.

He looked at its slightly evil, curling, rough cover and drew a breath. "By my beard, this is the very grimoire that every Burning Legion demon in all of Draenor fears? You lot just stole it out of there?"

"Hard to call it stealing, Overlord Kurdran," Grede said with a chuckle, leaning toward the Wildhammer lord. "Though... we were planning to steal it at first."

"You're going to have to tell me the whole story in detail, Grid!" Kurdran said excitedly.

"The air here's terrible, everyone. Let's talk back inside the fortress," Khadgar said, waving them in.

The soldiers above and below the wall whispered to each other, as if curious about what these people had brought back. Jace clearly heard some of them mention "demon names" or "a treasure that can frighten demons." It seemed the leaders of the Sons of Lothar had already briefed the stronghold's soldiers on the goal of Jace and the others' trip to Nagrand.

The Expedition's internal affairs were indeed remarkably transparent. Given the immense pressure they faced from the Burning Legion, these elite soldiers had to rely on and trust each other. There was no room for secrets. Khadgar, Turalyon and their leaders didn't set themselves up as rulers lording over everyone either.

Entering the fortress and arriving at the command room with the Draenor map laid out, Jace walked up to the table and placed his hand on the Nagrand position of the Draenor map, which had been modified many times. Pointing at the "Ogre Highspire" marker on it, he said: "This place doesn't anymore, Archmage Khadgar, Overlord Kurdran."

"Doesn't exist," Khadgar said, his eyes widening slightly. "You mean it's fallen into the Void? According to our observations recently, this world's collapse has entered a relatively stable phase, unless..."

"Yes, Archmage," Vereesa said. "To stop us from getting this grimoire, the demons blew up Highspire's ley line nexus. Jace and the others nearly didn't make it out alive."

"Does that mean the Legion teleportation gate in Nagrand can no longer take shape?" Khadgar asked. "And that eredar, Ranger-Lord?"

Vereesa said, "The eredar lord's name was Bathas. He was indeed a special envoy Kil'jaeden had sent to monitor Magtheridon. But learning this intelligence a bit late is a bit late — he's already been torn apart by the Void's power."

"That's truly rare good news!" Kurdran said. "This way, even if we can't go back for a while, at least Alleria's Hold doesn't have to worry about danger coming from the west anymore. A demon legion is a hell of a lot more dangerous than Gorduni ogres and brown-skinned orcs!"

"Yes..." Khadgar nodded. "Since the Great Catastrophe, this should be the most important victory we've gained. Every Legion teleportation gate we tear down means a little more safety for us."

"I have to say," Vereesa exchanged glances with several of the companions beside her, "on this Highspire operation, those brown-skinned orcs who call themselves 'Mag'har' helped us a great deal."

"Mag'har — the Uncorrupted?" Khadgar asked.

"Yes," Jace said. "They take pride in never having drunk the demons' blood and invading through the Dark Portal alongside the Horde's Legion, and they are likewise enemies of the Burning Legion. It's just that they aren't very numerous. Kilrogg Deatheye's son Yorlin Deatheye... nearly sacrificed himself protecting us on this operation. Without his help, we couldn't have broken the ward that demon lord had set up over Highspire either."

"How could Kilrogg Deatheye have a son like that?" Kurdran asked, disbelieving. "That orc is a cunning, vicious one-eyed snake — a complete lunatic!"

"I don't find it strange. On one hand, the corruption of Mannoroth's blood can change a lot. On the other... orcs aren't dwarves, Overlord," Khadgar said, looking at the orc sightings marked on the Nagrand map. "Since the new chief of the Bleeding Hollow orcs has clearly expressed his position, then we must reconsider our relationship with them as well."

"Next," he set his gaze on the evil grimoire beside Jace's hand. "We should talk about this book. Do you know how to use its power, Jace?"

Frankly, Jace had no idea how to use the immense magic contained in this book. In the game, the process by which the demon hunter Varethis drew its power was only briefly described in the story.

The story mentioned that he infiltrated a Shadow Council vault, tried to take the book but couldn't counter the Shadow Council's sealing ward, and could only secretly siphon some of its power and leave. The whole process didn't mention what incantation or ritual he used.

"I only know that this book's mana can be drawn from, and that one can gain the ability to see through demons from the process," Jace said with a helpless shake of his head. "Sorry, Archmage Khadgar. The more detailed stuff, I don't know either — after all, not even the Shadow Council's members know much about it."

Khadgar stared at the grimoire, narrowed his eyes, and after a thought looked at Kurdran. "We need to find a safer place to see what this book is really about, Overlord."

He looked around the hall. "This place isn't very suitable for this level of magical experimentation."

"Suit yourself," Kurdran said, propping both hands on the table and looking across at Grede. "Young lad of House Thunderfist, tell me the whole process — starting from when you arrived at Highspire! It has to be detailed down to every single hammer throw!"

"Actually, Overlord, I never went inside Highspire," Grede said. "Me and this Ranger-Lord were in charge of the bloodbath outside. That warlock and that orc handled the shady business..."

"I went in too, Grid," Sola said unhappily.

"Lady Dragon," Kurdran said, looking at Sola. "We need to have a chat."

"What, does the part about killing the mighty Gronn lord 'Durn' still need mentioning, Overlord?" Grede asked with a smug expression.

"What?!" Kurdran's braids stood straight up in shock. Even Khadgar, who had already picked up his staff and was preparing to leave, looked back in surprise.

"You killed a Gronn lord?" the archmage asked Jace in disbelief.

"We helped the Bleeding Hollow orcs kill one of Gul'dan's sons," Jace said. "In exchange for help from their young chief."

Khadgar rubbed his white beard. "The way you mention this is no different from catching a Bloodthistle boar outside Goldshire."

Jace smiled sheepishly. "It turns out that if he hadn't found a Highspire artifact that could control ley line power in the lair of Gul'dan's son, the difficulty of us getting this grimoire would've been higher by god-knows-how-many times."

Khadgar raised an eyebrow slightly. "Don't tell me it was the Felwaste Staff?"

"You know about that thing?" Jace asked in surprise.

"You don't know how much varied information — credible and incredible alike — I gathered from the draenei, orcs and ogres to fight the destruction of Draenor, Jace," Khadgar said with a wry smile. "But I thought the Gorian Felwaste Staff had already been brought north to Gorgrond by Highspire's deserters. I had no idea it was still in Nagrand. Of course, given how well we know Nagrand, we don't even know whether that staff was just a legend. But if I'd known your plan was to wipe out all of Highspire..."

"...I should have told you long ago."

"No need for that now. Come with me, Jace," the archmage said. "I'll take you up to my mage tower... though it's hardly a real mage tower. But I've set up some simple magic wards inside to counter the erosion of dark magic. We can study this grimoire carefully there. Remember to bring your demon servants along."
