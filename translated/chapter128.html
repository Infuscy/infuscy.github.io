Chapter 128: The Great Baradin Conqueror

The day of departure had finally arrived.

Jace had told the imp to watch the pocket watch and wake him at five. But he'd woken up past four on his own — he'd gone to bed early the night before, and he had something critically important to do today. No way he was sleeping in.

He stepped out with his luggage, checked the lock over and over to make sure it was secure, then woke his landlord before leaving to remind him once again to keep an eye on the rental and not re-let it behind his back before the rent was due. Only then did he head for the harbor.

Dawn was coming later and later. At five in the morning, the sky was still the color of blue-black ink, and the streets were empty except for drowsy watch guards.

But once he reached the docks, everything was a different story.

Even though it was barely dawn, the Stormwind harbor was already packed. No wonder the Alliance would need to expand the port when they launched the northern campaign later on.

Then again, probably also because the Winter Veil was approaching. Even someone like Jace, who rarely had business at the harbor, was squeezing into the port crowd at a time like this, wasn't he?

Unlike the uniform Stormwind accents on the usual streets, the harbor was full of humans speaking in accents from everywhere — Lordaeronian ones dominated, but there were Kul Tiran ones too.

Completely unlike the game map, Kul Tiras was actually closer to Stormwind than Lordaeron. Its island sat in the waters just beyond Baradin Bay, and from here it was only about a week by ship to Boralus.

Even so, he rarely saw Kul Tirans. This time he spotted several Kul Tiran soldiers in green, a golden anchor insignia on their shoulders. Big men, broad-chested and broad-shouldered like a pack of hyenas, shouting to each other in Common that was barely intelligible.

Out beyond the harbor, several large ships were circling — the docks were nearly full and they couldn't come in. The dockmasters seemed to be urging the sailors to set sail quickly and make room for the ships behind them.

Jace threaded through the crowd, craning his neck for a glimpse of the so-called "Great Baradin Conqueror." If Grede was to be believed, this was a fairly large ship — it should stand out.

He was looking far ahead and ignoring his immediate surroundings when he tripped over something slippery and nearly went sprawling. He looked down — seemed he'd just stepped on and killed a fish. Somewhere a middle-aged man was cursing up a storm, but the crowd quickly blocked Jace's view, and there was no telling where the voice had come from.

Just as he was checking whether his shoes had sustained any damage, a powerful hand suddenly grabbed his cloak…

"Shit!"

Jace yanked the hood back over his neck. He'd almost thought some thief was going to snatch the most expensive thing on his person — but when he turned around, it was Grede. "Hey, lad, lost?"

Grede was wearing a dark brown leather outer garment, his arms, wrists, and waist bound with heavy metal chains. On his head he wore a pale greenish-yellow headband, pulling his dark-brown-nearly-black hair back into a ponytail.

Jace had never seen him in an outfit like this. It was a different style from before — different from every other dwarf in all of Stormwind, for that matter. For a moment, he almost didn't recognize him.

Grede saw the look of surprise on his face and said, "Handsome, isn't it? This is the outfit I brought from Aerie Peak, Jace. I may have left the knights, but they never took this gear back. I'm going to wear this Wildhammer battle-garb all the way back to the Hinterlands and remind them I exist."

"Distinctive, alright," Jace nodded.

"Come on," Grede said. "That Baradin whatever is over there, not far."

He was right. The Great Baradin Conqueror was berthed in a small cove at the northernmost end of the harbor. It was indeed a large, dark-brown ship, its name carved at the bow in ancient runic script that looked like some kind of magical rune.

When Grede and Jace arrived, several burly dockworkers were trudging up the gangplank one step at a time, carrying a large crate covered in cloth and ropes. But more eye-catching than the crate, to Jace, was the little creature screaming at them from below — a goblin.

It was no exaggeration to say this was the first time he'd seen a goblin up close. The appearance of this little creature instantly lent the whole plain, medieval-style ramshackle dock a fantastical flair.

"Fuck!" The goblin pointed at the panting workers and bellowed in perfectly fluent Common. "If your king didn't keep ogres out of the city, why the hell would I pay you idiots? If you dare break anything belonging to the guests, you're dead — Stormwind law doesn't explicitly say trolls aren't allowed in! Trolls not only have more strength than you pasty lot, they're not picky eaters either."

The goblin was slightly shorter than a gnome, but the proportions of his head looked even more exaggerated — mainly because of those long ears jutting high up and back, and that wildly voluminous red hair. His green skin was rough and dry all over. A pair of sallow yellow eyes darted around restlessly, looking here, looking there. When he talked, thick spittle flew from his mouth, and his scrawny limbs flailed about like a startled mouse.

"Faster! Faster! Faster! Can't you hear that bald bastard rushing us? We're setting sail in two hours and the guests' cargo isn't even loaded yet. I swear, you big idiots…"

But the moment Jace and Grede approached, the goblin glanced at the Wildhammer dwarf and coughed, awkwardly.

"Ah, are you two guests of the Great Baradin Conqueror?" The goblin smiled, his eyes nearly disappearing into the green wrinkles of his face. "Just give your names and you can board! We guarantee we'll set sail within two hours, and we won't leave behind a single piece of our distinguished guests' luggage. Our service quality is known from Booty Bay to Wintervale — everyone knows us!"

"Jace Sesso."

"Oh, let me see — Mr. Jace Sesso, welcome!!"

"Grede."

"Grede… what?"

"Just Grede. What else?"

The goblin scratched his head. "Grede isn't exactly an uncommon dwarf name, Mr. Grede. I need your family name, otherwise if I let you slip through I can't take the responsibility!"

Grede frowned. "Thunderfist. Grede Thunderfist."

"Master Grede of House Thunderfist, right here!" The goblin pointed at the manifest in his hand. "Name's Gazzik, first mate of this ship. I handle all the crap the captain can't be bothered with. We'll be spending the next half month together — wonderful to meet you both! Please, board!"

The two of them followed the crew carrying the big crate up the gangplank. Jace saw the goblin's smiling face turn dark again in an instant as he went back to berating the workers. He looked at Grede, who was walking with his head down, and asked, "Even I've heard of House Thunderfist of Aerie Peak. If you hadn't brought it up on this trip, I never would have heard you mention it."

"Nothing worth mentioning," Grede said, scanning the deck. "No matter how famous your family name is, it stops ringing when there are hundreds of your kin scattered across the world — especially when you're the most useless one in that whole bewildering crowd of cousins."

Translator Notes

Flags

- 伟大的巴拉丁征服者号 = the Great Baradin Conqueror: Ship name. 巴拉丁 = Baradin (Baradin Bay is in the glossary); 征服者 = Conqueror. - 雷拳 = Thunderfist: Dwarven family name. Rendering tentative — could also be rendered as Thunderfist or another "Thunder-" compound. - 加齐克 = Gazzik: Goblin first mate's name. Rendering tentative. - 热砂财团 = the Trade Cartel: Goblin commercial faction. 热砂 is associated with the Steamwheedle Cartel in lore; rendered as the generic Trade Cartel. - 暮冬镇 = Wintervale: Town name at the other end of the route. Rendering tentative; not a canonical WoW location to my knowledge. - 冬幕节 = Winter Veil: WoW holiday, not in the glossary. - 狮子王旗 = the lion king's banner: The royal standard of the House of Wrynn (Stormwind). - 蛮锤战衣 = Wildhammer battle-garb: Grede's Wildhammer clan outfit. - 鹰巢山 = Aerie Peak: Canonical Wildhammer location, not in the glossary. - 库尔提拉斯士兵徽记 = golden anchor insignia: Kul Tiras military emblem.
