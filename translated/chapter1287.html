Chapter 1287: A Solid Step

Though Jace had gotten past Turalyon by cooking up the excuse of preparing a demon ritual circle, it was time to actually start making preparations.

When communicating with Karlez back in Auchindoun, the portal linking to Ilgalath Tower hadn't disappeared yet — meaning the distance barrier to the ritual circle had likely been weakened somewhat by the portal's existence. Much like how Sazeno had been able to use the elemental plane rift held open by Krasus to channel Karlez into Onyxia's flame traversal passage.

In other words, here in Wildhammer Keep, the communication circle linking to Karlez might require more soul shards as well as stronger demon blood.

Fortunately, neither was in short supply in Shadowmoon Valley. And now that Jace himself could produce soul shards, things were much more convenient.

Over the next few days, Jace went out almost every day to the orc or draenei ruins near Wildhammer Keep that had been ravaged by demons, searching for fel-corrupted beasts or wandering felhounds, draining soul shards from them and collecting demon blood.

Sola stayed at Wildhammer Keep treating the wounded soldiers brought back by Turalyon's force, while Vereesa accompanied Jace on the "hunts." With her by their side, the corrupted things prowling through the blighted forests were hardly a threat.

Whether it was a massive phase hunter or a furious hell boar covered in spikes like demon horns, none of them could withstand a single one of her arrows.

And so a patrol mission that was exceptionally dangerous for the soldiers of Wildhammer Keep had become tedious work for the two of them. Every day they brought the fel-tainted blood and soul shards back to the basement of Khadgar's mage tower, then directed the soldiers to go retrieve the corpses — these chores were even more troublesome than killing those dangerous creatures.

Still, the two of them hadn't mentioned anything about going back at night for a few rounds again these past few days. Whenever Vereesa and Jace made eye contact, she couldn't help but look away, and Jace felt plenty guilty himself when he recalled the big talk he'd dished out that day.

Two days after the ritual circle was completed, at noon, Jace was in the keep reading a record of the Farallon Plains by a Kirin'var Village mage — also one Khadgar had left in the mage tower.

In the level-70 Burning Crusade version, the Farallon Plains had been torn into a vast swath of purple wasteland by the void energy of the Staff of Sargeras. The entire landmass had shattered into floating islands, barely kept from drifting completely into the depths of the Void by their own gravity.

The Burning Legion and the Sunfury led by Kael'thas had occupied the area, building massive numbers of fel camps and manaforges, fighting for control of the void energy and the land.

The naaru vessel in which A'dal and his followers had arrived in Draenor, docked there, had also been seized by Kael'thas Sunstrider, becoming the Sunfury's "palace" in Outland.

Jace had originally thought that by the Warlords of Draenor expansion, he'd get to see pre-shattered Farallon thanks to the alternate-universe setup, but in the end, for one reason or another, he never got to see it.

So that day, when he was wandering around Khadgar's little library and spotted this record, he couldn't resist pulling it out and flipping through it.

The record described Farallon as a vast island adrift on a isolating sea, comparable in size to all of Shadowmoon Valley. Between the northern and southern mountain ranges lay a seemingly tranquil great plain — quiet and gloomy at night, and dim as dusk throughout the entire day.

The cause of this was likely the strange magical energy sources spread across the entire island, which had also attracted countless magical creatures and elementals to settle there. As a result, though the plain appeared peaceful and harmonious on the surface, it was actually brimming with danger.

Even the natives of Draenor — whether orcs or ogres — could only settle as much as possible between the northern and southern mountains. Only the draenei, who came later, had dared to venture deep into it and establish outposts, attempting to forge a friendly connection with the volatile magic here.

Out of curiosity about these magical energy sources, the Kirin Tor mages who came to Draenor had organized a mage expedition to Farallon as soon as they arrived, and built a village near the southern coast, naming it Kirin'var.

However, that village was frequently raided by orcs, causing the Kirin Tor's Farallon investigation plans to be continuously obstructed. And before a few years had passed, the power of the Staff of Sargeras had completely torn apart all of Draenor, and further surveying of Farallon had been thoroughly sunk.

The author of this record, Ravendrath, believed that the reason Farallon had been completely destroyed was likely because the terrifying magical energy stored locally had produced some catastrophic resonance with the Staff of Sargeras's power. But at the same time, because this magical energy hadn't completely escaped along with Farallon's collapse, it had formed terrible void storms that trapped the battered land within, preventing it from falling entirely into the Void like other fragile places.

Reading about how the mages in the record had survived a catastrophe far more violent and horrifying than the doomsday on the southern continent, using whatever resources were at hand — and even managed to preserve most of the village — Jace couldn't help but feel genuine admiration.

Yet all that effort couldn't withstand a single mana bomb from the future Kael'thas. In order to completely seize the residual magic of the Netherstorm, the prince, having lost his reason, had easily destroyed Kirin'var, killing the vast majority of the surviving mages and turning the village into a dead zone haunted by mana specters and wrathful spirits.

He wondered if these mages would be able to leave the Netherstorm safely, cross the Blade's Edge Mountains, reach Terokkar Forest, and link up with the garrison at Alleria's Hold.

Khadgar was still fairly optimistic about this. The leader of Kirin'var Village, Archmage Vargoth, was an outstanding scholar and explorer. They weren't facing the threat of Kael'thas and the entire Sunfury, and the greatest danger along the way was nothing more than gronn who liked to hide in caves. As for the black dragons, on one hand they were still searching for their missing leader Sabellian, and on the other they feared gronn hunters — they probably wouldn't take the initiative to provoke members of the Expedition.

So compared to the mages of Kirin'var Village, the Expedition's leaders were actually more worried about the evacuation of Honor Hold, since they were locked in a standoff with the orc legions of Hellfire Citadel at all times.

Just as Jace was worrying about whether Grede and Khadgar, riding griffins across the Bone Wastes, could avoid the black dragons' patrols and reach Auchindoun, a deep voice suddenly struck at the thoughts.

"Jace… Sesso!"

During the Shattrath ordeal some time ago, Jace had heard far too many demonic whispers — from the Tome of Fel Names to Ursales taking turns. This sudden, unprepared shout jolted him all over.

It was the imp's voice. Jace had told Grede to have the imp shout for him once they reached their position. Looked like the time had come.

He turned his head and looked toward Turalyon, who was sitting on the other side in conversation with Kurdran. "They're there."

Hearing Jace's words, the entire keep hall fell silent. The Wildhammer lord and the paladin almost rose to their feet at the same time. "Let's go."

Everyone followed Jace quickly through the corridor to the basement of Khadgar's mage tower. Vereesa and Sola, having heard the news, had also rushed over. Morphel was already waiting in front of the demon ritual circle. Under the illumination of the faintly glowing shadow light from below, the demoness guarding the ritual circle really did look exactly like a terrifying queen deep in a dungeon.

Turalyon glanced up at her with some discomfort, and like Kurdran, could only force himself to lower his head and pretend this demon didn't exist. Morphel also restrainedly dimmed the fel patterns burning across her body, giving each other a little face. Everyone knew what mattered most right now.

Jace came to the ritual circle, picked up the scroll placed beside it, and carefully chanted the lengthy activation incantation written on it, tossing soul shards onto the circle one by one as he went.

As he'd expected, activating the circle this time consumed far more than the last time in Auchindoun. It wasn't until he'd tossed the fifth soul shard that he finally felt he'd established contact with Karlez.

"Mmrgh, Master, why do you call?"

Jace had never imagined that one day he'd feel a sense of relief upon hearing Karlez's voice. But regardless, this was a voice from Azeroth — even if it was tinged with a trace of contempt and discontent.

"Open the portal wide, Karlez. Someone's going back. Thank you very much." Jace said in demonic, eyes closed.

With every word he spoke, he distinctly felt the drain on his mana. Guiding this ritual was tremendously demanding — he couldn't afford to let his focus slip for even a moment. No wonder that in Dire Maul, Dasomora had to rely on a highborne energy tower to sustain this demon communication ritual. God only knew which corner of the Twisting Nether Karlez had been communicating with her from back then.

"What's going on here?" Kurdran couldn't help but ask.

Turalyon shook his head. The others could only shake their heads too. If Jace were to explain in Common right now, it might disrupt the continuity of the ritual, so he could only keep silent.

"The portal will open soon," Morphel said in Common. "My master must concentrate on maintaining the communication now. Please keep quiet."

Kurdran cleared his throat and said nothing more. Turalyon said, "Thank you very much, demon."

Morphel gave a graceful, slight nod to the paladin. The scene was so surreal that everyone present widened their eyes in shock.

At that moment, Jace felt the energy of his ritual waver as if being pulled by something. Not long after, Karlez's voice came through again.

"A human mage has just arrived here successfully. Amy Marin calls him 'Archmage Khadgar.' Is this the one you want to teleport, Jace Sesso?"

"That's right, that's right." Jace took a deep breath, suppressing his excitement. "That's him. Thank you, Karlez. Thank you very much."

"When can we eliminate Lord Kazzak?"

"I'll make my move as soon as I see him. Stay patient, Karlez. If we're right, Kil'jaeden will very likely want to send another commander here — and the odds of it being Kazzak himself are very high, don't you think?"

"Mmrgh."

This time Karlez's response had actually calmed down quite a bit, as if he truly believed Jace's pitch.

"Per the orders you left behind, I must relay what the people here want to tell you… Khadgar sends his midday greetings. He says he'll set out for Stormwind immediately. Also, Amy Marin wants me to tell you she's very glad you're doing well."

"Tell her I miss her too, and ask her to pass a message along to my parents — tell them I'm still alive," Jace said.

"Understood." The demon acknowledged.

"Then I'll end the ritual," Jace said.

"End it…"

With Karlez's final low voice fading from his mind, Jace released his hands, riding the void force channeled by the circle. The demon blood at his feet burned dry in an instant. The soul shards on the ground completely crumbled to powder. The room fell quiet, leaving only a sharp, charred stench.

"How did it go, Jace?" Turalyon asked.

"Done." Jace turned his head. "Khadgar has returned to Azeroth."

"By the ancestors!" Kurdran roared and threw a heavy punch at Turalyon's shoulder. Turalyon also smiled and gripped the dwarf king's shoulder. Vereesa beside them burst into tears and embraced Sola tightly.

Jace also let out a long breath. The tension of this period could finally ease up a little.

The most solid step had been taken. All that remained now was to hold on here and help all the Expedition members work with Khadgar and the Kirin Tor to explore a way to return to Azeroth together.
