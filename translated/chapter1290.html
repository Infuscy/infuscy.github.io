Chapter 1290: The Void's Gaze

The three of them rushed back to the keep. Fortunately, the earthquake hadn't lasted long — it was just that the thick clouds over all of Shadowmoon Valley seemed to have been frightened by something, churning and rolling.

Wildhammer Keep didn't seem to have suffered much damage, but everyone here had sensed that something was amiss. Quite a few soldiers were busy rushing in and out, and the griffin riders in the sky were hurrying back one after another.

The anxious cries of the griffin beasts overhead couldn't help but make Jace worry about Grede's situation. Even if he didn't stop at Alleria's Hold and came back at full speed, it should still take four or five days.

Vereesa grabbed an officer maintaining order and asked, "What on earth happened?"

"We don't know either, Ranger-Lieutenant!" The dwarven officer saluted. "Many mages and elves have been injured, some quite seriously. The outposts have had a similar situation… some of the magical implements in the infirmaries malfunctioned — some exploded, some went out of control. It wasn't just mages; a lot of warriors got hurt too. So Lord High Thane Kurdran ordered us to immediately isolate all magical items that still have power!"

"Where's Marshal Turalyon?" Jace asked.

"Our griffin riders have already gone to the front to find him, sir," the officer answered honestly.

Jace nodded. He turned to look toward the slopes and plains to the east and north, confirming there were no demon or orc legions appearing and no doomguard blotting out the sky before he relaxed a little — this didn't seem like the prelude to a demon all-out offensive.

But what on earth was going on? Was this related to the Burning Legion?

All magical items… what about the Flowing Ice Crystal?

Jace felt at his waist — at least the crystal was still lying quietly in its small box, showing no signs of anything amiss. At that moment, his gaze turned toward the mage tower standing beside the keep.

"You go help out first, Wen. I'll go find Morphel and ask what's going on," Jace said.

"All right." Vereesa glanced toward the infirmary. "Be careful, Jace."

Jace gave an "mm," gripped his staff, crossed the yard in the middle, dodging the soldiers rushing back and forth, and headed toward the keep.

High above, the purple-blue anomaly flickering between the torn black clouds was as clear as a spider's web, pressing down on the land oppressively, making it hard to breathe.

The tremors waxed and waned, and the ground emitted an intermittent humming in response. Though the grand keep forged by the Wildhammer dwarves sat firmly nestled against the cliff, the people busy on the wall, in front and behind the towers, and in the streets swayed and staggered, appearing so fragile before this unknown, ground-shaking force.

Just as he was about to enter the keep's gate, Jace noticed an ominous flash of light from the top of the mage tower overhead — the room where Khadgar had inscribed countless protective runes. His heart seemed to skip a beat.

A buzzing like tinnitus exploded. The top of the mage tower was blown to pieces by an unseen force. A shockwave that distorted the air spread out, and large chunks of rubble scattered like bullets!

Jace was flung to the ground by the force bearing down on him. For a moment, cries of pain and curses rang throughout the keep. People fled into the bunkers, using the steam tanks as cover. Rubble and debris poured everywhere, venting the fury of chaotic magic. Quite a few nearby towers and wooden structures were struck by the force and collapsed along with it…

Was that the arcane crystal from Dalaran…?

Just as his thoughts were blown into disarray, a refreshing, fragrant scent suddenly drove away all the charred stench of fel fire, swiftly enveloping and purifying his mind. Streams of starlight rose alongside a pleasant ringing, like some strange melody.

In the center of the yard, Sola held the Staff of Domination high with one hand, channeling a powerful Tranquility. Ysera's pendant at her chest released the iridescence of the Dream. In an instant, all the panic, anger, and helplessness permeating the entire keep settled down.

The Tranquility continued, and Jace rallied his spirit and strength and climbed back to his feet. He looked toward Sola in the distance and nodded his thanks. Sola nodded back in relief, but didn't let up on the powerful nature magic she was channeling.

Turning his head again, the mage tower before him had been blown apart by the unstable magic like a breech-loading dwarven handgun, leaving only the lower half, battered and askew.

If Khadgar were here, he'd definitely figure out what happened. No — perhaps it was precisely because Khadgar wasn't here anymore that all this had been set off.

When this thought surfaced in Jace's mind, a powerful sense of foreboding immediately rushed to his brain.

The force of the explosion at the top of the mage tower hadn't been able to destroy the solid lower part — after all, this was part of the keep's main fortress structure. Jace made his way to the basement. Morphel was here clearing away all the rubble, trying to reorganize the entire room.

Jace didn't have time to ask her what was happening. He immediately set about repairing the communication ritual circle with the demon blood stored here, chanting the incantation to activate the ritual and tossing soul shards to inject energy into the circle.

One, one, one…

As he chanted, he felt the back of his neck growing tighter and tighter — until Karlez's low voice rang by his ear, and he felt as if he'd been saved, his whole body nearly collapsing.

"What happened, Karlez?"

"The teleport… rift, Master, no longer…" The great demon's voice was fragmented, barely intelligible. "The teleport failed?!"

The moment Jace shouted those words, he felt a massive drain of energy. The ritual circle at his feet also burned rapidly. Before the doom lord on the other end could respond, the last few soul shards couldn't hold on any longer.

By now he was drenched in sweat. Shadow energy from the Twisting Nether, far too powerful, was bombarding his consciousness. He wanted so badly to say a few more words to figure out the situation that he neglected just how much he'd strengthened this communication ritual circle, and ignored the pain of the backlash.

But Jace knew very well that no matter how difficult activating this ritual circle was right now, at least communication was still possible — it just required more mana, more souls, and more demon blood…

He felt his body, then looked toward the black barrel of viscous demon blood in the corner. No — this was exactly when he needed to calm down. The shadow was agitating his restlessness. He had to control his emotions and restore his reason.

After catching his breath for a moment, Morphel's voice came by his ear. "Master…"

Her burning eyes were fixed on him. The succubus said nothing more, but Jace knew from his soul that she was thinking the same thing he was.

Leaving the mage tower and coming outside, the continuous earthquake had stopped. The unknown buzzing outside seemed to have gradually come to an end too. Jace only then realized that this hell brimming with fel fire and fissures could be this quiet — quiet as if it were completely dead.

The "blockade" of this shattered world had intensified — or perhaps it was only at this moment that Jace confirmed the blockade he'd always suspected was real.

He raised his head and looked toward the dark sky. The ink-black clouds were re-mixing, and Shadowmoon Valley had returned to its usual evil and gloom. Is that you, Kil'jaeden?
