Chapter 1294: Divvying Up the Work

The repairs to Wildhammer Stronghold and the relief efforts for the wounded proceeded methodically. At the same time, as signs of fel orc activity around the stronghold increased, the gryphon riders stepped up their patrols, and both the stronghold itself and the nearby outposts went on higher alert.

But in the following days, the Burning Legion made no moves.

Everyone was on edge. Some soldiers almost wished the demons and orcs would just attack while the stronghold was damaged, instead of waiting. But the only answer they got was the rumbling roar of Shadowmoon Valley.

This didn't surprise Turalyon or Jace. The eerier the stillness, the more likely it meant the Burning Legion was preparing something.

Maybe, right now, the summoning ritual for a new demon lord was underway. More and more demon soldiers were flooding into Draenor through the portals at Karabor and Hellfire Peninsula. A new offensive was brewing, free from the magical control of the Book of Demon Names.

For the moment, though, Wildhammer Stronghold's scouts hadn't spotted anything new from the demons. The messengers from Kirin'Var Village and Honor Hold hadn't arrived yet either. Everyone in the stronghold was both tense and anxiously waiting for good news from afar.

An elite squad of Farstriders had already departed on dragonhawks alongside two elven arcanists, heading north to find the draenei leader, the Prophet Velen, and the leader of Kirin'Var Village, Archmage Vargoth.

Jace also hoped Vargoth would have a different perspective on this magical barrier. After all, the source of the lockdown might well be Netherstorm — that is, the shattered Farahlon. Eventually, Prince Kael'thas would build several huge mana forges there to siphon power for his fleet's citadel, which meant he must have had a reason.

The Farstriders were already moving, so Jace couldn't just sit around either. He had an equally important task.

The forest Turalyon had mentioned, near the Skettis peaks where black dragons were sighted — if he flew there by gryphon, it would take two or three days. Round trip, a week. If Sora took him, it should be about half that.

Of course, Jace wanted to bring Sora not just for the shorter travel time, but because showing up with an actual dragon would be more appropriate for negotiating with black dragons.

After the magical barrier fell, the green dragon had been helping the paladins and priests treat the soldiers and mages injured in the catastrophe, then slept for two full days. At noon, Jace finally saw her leave her quarters and head toward the stronghold. He grabbed the pre-prepared smoked clefthoof jerky and followed.

"Sora. Sora."

"Jace? You look like you're in pretty good shape." Sora turned around.

"Those falling rocks couldn't touch me. And with your magic, I feel healthier than before." Jace patted his arm.

The green dragon reached out, lifted Jace's eyelids one by one to examine his eyes, then said softly, "But the mages in the stronghold were all injured to some degree."

"Well, that's just my arcane affinity — so bad even Amy wouldn't want it." Jace rubbed his fingers. "Wearing a Wizard Sanctum ring doesn't make me a mage."

He rubbed for a while and found nothing. Then he remembered he'd given the ring to Ursula before coming to Draenor. This time, he really had nothing left to qualify as a mage, inside or out.

He casually handed her the paper-wrapped package. "Take a look at this."

"Is this the smoked jerky the orcs from Garadar gave us?" Sora opened the package in surprise. "Those orcs really do use distinctive spices. You can smell it right away."

"Sora and I have been saving it for you," Jace said. "The other day when I saw how much you liked it..."

Sora's eyes narrowed happily, then she gave him a suspicious look. "You need something from me, don't you?"

"It's not exactly my thing," Jace said. "Turalyon asked me to take care of a task earlier. It'll be hard to finish without your help."

"Hmm?" Sora said. "What kind of task can't be done without me..."

"A few of us think we should establish contact with the black dragons and fight the demon threat together," Jace said.

The green dragon's expression shifted slightly at the word "black dragon." She said, "Turalyon wants to ally with the Blackwing against the demons... I heard some rumors earlier. I didn't expect you to actually reach a consensus on it. So, you want me to go meet them with you."

"Right," Jace said. "Turalyon thinks I'm the best person for it."

"That paladin is absolutely right." Sora said. "I'll go, Jace. If I weren't there and you went to meet them alone, I couldn't rest easy in the stronghold anyway."

"Thanks a lot, Sora." Jace pressed his palms together.

"So... do you have any more of that jerky?" The green dragon licked her fingers.

"Uh." Jace said. "No."

"Then I'll just have to be a little mean to my purple-winged cousins," Sora said, feigning helplessness.

"Hard to imagine their attitude being any better when they're forced to catch bugs in the forest every day to eat..." Jace said.

Sora grabbed Jace's cloak and tugged it. "Remember not to wear this when we go. Otherwise, it won't just be a matter of 'attitude.'"

"Ah... right." Jace grabbed his cloak.

Just then, a commotion rose from the outer walls. It sounded like gryphon riders had returned.

When they reached the stronghold gate, three gryphons had already landed on the wall. Kurdran was running up to the battlements with a few gryphon keepers. Could it be Grede coming back?

"Grede!" Jace shouted. A big black head immediately leaned over the wall.

"Hey, Jace!" The dwarf waved and bellowed. "You alright?!"

Sora and Jace exchanged a look. So Grede had also been through the earthquake a few days ago.

Grede came bounding down the wall stairs, grabbed Jace in a hug, and patted him on the back. He greeted Sora, then looked up at the shattered mage tower. "Alleria's Keep is about the same. Damn it, what the hell happened?"

Jace briefed Grede on what they'd figured out so far. Grede muttered, "Ah, hell. Those demons really have every trick in the book. How do you cover a whole landmass like this?"

Jace glanced at the bag on the dwarf's belt that held his imp. "What does it think?"

"It thinks it's eredar magic," Grede said. "According to it, it's the Deceiver Kil'jaeden himself! The little guy's so scared he can barely talk. Do your confidence thing and cheer him up."

The dwarf shoved the cloth bag into Jace's hands. "Considering how much he helped me and that Wildhammer brother escape Auchindoun, I'll overlook him talking up the enemy and talking down our side..."

"So, uh, is it really the Deceiver?"

Grede's tone sounded casual, but it was only a front. Jace could tell that even a dwarf couldn't stay totally calm when Kil'jaeden was involved.

"We still don't know for sure," Jace said. "But what I can say is... even if he didn't personally make this magic happen, he's the one who ordered it."

Grede nodded. "I figured. If we want to get the expedition back to Azeroth, killing one eredar head sorcerer and one gronn king's son isn't enough. We still have to go toe-to-toe with someone important."

"Let's try not to go 'toe-to-toe' with him if we can help it," Jace said.

"Grede!"

Vereesa came running over from a distance, unable to hide her urgency. "How's Alleria?"

"She's fine, little elf." Grede shouted. "That keep has two mage towers. One was badly damaged, the other can still hold up. Either way, neither got hit as bad as this tower of Khadgar's..."

"When I first landed, I thought Jace Sesso might have lost a leg at least. Never expected to see him still walking on his own."

Vereesa's worried expression vanished instantly. She folded her arms irritably and leaned against the wall. "Shame the disaster didn't blow that mouth of yours off."

"To blow this mouth off, the Burning Legion would need a few hundred more pounds of explosives!"

Grede finished shouting at the ranger, then let his eyelids droop and heaved a long sigh, as if ninety percent of his strength had been released in that one breath.

"We were helping with the rescue at Alleria's Keep, dealing with out-of-control magical artifacts, then coming back day and night — that brother and I haven't slept in three days... Good to see you two safe! Goodnight. No, see you tomorrow!"

Jace nodded. The dwarf raised a hand and trudged off toward the stronghold, looking like he was already sleepwalking.

"Are we waiting for him to wake up before heading out to find the black dragons?" Vereesa asked.

"Let him sleep. I'll take the imp. Sora can fly me there," Jace said. "You, that guy, and Morophil stay at the stronghold in case the demons make a move. There might also be other fel orc outposts nearby. The gryphon riders and Farstriders are needed here as much as possible."

"We're not going to fight the black dragons. Remember what Turalyon said — you all are more useful at the stronghold. Khadgar probably won't make it back to the Tower of Il'gara from Dalaran anytime soon. I can't get any more information out of Karlrez. I'm not particularly useful here these next few days, so I might as well go find some allies."

"I understand, Jace." Vereesa looked at Sora and told her, "So, Sora — don't let this idiot show off and get himself eaten by a black dragon."

Sora shot the warlock a sidelong glance. "I'll keep an eye on him for you."

"That won't happen." Jace shrugged slightly.
