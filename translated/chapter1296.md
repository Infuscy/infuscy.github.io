## Chapter 1296: Little One

The green dragon carried the warlock into a canyon thick with sticky spiderwebs. Everywhere were widow spiders the size of worgs.

But they were nothing like the terrifyingly huge female spider from before. Most of the time, Jace barely felt their nervousness before the things in his perception skittered away.

"Strange..." Sora stretched her long neck, looking around as she muttered. "The sound... shouldn't be far now..."

"I can feel it too," the imp said. "That way, Lady Green Dragon. See that spider cave between the two trees?"

Sora nodded slightly and flew toward where the demon pointed.

The webs hanging around them grew thicker. These webs didn't seem made for catching prey. More like walls reinforcing a "home." Bones of all kinds of corpses were scattered among the webs. Judging by the skulls, the most numerous besides various beasts were orcs.

"Is she trapped in a web?" Jace asked.

"But the sound was loud. Doesn't seem like something's covering her," Sora said.

Even so, she spat a mouthful of acid at a thick patch of webbing. This web was much tougher than the common webs in Duskwood. It took a while before the strands started snapping one by one.

But after that spray of acid, Jace sensed a shift in emotion from some corner.

There was another patch of web over there. Jace drew Quel'Serrar, walked over, and cut through it. Behind it, a black shape bound to a tree by thick, magically-charged webbing came into view.

"There you are," Jace said quietly.

It was a very small black whelp. Judging by her tail and head, she'd passed the hatchling stage, but she was much smaller than any young dragon Jace had seen — let alone compared to Sora.

Realizing she'd been found, she immediately spat a jet of sooty black smoke at Sora. But the smoke dispersed before it even reached its target. No sparks, no magma.

Seeing that her breath attack had produced nothing, she started shrieking like a stuck pig. Sora growled impatiently in the dragon tongue. "Calm down! Do you want to attract another giant spider?"

"Why should I listen to you?!" The whelp shrieked in a piercingly high voice. "Get lost! I didn't call you, you green freak!"

Then she lifted her head, opened her purple-red maw, and screamed at the sky in bursts — until Sora spat a pale pink mist right in her face.

Even Jace, who only caught a trace of the powder in that mist, felt whatever irritation the chaotic young black dragon had stirred up in him vanish completely.

Some kind of tranquilizing breath?

If it worked on him, it certainly worked on the black dragon. Caught off guard, she panted a few times as if drained of strength, then lowered her head and muttered, "Treacherous, despicable magic... Get out of here. Get out of my sight."

"Enjoy being stuck here waiting to die?" Jace asked.

The black dragon didn't answer. Jace tried again in the dragon tongue. This time, she flinched like she'd been stabbed, staring down in shock.

"How dare you! Mortal, what language do you think you're speaking?! Shut up!" She struggled to muster anger, but the lingering powder kept smothering her fury. What came out instead was a strained, tearful squeak.

Jace shook his head and said to Sora, "You talk to her. Tell her to take us to her elders, or we don't free her."

"Do you think I don't understand your crude language?" The whelp continued in the dragon tongue. "You're both liars. I will never—"

"Calling others liars?" The green dragon reared up, spreading her wings as she lunged at the whelp, her claws digging into the small dragon's throat. "You shameless wretches! Think about what Deathwing did to the Weavers!"

"I wish Morophil were here right now, you little beast. Mind control would be more appropriate for your kind!"

The whelp was terrified by the display. She trembled uncontrollably, hissing like she was begging for mercy.

Jace didn't blame her for being scared. The green dragon's tail club thumped against the ground as she swayed, sending tremors through the thick old tree where the black whelp was strung up. Even Jace felt a little uneasy watching this massive green dragon lose her temper.

But seeing how scared the whelp was, Sora seemed puzzled. She studied the young dragon more carefully.

"She really is different from the black dragons I know. She actually understands..."

"Fear." Jace nodded. "She doesn't want to die."

"Fear?" The black whelp swallowed hard, fixing her yellow snake eyes on Jace. "If I die, you'll be skinned, boned, and drained dry by my father!"

"Who's your father?" Jace asked curiously. "You mean the Dragon Father?"

"No, not... no, I mean..." The whelp hesitated in terror at the title, then suddenly found her courage. "You don't get to know his name!"

"Right." Jace nodded. "Looks like this whelp is useless to us, Sora. Let's go. We accidentally killed this place's spider mother. Might as well not leave its emergency food supply to starve."

"Uh... are we really leaving?" Sora asked.

"Of course!" Jace stared wide-eyed. "Unless you want to waste more time on this little idiot?"

"I just think if we leave, she might actually..."

Sora was cut off mid-sentence. Jace interrupted. "It's fine. There are plenty of black dragons around."

"Alright." Sora answered quietly, lowering herself to turn away.

"No, wait!" The black whelp shrieked in panic. "Fine. Get me down. Get me down, you two, and I'll tell you!"

Jace turned back and couldn't help sighing. Sora was way too soft-hearted toward their sworn enemies. But this little black creature was even more naive than she was.

He drawled, bored. "Just telling us his name won't cut it. You have to take us to him."

"Take you to him... can't do it," the black dragon said.

"Then we've got nothing to talk about, little girl." Jace spread his hands.

"Because we can't see him either, human," the black dragon said. "He's trapped by demons and orcs deep in Shadowmoon Valley. None of us know if he's alive or dead. How am I supposed to take you to him? Fine... once we save him, we'll skin you and break your bones. But we can't do that now."

"You'd better show some respect," Sora warned.

The whelp stole a glance at her, shrinking down a little in fear.

Jace thought for a moment. "Your father is Sabellian?"

The black dragon's eyes went wide. "You know him? And you're still this calm? You've got guts, mage."

"He's trapped right now. What's there to be afraid of?" Jace looked up. "So, was your attack on the Dragonmaw orcs' fortress recently an attempt to rescue him?"

"Because we can't lose him!" the black dragon rasped, as if declaring something momentous. "We can't lose Father. If Lord Gorlorio allowed it, I would've joined the assault on that fortress instead of hunting here! I'm not some hunter. I was born to be a warrior of the Blackwing!"

"Alright then, warrior of the Blackwing. I'll let you down. You go back and tell your elders that the Alliance is willing to cooperate with you against the Burning Legion," Jace said.

"That easy? You're just letting me go?" the black dragon asked.

"Tell them to meet me at the border between this forest and the wasteland to the west." Jace pointed westward. "What do you think?"

The black dragon hesitated. Jace continued, "Your Blackwing has already failed once. And you paid a heavy price..."

"We killed many demons. Countless orcs!" the black dragon cut him off.

"So did you rescue Sabellian?" Jace pressed on. "Bottom line — if you don't cooperate with us, your chances of success aren't great. Without a leader, you'll be wiped out soon enough by the Nagrand and northern gronn, and the demons."

The mention of gronn was quite effective. The black dragon's bravado visibly deflated. "I agree... I agree to tell the elders. But I'm not promising they'll believe you."

Jace nodded. "Lift me up, Sora."

The green dragon tried using her head to raise him to the height of the hanging whelp. Jace grabbed Sora's horn with one hand and cut at the webbing with the other. Even with the sharp Quel'Serrar, it was hard work. After considerable effort, he finally freed the whelp. She dropped with a thud.

The black dragon hit the ground hard, looking completely weak, barely able to stand, let alone fly.

"I can help heal you," Sora said, a little reluctantly.

"No! Keep your forest magic away from me!" The whelp screeched at her, then beat her wings desperately, forcing herself off the ground. She grabbed onto a nearby tree trunk, panting.

"See? I can fly. What about it, green sister?" She shot Sora a smug look. "Now I'm leaving. You two stay here and wait."

"What's your name? Do you have a name?" Jace asked.

"Needleflame."

The black dragon proudly uttered the words in the dragon tongue, then turned and flew high, disappearing into the gray haze.

"Sabellian must really care about her," Sora said, looking up.

"You can tell that?" Jace said.

"Not every young dragon this age gets a name," Sora said. "Maybe that black dragon leader the arakkoa call 'Teribus' will actually take her words seriously."

"Are you actually planning to rescue Sabellian?"

"We'll see," Jace said. "Come to think of it — a Dragonmaw fortress, a trapped dragon leader. Sounds familiar."

"The difference is, I'll be part of it this time," Sora said.

"You're willing to rescue a black dragon?" Jace asked.

"I'm willing to be with you and them. As for what we do..." Sora thought for a moment, then added, "Well... as long as it's not something too bad."
