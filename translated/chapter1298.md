## Chapter 1298: The Blackwing's Pledge

Wildhammer Stronghold was getting closer. Beneath the dark clouds of Shadowmoon Valley, a massive brown-black shape glided ahead. His eyes were fixed on the faint firelight spilling from the dwarf architecture ahead. His body didn't look like a living creature — more like a small floating castle in the sky.

"I really didn't think he'd come alone," Jace said quietly from Sora's back.

"Black dragons are always this arrogant," Sora beat her wings twice. "Or maybe he doesn't think it's worth the risk of bringing the young ones into your territory."

Seeing the stronghold walls approaching, Jace shouted, a little urgently, "Gorlorio! Shouldn't you shift into mortal form to enter the stronghold? This is going to make the Wildhammer soldiers think you're launching a frontal assault!"

Gorlorio acted as if he hadn't heard Jace at all, flying straight ahead until the gryphon riders noticed something was wrong and started closing in. Warning horns wailed over the stronghold. Jace sighed and said to Sora, "We need to stop those gryphon riders before this turns into an actual attack..."

Just as Sora started to speed up, the enormous black dragon let out a roar. The gryphons ahead lost their rhythm, their wings faltering. Though they kept flying toward them, gritting their beaks at their dwarven riders' commands, their morale had been shattered in a single bellow.

It wasn't just the gryphons — even Sora felt a pang of panic. The dream magic she'd gathered through Ysera's pendant was scattered by the roar, unable to coalesce.

"I'm not here to kill anyone today, dwarves." The black dragon bellowed in flawless Ironforge Dwarvish. "Go tell your High Thane that Prince Sabellian's adjutant, Gorlorio, has come to your little keep at the invitation of your Marshal Turalyon. So put away your war hammers and spears, and show a little respect for this honored guest."

The gryphon riders looked over at the green dragon — looking rather pitiful next to the black beast — and confirmed it was indeed Jace Sesso and the green dragon. Only then did they relay the order and call their gryphons back.

The moment the gryphons realized they could turn around and head home, they flapped their wings and bolted away like they'd just been refueled. It looked more like panicked retreat than a return flight. After all, they were just smarter beasts. No matter how brave in battle, they couldn't face a magical giant like that without wavering.

The black dragon snorted impatiently, then beat his massive wings faster, surging ahead. Sora had to push herself just to keep up.

Jace had originally imagined walking through the gates of Wildhammer Stronghold with a human who seemed a little oddly dressed and carried himself strangely. Instead, he watched helplessly as a dragon nearly as tall as the city gate descended slowly under the gaze of countless soldiers, mages, and terrified gryphons, landing squarely in the middle of the training ground, trailing a whirlwind of debris.

Kurdran stormed out of the keep, war hammer in hand, and scrambled up beside Turalyon — who was already standing there, staring up at the looming presence — with his mouth hanging open. Sora and Jace touched down just then.

Vereesa came down from the wall, circling around the edges as she approached, her eyes locked on the enormous black monster in the center. Grede ran over from the gryphon roost, putting his helmet on as he ran.

The dwarf sidled up to Jace and muttered in disbelief, "What the hell were you two thinking..."

"I couldn't control him. He wouldn't listen," Jace said helplessly.

But maybe this wasn't so bad. If the cooperation went smoothly, Alliance soldiers and black dragons would have to get used to each other anyway. Better to jump straight into "desensitization" now and make things easier later.

The black dragon's form shrank rapidly in a whirlwind of fire and dust, eventually leaving only a human figure and a thunderous dragon growl.

"That's a show of force." Kurdran said, dissatisfied. "Too arrogant. Even the Dark Iron dwarves wouldn't dare be this arrogant in front of me! And you heard what he said before, Turalyon. That was a direct threat!"

"You're not wrong, Kurdran." Turalyon said.

"Yes." The dwarf king nodded firmly. After a moment, he looked up. "So? Don't you want to say something about it?"

Turalyon squinted through the dust. "Let's hear what he has to say first."

"Well—" Kurdran started to argue, then coughed hard, covering his mouth as grit scraped his throat.

"Jace..." Vereesa gripped the sword at her hip, rising on her toes. "Are you sure he's here to negotiate? I didn't bring that bow. If he really intends to make a scene here, things could get out of control."

"Then he'd risk Prince Sabellian being killed by the orcs — or worse," Jace said.

"You mean their leader's been captured by the orcs too?" Grede asked.

Vereesa snorted. "Enslaving red dragons in Azeroth, controlling black dragons in Draenor. The Dragonmaw orcs really do have a dragon addiction."

"And it all goes back to Deathwing," Jace said. "That's exactly why the black dragons here don't have much goodwill toward their father. It's the basis for our negotiations."

Turalyon looked at Jace, as if asking him to confirm whether the black dragon was playing games.

Even though Jace knew the Outland black dragons would do anything to save their prince and were unlikely to play tricks here, convincing an entire room full of people to trust someone who clearly looked like trouble at first glance was still a challenge.

But then Sora spoke up. "Then please swear by your Blackwing dignity and pride, Lord Gorlorio."

Gorlorio looked at the green dragon with some surprise, and couldn't help smiling.

But he quickly composed himself, clenched his fist, and said solemnly, "I swear by the dignity and pride of the Blackwing flight. Everything I have said is true."

When he finished, his eyes were practically blazing. But even Kurdran nodded, lowering his hammer from the table. The tension in the room eased considerably.

Turalyon let out a small sigh of relief, casting a grateful glance at the green dragon across from him. "Then tell us about Dragonmaw Fortress, Lord Gorlorio."
