## Chapter 1301: Preparation for the Raid

Before the Blackwing brought word back to Wildhammer Stronghold, every soldier who'd be taking part in the assault began intense training and rehearsals.

Vereesa spent every day with the Farstriders, exchanging experience on fighting fel orcs and drilling alongside them. Grede, meanwhile, was with the Wildhammer gryphons from morning till night, bonding with them and building trust.

For gryphon riders, the harmony and understanding between man and beast was especially critical — even more so in the days leading up to battle.

This was probably the first offensive Wildhammer Stronghold had initiated since the collapse of Draenor. Scouts intensified their patrols to watch the Burning Legion and the fel orcs, making sure they didn't notice the changes in the stronghold or pick up on anything else.

Everyone was on edge. Jace was no exception.

He knew his role. Fighting groups of archers hiding behind walls and dealing with Dragonmaw flyers in the sky weren't his strengths. His job would ultimately take him face-to-face with the orc warlocks inside Dragonmaw Fortress — especially Zuluhed himself.

So he had to prepare as many shadow protection potions and various elixirs for enhanced stamina and magical power as possible.

The only regret was that he'd already drunk the two flasks of Purified Wisdom from Ursula. He had one flask of "Ursoc's Strength" left in his hand. Draenor didn't have Black Lotus, so making more of those flasks was out of the question.

But from the flasks Alleria had given Vereesa before, the draenei had apparently taught the Alliance Expedition some flask recipes too. Maybe there was a suitable substitute.

"Welcome to my alchemy lab, Mr. Jace Sesso!"

In the stronghold's alchemy lab, a bald gnome with a handlebar mustache and unibrow waved from a ladder. "I heard that black dragon who landed in the yard yesterday is going to cooperate with us! If Feldbart hadn't warned me in time, I'd have tested the Mark II Ultra-High-Energy Shrinkage Ray on him. The result would have stunned half of Draenor!"

"And the other half?" Jace asked.

"Fell into the Twisting Nether." The gnome jumped down with a grin. "If it were still around, it'd be stunned too. The more fragile something is, the easier it is to surprise."

"But wait — this is an alchemy lab, right?" Jace looked around at all the bottles and jars.

"Ah, well." The gnome said. "I'm doing this for my cousin back in Gnomeregan. I'm no engineering expert, but I happened to get a slot in the expedition. Not everyone gets to go on an adventure to another world, so he asked me to test his new creations in an alien environment. But that's beside the point. How can I help you, honored sir? I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Gribby, the alchemist of Wildhammer Stronghold."

"Jace Sesso." Jace nodded politely. "You should know about the plan to assault Dragonmaw Fortress with the black dragons, Mr. Gribby."

He patted the potion pouch strapped to his belt. "I need some potions for dealing with Shadow Council warlocks. Three shadow protection potions to start. Any other recommendations?"

"One moment." The gnome nodded, then brought out three potion bottles from the back and set them on the table.

The liquid inside was a very bright purple, with a hint of starlight. It clearly wasn't ordinary.

"The shadow protection potions you asked for." The gnome said. "But the Draenor version. You have no idea how convenient it is to extract shadow motes in Draenor after the collapse! You can't imagine. Compared to here, Azeroth's connection to the Twisting Nether might as well be completely severed — even with the Dark Portal! Of course, political correctness in the stronghold means I can't freely celebrate Draenor's destruction. So I can only chat about it here with a warlock like you. Heh heh!"

"Er, I mean — a mage of your particular kind, Mr. Sesso!" He quickly corrected himself.

"So these use Draenor's shadow essence." Jace uncorked one and smelled a strange, fishy sweetness. But the shadow resistance was unquestionable.

"And a blue flower called mana thistle," the gnome said. "Another miraculous plant that only appeared after the Cataclysm! A lot of Draenor's native species did die in that disaster. But even more bizarre, more unique things keep emerging. Every coin has two sides, Mr. Sesso!"

"True," Jace said.

He remembered that before coming to Draenor, he'd promised Ursula he'd bring back some Draenor herbs. Mana thistle was a perfect example.

Back before leaving Azeroth, he'd thought Karlrez could open a portal crack so easily — even if he couldn't get the whole expedition back immediately, he could at least create a channel for communication, quietly exchange information and supplies, support each other...

At the very least, he and his friends should be able to return for rest and to seek other help. He never imagined the Burning Legion could trap everyone so cleanly and completely.

When he'd told the black dragon yesterday that he wasn't counting on getting back to Azeroth, it was an exaggeration — but it was also genuine feeling.

"Besides mana thistle, are there any other notable Draenor herbs or alchemy materials, Mr. Gribby?"

"You want me to write you a book?" The gnome put his hands on his hips.

"Just the representative ones," Jace said. "Truth is, before I came, I promised an alchemist friend I'd bring back some local plants to help with her research."

"Hmm." Gribby nodded. "Tell you what — I'll put together a 'gift pack' for you. Things that are easy to preserve, plants and materials that can survive outside their native environment for a while. That way, when we get back to Azeroth, you can take the pack to your friend and call it a job well done."

"Thanks a lot, Mr. Gribby." Jace said.

"Nothing to thank me for, Mr. Sesso." Gribby waved a hand. "You and Lady Vereesa Windrunner coming here gave everyone a huge morale boost. Knowing the other side hasn't forgotten us..."

"You think people were afraid because you used demon magic to get here? No. In the years we've been here, we've gotten used to every horror imaginable. Your attempt made us feel like... there are still countless people in the Alliance doing anything they can to reach us — anything to bring us back..."

"No, I mean — throwing every resource at it." The gnome wiped the corner of his eye. "Knowing that, even if we never get back, it's fine. And it's not so bad here anyway. Who knows — maybe me and the relatives I left in Gnomeregan will end up with different fortunes."

Jace nodded, trying not to think about the terrible fate awaiting Gnomeregan. But the gnome's bravado didn't last long either.

He sighed, then mustered his courage again. "We're really not going back, are we? Give me some inside info, sir! The people in the stronghold haven't wanted to talk about it lately. Has Marshal Turalyon and the other Sons of Lothar leaders already decided to fight here forever? I mean, we're already allying with black dragons. Bloody hell... uh, I'm not cursing our own side."

"No," Jace said. "All the leaders are thinking about how to break through the demon blockade. I think once we make contact with Kirin'Var Village or the draenei leaders, things will become clearer. But for now, we have to survive the Burning Legion's threat first."

"Well, I believe you." The gnome managed a weak smile.

"Back to the point. About dealing with the Shadow Council orc warlocks..." He pulled out a tattered booklet and flipped through it. "But whether it's the draenei or the mages from Kirin'Var Village, none of their newly researched temporary recipes include potions that enhance shadow manipulation. I do have potions that temporarily improve arcane affinity here. Think you can use them?"

"Uh, Archmage Khadgar had some wisdom potions before," Jace said.

"Those were brought from Azeroth. If any were left, they'd be stored at the top of that tower." The gnome glanced outside. "If it weren't for the stored potions, I doubt the tower would have blown up that badly. Even my storeroom got wrecked that day."

"In short — there's no way to make wisdom potions in the stronghold anymore. No briarthorn, no blindweed..." The gnome shrugged helplessly, flipping to the back of the booklet. "Maybe try this potion invented by some strange, shamanistic ogres in Blade's Edge Mountains."

"What potion?" Jace asked.

"Olg'gar," the gnome said. "I definitely can't pronounce ogre language accurately. Translated, it's something like — Destruction Potion! Scary name, and a scary effect too. Lasts about 10 to 40 seconds, depending on the user."

"During that time, you become an incredibly powerful magical freak. One of Archmage Vargoth's students used it to forcibly wrest control of a Shadow Council death knight's skeleton soldiers away from them, turning them against the death knights — tearing them apart. It saved Kirin'Var Village! And Archmage Khadgar was even more impressive — with its power, he pieced a shattered Farstrider outpost and the floating island below it back together on the spot, saving people who were as good as dead. Let's see what you can do with it."

"Side effects?" Jace asked.

"Not clear yet. The most obvious one right now is — one fewer bottle every time you drink one. One of the ingredients comes from Farahlon. I've already asked a Farstrider team heading to Gorgrond to look for more. Hope they have good luck." As he spoke, the gnome set a tiny bottle on the table — only as big as a human's little finger, filled with light pink liquid. "Before he left, Archmage Khadgar said if anyone wanted to take this potion, they had to ask Jace Sesso first. So, what do you think, Mr. Sesso?"

"Give me one bottle," Jace said.

Leaving the alchemy lab with his bulging potion pouch, Jace thought that if they ever made it back to Azeroth, at least he'd have something to show Ursula.

He took out the totem stone that the orc shaman Bazhun had given him — the one carved with a small totem. When he held it, he could still feel the bitter, frozen wind of Alterac Valley.

Bazhun had wanted him to throw this stone into the mountains of Draenor. There were black mountains everywhere he looked, but throwing it into the fel-fire-consumed Shadowmoon Valley probably wasn't what the old shaman had in mind.

The Thunderlord clan's home was in Blade's Edge Mountains to the north. He'd thought about asking the Farstriders to take the stone with them when they went north, but in the end, he figured he might as well toss it himself and fulfill Bazhun's request.

If he really was stuck here for a while, there'd still be plenty of chances.
