## Chapter 1304: The Dragonmaw Wall

The deeper they flew into eastern Shadowmoon Valley, the more monotonous the restless, blackened earth became. But judging from the gryphon riders' shouts, their speed was increasing — from a steady cruise at the start to something noticeably faster. The gryphons themselves were growing more agitated. That meant their destination wasn't far.

The volcano known as the Hand of Gul'dan had shifted from the northeast to due north. Occasionally, abandoned siege engine wreckage appeared on the ground — some orcish, some collapsed Bronzebeard siege tanks.

Jace knew that once they crossed this endless black plain, they'd reach the dark heart of all Draenor — Karabor.

According to the map, at gryphon speed, it would take less than half a day to reach Karabor's outermost walls from here. The Doomguard legions would be slower, but not by much...

The young dragon squad tasked with drawing out the Dragonmaw Skybreaker elites was near the road between the ruins of Ambrosial to the northwest and Dragonmaw Fortress. Their return from there wouldn't take long either. That was the time window the young dragon had spoken of. They had to breach the fortress and rescue Sabellian within half a day, before demon reinforcements from Karabor could reach Dragonmaw Fortress to the south.

Once Deathwing's own son was free from his cage, the Skybreaker elites and the Doomguard flying regiments probably wouldn't be much to worry about.

The Blackwing flight was much faster than the gryphons and dragonhawks. They'd already shot ahead, disappearing into the black vortex of the void.

After crossing a ridge, clusters of brilliant crystals spread across the mountains and valleys.

After hours of staring at black earth streaked with fel-green fire, suddenly facing this fantastical — almost magnificent — sight, Jace couldn't help pausing for a moment.

He'd been to this place in the game — the Netherwing Fields. But seeing these multicolored crystal clusters taller than houses and cathedrals with his own eyes was something else entirely.

The power of the Scepter of Sargeras hadn't just torn this world apart. It had also warped parts of it.

Countless things had died because of it, and countless new things had been born because of it. Just like that gnome alchemist had said. Including these crystals — and the netherwing dragons hiding within them, still not fully grown.

Soon, under the influence of the void, they would grow rapidly, gradually becoming another rather substantial new dragon breed. But this time, Jace hoped they wouldn't have to struggle and survive under the Dragonmaw's slavery.

Not long after, orc war horns sounded from behind the crystal mountains. Flames shot into the sky. A terrible, muffled explosion followed. The spreading shadowflame energy hit Jace's senses like a shockwave.

"Should we follow?" Jace asked.

"Watch and see, Jace," Grede said.

Kurdran Wildhammer's command rang out from ahead. The swarm of gryphon riders echoed each other's calls as they turned sharply northeast. The dragonhawk riders and Sora, who was leading them, veered south.

Enchanted arrows arced high across the sky. The dark orc walls soon emerged behind the crystal mountains. The black dragons had already descended upon the castle, roaring as they spewed magma at the walls. Bright blood-red fire lit up the entire valley, again and again.

Gorlorio had even thrown himself onto the wall, his jaws full of flame as he slaughtered every enemy in reach. Orc shouts and howls rose and fell. But the dragons' assault wasn't going smoothly.

Those enchanted arrows seemed to have a devastating effect on dragon scales. Even a massive adult black dragon like Gorlorio couldn't push farther under sustained fire from the spiked black walls and fortifications. He had to spread his wings and pull back, retreating under the cover of his kin to avoid the worst of it.

On the walls and behind them, a sea of heads — more and more red-skinned fel orcs. Fel-green Legion barriers flickered along the walls. Eredar runes burned like fire.

The black dragons' first wave was already faltering. The fortress, initially thrown into chaos by the surprise attack, was recovering its composure.

Between the battlements, fel orc archers were shouting orders, shifting toward the southwest where the black dragons had concentrated their assault. Occasionally, fel fire flew out from behind the walls, arcing toward the high-flying Blackwing flock — Doomguard magic.

Glowing arrows grew denser across the distant sky. The gryphons flew silently northeast. Grede watched it all in silence — until a familiar Quel'Thalas war horn sounded from farther south...

"Should be now," Grede murmured, then looked ahead at Kurdran and Sky'ree.

"Now!" The Wildhammer High Thane roared almost immediately in Dwarvish. Though his voice was barely audible over the rumbling flames and orc war cries, it was enough for every gryphon rider here to hear.

A gryphon shot past Jace to the front. On it rode a Kirin Tor wizard, holding a pale blue staff. The light on the staff grew fainter as it receded — but brighter and brighter.

At the same time, all the gryphons accelerated, following his lead. They formed a wedge formation, stabbing straight toward the northern wall!

"Jace... try not to fall to your death!" Grede warned.

"I'm not that easy to kill." Jace adjusted his collar.

Gradually, golden or pale blue light flared behind the foremost gryphon riders. The entire formation grew more visible.

Jace noticed some orcs on the wall seemed to have spotted the anomaly in the north. Halting horn calls blew weakly, but it was too late.

The lead wizard raised his staff high, conjuring a wide arcane shield that scattered like starlight. It blocked the arrows hastily fired their way, protecting the gryphons as they closed rapidly on the walls.

The shield didn't hold long — Draenor's unstable arcane energy couldn't sustain it — but it lasted long enough.

After weathering the first volley, the gryphon riders cleared the wall and hurled crackling war hammers down into the fel orc archer ranks. Continuous lightning explosions and orc screams erupted everywhere.

At the lowest point of their dive, the paladins riding behind the gryphon riders leaped off their mounts, landing on the wall and charging straight into the fel orc formations...

The moment these fighters — glowing with golden light, swinging longswords and war hammers — entered the fray, they broke the fel orc defensive lines on the wall. Even the distant arrows weakened by half.

Grede hurled Skycrusher behind a paladin who was about to be surrounded. The wild lightning explosion sent several orcs flying off the wall, breaking the encirclement. As the war hammer flew back into Grede's hand, the gryphon was already close to the wall.

"Do your thing, Jace!"

Jace nodded, leaped off the gryphon, and landed firmly on the wall. Several fel orcs came charging at him with battle axes and swords.

He raised Ussales and cast Fear on the foremost one. The orc's legs gave out, and he dropped to his knees. The two behind him crashed into him, and one was pushed off the wall, torn apart by the spikes below.

Two Farstrider arrows whistled past, hitting fel orcs who'd been trying to flank Jace from the other side. The rangers had already made it onto the wall under dragonhawk cover.

As Jace fought alongside the paladins against the fel orcs, several fearless young black dragons in the air, taking advantage of the reduced arrow fire from the wall, swooped over and plunged into the fortress interior.

But several Doomguard flew out from between the towers and high walls, beating their wings as they landed on the young dragons. They hacked at the scales and wings with war blades and axes. One whelp screeched and tumbled into the crowd of orcs in the middle of the fortress.

Then a blood-red figure appeared on that whelp's back. Several orcs who'd been about to rush forward and finish off the young dragon jumped back in fright.

Three Doomguard laughed as they lunged at the figure — only to have their demon wings torn apart and their demon hearts pierced in seconds.

After dealing with the nearby demons, she didn't rest. She dove straight into the orc ranks, cutting them down at will. Even the demon guards who got close were swiftly executed with clean heart-stabs.

The empowerment from the Book of Demon Names was terrifying. She didn't even need to turn invisible when taking on those towering demons. Even in a straight contest of strength, they were no match for her. Jace had felt this himself.

"Morophil..."

Taking advantage of a brief lull after breaking one fel orc squad, Jace pointed at her and chanted a demon empowerment spell.

The moment the spell took effect, the runes he'd personally carved on her body blazed with a light more sinister than the ered'ar runes on the Dragonmaw walls. She rampaged like a wildfire of mad demonic flame, and the bloodlust and frenzy transmitted through the soul link were so intense they made Jace's chest burn.

The Burning Legion demons inside the fortress quickly realized what this she-devil was. Many of them didn't even dare surround her anymore. Some fled back into the fortress in terror. Others flew over the wall, trying to escape north.

Even so, there were too many Dragonmaw fel orcs. And more kept coming.

The archers and guards on the wall were locked in a bitter fight against the Alliance and the black dragons, but there was no end in sight.

The Farstriders had successfully taken the southern wall, coordinating with the black dragons in the air to intercept Dragonmaw reinforcements trying to approach from the southern mines and villages. They could hold for now, but their arrows were limited. Not everyone had a magic bow like Vereesa.

One young black dragon was already badly wounded. If Morophil hadn't been protecting him in the castle — and if several gryphon riders weren't trying to close in and support their fight — he'd have been torn apart by now.

The expedition was gaining the upper hand. The wall was almost theirs. But this kind of grueling battle wasn't what they'd planned for. They had to get deep into the fortress and find the Dragonmaw chieftain — and more importantly, find Sabellian — before the large demon force from Karabor arrived.

But given the current situation, if Jace and Morophil charged inside, the soldiers outside the wall would be overwhelmed by the increasingly numerous fel orcs and demons...

Gorlorio reappeared, slamming into and demolishing a section of wall still firmly held by the fel orcs. Soldiers were sent flying, their bodies torn apart. But his own scales were badly damaged, covered in embedded enchanted arrows.

One eye locked onto Jace, as if urging him to stop worrying about the wall and press into the fortress. Then — a different sort of dragon roar rang out from the distance.

Jace and a nearby Silver Hand knight both looked over. A group of red shapes was approaching.

The red dragon riders Gorlorio had mentioned. The last remnants of the Dragonmaw returning from Azeroth.

The expedition's hearts sank at the sight. Several black dragons had to climb higher, preparing to intercept this dragon rider squad.

But Jace's heart surged. He shouted to a gryphon rider flying over to support the black dragons, "Take me up! Take me up!"

The gryphon rider heard him and dipped lower. Jace used a Shadowstep to land behind him. "Take me to meet those red dragons."

"You sure about this, Master Sesso?"

"Positive. Let's go!"

The gryphon rider gritted his teeth and muttered in Dwarvish to his gryphon, "Damn. We're going to die."

"I understand Dwarvish!" Jace shouted.

"Bloody hell." The rider cursed under his breath. Even so, he turned and charged straight at the approaching red dragon riders.

Just as the red dragon riders raised their bows, ready to shoot the gryphon rider out of the sky, Jace drew Quel'Serrar, cut his palm, and raised the bloodstained blue sword high. "Wake up, children of Alexstrasza! See whose sword this is! See whose blood this is!"

"Your queen is free! I bring her command from Azeroth. Kill every Dragonmaw orc! Make them pay for the years of torment and suffering they've inflicted on your queen!"

The light of Quel'Serrar, soaked in the blood blessed by the red dragonflight, shimmered differently. The red dragons clearly saw it.

The dwarf rider had already raised his war hammer, muttering elemental incantations, ready to fight to the death. But then — the foremost red dragon craned her neck, grabbed the fel orc on her back with her jaws, and flung him off. The other red dragons followed suit, shaking off their orc riders to fall to their deaths on the cliffs and crystal formations below.

The red dragons immediately turned on the two-headed nether drakes and their riders. The Gorgrond beasts were no match for the young dragons. The outcome was decided quickly amidst ferocious biting and blazing breath.

"They actually believe you!" The dwarf shouted in astonishment.

"They have to believe me, Wildhammer brother! They have no choice but to believe me!" Jace shouted back, raising his sword. "Join us, children of the queen! Join our ranks! Join Gorlorio's Blackwing flight and press the attack on Dragonmaw Fortress! The time for vengeance has come!"
