Chapter 1306: Zuluhed's "Treasure"

Mog'dorg was the ruler of the Netherwing Ledge in the game. After Zuluhed was killed by players and the Netherwing dragons, Illidan Stormrage had ordered him to succeed Zuluhed as chieftain of the Dragonmaw clan, styling himself King Mog'dorg.

That meant his status was no lower than Nekros, the Dragonmaw chieftain of Grim Batol. Within the Dragonmaw clan, he was second only to Zuluhed himself.

And his current size and build were far more imposing than in the game — he was practically a blood-red monster in black heavy armor. Which also meant Sabellian was probably deep in this passage. Zuluhed had placed the Dragonmaw's strongest forces here, making a final stand to protect the Dragonmaw's key asset.

Mog'dorg didn't hesitate. With a roar, he lunged straight at the nearest ranger. Vereesa loosed an arrow, but the monster dodged it with surprising speed!

His reaction speed was almost incongruous with his massive build. Even Vereesa was taken aback. As she reached for a second arrow, Mog'dorg's axe was already swinging at her face!

A streak of green light — then a flash of gold slammed into the axe, deflecting it. The guardian paladin roared and met Mog'dorg head-on with his warhammer. Backed by the radiant Holy Light, he somehow held his ground against Mog'dorg despite being half the size!

A dozen elite Dragonmaw axe-orcs rushed in from the sides, trying to flank the paladin and rangers. Morofel was already in front of Vereesa, her blade Apocalypse cleanly severing several axe-wielding arms, then casually piercing foreheads and slitting throats.

Vereesa aimed another arrow at Mog'dorg's skull, but a dragonkin blocked it midair with his warhammer. The crystal hammerhead shattered into fragments that exploded across the room. The dragonkin kept swinging the hammer's shaft, charging alongside several fel orcs at the Farstriders. Both sides quickly became a tangled melee.

Farstriders locked blades with orcs, Mog'dorg and the paladin wrestled in raw strength. This wasn't the time for a Hand of Gul'dan — a shadow explosion in this narrow cave would probably take out all the Farstriders and collapse the entire chamber on top of them. That would waste who knew how much time.

Even with the Holy Light's blessing, Magtheridon's boiling blood was too strong. The crimson demon towering over everyone soon overpowered the paladin's momentum.

Then a silver stone golem hurled a fel orc guard aside with a shout, slammed into Mog'dorg's chest and staggered him, following up with a hammer blow square on his fang-filled jaw!

The lightning from the electrified hammer arced out, electrocuting the nearby fel orcs who'd tried to support their leader. But Mog'dorg was only knocked askew, fel blood gushing from his mouth.

A dragonkin grabbed Grede from behind and pulled him away. Mog'dorg raised his axe for a killing blow at Grede's chest — but it was caught mid-swing by a sinister bloody force.

Jace, already in his dark transformation, used Grasp of the Blood Demon to halt Mog'dorg's strike. He was about to cast Cripple with the language curse Sazeno had prepared simultaneously, but Mog'dorg sensed the coming spell. Faster, he pulled a throwing axe from his back and hurled it.

Jace couldn't dodge in time. He swung Ulthalesh, cleanly slicing the axe in two midair — but the Cripple failed.

Mog'dorg had never expected that axe to do real damage to a warlock. He lunged forward like a crimson train, crashing into Jace's space, raising his axe high and bringing it down in a clean horizontal slash!

Fel light flashed. A demonic shriek exploded overhead. In a spray of blood, the skull axe was cloven in two. Only a demonic skeleton remained, its passive but sinister face revealed amid the bursting mana.

"A perfect artifact!"

Mog'dorg realized the staff was terrifyingly sharp. But he showed no fear. Roaring in orcish, he grabbed the twisted staff with his bare hands, dodging the blood blade as he tried to wrench it away.

Jace might have superhuman strength compared to ordinary people, but that was only against regular warriors. Even if he could match an ordinary fel orc in raw power, how could he contend with a giant who'd clearly drunk who-knew-how-much of Magtheridon's blood?

They wrestled back and forth for a moment. Jace took the opportunity to lay a Curse of Weakness on his opponent, then a Corruption. Once he'd stabilized his footing through the shifting balance of physical strength, he grabbed the orc's tusk and began chanting Life Drain!

Mog'dorg felt his power draining at an unbelievable speed. He had to let go and stagger back.

At the same time, Jace realized the filth of Magtheridon's fel blood he'd drawn from this fel orc was visibly corrupting his soul. Even his shadow form was being rapidly stripped and consumed by the corruptive power of the fel blood inside him!

He had to forcibly vomit it out onto the ground to avoid being quickly transformed into some kind of demonic monster. But in that moment, Mog'dorg picked up an enchanted axe from the ground and charged again. Jace gritted his teeth through the burning pain of the fel blood and completed the Cripple, finally locking the monster in place.

But Mog'dorg gritted his teeth and muttered something. His skin grew redder, dark veins bulging, muscles swelling further — he was clearly about to break the Cripple with some demonic power!

Grede spun around, crushed the dragonkin still clinging to him to death with a hammer blow, then wound up and hurled his warhammer. A lightning blast exploded right in the warlord's face.

The lightning flashed. After the white light, half of the monster's face was obliterated. Brain matter and teeth were exposed. Yet he still swung his axe wildly in all directions like a mindless beast.

One of those swings caught a nearby orc square in the waist, sending the unlucky bastard crashing to his knees with a scream.

But Mog'dorg didn't struggle much longer. The stench of fel blood filled the cave. By now, the other fel orc soldiers had been cut to pieces by Morofel and the Farstrider rangers. None were left standing.

Vereesa put an arrow through the chest of the last dragonkin guard still fighting, then looked worriedly toward Jace. Seeing him covered in blood, her expression wasn't exactly relieved.

After this brutal battle in the cramped cave chamber, everyone was drenched in filth — whether it was enemy blood or their own was impossible to tell.

A Farstrider collapsed to the ground, one arm severed, clearly unable to continue fighting.

Grede handed her one of his healing potions. After the others bandaged her up and performed some basic treatment, they stepped over the dragonkin's and Mog'dorg's corpses and pressed forward. There were more paladins behind them; she shouldn't be in mortal danger.

But by now, the group's mood and morale were far from what they'd been when they first charged in. No one knew how many more terrifying Dragonmaw orcs guarded "Zuluhed's treasure" ahead.

"Holy Light above, your faithful servant needs strength and courage to enter this demonic lair. When I walk with your radiance, all dust and corruption shall be dispelled..."

The paladin murmured his prayer as he walked at the front. Soon, his body seemed to be enveloped by some kind of light. The Holy Light had truly answered him — even in this dark, gloomy crypt, where even the light came from fel fire.

The Holy Light's blessing didn't just accompany the paladin. It passed to everyone around him. Jace felt his spirits lift too.

Whether this was some kind of power blessing or some other blessing he'd encountered in the game, he couldn't say. He'd never studied the divine magic of priests or paladins.

But as if fate was playing a joke on them, the long corridor ahead — far too wide to be an underground mine — was completely empty.

Vereesa and two Farstriders carefully scanned for hidden enemies that might be lying in ambush. But Jace knew the truly terrible thing was still deeper in.

The thick, deep, terrifying shadow was getting closer. Was this Zuluhed's magic, or Sabellian's power?

The paladin captain glanced at Jace. Jace could tell from his expression that this Silver Hand knight sensed the same sinister, evil presence he did.

The sound of infernals pounding the fortress had faded to a muffled murmur, nearly inaudible now. An ethereal magical hum gradually replaced it.

Passing through a narrow, iron-reinforced passageway, an incredibly spacious but eerily dark hall revealed itself.

In the center of the hall, an aged orc warlock in a maroon robe turned to face them. He held a skull staff. In the vast, strange, empty hall, he stood alone.

"Zuluhed!" Vereesa drew her bow, aiming at the warlock. "Where is the black dragon?"

"Lord Magtheridon's legion is already on its way," the orc said in remarkably fluent Common. "There's nothing you can do."

"The Dragonmaw clan has already lost. There's only you left here!" the paladin shouted. "Tell us where you're keeping the black dragon prince, or you'll learn how much suffering the Holy Light can bring to an evil half-demon!"

"Who said there's only me here, paladin?" Zuluhed laughed coldly, raising his hand. "Open your eyes and look. These are the true children of the noble Prince Sabellian..."

"Enough talk. We'll find him ourselves!" Vereesa loosed an arrow. But to everyone's surprise, the dream-glow arrow sank into a patch of invisible darkness mid-flight, never reaching Zuluhed.

The "darkness" that had swallowed the arrow stirred slightly — like a night sky, filled with stars... Jace looked up, startled to realize the "night sky and stars" were actually solid!

The shadow spread its wings. Stars and darkness suddenly filled the entire hall. Everyone stared in amazement as the undulating dark came into focus.

It was a Netherwing dragon! An adult Netherwing dragon stood before them.
