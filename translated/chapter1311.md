## Chapter 1311: The "Secret" of the Netherwing Mountains

"What?"

"What did you say?"

Everyone in the hall stared at Sabellian in shock. The black dragon, draped in that terrifying blood-red robe that looked woven from dragon wings themselves, was unlike anyone else in the room. In stature, in bearing, in the mood he projected.

Even the two black dragons behind him couldn't hide their surprise, looking to their prince.

"Black dragon, you'd better explain yourself. Are you saying the Scepter of Sargeras is still in Draenor?!" Kurdran demanded.

"If I'm not mistaken about the name, isn't that the artifact that destroyed half of Draenor?" Grede asked beside him.

"Yes..." Vereesa murmured.

For some reason, Jace didn't seem surprised at all. In fact, hearing the weapon's name from Sabellian's mouth made everything start to feel coherent.

"Khadgar saw Ner'zhul disappear through the portal with the Scepter of Sargeras with his own eyes. There's no way that's wrong," Turalyon said calmly.

"Of course. Khadgar is no fool," Sabellian said. "But the scepter came back. Clearly, Ner'zhul escaped Draenor, but he didn't escape the Burning Legion's grasp..."

"Do you have proof, black dragon?" a Quel'Thalas magister asked.

Sabellian chuckled, glancing at the mages in the corner. "How could a wretch like Zuluhed use our dragon eggs to cultivate such a powerful individual? That shadow dragon was not my child, nor his — she was a twisted creation spawned by the Scepter of Sargeras! Zuluhed called her Sagrol'ku in draconic, as if she had Sargeras's own blessing... what an unprecedented joke!"

Vereesa thought for a moment. "The Scepter of Sargeras was in the Dragonmaw chieftain's hands? No..."

"It was in his hands before," Sabellian explained. "Magtheridon wanted Zuluhed to make good use of the Scepter of Sargeras's power to carry out Kil'jaeden's orders. Zuluhed wasn't a member of the Shadow Council, but he was a talented spellcaster. Either Kil'jaeden or Magtheridon had clearly stopped trusting Gul'dan's students."

"And when Zuluhed got the scepter, he had his own agenda. He wanted to use that demonic artifact and my power to create a new breed of dragon. He saw hope in those void-corrupted eggs."

"When Magtheridon found out, he took the scepter back. But he was intrigued by the experiment, so he didn't punish Zuluhed. Instead, he confined him to the Dragonmaw Fortress and put Warlord Mor'ghor in charge of monitoring the experiments. To that end, Magtheridon fed Mor'ghor his own blood, turning him into a deformed monster."

"But clearly," Sabellian said, glancing at Jace, "not deformed enough."

"What else but the Scepter of Sargeras could create this terrible barrier?" Sabellian spread his hands to address the room. "Even if you don't trust a black dragon's word, at least open your eyes and face the undeniable facts."

"And what do you mean by 'wielding the Scepter of Sargeras'?" Jace asked.

Sabellian smiled and turned back to him. "That's something you should ask yourself, warlock. You cunning little creature. You've killed Teron Gorefiend, ruined our plans, rescued the Red Dragonqueen, and driven my father to the brink. Don't tell me you've never considered what the Scepter of Sargeras could do."

"Send everyone back to Azeroth," a Kirin Tor mage said. "Khadgar mentioned that possibility. If the Scepter of Sargeras was still available, he could transport everyone back to Azeroth at once... He seriously considered that option. That's why he searched Karabor for so long, only pulling back when the Burning Legion arrived."

"I knew he'd thought of it," Sabellian said. "Once you have the Scepter of Sargeras, never mind sending you all back — the Burning Legion itself couldn't set foot on Draenor. No one in this world can stand against its power. Even without the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh."

"Then do you know where the Scepter of Sargeras is now?" another Kirin Tor mage asked.

"How would I know that?" Sabellian said. "Don't forget, I've been locked in Dragonmaw's underground prison this whole time. But I can't imagine many other possibilities. The Temple of Karabor. Hellfire Citadel. The Throne of Kil'jaeden. The demons would certainly keep an artifact that can decide Draenor's fate in the safest possible place. Maybe even in Magtheridon's hands. Though I doubt that overfed fel toad even knows how to use the thing — otherwise he wouldn't have handed it to Zuluhed to experiment with. Wouldn't you agree?"

Turalyon stared at the black dragon for a long moment. The hall buzzed with quiet debate.

"We have the demon tome now," a Quel'Thalas mage stepped forward to say. "No demon lord dares fight us at full strength, afraid of having their true name exposed. With the power of that grimoire, we could seize the Scepter of Sargeras and solve every problem at once. I can confirm that Khadgar proposed this plan before, but dropped it when the scepter went missing."

"Wouldn't that bring about an even greater danger? The Scepter of Sargeras already destroyed half of Draenor. Are we going to destroy the other half?"

"Ner'zhul couldn't control its power. But could Master Sesso?"

"Yes. Master Khadgar greatly admires Master Sesso's control over shadow magic."

"I saw with my own eyes how he destroyed that void dragon in the cavern, everyone. I guarantee it..."

"This is fate giving us a chance! We have no other options! Master Khadgar's library still contains his research on the Scepter of Sargeras — studies passed down from the ancient Order of Tirisfal centuries ago, combined with Karazhan's research and Khadgar's own observations... That's leagues beyond Ner'zhul, who grabbed the scepter and started swinging!"

The mages excitedly argued among themselves. Driven by the twin desires to destroy the Burning Legion and return home, even Turalyon, the nearby Silver Hand knights, and the Church members found themselves at a loss for words.

"What do you think, Jace?" Turalyon asked.

Every eye in the hall turned to him. Grede's eyes were wide, fixed on his face. Jace glanced at Vereesa. The ranger-lord's gaze seemed to waver, avoiding his.

Sora kept her head down. Fear radiated from her, barely contained. She didn't even dare look his way.

From the moment they'd entered Draenor, she'd been terrified of the scepter's power. Especially after entering Terokkar Forest and seeing the landscape that looked alive on the surface but was secretly dying. Jace wasn't surprised she didn't want to accept this.

"I need to consult Master Khadgar," Jace said. "Get his opinion. Besides, two of the expedition's leaders haven't arrived yet. Their input matters too."

Turalyon nodded, his brow relaxing slightly. The whole hall let go of its arguments and questions. Yes. There was no need to rush this.
