## Chapter 1314: The Black Prince's Gift

After eating, the four of them planned to help out with the wounded over at the priests. A few gryphon riders who'd fought at the Dragonmaw Fortress happened to walk in and immediately recognized Vereesa and Grede, insisting on pulling them aside to hear all about the battle.

Jace checked his belt and noticed that Angelista's Sash also seemed to have burn marks. Nothing serious yet — at least the enchantment was still intact.

"Jace..."

Sora approached. Jace straightened his robe and smiled. "What is it?"

"I'm sorry, Jace." Sora said. "I shouldn't have doubted you. I just... couldn't relax."

"It's the Scepter of Sargeras," Jace said. "You can't be too worried about something like that. Nothing to apologize for, Sora."

Sora nodded. "Do you remember what we talked about in Terokkar Forest?"

"I do," Jace said. "You said the scepter had done terrible damage to this world. That stuck with me."

"You said it was because Ner'zhul couldn't control the scepter's power," Sora said.

Hearing that, Jace's heart skipped a beat. Yes. That was what he'd said.

He realized that back when they'd first come to Draenor, he'd genuinely believed that. Believed he could control the Scepter of Sargeras. Sure, everyone thought the scepter had left Draenor by then, so it was just a thought. But... he really had thought it.

Jace found himself pinching the bridge of his nose, a chill running through him.

He knew that if he'd stubbornly charged toward the Legion's ultimate artifact, Vereesa, Grede, and Sora would probably have been convinced to follow him.

Maybe even allying with the black dragons was part of Kil'jaeden's plan. After all, there weren't many forces on Draenor that could stand against the Burning Legion. The draenei. The black dragons. What, was the expedition supposed to recruit the gronn?

Whoever it was, they'd inevitably reveal to the expedition that the Scepter of Sargeras was still on Draenor. One way or another, it would all march steadily toward his design.

And Kil'jaeden didn't care how many people joined the expedition to fight the Burning Legion. He didn't care who took the demon tome.

Once the Scepter of Sargeras fell into the hands of someone capable of wielding it, everything would either be destroyed or return to the Legion, becoming another asset in the invasion of Azeroth. No one would escape.

"I can't control it either, Sora." Jace shook his head.

"No." Sora's voice was quiet. "I believe you could control its power. That's why the Deceiver chose you. But choosing not to take it — that's what makes you different, Jace. That's why Queen Alexstrasza believed only you could protect the chain of will... She only met you once, but she understood you better than anyone. I've always known that."

Jace was taken aback. Sora smiled, but it was fleeting. She looked past him. "He wants you."

Jace turned. A tall figure in crimson stood by a stone pillar, watching him.

Sora walked over to the elf and the dwarf, said something. Vereesa looked at the figure. "We'll wait for you at the priests, Jace."

Jace nodded. When they'd gone, the figure walked toward him.

"Jace Sesso." Sabellian said the name, stroking his rather comical curled mustache. It didn't diminish his striking handsomeness, inhuman as it was — just like his father.

"Prince Sabellian." Jace inclined his head.

"About the scepter. What have you decided?" the black dragon asked.

"I said this morning — I need to wait for General Danath Trollbane and Lady Alleria's input, as well as Archmage Vargos," Jace said. "Master Khadgar doesn't know yet either. I need to speak with him directly when he's on the other side."

Sabellian raised an eyebrow. "You actually mean that. But Danath Trollbane is still at Honor Hold, and Vargos could be anywhere. I sometimes find it hard to imagine how you manage to assemble all your companions across Draenor."

"That's why we allied with you." Jace said.

"You think we're goblin zeppelins?" the black dragon said coldly.

"I prefer to call it 'cooperation,'" Jace said.

Sabellian let out an involuntary snort, glancing at Sora's retreating figure. "Ysera's children have always been without principles."

"Then how did Teron Gorefiend and his death knights invade Alterac?" Jace asked.

Sabellian narrowed his eyes, pausing for a moment. "I came to express goodwill, Jace Sesso. No need for the pointed words. I'm easier to deal with than you think. The Blackwing can help transport your personnel."

"You're willing?" Jace asked, surprised.

"Yes." Sabellian said. "You destroyed the Dragonmaw Fortress, executed Zuluhed and Mor'ghor — two faithful hounds of the demons — and saved countless dragon eggs from the orcs' and demons' abuse and torture. I'm grateful from the bottom of my heart. Compared to that, helping transport your soldiers is nothing. Besides, the young dragons can use the exercise. Better than sending them straight into battle with demons."

"Did you bring back the eggs?" Jace asked.

"Not all of them. But enough." Sabellian nodded.

"About the young dragons..." Jace said. "I know you have a daughter. Needleflame."

"Mm." Sabellian nodded. "She's a brave girl."

"Have you considered letting the black dragons — the younger generation born in Draenor — return to Azeroth to grow up under Queen Alexstrasza's care?"

Sabellian let out a laugh. "Did Alexstrasza ask you to say that? Thank her for her kindness, but absolutely not. Not a single one."

His tone was casual, but the words were ironclad. Jace could tell there was almost no room for negotiation. How was he supposed to report back to Sora now?

"Ah. Speaking of Her Majesty Queen Alexstrasza." Sabellian pulled a magnificent crimson cloak from his robes. "I almost forgot why I came."

"I know you lost a very beautiful and valuable cloak in the Dragonmaw Fortress battle. I could feel red dragon magic on it. Whatever it was, it was exceptionally powerful and exquisite."

"Considering that battle was fought to rescue me from my own foolishness... I must give you this gift myself. I hope it can compensate for your loss and express my gratitude — and the Blackwing's."

"Ah. Well. Thank you, Prince Sabellian."

Jace examined the cloak. At first glance, it was clearly a cloak with terrifying fire resistance. But deeper than that, even with his perception, he couldn't sense much more. He knew it wasn't as simple as it looked.

"This is..."

"It's called..." Sabellian cleared his throat. "Wing of Olastrasz."

"What?!" Jace's hands trembled. "This is a red dragon's hide?"

Sabellian smiled, lifting an eyebrow. "He was the Guardian of the Demon Soul. The previous guardian. Like me, he was careless enough to fall for the Dragonmaw orcs' trick."

"Well then. He was a red dragon."
