## Chapter 1315: A Special Flame

"Do you like it, darling?"

Morofil sat sideways on a log, draped in the scarlet cloak, half-lidded eyes looking his way. Her pink curves were visible in the shadows beneath the cloak.

The cloak wasn't simple scale-hide. It was more like fur — except the "hairs" burned like brilliant flames, nothing like normal animal fur. Red dragons only had such fur on their necks and chins, and only the most powerful ones at that. Seeing these "burning" hairs reminded Jace of Krasus's true form back in Grim Batol.

Even without magical light shimmering on its surface, the cloak looked like a strange cape woven from elemental fire. On Morofil, with her body covered in demonic flame patterns, the effect was even more striking.

Jace seemed distracted. The succubus crooked her lip and lifted a leg, tapping his knee with the tip of her hoof.

"I told you to wear it so you could check if there's any mind control or dark magic curse on it," Jace said, chin resting on his hand. "Not so you could show off."

"Then I'll just take it off." Morofil grinned wickedly, pulling the cloak down to bare half her chest.

"Hold on." Jace sighed. "So, is there anything or not?"

Morofil placed her hand on her chest. "No, master. This cloak is extremely powerful. Nearly immune to flame. Unusually resistant to shadow as well. And ordinary blades couldn't possibly do any real damage to skin made from an ancient beast like this."

She lifted a corner of the cloak and continued. "Not only does it have no mind control ability — it even resists mental corruption. And not just a little. It's as if it was deliberately enchanted for that purpose. But it wasn't enchanted."

"That's not surprising," Jace said. "Olastrasz was once the Red Dragonqueen's most favored guardian. His power rivaled her consorts. Otherwise, the Aspects wouldn't have trusted him to guard the Demon Soul. The Demon Soul was an artifact corrupted by dark forces, a tool of enslavement with a powerful hold over dragons. Its guardian had to have strong resistance to mental enslavement."

"But he still died," Morofil said.

"Because his opponent was Deathwing and the Dragonmaw Legion under his command," Jace explained. "The Demon Soul's location shouldn't have been discovered by the father of black dragons."

The imp jumped onto the succubus's shoulder, sniffing the cloak. "So, my master. You're worried that Deathwing's son giving this to you... hides some sinister black dragon curse?"

"Sabellian didn't hide where it came from," Jace said. "He told me this cloak was personally crafted by Deathwing for Zuluhed. It helped him use the Demon Soul to enslave Queen Alexstrasza. But after Zuluhed returned to Draenor, he stopped wearing it. He was afraid Deathwing might use the thing to control him."

"The prince told you all that?" Morofil asked, a little surprised.

"Yes," Jace said. "He didn't say it outright, but I think his meaning was clear — whether or not Deathwing had placed a mental enslavement effect on it, it died with him. Still, I couldn't be completely sure, so I had you check it for me."

"On mind magic, he's not lying to you." Morofil lifted her hand. "But this cloak's abilities aren't limited to strong resistance and a beautiful exterior. There's clearly another power hidden in it. The power of flame."

Jace worked his jaw, staring at Morofil for a moment. He didn't say it.

"What secrets are there between us, master?" The succubus smiled, nudging his shin with her hoof. "Or does Deathwing's son want to keep you guessing, so you can discover it yourself?"

"Your old cloak could turn you into an orc. Could this one turn you into something even more terrifying? A drakonid, maybe? I didn't pay much attention to those strange creatures in the Dragonmaw caves. But picturing my cute master looking like that is a whole other matter..."

She scooted closer with a wicked grin. Jace looked at her and touched his nose. "No. This cloak lets the wearer... uh... fly."

The smile on Morofil's face froze instantly.

"Ha!"

The imp barely got a laugh out before the succubus's tail coiled around his neck, choking the second "ha" into a sharp squeal. Then she flung him out the window.

The succubus fixed her gaze on Jace. Jace coughed hard twice. "That's what he said."

"How does it fly?" Morofil asked, pulling off the cloak entirely and setting it aside.

"I don't know." Jace carefully folded the cloak and covered it with a piece of linen. "Sabellian said the cloak's flame power lets anyone skilled with fire fly through the air. And since I have the Red Dragonqueen's blood flowing through me, I should know better than him."

"But I really don't know..."

"A lot of orcs knew that when Zuluhed served Warchief Blackhand, he still held onto his shamanic power. For some reason, the elements in both Draenor and Azeroth still trusted him. That might be part of why Olastrasz and so many red dragons misjudged their opponent."

"Sabellian said Zuluhed used to fly with this cloak, using the power of flame. But after returning from Grim Batol to Shadowmoon Valley, he never tried again. Maybe it has to do with his special nature — an orc chieftain who held both the elements' trust and shadow power."

"Just like you?" Morofil said.

"That's a weird way to put it." Jace put the cloak away in a chest. "I think it has something to do with Deathwing. A lot of fire and earth elementals in Azeroth are willing to obey black dragons. Maybe Deathwing initially disguised himself as the voice of the elements to trick him into stealing the Demon Soul..."

"Very possible," Morofil said. "When a caster loses the power they rely on, the easiest way to make them dependent and trusting is to give it back — even a false return. Deathwing knew exactly how to exploit that weakness."

Jace glanced at her and nodded. "Anyway. Wildhammer Stronghold just finished a major raid. Vereesa and Grede need time to recover. I also need to wait for Master Khadgar to get back to Ilgara's Tower so we can discuss next steps. Plus we have no word from the Farstriders who went to Gorgrond. So there's time to rest and figure out how this cloak works."

"Which means you have more time to spend with me, darling." Morofil grinned, throwing her arms around his neck. "You know how much mana it took to face down a shadow dragon and kill a transformed dragonkin?"

"Did it?" Jace tilted his head, looking into her eyes. "I took a full breath head-on. Didn't feel like much."

"There might be hidden damage we haven't found. Let your most loyal servant give you a thorough examination?" Morofil slowly slid her hand into his collar.

When her sharp nails traced across his collarbone, Jace drew in a breath. "I'm glad, Morofil."

"What a surprise. I thought our last time had left you traumatized." Morofil said.

"I'm not talking about the mana." Jace caught her hand. "I mean I'm glad you're back to your old self. I was worried that demon tome had some unknown effect on you."

Morofil slowly released his neck, pushing him onto the bed. She leaned over him, eyes locked on his. "Don't forget. This isn't my first time facing Kil'jaeden. And you're not even my first master to face him."

"You mean Brutalus," Jace said.

"Yes. I thought..." Morofil's voice dropped.

"You thought I'd end up like him. Surrendering to the Legion." Jace said.

"Don't blame me for thinking that. The terms Kil'jaeden offered you were far richer than what he gave Brutalus." Morofil's eyes flickered with burning emotion. "The Scepter of Sargeras. The ultimate artifact forged from countless sacrificed demon souls. Capable of twisting the boundary between illusion and reality... You can't imagine how attractive that is to the countless demons in the Great Dark Beyond. Even I..."

She paused, then continued. "But I know what it means. The Deceiver... I can picture him right now, sitting on his throne, staring into the Twisting Nether with that smug, disgusting face, acting like everything's going his way. And you refused him, darling. You took less time to consider than it took me to cut off Brutalus's hands."

"So. Still blind optimism?" Jace asked.

"You mean Kil'jaeden?" The demon flashed a challenging smile, then bent down and kissed him lightly on the lips.
