Chapter 1319: Grasp Your True Power

It was night by the time they reached the Church. The area was quiet.

Jace walked into the infirmary, but the sight of the kite shield hanging overhead — silver-white, embossed with the symbol of the Holy Light — made him feel oddly uncomfortable.

Most of the wounded and sick were already asleep. An elderly priest on night duty quietly alerted Sora nearby. Sora looked up, noticed the warlock, and hurried over.

"Jace. Were you looking for me?"

"Strictly speaking, I wasn't." Jace kept his voice low. "Let's talk outside."

The green dragon nodded. They stepped into the corridor outside.

"Were you wondering about Vereesa?" Sora asked.

"Ah, right. Her..." Jace ran a hand through his hair. "How is she?"

"I might not be the best teacher when it comes to nature magic, Jace." Sora said. "But I know this much — whether in the forest or the plains, everything natural I've ever seen loves Vereesa. And she enjoys being protected by it, even if she doesn't realize it herself."

"Maybe when we get back to Azeroth, she could go to High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind in Astranaar for guidance. Lady Tyrande has been wanting to meet you both. But every time I mentioned her name before... Vereesa would change the subject with a sour look."

"Doesn't surprise me at all," Jace said. "Changing these elves' — especially elven nobles' — attitudes toward their Kalimdor cousins is no easy task. Let alone asking for their guidance."

Sora sighed slightly. "That's not why you came, is it?"

"It's about the black dragons." Jace explained. "I had a brief talk with Sabellian last time. He was very firm. He won't let us take Needleflame or any of the black dragons born here back with us."

"He doesn't trust us," Sora said.

Jace considered this. "Hard to say if it's us he doesn't trust, or his own siblings. But I brought Needleflame with me. She wanted to see you."

"See me? For what?" Sora asked, surprised.

"She wants to learn the shapeshifting spell from you," Jace said.

Sora frowned slightly. "This isn't your idea, is it? Get her to agree to come back with us in exchange for teaching her?"

"Is that how you see me, Sora?" Jace asked. "Taking advantage of children?"

"Don't forget, we were together the whole time in Shadowforge City, Jace. I saw how you treated orcs and dwarves both." Sora smiled.

"Alright, I did think about it at first." Jace gave an embarrassed smile. "But in the end, I decided to just teach her for real, Sora."

"That whelp has a temper and she's sensitive. Even if she agreed to come back to Azeroth in exchange for learning to shapeshift, if she changes her mind later and feels like I tricked her — that's planting an unstable seed. It would slowly twist her mind."

"And she's a black dragon, not some ordinary beast or mortal. There are things in the shadows back in Azeroth that would love to exploit her resentment. I'd rather she someday willingly comes to you to see Alexstrasza, not because her father ordered it, or because she's fulfilling a deal with me. So... try not to bring up taking her back to Azeroth."

Sora studied his eyes. "I understand, Jace. I'll do what you say."

Even with the expedition allied to the black dragons, you couldn't let these black beasts run rampant around the Church buildings. So before going to find Sora, Jace had told Needleflame to wait outside.

The whelp was crouched beneath an ancient orc wall, her amber eyes floating like will-o'-wisps in the darkness.

The moment she spotted Sora, she crawled over like an alert predatory lizard, folding her wings close. She craned her neck, sniffing gently at the green dragon's neck and hands.

"Sister Somaliel... your transformation barely gives off any dream-dragon aura. How do you do it? Even my father can't."

"Your father and his kind don't lack the ability. They lack the will." Sora said. "We change shape to move unseen, to enter tight spaces, or to observe mortal society and learn their cultures. Concealing our presence is the most important part. You black dragons only transform to show off your power and enslave other races."

"So you have to promise me — once you learn to shapeshift, you can't use it for evil..."

"I promise." Needleflame answered instantly.

"Completely unconvincing," Jace said.

"I agree." Sora sighed.

"Though it'll probably take a few centuries before she can do any real harm." Jace leaned against the nearby wall. "Teach her. Whether she learns or not, at least she'll stop bothering us. It's not like we'd miss one more black dragon running around causing trouble in mortal form."

Sora shot him a look. Needleflame was already swishing her tail eagerly back and forth.

"For many dragons, shapeshifting is an innate ability, Needleflame." Sora said. "If you've never mastered it, it's probably because you don't know yourself well enough."

Needleflame said. "Father says we should be able to shapeshift more easily in Draenor after its destruction. In the mountains farther north, many of my elders who couldn't do it in Azeroth mastered the ability after Draenor was shattered. They can transform into arakkoa, even ogres or orcs."

"Because the endless shadow power pouring in through the Scepter of Sargeras gave you a boost." Sora said. "Now. I'll try to transform slowly. See if you can learn anything from it."

"Yes!" Needleflame said excitedly.

Sora first reverted to dragon form in a swirl of mist. Even as a young dragon, she was easily twice Needleflame's length, making the whelp look pathetically small beneath her shadow.

Before the mist cleared, the green dragon's wings, long tail, and horns scattered like dandelion seeds into the fog, her body melting and shrinking back into elven form.

The continuous transformation had clearly drained her, though. She looked noticeably tired.

"I can't feel what I'm doing wrong..." Needleflame said. "No matter how I try, it won't work."

"Try it. Let Sora see what's wrong." Jace wiggled his fingers.

Needleflame bobbed her head like a mortal nodding, then backed up and spread her wings.

Jace could almost immediately sense her deliberate emotional shift. This whelp could actually channel her emotions to control shadow. Of course. Black dragons were natural shadow-manipulators. A child raised by Sabellian himself was bound to be special.

But even as Needleflame gritted her teeth and snarled, gathering shadow energy around her, her intense emotions churning into rolling waves of darkness that even made Jace lower his folded arms — she still couldn't seem to find the key point of transformation.

In the end, she slumped, coughing out a few puffs of cinders and sparks. Her body sagged, her wings drooping.

"I don't know what's wrong either..." Sora said. "Have you learned from other black dragons?"

"Just the lady who went into the Dragonmaw fortress with you." Needleflame sounded dejected. "Am I really just incapable of learning, like my father said, Sister Somaliel?"

"It's too early to say, Needleflame." Sora tried to comfort her. "As you grow older, you might suddenly grasp the trick one day. You're growing faster than the other dragons. That day will come soon."

"But not as fast as that void dragon..." Needleflame whispered.

Jace frowned. He suddenly realized something he'd overlooked.

"What if it's because you shouldn't rely on shadow power to transform, like other black dragons? Try asking the fire and earth elementals for help."

"Yes, Jace is right." Sora's eyes lit up. "You were born in Draenor, Needleflame."

The black dragon's reptilian eyes darted between the two of them. She said nothing, but lowered her head, her expression growing more and more serious.

Flames spurted from her nostrils. The ground trembled beneath her feet. Currents of air stirred up dust and sand. A passing dwarf guard felt something wrong and glanced their way. Jace turned and nodded at him. The dwarf turned and walked off.

When Jace looked back, Needleflame was surrounded by a whirlwind of burning earth. This power wasn't like Gelorior's or Sabellian's transformations — dark and shadowy. This was almost pure elemental cyclone. If there was any shadow in it, it was born from the whelp's swelling excitement and pride...

When the hot earth subsided, a vibrant young girl stood before them.

She wore a form-fitting tunic, brown-black leather boots, and a gray-black cloak that looked heavy for her small frame. Her skin was a deep wheat tone. Her eyes glowed with an eerie amber light. Her teeth were a mess of crooked white, and with her sturdy, healthy build, she looked like a little bandit who'd run away from home before she'd even lost her baby fat.

"I did it! I finally did it! Sister Somaliel!"

She stumbled forward two steps, then threw herself into Sora's arms, her voice still carrying that whelp-like rasp. Sora stroked her wild black hair. "You should thank him, not me."

Needleflance stole a glance at Jace. Shuffling awkwardly, she walked over and mimicked the handshake she'd seen the expedition leaders use at the meeting, putting on a serious expression. "Thank you for the advice, Jace Sesso. I apologize for saying you mate with demons all the time."

Jace coughed several times. He shot a guilty look at the green dragon, who was barely holding back laughter, then shook Needleflame's hand with appropriate formality.

"Your tail's showing."
