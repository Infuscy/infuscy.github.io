Chapter 1320: Cracking

In the days that followed, the training ground at Wildhammer Keep often saw a small child in a dark gray cloak darting across it at a run.

This wasn't like Alleria's Keep, where children born here played and made noise all day. So having a kid suddenly appear struck both the mages, soldiers, and Church priests as something novel.

Even the black and red dragons perched on the cliffs above poked their heads out curiously to watch the strange little creature below. To them, whether she was cute or not was secondary -- the real surprise was that she'd already learned to shapeshift while still carrying that hatchling's scent. That put her not far behind children raised personally by the Dragon Aspects themselves.

Around this time, the dwarven knights and mages who'd suffered lighter injuries in the battle at Dragonmaw Fortress were being discharged one after another, lightening Sora's workload considerably. She now had plenty of free time to look after -- or rather, keep an eye on -- the little one. At this very moment she stood at the infirmary entrance, watching.

Needleflame was charging toward the gryphon roost. The gryphons resting inside sensed something wrong and lifted their wings nervously, screeching as if to drive the danger away. Jace was weighing whether he should intervene before things got out of hand when a more obvious "danger" approaching nearby made him reconsider.

"I know what you're planning." Sabellian walked up slowly beside him. "She's not a traditional black dragon. You want to use her good nature, earn her trust bit by bit, and eventually have her follow you back to Azeroth -- driven by curiosity about her origins, or by the dependency you've deliberately cultivated..."

"You know she's more traditional than most black dragons in Azeroth," Jace said.

Sabellian paused. From the gryphon roost came a sudden yelp -- Needleflame had fallen.

The child still couldn't control human legs and feet very well. She'd tumbled onto her back. At the sound, Sabellian whipped his head around, locking onto her like a startled hawk.

In that instant, Jace saw the connection between this slightly arrogant black-haired man and the black dragon he'd encountered deep in Dragonmaw Fortress.

The old dragon kept his brow furrowed until Needleflame scrambled up, dusted off her hands, and happily ran toward the gryphon roost again. Only then did he relax. The gryphons, of course, were far from relaxed.

"You know when I found her, she was about to become a Terokkar spider matriarch's snack?" Jace asked.

Sabellian glanced at him sideways, moving his lips before answering: "A black dragon whelp eaten by spiders... so be it. And she's not the only new whelp born in Draenor. Blade's Edge Mountains are full of them."

"If you care about them so little, why won't you let even one go back to Azeroth?" Jace asked.

Sabellian turned to face him. "We finally escaped that cursed world and came here. Yes, it's broken -- more shattered than my father's armor. But no matter how barren it is here, no matter how many enemies we have, no matter how miserably we might die -- this is a purer place. Here we no longer serve fear and death. We don't have to act against our will..."

The Black Dragon Prince seemed to realize he'd said too much. He took a deep breath. "Fine. Needleflame does mean a great deal to me, and I'm very grateful you rescued her from that spider nest. But don't be naive enough to think you can protect her. And I don't understand why you're so invested in the red dragons' requests."

"Queen Alexstrasza will reward me," Jace said.

The anger Sabellian had been building up sentence by sentence shattered completely at that. He let out a laugh. "What are you planning to ask her for?"

"I haven't decided yet." Jace said. "But I have a few options. Sending her flight to help the Alliance attack Blackrock Spire, for one."

"Blackrock Spire." The smile froze on Sabellian's face. "That place taken by Blackhand. Has Nefarian already claimed it? I know my father gave that narcissist the order before he came to Draenor."

"Yes." Jace said. "The Blackrock Spire orcs, along with your brother and his legion, are threatening Stormwind. Threatening my home. Why else do you think I came to Draenor? The Alliance back home doesn't have the strength to assault Blackrock Spire again."

"You're not from Elwynn. You're from Lordaeron." Sabellian said. "Why would you settle in Stormwind of all places? It's close to the Dark Portal, close to Blackrock Mountain, just a few peaks away from the Gurubashi trolls. You deliberately chose a miserable spot."

"Maybe I just like making terrible decisions," Jace said. "Right now I'm trapped in Draenor with you. Compared to that, moving to Stormwind doesn't seem so bad anymore."

Sabellian smirked, stroking his beard. "You're a very interesting fellow, Jace Sesso. But even if the Expedition makes it back to Azeroth, you'll have a hard time defeating Nefarian. One of my sisters is helping him in secret, and you can't handle her. If I let Needleflame go back with you, she'll end up in that one's hands for sure."

Jace pulled the Bronzebeard dwarf-crafted Dragon's Blood Pendant from his shirt and held it out. Sabellian took the mithril chain, examined the pendant, and his expression turned cold.

"She's dead?"

"No." Jace said. "But she lost an arm and fled to Kalimdor. She disguised herself as the Queen of Stormwind, trying to take control of the entire kingdom, but she failed in the end."

It was hard to read what the Black Dragon Prince was feeling now -- relief, inner conflict, or something else. Even Jace couldn't pinpoint the old dragon's exact emotions.

When talking about Nefarian, his tone carried annoyance. But Onyxia was different.

Jace knew that compared to Nefarian's relentless attempts to create new dragon breeds from black dragon eggs, Onyxia at least valued her own children. Maybe she and Sabellian weren't entirely without common ground.

"Here's the deal, Jace Sesso." The Black Dragon Prince said. "I'll tell her why Alexstrasza wants her back. Tell her what she'll face when she returns. Then I'll let her choose for herself. I don't want to make Draenor into another cage with my own hands. If she understands the stakes and still wants to go with you, I won't stop her."

Jace asked, "How will you describe it to her? The same way you described the other side when you told her I spend every day carousing with demons?"

"What, am I wrong?" Sabellian asked with a challenging lilt.

"Not every day," Jace said.

Sabellian chuckled, holding up the Dragon's Blood Pendant. "Mind if I keep this as a souvenir from Azeroth? The Bronzebeard dwarves do fine work."

"If I don't give it to you, you won't let her go?" Jace asked.

Sabellian snorted. "You think one drop of that female dragon's blood is worth my daughter? Those two things aren't connected." Jace snatched the pendant back. "Then no."

Under the Prince's gaze, Jace tucked the pendant back into his shirt, then walked down the stairs toward Dreg's Tavern. He was supposed to meet Grede for a drink around noon.

Although Sabellian had somewhat relented, Jace still couldn't be sure Needleflame would actually want to return to Azeroth.

For the Expedition soldiers, Azeroth was home. For Needleflame, it was a completely foreign place. If she learned about the black dragons' situation back there, she'd probably be even less willing to go.

But staying in Draenor had its advantages too. Like Sabellian said, Deathwing had searched far and wide and actually found them a place to call home.

Assuming they defeated the Burning Legion first.

That was why Sabellian was so eager to ally with the Expedition. Defeating the Burning Legion's demons was the most critical prerequisite for his Blackwing Legion to rule Outland.

Jace hadn't even made it down the stairs when a horn blast rang out from atop the fortress wall.

He looked up. Someone above shouted, "It's Captain Alleria and General Danath Trollbane! They've arrived at our keep!"
