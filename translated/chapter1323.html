Chapter 1323: Setting Out for Hellfire Citadel

The Farstrider squad sent north to Blade's Edge Mountains to find Velen had still not reported in.

But it had only been about a month since they'd departed. Considering that dragonhawks were slower than Sora, that traveling through the treacherous terrain of Blade's Edge -- with its constant up-and-down, and gronn roaming everywhere -- was incredibly difficult, and that Blade's Edge was farther than Nagrand on the Expedition's maps, with the added leg around the Zangar Sea...

Jace and the others had taken over a month just to travel to Nagrand and back. It was too early to panic.

But that Farstrider squad wasn't just looking for Velen. They were also supposed to make contact with Kirin'Var Village and Archmage Vargoth. As long as they hadn't returned, Kirin'Var Village was still quiet.

The Expedition at Wildhammer Keep couldn't just sit around waiting for a reaction from the north before acting. So now, as Sabellian had said, they had to rely on the black dragons' magic.

After discussion, Gorilor was assigned to disguise himself as a Bonechewer clan orc and scout around the Ruins of Ambore for information. Sabellian would head to Hellfire Citadel with Jace. There was one adult female dragon who could shapeshift, but she still had to guard and protect the young black dragons -- the Blackwing couldn't deploy their full force.

"Your cloak is ruined. How are you going to turn into an orc?" Grede asked, raising his tankard.

"Sabellian can transform me." Jace shoved a pastry into his mouth. "But I won't be able to cast spells like with the old cloak. Kind of like that orb Vaelastrasz gave me that I lost. Magic interference might expose the disguise. The upside is, as long as he's around, he can fix the magic anytime, so I don't have to worry about it wearing off."

"Are you sure you don't need me along, Jace?" Sora asked.

"Wildhammer Keep needs you more," Jace said. "And Needleflame needs you too. I'm not sure this is the best thing to say, but if you want her to understand Queen Alexstrasza's intentions... you should spend more time with her. Besides, we're not going there to fight. And if it does come to a fight, with Sabellian beside me, do you really think those fel orcs in Hellfire Citadel could stop us from escaping?"

"You're right." Sora shook her head. "Maybe it's just prejudice. I'm having a really hard time fully trusting Prince Sabellian."

"Don't worry, Sora. His eggs and whelps are all in our hands." Grede laughed. "If he tries any tricks, he can come back to Oreba fried eggs!"

Sora shuddered at the mention of that dish, but knowing that the black dragon's leverage really was in the Expedition's hands calmed her a bit. Her expression and feelings were a tangle by the end.

"But I'm still surprised that black dragon agreed so readily to Marshal Turalyon's infiltration plan. Even offered to help with magic." The dwarf took a sip of ale, wiped the foam from his beard, and said, "Think about it. Not long ago they were at the end of their rope, but they were still dragging their feet about allying with us... I heard from Vereesa that Sabellian's subordinate even gave everyone a scare when he came to Wildhammer Keep to negotiate the alliance!"

"Prince Sabellian has always enjoyed mingling among mortals. Even my kind knows that." Sora said. "If you ask me, Deathwing disguising himself as that Alterac noble to infiltrate the Lordaeron Alliance was probably his idea too. He was imprisoned by the orcs for so long -- now that he has a chance for revenge, he won't miss it."

"I still think we should wait for Archmage Vargoth to return," Vereesa said. "They say his magic can even teach a person orcish. Then not just Sora and me, but even Grede could go with you. That would be safer."

"I'd prefer that too. But we don't know what's happening up north. We can't just sit here waiting. Turalyon feels the same way," Jace said.

"But are you sure Archmage Vargoth's magic can really teach someone orcish?"

"At least that's what Alleria says," Vereesa said.

"There are spells that can teach a person other languages," Sora said. "Archmage Vargoth might have combined different spells and incantations in clever ways..."

"Then all that effort I put into learning orcish was wasted," Jace said.

"Spells always have flaws, Jace," Sora said.

Aside from the Hellfire Citadel mission, everyone chatted about other things that evening. But the overall atmosphere was a bit subdued.

After all, this group had gotten used to going out together, cooperating to solve problems, sharing the danger. Having Jace go alone this time -- unable to even bring Morophil because the power of the Book of Fel Names was too obvious -- brought back some unpleasant memories. Coincidentally, it involved facing a terrifying black dragon and a mob of orcs...

Though it was an infiltration mission and shouldn't lead to conflict if things went smoothly, Jace still prepared basic mana potions and Shadow Protection Potions, just in case.

When it was time to leave, he arrived at the keep's central training ground. Sabellian was already waiting.

He looked Jace over. "The cloak I told you to bring?"

"Here." Jace patted the bundle he was carrying. "You're not going to make me put it on under the eyes of all these red dragons, are you?"

"Why not?" Sabellian said. "You're so close with the red dragons. Perfect time to wear a red dragon's hide and join them."

The black dragon laughed smugly. Jace said, "You had me bring this cloak just for that stupid joke?"

Sabellian's eyes rolled, as if disappointed the fun had been spoiled. "Of course not. In Hellfire Citadel, wearing a cloak made of red dragon skin is a status symbol. It tells everyone you're a conqueror of Azeroth, a slave master of red dragons, the warden of the Life Queen Alexstrasza... You know why the Dragonmaw Skybreakers have such high status in the Horde? That's why."

"I really can't trust you," Jace said. "Who knows if you're just setting me up for a laugh."

"Everything I said is true." Sabellian said. "But I also want to see you make a fool of yourself."

Jace shot him a helpless look. The black dragon continued, "So for the sake of making our cover more convincing and gaining access to higher-level secrets inside Hellfire Citadel... will you wear the cloak or not?"

"I'll wear it when the time comes, damn it." Jace said. "But this is Orastrasz's hide, made by your father himself. Won't it be too recognizable among the Fel Horde?"

"Orastrasz was nearly as big as me. You don't think this was the only good piece of hide on his whole body, do you?" Sabellian said. "After my father took this piece, Dragonmaw Fortress used the rest of his body to make other cloaks. Some were lost or destroyed, some remain. Zuluhed kept this one as a personal treasure. Not many people inside Dragonmaw Fortress knew about it, so rest assured."

Jace looked at the bundle in his hands and nodded. "Alright. Let's go, then."

The moment the words left his mouth, the black dragon was engulfed in a whirlwind of sand and flame. A dark vortex spiraled upward, and from it emerged a dragon's claw the size of a tent, snatching Jace up. Then he felt a violent spinning of heaven and earth...

When he came to his senses, he was sitting on a jagged stone platform.

This wasn't a platform at all -- it was Sabellian's back. Jace grabbed onto the purplish-red membrane-like fin that rose like a wall from the dragon's spine, trying to look around. But the creature was so wide he couldn't see the ground below. Everything around them was dark chaos.

The massive wings on either side beat slowly, raising swirling black winds. The air reeked of fel, the gusts so powerful Jace could barely make out his own shouted words.

"Thanks a lot, Sabellian! I thought you were going to carry me all the way to Hellfire Citadel like that!"

Hearing this, Sabellian reduced the amplitude of his wingbeats. The airflow eased slightly. In a deep, rumbling voice, he said, "Don't tell anyone you've ever sat on my back, Jace Sesso."

"I promise I won't!"

Even as he shouted it, Jace's mind was already racing with how he'd brag to Grede about riding the Black Dragon Prince's back once they returned to Wildhammer Keep. Grede's reaction would be priceless.

No way he was passing up a chance to show off like that.

At most he'd make sure Grede didn't tell anyone. And Vereesa. And Sora. And his mom and dad. And Amy...

...Fine, maybe just Amy.

"One thing I didn't make clear!" Jace shouted, steadying himself against the bones between the wing membranes. "Don't we need to come up with identities?"

"Dragonmaw orcs, of course," the black dragon said.

"You're not worried some survivors from Dragonmaw Fortress might have escaped to Hellfire Citadel and expose us?" Jace asked.

"Why would I be worried?" The Black Dragon Prince laughed smugly. "We know more about what really happened than any Dragonmaw orc who survived. So who's telling the truth and who's lying... well, that's up for debate."

"Besides, Jace Sesso."

Sabellian turned his head, and Jace finally caught a glimpse of that terrifying great eye glowing with sickly yellow magical light, half-hidden in the darkness ahead.

The reason he couldn't see the ground or the sky clearly was because Sabellian had already flown into the gray clouds of Shadowmoon Valley...

"You've been to Grim Batol. You've met Zuluhed's lieutenant, Nekros. I know why Vaelastrasz gave you that enchanted cloak that defiled the black dragons. Don't think I haven't figured out how you tricked them. So when it comes to playing the part of a Dragonmaw orc... I don't think I need to say much more to you, do I?"
