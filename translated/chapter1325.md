## Chapter 1325: The Hellfire Ramparts

Tall watchtowers dotted the route at regular intervals all the way to Hellfire Citadel. Sabellian knew exactly where each one was -- after all, when the Alliance Expedition first stormed through the Dark Portal, he and his black dragons had helped the orcs defend Hellfire Citadel.

After traveling across the red wasteland for most of a day, Jace finally spotted a stretch of black walls on the horizon. The spiked towers atop those walls looked like demon heads, even flickering with specks of baleful flame, watching them like evil eyes.

"How do we get in?" Jace asked.

"I haven't been here since Ner'zhul left," Sabellian said. "But the Expedition blew several holes in Hellfire's walls with Ironforge tanks and cannons. We should have plenty of options."

Sabellian was right. As they drew closer to Hellfire Citadel, the path was increasingly littered with abandoned fortifications, collapsed towers and walls, and dwarf tanks stripped down to scrap. The earthquake-like rumbles from deep within this "peninsula" of the Twisting Nether told the story of the war and chaos that had engulfed this place years ago.

The stretch of wall Jace had seen earlier was only part of Hellfire Citadel's massive fortifications. The entire wall stretched across the lowlands, reaching another towering structure in the distance. From here, it was just a vague, dark silhouette, nearly blending into the black sky threaded with shadow energy.

The citadel's main structure stood behind the walls. Part of it had collapsed. Countless fel orcs were hauling building materials from the collapsed sections to repair the parts still standing.

But even this ruined fortress was profoundly awe-inspiring to Jace -- like a wounded dark beast crouching low, covered in spikes and steel-plated skin, still possessing terrifying power despite its injuries.

In the game, Jace had been inside Hellfire Citadel both before and after its destruction, many times. But even being this familiar with the place, he couldn't quite match the game version to its current state.

Standing before these massive walls, feeling the foul, anxious wind from the canyon, looking up at the fel orc guards staring down from the battlements, hearing the ringing of steel and barking commands echo around him... The pressure was something entirely different from seeing it through a screen.

It was hard to imagine how the Expedition had stormed a fortress like this in just a few days, with black dragons and fel orcs holding the line. But considering that the place was built to project power into Azeroth, the main Expedition force coming through the Dark Portal would have come in at their strongest, driven by the deepest hatred, and hurled themselves at this source of invasion...

"Follow me, Nai'jagg." Sabellian pointed at a sloped high ground nearby. "That entrance is still intact. We'll go through there."

"Zog -- zog," Jace replied.

The black dragon's lip curled. He turned and headed up the slope. Jace followed.

Quite a few fel orc soldiers sat in hide tents below the walls, watching the two unfamiliar warlocks climb the slope. But no one stopped them. No one called out.

Jace was starting to think Sabellian's disguise might let them slip right into the citadel when a shout like a firecracker exploded from above.

"Halt! You two -- I don't know you!"

On the high wall, a massive fel orc in blood-red claw-like pauldrons was staring down at them.

"Strength and honor, esteemed Watcher Gargom." Sabellian looked up. "Last time I had the fortune of seeing you, you didn't have so many teeth yet."

Gargom. Jace vaguely remembered the name. The orc called Gargom looked suspicious at first, then climbed over the wall and dropped directly to the ground in front of the entrance.

His heavy armor crashed like a pile of scrap metal as he landed. When he straightened up, revealing the weapons that replaced his hands -- a scimitar and a battle axe -- Jace suddenly remembered who this was.

He was the wall's guardian. Watcher Gargom, a Shattered Hand taskmaster. Also Kargath Bladefist's most trusted lieutenant.

"You don't deserve to say 'Strength and honor,' warlocks." Gargom's blood-red demonic eye swept back and forth across the two orcs. Jace noticed his skin was so red it was nearly black, and his build was half again as large as the Shattered Hand soldiers guarding the wall beside him. He'd clearly consumed as much Magtheridon's blood as Warchief Moghul.

"For the Horde. For the Warchief. Give me your names and origins. Every warlock in Hellfire Citadel is recorded. But I don't recall you two being on the list."

"Nai'horgar, Chief Zuluhed's most loyal servant." Sabellian said. "And this is Nai'jagg. He may look hideous from Magtheridon's blood, but he enjoys our chieftain's trust as well."

Gargom laughed loudly, then cut off abruptly. He cocked his head. "Wait. Zuluhed... You're Dragonmaw?"

He squinted, studying their attire closely. Finally, his gaze settled on the crimson cloak draped over Jace's shoulders. "You broke through the Alliance blockade?"

At that, Jace and Sabellian exchanged a glance.

Did that mean there were no Dragonmaw clan orcs in Hellfire Citadel at all right now? At the very least, Gargom was surprised that orcs had managed to travel from Shadowmoon Valley to Hellfire Peninsula. Which meant that even if there were Dragonmaw orcs in this fortress, none had come from Dragonmaw Fortress yet.

Which also meant... they had no idea what was happening in Shadowmoon Valley.

Actually, that made sense. The entire route was within the patrol range of gryphon riders and rangers from Honor Hold, Alleria's Keep, and Wildhammer Keep.

Even if Dragonmaw orcs or messengers from Karabor were heading this way, they'd have to avoid the patrols and sneak in carefully. There was no way they could match the speed of a black dragon like Sabellian, who was bold enough to fly near the void.

But if orcs couldn't get through, couldn't demons?

After that exchange of glances, Jace looked up at the wall behind them. There were no demons up there. No demon guards or doomguard in sight.

"Yes, we were sent by Chieftain Zuluhed." Sabellian turned back. "Karabor is in grave danger, Watcher. The Alliance scum could attack us at any moment..."

Before the black dragon could finish, Gargom growled, "Everyone knows Gul'dan's Black Temple is in danger. The demons lost their precious registry. What a joke!"

"Those eredar are all flashy, useless trash." The Shattered Hand taskmaster laughed loudly. "The Burning Legion ambassador to Hellfire Citadel has already slunk back to the Throne of Kil'jaeden to beg his master for advice. Warchief Kargath Bladefist will soon prove that even without the Legion's help, we can crush that ridiculous pink-skin castle to the south!"

The Throne of Kil'jaeden was a towering peak in northern Hellfire Peninsula, the highest point in the entire region. Couldn't see it clearly from here.

Gul'dan had named the place after Kil'jaeden, building an altar there for communicating with the demon lord. It was where the orcs had drunk Mannoroth's blood, turning their skin green, their eyes glowing with furious crimson, taking the first step toward becoming demon servants.

The demon army that had invaded Azeroth through the Dark Portal years ago had likely been summoned through the altar at the Throne of Kil'jaeden. But with the Dark Portal destroyed by Khadgar and countless demons trapped in Azeroth to be eliminated, the demons at the Throne of Kil'jaeden should have receded considerably.

"But Dragonmaw Fortress is right next to Karabor." Jace said. "Chieftain Zuluhed believes the Alliance has allied with those treacherous black dragons. They'll definitely try to rescue the black dragon leader first -- that cunning, vicious beast named Sabellian..."

Sabellian shot Jace a sideways glare. Gargom said, "Zuluhed thinks so?"

"Yes, Watcher." Sabellian quickly added. "This is the most critical moment. I know that Warchief Kargath has been determined to acquire red dragons and build his own 'Skybreaker' force. The Chieftain has always kept the Warchief's command in mind, and has already made progress."

"He speaks truly." Jace picked up the thread. "The Chieftain is creating a new dragon legion... The breeding experiments are at their most crucial stage. We already have a good enough specimen -- a completely obedient experimental dragon with sufficient power. If the experiments are disrupted now, the Warchief's plan will never be realized."

"So Chieftain Zuluhed urgently sent us to meet with Warchief Kargath." Sabellian said. "The Chieftain believes only he knows the key to saving our clan and the dragons. Only he knows how to foil those treacherous, shameless Alliance schemes."

"But our fortress cannot reinforce Dragonmaw Fortress," Gargom said.

"Because there are other means, Watcher." Sabellian said. "But the Chieftain ordered us to speak only with Warchief Kargath."

"We are Chieftain Zuluhed's most trusted servants." Jace stared into the taskmaster's blood-red eyes. "We used who knows how many dark magics just to slip past those gryphon riders and elven rangers. We patiently maneuvered around those flying black-scaled lizards in Terokkar Forest, all for a chance to meet Warchief Kargath. Please believe us. Any later and it'll be too late."

Gargom nodded. "Chieftain Zuluhed's most trusted lieutenants all wear dragon's fang pendants. Let me see yours."

"Look at my cloak, Watcher." Jace said. "You know what this represents. That should be enough to prove I'm a confidant who returned from Grim Batol with Chieftain Zuluhed himself."

"Fair enough." Gargom nodded, turning to Sabellian. "Yours."

Sabellian stared at Jace, his lips twitching. Then, frowning, he pulled a tooth fragment bound by enchanted chain from beneath his robe and handed it to Gargom.

The orc taskmaster examined the fragment. "Damn. This is as fresh as if it were pulled straight from a dragon's mouth."

"Exactly." Sabellian smiled. "My previous pendant was lost in a fight with a human mage who'd dabbled in some dark magic. I used every trick I had to beat him. In the end, I drove that dragon fang into his eye socket so deep I couldn't pull it out. That blind, crippled fool fled in a panic, dragging my dragon fang pendant with him as he fell into the void and got torn into a lump of black meat. A real shame."

"So before I left, Taskmaster Moghul had the smiths craft me a new badge like this, to earn the trust of Hellfire Citadel. That's why it looks new."

"This is definitely a dragon's tooth. Genuine." Gargom's attitude shifted markedly. He turned and shouted up at the wall, "High-rank Dragonmaw warlocks have arrived, bearing news from Karabor! Notify Warchief Kargath Bladefist!"

"Follow me."

Gargom started up the wall. Jace glanced at the black dragon beside him, took a deep breath, and swallowed the laughter threatening to burst out. "Don't get cocky, little wretch. It'll grow back faster than you think," Sabellian muttered quietly.
