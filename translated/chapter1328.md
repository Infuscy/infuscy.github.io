## Chapter 1328: The Ethereal Envoy

The gladiator matches at the War-Bringer's Ring were still going. The orcs were shouting again, but the ethereal never reappeared.

Sabellian arranged to meet Jace near the stables at the Shattered Halls entrance after the matches ended, then dove straight into the crowd of orcs.

Jace had no doubt he'd find some fel orc playmates. Even earlier, when they'd been sitting to watch Kargath fight other gladiators, several female orcs had been drawn to the black dragon while completely ignoring Jace.

Given the guy's human form, his orc appearance was probably quite appealing too. And he'd definitely given Jace something ugly by orc standards.

Sabellian had claimed it was "for the credibility of the infiltration -- a celibate fel orc would raise suspicion." Jace wasn't inclined to argue.

But the ethereal's presence meant Jace couldn't focus on anything else. He'd discussed contacting the ethereals with Turalyon back at the keep.

Now that they'd allied with the black dragons, how could they not reach out to these magic-wielding beings who specialized in traveling between dimensions? Maybe they'd have unique insights into the power of the Scepter of Sargeras, or the Legion's lockdown of all Draenor.

He made his way out of the Shattered Halls but couldn't find the ethereal anywhere. The light on the walls was starting to dim -- perhaps even Hellfire Peninsula had a day-night cycle.

Before long, Jace spotted Gargom returning from the other end of the wall with two orc guards. A warg handler stopped him midway, and they chatted for a bit.

When their conversation ended, Jace walked up. "We meet again, Watcher Gargom."

"Nai'jagg." Gargom said. "I was just thinking that after you two finished with the Warchief, I could ask you a favor. About those new dragon breeds you mentioned... the ones Chieftain Zuluhed is cultivating. When there are actual results, I hope you'll pick me an obedient, battle-brave whelp as my mount. What do you say?"

"Of course." Jace said. "The high taskmasters of Hellfire Citadel will be the first to become dragon riders. Even if you hadn't mentioned it, the Chieftain would remember."

"Sounds good." Watcher Gargom rumbled with laughter. "If you need anything in our fortress, come find me, Nai'jagg. I can see you're a reliable orc. At least more reliable than that Nai'horgar."

"Nai'horgar is still my friend, esteemed Watcher." Jace gave a slight orcish salute. "Actually, I do have something I need your help with right now. About that... Kareshi."

"You mean Kolez." Gargom said. "That void monster wrapped all over with cloth strips."

"Yes." Jace said. "His name is Kolez?"

"Yes." Gargom said. "He calls himself a 'Hath'ur,' sent by someone who calls himself 'Node Prince Haramad' to negotiate with Warchief Kargath."

Node Prince Haramad. Jace remembered the name. He was the leader of the Consortium. This one belonged to the Consortium, which meant he was one of the more reasonable ethereals. Lucky break.

"What do the ethereals want to negotiate with the Horde about?" Jace asked.

Gargom's blood-red eyes narrowed slightly, as if wary of this Dragonmaw orc's questions.

"My apologies, Watcher." Jace lowered his head slightly. "I only ask because I heard these outsiders possess some rare treasures not seen in Draenor. I was hoping to trade for something. I didn't mean to pry into the Warchief's affairs."

Gargom chuckled. "You're Chieftain Zuluhed's confidant, wearing the red dragon lord's hide. Knowing these things won't hurt. Besides, the Warchief didn't reach any agreement with that Node Prince anyway."

"These outsiders wanted to hire Horde soldiers to help them establish outposts in Blade's Edge Mountains and around Farahlon. But the Warchief saw right through them. They want us to act as mercenaries clearing out gronn and ogres... but they offered laborer wages."

"What did they offer?" Jace asked.

"That's not important." Gargom said. "What's important is that the Warchief understands perfectly well that these clever bugs are just as trapped here as the Alliance scum. So no matter what wealth and treasures they have, it'll all belong to the Horde in the end. We don't need to fight for them."

Gargom laughed loudly. "That one still doesn't get it. He insists on staying in the fortress, waiting for the Node Prince's 'further instructions.'"

Jace chuckled along, but Gargom's words disappointed him. So the ethereals were trapped in Draenor too.

If even they -- masters of dimensional travel -- couldn't leave, what other options were there?

Would he really have to go to Shattrath, pray with the Aldor priests, and hope for a Naaru's arrival?

Or pick up the Scepter of Sargeras.

The thought that had crossed his mind made Jace shudder.

But he knew that the more he learned about Draenor, the more he understood their current predicament, the more he'd realize the Scepter of Sargeras seemed like the only answer.

Because that was the path Kil'jaeden had chosen for him. No matter how you refused, no matter how clear-headed you were, you could only walk it step by step.

Was the scepter really at the Throne of Kil'jaeden?

He looked north at the hazy black sky. Fel-tinged darkness and void whirlpools wove together into terrible light shows. Even if he'd realized it early on, he was getting closer to that thing.

"If you want to see what that Hath'ur has worth trading, follow this path down. You'll see the little hut Kolez is temporarily using," Gargom said, pointing at a narrow path in the distance.

"Thank you very much, Gargom." Jace nodded slightly.

Whatever the case, he'd go talk to the ethereal. Whether to make the lie he'd told more convincing, or to report back to Turalyon about his contact with them when they returned.

It wasn't like he had anything else to do. It's not like he was going to follow Sabellian and hook up with female orcs. But a moment later, a question occurred to him.

Waiting for their prince's instructions? Meaning the ethereals could still communicate over long distances in the current situation?

Of course, even Jace could communicate with Kalrez using demonic rituals. But he remembered that in the game, ethereals used some kind of device capable of projecting three-dimensional images for long-distance communication. If he could get his hands on one of those devices, even if they couldn't return to Azeroth, it would be a huge help in coordinating between the Alliance's various outposts.

With that thought, his steps quickened.

Following the direction Gargom had indicated, Jace walked down the shadowy path until he reached the deep crevice between the fortress wall and the fortifications.

The ethereal had set up a small awning-like shelter there using copper-colored metal strips and dark blue fabric. Beneath it sat a small table, with a bit of arcane light burning on a metal stand, casting the whole crevice in pale purple.

Below the metal light stand sat a translucent purple energy cube. That must be the so-called "mana crystal lattice."

"You're Kolez," Jace said.

The ethereal in the bright gold-brown robe heard him, and the blue magical light in the gaps of his wrapped head brightened slightly. "Greetings, warlock."

The ethereal turned. His voice didn't sound like a living person's -- more like the synthesized speech of some gnomish machine, deliberately mimicking the Shattered Hand clan's orcish accent.

"I am Kolez, a Hath'ur of the Consortium. How may I help you?"

"I'm Nai'jagg, a Dragonmaw warlock. You might have heard me and my companion at the War-Bringer's Ring," Jace said.

"Yes," the ethereal replied.

"I heard you're waiting for your leader's further instructions," Jace said. "You can still communicate even in Draenor's current state?"

"There are many difficulties, but we can manage," Kolez said.

"My clan is very interested in your method of communication," Jace said. "Would it be possible for you to share this..."

"As a condition of trade?" The magical light in the ethereal's body flickered slightly. "Perhaps... if you and your clan provide sufficient assistance to the Consortium, the Node Prince might consider it."

"Then how do I speak with this Node Prince?" Jace asked.

"I am currently... recharging my tool, warlock." The ethereal pointed at an exquisite glass disk on the ground, purple arcane light flickering across its surface.

"Please wait a moment."
