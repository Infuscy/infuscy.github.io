Chapter 1330: Back to the Mountains at Last

The dragon soared high above, and once they left Hellfire Peninsula behind, the weight of fel energy lightened. Stretching across the view were rolling red-brown hills and enormous briars that fought against the vile wind blowing from the east with their own bodies, forming a "forest" like a vast web of barbed wire.

Amidst the rustling chirps of insects that rose and fell, Jace lowered the map in his hand and looked out past the distant mountains to the west. The deep ink-blue revealed nothing clearly, but he knew it was the direction of the Zangar Sea.

"I hear there are draenei villages built on the shores of the Zangar Sea," Jace called out. "Since many of your kin live in Blade's Edge Mountains, have you ever ventured deep into the Zangar Sea to have a look?"

Sabellian flapped his massive wings twice, leveling his flight speed slightly. "Are you sure those coastal villages could survive the orcs' hunting parties?" he said. "All I know is that there are many mushroom-islands out on the sea, and some draenei live on them. They figured orcs aren't good sailors, so some fled out to sea to avoid conflict with them. I saw this myself in Honor Hold — intelligence your expedition's rangers collected."

"So just how long did you stay in Honor Hold?" Jace asked.

"Long enough for several of Lady Alleria Windrunner's subordinates to fall madly in love with me, Jace Sesso." Sabellian said with considerable pride. "I guarantee you, if they hadn't died in Draenor's destruction and were still alive today, they'd still be pining over the unforgettable dreams that the charming Baron Sabermane gave them."

"Those dreams were far more exquisite than anything Queen Ysera could offer — what's the point of some flowers and grass you can barely make out even with your eyes wide open? I brought them pleasures they could never experience in centuries of dull existence..."

"I'm not interested in your romantic history with elves, Baron Sabermane," Jace said.

"You're sitting on my back right now," the black dragon muttered, turning his massive head sideways. "So listen up and shut up, or don't blame me if I force you to learn how to take flight using Orastrasz's hide in thirty seconds."

Jace glanced downward. "Alright, alright. I just want to know if you can take me to those islands in the Zangar Sea to find those draenei."

"It would take two more days to fly west from here to the Zangar Sea," Sabellian said with a hint of irritation. "And then search for a few mushrooms across the Zangar Sea, which is vaster than Nagrand? Are you sure? Do you want to go to Blade's Edge Mountains to the north, or the Zangar Sea?"

"Sorry, forget I said that," Jace said. "Let's focus on our current goal."

If the rangers already knew there were draenei villages on the Zangar Sea — namely Telredor — then if they were looking for Velen, they would have sent dragonhawk riders to search there immediately. If they could have found him, they should have had some result in the past month.

If there was no result, flying there himself with Sabellian would probably not turn up anything new either.

Flying northwest from Hellfire Peninsula, the further north they went, the worse the ground below became. The brown mountains, once thick with briars, gradually turned into vast, cracked black rockfaces undulating like waves. Dark void energy corroded them into layers of purple arcane light, reminding Jace of Farahlon from the game — the Netherstorm region.

They were still very far from Netherstorm. Even for Sabellian, it would take several more days to reach it. But this area was already being tormented into this state by the void. As for lands further north... he had no idea what they'd become.

The Black Dragon Prince showed no fear of the dark lightning churning beneath them or the ground that could collapse into floating islands at any moment. He even used the shadow magic to speed up his flight, gliding more effortlessly.

The further north they went, the more aggressive the rock "waves" thrown up by void energy became. The "tips" of those waves gradually turned into terrifying spikes, even slightly curved blades.

Every time Sabellian swooped past one of those towering spikes, Jace felt his scalp tingle, always expecting that a slight acceleration would send him crashing into one and piercing straight through his body.

After flying for just under two or three days, these blades and spikes filled the entire horizon. Sabellian climbed higher and higher, and they had reached Blade's Edge Mountains.

Jace had always been a bit puzzled. Even now, many races on Draenor still liked to call this place "Gorgrond," especially some older orcs and ogres.

But no matter the race, they all seemed to have tacitly agreed to give Gorgrond the nickname "Blade's Edge Mountains."

Even if there were slight differences across languages, they all roughly meant "mountains full of blades."

But when he saw with his own eyes the mountain rocks that jutted out like dense blades from a tight formation, all pointing diagonally at the dim sky, he couldn't easily find another word to describe it.

The dust of Blade's Edge Mountains was swept everywhere by the fel winds of the Twisting Nether. Before long, the black dragon carried Jace into an endless dark yellow haze.

Jace hugged one of the thick, sturdy horns on Sabellian's neck tightly, glancing at the map the expedition had given him — which nearly got sucked into the void, forcing him to stuff it back into his bag.

"Where does your clan live, Sabellian?" he shouted.

"Further north. Much further north than here," Sabellian said. "Why do you ask? The location the ethereal gave you doesn't need to pass through there. Don't even think about it — I won't give you a chance to sneak into our nest to steal unpolluted eggs. There have to be some boundaries in our cooperation, Jace Sesso!"

"I thought we'd built enough trust from the Hellfire Citadel trip!" Jace shouted forward.

"Trust?" Sabellian slightly bared his iron-lock reptilian lips. "You want me to trust a cunning warlock who lies through his teeth and doesn't show a single flicker of emotion when deceiving orcs? Do you think I'm that little girl who ranks among the most naive dragons in the entire Emerald Dream?"

Jace smiled. "Right back at you, Prince Sabellian."

"Anyway, are we in Blade's Edge Mountains now?"

"Yes," Sabellian said. "This was once Gorgrond. A bit further north and we'll enter the gronn's territory."

"I want to ask a favor. Find a relatively safe place to land."

"What for?"

"To fulfill a promise."

"Anywhere will do?"

"Yes."

After confirming, the Black Dragon Prince stopped flapping his massive wings and began descending slowly.

He carefully descended between the brown stone spikes, finally landing on a relatively open slope. Even so, he broke off one stone spike in the process. Rubble cascaded down, the rumbling echoes traveling far through the mountains.

Compared to Hellfire Peninsula, Blade's Edge Mountains still had some signs of life — shrubs and weeds everywhere, and glimpses of forests at the lower parts of the cliffs.

This was the homeland of the Thunderlord clan. Bazun's homeland.

Jace climbed down from the dragon's back and pulled out the stone carved with shamanic runes that he'd been carrying in his bag.

He remembered the day he'd received this stone in Frostwolf Village — snow filling the mountains, cold wind biting. Compared to this place, it felt like a lifetime ago. No, it really was a world away.

I hope that one day we can go home too, just like this stone. Thinking this, he bent down and buried the stone under a shrub.

"What's that?"

Sabellian had come up beside him, now in human form.

"A shaman gave it to me," Jace said. "He wanted me to bring this stone back here."

"An orc shaman?" Sabellian asked. "How are there still..."

"They live in seclusion in the snowy valleys of Alterac," Jace said. "The exiled Frostwolf clan. An orc from the Thunderlord clan was hiding among them."

"Ah, I know those outcasts. But you should know the Frostwolves and Thunderlords are ancient enemies," Sabellian said.

"I know," Jace said. "Like us and the black dragons."

Sabellian chuckled. "Did that orc promise you some reward too, like Alexstrasza?"

"No." Jace shook his head. "He did say this stone might help me. And I'm pretty sure the elements of Nagrand did give us a hand at Highmaul, but I don't know if it had anything to do with this stone."

The Black Dragon Prince's smile faded. He stared at the ground under the shrub in silence for a moment, then said, "Hard to imagine you helping an orc like this."

"I don't know why either," Jace said.

"If you really have the luck to make it back to the other side of the Dark Portal," the black dragon said, looking at him, "take Needleflame to the red dragons. I won't stand in the way anymore."

"You changed your mind?" Jace said.

"On the condition that she's still willing," Sabellian said. "Don't make me regret this."
