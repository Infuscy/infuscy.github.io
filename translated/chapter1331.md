## Chapter 1331: The Consortium's Plan

Sabellian flew all the way toward the location marked on the ethereal's scroll. Once they entered Blade's Edge Mountains, Jace scanned the brown-yellow wasteland slopes and cliffs, worried it might be hard to find the ethereal outpost. But as they approached the location, he immediately understood what the ethereal had meant by "that place will be very easy to find."

From a rough glance, the ethereal outpost looked like the camp of a high-tech colonial civilization stationed on a barren planet. Pale violet arcane lights, frames made of unknown metallic materials — it was visible from very far away.

The Black Dragon Prince landed on a cliff edge, observing the camp from a distance. "Are you planning to meet them as an orc?"

"I think I'll go as myself," Jace said. "After all, I'm representing Turalyon and the expedition soldiers. Let's not make this too complicated."

"But they're waiting for an orc," Sabellian said.

"Then they'll be waiting in vain," Jace said.

Sabellian smiled slightly and shifted into human form. The two walked down the slope together. As they entered a sparse forest, several pale-white lights flickered around them.

Several ethereals stepped out from these phantoms, each holding translucent glass-like glowing blades. Their forms flickered unsteadily, like a video signal on the verge of cutting out.

"Quite a magnificent phase shift, ladies and gentlemen," Sabellian said, raising his hands. "We've come here with good will. No need for such elaborate measures."

"We're not here for you, human." One of the more distinct ethereals stepped forward, speaking Common with a remarkably standard Lordaeron accent. "A black dragon of unusually massive size just landed nearby. Did you notice anything unusual?"

"That black dragon was chasing us. It lost track of us and left. I think they probably didn't want to involve you ethereals," Sabellian said.

"His choice was wise," the ethereal said.

"Indeed," Sabellian smiled.

Jace could tell that if not for the fact that this trip was meant to find the Consortium, these ethereals in front of them — and the distant camp — would probably have been engulfed by a ball of Shadowflame right after that statement.

"We're members of the Alliance Expedition," Jace said, handing over the map. "We've come to find a Consortium member named Jabal. An envoy of yours named Kolaez referred us."

"The Alliance Expedition?" The leading ethereal took the map and looked it over. The ethereals beside him lowered their weapons.

"I heard Kolaez went to Hellfire Citadel," the ethereal said. "Three days ago, Jabal notified the Void Hunters that a Dragonmaw orc was planning to come here..."

"Nagg'jag," Jace said. "Is that the orc you were supposed to meet?"

"Yes, Nagg'jag," the ethereal said. "What happened to him?"

"You don't need to wait for him. He's not coming," Jace said. "Our rangers intercepted his intelligence. That orc warlock was commissioned by Warchief Kargath Bladefist to come here on a reconnaissance mission, to pave the way for a future Horde attack."

The ethereal said skeptically, "The Horde can't even handle the Alliance at Honor Hold. They're busy rebuilding Hellfire Citadel right now. They don't have the strength to march all the way to Blade's Edge and Farahlon to infringe on our interests. We Void Hunters would easily crush those barbarians' ambitions."

"But they already know you're trapped here and can't get out." Sabellian curled his fingers into a cage shape. "You have no reinforcements and no means of leaving. And at this rate, the Alliance will eventually be destroyed by the Burning Legion's demons. Then it'll be your turn."

"This is why Kargath Bladefist has been dragging his feet, refusing to reach an agreement with the Consortium," Jace said.

Jace couldn't read the ethereal's emotions, but the hesitation — the pause without answering — was telling enough. No matter how advanced their civilization, ethereals were ultimately souls, not artificial intelligence systems.

Finally, this Void Hunter said in a low voice, "But the Horde doesn't know what we can do, human."

"They really don't understand what you can do. But they're equally certain that you can't handle your current situation," Jace said. "I know what pretext that orc used to approach your Node Prince Haramad. By coincidence, the Expedition has similar needs, and our understanding of the current situation is far more detailed than the Horde's."

"The difference is that we're also trapped in this world against our will. We don't have the same motive as the Horde to make enemies of you."

"I'll take you to Jabal," the ethereal said. "You can discuss this with him."

"Thank you very much, Void Hunter," Jace said.

After the ethereals turned and walked toward the other side of the forest, Sabellian said quietly, "These fellows remind me of the netherwing dragons transformed by void energy."

"True," Jace said. "Same situation — their physical bodies replaced by violently surging energy."

"No, I mean equally disgusting," Sabellian said.

Jace cleared his throat, looking at the ethereal's back ahead.

The Void Hunter led them into the camp. The ethereals working there seemed uninterested in the Alliance's presence. Jace figured they'd probably already had contact with other Alliance members — most likely the mages of Kirin'Var Village.

They arrived before a metal framework filled with mana grids and various strange instruments. An ethereal wrapped in grey-white runic cloth strips, simply draped in a light green cloak, stood up and looked their way.

After the Void Hunter communicated something with this ethereal in their own language, the grey-white-clad figure looked at them through the gaps in the cloth strips wrapped around his head, which glowed with pale purple arcane light.

"Jace Sesso," Jace said. "Envoy of the Sons of Lothar."

"Baron Sabermane," the black dragon nodded slightly.

"An honor to meet you both." The ethereal bowed slightly. "I am Jabal, the one you're looking for. But regarding that orc matter... I don't much like this feeling, gentlemen."

"Don't act innocent, bandage-man," Sabellian said impatiently. "You know very well what kind of scum the so-called 'Consortium' is, and what kind of business and methods your leader, Node Prince Haramad, used to get where he is today. Selling communicators to the Horde is selling — selling to the Alliance is the same. What's the difference? And our terms are clearly more valuable."

Jabal let out a series of ethereal laughs. "That's not untrue, mage."

"The timing is about right. Before we discuss our further cooperation, come with me first, gentlemen. Let me introduce why the Consortium is confident it can break through Draenor's current magical lockdown and return to the void."

Sabellian glanced at Jace. Jace nodded and said, "Lead the way."

Jabal led them out of the camp, following a path fitted with indicator lights by the ethereals, climbing steadily upward along the rugged mountain trail, eventually reaching the high ground behind the mountain where the Consortium's outpost was anchored.

What they found was a small ethereal research station guarded by several ethereals. Once Jace reached this cliff, he realized how close this place was to the Twisting Nether's precipice. From here, he could directly observe the terrifying void abyss, with countless shattered floating islands scattered before his eyes. But he couldn't hear the roaring crashes that were always audible in Shadowmoon Valley, nor feel the violent demonic winds rushing out from the void.

Looking up, Jace sensed a faint arcane film covering the cliff, its energy source located in the research station behind them.

"Impressive shield technology, Jabal. But what do you want us to see?" Jace asked.

"Wait a moment." The ethereal pointed into the darkness of the void in the distance. "You'll see soon enough."

Jace took a deep breath. The distant black thick clouds occasionally flashed with purple lightning. But before long, the lightning frequency decreased, and even the thick clouds began to dissipate.

As the harsh weather weakened, Jace seemed to glimpse shadows on the other side of the void — another continent. Another continent facing this cliff!

Was that Farahlon? The Netherstorm?

As those shadows grew clearer, Jace's eyes widened more and more... There were still stretches of forest and grassland on that continent.

Though surrounded by lightning, though large areas had been scorched black by void energy, many patches of land still retained surviving vegetation. But this scene was as illusory and blurry as a mirage, and it was fleeting. As thunder, lightning, and harsh thick clouds surged back, everything vanished.

"Farahlon. A great land full of wonders." Jabal said. "Node Prince Haramad is planning to use its abundant arcane energy to construct a teleportation device powerful enough to break through the magical lockdown."

"This is also the condition of our cooperation. As you can foresee, there will be many enemies up there — gronn, ogres. We need an army of at least several hundred to protect the construction of the device."

"So you approached the orcs?" Jace asked. "It seems you really don't know where this magical lockdown comes from."

"Do you know the exact cause?" the ethereal asked.

"Wait, Jace." Sabellian looked at the ethereal. "How long do you estimate it would take to build this teleportation device?"

"Based on our assessment of the lockdown spell's intensity — adding together the time to construct the teleportation device and the time to extract arcane energy to charge it — it should take about twenty years," Jabal said.

Sabellian burst out laughing. "Twenty years is enough for this world to collapse several more times over. You poor naive wretches."

"The Scepter of Sargeras has already left this world, hasn't it?" Jabal said. "Draenor has stabilized now. We judge that no large-scale collapse will happen again. The construction of the teleportation device is absolutely feasible."

The black dragon turned to look at the ethereal with an expression of pity. Jace let out a defeated sigh.
