## Chapter 1332: Jace's Plan

"Going directly after the Scepter of Sargeras — that's a first."

The image of Node Prince Haramad on the disc in the ethereal camp looked displeased. "We suspected this before, but the members of the Shadow Council all believed the Horde's former warchief had taken the thing out of Draenor. The same claim was confirmed by the ogres of Nagrand and your mages. And now the two of you come along and overturn everyone's intelligence?"

"Someone saw traces of the Scepter of Sargeras in Shadowmoon Valley after Draenor's collapse," Sabellian said. "There were even signs of its magic having been used. There's no doubt about this."

"The Sons of Lothar haven't been able to contact Kirin'Var Village since the lockdown fully fell," Jace said. "It's normal for their intelligence to be outdated."

"I understand," Haramad said. "The Netherstorm between Blade's Edge and Farahlon has become even more unstable than when Draenor first collapsed. Communication difficulties have increased tenfold. The mages in Kirin'Var Village are in a rough spot."

"So you're in Farahlon right now?" Jace asked.

"Is where I am important, Mr. Sesso?" Haramad's image flickered, sounding rather annoyed. "Let's focus on more important topics."

*This guy really is in Farahlon*, Jace thought.

"But if it's the Scepter of Sargeras, then everything makes sense." Haramad continued. "Several of our engineers had already determined that this lockdown was a form of demonic magic. After all, aside from fel energy, all other forms of communication channels have been shut down. We can't contact Consortium members outside, nor can we reach any other allies for any form of support..."

"But why would the demons use the Scepter of Sargeras to lockdown this shattered world?" the Node Prince said.

Sabellian said nothing. Jace was silent too. Finally Haramad said, "Then let's skip this discussion."

His tone was noticeably more polite than when Jace had seen his image in Hellfire Citadel. It seemed even the Consortium became all sweet and friendly when they heard the name of the Scepter of Sargeras.

"We've informed you in time just how laughable your plan to build a teleportation device in Farahlon to break through the Scepter of Sargeras is," Sabellian said. "So as thanks for the manpower and resources we've saved the Consortium, I think transferring the ethereal communicator technology to us would be a reasonable expression of gratitude."

"You will certainly be thanked, Baron Sabermane." Haramad's image looked toward the black dragon. "But I must stress that our plan is not laughable. Farahlon's Netherstorm possesses enough arcane energy. Once we properly collect it, twenty years from now, it will definitely break through the lockdown — even the magic imposed by the Scepter of Sargeras. This has been rigorously calculated by our engineers."

"Then that's none of our business. Good luck." Sabellian said. "Now let's discuss the technology transfer. Start by putting away that disc you're standing on."

"Wait, I have another question," Jace said. "Is there no other possibility besides using the arcane energy of the Netherstorm to break through the lockdown?"

"Of course there is," Haramad said. "Crawl into a Burning Legion portal and head for a demon world... If you're lucky enough, you might survive for a while under the demons' hunt."

The Node Prince laughed unkindly. Though his tone was dripping with sarcasm, Jace couldn't help considering the possibility anyway.

But setting aside the fact that every Burning Legion portal was now heavily surrounded by demons and orcs — making it incredibly difficult to storm through — who knew what terrible situation awaited on the other side of the portal?

Not to mention that even demons could only come through in waves. Even if the Expedition could get through a Burning Legion portal, they'd have to go in batches. Fighting demon reinforcements on one side and being besieged by demons on the other — it was basically a death sentence.

Sabellian glanced over, apparently confused about what Jace was trying to do.

"Unless you get lucky and end up in a demon world with no demons," Jabal said seriously from the side. "But considering that every legion portal is used to deploy demons, every passage leads to a demon stronghold on the other side."

"You're actually considering this method, Jabal?" Haramad said unhappily.

"If this lockdown really was created by the Scepter of Sargeras, Node Prince," Jabal said, "then I have to consider the possibility that this world could collapse again..."

"Why don't you consider something more positive," the Node Prince said coldly.

"Earlier I tried to use an enslaved demon to open a passage to the outside world, but it failed," Jace said. "So how does the Burning Legion get through the lockdown into Draenor?"

"No, no, no." Haramad said. "Every demon stronghold is a pure fel world. You can only establish a connecting passage from a world like that... Have you heard of the Sargerite Keystone? A teleporter that can reach any Burning Legion world. Same principle. I know what you're thinking. Don't try to do the reverse. We're inside the lockdown, not outside."

"You're still looking for another way, Jace Sesso," Sabellian said.

"We'll discuss that later." Jace looked at Haramad's image. "The Burning Legion created a fel-corrupted land on the other side of the Dark Portal, just like Shadowmoon Valley. Could we try to establish a teleportation channel on that land?"

"I don't think you're listening to me, Jace Sesso," Haramad said with extreme impatience. "Is Azeroth a Legion world? Of course — if the demon you enslaved were powerful enough to burn Azeroth to a cinder, turning it into a fel planet, then I'd agree with you. We could try building a portal there with enough demonic energy to connect in..."

"But no demon is powerful enough to burn a world like Azeroth," Jabal said.

"Can't you tell that was sarcasm, Jabal?"

Haramad then switched to the ethereal language and grumbled a long string of words at Jabal. But Jace's heart was beating faster and faster without him even noticing.

He lowered his head and pulled out the bundle of scrolls he'd brought from Azeroth — records about the Telogrus Rift. "What about this world?"

"What's that?" Haramad asked.

"Information about a rift fragment in the Twisting Nether." Jace spread open the scroll. "I found it from a researcher in the Twisting Nether."

Haramad said something in the ethereal language to Jabal beside him. Jabal walked over and said, "Could I take a look at these materials, Mr. Jace Sesso?"

Jace handed the materials over. Jabal examined them while exchanging words with Haramad, and Jace finally remembered where he'd heard their language before.

"Are you speaking the Abyssal language? The language of the void lords?" Jace asked.

"Yes, but modified to some extent," Jabal explained. "This language is easier for energy beings to control. I have to admit that even void beings have things worth learning from."

"Can you decipher the information in these materials? Can you help us find a way to reach this rift?" Jace asked.

"We can," Jabal said. "For us, navigating to this rift based on your records is not very difficult."

"Of course we can!" Haramad said. "But what's the use? In the end, we'd still need to build a powerful teleportation device in Farahlon. That's the precondition for leaving Draenor!"

"We don't need you or us to go there, Node Prince," Jace said. "Can this method be learned and used by people outside your species?"

"Can it?" Haramad asked Jabal beside him.

"Yes," Jabal said. "As long as you have a sufficiently powerful magic energy source and a group of arcanists skilled in teleportation rituals. But in the current Draenor, it's absolutely impossible."

"Teach me this method," Jace said.

"I don't understand," Haramad said. "What are you planning to do?"

Jace organized his words, sorting out the idea that had been forming in his mind. Haramad said somewhat urgently, "Tell me your thinking. If it's feasible, I'll fund the teaching of the method to reach this 'Telogrus Rift' myself."

"Alright. I have a way to communicate with the outside," Jace said. "It's not stable, it consumes enormous energy, and I can only communicate with one person. But at least it works. It's the demon I mentioned earlier — a high-ranking Ered'ruin bound to me by pact. I can..."

"Wait a moment." Haramad said. "You said you've enslaved a high-ranking Ered'ruin demon?"

The ethereals nearby all looked over. Jabal seemed to want to say something, but his voice came in fragments, ultimately failing to form a complete sentence.

"We've come this far. We're all trapped here. There's no reason for me to lie," Jace continued. "I can use him to transmit your method to Azeroth, then have the most powerful mages on that side figure out how to implement it together. Then..."

"Then use this high-ranking demon you've enslaved to transform these void fragments into a fel world," Jabal said. "Establish a teleportation channel connected to Draenor's lockdown?"

"Yes," Jace said. "What do you think?"

"Absolutely insane..." Node Prince Haramad murmured. "But I like it."
