Chapter 1335: The Secret Passage

"The Black Dragon Prince is back! Master Jace Sesso is back!"

The gryphon rider's shout swept across Wildhammer Stronghold. Hearing Jace's name, Vereesa immediately looked up toward the distant sky from the battlements. Grede stepped out of the stronghold hall and glanced upward.

Sabellian's form, like a black mountain, crossed over the thick walls and slowly descended into the central training ground. Dust blotted out the sky. Anyone witnessing this would feel grateful that this legendary Prince of the Black Flight was now on the Expedition's side.

Jace jumped out from the black dragon's claws and straightened his clothes, shielding his eyes from the hot wind kicked up as the black dragon shifted back into human form.

"I'll go brief Gralorio on the situation first, Jace," Sabellian said quietly. "Won't keep you from flirting with your little sweetheart."

"Sure." Jace nodded. The ranger had already run over, arm raised, charging through the dust.

"You were gone a long time, Jace!"

"Did get delayed by a week or two." Jace waved away the dust. The black dragon beside him had already slipped off somewhere. "Any news from the Farstrider squad that went looking for the draenei leader while we were away, Vereesa?"

"No." Vereesa coughed twice. "I went to Alleria's Stronghold earlier to ask my sister about the Farstriders' situation. No word at all. Maybe I should have gone with them..."

Jace felt a knot in his stomach. That Farstrider squad had probably run into some kind of trouble. Either that, or Velen himself had encountered difficulties.

Getting Velen and the surviving draenei off Draenor was an indispensable part of this plan.

If Velen was still here, it was hard to say whether Kil'jaeden would give up on invading Draenor. After all, capturing his former colleague had always been one of his important goals.

The dust had settled. Jace glanced toward the stronghold hall. Grede was running over, but there was no sign of Turalyon.

"Where's Marshal Turalyon?"

"He's still patrolling the northern outposts," Vereesa said.

"Then I'll try to contact Archmage Khadgar first," Jace said. "Notify High Thane Kurdran and Marshal Turalyon for me, Vereesa."

"I will." Vereesa seemed to sense something. She couldn't help asking, "You didn't just go to the orc's fortress, did you?"

"We contacted the ethereals." Jace looked at Grede, who had just trotted up to him with short steps. "And with their help, we've come up with a way to possibly break through the lockdown."

"We can go back?" Grede's eyes went wide.

"Honestly? I can't be sure."

Saying this, Jace's eye twitched involuntarily. Thinking about the obstacles they'd need to overcome to make this work made his heart ache.

Thankfully, Khadgar was helping with everything he had on the other side. King Varian Wrynn was supporting them. The Alliance of Lordaeron had the absolute political imperative to rescue the Expedition. Otherwise, he couldn't imagine how this could possibly work.

Down in the basement, Morophil caught sight of Jace's expression and quipped lightly, "That look — like you want to be excited but can't quite manage it — is kind of nostalgic, master..."

"I bet master found the Scepter of Sargeras!" The imp hopped twice on the table. "And he's decided to pick it up and take everyone out of this godforsaken place!"

Morophil looked at Jace sideways.

"Take out 'pick it up' and you're close," Jace said.

"So you really found the scepter," Morophil said.

"Not sure yet. First I need to confirm something else, Morophil." Jace put a finger to his lips. "Shh."

Morophil smiled slightly, then watched her master pick up a draenei soul fragment and initiate the communication ritual.

Before closing his eyes and sensing the void, Jace glanced at the two remaining fragments on the table. Once these are gone, I'll be back to hunting monsters and consuming souls.

"Kael'rez?"

"Master, I hear you."

"Is Archmage Khadgar there?"

"He's not... now he is. He's already teleported to the front of your mage tower."

"Alright." Jace took a breath. Every single energy fluctuation from his mage tower was probably under the Kirin Tor's tight surveillance.

"I have something important to discuss with him."

As he spoke, he felt the consumption of his soul. He could still focus most of his attention on maintaining the communication ritual — because he was speaking demonic. He'd become fluent in demonic through constant communication with Morophil and Sazeno, and through chanting countless spells. Demonic also happened to be very compatible with the ritual itself, which helped reduce mana consumption to some extent.

But what about the Abyssal language? Right now Jace couldn't even speak a single complete sentence in Abyssal.

Even if Archmage Vargoth had some magic to make him instantly fluent in Abyssal, the actual consumption when applied to the communication ritual would need to be explored through practice.

"Khadgar is in front of me now. Aside from him, it's just me, Amy Marin, and your felhound," Kael'rez said.

"Listen carefully," Jace said. "Before I left Azeroth, I found traces of a void rift called Telogrus in Dar'Khan Drathir's research. Here in Draenor, I gave that information to the ethereals. Based on the ethereals' understanding of this Legion lockdown, they believe that if we transform that void rift into a Legion world, we can break through — or rather, link to the lockdown and disguise ourselves as a Legion passage."

"That's impossible." Kael'rez said almost immediately.

"You didn't relay that to Khadgar. Is that your own opinion?" Jace asked.

Kael'rez explained, "The Legion's passages draw on the power of the Scepter of Sargeras, my master. Without power comparable to that, it's impossible to..."

"The Scepter of Sargeras tearing apart Draenor had the support of the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh," Jace said firmly. "Now relay my words to Khadgar. That's an order."

"Yes, master," Kael'rez replied.

This demon doesn't know that in the future, Kel'Thuzad, reborn from the Sunwell, would use the Book of Medivh to summon Archimonde the Defiler himself.

Even as a lich, Kel'Thuzad's understanding of the void, summoning, and teleportation couldn't compare to Khadgar's, who had studied in Karazhan. And their arcane power levels were different. I trust that Khadgar knows better than Kel'Thuzad how to use his teacher's grimoire — not to mention having the Eye of Dalaran as support.

After a moment, Kael'rez spoke again. "Khadgar is very excited, but he still wants to see the ethereals' plan first."

"Yes," Jace said. "That's my intention too. The ethereal technicians are already on their way. They haven't reached Wildhammer Stronghold yet, but I think they'll arrive within a few days. But transmitting all this information will probably consume enormous energy. We need to prepare for that."

"But master, even if the Kirin Tor's mages have the ability to establish a portal, even if they have power matching the Scepter of Sargeras..."

"...What about Draenor?" Kael'rez asked.

"What do you mean?" Jace asked.

"Corrupting a void fragment isn't difficult for me. I've done it countless times. But even if Dalaran's two artifacts are as powerful as you and Khadgar believe, Draenor — as the other end of the portal — also needs sufficiently powerful arcane energy..." Kael'rez's voice dropped almost to a mutter. "You have to build a portal that can withstand this power. Otherwise, that arcane energy will just become a beam of fel energy piercing through the barrier and slamming into that already shattered world. Nothing more."

Jace froze for a moment. Even the ritual array supporting the communication flickered slightly, because Kael'rez's concern was valid.

Even though Jace was no master of teleportation magic, he knew that even a mage like Medivh needed to construct portals on both sides to connect two worlds — one on Hellfire Peninsula, one in the Black Morass. That was the Dark Portal.

On one side were the countless draenei souls Gul'dan had sacrificed. On the other was Medivh's own arcane power, his body possessed by Sargeras's spirit.

In the current Draenor, the Burning Legion had achieved this directly using the Scepter of Sargeras to cross planes and dimensions. How was the Expedition supposed to do the same?

If they could get in touch with Archmage Vargoth and Kirin'Var Village, building a sturdy enough portal with their arcane energy might not be hard. But what about the energy source?

Ursaleth?

Maybe Ursaleth could do it. But using its energy to force open a portal while simultaneously destroying the Scepter of Sargeras would be completely impossible. Sabellian and his black dragons would immediately betray him once they found out, and any hope of using them to gather the Expedition's soldiers at an evacuation point would be gone.

More critically, the entire process had to be hidden enough. If Kil'jaeden caught wind of the Expedition's escape plan, and the unfinished portal was exposed, it would mean fighting against all the demons and orcs in the world until the end of time. Collecting souls and continuing construction of the portal would become extremely difficult. Kil'jaeden might even find a way to seal off this last smuggling route.

If only they could use an existing portal. But at this point, barely three years after Draenor's collapse, even Magtheridon hadn't built many Legion portals. The portal at Twilight Ridge had gone up in smoke along with Highmaul. The remaining portals were all under tight demon guard.

So... was there a way to get the demons or orcs to build a portal for them, to help the Expedition sneak through?

Then Jace remembered something he'd almost completely forgotten.

When he'd left the Shadow Council in Auchindoun, he'd mentioned to Master Vorpil that they would build a portal in Auchindoun in the future, using the draenei souls there to summon a demon lord, helping the Shadow Council eliminate the remaining Auchenai priests. That way, they could later join forces with the orcs at Hellfire Citadel to attack Alleria's Stronghold from both sides, removing this thorn between Warchief Kargath and the Shadow Council.

Of course, back then he'd just made up this story to find an excuse to kill the beholder demon lord Hillak and steal its soul.

Sabellian had mentioned that Magtheridon had handed the Scepter of Sargeras to Zuluhed because the Burning Legion no longer trusted the Shadow Council. Gul'dan's double betrayal of both the Horde and Kil'jaeden had left the Shadow Council under double suspicion at this critical juncture.

Maybe this was exactly the opportunity for Jace and the Expedition.

And Auchindoun's soul energy... was absolutely abundant. In another timeline, Illidan Stormrage had tried to use Auchindoun's soul power to open a portal to a Legion world.

Now it was his turn. So the next step was to fulfill Ambassador Yuruk's promise — in the name of Negal, the Legion's loyal hound.
