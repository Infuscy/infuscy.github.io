Chapter 1336: Auchindoun, Auchindoun...

In the stronghold hall, Turalyon, Kurdran, and Alleria were gathered — all the Expedition's leaders except Danath Trollbane, who had returned to Honor Hold due to the intermittent fel orc harassment. Beside them were Sabellian, Vereesa, Grede, and Sola. They had all heard about Jace and the Black Dragon Prince's contact with the Consortium. They knew Jace had brought back an escape plan. Everyone's eyes were full of anticipation and excitement.

Jace felt the same. He'd barely finished the communication ritual before he wanted to share everything with everyone in the stronghold.

From Azeroth to the Telogrus Rift, from Dalaran's artifacts to Auchindoun's soul energy — it all connected into a complete line. Even Khadgar hadn't found anything to object to.

If they could harness Auchindoun's soul energy, even transmitting the ethereal plan to Azeroth might become much simpler. He could set up a second demon communication array right in the Shadow Council's territory, then discuss with the demon lord they wanted to summon — right in front of those orcs...

After Jace finished explaining the plan he'd worked out with the ethereals, everyone in the hall was quite energized.

Having a solution besides the Scepter of Sargeras — not having to storm a fortress heavily surrounded by demons — was obviously much better. But a few Quel'Thalas elven mages still looked hesitant.

Jace knew what those mages were worried about. He took a breath. "The key is Auchindoun, mages."

"The orcs have been planning to use Auchindoun's power to summon a demon lord to Draenor, to help them eliminate the remaining draenei inside Auchindoun. I'm going to fulfill that wish for them. They just don't know what will be on the other end of the portal they're about to build, fueled by soul energy."

"When the time comes, I'll tamper with the summoning ritual, linking it directly to the Telogrus Rift instead. By the time Archmage Khadgar and the Kirin Tor's mages arrive here, they won't have any say in the matter."

"Absolutely brilliant, Jace Sesso!" Sabellian laughed heartily. "I've been wanting to wipe out those Shadow Council bastards for a long time. I'm deeply grateful you could do me this favor before you leave."

"I'll need you to help me with the disguise again, Prince Sabellian," Jace said.

"Of course," Sabellian said. "I'll go with you. There's no way I'm missing the look of shock on those stupid orcs' faces when they see what they've summoned!"

The elven mages exchanged quiet words with each other. Judging by their expressions, they seemed to acknowledge Auchindoun's soul energy intensity after all.

But the paladins and priests, including Turalyon, had lost their excited looks from earlier. They exchanged heavy glances.

Vereesa and Sola were also looking at Jace and Sabellian with rather unusual expressions. Grede coughed and leaned in quietly. "Are you sure about this, Jace? Using those draenei souls... you remember how they led us through Auchindoun."

Before Jace could answer, Turalyon spoke. "I think we need to consider this carefully, Jace. About Auchindoun, I mean."

"Consider what?" Sabellian asked. "This is the only chance. And wiping out those necromancers and demon warlocks in Auchindoun at the same time — isn't that exactly what you Light believers most enjoy doing?"

"Sabellian is right. Almost all of Draenor's energy sources have been shattered along with the catastrophe, Marshal Turalyon," Jace said. "If we want to leave here, we have to rely on..."

"Rely on souls?" a priest asked. "Master Jace Sesso, the draenei helped us a great deal when we attacked the orc-occupied Auchindoun."

"Jace..." Alleria said. "He's right. And we're going to face the Burning Legion next. The draenei are important allies. Imagine how they'll react when they find out we plan to seize their holy site and drain their souls, just like the Horde."

"Then what's the difference between us and Gul'dan?" another priest said.

"Interesting." Sabellian looked at the gathered leaders with a cold smile. "You're all going to die here, and the draenei's holy site will be utterly destroyed by the Burning Legion! If these souls won't help you escape for the sake of some so-called purity — and more importantly, if they let their own descendants be trapped here, letting the last survivors they barely rescued from Argus go completely extinct — that would be the biggest joke in the whole Twisting Nether!"

"You know I'm right, Jace Sesso!" the black dragon said, looking at Jace.

A female priest turned and left the hall. A Quel'Thalas magister tried to say, "We haven't tried to seize the Scepter of Sargeras yet, everyone. If that truly fails, we can consider this backup."

One of Turalyon's adjutants said, "Don't forget why we came to this world... to stop the orcs and demons from continuing to poison other worlds. We've destroyed the Dark Portal. We've taken away their tool for invading other worlds. Only the Scepter of Sargeras remains. Don't let us become the demons at the very last moment."

Kurdran sighed. "I can fight here until I die... I don't want to plunder those giants' souls like the Shadow Council."

"Let's think of another way together, Jace." Alleria said with concern. "We're not at the last step yet. The ethereals haven't arrived."

Jace understood why Khadgar had been silent when he proposed the Auchindoun plan. Khadgar hadn't found a flaw in the plan — he was hesitating too.

Just like Turalyon, frowning deeply in front of the table right now. Choosing between the Scepter of Sargeras and the souls of Auchindoun... neither was easy to stomach.

What is this, some kind of Stratholme?

Jace couldn't help the bitter laugh. Was I really going to go through all of it?

But at least he wasn't sacrificing the living. That's what he told himself. Compared to what Prince Arthas would face in the future, his situation was much less grim.

There was no doubt, though... he had walked a fair distance down this path himself.

Ursaleth, leaning against the wall, let out a series of evil laughs. It was hungry. It couldn't wait to get a share of Auchindoun's soul energy.

Once I drain that power, destroying the Scepter of Sargeras will be easy for me too.

The Scepter of Sargeras isn't a weapon. It's a teleporter, forged from hundreds of Eredar warlocks and countless powerful demon lord souls. And I specialize in harvesting souls. Sargeras himself forged me, and it's fated that I will destroy the Burning Legion. Jace Sesso. Don't tell me you're hesitating now, at this very moment...

Auchindoun... Auchindoun... Auchindoun...

In the Church's infirmary, Jace slouched in a chair, staring at the shield painted with the Holy Light symbol hanging on the wall. The symbol was somewhat glaring. Still, Jace kept staring at it.

"Grede and Sola don't dare come talk to you, Jace." Vereesa sat down beside him, glancing at where Jace had been looking. She teased, "What's this? A few days apart and now you're praying to the Holy Light?"

"Maybe the Holy Light will give me some inspiration," Jace said. "Remember in Dragonmaw Fortress, that paladin from Stromgarde? He couldn't even remember the prayers properly, but his Holy Light power was stronger than most priests I've seen. What does that mean?"

"It means our Holy Light teacher doesn't check homework on memorizing scripture." Vereesa's long eyebrows twitched.

Jace couldn't help laughing. Then he looked up, staring at the symbol again. "Am I a demon, Vereesa?"

"No." Vereesa lowered her head, subtly wiping the corner of her eye. "You're the kindest, bravest person I've ever met, Jace Sesso. You always have been."

"Ranger-Lord."

A call came from the infirmary entrance. A Farstrider stood at the door. Seeing Jace, he said, "Apologies, Master Sesso, Lady Vereesa. The Farstrider search squad has returned. They've brought the draenei prophet."

"I understand." Vereesa stood up. "Want to step out this door and see what that prophet looks like, Jace?"

Jace looked at the Farstrider's silhouette against the light at the entrance, then back at the Holy Light symbol on the high wall.

"Let's go."
