Chapter 1348: Vorpil's Decision

Vorpil was an accomplished Shadow Council warlock, but he was also quite old and frail, so he retired early.

That night, though, the group took the opportunity to discuss what to say when they met Vorpil the next day — how to guide him toward confirming that Blackheart was putting on an act.

Meanwhile, Jace familiarized himself with the palace's internal layout to make it easier to slip in and out for communication with Allerian Stronghold and to meet Morofil for setting up Kalerez's communication array.

Sazeno had found where Instigator Blackheart stored his soul shards, but the imp couldn't carry the shards out of the palace on his own. That was where Needleflame came in.

Naigag and Naihogar, as high-ranking Dragonmaw warlocks, along with Sora playing the warlock attendant Sosala, would draw attention within the Shadow Council-controlled crypt.

The lower-ranking Dragonmaw guard — Needleflame playing Karosha — was less conspicuous and important, so she could slip away occasionally to make small moves.

Jace had Sazeno take Needleflame to the soul shard storage area to observe the guard situation there, and see if they could steal a few shards at intervals during their time in Auchindoun, preparing for the later ethereal spell transmission.

The next morning, the three of them went to Vorpil's chamber. The old warlock was carefully comparing the contents of two books and only looked up when they approached.

"I trust the master's voice answered you last night," the old warlock murmured in Demonic.

"The master did answer, but was clearly displeased with our work," Sabellian grumbled.

He was still annoyed about Blackheart's arrogant attitude earlier. Not that it was unusual — fel orcs were more irritable and aggressive than other orcs anyway. That was why many warlocks were reluctant to drink the demon blood a second time — it clearly wasn't conducive to controlling shadow magic.

"The master was furious, believing we'd been fooled by false Alliance Expedition intelligence. We need to make some amends." Jace showed Vorpil a hint of tension, then tentatively suggested, "I'm considering bringing the cult's revealing spell back to the Dragonmaw — to protect our mines and villages."

Vorpil laughed softly and coughed. "But that spell originally belongs to the Dragonmaw, masters."

"Indeed." Jace looked back at the cult guard at the door, then stepped closer. "The Alliance and black dragons' joint assault was too swift. Chief Zuluhed couldn't evacuate the treasures and knowledge Deathwing left on Draenor in time. But right now, both Karabor and Hellfire Citadel face the same threat of black dragon infiltration. Yet we have no precise screening method like the 'revealing spell,' Master Vorpil."

Vorpil furrowed his brow slightly. Despite being a high-ranking Shadow Council warlock, he couldn't suppress the emotions churning within him.

Jace could sense some anger in it, but more than that — excitement at finding a breakthrough.

Yes. The guards outside Auchindoun claimed the "revealing spell" for screening outside orcs was Magtheridon's order. But Jace and Sabellian deliberately let slip to Vorpil that the Dragonmaw didn't have this spell.

If even the Dragonmaw villages and mines near Karabor didn't have the revealing spell, how could Auchindoun, thousands of miles away, have received such an order?

Vorpil clearly recognized the inconsistency. Suspicion about Blackheart's ritual was growing.

The old orc said quietly, "I'm surprised Chief Zuluhed didn't share such an important spell with his two high-ranking deputies."

"Perhaps that's why he's dead now, while we're serving the Burning Legion's future, Master Vorpil." Jace's tone was icy. "Absolute loyalty to the Legion is the future of the Horde and the Shadow Council. I know you understand that."

Vorpil slowly bowed his head. "Glory to the Legion, Master Naigag. I will compile that spell for you. But I must warn you — that spell cannot deal with Deathwing's own children."

"Master Magtheridon will find a way to strengthen the spell's power, Master Vorpil," Sabellian said. "Don't worry about that."

"Then wait here, warlocks." The old warlock nodded thoughtfully and walked behind the altar.

Sabellian glanced at the scattered orc guards standing nearby. They couldn't talk here.

But a look exchanged with Jace was enough — both knew Vorpil was increasingly sure Blackheart was up to something.

Jace adjusted his breathing, listening to the old warlock's slow footsteps returning through the empty hall.

If his guess was right, the reason Vorpil hadn't fully shared his suspicions with Magtheridon's emissaries was either because he also doubted their authenticity, or because he wasn't completely certain Blackheart was acting.

The old warlock emerged from behind the altar, handed a bundle of scrolls to Sabellian, and asked, "Have you told Blackheart Master Magtheridon's orders?"

"The master demands we deliver the orders directly to you, Master Vorpil." Sabellian said. "You are to build a portal in Auchindoun. Use all the soul energy in this Draenei necropolis to summon a Legion lord and his demon soldiers to Draenor. He will help you coordinate with the Legion's forces to crush the Alliance and let the Legion rule this world completely."

Hearing Jace's words, Vorpil was silent for a long time, head bowed. Finally, he said, "Come with me, guests of Karabor. There are some things I want to ask Master Magtheridon myself."

Sabellian and Jace exchanged glances. Vorpil clearly wanted to use this chance to force a confrontation and see who was really the problem.

But Jace and the others needed a moment to discuss how to handle this. They couldn't just be dragged along.

Jace said, "The Instigator only just performed the ritual, and Master Magtheridon angrily cut it off. The Instigator has also expended considerable mana. He may need time to recover. Perhaps tomorrow?"

"Think of our lives, Master Vorpil. We've only just been rebuked by the master..." Sora added nervously.

"I won't make things difficult for you. Please, follow me." Vorpil strode purposefully on his aged legs toward the exit. The three fel orcs had no choice but to follow.

Vorpil made a simple gesture to the guards at the hall entrance, and they flanked Jace and the others, giving them no chance to discuss strategy.

Jace looked at Sabellian, then at Sora. From her expression, she too understood what Vorpil was planning.

The old orc was worried that if he waited another night, the situation would slip further away. Blackheart was using dubious Karabor orders to sideline him. Now these fel orcs who might or might not be aligned with anyone had arrived to challenge the ogre. At least one side had to be fake...

If Vorpil, the nominal leader, didn't react now, another night might pass and the Shadow Council might no longer be under his control. Hell, he might not even keep his life.

Blackheart was sitting in his seat, wolfing down roasted black vulture meat. When he saw Vorpil approaching with the fel orcs, he paused, then put down the bone rack. The horned head asked, "What's the urgency, everyone?"

"I need to seek Master Magtheridon's guidance immediately, Blackheart," Vorpil said.

"Whoa, whoa, don't be so hasty, my dear Master Vorpil." The horned head laughed twice. "I was just scolded by the master along with these emissaries last night..."

"This is extremely important to the Legion and the Shadow Council." Vorpil suppressed the emotion in his voice, speaking as steadily as he could. "I know Karabor trusts you greatly. You requested reinforcements from Karabor weeks ago... Crossing Shadowmoon Valley and half of Terokkar Forest on foot is no simple task! Yet the master was willing to let his most trusted subordinates take that risk!"

"So the master will surely answer your call again. And once he knows my question, he'll forgive our impertinence."

Blackheart looked at Vorpil, then at the three fel orcs behind him. "Then... since it's Master Vorpil's request."

"I want this ritual performed on the Arcane Sanctum's array," Vorpil said. "And as many cult members as possible should witness it."

"As you wish." The one-eyed head murmured. Then the whole body shot up, roaring: "Students! Prepare the Karabor ritual for me!"
