## Chapter 1349: Another Kind of Shadow

Word spread through the Arcane Sanctum that Blackheart would be speaking publicly with Karabor today. Many orcs gathered around the altar at the sanctum's far end.

Up on the dais, the acolytes had finished drawing the array. Soul shards were arranged.

Nearby, at the foot of the steps, Master Vorpil stood with a solemn expression, watching the ogre Blackheart. Hard to tell whether he feared Magtheridon's reaction or worried the situation might spiral out of control.

Jace and Sora were crowded at the front by a group of cult members. Sabellian, standing near Vorpil, seemed to want an opportunity to confer with the other two but couldn't find one.

Blackheart shifted his massive, bloated body to the center of the array. Raising both hands, his one-eyed head narrowed its eyes, while the horned head muttered a brief incantation. He was probably testing the soul shards' power. The array's demon blood flickered once and dimmed. Many cult members turned to watch the dais.

Seizing the moment when everyone's attention was diverted, Sabellian made a subtle cutting gesture with his hand beneath his cloak.

Jace understood. He didn't mean to kill anyone — he meant to take out the head that was conducting the ritual, forcing the other head to take over consciousness.

Because as the imp had said, if they forced the other head to speak, the soul energy flow would instantly expose the ritual as fake. Every seasoned orc warlock present would immediately see through Blackheart's trick.

But Jace couldn't cast. A spell of that magnitude would instantly shatter his disguise, and a human face would completely blow their cover.

Sabellian couldn't conveniently act either. He was right next to Vorpil. Any magical manipulation would expose that he was up to something, which would let Blackheart eliminate them as an eyesore.

The Black Dragon Prince kept saying they might as well fight their way out and kill everyone, but when it came down to sabotaging their own cover, he was reluctant.

So the task fell naturally to the least conspicuous of the three — the female orc warlock.

Jace looked at Sora, who was a head shorter and a frame thinner than him as a fel orc. He gave her a subtle signal.

On the dais, Blackheart had begun formally casting the communication array. Void flame burned in the three eyes of his two heads. Sora watched the scene, shot a quick look back at Jace, but he could feel her emotions churning — he couldn't tell if she'd understood or not.

Sabellian kept glancing their way too, trying to see if they had a plan. The look in his eyes said "if you don't act, I will." Seeing his impatience, even Jace felt a knot in his stomach.

Jace was certain that even combined, Vorpil and Blackheart together couldn't match Sabellian alone.

But once fighting started, word would inevitably spread — through fleeing orcs, and even through the Auchenai death priests in their crypts — to every corner of Draenor. The Expedition would have to build the portal themselves.

The element of surprise would be lost. The Expedition members currently being secretly evacuated with black dragon help would likely suffer for it too.

Vorpil looked confident, as if he'd prepared a gambit that would force one side to fight to the death with the other. He seemed ready to settle everything with a single word once Blackheart claimed to have contacted Magtheridon.

More unsettling was Blackheart's equal confidence — as if he could handle anything. Even the wariness he'd shown toward Jace and the others earlier was completely gone.

So the fel orcs had become the most vulnerable of the three parties...

"Master Magtheridon... forgive me for calling upon your attention again. My teacher and dear friend, the Legion's faithful servant, Master Vorpil, seeks your guidance in this dangerous and important hour..."

Blackheart murmured in Demonic. Jace felt the energy flow, carefully watching the silent one-eyed head — its face and crown covered in complex runes.

Even with the imp's hint, it was hard to tell whether those runes connected to the array beneath the ogre's feet. No wonder even Vorpil and the cult's warlocks couldn't see through his trick.

Who knew where he'd found these strange arrays and spells. This ogre's talent for treasure hunting was no joke. If not for him, Jace would never have gotten his hands on the Burning Legion's demon registry.

"Glory to the Legion! Lord Magtheridon has answered my call!"

Blackheart's horned head suddenly roared. Vorpil couldn't wait any longer. He stepped forward immediately and shouted, "Ask Master Magtheridon for me — our great teacher, Lord Kil'jaeden..."

He got halfway through the sentence when Blackheart suddenly frowned. The entire array shuddered. The purple flames on the lines and runes began flickering wildly, out of control.

The ogre's one-eyed head made choking sounds, "Urk... urk..." His whole body wobbled as if unsteady. Vorpil frowned, watching the ogre, not sure what to do. All the orcs in the Arcane Sanctum began murmuring. No one knew what was happening.

"Urk..."

The ogre's horned head let out a deep sigh, then slumped, a thick strand of drool running down onto its belly...

At the same moment, the other head roared as if struck by some stimulus. Soul energy suddenly went haywire. Jace clearly felt a powerful beam of shadow energy being pulled and dragged between that head and the soul shards around the array — as if the head was struggling in extreme pain from this out-of-control force!

"Someone cursed me! Someone placed a vile curse on me!" The rune-covered one-eyed head spat blood from the corners of its mouth, tortured by the shadow magic. The cult members in the hall — including Vorpil himself — looked around frantically, searching for the source of this "vile curse." But no one detected any dark magic disturbance. Sabellian hadn't acted. Jace certainly hadn't either.

Then Sabellian's gaze shifted toward Jace's companion.

Sora was calmly watching the ogre's wobbling, undignified display.

Yes. This wasn't any vile curse, let alone shadow magic. It was just a simple hypnotic spell — so weak it had only put one head to sleep.

Not even Jace, standing right next to her, had sensed the casting or heard a single incantation. For a green dragon, this was practically innate natural magic!

"Vorpil!" Blackheart's one-eyed head spat black blood as it screamed. "It was your trick! You set a trap! You did this! Iilth sythn aqev, aqev..."

What?

The head's murmuring was as if it had been afflicted by a shadow curse. Jace couldn't understand a single word.

In fact, it went beyond "couldn't understand." Just hearing it was intensely uncomfortable. As it muttered, the eye in that head rolled wildly upward, purple veins spreading across the white...

"Instigator!" an orc warlock shouted, as if trying to wake him.

"He's like those ogres! Like the Pale! That's the language of those monsters hiding in the dark!" another orc called out in warning. "This is a complete deception!"

An orc warlock yelled, "He was never connected to Master Magtheridon! The soul energy here never went to Karabor — it was pouring into his own head! He was using this ritual to devour the soul shards we created! Sacrificing them to his dark master!"

"Damn ogre! I understand now! The distorted flow of soul energy explains everything!" another orc warlock shouted.

A few warlocks quickly came to their senses, but most of the cult warriors seemed not to understand what was happening. Some even reached for their weapons, ready to attack the warlocks questioning the Instigator!

"The Instigator is utterly loyal to the Twisting Void!"

"Glory to the Legion! Loyal to the Legion!"

"No!"

Blackheart's frenzied roar cut off all argument. His consciousness seemed to twist further. The rune-covered head's single eye rolled back and forth wildly.

Soon, deformed lumps sprouted from his shoulders and chest, skin splitting open to reveal more eyes! "Shath gral ynyq, weq..." The head kept murmuring. Even Vorpil stumbled backward in fear, falling to the ground...

Old Gods...

Sora clearly hadn't expected this either. Trembling with fear, she backed away. Jace grabbed her arm to keep her from falling.

Suddenly, a surge of blood-red flames completely engulfed the ogre!

Sabellian roared, raising both arms. The flames under his direction quickly enveloped Blackheart's massive body. Piercing, unnatural shrieks — sounds no ogre could make — came from the flames. The watching orcs — warlocks, warriors, and Broken alike — covered their ears in pain. Some even collapsed, rolling on the ground...

Jace stared into the flames until the figure inside rapidly shriveled. The fire subsided. Sabellian let out a cold breath. The Arcane Sanctum finally fell silent.

Behind him, Sora stood rigid, her eyes wide, fixed on Jace. Every muscle was taut. It took a long moment before she finally blinked.

"It's over. Don't worry."

"I — I didn't..." Sora seemed to suddenly realize something, looking behind herself. Jace said quickly, "Nothing went wrong. You didn't change at all."

Only then did Sora nod in relief. On the dais, only a pile of bone ash remained.
