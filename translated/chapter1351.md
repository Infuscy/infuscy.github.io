## Chapter 1351: The Blackwing Warrior's Achievement

On the way back to the fel orc emissaries' resting room, Sabellian couldn't help remarking, "Quite a productive day. The only thing I'm not satisfied with is that Blackheart — he seemed tough on the surface, but in the end, he was nothing but a worthless weakling. If his magical power had been half as good as his mouth, his dark master's efforts in cultivating him might not have been entirely wasted."

"So even on Draenor, those dark things exist," Sora said, walking beside him.

"I'm not surprised," Sabellian said. "When I helped my father search for nesting grounds on Draenor, I came into contact with a group of Arakkoa in Shadowmoon Valley. They seemed to worship some familiar entities."

Looking at the gloomy end of the distant passage, the Black Dragon Prince continued, "I just didn't expect that even in the Burning Legion's territory... they could still find a lurking 'agent' to stir up trouble. And it seems Instigator Blackheart was already beyond mere worship. He'd clearly received 'blessings' — something even countless worshippers of those ancient beings on Azeroth never obtained."

"What do you think the reason is?" Jace asked.

"Don't tell me you haven't considered why." Sabellian said. "The specific reason is impossible to know now. But my guess isn't complicated."

He adjusted his breathing, speaking slowly. "Either Draenor, like Azeroth, has some ancient evil buried where we can't reach, and they're looking for followers on the surface to help them see the light of day again. Or — they haven't arrived here yet, and are seeking a summoner capable enough to guide their summoning to this land."

"So you still plan to stay here?" Sora asked tentatively.

"Why not?" Sabellian said. "This broken, declining world has given me a chance to face this corruption head-on. My kin and I come with vigilance and enough experience. The enemies here are far weaker than those on Azeroth. I won't claim I can do better than the Earth-Warder did ten thousand years ago. But I'm willing to try. Call it escape or cowardice if you want, little girl. I don't care."

"I don't think that," Sora said.

"I hope your sleepwalking relatives are all as clear-headed as you," Sabellian said.

They reached the cave entrance where they were resting. Sabellian clapped his hands. "Alright. I'm going to find some female orcs to have some fun with. After all, we've demonstrated the combat power of Karabor's emissaries and played the cunning part of the Burning Legion's lackeys. It's only right to show the free-spirited, indulgent side of our personality too."

"And you, Naigag — just stay here and be the gloomy, dreary, stifling, boring part. I'm sure you'll do great. Farewell."

With that, the fel orc strode off toward the cult camp. Jace entered the cave room, rubbed his brow, and muttered, "What the hell was that all about? Are the male green dragons in the Emerald Dream the same?"

"No, because they have Queen Ysera." Sora raised an eyebrow — though the slightly mischievous expression looked strange on a red-skinned female orc's face.

"Fair point," Jace murmured.

Sora sat down nearby. "Even though Naihogar made today sound so easy, Naigag... I think everything that happened earlier — including Vorpil's probing afterward — was really nerve-wracking. I was thinking, that warlock seems old and frail, but he's undoubtedly clever and sharp... Why did he fall for your trick so easily?"

"Because he's very, very afraid that Kil'jaeden will punish him for Blackheart's betrayal," Jace explained. "So he's desperate for a chance to prove himself — to show he can still serve the Burning Legion."

"Alleria's plan really worked. At that moment, I was worried that actively suggesting an attack on the Auchenai death priests would seem too deliberate. But with the Auchenai priests busy fending off the Farstriders' harassment, Vorpil felt like he was seizing the opportunity himself..."

"You think we're using him, but he thinks he's using us. Whether it's seizing control of all of Auchindoun, earning Kil'jaeden's trust, or climbing higher in the Burning Legion... He just has no idea what's waiting for him on the other side of that portal."

"I see." Sora nodded. "At least if it had been me, I probably would have given myself away immediately."

"But none of it would have worked without your hypnotism, Lady Sosala." Jace smiled. "If we'd used shadow magic, we'd have been exposed immediately. You saw how sensitive those Shadow Council warlocks are to abnormal shadow energy flow. And thanks to that sensitivity, they were able to see through Blackheart's trick so easily."

Sora might have been a bit naive, but she wasn't stupid. Jace had only meant to praise her quick thinking. But at his words, her expression shifted.

Jace knew — she must have been thinking back to that moment when Instigator Blackheart's body started sprouting eyes everywhere.

Truth be told, it wasn't just her. That scene reminded Jace of the terrifying Nightmare forest he'd seen in Winterspring, when he'd entered the Emerald Dream using a satyr's hypnotic device.

"Every time I think I've escaped that darkness, something happens to hit me hard and wake me up." Sora's voice was barely a whisper. "Just now, I felt like a hare startled into convulsions, fleeing in panic."

"At least that means you're a normal hare," Jace said.

Just then, something approached from the distant passage outside. Jace gestured for Sora to stop talking.

Soon, footsteps came down the passage, growing closer to their room. A short figure appeared in the doorway, peeking in. "Hope I found the right place!"

"You did." Jace stood up. "How'd it go?"

Needleflame grinned, walking in and slamming the bag holding Sazeno into Jace's chest. "You didn't go find some female orcs too?"

"Not everyone shares his peculiar taste," Jace muttered, taking the bag. He weighed it in his hand — it felt noticeably heavier than when Needleflame had left, clinking with something like stones.

"Soul shards?"

"Of course." Needleflame said. "While you were making all that noise in the Arcane Sanctum, I snagged quite a few from that ogre's storage room. I didn't get too greedy — there are seven shards here. Three of them are made from Draenei souls, or so your imp servant says. You'd better use them up as soon as possible. Don't store too many soul shards here, or the orcs might notice..."

"Nobody followed you?" Jace asked.

"You're insulting me? Don't forget whose daughter I am!" Needleflame said indignantly. "You asked me to find soul shards, so I found them. Why so many annoying questions!"

"Alright, alright. Sorry." Jace said, thinking to himself how different this little one was when her father was around versus when he wasn't.

But he hadn't heard any following footsteps either. He opened the bag. Inside, Sazeno was hugging a richly charged soul shard, rubbing it with his claws as if trying to polish it.

"Alright, stop getting lost in the moment." Jace flicked the imp on the head.

"Ouch!" The imp clutched its head resentfully.

Jace said, "You know this palace. Find me a way to slip out. I need a place to draw an array."

The imp's eyes darted. "I'm... very tired right now, master. That little monster made me inspect all the soul shards, organize the shelves and boxes, and lure the guards away. I did all that work, and she wouldn't let me eat a single one. Now my head hurts! I need to eat one to regain some energy, or I'll be dizzy and can't even guide you properly. What if I accidentally lead you to the hall where Murmur is sealed..."

Jace picked up a small shard. "This one's yours."

"Ah... well, I suppose it's not bad..." the imp muttered quietly.

"Keep Needleflame safe." Jace glanced at his pocket watch, then looked at Sora. "I'll try to be back before dawn."

Sora opened her mouth but Needleflame cut in first: "Don't worry, Jace Sesso! I'll protect Lady Sosala well!"

"Then I'm counting on you, Blackwing warrior." Jace smiled slightly, picked up the cloth bag, and left the cave entrance.
