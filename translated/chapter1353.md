## Chapter 1353: Testing a New Contact Point

With Sazeno and Morofil's help, setting up the summoning circle went smoothly. Jace didn't even need to do much himself — the two demons had practiced it so many times back at Wildhammer Stronghold that they could do it in their sleep. Jace watched Morofil carefully tracing the rune-lines and asked, "How's the Expedition doing?"

"You mean how's Vereesa doing?" Morofil tilted her head and smiled. "She's near Auchindoun, attacking the soul-towers the draenei built alongside the Farstriders. If anything goes wrong for you here, she'll charge in to save you without a second thought."

"Oh, and before I forget — Vereesa's been learning nature magic from our dear Solara. Want to guess which spell she's been studying the most diligently?"

"Entangling Roots?" Jace asked.

"You know her pretty well." Morofil straightened up. "She's dead set on learning it. I can see the intent clearly."

"Does that worry you?" Jace asked.

"Now if Lady Solara used it on me, I'd actually be concerned." Morofil twirled her bloodstained finger. "The trees and grass look after her like she's their own daughter."

"Vereesa, though... we'll see."

"I think you're underestimating her talent for this." Jace said. "What about Kirin'Var Village?"

"Gelorion has already led a group of black and red dragons north." Morofil replied. "They'll return in about ten days, bringing the mages and the language potions they prepared for you."

Jace let out a deep breath, looking at the nearly completed ritual circle. "And the evacuations elsewhere?"

"Honor Hold's already evacuating some people toward Alleria's Fortress." Morofil replied. "According to the rangers, they're moving from the southern armory, using the thorn-covered mountains as cover to avoid orc scouts."

"The ethereals in Hellfire Citadel said that, at least when I left Alleria's Fortress, the fel orcs hadn't noticed Honor Hold's movements yet. What about inside Auchindoun, master?"

"Proceeding according to plan." Jace said. "After this, tell Vereesa and her sister to keep pressuring the Auchenai priests... Wophil's already taken the bait. He's planning to attack those necromancers. We need to give him an opportunity he can't pass up."

"So, does your loyal servant get to help?" Morofil asked.

"Not unless you've learned Polymorph." Jace said. "Last time you came in with me, you left quite an impression on those demons and orc warlocks."

"I'm not the same as last time, am I?" Morofil placed a hand on her chest. "In power, or in figure."

"Too bad you didn't see that Wophil master's face when he mentioned you. Ha!" The imp said. "Nobody's going to forget a succubus with a high-ranking Burning Legion brand..."

Hearing that, Morofil smirked with pride. The imp added, "Especially one without wings!" The succubus's expression froze. Jace asked coldly, "Are you done drawing?"

"Done, done." The imp scurried behind Jace's boot.

Jace sighed and stepped into the center of the ritual circle to begin chanting.

The demon blood ignited. Using that demonic power, he lit the soul shards. The searing dark magic quickly cast his spirit into the void, reaching the other side.

Even though this wasn't Shadowmoon Valley, the shadow energy saturating Auchindoun gave him a favorable environment for spellcasting. He searched with his eyes closed for a while, and before long, he'd touched a landing point on the other side of the void.

"Kael'rez."

"Master... Khadgar has been waiting a long time."

Jace said, "Tell him and Dalaran to be patient a little longer. I've entered Auchindoun now. I've found a source of soul shards from those orc warlocks. Hopefully it'll be enough to send all the ethereal incantations to the other side."

"I'd say it's definitely enough, master!" The imp muttered beside him. "Just can't steal too many at once, or the orcs will notice." After a moment, Kael'rez said, "Khadgar says he's already contacted Garlders of the Blackclaw, and his Knife's Edge crew. Also, the Council of Six in the Kirin Tor has been informed about the Telogus Rift. Kael'thas Sunstrider in Quel'Thalas has already ordered Sylvanas Windrunner to bring in the shadow mages arrested in the Darkan case, to squeeze every detail out of them about the Telogus Rift. Khadgar is optimistic about the progress."

"Ask Khadgar how Archmage Antonidas views the conditions I proposed earlier." Jace said. "Has he brought it up to the Council of Six?"

Kael'rez was silent for a while before saying, "Khadgar only told him that after the entire Expedition arrives at Telogus, the portal will be destroyed to cut off any further danger entering Azeroth through that passage. He didn't tell him about your conditions or the Knife's Edge crew's."

"Why?" Jace said. "Well, at least I appreciate his honesty."

The demon lord said, "Khadgar says the Kirin Tor doesn't like being threatened. If it came out like that, it could provoke a pretty intense reaction in Dalaran, and even among the various mage organizations across the Alliance."

"His point is, once the entire Expedition is back in Azeroth, we can rely on the authority and reputation of the Sons of Lothar's leaders to pressure the Council of Six. He said he'll stand on your side and the Knife's Edge crew's side then. He believes Turalyon and his wife will too, along with the Wildhammer dwarves. And as for Quel'Thalas — even if it's just to push back against the Kirin Tor and Dalaran's pride, the Silvermoon Council will back you up riding the wave of the Expedition's return."

Jace closed his eyes and steadied his breathing. He'd known it wouldn't be that simple.

This wasn't just about winning the Telogus Rift as a sanctuary for the Knife's Edge crew. It was also about getting him off the hook for secretly summoning a doom lord and a void lord.

He'd had to show all his cards to the Kirin Tor to save the Expedition. After this was over, the Kirin Tor would probably try every way they could to "strip" him of those assets, nice and legal.

Just like King Varian had said — there probably wasn't a single kingdom in the Alliance besides Stormwind that genuinely wanted the Expedition to return.

The people of the Alliance might see Jace and his crew as heroes, the same as the Sons of Lothar. But the kings and nobles — and even some of the high mages in the Kirin Tor — wouldn't see it that way.

Khadgar was right. And nothing was finished yet. It wasn't the time to be making demands. Assuming they could even get back.

Just thinking about it gave Jace a headache. He wasn't good at this kind of thing.

"Tell Khadgar I understand his concerns." Jace said. "Let's set this aside for now. I don't want disagreements over this to mess up the entire Expedition's escape plan."

"Archmage Vargoth's language potions haven't arrived yet. It'll take about two more weeks. Tell the Kirin Tor mages to wait patiently. Once the potions arrive, I'll start transmitting the ethereal incantations immediately."

"He understands, master." Kael'rez said.

Jace nodded. "There's one more thing I'm worried about. Murmur."

"Tell him I don't know how to deal with Murmur right now. If it breaks free from the Shadow Council's control, it could cause incalculable damage to the portal itself and to all the soul energy in Auchindoun. So once I take out Wophil, his warlocks might waver... ask Khadgar if he has any good ideas."

Kael'rez said, "Khadgar believes the Shadow Council summoned Murmur in Auchindoun specifically to subdue Patriarch Maladaar and his Auchenai necromancers. Once the Patriarch is dead, the Shadow Council probably won't keep this uncontrollable elemental lord around."

"So they have a way to 'banish' it?" Jace asked.

Kael'rez replied, "Khadgar isn't sure about a 'banishment' method, but he knows Teron Gorefiend used a grimoire related to Murmur to conduct the summoning ritual. That book is now in Wophil's hands. The records are detailed — the Shadow Council used that book to keep Murmur bound. He says you should try to get a look at it if you can."

"I understand." Jace said. "Thank Khadgar for me. I have nothing else to say."

"Khadgar wishes you good fortune." The demon lord said.

"The same to you."

Jace ended the ritual. The circle erupted with black flame. Morofil sighed. "So I still need to go fetch more demon blood..."

"Sorry for the trouble." Jace said.

"That's all you have to say?" The succubus said crossly. "I came all this way!"

Jace swallowed. Before he could speak, Morofil stepped closer and said, "I know, master — you can't cast spells while in disguise. So from the moment you entered Auchindoun until now, you haven't spent a single drop of mana, have you?"

"All that sweet mana, swirling and pulsing inside you... don't you want to find a place to let it all out? You won't need it for a while anyway."

The tall demon pushed him down easily onto a pile of rubble. Morofil leaned in and whispered, "Show the souls of Auchindoun what you're made of, my master. Let them know they didn't back the wrong person."

Remembering that Sabellian was probably back inside, locked in combat with who knew how many orcs, Jace decided to drop his guard. He reached out and grabbed the succubus by the throat.

This shivarra, whose fearsome body had carved a path through countless demon warriors, went soft and pliant under his grip, collapsing beneath him. She squinted her eyes in bliss and licked her lips.

"That's it, darling. Just like that..."
