Chapter 1357: The Legion Permits No Doubt

The gates of the Auchenai crypts were forced open by towering demon guards. Orcs poured through, crashing into the shrieking skeleton soldiers within. The Shadow Council's warlocks and the Auchenai necromancers traded soul and shadow magic, the deep passages shuddering with each powerful blast.

Debris rained from the walls with every tremor. Massive statues toppled and crashed to the ground.

Sabellian seemed more worried about the tomb chambers collapsing from the battle than about the orc warriors falling at the front. He kept looking up at the ceiling.

"The Auchenai are putting up fiercer resistance than we expected!" an ugly orc warlock shouted to the others. "They've massed a huge number of undead servants here!"

"Master Wophil ordered us to press the attack. There's no turning back now!" another orc warlock yelled.

The elderly Wophil had already pushed far to the front. Frail as he was, his legs trembling, his bad eye nearly blind, his sorcery was still formidable.

Protected by his cult followers and summoned voidwalkers, he didn't need to worry about his personal safety. He just drew on the soul shards he'd brought from the Arcane Sanctum and hammered the draenei priests hiding behind bone piles and walls with Dark Soul Nova after Dark Soul Nova, clearing the orcs' path.

Inside the Auchenai crypts, the draenei's mad howls echoed through the passages. Jace could understand them — they were calling for Patriarch Maladaar to come himself. It didn't sound like they were asking for reinforcements. More like some religious rite, some invocation of a deity.

Meanwhile, wave after wave of skeleton soldiers poured through the gates, hammering the orcs' lines. The cult fanatics were clearly flagging.

An orc warlock was hit by a soul-skull flying out of the gate. His soul was ripped away instantly, leaving an empty-eyed stiff body crumpling to the ground. The dark energy gathered around him rippled away like water.

Sabellian watched the warlock's corpse tumble down the nearby steps. "Today really doesn't seem like a good day to attack the Auchenai priests."

"No day is a good day." Jace said. "Our goal is to make Wophil think today is."

"True enough." Sabellian said. "But I don't like spending mana helping these inferior demon worshippers..."

"I can't help either." Jace said.

"Then try to make yourself useful." Sabellian said. "Pick up your sword, or whatever weapon you can find on the ground. Tap into that red dragon blood Alexstrasza gave you..."

Jace pulled a rusted longsword from a nearby bone pile. Lordaeron make, no question — left behind from the first time the Alliance and the Auchenai priests had attacked the Shadow Council's orcs in Auchindoun together.

Just as Jace was examining the blade, a terrible shriek erupted from the gate!

Several warlocks channeling shadow magic at the skeleton soldiers were struck by the scream — blood poured from their seven orifices as they screamed and fell. Even the black dragon prince winced, glaring warily into the gate.

"Maladaar... Maladaar... Maladaar..."

The whole Auchenai crypt was chanting the Patriarch's name like a hymn. Jace knew the bastard had finally decided to show himself.

"Wophil!" A deep roar came from below the steps. "After more than a decade of cowardly hiding, you and your dark followers have finally grown impatient enough to offer up your souls!"

"But that's not nearly enough to atone for the damage you've done to Auchindoun. Every last base orc here will be annihilated eternally today. Your souls will go nowhere!"

A towering shadow in blood-red armor strode straight toward the orc formation, trampling the bones of countless fallen undead soldiers. He carried a wicked curved blade infused with immense soul power. His pauldrons jutted up like the corrupted wings of a fallen angel.

Nothing about him suggested a former High Priest of the sacred tomb. He looked more like a death knight reborn in a priest's body... Teron Gorefiend's archenemy had ultimately met a fate similar to Gorefiend's own.

Maladaar seemed barely shorter than Velen. The sturdy cult warriors before him were like pitiful gnomes.

His absurdly long curved blade swept through the crowd like a scythe. Whether zombie soldier or orc — anything it touched was hurled back or cleaved apart by the terrible soul-force.

One demon guard blocked a single blow before having his chest carved open, his demon heart ripped out and sent back to the Twisting Nether. And the far more fragile shivarra couldn't even get close.

Sabellian took in the cult's casualties with a raised eyebrow, but his tone remained casual. "Building a portal needs laborers, not soldiers and warlocks. Don't you agree?"

Jace didn't answer. He was fixed on Wophil, surrounded by orcs. The old warlock was channeling some powerful void ritual. Countless voidwalkers condensed from the shadow energy around him, lunging at the approaching Maladaar.

He was trying to learn something from Wophil — a spell or casting gesture — but Wophil was chanting too fast, too garbled. His shadow talent was top-notch; he wouldn't have climbed so high in the Shadow Council otherwise.

"You and your prophet refused the Dark Lord's gift! You self-righteous fool — you call us defilers, yet your very existence is a defilement of the greatest true god in the Twisting Nether! Stupid, arrogant, conceited — Maladaar!"

Wophil screamed. He was genuinely jealous of the opportunity the draenei had once been given...

"Stupid?" Maladaar grabbed an orc's head and crushed it, inhaling his soul directly with blackened lips. "Speaking of stupid, Wophil..."

The Patriarch laughed wildly, cleaving through Wophil's summoned voidwalkers like parting mist with his monstrous demon blade. The Auchenai priests and vindicators behind him broke through the gap Maladaar had torn in the orc lines. Several warlocks chanting in the rear were crushed by blood-red crystal hammers — their bodies broken, their skulls shattered. The gate they'd just forced open was already back in the hands of the fallen draenei.

Those draenei who fell — whether warriors, priests, or necromancers — rose again under Maladaar's whispers to rejoin the fight. Though their eyes now burned with pale necromantic light, their combat power was undiminished.

A reanimated draenei warrior broke through several orcs and charged straight at the bone pile where Jace and Sabellian stood. Sabellian swung his staff, casually sending his warhammer flying, then gripped his neck and bit off half the undead's head before tossing the broken body off the bone pile.

Jace leveled his sword at another draenei vindicator charging his way. But the undead never made it — another orc warlock used Drain Soul to rip away the animating spirit-force and the thing stiffened, collapsing and rapidly rotting.

"Don't tense up." Sabellian reassured him. "Wophil can beat that mad draenei. Don't assume just because he looks like a wreck that he actually is one."

Jace eyed the black dragon prince doubtfully, then looked ahead. Wophil's voidwalkers were multiplying. There were almost as many as the orc soldiers, who were now retreating while fighting. Some had even charged back through the gate to attack the necromancers inside.

Maladaar realized Wophil's mana was too deep to handle easily, but he also knew the old orc's body was fragile. He cleanly beheaded several orcs surrounding him and called the undead to him in some strange tongue, presumably to prepare a concentrated assault on Wophil.

But just as the most powerful draenei warriors gathered, Jace felt the hall's shadows tighten as if seized by some force. Wophil bellowed an orcish incantation — and every voidwalker exploded in rapid succession!

Black demonic light blasted across the battlefield. Many orc warriors were caught in the blast and obliterated alongside the enemy. Blood and gore splattered everywhere. Bone fragments flew like bullets, embedding themselves in walls...

Jace raised his cloak to shield himself from the shrapnel, forcing a look through the debris. Most of the draenei near the damaged gate had been blown apart. Only mangled halves of bodies crawled pitifully across the floor. But the orcs were in no better shape — those vanguard who couldn't get clear were already mixed with the draenei corpses into a pool of black-red gore.

But Maladaar still stood. One pauldron was shattered, his body covered in wounds — but he still raised his dark blade and charged at Wophil.

Many of the orcs near Wophil had been wounded or killed in the blast. He'd lost almost all his protection, exposed directly to the Patriarch.

Maladaar seemed delighted at the opportunity. He laughed wildly as he lunged — only for Wophil to bind the hulking draenei warrior — nearly twice his size — with some strange spell. Shadow energy gnawed at Maladaar, keeping him at bay. His wounds split open one by one. New flesh burst and bled, as if invisible whips were lashing him over and over.

Finally, Maladaar collapsed to his knees less than five or six yards from Wophil — but then he burst into wild laughter. "You've been tricked, Wophil! You naive savage..."

"What?"

That single word disrupted the old warlock's magical focus like a counterspell. Wophil stared at the battered, barely-moving Patriarch with sickly pale eyes.

"What are you talking about?"

"Wait." Sabellian murmured. "Shit."

"The spirits of the martyrs guarding Auchindoun told my necromancers." Maladaar said. "Black dragons have infiltrated Auchindoun, using illusion magic to deceive your eyes. Those fel orcs are disguised by black dragon magic..."

Jace's scalp went numb. These Auchenai priests could forcibly enslave souls. If they commanded the souls to tell them about the Shadow Council's situation, the souls had no choice but to talk.

He'd actually considered this possibility. He just hadn't expected Maladaar would expose the moles to Wophil just to sabotage the Shadow Council.

But Sabellian made no move either. Forcibly stopping Maladaar from continuing would only invite suspicion.

Wophil didn't believe it immediately. "Detection spells haven't... even if they entered, magic would strip a dragon's disguise. Don't think you can shake me with—"

"Cast a spell on that orc, Wophil." Maladaar cut him off, pointing straight at Jace. "See for yourself. Let him tell you who he really is!"

Wophil's gaze snapped to Jace. Yes — he knew too. This warlock called Naigag hadn't cast a single spell since entering Auchindoun...

Sabellian turned to look at Jace. But Jace was already walking toward Maladaar. He raised Uuless and murmured in Demonic, "Indulge..."

The magic-cloth wrapped around Uuless suddenly wept blood-red tears. The demonic bones seemed encircled by spinning evil red light, layer upon layer. Maladaar's eyes went wide with what looked like terror.

Throughout the hall, countless dying orcs and badly wounded draenei priests screamed under the violet-red glow. Pale blue souls were drawn out from every direction, converging and spinning with the red light — before being devoured by the staff!

Maladaar tried to speak but couldn't. His whole body convulsed wildly. His soul poured from his eyes, nostrils, and mouth, shrieking as it was directly consumed by the Uuless before him. Nothing but an empty shell remained, collapsing to the ground.

When the echoes died, not a single Auchenai was left standing.

Wophil and every orc warlock was struck speechless by the sight — eyes wide, bodies rigid. Jace swept his gaze around the hall and noticed a shivarra — summoned by someone — backing away in terror.

He could sense from her emotions that she recognized something. Did she know Uuless?

Before Jace could figure out how to deal with it, Sabellian lashed out — a blast of Shadowflame burned the succubus to ash. She didn't even leave a scream behind. Her soul was wholly consumed by Uuless. No returning to the Twisting Nether.

Jace looked at the black dragon prince. The other gave a slight nod, confirming his disguise hadn't been torn by Uuless's soul-devouring.

Just as he'd thought — spells he cast, or spells that affected his body, would break the disguise. But Uuless's energy didn't reach him.

After all, this insatiably greedy demon bone wouldn't let a single scrap of soul energy escape.

Still, from the corner of his eye, Jace spotted his staff-wielding hand... it didn't look quite like a fel orc's demonic skin anymore.

But everyone present who had witnessed that terrifying soul magic was too shaken to notice the detail. "What magic was that?" Wophil's voice trembled.

"A small reminder." Jace picked up a dead Auchenai priest's tattered robe and began rewrapping his staff. "If anyone dares to question Karabor again, question Lord Matheridon — that demon servant's fate awaits them."

"The Legion will rule, Master Wophil. And we must faithfully await that day."
