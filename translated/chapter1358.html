Chapter 1358: All in the Name of Karabor

The fall of Patriarch Maladaar didn't crush the Auchenai's will to resist. Through the corridor beyond the gate, amidst the towering bone piles, half-dead draenei necromancers still used every means at their disposal to fight the Shadow Council's invaders.

But without Maladaar's power, these resisters were no match for the tide. With the Patriarch struck down, morale flipped on both sides. Undead soldiers felt no fear, but their masters certainly did. The orcs quickly pushed into the deepest reaches of the Auchenai crypts, looting the soul shards the priests had left behind — and even more powerful soul crystal blocks.

Jace had never seen soul crystals this strong outside Auchindoun. He couldn't help eyeing them.

But these crystals — roughly the size of a water flask — were too big to smuggle out of the cult's Arcane Sanctum the way he'd done with soul shards. He'd have to wait until the portal was built and they'd fully broken with the cult, then see if he could find a chance to snatch them for other uses.

The Auchenai of today were nowhere near as powerful as when Teron Gorefiend had attacked Auchindoun. That was why Maladaar had chosen to make his last stand at the gate with all the elite of his priesthood. The Auchenai desperately needed a strong enough "second-in-command" to help the Patriarch guard the gate.

In the game, Maladaar had used necromancy to resurrect the eye-lord Shirak — killed by the Auchenai priests for stealing soul energy — as the gate's undead guardian.

But here, Jace had killed Shirak with a single shadow bolt, leaving his corpse in the depths of the cult's palace. The Auchenai couldn't claim it. Add in the fact that the Farstriders had drawn off much of the Auchenai's strength, and the crypts were left hollow.

But the Shadow Council of now wasn't the one Teron Gorefiend had led either. So the battle was brutal on both sides.

Before long, the Auchenai catacombs were taken by the cult. Every Auchenai necromancer who hadn't managed to flee was cut down. The only remnants were the occasional fallen Auchenai spirits that rose again from orc or draenei corpses — trying to take one more with them before they went.

Wophil had no choice but to order all the bodies burned. Smoke quickly filled the poorly ventilated crypts, acrid dust hanging thick in the air.

The Shadow Council orcs who'd drunk Mannoroth's blood weren't fazed by a little smoke. Neither was Sabellian, a black dragon prince born from magma.

But Jace's respiratory system hadn't been transformed by the Dragonqueen's blood into something alien. Though the smoke didn't hurt him, it kept making him want to cough. He had to stay as far from the other orcs as possible, to avoid raising suspicion about why a fel orc from Shadowmoon Valley would be bothered by smoke.

"Looks like you don't even need to learn Shadow Bolt Volley, Naigag." Sabellian said as they walked. "Your little show earlier was impressive enough."

"A 'show' forced by circumstance. I still need to learn it." Jace said.

The two reached a bridge and stepped aside to avoid the orcs hauling loot past. Thanks to the ritual that had drained the souls of countless dying Auchenai moments ago, none of the orcs dared get close to the two Karabor emissaries by the bridge.

Jace peered over the edge. Below was a bottomless abyss glowing with blood-red shadow-light, as if countless twisted souls were buried in depths no eye could reach.

"Shadow Bolt Volley can wait." Jace turned and leaned against the railing, lowering his voice. "The priority now is stopping Wophil and his warlocks from learning those Auchenai soul-commanding spells. If they do, the rest of our time here is going to be rough. Those souls will spill everything about us — just like they did to Maladaar."

"If you ask me, why bother negotiating with those souls that sold us out?" Sabellian bared his fangs, his tone thick with anger. "What happened back there... you'd have exposed either yourself or your staff. If any demon found a way to report it to Kil'jaeden, it'd be over. We can only thank my quick thinking and your share of good luck."

Jace drew a deep breath. "Enslaved and corrupted souls can't control their own will either. Just like you couldn't, back in Azeroth, Sabellian."

"Ah, damn it." The black dragon prince waved his hand irritably but said nothing more.

"Besides, this isn't the time to tip our hand anyway. We should see what else in these crypts might be useful." As Jace said this, Uuless's greedy desire surged again — urgently wanting him to go deeper, seeking more souls.

Jace had to do his best to ignore its temptation, suppressing the power he'd just unleashed so freely.

Beyond the bridge was a spacious hall filled with seats — much like the one where Brakkat had preached.

It made sense. Auchindoun was divided into "sectors," so a similar chapel hall wasn't strange. But what drew the Shadow Council warlocks' curiosity more than the hall itself was the half-open door beside it.

Though the Auchenai crypts were filled with soul-ash and bone dust everywhere, the short corridor beyond that door was spotless — and the chambers beyond were likewise clean. That made the remnants of draenei's ink-blue blood on the floor stand out all the more.

Several draenei priests had been killed here. They didn't look like combatants, but they'd still been clutching blades and staves. What they'd died defending were the heavy stone bookshelves in the chambers — shelves full of centuries of Auchenai soul-research.

Wophil was already there, flipping through the spines of the draenei tomes. A warlock came running excitedly, clutching a thick grimoire. "The Auchenai's necromantic knowledge must all be here, Master Wophil! Their research will help us control Auchindoun further. Now that we have no enemies left here, we can complete the great work Gul'dan and Teron Gorefiend never finished!"

You could see Wophil was excited too. He was nearly pressing his failing old eyes right against the books, examining them even more carefully than he'd studied Murmur's Blood Codex.

But he didn't dare answer the warlock's suggestion right away. Instead, he glanced at Jace and Sabellian, who had just entered.

"Lord Matheridon will be very pleased with this place, Master Wophil." Sabellian looked up at the tall, heavy bookshelves. "Karabor was another draenei soul-sanctuary... though suitable as a Legion stronghold, it also has countless hard-to-control souls that harass the Legion's soldiers from time to time."

"Once the portal is established, the Legion's emissaries will have these books transported to Karabor. You will receive appropriate rewards, Master Wophil." Jace said.

"The portal won't take long to build." Wophil said. "Many of us participated in the construction of the Dark Portal. We're familiar with this kind of work. Even building to the specifications of the great Twilight Ridge portal, it won't take more than a month. But a month... is absolutely not enough for me to study these materials. Maybe not even enough for one or two books."

The old warlock's voice trembled. His tone carried some anger, but more than anything — fear and tension. In front of these two fel orcs, he could no longer hide his emotions. And Jace knew why.

Even the last time Jace had come to Auchondoun as Yuruk, he'd only used Uuless to collect souls after the demons and orcs had died — stealing the souls released from their bodies.

But this time... he had actively used Uuless to drain the souls of the dying necromancers — including Maladaar himself — while killing them all.

This was far more evil than last time. Even Jace felt uneasy about it himself.

Of course, the Shadow Council orcs were no strangers to such scenes. But the last time they'd seen it was at the Dark Portal — with Gul'dan himself doing the work. That scale was many times larger than Jace's. The draenei corpses had even paved a "Path of Glory."

Even so, this smaller-scale "reenactment" was enough to stir up the shadows lurking in the hearts of Gul'dan's former followers.

"This is not doubt or defiance..." Wophil noticed the hesitation on Jace's face and added quickly. "We paid a great price to attack the remnants of the Auchenai guards, Master Naigag. I believe that price is worth a little reward."

"What do you think, Naigag?" The black dragon looked his way.

"I suppose... not every book has to go to Karabor." Jace said. "We can allow you to keep some grimoires for study. But before the portal opens, don't waste a single soul on your necromantic experiments. Since you claim the portal can be completed within a month, you won't have to wait long."

"During that time, the souls in the Auchenai crypts will be watched by us, the Karabor emissaries. Don't let me catch you or your subordinates secretly wasting fuel meant for the Legion's portal. Otherwise, I don't think Lord Matheridon would want to hear the story of Brakkat and his 'Dark Master' again."

"Also, don't rush the portal at the expense of quality." Sabellian added. "Build it as fast as you can, but do it properly. Now that the Auchenai are beaten, the Alliance might set their sights on us next."

"I have taken it all to heart." Wophil bowed, his back hunched. "My gratitude to the Lord of Karabor. My gratitude to the two emissaries."
