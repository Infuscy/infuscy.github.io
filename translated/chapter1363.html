Chapter 1363: Divinatory Travel?

After getting in touch with Khadgar, Jace used Karlrez's voice to give him a brief overview of the current situation at Auchindoun and the Expedition's ongoing withdrawal. But when he got to the Blood Codex and Murmur's soul, he hesitated for a moment, thinking about what he was about to say. After all, Khadgar still didn't know what Jace was carrying, or how he planned to deal with the Scepter of Sargeras. But surrounded by Kirin Tor mages, Jace could never reveal the secret of Ulthalesh. At the same time, he couldn't keep hiding this from Khadgar forever. When the portal was about to open and he had to leave Auchindoun with Sabellian for the Throne of Kil'jaeden, he couldn't just use "I've got something to do" as an excuse and expect the entire Expedition to hold off the Burning Legion's demon army for him, could he? For now, though, the most important thing was transmitting the ethereal spells. The potion's duration was ticking down, and the soul shards' energy was burning. Once Khadgar had absorbed the ethereal magic techniques and found a way to reach the Telogrus Rift, they could discuss the specifics of destroying the Scepter of Sargeras. After giving a brief rundown of the situation here, Jace took out the first scroll from the Consortium, unrolled it, and began reading the text aloud. The content the ethereals had recorded on their scrolls was somewhat difficult for Jace to understand. Under the potion's effect, he could certainly read and comprehend this distorted Abyssal language. But it was like reading a paper in a field completely outside his expertise — he could understand every word individually, but together they made no sense. So he tried to set aside his thinking and focus entirely on accuracy, transmitting the text itself to Karlrez sentence by sentence. After each sentence, Karlrez would try repeating what Jace had just read, partly to recite it for the mages recording in Ilgala Tower and the void lord Gaothros, and partly to let Jace hear it back for correction. After each sentence, Jace glanced at the consumption rate of the soul shards in the array. Based on his observation of soul energy, he roughly estimated it was running thirty percent higher than the ethereals' earlier measurements. Meaning fourteen Draenei soul shards might not be enough, and even with the smaller ones added, it'd be tight. So he only glanced once, then refocused on the scroll. Concentration affected the efficiency of guiding magic too. He had to quickly adapt to transmitting Abyssal runes through the communication array and keep optimizing this void channel. Needleflame might talk about it lightly, but Jace didn't want to send the two whelps to risk stealing more soul shards. The last opportunity came from the chaos of Bleakheart's sudden death, and this one from the aftermath of the attack on Auchenai. With only portal construction ahead, there wouldn't be a good chance to hit the Shadow Council's treasury in the confusion. After steadily chanting for about an hour, Jace felt his mana approaching its limit. His head was spinning, and the hand holding the scroll had started trembling. Under these conditions, the array's efficiency would drop too, so he had to temporarily release it and rest. He downed two mana potions he'd brought along, which helped a lot. He couldn't help noting how much his mana had grown since he'd first arrived in Draenor. He remembered the first time he'd tried communicating with Karlrez — after less than ten or twenty minutes he'd felt his mana bottom out. But now he could maintain it for a full hour. Some of that was definitely thanks to the Draenei soul shards, but Jace thought the more important factors were the soul energy shared by the Tome of Infamous Names and the void energy he'd drawn from that netherwing dragon named Sargol'ku. One was Kil'jaeden's key stronghold left in Draenor, the other was Zuluhed's precious work, carefully crafted with the Scepter of Sargeras... In the half-year or so he'd spent in Draenor, Jace felt like a completely different person from when he'd first arrived. His sensitivity to shadow energy now surprised even himself. Especially when he'd encountered that revealing scroll at Auchindoun — he'd almost felt like he could control the scroll's magic effect, so he'd consciously twisted its power. Back in the day... He remembered trying to open that metal box containing the Shadow Council's teleportation coordinate crystal in the forest at the foot of the Alterac Mountains. No matter how he manipulated its power, it wouldn't budge. Looking back now, that little box's enchantment was indeed quite strong. Jace had suspected it might be something Deathwing had commissioned a powerful Shadow Council member to secretly deliver to Kel'Thuzad, to help him escape Dalaran and come to Draenor seeking refuge. Even old shaman Drek'Thar had struggled to open that little box using the Shadow Council's own methods. But compared to High Warlock Wopil's carefully crafted revealing scroll, it was still lacking. If it were present-day Jace trying to pry it open... it'd definitely be a completely different story. That morning, when he'd told Needleflame about leaving Draenor and described the terrors of the other world, Jace had also hesitated about whether to keep Needleflame by his side. It wasn't just to make her clearly understand how dangerous Azeroth was, how many threats there were, and be honest with her about the situation. It was also because he wasn't entirely sure whether staying by a warlock's side was a good thing or a bad thing for Needleflame. Even Sabellian, an arrogant black dragon who talked about Auchindoun's countless spirits like he was discussing what to have for dinner, got as cautious as an old Kirin Tor fossil when it came to Jace's shadow power and his connection to Ulthalesh. Grede was right. He wasn't the Stormwind mage apprentice anymore. The room was pitch black. The void energy released by the communication ritual cast a faint purple glow in the shadows. Morophil stood in the center, helping Jace repair the burnt array with fresh demon blood. The fiery runes on her body took on a dreamlike color in the purple light. She looked over, and the crimson flames in her eyes flickered, as if sensing the churning emotions inside Jace, but she said nothing. By the time the array was fixed, Jace checked his pocket watch. About half an hour of potion time left. He didn't waste any more time. He shook off the messy thoughts in his head, stood up, and restarted the array. The next section was more of the same — things he could read aloud but didn't really understand. When he finished this scroll bundle, the first part was done. After Karlrez repeated everything back, he said, "The Kirin Tor has recorded it all, master. Gaothros is making corrections to the runes..." Jace couldn't help asking, "So what exactly is the ethereals' method? Can you or Khadgar tell from what we've got so far?" Karlrez replied, "Khadgar says when he heard Medivh talk about astral travel in Karazhan, he mentioned something like divination. Or a prophecy spell." "What, divinatory travel?" Jace was taken aback. "A sufficiently powerful mage can see worlds deep within the Twisting Nether, even the activities on those worlds." Karlrez explained. "Even I can do it to some degree. For instance, I can sense Draenor's fragmentation and fragility from Azeroth. But anything more detailed, I can't see clearly." "Khadgar thinks Medivh used some prophecy magic to find the Draenor orcs, to find Gul'dan and establish the Dark Portal. The ethereal arcanists can use the ethereal, elusive mana of prophecy magic to directly establish contact with a target world... Of course, 'elusive' to humans. To pure arcane beings like ethereals, it might be more 'concrete.'" "But the content you transmitted didn't mention a specific method for establishing this contact. The mages here speculate it might be some kind of spiritual ritual requiring powerful energy... But since the ethereals think Kirin Tor human mages can also do it, there should be a more practical method." "As for how to accomplish it, Khadgar says it'll depend on the next scrolls you bring, my master." Hearing this demon's explanation, Jace felt like the mana he'd just spent was replenished by renewed energy. But he couldn't afford to be gone too long and arouse Wopil's suspicion. "I'll transmit the rest at this time tomorrow." "Yes, master..." Jace waved his hand and clenched his fist. The array burned out, and the chamber fell silent again. He shook off the void fire still burning on his hand and bent down to organize his bundle. He looked at Morophil's two burning eyes. "Tomorrow I'll bring the little black whelp. I'll have her fly to Allerian Stronghold to get me some stronger mana potions. And keep a close eye on this place." "Don't worry, master." The succubus smiled. "I've already talked to two orc officers who were nearby hunting Draenei. They're convinced there's nothing here." Jace nodded and turned to leave the cave.
