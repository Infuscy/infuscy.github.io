## Chapter 1364: The Fault Is All Yours

Over the next few days, whenever he had a chance to get to Morophil's chamber, Jace would go transmit ethereal spells. Sabellian stayed with Wopil, looking for opportunities to study the Blood Codex and prepare for meeting Murmur.

Sora remained at the Arcane Sanctum keeping an eye on the Shadow Council's movements, reporting any anomalies to the other two. Needleflame found a chance to leave Auchindoun and fly to Allerian Stronghold to get resources from the Expedition for the others.

Late one night, Jace finished the day's spell transmission and returned to the Arcane Sanctum after the language potion's effects fully wore off. Sabellian met him outside the Shadow Council's watch post as arranged and reapplied the fel orc disguise.

"How's the transmission going?" Sabellian asked as he cast.

"Mostly specific spells today. Complex, hard to decipher." Jace adjusted to his orc appearance and rolled his wrists. "The mages in the tower are stumped for now. Khadgar told me he'd gather the mages from Nethergarde too, have a meeting to analyze this section in detail. Might not have results until the next time I contact them."

"As long as we don't end up with everything going smoothly on our end while the other side keeps messing up." The black dragon prince snorted dismissively. "I'll tell you upfront — no matter whether the portal works out in the end, when the time comes... we have to destroy the Scepter of Sargeras."

"You've said that before. It's inevitable." Jace said. "Even if we have to fight the demons to the death in this world, that scepter can't stay. But it also depends on how your 'mission' goes. How much have you figured out about Murmur?"

"The situation's more complicated than I thought." Sabellian said. "The Blood Codex you and Khadgar had such high hopes for might not help us much. In short, it records that some civilization in some ancient corner of the Twisting Nether worshipped Murmur as a god and used soul energy to summon it. That triggered the destruction of their entire world. Only a few escaped, and one of them brought this codex to Draenor."

"Somewhere along the line, the codex was found by a group of Arakkoa. They contacted some renegade fallen Draenei priests to try summoning Murmur, but never succeeded."

"Later, during the Horde's attack on Spires of Arak, the Blood Codex was recovered by the Horde. That fool Terongor took the 'Worldbreaker' described within to be a commander of the Burning Legion and planned to ask for this so-called Worldbreaker's help when attacking Auchindoun. He and the Shadow Council succeeded, and what happened next..."

He pointed at the vast ruins ahead. "This."

"So the Blood Codex contains the summoning method." Jace said.

"More or less." Sabellian said. "But the summoning method is useless to us. We're not planning to summon it again. But don't you think there's something interesting about this story?"

"What?" Jace asked.

"Why didn't the Arakkoa and Draenei priests succeed in summoning Murmur, but the orcs did so easily?" Sabellian leaned in and lowered his voice. "There was no shortage of shadow and void worshippers among the Arakkoa. Death God Sethe and the Raven God Anzu had huge followings, and the Arakkoa who worshipped the Worldbreaker were the cream of the crop. As for the Draenei, think about the Auchenai's research into necromancy. Don't forget they're actually Eredar."

"So the orcs have something special." Jace muttered, taking a few steps before stopping. "Shamans... the relationship between elements and souls. Wopil mentioned something like that before too..."

"That's what I was thinking." Sabellian stopped beside him. "The codex mentions that the summoners of that unknown world used a special soul device when summoning Murmur. Though the book doesn't detail how this soul device works, I suspect the orcs used some way involving elemental power, or shamanic methods, to mimic that device's principles. Do you think the Shadow Council's success in sealing Murmur deep in the hall might also be related to this?"

"Very likely." Jace said. "Gul'dan led the Shadow Council in forcibly enslaving some elements with dark magic. It's entirely possible they used similar methods to control Murmur."

"I'd say you're half a shaman already." The black dragon continued. "You proved that in Nagrand. So I think you might figure this out faster than me. Anyway, don't count on me. Draenor's elements are weak and suspicious, and they don't like us."

Jace asked, "Didn't you ask Wopil himself?"

"Of course I sounded him out." The black dragon prince replied. "The Blood Codex doesn't have a method for destroying Murmur, but Wopil swears he can do it. I've tried prying some information out of him these past few days, but he's been tight-lipped..."

"He still doesn't trust us?" Jace said.

"Obviously." Sabellian said. "But probably not for the reason you think."

"Wopil should be fully convinced of our identities by now. He has other concerns. More simply put, he might already be worried you're planning to go after him."

"That's normal." Jace said. "He knows the Burning Legion doesn't trust the Shadow Council... Wait, me? 'I' plan to kill him? What about you?"

"I'm not the one who drained the Auchenai leader's soul with a terrifying staff dripping purple shadow blood." Sabellian spread his hands in an innocent gesture. "When faced with your magic that day, he felt the same bone-deep fear I did."

"Enough with the useless talk." Jace said. "He's still hung up on that day. What does he want?"

"He hasn't made his intentions clear." Sabellian said. "But these past two days, I can sense he's trying to court me, Sora, and Needleflame, while fishing for information about you — your abilities, your relationship with Terongor and Gul'dan, your connection to Karabor, especially Magtheridon. He's very worried about your soul magic. I think he's trying to imply to us that your magic and spells are a huge potential threat to the Legion and the Shadow Council."

"And your response?" Jace asked.

"Pretty much nothing." Sabellian said. "I can only play dumb for now. The information I've given him is the same stuff we told Kargath Bladefist. I needed to hear your thoughts first."

Jace moved his lips, adjusting the position of the fangs in his mouth, and stood thinking for a moment. Then he said, "You need to help him kill me."

"You mean right now?" Sabellian raised an eyebrow.

"No, no, no." Jace cleared his throat. "What I mean is, from now on, you'd better go along with him. Express fear of my magic, mix in some dissatisfaction with me, complain about my uncontrollable ambition — like wanting to skip Magtheridon and pledge allegiance directly to Kil'jaeden... In short, make him believe you want me gone too."

Sabellian nodded. "And then."

Jace scratched his teeth with his nail, thought some more, and said, "You need to scheme with that old warlock to eliminate me in the Shriekhall."

"Why." Sabellian asked.

"I've been spending the last couple of days thinking of a way to kill Wopil, destroy Murmur, and keep the Shadow Council orcs stable." Jace explained. "Wopil helped us take out the Auchenai crypts, but his prestige has gotten dangerously high the past few days. He's become hard to deal with."

"I'm worried that after we kill Wopil, the souls won't have time to help us open the portal before these Shadow Council soldiers start swarming us. Once fighting breaks out and things spiral out of control, the portal itself might get damaged. That's a risk we can't afford."

"True." Sabellian said.

"So the best way is to pin the blame for Wopil's death on him." Jace said. "Wopil fears my magic. That actually gives us the opportunity."

He continued, "Listen to this story. In the Auchenai crypts, I demonstrated my power to the Shadow Council's many warlocks, which made Wopil covet my powerful staff. So he plans to lure us into the Shriekhall, use Murmur's power to eliminate us Karabor emissaries, seize the staff, take full control of Auchindoun, then blame everything on Murmur running amok. After that, he'll pledge the staff and all of Auchindoun's soul energy to the new demon lord who's just arrived in Draenor."

Sabellian immediately added, "However, the Karabor emissaries heroically foiled Wopil's scheme, even destroyed the out-of-control otherworldly being Murmur, saving the Legion's portal and all the Shadow Council members, preventing a second traitor Gul'dan from emerging..."

"That's it." Jace said.

Sabellian slowly nodded twice. "That's a complete, good story. But there are two difficulties. First, how do we make the Shadow Council members believe everything that happens in the Shriekhall is exactly what we say it is."

"I don't know black dragons or orcs well." Jace smiled. "That's up to you." Sabellian laughed hard a few times. "Alright, alright, I understand. And second, destroying Murmur."

"Right." Jace said. "I think we need to visit the Shriekhall in person before we make our move, check the actual situation, observe exactly how Murmur is being restrained. Like you said, observe what role the elements and souls play in all this."

"And after this observation trip, you can more plausibly suggest to Wopil that he use Murmur's power, use my greed for souls, to get rid of me."

The black dragon pondered for a moment, then raised a finger. "Let's do it your way."
