Chapter 1366: The Shriekhall (Part 2)

Anyone who made it deep into the Shriekhall and witnessed everything in this rotunda would understand — Murmur had never truly submitted... Ever since it was summoned into this world, it had been constantly struggling, trying again and again to break free from the Shadow Council's control. Every attempt to throw off its bonds could be heard throughout Auchindoun and across most of the Bone Wastes — those heart-shaking roars. Jace remembered that even from the hills near Terokkar Forest, he could faintly hear the distant echoes. Without the Shriekhall, without the dense soul and shadow magic runes carved into these walls, its cries might have carried even further — to Allerian Stronghold, maybe even Shattrath and Wildhammer Stronghold. Jace realized that despite all the mental preparation, he had severely underestimated Murmur's power. But not everyone in the hall was intimidated by Murmur's sonic presence. Even as the ear-shattering echoes bounced between walls and pillars, Jace could clearly feel the hungry gasp from the bone staff in his hand. The Shadow Council's warlocks continuously channeled soul shard energy into the two massive elemental stones ahead, using them as mediums to suppress Murmur's energy bursts... And Murmur's summoning and very existence relied on the endless soul energy Terongor had sacrificed. So in Ulthalesh's eyes, this entire hall was probably filled with chaotic soul energy on a scale rarely seen even in Azeroth. They tore at each other, battled each other, stirring up a fierce hunger in Ulthalesh that made Jace's hand tremble as he held it. But it was far from time. "A pity, gentlemen." As Murmur's intensity dipped to a low point, Wopil shouted excitedly at the rotunda's center. "You didn't witness the terrifying sight when this alien god first descended on Auchindoun. It effortlessly destroyed half the Auchenai, annihilated the entire Draenei relief force. Lord Terongor and the Shadow Council paid the price of dozens of talented warlocks to control it. He himself nearly died. As for the other Horde soldiers who died at its hands — from the Bleeding Hollow to the Shadowmoon — countless." "It's nowhere near what it was back then. But still impressive enough. Right now, I'm the only one in this world who can control it, gentlemen." Wopil laughed twice and looked back at Jace. That look was almost a challenge. It was like saying that if Jace dared to take his life, he'd face the danger of Murmur breaking loose. And right now, in all of Draenor, few could stop it from causing incalculable disaster. And honestly, once you'd actually seen Murmur, that threat carried serious weight. Jace wasn't nearly as optimistic anymore. Even Sabellian was stone-faced and grim. Sora behind them was frowning slightly. This really was the Shadow Council's trump card. Enough to keep the black dragons from attacking, the Auchenai priests from moving, to leave Khadgar and Turalyon helpless and make the Alliance Expedition abandon the siege. Enough to keep even the Shadow Council itself from making too aggressive a move — only daring to strike when Bleakheart was killed and the Shadow Council's faith was on the verge of collapse, when the enemy's forces were divided and weakened. It had to be said, the Shadow Council truly lived up to being the highest hall of warlocks — gambling with their own lives and countless comrades' to summon the most powerful entity possible, then using that summoned being to make themselves unbeatable. The chaotic empty rotunda center exploded again with a visible shockwave. Distorted space ripples and silver magical light together outlined the alien god's silhouette. The black scepter swept through the air like a speeding phantom, accompanied by another thunderous magical blast. Wopil stepped back. The shadow mist surrounding his robes evaporated from that blast. But his eyes stayed fixed on the two elemental stones behind the elemental lord. Last time Murmur burst out, Jace had been too stunned by the noise to observe the elemental stones' reaction. This time, prepared, he followed Wopil's gaze. Now he could clearly see that the shadow and soul energy within these two elemental stones, already corrupted by dark magic, seemed to be vibrating at extremely high frequencies, pulling at each other... At first, he thought the vibration was resonance with this sound elemental lord's body — maybe through the air Murmur had "enslaved" in the hall, or through soul energy. But then Jace realized — if the elemental stones' vibration was being influenced by Murmur, wouldn't that make them a help to Murmur's power? Jace understood. The warlocks were using soul energy to manipulate the elemental stones, releasing another kind of sound that could interfere with Murmur's power, suppressing its struggles, keeping the Shadow Council's seals here continuously effective... So the way Wopil claimed to destroy Murmur was probably some method of manipulating the elemental stones. Just far more extreme and powerful. But how could he figure out that method? Jace felt discouraged. There was no way he could run tests in the Shriekhall. Even if Wopil gave him the chance, and Murmur stayed quiet long enough not to interfere, he didn't have much time. The less-than-a-month portal project had felt generous at first, but now it was a sword hanging over his head, slowly descending. Leaving the Shriekhall and returning to their resting area, Jace, Sabellian, and Sora were all silent. The surroundings suddenly felt deathly quiet. So quiet that Jace could hear every drop of water falling outside, the sound of a felhound's claws scraping the floor from who knew how far away — like Murmur had given him some kind of auditory blessing on the way out. "Maybe we can wait until the Kirin Tor comes through the portal." Sora ventured. "They might have a way to deal with Murmur." "Assuming we kill Wopil first, and the Shadow Council's remaining elite warlocks are willing to keep controlling Murmur for us." Sabellian said. "And assuming the portal doesn't screw up at the last moment, and when the Burning Legion chases us here, even besieges Auchindoun, they can still keep this alien monster stable." Jace propped his cheek on his hand and sighed. "Maybe Magtheridon shouts from outside the gate and those warlocks will dutifully release Murmur to destroy the portal from inside and wipe us all out." "Yes, it's a bizarre elemental." Sabellian said. "I've never seen anything like it in Azeroth. I can't even understand how it exists in that place, let alone kill it." "At least we know soul energy can control it. The key is those two elemental stones behind it." Jace said. "I could see that too." Sabellian looked at Jace. "So have you figured out how to use them?" Jace shook his head. "Wopil wasn't lying. He's the only one in the world who knows how to kill Murmur." "Maybe even he doesn't know. Just bluffing." Sabellian raised his hand, then dropped it on his knee like giving up. "That'd be even worse." Jace said. "Indeed." The black dragon prince grunted. "Looks like we need to find a way to make him talk. That's probably our last breakthrough." "Want to try hypnotizing him?" Sora said quietly. "I've barely used hypnosis to make anyone confess, but... if there's no other choice, I can try. I know many of my kind can do it easily." "Your hypnotic magic? I'm not optimistic." Sabellian said. "Gul'dan is dead. Teron Gorefiend is dead. Kargath Bladefist is in Hellfire Citadel and doesn't acknowledge his past service to the Shadow Council anymore... So Wopil is the Shadow Council's de facto leader now." "So you think the most powerful warlock in the Shadow Council can be controlled by your hypnosis? If it were that easy, Bleakheart would have used whatever 'Dark Lord' granted him to make a move long ago." Sora lowered her head, deflated. Jace's eyes flicked between the black dragon and the green dragon. "What about mine?" "Who..." Sabellian's eyes slid over, then widened as realization dawned. "Uh, wait, you mean... well, I'll admit Lady Morophil might be the most powerful Sayyad I've ever seen, but..." "I once made Morophil enslave Nek'rosh, Zuluhed's second-in-command, in Grim Batol." Jace said. "And back then, Morophil's power was leagues below what it is now. She hadn't even absorbed the Tome of Infamous Names. Now, could she use charm magic to forcibly control Wopil?" "Nek'rosh?" Sabellian frowned. "You're telling me that Dragonmaw chieftain who swore to defy my father was controlled by your succubus? Are you joking?" "Only for a short time. Then your old man flew over and flattened Wildhammer's homeland in one go." Jace said seriously. "But I'm telling the truth. Do you think it's feasible?" Sabellian stared coldly at Jace for a moment, but finally just sighed. "We don't have any better ideas." "Then we proceed as planned." Jace said. "You set a time with Wopil. Don't rush it. We need a process where he builds trust and leaves me and Lady Sosala out. During that time, try to get him to reveal his method for destroying Murmur. Let's minimize unnecessary risks. Use... your worry that Murmur might break loose during my assassination as the excuse." "You don't need to tell me the details." The black dragon prince said impatiently.
