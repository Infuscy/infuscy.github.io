## Chapter 1367: Indelible Memories

Night fell. The central plaza of Auchindoun rang once more with the crack of whips and the howls of the beaten.

Using the brief chaos, Jace dodged the soldiers patrolling the passages and corridors, made his way up to the hidden ritual chamber, and dispelled the fel orc disguise with a simple Light spell before stepping into the pitch-black room.

Suddenly, a shadow emerged behind him. Morophil lunged from behind and wrapped her arms around Jace, her hand slowly sliding toward Angelista's crimson sash... Jace caught her wrist.

"Darling, the allure of your abundant mana... there's nothing in the whole Twisting Nether that could resist it." Morophil whispered almost directly into his ear.

"Is that supposed to be a compliment?" Jace asked.

Morophil giggled twice, walked to the side and sat on the stone platform, crossing her hooves. After observing her master for a moment, she said, "You seem troubled. Things not going well?"

Jace bent down to check the array the succubus had redrawn. "I might need you to come into Auchindoun soon and help us deal with Wopil."

"Wopil?" Morophil said. "I don't think that guy can beat you and that black dragon prince, let alone with two dragons helping..."

"More specifically, Murmur." Jace brushed the dust off his hands. "I want you to use charm magic to enslave Wopil, then make him use his method, his spells or whatever, to get rid of that Murmur problem. If Murmur isn't eliminated, I'm worried the portal and our escape afterward will be threatened."

"You've seen Murmur." Morophil said. "I have." Jace said.

"You should go in and see it yourself, Sayyad!" The imp poked its head out of Jace's demon pouch. "You know that so-called Shriekhall... It really is a shrieking hall! I hadn't even phased when it knocked me out with its scream! What kind of stupid world would worship something like that as a god!"

"It getting knocked out doesn't prove anything." Morophil mocked. "Even the carrion vultures outside could knock you out."

"You... let master tell you!" The imp hopped up and down. "Many people call it the 'Worldbreaker'!"

"It is terrifying." Jace admitted. "We can't underestimate that thing. Even Sabellian isn't confident. By that standard, it should be the toughest opponent we've faced in all of Draenor."

"How will I get in?" Morophil asked.

"Let Lord Sabellian give you a disguise spell!" The imp suggested.

"Turning her into a new fel orc to enter the Shadow Labyrinth wouldn't be a problem." Jace said. "But a fel orc suddenly appearing? Wopil would never let her into the most important Shriekhall. We earned this trust by helping him defeat High Priest Maladaar and execute Bleakheart. She'll just have to go invisible the old way."

The succubus shook her head. "But the depths of that palace are full of Eyes of Kilrogg, master. I can barely avoid the outer ones. What about the most important Shriekhall?"

Jace crossed his arms. "That's the interesting part. The Shriekhall is packed with the Shadow Labyrinth's most elite warlocks and warriors, but not a single Eye of Kilrogg. Murmur's sound waves can't even sustain the black dragon prince's disguise for long, let alone fragile eyes. They can't hold up in there. So that's your chance."

"Sounds good." The succubus smiled.

Jace said, "The key now is, you've met Wopil before. Do you think you can successfully control him with mental magic now?"

"His will is strong, master. I can only try." Morophil said. "If you could find a way to drain his mana first, I think it'd be almost certain."

"Mana burn." Jace said. "I can burn through it fast."

The succubus switched which leg was crossed and asked, "Does that mean open combat with Wopil in the Shriekhall? In front of all those elite Shadow Council members? Aren't you worried all the orcs outside will panic?"

"It won't be us fighting him openly. He'll come after us first." Jace said. "Don't worry. Sabellian and I have already worked out how to calm the Shadow Council."

"Actually, this time I'm not going after Wopil. I'm waiting for Wopil to come after me. Sabellian will pretend to scheme with Wopil on an assassination — an assassination targeting me. He'll lure me into the depths of the Shriekhall, use Murmur's sound energy to disrupt my casting, and kill me..."

"He's after Ulthalesh?" Morophil smiled. "And you'll use that to frame him as a traitor who betrayed the Burning Legion for treasure..."

"Exactly." Jace said.

"I wonder what you've all been cooking up in there. Sounds interesting." The succubus chuckled, looked up at the dark chamber, and asked, "What about this place?"

"I'll do this after all the ethereal scrolls are transmitted. Then I'll burn this place down, or collapse it. Leave no trace." Jace said. "So I need to start the communication ritual now. But Needleflame isn't back yet, and I'm almost out of mana potions."

"No need to rush. She hasn't been gone that long." Morophil said. "Give her some time to fly. She's just a little thing whose horns haven't even grown much."

"Sounds like you two get along well?" Jace asked.

"Not bad. Honestly, who wouldn't like a clever, wicked little menace?" Morophil smiled with a sigh. "Ah, Sayyad, nobody cares what you like!" The imp shouted.

"Don't flatter yourself." The succubus's face went cold as she glanced at him. "You're neither clever, nor wicked, nor a menace. You're just an irrelevant booger."

"KAA!" The imp was so angry a ball of fel fire burst from under its feet, almost shooting to the ceiling before Jace caught it mid-air with a "ow" and threw it away.

"Don't burn my array, damn it." Jace yelled. "Go keep watch outside. Stay alert for any approaching orcs!"

"I know, I know, master. Actually... I was just flying out to do my job!" The imp's voice came from outside.

"Sigh, master's favoritism is truly overwhelming." Morophil tilted her head.

"One more useless word and you're out too." Jace said.

The succubus immediately put a finger to her lips and curled back onto the stone in a wounded pose. Though with her current body, curling up didn't make her look like a cat anymore. More like a coiled crimson viper, covered in demonic spikes.

Jace pulled out the language potion and was about to drink it when Morophil immediately reminded him, "Arcane Intellect first, master... I don't think that counts as useless."

"It doesn't. Thanks for the reminder." Jace nodded and chanted the Arcane Intellect spell.

But after casting Arcane Intellect, when the memories came flooding in, the image of facing Murmur in Auchindoun seemed to linger, refusing to fade, burned into his mind.

He rubbed his eyes, trying to shake the image. But after the dim Shriekhall space, lit only by braziers, faded, Murmur's reflection remained.

Only this time, it wasn't in Auchindoun. More precisely, it wasn't in the Auchindoun he knew...

In his mind, beneath a grand hall of white and blue-violet, groups of orcs in black robes were chanting. Including him. And he was the center of these orcs.

He stood in the middle, looking up at the silver-white storm spiraling in the center of the space. The image in his memory tore apart. A shockwave burst without warning, then everything went black, and he was back in the pitch-black chamber.

Jace gasped for breath. What was that? Was something wrong with Arcane Intellect?

No. That's not right. He suddenly realized — that wasn't something he'd experienced. It was Teron Gorefiend's memory!

Back when Teron Gorefiend still used the name Terongor, he had led the Shadow Council's attack on Auchindoun and summoned Murmur.

And the impression of Murmur's descent had been so profound that even though the memory had already dispersed in Jace's mind, Arcane Intellect could still bring out a brief flashback.

But it was just a blurry image. Jace tried to recall the details, hoping to remember some Murmur-related spells like he had with the Dark Shield incantation, but he couldn't.

The memory fragment vanished in an instant, with no sound at all. "What's wrong, master?" Morophil asked.

"Just a fragment of Teron Gorefiend's memory. It's nothing." Jace downed the language potion in one gulp. "It's happened before."
