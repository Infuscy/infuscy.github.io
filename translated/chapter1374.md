## Chapter 1374: Vopiel's Incantation

Vopiel clearly couldn't accept this sudden reversal. He stumbled back toward the circular chamber, screaming at the black dragon prince who stood by watching it all indifferently.

"...You won't get away either! You won't get away either!"

Morofel closed in on the increasingly desperate Vopiel, her apocalypse blades rising slowly. Vopiel was practically wailing now. "Naihuogar, it was your idea! You masterminded everything! And now you're playing innocent..."

The front-row warlocks' efforts seemed to be paying off. Vopiel's screams grew clearer. When he realized this too, the expression on his face gradually froze.

The window to kill Naijag had passed. The power of silence in the Screaming Hall was waning.

"No, no... it's you!"

Vopiel suddenly raised his black staff and pointed at the demon, chanting a spell. At the same time, voidwalkers began seeping through the walls on both sides.

"Monari, Katali, Shukil!" Jace roared the Mana Burn incantation, raising his staff at Vopiel. Violet-red demonic fire immediately engulfed the old, hunched figure...

Vopiel screamed in madness. Most of the newly manifested voidwalkers dissipated along with him. But at the same time, Jace felt his disguise rapidly peeling away under the shadow energy, his form growing thinner...

Morofel channeled a ball of shadowfire with both hands, sending it at the barely-standing Vopiel. The flames still hadn't burned out on the old warlock's body when he went crazy, clutching his head and staggering around. But how could that resist the psychic control of a Sayade like Morofel? His delirious muttering soon became, "You are my master. I am just a worthless mongrel. Master, dog..."

"Destroy Murmur, my loyal servant," Morofel said simply in Demonic.

"Destroy Murmur, destroy..."

Vopiel nodded vigorously, then turned to fix his gaze on the two elemental stones.

The warlocks at the front, who had been channeling the elemental binding, seemed to sense something wrong. One of the Broken priests turned and caught sight of Vopiel's movement. "Stop, Master Vopiel!" he shouted in alarm.

"Destroy Murmur. As Gul'dan taught. Destroy Murmur..."

Vopiel ignored the Broken's warning and walked closer to the circular chamber.

"No, don't come closer, Master Vopiel!" The other front-row warlocks sensed the danger too, turning around one by one.

But Vopiel still walked up behind them, staring intently at the two elemental stones, raising his staff high. "Loghan... Hamar... Nok... Gar..."

Vopiel chanted the elemental words passed down from the ancient clans. For a moment, the scene seemed so familiar that Jace couldn't help but frown. By now, his disguise had been almost completely torn apart by the magic — even his face was beginning to look more human.

But everyone's attention was fixed on Vopiel at the front — the cult guards, Sabellian, even Morofel. No one had the presence of mind to closely observe what was happening beneath his crimson cloak.

The two elemental stones let out a pleasant chime, both flickering with blue arcane light. Several of the front-row warlocks abandoned their positions and ran to Vopiel's side, begging him to step back. But Vopiel only continued chanting stubbornly, paying them no mind.

Just then, a discordant noise broke through the chiming. The empty space in the center of the circular chamber warped again, like a figure struggling frantically behind glass, growing clearer.

Was it working? Was Murmur being strongly disrupted by the elemental stones?

A violent surge of soul energy erupted from the struggling figure, shooting straight up to the dome and spinning like a storm of undeath!

Murmur manifested again, like a silver-white storm. The crown atop its head shattered, and torrents of steam-like energy burst from its entire body...

Everyone present was thrown off-balance by this overwhelming force. Even Sabellian's dragon claw magic was blown apart. Sora was hurled out the moment she emerged, crashing to the ground.

The poor warlocks at the front fared even worse — sent flying from beside Vopiel, slamming into walls or landing among the orcs' tents.

Now only Vopiel himself, still channeling elemental energy, stood at the front. Sabellian and Morofel remained steady, but even Jace, further back, had to lift his cloak to shield himself. The Nature Protection Potion's effects had long been worn clean by the successive blasts.

Yet despite this, Vopiel wasn't bluffing. Murmur's form was rapidly disintegrating...

It was working. According to the Blood Codex, the foundation of this otherworldly elemental's binding was soul, and its foundation for existing in the material plane was also soul.

So as long as this incantation released the souls, it would naturally be unsummoned. And these freely escaping souls would become Ulthalesh's...

No. Wait.

Vopiel's robe, pauldrons, and black cloak all danced wildly in the violent currents. But the old, withered warlock showed no sign of backing down. He kept his staff-like arms raised high, shrieking the twisted elemental curse even more stubbornly!

This gave Jace a strange sense of deja vu, and a flicker of unease rose in his heart.

"He's too close." Jace muttered. Then, pushing against the waves of turbulence, he stepped forward and shouted, "He's too close! Get him farther away!"

Morofel's soul link picked up on Jace's rising emotion. She looked back. Jace roared in Demonic, "Get him farther away, Morofel! Make him cast from farther away! Now!"

Just then, Vopiel's incantation suddenly became punctuated with insane laughter. But he didn't stop chanting. The louder he chanted, the more uncontrollable the laughter mixed in became...

Before Morofel could even give the order, the spinning storm swept out a massive black staff!

The next second, the staff dragged a sonic boom that warped the air as it swept across the circular chamber. The fallen warlocks — including Vopiel himself — were instantly reduced to a cloud of blood mist...

"What—"

Sabellian stared in shock. Too late.

Had Vopiel done it on purpose? Following the succubus's command, but taking everyone with him to die?

Regardless, with the warlock's incantation broken, the elemental stones flickered and dimmed. In the center, Murmur's form grew increasingly distinct, soon coalescing into a white, raging beast dragging a torrential body!

It opened its vast, empty maw and let out a violent howl. All the souls spinning under the dome shattered into fragments, flickering and dispersing...

This howl was even more terrifying than every struggle before it. It felt like Jace's mind and consciousness were being torn apart in an instant. Not just him — countless orcs behind him had blood streaming from all seven orifices, crying and collapsing to the ground. Sabellian trembled all over and staggered back several steps. Sora covered her ears and screamed in agony. Her red skin couldn't hold any longer, exposing large patches of green scales, and her tail slipped out from beneath her cloak. But who among those present had time to care about any of that?

Murmur was free!
