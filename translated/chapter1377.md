## Chapter 1377: Everything for the Portal Now

After Murmur was driven back to the elemental plane, the large stockpile of soul fragments that had been prepared to bind it — along with the magical artifacts the warlocks had gathered from Auchindoun — became useless.

Plus there was the enormous staff Murmur had left behind. Dealing with that was going to be quite troublesome.

The staff was called the Shockwave Scepter. In the game, it was a main-hand healing mace — not even a two-handed weapon.

Anyway, Jace couldn't imagine how an ordinary humanoid could lift a hammer-staff roughly the size of a stone pillar — one that could send Deathwing's son bleeding from two hits — with a single hand.

Although Murmur's physical form had been destroyed, the staff had been infused with enough of its soul to take on a physical form. The remnant magic left behind in the material plane still held some power.

So that day, the warlocks of the Screaming Hall were passionately discussing how to repurpose the residual magical energy.

As for the other artifacts — mana gems, bottled elemental essence, soul devices, and countless more — they were incalculable. Add to that Vopiel's not-completely-destroyed shadow staff, the magical orb he'd used for some kind of shadow divination, and the research on the void, fel, demons, and souls left behind by the Auchenai and the Shadow Council...

Some of that material might not even exist in Karazhan's great library.

If all of it could be brought back to Azeroth, even if they couldn't make full use of it, at least they could sell it for a fortune.

A few months ago, when Jace first came here, he probably wouldn't have had many thoughts about the stuff here. But now, with the portal nearing completion and the channel between Azeroth and Draenor about to open again — a gate wide enough for thousands of expedition soldiers and Kirin Tor mages to pass through — bringing all this back shouldn't be hard, right?

In reality, though the orc warlocks were discussing animatedly, they kept glancing over to gauge Jace's reaction. They knew that all these items were effectively already under the Karabor emissaries' control. They didn't have much say anymore.

No matter how fancy the discussion got, the final decision was Jace's.

Jace coughed, covering his rough lips where his tusks protruded. The noise in the Screaming Hall died down by more than half.

"Send the staff — and every moveable magic item here — to the portal site," Jace said. "Including the ones in the Auchenai crypts. They'll all be used together with the souls of all Auchindoun to power the portal's opening. Whatever's left will be offered along with the souls collected by my staff to the master about to arrive."

"I agree," an orc warlock nodded.

"No objection."

"Agreed as well."

Several of the warlocks who'd previously clustered around Blakart and Vopiel switched sides without hesitation, fixing their loyalty on a new target. It seemed that for these deeply dark-path individuals, clan honor and ancient tradition were worth less than a few soul fragments.

The reason Jace wanted all these things moved to the portal site wasn't purely to save Auchindoun's souls some effort. It was also so that once the portal channel opened, the mages on the other side could use these artifacts to deal with the demons closing in, buying time for the retreat...

More importantly, it made it easier for the other side to pack up everything left over, instead of having to collect it from all over.

Just as he thought things were settled, an orc called out loudly, "The traitor Vopiel never told us who this new master is, honored Master Naijag. The portal's construction is already halfway done. We want to know his name, so we can cheer his glorious arrival!"

"What's your name?" Jace asked directly.

The other warlocks seemed to sense the hidden anger and faint threat in Jace's tone, and most shrank back. But this orc warlock stuck his neck out and shouted, "Togun! Of the Shadowmoon clan!"

"Very well, Togun." Jace's voice turned cold. "Neither Lord Magtheridon nor Lord Kil'jaeden has seen fit to tell me or Naihuogar exactly what great being is about to arrive in this world to help the Black Temple conquer Draenor once and for all... I imagine it's because a lowly servant of my caliber isn't worthy of knowing Lord Kil'jaeden's plans."

"The portal at Auchindoun will be powered by countless draenei souls to connect with a massive Legion world — one that may harbor several lords comparable to Lord Magtheridon, all awaiting the Master's command. Every one of them has the power to easily destroy multiple worlds."

Jace spoke in Orcish, almost like reciting poetry. His Blackrock accent added considerable weight to the rhythm.

"But if I had to guess who's coming to Draenor, I'd say it's Doom Lord Kazzak himself — the King of Xoroth. The demonic scriptures record that he has the power to cleave the void itself. Only he could fulfill Lord Kil'jaeden's wishes, perhaps even reactivate the Dark Portal..."

"Doom Lord Kazzak?" The warlocks murmured among themselves, sounding intimidated. "Khadgar used the Skull of Gul'dan to wipe the Dark Portal's magic. It's impossible to reactivate it from Draenor's side. Even Lord Kazzak couldn't do that..."

"Can Auchindoun bear Lord Kazzak's stride? Magtheridon's arrival destroyed half of the Karabor Temple when he came to this world."

Jace was somewhat surprised by the warlocks' concerns. They weren't just a rabble scraped together — they lived up to the Shadow Council's reputation.

Because later, Kazzak indeed didn't directly come to Draenor. Instead, he reactivated the Dark Portal from Azeroth's side and then entered Outland — exactly as they'd predicted.

But Jace didn't need to feel guilty now.

Facing the warlocks' doubts, Jace simply raised his voice. "I'm just a messenger. An executor. If you want to question anything, go question Lord Kil'jaeden!"

The moment Jade invoked the Deceiver's name, the warlocks all lowered their heads, no longer daring to argue.

He looked around at everyone present. "If there are no other questions, then begin work immediately. Prepare everything for the portal's power source!"

"Yes!" The warlocks answered in unison.

As he left the Screaming Hall, an orc warlock fell into step beside him. The guy seemed to have been waiting at the entrance for a while.

Unlike the other Shadow Council members who maintained an arrogant demeanor even in submission, this one bared his teeth in a sycophantic grin right away. "Master Naijag, I'd like to ask you about summoning Sayade demons..."

Jace had assumed, based on Sabellian's warning that warlocks wanted to learn the summoning spell, that they'd wait until the portal was built, the demon lord summoned, the expedition force eliminated, and relations between Karabor and the Shadow Council had cooled down...

He hadn't expected someone to show up before Vopiel had even finished cooling into a necklace for the black dragon — before Jace could even visit the Tomb of Lights to confirm the pact with the former high priest's soul.

"You're already a Shadow Council member. You can't summon a Sayade?" Jace asked.

"You know what I mean, Master Naijag." The orc lowered his voice. "I'm talking about a Sayade Lord. I could see a trace of the Mark of Sargeras in her soul. Not particularly obvious, but the characteristics were clear enough. It means she might have been a high-ranking officer, executor, or even ruler in the Burning Legion! Her status is no lower than Observer Lord Hilrak. And I've seen a Sayade like that before — though far weaker than the one who serves you..."

"Where did you see it?" Jace asked.

The orc answered, "Another of Lord Magtheridon's emissaries came here once. The huntress banshee who served him had a similar Mark of Sargeras... I passed her in the Arcane Sanctum. It made an impression because for a demon bearing a mark like that, her power seemed somewhat weak."

He pressed eagerly, "So have the warlocks of Karabor already mastered a higher form of demonic domination under Lord Magtheridon's tutelage? Can they enslave demon lords like Eredar warlocks? Does it have to do with Lord Magtheridon's blood? But that previous emissary hadn't drunk the demon blood..."

"Could I have a chance to go to Karabor? Not in the name of the Shadow Council — as a new apprentice, Master Naijag."

"The Shadow Council hasn't yet atoned for Gul'dan's betrayal." Jace looked at the warlock. "The portal is a good opportunity. For now, focus on the portal's opening. Once Karabor and the Shadow Council reestablish contact, your questions and requests will be answered."

"Yes!" The orc could barely contain his ecstasy. "I understand, Master Naijag!"

After the warlock left, Needleflame walked over slowly. "You personally eliminated Murmur and demonstrated your power. You're now an idol worshiped by the entire cult..."

After stating this coolly, she couldn't help shedding her fel orc guard's composure, grinding her teeth and muttering, "I hate that I wasn't there to witness it!"

"Shh." Jace said. "Yesterday and today — have you heard any rumors from the Screaming Hall about a human-looking person appearing?"

"Watch your words, Master Naijag." Needleflame leaned in close. "You wouldn't want to be mistaken for a secret worshiper of that 'Dark Lord,' would you?"

Jace snorted twice, then pointed at the retreating orc warlock's back in the corridor. "Tell your father. Have him make this one disappear. Say he defected from the Shadow Council and fled Auchindoun."

"Yes." Needleflame acknowledged.
