Chapter 1380: That Day Draws Near

Near a mesa outside Auchindoun that hadn't been completely swallowed by sand, Jace and Sora landed behind the cover of a curved ritual structure.

Auchindoun was visible from here. Since the Shadow Council orcs had defeated the Auchenai, losing their opponents in the holy crypts, and had begun building the portal in the open plaza, they'd expanded their patrol range far beyond Auchindoun. Flying any closer than this — even before dawn — would risk being spotted by the orc sentries.

Sabellian was already waiting here. After transforming Jace back into a fel orc, he asked immediately, "What did those so-called spirits say?"

"Not only are they willing to help open the portal, they're also willing to help us deal with the Shadow Council when we show our hand," Jace said. "Seems Maladaar's death freed a lot of souls from the twisted state he'd put them in. With their sanity restored, they're more than willing to do what they can for their living kin."

"How disappointing." Sabellian crossed his arms. "I was hoping they'd refuse, so I could rightfully claim all of Auchindoun's souls for myself. Find some time to raise all the corpses and build myself an undead army in Draenor to serve the black dragonflight."

"Don't pretend I don't exist, Prince Sabellian." Sora said unhappily. "Since you're in Draenor, can't you learn to coexist with this world like your ancestors did ten thousand years ago?"

"Alright, alright, alright." The black dragon raised a hand. "I was just making a villain joke that matches your expectations of us. Don't take it so seriously. I really wonder how you, Jace Sesso, manage to travel with a female dragon who has absolutely no sense of humor."

"Much easier than traveling with you, Your Highness." Jace said.

Sabellian chuckled twice. "And what about Khadgar's side?"

"Aegwynn has agreed to help." Jace checked his pocket watch. "If everything goes smoothly, Khadgar might have already delivered the ethereals' spell to Aegwynn by now. Maybe they're already inside Karazhan tower. I just hope his teleportation magic doesn't go wrong in Deadwind Pass's chaotic shadows."

"You're similar to Khadgar in one way, Jace Sesso." Sabellian said. "No matter what kind of mess he runs into, he always finds some strange way to correct it in ways you can't imagine. Anyway, I never expected an otherworldly horror like Murmur to be taken down by a human warlock in a dream."

"Don't underestimate the power of dreams, Prince Sabellian." Sora said. "Azeroth was born from dreams. And the Old Gods... have repeatedly tried to destroy her through dreams too."

When Sora mentioned the Old Gods, the teasing expression on the black dragon's face quickly faded.

"Let's get back to business." He cleared his throat. "Now that Aegwynn's involved, I imagine their entry into the Terokkar Rift shouldn't be a problem."

"Right." Jace said. "Next, Kalrez will enter the rift opened by the Kirin Tor and use one of his best skills — corrupting a void fragment with fel energy to establish a Legion portal there. With a doom lord leading, plus the archmages of the Kirin Tor and the Silvermoon Convocation, their pace should be much faster than the Shadow Council's. Then they'll use the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh to open it. We'll coordinate from this side."

He continued, "I'll agree on a specific time with Khadgar so our portal accurately catches the channel passing through the Legion's barrier. We can't open too early and waste energy, and dragging it out too long might make the orcs suspicious."

"Excellent." The black dragon prince glanced sideways. "When do we head for the Throne of Kil'jaeden?"

Jace said, "Once the portal opens, the Burning Legion will immediately realize we're about to run. They won't care about the Tome of Damned Names anymore by then. If they don't act now, we'll escape without any losses. So once the expedition withdraws into Auchindoun and Khadgar takes over Auchindoun's defenses, we leave for the Throne of Kil'jaeden immediately. How long will it take you to fly there?"

"At full speed, maybe less than two days." Sabellian extended his thumb, then his index finger. "But you'll need to find a way to hold tight to me. No comfortable sightseeing like before — that's absolutely out of the question."

"You call that comfortable sightseeing?" Jace sighed. "Fine. Since it's that fast, I'll make sure I can hold on."

He looked northeast. "We have to be careful. The doomguards at the Throne of Kil'jaeden might be dispatched in large numbers, intercepting expedition soldiers who haven't made it to Auchindorn yet. Ground demons might join forces with the orcs of Hellfire Peninsula to attack Honor Hold and the armory. But even so, there'll definitely be plenty of demons left on that mountain. They've sent so many troops through the portal recently."

"Unavoidable." Sabellian said. "Whether or not Kil'jaeden is actually waiting for you to come, at least Magtheridon is undoubtedly preparing for a final reckoning. If the Tome of Damned Names hadn't been lost, this general offensive might have started two or three months ago."

"So we can't just charge in head-on." Jace said. "We're not going there for a war of attrition with the demons. We're going for the Scepter of Sargeras."

"I understand." Sabellian said. "I'll approach under the cover of the Twisting Nether. Fortunately, the massive demon teleportation at the Throne of Kil'jaeden destroyed all the land at the mountain's base. It's full of cracks and cliffs dropping straight into the abyss, almost directly connected to the endless void. Easy for me to get close without being detected — like when we ambushed Dragonmaw Fortress from the Gor'loreth. You saw that yourself."

"Unforgettable." Jace said.

Sabellian took a deep breath, looking west at Auchindoun's shadow just beginning to catch the light. "I'm not one to believe in fate. But whoever's behind all this... they've got a pretty trashy sense of humor, Jace Sesso."

The black dragon's eyes locked on the distance. "A few years ago, I followed my father's orders, risked my life to carry several of Teron Gorefiend's men across the Great Sea to seize the Scepter of Sargeras and bring it to Draenor. Now I'm about to risk my life carrying Teron Gorefiend's soul across half of Draenor to destroy the Scepter of Sargeras."

"I think fate gave you a chance to atone, Prince Sabellian." Sora said. "After all, if you hadn't helped those death knights find the Scepter of Sargeras, Draenor and the countless lives on it wouldn't have ended up like this. Don't you think?"

"I think I shouldn't be lectured by a little girl like you." The black dragon gave a perfunctory smile, then was silent for a long moment. "But you're right."

"I want to come with you too, Jace." Sora said.

"You're too important to the expedition, Sora. You can't leave Auchindoun then." Jace said. "The doomguard legions at Karabor and the Throne of Kil'jaeden could fly to Auchindorn at any moment. The gryphon riders and dragons will have an extremely hard fight. You need to be here providing support. Your role is unique."

"Listen to him, Lady Somaliel." Sabellian said. "Help my flight get through this crisis. When we fight again later, I'll kill a few less green dragons as repayment."

The black dragon grinned. Sora shot him an unhappy look, but could only nod in the end.

She understood too. At the expedition's most difficult moment, a nearly-adult green dragon like her, channeling calming magic, could turn the tide. For Jace and his group's "decapitation strike," her role was limited.

"Between now and then, I can teach you something. Shadow Bolt Volley, for instance." Sabellian turned to Jace. "What if there really are hundreds of demons waiting for us at the Scepter of Sargeras? My breath alone will always miss some."

"I've been waiting." Jace said.

"Good. Tomorrow at dusk, meet me by the broken sacrificial pillar on the southeast side. I found a pretty good spot for practicing that kind of magic." Sabellian said.

"I'll be there." Jace said.
