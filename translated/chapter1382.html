Chapter 1382: The Black Dragon Prince's Clever Trick

Shadow Bolt Volley... for a long time in the game, it was a lethal technique only the most terrifying evil sorcerers could unleash. The leaders of the Shadow Council, the high priests of the Amani trolls who worshipped dark gods, or dead mage ghosts and undead necromancers steeped in concentrated shadow and hatred.

For demons like Kazzak, they could casually fire off Shadow Bolt Volley after volley, just to quickly erase those self-overestimating pieces of debris who dared block their path.

Sabellian probably thought mastering Shadow Bolt Volley was no big deal, but to a transmigrator like Jace, it carried a completely different significance.

Still, Shadow Bolt Volley was ultimately just splitting a single shadow bolt into countless spreading projectiles. It worked great against the fragile undead swarming around here, but against a large group of powerful demons — especially ones at the level of Doomguard — it would probably still fall a bit short.

Sabellian walked down the slope, carefully stepping around the orc corpses Jace had struck down, and made his way to the top of an embankment on the opposite side.

He shouted over, "Think back, boy. How did the fire help you enter the rift into the Obsidian plane? Use the same method. Ask the fire to bring you to my side... the elements of Draenor should be willing to help you!"

Jace tried conjuring a flame from his hand. He moved his fingers, observing the warm fire burning in his palm, watching the changes in its bright blaze as he attempted to establish a connection with the elemental fire.

When he heard the fire whisper in his hand, and when the wind around him and the sound of rolling sand and gravel all fell quiet, he murmured in the elemental tongue: "Flame, please carry me to the other side of the slope. You can feel it..."

Before he finished speaking, he felt the fire in his hand blaze up violently, spreading across his entire body. The fire burned so fiercely, carrying with it an intensely eager desire — as if it were striving desperately to achieve its goal...

But as it burned on, even Jace began to wonder whether they'd been summoned to burn him alive or to help him.

Wait, no.

Even Jace — who had Red Dragon blood, wore enchanted robes, and a Red Dragonhide cloak — started finding the heat unbearable. The powerful elemental fire blazed hotter and hotter, quickly turning him into a fireball with no sign of diminishing...

Jace Shadowstepped away, shaking off the flames. Even after emerging from the shadows, he was still smoking.

Thankfully, at this point everything he wore was enchanted magical gear. Aside from a hole burned in his pants, his other equipment and bags were undamaged. But his neck and face were flushed red, and touching them still hurt.

On the other side, Sabellian was already doubled over laughing. Between laughs, he called out, "I'm starting to wonder if you've been bullshitting me! Are you sure you actually chased Onyxia into that dimension?"

"I didn't open that dimension," Jace shouted back. "I just got in with the flames' help. Having to drive the fire myself to cross a distance is a completely different level of difficulty."

"Say, do you need to drink a mana potion, hmm?" Sabellian teased. "Did that Shadow Bolt Volley just now drain all your mana?"

"Still got plenty left," Jace said.

"Maybe Queen Alexstrasza's blessing isn't as powerful as you thought," Sabellian said meaningfully.

Jace thought for a moment, then touched his cloak. "You mentioned this red dragon cloak lets the wearer fly using the power of fire."

"At least Zuluhed could do it," Sabellian said. "Don't ask me. It's not a gift from my father, and I don't need to wear it to fly, you know?"

Jace nodded, wrapped the cloak tighter around himself, and conjured another flame from his hand.

He felt the red dragonfire contained within the cloak, establishing an elemental connection with it, while the flame in his hand burned ever brighter.

The element's voice called out, murmured, as if waiting for Jace's summons. Jace called out again in the elemental tongue: "Flame... take me to that slope. Take me..."

The flame spun into a howling storm of fire, enveloping him. Jace felt as if he'd stepped into a burning corridor. He charged through recklessly to escape the heat, and the fire rapidly receded as darkness returned around him.

He patted the ash from his hair, slapped out a few embers on his cloak, looked around — and suddenly found Sabellian standing right behind him.

"I did it?" Jace asked in surprise.

"You'll have to learn to perform elemental teleportation without the cloak's help eventually, Jace Sesso." The black dragon narrowed his eyes. "You've already entered the Obsidian plane through a similar method. In theory, mastering this shouldn't be so difficult."

"You said it yourself — this is Draenor," Jace said. "Both arcane magic and elemental magic are somewhat disrupted here. Besides, I did it. Maybe I'll learn to fly with it soon, just like Zuluhed."

"I suppose," Sabellian said helplessly.

"By the way, is there anything else? Spells, or abilities I could learn, Prince Sabellian?" Jace asked.

"What do you take me for, a Dalaran spell instructor?" the black dragon prince said, cocking an eye.

"I never studied at Dalaran," Jace said. "But I know even the archmages of Dalaran acknowledge that black dragons are masters of dark magic — your father, and both your siblings."

"I don't think there's anything else I can teach you," Sabellian said after a moment's thought. "But since you're going to Blackrock Spire to deal with Nefarian eventually, I can tell you about one of his... little tricks. So you don't die too quickly when the time comes."

"If he found out you'd studied under me and still got taken down that easily, he'd mock me endlessly. He'd tell Onyxia the story over and over for centuries, and I absolutely couldn't stand that."

"He can manipulate other people's spells and incantations, twist their abilities, through abnormally powerful shadow..." Sabellian said quietly. "When you face him, you'll discover that your magic might not be your ally — it could become his servant instead."

Having said that, the black dragon seemed to feel he'd already said too much and stopped.

But Jace already knew this anyway — after all, he'd run Blackwing Lair countless times in his previous life.

Nefarian could force mages to polymorph their teammates into sheep, make warlocks summon uncontrollable Infernals, turn priests' healing spells into agonizing curses. Jace was sure he could do far more than that — he used this ability to toy with his opponents.

"So, can you do it too?" Jace asked curiously.

"No." Sabellian shook his head. "Though I don't want to admit I'm inferior to Nefarian."

"Thanks for the warning. I'll be careful," Jace said. But inside, he knew the black dragon was lying.
