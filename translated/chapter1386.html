Chapter 1386: Telogrus's Mysterious Relic?

"Lord Karelz?"

Jace maintained the demonic circle, listening carefully to the voice on the other end. Karelz didn't respond as quickly as before either.

This time, the void felt different to him. The connection on the other side seemed less stable. He couldn't quite place the source of the fluctuation.

Just as he was starting to think something had gone wrong with the communication, the demon's voice came through slowly.

"Master."

"What's going on?" Jace asked.

"The phenomenon at Karazhan has reached as far as Redridge." The doomguard answered. "The Kirin Tor is investigating the cause, but you must know what happened. In Stonewatch and at the Tower of Ilgara, only you, Khadgar, and I know what happened there."

"Is everything going well?" Jace asked.

"Khadgar asked me to tell you that if you're asking about the progress of locating Telogrus, it's going very smoothly. With Aegwynn's cooperation, it's hard for things not to go smoothly." Karelz paused, then said, "But Khadgar's having some trouble with the Kirin Tor. Many mages suspect he snuck into Karazhan and tampered with the ley lines — especially those Violet Eye members."

"Have they discovered Aegwynn?" Jace asked.

"The Kirin Tor is good at many things, but discovering Aegwynn definitely isn't one of them." The demon laughed, but Jace wasn't really in the mood for Khadgar's dark humor.

"Then," Jace tried asking, "when Aegwynn left, did she mention... mention me?"

"No." The reply came quickly. The demon said, "Khadgar says she left the coordinates on a scroll and left Karazhan. She didn't leave any messages, marks, or anything. Khadgar thinks Aegwynn wanted to get as far away from Khadgar, the Kirin Tor, and the Kingdom of Stormwind as possible, then continue whatever plans she has in mind."

"Is that so?" Jace murmured.

Frankly, he'd kind of hoped Aegwynn would leave some kind of warning. Something like, "Tell that presumptuous little brat Jace Sesso that if I ever see him again, I'll make sure he dies without a burial." Or at least give him some warning to behave himself.

The fact that Aegwynn had left without saying anything actually made him more uneasy.

Sensing the lack of response on this end, the demon said, "Khadgar hopes you won't dwell too much on Aegwynn's opinion. In the history of the Order of Tirisfal, plenty of people have found traces of her, and she hasn't hunted them all down."

"Not all of them?" Jace complained. "He's really good at comforting people. But he's right — let's get back to business."

"The teleportation coordinates for Telogrus have been locked in. The rest is just minor details." The demon said. "Khadgar wants to know the current situation on the Draenor side."

"The expeditionary force is gathering near Alleria's Fortress," Jace said. "As far as I know, only the essential combat personnel remain at Honor Hold and Wildhammer Stronghold now... Hellfire Citadel and the Black Temple probably haven't noticed their movements yet — at least they haven't confirmed them. The remaining fighters can be evacuated quickly with the black dragons' help."

"As for the portal construction progress, I think it's about sixty to seventy percent done. The Murmur incident delayed things by a few days. I don't think it'll take more than two weeks to finish now. And the souls of Auchindoun have promised to help open the portal and help the expeditionary force eliminate the Shadow Council remnants still in Auchindoun. We've already got the Shadow Council under control, and with the black dragons' help, this shouldn't be difficult."

"Khadgar wants to confirm what 'got the Shadow Council under control' means," Karelz said.

"More or less in control of the situation," Jace said. "So when does the Kirin Tor plan to head for the Telogrus Rift?"

"In the next couple of days." The demon relayed slowly. "But Khadgar isn't sure about the conditions on Telogrus, so he can't confirm the exact timing for the portal construction here either. Everything depends on a detailed reconnaissance after arriving there. The environment in the Twisting Nether and the terrain of the void fragment could both affect progress."

Khadgar was right. And Telogrus wasn't completely barren. Most critically, after Magister Ubrich led his students and followers to Telogrus, they were quickly captured by a group of void creatures and nearly fully converted into void energy.

Jace couldn't remember exactly how those elves had encountered the void creatures. The Kirin Tor would likely face similar problems when they arrived in that world. But how was he supposed to plausibly explain the information from his past life to Khadgar?

Considering Khadgar had just mentioned Aegwynn, and there were probably no other Kirin Tor mages around at this moment, Jace said, "The ethereals also told me something. Tell Khadgar that these forgotten void rifts may harbor powerful shadow beings — masterless free voidwalkers, or even void lords. When they try to open a portal to the Telogrus Rift, and right up until they arrive in that world, they need to be cautious of anything suspicious."

After a moment, Karelz said, "Khadgar says the ethereal texts you transmitted from this side don't contain any such content. The ethereals specifically mentioned that according to Dar'khan's research, the Telogrus Rift is a very weak, shattered world that probably can't sustain powerful void beings drawing energy from it for long, so there's no need to worry too much about danger..."

"The ethereals mentioned this stuff? Why do they have to yap so much?"

Jace started to wonder what exactly he'd expended so much effort — and so many soul shards — to transmit over.

But if the ethereals judged that Telogrus Rift didn't have anything powerful on it, then how had the elven magisters been so easily captured?

He strained to recall his past-life memories. He hadn't played much of the void elf storyline, so his knowledge of this area was shallow.

But eventually, based on vague impressions, he remembered that those elves had apparently attracted the attention of a powerful void lord from another plane while trying to open a mysterious relic. Telogrus itself didn't have any strong enemies on it.

Yes, that seemed to be it.

But now he'd said something contradictory to what the ethereals had told him. What was he supposed to do?

He took a breath, stabilized the circle's guidance, and said in Demonic, "Sorry, I lied. The ethereals didn't tell me that."

The other end was quiet for a while. The demon asked, "Are you sure you want me to tell this human mage what you just said, Jace Sesso?"

"Go ahead." Jace continued. "Tell him I actually discovered this from Dar'khan's secret research. The reason I tried to con..."

"Wait, don't tell him why I was hiding it. Just say this — Dar'khan wanted to go to the Telogrus Rift partly to find an alternative to the Sunwell for the elven kingdom, just like his partner Marysia said. But he might also have had his own agenda — to find a very special artifact. The artifact he wrote about might be dangerous, Master Khadgar. If that thing is really on that fragment, like Dar'khan said."

"Do you want it?" Karelz asked. "Khadgar asked me to ask if you want this relic too."

"Am I really in a position to decide at this point?" Jace said. "You can let the Kirin Tor take it. You can keep it for yourself and find a safe place to study it. But whatever you do, don't try to open or use it on the Telogrus Rift. The consequences will be severe."

"Khadgar says he'll try to preserve whatever you're looking for," Karelz said.

"I..."

Jace cleared his throat awkwardly.

Khadgar had definitely interpreted his safety warning as some kind of veiled threat, so he was trying to "preserve this mysterious artifact for him" as a way to keep him placated.

Jace said quietly, "I just want to help the Daggerfang secure the Telogrus Rift, Khadgar. Nothing else interests me. Of course, if you have the ability to set aside any interesting trinkets you find there for me, I won't say no."

"Khadgar says he understands," Karelz said.

Jace let out a small sigh. "Then I'll try this circle again in two days. By then, Karelz will probably already be in the Telogrus Rift, right?"

"Yes," the demon said. "The Kirin Tor has been 'discussing' how to release me from the Tower of Ilgara recently. They're studying your binding circle with the Daggerfang."

"Stay calm, Karelz," Jace said. "Don't try to hurt any mages or Alliance members during this process, or you'll end up in the Violet Hold sooner than I will."

"A cowardly and timid warlock." Karelz snorted. "If Kil'jaeden knew the one he's been focusing on is a creature like you, he'd be disgusted."

"That would be my honor." Jace smiled, waved his hand, and burned the circle away.
