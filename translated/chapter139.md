## Chapter 139: The Escaped Orc

He'd expected to be surprised when he finally saw a real gryphon, but he hadn't expected to be struck speechless.

Because the thing was just so goddamn beautiful…

Unlike the wide-eyed out-of-town gawkers, the horses and the depot workers alike just glanced up at the gryphon as it soared into the night sky, without any surprise or extra chatter, and went right back to their business.

If they were that common, how come he'd never seen one growing up?

As a kid, he'd spotted gryphons soaring across the high sky, but that was no different from seeing a passenger jet fly overhead from the ground — strictly speaking, that didn't really count as "seeing one."

Maybe it was because of the war. The past two years, more and more gryphons had been passing through here, and Jace had spent that time in Stormwind.

The crowd of onlookers dispersed one by one, but Jace stayed rooted where he was, staring at that white speck in the dark red dusk.

"Are gryphons always this big?" Jace asked.

"The ones in the Hinterlands are smaller. This is an Alterac mountain gryphon — there aren't as many of them as the Hinterlands breed," Grede said, shaking his head in awe. "I don't see that kind often either. It really is a magnificent beast."

"When we get to the Hinterlands, you can stare your fill, Jace," Grede said. "Let's get back to the depot — I told you it was crowded. We might not get a spot." Jace nodded and followed Grede back, but his mind stayed fixed on that white monster.

At some point, a man in a heavy coat had fallen into step beside them. He wore glasses and clutched an old book to his chest. The moment he caught up he asked, "Gentlemen, have you just come off that goblin merchant ship? Any cargo you need shipped to Stratholme?"

"You talk to him," Grede elbowed Jace.

Lordaeron's depots had plenty of middlemen like this — either professional brokers or caravan hires looking for jobs. Jace had never dealt with them, but he'd known they existed back when he was in the capital.

"Not cargo. People. Will there be a carriage heading to Dalaran tomorrow morning?" he asked.

"No, I'm afraid not," the man answered. "I'm sorry, sir — all carriages heading west and north have been suspended for the past two days. The kingdom has ordered a four-day halt on freight and passenger traffic, and it won't resume until the day after tomorrow. Right now only the routes to Arathi, Stratholme, and Darrowmere are still running. You might want to consider going around that way… though after that, who knows if the policy will change again. If you're in a hurry, you could try crossing through Alterac."

"What happened?" Jace asked.

"Don't you know?" The man tucked his book under his arm, pushed his glasses up, and gave the human and the dwarf a long look. "Ah, you just came back from the south — no wonder you haven't heard. That orc chieftain's escaped, from the capital's dungeons. I don't know what Menethil was thinking. How long was that fellow locked up? A year? Probably hadn't even warmed the seat yet. Now, I'm only going by what I've heard, but the one thing I can confirm is that the military has sealed the roads for two days. The rest is up to you to figure out."

"An orc chieftain?" Jace asked. "Orgrim Doomhammer?"

"How am I supposed to remember those strange orcish names?" The middleman gave a slight bow. "Since the two of you still need to think it over, I'll go ask the others. My apologies."

With that, he hurried off to find more potential customers heading east. Jace had wanted to ask a lot more.

The orc leader who'd escaped from the capital — was it Orgrim?

If it was Orgrim Doomhammer, that meant he'd spent a full decade wandering the forests and mountains of Lordaeron, until the new warchief of the Horde came of age and finally met with him?

No wonder the Orgrim of later years had none of the fire he'd had when he led the Horde from Stormwind all the way to Quel'Thalas. Years of life as a fugitive, combined with the gradual fading of fel influence, had been more than enough to grind down his edge in a decade.

But Jace didn't quite understand this road-sealing policy. If it was because the orc warchief had escaped, why only seal the roads for four days? Did the Kingdom of Lordaeron really think they could catch him in four days?

The truth was, Lordaeron wouldn't recapture Orgrim Doomhammer in four years, let alone four days — or even fourteen years. Thinking that far ahead, Jace started to suspect there might be another reason.

The two of them sat by the roadside near the depot for a while, watching the endless stream of people passing by, feeling a little lost. At least now they knew why this town was so busy — all the trade carriages heading to Dalaran, the Kingdom of Gilneas, and the Lordaeron capital were backed up here. No wonder it was chaos!

Of course, if they wanted to reach Gilneas, they could still take a ship. Getting to the capital, though, was a different story.

Strictly speaking, it wasn't completely hopeless.

They could take a ship — Jace had looked into the route before. In theory, going from Southshore by sea to Dalaran and the capital was perfectly doable.

It would just take another month to sail around the Gilneas peninsula, follow the coast of Silverpine Forest to the Alveras River in the south of Tirisfal, and go upstream to Lordamere Port on Lake Lordamere.

The whole journey would take less than two months, and as a bonus they'd get to tour the entire coastline of the Kingdom of Gilneas.

Jace scratched his head. "What are the odds — we just happen to arrive right when they're sealing the roads?"

"The war only just ended recently, Jace. The whole continent of Lordaeron is unsettled," Grede said, pulling a biscuit from his pack and stuffing it into his mouth as he chewed. "I'm not the least bit surprised something like this happened. And this ship arrived a few days earlier than we expected, so it actually works out — we can stick to the original plan. We might as well enjoy a few days in Southshore while we're at it."

"You really think the roads are sealed because an orc escaped?" Jace asked.

"Why else?" Grede said. "We just got here. These people say what happened, that's what happened."

Jace rubbed his eyelids and let out a weary sigh.

"What if they still won't let traffic through the day after tomorrow?"

"Then we walk," Grede said. "Across Alterac Pass. I did the math — if we hike over and head down the other side to Dalaran, it's about six days. Push it a little and we can make it to that gathering just in time. After that, we head to the Lordaeron capital. They can't be sealing the Lake Lordamere ferries too — orcs aren't going to build a dock out there and start robbing people. Once you've seen your parents, we can head to the Hinterlands a few days late, no big deal."

Jace looked north. Beyond the stretches of pine forest, he could just make out the faint blue shimmer of snow-capped mountains at the edge of his vision.

"That'll have to do. Here's hoping everything goes back to normal the day after tomorrow."

Grede shot him a look. "There's nothing we can do about it right now. How about we head to the bar out back and drink? Don't let that coin from the ship go to waste."

"Fine." Jace stood up, chewing over Grede's words — something about that didn't quite sit right. "And how exactly is that not wasting money…"

"What if we spend our last copper and then the two of us get killed by orcs the day after tomorrow?" The dwarf drew a knife-hand across his own neck. "That'd be a waste."

## Flags

- **奥特兰克山谷** = Alterac Pass: glossary has 奥特兰克山脉 = Alterac Mountains, but the Chinese here says 山谷 (valley). The canonical WoW location for the road through the mountains is Alterac Pass, so I used "Alterac Pass" rather than literal "Alterac Valley" (which is the battleground name). Flagging the deliberate choice.
- **银松森林** = Silverpine Forest: not in glossary. Canonical WoW region.
- **阿里瓦斯河** = Alveras River: not in glossary (glossary has 纳菲瑞提河 = River Neltharion/Nafeltari River). Tentative; this appears to be the fic's name for the Silverpine river. TODO: verify canonical name.
- **洛丹米尔港** = Lordamere Port: not in glossary (has 洛丹米尔湖 = Lake Lordamere). Derived from the lake name.
- **达隆米尔** = Darrowmere: not in glossary. Known WoW location in northern Lordaeron.
- **暗夜精灵** = Night Elf: glossary has 卡多雷 = Kaldorei but does not list 暗夜精灵 explicitly. Used the standard WoW English term "night elf" (Kaldorei is their own name for themselves).
- **兽人头子** = orc chieftain: the middleman's vague term; Jace presses for whether it's Orgrim Doomhammer. Kept the ambiguity of the original.
- **奥格瑞姆·毁灭之锤** = Orgrim Doomhammer: in glossary.
