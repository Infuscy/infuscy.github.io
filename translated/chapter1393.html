Chapter 1393: The Shadow Council's Struggle

Most of the orcs in the Auchindoun plaza were still frozen in shock. Jace had already locked onto everyone's stunned disbelief, despair, and fear. He gathered the shadow energy in his hands.

Up above, Sabellian was unleashing a volley of Shadow Bolts from another direction. He cast without mercy — showing no sign of regret at the prospect of wiping out these shadow orcs entirely.

But neither of their magic could match the Doom Lord Car'lez's Shadow Bolt volley. The demon seemed determined to vent all the rage of his long imprisonment in just these few minutes. He chanted vicious curses at every moving thing before him. Even the void energy he released was ignited by the evil demonic power, laced with green fel fire.

Countless purple magic serpents dragged long trails across the dark, gloomy plaza, crisscrossing like a chaotic leyline network. For a moment, the screams of orcs and broken ones filled the air, rising and falling within the ruined high walls along with orcish curses shouted at irregular intervals.

It wasn't just the plaza. Beyond the high walls, enraged roars of spirits and the shouts of orcs echoed as well. Without a doubt, the spirits weren't just fueling the portal — they had also surged into the damaged passages and collapsed halls, hunting down the lackeys, laborers, and panicked soldiers who hadn't been worthy of a place in the central square.

The towering felguard that Gul'dan had left as guards in Auchindoun didn't panic at the surprise attack. They raised their massive blades and marched in orderly steps toward the portal. But what greeted them was a fierce ball of blood-red flame!

Mor'phil charged straight at one of the marching felguards from the side. The Apocalypse in her hand pierced through a felguard's heart cleanly, then a second. By the time the felguards thought to counter this frenzied female demon warrior, three of their souls had already been released...

"Destroy that portal! Destroy the portal!"

Some warlock was shouting madly from the center of the shadow magic vortex. After the initial chaos, the scattered cries of the cult fanatics gradually organized under the warlocks' commands, becoming chants of "Glory to the Legion!" and "The Legion rules!"

At the same time, the mass of orc warriors who had been prostrate at the foot of the steps hadn't even gotten to their feet before stray shadow bolts pierced their bodies, burning them into black piles of ash. But still, hundreds of orcs managed to barely organize themselves under the suppression of dark magic.

Dozens of cult executioners in soul-enchanted black armor broke through the rain of shadow bolts and, led by a felguard wielding a halberd, charged up the portal steps.

The felguard raised his halberd and charged at Car'lez. But Car'lez simply lifted his black demonic hoof and stomped straight down on his face, crushing him into a pile of fel-green fragments.

The executioners who followed weren't intimidated by the horrifying sight. They screamed Sargeras's name, climbing onto the demon lord's body one by one, grabbing at the gaps in his armor and chains, hacking and slashing as they climbed — as if that could bring down a demon lord.

But for a demon like Car'lez, this level of harassment was nothing. With one hand, he continued channeling shadow bolts, blasting every orc in sight. With the other, he casually plucked them off like rats crawling on him, killing them in his grip and tossing their bodies away.

"You smug liar! Your soul will burn in the void! Die, human!"

"Jace Sesso! Jace Sesso! Die!"

From the western side of the steps, twenty or thirty orc warriors staggered toward them. Some hadn't even picked up proper weapons — they just grabbed laborers' picks, or even chunks of rubble, and charged anyway.

Jace coldly watched these frenzied warriors raise their battle axes and swords, pouring dark energy onto them without hesitation. By now, the sustained channeling of shadow magic had formed a chaotic dark mist around him...

At his current level of magical power, even the heavily shadow-corrupted black-skinned orcs couldn't withstand his Shadow Bolt volley. After two or three hits at most, they'd be vomiting black blood and collapsing to their knees.

On the steps below the portal, some elite orc warriors in enchanted armor managed to weather the Shadow Bolt volleys — only to be precisely struck by the arcane barrage drifting out from behind the demon lord. They screamed and writhed in the explosion of arcane energy and fire.

The rumbling roar of arcane magic echoed. The Kirin Tor mages emerged one after another from the veil. Cloaked in various magical protective barriers, they stood alongside Jace, casting together to hold back the cult warriors charging the portal.

But Khadgar wasn't among them. "Where's Khadgar?" Jace shouted.

"He's channeling the Eye of Dalaran's energy with the archmages, Master Sesso!" an unfamiliar mage shouted back. "Keeping this portal open is harder than we thought. Just getting this demon lord through was already pushing the limit. If Archmage Khadgar himself or too many mages try to cross all at once, it'll cause irreversible collapse!"

So that was it. The Telogrus Rift was still too fragile to support a Legion portal of this size. And the spirits' support on the Auchindoun side, hampered by the Shadow Council orcs' resistance, was nowhere near ideal.

Up on the high terrace, Sabellian, who had been tying down dozens of orc warriors and warlocks, still hadn't reverted to his true form. Probably for the same reason.

A demon lord channeling magic near this portal was already putting immense pressure on the fragile structure. If that black dragon joined the fight at full size, the impact of landing and the sweep of his tail across the platform could trigger a catastrophic collapse at any moment.

"Kill that orc warlock — he's summoning more demons!"

A female mage pointed into the distance and shouted. But before she'd finished, a cult assassin, still wreathed in shadow mist, materialized behind her from thin air!

The orc raised his dagger and stabbed for the mage's neck. But Jace was faster — a Blood Grip tore the assassin's dagger and entire arm clean off!

The female mage spun around and blasted the assassin off the steps with an Arcane Shock. But yet another orc warrior dodged his comrade's corpse and charged at him with a raised sword!

More and more orc warriors were storming the portal steps. Even though the cult on the plaza had been caught completely off guard, with countless warriors and laborers already fallen, the Shadow Council — which had endured from the days of Gul'dan and Blackhand through decades to the present — wasn't about to let itself be butchered like panicked livestock, even if only a few hundred remained.

On the eastern side of the plaza, a group of orc warlocks had already gathered. They raised a powerful Shadow Protection barrier together and began trading spells with the mages who'd just come through the portal.

The cult guards holding the Shrieking Halls had regrouped with the felguards, forming a barrier between the portal and the orc warlocks. Under the barrage of Arcane Missiles, bitter frost cyclones, and burning fel-green flames, they held the line desperately, even watching for opportunities to launch another assault on the portal.

Wave after wave of Counterspell clashes rumbled out. The Kirin Tor mages had the upper hand thanks to the suppression of shadow bolts. But it didn't last long. From every direction came the howling of Felhounds, sending a chill down every mage's spine.
