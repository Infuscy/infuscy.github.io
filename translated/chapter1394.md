## Chapter 1394: Protect the Portal!

Through the rain veiled with dust, crazed beasts of all sizes appeared, barking and snarling. They waved their chaotic tentacles and converged relentlessly on every source of magic.

Car'lez kept channeling shadow energy with one hand. In the other, a shadow whip blazed to life. A single lash turned the closest pack of Felhounds into a spray of blood and shredded meat.

But he couldn't block all of them himself. Groups of Felhounds swarmed around from every side, lunging at the mages trying to stabilize the portal.

As the mages were forced to deal with these mana-hunters, the situation grew more chaotic. The entire portal platform began to shudder.

"Stick to Archmage Khadgar's orders! Keep the portal stable!"

An archmage shouted to his fellow Kirin Tor. But it didn't help much. Beasts from the Twisting Nether that specialized in devouring arcane energy had already reached the mages.

Their target was clear: attack the mages who had just entered through the portal, specifically to disrupt the portal's stability.

"I'll handle the Felhounds! You keep protecting the portal!" Jace shouted, still channeling Shadow Bolts with his free hand. Felhounds had exceptional shadow resistance even among demons, but they were still low-tier — they couldn't withstand Jace's shadow power.

Wave after wave of Felhounds were precisely killed by Jace's Shadow Bolts. But their numbers didn't seem to drop. More and more of these low-tier demons kept scurrying in from every direction. The count was far higher than Jace had estimated back at the Arcane Sanctum. Those orc warlocks hiding in the distance must have been summoning them continuously.

The portal creaked and groaned now and then — the Shadow Council warlocks trying to interfere with its operation using magical energy. Fortunately, the Eye of Dalaran and Auchindoun's soul energy weren't so easily shaken. But they still needed to find a way to finish off those warlocks who were fighting to the death.

Up on the southern heights, Sabellian had already burned all the orc warriors who'd tried to surround him and Needle into charred corpses. He was now channeling more powerful Shadowflame to blast the gathered warlocks who were summoning demons. But the orc warlocks fighting to the death had protection from the Broken priests.

Auchindoun's former guardians had gone fully over to the Burning Legion. Even now, with the Shadow Council on the verge of collapse, they wouldn't repent.

They mercilessly fought Ulthalesh for the souls of the fallen, all while draining the spirits of their own departed kin — all to fuel themselves and the warlocks with magic and protection, creating ethereal barriers that absorbed the shadow energy flying at them from every direction.

The entire Auchindoun plaza was filled with the eerie sound of necrotic energy and shadow magic twisting against each other, devouring each other.

"Help the mages stabilize the portal, Car'lez! Or all our efforts will be wasted!" Jace shouted.

"As you wish, Jace Sesso."

Car'lez turned and channeled shadow power into the portal. The gate's groaning immediately quieted down. But Jace felt there was something interfering with the portal beyond just the orc warlocks — some other energy, from much farther away.

Without the demon lord's combat presence, the Felhounds grew harder to deal with. Jace didn't have the energy to think about it further.

He kept hunting down the charging Felhounds while looking for an opportunity to channel a Hand of Gul'dan, bypassing the last few dozen orc warriors making their desperate stand and obliterating the warlocks directly.

Though he knew it would be nearly impossible to channel such a time-consuming, mana-intensive spell with swarms of Felhounds lurking nearby.

Besides, should he use Ulthalesh's soul power here?

He needed to save his strength for the Scepter of Sargeras... couldn't waste soul energy here.

Not only that — he had to compete with the Broken priests to claim the souls of dead orcs and demons. Ulthalesh had to keep devouring.

"Loose!"

A crisp female voice shouted from above. Arrows trailing arcane light rained down, aimed directly at the orcs trying to make a last stand behind stone blocks and collapsed walls.

Jace looked up and saw deep green figures emerging from Auchindoun's high ledges. A gryphon swooped overhead, hurling a lightning hammer at a group of orc soldiers trying to climb up, detonating into a blinding flash of electric light.

That was Alleria Windrunner. The expedition's reinforcements had arrived!

"Jace! Jace!"

Jace thought he heard Vereesa's voice, but with lightning, shadow, and fire everywhere, he couldn't tell where it was coming from.

The noise around him grew louder and more chaotic. Auchindoun's sky seemed to churn violently.

"Fel Reaver!"

A mage shouted upward. Jace looked up and saw a massive meteor burning with green fire breaking through the black clouds, falling straight toward the portal...

The orc warlocks had been fighting to the death just to buy enough time to summon a Fel Reaver meteor directly onto the portal!

Just then, the warlocks channeling the Fel Reaver meteor were pierced through the chest by a magic arrow, exploding into a spray of blood mist and collapsing into two broken corpses. Several other warlocks involved in the summoning collapsed in agony as a corner of their ritual array suddenly collapsed, magic rebounding on them.

But the summoning ritual was almost complete. The collapse of the array didn't destroy the fully formed Fel Reaver.

Mages and orcs alike looked up. The Fel Reaver's glow had already turned half of Auchindoun green.

A gryphon rider even raised his lightning hammer and charged straight at the Fel Reaver meteor. Car'lez and the mages were all still holding the portal — if they let go now, the portal would collapse on its own even without the Fel Reaver.

Sabellian looked over, but even if he reverted to his true form right now, he couldn't intercept that meteor in time.

Jace gripped Ulthalesh tightly, driving soul power at maximum speed into the shadow energy in his hands. It coalesced into a screaming Shadow Bolt that he hurled straight upward!

The Shadow Bolt collided head-on with the Fel Reaver meteor right above the portal. It sank in, tearing open a web of purple cracks, ripping the Fel Reaver apart on the spot. It exploded into burning rubble that scattered around the portal.

The orcs howled in fury. But the Farstriders had already reached the plaza through Auchindoun's gates and walls, covered by arcane arrows as they cut into the orc lines.

Mor'phil had already killed all the felguards. The cult fanatics still putting up resistance posed no threat to her at all.

She slipped past several fighting Farstriders, plunged straight into the orc lines, casually slaughtered a few warriors in her way, and reached the last remaining Shadow Council warlocks. One orc warlock raised his staff to face the sudden charge of this red devil — but before he could, half his body was carved away by the Apocalypse.

This demoness tore through the last few warlocks and Broken priests. Soon the remaining stubborn resisters were overwhelmed by destruction magic pouring in from every side.

The cult was completely routed. The survivors either fled for their lives or took the quick way out by suicide. All the warlocks were brutally executed by Mor'phil and the Farstriders.

With their masters dead, the remaining Felhounds and imps scattered...

A pale green figure emerged from among the Farstriders, sprinting toward him. "Jace!"

Vereesa threw her arms around Jace, and the two held each other tight.

Jace buried his head deep into her neck. He felt like he had almost forgotten what this warmth felt like.

"Vereesa..."

"The expedition is making a full retreat, Jace. At most three more days, and everyone will be here. Including the remaining soldiers from Honor Hold and Wildhammer Keep. Including Grede Thunderfist and Turalyon. They'll be here soon—"

Her voice caught as if something had choked it off.

Jace turned his head slightly and saw her staring at the sky, her eyes full of confusion.

It wasn't just her. Behind them, Captain Alleria and the remaining Farstriders, the dwarves who'd jumped off their gryphons, and Mor'phil standing nearby — all of them were looking up at the sky.

Jace followed their gaze. The thick clouds blackened by the Fel Reaver's fire had dissipated, but something was wrong with the sky.

Near the vortex connecting the Eye of Dalaran and the Scepter of Sargeras's power, several thinner, less distinct vortexes had appeared.
