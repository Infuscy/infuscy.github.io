Chapter 1395: Target: The Throne of Kil'jaeden

High above, the smaller vortexes pulled at the massive vortex connected to the portal. While they weren't strong enough to tear the central vortex apart immediately, they were undoubtedly affecting it.

So that was what he'd felt earlier. That power disrupting the portal's stability, coming from beyond the Shadow Council.

Jace could sense Khadgar on the other side, maintaining the Eye of Dalaran's energy, struggling to keep the vortex from being unsettled. But the ground shook more violently. Even with Car'lez and the ever-increasing number of Kirin Tor mages channeling at full power, there was no significant relief.

"Eredar magic." Car'lez gripped the shadow energy in his hands and said quietly, "The Legion has noticed our portal. They're trying to tear our channel apart with the power of their other Legion conduits, Jace Sesso. That power originates from the far east — I can feel it through the portal's struggle."

"The Black Temple." Sabellian walked closer, looking up at the sky. "Magtheridon must have ordered his Eredar advisors to manipulate the void channels. He wants to destroy our portal this way."

A Wildhammer rider spoke up. "According to the plan, Wildhammer Keep has already been abandoned. The Burning Legion's demon army may have already left Shadowmoon Valley. The doomguard legions could be flying here any minute!"

"But in these conditions, we can't keep the portal stable, let alone get the expedition soldiers through!" a mage shouted as he kept funneling magic into the portal. "We need more energy to counter the demons' disruption!"

Jace gripped his staff. The Black Prince suddenly turned his gaze toward him, his eyes full of warning.

Don't waste the soul energy you fought so hard to gather on holding the portal. The Scepter of Sargeras must be destroyed. The Scepter of Sargeras must be slain.

As Jace hesitated, a stream of arcane energy like golden sunlight — like the Sunwell itself — arced over everyone's heads and poured straight into the portal.

Everyone turned to look at the source of that golden energy. A tall draenei priest in white robes stood there, raising his purple crystal staff, channeling an overwhelming torrent of magic.

"The Prophet."

Alleria breathed.

Velen's magic illuminated the darkened Auchindoun entirely. More and more draenei spirits gathered as well, revitalized by the glow of the Prophet's magic, joining the effort to maintain the portal.

Soon even the thin strands writhing in the sky seemed to shrink significantly. They could no longer shake the Eye of Dalaran's energy. The portal's trembling subsided.

More Kirin Tor mages — and even Alliance soldiers — crossed over from the other side of the portal. Before long, the familiar figure with white hair and blue robes emerged from within.

"Jace!"

Khadgar walked down the steps and came to Jace. Jace nodded. "Archmage Khadgar."

"Finally getting to hear your voice for real. You have no idea how many times I wondered if I'd been making a fool of myself talking to a Doom Lord who was just playing along the whole time." Khadgar let out a long breath, then looked at Velen. "Thank you for your help, Prophet. The Burning Legion's ability to manipulate magic on Draenor far exceeds our estimates. Without your aid, the consequences could have been incalculable."

"Jace Sesso saved this sanctuary." The Prophet Velen's eyes swept across Auchindoun's broken walls. "Without him, every wanderer including myself would have suffocated to death in this cage. This is not so much our help — we are all saving ourselves with all our might."

Khadgar nodded and turned to Jace, lowering his voice. "No time to waste, Jace. We'll hold the portal from here with everything we've got. It looks harder than we expected, and we'll have to hold off the demon army that could arrive at any moment. Hard to say how long Auchindoun's walls will stop them. As for you... I think you know. Kil'jaeden should give you a chance to see that thing."

"I know," Jace said.

"I'm ready to leave whenever," Sabellian said impatiently.

"I'm glad to see you so eager to help the Alliance, Baron Sablemane," Khadgar said.

"This is no time for jokes, mage," Sabellian said coldly.

"Indeed," Jace said. "Then I'll set off immediately."

"Let me come with you, Jace." Vereesa slung her bow over her shoulder. "Let me go to the Throne of Kil'jaeden with you."

Khadgar, Sabellian, and Alleria all stepped aside, giving the two of them some space.

"Help here, Vereesa." Jace glanced at them, then said quietly. "Mor'phil is coming with me. That means the demon lords attacking Auchindoun just lost their biggest threat. You, Grede, and Sora need to stay here and help the expedition fight them. Don't forget — the mages here, including Archmage Khadgar, need to keep the portal stable. Thousands of people will be crossing..."

"But we just found each other again." Vereesa lowered her head, unable to hold back her emotion any longer. "You're right. You're right, and I can't argue with you."

Her voice was almost choked with tears. "But I know that look, Jace Sesso. You've already prepared yourself to lose everything. You fool those orcs. You fool the Shadow Council's warlocks. But you don't fool me."

"You think I'm planning to go kill myself?" Jace smiled slightly. "Look at Prince Sabellian. Does he look like someone who'd go die with me, Vereesa? You know what kind of creature he is. Stay with the Farstriders, Ranger Lord. I'll be back before the portal closes. Maybe I'll even be back before Grede."

"After all, he's only riding a gryphon. Look at my ride." He added quietly.

Vereesa rubbed her eyes. She was silent for a long time, then looked at Mor'phil, who was standing with her back to them not far away.

"Protect him, demon."

"You've said that more than once, dear," Mor'phil said with a smile.

Jace stuffed the demon-leather pouch containing Gul'dan's manuscripts into the ranger's hands and said quietly, "Keep this safe, Vereesa. Don't give it to any outsider. I mean — besides Grede, Sora, and the little black dragon, don't tell anyone. Not even your sister!"

Vereesa clutched the pouch and nodded. "I've got it." That seemed to put her a bit more at ease.

"Protect the Prince for me, Jace Sesso," Needle said quietly beside him.

Jace didn't have time to respond before Sabellian, not far away, fixed the brat with a glare. "Don't waste time with this nonsense. Do I look like I need his protection?"

Needle flinched and nodded, stepping back. Sora sighed and wrapped her arms around the half-grown girl, murmuring a few words of comfort — almost like she'd become a real wise big sister.

"I'm going now, Sora." Jace looked at the green dragon. "Take good care of everyone here. Their lives depend on you."

"I wouldn't go that far," Sora said. "Safe journey, Jace."

"Come back alive, Jace." Khadgar said quietly. "If something happens to you, I'd have a lot of trouble explaining things to the Kirin Tor. You know what I mean?"

"Need a scapegoat," Jace said.

"That's not quite it." Khadgar sighed.

Alleria held Vereesa and nodded at Jace. Velen watched them, his expression serious but silent.

Jace nodded to each of them in turn, lifted his staff, tightened his potion pouch one last time, and gestured for Mor'phil and the imp on her shoulder to follow.

Sabellian pointed outside. "Let's depart from out there. I'd rather not shatter this flimsy little door with my power."
