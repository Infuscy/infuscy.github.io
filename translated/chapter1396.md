## Chapter 1396: The Summit of Hellfire Peninsula

The black dragon plunged into the fel-and-shadow-choked black clouds like a ship crashing through dark waves. Sabellian had unleashed all his power, charging northeast. Jace and Mor'phil beside him were held fast by the flaming chains the dragon had summoned, bound to his neck. Even so, Jace felt like he could be torn apart by the violent, evil winds at any moment.

Jace finally understood why the black dragon had called their last trip to Bladespire a "sightseeing tour." This flight felt like he wouldn't even make it to the Throne of Kil'jaeden — he'd disintegrate mid-journey.

And it wasn't just him. Even Mor'phil beside him was struggling, and she was a demon who'd spent who knows how many millennia drifting through the savage, brutal Twisted Nether — with the dragon's massive horns splitting the airflow ahead of them!

Of course, by now Hellfire Peninsula wasn't much different from those Legion worlds whose environments were downright terrifying in the Twisted Nether.

The piercing screech of the air swallowed everything. All Jace could see were the shadow vortexes kicked up by the black dragon's massive wings. He had no way to observe what was below, no way to tell where the dragon had flown.

But he had a general idea. Sabellian had chosen to head north into the Zangar Sea, then follow the coastal mountains along the edge of Bladespire to reach Hellfire Peninsula. A roundabout route that would dodge the Legion forces hunting the Alliance army, hugging the void cliffs in the most stealthy way possible all the way to the northernmost mountain peak — the Throne of Kil'jaeden.

He looked back toward Auchindoun. Even though Sabellian had been flying for hours now, and despite the swirling black clouds blocking the view, he could still faintly make out several void vortexes writhing in the dark sky. So unnatural. So supernatural. Like nightmares lingering as waking visions.

The Eredar hadn't stopped interfering with the Eye of Dalaran's power. They were dead set on twisting the power supporting that portal — maybe even trying to replace the channel the Eye had opened, striking directly at the portal itself, and having the Legions from Karabor and the Throne of Kil'jaeden manifest in the heart of Auchindoun...

Could Khadgar and the Kirin Tor mages, alongside Prophet Velen, stand against the Eredar warlocks of Karabor?

Jace wouldn't question Khadgar and Velen's power. But this was still Burning Legion territory, a black sky held up by the Scepter of Sargeras. And the Legion's footsteps were drawing closer — in Shadowmoon Valley, in northern Terokkar Forest...

He didn't want to imagine it. Days from now, when demons charged at Auchindoun's crumbling walls from the sky and the wilds... the moment when Velen, Khadgar, and the other mages would have to choose between holding the portal and holding off the demons.

That was why he had to be fast. Not waste a single second.

If it weren't for the need to pull off a surprise attack, Jace would have had Sabellian fly directly over the demons' heads to shave off another two or three hours. But that might have led to unexpected complications.

Jace didn't want to waste Ulthalesh's soul power dealing with a sky full of doomguards. Everything had to be saved for the end — the critical moment. Sabellian felt the same. So did Mor'phil.

"Too late to change my mind, master?!"

The imp clung to Jace's belt, screaming hysterically. The flames on its head had been stripped clean by the gale, leaving it looking like a bald rat. Jace shoved it back into his pack and cinched the pouch tight, but even that simple motion nearly sent him flying. The flaming chains wrapped around his chest and shoulders were the only thing keeping him from being torn off completely.

"I'm entering the void soon. Get ready."

Sabellian's voice boomed like thunder. Before Jace could think about what that meant, the darkness around him swallowed everything. Sounds grew muffled. His skin prickled and ached like it was under a constant Curse of Agony.

Jace fumbled out a Draenic Shadow Protection Potion from his pouch and drank it, then eyed the Destruction Potion Needle had brought back — a single bottle, painstakingly brewed from herbs the Kirin'Var Village mages had recovered.

The Black Prince had entered the void. The surrounding air and black clouds were no longer as violent. Looking southeast, Jace could even make out the fractured remains of the continent like dark silhouettes — cliffs and floating islands tinged with purple-red.

After what felt like an eternity, the black dragon stopped beating his wings. He drove forward purely with his powerful shadow energy, like a torpedo underwater. And inside this churning vortex of thick magic, Jace couldn't tell what speed Sabellian was actually making.

All he knew was that the cliffs were getting taller, steeper. Sometimes he could even see green fel fire streaking across the chaotic sky, crashing into them. Dark, spike-like shapes appeared on the blood-red rock...

Above the mountains, a purple vortex covering almost the entire sky rotated slowly, glittering with dark green magic. Just looking at the center of that vortex made his mind churn with anxiety and restlessness — a nameless rage burning in his throat.

This was fel energy. The Throne of Kil'jaeden was near.

The highest point of the entire Hellfire Peninsula. Maybe the highest point in all of Draenor.

In the game, this was where Doom Lord Kazzak resided — the Burning Legion's headquarters in Outland. An impossibly massive altar and countless Legion teleportation devices sat at the mountain's peak, within the caldera carved out by fel fire.

That altar, the key point guarded by countless Legion elites, was also an important entry point for the Legion to send soldiers to Draenor. It was probably where the Scepter of Sargeras was placed.

At the edge of his vision, the void-facing cliffs grew higher. Jace was plotting how to strike the altar while the Legion was busy descending the mountain to attack Auchindoun, when a massive black figure appeared without warning...

It was like a dark cathedral. Sinister, spike-like walls seemed to merge with the blood-red mountain body that had been corroded by shadow and fel, yet there were still vague boundaries.

It was indeed a giant tower. It reminded Jace of Blackrock Spire on Blackrock Mountain, but the architectural style wasn't as sturdy and thick as the Dark Iron's work. It looked more like a cluster of broadsword blades jammed into the mountainside.

What the hell was that? Since when did the Throne of Kil'jaeden have a demon fortress like this?

But as the black dragon flew closer, Jace realized it wasn't really a fortress.

It was a temple. Or a demonic shrine. Like a withered claw clutching the entire mountain peak. The enormous, faintly flickering vortex in the sky was directly above this demonic temple. Fel Reaver meteors kept falling from the sky around it.

Dim fel fire blazed from the temple's numerous massive balconies. The profound power within it made Jace's heart grow more chaotic, more taut.

If Jace had still harbored any doubt — that maybe the Scepter of Sargeras had been taken elsewhere by other demons, or had never been here at all, that Kargath Bladefist had given him and Sabellian false hints...

Then now, as he stared at the fel fire spewing forth, as he gazed up at the dark vortex that had completely twisted the sky — all doubts dissolved.
