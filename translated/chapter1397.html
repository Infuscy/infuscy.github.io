Chapter 1397: The Guardian of the Black Temple

The black dragon flew toward the demonic temple, riding the void's shadow power like a bank of shadowy clouds rolling in. In the distant blood-red mountain passes, legions of felguards were assembling. The specks of fel light from their demon eyes were already visible here.

The doomguards watching over these felguards were numerous, scattered across the ridges and between the dark spires — but still far too few for a Legion headquarters of this size.

"The doomguards have already deployed," Sabellian said quietly. "Now's our chance."

Jace tried sweeping the entire black temple with demonic detection. The interior was pitch black, but at the very top, a blinding fel fire like an evil star flickered madly. The vortexes in the sky were all being pulled by that flickering thing.

When he looked at it, every other demon — large or small, near or far — seemed to dim into insignificance...

Jace had only glanced at it for a moment when he felt the chaotic demon-star's evil power drilling into his brain through his gaze, stabbing sharply. He was forced to drop his demonic detection and look down.

Was that the Scepter of Sargeras?

"I can't see what's inside," Jace said.

"No choice, Jace Sesso. Whatever's in there, we have to go in and see," Sabellian said quietly.

Jace nodded. "Let's go."

The black dragon beat his wings twice, diving from the void toward the temple's peak.

When he emerged from the void's embrace, the dark power around his body was stripped away by streams of foul-smelling fel wind. As his shadow fell over the ridge, several floating chaotic demon eyes on the Throne of Kil'jaeden suddenly turned toward them. Countless demons shrieked with violent frenzy.

The Eredar standing atop the towers, channeling the arrival of Fel Reavers, roared vile curses in Demonic. The felguards marching down the mountain all turned their heads at once toward this direction — like a vast field of eerie flames lighting up simultaneously.

Massive flocks of doomguards took flight like startled blood bats beneath the dark canopy, chasing after them. Wild laughter echoed from within the temple.

Mor'phil suddenly turned her head toward the temple and couldn't help frowning.

"I assume you were mentally prepared. Everyone knows what you're getting into when you plunge straight into the Burning Legion's lair." Sabellian's massive fangs ground together angrily. He rotated his enormous tawny-black head, sizing up the doomguards closing in from several directions, then said quietly, "I hope the fear and panic I'm sensing aren't coming from you two."

"Might be the imp's," Jace shouted. "Drop us off, Sabellian!"

The Black Prince laughed coldly. He extended a dragon claw capable of crushing an entire Mag'har hut and slammed it into the black temple's wall. A doomguard wielding a black spear flew at them with a hoarse screech — only to be shattered into a heap of burning fel-reeking pulp by Sabellian's tail.

Jace, Sazeno, and Mor'phil landed on the outer wall. As they grabbed the exposed spikes and chains to steady themselves, more and more doomguards were landing around Sabellian.

They spat fel fire. Cast Cripple. But against a black dragon prince of this terrifying size, it barely did anything. He spread his wings, opened his massive jaws in fury, and swept Shadowflame across the temple's spike-covered outer wall. The vanguard of flying demons was all burned and sent tumbling down into the blood-red valley.

A Fel Reaver meteor crashed straight into the black-metallic outer wall. Amid the exploding fel fire, it assembled into a Fel Reaver climbing desperately upward. But before it could climb more than a few steps, Sabellian caught it in one claw and blasted dragonfire into its burning stone head. The fierce green fel flames engulfing its body instantly turned purple, and the entire thing shattered, sliding down the wall.

Mor'phil had already killed a Dreadlord spraying yellow fel fire from its gut and a doomguard wielding a fel-iron battle axe. But the bat-like doomguard swarms were growing thicker. Fel Reaver meteors fell one after another. The sky was full of Ered'ruin shrieks.

And below, the felguards were roaring in unison, charging in groups toward the bottom entrance of this corrupted structure.

Jace watched the demons pouring into the castle and gripped his staff. "Kil'jaeden will give me a chance to see the Scepter of Sargeras, right?"

A fel fire meteor streaked across the distance, briefly illuminating the dark hall within. Nothing seemed to be moving in there. Sabellian's yellow reptilian eyes darted, then he curled his lips. "Go inside and do what you need to do. I'll hold off the trouble out here. Once I've dealt with it, I'll catch up."

"And what counts as 'dealt with'?!"

Jace couldn't help asking. But before he'd finished, the Black Prince had already raised his great wings and flown to the heights, dousing a cluster of felguards below with a torrent of dark Shadowflame. Many demons were drawn away by the massive dragon, flying up into the air after him.

"We shouldn't linger here fighting these demons, master. Let them chase the black dragon," Mor'phil said.

"Let's go," Jace said.

The succubus leaped first through the crack-like "window," and Jace jumped down after her.

Taking advantage of the moment's quiet, he smoothly cast an anti-evil version of Mage Armor, then added Arcane Intellect, preparing for any powerful enemy that might appear.

By the dim, slightly muted glow of the freshly cast Mage Armor, he noticed the hall before him wasn't as wide as he'd imagined.

Circular steps. Black metal pillars studded with spikes everywhere. The gloomy ceiling occasionally shook with the impact of Fel Reavers. Everywhere, the roar of demons. The bellow of the black dragon.

Below, the heavy footsteps of felguards echoed. Mor'phil and Jace, one ahead of the other, sprinted up the wide staircase.

The long black staircase seemed to stretch on forever. Jace couldn't help glancing back. He had no idea how high they'd climbed. But that thing — the thing that seemed to command all of Draenor — felt like it was right above their heads, close enough to touch.

When he turned back, Mor'phil had stopped.

She was staring at the top of the stairs. At the edge, lit intermittently by flashes of the black dragon's flame outside, Eredar runes covered the walls. It made this place feel as dark and dreadful as some ancient ruin buried for tens of thousands of years.

"What's wrong?" Jace asked.

Mor'phil shook her head and continued forward.

The succubus had both Apocalypse blades in her hands now, as if she already knew what was waiting for them ahead.

Jace followed behind her. He tried peering forward with demonic detection, but was immediately forced to shut his eyes again by the raging fel fire blazing like a star. He took a deep breath.

They reached the top of the stairs. A wide, dark platform stretched before them. Behind it, a spiral staircase wound up around a tower, leading to heights too dark to see.

And in the center of the platform, a massive figure lay crouched. Only when the flames outside illuminated it again did Jace get a clearer look at its bloated silhouette.

A Pit Lord.

Mor'phil's sudden two-note laugh shattered the surface silence of this circular demonic hall.

Her laughter grew into hysterical cackling. Jace looked at her. The demon who'd been at his side for years suddenly seemed completely alien.

In the midst of this laughter, the massive dark figure lifted its head. Flames ignited atop its head and spread down its spine, illuminating its entire bloated, stegosaur-like blue-purple body.

This was an impossibly ancient Pit Lord. Covered in scars, wrinkles densely packed across its form. On either side of its massive head sat two heavy, spiked, dark-green pauldrons like thorned thick walls. On its chest hung a fel-fire chestplate pendant shaped like a demon's heart — soul-light flickering powerfully from its crevices.

The dark light swayed as it slowly rose, illuminating the weapons in its hands: a vicious dagger and a spiked curved blade. They reminded Jace of Kargath Bladefist's appearance in the Shattered Halls arena.

No — it wasn't just the shape of the blades. Even the way they were mounted was the same. This Pit Lord's hands...

When the flames all over its body burst to life, Jace realized why he hadn't recognized this thing as a Pit Lord at first.

It had no wings.

"I know why Lord Kil'jaeden had me wait here, Reaper." The Pit Lord snorted with laughter. "He knew I've been waiting for this day."

"Then I should thank your Lord Kil'jaeden for giving me this precious opportunity." Mor'phil's voice rose as she spoke, then dropped to a venomous growl: "For letting me kill you with my own hands, Brutallus."

Yes. This was Brutallus. There might be many Pit Lords, but one without both wings and hands — even in the entire Burning Legion — there was probably only this one.
