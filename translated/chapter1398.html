Chapter 1398: Embracing Fate

"Kill you with my own hands?" Brutallus laughed twice. "You found Ulthalesh's wielder to be your new master. You're still the same coward you always were, Lady Molfaredes. I doubt this time will turn out much different. You still don't understand what you really lost in that fight back then." "You're wrong," Mor'phil said. "This time I'll face you alone." "No way," Jace said quietly. "You think I've lost my mind?" Mor'phil kept her voice low. "You think I've thrown all caution to the wind? Don't waste your power, Jace Sesso." She was right. Brutallus was one of the Burning Legion's most crazed fighters. If he and Mor'phil tried to take him down quickly, there was no way they could do it without tapping Ulthalesh's soul power — and a lot of it at that. Then what would be left for the Scepter of Sargeras? Maybe they could still destroy it. Maybe. But what if it failed? What if those souls were exactly what was missing? Demons were already entering the main hall. It wouldn't be long before they climbed this high. If Brutallus pinned them here, Sabellian's efforts to buy them time outside would be wasted too. But the Tome of Dark Names didn't record Brutallus's name. Which meant he was probably the new master summoned here by the Burning Legion. "Don't give me orders, my master." The succubus said quietly. "Go. Let me finish what I have to do. Go finish yours." "It's not that hard a choice, human." Brutallus tilted his enormous mouth full of fangs, corrosive drool dripping as he rumbled, "Today, I'll sever the lives and souls of all of you... including Ulthalesh. Then I'll put them in my soulstone." He snorted, patting the enormous armor hanging on his chest. "Come on... come on." "Your soulstone?" Mor'phil froze, staring at the softly glowing piece of armor. "Inside that?" But in that moment of hesitation, Brutallus's muscles suddenly tensed. The bloated, fat monster charged with a speed wildly incongruous with his size. His thick, sauropod-like demonic claws pounded the floor, making the entire black temple shudder. And a second later, the curved blade mounted on his arm was already swinging at her face! Jace Shadow Stepped out of the way of the swing. On the other side, shadow and life-fire collided head-on with the fel fire wrapped around the blade. Mor'phil roared in fury, but Brutallus's bellow overwhelmed her completely. "Go, Jace Sesso! Go! Go!" The succubus screamed. Jace had no choice but to summon another Shadow Step and dash toward the dark staircase behind him. But he could feel the scorching fel flames growing hotter at his back. He glanced back — Brutallus was spewing a massive wave of fel fire, igniting half the hall. Mor'phil leaped up and grabbed his fat head, wrenching it aside just in time to stop the fel fire from swallowing Jace whole. "Go! Stop holding me back! Let me kill him!" The demoness's shriek was soon muffled by the Pit Lord's massive bulk. Blade clashed against blade. Flame roared against flame. Jace gritted his teeth and sprinted up the stairs. As he climbed, the soul link kept transmitting blows that nearly shattered his consciousness. He couldn't imagine what kind of power Mor'phil was facing. But he had come here to destroy the Scepter of Sargeras. Him, Mor'phil, Sabellian — all of them were here for this. He couldn't hesitate any longer. Jace hurried up the spiral staircase. The higher he climbed, the darker it grew. And the indescribable thing above his head grew hotter and hotter. Gradually, he realized he was enveloped in total shadow. He couldn't see anything around him — only the stairs leading up beneath his feet. Below the stairs stretched endless, eternal darkness. Mor'phil's shouts, Brutallus's roars, the dragon's breath, the battle cries of countless demons, the crashing of Fel Reaver meteors... all grew indistinct. The thousands of draenei head carvings on the black temple's walls all seemed to have their eyes open, staring at him, watching his every move. Sazeno cowered in the demon pack, trembling, silent. As if it already knew what was waiting for it and its master. Jace stopped running. He slowed down. Otherwise, he felt like he might accidentally plunge into this bottomless abyss. He lifted his staff and climbed step by step, steadily. At the same time, he tried to shut out everything that could affect his emotions and awareness, forcing himself to calm down. Almost there. It's right there. Do you see it, Jace Sesso? You're getting closer. You know it... Jace's lips moved. He closed his eyes. He could no longer tell if it was him speaking, or Ulthalesh whispering to him, or something else entirely. You've come this far after all. So accept your fate. Accept... Jace opened his eyes. A twisted, dark-red scepter lay on the high platform before him. The highest point of the black temple was like a demon's throne. The scepter stood there, surrounded by the powerful void energy emanating from the purple-red orb at its tip, floating unnaturally in midair. That orb was so deep. With just one glance, Jace could feel the resentment, pain, and fury of countless ancient demons... Compared to the orb's power, the scepter's corruption-veined shaft seemed so ordinary. Even fragile. The Scepter of Sargeras. Like you could just reach out and take it. Reach out, and I could possess the entire Twisted Nether. Tear worlds apart. Pierce through planes. Demon or mortal — before it, they would all be as powerless as dust... The entire Draenor. Only you can wield it. Because you are the chosen one. Crossing infinite time and space to reach this place. Accept your fate. Accept my gift. Accept my path. Accept... to rewrite... Power. Endless, unto eternity. "Let me just drink this first. One sec." Jace muttered, grabbing the Destruction Potion. He took a breath and downed it in one gulp, tossing the empty bottle into the darkness. Surging magic suddenly erupted from his chest, instantly scattering all the confusion and hesitation. Jace's trembling lips finally spat out just a few words. Be right there to kill you. With that, the warlock raised Ulthalesh. The scythe blade spun from the demon's jaws with his roar, flying out! The blood blade carved a trail of purple-red demon light, slicing down from above straight into that eye-like purple gem. A piercing shriek tore through his consciousness. A violent shadow impact exploded from the center of the universe! Jace felt part of himself blasted into darkness. Another part seemed to stay where it was. Yet another flew even faster. Everything before him was swallowed by a shadow vortex. And only one question remained in his mind... Did it break? Did it break? He forced his consciousness and flesh back together, barely managing to get up. Ulthalesh lay not far away. When he stumbled over and picked it up, he noticed the staff had become utterly hollow — like a dried, brittle bone, covered in cracks, as if it would shatter at the slightest movement. A fierce hunger immediately consumed Jace's awareness. As if the most important thing now wasn't confirming whether the Scepter of Sargeras had been destroyed, but to devour a demon right away. Then another demon. Then another. Then another— He found his reason again through a scream he couldn't even hear himself. When he opened his eyes, he saw the black temple's dome collapsing. The screech of twisting metal filled his ears. Hellfire Peninsula's terrible dark sky was right above. And that massive void vortex had become a chaotic, illegible mess... Not only that. Looking southwest, even the magical vortex above Auchindoun was distorting, as if it might collapse into chaos at any moment. The Scepter of Sargeras? Jace braced himself on the ground and climbed back up to look at where the scepter was. The orb had dimmed. Some ornaments on the shaft had shattered. But the whole scepter still floated there unnaturally, still straining to pull at the entire world. He could feel it. How was that possible... "You failed." At some point, another figure had appeared beside the scepter. Jace had put all his attention on observing the Scepter of Sargeras's condition. He'd completely overlooked this rather tall, very familiar figure. The man turned around. His eyes glowed with a dim yellow reptilian light. His tattered robes had lost their blazing splendor, stained with the filthy blood of demons. "Sabellian?" Jace coughed. "You failed, Jace Sesso." The black dragon stared at him. "You couldn't destroy the Scepter of Sargeras. So now I have to help you." "What are you going to do?" Jace asked warily. "I'm going to take it." Sabellian's voice was flat. "You shouldn't have forgotten. That was our original plan from the start." Jace forced down the pus-blood in his throat. "Don't do this." "I gave you your chance." The Black Prince raised his hand toward the scepter. "I did everything I could to help you. From beginning to end, I believed you had the power to destroy this scepter. That was the foundation of our cooperation. But that foundation is gone. I don't blame you. But that's how it is." "Try again," Jace said. "It's already weakened, Sabellian. We can destroy it. You know that!" "Enough." Sabellian's eyes trembled. "You sacrificed every soul and every bit of magic you brought. What's next? What do you have left? I don't see any point in struggling anymore, Jace Sesso..." "You know what happens if you take it!" Jace shouted. "You know. You've just been running from it the whole time!" Sabellian laughed coldly. "I held it once before. Now it's right before me. And in the future, it will still be mine." "Try again." Jace raised one finger, biting off every syllable. "Let's try again. Just once." Sabellian's finger, about to touch the Scepter of Sargeras, trembled. He didn't actually make contact. He closed his eyes. An unconcealed fury welled up in his chest. "What makes you think you can definitely destroy the Dark Titan's treasure? Ulthalesh failed. It lost its power. Compared to it, what are you? What am I? Taking this thing will solve everything. It's that simple. We're already here. Haven't you noticed? It's been waiting for us. All we have to do is reach out." He looked at the scepter, hesitated for a moment, then lowered his head and asked through gritted teeth, "Have you ever considered, Jace Sesso, that you're just a supporting character, and I'm the real protagonist of this story?" "What makes you think you have to be the one to do all this? Don't forget — I am the eldest son of Neltharion the Earth-Warder. After that old bastard got himself killed, I should have been the one to become the mightiest of all black wings! And after enduring so much humiliation, torment, and pain to reach this point, to come to this place... is it possible that I'm the one destined to save everything? Is it possible that the step I need to take is right before me?" "You think the Scepter of Sargeras will twist me, make me a slave of the Burning Legion? Don't forget, little thing — I already overcame the enslavement of C'Thun and N'Zoth! I regained my freedom! What in this world could still twist my will? I don't even need that ridiculous chain to help me. And you — the one whom Alexstrasza the Dragonqueen trusts most as the successor of the 'Chain of Will' — you're so cowardly you can't even speak the names of those dark aberrations! Do you think I don't recognize it, huh? You think I don't recognize the Dragon Soul's chain, the treasure my father once treasured most? You naive fool!" "Listen well." His voice was vicious. "It was always supposed to be me who saved you insignificant mortals. Me — the future King of Black Wings, master of the Obsidian Sanctum and the Wyrmrest Temple — to redeem your failed fate." With that, Sabellian reached for the scepter. But a black shadow chain clamped tightly around his hand, preventing him from opening it, let alone grasping anything. "You still have mana," he muttered. "Was saving it to take another swing at it," Jace said, lowering his hand. "Guess I'll have to use it on you now, huh?" Sabellian laughed. He flexed his hand and snapped the chain. A burning black staff extended from between his fingers, illuminating this claw-like platform. At the foot of the steps, Jace drew the Quel'Serrar. The crystal-blue magic light pushed back slightly against the dark flames.
