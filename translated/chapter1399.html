Chapter 1399: Blackwing

Beneath the collapsing, shattering temple dome, Sabellian suddenly erupted in twin bursts of flame that coalesced into burning black dragon wings in midair. Spreading wide, he leaped higher like a demon, then lunged straight at Jace, wreathed in trembling fire!

The black dragon's pressure was like a falling mountain. Jace gritted his teeth and triggered Shadow Step to avoid a direct hit — but the explosion kicked up by the dragon's landing blasted him right out of the shadows.

The shockwave from Sabellian's impact spread outward in rings, igniting the entire platform. Flames surrounded the two of them. The twisted Eredar faces in the hall were illuminated by the flickering fire, looking maniacal and grotesque.

"Trying to run? Too late!"

Sabellian pointed without turning. Jace hadn't even regained his balance when his legs locked up. Agony shot from his feet through his legs to his waist, and soon his arms grew sluggish too.

He looked down and saw his lower body rapidly covered in black stone. Unable to break free. Petrification?!

"Fire, free me from the earth's grip!"

He prayed to the flame. Fire rapidly spread over his body, halting the petrification. But before it could be dispelled, a screaming Shadow Bolt was already flying at his face!

Jace had no way to dodge. He took the hit head-on. The Shadow Bolt slammed viciously into the Blood Gem hanging on his chest. Intense pain and corrosion spread instantly — the torment nearly made him want to end it all right there. His robe and Mage Armor barely reduced it.

The fire elemental didn't weaken just because of Jace's pain and weakness. It actively swirled around him, wrestling with the spreading petrification, and soon helped dissolve most of the stone encasing him.

Fueled by the unbearable pain, Jace savagely broke free of the most solid stone around his legs. But Sabellian had already spread his dragon wings again, descending like a black angel falling from heaven!

"Jace... Sesso!"

The black dragon shrieked. The flames on his raised staff blazed like an infernal's fire whip, lashing through the air with a crackling roar as he swooped down. Jace couldn't dodge. He swung the Quel'Serrar to meet it head-on.

The whip struck the azure dragon-scale blade with an explosive shriek. Arcane light and bursting fire snarled together for a moment, then the fire scattered into black smoke. But Sabellian, riding his wings, had already flown to the other side of the chamber, looped around in less time than a short incantation, and swooped again — another fire whip already blazing in his hand.

At the same time, he raised his other hand. He was going to petrify again!

"Flame Shield!"

Jace bellowed the elemental command. The fire that had been burning across the entire demon throne rose up around him like a cyclone, eagerly wrestling with the rapidly clumping, scalding black stone. But Jace could still feel his steps growing heavy, slowed. It didn't stop him from raising the Quel'Serrar again!

The fire whip and the crystal-blue sword clashed violently once more beneath the witness of the Scepter of Sargeras. The scorching shockwave blasted away a cloud of dust and snapped a demon-fang pillar in two. Sabellian flapped his wings and flew to another direction, ready to repeat the trick — whipping away, never giving this mortal any chance to counter or even breathe, draining his mana one lash at a time.

But this time, Jace didn't even try to channel any spell. He just turned and hurled the Quel'Serrar with all his might straight at Sabellian!

He wasn't Grede. He wasn't some famous Amani champion axe-thrower. But with the hunting experience his father had taught him in the forests, the brute strength blessed by the Red Dragonqueen, and most importantly — the Quel'Serrar's sharpness — it was enough to land a solid hit on Sabellian!

Sabellian clearly hadn't expected this move. His yellow eyes narrowed with sudden tension as he turned. But he still easily moved his wings in the air, tilting his head to dodge the blow.

Then he laughed loudly, raised his staff, and conjured another whip of fire. Jace instantly stretched out his right hand and summoned Blood Grip. The black dragon, anticipating this, casually shifted his body to dodge the warlock's grasp as he continued his dive. But when he realized Blood Grip's target wasn't him at all — it was too late.

The Quel'Serrar, caught in midair by Blood Grip, spun back. Sabellian heard the screech of air being cut. He only had time to tilt his head before the sword carved a gash across his chest. He cried out in pain and dropped a considerable distance, nearly falling off the demon throne.

Jace had no intention of letting him go. He kept a death grip on the Quel'Serrar with Blood Grip and swung it again, hurling it like a shadow-powered meteor hammer straight at the black dragon barely steadying himself!

He had never wielded this kind of bizarre, improvised weapon before. But he didn't need any technique. Didn't need any practice.

Because Blood Grip's crimson connection was born from his very soul. Shadow power had almost become one with him!

A flash of bright blue magic, dragged by a red thread, stabbed straight at the black dragon. Sabellian cried out and raised his wings to barely dodge. Before he could catch his breath, Jace triggered Blood Grip again, precisely catching the spinning Quel'Serrar in midair and stabbing from a new angle. The black dragon was forced to raise his burning staff to block.

A sharp CLANG. The Quel'Serrar sheared clean through the staff. But the weapon wasn't a solid object. The black dragon summoned a second staff from the burning pillar of fire and used its tip to flick out another whip toward the Quel'Serrar. At the same time, Jace triggered Blood Grip a third time, grabbing his sword before the whip could reach it, then brought it smashing down from above against the incoming lash!

"This isn't your power, Jace Sesso! Does Malygos know what a depraved, arrogant demon-worshipper you've become? Does he?!"

"At least he knows I ended your father's madness, Sabellian."

"You don't deserve that! You don't deserve to be in the same sentence! Your name, your pronoun, your existence — none of it deserves that! So I'm going to kill you! I'm going to kill you! I'm going to erase you completely, Jace Sesso! Today!"

The fire whip's glow carved across the crack-veined temple walls again and again, clashing with the arcane-glowing blue dragon blade. Jace's attacks grew more ferocious. The black dragon's flames grew more vicious. Both had murder in their eyes, sworn to crush the other's will to fight completely!

With one hand, Jace kept pulling the Quel'Serrar with Blood Grip, constantly attacking the black dragon. With the other hand, he raised a vicious shadow vortex.

The powerful mana-wind from the vortex made the flames around the demon throne flicker violently.

Sabellian had just deflected a sword slash when he noticed the magical disturbance and looked toward Jace — just in time to see a Shadow Bolt flying at his face!

"Arrogant little wretch! You dare attack a Blackwing Prince with Shadow Bolt?!"

The black dragon casually crushed the Shadow Bolt in his grip. But he didn't expect the buzzing of shadow approaching from above, from both sides, from behind.

Over a dozen Shadow Bolts were closing in from every direction. His face went ashen. He had no choice but to cover himself with his dragon wings and take every Shadow Bolt head-on...

His fiery wings erupted in bursts of dark impact, revealing purple-black veins. At the same time, the Quel'Serrar, controlled by Blood Grip, slashed horizontally. The black dragon desperately swung his fire whip to knock the sword away, while raising his other hand at Jace again — Petrification!

While the flames around Jace fought off the siege of earth elementals once more, Sabellian raised his right hand and bellowed something in ancient draconic.

Under his call, all the flames on the throne sank noticeably. Even the fire elementals protecting Jace were drained away. The fire's heat was devoured and wrapped around Sabellian, and the dark corrosion that had been eating at him rapidly receded.

He was healing himself with fire!

But Jace's Flame Shield had been drained by his channeling too. Petrification was spreading rapidly up his body. About to become unstoppable. Jace, his upper body still mobile, decisively chanted the Life Drain incantation!

A fel-green life-draining stream connected the warlock and the airborne black dragon over the burning flames. Jace felt the searing black dragon blood being devoured by him, transforming into his own power. He also felt his constantly draining stamina and the damage all over his body begin to recover. But the black dragon, struggling to channel fire for self-healing, roared in agony.

The flow of fire was disrupted. The fire elementals regrouped around Jace, holding back the petrification's advance. Soon, Jace used his newly gained life force to freely break free of the stone. With one hand, he sustained the Life Drain. With the other, he used Blood Grip to whip the Quel'Serrar around for another strike!

But this time, Sabellian, worn down by the continuous Life Drain, caught the incoming Quel'Serrar in midair. With all his might, he roared and ripped apart the Blood Grip's connection to Jace.

Jace had to immediately stop channeling Life Drain. At the same time, Sabellian, wreathed in black fire and clutching the blue magic sword, dove down from above with all his might!

Jace couldn't dodge. He raised a Dark Ward. By now, his Dark Ward was as sturdy as a fortress wall. Sabellian's full-strength slash only managed to cut it halfway through — didn't shatter it completely!

Under the cover of darkness, Jace used the brief moment to chant the spell he knew best, the shortest incantation — Curse of Agony. Sabellian had undoubtedly reached his limit. Every spell he could find a chance to fire off would deal a blow to his opponent and buy himself a sliver of victory!

Through the dark membrane, Jace stared into his eyes and chanted. Sabellian's eyes turned orange in an instant, then blood red. When Jace released the Curse of Agony, a searing pain suddenly shot through his entire body from head to toe!

The pain instantly cut off all his senses. Violent tinnitus followed immediately. It felt like countless Felhounds were swarming, biting at every inch of his exposed skin...

This was Curse of Agony. His own Curse of Agony. No — maybe even stronger...

"You must have figured it out by now, Jace..."

Dark Mimicry?

A wild laugh seeped through the tinnitus. The Dark Ward was shattered by another heavy blow. A black shockwave blasted across the demon throne. The floating Scepter of Sargeras swayed, its dim light flickering.

Sabellian lowered his wings, which had shielded him from the shockwave, and conjured a Shadow Bolt in his hand. Jace gritted his teeth through the pain and decisively cast Strangulate. Even if he couldn't fully interrupt Sabellian's casting, at least he could interfere with his mana. But when he made his move, he noticed Sabellian's eyes had turned blood red again!

An icy shadow claw hoisted Jace into the air. At the same time, the black dragon's Shadow Bolt was already flying. Suspended by Strangulate, Jace couldn't struggle at all. He had no choice but to take the hit head-on. The combined torment of Curse of Agony and the Shadow Bolt was unbearable. Only the last shred of will kept him going.

"Game over. We've wasted too much time."

At the dragon's low murmur, Jace forced his eyes open. The blurry figure before him, with those blood-red demon eyes staring straight at him, was utterly terrifying — like an evil god walking out of hell.

"Luh'ravinkta, lu'arakip'ta," Jace whispered.
