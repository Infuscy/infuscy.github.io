## Chapter 1401: What You Truly Value

Despite how defiantly he'd spoken, Jace still felt like a dead man walking. Was this really it?

He looked at the withered Ulthalesh in his hands. The Quel'Serrar still glimmered faintly. His robes were shredded. If not for the red dragonhide protecting him, he'd have been consumed by that sudden blaze of fire.

"Sabellian!"

Jace roared into the collapsing hall. But the black dragon was already gone.

Good. He got away in time. Back to Bladespire, or somewhere else to hide. Protect his brood.

The Scepter of Sargeras was destroyed. The expedition had made it back to Azeroth. He was going to die. The Burning Legion's demons had no reason to stay here wasting time and power. The black dragon's days ahead would be much easier.

As long as Sabellian had the skill to control Kargath's orcs against the king of Bladespire, like he'd said.

At the bottom of the stairs, demon blood had completely soaked the circular hall.

Brutallus's corpse lay in the center of the blood pool, all soul and life utterly drained. Like a whale rotting on the shore.

One of his blade-fists was missing somewhere. The other arm, fitted with a dagger, lay in a pool of blood a dozen meters away. His massive head was pinned to the wall beside the stairs, impaled on a high spike.

"I'm honored that you get to be the first viewer of this contemporary art exhibition, darling." A weak laugh came from the other side of Brutallus's corpse. Jace stumbled over and found the succubus slumped in the pool of demon blood.

Her body was covered in terrible wounds. Her chest had been torn open, ribs exposed. Several of the horns on her head had been broken off. One arm was nearly severed, hanging by a thread of bone.

But she still looked at him from a confident tilt of her head. "Oh, don't give me that pitying look, master. You're not exactly in better shape than me. I'm actually fine. Especially when you see this..."

The succubus laughed coldly. Suddenly she lowered her head. Two broken bones thrust up from her back, spreading into a pair of bloody skeletal wings. Like a dead bird half-eaten by scavengers.

But these were real demon wings. True demon wings!

"Look." Mor'phil shouted triumphantly. "Look at this, Jace Sesso! Now call out that imp and see what it has to say. Sazeno! What have you got to say now?! Huh?!"

"You're still worried about picking a fight at a time like this?!" The imp poked its head out of the demon pack, its voice trembling. "We're done for... done for... Kil'jaeden is up there! He's right up there!"

"I don't care at all," Mor'phil said through gritted teeth. "All I know is that I'm with the legendary Jace Sesso. The warlock who refused the Deceiver."

Jace walked over and hugged the demoness tightly. Until Mor'phil started coughing repeatedly. After a moment, she said quietly, "Sorry, master. If I had a little more strength, maybe I could fly you out. But for now... I can't."

"You won, Mor'phil." Jace whispered in her ear. "You defeated Brutallus. Nothing else matters that much."

"Yes. That's what I've been waiting to hear." Mor'phil moved her head, covered in horrifying wounds. "It sounds different coming from you."

The intense pain gradually set in. Seeing Mor'phil still alive, Jace felt like the last string holding his mind together was about to snap.

Was he going to die?

Cold. Stench. Pain. They surrounded him. His mind started wandering uncontrollably.

"Jace..."

"Amy?" Jace answered instinctively, quietly.

"Remember to go see Archmage Andormas, Jace. Remember to go see him."

"I know. I know."

"Jace, what are you doing here? Ah, right. I almost forgot. Is it raining outside? I meant to remind you to check on Alna. With the rain coming down like this, that mare probably won't want to go for a walk with you."

"Maybe not."

"Jace..."

"Was it you who killed that 'Hogger, Hogger' one? The Riverpaw tribe's chieftain? That two-meter-tall black monster!"

"Yeah. It was me. It was me."

"I'm a little worried the Riverpaw gnolls might attack this place someday. Hey, are gnolls on the same side as the orcs?"

"Unfortunately, we don't have any gryphon riders here right now. I can't leave my post. Do you have any trustworthy riders?"

"I am... I am one. I'm a mage from the Wizard Sanctum. An apprentice mage."

"A mage from the Wizard Sanctum? You need to open this thing up and show me what's inside."

"Jace..."

"You've been assigned to the battle mage roster. Keep this Violet Battle Mage emblem safe, Mr. Jace Sesso."

"Emblem? Right. Emblem."

"You're a mage now? No wonder you're so fragile. But didn't you go to Stormwind?" Father? Dad!

"I'm a mage now, Dad. If you don't believe me, look at this. My ring. My apprentice mage ring." Jace's trembling hands searched his whole body. But he couldn't find anything. Nothing was left.

"Dad... it's really here. My ring is right here... wait. I'm not bragging to you."

"Jace, remember to go to Archmage Andormas for your ring."

"The ring. Damn it... Dad, I'm sorry. I... I think it's still with Archmage Andormas." "Dad..."

"No." Mor'phil grabbed his shaking hand. "Your ring is with Ms. Ursula Delryn. Remember?"

"Ursula..." Jace stared blankly for a moment.

Ursula held the ring and nodded, looking at him. "Jace... Jace, can you hear me?"

"I hear you. I hear you."

"Jace, answer my summoning! Now! Now!"

"Now." Jace looked at Mor'phil and repeated. "Now."

"Remember to summon me and this booger back, darling. I know you won't forget," Mor'phil said with a smile.

The demon. The black temple. The chaotic meteors. The disintegrating black vortex swirling apart. The starry sky. The blood-red Hellfire Peninsula. Terokkar Forest in the distance. Gray-white Auchindoun. The spinning Eye of Dalaran... All of it shrank before his eyes in an instant.

"You have won. But nothing is over, Jace Sesso. You know it will never be over."

"You lost — shut up!"

Jace couldn't help roaring. Void energy tore at his flesh and will like a storm. He could only use all his strength to enslave everything trying to destroy him.

He could control this power. Even now. He could grab anything that could take him away. Use chaotic, insane emotions to enslave the void. Enslave the shadows. Until he broke through... broke through...

He had to hold on.

BAM!

Jace felt like he'd been vomited out by something. He passed through countless layers of illusory afterimages, plunging into a low, dark nest. Then he landed hard on a broken shelf...

Everything around him fell silent instantly. Pitch black. Nothing.

Jace was sitting in a broken recliner he'd just collapsed. Everything here felt familiar. But also different.

Where was this?

Wildhammer Keep? Or somewhere in Auchindoun?

He patted himself down. His clothes and cloak were still there. His staff had rolled to the side. The Quel'Serrar was still clutched tightly in his hand.

He picked up Ulthalesh. Raised the Quel'Serrar to light the area. The place was empty, just some discarded junk piled in the corner. But it looked very familiar.

Except Jace's mind was a tangled mess. The torment from the summoning ritual had nearly robbed him of his ability to remember. No matter how hard he tried, he couldn't place where this was.

He walked out, sword raised. The Quel'Serrar's magic was very weak now, barely lighting a small area. But he still found his way to an upward staircase by instinct.

There was light above. Even bright enough to hurt his eyes. Was there a fire outside too?

Khadgar had warned him — teleportation magic on Draenor could produce huge deviations. Had Jace been sent to some unfamiliar space? Or even... time?

He rubbed his eyes and climbed the steps. The most important thing now was to find a safe place and summon Mor'phil and Sazeno.

Though he wasn't sure he could break through Kil'jaeden's barrier to summon them. Especially in his current state.

When he reached the top of the stairs, he was in a storage room. Wait. This couldn't be...

"Come out! Whatever the hell you are!"

A familiar voice was shouting from outside.

"Tharle?" Jace tried.

The black man stuck his head through the doorway. Then he stared at Jace, sword raised, his face full of shock as he stepped inside.

He looked at the basement entrance. Then back at Jace. "My god, Jace. What the hell is going on? Didn't they go to the other side looking for you? How are you back first? And how the hell did you sneak in?"

Jace swallowed. He didn't know how to explain. Outside, a crowd of terrified customers was staring wide-eyed at the two of them.

"Pack me some food. Bread. A piece of mutton. Ham. Anything. I need to... I need to find someone to teleport me to Stonewatch. Anyway, it's urgent, Mr. Tharle Mor. I'll tell you the whole story when I get back."

Tharle looked him over sympathetically. Then asked, "Are you sure you don't want to find a priest at the cathedral first? You look like a roast chicken."

"I'll get around to it." Jace ran a hand through his dust-covered, messy hair. "I'll get around to it."

(The volume ends)
