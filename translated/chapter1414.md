## Chapter 1414: The Most Tempting Trick

The Telogrus Rift was a chaotic jumble of floating islands, but from the highest point you could take in all of them at once.

There were no more Wizard Sanctum or Kirin Tor mages in the rift now. Only a group of Stormwind officers and soldiers on a distant floating island, gathered in front of some void-corrupted supply crates, discussing something. Probably figuring out how to move the last batch of Expedition supplies out before they rotted from the corruption.

They were far away, with howling void energy between them. And besides Jace, only three Dagger Society members were here, all eager to get their hands on Gul'dan's Auchenai manuscripts.

So Jace stopped worrying about the soldiers. After all, even the Kirin Tor knew everything about this rift and the Dagger Society by now.

He pulled the manuscript from his bag, thinking how lucky it was that he'd left it with Vereesa. If he'd carried it from the Throne of Kil'jaeden through the Twisting Nether to Stormwind, who knows what it might have been twisted into.

The Dagger Society members certainly knew the name Gul'dan. They handled the manuscript with extreme care, turning the pages with great caution.

"This book records Gul'dan's research into souls," Jace explained. "A significant portion was probably taught to him by Kil'jaeden, the Burning Legion's leader. Auchindoun is a draenei tomb filled with countless spirits. Gul'dan wanted the warlocks left on Draenor to study the contents of this manuscript and harvest the souls of Auchindoun as much as possible."

"Soulwell," Galders spoke the name of the spell in Demonic.

"Right." Jace said. "There are other things later on. All extremely troublesome spells. Even in Auchindoun, few warlocks fully mastered them. I was so focused on the portal during my time there that I only skimmed through it. Forget about learning anything from it. So don't expect me to teach you what's in here. It'll be more efficient for us to discuss it together."

"In any case, I don't want anyone outside the Dagger Society knowing about this manuscript. We've known each other long enough to trust one another. But you all know about the lighthouse."

"I understand, Jace." Galders said. "Once Erland and Sulenna come back from Booty Bay with the demonic casting materials, we can all gather and discuss the contents of this manuscript. Meanwhile, the two of them and I can try making a copy for easier study."

Jarien shook her head and took a deep breath, forcing her eyes away from the manuscript. "I don't know why, but I feel uncomfortable."

"Still not used to the void storms of the Telogrus Rift?" Jace asked.

"No." Galders said. "This manuscript itself carries an incredibly twisted shadow aura. Just looking at it for this long is hard to bear. Your control over shadow is much stronger than ours, Jace. Reading this manuscript probably doesn't bother you."

"It bothers me too." Jace shook his head. "That's actually one reason I haven't been able to read it properly."

"Was this written by Gul'dan himself?" Ursula asked.

"I can't say for certain it was Gul'dan's own hand." Jace said. "But the Shadow Council's warlocks treated it like a holy relic. They paid with their lives trying to move it out of harm's way."

Though thinking back, Sabellian had copied it pretty fast. Three or four days and he was done. The difference between a black dragon's constitution and a human's really couldn't be overstated.

"This makes it even more important to wait for Erland and Sulenna to help." Galders closed the manuscript and handed it back to Jace. "By the way, you mentioned the production of soul shards earlier?"

"Yes." Jace said. "Soul Drain, essentially. Hold on. While I was waiting for the portal to be completed in Auchindoun, I wrote two detailed scrolls recording my study of how the Auchenai priests drain souls."

"Because draining a soul isn't just about adjusting your own emotions — you also have to capture the soul's own emotions. If you've encountered the wandering spirits in Duskwood, you probably know how erratic a soul's emotions can be. That's the hardest part of soul draining."

"Is that so? I always thought humans couldn't learn soul drain — that it had to rely on demons. Is that what the Dark Iron dwarves said too?" Ursula asked.

"Seems more like they just didn't want to teach it." Galders said coldly. "That way they could sell soul shards at a premium."

"Exactly." Ursula said. "If we could learn soul drain, we could hunt the corrupted wild beasts in Duskwood and make soul shards ourselves."

Jarien smiled too. "Hopefully Erland and Sulenna will remember to bring me some quality spider silk once they learn this spell. Especially the clean kind — not covered in dust and crushed leaves. Cleaning that stuff is a nightmare."

"Don't get your hopes up, Jarien." Galders took Jace's scrolls. "I don't think capturing a soul's emotions is going to be easy. Among us, besides Jace, Ursula probably has the best chance of learning it fastest. But as you know, our alchemist can't exactly go out hunting spider silk for you."

"Then leave it to Jace," Ursula said.

"Isn't that a bit much?" Jarien said.

"What's wrong with it?" Jace sat on a nearby rock and crossed his legs. "As long as my new robe..."

"Those spider silks are for making robes to sell, Jace." Jarien wagged her finger. "The new robe I'm preparing for you definitely won't use that kind of material."

"You've got me even more curious now," Jace said.

"Speaking of which, could I borrow your red cloak to take a look?" Jarien asked tentatively.

"Sure." Jace nodded. "I won't be wearing it to Grim Batol anyway."

Galders, who had been looking at the scrolls, looked up. "You wrote these yourself, Jace?"

"Yeah." Jace said. "Like I said, during the time when the black dragon's magic helped me infiltrate the Shadow Council... I had some spare time and wrote them."

"Just like that manuscript," Galders said. "I can already feel some unsettling shadow power coming off your writing."

Jace blinked involuntarily. Jarien said, "I'd heard a theory when I was studying in Alterac — sympathy. If a powerful caster isn't careful, the things they touch can leave arcane traces that others might detect."

"Maybe. But the situation was special then. I borrowed some power to deal with demons on Draenor." Jace looked at Ursula. "You saw the Book of Fel Names before. Those powers have mostly faded or been lost since leaving Draenor. I'll be more careful from now on."

During the final portal-opening, Ulthalesh had drained the souls of Momor and a horde of Auchenai priests, plus the shadow soul of the netherwing Sargol'ku, and the soul power of the Book of Fel Names had all converged into one. Jace really had reached a terrifying level of power.

If he'd been able to drain Brutaulus's soul in the end — most importantly, if he'd also gotten that soul crystal and Sabellian's soul too...

Maybe in the end he wouldn't have needed the Holy Light's help at all. He could have destroyed the Scepter of Sargeras in one blow. But...

How many people would you have to betray for ultimate power?

Jace shook his head, trying to push away the uncontrollable thoughts that had suddenly invaded his mind.

Whether Kil'jaeden's plan succeeded or not had almost depended on a single thought Jace had in the Black Temple.

Galders tried summoning a felhound. Summoning a demon from the Twisting Nether in the Telogrus Rift was several times easier than on Azeroth. This felhound looked terrifyingly strong, even more solid than the two-headed Dazalm Jace kept circling outside his tower.

The three warlocks discussed how to gauge the felhound demon-soul's emotions and use the felhound's soul to make a soul shard.

Jace listened to their discussion, occasionally offering practical advice from his own experience. But in his mind, he was thinking about something else.

Compared to Gul'dan's still-difficult-to-decipher manuscript and the soul drain he'd already mastered, what he most urgently wanted to figure out right now was Sabellian's Dark Simulation.

How exactly had he twisted his opponent's magic and applied it to himself? Simply using shadow to twist it?

When the Black Dragon Prince had summoned dark power, his eyes had turned blood red, and an eerie shadow had shifted across his body. Jace had observed it closely, trying to find a way to avoid it, even counter his Dark Simulation.

He had to find a way to recreate this spell. Being able to copy an opponent's spell and turn it back on them... that was just too tempting.
