## Chapter 1415: Sesso's Demon Assembly

The Dagger Society warlocks practiced in the void rift all afternoon, but nobody got any results.

Jace wasn't surprised. When he'd been practicing at Wildhammer Stronghold, it had taken him several days to finally succeed. And back then, his practice partners had been the warp stalkers roaming Shadowmoon Valley. Those mad beasts, infected by fel energy, were incredibly aggressive, simple-minded, and easy to control emotionally. Felhounds, for all their demonic bestial nature, were still demons. Their thoughts were more complex than a warp stalker's, naturally making soul drain much harder.

By evening, the warlocks left the rift together, grabbed a simple meal at the camp outside the Tower of Ilgala, and went back to their own tents to sleep. Jace returned to the rift with Morofil and Sazeno. By midnight, even the soldiers moving supplies had left. The floating islands held only Jace, a few demons nearby, and the flickering portal.

"Your wings are back, Morofil." Karlez narrowed his little eyes and spoke in Demonic. "Now you look more like a true high-ranking member of the Burning Legion."

"I'll take that as a compliment, Lord Karlez." Morofil spread her wings, stroking the approaching Dazalm III, and gave a slight nod. "Thank you very much."

"Karlez, um..." the imp muttered quietly.

"What?" Morofil asked.

"Next up are Lord Grede, Lady Vereesa, and a whole list of living people who know you." The imp looked at the succubus. "Everyone's going to flatter you. Everyone's going to compliment your wings. So annoying!"

"How could that be annoying?" Morofil smiled. "Let more people praise my perfect form. Or let my master say one more word. Just one. In Demonic."

"They really are beautiful." Jace said in Demonic.

"Mmm. Your voice is very sexy, master, but maybe don't give in so easily. I kind of liked it more when you had that slightly disdainful tone..." Morofil wrapped an arm around Jace's neck.

"Fine. Your wings aren't that beautiful."

"A bit too far." Morofil's eyes slanted. "Find the middle ground... my dear."

"Enough!" Karlez lowered his head, his voice cold. "Either release me to a far corner now, Jace Sesso, or get to the point! Stop wasting my time with this useless chatter!"

"There's still one more who hasn't arrived yet, Lord Karlez." Jace said. "Wait a moment."

"Could we do it now, master? Look, there's no one else around..." Morofil whispered.

"This is the Twisting Nether, succubus." Karlez said. "If I kill you here, you'll be annihilated forever."

"Same to you, doomguard." Morofil's face twisted into a snarl. "You think I haven't killed leaders of your kind before?"

"I trust this time, my master has finally summoned me into battle."

A pitch-black mass drifted between the succubus and the doomguard. From the top of this shadowy form, two pieces of black-purple crystal armor rapidly coalesced, and beneath that armor, a humanoid body began to take shape.

"Gaothros answers your summons, master." The void lord's deep, dark eyes suddenly lit up. He glanced at the doomguard watching him nearby, then said, "My form would struggle to destroy a high-ranking Eredar. To achieve victory, I must..."

Karlez gave a cold chuckle. Jace immediately cut in. "There's no battle for now, honored Lord Gaothros. I haven't had a chance to thank you yet for helping the Dagger Society and the Kirin Tor enter this rift."

"In the past, my travels were always for consumption. Now, they are for remaining." The void lord murmured in a low voice.

Jace wasn't sure what he meant by that, but he cleared his throat anyway. "Regardless, I've gathered everyone here for an important purpose — I need you all to help me analyze a strange shadow spell."

"On Draenor, I encountered a black dragon. He could turn his eyes red and then... 'copy' my spells."

"Copy?" Karlez asked.

"When his eyes turned red, I tried casting a Curse of Agony. He cast a Curse of Agony on me at almost the same time." Jace looked at the assembled demons. "If I tried a Life Tap, he'd severely drain his own health for mana too."

"And it didn't even interfere with his own actions." Sazeno jumped onto Jace's shoulder, raising both hands. "He could attack my master while simultaneously copying his spells. It was terrifying! I was right there when my master dueled that Black Dragon Prince!"

"Any incantation?" Karlez asked.

"I tried recalling everything with Arcane Intellect. I don't remember any incantation." Jace said.

"Interesting." Morofil said. "But he didn't seem able to control which spell he mimicked. Even spells he didn't want to copy ended up being copied."

"Maybe the spell itself doesn't tell him what he's copying!" the imp said. "That's why he fell for master's trap..."

The succubus looked up at the doomguard. "Have you seen anything like this in the Burning Legion?"

"It does sound like a demonic ability." Karlez's tone was cold. "But I haven't heard of any demon mastering such a strange spell. Of course, the Eredar command an endless variety of bizarre incantations. I'd think a huntress who's served countless Eredar would know better than me."

"Hmph. Those mediocre, tedious Eredar sorcerers only ever sent me into the Twisting Nether to hunt down demons they didn't like. Who among them would share any knowledge with me?" Morofil folded her wings. "You, on the other hand, honorable Lord Karlez. You said you once served Kil'jaeden. Don't tell me you never even saw his face. Surely they must know spells like this?"

"Of course I've seen the Deceiver!" Karlez's enraged roar made the stones on the ground tremble. "As for this kind of spell... Hah! Does Kil'jaeden need to copy anyone's spells? He himself is the incarnation of countless dark incantations!"

"Sound logic, Lord Karlez." Morofil pursed her lips.

"Then..." Gaothros, who had been silent for a while, spoke up. "Can this black dragon copy or replicate spells outside of shadow magic? For example, arcane, or..."

Jace shook his head. "No. He copied three of my spells. All shadow magic."

Although Sabellian had only copied shadow magic, in the game, Nefarian could genuinely make mages cast Polymorph and warlocks summon Infernals.

But that was different from Dark Simulation. After all, the mages and warlocks hadn't been trying to cast those two spells for the Black Dragon Prince to copy.

What matched "Dark Simulation" best was the priests' healing spells. When priests tried to cast healing, the spell would be twisted into painful torment applied to the priest's own friendly targets.

Jace knew he shouldn't apply game designs wholesale to the current situation. Sabellian had genuinely demonstrated an ability much closer to Dark Simulation, and he hadn't even met Nefarian yet.

"This sounds like a variant of some mind control magic." Gaothros spoke in deep, echoing Demonic. "A mortal's mind can directly spawn shadow, especially during spellcasting. A powerful shadow manipulator might be able to reach into your mind and steal your spells."

"So during a fast fight, he didn't have time to think about whether the spell my master was casting was suitable for copying. He had to act on the spot, which led to mistakes." Karlez said.

"Maybe it was just arrogance from thinking he was about to win. All the black dragons of Azeroth are like that!" Sazeno said.

"I was not my master's opponent at the time. I cannot tell you what he was thinking." Gaothros said.

"The fight really was fast-paced. He was constantly attacking hard, trying to overwhelm me as quickly as possible, not giving me a moment to breathe." Jace said.

"He must have been afraid you'd have time to cast your meteor." Morofil smiled.

The two felhounds beside them barked excitedly, as if the demons had already reached some conclusion.

But in the end, these were all just guesses from demons. Gaothros's theory about a "variant of mind control" was a possible explanation.

After all, several of Deathwing's children were skilled in mind magic — both Onyxia and Nefarian. Nefarian's array of mind control spells was particularly unpredictable. That also explained how he'd tortured players with his "Marking" mechanic.

Sabellian wasn't as powerful as Nefarian. So his mind magic might be cruder, less refined. Closer to what death knights would later use.

But to learn more about this kind of mind magic, where would he even go? He couldn't exactly go ask Onyxia herself.

As he thought about it, Jace grew more interested in the Great Library of Karazhan that Khadgar wanted to take him to. He wondered if Medivh had studied this kind of mind control magic.

What a stupid question. Of course he had.
