## Chapter 1423: The Black Dragon's Choice

Between the garden-like walls of Grim Batol, the red dragon queen Alexstrasza gazed down at the young dragon — who, in comparison, seemed no larger than a little black mouse — and remained silent for a long time.

Around them, the entire company — Jace's group, the Wildhammer dwarves, and the red dragons — all watched in silence.

Needleflame held herself as straight as possible, as if she represented not just herself but the entire black wing. She seemed less like a child brought here and more like an envoy sent by Prince Sabellian, the ruler of Draenor, to stand before the queen.

Unlike the formidable black dragons, Alexstrasza was not skilled — or rather, did not try — to hide the burning emotions within her. They simply didn't show much on the surface.

"Child, though you are barely more than a whelp, I can feel that Prince Sabellian invested great care in raising you. Whether he intended for you to become a warrior quickly, or to survive the harshness of another world like your elders, I believe you are now capable of making decisions about your own future. Tell me — am I mistaken?"

The red dragon queen's voice was as gentle and calm as a sorrowful mother's, making it difficult to associate with her scaled, thick-hided crimson reptilian visage.

But her voice was enough to make even humanoids like Jace overlook her terrifying size and chilling appearance, to see her as a true mother. Jace believed that if even they — of a different species — could feel this way, Needleflame must feel it even more strongly. And the queen was speaking directly to her, after all.

"I... can make decisions about my future, Queen Alexstrasza." Needleflame lifted her head. "In fact, I have already made my choice."

Her tone was remarkably serious, even rigid. It reminded Jace of how she'd been in Auchindoun, when she'd discussed returning to Azeroth with him.

Except back then, she'd looked like a fel beast, her Common coming out haltingly through a mouth full of jagged teeth, which took about thirty or forty percent off the gravity of it.

"My father commanded me to return to Blade's Edge Mountains. But I chose to stay with the expedition, to honor my agreement with Jace Sesso and Somalliar, and to cross through the Auchindoun portal to come before you."

When Needleflame finished, Jace could feel that, except for the red dragon queen, most of the red dragons present were agitated.

They seemed to have things they wanted to say, but dared not speak in the queen's presence. It wasn't until Korialstrasz turned his head and gave them a warning look that they settled down.

Vereesa and Grede might have found the dragons' restlessness hard to understand, but Jace could guess what was troubling them.

A black dragon willing to defy her elder — to defy the son of Deathwing — in order to keep an agreement with mortals? Especially when there was no clear benefit to her? Could such a black dragon truly exist?

Jace recalled how, not long ago, Korialstrasz had negotiated with Needleflame about releasing her and asking her to promise not to cause trouble. She'd actually kept that promise and hadn't tried to escape. Even Korialstrasz had seemed surprised, even asking her age... which meant that even in the eyes of an old dragon like him, young black dragons were generally illogical, innately evil lunatics — not much different from beasts driven mad by shadow corruption.

Jace suddenly felt that the two red dragons who'd been ready to fight Needleflame to the death — even with Jace trying to mediate — weren't so unreasonable after all.

Ten thousand years of betrayal. Ten thousand years of conflict and slaughter. Apart from ancient dragons like Alexstrasza, who had existed since the Dragon Aspects were born, most of the younger generation — especially those born after the War of the Ancients — had formed an unshakable stereotype of black dragons.

"In your burning eyes, I see his shadow. But I also see chaos... and ignorance." The red dragon queen's voice dropped low. "Do you know what your burning blood represents? Do you know the history that blood carries?"

After the queen's words, Needleflame bowed her head in thought for a long time.

Jace knew that Sabellian had told her some things — his perspective on the past ten thousand years and beyond. Sora had also shared some versions of events from the other dragonflights.

Sora watched her thinking for so long, as if she wanted to speak for her, but in the end, she said nothing.

After another moment, Needleflame finally answered, "I think... I've heard some things. But I can hardly claim to understand, Your Majesty."

The red dragon queen tilted her massive head slightly and let out a long sigh.

"You are innocent, child. But the things you do not understand have shaped you. Your skin, your appearance, your eyes, your resolve... all remind me of that age ten thousand years ago. Of everything the greatest among us represented. Of all we have nearly forgotten. That bloodline connection cannot be severed."

"The tragedy ten thousand years ago wounded every one of us deeply — including your kind. I want to do everything I can to heal those wounds and that pain. Child, would you be willing to stay here? With the red dragons?"

When the queen said this, the red dragons around her understood her intent.

Deathwing's offspring were either still carrying out their father's evil plans, or had hidden themselves in remote corners of the world — even in other worlds.

But this one — a legitimate descendant of Deathwing who had suddenly appeared, carefully raised by him and his eldest son, superior in both power and talent to the whelps used as cannon fodder and experiments by the Blackwing — was even more precious. She had not yet been touched by dark power, still retaining the black dragons' original appearance... This was something Alexstrasza hadn't heard of since the black dragonflight's betrayal.

Alexstrasza surely wanted to protect this sole sprout. Perhaps once she grew up, she could become a new Earth-Warder, nurturing a new generation of the black wing.

But could she truly bear all of that?

From the moment he'd encountered this little wretch trapped by the giant spider mother in Terokkar, Jace had known that what the red dragon queen and Sora wanted was exactly this — a young dragon with the potential to become a new black dragon king.

But Jace had always seen this little dragon as a girl from an extremely troubled family. And given that background, she had still managed to retain a surprising number of precious, positive qualities. That certainly had to do with the powerful black dragon bloodline, but it couldn't be separated from her innate character either.

So when Alexstrasza actually proposed keeping her by her side, Jace — who had always wanted to shake off this burden — suddenly found himself worrying about her future.

But then again, who was he to worry about Needleflame? The one who would care for her was the most powerful and kind Mother of All Dragons in the world. And hatchlings valued by the Dragon Aspects had lifespans that casually spanned thousands of years... That was hardly something a mortal like Jace — who never knew where his next meal was coming from — could worry about.

It was clear that not only the red dragons understood the queen's intent. Grede, Vereesa, and High Thane Kurdran could sense it too.

"Responsibility, lads." Kurdran Wildhammer said in Dwarvish to Grede and his two guards. Grede nodded and leaned toward the ranger, whispering teasingly, "She looks more like a real black dragon princess than Onyxia did, if ye ask me, little elf."

"Hush." Vereesa pressed a finger to her lips.

But then Needleflame answered, "I must refuse... Queen Alexstrasza."

"You don't wish to stay by the queen's side?" Korialstrasz sounded surprised.

The red dragons all stirred again, wanting to whisper among themselves but afraid of being overheard by the queen and prince. In the end they could only exchange bewildered glances.

Jace was stunned too. Had the little thing been serious in Auchindoun? Was she really planning to stick with them?

"You said, Jace Sesso." Needleflame suddenly turned around and fixed her burning yellow eyes on Jace. "You said that once we returned to Azeroth, you and Sister Sora would help me live here. That's the only reason I agreed to come with you — because I trusted her. Because I trusted you."

Jace guiltily glanced up at the red dragon queen's expression and felt that, in the eyes of the red dragons, he must look like a man caught red-handed in child abduction.

"You'd only be safe here, with the queen, Needleflame."

"You think I'm afraid?" the black dragon asked.

"No." Jace shook his head.

"You promised Needleflame you would help her adjust to life after leaving Draenor, Jace?" Alexstrasza asked.

"I did promise," Jace admitted.

"Then you must keep your word." Alexstrasza said. "You should not... betray her trust."

Jace hadn't expected the red dragon queen to let her leave and come with him. Sora looked up, visibly moved — clearly, she too had grown attached to the little one.

Jace wasn't the only one surprised. Korialstrasz clearly didn't understand why the queen was taking this risk either.

"My queen?" he asked, looking at his consort with some doubt. But she only lowered her gaze at him briefly, saying nothing. Just that one look was enough — the prince's doubts dissolved.

Jace let out a quiet sigh and smiled at the little black dragon. "I thought you'd like it here, Needleflame. You hate Stormwind so much."

"I do hate Stormwind, Jace Sesso." Needleflame snorted a puff of black soot at him from her nostrils, coating his face, then lifted her chin to look at the green dragon beside her. "Take me to see more places, Sister Sora. I believe Azeroth has more to offer than just Stormwind!"

"I will, Needleflame." Sora said, leaning in a little closer to the small black dragon.

Jace brushed the ash from his hair and watched a green dragon and a black dragon nestling together before the red dragon queen. Even by Azeroth's standards, this was fantastical enough.

Just then, he felt a vague echo of a voice deep in his heart.

*— Needleflame trusts you, not us so-called kin, Jace Sesso. I think you, as a shadow-wielding warlock, know best from what angle dark forces most easily corrupt a noble soul... I imagine that's precisely why you chose to treat her with sincerity and honesty, rather than deception and temptation — to avoid planting a seed of corruption in this bond.*

*— I can feel that you lacked confidence in caring for her, which is why you came to me. But am I any different? I watched Neltharion betray us all, powerless to stop it... So I, Malygos, Ysera, and Nozdormu have already failed. But you have not.*

*— I love this child, Jace Sesso. From the first moment I saw her... from the moment I felt her arrive at Grim Batol and spew her first glob of molten slag, I knew she was special. So even if she never lives up to the dragon leaders' expectations of her, if she can grow up peacefully — free from interference — as Neltharion's descendant, I will be satisfied.*

At that moment, the dwarves, the elves, and the red dragons — including the prince — were all watching Sora and Needleflame. Only Alexstrasza fixed her fiery eyes on Jace.

*I understand, Your Majesty,* Jace thought.
