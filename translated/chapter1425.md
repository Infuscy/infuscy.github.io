## Chapter 1425: A Message from the North

It was getting late. Korialstrasz suggested everyone rest or have something to eat, after which a red dragon guide would take the Wildhammers on a tour of Grim Batol as it stood now.

But the red dragons didn't have much in the way of varied cuisine, and magically conjured food had no nutritional value — nowhere near enough to satisfy the Wildhammer dwarves, who detested arcane magic.

Though there was an abundance of fruits and nuts gathered from all over Khaz Modan, the most critical thing was missing: there was no ale.

So as soon as they heard they could tour Grim Batol after resting, the Wildhammers unanimously suggested they skip straight to the key part and forget about the "rest."

But when the guide appeared, the guests from Aerie Peak immediately shifted their attention from the solidified lava and obsidian around Kardros' Anvil to him.

It was a rare red dragon — female — who preferred to shapeshift into a Kaldorei form. Most dwarves had never seen a Kaldorei before, so as they followed the guide away, their conversation seemed less about their old homeland destroyed by Deathwing and more about curiosity toward this tall, dark-skinned elven figure.

Jace had seen enough of Grim Batol on his previous visits, so he didn't join the tour. Besides, he hadn't eaten anything proper during the flight and was planning to grab some fruit. But it was clear Korialstrasz had other things to say.

"Do you remember the news you and Somalliar brought back from Northrend, about the Nerubian kingdom?" Korialstrasz began.

"Any progress, Master?" Jace asked.

"Since Onyxia fled Stormwind, I've been keeping an eye on Northrend," Korialstrasz said. "On one hand, I've been looking for talented mages in the Kirin Tor to send north to investigate the Nerubian kingdom's current state and the arcane anomalies. On the other, I've sent dragon scouts to Northrend to search for signs of undead activity."

"But from the latest reports, Northrend has been eerily quiet. Forests and abandoned villages where Nerubians were once active have gone completely still."

"Red dragon offspring can survey from the air, but it's hard for them to explore deep underground in detail. A more thorough investigation is needed."

The moment Jace connected dragons, Northrend, and undead, he immediately thought of the dragon skeletons the Lich King would raise into legions of frost wyrm skeletons.

"Don't let the dragons fly too deep, Master Krasus," he said.

"I understand, Jace." Korialstrasz said. "To be honest, even without your warning, I wouldn't send them to fight the harsh climate of the snowy mountains at Northrend's northern end."

"Queen Alexstrasza just helped Queen Ysera drive back the Nightmare's corruption in the Emerald Dream. And don't forget — she did that on the heels of barely recovering from the devastating wound she suffered in the Battle of Grim Batol. The Dragon Soul's aspectral power shattered Deathwing's body, but the Dragon Aspects' own power was also severely weakened. The dragonflights are truly at their weakest right now."

"Vaelastrasz and Rheastrasza are monitoring Nefarian's movements around Blackrock Spire and the Badlands. Other red dragons are helping the green dragons watch the remaining Atal'ai priests near the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, making sure they don't sneak back in to perform dark rituals before Queen Ysera can take control of the temple. So for now, I can't send too many mature dragons into unknown conditions in Northrend."

"Fortunately, the Kirin Tor team is elite enough. Though their focus has been on the expedition's portal, the Council of Six still found a group of excellent mages to form an investigation team. The leader is that brave young man who rescued me from Blackrock Spire."

Korialstrasz was referring to Rhonin, Jace knew.

In the game, Archmage Rhonin would later replace Antonidas as the leader of Dalaran and lead the Kirin Tor in the war against the Scourge in Northrend. He hadn't expected that here, too, Rhonin had become the Kirin Tor's point man for heading north against the Scourge.

"Speaking of which, Master — a group of Eredar calling themselves 'Draenei' came through the expedition's portal," Jace said. "They're followers of the Holy Light, and they're powerful — completely different from their demonic kin. Their leader wants to go near the Black Morass to repair the damage the demons left there. I think they'll probably settle near Stonard after discussing it with King Terenas and King Varian. They might become allies against the Atal'ai fanatics and relieve some of your pressure."

"I did hear about these holy light followers from another world." Korialstrasz thought for a moment. "Eredar... I might need to quickly shake off the impressions Dalaran's library has built of them over the centuries. In any case, I didn't know they planned to settle near the Black Morass. That's good news, Jace. I'll send one of my offspring to observe them."

"As for the situation in Northrend, don't worry too much." He continued. "Now that the queen is awake, I don't need to stay and guard her at Grim Batol. I'll head to Northrend myself soon to help the Kirin Tor investigate."

"I know you're a Lordaeron man, Jace. You have many acquaintances, even family, in the north. You're worried that Stratholme — which borders the North Sea — and the entire Lake Lordamere region might be affected by the anomalies in Northrend. But it's not just us and the Kirin Tor... Now that the orc war is over and the expedition has returned from the other side of the Dark Portal, the mortal forces in the entire north will be stronger than ever. Neither the Silver Hand nor Dalaran will allow that region to be threatened by the so-called 'death king' those Nerubians mentioned."

Jace nodded.

But in another timeline, the one who would ultimately take up the runeblade and raze Lordaeron was the Silver Hand's golden boy. And the one who summoned Archimonde the Defiler was former Kirin Tor council member Kel'Thuzad.

Then again, he knew that this time, Turalyon had already returned to Lordaeron. Khadgar was back in Dalaran too. And with the expedition's veterans — who had fought the Horde and demons for years — returning... Neither the Silver Hand nor the Kirin Tor could be compared to their counterparts in that other timeline.

Plus, with the red dragon queen alerted early to the situation in Northrend thanks to his warning, Jace dared to hope things might turn out differently.

At the very least, the entire kingdom of Lordaeron — from Tirisfal Glades, through the Eastern Plaguelands, to the unbroken forests and fertile lands of Quel'Thalas — didn't have to be completely covered in plague and blight. Hundreds of thousands, even millions of innocent people didn't have to be reduced to zombies and ghouls...

After resting for a night at Grim Batol, Jace prepared to return to Stormwind the next morning.

Kurdran and his attendants were feeding the gryphons, discussing flight routes with them in Dwarvish. Jace had no idea how the gryphons could understand such complex language, but the Wildhammers seemed to believe it wholeheartedly.

Vereesa was asking Queen Alexstrasza about natural magic. The queen explained with remarkable patience, and Vereesa listened intently, terrified of missing anything.

In the distance, Sora was excitedly chatting with Needleflame — who hovered in midair — in the dragon tongue, waiting for departure. Grede shielded his eyes from the sun with his hand, watching them. "When I get back to Aerie Peak, I'll tell the Wildhammers all about your adventures, Jace. The bards' spittle might fly faster than gryphons. By the time I return, the girls in Goldshire will be singing your name."

"I don't want to be turned into a song that fast — whether it's a tragic epic or a clownish comedy." Jace took a deep breath and puffed out his chest. "Anyway, when you get to that dig site, be careful. Your target is at the bottom of Blackrock Mountain. Don't let the Dark Iron dwarves get the jump on you."

"You talk like it's already decided I'm the one who'll lift that hammer," Grede snorted.

Jace looked at him. "Lokhtos Darkbargainer wants you to lift it. The Bronzebeard king wants you to lift it. And it sure looks like High Thane Kurdran agrees you're the one for the job. You're a dragonslayer, a savior of the expedition, a demon-killer, one of the greatest warriors of the Wildhammer clan. If you ask me, there's no better choice."

"Is that so?" The dwarf lowered his hand. "Honestly, that stuff you said — it doesn't really move me much. Maybe because it sounds more like your titles, Tyr's Guardian?"

"Protector of Tyr," Jace corrected, deadpan. "Get my title right, you illiterate heathen."

"Pah." Grede waved dismissively. "I was going to invite you to Aerie Peak, you know. But sadly, the Holy Light's faith hasn't reached our backwater yet. I wouldn't want to dirty this paladin's fancy boots with our filthy mud!"

Kurdran called out Thunderfist's name from over there. Grede answered loudly.

"Winter Veil." Jace smiled. "I'd like to attend one of your Winter Veil feasts again."

"Winter Veil it is, then!" The dwarf called back as he walked away, not turning around. "Safe travels, lad!"
