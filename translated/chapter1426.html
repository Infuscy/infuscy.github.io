Chapter 1426: A New Research Consultant

On the way back, perhaps because she was finally bringing Needleflame to Stormwind, Sora flew high and relaxed — not a trace of drowsiness. She chatted the whole way in the dragon tongue with the little black dragon circling her, covering topics Jace could barely follow. It was the kind of "accent" that could only be formed from peculiar sounds produced by a dragon's unique magical organs.

Meaning that although Sora was the one who'd suggested bringing Needleflame to Queen Alexstrasza, she'd also become the one least willing to let the little scamp go. The entire flight to Grim Batol, she'd probably been worrying the queen would keep Needleflame.

Jace felt the same, though his worry was that once other black dragons in Azeroth learned of Needleflame's existence, they might get some twisted ideas.

Then again, he figured it wouldn't be long. Once the Sulfuron Hammer descended upon Shadowforge City, once the Dark Iron dwarves dissatisfied with the Firelord's rule joined the Thorium Brotherhood in rebellion, once the Dark Iron priests loyal to the Firelord had their faith shaken — even if the Thaurissan family had the slightest unwillingness to remain puppets...

The balance of Blackrock Mountain would shatter. The Alliance army would once again arrive at the foot of Blackrock Spire, and Nefarian's good days would be numbered. He wouldn't have the capacity to scheme after Needleflame.

"At Grim Batol, I heard you mention Shadowforge City, Jace — the city of those dark dwarves!" Needleflame flew closer. "Is this city buried deep beneath that Blackrock Mountain, Jace Sesso? Could I go live there someday? It sounds way more interesting than Stormwind."

"First you'd need to learn Dwarvish," Jace said. "Then you'd have to turn into a short, dark-skinned, red-eyed dwarf girl."

He answered casually, and only afterward realized — short, dark, red-eyed, terrifying image. For Sora, that might be hard to stomach. But for a black dragon, especially this restless little one... wasn't that exactly her sort of thing?

Sure enough, her eyes went wide. "Did you hear that, Sister Sora? A city buried inside a volcano, where the residents all have the same skin color and the same temper as me! I shouldn't stay in Stormwind — I should move there! You should have told me about such a wonderful place in Azeroth much sooner!"

"You only heard half of what I said," Jace replied. "Your uncle lives on top of Blackrock Mountain."

"Uncle?" Needleflame was confused at first, then seemed to remember. "Oh... Prince Nefarian."

Seeing the little dragon's rare look of fear, Jace pressed the teasing. "When Grede carries that warhammer into Shadowforge City, I'll bring you along, little thing. Just make sure you spend this time asking Sister Sora to tutor you in Dwarvish, and learn how to turn into a Dark Iron dwarf. That way you can actually help!"

Needleflame huffed a few times and flew farther away without responding. Now it was Jace's turn to laugh.

No matter how fearless these little black dragons acted, the moment one of their elders was mentioned, all their bravado evaporated.

"By the way, since Needleflame will be living with us from now on, where will she stay?" Vereesa asked.

"She doesn't like Stormwind. She could stay at Ilgara's Tower," Sora suggested.

"Please, no," Jace said.

The little pest buzzing around the tower all day would be a constant disruption to his... quality time with Vereesa.

He'd barely made it back from Draenor alive, recovered from his injuries, and was trying to squeeze in a few comfortable days before Vereesa left the Kingdom of Stormwind. The last thing he needed was that little menace interfering.

"I don't want to live in your evil tower anyway," Needleflame said sourly.

"You're living in a mage tower now, Jace. She could stay in your old rental," Vereesa offered.

"I gave up the lease before I went to Draenor," Jace said. "Unless we find a new one. But are you really comfortable stuffing a black dragon into an ordinary civilian's house?"

"You think I can't hide myself well?" Needleflame said. "Don't forget — in Auchindoun, even those orc warlocks couldn't see through my disguise! Let alone humans who can't cast spells."

"But your tail was showing at the Pig and Whistle," Jace said.

"It was NOT showing! Who are you trying to fool?!" the black dragon roared.

"Maybe I could find her a room at the Quel'Thalas embassy," Vereesa said. "But now that the expedition is back, and both my sisters are in Stormwind... the embassy is probably full of Quel'dorei. I heard some Farstriders are even staying in Goldshire. I don't feel right throwing them out just because I'm a ranger-lord."

"How about we find her a cave in Elwynn Forest and let her fight the bears for territory?" Jace lay flat on Sora's back. "The first time I met her, she seemed to get along pretty well with the local wildlife in Draenor."

"RAAAWR!"

Needleflame lunged at the top of Sora's head and blasted a cloud of black smoke at Jace. Sora, completely unprepared, flinched and had to flap her wings hard to keep her balance.

"If that had been a real breath attack, you'd be dead, Jace Sesso!"

"Next time you go through the Dark Portal, ask your father if I'd be dead, you little brat!" Jace shook the soot from his hair and fired back.

"You just got lucky! If he could use his full power, he'd crush you like a bug!"

"I could crush YOU like a bug right now, squirt!"

"Your staff is broken and you're still this cocky, you stupid warlock?"

"Want me to call down a blast of Holy Light and smite you, black dragon?"

"Go ahead, you evil warlock! Let's see who dies first!"

Vereesa watched the two of them trade insults with a helpless expression. Below them, the barren black and brown earth had given way to green forest and flowing rivers again. They had entered Stormwind's borders.

"Sora, let her stay with you for now. We can't really make her sleep in the gryphon quarters, can we?"

Sora tilted her head, letting out a small breath. "Would she be willing to live in the Mage Quarter?"

"As long as I don't have to stay with THIS guy, I'll go anywhere!" Needleflame rasped.

"That's good to hear," Sora replied. "I wonder if we could ask Master Marin to prepare another room for her."

"Right, I'd completely forgotten about that." Jace looked up at the black dragon circling above him. "Looks like we've found him another 'consultant.'"

"What consultant?" Needleflame asked.

Stormwind. The Mage Quarter.

Jace ran through the rain all the way to the garden outside Master Marin's study and knocked before it got fully dark.

"Come in, the door's unlocked. No need to rush." Marin's voice came from inside.

"Master." Jace pushed the door open and raised a hand. "It's me."

"Mr. Sesso?" Marin's eyes lit up. "What a surprise. What's so urgent?"

Jace gave an apologetic smile. "You've heard about the black dragon the expedition brought back from Draenor, right? The one that was staying at Stonewatch?"

"I have," Marin said. "I was told by the Kirin Tor mages that the black dragon would be sent to the red dragonflight as a prisoner and research subject, to study the connection between black dragons and shadow corruption."

Jace cleared his throat. "Well, the black dragon is still with me."

"With you? At Ilgara's Tower?" Marin asked.

"No. In the garden," Jace said.

"Wait." Marin frowned and looked toward the crack in the door. Only then did he realize that Jace wasn't talking about an actual black dragon crashing into his garden.

"You want me to find another room for this... black dragon?"

"Can you, Master?"

"Are you sure about this, Mr. Sesso? An evil black dragon?"

"She's not like the usual impression of black dragons. And I'll do my best to make sure she doesn't disrupt the Mage Quarter's order. Most importantly, I believe she can assist with your research, just like Sora!"

"It's not that I don't trust you. If you're that certain... finding an empty room in the Mage Quarter isn't hard, especially for you." The archmage twirled the quill in his hand. "There's no need to mention dragon research or anything like that. I'm not helping for that kind of return. Between the Wizard Sanctum and the Kirin Tor, there's already plenty of research on black dragons. They don't have nearly as many gaps as the green dragon research."

"But she's an uncorrupted black dragon, Master," Jace said.

"Uncorrupted? What do you mean by that?" Marin asked.

Even though he asked, his expression and demeanor showed that he already suspected something was off.

"That's the main reason we brought her back from Draenor." Jace leaned over the desk. "A black dragon untouched by the shadow."

Marin was silent for a long moment, then immediately pulled a ledger from under his desk. "Let me see where she can stay."

"Somewhere close to Lady Sora," Jace said with a smile. "Thank you so much, Master!"
