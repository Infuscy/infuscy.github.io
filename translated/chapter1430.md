## Chapter 1430: Dragon Fishing

Knock knock knock.

Jace knocked on the door. No answer.

It was raining outside. The Mage Quarter looked hazy through the window. Had Master Marin taken her to the Sanctum tower at this hour?

Just as he was wondering whether to knock again, the door swung open with a bang.

A fierce-looking girl glared at him with dark yellow eyes that were downright unsettling in the gloom of the overcast day.

Jace glanced past her into the room. "Didn't expect a little thing raised in a cave to keep a human's room so tidy."

"Sister Sora cleaned it for me," Needleflame said. "What do you want?"

"Ah. I came to apologize." Jace pulled a crab cake from behind his back. "Try this first."

Needleflame grabbed the whole cake and shoved it into her mouth, chewing twice. "That's not crab meat!"

"Could be spider meat." Jace grinned. "Good?"

"Mm." The black dragon chewed and swallowed. "Not bad."

"I'm sorry for what I said the other day, esteemed Neltharion-descended Lady Needleflame." Jace gave a slight bow. "Do you forgive me now?"

"One snack and you think I'll forgive you?" Needleflame licked her fingers one by one. "Nothing else?"

"Then what do you want?" Jace leaned against the doorframe.

"Take me out." Needleflame pointed at the window down the corridor. "The red dragon queen said you had to take me out."

"When did she say that? It's raining outside."

"So what? Are you afraid of water?" the black dragon asked.

Jace looked at Needleflame for a moment, thinking. Why was he afraid of rain, anyway?

"Where do you want to go?"

"Where's fun?" Needleflame asked. "Want to go drinking with me?"

"Save that for Grede." Jace said. "Let's do something different. How about I take you fishing?"

"Fishing?" Needleflame scratched her ear. "I don't know how to fish."

"There's nothing to 'know.'" Jace said. "You think those old men fishing along the canal every day actually catch much? Come on."

"Let's go!"

Needleflame shouted happily and shot down the stairs in a blur. Jace had to close the door for her and hurry after.

Even in the rain, a few people in cloaks were squatting along Stormwind's canals with fishing rods.

Needleflame carried her rod to an empty spot and sat down, waving at Jace. "Hurry, hurry! Over here! There's fish here!"

Before Jace could even catch up, she'd already cast her line into the water like the other fishermen and plopped down beside it.

Jace cast his own hook, sat down, and rested the rod. He glanced at the distant Cathedral spire.

Raindrops kept pattering against his red dragonhide hood. Everything around them fell quiet. The immediate crises were over; the future hardships hadn't arrived yet.

He liked this blank stretch of time. When he didn't have to think about anything, didn't have to worry about anything.

He didn't even need to worry about the fish. Two broken staves he'd borrowed from Master Marin, a couple of silk threads Master Marin had temporarily enchanted with Arcane Toughness, quills tied on as floats, bugs dug up from the garden around the Sanctum tower during the rain...

A complete set of gear cobbled together on a whim. He hadn't actually expected to catch anything.

But Needleflame didn't see it that way at all. She stared at the quill floating on the river without moving, occasionally observing the other fishermen in the distance, then adjusting her sitting position, as if that would somehow increase her chances.

"Ever heard of the Stormwind sewer crocodile?" Jace asked.

"No," Needleflame said.

"A terrifying legend passed down among the citizens." Jace said. "They say there's a giant crocodile in these canals... Of course, not as terrifying as the black dragon in the Mage Quarter."

Needleflame narrowed her eyes unhappily. Jace coughed. "The black dragon in the Mage Quarter doesn't eat people. But according to the legend, this crocodile really does. If you caught it, King Varian might even knight you."

"Is a knight of the Kingdom of Stormwind more powerful than Protector of Tyr?" Needleflame asked.

"Nope, still less powerful than me." Jace touched the badge on his cloak.

"Then I'm not interested."

Needleflame shot him a dirty look and turned back to the water. Suddenly her eyes went wide. "Those fish — hey! They're right next to my float! Why won't they bite my hook? Is this some kind of insult?!"

"Keep your voice down. Just wait," Jace said quietly.

But he was thinking: these old fish, seasoned veterans of countless Stormwind fishing fanatics, weren't about to fall for that so easily.

"I'm going to jump in and grab them!" the black dragon said through gritted teeth.

"That takes the fun out of it," Jace said. "When I fish with Grede, he doesn't stick his hammer in the water and electrocute them either."

"Fish not biting is even LESS fun!" Needleflame grumbled.

"Patience, Needleflame." Jace said. "When you finally catch one, you'll find it's way more interesting than jumping in and grabbing them. Even a warrior has to learn how to solve special problems without violence sometimes."

Needleflame sat back down with a scowl, as if she somewhat agreed.

"The Midsummer Fire Festival is in a few days," Jace said. "Did you know Prince Arthas Menethil of Lordaeron is coming to Stormwind?"

"What's that got to do with me?" Needleflame said.

"Come watch the spectacle with me and Vereesa," Jace said.

"I'd rather fish here," Needleflame said.

"You're a tough one to please, you know that?" Jace sighed.

"How about this — get Lady Morophil to teach me combat in the future." Needleflame said. "Back in Auchindoun... I saw how she butchered those demons. I want to learn how to fight in this form from her. And Vereesa is going to learn natural magic from Queen Ysera anyway. She can teach me then."

"You don't hate her for being a demon anymore?" Jace asked.

"She's an evil, sinister female demon. But she's too good." Needleflame leaned in. "I heard Khadgar say she single-handedly defeated a pit lord as strong as Magtheridon. Is that true?"

"Absolutely true. Not a word of exaggeration." Jace said. "That pit lord was named Brutallus, the guardian of Kil'jaeden the Deceiver."

"Quick, quick!" The black dragon bit her lip in excitement, grabbing Jace's cloak. "Promise me you'll have her teach me, and I'll forgive you, Jace Sesso!"

"Fine." Jace wore a smug expression. "Since you're asking so nicely."

"And another thing," Needleflame said. "When Grede Thunderfist goes to Shadowforge City, I'm going too. I'm serious this time. I want to go on an adventure with you."

"You're not afraid of your uncle anymore?" Jace asked.

"I'm... not afraid anymore." Needleflame took a deep breath. "Father hates that guy. If I could beat him, father would be really happy."

Though it was hard to imagine what Sabellian would look like happy because his daughter had grown up, Jace smiled anyway. "And how's your Dwarvish?"

"Good evening, Jace Sesso, get out, Shadowforge City!" Needleflame shouted in Dwarvish.

Still a bit rough, but she had that Dark Iron rasp down — that unique quality of creatures who lived surrounded by lava and ash.

"Who taught you Dwarvish?" Jace asked, surprised. "Sora? Will Sora let you go to Shadowforge City?"

"Not her. Master Marin taught me. What do you think?" Needleflame showed off. "He said I learn very fast, very well — the fastest student he's ever seen learning languages. How could he compare mortals to me? Hmph!"

"Keep studying hard, and you can come with us to Blackrock Spire," Jace said.

"Awesome!" Needleflame threw both arms in the air. Just then, the quill on the water twitched. She lunged forward and yanked the rod up — and actually caught a long-snouted green loach!

"Did you actually catch something..."

Jace muttered and reached out to take the flopping, thrashing loach from the air. But Needleflame beat him to it — she craned her neck and swallowed the fish in one gulp!

"Hey! You little—" Jace flinched. He grabbed at the fishing line, but the enchanted thread had already been bitten clean through.

"Delicious!" The black dragon smacked her lips. "I beat you, Jace Sesso! You didn't catch a single one, you idiot!"

Watching her twist her waist happily, Jace slowly relaxed.

What had he been thinking? That some cheap fishhook he'd scraped together could actually hurt a real black dragon's esophagus?

Needleflame shuffled over and slammed her hip into Jace. "Next time, next time, Jace Sesso! Fishing is so much fun!"

"It's boring," Jace said flatly.
