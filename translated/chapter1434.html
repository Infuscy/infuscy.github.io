Chapter 1434: Sylvanas's Wish

On the surface, Turalyon had put himself at odds with all his comrades, mentors, and friends within the Silver Hand and the Church. But deep down, Jace actually felt somewhat reassured. At least Turalyon had immediately sensed where the problem lay.

With someone like him as an influence, maybe things wouldn't go so far off the rails this time.

Just as Jace's thoughts began drifting toward Andorhal, Stratholme, and Northrend, King Terenas rose to his feet and raised his glass.

"Everyone — today is not only a great day for me and Arthas, but I believe it is an immensely important moment for all of you here — from the rulers of Stromgarde to the harbormasters of Boralus, and the heroes who distinguished themselves in the Orc Wars. In the past twelve hundred years, humanity has never been as united as it is today. And as for our alliance with Quel'Thalas, fighting side by side as we have these past years — that, I suspect, has not happened in two thousand years."

"Now the Horde has not only retreated beyond the Dark Portal — we have pursued them to their very homeland and permanently foiled their terrible scheme to invade other worlds. Light above, it bears witness that the soldiers of the Alliance have achieved an unprecedented feat! The leaders of the Sons of Lothar, and the heroes who crossed the portal to rescue them — their names will be remembered by the entire Alliance."

"The victorious return of the Expedition is another testament to the unity of the Alliance of Lordaeron," Varian said. "And that unity will continue, helping us purge the dark corners that still threaten us."

"Well said, King Varian. And that was the very purpose of the Alliance's founding." Thoras Trollbane raised his glass.

"Innumerable sacrifices have forged this unbreakable Alliance." Daelin Proudmoore said. "Do not forget the glory, and do not forget the pain. Let us also raise our glasses to those who have fallen. For the Alliance!"

"For the Alliance! Long live the Alliance! Long live Azeroth!"

Everyone in the hall raised their glasses and cheered, clinking drinks with one another. The atmosphere ignited.

Before long, Varian came over to Jace and Turalyon with a smile. "How are you doing, Jace? There's plenty of Lordaeronian cuisine today. I bet you're finding this more to your taste than usual."

Jace set down his glass and chuckled. "You don't think I was eating emperor salmon and seal meat back in Lordamere, do you, Your Majesty?"

"Don't even mention Jace — I'd never tasted them either before leaving the capital," Turalyon said.

"To be honest, I'd never had seal milk before going to Lordaeron either." Varian looked over the dishes on the long table. "This brings back some memories of those days. Back then, uncertain and grieving as we were, at least everyone around me had one goal — defeat the orcs, drive them back through the Dark Portal. But now..."

"It sounds like the kings aren't keen on attacking Blackrock Spire," Jace said quietly.

"Are you sure the Dark Iron dwarves will actually help?" Varian asked in a low voice. "I know Archmage Khadgar used that argument to win over the northern nobles. From Archmage Drenden's reaction, at least the Kirin Tor is mostly convinced. So this point is crucial."

"At least I believe it." Jace leaned in closer. "The leader of the Thorium Brotherhood, Roktus Darkvow, believes that once the Dark Iron dwarves see a chance to break free from the Fire Lord's chains, they'll rise up. As an old man who's lived in Shadowforge City for so long, he probably knows the situation there better than we do."

"If you trust it, then I trust it." Varian took a gulp of wine.

"The Sons of Lothar have been ready to attack Blackrock Spire for a long time, King Varian." Turalyon said. "Ever since we retreated from the base of Blackrock Mountain, I knew this wasn't over. It's about time we went back."

"Having your confirmation is tremendously reassuring, Turalyon." The king said. "To be honest, I just have this vague unease. At least King Thoras and General Danath have already promised troops to attack Blackrock Spire and eradicate the Blackrock orcs."

Turalyon's eyes flicked to something behind Jace and Varian. Varian turned to look as well — Vereesa had just approached. Seeing that Jace was deep in conversation, she seemed hesitant about whether to come closer.

Varian shot a meaningful glance. "Turalyon, would you introduce me to the leaders of the Silver Hand among the Sons of Lothar? I've been so busy since your return that I haven't had a chance to meet them. Let's take this opportunity."

"Of course, Your Majesty." Turalyon nodded immediately. "Please follow me."

The two of them slipped away quickly. Vereesa gestured toward the exit with her finger, and Jace set down his glass, following her out of the banquet hall into the corridor.

It was much cooler outside. He could almost hear the cheers from the streets. Torches and lanterns for the Midsummer Fire Festival already lined the canal and the roads.

With the Lordaeron prince's arrival and the Midsummer Fire Festival celebrations, all of Stormwind was filled with joy and excitement.

"You finally rescued me." Jace leaned against the wall. "I felt like I was in there for a year."

"Turalyon was the one who came to rescue you." Vereesa whispered.

"He came to rescue me?" Jace said. "He came over and laid this heavy conversation on me — the Holy Light, the future of the Alliance, all that — and left me with no idea how to respond."

Vereesa rolled her eyes toward the hall. "That's what I mean. That guy makes everything so serious... He's a perfect match for Alleria!"

They nudged each other and burst out laughing, only straightening up when a guard walked past.

"Speaking of Alleria..." Vereesa licked her lips and glanced outside cautiously. "I was talking to Sylvanas earlier."

"What about?" Jace asked.

"About marriage."

The elf's eyes slid sideways, like she was trying to avoid his gaze. But she finally gathered the courage to look at him. Jace could practically feel the arcane energy in her eyes ready to spill out.

"I'm not in a rush... well, of course I'm not not in a rush. I mean, not setting a date right now, but—"

She babbled a mess of words. Jace nodded. "I can't wait, Vereesa."

"Hey." Vereesa lightly punched Jace's arm, unable to suppress a grin. "That's not what I meant. I meant Sylvanas... I know she's had a wish for a long time. That the three of us get married together. She wants to have a joint wedding."

"When Alleria and Turalyon got married in a hurry, it was because the war was still going on. No one knew what would happen next. So Sylvanas didn't mind, and neither did I. But now... the war is basically over, isn't it? And I have you, Jace. Maybe we could consider fulfilling her wish. Alleria would be willing to have another ceremony too — I don't even need to ask her. I know she's always loved Sylvanas. Whatever Sylvanas wants, she'll agree to, as long as she can make it happen."

"What about you? Would you be willing?"

"Of course I would." Jace said. "Lady Sylvanas has been so good to both of us. But..."

"You're wondering..." Vereesa watched a guard pass by, then smiled mysteriously. "Where her other half is, right?"

"To be honest, I'm not entirely sure either. But you know how many rumors are out there. She did tell me there's someone she has in mind. She just doesn't know when to tell everyone yet. But I think if she's bold enough to hint about us sisters having a wedding together, she must have been planning this for a while."

"Then I don't have any other questions. It's up to you to figure out the arrangements," Jace said.

Vereesa pursed her lips and leaned in. "Were you serious earlier when you said you can't wait?"

"Of course I was serious," Jace said.

"Ah, we'll have to wait a little longer then!" Vereesa clasped her hands behind her back and kicked at the base of the wall. "Who would've thought our Sylvanas Windrunner — always acting so cold and aloof — would be this shy? Having someone she likes but being too embarrassed to admit it! The whole Farstriders — no, the whole Silvermoon — must be laughing at her!"

She peeked furtively toward the door to make sure Sylvanas wasn't looking their way, and they both broke into quiet laughter again.

Just then, rapid footsteps echoed from the other end of the corridor. Jace assumed it was another patrolling guard, but it turned out to be Jaina.

She was walking quickly with a serious expression. When she noticed Jace and Vereesa leaning against the wall watching her, she seemed to startle, raising her eyes slightly. She gave a little bow. "Good evening, Master Sesso. Lady Vereesa."

"Good evening, Lady Proudmoore," they replied in unison.

Jaina nodded again and hurried into the banquet hall. Vereesa looked at Jace curiously. Jace glanced behind them — a tall figure was watching from further down the corridor.

Vereesa looked back. "Prince Arthas?"

"Lovers' quarrel," Jace murmured.

"Don't say that!" Vereesa whispered in warning, then glanced again. "What do you think it's about?"

"How would I know?" Jace said.
