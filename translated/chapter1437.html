Chapter 1437: Dreams in the Tavern

"Somniel told me in a dream that you would be here, Mr. Jace Sesso." The high elven woman walked up to the table. Her golden hair seemed paler and more subdued, as if she were older — that was the most obvious difference Jace could sense between her and Sorah. Just looking at the face, the thin, almost severe features could have been stamped from the same mold. The elven woman nodded to Amy beside her, then said, "I am Emeriss, one of the four Dream Guardians of the Emerald Dream. The Queen Ysera sent me here to find Lady Vereesa Windrunner." Jace felt his heart tighten at the name. Emeriss. In another timeline, one of the most vicious of the four Dream Guardians. Players affectionately called her "Mushroom Dragon" because of her infuriating explosive poison spores. But since the Emerald Dream's crisis had been resolved early by Queen Alexstrasza, she probably hadn't had time to be corrupted yet. Otherwise, Ysera and Alexstrasza wouldn't have sent her to Stormwind. "Emeriss... Lady, Guardian of the Dream." Amy hesitated. "Are you also—" "Yes." Emeriss smoothed her robe and sat down beside them, looking at Jace. "I know Queen Alexstrasza and Thalar must have already expressed their gratitude to you, Jace Sesso. But I still want to thank you personally. You ended the endless suffering of my sister. In the Dream, I can still hear the echoes of her lingering spirit. She was very grateful that you set her free." "You were the one who, entirely on your own initiative, warned Queen Alexstrasza of the danger. You rescued Lord Eranikus. The evil magic that the ogre god Hakkar laid down in that Atal'ai temple was also one of the sources of the Emerald Dream's corruption." Amy listened, unable to hide the surprise in her eyes. But she didn't interrupt the conversation between Jace and Emeriss. She just shot him a look, as if unhappy that he'd kept so many things from her. Of course, Jace hadn't just kept things from Amy. In the beginning, aside from him and a few demon servants, nobody had known he was going to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar to feed souls to Usselus, so he could summon Kallrezz back from the depths of the Twisting Nether. Even Vereesa, Grede, and Sorah had only found out at the very end. "Fortunately, after that troll temple returned to our control, and Queen Alexstrasza personally entered the Dream, the nightmare's corruption was contained." Emeriss smiled slightly. "The two queens found the source of the nightmare together — a traitor who had abandoned Azeroth ten thousand years ago to join the Burning Legion's demons. Honestly, none of us were surprised that he was the one behind it all. For thousands of years, he and his followers have been trying to destroy everything the Kaldorei and we built together. Perhaps this time he saw hope of success and revealed himself too eagerly in his haste, leading to his own destruction." "Xavius is dead?" Jace asked in surprise. "You know his name?" Emeriss asked. "In Winterspring, the satyrs who tried to use highborne artifacts to infiltrate the Dream mentioned that name. It sounded like he was a 'Satyr King.'" Jace explained. "Satyr King... a fitting title." Emeriss said quietly. "Or rather, he has died again. We don't know how he survived the destruction of the War of the Ancients. Archdruid Malfurion Stormrage and the Kaldorei seemed to destroy his physical form, but couldn't extinguish his soul." "This demon hid his soul between the Dream and reality, trying to secretly corrupt the Dream's most hidden corners. Unlike other Burning Legion leaders, his plan was to silently undermine Azeroth's foundations. He nearly succeeded. The nightmare was blurring the lines between reality and dream, drowning all the druids and us in confusion, unable to distinguish between the two... Fortunately, Queen Alexstrasza's fury burned him and all his work to ash before that could happen. Time in the Emerald Dream is difficult to measure against the mortal plane. But to me, that great victory feels like it happened only yesterday. Even now, I can hardly contain my joy." This green dragon said all this so calmly that Jace couldn't detect any "barely contained joy." But considering that when Sorah first met them, her fear and excitement both seemed half a beat slow, maybe that was just how green dragons were. "But since this demon Xavius died once before, how can you be sure he's really dead this time?" Jace asked. "Queen Alexstrasza confirmed his death. But I'm not capable of answering your question, Jace Sesso." Emeriss said. "Besides," Jace said, "it's very difficult for demons to be destroyed outside the Twisting Nether. Even if he's been purged from the Emerald Dream, he'll come back eventually." After all, in another timeline, Xavius was destroyed again and again, only to be reborn each time. One or two setbacks was hardly reassuring. "My kin have regained control of the Emerald Dream." Emeriss was speaking, and Jace added, "I know those satyrs are obsessed with finding things that connect the Dream and reality to corrupt, Lady Emeriss. Whether it's highborne dream gems, or dream rifts that connect the two sides... or perhaps even the World Tree you mentioned. If it were me, I'd increase my presence near those places, purify any potential threats in advance." The green dragon listened seriously to Jace's words, then lowered her head in thought for a moment. "I will convey your concerns and suggestions to Queen Ysera." "I'm not trying to tell the green dragons how to protect the Dream." Jace gave an embarrassed laugh. "It's just that knowing Vereesa wants to enter the Dream to learn from the green dragons, I keep thinking back to what I saw when I met Somniel. So I can't help worrying." "I understand," Emeriss said. "Since you're here, would you like to eat something, Lady Emeriss?" Jace glanced at the counter. "Vereesa is with her sister at the embassy. We can find her together after we eat." "Thank you very much for your kindness, Jace Sesso." Emeriss looked at the snacks on the table. "But before meeting Lady Vereesa Windrunner, I would like to visit Somniel in person. I apologize for interrupting your meal." "Then... alright. We won't see you out, Lady." Jace said respectfully. The green dragon bowed politely, then rose and left the Blue Heron. Amy blinked, took a deep breath, and looked around. "She was dressed like a forest fairy from a fairy tale book. I'm surprised none of the mages here paid any attention." "As far as I know, Emeriss is one of the most powerful green dragons." Jace said. "Maybe besides you and me, nobody in this tavern even remembers she was here... And at least she didn't walk into Stormwind looking like a Kaldorei, which is what they like most. That's already careful disguise." "You mean some kind of dream magic." Amy glanced warily at the door. "Now that you mention it, my eyelids do feel heavy... Thinking back on her face now, it's as hard as trying to remember something from a dream." "There's so much you haven't told me, Jace." The female mage rested her chin on her hand. "When you get back from Kalimdor, make sure you come to Dalaran and tell me all the exciting stories I missed." "I will, I will..." Jace smiled, then looked up. "Wait, Kalimdor? You're going to Kalimdor?" "Aren't you?" Amy picked up a slice of sweet bread, dipped it in jam, and popped it in her mouth. "The other day, Archmage Khadgar was at the Wizard Sanctum telling Archmage Anslaim Runeweaver that once he finished his business up north, he might go to Kalimdor with you. So he probably won't be part of the escort mission for those two artifacts." "Ah, right." Jace nodded. "That's a thing." He took a sip of apple wine. "Asking you to dig up information from that Proudmoore princess... I'm not making you work for free. I'll bring you a gift from Kalimdor." "It's not like I'm helping you for a gift, kid." Amy raised an eyebrow. "But a rare souvenir from another world wouldn't hurt."
