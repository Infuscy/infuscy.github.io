## Chapter 1446: Embers of Life's Essence

The flames in the thick mist blazed to life. With a roar like vomiting, countless blood-red flames trailing black smoke spilled down onto the staircase. Khadgar immediately raised a powerful fire protection barrier over everyone. The cascading wall of fire was partially deflected by the barrier's shell, but some still splashed onto the steps, which began to shake and tremble beneath their feet.

A burning shadow swept past through the mist and flew away, accompanied by indistinct muttering. The whole staircase was now ablaze.

Khadgar swung his staff to drive away the flames in front of them. "Move! Now!"

Jace raised his Mage Armor and climbed faster alongside Morofil. Khadgar kept moving while watching the direction the shadow of flames had gone. "There's always been a rumor among Karazhan's servants that an undying dragon circles this tower!"

"A wizard tower and a dragon — the classic pairing. I always thought it was just a story the older servants made up to scare the youngsters. Or is this an illusion too? Like the river outside? Jace, can you tell?"

Jace shook his head. Because he knew there really was a "dragon" here.

Arcanagos. More precisely, the Nightbane — a cinder-dragon reborn from the former's corpse.

In the game, you needed to perform a specific ritual on one of Karazhan's balconies to summon this dragon. Jace had no idea why it was appearing right in front of them now.

Was it because of Khadgar's spell to reshape the broken floors? Or because of the shadow incantations he'd used against the flesh-beasts?

Mad roars echoed through the mist. But soon it wasn't just the dragon's deep bellowing. More hoarse shouts — even cursing — came from the burning flames above and below the staircase.

"Watch out. More coming. Undead." Morofil warned quietly.

As she spoke, several burning figures appeared on the stairs above. Jace raised his hand and summoned the Grasp of the Blood Demon, yanking one of the emerging figures out of cover.

The figure tumbled, hitting the railing and stone steps as it fell straight into the abyss below. As it flashed past, Jace saw a skeletal soldier with a twisted face — roughly human in shape, but with several spikes growing from its skull, like a demon's bones.

Several burning skeleton soldiers emerged from the flames the dragon had left behind, surrounding them from above and below. Khadgar summoned an Arcane Explosion between several of them, blasting some into the mist. Morofil took to the air and knocked two more down.

But as Jace watched Morofil fight the skeleton soldiers, something felt off.

These skeletons weren't truly undead... They were different from the reanimated ghouls and zombies of Duskwood. Strictly speaking, they were more like bones that had been given "life."

Most of Morofil's power now came from his life force. Even though the demon claimed she could convert life force into demonic energy, she'd been heavily affected by Jace's red dragon blood. Even the demon-fire in her eyes was nearly "corrupted" by life energy.

And those skeletons were almost the same — literally. It was as if they'd been forcefully animated by demonic power and life energy. The flames burning on the staircase were much the same, giving off an indescribable stench.

Soon, the mad roar came from another direction again. The flames blazed, and blood-red fire trailing black smoke lunged at them. Khadgar gathered another fire protection barrier, barely blocking the breath attack. But the steps beneath them cracked and shook from the impact. Khadgar quickly chanted to repair them, keeping the whole thing from collapsing.

At the same time, more skeleton soldiers were born from the flames, surrounding them from above and below — many more than before.

"We need to bring him down! Can't let him keep toying with us!" Khadgar shouted from within the flames. "But I can't pin down where he's approaching from. I can't guide a spell to hit him."

"I can sense him, mage!" Morofil yelled.

"Then I'll try to bring him down!" Jace shouted. "I'll have my demon pinpoint the dragon's position through our soul link!"

"I'll hold off these skeleton soldiers and keep this staircase together!" Khadgar called back.

He immediately began chanting to dispel the flames, then used some strange teleportation magic to make the skeleton soldiers vanish in flashes of light, one by one.

Jace glanced at Khadgar. With one hand, he raised Ulthalesh and grabbed three soul fragments he'd brought from Auchindoun.

"Savia, Avarus..."

He closed his eyes and chanted the Hand of Gul'dan. In the mist, something began to shriek hysterically in response to his words.

"Antea, Minuerel."

Karazhan's endless shadow energy and dark power swirled at his will, gradually coalescing into black serpents of magic.

Jace felt like he'd never assembled a Hand of Gul'dan so easily. And this dark soul-star he was shaping looked incredibly powerful. Judging by the effect when he'd hit Onyxia last time, this one would be enough to deliver a devastating blow to a dragon too.

"Minuerel..."

Jace stood on the staircase, swinging the demon bone staff as he guided the Hand of Gul'dan to circle in the mist nearby, while feeling the sensations the succubus was transmitting through the soul link.

Morofil was staring at a point in the mist. And Jace could feel where she was looking. When that point drew closer, when the mist was lit by flames, he gritted his teeth and suddenly whipped his staff around.

"Velma!"

The dark meteor tore through the mist and slammed into the front of the flames, triggering a massive violet arcane explosion. In the midst of an enraged roar, a giant wing like dead branches swept up a sheet of black flames, driving back the mist. The dragon's hideous, enormous skull — all charred flesh and dried meat — emerged before them.

It hadn't taken a lethal hit? What kind of freak dragon was this?!

Before Jace could process it, the skeletal dragon angrily spat a torrent of savage black fire in his direction. Khadgar in the distance frantically shouted Jace's name while raising a fire protection barrier for himself. Jace felt the effects of his fire protection potion stripped away almost instantly.

He realized the fire's power was wrong. He grabbed his red dragon hide cloak and dove to the side, avoiding the full brunt of the flames. Just as he was about to fall into the abyss, Morofil snatched him up and pulled him back onto the staircase. He'd barely escaped.

On the stairs, Khadgar's beard was more than half burned off. His face was pale white — and he hadn't even been in the direct path of the breath.

"God damn it. If that dragon isn't an illusion, then he's way too powerful. I could feel Medivh's own mana in that fire. What is he?" The mage said through gritted teeth. "We can't beat him on this staircase. There's no choice but to climb as high as we can."

"Medivh's own mana?" Jace asked. "Why?"

"I don't know." Khadgar scratched his hair. "Maybe he was originally part of Medivh's will, part of this tower's will. And we're in his domain."

That explanation triggered something in Jace's memory. The quest in the game to summon Nightbane. It mentioned that Medivh — or rather, Sargeras's soul — had used his own life force to ignite and kill Arcanagos. Later, the mages who wanted to find Arcanagos — the quest-givers — wanted to find Medivh's remaining life fragments, which was why they sent players into Karazhan.

But by the end of the quest, those mages had found Arcanagos's remaining bone fragments and concluded that Medivh's life force had run dry. To awaken Arcanagos again, they'd need to resurrect him through an evil ritual — which was Nightbane, a husk burned to ash.

But if Khadgar could feel Medivh's life force in this dragon's power, then did that mean... he really was part of Medivh or Sargeras's lingering soul-mana? No wonder he was so powerful.

But didn't that also mean this dragon wasn't fully dead yet?

"Dragon!" Jace decisively drew a high song and held it aloft — no, he drew Azuresong and raised it high. The magic sword glowed faintly blue in the mist. The mark left by the Scepter of Sargeras on it was nearly repaired by Malygos's magic, but a faint dark spot could still be seen.

"Do you recognize this blade?"

"What are you doing, Jace?" Khadgar asked.

From the mist came a sudden roar, followed by a rumbling draconic mutter that sounded like a spell.

"You dare... hold the Master's... arcane scale Medivh... Do you intend to humiliate me further?"

"I'm not Medivh!" Jace swallowed and shouted on. "We came into this tower not to fight you, but to save all of Elwynn from Karazhan's dark contamination!"

"If you want to save this kingdom, then kill Medivh! Kill Medivh, destroy the demon lord that possesses his body! Or is this all lies, nothing but illusion?!"

"Medivh is already dead, dragon!" Khadgar shouted. "I drove my own sword through his heart! That's what caused this tower's disaster... Can't you see that?"

A great force drove the mist aside. A burning skeletal dragon appeared before them. He hovered in midair, beating wings of bare bone, like a crashed zeppelin.

"Lies." The dragon said in a low voice. "Medivh's life still burns, here in this foul mist. I will destroy it forever and save the world from his grasp. You are merely illusions he created, like the countless demons and twisted beings hiding in this tower. You seek to deceive me."

"Is this blade also an illusion?" Jace asked.

The flames covering the skeletal dragon's body died down by half. He stared intently at the sword, silent for a long time, before saying, "But... I can feel..."

"It was Medivh's life that ignited your soul, dragon." Jace said. "But he himself has long since perished."

"That vile wizard dared to use his life to ignite my soul..." The dragon murmured, lowering his head. "Yes. You're right. I remember now. That treacherous madman killed me with such vile magic. He used his own life essence to ignite my heart, my soul. Pain. Torment. Arcanagos... Malygos's most trusted servant... is dead."

"I'm sorry, Lord Arcanagos." Jace said. "We're sorry."

Arcanagos didn't respond to Jace. He just hung his head, muttering. But at the same time, his chest began to glow. Terrifying flames swelled rapidly. His wings, claws, even his skull began to tremble...

"Jace. Jace..." Khadgar grabbed Jace's shoulder. "We need to leave. Now."

Jace nodded, sheathed his sword, and turned to run. But before the three had gotten far, a thunderous blast exploded behind them. Dazzling white light swallowed the view. In his peripheral vision, Jace saw the entire tower that Khadgar had painstakingly pieced together shattered by the shockwave of that corrupted life-fire...

"Master? Master?"

Morofil's voice came from beside his ear. Jace opened his eyes, squinting against the rain still falling on his face.

He forced himself up through the pain all over his body. They were on a wide balcony. Khadgar lay collapsed against a wall not far away. Jace ran over and patted the mage's face. "Khadgar! Khadgar!"

"Jace. Jace. Jace Sesso!" Khadgar's eyes snapped open. He struggled and grabbed Jace's shoulders. "Where are we? The Shadowlands?"

"Hard to say." Jace coughed, pushing wet hair out of his eyes. "Pitch black everywhere. Lots of ghosts."

Khadgar glanced at Morofil beside him and breathed a sigh of relief.

He leaned back against the wall. "There's good news and bad news. Which do you want first?"

"The bad news." Jace sat down beside him.

"Taking that staircase up is completely off the table now. We need another route." Khadgar tilted his head.

"I figured as much. What's the good news?" Jace asked.

"Do you like stage plays?" Khadgar asked.

"No." Jace said.

Morofil couldn't help but snicker. Khadgar pushed himself up with his staff, standing with difficulty. "Then that's two pieces of bad news. Let's go."
