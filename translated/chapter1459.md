## Chapter 1459: The Curator's Records

Once Nielas Aran quieted down in the center of the library, the whole library seemed to fall silent with him.

No more arcane fluctuations. No more strange metallic smells filling the air. The only thing was that the rats sticking their heads out from the floor seemed more frequent, making you worry about the state of the books stored here.

To keep the Curator from noticing anything unusual in the library, Morofil naturally had to stay invisible. And so, Khadgar led Jace to a tall wooden door at the end of a corridor.

The mage looked up at the door. Beside it was a metal plate inscribed in both Thalassian and Common: "Grand Library. Exhibition Hall."

"What's the password this time?" Jace asked.

"No password." The mage put his hand on the door. "But now I'm not confident about anything. This tower does have problems — problems I never expected."

"What might we find behind this door?" Jace asked.

"Worst case, we end up brawling with the most powerful arcane constructs in all of Azeroth." Khadgar said. "How bad could it be?"

Even without Morofil being visible, Jace could sense her smile. "Open it," he said.

Khadgar pushed the door hard. Light streamed through the crack. They squinted and stepped inside. The moment they looked up, two arcane constructs stood on either side of a stone-pillared doorway, looking down at them.

"Uh... wait."

Khadgar took an instinctive step back, but the two constructs made no further move. They didn't seem to notice the invisible demon behind Jace either. Their gem-like eyes simply glowed with arcane energy as they observed the two men.

"Long time no see, Master Khadgar." One of them said. "Welcome back to the Grand Library."

Khadgar cleared his throat, glanced at Jace, adjusted his collar, and stepped forward. "Hello, Guardian. This is my friend. May I bring him into the Grand Library to meet the Curator?"

"Of course, Master Khadgar." The construct rotated its upper body slightly. "The Curator is inside."

Jace looked at another construct patrolling near the entrance and used their soul-link to silently signal Morofil to stay nearby for now, not to sneak into the constructs' guarded area. But then the other construct's blue eyes suddenly flashed red.

"Unidentified void soul signature detected." The construct raised both hands, pointing at Jace. "Identify yourself, visitor."

Khadgar turned around. Jace raised his hand voluntarily. "Court mage of Stormwind, Jace Sesso. I wish to meet the Curator. I have many questions to ask him."

The construct scanned the rings on Jace's fingers with arcane sight. "Identity verified, Jace Sesso. The Curator has approved your request. Enter, both of you."

Once past the two constructs, Khadgar let out a small sigh of relief. "Close call. Before I met Master Aran, I didn't realize he held such hostility toward the Kirin Tor. I didn't want things to come to a magical showdown. Lucky for us, he and this tower still remember your ring — kept this trip from turning into a complete free-for-all."

Jace nodded, but his attention was fixed on a towering stone statue ahead.

He remembered this statue, of course. Back when he raided Karazhan, the Curator was a tough fight — it would release sparks that kept damaging everyone, until some clever soul thought of jumping onto this statue to dodge them. Jumping onto this statue became an essential skill for raiding Karazhan back then.

He'd jumped onto it so many times, but he'd never taken a close look at what the statue actually depicted.

It looked like some kind of golem. But compared to the arcane constructs in Karazhan, it seemed more like the dark iron golems from Shadowforge City, or the stone guardians he'd seen in Uldaman — squat and sturdy, with what looked like carved facial hair on its head.

Considering the dark iron golems of Shadowforge City and the stone statues of Uldaman both traced their imagery back to the keepers sent by the Titans, even to the Titans themselves... it was hard to say who this statue in the exhibition hall was meant to represent.

Just then, slow, heavy footsteps approached from a distance. Jace and Khadgar followed a patrolling construct through the smaller statue hall into the exhibition hall's main chamber, and the view opened up.

An arcane construct, perhaps ten meters tall, slowly walked toward them. Its shell and limbs looked like ornately carved grey-blue marble armor, with arcane energy already arcing into sparks, forming its joints.

The pure arcane power flowing through its body alone was enough to light up the entire hall. The braziers and the arcane chandelier high above were all dimmed by the construct's glow.

"Welcome back to the Grand Library, Master Khadgar. And welcome to you, Master Jace Sesso. I hope you had ample relaxation and enjoyment on your way here. I'm told you have questions you wish to consult about. The Curator is happy to answer any inquiries regarding this tower. That is the task my master assigned to me."

The Curator's voice sounded like that of a gentle young man — a strange match for his large, clumsy frame. But the friendly tone did ease them somewhat.

"Esteemed Curator." Khadgar walked up to the construct's feet. "Our journey here could hardly be described as relaxing or enjoyable, by any measure. I'm glad you're still maintaining order in the library. But I'm afraid the rest of the tower is not what you imagine it to be."

"Indeed, some guardians have lost contact with me," the Curator replied. "But Lady Aegwynn has promised to help repair the damaged guardians, return them to their duties, and re-establish contact with me. I trust that as the creator of this tower, she will arrange everything perfectly."

Khadgar paused. Jace whispered, "I thought you said you didn't go this high with Aegwynn?"

"We didn't." Khadgar looked up. "When did Lady Aegwynn make you that promise?"

"Forty-six days, seventeen hours, four minutes, and fifty-five seconds ago, Lady Aegwynn entered the Grand Library," the Curator answered. "Departure time: no record."

"That was after we got back to Stormwind," Jace said. "Before I went to Grim Batol?"

"Yes." Khadgar said. "When I came here with her to find the Telogus Rift coordinates, it was way before that."

Jace asked the Curator, "The two arcane constructs on the spiral staircase outside the Grand Library — did you send them to scout, Curator?"

"I know which constructs you refer to, Master Jace Sesso." The Curator answered calmly. "Those two constructs briefly lost control some time ago. The cause is still under investigation."

"Does that mean she's still in the tower?" Khadgar asked.

"If she was behind it — why would she do that?" Jace thought for a moment. "What's certain now is... after meeting me at the Pig and Whistle that day, she came straight back to Karazhan."

Khadgar studied Jace for a moment, looked around the empty exhibition hall, then looked up again. "Besides offering to repair the other arcane guardians, did Lady Aegwynn mention anything else?"

"No comment." The Curator said coldly.

"Did she go see Master Nielas Aran?" Jace asked again.

"No comment." The Curator said in the same tone.
