## Chapter 146: The Hunt!

Jace was starting to regret that rest break he'd taken halfway up. If he'd just toughened it up and kept going, he wouldn't have lost track of Grede.

But the bastard had basically lost his mind entirely — he'd been running the whole way like a startled rabbit, and the slightest delay meant falling way behind.

Jace lay flat on the ground and poked his head out, scanning the open ground for any sign of him. And, well… also checking for any dwarves lying face-down, that sort of thing.

The nearby clearing didn't have any standing dwarves, and it didn't have any prone dwarves either. Not even human corpses. Just two or three charred bodies by the river in the distance — probably unlucky soldiers who'd caught fire and tried to jump in the river to douse the flames, only to be cut down by the black dragon.

No soldiers were running over there anymore. Charging across open ground without cover was suicide. If you caught fire by accident, you ducked behind something and let your buddies stamp it out — not far off, a few soldiers were trampling away behind a wall at a comrade whose body was still smoking black.

Jace was drenched in sweat. He gripped his long staff tight, planning to wait for the dragon to circle back around and try hitting it with a Curse of Weakness when the moment was right.

At this point he'd given up all hope of scaring an actual dragon with Fear. The moment he'd come face-to-face with the thing, he'd realized trying to Fear it was a joke.

Thinking that, he quickly flipped open his spellbook and ran through the Curse of Weakness incantation one more time. This was way worse than facing down that black panther — he couldn't afford a single mistake. More importantly, he'd realized he couldn't keep his cool the way he had with the panther.

Around the black dragon, Jace could almost feel the shadow energy radiating off it, pressing down on his conscious mind, pushing him further and further toward panic.

If he hadn't been a shadow-trained mage, he wouldn't have been able to tell that the pressure was coming from the dragon's magic — he would've just thought he was scared.

But right now, Jace could feel his emotions being tugged in waves by the shadow energy pouring off the dragon's body. Those soldiers had to be feeling much the same.

This was probably what Sazeno had warned him about — that presence deliberately radiating dark power, the shadow creature circling in the distance…

As Jace was memorizing the incantation, he suddenly heard a rustling from the sentry tower's stone steps, not far behind the wall.

He listened closely. It was a stream of words in a language he couldn't understand at all — but he recognized the voice!

"Wait!" Jace hauled himself up onto the wall and looked over, just in time to see a short, barrel-shaped backside scrambling up the sentry tower's stairs, its back to him.

He shouted Grede's name again. No response whatsoever.

A clatter of loose rubble came from the tower — the black dragon seemed to have heard the commotion and shifted toward it. Jace immediately ducked back down to hide.

Goddammit… Grede, don't you dare go dying on me, wait up!

"Archers! Flank around back and drive him back here! Mages, hold position and wait for him to come to us — arcane energy isn't cutting it, switch to frost, hit him with frost magic! All arcane apprentices, get over there and help move the wounded, damn it!"

Some commander's voice rang out loud and clear — but then a deep, rumbling bass took everyone by surprise.

"Humans… think… I cannot understand?"

The black dragon let out a series of harsh, clicking laughs. It scrambled back up to the other side of the sentry tower, cutting itself off from the archers who were trying to flank around to coordinate with the mages. From that direction, a knight-captain shouted, "It's on the other side! Take cover! Everyone take cover!"

The dragon roared again. A shockwave blasted the air so hard it seemed to bend. Jace didn't see any flames, but he heard a violent explosion from the other side, followed by screams.

Jace left the wall and moved to the dragon's side, ducking into cover. When he looked up, he could see the black dragon's tail swaying back and forth right over his head.

He was ready to cast Curse of Weakness. He was also ready for it to fail completely and for the dragon to turn and breathe fire straight back at him.

His hand had already found the Shadow Protection Potion bottle. His heart was pounding right up into his throat…

"FOR KHAZ MODAN!"

The chaos of the battlefield screeched to a halt with that one roar. It was the only Dwarvish phrase Jace understood. He tore away from the wall and looked up, watching in disbelief as the little dwarf leaped off the top of the tower with his warhammer raised!

In a flash of crackling lightning, Grede slammed a thunderstrike straight into the dragon's skull!

A burst of white light, a deafening boom — and Grede didn't fall. The hammer's head had lodged itself in the dragon's uneven skull!

The dragon howled in agony and shuddered violently, shaking its head like mad, while Grede clung to the hammer handle for dear life, refusing to let go, swinging through the air like a ragged sack.

Hold on…

Jace burst from the wall, raised his staff high, and shouted at the dragon — "Anuniji, Durazala, Kana!"

His voice was completely drowned out by the dragon's roars, but all that mattered was that the shadow energy coiling around them could hear.

He didn't know if the Curse of Weakness had taken hold, but one of the dragon's claws suddenly loosened mid-shake, sliding down a good stretch — and that set off a chain reaction that brought down a huge section of the sentry tower's wall. The dragon couldn't hold on anymore. It crashed to the ground along with a cascade of broken bricks and rubble.

Jace leaped aside to dodge the flying stone and dust. The dragon collapsed against the broken wall, thrashing for a moment, trying to get back up. A nasty gash had opened across its head, and the purple-black fel blood pouring from it stained its whole neck.

"The dragon is down! Everyone with me! Kill it and save our dwarf brother! We'll mount its head in the town — defenders of Hillsbrad!"

The commander shouted the order, and soldiers with shortswords and spears came charging out from every direction. But where was Grede? Buried under the rubble?

The dragon shrieked and whipped its tail at the shield-bearing soldiers rushing toward it. Jace darted around its body and scrambled onto the pile of debris, where he found Grede — whether from being hit by falling stone or what, the dwarf was unconscious in a bed of gravel and sand.

Jace grabbed the two-hundred-pound dwarf, meaning to drag him out of the chaos first — when Grede suddenly coughed, spraying dust everywhere. The black dragon reacted like a switch had been flipped and whipped its head back around.

"Pathetic little maggot — still alive?" the dragon spat in broken Common. Its chest was already swelling, crimson fire climbing up toward its throat!

Shit!

Every hair on Jace's body stood on end. With a burst of strength he couldn't quite believe, he hauled Grede up bodily and hurled him into the still-standing remnant of the sentry tower wall.

Then he leaped after him… and the fireball detonated right behind him. The blast wave kicked him clean into the tower, where he crashed into a heap of battered tools, armor, and cloth — which at least cushioned the impact enough that nothing broke.

He patted out the flames on his trouser legs and checked the potions on his robe. A Mana Potion had shattered — no idea where the pieces had gone, just a few glass splinters stuck in the fabric. The other one seemed intact, and the Shadow Protection Potion was still good.

Grede was fully shielded by the wall and hadn't taken the brunt of the flames, but the soldiers outside — though they'd pressed the attack the moment the dragon turned — the fight didn't seem to be going well either.

The dragon was roaring like a mad thing. Even badly wounded, it wasn't something a few dozen hastily assembled soldiers and militia could handle easily. They needed more reinforcements!

Wasn't Southshore supposed to be Dalaran's staging ground south of the Alterac Mountains? Then where were all the mages? Where had the mages from last night gone — out to their day jobs, were they?

Damn it… Jace was trapped in this sentry tower. Once the dragon drove those soldiers back and found a moment to turn around and give him another blast, he'd be done for — along with Grede and the whole damn tower!

Jace slapped Grede's cheeks. "Grede! Wake up, come on, wake up!"

He realized his hands were shaking uncontrollably. That last effort of throwing Grede seemed to have drained something out of him — both his arms ached with a dull, deep soreness.

Grede cracked his eyes open, then coughed again. This time he spat up blood — and the blood was laced with purple shadow energy.

What the hell?

He'd been shadow-corroded?

If it wasn't treated urgently, shadow corrosion could cause permanent damage! Jace pulled the cork from the Shadow Protection Potion. He knew the potion could only block further ongoing damage, not heal what was already done — so it had to be used fast, no hesitation allowed.

He could not let Grede die.

Jace slapped him again to bring him around a little more, then pried open his lips and poured the potion in. Luckily the bastard still had his drinking reflex — even unconscious, when something harsh hit his tongue his mouth opened, and almost none of it spilled.

But what now? If that dragon attacked the tower again, what defense did he have left?

Nothing. Though Jace knew this potion wouldn't let him tank a fireball either. What's done is done.

There was no way he was risking a Mage Armor cast in a situation like this.

If he'd known he'd be dealing with something like this, he'd have practiced properly on the ship…

The real problem was that Grede's condition was looking grim. The shadow damage could be slowed with a potion, but what about the other internal injuries?

Since he was coughing up blood, he'd definitely been hurt in the fight — hit by falling stone, or slammed by the dragon, any of it could cause a fatal injury that didn't show on the outside.

Jace was out of options. He didn't know any healing spells. Was he just going to watch him get worse?

"No. There's one more method." Jace's panicked hands and feet slowly steadied. He pulled out the Mana Potion and took a swig, replenishing what he'd burned through casting Curse of Weakness and running all the way here.

The method was this — kill the dragon fast, so Grede could get treatment as soon as possible.

He flipped open his spellbook to the Life Drain entry and read through it silently once.

With Arcane Intellect active, he didn't need to go over it too many times.

He gripped his staff tight and walked to the doorway of the half-collapsed sentry tower, looking out at the black dragon — its back to him, breathing fire at the soldiers in front of it.

At his feet, half a dozen soldiers lay on the ground. Some were crawling away in pain. Some were dead or unconscious.

Let's see how you like getting drained.

He raised his staff high, pointed his other hand at the dragon, and shouted:

"Elamun, Silondor!"

Shadow magic flared from his staff, tearing through the ragged cloth wrapped around it. The dragon whipped its head around and locked its eyes on the warlock attacking it from behind — its wings, its foreclaws, all began to tremble violently.

"You… dare!"

It roared, swelling up another ball of flame — but Jace didn't flinch. He just shouted the incantation again, loud.

"Ngh—"

This time the dragon coughed violently, its swelling chest guttering out. A cloud of black smoke burst from between its teeth and engulfed its head.

Then the wound on its head split open without warning, spraying a jet of blood — and the blood didn't scatter into the sky. It all flew straight toward Jace, splashing across his body and the ground at his feet.

Jace didn't dodge either. He kept his focus locked. He knew the spell was still running, still channeling — he had to keep his concentration and drain this dragon dry!

He couldn't doubt his own power. Last time he'd doubted himself, his Mage Armor had collapsed halfway through. This time, even if he couldn't kill the thing, he had to believe he could.

Ringing ears replaced the dragon's roars. He felt his head and chest swelling tighter and tighter. A mouthful of blood came up and he vomited it out — but he didn't look away, just kept his eyes locked on the dragon's.

The dragon's scales dulled. Its already-damaged wing membrane shriveled. It seemed to be panicking now, cursing something — but Jace couldn't make out a word.

The dragon dug in its foreclaws, hauling itself up out of the rubble. Its lower body had been crushed by the collapsing wall and it could barely move — but it still pulled itself free entirely and lurched toward Jace.

It kept opening its mouth to roar, but to Jace it was all muted — until a spear suddenly drove into its lower leg.

The dragon roared, finally unable to hold itself up. Another spear. And another. Silver-armored soldiers bearing Lordaeron's crest charged in, driving their polearms into the gaps between the dragon's scales again and again.

The dragon let out a feeble, whimpering cry — and finally collapsed, crashing down right at Jace's feet. Its trembling eyes slowly closed.

Is it over?

Jace couldn't hear anything. Couldn't see anything. Just burning across his whole body and a wave of nausea from deep inside. He felt blood spots across his palms, his vision going red and blurred…

A surge of sickness rose up and he vomited a huge mouthful of purple ichor all over the dragon's head…

Only then did he start to feel better. Sound and vision came back, piece by piece.

He looked down dazedly and saw a crowd of Lordaeron soldiers rush in to hold him up. An officer shouted, "Both of these men took shadow damage from the black dragon — we need a priest, fast! Go get a priest! Plenty of Alliance soldiers defending Alterac were left permanently disabled because they didn't get treatment in time!"

"Priest! Priest!"

"The Wildhammer warrior's injuries are lighter — take the Dalaran mage back first!"

"He's not a Dalaran mage—"

"That's a Stormwind Mage Guild ring!"

"I don't care where he's from — you think I'm trying to get into the Kirin Tor? Move your ass, now!"
