Chapter 1463: Pointing to the Source of Chaos

The Curator stood before the enormous black owl statue, staring into its emerald magical eyes. Whether it was inspecting for damage or simply admiring, Jace couldn't tell.

Its figure was strikingly tall before the nearly two-story statue, yet thin and frail beneath the cavernous exhibition hall ceiling.

Jace knew that even setting aside the spiral stairs, the galleries, the theater, the grand banquet halls... just the height of this exhibition hall alone could take up most of the outer tower of Karazhan.

The more he traveled inside it, the more he felt that Karazhan Tower itself wasn't really a tower at all, but a portal to another dimension.

"How are you going to bring up Aegwynn with the Curator?" Jace asked.

"The question is how you're going to bring it up," Khadgar said. "Let me explain why the Curator is an excellent target. Arcane constructs are powered by arcane magic — their intelligence, their ability to act, all come from arcane energy. And when it records what it saw and thought in connection with Aegwynn, it inevitably collected some of Aegwynn's own mana. That's unavoidable."

"She has the same authority over it that Medivh had," Jace said. "Can't she just erase that?"

"Probably," Khadgar said. "But she didn't. We talked to the Curator earlier — it still remembers Aegwynn. Maybe she forgot, maybe she didn't care, or maybe she was worried that tampering with the Curator's records might cause unpredictable consequences. Either way, this is our chance."

"So, you talk to it. Get it to mention information related to Aegwynn as much as possible. I'll focus on collecting the traces of her presence." The mage rubbed his fingers together. "Collecting a construct's records is very different from collecting ordinary magical traces. I need to focus all my attention on capturing the arcane energy. Remember: try not to make it give you 'no comment.' A refusal from an arcane construct of this level is like a Counterspell — it'll have serious negative effects on recording magical traces."

"Did you study arcane constructs at the Wizard Sanctum? Have you ever communicated with other arcane constructs before?"

"Not that I know of," Jace said.

"Hmph." Khadgar said. "Can't be helped. At least you can still hold a conversation. Collecting arcane traces is something only I can do."

"Then don't expect too much," Jace said.

"Expect too much?" Khadgar said. "I'm hoping you get nothing useful from it. Did you think I was eager to see Aegwynn?"

Jace coughed into his hand, adjusted his cloak, and walked toward the Curator.

"Good morning, Curator." He looked up.

"It is currently nighttime, Mister Jace Sesso, Mister Khadgar. Good evening. How may I be of assistance?" The Curator rotated its upper body and replied calmly, its voice as resonant as ever.

"Could you tell me about Lady Aegwynn, Curator?" Jace asked.

The Curator's eyes flickered. "Aegwynn. Of Lordaeron. According to unreliable sources, born in the royal city in the 1,727th year of the founding of the Order of Tirisfal. Former Guardian of Tirisfal, former leader of the Order of Tirisfal."

Khadgar shook his head slightly, probably meaning this wasn't useful.

Jace could guess why: this information was extracted from the library's records, not related to Aegwynn's personal movements or traces.

"Forty-six days ago, Lady Aegwynn came to the library." Jace tried another angle. "Can you tell me if anything else happened in the library during that time?"

"No comment," the Curator replied.

"Anything unrelated to Lady Aegwynn," Jace tried.

This time the Curator fell silent, offering no response at all. Jace sensed something was wrong and decided not to press further on this topic.

Khadgar frowned slightly, lowering the hand he'd been preparing for his spell. This conversation was proving more complicated than they'd anticipated.

"Curator, are you still listening to me?" Jace asked.

"Good evening, Mister Jace Sesso, Mister Khadgar." The Curator stiffly returned to its original friendly tone. "How may I be of assistance?"

Khadgar's expression darkened. Jace felt he'd touched on something he shouldn't have. A few more rounds of "no comment" and things could get dangerous.

He thought back to his previous conversations with the Curator, knowing he had to change direction.

"About those two arcane constructs on the spiral staircase — have you found out why they lost contact, Curator?"

"Not yet," the Curator replied.

"Lady Aegwynn promised to help you repair the arcane constructs in this tower. Did she ever mention what might have caused the damage?" Jace asked again.

"Lady Aegwynn believed it was her responsibility," the Curator answered.

Jace raised his eyebrows at the mage beside him. Khadgar grew serious as well.

The Curator continued, "She believed she must repair the damage to the arcane constructs, to prevent the uncontrolled magic from continuing to affect the library."

Uncontrolled magic?

Jace immediately thought of Medivh's resurrection.

Could Aegwynn have tried to resurrect Medivh in Karazhan and something went wrong, causing the current state of the tower?

Jace worked his lips, organizing his words, trying not to trigger another "no comment."

He watched the energy fluctuations in the Curator's two arcane eyes and asked, "Do I have the authority to know what type of magic this 'uncontrolled' event involved?"

"No suitable authorization," the Curator replied.

It was a refusal, but the tone this time was relatively calm, and the magical fluctuations within its body were stable.

"Then, do I have the authority to know... the location of this so-called uncontrolled magic?" Jace tried the same tactic again.

"No relevant records, Mister Jace Sesso," the Curator replied.

"No records? Why?" Jace asked.

"It may be because the source of this uncontrolled event did not occur within the Grand Library, the area under the Curator's protection," the Curator replied.

"Thank you for telling me, Curator," Jace responded.

Beside him, Khadgar had already closed his eyes, presumably focused on capturing the traces of arcane energy. This direction of conversation was working.

Jace felt he'd gotten somewhere. He considered for a moment and continued, "From what I understand, Lady Aegwynn has suspended her repair work. So Khadgar hopes that he and I can take over from Lady Aegwynn and complete the restoration of the arcane constructs in Karazhan Tower."

"Mister Khadgar was an apprentice of Karazhan, and Mister Jace Sesso is Mister Khadgar's good friend. The Curator welcomes all friendly assistance..." The Curator's tone had noticeably softened.

"Since we're the repairmen now," Jace tried, "can you tell us the locations of all the faulty arcane constructs?"

"Then..." The Curator's arcane eyes flickered several times, then suddenly turned cold. "Access denied."

"Got it." Khadgar opened his eyes, grasping a wisp of arcane energy in his palm.

"Thank you for your help, esteemed Curator." He bowed to the Curator. But the Curator gave no response, simply rotating its upper body back around.

"That's it?" Jace said in surprise. "I thought we'd hit another dead end."

"I told you — I'm one of the best at this," Khadgar said. "But you did well too, Jace. For someone who's never spoken to an arcane construct before, you're no apprentice mage."

Jace said nothing, following the mage as he hurried quickly through the exhibition hall back toward the main library, then asked, "So the Curator is saying that all the chaos in Karazhan right now was caused by Aegwynn?"

"It seems so. And it sounds like she didn't intend for this to happen." Khadgar turned back. "If she really did this, what do you think that uncontrolled magic was originally meant to do?"

Jace's lips moved, but in the end he just said, "We'll know once we find the source."

Back in the library, Khadgar cast a spell at the stairs and halls before them. But after carefully examining the area for a long time, he just sighed in frustration.

He stared at the faint wisp of golden arcane energy in his hand and muttered to himself, "Nothing. Did I capture it wrong? That's impossible..."

"The Curator said that uncontrolled magic didn't reach the library, so the arcane constructs here are all normal," Jace said. "Maybe we'd have better luck leaving the library?"

"Possibly." The mage nodded. "Let's go to the old archives section. Pick up your Lady Morofil."

The two of them retraced their steps into the old archives guarded by Aran's shadow to rendezvous with Morofil, then found the door leading to the spiral staircase. It turned out to be just a few bookshelves and a corner away — no more than a few minutes' walk.

Such a short distance, yet earlier it had felt like crossing mountains and seas, fighting through every step...

Carefully pushing open the door and stepping onto the spiral stairs, Jace kept quiet, checking the stairs above and below, listening for sounds. Satisfied there were no rogue arcane constructs patrolling, he turned back to look at Khadgar.

But the mage was already standing still, staring up at the floor above, motionless.

"What's wrong?" Jace asked quietly. "Still no traces?"

"Quite the opposite," Khadgar said. "There are extremely obvious traces everywhere."

"The flow of arcane energy is very clear, divided into several... probably from going back and forth, casting different types of magic. It goes from here all the way up."

"I thought you said Aegwynn was very cautious?" Jace asked.

"I don't know, Jace. Right now, I don't know anymore." Khadgar murmured.

"So... do you still want to follow the traces and go up there to have a look?" Jace asked.

"Of course. Haven't you noticed? Our goals have merged into one." Khadgar said. "I want to repair the arcane constructs and have them help me restore order to this tower. And right now, it looks like the source of this severe chaos... is Lady Aegwynn's magic — which is exactly what you want to know."
