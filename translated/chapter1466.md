## Chapter 1466: Mistress, or Prisoners (Part 1)

"Ulthalesh will devour you soon, Jace Sesso. The pain won't last long." The sword-wielder's voice sounded in his ear like a voice from a dream, as if time itself had stopped.

"There is nothing in the entire Twisting Nether that can compete with it for your soul. That is why only resealing this staff can save this world from the darkness you spread. Every other method is wishful thinking, nothing but—"

"Yukroni, Morgak, Tam..." Jace chanted the Shadow Cleansing spell under his breath, shattering the hazy trance. Ulthalesh's demonic eyes oozed purple-red demonic blood.

The shadow protection the priest had granted the sword-wielder was dismantled by Jace, and the gaping wound on his chest and abdomen was rapidly repaired by the shadow energy he siphoned.

With this stolen shadow energy, Jace condensed a Shadow Bolt in his hand at almost instantaneous speed, blasting it into the figure to knock her back half a step. At the same time, through his soul link, he issued a command to his partner.

Morofil felt Jace's intent through the soul link. Spreading her wings like a phantom, she appeared behind the mage figure who was channeling ice magic.

A mist of frost suddenly exploded from the mage, forming a Frost Nova. Morofil swept the frost aside with her wings, shattering the incoming ice shards, and thrust Apocalypse viciously at the mage. She expected the strike to burn straight through the illusion, but it met a thick layer of shadow protection instead.

The priest-like figure kept chanting, slowing Morofil's rhythm while shielding her mage companion. The mage figure cooperated perfectly — she didn't try to tangle with Morofil, instead seeking an escape, aiming to keep attacking the warlock.

Jace understood clearly: while they wanted to focus on one target, the three on the other side wanted to eliminate him as quickly as possible.

But Morofil's fast attack had bought him enough time for a short spell.

Jace casually cast Immolate at the fawn in the distance. The fawn cried out in pain a couple of times before reverting to its original form!

Khadgar, freed from Polymorph and before even putting out the flames burning on his body, immediately pointed at the mage figure. But just as he began to cast, he shifted his finger slightly to another direction and cast an Arcane Silence!

The mage figure disintegrated on the spot, but from the empty space he'd been pointing at, a different figure suddenly appeared, doubling over in pain.

"Don't underestimate me, Lady Aegwynn!" he shouted in annoyance.

Jace immediately chanted a Demon Empowerment spell. Morofil's body erupted in flames, her size increasing dramatically. Spreading her wings, she shook off the Slow the mage had just placed on her and the dark torments from the shadow priest in the distance. Like a purple-red meteor, she crashed into the silenced mage figure.

Khadgar channeled a ball of burning flame — a Pyroblast big enough to light up most of the Observatorium in red...

Would that be enough to take out the mage?

The sword-wielder turned and raised a hand, making a gesture, chanting a Counterspell at Khadgar on the high platform. So this figure was indeed a warrior — but also a mage!

The Counterspell was simple, fast — but it couldn't shake Khadgar at all.

The spell meant to disrupt arcane energy only rippled across the archmage in waves of colorful magic, identical to the elixir Khadgar had just drunk.

Could his enhanced resistance elixir even counter Counterspell? That was too powerful.

Seeing the quick Counterspell fail, the sword-wielder immediately started channeling a stronger one. She'd caught him off guard the first time — no way he'd let her try again.

Jace lashed out with a line of blood from his hand, snagging her gesture, yanking hard. Though he couldn't pull the sword-wielder to him, he broke her casting rhythm.

The Grasp of the Blood Mage nearly twisted the figure's arm with its tearing force, but she shook it off, and the illusion quickly recovered. Still, the Counterspell was thoroughly interrupted.

"I'm not dead yet." Jace stared into the warrior's arcane-glimmering phantom eyes. "Didn't you say you'd kill every wizard who came knocking? Or are you just a weak fragment of her...?"

The sword-wielder's face twisted instantly, her rage erupting. Her whole body grew redder, her form suddenly swelling.

Fighting Fear with Berserker Rage? But I'm no ordinary warlock.

Jace seized hold of this surge of fury. As the sword-wielder dissolved into arcane and lunged at him again, he used the rage as fuel, burning out a Fear suppression even stronger than before through Ulthalesh's power.

The shadow's roar was now indistinguishable from a terror's howl or a rage-filled scream. The sword-wielder's crimson body turned purple, then black. Under this suppression, she staggered mid-charge, her rhythm broken. She couldn't land a killing blow on Jace. She fell to one knee, letting out unwilling sobs.

At the same time, Khadgar's Pyroblast had already been hurled. It exploded against the mage in a burst of white light, followed by crimson flames and thick smoke. The whole hall shook with it. The telescope and its chains swayed, tables and chairs were blown away, bookshelves toppled, and the paintings on the walls flapped loudly...

Morofil plunged straight into the explosion cloud, planning to use this chance to destroy the figure completely. Though Jace couldn't see clearly what was happening inside from his peripheral vision, the emotions coming through the soul link suggested... it wasn't going well.

A flicker of golden light on the other side caught his attention.

It was the shadow priest... no, at this point, she seemed to be more than just a shadow priest. Both her hands were channeling golden light, and another patch of golden radiance erupted from the heart of the Pyroblast, blasting Morofil back. The mage at the center, protected by this golden light, had weathered the full assault and survived!

The light was so dazzling that even Jace felt his face burning.

Had Aegwynn not only sought help from Tyr's priests, but also tried to wield the Holy Light herself? Or did some of the mages in the Order of Tirisfal also command the Light...

"The Holy Light stands against you this time, Jace Sesso..." The sword-wielding Aegwynn forced down the fear, lifting her head and struggling to choke out a few words through trembling breath. "Doesn't this prove my decision is the right one?"

The mage, surrounded by Holy Light, raised both arms and suddenly hurled a massive arcane lightning strike across the entire hall. Pale electric light drove back the Pyroblast's flames, nearly blinding everyone. And all those lightning bolts converged on a single target — Jace.

Khadgar tried a Counterspell immediately, but the arcane disruption couldn't penetrate the Holy Light's protection. Jace could only watch as the arcane lightning came for him...

He'd already been run through — the body damage repaired in time, but his condition was severely weakened. The mage figure saw victory within reach. Protected by the priest's Holy Light, she ignored Khadgar and Morofil's attacks as if bloodlust had taken her, channeling lightning to finish Jace off while he was weak.

But Jace saw something in the arcane lightning crawling along the walls, floor, and pillars.

These Aegwynn figures couldn't tap into Karazhan's terrible magic the way Nielas Aran could. And remembering what the sword-wielder had said earlier — "cage"...

Could they be trapped here by some magic?

If the mage figure couldn't draw on the tower's power against him, then maybe... just maybe he could weather this lightning.
