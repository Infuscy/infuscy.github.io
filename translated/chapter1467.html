Chapter 1467: Mistress, or Prisoners (Part 2)

"Stop wasting time! Kill the one channeling the Holy Light!" He raised a Dark Shield and shouted at Khadgar. "Kill the priest, Khadgar. Don't hesitate. Now!"

Khadgar had been about to channel magic to help Jace block the powerful arcane lightning, but hearing this, he could only grit his teeth and turn his attention to the priest channeling the Holy Light's protection.

Morofil was even faster than Jace's words. By the time he'd finished speaking, she was already back at the priest's side.

The priest — who'd been utterly calm from start to finish — finally showed a flicker of emotional disturbance. Unable to dissolve into shadow and retreat in time, she had to interrupt her channel and hastily raise a shield to protect herself. The next second, Morofil shattered it with a single strike!

Just as Jace had predicted: a Holy Light shield strong enough to withstand both a full-force assault from a demon like Morofil and a fully-powered Pyroblast from a mage like Khadgar must be an incredibly powerful protective spell. The priest would need undisturbed space and time to focus on channeling it, and the cost would be enormous — she wouldn't be able to cast a second one anytime soon.

So when she realized Jace had directed both Morofil and Khadgar at her, she finally showed a hint of panic.

By protecting the mage, she'd spent her last card.

And as long as she was interrupted, the mage would lose her protection. As long as Khadgar reacted fast enough, Jace would be freed...

Sure enough, Khadgar moved faster than Jace's thoughts. Seizing the moment the mage lost her protection, he bellowed a spell, channeling immense arcane power into a Silence that slammed into the mage, cutting off her casting. The arcane lightning in the hall collapsed and dissipated, and that figure flickered violently, as if on the verge of dissolution.

But Khadgar hadn't forgotten what Jace told him to do. He didn't press the attack on the mage while she was cut off from arcane energy. Instead, he kept channeling a dense volley of arcane bolts, hammering them with pinpoint accuracy into the priest's shield and even her spectral body. Because without taking out this priest, attacking the mage was just a waste of time and energy. There was absolutely no way he was going to give this priest time to catch her breath.

At the same time, Morofil didn't let up. Her demonic runes blazed as if ignited, and with the lingering Demon Empowerment from Jace, she kept pursuing the priest, stripping away her protection with Apocalypse.

Facing one Kirin Tor archmage and one demon-hunting banshee pressing her from both sides, the priest could barely hold on. Her shadow form grew fainter. She couldn't hold out much longer.

The mage figure released waves of blue light, as if conducting a Mana Evocation, or fighting the Silence, trying to reconnect with the arcane.

And the sword-wielder's form was growing brighter. Jace could feel she was about to break free of the Fear suppression.

But Jace hadn't been idle either. This had bought him enough time to summon a second felhound.

"Dazarm the First, take out the one with the crystal staff!" Jace shouted in demonic.

The felhound successfully crawled through the void rift and landed. At the same time, the sword-wielding Aegwynn figure finally broke free of the Fear suppression.

Seeing the foul beast from the twisted rift charging toward the silenced mage, she rose in fury. Flames like a dragon's maw burned around her body, and she spat scorching breath at Jace, triggering a massive explosion...

Jace was caught off guard by the sudden blast that came with almost no casting motion. He barely had time to grab his cloak before being blown backward into a pile of chairs.

Dragon's Breath?!

If this had hit an ordinary mage — even an archmage — they'd have been either killed instantly or knocked unconscious.

But this was Jace Sesso, wearing the Hide of Alastasthaz, having just drunk a Multi-Resistance Elixir, with the blood of the red dragon flowing through his veins. Even he had to admit the flames were somewhat agonizing...

The sword-wielder thought the blast had at least driven Jace back, buying her room to kill the felhound. But she hadn't taken two steps before a shadow chain lashed around her neck.

She staggered, breaking the chain, but by then the felhound was already far away.

Jace stared at her from within the burning Dragon's Breath, chanting spells, raising both hands to keep casting shadow magic to restrain her movements. Seeing she couldn't stop the felhound, with the priest being harried by Morofil and multiple Khadgar images, the figure's energy growing weaker and weaker, the sword-wielder's fury reached its peak.

But the angrier she got, the easier it was for Jace to control her with shadow magic. Curse of Weakness, Curse of Agony, Fear, Suffocation — one after another.

Jace had no intention of killing her. This warrior-figure was too resilient. But as long as he could keep this warrior pinned in front of him, the priest would eventually crumble under the pressure.

But just as he was struggling to suppress this warrior, the mage figure seemed to have finally weathered Khadgar's Silence.

Before the felhound could get close, the mage figure instantly split into multiple images. One of them even produced a pale blue magic blade and stabbed toward the felhound.

Though the felhound easily devoured that image, another took the opportunity to freeze the demon in place with frost.

Jace hadn't brewed any Elixir of Intellect this time, nor did he have enough summoning time. The felhound summoned in haste was far weaker than Dazarm the Sixth, who'd been killed by Aran. And Aegwynn — even a mere shadow of her — clearly had extensive experience dealing with felhounds.

After the images subdued the felhound, one of them quickly retreated toward where Khadgar and Morofil were ganging up on the priest. Amid the chaotic electric light, arcane missiles, and dancing shadows, Jace spotted a slightly different shadow — that was the mage's true body.

But the felhound was trapped. If this mage got back to pinning down Khadgar and Morofil, they'd be back to two-on-two, stuck in a war of attrition. The window of opportunity they'd fought so hard to create against the priest... would slip right through their fingers again.

Could they find another chance like this?

A warlock, a mage, a flying demon rogue... against a warrior, a mage, and a priest. They couldn't afford an attrition war. If this focused assault didn't take out the priest, there was no next round.

This wasn't like fighting the ghosts and spirits outside. Other than the fire spell that had burned off Khadgar's Polymorph, almost every spell Jace cast was at full power, grabbing every opening in the frantic rhythm. He didn't hold back on Shadowstep for dodging attacks either. That was the only way to hold his own against Aegwynn's shadows, but the mana drain was enormous.

This chance had to be seized.

"Kiorili, Felaos, Pakumili."

Jace chanted the spell decisively, melting the felhound's soul — which was between him and the mage — into a burning demonic skull that flew through the air, chasing straight after the mage's true body.

The mage didn't seem to sense the Death Coil coming from behind. She raised both hands toward Morofil, probably chanting a control spell. But the demonic skull was already at the back of her head...

Suddenly, the mage figure encased herself in solid ice, layer upon layer of frost stacking thicker and thicker. Jace's Death Coil was about to smash uselessly into the unbreakable ice shell...

At the last moment, he wrenched the burning skull upward, redirecting it toward the priest figure while the mage was frozen by her own ice magic!

The mage figure realized something was wrong and shattered the ice around her. But by then, it was too late.

The priest figure was already at her limit under Khadgar and Morofil's assault. When she realized the warrior was pinned by Jace and the mage couldn't help in time — and then a soul-converted fel spell came flying at her face — her already shaky composure finally collapsed.

The burning demonic skull slammed straight into her, erupting in a horrifying fel conflagration. The priest figure screamed and collapsed to her knees. Morofil drove Apocalypse through her heart, and in a strange flash of light, the figure dissolved into golden arcane starlight that scattered into the dome's constellations...

Jace lowered his hands and turned to the sword-wielder, still pinned under layers of shadow. "You've lost. Further fighting is pointless."

"Just another weakling eliminated." The sword-wielding Aegwynn's voice echoed around them. "Like all the others who came seeking help, trying to destroy this tower, or wanting to enter the library..."

She gathered her breath and shouted, breaking free of the shadow magic Jace had been neglecting to maintain while focusing on the Death Coil.

The tall female mage figure raised her blade, about to launch some kind of blink-charge for a killing blow on Jace — when suddenly needles of arcane energy locked her entire body. She froze mid-charge in that awkward pose.

With the priest figure gone, the mage figure had been overwhelmed by Khadgar and Morofil, vanishing without a trace. That freed Khadgar to channel far more powerful magic undisturbed, pinning the warrior figure completely.

Jace walked up and raised his staff. "How did you find out?"

The sword-wielder laughed coldly, then dissolved into golden starlight, destroying herself and breaking Khadgar's hold.

After the last figure vanished, the Observatorium rumbled with a deep, dull roar.

Jace stood frozen for a moment, then collapsed to the ground, coughing up black blood. The sword that had pierced him earlier hadn't been fully repaired by shadow energy.

He'd held on through the battle, but once it was over, he couldn't anymore.

Morofil stood to the side, holding her upper abdomen, her expression grim. She'd taken a nasty hit too.

"Jace, are you alright?"

Khadgar ran over and handed him a potion.

Jace drank the healing potion, his whole body tingling with intense itching, but at least he had some feeling back.

He took a few breaths and said, "Sorry, Khadgar. I never came clean with you."

"I can't say I never suspected... the possibility." Khadgar said. "Maybe my reverence for the ancient Guardians made me think there was no way you could have gotten something Scavell had so carefully hidden. But you found it."

"By the way, you've really never been to the Gurubashi Arena?"

"If you must ask." Jace sighed. "I've been to Durnholde. Watched a few matches."

"You learned all this from Blackmoore's orc circus?" Khadgar frowned.

"I hate to break up this victory celebration, you two, but can this tower hold?" Morofil looked up at the rumbling dome. "It looks like it's about to fall apart."

"No, quite the opposite." Khadgar looked at the demon. "The tower's order is being restored, little by little."

"Then what about those figures?" Jace asked, holding his chest.

"I have some guesses, but I can't be sure yet." Khadgar turned back to look directly under the telescope. Only then did Jace notice there was a chaotic magic circle there — he'd been standing too low on the platform when they entered and hadn't seen the pattern at all.

"Look. Uncontrolled magic, Jace. I just noticed it. We need to go see what that thing is."
