Chapter 1472: The Storeroom

It was hard to say which was more difficult — deciphering the contents recorded in the Chronicle of Dark Secrets, or understanding the Nathrezim language.

Jace wasn't new to the dreadlords' tongue. Dathsmora had used their language to record clues about Usaalas, and some of the spells he'd encountered before contained obscure Nathrezim vocabulary too — like the necromantic scroll that orc death knight Angor Deathwish had left behind in the Riverpaw forest camp.

But this was the first time he'd actually tried to read the Nathrezim language, and he was already considering summoning Sazeno here to help.

The reason he hadn't originally planned to bring the imp into Karazhan was because Khadgar had warned him that if Sazeno used Phase Shift in the tower, he might shift himself somewhere completely unknown.

Mor'fel's shadow concealment wasn't as thorough as the imp's hiding in most places, but in Karazhan it was a definite advantage.

Still, Khadgar had said that after clearing out the shadows Aegwynn had trapped here, Karazhan's mana wasn't as chaotic anymore. So maybe he could summon the imp inside now?

Even if the golem guards didn't allow any demons into the library, he could just take the book outside for the imp to look at — like this Chronicle.

But after a night of intermittent reading, he was growing increasingly doubtful about whether the legendary tales in this grimoire would actually be useful, especially for his mastery of Dark Simulacrum.

"We found a filthy, parasite-ridden planet deep in the barren universe. We pretended to submit to the parasite's will, eventually turning it into our puppet. We turned that puppet's insides and soul inside out for the Pantheon's emissary to see. We urged him to drive his greatsword into it. We showed him the truth of the Void with the splattered gray blood."

Jace couldn't quite imagine what Medivh — or Sargeras himself — must have thought when he read these "dark secrets."

But measuring immortal gods by a mortal's understanding was probably a waste of effort, much like his attempt to use this storybook to master more shadow spells.

Compared to those stories, Jace was more interested in the methods the Order of Tirisfal's linguists had devised for interpreting the dreadlords' ancient language.

Parts of the language were easier to parse using demonic logic; other parts had to be swapped into Common, or even Draconic... He ended up with a pile of language reference books at his side. At least his understanding of the language had improved considerably over the course of the night.

But looking at it from another angle, for the rest of his time in Karazhan, even if he couldn't thoroughly study Dark Simulacrum, he could at least study the dreadlords' language more.

After all, from the news Krasus had relayed from Northrend, the Lich King's movements had already advanced further. So what about the dreadlords who, under Kil'jaeden's orders, were nominally "assisting" but actually monitoring the Lich King?

"Jace Sesso!" A mage's shout came from above. Khadgar stood on the upper level, yelling down. "Are you asleep? Come try again!"

"Coming, coming!" Jace set down the grimoire and walked over to the mage. "Can you get the Curator to let demons into the library?"

"And then what — let you copulate with a succubus in the greatest repository of knowledge in all of Azeroth?" Khadgar asked. "What strange ritual is this? The dual pleasure of forbidden knowledge and slick flesh, seeking the thrill of breaking rules under the watchful gaze of an arcane golem? Sounds like some kind of Sayad priest's evil sacrifice."

Jace coughed. "That's not it at all. I want to summon my imp in here to help me read books. 'Sayad evil sacrifice,' 'breaking rules,' 'multiple sensations of forbidden knowledge and slick flesh' — I mean, honored Archmage Khadgar, what exactly do you usually read in this library?"

"Ah, I see." Khadgar put on a serious expression. "I'll need to look into that further. First, try asking the Curator if he's willing to take you to the books you want."

Jace nodded skeptically and followed the mage to the exhibition hall.

The towering Curator still stood there silently, looking unchanged on the surface.

"Hello, honored Curator."

"Hello, Mr. Jace Sesso. What can I help you with?"

"Check my full access privileges in the Grand Library."

"Stop!" Khadgar shouted at the Curator. "Suspend operation, Mr. Curator!"

The arcane glow throughout the golem dimmed. The mage looked at Jace. "Why did you ask that directly?"

Jace hesitated. "I wanted to see what I could ask—"

"Ask what you actually want to ask, Jace. Don't ask around randomly." Khadgar said seriously.

"Uh." Jace nodded. "Got it. But are you sure you can fully control the Curator?"

"It's only been a day and a night, Jace. Don't worry. Improving the condition of this tower and the entire region won't happen overnight." Khadgar cleared his throat and fixed his gaze on the Curator. "Continue."

Jace studied Khadgar's expression, then carefully chose his words one at a time. "I want... to find... the records of Masters Aegwynn and Medivh's research... on black dragon magic. Mr. Curator, can you take me to... that area? Is that permitted?"

"Of course, Mr. Jace Sesso." The Curator replied readily.

"Alright, that should be no joke this time," Jace muttered.

"Don't waste words on useless things," Khadgar reminded quietly.

The Curator rotated its upper body, glancing at a much smaller guard golem by the door. That golem immediately walked over to the two men.

"Please follow me, Mr. Jace Sesso." The guard golem spoke in the exact same voice and tone as the Curator. "The passage to that area cannot accommodate my passage. I must temporarily borrow a more convenient vessel."

Khadgar wore a smug expression that clearly said "see if I didn't get it done for you."

Jace watched the arcane golem walk step by step into the side passage of the exhibition hall, feeling more and more like the Curator was becoming an Azerothian version of the Terminator.

The Curator's smaller body led them through a corridor to a T-junction, stopping before a wall of shelves.

Jace glanced at both passages, then back at where they'd come from, looking up at the shelves labeled "Dwarves, Earthen, and Creator Ruins." "Is this it? How—"

He stopped mid-sentence, suddenly realizing the scene felt familiar. Wasn't there a secret—

The golem suddenly raised its hand and chanted some kind of password. The shelf slid aside as expected, revealing a narrow, sinister passage that reeked of shadow and fel energy.

The lighting inside wasn't arcane — it was bright shadowfire. Jace stepped in and saw row after row of shadowfire braziers stretching into an unseeable depth.

"I never knew the library had a place like this." Khadgar said in surprise. "I'll have to ask the Curator later what else in this library apprentices aren't supposed to know."

Jace used demon detection to glance into the darkness ahead, confirmed no demons were waiting for him, then followed the passage down.

At the end, around a corner, he came to a hall lit by countless candles burning with purple-red flame.

At the center of the hall was a dark ritual circle drawn with the congealed blood of something unknown. Outside the high windows, a blood-red sky was visible, as if this place was no longer in the same plane as the Grand Library.

The bookshelves stood behind the ritual circle, as if there was no concern at all that the dark magical energy might damage the pages.

But from what Jace could observe, it was hard to say which was contaminating which.

If he wasn't mistaken, this was where the satyr lord Terestian Illhoof hid in the game.

"See?" Khadgar spread his hands. "I told you back at the Tower of Ilgalar, Jace — follow me to Karazhan and you'll definitely find what you're looking for. Still, I hope you'll focus on consulting materials and not conduct any terrifying magical experiments... at least not until I've fully taken control of the library."

Jace picked up a grimoire titled Dragon Soul, looking at its dark gold cover. "I understand, Khadgar. Don't worry."

"Then I'll withdraw the guard golem so you can summon your demonic servant in here to help." The mage put his hands on his hips and sighed, then suddenly remembered something and raised a finger in addition. "Mr. Sazeno only."

"Understood." Jace nodded.

Not long after Khadgar left, a pair of burning eyes peered in from the entrance of the hall.

"Anyone else here, darling?" Mor'fel asked with a smile.

"There will be in a second." Jace walked to the center of the ritual circle.

"Who?" Mor'fel's smile vanished instantly. She looked past Jace at the piles of books and towering shelves, lowering her voice. "Damn it. Don't tell me it's that yellow lump."

"The very same." Jace smiled and began chanting the imp summoning spell. The irritated succubus walked over and plopped down onto a stack of books, looking away.

"Hey? Where's this? What, master... what kind of place did you call me to?! Ha, Lady Mor'fel, good evening!"

"It's morning, you never-shut-up little bastard!"

Amid the two demons' shouting, Jace couldn't help glancing at the entrance, wondering if any Violet Eye spies or mercenary squads might burst in.

He'd have to remember to kill the healer first.
