## Chapter 1474: The Tower Master's Secret?

Dark Simulacrum. Jace had never seen that name in this world. Before this, he'd never heard demons or black dragons refer to the ability to mimic or even distort others' spells as "Dark Simulacrum."

Because it was a skill name from the game in his *previous* world. The same magic didn't necessarily have the same name here — like Doomhammer... that was just the name of a fel volcano in Shadowmoon Valley around here; no one had formally used it to name that dark meteor spell.

So seeing the information he'd been thinking about constantly — but had never encountered in this life — appear in his hands, Jace felt like he'd transmigrated all over again.

He half-expected that right-clicking the book in front of him would make it glow gold and prompt "You have learned a new spell: Dark Simulacrum."

Of course, things were nowhere near that simple. Jace hadn't flipped through more than a few pages before he realized that Medivh hadn't actually learned this spell from the black dragons either. He'd just taken the abilities he'd heard about and witnessed black dragons using, then reverse-engineered them, tried to replicate them, and recorded the process in this book.

He tried flipping further back. Medivh had made considerable progress. Although he wasn't entirely sure he'd mastered the exact same ability as the black dragons, by his own judgment, it was close enough.

But between the lines, Jace could feel that Medivh didn't seem to think much of the spell's "power." Still, he admitted the spell was quite "interesting," hence worth studying.

For Medivh, imitating a mortal's magic naturally had no "power" to speak of. But for Jace, it was still an advantage he couldn't overlook.

The spell would catch enemies off guard. Even though Jace knew in advance that black dragons had this kind of trick, he'd still been caught by it at the top of the Seat of Kil'jaeden.

More importantly, he remembered that Sabellian had copied his spell while continuing to attack with fire... In a way, that was almost like fighting two opponents at once.

Jace flipped back to the first page, ready to read Medivh's notes from beginning to end and try to learn this magic.

"Master! This is truly a secret tome hidden deep in the terrible astral wizard's mansion! Without me, you might have spent much more effort finding it! So, um..."

Sazeno came to Jace's feet. Jace nodded, pulled a soul shard from his pocket, and handed it to him. "Use it well."

"Thank you, great master!" The imp yelled with a sob in his voice. "This is actually a powerful demon soul shard!"

"Use it to replenish your energy and help me organize the Nathrezim language in the *Chronicle of Dark Secrets*." Jace said. "Like I just said."

"Ha ya — I thought master had forgotten!" The imp's brow drooped. Nearby, Mor'fel leaned against a stack of books, propping her cheek on her hand with a cold smile.

The imp immediately pointed at her. "What about her?!"

"You too, Mor'fel." Jace pointed at the *Chronicle*. "You know the Nathrezim so well."

"Uh? Hah!" Mor'fel stood up and sighed. "I'm a succubus."

Jace watched her move to the other side with the imp, then turned his gaze to the spot behind her where the strange bookshelf had appeared — visible only in shadow vision but now gone.

So even in this secret storeroom, which required the Curator to open, Medivh hadn't put his real dark magic research out in the open.

Was shadow... or emotion... the key here, unlocking Karazhan's true dark magic vault?

Thinking this, Jace set the *Dark Simulacrum* book aside for now.

He summoned sorrow again, pushing out other emotions as much as possible. Waves of shadow energy swirled outward from around him. The candlelight flickered, illuminating the shelf suspended above the shelves once more.

Jace caught his breath and released that emotion. Then he tried to recall that day in Southshore — the black dragon crossing the sky, the gryphon falling from the rooftop.

A surge of anger rose from within. He seized that fury with his consciousness, savoring it again and again, until he fully let go of the sorrow.

When he opened his eyes, a bookshelf that seemed to be *burning* had appeared on the other side.

Just as he'd thought. Medivh had hidden many books here using different types of shadow energy. As long as you could mobilize different types of shadow like casting a spell, you could see the corresponding shelves.

Jace felt the excitement of prying open Medivh's "memory box." But that excitement dispelled some of the anger, making the bookshelf less distinct.

He recalled Kil'jaeden taking the form of his mother, using the Deceiver's cold but "everything under control" expression to crush the excitement, letting anger retake the high ground. The scorching shadow cyclone continued to bake the entire room, and the bookshelf became clear again.

This time he walked straight to the shelf and tried to reach out and touch the grimoires on it. Unlike the water-weed sensation before, this was more like stirring burned debris in a fire. The shadow scorched his hand with sharp pain, and whatever he touched felt charred and brittle, ready to crumble at the slightest touch — he couldn't grab anything at all.

It wasn't hard to deduce: to get these books, he needed to adjust his emotions again like he had with *Dark Simulacrum* — "materialize" the book inside, seize the one he wanted from the magical shelf before it dissolved along with the dissipation of the previous emotion.

His excitement had already proven unable to "manifest" any of these books. So he quickly shifted to another emotion, letting intense fear swallow the anger. The emotions that had found *Dark Simulacrum* weren't pure — they were all intense — so this time he chose an equally intense approach.

But the gloom and chill of fear only extinguished the shadowfire, making the shelf rapidly fade. Still, Jace wasn't discouraged. He could feel that by continuously adjusting — like turning the dial of a combination safe — he could keep probing...

So he let anger burn again, making the shelf reappear, then tried to recall that day on Loch Modan Lake — the starry sky, Vereesa...

*THUMP!*

Suddenly, all the candles in the room went out at once. Darkness swallowed everything.

What happened?

"Huh?" Sazeno looked up in surprise. Mor'fel stared warily at the piles of candles. Jace released all his shadow and grabbed Usaalas, which was resting on the physical bookshelf beside him.

Within seconds, the candles lit up again. But they no longer burned with shadowfire — just ordinary flame.

The entire room quickly shed its former strange void-like atmosphere. Even the ritual circle on the floor looked less sinister. The room felt almost warm.

Then, from the passage outside the storeroom, music began to sound without warning — trumpets, accompanied by horns and even drums, growing closer. It was an utterly cheerful, excited march.

Jace and both demons stared blankly at the entrance. A troop of gnome ghosts carrying long trumpets, short horns, and all kinds of instruments marched in, beating drums and playing in neat formation.

The leader, carrying a small flag, glanced at Jace and yelled, "UP!" The gnomes behind him sang in unison:

"In the magic tower, quiet's broken,
A little thief's bad spell has spoken!
Thought you'd steal and get away,
But now you're caught, and here you'll stay!"

"Little thief, you think you're clever,
In Karazhan, you'd best be cleverer!
If you don't start behaving right—"

One gnome ghost came right up to Jace, raised his trumpet, and blasted it in his face. "We'll teach you what you should've learned tonight!"

After singing, the gnome band continued playing as they circled the ritual hall once, then filed out the door with stern faces, eventually disappearing into the depths of the corridor.

"What... what was that?" Sazeno asked.

"If you don't start behaving right." Mor'fel glanced sideways at her master, softly singing along to the gnomes' melody. "Our master will—"

"I've got it." Jace swallowed hard, looked up at the ceiling, and whispered, "Sorry, Master Medivh."
