Chapter 1480: Return to the Dark Portal

Some Expedition officers and mages who'd returned from Draenor were still stationed at Stonard and Nethergarde Keep. They were there partly to help the draenei cooperate with the Alliance, and partly to keep watch over the Dark Portal — making sure nothing stirred again in that corrupted Blasted Lands.

Several Expedition members who'd come back from Wildhammer Keep offered to guide Jace to Nethergarde Keep, but he decided to go alone. It wasn't his first time on this road anyway. Besides, even with his eyes blindfolded, Midnight still didn't look normal. By evening, a priest even came to Jace voluntarily, suggesting that the strange illness afflicting the black horse might originate from Jace's own dark magic... and that he should try to control his magic more while riding.

Jace naturally understood the priest's good intentions and appreciated the advice. But he just thought to himself that if his magic could twist a horse into this state, that would be quite an achievement.

After dinner in Stonard, Jace set off south immediately. Along the way, he saw several Alliance-built outposts. One of them even had draenei. Velen and his followers truly seemed to have made repairing the damage the Burning Legion had caused their mission.

In another timeline, Kael'thas Sunstrider's attack on the naaru fleet in the Netherstorm had forced Velen to lead the draenei away from Draenor in one of the ships, crash-landing on an island chain north of Kalimdor.

The energy crystals from the ship had exploded on impact, causing magical anomalies that brought countless disasters, completely changing the islands' ecology. For many years afterward, the draenei had scrambled to stop the spread of contamination and environmental degradation.

But on this side, the naaru fleet had never made it to Draenor, let alone affected Azeroth. And the draenei who followed Velen had successfully reached this side of the Dark Portal in a different way.

Though they'd lost a city built from a massive crystal ship and were forced to live in burrows... Jace wondered if Velen, among his many visions, had ever seen the crash of the Exodar on Azuremyst Isle.

From the smile on his face when he'd welcomed Jace that morning, even if he had seen that vision, he probably preferred this gentler way of arriving.

Jace still couldn't forget that casual glance the prophet had given his staff. It had only lasted a moment, but to Jace, it was prominent enough, heavy enough.

Had he seen some kind of future too? Had he seen the same prophecy Aegwynn had seen, or something different?

"Follow your own choice."

It sounded like something anyone would say. But when Jace recalled Velen saying it seriously in front of the burrow, he'd immediately relaxed, letting go of all those prophecies and future concerns, and focused on the present.

Midnight's black mane trembled rhythmically with the clatter of his hooves. The scorching wind of the Blasted Lands came from ahead, blowing against their direction toward the swamp. The forests on either side grew sparse. Nethergarde Keep's gray walls had already appeared on the eastern horizon, high above.

Looking south from here into the lowlands, he could also see the Dark Portal — and the dim green shadow still clinging to it, radiating undisguised evil.

But supply carts and convoys were coming from the Portal toward the keep. The watchtowers that had once been occupied by Eredar now flew blue and white banners. The Alliance had basically taken control of the Dark Portal.

Jace followed a supply cart to the city gate, waiting to enter. Anti-demon runes were densely packed along the walls on both sides, glowing faintly under arcane energy. Jace could clearly sense Mor'fel's invisible form stir with a confrontational emotion — as if she wanted to see if those runes could drag her out of the shadows.

Jace wasn't optimistic. She'd already lost the Book of Evil Names. Although Khadgar hadn't noticed her last time, that was because he'd been completely off guard. Expecting the Kirin Tor wardens guarding the Dark Portal not to detect her was a stretch.

Sure enough, as she passed one of the runes, Jace clearly felt several alarm runes begin to warp and turn red. The Kirin Tor battle mages guarding the gate also noticed the rune's reaction and hurried over.

"Better give up, Mor'fel. That's the foundation of the Order of Tirisfal..." Jace said with a smile.

But though irritation flared in Mor'fel's emotions, she didn't immediately concede by appearing from the shadows.

"This bag, mage." A battle mage carrying both sword and staff glanced at the Kirin Tor Trust Ring on Jace's hand. "Please open this bag for inspection. I'm certain the runes' reaction has nothing to do with its Eredun leather material."

It was the bag causing problems? Jace was a bit surprised, considering what was inside...

Jace opened the bag. Sazeno suddenly popped his head out and raised his hands. "Don't kill me!"

"I'm Jace Sesso, a mage." Jace took off the Trust Ring and showed it to the mage. "I've come to Nethergarde Keep looking for a draenei herbalist named Morae."

He patted the bundles of grimoires hanging behind his saddle. "This imp is my assistant. I keep him supervised while he helps me organize these materials on the road."

"Jace Sesso?" The mage's eyes widened at the name, as if the genuine demon in front of him had been completely forgotten. He looked up in shock. The other battle mages also seemed very surprised. Even some soldiers on the cart ahead turned to look.

One of the soldiers turned to look inside the gate. Another battle mage also looked inside, then back at Jace.

Jace instinctively followed their gazes... then suddenly remembered. Right. The statue. That was his statue.

The young mage statue — holding a staff in one hand and a sword in the other — still stood there, its expression resolute as it gazed toward the Dark Portal. With everything on his mind — Velen's words and how to explain the demon to Nethergarde's soldiers — Jace had completely forgotten about it.

"He really is Jace Sesso. Looks exactly like the statue."

"He seems thinner in person."

"I saw him back then. How has he gotten so much taller?"

"He wasn't even twenty back then."

"That's a genuine Blade of the Eternals. Look at that sapphire hilt."

"His staff looks a bit different..."

Someone's comment snapped Jace back to reality. He shifted the staff hanging at his side slightly behind him and said to the mage in front, "I have more than one demon with me. I should tell you in advance."

But the battle mage didn't seem interested in the demon issue at all. Instead he asked, "You... destroyed Sargeras' scepter on the Seat of Kil'jaeden — the scepter even Aegwynn couldn't destroy? Is that true, master?"

"What did Sargeras' scepter look like?" Another mage chimed in. "I heard it was like an emerald green jade scepter, but the green was pure fel energy. They say the Eye of Sargeras was set in its tip."

"It looked like... a withered branch surrounded by demon claws." Jace said. "There was indeed a gem on it — an incredibly powerful demonic gem. I destroyed the gem, and the whole scepter shattered with it."

"So it's true." The mage before him said, stunned.

"Archbishop Alonsus Faol said it himself in the Cathedral of Light. How could it be false?"

"Archbishop Faol?"

"Yes. About two months ago."

"That's an unimaginable feat..."

"Master Sesso." The battle mage cleared his throat and pointed northwest. "That draenei researcher — the herbalist — is at the alchemy lab. I remember you once fought at Nethergarde Keep, so you should know where it is."

"Of course I remember. Thank you very much." Jace nodded with a bow and urged his horse into the keep. The mages and guards parted to make way.

Soon, the word "Jace Sesso has returned to Nethergarde Keep" spread up to the walls.

"Hard to say if this statue is better placed here or in the Valley of Heroes." Jace looked at the folds of the robe on the statue and the blood-magic staff — already destroyed at the bottom of Grim Batol — and said, "But this statue is smaller than the one in the Valley of Heroes. It might not look right together..."

"So they should build another one in the Valley of Heroes!" the imp shouted.

"Let Varian save his money for the siege on Blackrock Spire." Jace said.

"Why aren't you saying anything, Mor'fel?"

"Hmm... I want to climb that statue."

"Cough!"
