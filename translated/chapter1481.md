## Chapter 1481: Born from Destruction

The draenei from the other world had already integrated into every aspect of Nethergarde Keep's daily operations, especially in magic.

At the entrance to the mage tower, two draenei mages were talking with a group of Kirin Tor mages. From the expressions on the Kirin Tor members' faces, they had clearly come to recognize the draenei's unique understanding of arcane magic.

At the alchemy lab, there was a seemingly young draenei, following a book as he tried to identify Azeroth's herbs. He caught a glimpse of a cloaked rider dismounting nearby, looked up, and immediately recognized who it was.

"Jace Sesso."

"Hello." Jace walked over. "You know me?"

Even if the statue was a decent likeness, recognizing someone who walked up to you just from a sculpture seemed a bit ridiculous.

"All the draenei who came from Tyr's Fall know you, Jace Sesso." The draenei stood and bowed, speaking in Common. "The prophet didn't want us to stay too long at that fortress above the falls, burdening the human king's supplies. So we moved quickly through the dark forest and valleys to Stonard. We never had a chance to thank you in person."

"I wouldn't be where I am today without the prophet's help, sir." Jace smiled and glanced into the alchemy lab. "Are you... Herbalist Morae? I heard the prophet say she was a woman."

"Lady Morae is in the test forest to the east." The draenei said. "I'm her assistant."

"Test forest?" Jace asked. "What's that?"

"We're trying to use nature's power to repair this land corrupted by fel and shadow — trees and plants that can resist demonic magic... starting with the land around Nethergarde Keep." The draenei said. "Her camp is on the east road toward the mine, on the north slope. I can take you there, master."

"I know the place. I'll go myself. Thank you very much."

Jace gestured for Sazeno and Mor'fel to stay with Midnight, in case some passing priest or paladin mistook him for an evil undead and smashed him flat.

There were still plenty of Expedition members here who recognized Mor'fel. It shouldn't be a problem.

He walked east from Nethergarde Keep, and sure enough, before long he saw a patch of clearly man-planted saplings and shrubs. The air carried a subtle scent of herbs mixed with the metallic tang of arcane energy.

Under a tent in the distance, several draenei were guarding a floating purple draenei crystal nearby. That was probably the source of the arcane energy here.

A female draenei stood in the middle of the field, channeling the arcane energy from the crystal and "broadcasting" it. Under her influence, a warm magical wind swirled gently among the leaves. It was hard to tell if the rustling leaves of the saplings and low shrubs were trembling from the breeze or striving to meet the nourishing arcane energy, urging their own growth.

In the dim, sun-deprived Blasted Lands, this "test field" of plants stood in stark contrast to the deeper, pitch-black forests farther out.

But Jace had never seen any of these plants before — tall or short.

The purple crystal flashed. The draenei beside her reminded the caster in the center, and the channeling finally came to an end.

"Lady Morae?" Jace asked.

"That's me." The female caster draped on an enchanted cloak and walked over. She was quite a tall female draenei. Compared to the males, she was closer in build to a human or elf, but with her two-meter-plus height and a pair of silver goat hooves, it was still hard for Azeroth's natives to feel "familiar."

"Wait — you're Jace Sesso... I recognize your face. The prophet mentioned you to me."

"The prophet said I would come to find you?" Jace asked in surprise.

"No, no." Morae quickly shook her head. "The prophet isn't a time traveler. Of course he can't predict every detail of what hasn't happened yet. It's just that back at that mage tower, when he learned you'd returned from Draenor with serious injuries, he hoped we could find a way to treat your void corruption. But clearly, your magic healed you much faster than our wounded."

The draenei woman said all this with a calm, slightly smiling expression. If she weren't a draenei, Jace might have thought she was making a veiled jab at his Life Drain spell.

"Thank you very much for your kindness, Lady Morae." He smiled. "What are these plants?"

"The trees over there are Tanaan Talontar." Morae explained. "A kind of tree that grew in the Tanaan Jungle before Draenor's destruction. The orcs vividly called them Bloodclaw Trees. When they mature, the tips of their branches turn red, and the top unfurls red sword-shaped leaves — like a blood-stained claw."

"Though they look like demonic plants, after the Dark Portal opened, even when Tanaan's jungles and swamps were corrupted, they still managed to grow and resist the corruption under those harsh conditions. That resilience is very different from our inherent understanding of rainforest flora."

"Combined with several other plant species, we hope they can eventually help the Black Morass and Stranglethorn Vale to the west reclaim this corrupted land. Perhaps it can't restore the land around the Dark Portal to its original state, but at least it can regain life."

Jace didn't know much about Draenor plants or environmental restoration. When Prophet Velen had first proposed restoring the Blasted Lands to life, he hadn't thought much about it.

But seeing this field of lush vegetation growing from the demon-corrupted dead land, leaning against the keep... it really did seem like a glimpse of a beautiful future for this world.

Duskwood greeting the dawn. Nethergarde — the Keep That Needs No Keeping — standing watch. Could he really see that someday?

"Hard to imagine," he murmured.

"It takes time." The herbalist said. "And it might not go smoothly at first."

"Everything's hard in the beginning." Jace replied.

He reached down and touched a purple-leafed plant at his feet — something like a sea urchin — then said, "Lady Morae, I actually came here to consult you about void blossoms."

"Void blossoms?" Morae's blue crystal eyes narrowed. "We don't have any void blossom samples, unfortunately. Once you leave Draenor, that plant is very hard to preserve. Its composition... the void energy part is greater than the 'plant' part."

"My alchemist friends and some Dalaran herbalists are trying to grow new void blossoms outside of Draenor, but they haven't made any progress." Jace said. "Even in the Tyr's Fall rift, which has a Farahlon-like environment, they couldn't succeed at all."

"Ah... the Tyr's Fall rift." Morae looked up thoughtfully, staring at the gray clouds in the sky for a moment.

"My friend thinks void blossoms might be a native Draenor plant transformed by the void. Maybe a plant unique to Farahlon?" Jace asked. "They were wondering if transplanting that plant to the Tyr's Fall rift might eventually yield void blossoms."

"Void blossoms aren't transformed from any native Draenor plant." Morae said. "But I understand why that guess emerged."

"It's true that after the world shattered, the power of the Twisting Nether poured into Farahlon's sky, creating new shadow air and environment. That was the precondition for void blossoms' birth. Trying it on fragments within the Twisting Nether is a good idea." The draenei herbalist tried to explain. "But Tyr's Fall isn't Farahlon. It isn't... Draenor."

"So void blossoms can't grow anywhere other than Draenor?" Jace asked.

The draenei lowered her head in thought for a moment, then suddenly seemed to realize something. "Right. You're a shadow mage, Jace Sesso. You know how to manipulate shadow energy. So you should know that shadow exists within each of us — our emotions."

"Yes." Jace said.

"And Draenor had her emotions too." Morae looked toward the Dark Portal. "That world was destroyed by Ner'zhul's ruthlessness and Sargeras' scepter's arrogance. The mysterious, serene Farahlon was part of it. Tens of thousands of departed souls fell into the void's abyss. That despair and pain collided with the pouring Twisting Nether power, merged into one... Void blossoms were born from that chaos in that complex land. That's why they're such an important ingredient for many dangerous magical potions. The Tyr's Fall rift exists within the Twisting Nether, but it lacks Draenor as a participant."

Jace pondered this for a moment before saying, "So how do we..."

"Replicate it?" Morae said. "Wait a moment, Jace Sesso. I'll organize my notes and give them to you shortly. My research on void blossoms includes several records of Draenor's shadow energy after its rupture, as well as soil samples from Farahlon before and after its destruction. You can try referring to these materials... to create the corresponding shadow oil, providing 'nourishment' for void blossoms' birth. Then combine it with the pure Twisting Nether power of the Tyr's Fall rift, and maybe you really can cultivate genuine void blossoms."

"This is incredibly valuable." Jace said. "My friend will be very grateful, Lady Morae."

"If it really succeeds, I hope you'll share the results with me..." Morae said.

"Of course." Jace said.
