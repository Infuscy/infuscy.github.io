## Chapter 1485: Reporting to the Alchemist

That night, Jace brought Moray's research notes to the Slaughtered Lamb. He greeted Tharle, who told him only Ursula was in the basement.

Jace figured the others were probably inside the Telogrus Rift. Of all the Dagger Clan's bases, the alchemy lab in the Slaughtered Lamb's basement still had the best conditions and most complete equipment, so even with a new sanctuary in the Twisting Nether, Ursula preferred to stay in her original workspace as much as possible.

He found Ursula in the alchemy lab. She was holding a magnifying lens, examining a blue-spotted petal in her hand. She looked up at him. "Good evening, Jace. Lady Morofil said you'd be coming to see me later. I was hoping we could have dinner together."

"I ran into a friend and had to sort out some other business." Jace said. "Is that Taladoran orchid? How's your research on Draenei herbs going? Any new progress?"

"It's hard to make exciting progress without solving the Voidbloom problem." Ursula carefully put the petal away. "And I've been swamped... Oh, I almost forgot to tell you. A few weeks ago, shortly after you left, Galders, Elrand, Sulenna, and I went to the Jangolode Mine — the one with the gnolls, about a dozen miles west of Faolble's Farm."

"I know it," Jace said.

Ursula pressed a hand to her forehead and sighed. "Galders and Elrand wanted to test the Soul Well spell on those gnolls. Things did not go well."

"The gnolls were tough?" Jace asked.

"If only it were that simple." Ursula said. "Galders had prepared thoroughly — didn't use any means of amplifying shadow power, we didn't even attempt the later parts of the manuscript about drawing and refining souls from the well. We just spread out a very small-scale Soul Well for a first attempt... and it still triggered an uncontrollable shadow storm. I can't tell if Galders 'Blackclaw' has too much shadow talent, or not enough."

"The manuscript mentions how to suppress a Soul Well that's lost control," Jace said.

"Yes." Ursula said. "We followed the instructions — sent in all our demon servants, including felguards and felhounds, plus the succubi's mentally enslaved gnoll sentries — used their bodies to block the Soul Well's power and stopped the magic from spiraling further. It worked in the end, but Galders and Sulenna were both injured. Galders lost his felguard — its soul seems to have been destroyed by the well."

"Serious injuries?" Jace asked.

"The shadow storm's corrosion," Ursula said. "Galders got it worse than Sulenna. He was the caster, struggling to contain the storm's scale the whole time. Sulenna's succubus died in the Soul Well, and she couldn't dispel the backlash from the soul link in time."

"Sulenna's succubus servant — you mean that pleasure demon," Morofil said.

"Yes, the one you nearly killed — Jaxres. But that cunning demon's soul seems to have escaped again. Sulenna's been recovering for a few days and has been planning to summon him back." Ursula said. "The injuries are secondary — it's not the first time we've been hurt in dark magic experiments. Elrand nearly got himself killed in that felguard incident. That's why the Dagger Clan can't do without me."

"The real problem was that with things spiraling out of control, we couldn't clean up properly. The Wizard Sanctum and SI:7 both sent people. Matthias Shaw even approached Galders and asked if this was connected to us. In short, they don't want any more rumors about demons or dark magic around Moonbrook right now — it's been hard enough for people to forget about the Lighthouse incident."

"Clearing out gnolls for humans — isn't that a good thing?!" the green imp assistant in the alchemy lab exclaimed. "These ungrateful idiots!"

"The locals have lived with gnolls wandering around for centuries." Ursula looked at it. "But they still haven't gotten used to demons."

"Then we'd better get them used to demons," Sazeno said from Morofil's shoulder.

"Absolutely right, Master Sazeno! Get these humans used to demons! So we don't have to stay trapped in this cage anymore!" the green imp shouted in Demonic.

"Listening to two mongrels talk about 'we demons' is pathetic." Morofil sneered, glancing at the felhound crouched in the corner. "Even that dog is more 'demonic' than you two."

The green imp lowered its head in fear, then scurried back into its cabinet. Sazeno shut up too.

"You really have a way with words, Lady Morofil." Ursula smiled.

"They need to know who's in charge, Ursula Dreen. Otherwise they'll end up climbing right over your head," Morofil warned.

"Fair point." Ursula took a deep breath. "So what you brought today had better be good news, Jace. After weeks of chaos, scrambling around, day and night mixed up..."

"I heard about an ogre hideout in Darkshire, in the mountains south of Raven Hill heading toward the coast," Jace said. "If they don't want us experimenting near Moonbrook, Duskwood should be fine, shouldn't it? Besides, those mountains almost reach Stranglethorn. If anything gets wrecked, it's the Gurubashi trolls' territory."

"I heard about the ogres making trouble near Raven Hill too." Ursula said. "But Mount Wogul stretches all the way to Stranglethorn. Do you know exactly where they're hiding?"

Jace thought for a moment. "The king's army has tried to hunt them. SI:7 should have intelligence. I'll have Tharle tell Galders — we'll gather at the Darkshire sanctuary, then head toward Raven Hill. I'll contact Shaw in the next couple of days, talk to him about it, and ask about the ogres' location."

"Fine." Ursula said. "But that's not exactly good news for me, Jace Sesso. If the magic goes wrong again, I'll be the one running around mixing potions for you. It's not like you're going to ask the Church for help, right? You know what I'm waiting for. Don't tell me you forgot..."

"Of course not." Jace smiled mysteriously and pulled out Moray's research notes. "A Draenei herbalist's notes. Take a look."

"Really? You actually got Voidbloom research materials from those Draenei?" Ursula froze, then licked her lips and grabbed the notes from Jace's hand, flipping through them. "I thought the Draenei... wait, she even translated it into Common? I was ready to test my Demonic again."

"Draenei look very similar to eredar demons, but there are big differences. Even if they're equally arrogant and self-righteous, hmm..." Jace's voice trailed off as Ursula read intently, completely ignoring him.

After a moment, Ursula scowled at the shelf holding Draenei herb samples. "Damn it. So our entire research direction was wrong. Voidbloom... really does come from nothing."

"Not exactly nothing." Jace said. "Did you see the part about the emotions of the entire world of Draenor reacting with Twisting Nether energy?"

"I saw it." Ursula sighed. "So cultivating Voidbloom is almost identical to casting a long, complex shadow magic ritual. That's really..."

"You summoned me back from Kil'jaeden himself, Ursula." Jace said. "Why the lack of confidence now? You know what kind of demon Kil'jaeden is, don't you?"

"Of course I know." Ursula breathed heavily, her tone impatient. "But that was you. I don't have any emotional connection to Voidbloom."

"And that first summoning of you — it was a failure to me, from the start. Do you know how crushing it was that I couldn't bring you back from that mountaintop? I still have nightmares about it. It's still affecting me. 'Regret,' 'resentment' — they keep polluting every spell I cast."

"I'm sorry," Jace said.

"What does it have to do with you?" The alchemist let out a breath. "At least this lady gave a solution — shadow oil. I have some ideas about that."

"I think she meant using carefully compounded shadow oil to irrigate the 'soil' for cultivating Voidbloom," Jace said. "Simulating Draenor's chaotic shadow environment back then."

"In alchemy — especially shadow alchemy — the alchemist themselves is part of the 'environment.' The most important environmental factor, Jace." Ursula emphasized as she closed the notes. "But I still need to thank you properly. I didn't think they'd agree so readily."

"In a way, I saved their lives," Jace said. "And the herbalist mentioned that if we do get results, she'll need to share in them."

"Naturally," Ursula said.

"One more thing." Jace pulled *Dark Simulacrum* from his bag and set it on the table. "I need a reliable warlock to practice a new spell with me. Strictly speaking, it's a new kind of magic — there's no incantation... it's complicated. Would you be willing to help?"

"No incantation?" Ursula looked surprised.

"Right." Jace glanced at Morofil, who was smirking on the side. "And it's dangerous. There might be accidents during practice. I haven't fully mastered it myself, much less taught anyone else."

"You want my help — as a human punching bag?" Ursula frowned.

"As my 'learning subject,'" Jace said. "Specifically, it's a spell that simulates other spells. We'll know more when we try."

"Start tonight?" Ursula asked. "You staying over?"

"I'm not tired. Are you?" Jace asked.

"You know I never sleep at normal hours, Jace Sesso." Ursula put the notes in a chest. "I thought you'd go back to Ilgalar's Tower or stay at your parents' place."

"I just got back from Darkshire. I still reek of necromancy. You think I can show up at my parents' like this?" Jace said helplessly.

"So you're dumping that undead stench in my alchemy lab?" The alchemist put her hands on her hips.

"You know you have ways to get rid of it, right?" Jace put on an innocent smile.

The alchemist burst out laughing. "Fine, stay here then, you damn brat. Now tell me about your magic."
