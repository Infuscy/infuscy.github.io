## Chapter 1488: At Least There's a Spell to Share

On the farthest floating island in the Telogrus Rift, Karlez was speaking in low Demonic with a group of the Dagger Clan's demon servants. Only faint murmurs carried across the layers of Twisting Nether storms, but it looked like some kind of sinister demonic gathering deep in the blasted wastes of the nether.

The floating island near the Darkshire rift had already undergone some preliminary "decorating" and organization: tables, chairs, shelves for spell components, designated casting circles. They'd even replicated the big fire pit from the Slaughtered Lamb's main hall — it seemed to have become the group's cultural tradition.

A few succubi and incubi sat by the blazing shadow-violet flames, chatting and laughing, occasionally flirting with each other and touching each other's bodies, their laughter growing more unrestrained.

It made sense that Ursula preferred to stay in the Slaughtered Lamb brewing potions rather than just because the Telogrus Rift's facilities weren't ready yet. Unlike the rest of the Dagger Clan, she'd never liked getting mixed up with these demons.

Galders, on the other hand, seemed to be enjoying himself. He was deep in conversation with a female succubus Jace didn't recognize.

"Good evening, Jace." Galders waved. "Glad to see your Kalimdor trip didn't run into any problems. When Ursula told me you were back, I wondered if you'd returned so soon because something had gone wrong."

"Don't forget I traveled with Master Khadgar. Teleportation makes everything easier," Jace said.

Galders nodded. "Once we've fully mapped out the void rift, we might even try establishing a second one at the Slaughtered Lamb. Combined with Ilgalar's Tower, we'll be able to build a teleportation network linking Darkshire, Stormwind, and Stonewatch Falls with Telogrus as the hub. When that's done, traveling within the Kingdom of Stormwind will be just as efficient as the mages' portal network."

"So, what did Matthias Shaw say? Is he supporting the Soul Well experiment at Mount Wogul?"

"SI:7 will back us. They'll provide the ogres' hideout locations." Jace said. "But Shaw has one condition — SI:7 comes along."

"That's fine." Galders said. "I just hope the ogres don't recognize any SI:7 agents and cause us trouble."

He handed over two notebooks. "Here's the record from our mine experiment, plus my annotations and collation of Gul'dan's notes. This time, you'll be the one casting the spell, Jace."

"I'll practice the incantation a bit inside the Telogrus Rift before we go," Jace said.

"My advice is, it's fine to get familiar with the incantation and casting process." Galders lowered his voice. "But the shadow environment inside the Telogrus Rift is very different from Azeroth. For ritual magic on this scale, it could give people like us who are new to this kind of spell a false impression... the illusion that we can control its power."

"And controlling a Soul Well that just needs to be summoned is completely different from one that needs to drain gnoll or even demon souls. These are... lessons I learned the hard way. You brought the soul-draining spell from Draenor back to the Dagger Clan, Jace. You know that 'connecting' a target's soul to your spell isn't an easy task."

"I think I understand." Jace opened the mine experiment record, reading carefully through the part where the Dagger Clan had to use their demon servants to physically block the shadow power and contain the Soul Well.

"How's your injury healing? Will it affect the Mount Wogul trip?"

"It won't. I've mostly recovered." Galders said.

"Being resilient enough to recover quickly is a necessary quality for researchers of void and fel magic, dear Jace Sesso." The succubus beside Galders smiled smoothly. Jace could sense a hint of displeasure in her — as if his concern somehow diminished her master.

But the succubus only blurted out that one line before seeming to come to her senses. She stole a glance at Morofil, who was watching the demons around the fire pit, and nervously withdrew her smile.

"If not for our alchemist's potions helping me recuperate, I couldn't have recovered this much in under two weeks, Derenas."

Galders turned to Jace. "While a bunch of ogres who only dare to ambush caravans and travelers aren't worth fearing, I hear some of them are warlocks who once followed Cho'gall. Do you know about this?"

"And that's exactly our ticket into their camp." Jace said. "I've already told Shaw — I plan to make a deal with those ogres. Use dark magic exchanges and demonic treasures as an excuse to meet their advisor, and maybe even the warlord himself."

Galders bowed his head and chuckled, shoulders shaking. "Whatever you say, Jace. Sulenna and Elrand should reach Darkshire within three days. Let's meet at the Darkshire sanctuary in four days. I'll have SI:7's agent in Darkshire inform Matthias Shaw, and we'll go over the specifics then."

"Good." Jace replied.

"Were you testing some shadow magic at the Slaughtered Lamb earlier?" Galders asked curiously. "I could feel the shadow wards trembling almost to their breaking point all the way in Darkshire. Was it related to the Soul Well? Or was Ursula testing a new potion again? The last time she made that fire elemental elixir, it caused quite a mess."

Considering the shadow that had formed in Karazhan from Dark Simulacrum, plus what Medivh wrote in the book about a possible connection to Old Gods, and Galders's previous contact with Old God remnants — the Wind Stones — and the catastrophic consequences of carelessly sharing Summon Succubus with Jagin a few years ago... Jace decided to keep this hard-to-control magic from the rest of the Dagger Clan for now, Ursula aside.

"A dark magic I saw in Draenor. No reliable incantation, no reliable method — just memories and fragmentary accounts from other warlocks." Jace shook his head. "So several attempts had problems. Very resource-intensive too."

Galders, as a veteran warlock who'd spent years moving among rogue warlocks and Dark Iron dwarves, was an expert at reading and controlling emotions. If Jace was going to hide his thoughts, he'd have to be very careful.

"I once saw a two-headed ogre in Booty Bay cast a spell he called the 'Idiot Curse.' I tried to copy it by watching his gestures and overhearing the incantation. Didn't work out." Galders sighed. "But then, he was casting it on his ogre bodyguard. Who knows if it even worked?"

Galders laughed a few times at his own story. He probably hadn't caught what Jace was holding back. Jace chuckled along. "I did 'copy' a spell from an eredar lord in Draenor, and it came in handy during some 'experiments' the other night."

"What spell?" Galders asked.

"No official name. I'm calling it Devour Shadow for now." Jace said. "It can negate shadow magic — not just dispel it, but actually seize the dark power and turn it to your own use."

"A bit like a felhound's magic consumption," Galders said.

"That's what I thought, hence the name. But this only seizes shadow energy. The upside is you can do it at a distance — no need to actually bite someone like a felhound would." Jace said. "From the initial incantation formulation to repeated attempts along the way, I think I've gotten pretty good at it now."

"I heard from some Expedition mages that you used a shadow spell to devour a void-corrupted dragon. Was that this spell?!" Galders's eyes widened.

"Yes." Jace said. "But I wasn't as proficient with it then. And I had the help of a Demonic Destruction Potion."

"A Demonic Destruction Potion." Galders looked down, lost in thought. "Ursula is trying to grow our own Voidbloom here. Maybe she'll be able to make those potions the Kirin'Var mages brewed. If we also have this Devour Shadow..."

Jace wiggled his fingers. Morofil handed him the demon bag. He grabbed Sazeno and tossed him aside — the imp let out an "ow" — then pulled out a small notebook from the bottom of the cloth bag. "Here. It's in here."

"An eredar spell." Galders took the notebook, barely containing his excitement, and nodded. "The Shadow Council is pretty much finished. It's time to learn a thing or two from their teachers."
