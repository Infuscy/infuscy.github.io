## Chapter 1493: The "Hundred Souls" Robe?

In Org'ril's cave, Jace and the other warlocks gathered to search the place thoroughly. The Fissure-Fist ogres' plunder from South Elwynn naturally couldn't stay with them — SI:7 would take it all back to Darkshire to inventory, to see if any valuables could be returned to their owners. A substantial portion of the stolen goods originally belonged to Darkshire's ruling family, House Ebberlock.

But the items related to dark magic that Org'ril had left behind would, of course, have to be handled by the king's dark magic advisor.

Ever since Jace had taken a walk through Medivh's vault, it had become hard for him to feel excited about "treasures" out in the world — let alone the collection of some ogre tribe hidden away in who-knows-what corner.

Still, they found some interesting things. Draenei soul shards that had been preserved and never used, a Gorian-style staff, magic gems produced before Draenor's destruction, a few quite valuable Gurubashi coins, a bottle of potent mojo, and even some voodoo branches that had somehow been acquired from the Zul'Gurub trolls.

Erland held up a Draenei soul shard. "Gul'dan's notes mention that after a Soul Well successfully drains enough souls, it can condense into a pool of soul shards. Not only could it serve as a mana source for nearby warlocks and demons, but people could also draw soul shards from it."

"Yes, I saw that too," Jace said. "But at the end, I could feel the well starting to lose control. If I'd forced the next step, it might have dragged us all in, so I had to stop as soon as possible."

He turned and scanned the succubi, making sure none of them recognized Ussl'thras the way that Shadow Council succubus in Auchindoun had.

Though really, worrying about whether these demons could recognize his staff no longer made much difference — Kil'jaeden already knew about it anyway.

"Jace's decision was the right one," Galders said. "But just reaching the second stage... is already astonishing enough."

"Don't even mention it. My legs were so weak I could barely stand." Sulenna glanced around. "I need something to drink. Damn it, don't these ogres keep any alcohol in their fortress? I thought ogres loved drinking?"

"In the chieftain's cave," Erland pointed toward the entrance. "Shaw knows where it is. But I should warn you — it smells worse than bloodboar piss."

"What?" Sulenna frowned.

"And I know you weren't using your full power," Galders said, looking at Jace. "Just like you said — you kept the spell firmly within your control from start to finish."

"I can't wait to read your summary, Jace." Jalaine picked up a purple Draenor magic gem and examined it. "I'm thinking, since Jace and Galders can control the scale of the Soul Well the same way mages use Clearcasting to manage different magnitudes of arcane magic, maybe we can figure out from their notes how to scale the Soul Well down — make it smaller, safer."

Jalaine wasn't wrong. In fact, the Soul Well that players summoned in-game for "healthstones" was fundamentally the same thing as this Soul Well.

Then again, the Northshire Abbey and the Cathedral of Light were also the same kind of thing. And Jace knew his Soul Well was still far from the one Gul'dan had summoned in Suramar.

Gul'dan's magic had been empowered by numerous artifacts preserved in Suramar. Jace, relying purely on his own strength — even with Ussl'thras as it was now — would struggle to replicate that.

But if you scaled it down... could Gul'dan's Soul Well, by controlling its scope, achieve the same effect as the in-game version?

"Speaking of which, what's the story with that ring, Jalaine?" Erland asked.

"Found it," Jalaine said casually. "Lady Niresa Vardune is probably dead at the hands of some orc by now. Her ring was left in the ruins of Alterac Castle. With the Perenoldes all locked up in prison, who's going to stop us from going in?"

Jace didn't even need to look at her to know she was lying. And Jalaine made no effort to hide it, as if she didn't care that everyone knew she was lying.

Everyone present was a warlock, or a more perceptive sayaad demon. They all knew it too. Even Erland understood this was a topic Jalaine didn't want to discuss further. No one pressed the matter.

Respecting each other's secrets was an unspoken rule of the Dagger Society. Just like Sulenna and Jalaine hadn't bothered to be curious about why Jace's eyes had suddenly turned red when he was channeling the Soul Well, or what that shadow-energy-drenched appearance was all about. Strictly speaking, when Galders had channeled the Soul Well before, his eyes hadn't turned red. Even a casual question wouldn't have been out of line.

They knew that if Jace didn't bring it up himself, it wasn't related to the Soul Well's transformation. Rather than ask a meaningless question that would make everyone uncomfortable, it was better to pretend nothing had happened.

After a brief silence, Ursula tried to steer the conversation back.

"Whether or not we can eventually control the Soul Well — whether we want to make it stronger or more controllable, use it to help us cast spells, or even infuse its power into potions, staves, or robes — we'll need further experimentation."

She looked at Jalaine. "But the west coast is slowly recovering. Mathias didn't want our dark magic used on gnolls to interfere with the farmers' resettlement efforts before. Now that he's seen Jace's ritual, he's even less likely to give us the green light."

Erland sat with his arms crossed. "We've destroyed the Fissure-Fist ogres' fortress. Traffic between Moonbrook and Darkshire will definitely increase, and Raven Hill in between will benefit too. So neither the great cemetery in Raven Hill nor the Tranquil Gardens Cemetery south of Darkshire will be options."

"Cross the border into Stranglethorn?" Sulenna pointed south.

"You think the Dagger Society can counter-invade Zul'Gurub?" Erland's eyes widened.

"Uh... well, fair point," Sulenna muttered.

"We could try taking a boat to Menethil Harbor," Jace said. "Ironforge only controls the area around Menethil Harbor. Once you go deep into the wetlands, it's all murloc and gnoll territory. I've been there — you can walk for seven or eight days without seeing a soul. Even away from murloc huts and gnoll tents, you only run into crocodiles and raptors."

"Worth considering," Galders agreed.

Then Jalaine muttered to herself, "Right... right, why not try souls? Such an obvious option... why am I only considering this now?"

"What?" Ursula asked.

"The new robe I'm working on," Jalaine said. "Mooncloth's mana-carrying capacity is excellent. Combined with my deepening understanding of shadow magic and demonic magic, I can apply increasingly powerful enchantments to the mooncloth... and not just inactive spells like Demon Armor or Dark Intellect. I want it to be more active — to help the wearer tear apart incoming spells, or even launch attacks on its own."

An active defense system? A dark-magic version of explosive reactive armor?

"Remember that robe you and I made, the Black Claw Vestment? In our last experiment, it actively absorbed a lot of the shadow energy released by the Soul Well," Galders said. "Does that count as the kind you're talking about?"

"Yes," Jalaine said. "But... while mooncloth is strong enough to serve as a vessel for these enchantments, the enchantments themselves start fighting each other. Shadow and fel both come from the depths of the Twisting Nether, but they don't coexist peacefully."

"Could you sew shadow-binding runes onto it, to seal the power?" Sulenna tried.

"Then I might as well not enchant such high-level magic at all and just make an ordinary robe," Jalaine said, looking at her. "I went to Dalaran to seek help from an old friend of mine. He's both a master robe tailor and a master of enchantment and transmutation. I wanted him to use arcane energy to harmonize the two forces — the way arcane can bind fire and frost, two completely incompatible elements, together to form devastating Frostfire Bolts. But in the end, it failed. It even destroyed one of my samples."

"So last night's ritual made you think of demon souls," Galders said. "A demon's soul — something that naturally commands both shadow and fel — to control the robe's enchantment."

"Yes," Jalaine said. "Now that we have Gul'dan's notes, and the method for manipulating souls to create magical instruments, I can infuse demon souls into my new robe."

"Does a more powerful individual demon mean a more powerful robe?" Erland asked.

"Not necessarily. From what I know, the key factor is the number of demons," Jace said. "The Scepter of Sargeras was forged from the souls of over a thousand demon lords, according to records. These demon lords were elites of the Burning Legion, but their status was far below that of demon kings like Kil'jaeden. Yet the Scepter of Sargeras could accomplish things beyond even Kil'jaeden's personal abilities."

"Exactly." Jalaine smiled. "I didn't expect you to know about forging demonic artifacts, Jace. And... a single powerful demon's will tends to twist a robe. Even if you manage to seal it in, you'll eventually find yourself not knowing whether you're wearing the robe or the robe is wearing you. So even when demons forge artifacts themselves, they prefer to go with numbers."

The tailor's words immediately made Jace think of Ussl'thras.

"So how many demon soul shards are you planning to use?" Ursula asked.

"A hundred to start." Jalaine thought for a moment, then asked, "How many do we have right now?"

"Not counting imp soul shards, twenty-two," Erland replied, leaning against the wall. "There are ten on consignment in Booty Bay. No idea how many have sold."

"Call them all back," Jalaine said. "I'll check Dalaran to see if I can get some more."

"I happen to be going to Dalaran in a few days. Anything I can help with?" Jace asked.

"Really? Well then, Jace... could you ask Archmage Khadgar about it?" Jalaine asked cautiously.

"If I run into him, sure," Jace said.

"Wonderful." Jalaine clenched her fist. "It feels like all the magical energy that was blocked up has suddenly started flowing again."

Though, Khadgar definitely wouldn't be in Dalaran. Jace could ask other mages who'd returned from the Expedition — they'd hauled away plenty of demon and draenei soul shards from Auchindoun.
