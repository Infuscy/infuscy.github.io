## Chapter 1495: A Match Staked on Bloodlines

In the early morning, Jace headed to Sora's residence. Sora was sitting on the steps of the downstairs courtyard, staring blankly at the towering trees.

Jace approached quietly, watching the moonlight flowing in Sora's eyes. "What are the trees saying?"

Sora shook her head, took a deep breath, and looked over. "Ah... Jace. Nothing, really. I was just listening to them chat. A lot of the trees in the Mage Quarter think Stormwind is just a pile of rocks that sprang up out of nowhere."

"I only know that a flower that only exists in the Emerald Dream suddenly appeared in my parents' house. Any idea what's up with that?" Jace tilted his head.

"Ah, that." Sora looked a bit embarrassed. "That's not a flower from the dream, Jace. Flowers from the dream can barely exist in the real Azeroth. That one actually grows near the dream's threshold — on the boundary between the dream and reality. It's nourished by dream energies. It's very good for people's health and recovery from injuries. It can also dispel shadow and death energies, so it's perfect for protecting your mother and father."

"From me," Jace finished.

"Let's not put it so bluntly." Sora suppressed a smile. "But... pretty much."

Jace sighed. "Still, thank you. That's a really precious gift."

"I promised you I'd bless your parents' house, remember?" Sora said. "But a blessing spell doesn't last very long. A flower like this is much more permanent. Oh, I made contact with Vereesa. In the dream... I saw her. I told her about Grede's invitation and that we'd be going to Dalaran. She said she'd leave the dream soon and meet us in Dalaran."

"How is she?" Jace asked. "Is she doing okay?"

Sora said, "I can feel her under the protection of the Mother Tree G'Hanir... I think she's found her connection to nature there. It's the domain of the demigod Aviana."

"In elven legend, Aviana died in the War of the Ancients," Jace said.

"Yes," Sora said. "But her spirit returned to G'Hanir and continues to serve her mistress Elune there, caring for the creatures of the dream. Now Vereesa is part of that."

"So the demigod is Vereesa's teacher now?" Jace asked.

"I suppose you could say that," Sora said.

"But I remember Aviana... isn't she a bird goddess?" Jace said. "In elven paintings, she always has a pair of wings. Legend says she's the mother of the harpies, so she looks a lot like them."

"Vereesa used to lure birds in with their trust and then kill them for roasting. Won't Lady Aviana punish her for that?"

"I have no idea," Sora said. "I'm not old enough to have guarded the Mother Tree, so I'm not familiar with her."

Jace had meant it as a joke, but Sora actually seemed to be giving it serious thought, which made him worry a bit too.

"Jace Sesso! I could smell the shadow on you from a hundred yards away!"

A shout came from behind. Needleflame had appeared on the path outside the courtyard at some point.

"The other day you told me to study combat properly with Lady Morophil, but then you went and took her who-knows-where. I went to your tower twice and only found those stupid demon dogs of yours. They can't talk. Did you sneak off somewhere fun with her again?"

Needleflame strode over, poking Jace in the side with her finger while grumbling.

"I went to Darkshire with the SI:7 agents to deal with the Ebberlock family's nobility," Jace said. "Interested?"

"Nobility, Ebberlock, SI:7 — what the hell are all those?" Needleflame put on a bored expression.

"Hey, do you have time to go fishing with me today?" She tugged at his belt. "I think I'm about to catch the legendary sewer crocodile."

"The other day, I felt something bite my hook, and it was super strong. If Sora hadn't been screaming and shouting with excitement next to me and scared it off, I bet I'd have caught something even scarier than a sewer crocodile. It would've knocked Varian Wrynn right off his throne! Though shocking you humans isn't really that satisfying."

She wasn't wrong. If Needleflame turned back into her real form and flew around Stormwind, that would be a bigger spectacle than catching the great-granddaddy of all sewer crocodiles.

"You're lucky Sora's willing to go fishing with you at all, you little rascal. Stop complaining." Jace pinched her human ear and gave it a little shake.

"She's way better than you." Needleflame pressed down on his hand. "You never play with me anymore."

"We're going to Dalaran. No time to play." Jace lightly tapped her head. "Get ready now. We leave tomorrow."

"Really?!" Needleflame got so excited she grabbed Sora, who was sitting nearby staring blankly, and shook her violently. "I want to race you, green dragon! Blackwing Legion power! Let's see who reaches Ironforge first!"

"You're way too cocky," Jace scoffed.

"She has to carry you guys. That makes up for the age difference. I think that's fair!" Needleflame kept shaking Sora to a rhythm.

Jace expected Sora to dismiss the black dragonling's challenge, but instead she shot back contemptuously, "You don't actually think I can't outfly you while carrying Jace and Morophil, do you, little one?"

Were all winged creatures this excited about racing? Jace felt like he barely recognized Sora right now.

"Don't be so arrogant, spawn of Ysera." Needleflame bared her little white teeth. "You'll be too scared that Jace Sesso will fall off your back to go all out!"

"Truly inheriting your father's cunning," Sora said coldly. "When the time comes, I'll outfly you by three hundred miles even if I have to carry him in my arms. You'll be crying while lost in the snowy mountains of Dun Morogh."

"So arrogant! So arrogant! I can't take it! I want to race you RIGHT NOW!" Needleflame hugged Sora's neck and shook even harder.

"Don't rush. Tomorrow you'll learn the gap between us." Sora muttered quietly, swaying back and forth with a completely expressionless face, not taking the little one seriously at all.

The next morning, the woods were in chaos. A green dragon stretched her wings, glaring at the much smaller black dragonling who kept snarling beside her. But despite her size, the black dragon wasn't backing down in spirit — every breath sent black smoke snorting out of her nostrils, filling the whole area with sooty haze. The two beasts looked like they might start fighting at any moment.

"I gotta say, are you sure about going to Dalaran like this, master?" Sazeno poked his head out of the bag.

Jace clutched the cloth bundle containing the blue leather boots, glancing nervously toward Stormwind, then back with a pained expression. "Alright, I'm starting to reconsider the gryphon option."

The succubus raised an eyebrow. "Look on the bright side, darling. This time we'll reach Dalaran faster than ever before."

Jace looked at her back. "Well, you don't have to worry. Your wings are way more reliable than my cloak."

Morophil happily leaned her head against Jace's shoulder. "Say it again, darling. I love hearing that word from your lips. Say it in demonic, preferably."
