## Chapter 1518: Farewell, Menethil

Menethil Harbor. A light rain was falling. Jace didn't know if it had anything to do with him and Amy summoning the Tide Duke at Lake Valar two days ago, but the rain had been falling steadily for two days straight, from Bato Mountain to Baradin Bay.

Standing on the dock, the faint glow of the "Essence of Water" in his hand pulsed in time with the waves lapping at the shore. Jace noticed the crystal bottle had a lid. When it came time to deal with the Firelord's rune, would it be as simple as opening it and pouring it out?

The bottle was very small -- just slightly larger than Ursula's lesser healing potion bottles. Duke Hydraxis had said there were seven fire runes. Was he supposed to divide it into seven equal parts and pour them out separately?

Duke Hydraxis had vanished into the lake after speaking. It was clear that containing the presence of a Tide Duke was a strain on Lake Valar. Even Amy couldn't say for sure how this thing was supposed to be used. The water elementals summoned afterward couldn't explain it either.

Maybe once they actually saw the fire runes, they'd know what to do.

Fog shrouded the sea. The harbor was hazy. Jace remembered the beach where they'd said goodbye to Mossa during their first trip to Kalimdor. It had looked much the same.

"Before this visit to Grim Batol, the War of the Three Hammers was practically an ancient legend from another world to me." Amy said. "It's truly an honor to witness the Three Hammers coming together again after so long."

"I think we're still a long way off, Lady Amy Marin." Grede said. "Just wait till you hear how foul the Dark Irons' mouths are. But I have to admit, the leaders of the Three Hammers haven't been in the same hall together in over a hundred years. It seems that if you leave Thaurissan out of it, a lot of conflicts can be resolved."

"You're starting to sound like an Anvilmar already, Lord Grede Thunderfist." Amy said.

"What?!" Grede, holding the black-cloth-wrapped warhammer, stepped back in alarm. "Don't you dare link me with the name Anvilmar!"

"It might be a bit late to distance yourself now." Vereesa said softly. "Didn't you see the way those dwarves looked at you when you lifted that hammer? And that song -- it sings of the high mountains of Ironforge. But the 'Ironforge' in the song isn't the Bronzebeards' Ironforge. It's every dwarf's Ironforge. In other words, even though you're a Thunderfist, they're already treating you like an Anvilmar. That's the condition that comes with taking the hammer, Grede. I thought you understood that by now."

"I only said I'd help the Dark Irons deal with the Firelord. I never said I'd become some kind of king myself." The dwarf turned around. "That's what I thought then, and that's what I think now. At least in Grim Batol, no one brought up anything else. I didn't promise them anything more..."

"King Grimbatildeathwhistle of the Dwarves. A bit long, but otherwise not bad." Jace muttered to himself.

"Don't you dare say that name!" The dwarf pointed at the warlock. "Don't you dare say 'Dwarf King'!"

Seeing Grede like that, Jace couldn't help imagining what it would be like if one day the Three Hammers truly reunited, and he was forced to become the new High King, and this all got updated into the game.

Marching up to the throne in Ironforge, clicking on that black-bearded Wildhammer's big head over and over: "Greetings, champion. Welcome to Ironforge." "My name is Grede Thunderfist. I'm no High King. They forced me into it." "I never wanted to be king!" "This is all the fault of some human named Jace Sesso. I'd pay ten gold for his head."

Just thinking about it made it hard for Jace to suppress a grin.

"Trust me, mage. Next time we meet, I'll still be this same Grede Thunderfist. Not one bit different!" Grede emphasized solemnly.

"If that's how you feel, Thunderfist Lord." Amy said.

"Safe travels, Amy." Vereesa hugged Amy. "When you get back to Dalaran, don't forget to write to me and Jace."

"It'd be great if you could remind Jace to write to the people who care about him more often." Amy took her hand.

"This male witch..." Vereesa shot a sidelong glance at Jace. "Getting him to write a letter is harder than making him recite an entire grimoire from memory using demonic incantations."

"I'll remember. I'll remember." Jace said hastily.

"As well as you remembered to finish my father's homework?" Amy asked.

"Ahem." Jace cleared his throat guiltily.

"Come find me in Dalaran after you're done with Blackrock Mountain, Amy!" Needleflame said. "Why don't you teach me some frost elemental magic?"

"Are you serious, black dragon?" Jace asked.

"Why don't you teach me instead?" Needleflame shot back.

"That's not what I meant..." Jace muttered.

A cry came from the gryphon roost in the distance, as if reminding them that time was running short.

Amy lowered her head. "This reminds me of when you went through that door from Ilgalar Tower. I hope you can come back safely, just like that adventure."

"Your words, Lady Marin." Grede smiled.

Amy hugged Jace. "I really enjoyed the trip to Lake Valar, Jace. I hope we can go hiking like that again sometime."

"There'll definitely be opportunities." Jace said.

The mage smiled and nodded, slung her bag over her shoulder, and walked toward the gryphon roost. The group waved, their smiles gradually fading.

After seeing Amy off, it was time for them to return to Stormwind. They left Menethil Harbor, leaving the main road to head north toward the marshes, so as not to startle any unwitting dwarf travelers when they took off.

"When Blackrock Mountain is over, I want to go back to Kalimdor to find Mossa." Grede walked, watching his step on the wet mud, his tone casual. "Like you told me last time, Jace."

"You really aren't interested in becoming the High King." Vereesa said.

"You think I'm like you? Graduate and immediately want to be a ranger captain, then a ranger lord." Grede said.

Vereesa shot the dwarf an unhappy glare but didn't say anything.

"I'll go with you. Vereesa and I have been wanting to meet the Kaldorei leader, the priestess Tyrande Whisperwind, but we've never found the right opportunity." Jace said.

"Don't forget to bring me, Jace Sesso." Needleflame counted on her fingers. "I want to see kodos, thunder lizards, wind serpents, chimeras..."

"I'd like to go back to Kalimdor too, Jace." Solara said.

"Then after Blackrock Mountain, let's just arrange a trip to Kalimdor!" Grede walked ahead, walking backward. "Everyone go to the other side of the sea together. And this time, let's not have Solara carry us. Let's take a ship. What do you think?"

"Oh, I've never been on a ship before." Solara said, looking excited.

"I'm in favor. Let's do it." Jace said.

"Count me in too." Vereesa said.

"Ah, Vereesa! Then it's settled!"

Needleflame jumped up excitedly and ran past Grede, stomping straight into a stream and splattering mud all over her pants.

"Good thing she can fly, or I wouldn't want to carry this little dirtball." Solara muttered. The companions couldn't help laughing.

They found a clearing under an old tree. Solara and Needleflame transformed back into their draconic forms. Vereesa, Grede, and Morofel climbed onto Solara's back.

Jace was sorting his luggage on this side. Needleflame shook her corncob-like tail mace. "Hurry up and climb on, you're the only one left, Jace Sesso! Such a waste of time!"

"Oh, didn't expect this little black dragon to be so proactive about sharing the team's burden?" Grede scratched his beard. "Come to think of it, when did she start being willing to carry Jace?"

"What?" Needleflame froze, her tail stopping mid-swing. "Who said 'proactive'? They had no choice but to beg me! I don't want to carry this warlock. He reeks of shadowy evil stench!"

With that, the black dragon started to spread her wings to take off. Jace quickly shouted, "It is us begging you for help, Needleflame!"

"It's true that I was struggling a bit, so I asked Needleflame to help, Grede." Solara also chimed in.

Needleflame twisted her neck to study Jace and Solara with her amber snake-eyes, seeming satisfied, then slowly folded her wings.

"That's more like it... But this is the last time, Jace Sesso. Once we get back to Stormwind, it's never happening again! Never ever!"
