## Chapter 1520: A Chest Full of Soul Shards

Jace had dinner at his parents' house, told them about his trip to Dalaran, slept overnight, and headed to the Mage Quarter the next morning.

Archmage Vargoth in Dalaran had said the soul shards might take two weeks to arrive in Stormwind. Only about ten days had passed. But now Grede and Vereesa were already preparing meticulously for the trip to Shadowforge City, and Jace wanted to pick up his new robe from Jalain Aro before they set out. According to the tailor, the only thing left was a surge of powerful soul energy to integrate the various enchantments on the robe. So this should be the finishing touch.

Marin wasn't in his study -- probably teaching. Jace went straight up the Wizard Sanctum's tower to find someone to ask.

He hadn't been hopeful, just thinking he'd check. To his surprise, a Kirin Tor messenger was actually staying in the Mage Quarter, waiting for Jace to return to Stormwind, and had already been there for two days.

Following the address the tower staff gave him, Jace found the messenger's lodging. He'd expected an apprentice, or maybe a student mage who hadn't graduated yet. But the mage wearing a full Kirin Tor insignia was a full mage.

"An honor to meet you, Mr. Sesso." The mage introduced himself. "My name is Nabilius. I'm a student of Archmage Vargoth. You may not have heard of me, but in Draenor, I heard your name countless times."

"You were also part of the expedition?" Jace asked.

"Yes." The mage calling himself Nabilius said. "But at the time, I was still an apprentice -- not yet a full mage of the Kirin Tor. After returning from Draenor, my teacher Master Vargoth quickly secured this insignia for me."

He lifted his finger with some pride. "That's all thanks to you, Master Sesso. Without you and your team, Kirin'Var Village would have broken apart along with the collapsing Farahlon Plains into the Netherstorm. I never want to see that horrifying scene a second time."

Jace smiled awkwardly. The dozens or hundreds of mages who'd survived Kirin'Var Village were either elite members of the Kirin Tor or the close apprentices of those elites and archmages. Otherwise, they wouldn't have bravely followed Turalyon and Khadgar across the Dark Portal.

Antonidas's changed attitude toward him was probably due not just to Khadgar's advocacy, but also to the influence of these elites.

"I heard from Master Vargoth that you and your friends are trying to brew the Potion of Destruction here in Azeroth." Nabilius said. "Though my knowledge of herbalism is very limited, I did participate in the research of the Potion of Destruction's formula with some draenei scholars and alchemy masters in Kirin'Var Village. I was also the first to drink it. If Netherbloom can truly be reproduced in Azeroth, I might be able to help with the next steps."

"The first to drink the Potion of Destruction?" Jace frowned. "I feel like I've heard about you somewhere. Are you that apprentice of Vargoth who, after drinking the potion, reanimated some corpses and used them to repel the orc attack?"

"Ah." Nabilius's expression visibly changed. "It seems they've already told you about that, Master. I was... left with no choice. At the time, I was helping my teacher research a way to deal with Gul'dan's death knights, so I'd been exposed to some soul incantations. The first version of the Potion of Destruction was far from perfect. The side effects of the Netherbloom ingredient were extremely severe. When the orc legions of Gorgrond approached Kirin'Var Village..."

"I understand, Mr. Nabilius." Jace nodded. "In Draenor, even Master Khadgar had to resort to the fel magic of the Skull of Gul'dan to survive. We were all forced to make a lot of crazy choices."

Nabilius breathed a sigh of relief. "I seem to have taken up too much of your time, Master. The soul shards are right here. Let me get them for you."

With that, the mage went back inside. Jace waited outside for a bit, thinking about how this mage, who'd performed necromantic forbidden magic in Draenor, could still freely come and go in the Kirin Tor. They'd really shown great tolerance toward these returning expedition mages.

But the more he thought about it, the more familiar the name Nabilius seemed. Vargoth's student, necromantic magic Nabilius...

Wasn't he the lich that haunted the ruins of Kirin'Var Village in the game?

It had been so many years since he'd transmigrated. No matter how clearly he remembered things right after arriving, his impression of this Outland lich's name wasn't that strong. It just felt like it was probably this name.

But combined with all these clues... it was most likely him.

In another timeline, after Kael'thas followed Illidan to Draenor, in order to seize the Naaru's fleet and plunder its mana, he led the Sunfury Legion to attack Netherstorm and leveled the entire Kirin'Var Village with a terrifying mana bomb.

Though Archmage Vargoth survived thanks to his tower and powerful protective spells, leaving Kael'thas unable to do anything to him for a time, Vargoth himself was trapped in the tower and couldn't go out. Most of the other mages who'd survived Draenor's destruction died in that bomb -- either turned to ash or, sustained by residual mana, became mana ghosts wandering the ruins.

One of Vargoth's surviving students tried to use necromancy to drain the mana from these ghosts. By the time adventurers reached this village, he'd already transformed into a lich.

That student, skilled in necromancy, was very likely the man in that room.

It was hard to imagine how he'd transformed into a lich. Jace guessed it might have something to do with Netherstorm's corrupting magic, or perhaps even with the research on the Potion of Destruction. After all, it was hard to imagine that a potion powerful enough to drain the mana of a nether dragon and help him shatter Sargeras's scepter in one blow had only "high cost" as its downside.

Before long, Nabilius came out carrying a chest decorated with the Violet Eye and handed it to Jace.

"This is what you need, Master Sesso. Demon soul shards from the Auchindoun Shadow Council. I've already unlocked it for you. Master Vargoth counted a total of eighty from our Draenor magical item storage list. Do you need to count them?"

"No need, sir." Jace smiled.

Nabilius added, "Master Vargoth also asked me to pass on a message. He's very grateful that you informed Archmage Antonidas about the soul shards in advance, and he thanks you for the letter you left him."

"About that." Jace said. "Tell him there's no need to thank me. It was the least I could do. If there's nothing else, I'll take my leave."

"Go ahead, Master Sesso." Nabilius said. "If you need my help with the Potion of Destruction later, feel free to write to me anytime."

"Thank you. I'll remember." Jace nodded.

Leaving the building, Jace glanced back up at the floor where the mage lived.

It was hard to call Nabilius a particularly upright mage. But at the very least, before Kael'thas destroyed Kirin'Var Village, he'd followed his teacher to join the Sons of Lothar in the Draenor expedition. After that world was destroyed, he hadn't abandoned the Kirin Tor. It was also hard to call him a selfish, evil person.

Either way, Jace felt pretty good about having helped most of Kirin'Var Village's mages return safely to Dalaran. Even if that meant harboring a potential lich.

Box in hand, Jace hurried to the Slaughtered Lamb's basement. He'd originally come to find a spot to count the soul shards and check their quality, not expecting to see Jalain Aro. But the tailor was sitting in the main hall when he burst in, and they locked eyes immediately.

"Jace, you really are back from Dalaran." Jalain rose. "Ursula said you should be back around these few days. She really does know you well."

"You've been waiting here for me?" Jace asked in surprise.

"Pretty much. But only for two days." Jalain stared at the box in Jace's hand. "Erland hasn't returned from Booty Bay yet, but I know he can't guarantee bringing back all thirty-plus soul shards. I haven't been able to buy many real demon soul shards either. The key is what you've brought from Dalaran... Is this box for Ursula?"

"No." Jace put the box on the table. "Since you're here, let's count them together. An archmage in Dalaran told me there are eighty demon soul shards in here, all seized from the Shadow Council warlocks in Auchindoun."

Jalain's eyes widened. "How much did you pay?"

"An Alterac goat." Jace said.

"What?" The tailor was stunned.

Jace tried to lift the box lid but couldn't get it open. He felt around for a lock or opening mechanism but found nothing.

"What's going on? The guy who delivered it said he'd already unlocked it."

Jalain muttered a quick incantation, and the box popped open with a bang, revealing a black runecloth bundle inside.

"Basic unlocking spell. For an apprentice who's finished three years of magic schooling at any of the Seven Kingdoms' academies and reached the apprentice stage, it's about as hard as an unlocked door."

"I studied the Abjuration curriculum at the Wizard Sanctum." Jace said.

Jalain narrowed her eyes. Jace added, "And I didn't finish three years."
