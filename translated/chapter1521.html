Chapter 1521: Common Ground Between Enchanting and Simulation

The black runecloth bundle opened, and the dim basement hall grew even more eerie under the pale purple glow spilling out. Jace felt like he was back in Auchindoun, in the Shadow Labyrinth -- that place littered with soul shards and walking demons, where the light of souls swirled like mist through the colonnades.

Jalain's eyes went wide. She picked up one of the soul shards and studied it for a long time before muttering, "In the years since I left Alterac, the price of an Alterac ram must have gone up a hundred times over."

"This is a Doomlord's soul shard -- a variant of the Ered'ruin further twisted by fel magic. Its abdomen is a huge maw of evil used to spew the savage fel fire its body can barely contain." The tailor put the shard back. "Even among the army that attacked Nethergarde Keep through the Dark Portal, this kind of demon was rare. But look, Jace -- just from what I've seen, there are already three of them in here."

Master Vargoth had really come through. When Jace was in Auchindoun counting soul shards with those Shadow Council warlocks, preparing to power the portal, most of the shards he'd seen weren't as good as the ones in this bag. Only the ones drawn from the draenei ancestral spirits could compare.

"Is this enough?" Jace asked.

"When Erland gets back from Booty Bay, there'll be plenty of Felguard and Doomguard soul shards too." Jalain's gaze seemed stuck on the glittering shards in the bag. She had to force herself to look away, shifting her eyes to Jace's face. "Are you in a hurry to finish the robe? Erland took a gryphon to Booty Bay. Including the time he needs to handle formalities at the trade office... based on his usual efficiency, I'd say the latest will be this weekend -- the day after tomorrow."

"You know the king plans to attack Blackrock Spire." Jace said. "The northerners, the dwarves, and the Sons of Lothar have already responded to the king's call. So I think it'll be soon. After all, we're facing Onyxia's brother this time, and I still don't have a decent robe. But if it would affect the quality..."

"As long as you help personally, Jace." Jalain said. "This final step -- using demon souls to strengthen and guide the different enchantments on the robe -- doesn't require tailoring knowledge. With your ability to manipulate souls and shadow, I just need to briefly explain some key points of the enchanting school and a few special things to watch out for. You'll definitely do it better and faster than me."

"I took a few enchanting lessons at the Wizard Sanctum." Jace said.

"Even setting that aside, you at least know Arcane Intellect. That's enough." Jalain got up and quickly ran to another room, emerging with several bottles of dark yellow pigment. "Let's try to understand how Gul'dan's soul shard transformation ritual can be applied to tailoring enchantments. Actually, Gul'dan's notes also mention using demon souls to strengthen enchanted items. Not robes, but the principle is similar."

Jace followed the tailor to the practice room. Jalain quickly used the pigment to draw a simplified soul circle on the floor. Before long, Ursula appeared at the door.

"So it's you two drawing circles in here. I was wondering what that wild steelbloom smell was. Thought I might have accidentally put the wrong thing in my cauldron." Ursula looked at Jace. "You're back, Jace. I heard the noise from the alchemy lab just now and guessed you must be back from Dalaran. But I had a cauldron of Disaster Oil at a critical stage and couldn't get away."

"By the way, did you deliver my letter? Don't tell me you got so caught up touring the beautiful scenery of Lake Lordamere with Lady Windrunner that you completely forgot about my business."

"Of course I delivered it." Jace said. "Now every plant in the botanical garden knows that a Stormwind alchemist summoned the evil Jace Sesso back from the other side of the Dark Portal."

"That's not a bad thing." Ursula crossed her arms and leaned against the doorframe. "This way, the reputation of Arkhana's Secret Star Alchemy Lab can spread across all Seven Kingdoms, and I won't have to worry about money to keep perfecting the Disaster Oil."

"What exactly is Disaster Oil? I wanted to ask you earlier." Jace said.

"My own name for it." Ursula said, quite proudly. "I'm referencing the notes of that draenei herbalist and the accounts of many survivors of Draenor's destruction, summarizing what they saw and felt, to make a special Shadow Oil. I've named it Disaster Oil. Progress is going well, though it's extremely expensive. But as long as I think about the gold I can drain out of Dalaran once Netherbloom is successfully cultivated, it's all worth it."

Jace nodded. But thinking about the Kirin Tor mage Nabilius he'd just met, and the possibility that his transformation into a lich might be related to Netherbloom or even the Potion of Destruction, made it hard to feel completely happy about Ursula's progress.

Then again, he'd drunk the Potion of Destruction twice himself. Aside from extreme fatigue, nothing had gone wrong. Khadgar had drunk it once too, and it had saved everyone in a watchtower that was about to fall into the Twisting Nether, with no issues either. Probably, like Nabilius had said, the formula wasn't refined back then.

"So what about you two? What's this circle for?" Ursula asked.

"Soul ritual." Jalain said as she drew. "A dry run. That way, once Erland gets back to Stormwind in two days, Jace and I can immediately start the final step of the robe."

"You're planning to perform a Soul Well in Stormwind?" The alchemist was startled.

"No." Jace said. "A Soul Well... is essentially a large-scale, extremely destructive 'Soul Drain' that converts souls into usable energy, or makes soul shards. Now that we already have the soul shards, we don't need the Soul Well. We go directly to the next stage -- the 'utilization' phase. Like the enchanting ritual you see here."

"Alright." The alchemist finally relaxed.

Jalain stood up and pressed a hand to her lower back. "We'll perform the ritual at Telogrus Rift. I found a floating island fragment there that's nearly perfect in every way -- ideal for completing the final step of the robe."

"Almost done, Jace." She said. "Perfect timing, Ursula. You're here too. Help us sort the soul shards by strength. Place the most powerful demon souls in the center, the next strongest in the upper left, the weakest in the lower left circle. The rune on the right will hold the Nexus Crystal. When the time comes, Jace, you'll stand here holding the rune rod. Stand here for now. What material the rune rod should be made of -- I'll need to estimate how much soul energy we'll have at the end."

The three of them sorted the dozens of crystals by category. Jace walked to the spot the tailor had indicated. Jalain observed the entire circle as a whole and clapped her hands. "This might be the most powerful enchanting ritual I've ever participated in. We've got almost an entire demon legion here, plus an incredibly powerful demon summoner at the center. I'll need to prepare the enchanting rune rod carefully."

With that, Jalain handed Jace a scroll. "The enchanting incantation, and the method for waving the rune rod to channel the mana, are in here. But the whole ritual isn't just about chanting the spell."

"The souls in the soul shards are pure energy. They don't have consciousness like real spirits. Naturally. Otherwise, they wouldn't easily follow our incantation commands to power complex and intricate spells."

Jalain's words made Jace think, oddly, of the voices of the souls when he'd guided Gul'dan's Hand to blast Azgoth outside the walls of Nethergarde Keep.

The ancestral spirits on the battlefield certainly weren't pure energy. He could hear their shouts. He could feel each soul's intensely firm will. Yet they had let him guide them into that dark meteor so smoothly.

Because it was their last wish -- revenge. Rather than disappearing, they wanted to obliterate that demon lord who was trying to breach Nethergarde Keep's walls.

That killing intent had been so fierce that Jace felt if he hadn't gone along with the spirits' will, they might have torn him apart instead.

Jalain continued, "But the purpose of this enchanting ritual is to use demons to guide the shadow and fel enchanting spells to merge. So they need to have a certain degree of 'simple awareness' to achieve this. That depends on the enchanter's skill. With the help of the Nexus Crystal and enchanting rune rod, you need to use your own will to give these demon souls an initial 'driving force,' allowing them to merge with the robe and the competing enchantments on it."

"That sounds complicated." Ursula said.

"The mages of Dalaran often say there are no easy successes in this world." Jalain said. "That might not hold true in the realm of shadow, but it's pretty accurate for dark magic enchanting."

"Now close your eyes. Can you feel those ethereal soul energies, Jace?"

"Yes." Jace said.

The soul shards on the simple pigment circle began to pulse in time with Jace's breathing, their light brightening and dimming in the same rhythm.

Jalain observed carefully. Slowly, even her breathing matched the same frequency.

"This is what I mean, Jace... This is work meant for you." She couldn't keep the excitement out of her voice, even as she kept the volume down. "When we're on the real circle, painted with demon thick blood and Shadow Oil, with Nexus Crystals and rune rods, you'll feel it even deeper and more distinctly!"

But Jace knew that without the time he'd spent using the Book of Dark Secrets to understand Medivh's Dark Simulation, he would never have been able to control the remnant demon souls in these soul shards as quickly as Jalain had directed.
