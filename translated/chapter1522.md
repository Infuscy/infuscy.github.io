## Chapter 1522: The Beauty of Power

On a floating island at the edge of the Telogrus Rift, void energy swept through the cracks in the boulders, filling the entire space with a foul odor somewhere between thick blood and sea fish. Jace knew this was the smell of savage desire made manifest.

Erland, Jalain, Ursula, and Jace were gathered here. Also present were the warlocks' imps -- including Sazeno -- as well as Morofel and two Felhounds that followed Ursula and Jalain.

Jace and Jalain had finished drawing the circle and placed the soul shards in their respective positions. Once the circle was complete, Jalain waved behind her. Her succubus walked up to the circle carrying a delicate storage box.

The demon opened the lid and took out a black-purple coat. It was a short robe, just what Jace wanted. Ever since that time he'd tried to imitate the mages of Dalaran and the Wizard Sanctum by wearing a full-length robe and found it slowed him down, he'd gotten used to lighter enchanted coats.

And the other two in the Dagger Guild who frequently went out -- Sulenna and Erland -- were the same. They both preferred shorter robes.

Some parts of the coat had been sewn with demon hide. Jace didn't know what demon it was from, but it looked like scale armor covering the chest and shoulders -- apparently meant to reinforce protection, or maybe to contain something within the robe itself. These demon hides weren't just stitched on either; they'd been specially enchanted with metal chains binding them in place.

Looking closely, the whole coat seemed almost full of holes from all these additions. But it formed a unique, darkly aesthetic style. Very Jalain Aro.

"You've been going on about it for months, Jalain, but this is the first time I've seen the whole robe complete." Erland stared at it. "Is this still mooncloth? It doesn't look anything like the mooncloth I know. At least not like Galders's."

"It's not pure mooncloth." Jalain said. "Pure mooncloth can't handle pit lord hide, so I made some magical modifications after consulting a friend who's a demonologist in Dalaran."

"You mean..." Jace asked. "Those trimmed demon hides are Azgoth's skin?"

"I considered Azgoth at first." Jalain said. "But that demon lord's corpse was completely taken by the Kirin Tor. These days, forget getting his skin -- even getting his blood is nearly impossible. Communicating with the Kirin Tor supplies management people is harder than trading incantations with the Dark Iron dwarves."

"Later I heard the expedition had something similar, so I went to your beloved Lady Vereesa Windrunner. When I said it was for your robe, she agreed without hesitation."

"Through her connections, I got a very good piece of pit lord hide from the Farstriders. Then I went to Dalaran to commission someone to trim and further process it. It survived one failed enchanting attempt without any real damage, which proves my choice was right."

"What's that pit lord's name? It's not Magtheridon, is it?" Jace asked.

"It's definitely not Brutallus." Morofel said. "His corpse collapsed into the Twisting Nether along with Kil'jaeden's Throne. I don't believe the Farstriders could have fished out any pieces from that abyss."

"Draenor had more than those two pit lords." Jace said.

"A Farstrider told me the demons called that pit lord Achna." Jalain said. "The Alliance Expedition and the draenei defeated that demon lord together. But they couldn't recover the entire body -- they only took part of it as proof of the kill and as a sample for the mages in Kirin'Var Village to study. This skin is part of the Farstriders' 'spoils of war.'"

Achna. A greater demon that died in a fel-fire valley north of Hellfire Citadel. By the time players got there, only a giant skeleton remained of his corpse.

In the game, this pit lord's blood had flowed into Hellfire Citadel, turning the fortress's underground into a stinking demon-blood sewer.

Jace remembered passing through that sewer when entering the arena of the Shattered Halls. Though it was filled with demon stench, he hadn't seen massive amounts of demon blood. Back then, he'd thought Achna hadn't died yet. But actually, the pit lord's blood probably just hadn't seeped deep enough in the decade-plus since.

Achna called himself the Lord of the Abyss. Whether or not the true pit lord leader Mannoroth agreed, Achna was undoubtedly a stronger demon lord than Azgoth. Using a piece of his hide to strengthen a robe meant this robe would definitely be exceptional.

Jalain laid the robe on the stone altar in front of the circle and turned back to hand Jace a bright copper-colored metal rod with a gleaming Eye of the Dragon. "Hold this end, Jace. I'm going to use the magic crystal to reactivate the temporarily sealed enchantments on the robe. Shadow and fel will clash. That's when you use this rune-imbued Arcanite Rod to complete the final step of the robe."

Jace took the metal rod and walked to his position on the circle. "I'm ready."

"Then let's begin." Jalain chanted from a book in her hands. The mana crystal in her hand burned up in a flash of light, the last gleam carrying a tint of shadowy purple and strange fel green.

The robe on the altar suddenly burst into violent flame, accompanied by roars and sharp screeches like a demon's. Jace even felt the shadow-wind around the floating island churning in response.

"Now, Jace." Jalain stared at the robe. "Even if Achna's demon hide won't burn, the multiple enchantments I painstakingly applied will gradually be consumed in this struggle!"

Jace closed his eyes, immersing himself in the struggle between the demonic power and shadow magic. The fury and madness of fel, the gloom and cold of shadow. In his perception, it was almost like a battle between a group of Voidwalkers and a group of demons on that altar.

He began chanting Jalain's incantation, channeling the power of a hundred soul shards. One by one, the shards shattered -- from the outer edge inward, weakest to strongest. In his demonic vision, the souls swirled around the altar, gathering thicker and thicker under the guidance of his incantation. The howling of demon souls erupted into roars under the influence of his will.

The Voidwalkers and demons didn't stop fighting because of this. They kept clashing violently, ignoring the chaos gathering around them.

But before the incantation was complete, swarms of demon souls enveloped them. Some pulled at their arms and legs. Some devoured the magical energy they released. Some even poured into them, merging with both sides.

Gradually, in the storm of souls and the rift of the Twisting Nether, under the gaze of the ritual's director, the two warring shadowy masses were fused into a single whole by the souls.

In Jace's demonic vision, he seemed to behold something between a void creature and a demon. It flailed wildly, spread its wings, its horns curling upward arrogantly. But its entire body was the shape of a black-purple phantom, constantly emitting spinning shadowy arcane light.

The ritual was complete. His sight returned to reality. The entire circle was scorched. All the soul shards were reduced to powder and gray fragments.

He looked up at the robe on the altar. Green flame and shadow burned across it. Demon skull faces appeared in multiple places on the robe, like swollen pustules -- forming, materializing, opening their mouths as if bursting to spew clouds of dark magic mist, then fading away.

In the end, a few demon heads didn't fade. They stayed permanently on the robe. One had even burned a piece of Achna's hide on the shoulder into the shape of a demon skull.

When everything settled again, the robe was "disfigured." The warlocks stared at its "agonizing struggle," almost speechless. Even Jalain herself was stunned into silence, until the robe quieted and slid to the floor.

"How is it?" Jace asked.

"Close to perfect, Jace." Jalain nodded. "The enchantments... all the enchantments held. Not only did they maintain the power I originally estimated, but they've been strengthened by the infusion of so many demon souls. This robe won't just be an excellent aid for the wearer in hunting demons -- it even has potential. I can feel that there's still room for further enchanting. Instead of being... at its limit."

"But you said 'close to perfect'?" Ursula asked. "What's the problem?"

"If I had to say what's wrong." Jalain walked over and tentatively touched the twisted demon face frozen on Achna's hide, looking utterly anguished. "If I were you, Jace, I definitely wouldn't try wearing this to the king's court banquet. Our ritual has completely destroyed the last shred of... aesthetic restraint I'd maintained on it."

"I don't think so." Erland's finger rested on his lips as he studied the coat. "It looks great. It'd be very popular in Booty Bay, at least. It has a certain... a certain..."

"Beauty of power." Morofel completed his sentence. "For once, I agree with you, Erland Duthmoor."
