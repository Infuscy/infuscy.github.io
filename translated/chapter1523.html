Chapter 1523: Valuables Requiring a Dark Iron Escort

The Pig and Whistle was as lively as ever. Since it was only the beginning of October and the Brewfest atmosphere hadn't fully faded yet, the main hall was filled with groups of Bronzebeard dwarves clinking mugs, singing songs at each other, and shouting stupid jokes that were boring but still demanded laughter.

Clearly, "Brewfest is over" was as hard for dwarves to accept as "I can't beat him."

The whole team had gathered here tonight. The barmaid stood nearby for a while as Solara ordered a huge pile of various roasted and steamed meats and soups. The number of carnivores in this group just kept growing.

Jace sat in a corner, pondering one question: what should this robe be called?

The robe couldn't be worn immediately after completion. According to Jalain, she needed to do some brief post-processing to stabilize it -- what she called the "tempering" phase of enchanting.

Jace had no objection. It wasn't like he could wear this terrifying demon robe to the Gilded Rose to buy raisins. As long as he had it in hand before they left for Shadowforge City, that was good enough.

But without the robe physically present, naming it with nothing to go on felt a bit ungrounded.

Honestly, he didn't have a habit of naming things he owned. Even without a name, it wouldn't stop him from wearing it, and it wouldn't stop it from helping him cast spells and defend against attacks.

But Jalain Aro insisted he name the robe. And considering the rarity of Achna's demon hide, the rare opportunity of Jace Sesso personally enchanting it... this short coat was probably one of a kind in this world. Even if future products were made based on the design and crafting experience derived from it, they would struggle to compare.

So whether out of commemorative significance or respect for Jalain's hard work, it needed a name.

"Morofel's Defeat" from before wasn't bad. Even though that robe had been destroyed by Sabellian's own Flame Whip, calling it that still felt like destroying Morofel's defeat. Especially paired with the fateful battle between Morofel and Brutallus in the hall at the bottom of the spiral stairs -- a battle Jace had unfortunately not witnessed -- and that bloody victory... there was a sense of "echo."

Thinking of this, he looked toward Morofel, sitting in the shadowy corner by the firelight. She was staring coldly back at Jace, because she knew exactly what he was thinking. Her response was clear: don't come up with a stupid name like Sazeno did.

Jace lowered his eyebrows in a helpless expression that said, "Then you come up with one."

Morofel's eyes flicked to the side, then she rested her chin on her hand and drifted off.

Clearly, even for a demon who'd heard the names of all the greater demons and seen the titles of all the Legion's terrifying artifacts, naming wasn't an easy job.

The meaty aroma on the table grew stronger. Grede and Needleflame were drinking and trying to identify each piece of meat in a big plate of Redridge mixed grill. Solara was stealthily picking meaty bone scraps from the other side and sneaking them into her mouth.

Mid-chew, she suddenly stopped. Her gaze lifted toward the doorway downstairs. Her eyes twitched slightly, as if observing something carefully.

Last time they'd run into Aegwynn here, so Jace was instinctively alert whenever Solara got sensitive. He also looked toward the doorway, and saw a female dwarf walking in alone, standing at the entrance and looking up at them.

While female dwarves were much rarer than males outside of dwarven settlements, seeing one in a tavern right after Brewfest wasn't that strange. But just like Solara, Jace felt there was something off about this female dwarf.

As for what exactly was off... Jace figured it out. The clothes she was wearing were exactly the same as the casual outfits Vereesa usually liked.

The female dwarf trotted up the steps to their table. Grede noticed her and froze, half a piece of bread dropping from his hand onto the table.

"Well, strike me with lightning." Grede's eyes scanned this unfamiliar dwarf up and down. "Whether it's a female dwarf wearing elven clothes, or an elf who's turned into a female dwarf, either way it's terrifying!"

"Ver... Vereesa? Is that you?" Solara asked quietly.

"Of course it's me." The female dwarf sat down next to Jace. "This is my preparation for the upcoming plan. What do you think? The mage on my side couldn't turn me into a Dark Iron, so I could only try a Bronzebeard dwarf first, to get used to this clumsy new shape. Not bad so far."

Grede was already covering his mouth, trying to hold back laughter. The dwarf-formed Vereesa shot him a look. "Many times, even the most noble magisters have had to temporarily turn into sewer rats to deal with the shameless thieves who stole their important assets. This is not funny."

"Funny or not, I'm already laughing, Lady Vereesa." Grede forced out through clenched teeth.

Needleflame also choked out a mouthful of beer with a "pfft," muttering, "When you turn into a dwarf, will your arms suddenly be too short to draw your own bow?"

Grede's barely contained laughter disappeared completely. He blew out his beard. "Too short? Little thing, do you think I haven't drawn her magic bow before?!"

"Short short short, it's short!" The black dragon shouted with her eyes squeezed shut.

"Black black black, blacker than a Dark Iron!"

"Heh. Black is the noblest color. What would a dirt-clod raised in the woods know about that?"

"Still so noble after letting that warlock ride you?"

"Wah!" Needleflame shot up from her chair with a bang, turned, and dove into Vereesa's arms. "Find a chance to slit this dumb shorty's throat together, Sister Vereesa. I want him to shut up forever!"

"Better prepare a blade that can cut through thorium, you mini-killer!" Grede rumbled in a low voice.

"Wait another two hundred years for me to grow. I'll peel off a scale and split your shiny bald head!"

"By then I'll have long flown back to Kirthaven. No need for Her Highness to trouble herself!"

The dwarf and the black dragon went back and forth, while Vereesa, who should have been the target of their mockery, sat back and enjoyed the show.

"Jace." Solara leaned closer. "Do we still need to disguise ourselves as orcs and enter from the front of Blackrock Mountain?"

"Not this time." Jace said quietly. "SI:7 will send someone to lead us through a passage into Shadowforge City, bypassing the orc blockade and entering Blackrock Mountain from within. According to their intelligence, it's a very hidden passage for smuggling important supplies. Can't fit too many people. Sneaking in through there will definitely be easier than charging straight through the front gate past hundreds or thousands of orc soldiers."

"That's reassuring." Solara said. "Don't let Needleflame transform back and forth while carrying the warhammer. Severe mana drain plus the burden of that thing... will mess up the transformation."

"Like letting my tail slip out, Sister Solara?" Needleflame turned to ask.

"Go back to arguing with your good brother Thunderfist and leave me out of it!" Solara said crossly.

"So if we're using that passage, what important goods do we need to smuggle in?" Vereesa asked.

"What excuse did you use to enter Shadowforge City last time?" Grede asked.

"Exchanging warlock incantations." Solara said. "But that time there were only two of us, plus a demon. This time there are too many of us, and most of us aren't warlocks and don't have demon servants. That excuse seems a bit flimsy."

"We do have important goods." Jace looked at Vereesa. "Recently, an extremely rare demon robe has appeared in the human kingdoms. And its mysterious creator is looking for a discerning buyer."

"What kind of robe would be worth such a large Dark Iron mercenary escort?" Grede glanced into the shadows with a grin. "Morofel's Victory?"

Morofel tilted her head unhappily. Jace lowered his voice. "It'll be worth it... you'll understand when you see it."
