Chapter 1526: Transformed and Onward

After letting that group of Dark Iron dwarves pass, they immediately quickened their pace deeper into the tunnel, eventually emerging into a wide stone quarry-like cavern. Jace found the place somewhat familiar — it seemed they'd reached the main road into Shadowforge City. Though no figures were visible, the faint murmur of distant Dark Iron dwarves occasionally drifted over.

The group found a concealed high spot and waited quietly for a while, confirming everything seemed normal. Vereesa murmured to the little black dragon: "Didn't know you had some acting talent, Sora's bodyguard."

"I already showed it back in Auchenai," Sora said.

"To be honest, I wouldn't call it acting," Grede said. "She still had to hold back some of her innate evil nature, or even the cunning, vicious Dark Iron and Blackrock orcs would've been scared stiff. Just think about that panicky guy back there."

"Innate evil nature? I'm still far behind a certain sly male warlock!" Needleflame shook her head.

"Ahem." Jace cleared his throat. "Let's focus on finding Vaelastrasz... The Red Dragonqueen told me he'd be waiting halfway between Blackrock Spire and Shadowforge City. Based on my experience from my last visit here, we should be able to find him heading that way."

Vereesa followed Jace's gaze to a Dark Iron dwarf in a fiery robe standing on the stairs in the distance. "We need to wait for that dwarven mage to leave."

"But he's been standing there for a while," Grede said. "We've watched two squads go into the passage from here, and that mage still hasn't moved."

Vereesa narrowed her eyes. "Do you think he's waiting for someone?"

"How can you tell?" Jace asked.

"He keeps glancing toward that passage over there, every so often. His expression looks like he's waiting for something," Vereesa said.

"I can't even make out the color of his beard from here," Needleflame said, shocked.

"Don't think the Quel'dorei are inferior to black dragons in everything, little one!" Vereesa tapped Needleflame's helmet lightly.

"When I'm grown, I'll be able to see clearly..." Needleflame muttered, still unconvinced.

Jace scratched the stubble on his chin. "So... could that be Vaelastrasz?"

"I can't tell at all," Sora said, shaking her head.

"He's the master of disguise in the red dragonflight, Sora," Jace said. "Show him Vereesa's earring. He's the Red Dragonqueen's guard, so he'll recognize the Queen's gift. If he doesn't, pretend you're trying to sell it to him as a treasure. Don't sell no matter what he offers."

"Got it." Sora nodded, took Vereesa's earring, and stepped out from cover toward the Dark Iron mage.

"Did the Red Dragonqueen give each of you a gift?" Needleflame asked curiously.

"Yes," Vereesa said.

"Morophil too?"

"Of course," the demon replied. "Giving the master is as good as giving me."

Vereesa coughed sharply. Jace kept his eyes on Sora, who was now talking to the dwarven mage, and forced a bit of saliva down his dry throat.

Needleflame looked at the three of them, then wisely shuffled closer to Grede and whispered, "So what did the Queen give you?"

"Ah, I never mentioned it?" The dwarf pulled the necklace from his collar. "This. I shouldn't actually be showing this near Blackrock Spire right now, because if I tap into its power, it'll attract—"

Jace and Vereesa suddenly realized the problem too. Right. This necklace was the token Deathwing had given to the Red Dragonqueen before he went mad. Which meant the power it contained was the same as what Needleflame wielded.

"A beautiful necklace, beautiful stone, Thunderfist." Needleflame stared at the golden gem.

There was movement from Sora's direction. The red-robed Dark Iron mage was following her back toward them. Looked like Jace's guess was right.

"Ah, everyone. Was my disguise that obvious?" The dwarven mage climbed up to their hiding spot, dusting himself off. "Even the flamewardens guarding the fire elemental lords didn't notice anything wrong with me. Truth be told, I'd rather have caught you than been caught out by you."

"But I'm still glad to meet you all." A flicker of reptile eye passed through the dwarf's red gaze before finally settling on Needleflame. "The Red Dragonqueen's messenger told me there was one among you who'd returned from Draenor... a daughter of Deathwing."

"I am Prince Sabellian's daughter," Needleflame emphasized.

"Prince Sabellian, of course." The dwarf studied the black dragon carefully before speaking. "I am Vaelastrasz. An honor to meet you, Lady Needleflame."

"Likewise, red dragon." The black dragon's tone was solemn, carrying a hint of the dignity Sabellian or Grelorio might have mustered.

"Truly hard to imagine," Vaelastrasz said quietly. "In all my years in Blackrock Spire, I've dealt with countless black dragons — all sorts of dragon-blooded dragonkin, whelps, drakes, even Victor Nefarius himself."

"But looking at you... I know you're a black dragon, I know Nefarian's blood flows in your veins — or Deathwing's, rather — but something feels different. As if..."

"As if ten thousand years of hatred no longer exists," Sora said.

"Yes, Somniel, that's exactly it." Vaelastrasz's voice was low. "Strange. Truly strange. I can hardly imagine what the Queen would feel when she sees you — as someone who's witnessed the great changes of tens of thousands of years."

"Enough of that." Seeing Needleflame's slightly uneasy expression, he shook his head. "Welcome back to Azeroth, little one. Your true home. Jace Sesso is a genuinely good man. I'm glad he brought you back."

"Not at all." Needleflame curled her lip and muttered.

"Black dragons have no conscience," Jace said coldly. "In my eyes, she's no different from Onyxia, Vaelastrasz."

"At least she won't burn down Stormwind in one breath." Vaelastrasz chuckled and pulled three gray gems from his pocket, handing them to Jace, Vereesa, and Grede.

"Can't say that for sure now." Jace accepted the gem. "How do you use it?"

"The incantation is 'Valastelliquon.'" The red dragon said, "Grip the gem tightly and speak it."

Jace gripped the gem and intoned, "Valastelliquon."

Suddenly, a burst of flame enveloped him. When he opened his eyes, everyone around him was a head taller, and he himself now had a full face of reddish-brown beard, gray-black skin, and eyes that glowed with malevolent red light.

Grede and Vereesa followed suit, and soon everyone had transformed into Dark Iron dwarves. Grede looked largely the same except for his now-red eyes and gray skin.

"You're so ugly, Jace!" Grede coughed with laughter. "Even as a dwarf you look pointy-faced! That gem of yours must've been mixed with troll blood!"

"Then thank goodness for that troll blood." Jace smiled and tapped Grede on the forehead. "I'm still taller than you even as a dwarf."

"What?" Grede checked their footing. "You are! Hey, Master Vaelastrasz... this gem—"

"The three gems are identical. The transformation effect is influenced by many factors. I had to prioritize stability," the red dragon said. "These gems will maintain the transformation for a long time. Based on my own experiments, they should hold stable for at least two weeks, so you don't need to worry about waking up and finding it gone."

"During the transformation, you can use magical items, but try not to cast any particularly powerful spells. With weaker spells, at least the transformation won't collapse immediately. If it does fail, wait for it to fully dissipate before using the gem to recast the incantation. It'll need about three to four days to recharge using the heat of this volcano."

"That's more than enough, Vaelastrasz." Jace said. "Thank you very much."

"Don't mention it, Jace." Vaelastrasz said. "I need to get back to Blackrock Spire quickly. I wish you all the best of luck."
