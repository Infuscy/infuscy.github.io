## Chapter 1528: Inspection? Inspect This!

"Her master is right here!" Jace raised his wrapped staff and shook it, speaking in Dwarvish. "I'm right here, gentlemen. Have we done something wrong?"

"Nothing wrong, warlock." The Ironfury officer, wearing heavy leather armor with a short blade at his hip, looked Jace over with eyes like clotted blood, then glanced at Morophil before speaking. "Every group entering Shadowforge City must undergo a thorough inspection. Didn't you know?"

"After what happened at the gate just now, I thought that rule had been suspended," Jace said.

"Are they with you?" The officer looked at the others beside Jace.

"Strictly speaking, I'm accompanying this lady here," Jace said.

"Firelord protect." Sora nodded slightly.

"For Ragnaros's victory, madam." The Ironfury officer's tone immediately sharpened with respect. He puffed out his chest and gave a fist-to-chest salute. "We'll need to open your luggage, goods, and bundles for inspection by the lads. That staff's wrappings too. I trust this Shadowforge heat won't ruin the magic gems inside."

"Of course, sir," Sora said.

Jace nodded too and exchanged glances with Grede and Vereesa behind him. They'd all mentally prepared for this, so there was no resistance.

Bael'Gar the molten giant stood at the crossroads of the Dark Iron thoroughfare ahead, his burning, lava-encrusted form glaring in their direction. The heat radiating from him was even more oppressive than the Ironfury soldiers' interrogation.

Grede shot the giant a glance, his eyes full of contempt. He'd already helped kill one giant up north during the Excavation Site raid. He knew this one wouldn't live long either.

To free the Dark Iron dwarves from the Firelord's rule, this watchdog Ragnaros had sent to Shadowforge City would have to die too.

"While we inspect, I have a few questions, ladies and gentlemen." The Ironfury officer fixed his gaze on Sora. "What did you leave Shadowforge City to do?"

"I'm a traveling merchant, sir." Sora answered as Jace had taught her. "I've been searching for magical treasures outside all these years, bringing them back to Shadowforge City, making some coin in between."

"What kind of treasure... is worth slipping back through a secret passage at a time like this, risking the orcs?" The Ironfury officer frowned.

"A very worthy treasure." Jace smiled. Sora nodded in agreement.

The officer's gaze shifted between them. Nearby, a soldier held up the bow of Eranikus. "What's this? A wooden staff?"

"Yes." Vereesa answered curtly. "A staff."

"What surprises me is that the flowers on this could survive in Blackrock Depths." The red-robed mage took the bow from the soldier. "This wood must contain powerful magic."

"What are these potions?" A soldier pulled a bottle from Vereesa's potion pouch and handed it to the mage. The mage opened it and sniffed. "The formula... this is Fire Protection Potion. They're all Fire Protection Potions."

"Firelord preserve us... why would a Dark Iron dwarf carry so many Fire Protection Potions outside?" The officer stared at Vereesa.

Grede glanced sideways at Vereesa. Jace's expression didn't change, but his heart started beating faster. It honestly hadn't occurred to him that carrying Fire Protection Potions inside Shadowforge City would seem suspicious. He hadn't expected the Ironfury soldiers to be this sensitive.

"From a Farstrider ranger." Vereesa answered calmly. "A group of Farstriders tried to ambush us in the eastern wastes. We turned the tables and eliminated them. These are spoils of war."

"Farstrider elven rangers wanted to steal the treasure you were carrying?" The Ironfury officer said. "I know the Farstriders do roam the northeastern wastes. They've disrupted the Bitter Fortress prospecting expeditions several times. But I thought they were just harassing us and the orcs. Would they actually track a specific treasure's movements?"

"You rarely leave Shadowforge City," Vereesa said. "Those rangers also search the world for treasures for their elven kingdom."

"Those Farstriders are our main competition as treasure hunters," Jace added with a nod.

The officer nodded thoughtfully. Just then, someone shouted from a distance: "Need a few more men to come out the side gate for inspection! Not enough hands out here, lads!"

The officer glanced that way but didn't respond. A nearby soldier asked, leading a bloodhound: "Why did General Angerforge order the gate closed, sir?"

"Do your job, don't ask questions." The officer said coldly, then turned back to look at Needleflame. "Take off your helmet and let our enchanter inspect the enchantment, mercenary lady."

Needleflame removed her helmet and handed it to the mage. Jace noticed her movements were notably careful and strained. The anxious look in Sora's eyes confirmed it — this motion was pushing Needleflame to her limit while carrying the Sulfuron Hammer. After all, this "helmet" was part of the transformed hammer.

"The armor too." After inspecting the helmet, the mage handed it back with a complicated expression. "You're dressed like a tin can. To be thorough, I must—"

"Even the last time I returned to Shadowforge City, it wasn't this strict, sir," Sora said.

The Ironfury officer lowered his voice. "You should be aware of the Excavation Site raid at the Great Forge. We lost a powerful molten giant in that battle, and it exposed many problems. And just now — just now, General Angerforge received intelligence about gryphons circling near Blackrock Mountain. What does that mean? Those damned Wildhammers haven't given up and may push further. This vigilance is necessary, madam!"

"I don't understand. What could I hide in this armor?" Needleflame glared at the officer, then turned her furious gaze to the red-robed mage. "A dagger that could pierce your skull?"

"My staff is ready for inspection, gentlemen."

Jace unwrapped the cloth from his staff. The Dark Iron mage responsible for inspecting Needleflame glanced over — then let out a terrified "Ah!" as he crumpled to the ground, sobbing.

Of course he would sob. Jace had practically spoken the incantation for Fear under his breath. If he'd cast it with his full power, that red-robed enchanter would have gone mad on the spot, and none of the officers or soldiers nearby would have escaped.

"What—" The officer noticed the mage's collapse and looked over. The sight of the blood-red demonic skull inside made his legs buckle.

Forget the soldiers — even the two bloodhounds that had been snarling excitedly recoiled, refusing to take another step forward, frantically tugging their handlers backward.

"Damn it. This is exactly why I hate inspecting warlocks' things." The officer turned his head away, waving his hand. "Quickly! Wrap that back up, warlock!"

Jace nodded and re-wrapped the cloth. The officer spat, pointed at the weeping mage on the ground, and stammered, "Get... get this cowardly waste out of here... Has he never seen a demon... corpse before? Damn it... Who... who brought him into the Ironfury Legion as an enchanter?"

Morophil smiled faintly beside him. The officer glanced at her, took a deep breath to steady himself. He wanted to look at the warlock's staff again, but lowered his head and held back.

"The group you've hired is undoubtedly a squad of formidable, highly skilled bodyguards, madam." He looked at Sora. "What exactly is the treasure you're escorting? This box — you need to open it."

"Must this be inspected too?" Sora asked.

"Normally, I might bend the rules." The officer wiped sweat from his brow with his sleeve. "But these are special times."

"Taking a look won't hurt." Jace said. "It won't damage the contents, madam."

Sora nodded. Jace bent down to unlock it. The moment he lifted the lid, the demonic face on the robe inside seemed to lunge out, claws bared. The officer, who'd been leaning in to look, yelped and fell flat on his backside.

But when he gathered himself and looked again, the garment inside hadn't moved at all...

"Close it! Close it!" The officer flapped his arms. "When I saw that demon being stopped at the gate, I knew there'd be trouble... I'm here to search for Wildhammer spies, not deal with this! Damn it, send a proper enchanter over!"

"May we pass now, sir?" Sora asked.

"Go ahead, madam... please, go ahead!" The officer grabbed an unlit brazier to haul himself up and waved them off. "We still have more people to check. Hurry along!"

Sora nodded. The group quickly packed up their things and headed deeper into the Dark Iron thoroughfare, joining the flow of people entering the city.

"Ha! He completely forgot to finish inspecting Needleflame!" Grede whispered.

"Still want to inspect me? Look at them — look at that enchanter. For the rest of his life, he probably won't even be able to tell if he's wearing his pants backwards!" Needleflame adjusted her helmet with a grin, elbowing Jace. "You're vicious, you wicked warlock!"

"Hah. This counts as vicious?" Jace snorted.

"That's true!" Needleflame shook her head proudly. "Still not as bad as the time in the Auchenai Crypts! I saw it with my own eyes—"

"Auchenai Crypts?" Vereesa asked. "You mean when Jace helped the Shadow Council attack High Priest Maladaar... You never told us about that."

"I want to hear it too," Grede leaned in.

Needleflame grabbed Jace's arm and shook it excitedly. "Well then, let me tell you two all about what happened in Auchindoun!"
