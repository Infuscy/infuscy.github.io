## Chapter 1531: The Ritual of Molten Core

Roktus walked to the entrance of the deep passage, looked out at the Black Forge's main hall, closed an outer iron door, then returned and called everyone into his room.

"So what exactly are these flamewalkers?" Grede asked, sitting down. "I only know that some Thorium Brotherhood folks back in Grim Batol called them fire lizards."

"Yes, fire lizards, Grede." Roktus said. "As Dagran Thaurissan's de facto prisoner, the last time I saw their true form was nearly a hundred years ago. They're a group of horrifying crimson creatures — humanoid upper bodies and serpentine lower bodies."

"Sounds like a naga's cousins," Grede said.

"No, they're completely different things," Roktus explained. "They crawl through lava and black rock like living flames. They have demonic faces, and their heads and arms grow long, flame-like horns — or tentacles. When these mad monsters swim through lava, they look like a burning giant salamander. Just one glance makes your scalp crawl..."

"The most obvious difference is this: sea witches are tall, but not much taller than humans or elves. But these devils, who call themselves Sulfuron's spokesmen — even the lowliest escorts among them stand over a dozen feet tall. Their leaders are larger than the sea giants of legend. And among them, Majordomo Executus's presence is practically divine... I remember the first time I saw him. He coiled high above, while several hundred Dark Iron dwarves and I groveled below. He felt like a burning red cloud floating overhead. I think he might have been nearly as tall as Shadowforge City's gate."

Grede swallowed and slowly turned his head toward Jace. "That's not the biggest thing I've ever swung a hammer at, is it, Jace Sesso?"

"Mm." Jace nodded.

Flamewalkers taller than the Dark Iron gate... These monsters didn't match his past-life memories.

But he did recall the wall paintings related to flamewalkers in the Shadowforge City ruins. A serpentine demon wreathed in flames, holding a trident, with dwarves prostrate before it like ants — not "ant-like" as a metaphor. In the face of these colossal demons, the dwarves really were as small as rows of beans.

Back then, he'd thought it was just Dark Iron sculptors exaggerating their idol. But based on what Roktus was saying, it wasn't purely artistic license.

"You have faced the legendary Deathwing himself." Roktus said. "I have no doubt that a flamewalker is no match for you, Lord Grede Thunderfist."

Grede hemmed and hawed nervously. "Strictly speaking, I can't say I *faced* Deathwing that time, Master Roktus. But I did fight the Black Dragon Princess Onyxia. Gave her a few good whacks on the head. Though that time, the Sky-Shatterer of Caldros Wildhammer that you gave me didn't exactly cooperate..."

"Sorry about hitting your aunt so hard, Needleflame. I didn't have a choice." He tilted his head toward the little black dragon.

"I never met her anyway," Needleflame muttered.

Roktus looked at the little black dragon again. Jace could feel the old blacksmith still warily sizing up this new friend who shared a lineage with their mortal enemy.

But compared to when he'd first learned she was a black dragon, he was much more relaxed now.

"This time I've got the Sulfuron Hammer," Grede said. "There's no way I'll let those so-called fire lizards survive like the black dragon did... Where do they live in Shadowforge City?"

"As far as I know, one flamewalker lives in Shadowforge City's deepest reaches, near Thaurissan's palace." Roktus said. "It's the flamewalker closest to ordinary dwarven citizens, responsible for relaying messages between the depths of Molten Core and Shadowforge City. Dagran Thaurissan likes to call it the Ambassador."

When Roktus mentioned the Ambassador, Jace immediately knew who he meant.

Ambassador Flamelash was a fairly easy boss in Blackrock Depths, but memorable — he dropped a rather distinctive flaming helm transmog, and in early versions he also dropped an AoE polearm that was great for druids leveling by farming mobs.

But...

"He's just a small fry," Roktus said. "A genuine fire lizard, sure, but killing him with the Sulfuron Hammer... would nowhere near shake the authority of Ragnaros the Firelord. If striking means declaring the Sulfuron Hammer's existence to all of Shadowforge City, we'd better pick a more important target. I believe Bohnett shares that view."

Yes, Jace thought the same. Killing a mere messenger — what impact would that have on a city of this scale?

"So who do we pick?" Grede asked impatiently.

Roktus paused, as if deliberating, before speaking. "Executus. The Majordomo himself."

Going straight for Executus?

He wasn't in Shadowforge City at all. He was in Molten Core, at its deepest reaches.

Not only that — in the game, without using water essences to extinguish all the flame protection runes, he wouldn't even appear. Those runes were guarded by seven other flamewalkers and elemental lords. You couldn't touch the flame runes without defeating them first. Which meant there was no skipping these seven guardians to face the fire lizard chieftain directly.

In Jace's subconscious, the step of facing the Majordomo was still far off... By then, maybe even the Wildhammer and Bronzebeard reinforcements would have already entered Molten Core.

"Where is he? Inside Shadowforge City?" Grede asked.

"No." Roktus said. "Right now, he's in the deepest reaches of Molten Core, consulting with his advisors and lieutenants about how to drive the Dark Iron dwarves against the black dragons and orcs, spreading flames and heat around Blackrock Mountain... But he doesn't stay hidden in there forever."

"Molten Core... isn't the fire elementals' homeland. It's just the borderland between the plane of fire and our world. Ragnaros the Firelord, his children, his servant the Majordomo, and countless fire lizards — their ultimate goal is to merge the place they call Sulfuron with Khaz Modan and the entire continent, burning everything here to ash. Creating Molten Core and enslaving the Dark Iron clan is only the first step in that grand plan. If neither we nor the black dragons can stop his ambition, then one day, Stormwind and Ironforge, even Lordaeren, will become a burning hell."

"Molten Core connects Sulfuron and Blackrock Mountain. And the 'chains' binding the two together are the seven protection runes forged by Majordomo Executus in the depths of Molten Core. Every year, Executus holds at least one ritual to strengthen these runes and stabilize the connection between the two planes. One rune strengthened per year — a seven-year cycle."

"When the ritual is held, he invites all of Shadowforge City's residents deep into Molten Core, to the foot of his throne-mountain to receive the Firelord's blessing. The most noble nobles of Shadowforge City and the most trusted flamewardens will gather around his throne to complete the ritual with him."

"Molten Core has a throne-mountain? A mountain?" Grede frowned. "My god... how big is that place?"

"Very big. Like another black world," Roktus said. "Not every resident of Shadowforge City can make it to the foot of that black mountain. But think about it — the most powerful people in the entire Dark Iron kingdom will be gathered around Executus, the Firelord's representative in the mortal world, while tens of thousands of commoners and soldiers watch the sacred ritual from below. If you strike Executus down with one blow from the Thorium Brotherhood's self-forged fire power, Thunderfist — not like felling an emissary of a god, but like slaughtering a stubborn ram — that would be the heaviest blow to this ridiculous false god!"

He finished in a low voice, looked up at the dark ceiling, and let out a long breath. "This is our chance, everyone. A once-yearly chance."

Grede said nothing. He just reached out and touched the haft of the Sulfuron Hammer beside him.

"When is the next ritual?" Jace asked.

"A month before Winter Veil. I believe it's November 14th," Roktus said. "Every Winter Veil, the runes' power weakens somewhat, so they need to be reinforced in advance."

"Forty days from now," Needleflame said.

Grede snorted a laugh. "So I take it Shadowforge City doesn't celebrate Winter Veil?"

"Public celebrations are rare in Shadowforge City, Lord Thunderfist," Roktus said. "But two hundred years ago, Winter Veil was also an ancient Dark Iron holiday. Old fossils like me have never forgotten that tradition."

Jace thought back to the "mountain" Roktus had mentioned. He figured it must be the black platform where Majordomo Executus stood in the game.

"Have you been to that place, Master?" Jace asked.

"A long time ago, before I was confined here as I am now," Roktus said.

"If it really is a 'mountain,'" Jace said, "then we'd better have a chance to get near the throne for the ritual, wouldn't we? That would be more favorable for achieving our goal."

"Which means we need to be invited to the highest point as the most powerful people in Shadowforge City." Grede nodded.

"You have transformation magic." Roktus pointed at Jace's dwarven beard.

"This transformation magic isn't that convenient." Jace glanced at Needleflame beside him. "But could you tell us who qualifies to go to the highest throne during the rune ritual?"

"Dagran Thaurissan and his family members will certainly attend, along with the highest-ranking flamewardens," Roktus said. "But as for the rest... I can't say for certain. I've been stuck in this tavern for too long."

"But I do know someone — Kremhold. He's Dagran Thaurissan's magical advisor and one of Shadowforge City's highest-ranking mages. He would surely know who's eligible to approach Executus's throne at the rune ritual in forty days."

"A mage? Can he be trusted?" Grede asked.

Roktus said, "The mages of Shadowforge City have always had their loyalty to the Firelord questioned. That's why even someone as high-ranking as Councilor Kremhold — an advisor to Thaurissan and a city councilman — can't gain Executus's trust to attend the ritual at the highest point. But Dagran Thaurissan can't afford not to have a group of sufficiently powerful mages to counter the Kirin Tor's magical schemes."

"Their suspicion is well-founded. Kremhold and many mages are indeed secretly plotting to overthrow the Firelord's rule. After the Thorium Brotherhood failed to forge the Sulfuron Hammer last time, he was already planning to flee Shadowforge City for Dalaran. But the heat hadn't died down enough for him to risk forcing his way out. Then the Dark Portal and the orcs' occupation of Blackrock Spire interrupted his plans."

"If that's not enough — the Thorium Brotherhood has many of his old correspondence records. If those fell into the flamewardens' hands, his fate would be more than ten times crueler than the Thorium Brotherhood members caught at the Dark Iron Anvil. The entire mage circle of Shadowforge City would be implicated."

"That's reassuring to hear, Master Roktus." Jace said.

"Take my ring. It'll let him know you can be trusted." Roktus handed Jace the Dark Iron ring.
