## Chapter 1532: How to Conquer "Sulfuron Fire"?

After repacking the Sulfuron War Hammer, Jace left the storeroom and returned to the bar's main hall. The various filled glasses were already waiting on the table.

As the three of them reached the table, Vereesa couldn't wait to ask, "So?"

"It's a bit more complicated than I expected, but at least we've got enough time to prepare." Grede sat down on the table, looked around, and asked, "Which one's mine... ah, it's gotta be this one!"

He stared at a bubbling, foaming drink that looked less like ale and more like a thick, fiery porridge. "This must be the legendary Sulfuron Stout!"

"Forget the drink for now. What did the smith say?" Vereesa asked.

"Forty days from now, there's an important ceremony at the very bottom of Shadowforge City. Looks like we're going to have to attend too," Jace said quietly. "But exactly how we attend... that still needs preparation. Word is, only nobles and the most high-ranking faithful can reach the highest level and get close to the manager. To guarantee success, we'll have to blend in with that crowd."

"I'm guessing this so-called 'manager' is the monster we need to see," Vereesa said.

"Correct," Jace said.

"Can't our Sorsa just be a Dark Iron noble?" Needle-Flame muttered. "Look at those Ironwarden soldiers — they heard her speak, saw her face, and they treated her with respect... isn't that enough to get access to the highest level?"

"There's no way you can bluff your way into a ceremony like that just on accent and appearance," Jace said. "Besides, there are nobles and then there are nobles. Remember what the old smith said about that wizard? Even he never got a chance to enter. If it's that important a ritual, there'll be a very strict identity check. Only people who qualify in Shadowforge City will get through."

"And we can't just send one or two people in," Vereesa said, taking a small sip of her ale. "To be safe, all of us need to reach that place together."

"True. If he jumps up and starts fighting, and the people around him pin him down, we'll need plenty of help." Jace patted Grede on the shoulder as he spoke. The dwarf had already picked up the Sulfuron Stout and taken several gulps.

"You bastard, how dare you drink before I do?" Needle-Flame grabbed a bubbling cup of the stout and took a big swig too.

The two cups weren't just thick in appearance — drinking them was a serious effort. Every mouthful Grede took looked like he was swallowing sticky porridge overloaded with rice. Needle-Flame had it even worse, squeezing her eyes shut and forcing it down.

The other three watched the pair go back and forth... their own faces twisting in sympathy, as if they were the ones drinking that unnameable concoction.

"Hoo-hoo-hoo, this intense feeling is absolutely unmatched!" Grede suddenly looked up halfway through his cup and burbled, excitement crinkling his forehead into several layers of frown lines.

"Look at you suffering like that, little one. You just can't appreciate a good brew! To a true warrior like me, the burn is like a blade wound, the bitter tang is like blood. Drinking this Sulfuron Stout is like stepping onto a battlefield, and a warrior doesn't fear the battlefield. When the cut becomes pleasure, with the bloody taste as seasoning, what's left is pure enjoyment — the thrill of crushing an enemy, the pleasure of devouring their strength, just like the unparalleled smoky fire flavor in this cup! I dare say the master who brewed this Sulfuron Stout must be a battle-hardened veteran! Otherwise... otherwise... wait. What's happening?!"

The people around watched in alarm as Grede's already thinning hair suddenly erupted in flames with a *whoosh*, then his beard, and even his clothes began to burn.

Grede leaped up from his seat in terror, madly slapping at the flames, but it was useless. In the blink of an eye, he became a human torch, rolling around on the ground.

"What the hell?!" Jace immediately tore off his cloak and started beating at Grede, trying to whisper Elemental-speak to dispel the flames. But the fire on Grede seemed utterly insane — Jace heard only mindless, maniacal laughter. Convincing them to leave was completely impossible.

And the red dragon cloak was useless. In fact, that already-sweltering fiery robe only made the flames burn harder when slapped on the fire. Pure sabotage.

Vereesa and Sora both got up to help, but the fire was too sudden and inexplicable. Everyone was scrambling about equally frantically. The ranger glanced at Jace and said, "Conjure Water!"

"Conjure... water? Right." Jace felt for the Azure Song at his waist, then reached for his spell book containing the Conjure Water incantation, only to remember his bag was still with Molotov. He gestured for the demon to toss it over.

Sora shouted in Dwarvish to the other dwarves ahead: "We need water! There's a fire here!"

Quite a few Dark Irons noticed the blaze and glanced over. But instead of helping, they showed no surprise at all — just burst into laughter one after another.

Just as Jace opened his pack, two goblin barmaids carrying a large basin of wet earth shoved through the crowd and threw it without hesitation onto Grede. The flames *whooshed* out, the elemental laughter vanished, replaced only by the relentless mockery of the Dark Iron dwarves.

"Ha, another unlucky fool who bit off more than he could chew!"

"If you can't handle your drink, go easy!"

"Waste of money!"

Jace brushed aside the dirt to check Grede's face and ears. He wasn't worried the fire would burn the Mountain King to a crisp — he was afraid it would destroy Vaelastrasz's magic. But it seemed the fire's power hadn't exceeded what Vaelastrasz had called "powerful magic." Grede still looked like a normal Dark Iron dwarf.

Before Jace could thank them, the two goblins had already headed back with their empty basin, their faces expressionless, as if going through the motions... judging by their preparedness and reaction speed, this definitely wasn't their first time dealing with this.

"By the Firelord's name, look at that girl!"

Some Dark Iron drinker shouted. Jace followed his pointing finger — Needle-Flame was still drinking away at the table, already working on her second cup.

"She hasn't caught fire yet!"

"Good, good, good!"

Hearing the dwarves' cheers, Needle-Flame drank even more enthusiastically. Soon she moved on to a third cup, drinking faster and faster. Finally, she drained the whole cup, tossed it aside, straightened up, and raised both arms. Half the bar erupted! Even Miss Namara, leaning by the counter watching, smiled and clapped.

"Look at that! That girl conquered the Sulfuron Fire! Damn impressive!"

"She's a true Dark Iron! The real deal!"

The little black dragon had become the star of the Dark Iron Bar. Though Jace worried she might be drawing too much attention, seeing Needle-Flame's smug, triumphant pose with arms raised, he stopped worrying.

Grede coughed a couple of times, dusted off his head and clothes, picked up a few broken mushroom bits, and sat down under a stone pillar to catch his breath.

"You... you okay? Need a doctor for burns or something?" Jace asked. The crowd in the bar had already forgotten about them.

"Burns?! Go to hell, you damned warlock." Grede hung his head and muttered. Jace chuckled. The dwarf muttered again, "That little one was cheating."

"Sure was. But what can you do about it?" Sora shrugged.

"Looks like you're having a good time, Lady Sorsa!" Grede gritted out.

"Not bad." Sora said, deadpan.

"Fine!" Grede waved his hand around vaguely. "Did you guys book rooms? I need a nap. Haven't slept all night... plays hell with my tolerance!"

"I'll take you guys." Vereesa said, suppressing a laugh.

The corridor of the guest rooms at the Dark Iron Bar was much quieter. Grede rushed into the room across the hall, slammed the door, and ignored everyone.

The group sorted out their things, especially the wrapped Sulfuron War Hammer. Jace waited for Molotov, who had just materialized in the hallway, to confirm no Dark Iron dwarves were following or eavesdropping, then closed the door.

"You've really pissed off Grede this time, Needle-Flame." Sora said with a smile.

"If losing a game makes him mad, then he's no real Wildhammer." Needle-Flame pouted.

"Get some rest at the bar for now, especially you, Needle-Flame. Keep an eye out for any side effects from that weird stout." Jace said. "This afternoon, I'll go see the old smith's friend, that Shadowforge City councilman. See if he can tell us which specific nobles can attend."

"What's your plan?" Vereesa asked. "Get Vaelastrasz's help?"

"Great minds think alike." Jace said. "We've got forty days. I think with his mastery of illusion magic, that's enough to prepare the right transformations. Once we find suitable targets to impersonate, show their appearances to Vaelastrasz, have him make the corresponding transformation magic, then make them disappear, and finally we use the magic to replace them at the ceremony."

Vereesa thought for a moment, then shook her head. "Shadowforge City's nobles are notoriously cunning and paranoid. Even if we complete the whole process without getting caught, when the ceremony actually comes, there'll be five of us pretending to be nobles. The margin for error is high when playing itinerant mercenaries from outside, but Shadowforge City nobles... appearance, accent, behavior, the information they know, their knowledge — every single one of those will be tested. If any one of us slips up on anything, it could expose the rest. In the end, we might have to fight our way out before we even get to the black hill the smith mentioned, whether it's toward the throne or out."

"I agree with you, Vereesa." Sora said. "Actually, it was already a bit dangerous at the city gate. When you were talking about the fire protection potion, that enchanter kept looking suspicious. I think he'd already sensed something off from the way you talk."

"That enchanter stared at my helmet for a while too." Needle-Flame added. "I thought he'd really figured it out. Lucky we got through without having to carry that hammer on my back the whole time."

"I felt it too." Vereesa scratched at the thick dwarf eyebrows she found extremely uncomfortable. "I was so nervous. If one of those Ironwarden soldiers had been a Jace Sesso, I'd have been done for."

"I get it, everyone. This is just a preliminary idea." Jace said. "It's been less than an hour since we left Lokthos Darkbargain, and then there was that whole commotion with Grede catching fire. I haven't really thought it through... Let me talk to that wizard first, ask around, and then figure it out."
