Chapter 1537: The Enemy of My Enemy

Back at the Dark Iron Bar, Jace immediately gathered everyone in his room and relayed everything he and Molotov had seen, heard, and discussed with Gestane herself in the interrogation room by the prison.

As soon as he finished, Grede forced down the half-eaten bread in his mouth and asked, "How much did you say? Over nine hundred gold? And you actually dared to ask for that much?!"

"What does nine hundred gold get you?" Needle-Flame asked.

"Knock out all your little white teeth and you'd be lucky to get fifty gold, little one!" Grede pointed at her nose.

"Heh. Anyone who wants that money can come try..." Needle-Flame put up a tough front, but still winced, opening her mouth wide and licking her teeth.

"You think I made up that story about the elf in Booty Bay who wanted to buy the treasure, Grede?" Jace leaned against the head of the iron bed. "A goblin from the Steamwheedle Cartel trade house told me an elf once offered over a thousand gold for the Azure Song! This robe I made with the Dagger Clan contains the finest batch of demon soul fragments the Shadow Council collected in Auchindoun. How is it not worth nine hundred gold?"

"Well, when you put it that way, it doesn't sound so unreasonable, comparatively." Grede counted on his fingers. "But nine hundred gold... I've never even seen that much money in my life."

"Neither have I. I was this close to just selling it and going back to the Trade District to open a shop." Jace laughed.

"Wait." Vereesa had been sitting nearby thinking about the Twilight's Hammer, but when she heard Jace and Grede's exchange, she shot up from the bedside.

"You went to Booty Bay to ask how much the Azure Song could sell for?!"

"Uh... ah..." Jace paused, then quickly explained, "I didn't know what the Azure Song was back then, did I? So, you know, I had to ask how valuable it was, then figure out how to protect it..."

"You never tell a straight story, do you." Vereesa snorted and sat back down. "Listen. You'd better be careful. When Twilight's Hammer was an orc clan, their members were already a bunch of lawless, chaotic, crazy bastards. Now they're cultists worshipping dark gods. Do you think they've gotten any better?"

"She might have kept you hanging with that nine hundred fifty gold offer just to string you along, holding back because of your connection to the Dark Iron emperor's court wizard. There are probably Twilight's Hammer spies outside the Dark Iron Bar right now, waiting for a chance to stab you in the back and take the robe."

"I don't doubt she has similar thoughts. But right now, she's probably trying to find out who my backer is. That gives us time to investigate her." Jace said.

"Who is your backer?" Sora asked.

"You, of course, Lady Sorsa." Jace smiled. "And the person behind Gestane is clear enough."

"The so-called Old Gods," Vereesa said.

Twilight's Hammer worshipped the Old Gods. There was no doubt about it. When Molotov mentioned she'd had trouble using mental magic on Gestane, Jace's first thought was the influence of the Old Gods.

But could those dark beings, sealed in the depths of the earth and the abyssal ocean in Titan prisons, exert such a direct magical influence on their surface followers? So much that even Molotov's magic couldn't touch them?

Jace chewed on some dried black mushrooms, recalling the shadowy energy in Gestane's Twilight's Hammer room. He felt there was some mysterious connection there he hadn't fully figured out.

"Does that mean the plan to disguise ourselves as Twilight's Hammer cultists is also a dead end?" Grede said. "Honestly, I wasn't too comfortable with the idea of pretending to be followers of some vague dark thing anyway... Remember that stone the Explorers' League Bronzebeard had in Uldaman? That woman's face. I still can't forget it. That Bronzebeard was acting like a madman too, and he'd only barely touched that stuff in Northrend. Who knows what these Twilight's Hammer people have actually seen."

"I remember," Jace said.

"But for the Dark Irons to join the Alliance's attack on Blackrock Spire, we have to get rid of these cultists." Vereesa said. "Since you know what kind of effect the elementals they worship, and those deeper things, can have on ordinary people, you should know that even if we defeat the Firelord and lift the flames' enslavement of the whole city, it'll be wasted effort if we don't wipe out the Twilight's Hammer's influence."

"You're right, little elf. That's true." Grede nodded.

"Maybe staying hidden in the shadows interfered with my perception and judgment, master." Molotov sounded a bit unwilling to accept it. "If I got another chance to face that human without needing to stay invisible, maybe I could peel away that mysterious mental protection."

"Are you sure about that?" Jace thought for a moment. "That doorman won't let demons into Twilight's Hammer territory. I wonder if we could invite Gestane out to meet somewhere else? She's basically acting as a supervisor in Shadowforge City — she can't be so cautious that she never leaves her own base, right? But next time we meet, Lady Sorsa will take point."

"Me? Talk to her directly?" Sora asked, a bit uncertain.

"On the surface, she just looks like a slightly unhinged human noble with a bit of a Stormwind accent. I was thinking — maybe King Varian knows her family or elders." Jace squinted, recalling Gestane's appearance in that room. "Don't worry about her. Just channel Queen Ysera's majesty and talk to her with the arrogance of a dragon looking down on ant-like mortals. I'm sure you're great at that, Sora. Like when you first met us in Winterspring."

"Don't make fun of my arrogance back then, Jace. I feel terrible thinking about it." Sora shook her head. "But you do have a point. I'll try."

"On one hand, we'll have Molotov meet her directly and try again. But on the other hand, I need to find out what other secret methods she has." Jace said. "Every meeting could raise more suspicion."

"These Twilight's Hammer people are so wary of demons they won't even allow a meeting?" Vereesa said. "Living in this dark kingdom full of succubi and imps must be torture for them."

Grede grinned sarcastically. "These people should move into the Cathedral of Light. Sounds like it'd suit them better."

Twilight's Hammer living in the Cathedral of Light? Jace glanced sideways at Grede. Sometimes that guy's wild comments were pretty alarming, especially for a transmigrator like him.

"Because fel fire easily burns through these void creatures' magic, Lady Vereesa Windrunner. We are the Twisting Nether's creations least susceptible to their mental disturbance and corruption." Molotov said. "Though I have no love for the Burning Legion, I have to admit they did some things right. Shadow must submit to demonic enslavement and control."

"If you hate each other that much, why doesn't Twilight's Hammer purge the demon summoners in Shadowforge City?" Vereesa asked. "If, as you say, Molotov, demons are also the thing Twilight's Hammer hates most, then warlocks must be a thorn in their side."

"Probably because the rulers of Shadowforge City don't want them eliminating rivals so easily." Jace said. "That wizard Kremhoud told me earlier that while Thaurissan craves the power the Firelord gave the Dark Iron dwarves, he also doesn't want to wear a leash. So he's been quietly sabotaging the elementals' efforts to control the city. I think letting demon summoners run rampant through the city is one of those 'sabotages.'"

As he said this, Jace suddenly thought — maybe he should go ask that Dark Iron dwarf warlock he'd met during his last visit.

Thaurissan's magic advisor might be limited by his position and the sensitive relationship with Twilight's Hammer. He couldn't investigate too much. But those Dark Iron warlocks would pay any price for a handful of dark incantations.

A group of people from another continent, possibly carrying all kinds of dark relics and shadow spells, had entered Shadowforge City. How could those Dark Iron dwarves, who raked in gold by teaching spells to outside warlocks at high prices, just sit by? Those dwarves had probably already fleeced the Old Gods' followers for plenty, and might even know how Gestane was immune to Molotov's mental influence.

"Come with me again, Molotov." Jace grabbed a pack of dried black mushrooms from the table and stood up. "I won't eat lunch at the bar, everyone. This'll be enough."
