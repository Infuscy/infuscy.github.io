## Chapter 1538: The Dark Iron Warlocks' Intelligence

Molurus Blackparch. Jace still remembered the name of that dwarf who loved wearing a full-length black robe with fiery patterns. Both his given name and his clan name had quite the ring to them.

But his memory of where the warlock Molurus lived was less clear. Before leaving the Dark Iron Bar, he'd been confident. After walking around the perimeter of the barracks and ending up back near the Ring of Law — quiet since there were no matches — he was starting to question his life choices.

Because he remembered Molurus's place being pretty far from both the Ironwarden barracks and the arena.

Even worse, neither Molotov nor Sazeno, who'd accompanied him to the warlock's residence last time, remembered the exact route. Jace was getting confused, and the two demons were still arguing about which way to go.

Just when he'd run out of ideas and was considering risking exposure by asking an Ironwarden patrol officer with a bloodhound for directions, he suddenly remembered something that wizard Kremhoud had said during their chat — originally meant as an insult.

— *You look maybe thirty or forty, still a clueless brat, led around by the nose by shadow and fel like those wretches below the Hall of Magic, your brain sucked dry by a succubus.*

Below the Hall of Magic.

Jace mulled that over. "Follow me."

He pushed aside his earlier memories and followed the route Lokthos had written down for him to find Kremhoud in Shadowforge City's Hall of Magic. Sure enough, as he crossed a bridge over a chasm, he noticed a black hall off to the side that looked like a prison. The scenery matched what he'd seen last time when following Molurus to his home.

"That way." Jace pointed at a door leading to a stairwell. "Follow me, Molotov."

"I told you, succubus! I win!" The little imp grabbed Jace's cloak collar and laughed. "You memory-addled thing, I don't know how you ever hunt Nathrezim in the Twisting Nether!"

"By sensing the real plane's scenes... something you could never do to catch a Nathrezim, imp." Molotov said calmly. "You don't actually think that laughably ignorant insult would make me angry, do you?"

"All talk and no bite!" The imp wouldn't let it go.

The stairwell was a spiral staircase. At the bottom, a short corridor. Jace knew they'd found the right place.

Blackrock Depths — this damn place had made him run around in circles in his past life, and it wasn't letting him off in this one either.

At the end of the corridor was a dark grey stone door. Jace remembered it was magically locked with arcane magic. He tried knocking with his staff. No response. Then he called out, "Is Master Molurus Blackparch home?"

Either the person inside was slow to react, or the name did the trick. The door rumbled, glowing runes one by one, then slowly creaked open.

"Who?" A red-bearded dwarf peered through the crack with one eye, glaring fiercely at Jace. "Who the hell are you, and how do you know where I live? If your next word is wrong, you and your head are going down into the fire..."

The dwarf finally noticed the demon's shadow behind Jace. His fierce expression instantly turned timid. He muttered a short incantation, pulled his head back, and tried to close the door. But a sharp, pale blue magic blade wedged itself into the gap, and the stone door refused to shut.

"Master Molurus Blackparch, I'm Slovakia. Remember me?"

"That was a human, you idiot! If you're going to fight or kill, just do it! Don't insult my intelligence!"

"Got into some trouble in Shadowforge City last time. Couldn't come in looking the same, so I changed to look like you lot."

"His succubus didn't have wings!"

"She does now." Jace said.

"Uh... then what did I serve Slovakia last time he came? Answer me that!"

"Black wine."

"No, Frost Black Wine!" The dwarf inside emphasized. "Frost doesn't mean chilled wine — it's part of the name. Remember that... But, it really is you?"

The stone door rumbled open. Molurus looked Jace up and down. "What kind of shapeshifting magic are you using? I can't see through it at all."

"Secret, Molurus." Jace spread his palms. "You'll have to pay something for that answer."

"I'm not that interested in illusion magic." Molurus coughed. "Come in, Master Slovakia. Please, come in, Lady Molotov."

"Thank you very much, Blackparch." Molotov nodded politely.

"Bring out that Blackrock cellar ale we got from the other side for Master Slovakia, dear. It's in the corner on the second floor with the little statue. Go down and you'll see it. Has orc runes on it!"

A succubus's sweet voice answered from the corridor inside. Molurus rubbed his hands together, gestured for Jace to sit, then sat across from him. "What business brings you to Shadowforge City this time, master?"

"Seems you're satisfied with the demon enhancement spell I taught you last time?" Jace asked.

"Of course!" Molurus nodded emphatically. "Not just me — all my colleagues who learned it are very satisfied... So that Blood Summoning spell I taught you — how's your practice going?"

Images flashed through Jace's mind — in Nagrand, on the edge of the Shattrath Hold about to collapse into the Void vortex, successfully dragging Molotov, who had swallowed the demon roster, back to the floating island, summoning her from Draenor back to the Terokkar Rift...

Thinking about it now, if it weren't for the grinning dwarf in front of him, who knew how everything in Draenor would have turned out?

"Pretty good." Jace nodded. "This time I came to Shadowforge City carrying some spells I got from the Shadow Council. Spells few orcs even know, let alone the warlocks here."

"If anyone else said that, I'd think they were bragging. But you, powerful Master Slovakia, master of Lady Molotov..." The Dark Iron dwarf rubbed his hands vigorously. "Name your price. What do you want this time? Spells, gold, spell materials..."

"I want to ask you about something." Jace said. "About the source of a new shadow spell."

Molurus's red eyes darted between the warlock and the succubus in front of him. His tongue rolled around in his mouth. "I know what you're referring to, Master Slovakia."

"That easy to guess?" Jace asked.

"Every scholar in the dark arts who pursues power, who never stops striving, who presses forward relentlessly, wouldn't miss this opportunity. You're no different."

Even inside Molurus's magic-warded residence, his voice dropped very low, almost a hoarse whisper.

"Now, I'm not at all surprised you're interested in Twilight's Hammer's arrival. The problem is, Master Slovakia, those people are very, very distrustful of us. No contact, no exchange, let alone trade. As far as I know, they've even suggested to His Majesty Thaurissan that it would be best to drive all demon summoners out of Shadowforge City as soon as possible, to keep the Twisting Nether summoning rituals from destabilizing the connection between the fire elementals' world and ours. These bizarre outsiders sure know how to make excuses!"

"I don't mean you humans look bizarre — you know how those people dress. Like a bunch of bondage enthusiasts dragged out of a cellar. And then those damn tauren. I've lived this long and never thought I'd see a live tauren in Shadowforge City!"

"They want to destroy you," Jace said.

"Destroy us, exactly." Molurus took the ale jug his succubus brought, filled a cup for Jace and one for himself, took a sip, and said, "Sure, we're not exactly experts at serving the Firelord, but at least we haven't done anything to harm him. Why the purge?!"

The dwarf warlock complained with an innocent look, but Jace sensed he was hiding something.

"Isn't there any other way to get those Twilight's Hammer secret spells?" Jace took a drink too. The ale had a foul smell, but beneath it was a distinct Nagrand prairie scent. He remembered that flavor well — the grassy aroma made the foulness almost bearable.

"The Twilight Lord is on guard against us warlocks, Master Slovakia." Molurus set down his cup and looked up. "But honestly, you don't need to learn spells from that shadow priestess. I have some very unusual spells here too. If you have some special magic, we can trade again, like last time."

Jace said nothing, just looked at the dwarf. Molurus relented. "Fine. If you really want to learn their spells, you could sail to the great desert of Kalimdor, like the warlocks who fled the Kirin Tor's pursuit. Give up your connection to the Twisting Nether, give up beautiful Lady Molotov, and throw yourself into the 'master's' embrace."

"Have you tried using a succubus?" Jace asked.

"You mean using charm magic to control the Twilight Lord?" Molurus shook his head. "You want to die?"

"Don't forget, I'm a warlock too, Molurus Blackparch." Jace took a sip of ale and looked at him. "You've tried it, haven't you?"

Molurus froze for a moment, then gave an awkward smile. "You caught me. You're sharper than I thought, master."

"We did try. It didn't go well."

"Why did it fail?" Jace asked.

"That Twilight Lord has a treasure — a magic gem set in a short scepter." Molurus said quietly. "From our investigations over the past year, she's likely bound part of her soul to that gem. It uses the gem's magic to disrupt a succubus's charm."

"A magic gem? Do you know where it is?" Jace asked.

"No." Molurus said. "But where else could it be? The Twilight's Hammer embassy, established just last year. A place surrounded by Thaurissan's personal guards and powerful fire elementals. With those elementals around, our demons can't get close enough to sneak a look even invisible. She came prepared, Master Slovakia. But we're not surprised. If you're going to enter a city like Shadowforge City, full of demons and warlocks, and make enemies of all of them, you need something special."
