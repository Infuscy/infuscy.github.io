## Chapter 1540: Both Sides at Once

Returning to the bar late at night, Jace found that none of the people waiting there for news had slept. Vereesa was sitting by the bar entrance, staring blankly at the windows across the terrace on a high cliff reflecting the lava river's glow, until she saw Jace appear on the bridge, waving toward her.

When she saw his cloak, she jumped up excitedly. Even though the ranger was currently in Dark Iron dwarf form, seeing her from all the way across the Hall of Magic to here made Jace feel a lot more at ease.

It was very late. Many Dark Iron dwarves had finished their drinks and returned to their rooms. The guest corridor and stairwell of the bar were littered with dwarves slumped every which way. Jace and Vereesa called Grede, Sola, and Needleflame to leave the guest area and head to a secluded corner on the bar's lower floor, where they could discuss the next steps.

"So, Jace? Did you figure out what Gerstahn's deal is?" Vereesa asked.

"She has a gem scepter that blocks mental disturbance. According to my warlock contact, it's specifically designed to counter demons." Jace said. "But based on the warlocks' intel, the gem is most likely hidden in the Twilight's Hammer embassy. I confirmed this with Kremhoud, that Shadowforge councilman Lokhtos Darkbargainer introduced me to."

"Just a gem? What kind of gem can block this demon's charm?" Grede glanced between the warlock and the succubus. "Don't forget, in Grim Batol, even that Dragonmaw overseer who could turn into a demon was enslaved by her mind. And she's way stronger now than she was back then!"

"Gerstahn was sent to Shadowforge City by Cho'gall." Jace said. "Cho'gall is the leader of Twilight's Hammer, a student of Gul'dan. He's a priest who worships the Old Gods, and he used to be a powerful demon summoner too. So he knows exactly what demons are capable of and how to counter them. Of course he'd know just the right countermeasure."

"What's your plan?" Grede asked.

"Right now, I'm thinking we find that gem while arranging a meeting with Gerstahn about the robe. Kremhoud will help us temporarily pull some of the Twilight's Hammer embassy's security away, to make it easier for us to sneak in and find the gem. Once the gem is destroyed and Gerstahn loses its protection, Morophil can charm her. Then we'll have the entire Twilight's Hammer in Shadowforge City under our control, and we can join the ceremony in a month openly as Twilight's Hammer members."

"But we only have one succubus." Vereesa said. "Morophil can't both charm the Twilight Lord and sneak invisibly into the embassy..."

"It doesn't have to be that absolute, ranger." Morophil said quietly.

"What are you implying?" Vereesa stared at her.

"The problem is, a hundred succubi wouldn't help!" Sazeno jumped onto the table. "That wizard said the Dark Iron dwarves set up a massive net in that shabby temple, all waiting for a demon to walk into. Even our demon, who dared to go one-on-one with the pit lord Brutallus, can't guarantee coming out unscathed. How are we supposed to summon another succubus who can infiltrate perfectly?"

"Dark Iron demon-binding arrays are nothing to sneeze at." Jace said. "The demon-binding array we used to summon Kallrez was something those warlocks learned from Shadowforge City. Even a doom lord's brute strength couldn't break free."

"Doesn't mean I can't." Morophil muttered in Demonic.

"Now's not the best time to prove that, sayaad huntress." Jace said sidelong.

"It's not like we can't steal it if Morophil's out!" Needleflame, leaning against the wall behind them, spoke up. "Don't forget, Jace Sesso — who helped you and Sister Sola steal those soul shards in Auchindoun and transfer them to the Expedition? I don't think these Twilight's Hammer goons are tougher than the Shadow Council warlocks. That place was crawling with observers!"

"Of course I remember." Jace said.

"So I'll handle stealing the gem from the embassy. My dear teacher Lady Morophil can handle stealing the person's mind." The little black dragon said. "Both sides at once — sounds like a solid combo."

"I'll handle the meeting with Gerstahn." Sola said. "Like you told me before."

"Yes." Jace said. "Write to Gerstahn saying you'll accept the nine hundred fifty gold coin price, and invite her to the Grim Guzzler sometime to discuss the details. There are Dark Irons everywhere here, and a succubus barmaid. She shouldn't be too suspicious."

"I need to stall for time, right?" Sola asked.

"Yes." Jace said. "You can make up a story about the robe — what kind of demon souls are in it, how much effort went into making it."

"Right, the robe's base is magically altered mooncloth, Sola. You know mooncloth well. You can build on that. Make the Twilight Lord feel like you're not really willing to sell at that price. Hint that she should throw in some extra benefits — like introducing you to other buyers of dark magic items, or pointing you to some supply sources..."

"You could even ask if there are any spells or dark magic scrolls for trade. After all, you're a merchant who knows nothing about Twilight's Hammer's unspoken rules. She'll definitely refuse and have to explain why she can't — that'll buy you more time."

Sola hesitated. "But I'm not good at making up stories, Jace. Especially about demons or dark magic."

"Morophil and Sazeno will be right there with you." Jace said. "Put your talent for bullshit to good use in front of your new mistress, Sazeno."

"Got it, master." Sazeno nodded. "Ah, no — 'great ex-master'!"

"He's already in character." Grede said.

"You're going to the embassy side?" Sola asked.

"Vereesa and I are going to help Needleflame." Jace glanced at the black dragon. "Vereesa will keep watch and handle any emergencies quickly."

"Don't worry, Jace." Vereesa said.

"I can actually do it alone, Jace Sesso." Needleflame said.

"I know you can do it alone." Jace touched Ulthalesh at his side. "But I need to be one hundred percent sure that gem gets destroyed."

Needleflame glanced at the slightly curved bone staff and nodded. "I understand."

"What about me?" Grede asked quietly. "Should I go to the embassy with you? If that staff can't handle it, you'll want to see my strength..."

"You're delivering the message," Jace said.

"You're just having me deliver a message?!" Grede's voice rose, then he lowered it again as he heard vague cursing from above.

"To Gerstahn and the other Dark Irons, Sola is an unidentified Dark Iron noble." Jace said. "You need to be here as her guard and attendant, or it won't be convincing. Morophil and the imp can't fill that role."

"More importantly, if Gerstahn really loses it and tries to cause trouble in the bar to grab the robe... or if something goes wrong on our end and Gerstahn senses something off with the mental magic and attacks you, Sola can't use wide-area spells without risking exposure."

"You're better suited than Morophil for getting into a big fight. Even if you punch all the Twilight's Hammer lackeys, the drinkers, and the patrol golems in the whole bar to death, the Dark Irons won't bat an eye. They'll just think it's lively. That makes it easier for us to retreat and figure something else out. Besides, not all the embassy guards will be pulled away. Having too many people makes sneaking in harder."

"Fair point." Thunderfist nodded. "Then I'll be in charge of protecting our Dark Iron noble lady."

"What about those flame guards, master?" Morophil asked.

"Will they listen to our Elemental-speak?" Grede asked.

"Next to Ragnaros?" Jace shook his head. "Even if there are fire elementals here willing to help us, those flame guards wouldn't be among them. But we're not completely without help among the elementals."

"Ah, you mean that lava elemental..." Morophil smiled slightly.

"Yes." Jace fiddled with Lokhtos Darkbargainer's Dark Iron ring on his finger. "We'll have to ask Lord Roccor for a favor."
