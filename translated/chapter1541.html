Chapter 1541: The Thief

On an abandoned clifftop tower, Jace, Vereesa, and Needle-Flame hid behind a high-level stone railing, watching the small temple that housed the Twilight's Hammer embassy in the distance.

The angle was different from the tailor shop Kremhoud had taken Jace into a few days ago, but it still gave a decent view of the platform in front of the temple.

Gestane had agreed to Sora's invitation to come to the Dark Iron Bar to discuss transaction details. The time was set for three o'clock this afternoon. It was now quarter past two. Gestane should have already left her prison office and headed for the bar.

Thaurissan's guards in front of the embassy hadn't withdrawn yet. Jace had agreed with Kremhoud on today's timing yesterday, and the wizard had readily agreed, insisting there was no problem.

But Jace was still nervous. Kremhoud had told him there was only one chance. Jace figured he had some urgent excuse to trick the emperor or the guard commander into temporarily leaving their posts to respond.

That kind of excuse definitely couldn't be used twice in quick succession. If both sides' timing missed each other, they'd be in trouble.

Then again, a little delay might be fine. Jace's side needed to destroy the gem while Gestane was in the bar with Molotov. That way, they could charm her the moment she realized her mental protection was gone and that something had happened to the gem.

If they acted too early, Gestane might sense something wrong and rush back immediately, or even cancel the meeting entirely and head straight for the embassy to check, or go to the elementals for protection. That would blow the whole plan.

Jace glanced toward the Dark Iron Bar and the Hall of Magic. He didn't see anyone rushing this way. At this point, no news was the best news.

"Look, Jace. They're leaving." Vereesa said.

Needle-Flame and Jace both moved to the railing beside the ranger and looked toward the small temple. Sure enough, Thaurissan's guards were orderly withdrawing from the platform, heading toward the Hall of Magic. The last guard captain had a brief exchange with one of the fire elemental guards before leaving to catch up with his comrades. Before long, only two fire elemental guards remained at the door.

"I can't hear what they're saying, Jace. But I don't think... they'll be gone long." Vereesa said. "Should we go now?"

"Let's move." Jace replied.

The three of them descended the stairs as fast as they could, watching Thaurissan's guards disappear into the magic district as they approached the staircase. They climbed from another direction to the edge of the platform and looked toward the temple entrance. The two flame guards were still standing motionless in front of the door.

There were less than twenty minutes until the agreed three o'clock. Vereesa swallowed nervously, glanced toward the magic district, and asked quietly, "That Lokor lord — I've never met him. Are you sure he's reliable? How did you negotiate with him?"

"You'll find out soon enough, Vereesa." Jace stared at the road below the steps. In the distance, a rumbling sound of rolling stones could already be heard.

A giant elemental made of several burning black boulders appeared at the end of the road. Lokor radiated black smoke, "climbing" the stairs on rubble rolling beneath him. He raised one hand and bellowed in Elemental-speak at the two flame guards at the entrance: "Where have Thaurissan's guards gone?!"

"Lecture hall, strong lava-son." One flame guard's voice sounded like hot wind scraping past ears.

"Take me to them. It's... Raghi-Grichi-Lechi! And you — take over guarding this temple, including the sides! Ah, Kaliz, Kans... Patrol, flame!"

"At your command, lava-son. Ignoi, Clitch..." The other flame guard's voice came through.

Lokor swept away with boiling fury toward the south, one flame guard barely managing to chase after the charging lava elemental. The heat from the two elementals was so intense that even Jace, crouching at the edge, felt waves of hot wind hitting his face.

After the two elementals disappeared around the street corner, Jace peeked toward the small temple. The other flame guard was indeed following Lokor's instructions, patrolling toward the far edge of the platform.

But that edge wasn't far away. The temple was very small. They had to seize this moment when the door was unguarded.

"Needle-Flame, you're up." Vereesa crushed a stone in her hand. "Remember our evacuation signal."

"Three stone breaks, two together, then one." Needle-Flame looked at the remaining stones in Vereesa's hand. "I remember."

"If you find the gem, don't touch it. Bring it out to me." Jace said.

"You've said that many times, Jace Sesso." Needle-Flame said. "I could forget who my mother is before I forget that."

"One more thing." Jace took off the Chain of Will and put it around her neck. "Wear this."

Needle-Flame touched the cold iron chain, nodded, then vaulted onto the platform. Like a sharp young deer, she sprinted into the dark temple entrance.

"Who is her mother?" Vereesa asked.

"Technically speaking, the Red Dragonqueen." Jace said. "She's one of the eggs the Dragonmaw helped Deathwing smuggle out of Grim Batol."

Watching Needle-Flame's disappearing back, he felt like he and Vereesa were a pair of parents sending their kid to school for the first time.

Not that he knew exactly what that felt like from his past life, but he guessed it wasn't far off.

Not long after Needle-Flame went in, the ground behind the other wall gradually lit up. The flame guard returned from its patrol, passed in front of the door, and then slowly drifted toward Jace's side.

Jace and Vereesa stepped back a few paces, hiding below. The light from the flame overhead brightened, then faded.

After a while, a human Twilight's Hammer member walked out of the temple, looked around, called out to the flame guard, said something quietly, nodded, stood at the entrance for a moment, then turned and went back inside.

Jace couldn't detect any nervousness or alertness in his demeanor. Needle-Flame must have found a way in.

According to Kremhoud, the temple wasn't big. The rooms and corridors inside weren't complicated either. Finding what they needed shouldn't take too long.

Given Needle-Flame's experience sneaking through the Shadow Council's storerooms for soul fragments and treasures, there shouldn't be a problem.

Most of these thugs Twilight's Hammer had scraped together weren't even spellcasters. Just a bunch of fanatical brutes. They didn't compare to the demons the orcs summoned, let alone the floating Eye of Kilrogg. Otherwise, Gestane wouldn't have needed Thaurissan to send so many elite guards to help protect the place.

And Needle-Flame had been raised by Sabellian. She'd grown up matching wits with those cunning, shadow-corrupted black-skinned orcs in Auchindoun.

So why did Jace still feel uneasy?

Vereesa stood a step higher, constantly watching the direction of the Hall of Magic, on guard against any returning Thaurissan guards — or Gestane showing up early if something went wrong on her end.

Jace glanced at the calmly watchful ranger. His mouth felt dry with tension. He wanted to take a deep breath, exhale hard, and push the lump of anxiety back down from his throat. But no matter what, he couldn't.

It was just a mental-protection magic gem. According to Kremhoud, a yellow cat's eye.

In the game, Gestane was just a minor boss with no presence. Aside from overly nervous new players exploring Blackrock Depths for the first time, veterans usually killed her like a trash mob and moved on without a second thought.

And even when Twilight's Hammer members had thoroughly infiltrated every aspect of Shadowforge City in later years, taking over Thaurissan's Temple, controlling the Black Iron emperor's court council, they were still just speed bumps before the Alliance and Horde's adventurers.

Neltharion was once the most honorable among us...

Swear by the pride and dignity of the Black Flight, Gerlorio...

Now I understand why Queen Alexstrasza chose you to guard this thing, Jace.

This crappy chain isn't all that. Using it to fight the Old Gods? Its previous owners all became slaves of the Old Gods...

What kind of gem could counter Molotov's charm, Jace?

"Maybe I shouldn't have given her that chain."

"Why?" Vereesa asked quietly. "Sora wore it when she retrieved Usarl's from that corrupted dream fragment. If not for that chain, she'd have been completely lost in there, turned into an undead monster. Your call was the safer one, Jace."

"But Needle-Flame isn't Sora, Vereesa."

Jace took another deep breath and looked toward the pitch-black temple entrance. Just then, the remaining flame guard passed by the door, briefly illuminating the corridor inside, the floor, and a hanging purple Twilight's Hammer banner.

At that moment, a vaguely wrong shadow-energy scent seeped into his perception.

"I have to go in."

"Jace... I'm coming with you."
