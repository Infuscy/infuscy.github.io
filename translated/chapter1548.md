## Chapter 1548: First Day as Twilight's Hammer

In the prison corridor outside the Ring of Law, Jace had arranged to meet Gestahn here today. Morophil and Vereesa had come along too.

If he was going to enter the Molten Core to meet Garr or Geddon, Jace would have to go with Gestahn.

As it turned out, Morophil's charm magic couldn't make Gestahn follow Jace's instructions with perfect precision in conversation and action. For the charmed Gestahn, Morophil had become her "new god," surpassing the Old Gods and Ragnaros — but her devotion to her old faith still remained.

So Jace had to go with her, to guide or fill in where needed, and more importantly, to correct mistakes in time.

As for Vereesa, Jace thought if they actually managed to get into the Molten Core this time, she could familiarize herself with the layout ahead of time. When the time came to use the Essence of Water to extinguish the runes, she'd be more efficient.

In the game, the flame runes were scattered throughout the Molten Core. In reality, they'd be even harder to find. And Vereesa was the best navigator in the group.

Just then, Gestahn approached from the other end of the corridor. Two Twilight's Hammer guards flanked her, then stayed at the intersection to stand watch as she ordered.

When the Twilight Lord saw Morophil beside Jace, her eyes practically lit up. The excitement on her face and the smile tugging at her lips were completely unrestrained.

"Morophil... noble and beautiful Lady Morophil, I didn't expect you to come too."

"Don't stand on ceremony, Gestahn." Morophil put her hand on Jace's shoulder. "I'm just accompanying this meeting. The important thing is whatever my master commands."

"Of course, of course." Gestahn's demeanor was nothing like a woman in her forties. She was more like a nervous adolescent girl seeing her beloved.

Combined with her weathered, even somewhat sharp-featured, thin face, it was incredibly strange.

Standing nearby, Vereesa cast a sidelong glance at the succubus with her red eyes, then at the Twilight Lord. She swallowed awkwardly.

"Mr. Slovakia, the church vestments for the elf, the human, and the dwarf are ready." Gestahn glanced at Morophil. "I wonder what kind of adornment or robe could complement that pair of exquisite demon wings..."

Damn. She really knew how to win her "master's" favor. Jace expected Morophil to be ecstatic, but this time the succubus only gave a faint twitch of her lips, showing no obvious reaction.

Still, Gestahn didn't seem discouraged. She quickly steered the conversation back and asked Jace, "Are you here to see the vestments and identification insignia this time?"

"Partly." Jace said. "But more importantly, there's a new mission, Gestahn. Does Twilight's Hammer know about the battle between the Firelord and the wind prince Thunderaan?"

"Of course." Gestahn said. "Legend says that Firelord Ragnaros fought a decisive battle with Wind Prince Thunderaan on a vast, boundless plain. The wind prince wielded his Thunderfury, and the Firelord struck back with his Sulfuras. But in the end, the young wind prince was defeated by the world's most powerful elemental. Most of his power was drained, and the remains were sealed in two elemental ornaments."

"An important goal of Twilight's Hammer is to recreate such a great battle... Elemental surges. World-shaking cataclysms. Repeated violent struggles like these will eventually reshape all of Azeroth, Mr. Slovakia."

The High Lord Demitrian who was trying to summon the wind prince in Silithus probably had a similar goal. Maybe he'd already expressed something like this to Gestahn before she came to Shadowforge City.

"Then I think our goals happen to align this time." Jace said.

"You mean..." Gestahn asked.

"You know the elemental ornaments are sealed with two of the Firelord's servants." Jace said.

"The lava lord Garr and the firelord Baron Geddon." The Twilight Lord said. "The Flamekeepers say they each guard the Firelord's precious trophies."

"I know the Firelord's servants are willing to let you into the Molten Core, Twilight Lord." Jace said. "We want you to help us borrow one of those ornaments. Like Twilight's Hammer, we want to hear the voices of the elements, to understand their power. As you said, a great wind prince has been locked in a miserable little cage for who knows how long. Isn't that a waste?"

"It's not that simple." Gestahn shook her head. "The Firelord and the Majordomo do respect Twilight's Hammer, but..."

"We've already thought of the reason." Jace took the small vial from his potion pouch and held it up. Gestahn frowned as she looked at it. "What is this?"

"Essence of Water." Jace said. "Have you heard of it?"

"The... what?!" Gestahn said. "How is there a bottle of Essence of Water here? You brought this into Shadowforge City? The Essence of Water is something that could shake the entire Molten Core... You must be joking."

"Do we look like we're joking?" Morophil said, her eyes faintly glowing.

"Is... is this really the Essence of Water, Lady Morophil?" The Twilight Lord stared at the succubus fearfully, her gaze growing more and more dazed.

Morophil followed the script Jace had taught her. "I have traveled across the Twisting Nether, witnessing the great powers of every plane, Gestahn. Now I have been summoned to Azeroth, and I have witnessed the power of the elements. It has piqued my interest."

She glanced at Jace and continued. "In my search for the great water elementals, I obtained this Essence of Water. But I could not find anyone who truly understands its power. In seeking an audience with the Firelord or his greatest servants in Shadowforge City, I learned of the wind prince's tale. I know the wind prince once allied with the water lord, and may know something about this bottle..."

"I imagine the fire elementals cannot ignore the risk of Essence of Water appearing in Shadowforge City. They surely want to learn the secrets of this dangerous object from the wind prince as well. And you — don't you want to help me understand more about the great elements and ancient gods you worship?"

"Of course, Lady Morophil!" Gestahn's eyes grew moist, her voice trembling. "I once thought the divide between demons and elements could never be bridged. That as a servant of the elements, I could never earn your recognition and respect. I was wrong. Clearly, demons are more open-minded than I imagined. We are the narrow-minded ones."

"I agree. I agree." The Twilight Lord reached for the demon's hand, but Morophil twisted her arm away, dodging.

Gestahn swallowed awkwardly, a sour ache rising in her chest. But she quickly composed herself and asked calmly, "Who do you want to see?"

"Any recommendations?" Jace said. "Which of those two elemental lords is easier to talk to?"

"Baron Geddon." Gestahn wiped her tears. "He is more straightforward, a purer fire elemental. Compared to the lava lord Garr, he'll take the potential damage the Essence of Water could do to the Molten Core more seriously."

"Then Geddon it is." Jace said. Vereesa nodded.

"Then I'll prepare to leave for the Molten Core immediately. I'll do everything I can to secure one of the Bindings for you, Lady Morophil. Please rest assured." Gestahn said. "Come with me, all of you. I'll get you your robes and insignias."

At the Twilight's Hammer quarters in the Shadowforge City prison, in the changing room, Jace and Vereesa were putting on their Twilight's Hammer vestments. Morophil watched them.

"Your charm magic is powerful, demon. I almost feel sorry for that Twilight Lord."

Vereesa draped the purple cloak over her shoulders and sat down, asking Jace quietly, "Has she used charm magic on you before? Or..."

"Good heavens, do you still think I'm using some dark magic to enslave your lover?" Morophil asked. "If he were even a tenth as good to me as he is to Lady Gestahn — no, if he were a hundredth as good to me as he is to you — I'd be satisfied. But humans can never truly have feelings for demons, can they? Perhaps everything that's happened since I came to Azeroth has just been a beautiful illusion..."

"Demons don't dream." Vereesa said.

"You truly have no sense of romance, no flair at all. Were you really born in Quel'Thalas?"

"If judging by birth determined character, I'd have destroyed your demonic soul long ago!"

"The only reason you haven't is because you don't have the skill, dear."

"Hah. That's debatable now."

Jace silently fastened his sash on the sidelines, not daring to say a word.
