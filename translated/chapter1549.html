Chapter 1549: Molten Core

Even though he'd been to the Molten Core countless times in his past life, this wasn't a game. Jace couldn't stay calm.

And this time he wasn't here as a mercenary for the Alliance or the Horde. He was heading there as a disguised Twilight's Hammer cultist. It was perfectly natural to be uneasy.

Plus, the vestments Twilight's Hammer had found him were a bit tight. Jace was already quite tall for a human, so finding a good fit was hard. The discomfort probably didn't help.

And one more thing... he was currently carrying Azuresong Mageblade for self-defense.

In the game, Azuresong Mageblade wasn't some gift from a Quel'Thalas noble — it dropped from a boss in the Molten Core: Golemagg the Incinerator.

Now, walking step by step behind Gestahn down into the depths of the wide passageway, Jace kept feeling like some weird accident was going to happen, forcing him to leave Azuresong Mageblade behind here, just to keep the timeline intact.

At the bottom of the walkway stood two Dark Iron dwarves in heavy armor. The armor was bright silver, engraved with thick dwarven runes and the faces of Dark Iron ancestors, making the two guards look like statues.

When they saw Gestahn, they bowed respectfully and let her through. No questions asked, no ID checked. Jace and Vereesa followed smoothly behind her.

Past the door behind the two dwarves, they stepped onto a scorching hot Dark Iron bridge. Below it flowed a river of molten lava.

The churning lava baked those crossing the bridge. Whenever the glowing magma surged up, the blinding light was impossible to look at directly.

The whole space had turned fiery red. Aside from the black iron bridge underfoot, almost everything was drowned in that red.

Jace glanced at Vereesa beside him. Her expression was still fairly calm.

Before setting out, Gestahn had given each of them a flask of fire protection potion, since the depths of the Molten Core weren't somewhere mortals could casually enter. Jace gave both his potions to Vereesa — in all his years in Stormwind, aside from shadow magic, the thing he'd trained most was his fire resistance.

But even with Gestahn's fire protection barrier, he was already struggling by the time they reached this point.

It wasn't just the bridge. On the other side, entering a vast cavern untouched by Dark Iron dwarves' masonry, a wave of intense heat slammed into them. The environment hadn't grown any milder just because they'd left the lava river behind.

The view ahead rippled like waves. Thick smoke billowed from side passages from time to time. The pungent stench of sulfur grew stronger. His boots grew hotter and hotter beneath him, as if they might catch fire at any moment.

Jace knew this wasn't just the pain of absolute heat. He could hear roaring flames — it sounded like prisoners screaming under torture in some horrible dungeon, mixed with filthy curses.

Further in, there was chanting. Countless elementals were intoning "Ragnaros."

Those voices were like a spell, driving the heat at the lowest depths of Shadowforge City, baking Jace — who could understand elemental language — like an outsider.

After walking through this wide cavern for a while, the surroundings seemed to quiet down. The sound of the distant lava river was muffled as if behind countless walls.

The elementals' chanting rose and fell to a lower level, with only the occasional sound of falling stones from deeper ahead.

But the temperature was still rising, higher and higher.

Though Jace couldn't accurately judge the heat, he knew that in this place, any mortal outside of Dark Iron dwarves could not survive normally, let alone fight or cast spells.

Just from walking this one path, Jace could tell Gestahn was no ordinary person. There was a reason Cho'gall had left her in Shadowforge City to lead Twilight's Hammer.

Before joining Twilight's Hammer, she might have already been a fairly accomplished mage or warlock. From her accent, she might even have been a graduate of the Wizard Sanctum.

After about twenty minutes of walking, Gestahn led them into an even larger cavern.

This could hardly be called a "cavern" anymore. As far as the eye could see, black stone pillars stretched upward. The ceiling at the highest point was lost in the smoke and firelight.

At the edges of his vision, brilliant lava rivers flowed everywhere. Flames burst sporadically from massive cracks in the stone.

Vereesa stared in awe, barely managing to keep her composure. Jace understood her shock. Even for him, seeing this hellish apocalyptic fire was deeply awe-inspiring.

If Roktus Darkwhisper's description of Executus's throne as simply "an underground black mountain" had been hard to imagine before, walking all the way here made Jace understand completely.

Even if an actual mountain appeared ahead now, it wouldn't seem out of place.

A massive rock on the distant cave wall shifted. Firelight gleamed through a crack at the top, forming the shape of two eyes.

The great boulder, almost fused with the mountain itself, lowered its head-like part. The rocky texture and flickering firelight intertwined like a beard and wrinkles, giving it the appearance of an old man.

This was a molten giant. Jace knew it was the same kind of being as Bael'Gar outside the Shadowforge City gate.

But Bael'Gar looked more like a living giant, while this one seemed like a piece that had just sheared off a solidified black boulder. Just looking at it, it was hard to tell where its arms and legs were.

"State your purpose, worshipers." The giant rumbled.

"We wish to meet with Baron Geddon, great guardian of the Molten Core." Gestahn shouted in elemental. "Twilight's Hammer brings important intelligence from the outside world."

The great rock was silent for a moment. Then a crack wider than the eyes opened below them — a mouth — and let out a deep, rumbling roar.

"Baron Geddon... is aware... of your summons... Twilight Lord." At that, the flames on the giant's body slowly dimmed.

The elementals here truly did respect Twilight's Hammer. When Jace first heard that Executus would unconditionally allow Twilight's Hammer members to participate in the rune ritual, he'd thought the fire elementals were taking these cultists too seriously.

But after seeing that stalk-eye in the box, he understood why.

They followed Gestahn further in. As Jace looked back at the molten giant, Vereesa's eyes suddenly locked onto the lava river below the bridge. Though her expression didn't change, Jace could sense a flicker of tension in her heart.

Following her gaze, he caught sight of a dark green "giant lizard" swimming through it. This enormous creature looked longer than a whelpling from head to tail. And its legs weren't a reptile's feet — they were arranged like a centipede's, paired along both sides of its body. Its antennae reached forward, long and thick, more like a centipede's pincers...

Just as Jace's eyes were following this terrifying creature swimming through the magma, its head suddenly twisted a hundred and eighty degrees to look at the bridge, as if it knew someone was watching it.

It was a twisted, gaunt human face. Like a dark night terror. Its lifeless eyes stared straight at Jace.

Jace immediately felt an intense fear wrap around him, as if someone had cast a powerful fear spell on him.

He tried to lower his head, using his will to dispel some of the fear, forcibly enduring part of it. Then he tried to steady his mind, slowly digesting it.

Feeble. Futile. Struggle...

The strange elemental chanting struck at his will like shadowy whispers, accompanied by the grinding of lava and stone, like sneers mocking his efforts to resist the shadow enveloping him.

But in the end, Jace pushed through. Though probably due to the intense heat, he'd spent far more energy than usual digesting those shadows. If he still had his Dark Iron dwarf appearance, the magical disturbance would definitely have broken the illusion.

When he looked back at the lava river, the great creature had already swum far away. Several golden ones of its kind followed behind it, heading toward a distant lava cave. Their figures disappeared behind a giant rock.

That was a group of Flamewakers. The green one was their leader.

A creature born from the Firelands, yet it was an exceptionally skilled warlock of shadow magic.

Jace remembered who that shadow-manipulating creature living in the Molten Core was — Lucifron. A Flamewaker leader who could cast shadow damage over time and put a curse on players to increase their spell costs.

Was that glare just now the curse? Jace could indeed feel his willpower straining much harder to resist the shadow's envelopment.

Interesting. Was this a shadow spell driven by elemental language? Jace had never seen anything like it.

He silently recited the whisper the shadow had transmitted. He didn't know if it was part of the incantation, but this elemental phrase was very distinctive.

If that was Lucifron, then the cave he'd swum into was probably the one holding Magmadar the ancient core hound. Lucifron was Magmadar's keeper. And that cave also held the first protection rune of the Molten Core.

So Jace gave Vereesa a look, glancing toward the entrance of that cave. Vereesa nodded and studied the surrounding terrain to memorize its features.

Baron Geddon's location was fairly deep in the Molten Core. From what Jace remembered, even after getting close to Magmadar's cave, they'd still have to go a good distance deeper.

Along the way, they'd see at least two or three more flame runes and most of the Molten Core's terrain. Maybe they'd even get a distant view of Executus's high seat...

But as they crossed this great black bridge, a blazing fire suddenly appeared on the blackened ground beside the rolling lava river in the distance.

The fire grew brighter. An enormous flame was rushing toward them across the scorched earth, like a fireball the size of a small house flying toward them!

Vereesa tightened visibly. "What's that? What is that?"

"He's..." Before Gestahn could finish, the rapidly approaching flame released a ring of scorching heat that collided with the stone pillars and churning lava, drowning out all sound.

"Baron Geddon is here! Who wants to see me?!"

The flame's roar was like a bomb going off. The elemental lord had already reached them by the time he finished the sentence.
