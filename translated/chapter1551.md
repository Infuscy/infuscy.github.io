## Chapter 1551: Lord Incendius

"I knew it. Baron Geddon is undoubtedly the Firelord's most loyal lord in all the Molten Core. There's no way he could ignore such a terrifying potential threat. He had to take our news seriously."

At a wide intersection in Shadowforge City, Gestahn was talking — whether to Jace and Vereesa behind her, or to herself.

"Now Morophil will be satisfied. When she finds out I had the chance to communicate with the Wind Prince's cage, she'll surely recognize my ability!"

"What do you think, Jace?" Vereesa asked quietly. "If I heard right, the elemental language she was explaining to us... the Fire Baron is sending one of his subordinates to Twilight's Hammer to help us communicate with the wind prince?"

"Yeah." Jace said. "Or more precisely..."

"To watch us," Vereesa finished.

"Right. I didn't expect the Baron to completely trust Twilight's Hammer anyway." Jace stretched. After walking through the Molten Core, the temperature in Shadowforge City felt cool and comfortable now.

"What kind of elemental is this Incendius?" Vereesa asked.

"The watcher of Shadowforge City, the guardian of the Dark Iron Anvil. The Thorium Brotherhood's first attempt to forge the Sulfuron Hammer was discovered by him." Jace said quietly. "In short, this guy is Roccor's mortal enemy. Think about whose side he'd be on. Expecting help from him is impossible."

"Why would you think that?" Gestahn seemed to have caught bits of their conversation. She walked over and said, "With Lord Incendius helping us, the wind prince will surely reveal more secrets, allowing you and Morophil to gain a more thorough understanding of knowledge and power. This is Baron Geddon's grace... He could have just let us figure out this powerful cage on our own. We might have wasted days here!"

"You're right, Lady Gestahn." Jace gave her a tight smile. "We'll wait for Lord Incendius in the passage on the other side of the bridge. You stay here in case we miss him."

"That makes sense. I'll leave it to you, Mr. Slovakia and Lady Anya." The Twilight Lord said.

Jace and Vereesa walked onto the bridge. Jace asked quietly, "Why aren't you using that Aselwen Goldthorn alias anymore?"

"Thanks to Sylvanas." Vereesa said sourly. "Now half of Quel'Thalas knows I have that tiresome alias. And it's attracted plenty of Goldthorn family members looking to pick a fight with me over it."

"Aselwen is Vereesa backwards. So what's Anya?" Jace asked.

"If you rounded up every Anya in Quel'Thalas into an expeditionary force, they could probably conquer Zul'Aman, Mr. Slovakia." Vereesa arched an eyebrow.

Jace chuckled. "What did you think of the Molten Core's layout?"

"The Molten Core is roughly circular — a central area surrounded by several ring-shaped passages and halls." Vereesa said. "From where we met the Fire Baron, going further down through an area full of stone pillars, there's a group of powerful Flamewalkers stationed there. They seemed to be guarding a large burning stone slab with a rune I didn't recognize on it. Was that one of the protection runes? I'm not sure."

"That should be it," Jace said.

"Going deeper past that rune, there's an area protected by countless lava elementals. I could see a massive lava elemental lord at the deepest point, with another similar rune there." Vereesa said.

Jace looked at her with some surprise. From where they'd been standing, that location had been completely obscured by darkness and smoke.

Vereesa continued. "Crossing a wide lava river... I couldn't see clearly on the other side, but I could feel an arcane barrier protecting something. I'm guessing another rune. The master of that arcane barrier should be the legendary Flamewalker arcanist, Shazzrah. He's hiding in the southern part of the Molten Core."

"That cave you pointed out to me when we first entered — there's a rune inside too, isn't there?" the elf asked.

"Yes." Jace said. "I could sense something. There should be a powerful elemental seal inside."

"So that's four now." Vereesa said. "If the center of the Molten Core — the true center — is where the Dark Iron dwarves summoned the Firelord, where the two planes meet, and the runes are the 'chains' binding it... I think I can deduce the locations of the remaining three."

Jace couldn't help asking, "How could you see that far... especially with a firelord right in front of us?"

"However you sense shadows and emotions is how I see that far." Vereesa teased. "And I think for an ordinary person, good eyesight is easier to explain."

Jace raised his hands in surrender. He felt like by now, Vereesa knew the real Molten Core better than he did.

"When the time comes to find the flame runes, I'll be counting on you to navigate."

"I won't let you down." Vereesa tightened her belt confidently. "But right now, the most important thing is how we deal with this Incendius."

"He'll insist on being present through the whole process, listening in on our conversation about the Essence of Water for Geddon." Jace said. "That means I definitely can't ask directly how to use the Essence of Water to extinguish the flame runes."

"You understand how elementals communicate." Vereesa watched several Dark Iron dwarves passing through the passage warily. "Couldn't you use some kind of mental conversation? Like the soul link between you and Morophil, or some kind of shadow whisper."

"I don't know." Jace said. "At least in the Molten Core, I didn't hear the wind prince say a single word. Maybe we should ask Gestahn... but then again, Incendius is also a powerful elemental. A secret conversation like that would probably be caught under his watch."

"The wind prince doesn't seem willing to talk to that firelord." Vereesa said. "Maybe we can stall by saying the ritual isn't going well. For example, have the fire elemental leave the wind prince's cage with us for a while to study it, improve the ritual format, and so on... buy some time when he's not around."

"We can only try that." Jace nodded and looked toward the bridge outside the passage. Gestahn was walking toward them. Behind her followed a rather massive fire elemental — nowhere near the size of Geddon's fire cyclone, but still several times larger than the many flameguards in Shadowforge City.

That was Incendius. Ragnaros's cruel executioner stationed in Shadowforge City. Just his slow approach made Jace feel like the heat of the Molten Core was slowly creeping back around them.

"Lord Incendius." Jace said. "We are honored that you would come personally to our lodgings to help us complete the interrogation of Thunderaan the Windseeker."

"I only comply because Baron Geddon commands it." Incendius said in Common.

"Where is the cage?" Jace asked.

"I carry it with me, mortal." Incendius stared at Jace, his voice low. "This piece of the cage is under Baron Geddon's protection. He values its safety. Without his permission, I will not let it appear in Dark Iron territory."

"Understood, my lord." Jace nodded.

"Lead the way." The fire elemental spat a puff of flame.

The three of them led Incendius toward the Twilight's Hammer embassy grounds. Jace said as they walked, "The Windseeker's power far exceeds what we estimated, so the ritual may not go smoothly, Lord Incendius."

"Neither I nor Baron Geddon had high hopes for your ritual to begin with." Incendius said. "Since being sealed in the cage and shattered by Ragnaros, the Windseeker has barely spoken a word... Perhaps in the earliest days, he cursed the comrades and enemies who participated in that elemental war, but that was a very, very long time ago. So long that even I have forgotten. Still, the Old Gods' power might make him yield. If he does speak, I won't be surprised."

"Thank you for your trust, Lord Incendius." Gestahn smiled. "You will be impressed by the skills we learned in Silithus."

"Yes, my lord. We will treat the ritual with utmost seriousness and guarantee the cage's absolute safety," Jace added.

"You don't need to guarantee the cage's safety." Incendius stopped. "I will guarantee its safety."

"Baron Geddon has ordered that I must be present during the ritual. When the ritual concludes or is interrupted, I will return with it to the Molten Core. I will not leave its side for a single step."

"Then that's even better, my lord." Jace said immediately. "After all, this is an important treasure even to the Firelord. If anything happened to it, none of us could bear the responsibility."

Incendius said nothing more. He turned and continued forward. Behind him, Jace looked at Vereesa. Vereesa was looking back at him.

Jace gave no sign. He just pressed his lips together in resignation.
