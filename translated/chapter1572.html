Chapter 1572: One Each, Or Two?

As the rune-reinforcement ceremony approached, Ghestan had been going into Molten Core over the past few days, attending meetings with Dagran Thaurissan about the ceremony arrangements. Executus hadn't allowed Thaurissan or Ghestan to bring attendants, so Jace hadn't been able to go with her. Frankly, while this meeting could bring in a lot of new information, Jace was pretty worried — Ghestan would likely meet Executus face to face, which meant Morophil's charm spell would face a serious test. Before Ghestan went in, Jace even considered having Morophil temporarily weaken the charm's intensity so Executus wouldn't notice anything off. But he ultimately abandoned the idea. If Ghestan suddenly snapped out of it in there, like she had at the embassy, wouldn't that be an even bigger problem? So the moment she got back from Molten Core, Jace took Morophil and intercepted her on the road toward the Hall of Magic. "Lord Ghestan." Jace called her name as he approached. The moment Ghestan saw Morophil, her face lit up with a broad smile, her emotions swelling like a blooming flower. Toward Jace, she was as cold as ever — especially seeing Morophil leaning close to her. Jace couldn't care less about her attitude toward him. The fact that the charm spell was still holding was enough. It proved that deep in Molten Core, even in Executus's presence, the intensity of the flames wasn't enough to disrupt a Sayad's magic. Also, Executus at least hadn't noticed anything wrong with Ghestan's mind. Morophil's charm was far more subtle than other succubi's, but it wasn't completely undetectable. Jace could sense that Ghestan's mind was being continuously influenced by some kind of shadow. But the best part was that even he could barely tell whether this shadow power was coming from an ancient god artifact or a demon's charm magic. Just like before — he could feel Ghestan's mind was blocked by something dark, but he couldn't tell if it was a shadow barrier or some chaotic magic item backing it. Knowing Ghestan carried the ancient god's token, Executus probably hadn't paid much attention to the magic lingering around her mind. Either way, this hurdle was cleared. "The winter this year seems to have come earlier than usual because of the climate shift, Mr. Slovakia. So the fire rune reinforcement ceremony has been finalized for November 12th — two days earlier than we estimated." Ghestan said. "But this suits me fine. I can't wait to prove my standing with the elementals to you, Madam Morophil. Master Slovakia will return to Shadowforge City then and tell you all about my perfect performance at the ceremony. You'll be proud of me." "I look forward to it, Ghestan." Morophil said. November 12th. So there was about a week left. That was enough time for Solara, disguised as a Dark Iron noble, to contact the SI:7 agent outside Blackrock Mountain and have them notify the dwarves. Earlier, Solara said the SI:7 agent told her the Wildhammer and Bronzebeard forces had been assembled at the Thorium Point outpost for a while, ready to move at any time. These dwarves had been prepared to use the chaos to stage a surprise attack on Shadowforge City through those passages. Quite a few of them were veterans who had fought the Dark Iron dwarves in Khaz Modan. Finding out that Dagran Thaurissan might clear those passages and let them into Shadowforge City to fight the elementals — some were almost disappointed. The Bronzebeard and Wildhammer warriors' optimism was based on Executus being successfully killed. If they failed, the dwarves' surprise attack would turn into a rescue mission. Jace had been through those passages. He knew gryphon riders couldn't maneuver there — it was easy to defend and hard to attack. Even the orcs, with black dragon support, hadn't wanted to assault it. And thunder power would be limited by Ragnaros's enslavement of the elemental forces throughout Blackrock Depths. A forced push would result in heavy casualties. The pressure wasn't on Grede alone. Jace remembered that even without thunder power's blessing, Grede — wielding Kadros Wildhammer's Stormfury — had already been enough to make Onyxia stagger in pain. And that was after prolonged combat with the black dragon, when his stamina had been heavily drained. Grede had said his ancestral power grew closer through continuous combat and training, and the strength it brought rose accordingly. And it was true. Since returning from Draenor, Jace felt Grede was even more towering than when he'd first emerged from the Tower of Ilgalar in Auchindoun, in his Avatar form. So he was different now from when he'd fought Onyxia. Combined with the Sulfuron Hammer... Jace had no doubt that if he was prepared and launched a successful surprise attack, this dwarf could kill Majordomo Executus in one blow. In the game, the Majordomo could dodge the final blow through plot mechanics. But not here. So it was partly up to Grede, but also up to everyone else creating space for him to land that hammer blow. "What about the ceremony arrangements?" Jace asked. "For example, how many Flamewalkers will attend the rune reinforcement ceremony, and the process." "The Majordomo didn't share the elementals' participation details with me, Master Slovakia." Ghestan said. "If you want to know more, I can tell you about last year's ceremony. That'll give you some preparation." "I'd be very grateful." Jace nodded. "It's Molten Core, after all. This is my first time attending such a formal elemental ceremony." "Last year's ceremony, the Majordomo had ten powerful servants participate." Ghestan explained. "Two priests assisted Executus in performing the ritual. Four Flamewalker guards with tridents were responsible for protecting the throne. Another four guards with spears were stationed below the throne dais alongside the Flamewalkers, Lava Lords, and the massive Core Hounds, monitoring the Shadowforge citizens attending the ceremony." "There might be hundreds or even thousands of nobles, mages, and citizens attending. The ceremony will last a long time, and the Majordomo certainly doesn't want chaos. So as long as you remain humble, chant the prayers quietly alongside me and the most powerful elementals, don't do anything out of line, and let the ceremony proceed smoothly, that's enough." Jace listened, recalling Ruk'tus's words. "After it starts, will the Majordomo guide the ritual together with the two priests?" "Yes." Ghestan said. "The fire protection rune stabilizes all of Blackrock Mountain. It possesses supreme power, so the ritual's guides also need to prepare enough mana and maintain absolute focus." "So there aren't other elementals helping at the top?" Jace asked. "Only the Flamewalkers, Master Slovakia." Ghestan smiled slightly. "Just as the Twilight's Hammer holds a transcendent position in Shadowforge City, the Flamewalkers' status in the Firelands and Molten Core surpasses all other elementals." Back at the Black Forge, Jace immediately gathered everyone in his room and relayed what Ghestan had told him. "I'll head out this afternoon to contact the SI:7 agent outside and inform them about the date change." Solara said. "Two priests, four guards." Vereesa wiped her lips with a finger, thinking for a moment. "We just don't know how strong the guards protecting Executus himself are. These flame monsters are enemies we've never fought before." Grede pointed at himself with his thumb. "At least we know one Flamewalker guard can't handle Thaurissan himself." "Why?" Vereesa asked. "Otherwise why would Executus need four Flamewalker guards to watch the dwarven nobles who could reach the top of the throne?" Grede spread his palms. "What other nobles are worth noticing? The mages aren't allowed at the top. Dagran Thaurissan is the most powerful warrior in Shadowforge City. To the Majordomo, he's the biggest threat to watch." Vereesa raised an eyebrow slightly. "I have to admit, there's some truth to that, Grede." "Then we'll each take one." Needleflame said. "Me, Vereesa Windrunner, Sister Solara, Morophil..." "We two can't attend the ceremony, little black dragon." Morophil said. "I can only follow once you've caused enough chaos inside. Otherwise, I might get pulled out of stealth by those powerful fire elemental watchers and cause trouble." "And Morophil and Sazeno need to bring the Wind Prince's half-cage to us." Jace said. "The cage's radiating power can't be properly hidden in Molten Core. We can't take it in advance." "I have to go too?" The imp looked up. "Can't I wait outside for your good news? Do you think my fire magic can deal even one point of damage to those fire elementals?" "You can at least take one fireball to spread aggro, Mr. Sazeno." Needleflame smiled at him. "Hey hey?" Sazeno planted his hands on his hips. "Now that I think about it, I can do a few other things too." "Then you help your master watch the fourth one..." Needleflame started. "Don't forget about the two priests." Solara said. "We need to be prepared for the possibility that they might abandon the ritual and come stop us. Jace should probably deal with them, clearing the last obstacle for Grede." "Solara's right." Jace said. "If those two priests can free themselves, they're no less threatening than the four guards." "So the three of us need to keep four flame demon guards occupied." Vereesa said. "It's nearly impossible to summon natural forces for protection inside Molten Core. That sounds a bit difficult." "Don't be so pessimistic, little elf." Grede said. "I'll make sure it ends quickly. The flame demon guards might not even have time to lay a finger on you before their leader is dead. Then I'll join you quickly, and the numbers will even out." "Maybe you'll be the one struggling against the Majordomo, and we'll have already killed all his guards and have to come help you!" Needleflame shot back. "Then let's see what happens." Grede stared at her. "Better make a bet, kid!" "I'll bet a cup of Sulfuron ale!" "By then the whole Shadowforge City might be blown sky-high, and you're still thinking about your Sulfuron ale?!" While they were bickering, Jace was silently wondering whether Needleflame could really handle one of Executus's guards on her own. His main concern was that carrying the Sulfuron Hammer the whole time would drain her stamina. But Jace didn't want to say it outright, worried Needleflame would jump up in anger. Vereesa noticed Jace's look. She glanced at Needleflame, then nodded subtly toward him. It was probably a signal that she'd keep an eye on the little black dragon. That put Jace somewhat at ease.
