## Chapter 1574: The Protection Rune

Jace had no idea how many soldiers Dagran Thaurissan could actually mobilize while chaos reigned inside Molten Core, but the thousand-plus Dark Iron nobles and citizens crammed into the lecture hall looked like a scattering of refugees beside this lava lake -- a dark swarm of ants creeping along the deep-red shoreline, as though a single ripple from the lake could swallow them all.

In the game, the area where Arcanist Shazzrah stood was also part of Baron Geddon's patrol route.

But right now, Jace didn't see any sign of that rampaging Fire Lord. Maybe the Baron was patrolling the whole of Molten Core and had wandered off somewhere else.

And what about Garr, the other half of the binding?

Jace looked past the black hill in the opposite direction -- behind the column's path of travel. In the game map, a raid pushing into Molten Core would first pass through the cave guarded by the Fire Lord Garr before reaching this lava river where Geddon and Shazzrah stood.

The distant view behind him was blurred and distorted by the glow of a lava river cutting across the field of vision, but Jace could still make out the pitch-black shadows beneath the rough stone walls.

Vereesa had mentioned that she could sense Molten Core was roughly a ring-shaped structure -- broadly similar to the game. If that was right, those shadows should be the entrance to Garr's cave.

That more or less pinned down the layout of this area.

The Fire Lord Garr was one of the closer targets. After all, to use the Eternal Quintessence to destroy the protection runes, they first needed to take the other half of the Wind Prince's binding from Garr. And Garr was also the guardian of one of the runes -- they had to kill him regardless.

But looking back across the whole lava lake, Jace realized the depths of Molten Core were far more expansive than he'd imagined.

They'd been following the Flamekeepers for a while now, and the black hill on the opposite shore seemed to grow clearer through the haze, yet somehow no closer.

It made him worry. The runes were spread all over Molten Core. Would there be enough time to reach each one and destroy them all?

He wasn't the only one worried. Vereesa was also gazing into the distance, studying the underground lava lake, occasionally glancing at the Arcanist on the island in the center, holding his gem-studded staff.

Sora was craning her neck, examining the cave roof, looking near and far, as if sizing up the length of the stone pillars descending from above.

What was she planning? Fly?

Right. Jace realized he'd overlooked that.

He'd run Molten Core so many times in his past life that he'd defaulted to assuming no mounts were allowed here, let alone flight.

Sora and Needleflame could of course revert to their true forms and fly in here. This place was definitely spacious enough for gliding.

But no matter whether they ran or flew, they couldn't bypass the Flamewalker Lord Shazzrah in the center of the lava lake.

A powerful Arcanist was far too big a threat to their group. He stood between Executus's throne and Garr's cave. If a fight broke out, they'd need to deal with this guy first, right here in the lake.

If Jace were alone, he wouldn't be confident about taking down a fire-owl wizard on his home turf this quickly. But with Vereesa, plus the advantage of surprise, maybe they could eliminate this problem before the other elementals could process Executus's defeat.

Twilight's Hammer cultists were chatting nearby. Some who'd arrived in Shadowforge City this year were visibly thrilled by the grandeur of Molten Core. A dwarf Flamekeeper was right beside them. Under these circumstances, he couldn't discuss attack plans with his companions. But waiting until the action started to tell them everything he'd been thinking would be too late.

Jace thought for a moment, then sighed. "Truly magnificent."

"Chaotic. Blazing." Ghestan said from ahead. "Perfectly matches everything I imagined the Hour of Twilight would be."

The Hour of Twilight seemed a bit too specific for this place, Jace thought.

He nodded in apparent agreement, then turned to Vereesa, who was following behind. "What do you think the distance is from that hill to the center of the lava lake?"

"Hard to tell from here." Vereesa said flatly. "We'll need to get to the top before it becomes clearer."

Her tone was exactly like those insufferably arrogant elves you'd meet in Dalaran. For a moment, it made Jace feel she'd become a stranger. Clearly, the Ranger-Lord had put some real work into how to pose as an elemental worshipper.

But just as she finished answering, she seemed to suddenly understand what Jace was getting at. She glanced back at Sora, who noticed their looks and nodded back.

Obviously, the three of them had the same thought.

But if Sora took flight, she'd instantly become the most eye-catching thing in all of Molten Core. Every elemental would try to blast her out of the sky. The flame intensity at that point wouldn't be something a few flasks of Fire Protection Potion could handle.

Better to have a flying creature that wasn't afraid of fire handle the problem. Needleflame was trudging forward, laden with heavy armor. Jace could see with his naked eyes that the decorative patterns on her armor bore faint amber light -- barely noticeable, but definitely there. The heat from the Sulfuron Hammer was growing harder to contain.

All of Molten Core was the embodiment of Sulfuron's power. The Sulfuron Hammer had basically come home here. Naturally, its strength would be difficult to control.

Even though black dragons weren't afraid of fire, Needleflame was surely suffering more than any of them. Suppressing the hammer's power was a severe mental drain. Better to let her focus on carrying it to the Majordomo's throne.

Vereesa still had the Queen Ysera's earring. Even though it couldn't hide their presence in Molten Core, the Dream's power within it should provide Sora with some mana, letting her use the Dream's energy to protect herself in the center of the lava lake and withstand the flames.

Walking along this scorching shore, even with Fire Protection Potions, every step was a torment.

As they neared the black hill, a Flamewalker about four meters tall emerged from the fiery haze at a junction guarded by several burly Fire Elementals and Lava Elementals.

He was covered in dark yellow scales, his strange wrinkled face bearing a pair of small black eyes that stared at them with no expression at all.

His body was covered in things that were hard to call either tentacles or spikes -- they looked like the tips of flames, making him stand there like a grotesque flesh statue of flame.

The monster carried a spear whose tip burned continuously. Based on Ghestan's earlier description of the ritual, Jace guessed he was one of the four Flamewreath guardians stationed at the base of the Majordomo's throne.

Vereesa and Grede both eyed this guard sideways, probably gauging how tough it was. Most of the dwarves didn't even have the courage to meet its gaze. Many had already lowered their heads, muttering prayers in Dwarvish.

These Dark Irons -- usually arrogant and sharp-tongued -- had all shrunk like curled-up woodlice. Some were even crying, whether from fear or religious fervor.

Unlike the dwarves, the motley collection of Twilight's Hammer cultists nearby kept their faces cold, showing no fear at the Flamewalker's appearance. After all, their patron was the Firelord's "boss." They had standing.

Passing under this Flamewalker guard's gaze, they entered a junction and began climbing an upward slope. A burning flame could be seen ahead at the top.

It wasn't a Fire Elemental Walker, nor some random wildfire. It was a quite beautiful garland of flame.

As they got closer, Jace noticed a massive metal pedestal beneath the garland.

It was a rune. One of the seven.

Despite spending over a month studying those seven runes in Shadowforge City, and having glimpsed something that looked like a protection rune from the edge of Molten Core, this was the first time he'd seen one up close.

The protection rune was surprisingly huge. He estimated it was about the size of the entire throne platform inside Stormwind Keep, far larger than the impression he'd gotten from the game. It was like a grand altar surrounded by flames.

The rune character was something like an upside-down V, pronounced "Meth" in Elemental. Just thinking the sound "Meth" in his mind felt like the rune's power had given him a violent shake. Then the power of the entire cave pressed in from all directions, nearly making him feel like he was suffocating.

Now Jace understood why, when he'd mentioned pouring the Eternal Quintessence directly onto the runes, Lord Rokor had found it so laughable. These runes truly possessed immeasurable power, enough to stabilize all of Molten Core.

Based on the path they'd taken, plus the relative positions of Shazzrah and the black hill ahead, Jace could roughly guess which in-game rune this corresponded to.

In the game, heading from the lava river where Geddon and Shazzrah were defeated toward Executus, you'd find a rune bound to Baron Geddon on a ridge. On the left side was a deep, vast cave where the core hound leader Golemagg the Incinerator, another rune's guardian, lurked below.

This path was much wider than the narrow ridge in the game. Combined with a strange sense of being swallowed by the lava world, it took Jace a moment to realize they'd already reached this position. But when he looked to the other side of the slope, he couldn't see the chasm cave where the Incinerator was. They probably needed to go further.

He turned and exchanged a look with Vereesa, wanting to confirm the rune's shape with her. He raised an eyebrow -- a signal asking if the rune she'd seen when she met Baron Geddon had looked like this.

Vereesa understood and nodded, confirming she'd seen something similar to this rune among those deep caves.

That gave them a reference. It would make navigating through the chaos clearer.

Jace then twisted his head in the direction he thought Golemagg was and gave another look. Vereesa caught his meaning and squinted in that direction, observing the surroundings. She was probably memorizing the route.

But just then, the Flamewalker guard who'd been following them since the entrance of the upward path lowered his head and looked toward the Twilight's Hammer group. Vereesa withdrew her gaze and kept a low profile, no longer looking around.

After climbing the slope, they reached another gentle incline. Many Dark Iron dwarves had already gathered here spontaneously. Some knelt, some lay prostrate, calling on Ragnaros's name and begging for blessings. More dwarves who'd accompanied them this far hurried up to join them.

For a moment, officers, minor nobles, merchants, and blacksmiths -- at least a thousand or two dwarves of all kinds -- gathered at the foot of this black hill, facing the pitch darkness above.

Prayers, wailing, mingling with the rumble of rock and flame from deep within Blackrock Mountain, made Jace's scalp tingle.

"We go, Master Slovakia." Ghestan said woodenly, looking at those dwarves. "They are not where we should stop. We stand closer than they do to the Majordomo, closer to the great Firelord's power."

Jace grunted in acknowledgment and looked up at the heights of the black hill. Between layers of ash, he could already make out the shadows of those enormous Flamewalkers.

Was Executus among them?
