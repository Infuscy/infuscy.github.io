Chapter 1575: Executus

The Twilight's Hammer column left the Dark Iron dwarves behind and continued up the mountain path. This was a carved trail winding around the hill -- not only steep but shrouded in ash. The air seared the lungs with every breath, thick with the choking stench of cinders. No mortal could survive here even for a moment.

As the switchback brought them to the other side, the dwarves' prayers faded into indistinct noise. Vereesa tapped Jace's shoulder from behind and pointed down into the ash haze.

At first Jace couldn't see anything, but after slowing his pace and staring for a while, he did spot another flaming garland.

Compared to the rune they'd seen on the mountain path, this one was weaker. It wasn't just the haze blocking his view -- Jace could clearly feel how feeble this rune's power was, and the flame garland was noticeably dimmer.

Getting closer, Jace noticed the rune character flickering above it, like an S with a dot underneath. In Elemental, it was pronounced "Kro."

So this was the target rune for the reinforcement ritual?

Having burned for seven years and now approaching winter, its power was very weak. Based on the rune's position relative to the hill, Jace guessed it was the one guarded by the fire-owl lord known as the Sulfuron Herald.

In the game, that lord was accompanied by four priests -- five of them together in that area. But here, no matter how Jace looked, he only saw three Flamewalkers with bright yellow scales and trident-staffs near the rune. Was the Sulfuron Herald among them?

No, he remembered the Sulfuron Herald had a noticeably larger build. Even if that was just the model scaled up to make it visible to players, Jace remembered at least that his skin color was different from his attendants -- a clear contrast.

Like green-scaled Lucifron, blue-shadowed Shazzrah... these powerful fire-owl lords looked very different from ordinary flamewreathes because of their swollen power.

So the Sulfuron Herald wasn't here. Jace felt a faint unease.

Continuing up the switchback, they rounded a cliff and the "Kro" rune disappeared from view, but his worry only grew.

Piercing layer after layer of ash, the Twilight's Hammer column finally neared the hilltop under the Flamekeepers' guidance.

At the top, surrounded by several heavily armored guards, stood Dagran Thaurissan wearing a crown of flame and a noble black-and-red robe, along with a cluster of similarly dressed Dark Iron nobles. They were already waiting.

Under the watchful gaze of two flamewreath guards holding fire tridents by the entrance, Thaurissan approached and greeted the red-robed Flamekeeper and the Twilight Lord Ghestan in Elemental, one by one.

"We are late, Your Majesty." Ghestan bowed.

"The Majordomo Executus will soon descend upon the throne. The ritual is about to begin, Lord Ghestan. You have arrived just in time." Thaurissan said respectfully. "In the name of the Firelord, may this ritual proceed smoothly."

"In the name of the Firelord." Ghestan echoed.

"In the name of the Firelord. Praise the Twilight's Hammer. Praise the Old Gods and their servants."

Those around them bowed and chanted in unison. Jace and the others didn't miss their cue, blending in perfectly. They'd rehearsed this many times.

Raising his head, Jace noticed Dagran Thaurissan's gaze sweep past him. In that instant, he sensed the Emperor's inner turmoil. He wondered if Thaurissan was wrestling with the coming upheaval.

Turmoil was good. Turmoil meant the Emperor was genuinely preparing to rebel against the Firelord, not set on helping Executus wipe out the assassins.

Reaching the top of the black hill, Jace noted how different this place was from the game. Maybe the game had made the arena bigger and flatter for the convenience of fighting Executus and his many guards. Here, everything else was larger than in the game, but this spot was actually narrower.

In the center of the summit stood a real altar -- a dark brown stone seat carved with runes, images of Flamewalkers, and dwarves. A literal throne of fire.

But what really caught Jace off guard were the shadows of six fire-owls of varying heights standing behind the altar, faintly visible through the smoke.

Executus hadn't arrived yet. So with the two trident guards at the entrance, that made eight?

How was that two more than what Ghestan had said?

Grede looked back and forth, clearly counting heads. Sora's expression also shifted. Everyone realized the problem.

Vereesa fixed her gaze on the tallest of the fire-owl shadows behind the altar. Jace followed her eyes. That fire-owl carried a flame-shaped lance. His firepower was extraordinary, unlike any of the other seven at the summit.

"Wait in humility, mortals."

The fire-owl muttered as he slowly crawled out of the smoke. He looked about five meters tall, making the two nearby guards seem like pitiful children. The spikes on his belly and body were charcoal black, but his scales were the red of blood.

"The Herald of Ragnaros."

Ghestan and Thaurissan both recited the title as they bowed.

So... the Sulfuron Herald. The guardian of the Kro rune and one of his priests had also come to attend the ceremony. Well, that knot that had been in Jace's stomach could stop twisting now.

The Sulfuron Herald lowered his crimson face -- wrinkled like an ancient witch -- and swept his gaze across the assembled dwarves and Twilight's Hammer cultists. "Today, Ragnaros will witness your loyalty."

Each syllable of Elemental carried power that seared the heart. Apart from Dagran Thaurissan, whose dark face remained impassive, everyone present -- even Ghestan -- showed signs of pain. And that was without mentioning the cultists who'd drunk Fire Protection Potions.

Jace took a deep breath and gritted his way through on the red dragon blood. Grede was fine, just a slight frown. Vereesa clenched her fist and held on through gritted teeth. Sora had her eyes gently closed, looking a bit pale.

Beside him, Needleflame kept her head low, silent. The amber glow showing through her "armor" was brighter than when they'd entered Molten Core.

Damn. The Herald's power must have stirred the Sulfuron Hammer's magic in some way, making things harder for the little black dragon.

"Needle..." Jace whispered her name. Needleflame turned her head and looked at him. Her dusky human girl face, which had seemed healthy before, was now all scrunched up.

Jace reached out, trying to touch her "armor" to feel what was happening. But the black dragon suddenly grabbed his fingers, gripping them tightly.

Jace felt like his fingers had been clamped in red-hot pliers. The sudden searing pain almost made him cry out, but he just gritted his teeth and let out a quiet breath.

Though it nearly sent him packing, the young dragon's expression did relax a bit. She even tilted her head and gave him a sly little grin, like she was saying "you asked for it."

Jace looked around. Good. None of the Flamewalkers present had sensed the Sulfuron Hammer's power fluctuation.

Partly that was because this was Molten Core itself, overflowing with Sulfuron flames, which would mask the hammer's magic to some degree. But it also showed how hard Needleflame was working to keep the magic from leaking. She was bearing too much.

Under the pain, the hammer problem was at least shared now. The issue was: how were they supposed to deal with this unexpected fire-owl lord?

The Sulfuron Herald was a pure fighter. No fancy magic. But as the Firelord's and Executus's most trusted lieutenant, his strength couldn't be underestimated. This complicated things considerably.

Before he could figure anything out, the throne ahead suddenly ignited. The many fire-owls, including the Sulfuron Herald, bowed their heads respectfully. Dagran Thaurissan and the Dark Iron councilors and high nobles hastily prostrated themselves on the steps before the throne.

"Executus. Executus."

Half of Molten Core was chanting the name. Even the roar of the churning lava seemed to accompany the calls.

A black serpentine figure emerged from the flames billowing up around the throne. His form was like a true god... even the Sulfuron Herald beneath him looked like a pitiful attendant.

The true master of the fire-owls and all Shadowforge City appeared above the altar, wielding a blood-red trident. His head emerged from the swirling flames. His face was like a stiff corpse, his eyes burning with amber demon-light, silently fixed on the Dark Iron Emperor before him.

Jace kept his head low, chanting the Majordomo's name along with everyone else. At the same time, he glanced at Grede ahead of him. Whether because he sensed the stare or not, Grede also snuck a look back.

Now? Still think you can bash his head in with one hammer swing, Grede?

Your turn, Jace Sesso? Yeah, your turn.
