Chapter 1576: The Ritual Begins

On the Majordomo's throne, Executus's gaze darted between the prostrate dwarves below and the fire-owls around him with their heads bowed.

Jace saw him glance at their group a few times, but he didn't seem particularly interested.

Eventually, his attention returned to Thaurissan before him. In a raspy, fire-elemental voice, he said: "I am not satisfied with your performance, Dagran Thaurissan."

The Twilight's Hammer cultists stood behind the dwarves, with a tauren blocking Jace's view of Thaurissan. But even without seeing the Emperor, Jace could feel his heart clench.

The emotion was so powerful it was like a warlock's spell gone wrong, releasing chaotic, disordered shadow.

Executus must have sensed this emotional shift too. He couldn't help but snicker, as if mocking the dwarf's helplessness.

"The Flame Rune grows stronger every year, Dagran Thaurissan. In at most another seven years, Sulfuron will merge with Blackrock Mountain. Ragnaros will descend upon Azeroth with his full power. When that day comes, even the Dragon Prince Nefarian will be nothing more than a pitiful insect before him. Without Neltharion, the Black Flight is nothing."

"Before that, you still have a chance to prove your loyalty -- and more importantly, your value. When the legions of the Firelands flood this world, when endless Flamewalkers walk the freshly charred earth, what can the Dark Iron dwarves and Shadowforge City still offer Ragnaros?"

"Two hundred years ago, Ragnaros saved your ancestors from the armies of Wildhammer and Bronzebeard. Ragnaros gave you and your people the searing power to stand firm in Blackrock Mountain. Remember to repay this debt, pathetic dwarf."

"We will always remember this debt, Majordomo Executus." Dagran Thaurissan swallowed his pride and spoke humbly.

All the other dwarven nobles and Flamekeepers kept their heads down and echoed the Emperor's response to the Majordomo. They went on repeating their endless thanks to these fire-salamanders until the Molten Core ruler's rigid face finally twitched, the corner of his mouth curling up slightly.

Grede was clearly getting impatient. He looked back at Jace again, rolling his eyes toward Executus on the high throne, asking if they should go now.

Vereesa frowned. Jace lifted his head slightly and mouthed the word "Ritual." Grede understood, sighed, and turned back, studying the flamewreath guards on the other side of the altar, including the Sulfuron Herald, who was gazing up at the fire-owl lord with reverence.

Rokut specifically mentioned they had to strike when Executus and the fire-owl priests began the ritual.

When they'd first entered Molten Core, Jace had considered attacking Executus on sight, worried that Needleflame wouldn't be able to carry the Sulfuron Hammer for long. But now, with an extra priest and the Sulfuron Herald, they had to wait for the ritual to start and keep Executus and the priests occupied.

Though Jace didn't know exactly how much energy the ritual would drain from the Majordomo, having just seen the protection rune's power up close, he was certain reinforcing it wouldn't be easy. So he could only signal Grede with his eyes to be patient and wait.

At that moment, Needleflame loosened her grip on Jace's hand. She'd held against the Sulfuron Herald's pressure earlier and now seemed a bit more comfortable.

Jace pulled his hand back. His fingers had nearly gone numb from her scorching grip.

"The winter from the north grows more vicious by the day. This cold is not from Khaz Modan's snowy peaks, but from much farther away." Executus said, turning to look at the two fire-owl priests behind him. He said something in a barely intelligible language, and the two priests gathered near the throne.

Farther north? That must be Northrend.

Jace had no expertise in Azeroth's magical climate. The winter that had forced Molten Core to reinforce the rune two days early -- he wondered if it had something to do with the Lich King.

The fire-owl master exchanged a few low words with the two priests, then turned and said: "Begin the ritual."

The sound carried across the entire black hill. Soon, excited dwarven shouts came from below.

"By the Majordomo."

The nearby Flamekeepers answered in unison. Along with Ghestan and two Twilight's Hammer cultists in robes, they climbed the stairs past the prostrate Dagran Thaurissan.

Executus held his trident in one hand, slowly lowered his head, and closed his eyes. Soon, everyone at the summit could hear a low chanting. Everyone knew the sound came from the fire-owl master before them, but his lips never moved. The voice seemed to come from nowhere.

As the Majordomo began his incantation, the two fire-owl priests joined in, matching his rhythm. Their spell wasn't a continuous stream like an Arcane or Shadow incantation. It sounded more like a conversation -- back and forth with something.

From deep within Molten Core came an uncomfortable low growl, like a response to the chanting. But the sound was weak and helpless, hazy, like an old ox about to be slaughtered. The fire-owls' intermittent chanting -- Jace couldn't tell if it was comforting the thing or pronouncing its death sentence.

Then the Flamekeepers and Twilight's Hammer priests began chanting in Elemental. Jace didn't recall any decent fire-shamans in the Twilight's Hammer embassy. As it turned out, aside from Ghestan, the others in robes who looked like priests didn't have much real power either. They were almost just going through the motions beside Ghestan.

But the Flamekeepers invited to the throne actually had some skill. Flames were already burning around them, and together with the fire-owl priests on the other side of the throne -- whose bodies towered several times higher -- they raised spinning rings of fire.

When everyone around the throne was wreathed in these rings, the dim rune characters on the throne suddenly flared, like molten iron. Their light overwhelmed all the relief carvings of Dark Iron dwarves and Flamewalkers.

The gravel beneath their feet began to shake. All of Molten Core trembled. Executus suddenly gripped the blood-red trident with both hands, holding it before his chest. The head burst into roaring flame.

The heat at the summit rose higher and higher. The throne and surrounding priests were like a bonfire burning too fiercely, baking everyone nearby. Some Dark Iron dwarves prostrate nearby groaned in pain. As for the Twilight's Hammer cultists, many already had Fire Protection Potion bottles in hand. The ones fumbling for their potions in a panic were probably first-year attendees.

Though Grede's ancestral gifts kept him from being roasted alive, he still pulled out a potion.

But it wasn't Fire Protection Potion. It was a flask of Ursoc's Strength Jace had given him earlier. Looked like he was getting serious.

But that yellowish potion looked nothing like the dark red Fire Protection Potion. What if someone noticed?

Jace looked around. Not all the Twilight's Hammer cultists were holding the same potion. Some were drinking elixirs that boosted their resistance to heat rather than absorbing flame damage. The liquid wasn't red either. So Grede's potion didn't stand out too much.

Watching people drink their potions gave Jace an idea.

Since he was worried about the Dark Irons searching their packs, he hadn't brought any suspiciously strong potions into Shadowforge City besides some Mana Potions and Healing Potions. The only thing he had left was a flask of Multi-Resistance Potion, which Ursula had prepared for his last trip to Karazhan.

But one Multi-Resistance Potion was enough for now, as long as it let him withstand his own Shadow spells.

Amid the fire-owls' deafening chant and the lava lake's roaring echo across the cavern, Jace lowered his head and handed the Multi-Resistance Potion to Sora, who was about to drink Fire Protection. "Drink this."

"What is it?" Sora asked.

Jace mouthed a "F" sound. Sora's eyes widened slightly. She glanced at his staff and immediately understood his plan.

"After it hits, make sure to help dispel it for the others." Jace warned.

Sora nodded firmly and downed the potion in one go. Vereesa and Needleflame noticed their exchange, looked at each other, and more or less figured it out. It wasn't their first time enduring Jace's Fear spell.

Since the ritual began, Grede had been glancing back at them regularly. When he saw Jace handing Sora the potion, he stared intently, asking without words: When do we start? What do I do?

Jace stared back and bit his lip, then made a small explosion gesture near his head with both hands. Catching the hint, the dwarf's nostrils flared slightly as he took a deep breath. Jace knew he understood.

Just then, a tauren seemed to notice their signals and exchanges, staring down at Jace.

But noticing now was clearly too late.

The two priests raised their sharp forks high, pouring all their strength into helping Executus channel Molten Core's power. The surrounding fire-owls, the Sulfuron Herald, and the guards meant to watch the throne were all involuntarily drawn to the immense energy.

Executus's enormous body -- so big it sent a chill down one's spine -- trembled continuously. The power he was channeling in this ritual was terrifying. It felt like the entire Blackrock Mountain was struggling against him and the Flame Rune's power.

This was their best chance.

"Go." Jace said quietly.
