## Chapter 1577: The Black Mountain Summit

Grede shot a look at Needleflame and then charged silently toward the throne. Every Twilight's Hammer cultist was too stunned to react -- his movement was jarringly out of place in a scene where everyone stood frozen, not daring to stir.

Needleflame abruptly shifted form in a burst of ash, her body swelling. Her dragon wings knocked several nearby cultists flying.

"Why is there a black dragon?!"

A fire-owl guard cursed in Elemental and turned their way.

"Catch!" Needleflame, already gripping the hammer in her jaws, hurled it toward the charging Grede. Grede leaped high, caught the hammer with both hands, and erupted with crackling lightning. When he landed, he had already grown into a massive silver demigod, crushing a huge section of the staircase beneath his feet!

"Sulfuras?!" The Sulfuron Herald called out in surprise, his whole body tensing as he charged toward the stairs, bowling over several Dark Iron dwarves, trying to intercept Grede. But from an inconspicuous corner, a warlock had already raised a bone staff glowing with crimson demon blood.

A horrific shriek from the Twisting Nether drowned out every sound at the summit, followed by screams from countless dwarves and Twilight's Hammer cultists.

No one knew how many people shouted as they leaped off cliffs or slid down steep slopes. Some crashed straight into the throne. One fire-owl guard, driven mad by fear, swung his heavy trident one-handed and cleaved a dwarven noble in two in a single blow -- a spray of blood.

Two dwarven Flamekeepers on the throne were caught in the Fear spell. The moment their concentration wavered, the flames surrounding them consumed them instantly. They didn't even manage a scream of fear or pain.

Sora, with the help of the Multi-Resistance Potion, weathered Jace's Fear. But it was hard to say whether the potion deserved more credit, or Jace's advance warning.

Her left hand clutching Vereesa's earring, she spread her wings and took flight in a soft pink glow. The cool mist she pushed out was faint against the scorching air, but enough to dispel the fear's hold on Vereesa nearby.

The elf got to her feet and took a breath. She glanced at Jace, shook off the lingering Fear illusion, and readied her staff.

The flowers on her staff suddenly bloomed, emitting flashes of brilliant white light. A bright arrow was already nocked to her bowstring -- its glow stark against the blackened ash, crimson flames, and ominous shadow energy.

The Ranger-Lord moved fast. The arrow carved a clean path through the ash, flying straight at a fire-owl guard who was lowering his head, trying to shake off the Fear's influence and steady himself.

The radiant light easily pierced the fire-owl's ugly face, tearing through its skull. A second arrow followed, punching through the monster's chest. Flame sprayed from the wound, instantly consuming the creature and blasting it apart into splattering, burning lava.

As Vereesa loosed her arrows, Sora puffed out her chest and spat a great billow of yellow mist at another fear-stricken fire-owl on the opposite side. The highly corrosive acid splashed onto the flamewreaths, drawing agonized howls from the two still struggling with fear.

The other fire-owl priest had no time to spare for his comrade, who'd been shot dead in an instant. He raised his crocodile-claw arm and swung it at Sora. By the time his hand reached the apex of its arc, a ball of fell flame had already gathered. These fire-owls didn't even need to waste time chanting Elemental to throw spells like this!

The fireball tumbled straight at Sora. It had gathered too fast, been thrown too fast. Sora, fully focused on spewing acid to kill the fire-owl guard as quickly as possible, didn't have enough attention left to react.

A black shadow lunged from midair and swallowed the fireball whole. The startled priest froze. The black dragon had already opened her massive jaws, trailing black smoke as she beat her wings and dove toward him. The fireball she'd just swallowed was already churning back up her throat, mixed with molten rock!

Before the priest could throw his own fireball, the incoming glob of molten rock blew him and his spell apart. The priest clearly wasn't badly hurt by Needleflame's fire, but the explosive impact was enough to stagger him. And then Needleflame slammed into his chest like a giant iron bomb.

The black dragon and the fire-owl rolled across the ground in a tangle. Needleflame tore off one of his arms in a single bite and swallowed his molten blood.

Black shadows in the sky. Flame jets. Streaks of white light. The Twilight Lord Ghestan stared at Jace in horror, as if the charm spell had finally weakened amid the madly churning magical energy atop the mountain.

All of this... because of her?

But she didn't dare say anything, didn't dare look any longer. The cultist priests around her had been overwhelmed by the Fear spell, just like the dwarves, and were immediately burned to death by the ritual's backlash.

The lava that had splattered from the fire-owl Vereesa had shot and blown apart hit the steps before Ghestan -- and also struck the two fire-owl priests beside Executus, who were still single-mindedly channeling the ritual.

Pain-filled growls crept into their chant, but like Executus at the top of the throne, they made no other reaction to the chaos.

Grede spat at the sight of Executus. Jace's mass Fear hadn't shaken the Majordomo -- it had only made him hesitate in his steps for a moment. Of course, Jace had known that would happen.

In the utter chaos, the dwarf raised the Sulfuron Hammer and leaped up another huge section of stairs. But a shadow was already closing in behind him.

The Sulfuron Herald!

This fire-owl lord, trusted by Ragnaros, hadn't been affected by the Fear either. The reason he hadn't joined the melee was that he'd already recognized the key to this whole mess.

The Herald, burning red, raised his flaming spear at Grede's back. But his arms shuddered -- he couldn't thrust.

Several blood-red threads had caught the Herald's arms. Blood Magic's Grasp yanked him off balance mid-step. His thick serpent tail swept across the broken staircase, sending him sliding down a section, scattering scalding rubble and dust like bullets.

"Keep going, Grede!"

Jace bellowed as he began chanting Curse of Weakness. But the Sulfuron Herald turned and fixed his gaze on Jace, opening his black maw wide. The fire-owls' stiff faces -- lips barely moving even when they spoke -- made this sudden, violent expression shift deeply unsettling.

An explosion of sound powerful enough to shatter thought blasted toward him. Jace couldn't even tell whether his Curse of Weakness had landed on the Herald...

He pulled himself together, widened his eyes, and tried to find the figures of Grede and the Sulfuron Herald on the altar through his blurred vision. Dwarves were fleeing in all directions. Countless were being swallowed by the wild flames splashing everywhere from the black dragon and fire-owl's brawl.

Until he saw Grede, before Executus's enormous body, already raising the flaming hammer high.

A silver line tore through the air and struck the dwarf square in the shoulder. A piercing metallic crash exploded.

Grede took the hit from the spear -- several times longer than his own body -- thrown at him. The spear bounced high into the air. Grede, knocked back by the force, roared and tumbled off the altar...

The first window was gone. They had to kill the Sulfuron Herald first. As long as he stood guard here, Grede would never succeed.

Grede climbed back up from behind the steps. He didn't look badly injured, but his focus had shifted from Executus to the Herald, who was retrieving his spear. Jace knew they were thinking the same thing.

Then Executus opened his eyes.

He shouted something in barely comprehensible Elemental. The two priests immediately pulled themselves out of their single-minded ritual channeling.

The black hill began to tremble violently. People who hadn't yet recovered from Jace's Fear stumbled, crashed against the steps, and slid off cliff edges. Crying and shouting everywhere.

But the dwarves' and cultists' suffering wasn't over. The two priests raised their hands together. Jace felt the shadow energy in the air suddenly get snatched away, gathering toward the priests. The power was unmistakable... Shadow Bolt Volley.
