Chapter 1578: Witness the Hammer

Dozens of Shadow Bolts rained down on them. Vereesa had been nocking an arrow to shoot down a fire-owl guard who was raising his spear to hurl at the green dragon in the air. Seeing this, she loosed the arrow and scrambled back to dodge. The volley hammered into the struggling fire-owl priest and the black dragon. Needleflame let out pained cries but kept her jaws clamped on the priest's neck. The fire-owl's tentacles were already half torn away.

Sora in the sky swept her massive wings, batting away several of the incoming Shadow Bolts. But there were too many, and in Molten Core, the two priests seemed to have inexhaustible mana, firing continuously. Apart from Grede, who simply ignored the Shadow Bolts entirely and charged at the Herald with his hammer raised, almost no one could keep defending.

At the top of the black hill, around the throne altar, the stench of shadow was nearly drowning out the fire's scorching heat. Some dwarves hit by Shadow Bolts were instantly corroded into half-skeletal frames as they fell. A tauren, struck by several bolts, lay gasping on the ground, his hooves already completely rotted. He wouldn't be getting up.

You can affect these shadows, Jace Sesso. You know you can.

Even if Ussales wasn't saying it, Jace knew.

It wasn't his first time consciously redirecting Shadow Bolts.

Jace raised Ussales high, reaching out with his awareness to catch the rain of Shadow Bolts. He couldn't reverse them all and send them back at the priests in such a short time, but he could at least make them miss their targets, buying Vereesa and the others room to fight.

Soon, most of the Shadow Bolts seemed to lose their aim. The black spheres that had been tracking living things one by one started hitting the ground. Gradually, all the bolts veered toward Jace. The two priests realized the problem too, but they couldn't wrest control of the already-fired bolts away from the human warlock in front of them.

Got it...

Jace couldn't celebrate for long. He had to attune his emotions to the storm of Shadow Bolts.

Two bolts struck Jace. The shadow ate through his Twilight robes, revealing the ugly demon face beneath, which flickered under the shimmering Mage Armor and let out a low "Hah." The next bolts that hit him were greedily devoured by the demon souls.

Gaelen Arrow, no wonder you got this excited, huh?

But just letting them hammer him wasn't going to work. The robes couldn't absorb all the shadow damage -- some was still eating through his magic and flesh. And the bolts he hadn't managed to redirect were still threatening his companions.

Jace swung his staff, gathering the shadow energy exploding nearby into his own volley and hurling it back at the altar.

Deep within the black Molten Core, three shadow casters' volleys crossed each other. But it was the two priests who broke first.

One of the fire-owls stopped channeling his volley and raised both hands, using the shadow energy flooding the summit to summon a fairly powerful shadow spell. In the next instant, every surviving fire-owl -- including the other priest, the two guards, and the Herald's attendant priest still locked in combat with Needleflame -- was covered by a magic shield.

A shield like that...

Vereesa's arrow had just been flying at one of the guards. The moment the magic bolt touched the shield's membrane, it shattered like ice, splitting into several finer magic bolts that shot back toward her!

The elf startled, immediately spun backward into a somersault to dodge the chain of magic bolts, barely avoiding being hit herself.

Jace's Shadow Bolts were slower, but it was too late to pull them back. They struck the fire-owls' shields and were all reflected!

He quickly raised a Dark Ward to defend. But the energy from his volley plus the other priest's still-channeling bolts was overwhelming. His Dark Ward held for only seconds before shattering. Then the anti-evil Mage Armor was easily torn through. Even the robes couldn't absorb everything. Several more Shadow Bolts slammed straight into him. The intense corrosion nearly made him lose consciousness.

The Sulfuron Herald, still fighting Grede, also had one of those shields. The dwarf leaped high, swinging his hammer at the Herald. The Herald -- whose body was the size of a small building but whose upper body moved with startling agility -- coiled sideways to dodge the heavy blow. But Grede, as he passed, twisted and hurled Karthus's Hammer. The hammer, trailing a chain of lightning, punched through the shield and smashed into the Herald, knocking him off balance.

"What kind of madman dares to challenge the Firelord's guards in the deepest heart of Molten Core?!" the fire-owl lord roared. Though his body was covered in gashes and one arm was broken and useless, the blessing of the fire domain seemed to let him ignore his wounds entirely. He still fought with fierce determination, holding his spear one-handed as he clashed with the dwarf.

Jace watched the spinning electric hammer punch through the shield. The lightning peeled off and scattered, some of it hitting Grede.

But the lightning's power had mostly been spent on propelling the hammer. By the time it neared its target, most of it was already discharged. And Grede, already in his Avatar form, wasn't bothered by the remaining shocks. He broke through the crackling electricity and charged again.

But it proved one thing: the shield could reflect magic, but not direct physical blows.

"Don't use magic arrows, Vereesa!"

Jace shouted at the top of his lungs. After taking his own volley's worth of Shadow Bolts, he could barely stand, but at least he could still make noise.

Vereesa nimbly dodged two Shadow Bolts, grabbed a handful of ordinary arrows from the corpses of nearby Twilight's Hammer thugs, nocked them all, and chanted a spell to enchant them with Arcane energy.

The arcane-infused arrows scattered toward the two priests and Executus at the highest point. One priest took an arrow through his shield and into his eye, letting out a strange roar. The other was hit too, but though it didn't seriously wound him, his channeled Shadow Bolt volley was interrupted.

It worked, but the arrows from the Twilight's Hammer soldiers were too brittle. Some burned up mid-flight from Vereesa's own Arcane charge. Even the ones that made it into the fire-owls' massive bodies couldn't inflict fatal damage.

Then Jace felt a cool breeze surround him. Sora was channeling a healing spell from above, repairing the shadow damage. For him, natural energy was far more agreeable than the Holy Light.

He pushed himself up. With the brief respite Vereesa had bought him with her arrows, he could take some action of his own.

Shadow shield, huh? Pulling that trick on me...

Jace held his staff in one hand and aimed the other at the two priests like he was grabbing something, all while chanting.

Those shields could block incoming projectile magic. But they were still made of shadow energy. So he just needed to peel them away... absorb them, convert them into his own vitality. He didn't even need Sora's healing.

"Yukroni, Morgak, Tam..."

Jace chanted loud and clear. The shields on the two priests began to ripple with unstable waves. Meanwhile, Jace's shadow power grew stronger and stronger, his energy rising.

This finally broke the priests. Their fear swelled. Jace knew they didn't have much left.

"Who gave you the nerve to infiltrate my throne?!"

Executus, who had been holding the rune ritual steady, finally seemed to realize things were spiraling out of control.

At the highest point, he held his blood-red trident, his face twisted into something like a pure demon. Finally, he slammed the trident hard into the altar. The ritual was broken.

Jace felt Molten Core, which had been stabilized by the Flame Runes, begin to churn. The rune guarded by the Sulfuron Herald, in the distance, went out completely -- vanishing into the drifting ash, not a spark remaining.

Fissures split under their feet. A huge chunk of the altar collapsed. Everyone present, even the fire-owls, struggled to stay upright.

Only Executus stood firm. He raised one finger and pointed at Sora, who was channeling healing magic from above, dispelling shadow corrosion.

A flash of light. The enormous green dragon simply vanished! What kind of teleportation magic was that?!

"I'm going to crush every last one of you, ungrateful maggots!"

Executus's fury poured out like a river of flame. The ground below the altar kept splitting apart. A surviving Flamewalker guard was crushed on the spot by shifting black rock. Needleflame fought her way into the air, while the priest she'd been grappling with fell into the cracked earth and disappeared.

Executus moved easily across the crumbling mountain on his massive serpent body, laughing as he raised his trident, searching for any maggot he could find.

Jace used Shadowstep to leap onto a relatively stable boulder. But Executus caught him immediately.

"You die." the Majordomo laughed coldly, raising his giant trident to aim. Before Jace could steady himself and raise a Dark Ward, a roar came from behind the fire-owl master.

"Executus! Do you recognize this hammer?!"

"You think a pathetic fake like that can shake the true Sulfuron?!"

Executus laughed and turned. He reached out to grab the flying, silver-glowing Grede. But the fiery hammer met his arm instead, smashing it to pieces. Spraying lava flew across half the black dome...

"What... Sulfuras?!"

Executus cried out, unable to accept reality. But it had already happened.

"The Majordomo's hand is broken. By the Light..."

"That must be a fake! That dwarf is a Wildhammer!"

"Firelord..."

The earth rumbled. The surviving Dark Iron dwarves were too stunned even to flee. They just stared.

The fire-owl guards below were still trying to climb the crumbling slope, but Executus no longer cared about them.

"This isn't right. This isn't what should happen."

The fire-owl master murmured, then turned and fixed his gaze on the Sulfuron Herald, who was charging toward where Grede had fallen among the collapsing rocks, spear raised. Executus extended his remaining arm. With a flash of fire, the Herald jerked. His whole body suddenly flashed red and flickered.

Before Jace could process what was happening, the Sulfuron Herald exploded. The shockwave obliterated what was left of the altar and blew half the mountain apart. Countless dwarves were swallowed by the fire before they could even scream. Debris scattered all the way to the distant lava lake.

Jace's Dark Ward took the blast, but it still sent him flying. His mind went blank...

No, no. They had to kill Executus. That was the only thought left in his head.

He reached out with every sense to track the enormous presence. But Executus seemed to be getting farther and farther away. He was using the explosion to cover his escape.

Then Jace saw a massive shadow, surrounded by a flame shield, darting between the collapsing black rocks. That was him. The bastard was running!

"He's running, Grede! He's running!"

Jace shouted, urging the Orostaz's Hide to help him fly and chasing in that direction. But flying debris and dust kicked up by rolling rocks kept battering him, interfering with his movement. The flames of Molten Core twisted his cloak as if they had a mind of their own, making it impossible to stay stable.

But he knew Grede, without a gryphon, would have an even harder time catching him.

Sora had been hit by some spell and was gone. Needleflame was clearly exhausted; carrying the Sulfuron Hammer had already drained most of her strength. Fighting a priest on top of that was already a lot.

Damn it. Were they really going to let Executus escape into the deepest parts of Molten Core? If he did, Dagran Thaurissan would likely turn on them on the spot... if he was still alive. But if he was dead, the situation would be even worse.

Then a flash of magical wings streaked past behind Jace.

Vereesa, holding her staff-like bow, was lifted by the magic wings. She leaped off several collapsing stone pillars and lunged like the wind toward where Executus had fled.

A series of Dream Arrows tore through the black dust, stabbing straight at the flame shield. The fire around the shield grew dimmer and dimmer. Until a low growl echoed from the depths.

She'd hit him.

Jace pushed the cloak with every last bit of strength toward that direction. Even if Grede couldn't bring Executus down with the Sulfuron Hammer, he and Vereesa had to.

But another stone pillar collapsed from above. He dodged with Shadowstep. The repeated Shadowsteps had already cost him too much energy. Executus wasn't just a simple elemental priest either. Jace didn't know if the magic he had left would be enough to kill the bastard.

The whole mountain was collapsing, sliding. Dwarf screams. Elemental roars... By now, probably all of Molten Core knew what was happening.

"Right there, Needleflame! Put me down, put me down!"

Jace thought he heard Grede shouting. He rushed toward the sound, dodging falling rocks, stepping on ground that was still solid, until he saw something in the distance.

On a black serpentine body rolling down from a high cliff, a silver figure was raising a burning hammer, smashing it again and again into the head covered in spikes and tentacles.

"Stop! Stop, you mad dwarf!"

"Mercy! Great Mountain King -- no, Firelord! I'll serve you, I'll serve the new Firelord! I can be your Majordomo..."

Executus's trident was long gone, somewhere. His roars had turned to wails, and soon even that fell silent.

"The Majordomo is dead! The Majordomo is dead... This can't be!"

Countless shrieks of despair came from the Dark Iron citizens below. Jace swallowed. Even he couldn't quite believe it.

Executus was really dead?

Grede had killed him? They'd actually done it?

Before the feeling could settle, a cold, calm elemental command came from somewhere across the lava lake, its speaker hard to identify.

"The Majordomo is dead. Return to Molten Core. Summon the Firelord."
