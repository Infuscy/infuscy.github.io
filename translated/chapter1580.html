Chapter 1580: Shazzrah's Ambition?

Needleflane carried the three of them through the collapsing black-brown rubble, flying with her claws -- one gripping Grede tightly, the other clutching Vereesa, with Vereesa holding Jace dangling at the bottom.

Needleflame wasn't Sora. And after carrying the Sulfuron Hammer for so long, on top of the brutal fight, she had nowhere near the strength she'd had when they entered Molten Core.

The black dragon flew in the direction Vereesa pointed, her wings beating with obvious strain. Several times she nearly slammed Jace into the cliff walls.

"I saw you fly earlier, elf!" Grede yelled. "Since you know flight magic... can't you fly yourself?"

"It's just a spell." Vereesa sighed. "And I don't have much practice with it. Summoning it here is many times harder than in the Emerald Dream. I can barely keep myself in the air for a moment with it."

"Not to look down on you, Grede, but if you'd been in my position back there, even with wings that could fly for twenty minutes, you wouldn't have been able to hit Executus from the air!"

"Completely unreliable magic!" the dwarf yelled.

"You weigh more than both of them combined, Thunderfist!" the black dragon shouted. "Now shut up and let me focus on flying. There's debris everywhere!"

"Fine, fine." Grede grumbled.

Thaurissan was right. Down below, countless fire and lava elementals were gathering toward the collapsed black hill, forming slow-flowing rivers of melt between the fissures and canyons.

Some of them noticed the black dragon above, roaring and hurling rocks and fireballs. But Needleflame dodged them nimbly, or Grede knocked them out of the air with Skycrack.

"Have you noticed, Jace?" Vereesa said, looking down. "Not a single fire-owl."

"Yeah." Jace said. "After Executus blew up the summit, those four that were climbing up disappeared too."

"Now that you mention it, I didn't see them either. I was ready to fight them when I chased after him." Grede said.

"Me neither." Needleflame said.

"The Arcanist Shazzrah is gone from the lava lake too." Vereesa said. "Since you asked me to note the distance from the mountain to the center of the lake on the way up, I checked that spot when Grede killed Executus. There were no fire-owls there anymore -- just some fire elementals."

"He's not coming with the other elementals to surround us?" Grede asked.

"No. No fire-owls are coming this way, Grede." Vereesa said.

As they briefly talked, taking advantage of the lull in elemental attacks from below, a faint dragon roar echoed from somewhere ahead in the distance.

"Did you hear that?" Vereesa asked.

"That's Sora..."

Needleflame muttered hoarsely. Her wingbeats suddenly picked up dramatically in range and frequency, as if she'd been startled like a giant bird. The exhaustion vanished. Jace and the others dangling below nearly lost their grip and fell from the sudden acceleration, but Needleflame kept charging forward, heedless.

At the dead end of a gorge in the stone wall ahead, clusters of flames were fighting something. Clouds of corrosive yellow mist kept spraying out, driving back the rolling lava.

"There!" Jace shouted.

He didn't need to point. Needleflame had already folded her wings and was diving at full speed toward the low ground.

Her speed increased. Jace was swung back and forth by the gale of the black dragon's frenzy. Finally, all three of them were thrown off. Jace hit the ground just in time to see the black dragon ram straight into the elementals like a bomb, triggering a violent explosion. Shattered molten rock and ash burst outward.

"Damn crazy girl..."

Grede had already shifted into his transformation when he landed. He raised the Sulfuron Hammer and sprinted into the smoke where the dragon roars were coming from. Vereesa overtook him.

The smoke seemed to tighten as if something was grabbing it. Then a flash of fire. A massive fire elemental surged out, charging straight at Vereesa.

The elf squinted, nocked two arrows, and aimed at the roaring flame. She released. The two arrows spun in opposite directions, precisely shattering the braces on the elemental's arms. The fire elemental tried to keep charging the ranger, but couldn't escape its planar bindings and burned out on the spot.

Several lava elementals rolled down from the cliff wall. One dropped onto a low outcrop and leaped high, aiming at Grede as he ran sideways past the ranger.

Grede raised the Sulfuron Hammer and smashed the lava elemental apart with a single blow. Another lava elemental churned through layers of rolling stone beneath it, moving at an incredible speed that was hard to believe unless you saw it with your own eyes. It caught up to Grede and punched him hard in the back of his silver head.

The Wildhammer dwarf, having just thrown his heavy two-handed hammer, couldn't swing it back in time. He took the punch to the head -- and actually shattered the lava elemental's arm!

Before the elemental could throw its other arm, a warlock behind it set its molten core ablaze with shadow, disrupting its elemental balance. The creature crumbled with a roar.

A smaller fire elemental fighter burst from the smoke mid-air, clenching its flame-like fists, ready to pounce again. But a black dragon head shot out and swallowed it whole.

The explosion's smoke gradually cleared as various forces churned through it. Grede raised his hammer and scattered a cluster of gathered lava elementals. Vereesa drew and shot, breaking the bracers on two more fire elementals. The force that had looked so fierce from above, ready to crush whoever was trapped here, had been thoroughly broken apart.

After Needleflame's tail spike took out the last lava elemental, everyone rushed to the green dragon in the corner.

"Sora, are you okay?" Vereesa asked.

"I'm fine." the green dragon said quietly. "I didn't think I could hold them off much longer. Executus teleported me into this ravine, and before I could get back, a bunch of elementals surrounded me."

"I thought you'd been teleported out of Blackrock Mountain." Grede said. "Good thing Jace figured Executus wasn't that powerful."

"Even if she had been teleported out of Blackrock Mountain, we'd still have to come look for her first, you stupid dwarf!" Needleflame shouted.

"I didn't say we wouldn't come!" Grede shot back.

"I heard some fire-owls shouting earlier. They said Executus was dead... You did it."

Sora said this, looking ragged. Many of her scales had been knocked off. She was probably pretty badly hurt.

"But not completely." Jace looked at her legs -- once golden, now scorched black in patches -- a bit worried. "They're planning to summon the Firelord. We need to find the other half of the binding and destroy the Flame Runes as fast as possible. Morofil has already slipped into Molten Core during the chaos. The Dark Iron dwarves are holding back the elementals for us. Now's the chance."

The green dragon let out a low growl, then was enveloped by a cool breeze.

"Don't worry, Jace. I'm not as bad as I look. At least I'm still faster than rolling rocks. Everyone, get on."

The group scrambled onto Sora's back. Grede asked: "Did any fire-owls come to attack you, Sora?"

The green dragon said: "A few guards followed the elementals and found this place. But once they sensed Executus was dead, they all left."

"Maybe they all went to summon Ragnaros." Jace said.

In the game, Executus -- after being beaten off by players -- only performed a short ritual to summon Ragnaros. But since Dagran Thaurissan thought there was enough time to destroy the Flame Runes, it probably wouldn't be that fast.

Anyway, this wasn't the time to panic. Finding Garr, who guarded the other half of the Wind Prince's binding, and regrouping with Morofil was the priority.

"I'm not sure, Jace." Sora lifted her wings. "But before they left, they seemed to be talking about Shazzrah ordering them to find the Fire Lord Garr."

"Arcanist Shazzrah is looking for the fire lord too? Why?" Grede asked.

"Probably trying to gather forces..." Sora shook her head.

Jace was silent. A guess was forming in his mind.

Was Shazzrah trying to seize both halves of the binding in the chaos? Rokor had mentioned that possibility.

Majordomo Executus was dead. The Firelord hadn't manifested yet. This was the perfect time.

But one half of the binding was with them.
