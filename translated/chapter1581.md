## Chapter 1581: The Ash-Filled Cave

Whether it was the Sulfuron Herald's violent explosion shaking the fire elementals of all Molten Core, or a Flame Rune's failure destabilizing the planar junction, Jace couldn't tell.

The shaking, cracking ground. The churning lava rivers. The ceaseless groaning of the earth like a monster's roar...

Add to that the spiking temperature, and everything in sight was distorted by the scorching air. It felt like Blackrock Mountain was about to be destroyed for a second time.

The green dragon led the black dragon, dodging who knew how many lava elementals' splashing magma and glowing rock throws. They finally escaped the gorge where Sora had been trapped. Looking toward the lava lake, the black hill, and the passage down from Shadowforge City -- either smoke and ash had swallowed everything, or familiar landmarks had changed beyond recognition.

"Where do we find the Fire Lord?" Grede yelled. Even though he was nearly tearing his throat out, his voice could barely compete with the roaring around them.

A lava river below was cut in two by a falling boulder, splitting into several branches flowing in different directions. Jace had been using this river to judge their position relative to the lake. With the distant landmarks gone, he suddenly wasn't sure which way to fly.

"Up to you, Vereesa. Do you remember those caves behind us, the direction we came in from? Damn it, I can't see a thing now."

"You're sure the Fire Lord is guarding those caves?" Grede asked.

"I'm not sure. But when we came in, I saw a lot of lava elementals guarding that area." Jace said. "Time's tight, Grede. We don't have other options. We have to go that way."

Vereesa nodded, pointing at a distant patch of rolling, fire-breathing ash and smoke. "You need to go through that, Sora."

"Needleflame." Sora looked at the black dragon beside her. "You'll have to lead the way..."

"Understood, Sora." The black dragon adjusted her heading and dove straight into the black smoke, smashing through the flames and ash like a black blade cutting through water.

Everyone pulled up their cloaks and hoods. Sora followed close behind the black dragon, moving forward before the smoke could close back in.

Even with Needleflame clearing the way, breathing the air here was comparable to being trapped in a burning oven. It was unbearable.

Fortunately, with the two dragons' flight speed, they didn't have to struggle through it for long. Soon, Needleflame led Sora to a black rock wall. Below, caves of various sizes had appeared. At the mouth of one enormous cave lay collapsed, still-smoking rubble, clearly the remains of destroyed lava elementals. There were also some recently killed fire-owl guards.

"Did Thaurissan's men take them out?" Grede asked.

"I doubt his guards reacted that fast." Jace said. "If the ones willing to follow Thaurissan unconditionally can hold Executus's corpse and keep the elementals busy for us, I'll already be grateful."

"I'm not sure how these fire-owls died, but these lava elementals were shattered by extremely powerful Arcane blasts." Vereesa said. "Even though most of the energy has dissipated, I can still clearly smell the lingering traces."

"Arcane?" Sora twisted her neck to look at Vereesa on her back. "Are you saying Shazzrah called the fire-owls away to find Lord Garr -- not to join forces with him, but to kill him?"

"I don't know... Could be a dwarven mage instead." Vereesa said hesitantly.

There was almost no doubt now. Shazzrah was planning to intercept and kill Garr and Geddon in the chaos, seizing the Wind Prince's binding for himself.

He wanted to take advantage of this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to merge the two halves of the binding and devour the Wind Prince's final soul fragment. If he succeeded, he'd become the most powerful being in the Firelands after Ragnaros himself -- and the next "Majordomo" would be his for the taking.

"If Lord Rokor was right, Shazzrah clearly has his eye on the Wind Prince's binding too." Jace said. "We need to be ready to face both that Arcanist and the Fire Lord Garr at the same time."

Hearing that, everyone's expression turned serious.

Grede raised an eyebrow. "If they're fighting each other, that's a good thing, isn't it?"

"That's true." Vereesa said.

The cave was spacious enough deep inside, but the tunnel wasn't straight. Sharp rock spikes jutted everywhere. Thick smoke from crevices blocked the view. It was no longer good for flying.

Sora and Needleflame shifted to human form and charged into the depths with Jace and the others. The remains of shattered lava elementals littered the ground, growing denser. But fire-owl corpses became fewer.

The air was unbearably foul. But fortunately, the tunnel wasn't as long as expected. They soon left the passageway and entered a wide, empty cavern.

Jace chanted a Mage Armor spell to prepare. Sora summoned a Wild Blessing.

The hall was filled with faint smoke. Compared to the lava lakeshore, it even felt somewhat chilly. This cold wasn't natural. Jace felt it was familiar... it was the --

"The Wind Prince's binding is here, Jace." Grede said quietly, staring into the depths of the mist, watching for any movement.

"What about Garr?" Needleflame asked.

"Garr is gone. And all of this is your so-called Twilight's Hammer's doing. I knew trusting those fools under Executus was a huge mistake."

A low voice in Common came from deep within the smoke. A faint blue Arcane glow drifted from the source.

"Damn it." Grede cursed under his breath. It was Shazzrah, as expected.

If he'd already taken the Wind Prince's binding, that meant Garr was either dead or had surrendered. But judging from the shattered lava everywhere, it was probably the former.

"How many?" Sora whispered.

"Only two." Vereesa said quietly.

"Five against two. We've got the advantage." Grede muttered, tightening his grip on the Sulfuron Hammer.

"You think I'd believe the Dark Iron clan's prophecy like Executus did? Let myself be intimidated by some ridiculous fake? The Firelord's might cannot be imitated by mortals. And the Sulfuron's flames are not something this cold and fragile mortal world can bear..." The voice from the depths switched to Elemental. "You want to prove your loyalty, Gehennas? Then help me kill these mortals who think they can challenge the gods. Prove you're worthy of being Majordomo."

"Yes, Lord Shazzrah." Another fire-owl responded from the smoke in Elemental.

"Good..."

The other one was Gehennas?

That was another Flame Rune guardian. Another Molten Core boss.

But even more shocking than facing two bosses right now was what Shazzrah had said. Prove you're worthy of being Majordomo.

He'd only guessed Shazzrah might want to replace Executus as Majordomo... but didn't that statement mean he was demanding Gehennas's loyalty while also promising him the Majordomo position?

Did that mean Shazzrah wanted to become the Firelord?

An Arcanist who wanted to rule Sulfuron? Had the Wind Prince's last remnants of soul given him that confidence, or was he just a power-mad lunatic?

"I see you."

As Shazzrah's words faded, Vereesa muttered a spell and suddenly drew her bow, firing two shots in quick succession into the mist -- one high, one low. The glowing arrows flew straight but didn't trigger any expected explosion. It was like they'd been swallowed by something.

The ranger froze. Grede had already hurled the hammer Karthus from his belt. The lightning-trailing hammer lit up most of the cavern, revealing two massive shadows in the smoke -- one large, one small. As it neared the larger one, the lightning flared into white light. But the big shadow suddenly vanished. Grede's face showed no joy of having hit anything.

"No, this is..." Vereesa's eyes went wide. The Arcane glow in her magical sight dimmed by half.

A clean, simple Blink spell.

"Spread out!" Jace roared. But it was already too late.
