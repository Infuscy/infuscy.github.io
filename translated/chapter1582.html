Chapter 1582: The Arcane Flamewaker

A massive azure figure appeared silently behind them, and in the blink of an eye, arcane energy swelled from nothing into a roar loud enough to split heads.

Jace immediately drove shadow energy, trying to use Shadowstep to get away, but he was one beat too slow.

A force he couldn't imagine resisting exploded from behind, accompanied by a sky full of arcane stars and an ethereal shriek that nearly pierced his eardrums. For an instant, Jace couldn't see or hear anything.

His vision tumbled countless times before finally stopping, and only when he rolled into a patch of scorching rubble did he realize he'd been blasted who knew how far away and slammed solidly into a stone wall.

That Arcane Explosion was absurdly powerful. Not that Jace was overestimating himself — based on his past experience dealing with all kinds of mages, he could gauge its intensity.

By his estimate, with his current physical condition, this robe, plus Mage Armor and the green dragon's Gift of the Wild boosting his magic resistance, he shouldn't have been blown apart this badly.

And after taking that Arcane Explosion, he couldn't even recover quickly. Blood was streaming from his eyes and nose.

Shazzrah's Arcane Explosion had churned the cave's dust into chaos. Jace lifted his head, but in his blurry vision, he couldn't see anyone else.

Aside from streaks of dream-light cutting through the smoke, he could still sense Shazzrah driving arcane magic to attack, which meant however the others had taken that ambush, they were still holding out.

"My arrows can't hurt him, Grede!"

"Damn... damn! I can't see... shit."

"Did Dagran Thaurissan kill Executus? Otherwise I can't imagine that fool — no matter how stupid, weak, or blustering — falling to a bunch of pathetic clowns like you."

Shazzrah's laughter echoed through the wide chamber. Jace suddenly remembered that in the game, Shazzrah not only had a powerful Arcane Protection that halved magic damage taken, but also a spell that doubled arcane damage taken by players — called "Shazzrah's Curse."

Jace caught his breath and forced himself to focus. He could indeed hear an unnatural arcane ringing in his ears. He'd already been hit with arcane magic — he just hadn't noticed in the chaos. Maybe that spell had resonated with the Arcane Explosion, making him take a far more devastating hit than expected. If his body hadn't been tempered to extraordinary toughness through years of training, he'd probably have passed out or just dropped dead on the spot.

He had to find a way to dispel this curse first. It wasn't shadow magic, so Jace couldn't dissolve it with Cleave Darkness.

"Sora! Sora!" he shouted with all his strength. Not far off, the green dragon answered twice.

"What, that human's voice? Still alive after my curse?! Maybe Executus's trust in you had some reason after all. Ha! Too bad—"

Before he finished, Jace felt a barrage of Arcane Missiles streaking toward him. He immediately raised a Dark Ward to block, but as he chanted, he sensed shadow energy seeping silently into him from somewhere, not even stopped by the Dark Ward.

Jace knew something was wrong. He instantly used Shadowstep to shift toward Needlefang's voice. The first Arcane Missile shattered the Dark Ward easily, and luckily the rest didn't hit Jace dead-on as he emerged from the shadows, or he had no idea if he could survive tangled up in both shadow and arcane curses.

That shadow energy had interfered with his casting and left him feeling utterly weak. With his keen perception of shadow energy, Jace knew it came from another direction — another flamewaker lurking in the hall, Gehennas.

That was Gehennas's Curse. Jace remembered this one — a shadow curse that reduced healing on the target. As a veteran who'd healed through Molten Core back in the day, he knew exactly what negative effects each flamewaker's curse had.

But he hadn't expected Gehennas's Curse in reality to be strong enough to leave someone utterly debilitated, even interfering with their spellcasting... The game version was really a simplified one.

Still, at the end of the day, this was shadow magic. He could clearly sense every trace of emotion in it — intense abjection, cowardice...

Savoring those emotions, Jace easily devoured the shadow curse with Cleave Darkness, recovering some of his strength. A questioning "Hmm?" came from deep in the cave. Gehennas seemed to realize his curse had been casually dispelled.

Then came a storm of Shadow Bolts. Jace gritted his teeth and raised a hand, deflecting the bolts past him, then stopped wasting time and sprinted toward Needlefang's voice.

"Our opponent has a powerful shadow manipulator, Lord Shazzrah."

"Don't make excuses for your incompetence. Don't make me think you're as useless as Executus believed, Gehennas!"

Shazzrah's voice had turned razor-sharp, and Jace could feel the intense fear in the other flamewaker's heart — not fear of them, but of Shazzrah, or rather of following Shazzrah in the first place.

Jace ran into a heap of collapsed, still-burning boulders. In the distance, he could hear Grede's roaring and the sharp whistle of Vereesa's arrows cutting through the air. But from Shazzrah's manic laughter, their attacks weren't having much effect.

Shazzrah could hide in the smoke, dodge with Blink to avoid Grede catching him, and block Vereesa's magic arrows with his Arcane Ward. As for the arrows the Twilight's Hammer thugs brought... even hitting Executus's priests wouldn't have done lethal damage, let alone against a powerful flamewaker lord like Shazzrah.

Tracking by emotion, Jace found Needlefang, who was flustered. She was beside Sora, who was leaning against a rock, gasping for breath, looking utterly drained.

"She's been hit by some shadow curse... Jace Sesso, help, quick!" Needlefang shouted.

Jace nodded and immediately raised his hand, chanting Cleave Darkness to strip the curse from Sora. Sora finally opened her eyes, and in her weakened state, some draconic features appeared on her face, her tail emerging behind her.

"Sora, can you sense the curse on me?"

"Arcane curse." Sora nodded with difficulty, raised a hand, and moonlight streamed from her eyes. Soon, Jace felt the ringing in his ears that had been bothering him fade away.

"We need you," Jace said. "That arcane-user weakens our resistance and strengthens his own protection. You need to find a way to dispel his magic."

"I don't know if I can withstand his attacks, Jace. If he senses me working against his spells, he'll target me first."

"I'll be your shield, Sister Sora." Needlefang grabbed her shoulder. "If he wants to touch you, he'll have to tear off all my scales first!"

"Needlefang..." Sora said. "I should be protecting you."

"I'm Sabellian's daughter, of the Earth-Warder's bloodline. I protect others." Needlefang suddenly reverted to her true form, her wings blasting countless nearby fragments of stone. With this surge of momentum, Jace felt like she'd gotten even bigger since entering Molten Core.

She was a true child of flame — more so than any flamewaker born from lava and volcanoes.

Sora nodded, raised the Scepter of Dominion, and spun it in the air. A chill, fierce wind danced around her staff. Jace could even smell earth and grass in it, a hint of floral sweetness — faint though it was in the depths of Molten Core.

The wind blew away most of the smoke, making things clearer in the cave. Another Arcane Explosion detonated in the distance, and Grede screamed as he went flying, tumbling several times before hitting the ground.

"Sora... if you'd called that wind a bit sooner, I wouldn't have gotten blown up!"

The dwarf spat out a tooth, blinked hard, and stood up. Sure enough, he'd been caught by Gehennas's shadow curse too. Jace dispelled it with Cleave Darkness as fast as he could. Grede got up, caught his breath, and swore: "These... gutless fire lizards. Face me in a fair fight, Shazzrah!"

But no sooner was Grede's curse dispelled than Sora was wrapped in Gehennas's shadow again.

Jace immediately moved to dispel hers too, but a blue figure suddenly appeared out of thin air among the rocks between them.

Shazzrah's body looked like a flamewaker statue carved from sapphire. His appearance looked less like a demon or a corrupter and more like a wizened old man — you could barely see any evil at all.

"Since you asked, here I am. And conveniently, everyone's right here."

The flamewaker lord sneered, the arcane energy in his hand suddenly swelling into blinding white. The group's faces went pale, and Jace felt that piercing arcane ringing hit his ears again. Shazzrah cast his curse so fast it was impossible to track — the guy was a natural-born arcanist.

But Jace had to dispel Sora's curse first. She had to survive this hit and keep removing everyone's debuffs so they could counterattack Shazzrah. He'd already taken one Arcane Explosion, and after devouring quite a bit of shadow energy this time, he'd recovered some stamina. One more hit wouldn't kill him.

"Shazzrah!"

The black dragon suddenly slammed into the flamewaker. The Arcane Explosion in his hand went off uncontrollably, but it was far weaker than last time.

Shazzrah cursed and shoved the obsidian staff one-handed into the black dragon's chest. With a build even taller than the Sulfuron Harbinger, he pushed Needlefang back, then cast a powerful Slow spell with his other hand, practically freezing her in place.

"Shazzrah, I'll bite your head off and pull out every one of your tentacles to pick my teeth!" Even immobilized, Needlefang's mouth never stopped.

"Damn black dragon. The Twilight's Hammer really will take any lunatic these days!"

Shazzrah turned his gaze toward Jace and Sora, but Sora — who'd just had the shadow curse stripped by Jace — had already been channeling her dispel magic for a while. Even the Arcane Explosion hadn't interrupted her.

The arcane energy shimmering on the flamewaker lord's body suddenly dimmed. His form looked less blue. A streak of rainbow light shot after him.

"Die, Shazzrah!" Vereesa shouted.

Shazzrah sneered. With just a slight lift of his hand, his Arcane Ward rose again, his whole body glowing with unnatural blue light.

Fast as the dream arrow was, by the time it struck Shazzrah, most of its power was blocked by the barrier, dispersing into rainbow light. Shazzrah's body just shuddered — no real damage.

"Just a moment, Quel'dorei." "Over here, idiot!"

As the flamewaker cast to block Vereesa's arrow, Grede took a running start, leaped high, and swung the Sulfuron Hammer right at Shazzrah's face.

Shazzrah's expression tightened, and his form began to fade. He was about to Blink away! But Jace had anticipated this. When a warrior intercepts you, the best escape is Blink, isn't it, arcanist?

After dispelling Sora's curse, seeing Vereesa's arrow fly and Grede take his first step, Jace had already started chanting his spell of suffocation.

The arrow met the barrier. Grede leaped high, raising his hammer. As Shazzrah's form began to fade in a flash of light, his raised arm was snared tight by shadow chains.

That one disruption slowed his Blink. The Sulfuron Hammer smashed through the Arcane Ward and cracked into his jaw. At the same time, arcane flashed — Shazzrah had reappeared two or three hundred yards away, but his lower jaw was completely shattered. Crimson lava flowed from his blue face. He was, after all, an elemental born from the Firelands.

"One lucky hit. How much longer can you hold out?" Shazzrah said coldly.

Even without a jaw, these monsters could still talk. Jace knew — their mouths didn't actually move when they spoke. They didn't use flesh-and-blood organs to make sound.

Just then, Sora and Grede — who'd just landed — were immediately tangled in Gehennas's shadow curse again. Sora coughed violently, and the cave's dust was starting to pour in from every entrance. Grede tried to charge the blue flamewaker but collapsed from weakness, cursing like a madman.

"Well done, Gehennas! Keep it up! Keep it up!"

Elemental roars came from outside. The flamewakers seemed to be surrounding them. Gehennas's orange-red figure flickered in and out of sight. The chanting of shadow spells echoed...

"Hm? What's this? My magic... Gehennas, what did you do?!"

"No, Lord Shazzrah!"

"I haven't failed yet, you fool. One little mistake and you can't wait to turn on me from behind?"

"Has that pretender scared you too?! Do you dare betray me like this?!"

"That wasn't me cursing you, Lord Shazzrah!"

"I knew a two-faced coward like you was never trustworthy... Remove my curse now!"

Shazzrah's arms trembled as he chanted, trying to dispel the curse himself. But several dream arrows slammed into his body in quick succession. Under the curse's influence, his Arcane Ward was greatly weakened, and his lava blood splattered.

"I'll kill you, traitor! Die with them!" Shazzrah was furious past the point of reason. He didn't even try to counterattack Vereesa — he charged straight at Gehennas.

But the other flamewaker's shadow had long since vanished without a trace. The Arcane Missiles that chased after him hit nothing. The arcanist's power had been severely weakened — not only were they less powerful, they were slower too.

"Until we meet again, you worms."

Shazzrah sneered as he looked toward them, his gaze already unfocused.

He struggled to raise one blue hand, and his form began to grow translucent. Then a war hammer trailing lightning flew through the air and struck him square in the chest. The massive flamewaker lord toppled over, shaking the entire cave.

"Blink again, you arrogant bastard. Pull another disappearing act for me!"

The dwarf gritted his teeth and charged after him with the Sulfuron Hammer. Shazzrah screamed and thrust his staff forward. Grede smashed the staff head with one blow, then leaped onto his chest, ran a few steps, and slammed the hammer into his face. Silence.

Grede snatched the other half of the Wind Prince's cage that the flamewaker had been wearing as a necklace on a magic chain, then raised the Sulfuron Hammer and kept pounding. With each blow, the flamewaker's lower body twitched wildly, but there was no life left in him.

Vereesa, covered in arcane burns, collapsed to the ground. Needlefang, who'd taken half the Arcane Explosion, didn't even have the strength to return to human form, just lying sprawled in the rubble.

Sora leaned on her staff and looked at Jace. "That was you, wasn't it?"

"Yes and no."

Jace rubbed his eyes, smiled mysteriously, then slumped over, completely drained. Constant Shadowsteps and endless Cleave Darkness had consumed all his energy. But the biggest drain had been that last Dark Simulacrum.
