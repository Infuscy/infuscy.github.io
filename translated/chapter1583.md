## Chapter 1583: Siege of Molten Core

The arcanist's corpse lay at the base of the far wall. When even his unconscious twitching stopped, Grede finally relented, stepping down from his chest.

After shedding his Earthen ancestor form, the dwarf looked visibly drained, covered in all kinds of wounds. His shoulder was the worst — the spear the Sulfuron Harbinger had thrown had done more damage than expected.

None of them had recovered from the brutal fight atop the black ridge against Executus and the Sulfuron Harbinger before they'd gone looking for Sora, then beaten a series of fierce elementals to reach Garr the Lava Lord's cave, and then faced two flamewaker lords.

Deep in Molten Core, these flamewakers showed no sign of fatigue until the moment they were killed, fighting with boundless energy. Even if Shazzrah might have harbored thoughts of betraying the Firelord, he was born in the Firelands — this was the closest place to his domain.

Needlefang, who'd been hit point-blank by the Arcane Explosion, coughed violently. Sora was carefully tending to her, trying to heal her wounds, but she herself looked seriously drained, exhaustion written all over her face.

"How are you holding up, Vereesa?" Jace asked. "Is the Fire Protection Potion still active?"

"Still active," Vereesa said. "I can handle the heat here better than you think, Jace. Don't worry about me."

Jace opened the Kirin Tor's sealed box, took out the Flowing Ice Crystal inside, and handed it to her. "Here, Vereesa. We've already fallen out with the fire elementals, and the Dark Iron dwarves know who we are. There's no need to hide this thing's power anymore."

Looking at Aegwynn's treasure, it was hard to say whether the longing in the elf's eyes was for relief from the heat or the magic addiction from being away from Quel'Thalas for too long. But for a high elf fighting in Molten Core, both were indispensable.

Vereesa nodded and took the Flowing Ice Crystal. The tension in her brow eased.

By now, with Gehennas fleeing in panic, the flamewaker roars from outside had faded into silence.

Behind the massive pile of rubble where they stood, a seal-like protective rune blazed with a spinning ring of fire.

"I found it." Grede held up the half-face-shaped sealed cage. "Where's Morophil, Jace?"

Jace looked at the cave exits around them. "She's making her way here. Won't be long."

"I can feel these fire runes locking the plane, Jace. I can't even hear the wind or thunder," Grede said. "Are you sure your soul link with her is still working?"

Jace said, "But you can still summon lightning to empower your hammer. These runes do interfere with my connection to Morophil, but a soul link isn't communicating with elementals or a cross-plane summoning ritual. She'll find us. Don't worry."

Grede glanced at the hammer still crackling with electricity and muttered, "Fair point."

"Don't rush," Jace said. "We'd better wait here, rest a while, and prepare for what's coming."

"Counting the rune on the ridge, Shazzrah's rune on the lava lake, this one, and the one already destroyed... we still have three rune guardians to deal with. I'm pretty sure even if Gehennas got away, he's not going back to his post to guard his rune. So that leaves two."

Grede looked at the blazing rune behind them. "Aren't those elementals coming to protect this one?"

"This rune was guarded by Garr," Vereesa said. "Now that Garr is dead, the other flamewakers won't come here to help a lava lord. At least, from what we've seen of the situation deep in Molten Core, that's one possibility."

"Maybe they don't think we can destroy it," Jace said. "And honestly, that's about right. We need that wind elemental's help."

A strange scraping sound came from a narrow cave passage. It didn't sound like humanoid footsteps, nor like the rumbling of molten elementals moving boulders. It wasn't the heavy tread of a core hound or molten giant either, nor the whistle of a fire elemental's furious flight.

Vereesa already had her bow trained on the direction. Sora, who hadn't rested much, stood up again. Needlefang spread her wings, smoke curling from her mouth. But to everyone's surprise, a pack of fire lizards scrambled out of the cave.

Literal volcanic lizards, not the flamewakers' derogatory nickname. A bunch of six-legged brown-black reptiles crept in, hissing, each carrying several Dark Iron dwarves on its back.

These lizards looked low to the ground, with short, clumsy legs. But their constant life in the volcano had made them perfectly adapted to the terrain, and their thick legs moving at high frequency made them scuttle like crazed cockroaches. No wonder the Dark Iron dwarves chose to ride these monsters through Molten Core, where ordinary beasts couldn't survive.

The leader was a wizard in a purple-blue robe — the Emperor's wizard advisor, Craelmhold.

"So you are here, Master Sesso! I've imagined what you might look like, considered many possibilities, but I never thought you'd look this young."

Craelmhold trotted over with his staff. "You've dealt with Garr the Lava Lord... wait, wait! Shazzrah? He died here?!"

The dwarf finally noticed the dim blue corpse in the distance. Jace said, "He was the one who defeated Garr. He wanted to take the other half of the Wind Prince's cage. Are you here to help him, or to help us?"

"Why would I help that arrogant 'Incarnation of Arcane'?" Craelmhold laughed. "But clearly, he's no true immortal. Executus, the so-called prophet, the great guardian chosen by the Firelord, the Sulfuron Harbinger — they've fallen one after another. That proves these flamewakers are nothing special."

"I'm here under orders from Emperor Dagran Thaurissan to see if you need any help. Looks like I'm late."

"Not late." Jace looked at the dwarves behind the wizard — mostly Thaurissan's royal guard, plus a few other wizards.

"Can you summon some food and water? Especially water."

"Summoning water elementals in Molten Core would take too long. We have some ready." Craelmhold waved and called out in Dwarvish: "Give our water and food to these Alliance folk. We'll arrange the summoning ritual later."

Jace took the magically preserved water flask, uncorked it, and poured it down his throat. To his surprise, the water was still cold. Drinking ice-cold water deep in Molten Core was more healing than a Healing Potion.

"How's the situation at the Executor's throne?" Grede asked.

"Golemagg the Incinerator is leading elementals to attack Executus's throne, but he won't succeed. The Eru cavalry has already reached the Emperor. As long as the Flamewalkers don't reinforce, Golemagg is just wasting time," the wizard replied. "As soon as you destroy the fire runes, Golemagg's power will weaken along with them. He and the core giants here are the elementals most deeply bound to the Firelands' power."

"What about our army and the Bronzebeards?" Grede asked.

"They've already entered Molten Core from Shadowforge City," Craelmhold said. "Magni's brother, the Mountain King, is over by the lava lake right now. He and his forces recently defeated Baron Geddon and Itherion's fire elemental legion in Shadowforge City. Geddon has retreated deep into Molten Core. I hear the Wildhammers who came in from the other side of the lava river have also fought him. Kurdran Wildhammer's legion is planning to attack Magmadar's cave."

"Why did Baron Geddon enter Shadowforge City?" Jace asked.

"Looking for the other half of the Wind Prince's cage." Craelmhold looked at the sealed skull in Grede's hand. "That one handed the cage to the Twilight's Hammer. Clearly, with the chaos in Molten Core, he realized... wait, that skull isn't in your hands too, is it?"

"Obviously," Jace said. "The Wind Prince is the key to destroying the fire runes, wizard."

"You're counting on that remnant elemental soul to destroy the fire runes?" Craelmhold frowned. "Never mind. I won't question your decision — after all, I wasn't the one who killed Executus. Either way, is there anything I can help with?"

"Tell Lord Muradin Bronzebeard," Jace said, "to find the Wildhammer forces and attack Magmadar's cave if possible. That's where the last rune is. Break through there, and all the fire runes will be exposed to us."

"If the pressure on the Executor's throne isn't too great and Emperor Thaurissan can handle it, you and your followers should go help the Wildhammers too, Craelmhold. Once we've assembled both halves of the Wind Prince's skull, we'll circle the lava lake and destroy all the nearby fire runes... then head to Magmadar's cave to join you. Lady Somneril is a green dragon — she'll get us there faster than your fire lizards."

"I understand, I understand." The wizard nodded repeatedly. "Well then, good luck. You'll need it."

With that, Craelmhold mounted a fire lizard and barked orders to the Dark Irons behind him.
