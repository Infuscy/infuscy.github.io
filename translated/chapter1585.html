Chapter 1585: Lava Lake

After Garr's protection rune was extinguished, the whole cave began to collapse. Jace's group didn't even have time to wait for Sora to turn back into her dragon form and climb onto her back. Amid falling rocks and flying dust, Needlefang grabbed Jace and charged toward the still-intact cave mouth. Sora snatched up Vereesa and Grede and followed Needlefang. Morophil, farther away, managed to take flight and grab one of Sora's hind legs.

There was no time to worry about flight safety in the narrow cave passage. The destabilization from the second destroyed fire rune was far more severe than they'd imagined. After escaping through the collapsing tunnel, they looked back at the wall — tumbling boulders, billowing dust... the deafening roar made it feel like the whole world was ending.

It wasn't just Garr's cave collapsing. Needlefang carried Jace toward the lava lake — Shazzrah's former location, where his fire rune was — and the ground was cracking all along the way, fissures spreading from Garr's cave and widening.

The lava lake churned violently, even flooding into the cracks. The rumbling from deep within Molten Core, like a beast's roar, was far stronger than when they'd followed the lava lord's trail into the rear caves.

This made Jace worry. What about after the next rune was destroyed? What about after all the runes were destroyed?

Loh'Kalar and Rocktus had both said the fire protection runes were stabilizers connecting the Firelands to Azeroth, and Molten Core was the border between the two planes. Combined with the effect each destroyed rune had on this border, it was reasonable to assume that once all the protection runes collapsed, Molten Core itself would collapse too.

And when that happened, Shadowforge City — built on Molten Core — and even all of Blackrock Mountain... might be affected.

If Rocktus knew this, the Thaurissan family, as Molten Core's gatekeepers for two hundred years, certainly did too. But Dagran Thaurissan was firm in his determination to destroy the runes. He couldn't possibly want to destroy his own city. Maybe when the time came, things wouldn't be as bad as Jace feared.

Needlefang followed Sora across the lava lake. Bubbles bursting from the molten depths would occasionally splash magma up to their altitude, but Needlefang would just flick a wing and deflect it all. Jace knew that if he weren't dangling beneath her, she probably wouldn't have needed to block at all.

In the game, Shazzrah's chamber was a patch of charred ash surrounded by a lava lake. Here, the center of the lava lake looked like a naturally formed grey altar, surrounded by a few stone pillars — like the ruins of some ancient collapsed palace.

Jace could tell that even among all the flamewaker lords, Shazzrah held a very high rank, second perhaps only to Executus. He even had his own "throne."

But even with Molten Core's power giving him nearly infinite mana and arcane energy so strong that Jace's group could barely withstand it, Jace still felt Shazzrah was far too distant from the Firelord's throne.

After all, to take Ragnaros's place, you needed Ragnaros to die in the Sulfuron Keep in the Firelands, with his elemental soul unable to escape. With Shazzrah's power, he probably couldn't even win a one-on-one duel against Ragnaros in Molten Core. What gave him the nerve?

The rune Shazzrah protected sat atop the highest point of these ruins, its ring of fire spinning furiously, as if desperately trying to replenish the power of the two already-destroyed runes.

"There's someone there!"

Suddenly, Vereesa, held by Sora up high, shouted: "A flamewaker — trying to run!"

Jace strained to look in the direction Vereesa pointed. It was a cluster of collapsed pillars. Honestly, he couldn't see anything.

"Throw me over there, Sora!"

The ranger shouted. Sora hurled the ranger toward the ruins. Vereesa shot forward midair on a gust of wind, and Jace thought he glimpsed feathered wings again, though not as clearly as when she'd chased Executus.

But riding that air current, Vereesa found her balance, raised Belanore, drew, and aimed into the ruins.

In an instant, several glowing dream arrows streaked through the air, triggering a chain of explosions. A roar came from the ruins. An orange-red figure emerged among the rubble, fleeing toward the far end of the grey ground.

Now Jace could see clearly. This flamewaker seemed larger than the flamewaker guards they'd faced, but not as big as Shazzrah or Executus. It was... was this Gehennas, who'd just escaped?

Jace had thought he'd fled to summon Ragnaros, or to rendezvous with Lucifron guarding Magmadar's cave. But instead, he'd run to Shazzrah's "throne"?

"I see him! After him, Sora!" Grede roared excitedly.

He didn't need to say it. Needlefang and Sora, one low and one high, chased after the fleeing flamewaker, swooping past a cluster of pillars to grab Vereesa mid-leap. And there he was — the massive flamewaker lord, Gehennas, running for the shore of the lava lake.

So that's where he'd run.

Gehennas turned and roared, spraying a volley of Shadow Bolts at the two dragons. Grede hurled the lightning-wreathed hammer into the air, where it exploded, shattering the most accurate of the shadow bolts.

Vereesa's dream arrow arced from afar, grazing the strange rock Gehennas tried to use as cover and driving straight into his chest. The flamewaker lord, covered in orange-yellow scales, howled and fell, something clutched to his chest spilling across the ground.

"Stop! Stop! I have something to say!" Gehennas shouted in Common.

Sora landed not far behind the flamewaker. Needlefang brought Jace down between Gehennas and the lava lake, cutting off any attempt to slip into the molten depths.

Now Jace noticed what the flamewaker lord had been carrying. A pile of magical artifacts — magic gems, wands and staves of varying lengths, trinkets. Some of them looked very fragile, yet they hadn't burned even when they fell on scorched earth and still-glowing lava.

These were no ordinary items. Probably Garr's loot, or Shazzrah the arcanist's personal collection.

"Keep these." Gehennas pushed the items toward Jace, Grede, and the others. "Spare my life."

"Seems like I could kill you right now and take all this anyway, flamewaker." Grede raised his hammer. "Is this really all you have to say?"

"What makes you think stalling a few minutes will help?" Jace asked.

"You're the warlock who devoured my magic, who manipulated my Shadow Bolts." Gehennas clutched the wound Vereesa's arrow had torn through him, staring hard at Jace. Even his eyes had turned deep purple.

"Your control of shadow is astonishing. Stronger than many Flamewalkers I've seen who specialize in shadow manipulation."

"I know you used the Eternal Quintessence to destroy Garr's protection rune. I know that power too well. And I know you're here to destroy all of Molten Core, to interrupt the Firelord's plan."

"I defected to Shazzrah — Garr and Geddon both knew it. Now Shazzrah's dead, and I can't stop Geddon and the other flames and lavas from telling the Firelord what happened. So there's no reason for me to stay in Molten Core, and certainly no reason to stop you from preventing Ragnaros's return. Because once he comes back to this world, I'll be eliminated immediately."

"But even with your power, you can't defeat all the rune guardians and stop Ragnaros from manifesting so easily. You need a greater power, warlock. Maybe not enough to match Ragnaros himself in the Sulfuron Keep, but enough to make him afraid to accept the summoning, afraid to enter Molten Core and face you directly. That's exactly why Shazzrah dared to try seizing Molten Core amidst the chaos..."
