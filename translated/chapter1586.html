Chapter 1586: The Flamewakers' Treasure

"A power that can make Ragnaros afraid?"

Sora was taken aback. Even Vereesa, bow drawn, hesitated.

"You've already reassembled the two halves of the sealed skull. Now you just need a simple ritual to achieve Shazzrah's dream — to fully devour Thunderaan's soul essence." Gehennas raised one arm, a lizard-like claw pointing at the cage Grede carried. "Those elementals and Dark Iron dwarves told you that Ragnaros took most of the Wind Prince's power but couldn't devour the last trace, so he sealed it in a cage. Right?"

The flamewaker chuckled. "Then have you ever wondered why, having consumed most of it, he couldn't devour that last trace for so many years? Because that last trace is the foundation — the very core of an elemental soul! Once he finds a way to fully devour Al'Akir's son and claims that core, he'll eventually recover all his power. Don't forget — this is an ancient warrior who dared challenge Ragnaros himself, one-on-one!"

"So Shazzrah found that method?" Grede asked.

"Of course, Wildhammer!" Gehennas said excitedly. "Spare my life, and I'll tell you—"

The flamewaker lord suddenly felt something grip his throat. Chains of darkness bound his arms and chest tight. They hadn't restrained his neck or head, but shadow magic control had already silenced him.

Gehennas's features twisted rapidly, turning fierce and savage. He tried to scream but couldn't. His consciousness began fighting desperately against Jace's shadow control, but the badly wounded flamewaker was on his last legs — he couldn't even cast his own Gehennas's Curse.

On top of the suffocation spell, Jace raised Usarlas without hesitation and added a Curse of Agony and a Corruption. The wound in Gehennas's chest festered quickly, purple-red lava oozing from the shadow-corrupted gash.

Before long, the towering flamewaker collapsed. After a few convulsions, he lay still. All the spikes and tendrils on his body began to wither, dry up.

"Why didn't you let him finish, Jace?" Grede asked.

"He was just trying to create a rift between us and the Wind Prince's soul, Thunderfist." Morophil said. "The master made the right call."

The demon looked at Vereesa and Sora nearby and continued: "Right now, our most important goal is destroying all the fire runes. This flamewaker lord is treacherous. He knew that once he told us Shazzrah's method for devouring the Wind Prince's soul, it would mean the Wind Prince's risk in siding with us was far greater than staying imprisoned by the Firelord, who didn't know the method."

"A flamewaker's cunning is hard to escape a succubus's notice." Thunderaan's cage spoke coldly. "I'm glad you weren't blinded by the promise of power coming your way."

"I hate to admit that everything worked out exactly as the demon said, but... it makes sense." Grede frowned. "Damn it, I almost got fooled by that flamewaker. I don't know why, but he sounded so convincing."

It wasn't Grede making excuses for his lapse. This flamewaker's shadow manipulation was far stronger than he'd shown. His control over shadow let him influence the emotions of those around him.

That power had even fooled Jace at first, which was why he'd stopped to see what important thing Gehennas had to say.

But Vereesa's arrow through his chest had probably done too much damage, interfering with his shadow manipulation. Or maybe he thought he'd succeeded, and a slight emotional slip gave away a trace of deliberate control — which Jace caught sharply.

He wasn't just trying to influence Jace's group with shadow to make them believe him. He was actually lying too. In other words, Jace could tell this flamewaker had no idea how Shazzrah planned to devour the Wind Prince... He was just trying to sow discord with empty words before he died.

Even if Jace listened to everything, following his method might not have let him control Thunderaan's soul. Besides, if Jace really wanted to devour Thunderaan's soul, he could just split the cage open with Usarlas. Gehennas had severely underestimated him.

With the troublemaker dealt with, next was Shazzrah's rune on the lava lake.

Jace opened the Eternal Quintessence again following the incantation Thunderaan had taught him. Grede held Thunderaan's cage while the Wind Prince chanted. Soon, the rune in the palace ruins couldn't withstand the cold power of water and wind, and was suppressed into extinction.

As expected, the whole lava lake began to churn. The island's ground cracked open. The group was ready — they scrambled onto Sora's back to flee.

"Hey." Grede steadied himself on the green dragon's neck and turned around. "Did we forget something?"

"Those magic treasures, damn it!" Sazeno yelled. "I told you we should grab some before running! You were all too eager to destroy the rune! Don't forget, those magic items could help against the elementals too! Since we're not devouring the elemental prince's soul, master, we should at least take something else!"

"Is that area already submerged?" Vereesa asked.

"Let's go see," Jace said.

The green and black dragons took off immediately. Needlefang, faster, reached the spot where Gehennas had died first. "It hasn't collapsed yet! Hurry!"

"Hurry, hurry!"

Hearing the black dragon's voice, Grede was already itching to throw his hammer and let the lightning drag him into the pile of scattered treasure.

Whether because carrying too many people made her too heavy, or the ground cracked under the green dragon's landing, fissures burst open in every direction. Magma spewed up from the cracks, erupting everywhere.

"Shit shit shit... hurry!" Grede said. "Everyone grab as much as you can!"

He didn't need to say it. Needlefang was already scooping up enchanted weapons with her claws. Grede was stuffing magic treasures and gold into his bags by the handful.

Jace found a wide, dark-gold magic belt, covered in flame-like elemental runes.

It looked like something a flamewaker would wear — big and heavy. Even though Jace could feel strong enchantments on it, given its size, he wasn't sure what use it was besides being melted down to forge magic armor.

But the moment he tried to lift it, it shrank to fit a human's — well, Jace's — waist.

"Go, Jace! This place is really going to collapse!" Vereesa shouted.

Jace nodded and used Shadowstep to cross several crumbling lava fissures. Sora had already taken off, unable to stay grounded. At the last moment, Jace activated his cloak to fly higher, but the chaotic fire energy in Molten Core sent him tumbling through the air, about to fall back into the lava. Needlefang swooped in from behind and scooped him up with her hind claws, carrying him upside-down to a safe height.

"Can you turn me right-side up?!" Jace asked, craning his neck with difficulty.

"We'll reach the ridge protection rune soon, Jace Sesso!" Needlefang shouted. "Just hold on a little longer! I can't spare a claw right now!"

Jace twisted his head to look ahead and noticed Needlefang's front claws were clutching some enchanted daggers and blades. The little dragon was only interested in getting nice weapons to learn how to fight in mortal form from Morophil and Vereesa. Everything else could wait.

Jace examined the metal belt he'd grabbed. Even holding it, he could feel the enchantment boosting his energy. Combined with its dark-gold appearance and its origin deep in Molten Core... aside from the "Mana Igniting Cord" from the Molten Core raid, he couldn't think of what else it might be.

Except the Mana Igniting Cord in the game was cloth armor, and the one in his hand was a chain made of arcanite metal.

But whatever it was, that didn't stop Jace from wearing it to help him cast spells. He could even wear two belts — the one Varian had given him, Angelista's Sash — one inside, one outside. It was like wearing four or five rings on two hands. No need to be picky.

Of course, he'd need the others' approval. Vereesa probably wouldn't want a heavy metal waist chain. Sora liked shiny decorations. Needlefang clearly preferred bizarre weapons. Dragonkind's humanoid clothes were magically transformed, so she probably wouldn't be interested either. The one most likely to compete with him was Grede. He wondered if he could trade the Hand of Justice for this.

As he thought this, Jace saw Grede up ahead holding up a slab of grey-black stone wrapped in chains, about the size of a book, with spiky decorations. "Look at this, Jace Sesso! Do you recognize it? It's covered in dense runes I can't read! Vereesa says the magical energy in this stone is bottomless!"

From this distance, Jace couldn't sense the enchantment Vereesa mentioned. But looking at the shape, he remembered a very good trinket from Molten Core — the Talisman of Ephemeral Power, which could massively boost magic power for a short time. In the game, it was somewhat similar to Draenor's "Destruction Potion."

Damn it, he really wanted that thing. Why did Grede have it... could he trade the Hand of Justice for both?

Carried by Needlefang, they followed Sora down onto a black ridge. The protection rune was nearby, but the area was already overrun with elementals. Several molten elementals and a swarm of fire elementals surrounded them as soon as they appeared.

"We'll handle the fire elementals!" Vereesa shouted. "Take the cage and destroy the rune, Jace!"

"Protect our treasures, Morophil! Don't bother asking your master's opinion — I know he feels the same!" Grede yelled.

Sora refreshed everyone's Gift of the Wild. Needlefang had already opened her massive jaws and pounced into the fire elementals and molten lava before Grede and Vereesa could start, chomping away at everything.

To mortals, these elementals might be a terrible nightmare. But to a black dragon, burning elemental cores, fire essences, and mountain hearts were all excellent energy sources. To her, the elemental army was basically a bunch of waving-fist, tooth-and-claw barbecue and dessert.

Jace carried the Wind Prince's skull and climbed to the rune's altar. In the distance, he could hear Dark Iron dwarves shouting somewhere beyond countless gullies and cliffs, fighting Golemagg the Incinerator's legion.

As the Wind Prince finished his incantation, the rune went dark, and the ridge began to collapse.

Vereesa and Grede retreated to Jace's side early, grabbed the treasure, leaped onto Sora's back, and flew toward the Dark Iron dwarves' position, leaving the scattered elemental bracers and collapsed lava behind.

"Just three runes left. More than halfway there." Grede caught his breath. "Easier than I expected."

"That's right! We're almost there!" Needlefang shouted.

Jace nodded and looked back toward the center of Molten Core.
