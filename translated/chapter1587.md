## Chapter 1587: True Flame King?

After destroying the rune guarded by Baron Geddon, the nearest of the three remaining protection runes was the one guarded by Golemagg the Incinerator.

The valley ahead was littered with Dark Iron dwarf corpses and shattered elemental bracers. Further on, the thunderous roar of dwarves and the bellowing of core giants rang out.

Sora flew straight toward the valley, carrying the group. Jace noticed Bronzebeard dwarf corpses lying by the roadside here and there -- they had distinct pale skin and golden beards and hair, though most had their hair burned away and their skin charred black and covered in ash.

So Muradin Bronzebeard and his legion had already beaten the elementals blocking them and reached this area?

"Black dragon! Two dragons, one big one small, approaching!" a Dark Iron dwarf's shout came from somewhere. "Four o'clock!"

"Lower your guns, lower your guns! Those aren't black dragons, they're green dragons! His Majesty said they were here to help -- look more carefully!" another dwarf's voice bellowed.

Jace looked to his left front and spotted a group of Dark Iron dwarves gathered on a high cliff, watching them. These little black dwarves were barely visible hiding against the scorched landscape of Molten Core.

"Where is your emperor?" he shouted.

"His Majesty is on the front lines, leading Doomforge and Ironfury's warriors and Bronzebeard's whelps in attacking the Incinerator's lair!" The soldier raised his warhammer, pointing at a canyon ahead. "That bastard's power is growing weaker and weaker! Soon we'll achieve final victory! Long live the Ironfury! Long live the True Flame King!"

"Let's go, Sora," Jace said.

"Right." Sora answered and turned into a dive toward the canyon. Needleflame muttered beside her, "Do I look green to you?"

"You're still hung up on that?" Grede asked. "Didn't you hear what that Dark Iron said? He was shouting 'Long live the Flame King.' What are the Dark Irons thinking?"

"It was 'Long live the True Flame King,'" Vereesa corrected. "I'm guessing Dagran Thaurissan has already found a way to take Ragnaros's place early, at least in the Dark Irons' eyes."

"For some reason, I'm not surprised at all," Jace said.

"But I'm the one carrying Sulfuron Hammer." Grede said. "That bastard's taking credit for my work, isn't he?"

"Then you be the True Flame King," Jace said.

Grede choked on that notion and coughed. "I don't really care what the Dark Irons think, as long as Muradin Bronzebeard here knows what's really going on."

"Jace Sesso!"

As they flew, a shout came from ahead. Jace saw Dagran Thaurissan in his black-and-red robes running to a cliff edge, waving. "Just in time!"

Before Sora had even landed, Jace noticed fresh red blood on Thaurissan's scepter. He couldn't help staring.

After all, elementals don't bleed.

Just then, a dwarf in a huge horned helmet and scale armor followed from behind the high ridge. His reddish-gold beard and the pale skin on his prominent cheekbones marked him as a pure-blooded Bronzebeard dwarf.

Though this Bronzebeard was staring coldly at Thaurissan -- definitely not friendly -- he hadn't immediately started a fight. Jace breathed a little easier.

His first thought on seeing blood on Thaurissan's scepter had been that it was Bronzebeard blood.

Dagran Thaurissan looked at the people jumping down from the dragon. He seemed to notice them staring at his scepter and raised it. "Ah, this."

"Clearly, a considerable number of Dark Irons -- especially the pyromancers -- Executus failed to teach the virtue of discretion. To keep them from getting in the way, I had to send them out of this place as quickly as possible."

Jace nodded. The horn-helmeted dwarf walked over. "You're Jace Sesso. I'm Muradin Bronzebeard. I believe this is our first meeting. You're as tall as the stories say."

"Lord Muradin Bronzebeard." Jace responded respectfully. He wanted to say it wasn't technically their first meeting.

"Lady Vereesa." Muradin said. "An honor to meet you."

"The honor is mine, Lord Muradin." Vereesa gave a slight nod.

"Lord Muradin Bronzebeard!" Grede jumped off the dragon's back and strode over.

"Lord Grede." Muradin embraced Grede and clapped his shoulder, though his eyes never left the black dragon that had just landed nearby.

"I hear you killed his lieutenant with Sulfuron Hammer. I wish I could have seen it!"

"That bastard was weaker than I expected. Hard to believe the Dark Irons worshipped him as a god for two hundred years, Lord Muradin. If you'd been there, it wouldn't have been nearly as chaotic," Grede said.

"Stop wasting time praising each other." Thaurissan pointed behind him. "We defeated the elementals attacking that hill and counterattacked back here, but Golemagg the Incinerator is hiding in the deepest part and won't come out. His power keeps those core hounds, core giants, and fire elementals dying and being reborn endlessly. We can't kill them all."

"We've already destroyed three flame runes by the lava lake," Jace said.

"I can feel it." Thaurissan's expression was grave. "Including the rune Sulfuron Harbinger protected, that leaves three. But it's not enough. The elementals' resistance is still strong."

"What about the other two runes?" Grede asked.

"Both on the other side of Molten Core." Muradin answered. "Kurdran Wildhammer and your kinsmen are trying to attack there, dealing with the flamewakers and their caves. We have the advantage in numbers here, and only one core giant guards the rune on our side. Over there, they have a flamewaker lord and countless core hounds, plus a core hound king. So we can't wait for them to gain the upper hand."

"Maybe we should join forces, focus on one point, instead of splitting up like this," Vereesa said. "We could fly over and inform the other side."

"No time, Lady Windrunner." Thaurissan said. "There's no time."

"He's right." Muradin Bronzebeard shook his head. "By the time the Wildhammers get over here, even if we take the Incinerator and his legion more easily... don't forget, gryphons can't fly in here. And the Fire Protection Potions won't last for the trip back. The heat in here is too much."

"Dagran Thaurissan." He turned to the Dark Iron emperor. "You're the one who said Golemagg the Incinerator was a stupid, impulsive, berserk lunatic -- that once we attacked his lair, he'd charge right out to fight us. But that core giant turned out to be more cowardly than the flamewakers! Is there a single word of truth coming out of you Dark Irons' mouths?"

"Who knew he'd suddenly become such a coward?" Thaurissan said. "Maybe he felt the protection runes being destroyed one by one and realized they weren't invincible. He knows his time is short, so he's lost his former bravado. I'm standing here on the same side as you against these core giants. What reason would I have to lie?"

"Looks like we have no choice but to try a direct assault again." Muradin Bronzebeard raised his battle-axe. "But this time, we have two Mountain Kings. I don't think those core giants can withstand the both of us. Grede Thunderfist."

Grede snorted a laugh and hefted Sulfuron Hammer. "What do you think we rushed over here for, Bronzebeard?"

"Follow us, warlock." Muradin waved. "We'll charge one more time."

Jace and Vereesa exchanged glances. Behind them, Needleflame's overflowing battle-lust was already burning hot enough to singe the back of his head.
