Chapter 1588: True Flame King!

Outside the enormous cave where Golemagg the Incinerator was hiding, the ground was littered with destroyed elemental bracers, the collapsed remains of core giants, and shattered piles of molten elementals.

Several hundred Dark Iron and Bronzebeard soldiers were waiting there for the next order to attack. Deep inside the wide cave passage, the flickering firelight of elemental creatures occasionally glowed, proof that Golemagg's forces inside were ready for a desperate last stand to protect the flame rune.

When they saw Sulfuron Hammer in Grede's hands, the Ironfury soldiers' eyes nearly popped out of their heads. Even the Bronzebeard dwarves showed looks of astonishment, as if even after entering here from Shadowforge City, even after seeing Executus's corpse and hearing of Sulfuron Harbinger and the flamewaker lords' deaths, they'd never truly believed Sulfuron Hammer really existed.

Now that the hammer was right before their eyes, Jace could hear many dwarves murmuring in awe, forgetting to stay alert for elementals that might pounce from the cave depths at any moment.

"After today, Molten Core will be ours, Ironfury and Doomforge guards." Thaurissan walked to the front. "Only the Incinerator stands in our way now. Remember how the core giants mocked us in Shadowforge City? Now it's their turn to tremble, hiding in their caves. Let's give that coward what he deserves!"

"Long live Thaurissan! Long live the Sulfuron's might! Long live the True Flame King!" the Dark Irons roared.

"I've already said what needed to be said, lads!" Muradin Bronzebeard shook his battle-axe. "I've only got one thing to add -- don't let the Wildhammers on the other side think we're pushovers!"

Sometimes getting dwarves to attack just needed a simple reason. That one sentence was enough to get every Bronzebeard soldier blood pumping, shouting along with the rest.

Muradin Bronzebeard charged to the front. Thaurissan and Grede followed close behind. Jace and the others fell in with the dwarven soldiers.

In an instant, the cave filled with the clatter of armor, clashing blades, and the grinding of metal against stone. A few hundred might not sound like much, but every one of these dwarves -- Bronzebeard and Dark Iron alike -- was clad in heavy armor, wielding long weapons and sharp blades, carrying large shields, yet moving like the wind.

After all, anyone Magni Bronzebeard sent here had to be Ironforge's finest. And anyone Dagran Thaurissan had chosen to fight deep in Molten Core against the fire elementals, rather than keeping order in Shadowforge City, had to be the cream of the Dark Iron crop.

From their talk, Jace could tell they'd already launched several attacks, but they showed surprisingly little fatigue.

As they pushed deeper into the cave and rounded a corner, a core giant twice the size of an ogre lumbered toward them. Its black stone body grew brighter with each step, flames jetting from the cracks between its stones, lighting up the entire cave.

It had already lost one arm and was unsteady, but still raised its remaining arm and lunged madly at Muradin Bronzebeard, who was leading the charge.

The Mountain King looked like a comical golden bean before it. But in the instant before they clashed, Muradin suddenly grew into a massive silver giant and hurled his warhammer square into the fire giant's chest.

With a deafening crack, the core giant's other arm fell off from the impact.

The core giant wobbled, trying to swing down at this silver little thing, but didn't realize its arm had fallen off. It just shook its shoulder, then tilted the eye buried in its neck to look at its shoulder, confused about what was happening.

Before its sluggish mind could react, Grede had also expanded into a silver stone dwarf and leaped onto its shoulder.

The core giant's tiny eye rolled upward. Sulfuron Hammer came crashing down on its head. That single blow cracked half its body apart, and the whole thing began to collapse and shatter.

Fireballs and spinning boulders flew across the cave ceiling, some hitting the walls, some landing among the dwarves.

Countless fire elementals burst out from under the collapsing core giant. Bronzebeard dwarves raised their shields to block the fireballs hurled at them. Some were knocked down by powerful explosions, others bowled over by charging fire elemental guards. But more Dark Iron dwarves surged in from the sides, enduring the scorching flames, raising their weapons to shatter the elementals' bracers.

The dwarven legion drove forward like a black-and-gold battering ram into the flames. Countless fire elementals howled and dissipated. Magma collapsed. But more elementals kept pouring from the burning depths. The battering ram slowed, grinding to a halt just a few yards further.

Some fire elementals spiraled into the air, trying to fly over the dwarven lines to the rear. Needleflame gobbled some up. Others were shot down by streaking dream arrows.

After dispatching a few flying fire elementals with ease, Vereesa leaped onto the fallen core giant's back, drew her bow, and aimed at the masses of elementals behind the battle line. Rows of elementals exploded and toppled under the assault of dream power.

Sora, up high, healed the wounded dwarves who'd fallen and continuously bestowed the Gift of the Wild on these injured fighters, helping them resist flame burns and impacts.

Several core hounds appeared, wreathed in flame and black smoke, their heavy footfalls rumbling. The lead hound had a mouthful of magma ready to spit at the battle line, but Vereesa's arrow pierced its throat. The magma poured out of the burst wound.

The core hound howled and collapsed. Dwarves scrambled to avoid the lava spilling from its body, and the line briefly fell into chaos.

But just then, a core giant strode out of the flame and smoke, stepping through the core hound's lava-blood with long strides.

It fixed its gaze on Sora in the rear, marching forward recklessly, ignoring all nearby dwarves as if determined to strike a heavy blow against the green dragon healing everyone before it fell.

"Stop it!" Grede roared, raising Sulfuron Hammer to smash another core hound's skull before lunging at the giant, only to be blocked by several larger molten elementals.

Muradin Bronzebeard had just felled another core hound. He turned to look at the core giant, but the creature had already charged into the dwarven lines.

The dwarven soldiers couldn't withstand its massive body and had to give way. Muradin's thrown hammer struck the giant's waist, shattering a large chunk of stone, making it stumble sideways, but didn't stop its advance.

Grede hurled the Heavens-Shatterer at the giant's back, blasting off one of its forearms. But the core giant raised that forearm, aimed at the green dragon in the rear, and prepared to throw. In the cramped cave, Sora couldn't gain altitude fast enough to dodge. She could only retreat as best she could.

A spinning black meteor struck the core giant's head. The black explosion obliterated its entire upper body. Shadow-lava sprayed onto the cave ceiling, bathing the whole space in purple-red light.

The dwarves witnessing this turned to look at the warlock on the ridge behind them. But the warlock was already directing the splattering shadow-fire, aiming at the more fire elementals emerging ahead.

Shadow-fire rained down among the elementals, quickly staining them purple-red, even purple-black. The chaotic energy lost its balance, triggering a chain of explosions.

After the first wave of core hounds and giants passed, the elementals seemed to falter. Only one more core giant emerged, but the two freed-up Mountain Kings quickly dismantled it, one high one low, leading the Dark Irons and Bronzebeards ever deeper.

The battering ram drove viciously into the cave's depths once more.

As they pushed deeper with the dwarves, Jace could feel the intense, suppressed rage at the very bottom of the cave.

Though he couldn't see clearly from here, there seemed to be a core giant the size of a mountain in the fiery glow beyond the dust and smoke.

Countless fire elementals and core hounds still blocked the way, but the dwarves fought on wildly, showing no signs of fatigue.

"Well done, Grede Thunderfist! Without your help, we'd never have gotten this far!" Muradin shouted excitedly, his voice cracking after all the fighting.

"The Incinerator is right ahead." Dagran Thaurissan said. "He's right there."

But then Jace heard a low murmur from the core giant at the cave's deepest point, followed by the sound of flames surging behind them.

He looked back. The cave, which had just dimmed, was lighting up again.

"Damn it, he's using his power to revive the fire elementals! Even with their help, we're not fast enough!" Dagran Thaurissan screamed. "We have to retreat, or we'll be surrounded just like before! More men will die for nothing!"

"But we've already pushed this far, Dagran Thaurissan!" Muradin roared. "Forget the flames behind us! Charge straight in and kill the Incinerator!"

"Damn it, damn it..." The Dark Iron emperor screamed hysterically. "Golemagg the Incinerator, since when did you become such a coward, huh?! The Dark Irons you look down on are right at your doorstep, flaunting their power! Either kneel to the true Flame King Dagran Thaurissan, or come out and fight me!"

"The true Flame King?"

Jace heard a murmured elemental whisper from deep within the cave. The rage was about to burst forth, suppressed by some energy from an unknown source.

Thaurissan seemed to sense something was wrong too. He looked up, then glanced in another direction, muttering: "It's not that he wanted to hide in the deepest part... It's not him. It's his order. It's..."

"Hit him with a Curse of Recklessness, Morofiel," Jace said.

"Yes, master."

A hidden presence stirred in the shadows, then suddenly flew up high, out of sight.

Jace turned around. With these dwarven warriors forming a wall around him, he had enough space to chant Hand of Gul'dan and shatter any elementals or core hounds chasing from behind, buying time for the dwarves and, more importantly, for Morofiel.

But Morofiel was faster than he'd expected. Before the dwarves had fought much longer, Jace sensed a vague fluctuation of magic through the soul link. Morofiel was already chanting.

And under the influence of Morofiel's shadow power, the surging rage deep within finally broke through that last layer of suppression.

"No one challenges the master of Sulfuron. No one dares to claim the Sulfuron's might. Dagran Thaurissan, you ignorant filth..."

The deep elemental whisper emanated from the cave's deepest reaches. Jace saw the entire mountain ignite. A being a full head taller than the core giants rose up, nearly scraping the cave ceiling!

"Enough! I will crush you cold little insects myself! Let's see who can withstand the Fire Lord's power!"

The Incinerator roared, driving its body toward the dwarves. Every step was an earthquake. Stones flew from under its feet. Even the molten elementals staggered. The dwarves had no chance of keeping their footing.

"He's coming! He's coming!" Grede shouted. "Get ready!"

"Ha! Finally willing to face us, Golemagg!" Muradin Bronzebeard murmured as he watched the red giant, shoving aside countless core hounds and fire elementals, crash down before them like a collapsing mountain. He raised his head and lifted his crackling warhammer.

Golemagg's foot came down into the formation. Many elementals and soldiers too slow to escape were crushed instantly. Grede and Muradin immediately climbed the giant's burning leg, scrambling upward.

The King of Core Giants raised a fist to smash the two dwarves, but Vereesa's arrows struck its arm, making it flinch and miss its swing. When it tried again, more dream arrows hit its chest and head.

Though some arrows pierced the surface rock shell, this thing was massive -- far larger than the Sons of Gruul. Even these piercing arrows, exploding on impact, couldn't seriously wound its entire body.

But the two Mountain Kings had already climbed onto the giant's head. Lightning and fire exploded across Golemagg's crown.

"Golemagg is immortal! I am one... with Molten Core!"

Golemagg battered its own head frantically, staggering toward the cave wall. Its chest split open, lava pouring out. The whole cave shook violently.

The elementals that had been so formidable began vanishing, dispersing. The core hounds that had been blazing with flame, about to be reborn, dimmed again.

Without Golemagg's power sustaining them, these elementals that had been reborn countless times could no longer hold on.

But even as the King of Core Giants fell and the cave dimmed, the violent shuddering never stopped. These shockwaves didn't seem to come from Golemagg -- they came from somewhere else.

"We'll hold off the remaining elementals, Jace!" Grede shouted from Golemagg's corpse. "Go destroy the flame rune!"

Jace nodded and immediately ran deeper with the Wind Prince's cage, following the spinning ring of fire through the smoke.

When he reached the flame rune, he opened the Eternal Quintessence without hesitation. The Wind Prince chanted. The flame rune went dark.

But the shaking in the cave didn't get stronger or weaker -- it just kept rumbling on.

"He's coming... he's coming, Jace Sesso!" Dagran Thaurissan screamed hysterically. "It's him! He knows everything that's happened here! He gave Golemagg the order to protect the flame rune at all costs! I can feel it -- I can even hear his voice..."

"Who's coming?!" Muradin demanded.

"Ragnaros." The Wind Prince's voice was low. "Ragnaros is about to arrive. You were just a little too slow."

Jace and the others exchanged glances, then all eyes fell on the Dark Iron emperor. The dwarf clutched his scepter, panting for a few breaths, staring at Jace. His face twisted, vicious.

"What are you standing there for?! I've already come this far! Do you think I can go back to groveling before him now?!"

"Now I'm going to be the Flame King! I AM the Flame King! The Dark Irons must become the true rulers of Molten Core and Blackrock Mountain!"

"There are two more protection runes! Damn it, get him back where he belongs before we all die! Now go!"
