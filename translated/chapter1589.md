## Chapter 1589: Rune Collapse

The lava lake was boiling over. Surging magma even formed waves, battering the black stone shores. Countless Dark Iron dwarves trying to flee from Executus's throne back to Shadowforge City had been swallowed. Others were trapped on black rocks surrounded by lava, about to vanish under the next exploding bubble.

The green dragon dodged falling lava as she flew toward a lava river stretching across their path. The air grew hotter and hotter. The vision warped.

And this was with Jace carrying the Bindings of the Windseeker. Even the cold wind surrounding the cage couldn't suppress the incoming heat anymore.

Jace knew exactly where Ragnaros would appear if he manifested -- the very center of Molten Core, to the right of their flight path. Vereesa knew it too.

Grede, Sora, and Needleflame didn't know yet, but all three were instinctively looking to the right, as if even without Jace or Vereesa saying a word, they could feel where this shaking that rocked the foundations of all Blackrock Mountain was coming from.

After destroying Golemagg and his protection rune, Dagran Thaurissan was rallying every Dark Iron dwarf still capable of fighting near Executus's throne, preparing to march with Muradin Bronzebeard toward Magmadar's cave -- the last rune. Jace's group, with their superior speed, would head for the second-to-last rune.

With Garr's cave collapsed, Jace had no idea how to navigate through the middle section of Molten Core anymore, from Executus's black throne to Magmadar's cave where the Wildhammers were attacking. Only Vereesa could still find the way. Luckily, when they'd gone to meet Baron Geddon, she'd gotten a look at the terrain around Magmadar's cave.

"This plane is finished, master!" the imp squeaked, clutching its head. "Let's just run! Don't throw your life away for those Dark Iron dwarves! The Firelord's coming!"

"You don't need to worry about dying," Jace said, gripping Sora's shoulder. "You'll just go back to the Twisting Nether."

"I don't want to lose such a powerful source of shadow energy!" the imp shrieked.

"We don't have to fight Ragnaros to the death, imp." Grede said. "Didn't Rocktus say it before? Once we destroy all the flame runes, Molten Core will collapse completely, cutting this world off from the Firelands! That means the Firelord will be locked out, right? Just two more flame runes left! We've already killed the most powerful flamewakers and the mightiest elemental rulers! That Golemagg couldn't last more than a few rounds against me and Muradin Bronzebeard's hammers!"

Grede trailed off, realizing no one was responding. He looked at Vereesa and Jace. "That's right, isn't it? As long as we destroy the runes..."

"As long as we're fast enough, before Ragnaros truly enters this world... maybe," Jace nodded.

Grede swallowed. "Can you go any faster, Sora?"

"I'm trying my best, Grede." Sora's voice was low as she beat her wings harder.

Inside Molten Core, she couldn't fully channel nature's power for high-speed flight. And in this chaos, waves of heat blasted from the deepest part of Molten Core. Each blast shattered fragile black stone and kicked up clouds of dust. Jace thought it was a miracle Sora could stay this steady.

With five flame runes destroyed, Molten Core's power was severely weakened. Many powerful fire elementals could barely keep existing in this world anymore -- some had a significant portion of their power stripped away just to maintain their form, others had simply dissipated into the air.

The fire elementals and molten elementals still putting up scattered resistance across Molten Core were weaker than ever, easily beaten by squads of four or five Dark Iron soldiers loyal to Dagran Thaurissan.

On the ground, pyromancers still holding out, along with soldiers loyal to them, had lost their incredibly powerful elemental allies and were rapidly collapsing or trying to flee back to Shadowforge City.

The second-to-last flame rune was the one Gehennas had guarded. Gehennas was already dead. And the last flamewaker lord, Lucifron, who guarded Magmadar's cave, was presumably under siege by the Wildhammer dwarves.

Which meant the second-to-last rune was practically sitting there waiting for Jace's group to reach and destroy it.

Once it was gone, the elementals would be even weaker, the Wildhammer assault on that cave would go more smoothly, and the last rune would be within reach.

"There, I see it." Vereesa pointed at a corridor full of pillars beside the lava river. "There's a flame rune there."

Compared to the Incinerator's enormous cave, this place was practically a crevice between two stone slabs. Jace doubted a core giant the size of Golemagg could even fit in here.

But to save time, Sora and Needleflame took the risk and flew in anyway, following Vereesa's directions while dodging pillars and black stone spikes.

Soon, Jace spotted the spinning ring of fire deeper inside. Lucky for them, there were no elementals guarding it.

Gehennas had defected to Shazzrah, and the flamewakers who'd followed him had gone after Garr and Geddon on Shazzrah's orders to seize the Wind Prince's cage. Now that those flamewakers had abandoned this place, it meant they'd either joined Lucifron or had already been intercepted by Kurdran Wildhammer's Wildhammer legion.

"Perform the ritual, Jace."

Before Sora even landed, Vereesa jumped off, raised her bow, and walked a short distance away. "I'll keep watch."

Jace nodded, raised the Eternal Quintessence, and ran toward the flame rune. Grede followed with Thunderaan's cage.

But as he drew closer, Jace felt a heat far more intense than any previous flame rune blasting from it. Getting near was even harder.

"What's going on, Windseeker?" Jace asked in elemental tongue. "Can you sense this flame rune's abnormality? What's happening?"

"Without question, Ragnaros is trying to seal these runes to stop us from destroying them further..." the voice from the Wind Prince's cage answered. "Perhaps with ordinary Water Quintessence, breaking this would be very difficult. But such magic cannot stop the Tidehunter's spirit."

Jace nodded, chanted in elemental tongue, and opened the essence vial. The cold vapor pushed back some of the heat. He used the momentum to take a few more steps toward the rune. Behind him, the Wind Prince had already begun chanting.

As the incantation progressed, the essence vial began to tremble. The shaking was so violent that it seemed bizarre coming from such a small bottle -- like something was gripping Jace's hand and shaking it wildly.

But no matter how fierce the resistance, just as the Wind Prince had said, the protective ring of fire rapidly dimmed under the freezing energy, spinning more and more weakly. Eventually, amidst the terrible shaking of the rock crevice, it cooled completely, leaving only a massive metal rune.

"Jace! Elementals are coming! Keep going with the ritual -- we can handle them!" Vereesa's voice came from far behind, barely audible over the violent shuddering of all Molten Core.

Far louder than her voice was a sound like a great bell tolling from deep within the earth. In fact, this sound had been rising and falling for a while now.

It was elemental song. Jace could hear it more clearly now -- flames singing in joy, lava roaring in celebration. They were all rejoicing.

Rejoicing for the name of the Firelord who had ruled them since this world was born, the master of Sulfuron Keep.

Then a roar, so deep it seemed to come from the abyss itself, drowned out every other sound -- even the Wind Prince Thunderaan's elemental chanting behind him. In that instant, even heaven and earth fell silent. The world itself dared only to listen.

"Those who defile sacred ground shall be punished."

"Mor... Mor?"

Jace turned to look at everyone. They'd all heard it. "What does 'Mor' mean?!" Needleflame shouted.

"Mor." Grede stared blankly, raising his arm to point at the rune beside Jace. "Mor."

Jace turned back to look at the rune. Yes -- two slightly offset horizontal bars, with a cross mark at the diagonal. It read as "Mor."

Then the ring of fire began spinning madly. The rune flared bright again. The Eternal Quintessence vial trembled so violently Jace almost couldn't hold it.

Wait.

Jace's heart dropped. He seemed to sense something. Grede's eyes went wide too.

"Run... RUN!"

Everyone bolted away from the Mor rune at once. Jace Shadowstepped a huge distance, then ran while chanting a Dark Ward spell. Grede leaped over several ground fissures, his body suddenly swelling. Morofiel frowned and spread her demonic wings. Sora grabbed Vereesa and flew without hesitation. Needleflame launched like a bird desperately flapping out of water.

Moments later, the spinning flame rune released a piercing shriek. Jace glanced sideways just long enough to see light so bright it nearly blinded him.

Then, a ringing blast that shattered all senses. Jace felt his consciousness contract. Everything before him disintegrated, spun, and was swallowed by white light.
