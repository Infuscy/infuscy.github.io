## Chapter 1591: Sulfuras

Through billowing black smoke, a young black dragon carried three people. Jace swung beneath her, feeling every second flying deeper into Molten Core grow hotter than the last.

Some dwarves on the ground were running in the same direction. Others were fleeing back the way they came, probably toward Shadowforge City. At this point, Jace couldn't even tell which was which in Molten Core.

From this height, through the shifting smoke and firelight, Jace could barely make out the dwarves' gear. Were they Ironfury or Doomforge soldiers? Or pyromancers rushing to "defend the throne," or soldiers who'd betrayed Thaurissan and remained fanatically loyal to Ragnaros?

Ragnaros. Jace had thought about the possibility of facing him directly.

But actually charging toward this ancient being still made his heart hang heavy.

He gripped Needleflame's claw tight, fumbled in his pack with one hand, and pulled out a metal box. He flipped it open with one hand. Inside lay the crimson treasure forged for him by King Magni Bronzebeard.

Needleflame seemed to sense something. She turned her head to glance at what Jace was holding, then looked forward again.

Grede noticed the black dragon's reaction and saw what Jace was holding. "What's that? Why does it look so familiar?"

"You've seen it. Onyxia's blood." Jace took a breath. "Time to find out how heat-resistant it really is."

"Why didn't you bring it out when you entered Molten Core?" Grede asked.

"You mean carry a vial of dragon blood in front of a crowd of Dark Iron dwarves?" Jace said. "You think things weren't chaotic enough?"

"Could it be worse than this?" Grede looked into the depths of Molten Core.

Fair point, Jace thought.

He clutched the pendant in his palm. A surge of hot blood-energy flooded his body, reminding him of facing the princess in Onyxia's obsidian lair, of that terrible breath of flame.

Jace didn't feel the heat drop much, but he could feel this hot blood bravely pushing back against the heat waves surging from Molten Core's depths. He hoped it was as strong as the Ironforge smiths had claimed.

"Take it, Grede."

"You need it more than I do, Jace Sesso." Grede snorted.

"Take it."

"Fine."

Needleflame followed the lava river's current into a wide channel. Jace asked, "Any other potions left?"

"All in my stomach. Every dwarf for himself now."

"What about that talisman you found?"

"What?"

"The little magic stone tablet from Shazzrah's place!"

"You're taking advantage of me?!"

"You started it, dwarf! If you'd said you were out of potions, I wouldn't have given you the dragon blood pendant!"

"You son of a--"

Before he could finish, a thunderous roar exploded ahead. A blast of flame kicked up a wave from the lava river that splashed straight at them.

"Bloody hell!"

The dwarf barely got the words out before the black dragon raised her wings to block. But the low-altitude imbalance sent them crashing into a black rock and tumbling onto the lava riverbank. Jace and Grede both lost their grip and fell nearby.

"Run, Thunderaan..."

A low chuckle accompanied by golden flame blasted in from the cave mouth ahead. Rubble and dust flew like shrapnel against the rocks nearby.

Jace ducked to avoid the barrage and looked around. Needleflame wasn't badly hurt -- those fragments couldn't penetrate her dragon scales -- but getting flipped over and righting herself took some effort.

"Did you hear that? He's calling the Wind Prince's name! He must be after Sora and the others! We have to stop him!" the black dragon shouted.

More fire meteors fell ahead, splashing magma and debris with sharp grinding sounds. Grede tossed his now-warped helmet into the lava river.

"Don't fly anymore, little black dragon. We'll go on foot from here. Protect yourself!"

"Here, Jace Sesso. Don't say I'm stingy when we get out of this!" Grede tossed the horned magic stone tablet over. Jace caught it, and the dwarf gripped Sulfuron Hammer and charged ahead.

Running out of the lava river and out of the cave passage, wave after wave of heat pressed down from above, making it almost impossible to lift one's head.

Before them was a wide, open space whose ceiling was too high to see. The ground was covered in rock that had been pushed up like ripples spreading outward.

This was the source -- and the end -- of all of Molten Core, of all the Burning Steppes and Searing Gorge, of countless lava rivers.

Burning, glowing channels like cracks in a bullet-shattered car window converged from all directions toward the center. There, layers of ignited tornadoes howled, destroying everything they touched. Broken walls from Shadowforge City, oddly shaped black rocks -- everything that came close was crushed and incinerated by the fire tornadoes.

And in the center of those flames was a faint red silhouette, locked in place by countless tongues of fire, screaming from time to time.

That was Dagran Thaurissan.

Now Jace understood why those Dark Iron dwarves had gone mad, running this way. They were trying to rescue their emperor.

Jace couldn't look any longer. Just staring at the terrifying bright flames for more than a moment was enough to burn his eyes.

"I want you to witness this city's destruction... witness every hope you've placed being crushed, one by one, Thaurissan..."

A rumbling, nearly unrecognizable voice of fire came from above. Grede raised his arm and shouted, "What kind of damn spell is this?!"

Tendrils of violent flame, carrying molten stone fragments, suddenly spun out of the firestorm, spraying toward the unseen ceiling. Those lava fragments were like the scales of some magic dragon. And the tongues of flame blooming at the highest point looked like the claws of some enormous beast.

Or not "looked like" claws. They WERE a hand.

Grede's gaze followed that hand upward. His furious expression froze.

He understood now. This wasn't some damn spell. This was Ragnaros. The Firelord's actual body.

A low growl echoed from deep within the fire tornado. The heat carried by that sound alone was unbearable -- even Jace, who'd just entered this slope, struggled. The Olastraz-hide cloak flapped wildly in the flame blast. Every curl of hair poking out from under his hood was burned to ash and blown away.

Grede was no better off. His beard, practically enchanted by his ancestors' power, had mostly burned off too. He was pinned against a black rock, barely able to get up.

Dozens of meters above, in that hand of fire tongues, a warhammer appeared, wrapped in bright lava spikes and spinning flame runes.

"Sulfuras... is it?"

Grede stared hard at the heights, murmuring.
