## Chapter 1593: Battle at the Flame Heart

At Ragnaros's furious shout, the spinning firestorm swept across the field of vision. Countless Dark Iron dwarves who'd just been cheering -- those closest were instantly obliterated in the spreading heat wave, not even able to scream. Those further away were blasted high into the air or slammed into rocks, dead or alive unknown.

Jace was nearly knocked unconscious by the blast. But he forced himself to stay awake at this critical moment.

Ragnaros was the Firelord, the ruler of the elementals. But he was still an elemental.

So if only... "Jace Sesso!"

A Bronzebeard dwarf helped him up. Jace looked at his face. "Lord Muradin Bronzebeard..."

"I'm not Lord Muradin!" the dwarf took a breath and shouted. "But he'll be here soon!"

"Destroy those." Jace grabbed his beard tight. "Tell everyone -- destroy his! With everything you've got!"

The Bronzebeard warrior looked toward the heart of Molten Core in the distance, nodded, and shook off Jace's hand. "I understand, I understand, Master Sesso!"

"Destroy his!" the Bronzebeard screamed, running toward every dwarf still alive. "Destroy his!"

Some Dark Iron dwarves responded. Further off, countless Arcane Missiles flew toward the Firelord. Some of them turned toward the Firelord's arms.

But Arcane Missiles before Ragnaros were like fish swimming toward a whale -- laughable. One roar shattered most of them into nothing. And Sulfuras slammed down hard among the Dark Iron dwarves, instantly reducing everything living and dead in that area to ash. Mages or Ironfury soldiers -- it made no difference.

Between that shockwave and Sulfuras's swings, no one knew how many of Shadowforge City's finest warriors had been destroyed.

Jace could hear countless tormented, angry, unwilling ghosts wailing in the air. But those spirits didn't wail long before Ragnaros's fire consumed them.

And he wasn't the only one who heard those souls screaming.

"Ragnaros!"

A furious, almost tearful shout came from above.

"Enough! Enough! You'll pay for every Dark Iron you killed! I want you dead! I want you DEAD!"

At the source of the voice, a fierce blaze erupted from Ragnaros's grip. It was an enormous flaming dwarf. Dagran Thaurissan!

Jace had heard that Dagran Thaurissan could use Avatar, but he didn't acknowledge himself as a Mountain King. During the previous battles, he'd never summoned his aspect, and Jace thought he'd been bragging.

But now this Dark Iron dwarf was wreathed in raging flame, screaming as he tore free of the Firelord's grasp. Even Ragnaros was taken aback.

"Traitor..."

The Firelord swung his arm to grab him again. But Thaurissan raised his royal scepter -- now blazing with golden flame, shining like a sun -- and struck the Firelord's arm directly at the lowest of his bracers!

A crisp clang rang out, piercing and teeth-grinding. The runes flowing on it dimmed briefly but didn't shatter. Instead, Thaurissan was blasted somewhere unknown by the recoil.

The Firelord let out a triumphant laugh. But just as he casually raised his hammer to smash another group of Dark Iron mages, a black shadow dove silently from the black clouds above toward the bracer Thaurissan had just struck!

A black dragon?

Ragnaros clearly didn't sense this weak young dragon. But when a streak of fire flared up on the black dragon's back, his lava-forged, burning head tilted slightly.

Sulfuron Hammer. That hammer hadn't shattered yet?

Grede leaped from Needleflame's back. This time, he didn't shout or roar. He swung the hammer straight down, smashing into the slightly dimmed bracer!

A dull thud. The bracer shattered on impact. The Firelord's entire body shuddered. He roared and reached for Grede, but a volley of colorful arrows struck his palm, stopping the grab.

"Ragnaros!"

Vereesa's shout came from above. Belanore's dream-colored glow was so small amid the raging flames, yet so visible.

The Firelord clearly felt the power of this ranger's arrows. He raised a fist and sprayed a torrent of lava toward her, trying to incinerate the fragile elf on the spot. But Vereesa dodged every stream of magma with precision, standing exactly in the gap between two lava splashes, finding her footing at the most extreme line, nocking another arrow.

But with Vereesa's fire resistance, how could she withstand this heat? Bear this terrible pressure? Even if the lava couldn't splash directly onto her, this shouldn't be possible...

Then Jace felt a familiar power from Vereesa.

Not just Vereesa. Countless Bronzebeard dwarves pouring in from every passage, even the newly arrived Dark Iron dwarves -- all carried this same warm power. Different from Ragnaros's flame, this warm power was hot but not at all violent or chaotic.

It was the red dragon's blessing.

Then a massive crimson figure appeared high above. Jace felt a surge of energy fill his body, helping him shake off the mental suppression Ragnaros had placed on him.

"Jace Sesso! Sorry I'm late -- I've never been here before!" Vaelastrasz's voice rumbled from the black clouds.

Sora had already landed behind Jace. With the red dragon's blessing, she could fly under Ragnaros's flame suppression too.

And there was more than just Vaelastrasz and Sommaleer flying up there.

Flying creatures smaller than the red dragons darted out from behind them. At first, Jace thought they were red whelps or hatchlings following Vaelastrasz. But logically, Vaelastrasz had come here on a secret mission to Blackrock Spire -- why would he bring so many younglings?

Then one of them emerged from the black smoke, and Jace saw its shape clearly.

It was a brown-green Hinterlands gryphon!

"For Khaz Modan!"

The gryphon riders, blessed by the red dragon, had found their way into Molten Core from somewhere. Wildhammer knights shouted as they hurled their warhammers at the Firelord's arms. Though those crackling hammers, from dozens or hundreds of meters away, looked like insignificant stars in the Firelord's blazing light, they still struck the bracers, making their runes flicker.

"Focus on one! Hit the one that's left on that arm!"

"Die, Firelord! Go back where you belong!"

But despite the Wildhammers' shouting, the Firelord had no intention of being distracted. He seemed completely enraged by Grede shattering that first bracer, ignoring the red dragon, ignoring the gryphon riders. He raised his warhammer again, aiming at where Grede had fallen.

This blow carried Ragnaros's almost tangible rage-flame. It swept aside layers of smoke and dust in its descent -- but was deflected by a flash of golden light!

Muradin Bronzebeard!

The Mountain King roared, blocking the Firelord's blow with his warhammer and battle-axe. Beneath him, the other Mountain King, still gleaming silver moments ago, had gone dim.

"Get Thunderfist out of here, damn it! Is anyone still alive here?!"

Muradin roared, his voice already far less forceful than before. Everyone around him -- gryphon riders, other dwarves -- had either been killed by that blow or blasted to who knows where.

But Ragnaros was already raising Sulfuras again.

In the firestorm-filled space, purple shadow-serpents were rising from somewhere. When Ragnaros angrily swung his hammer down, a howling black meteor flew up from below, striking Sulfuras at an angle!
