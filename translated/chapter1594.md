## Chapter 1594: Just One More Blow to Block

Two unstoppable energies collided -- the roar of blazing fire and the howl of souls wrestled against each other, black-purple conflagration and hammer-flame churning into layer upon layer of black-red shadowflame that exploded chaotically.

"Warlock."

Ragnaros murmured as he braced himself with one arm, but only glanced briefly in Jace's direction.

It was clear he could not tolerate the dwarf who had shattered his hold with that fake Sulfuras continuing to live. He was determined to kill him.

But Grede couldn't hold out much longer. The scattered souls and shadows throughout the space were no longer enough for Jace to raise a second Hand of Gul'dan capable of blocking the Firelord's strike.

Even if Muradin could take another hammer blow, Grede wouldn't survive the shockwave of Sulfuras.

The gryphon riders overhead were still looking for openings to strike the Firelord. The mages below, and Vereesa, were dodging flames while continuing their assault. Jace had to keep buying time for all of them.

With that thought, Jace took a deep breath and spoke in a low voice.

"It was I who helped the Dark Iron dwarves forge the Sulfuron Hammer, Ragnaros."

"It was I who stole the prison of Thunderaan from Baron Geddon."

"It was I who retrieved Neptulon's Eternal Essence."

"It was I who orchestrated the assassination of Executus. I who controlled the Twilight's Hammer's leader. I who convinced Dagran Thaurissan. I who destroyed your protection runes, one by one."

"The Molten Core is nothing special."

Jace murmured these words as he was once again wrapped in Shadowform, Ulthalesh dripping shadowy demon blood in his hand.

He knew Ragnaros could hear him.

The Firelord knew everything happening in the Molten Core at this moment. He could hear every sound here. He knew the shadow energy swirling in Jace's mind. He knew everything Jace said was true.

"I am Jace Sesso, Ragnaros!" he suddenly roared. "Even Deathwing died before me -- what are you compared to him?!"

Boom!

Another rune was shattered by the gryphon knights and dwarven mages, but Ragnaros remained utterly unmoved.

"You killed Neltharion. Well done, mortal."

The whirlwind of flame shifted and surged toward Jace. The temperature in the Molten Core shot up. Now even the blessing of the red dragons could barely protect them. Gryphon feathers from those up high caught fire and fell. Some Bronzebeard dwarves could hardly endure any longer. Vereesa's expression grew grimmer by the moment.

"Keep attacking, lads! Only two left!"

Kurdran Wildhammer's roar came from above. At this signal, the fiercest Alterac gryphons joined the fight. These gray-white beasts thrashed wildly to shake off flames, then dove, helping the riders on their backs hurl warhammers at the Firelord below.

Jace's eyes darted among the dwarves. He licked his lips, chanted to renew his Mage Armor, and fixed his gaze on the Sulfuras in Ragnaros's hand.

*Ready, Morophil?*

*Ah, master. Ready or not... where else would I go?*

*Prepare the Blood Pact, Sazeno!*

*Huh? Me too? Damn it, hurry up!*

"Don't worry about me, Muradin! Get Grede out of here!"

"Hey, wait, Sesso--"

Muradin's roar was swallowed entirely by the howling flames of the rising Sulfuras.

The gryphons' cries, the dragons' roars, the dwarves' shouts -- all faded away. In his vision, only the bright spikes of Sulfuras's head remained.

Jace steadied his emotions, called upon Ulthalesh's power, and focused on chanting the Shadow Shield incantation as crimson blood-red patterns emerged on his skin.

*Sazeno... that little bastard actually came through. Didn't flee.*

Sulfuras's spikes suddenly expanded. The tips stabbed hard into the membrane of the Shadow Shield. Jace could barely see anything -- he only felt his entire body instantly scorched as if cooked, his eardrums bulging, the blood-red magical runes on his body collapsing...

The black shield shattered on the spot. The shadow impact blasted a black crater around him.

But he was alive.

"Again, Ragnaros!" Jace shouted, raising Ulthalesh. Only then did he notice the imp beside him had collapsed -- the power absorbed by that last Blood Pact had blasted it unconscious.

*Sora, I need a blessing... any blessing you can give. Vaelastrasz! I'm counting on you--*

--

Sora's sharp cry was crystal clear, but he didn't know if the green dragon could hear him. However, as Sora's natural magic took hold, a frigid whirlwind swirled around him, and a shield of flame wrapped itself around him -- that must be the red dragon's magic.

With the magical protection of two dragons as his trump card, Jace took another deep breath, activated the Talisman of Ephemeral Power, and a surge of incredibly sharp perception instantly dispelled the mental shock from that last hammer blow.

He watched the raised Sulfuras, lifted his staff high, and raised a second Shadow Shield. Raising a second shield this urgently consumed far more mana than the first, but at this moment he couldn't unleash any offensive spell strong enough to shake Ragnaros anyway. Keeping mana in reserve was pointless.

Now the Mountain Kings were spent. The others definitely couldn't hold out. It was his turn -- the warlock's.

"e..."

Ragnaros's second hammer fell with a thunderous crash, shattering the Shadow Shield and slamming into the red dragon's fire shield. Jace felt as though the blow had landed directly on his head. His Mage Armor shattered on the spot. Vaelastrasz's hide tore open. His vision went black, and a searing hot stream erupted from his mouth.

Vaelastrasz let out a pained cry. Sora tumbled down, nearly rolling straight into the nearby lava river. Even Morophil was knocked to her knees, coughing blood nonstop. Jace knew they had given everything to protect him, but the damage mitigation chain only went so far.

Suddenly, Ragnaros bellowed again. Under the dispersing rainbow light, the Sulfuras he had just raised lowered once more.

Under the sustained bombardment of gryphon knights and dwarven mages, Vereesa's arrow shattered the third--gone.

Ragnaros seemed not to care at all, as if he would destroy Jace no matter what. He roared and blasted out a massive shockwave, sending everyone nearby flying. Without the red dragon's protection, Vereesa -- who had been trying to rush over and pull Jace away -- was thrown outward by the blast. Morophil was helplessly flung away too. Jace rolled several times before collapsing to the ground.

Ragnaros had no intention of letting him go.

Sulfuras rose again. Jace thought he heard Sora calling his name from far behind him, felt the tremor through the soul link.

He didn't know if he could force himself to cast a Shadowstep, barely dodging the direct strike, barely surviving.

Only one broken one remained.

After the last shockwave passed, not many gryphons could still fly through the flames. The surviving dwarves on the ground were also forced to find whatever cover they could.

"I admired your courage and confidence earlier, mortal. But where are they now?!"

With the Elemental Lord's laughter, Sulfuras came crashing down.

Suddenly, an unusual surge of fury erupted from the side.

"Jace Sesso!"

Needleflame's screech and Grede's incomprehensible roar pulled close, overwhelming even the thunderous crash of Sulfuras's descent. The Sulfuron Hammer, empowered by a flash of silver light, slammed heavily into the last vambrace of Ragnaros's descending arm!

The shattering crack nearly slammed into Jace's chest. It was broken!

Sulfuras fell to the side. Ragnaros's massive body before him twitched uncontrollably.

"I will not... leave!"

"Jace Sesso! Jace Sesso!"

The Firelord roared, reaching out a hand of flame to grab the warlock, but was pulled backward by a force strong enough to tear the plane apart. The heart of the Molten Core blazed bright until nothing could be seen but blinding white light.

"Run! Run, everyone!"

Muradin Bronzebeard roared. Everyone fled for their lives. Vereesa ran over, scooped up Jace, and turned to bolt, but before they had made it more than a few steps, the planar shattering shockwave caught up and sent them both tumbling through the air...
