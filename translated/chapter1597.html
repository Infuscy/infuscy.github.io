Chapter 1597: The True Master of Sulfuras

Jace grabbed the Sky-Shatterer and everything Grede had left behind in this place. A lot of it was burned beyond recognition, but he stuffed as much as he could into his bags.

Of course, there was also that dagger. Its blade was like a piece of never-cooling, forked, sharp volcanic glass.

Jace remembered its name. The Fury of Ragnaros. A drop from the final boss of the Molten Core, a pretty good weapon. He hadn't expected Ragnaros, even as he was being banished from this world, to hand this dagger to a trusted servant for an assassination attempt out of spite.

The Sulfuron Hammer was gone. No trace of it in the crevice. Grede hadn't said anything more about it. They hadn't found it outside either. Vaelastrzasz had followed them all the way and hadn't noticed it either.

Jace couldn't spend much time searching. Before the boulders above came crashing down, they had to carry the dwarf out of here as fast as possible.

The last protection rune was gone. Even Ragnaros had been driven back to Sulfuron. Since this was the connection point between the two worlds, the Molten Core could no longer continue to exist either. What had been collapsing walls and churning lava rivers not long ago was now practically heaven-and-earth-shattering cataclysm. Surging magma relentlessly consumed the black and brown stone.

Following the soul link, and the call of Sora's plea to her kin that Vaelastrzasz could easily hear, everyone quickly regrouped.

There was no time to be excited that Grede was still alive. The whole world felt like it was crushing in, collapsing. No living creatures could be seen nearby anymore. They had either already fled or perished in the sea of fire.

The group couldn't even exchange words. They could only flee toward higher ground with the surging lava at their backs, as if a few seconds' delay would fuse them with this hell forever.

Part of Shadowforge City had already collapsed into the Molten Core. Tunnels and corridors everywhere were broken, but not all of these paths actually led into Shadowforge City.

After running into dead ends several times, just as the all-consuming lava seemed about to catch up, the Bound Soul of the Windseeker that Jace was carrying finally spoke up.

"On your left, Jace Sesso. Do you see that Dark Iron corridor with dwarvish runes carved into the wall -- Tia, Mafel?" Thunderaan said. "That leads to Shadowforge City."

"I see it," Jace said. "Are you sure?"

"Cold wind is sinking in from that passage. I can hear the voices of children. The gaps are absolutely big enough for mortal bodies to pass through," Thunderaan said.

Jace nodded and shouted to the others ahead.

"Take that passage! Thunderaan says it leads outside!"

Everyone knew Thunderaan the Wind Prince was no saint himself, but after finally escaping the fire elementals' imprisonment and having a chance at rebirth, he certainly didn't want to be buried beneath Shadowforge City again, waiting who knows how long to see the light of day again.

So Vereesa, at the front, decisively leaped across a gap and ran toward the road on the opposite side. The others followed without hesitation.

They crawled into the tilted passageway. Lava quickly swallowed the path they had just been standing on. Thick smoke poured into the tunnel, choking them, giving them no choice but to climb upward as fast as they could. If the path ahead was blocked, they'd all be trapped here to suffocate.

But they hadn't been climbing long before Jace felt a cool breeze against his face. Calling it "cool" was relative -- it was still far hotter than any normal breeze, but for a group that had just escaped a collapsing Molten Core, it felt cold enough.

The lava surge below seemed to be weakening a bit too. Vereesa let out a long breath. Jace felt the tension ease slightly as well. The searing pain all over his body and the crushing fatigue came flooding back all at once.

"Where did the Sulfuron Hammer go, Jace Sesso?" Needleflame asked.

Jace shook his head. "I don't know. I didn't see that hammer where I found Grede."

"How is that possible? That guy treats that hammer like his own life," Needleflame said. "If he couldn't hide the Sulfuron Hammer himself and bring it before Executus, he'd never have let me touch it! He'd sooner lose his own life than lose that thing."

"You said a fire elemental assassin was trying to avenge Ragnaros, and caught Grede while he was wounded," Vereesa said as she climbed upward. "Could those fire elementals have had accomplices who stole the Sulfuron Hammer first?"

"I'm not sure. But there was only one fire elemental nearby at the time. It wouldn't make much sense for one to take the Sulfuron Hammer and flee first while leaving a companion behind to finish the job," Jace said.

"Could it be that Ragnaros reclaimed it at the last moment?" Sora suggested. "I could sense that Ragnaros was deeply concerned about a mortal being able to wield such a hammer."

"Clearly, it was Jace Sesso he was more concerned about in the end, Sormaelir," Vaelastrzasz said.

"No..." The dwarf slung over Vaelastrzasz's shoulder mumbled. "Monster... couldn't take it. No."

"Grede?" Vereesa looked toward the red dragon. "Are you awake?"

"I... couldn't hold it... it changed..."

The dwarf muttered indistinctly, his burned-black hand trying to make a gripping motion, but then jerking back as if touching flame.

"Thunderfist?" Needleflame asked. "What do you mean, you couldn't hold it?"

"He's this badly injured, Needleflame," Sora said.

"Don't push him, little black dragon," Vaelastrzasz said. "He needs rest now. We should get back to Shadowforge City as fast as we can, find him a less hot place to rest properly."

Everyone assumed Grede was simply too weak to hold onto the Sulfuron Hammer, which itself possessed immense fire power. But Jace sensed something different in Grede's words.

When Grede said he couldn't hold it, even though he had passed out, his heart was still churning with excitement and exhilaration. In Jace's experience, that didn't feel like losing something important, like losing something you once had. It felt more like gaining something new.

Could it be...

In the game, after defeating Ragnaros, you could obtain the Eye of Sulfuras, which could transform the Thorium Brotherhood's Sulfuron Hammer into the true Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros. So here, too?

Jace glanced back at the darkened passage behind him. Had Grede lost his grip on the hammer he had learned to wield so well because he could no longer control the overwhelming power of Sulfuras?

Had that hammer already...?

But the connection between Azeroth and the Firelands had collapsed. Whatever form the Sulfuron Hammer had taken, a weapon forged in Azeroth had no way of falling into an elemental plane.

As long as it was still beneath Shadowforge City, they could retrieve it.

Vereesa stopped walking. Ahead was a warped iron door. From the dwarvish text on it, it seemed to lead to some kind of warehouse.

She tested the door. Needleflame waved her hand.

"Step back, Vereesa. You're all injured. I'm still fine. Let me handle this."

Vereesa nodded. The black dragon backed up a bit, lowered her head, and charged like a young heifer. Her forehead hit the stuck iron door with a loud bang, bending it out of shape.

She gestured to Vereesa that it was clear to come in. Vaelastrzasz and Sora exchanged a look behind her, as if to say, "Is this really a black dragon?"

Faint firelight flickered beyond the door. Needleflame stepped inside, then suddenly froze, involuntarily stepping back two paces.

Jace drew Azuresong and stepped through. Vereesa drew Dragon's Call and followed. Both prepared to fight whatever elemental monster might be waiting. But in the blue glow of Azuresong, they saw a hall full of Dark Iron dwarves.

From the bright red anvil insignias on their armor, these were members of the Shadowforge Legion. About a hundred of them, each leveling a rifle or spear at the broken door. Behind them, in the corridors and stone rooms beyond, were more Dark Iron dwarves -- mostly soldiers or civilians too wounded to stand.

They had all been glaring fiercely at Needleflame. Many were even about to open fire. But when they saw Jace step through with his blade drawn, their expressions shifted to hesitation. Some even lowered their weapons.

"You. Jace Sesso." A Shadowforge knight in bright red armor raised his battle-axe. "I saw him with my own eyes. He used shadow magic to block Ragnaros's fury."

Jace had been about to call himself "Slovak" again. Having the other guy suddenly call him by his real name actually made him a little more cautious.

But then he remembered -- Ragnaros had roared his name across the whole Molten Core more times than anyone could count. There was no need to keep pretending to be Twilight's Hammer anymore.

"Jace Sesso. It really is them."

The other Dark Iron dwarves lowered their weapons too -- until Vaelastrzasz carried Grede Thunderfist forward.

"That Mountain King. He's still alive?" A Shadowforge soldier recognized Grede. Murmurs spread through the hall of Dark Iron dwarves.

"Yes," Vaelastrzasz said. "Now we need to find a place to focus on treating him."

A Shadowforge officer stepped out before the soldiers.

"Sulfuras awaits its master, outsiders."
