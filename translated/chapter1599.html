Chapter 1599: King, or Hero?

Sulfuras, in the end, couldn't replace herbs, healing potions, restorative magic, or adequate rest. Not long after he picked up the warhammer, Grede passed out again.

But whether by coincidence or some actual connection, once Sulfuras returned to Grede's hands, the tremors in the Molten Core seemed to subside. Aside from a few cliffs and damaged structures already on the verge of collapse that continued to crumble occasionally, the depths of Blackrock Mountain had more or less stabilized.

Dagran Thaurissan arranged a fine room in Thaurissan's Temple -- one previously occupied by a flamekeeper -- for Grede to rest. Though to an outsider it was still less comfortable than an inn in Dalaran, the exquisite stone carvings, the near-perfect forging and cutting of the metal and blackstone furniture... for dwarves, it was the height of luxury.

To prevent demons or shadow energy from further harming the already weakened Grede, Jace didn't let Sazeno or Morophil enter the room. Instead, he sent them back to the room at the Black Iron Tavern to look after the luggage and sort through everything they'd looted from Shazzrah, hiding it all away.

Not just demons. Jace was the same.

He was badly wounded. His control over shadow was significantly weaker. His emotions were hard to steady. Staying in that room might cause more harm to Grede than a demon would.

But he couldn't bring himself to go back to the tavern either. So after leaving the Hand of Justice by Grede's bedside, he simply went out to the balcony. Listening to Sora, Vaelastrzasz, and a few dwarven priests discuss Grede's condition, he stared blankly at the half-collapsed Hall of Magic in the distance.

Vereesa had slumped into an iron chair by the door inside the room, listening to the discussion until she fell asleep.

Her injuries were worse than expected. After hearing Sora's explanation, Jace could hardly imagine she had been blasted away by Ragnaros's explosion and still managed to run back and carry him out.

Needleflame was about the same. Before the showdown with Executus, this young dragon had already been nearly exhausted from carrying the Sulfuron Hammer.

After that, she fought Shazzrah to the death. She gave her last ounce of strength to bring Grede back into the fight against Ragnaros. That was unquestionably thanks to the elemental creatures she had devoured along the way.

Right now she was asleep across from Jace, leaning against the railing, curled up on the ground with her mouth hanging open like a dead bug, drool running down her chin.

It wasn't that everyone was snoring away, unconcerned about Grede's safety. They had all simply reached their absolute limits.

No one had expected to actually face the Firelord. To face a Firelord who still stood at the heart of the Molten Core even after all the protection runes were destroyed.

"Sesso." At some point, Vaelastrzasz appeared at the door.

He glanced at the black dragon and lowered his voice further.

"The Alliance is planning to attack Blackrock Spire soon. News from Shadowforge City is being sent to Stormwind, Lordaeron, and Dalaran."

"Yes." Jace stood up from the railing.

"But Grede will likely have a hard time joining the next assault right away." The red dragon walked over, one hand on the railing. "His injuries are... in better shape than I initially estimated. But he needs a long time to recover."

"I know." Jace lowered his head. "Thank you for coming to the Molten Core in time to help, Vaelastrzasz. I know I've said it many times already, but truly -- thank you for saving Grede."

"I thought you already considered me a friend." Vaelastrzasz raised a dark red eyebrow. "You see, aside from some clever shapeshifting tricks and the ability to clean mage towers, I can still be useful in other ways."

Jace smiled awkwardly. He hadn't expected this red dragon to still be holding a grudge about the time Jace had asked him to help clean out Ilgalar's Tower.

"You clearly need rest too, Sesso," the red dragon said. "I'll take care of Grede. You should get a good night's sleep."

Jace nodded. Vaelastrzasz turned and went back inside. But before Jace could relax, Dagran Thaurissan appeared on the balcony from the other side.

He had changed into a more presentable gray coat. If you ignored his singed beard, still untrimmed, and overlooked his forced but still noticeably limping gait, he had almost regained some of his former appearance.

Watching the red dragon's back disappear into the room, the emperor spoke quietly.

"I feel like just a few hours ago, I witnessed the birth of another Mountain King alongside many warriors, Jace Sesso."

"Speak plainly, Your Majesty," Jace said.

Thaurissan's eye twitched. He stared hard at Jace. The heat of his anger could practically scorch the man before him. Ragnaros might have left this world, but his "blessing" still remained in this Dark Iron leader's body.

"Is Grede Thunderfist trying to become the Mountain King?" He glanced at Needleflame sleeping nearby, forcing his voice low as he leaned in. "He wants to raise Sulfuras, inherit Anvilmare's legacy, and rule the Three Hammers again? Is this young man ready to ascend the throne of Ironforge?"

Jace's gaze dropped to the space between the Dark Iron dwarf's brows.

"Honestly? I don't know."

"You're a warlock. A very powerful one. Maybe the most powerful warlock alive in the entire Alliance -- including Shadowforge City." Dagran Thaurissan clenched his teeth. "So I understand perfectly that hiding my intentions from you, pretending I don't want something... is pointless. So let me be frank with you."

"Ragnaros has been destroyed. I am deeply grateful, Jace Sesso. I am grateful to the Bronzebeards and the Wildhammers too. Half of Shadowforge City has collapsed. Many of our people died in this battle. But their sacrifice was worth it... unless you actually plan to find another dwarf to trample us again -- Bronzebeard or Thunderfist, it doesn't matter!"

"I can't make a dwarf do anything," Jace said. "Right now, all I want is to destroy Blackrock Spire, defeat the orcs and the black dragons. I only hope you'll keep your promise to help the Alliance attack Victor Nefarius. And when we win, I believe the Alliance will return Blackrock Spire to your people and establish friendlier relations with you."

"As for what happens with the Three Hammers after that, whether there will be another Mountain King -- that's not my concern."

"Don't play dumb with me," Thaurissan said. "We both know you can influence Grede Thunderfist. Maybe that was your plan all along, warlock!"

"If that were really the case, do you think my opinion now could make him give up the title of Mountain King?" Jace said. "Don't forget, even Ragnaros couldn't make you give up your throne."

Thaurissan faltered for a moment.

"So he really does want to become Mountain King."

"If he wants to become Mountain King," Jace said.

"That's not what we agreed on!" Thaurissan spat out a furious breath.

"Grede Thunderfist isn't a demon. I can't control him like I would a Felguard." Jace narrowed his eyes, speaking slowly. "But."

"What?" Thaurissan could barely wait another word.

"Everything requires a price, Your Majesty." Jace's voice was calm. "If you want a Wildhammer to give up such a grand ideal, there must be a corresponding price. And even if his family name is Thunderfist, he comes from gryphon rider stock -- a warrior, not a born noble or ruler..."

Dagran Thaurissan eyed Shadowforge City outside.

"Sulfuras."

"I'm not that stupid. I can see that Grede Thunderfist desperately wants that hammer of the Firelord. As the leader of Shadowforge City, as the leader of the Dark Iron dwarves, I can cede this weapon -- born at the heart of Shadowforge City, forged by the Thorium Brotherhood -- to him."

"Cede?" Jace asked. "Even from an outsider like me, it seems even the Dark Irons consider him the true master of Sulfuras."

"Don't talk to me about public sentiment." Thaurissan slammed a hand on the railing, then shook himself into a coughing fit.

"Sulfuras is now a symbol of the Three Hammers' reconciliation. He can't just carve his name onto that burning warhammer that easily! Never mind him now -- even if he actually becomes the Mountain King in the future, he couldn't!"

"Yesterday, you must have seen the look in Muradin Bronzebeard's and Kurdran Wildhammer's eyes when he picked up Sulfuras, Jace Sesso. Those two practically wanted to kill Grede Thunderfist, take credit for defeating the Firelord, and become the hammer's master themselves. You're a warlock. You must have seen it more clearly than I did."

"Maybe what we saw was slightly different, Your Majesty," Jace said.

"Enough." The Dark Iron dwarf's voice was hard. "No matter what, if he wants to claim the Firelord's treasure as his own, he needs the support of the Dark Iron lord."

"In that case," Jace said, "I don't know your dwarves. I don't know the traditions of the Mountain King. I don't know Ironforge or Shadowforge City. And I certainly don't know what Sulfuras means to the Dark Irons. I think these matters are best discussed with Grede Thunderfist himself, when he wakes up."

"Then so be it." Thaurissan's voice was low.

"I'm sorry for wasting your time, Your Majesty."

"Don't say that, Jace Sesso." Thaurissan didn't turn around, his voice cold. "Thank you for the reminder."
