Chapter 1611: The Power of Telogrus

In the early morning, Jace rode a gryphon back to Stonewatch with Morophil.

As they emerged from the Burning Steppes, the cold air kept hitting him. Breathing the clean air blowing off the winter-chilled waters of Lake Everstill felt so good Jace almost didn't want to waste it by breathing too deeply.

As he approached the Tower of Ilgalar, he saw two shadows charging toward him from a distance. It was the two Dazarm, the guardians of Ilgalar.

Both felhounds were visibly excited. When Jace saw them, they seemed even bigger than the last time they'd met.

That probably wasn't just his imagination. As long as they didn't have to maintain the portal to the Telogrus Rift, the energy from the Tower of Ilgalar was enough to feed the two guardian demons.

As he climbed the steps, a stench hit him. The smell wasn't coming from inside the tower. Walking around the base, he found a large pile — maybe a dozen, rotten and stinking gnoll heads — dumped in a nearby ditch.

When they saw their master had discovered the gnoll heads, the two felhounds suddenly shrieked, scrambling over each other to get to Jace. They excitedly waved their tentacles and claws, making a strange "dududududu" sound like a goblin steam engine starting up.

"Don't pile the heads of your kills in front of my tower." Jace covered his nose and glared at the felhounds, speaking unhappily in Demonic. "Get rid of them, now!"

The two felhounds were startled by Jace's reaction. They turned and dove into the ditch, gulping down the pile of rotten heads. Jace watched with a frown.

"Don't come into my tower until you've cleaned the stench off yourselves."

Even so, Jace was grateful for them. If nothing else, the mere presence of these two demons was enough to deter large packs of gnolls from returning to harass Stonewatch. That way, the Stonewatch soldiers could get used to coexisting with a group of demons.

Midnight was watching him from the shadow under the tower, standing silently, still as a statue, with only the eerie light flickering in his eyes.

"Take me to Stormwind tonight, Midnight," Jace said. "Be ready. After that, you're coming with me to Blackrock Spire. We'll deal with those black dragons and orcs together."

Midnight didn't respond with sound, just let the malevolent gleam in his eyes flicker once and shifted his hind hoof slightly. Jace knew that was about as much response as this undead steed was capable of.

Thanks to the Taralfsin Crystal and the green dragons' blessing, although the Tower of Ilgalar was a strange tower filled with dark shadow energy, inhabited by various demons and undead, decorated with dark runic arrays, and occasionally spawning portals to the Twisting Nether, it hadn't brought too many negative effects to this tranquil forest.

At least, within about twenty paces of the tower, the grass was relatively normal. The surrounding trees, except for those too close to the top of the tower, were mostly healthy with no serious signs of withering.

The faint purple light of the magic crystal shone on the green leaves rustling in the cool breeze. The glow wasn't exactly healthy-looking, but this mingling of arcane and shadow energy had basically crushed the Shadowhide gnolls' ambitions. Not long ago, Jace had heard that some people had actually started exploring and traveling in the Stonewatch Forest.

This was exactly what Jace wanted. He still remembered Amy Malin telling him about visiting this area as a child, but she couldn't stray far from Stonewatch because of the gnolls.

Maybe in the future, with his presence here, the vast forest stretching from here all the way to the Black Morass could become territory firmly controlled by the Kingdom of Stormwind. A "back garden" protected by him.

After those gnolls who refused to live peacefully with humans fled to Khaz Modan, yielding the forest to humans and the beasts living here.

After the draenei in the south finally healed the terrible scars the Dark Portal had left on the southern Black Morass. After Khadgar finally managed to control Karazhan's dark leaks together with the Curator, letting Duskwood heal back to its former self. After the damage Ragnaros had inflicted on the Redridge Mountains gradually faded, and the Morgan family could begin rebuilding their ancestral home.

The little Amys, little Jaces, or little Varians of the future could travel and adventure freely through these woods with their parents — though they'd need to watch out for bears and wolves. The citizens of the Kingdom of Stormwind could migrate to these increasingly habitable places. Life here would be visibly more peaceful than in another timeline.

Maybe Lordaeron would get more chaotic. Jace thought about Prince Galen Trollbane, whom he'd met last night. Once King Thoras and King Terenas had both passed, Lordaeron's King Arthas and Stromgarde's King Galen... what kind of sparks would fly between those two younger generations in the Hillsbrad Foothills? And how long would the Alliance of Lordaeron last?

But none of that had anything to do with him, Jace thought. After all, his parents were already here. And the Scourge invasion that should have happened had been nipped in the bud. What more could they ask for?

The Red Dragonflight was cooperating with the ice trolls in Northrend to stop the Scourge's rampage. The Green Dragonflight, with Queen Alexstrasza's help, had eliminated the nightmare crisis caused by Xavius early on. Deathwing had died early... The variables that the Old Gods had used to disrupt the Titan prisons had been suppressed early. Maybe those seals could hold a little longer.

The pity was that, except for him and the Bronze Dragonflight, nobody knew about any of these "things that never happened." So naturally, nobody could thank him for them.

But he had many close friends who'd faced life and death with him. So many friends. And Midnight. And these demons loyal to him. And this tower. When the time came, he could put aside all that messy business and just live quietly here, guarding the eastern border of the Kingdom of Stormwind, gazing out over this forest, listening to the sound of Stonewatch Falls...

But between him and this beautiful future lay Blackrock Spire. Thinking about it felt like something blocking the smooth flow of his thoughts.

And one of the key steps to dealing with that tower was right in front of him.

Jace walked inside, came to the demon communication circle at the center of the ground floor, tossed in a soul shard, and chanted the incantation. On the other end came the response of the doom lord.

In moments, a rift stretched open in the air before him. The energy of the entire Tower of Ilgalar trembled in response. The crystal spun faster and faster. Jace stepped into the Telogrus Rift, Morophil following close behind.

Kallrez spread his wings and flew high from the distance, then landed heavily in front of Jace, causing a violent tremor in the ground.

The doom lord puffed out his tawny-red chest, runes of demonic fire flowing across his armor. Jace could sense that after staying in Telogrus for this period, his power had grown noticeably since the fight with Onyxia.

"Looks like you've eaten well, Lord Kallrez," Morophil said with a smile in Demonic.

"The Ered'ruin and the Nether are one, huntress," Kallrez said. "That is why I can draw power directly from the Twisting Nether. While you... must beg for scraps from mortals."

"I can tell you're trying to be sarcastic," Morophil said. "Not that hard to understand."

"This female demon has no shame. She has no idea what dignity means, great Lord Kallrez!" Sazeno muttered. "Talking to her like that won't work. I'll tell you — if you say her wings are ugly, that'll..."

Morophil's gaze turned cold. Jace grabbed the imp and shoved him into his bag, cinching the rope tight. Speaking loudly in Demonic, he said: "Enough idle chatter. It looks like summoning you has gotten harder, Kallrez. I'm going to summon you at the Altar of Storms alone next time. You should remember that place. But I'm trying to bring you over intact, so give me a list of what you need."

"I hope this time it's about destroying something, killing some living thing, and not another mage tower you need to supply or another portal you need to open," Kallrez said with a bored expression.

"It's another black dragon," Jace said. "A black dragon more powerful than the last one. And he commands thousands of minions."

"Sounds interesting," Kallrez said. "But what about Kazzak, Jace Sesso?"

"What? Has Kil'jaeden already found out you betrayed the Legion? You still want to kill Kazzak and take his position as Lord of Ered'ruin?" Jace asked.

"It's personal now, my tiny master." The doom lord's voice dropped low. "You haven't forgotten, have you?"

"Last time was a pure accident. I thought Kil'jaeden would send Kazzak to deal with me on Draenor, so I could kill him and fulfill the terms of our contract," Jace said. "But I didn't expect him to go after Morophil instead."

"There's no difference," Kallrez said with a cold laugh. "Because once that sayaad is dead, you won't be hard to handle."

"I didn't know you thought so highly of me, big guy." Morophil's grin nearly split her face.

"He's not wrong about that," Jace nodded.

"Eh?" Morophil tilted her head. "Master, you really think so? I'm so—"

"Hold on." Jace grabbed her hand before it could reach his waist.

He'd suddenly noticed a small floating island glowing with purple light in the distance. That purple wasn't something else — it was clusters of long-stemmed, pinkish-purple flowers.

Netherbloom.
