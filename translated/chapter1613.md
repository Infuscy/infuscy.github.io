## Chapter 1613: Shadow Medicine Needs Shadow Fire

Inside Ursula's alchemy lab, Jace was hit by a sharp, fishy shadow-stench the moment he walked in. He frowned and looked around but didn't see anything obviously related to shadow energy.

"What happened?" Jace asked. "Did some shadow disaster hit this place?"

"Disaster? What disaster?" Ursula asked. "Are you comparing it to the time you risked your life deep in Blackrock Mountain to defeat a true god, causing an earthquake that reached Stormwind's harbor?"

Jace laughed. "Stop dwelling on that. Tell me what happened here."

"You're taking this way too lightly." Ursula said. "Look at the burn scars on your wrist. I know your mastery of the Life Drain spell — there are few in the entire human world who can match it. And you have the Dragonqueen's blessing, plus you prepared thoroughly against fire damage. Yet you still got burned this badly, and it still hasn't healed. I can sense that your aura is much weaker than before you left."

"Is Vereesa in Stormwind? If she doesn't mind, I'd like you to stay with me for a few days before you leave again, Jace. Let me take a good look at what kind of injuries you've suffered."

"Don't worry about me too much," Jace said.

Ursula wagged her index finger. "Varian Wrynn has already gone to Lake Everstill. Many troop ships from Stromgarde and Lordaeron have arrived at the harbor. Even though SI:7 hasn't told me directly, I know the king plans to attack Blackrock Spire around Winter Veil. And you'll be part of it. That's the Black Dragonflight's lair. You know that better than I do. Do you intend to drag a wounded body into battle against a Black Dragon and orc army? Or are you counting on being fully healed by the day the king gives the order? Sometimes I think... you just don't take yourself seriously enough."

Jace took a deep breath. "I'll listen to you, Ursula. I'll listen."

Despite his agreement, Ursula still looked him up and down with lingering concern. "Dammit. Before you told me about that Molten Core battle, I thought you looked so weak because of Lady Morophil."

"Could be, dear Delin," Morophil said softly.

Ursula glanced at the succubus, then back at Jace, and let out a heavy sigh, swearing in the local dialect of Silverpine Forest.

"How are you feeling now, Jace? Can you still help me? I mean..." She gestured toward the cauldron. "I've been trying to brew medicine with shadow fire recently. It's... one of the requirements for brewing destruction potions."

"Using shadow fire?" Jace said. "But in Draenor, the ones brewing the potions were Kirin Tor mages. Where did you hear about this method?"

"From the Kirin Tor people earlier," Ursula said. "They told me that in Draenor, because their village was surrounded by shadows, it was easy to find wood corrupted by shadow energy to use as fuel. I didn't expect it to be so complicated back in Azeroth. I tried using Corruption on local firewood before burning it, but the shadow always made the fuel burn too fast — never enough to last through the brewing time for destruction potions."

"I imagine the wood in Farahlon was contaminated by the void storm's energy and turned into a kind of shadow-infused plant," Jace said.

"So what solution do you see?" Ursula asked.

"Try using a Black Dragon's shadowflame," Jace said. "Switch to enchanted Quel'Thalas starlight wood as fuel. The cost would be absurdly high, but... this is destruction potion we're talking about, Ursula."

"Starlight wood... fine, cost is no object." Ursula put her hands on her hips. "So where am I supposed to catch a Black Dragon? Wait for you to return victorious from Blackrock Spire? Then what use would the destruction potion be anyway... Oh, right — didn't you bring a Black Dragon back from Draenor? Where is she now? Did she come back with you?"

Jace shook his head. "She's not in Stormwind right now. I can't call her back on short notice. But even if she came, she wouldn't help — she's a Black Dragon who can't use shadow magic."

"Can't use shadow magic? Is she still a Black Dragon?" Ursula asked.

"That's actually what a real Black Dragon is," Jace said. "Since there's no authentic shadowflame available, let me try first."

"Are you sure? With your injuries?" Ursula asked.

"No harm in trying," Jace said.

Ursula pressed her lips together, then nodded. "Since it's just a 'try,' I'll use regular wood first. No need to waste the real starlight wood. We can compare the difference anyway."

"Fine," Jace said. "Let's go to the practice room."

They went into the alchemist's practice room. Ursula arranged a pile of firewood. Jace set Ulthalesh aside, clenched his fist, and stared at the woodpile. It had been so long since he'd used even a trace of shadow magic after leaving the Molten Core that it felt almost unfamiliar.

"Did you learn your shadowflame from a Black Dragon?" Ursula asked.

"No," Jace said. "Actually, it's about infusing shadow energy directly into a fire elemental. Watch."

He murmured the incantation. A mass of black demonic flame roared to life, engulfing the woodpile.

Though he'd restrained his emotional guidance as much as possible, and could clearly feel that his power had weakened due to his injuries, this shadowflame was still far stronger than he'd intended.

Maybe it was because being back in this familiar practice room made him more relaxed, allowing him to cast more freely. In any case, some of the firewood was blasted to ash on the spot, and the remaining chunks flew everywhere.

Ursula's hellhound, watching from the side, howled frantically at the sight. It was hard to tell if the sound came from intense excitement or terror.

"Look at that piece, Jace." Ursula pointed at a splinter that had been blown out. "See the flame on it? It's burning much longer than shadow-corroded wood would. If we used higher-quality enchanted wood, it might really last a long time."

Jace picked up a bundle of firewood. "You try."

"Infusing shadow energy into flame." Ursula tilted her head, looking uncertain. "As an alchemist, I've learned some basic arcane spells for manipulating fire elements. But I've never tried infusing shadow into flame. I'm not even sure how to do it. The incantation you just chanted was just a basic Immolate spell, right? Just to ignite the flame. The process of infusing shadow was entirely your own manipulation of shadow energy..."

Jace thought for a moment. He realized he'd cast that spell so easily and naturally that he'd almost forgotten how he'd first mastered it.

After a moment's reflection, he remembered that day in Old Marin's office. He'd intended to hit the archmage's fire protection barrier with a fire spell, but ended up burning him with a shadowflame instead. That was practically the origin of his shadowflame manipulation. Later, the Dragonqueen's blessing had further amplified his shadowflame's power from the fire side. By that point, he could truly match an ordinary black whelp.

"Replace the arcane with shadow, Ursula," Jace said. "Use shadow to grasp the fire elementals, replacing the arcane's role in the spell. Use it to animate those fire elementals."

"Is that it? I never thought of it that way..." Ursula said.

"Here, I'll help summon the flame for you. That'll make it easier for you to capture them with shadow." Jace raised his finger and chanted in the elemental tongue, easily summoning a wisp of fire elemental.

Ursula pulled out a flask of pale yellow potion and drank it down. Then she stared at the flame in Jace's hand, her lips moving slightly. Suddenly Jace saw the fire in his hand turn faintly purple — though the color vanished almost instantly.

"There it is!" he said immediately.

"Really?" Ursula asked.

"Keep trying. I only succeeded after repeated practice myself," Jace said. "You could try brewing the destruction potion in Telogrus. The shadow energy there might help you grow shadowflame more effectively."

"I know." Ursula said excitedly. "That's what I was thinking too. But... to get the destruction potion brewed faster, I think the first batch needs your help. I still need to examine that orc finger, and I might not have enough time to learn this slowly."

"Of course," Jace said. "I just agreed to stay here a few days to recover, didn't I? Tomorrow I'll visit my parents, then come stay here for a few days."

"At least you remember," Ursula said, wiping the sweat from her forehead.
