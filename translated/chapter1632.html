Chapter 1632: One Mage After Another

After escorting the Elemental Earth shipment back to Stormwind City with the caravan, Jace didn't immediately head to the Slaughtered Lamb to find Ursula.

Varian's suggestion was that if he wanted more detailed information, he should talk directly to Matthias Shaw. So Jace arranged for Midnight with the palace staff and braved the rain to head to SI:7's headquarters late that night.

The Kirin Tor.

Jace had barely shaken off his own doubts. Varian's words had shaken him a little—the coming assault on Blackrock Spire involved the Kirin Tor too. Logically, Varian wouldn't want to create a rift between a trusted friend and key advisor on one side, and Dalaran—the most uniquely positioned among the Alliance nations—on the other. But Jace also knew Varian was looking out for him.

If SI:7's investigation was correct and those rumors really were coming from the Kirin Tor, then going into the Blackrock Spire offensive without keeping a careful eye on the Kirin Tor could mean walking straight into a trap set specifically for him.

The agent before him bowed slightly, saluted Jace, and left the room. The dim candlelight flickered with the thunder. Jace took a deep breath and looked out at the curtain of rain.

Was it Kel'Thuzad?

He was still in the Violet Hold. Had the Cult of the Damned already formed under Antonidas's supposedly tight watch? Or had something gone wrong with the prison?

That seemed unlikely. If there had been a breach—especially involving a prisoner Jace had personally sent there, charged with necromancy and black dragon crimes—Khadgar wouldn't have kept quiet about it, especially not after they'd spent so long discussing rumors from both Lordaeron and Stormwind.

Then was it Khadgar himself?

Jace couldn't help dragging that nearly-suppressed suspicion back to the surface, carefully replaying every detail of his two days traveling with the archmage.

He had to admit, Khadgar had changed quite a bit since returning from Draenor.

But he wasn't the only one. Every member of the Sons of Lothar had come back different after passing through the Telogrus Rift. They'd successfully led most of the soldiers who survived Draenor's destruction out from under Kil'jaeden's claws.

Khadgar had lightened up too. Even later in Karazhan, facing the terrible curses left by his teacher, or even the shadow of his teacher's mother, he wasn't as grim and serious as he'd been in Draenor.

Actually, Jace could understand that kind of ease. In Draenor, he'd been responsible for thousands of lives. In Karazhan, the worst he could do was drag Jace—the unlucky fool who'd stumbled in with him—down with him.

Maybe from Khadgar's perspective back then, that would've even been doing the world a favor, Jace mused.

A flurry of footsteps outside the door, and Matthias Shaw appeared at the threshold. "Jace..."

He paused mid-step as he entered, glancing toward the dark corner of the room. Jace looked too. Morophil sat there with her legs crossed, lazily raising a hand in greeting to Shaw.

Shaw gave a stiff nod. "Good evening, Lady Morophil."

Even someone like Matthias Shaw couldn't quite hide his unease under the gaze of Jace Sesso and his demon maid.

"Sit, Matthias," Jace said.

Shaw took a deep breath and sat down. He ventured, "Are you wondering if I'm a succubus in disguise, Jace?"

"Has that been a problem?" Jace asked.

"We've dealt with similar issues. You're from Lordaeron—you should know how much danger hides beneath the pastoral scenery of the Eastern Weald." Shaw's tone finally recovered some composure.

He tapped his fingers lightly on the table twice. "We've had help from your Dagger Club. Felhound and succubus surveillance is enough to rule out most demonic or black dragon infiltrations. But that doesn't mean we can let our guard down—especially now. Illusion spells, shape-shifters... they're everywhere."

Jace exchanged a glance with Morophil but didn't voice his most dangerous suspicion. If Kil'jaeden or the Lich King had indeed sent someone to secretly ruin Jace's reputation while his attention was on Blackrock Spire, the ideal tool for the job would be a Nathrezim dreadlord.

Jace hadn't even shared his dreadlord suspicions with Khadgar. When enemies were everywhere, letting them think you were ignorant was an advantage. Putting it all out in the open would only put him on the defensive.

"King Varian told you about the Kirin Tor, didn't he?" Matthias asked.

"Yes," Jace said. "How did you trace it back to them?"

"We've been investigating these rumors for months." Matthias rested his head on his hand. "At first, the rumors weren't connected to you at all. Mostly they were stories being passed around by people traveling between Moonbrook and Glen Town. Later, some showed up at Three Crossings. We tracked down the source of almost every supposed sighting through interviews and legwork, but most of those people hadn't actually seen anything. A lot of the information was spreading from Master Magno Dumas and his students."

"As you know, Master Dumas is the one at the Wizard Sanctum who monitors dark magic and magical crimes. So people tend to believe whatever stories come out of his circle."

"We conducted many investigations and interrogations targeting Master Dumas after that. It even created some friction with the Wizard Sanctum. If King Varian hadn't specifically explained the situation to Master Andormas, the investigation wouldn't have gone this smoothly."

"But in the end, we found no evidence that Master Dumas or his students were involved in manufacturing the rumors. What we did find was a very suspicious time window: the period when the Expedition troops returning to Azeroth were stationed in the kingdom of Stormwind, preparing to head north. Khadgar had just returned to Lordaeron to handle matters, and the Kirin Tor was preparing to send the two portal-opening artifacts back to Dalaran."

"During that time, the Kirin Tor was involved in many Wizard Sanctum affairs, including Magno Dumas's work—tracking dark magic, especially the necrotic curse in Duskwood. A lot of evidence points to that period."

Matthias lowered his voice. "And Dumas... the Mage Quarter has no motive to smear you, Jace. You came from there. If you had problems, the Wizard Sanctum's reputation would be ruined long before King Varian was questioned by the entire Alliance."

Jace shook his head. "What if there are black dragons or succubi disguised as Wizard Sanctum members? That situation is hard to judge by motive alone."

"Then they'd need a more plausible identity to hide behind." Matthias said. "The Kirin Tor is obviously a better fit than the Wizard Sanctum—especially given that your relationship with the Kirin Tor is already tense enough."

"True." Jace asked, "Any recent news on Kel'Thuzad? Is he still alive?"

Matthias said, "Mages like him tend to live a long time. Even in the Violet Hold, he'll probably still be kicking long after I've died of old age. Are you suspicious of him? He certainly has motive, but on one hand, he's under strict watch in a magical cage, cut off from the outside world. Even if someone could pass him messages, coordination would be difficult."

He paused, head lowered. "What about his students? Antonidas launched a large-scale investigation into him that's still ongoing, and their necromancy investigation is far more professional than ours. His students should be keeping a low profile during this sensitive period, not taking risks by manufacturing rumors—especially rumors connected to necromancy."

Jace rubbed his forehead, thinking. Suddenly he remembered the Kirin Tor member he'd met before heading to Shadowforge City—the one who'd delivered soul fragments for Vargus.

That was Vargus's apprentice, Nabrius. In another timeline, he was the one who transformed into a lich in Kirin'Var Village in Netherstorm using a mysterious necromantic ritual.

Could he be involved?

Jace didn't like the feeling of suspecting someone for crimes they hadn't committed yet. Moradin, the nightmare of Duskwood, was a simple priest faithfully serving the Holy Light in this world, doing everything he could to care for his wife and daughter in Stormwind City, and had even saved Jace's life at the cost of his own. Levixus the Soulflayer, the schemer of Auchindoun, was here a kind Auchenai priest trying to help the Broken of Shattrath City regain their standing, and had even spread Jace's good name to many draenei.

But at the same time, he couldn't just dismiss the suspicion. As the saying went, Dar'khan was still Dar'khan, and Kel'Thuzad was no better.

"What about Archmage Vargus?" Jace asked.

"He secured a seat on the Council of Six. Ansirem Runeweaver chose to drop out of the competition." Matthias replied. "Though even if he hadn't, Runeweaver's odds weren't great. Master Vargus won a lot of support thanks to his legendary experiences in Draenor. Do you suspect Vargus? He was indeed staying in the Mage Quarter during that period with many of those who returned from Kirin'Var Village."

"A little, especially his students." Jace said. "The Blackrock Spire offensive is imminent. I can't go to Lordaeron. I'll discuss this with Master Khadgar—he'll help you."

"I'll keep an eye on him," Matthias said.
