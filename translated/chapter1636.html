Chapter 1636: Next, Blackrock Spire

These days, Jace no longer had the privilege of quietly enjoying a drink at the Blue Heron. Even walking in to discreetly buy a loaf of bread at noon got him recognized by plenty of people. He grabbed a simple lunch of bread in the garden. In the afternoon, when he saw Archmage Marin returning to his study from the mage tower, he followed him in. "Master Marin." "Mr. Sesso?" Marin looked pleasantly surprised. "I thought you'd already left for Morgan's Post. Come in." "I'm planning to head out in a couple of days, Master," Jace said, following the archmage into his study. "I came to say goodbye." "Goodbye?" Marin said. "It's not really goodbye." "I'll be heading to the Storm Altar to take part in the assault on Blackrock Spire too, Mr. Sesso. I may be planning to retire, but I haven't retired yet. Besides, I need to put a capstone on the Wizard Sanctum and the Kirin Tor's ten years of research into Black Dragons." "So we'll fight side by side again," Jace said. "My honor." Marin smiled. The archmage hung his hat on the rack and turned to look Jace up and down. "You don't look like someone who just crawled out of the Molten Core..." "So you've heard about the Molten Core," Jace said. "The Kirin Tor and the Wizard Sanctum have been investigating Shadowforge City for a long time. The last three faces in the portraits at the top of the tower — and now you too — have all had some contact with the Dark Iron dwarves." Marin arranged the books from his arm onto the shelf. "So we've been keeping a fairly close eye on things over there." He continued, "So when I heard a few days ago that the Three Hammers had truly reunited to face a common enemy... the shock it gave me and my colleagues is hard to describe. My teacher, Trelane, would have been overjoyed." "As for the practical side — once Amy becomes an archmage, I plan to leave my teaching post and travel the mountains of Khaz Modan and Arathi to clear my head. That's the retirement plan I mentioned. A lot of the mountain passes were controlled by Dagran Thaurissan's men before. Taking a leisurely trip like that wouldn't have been simple." "Amy is going for the title of archmage?" Jace asked. "Yes," Marin said. "In Dalaran, earning that title before forty is no small feat. The last person to become an archmage in their teens like Khadgar is the stuff of history books. But I have faith in her. She's the most gifted mage I've ever seen in frost magic." "I couldn't agree more." Jace said. "You might not know this, Master, but Amy's help was incredibly — maybe even critically — important in our conquest of the Molten Core. She used water magic to contact Duke Hydraxis of the Abyssal Lords, and through that, we obtained what we needed to break through the Molten Core's planar defenses. Didn't she mention it?" "So that's the help she was talking about..." Marin paused. "She didn't tell me she could do something like that now." "The Kirin Tor will find out soon enough. It might even give her a big boost toward earning the archmage title," Jace said. "I hope so." Marin could barely hide his excitement. He sat behind his desk and let out a long breath. "Archmage Vargus has won Dalaran's recognition and joined the Council of Six. He came to me specifically to express his gratitude to Amy for her role in rescuing the Sons of Lothar. With his support, Amy's chances are looking much better." Though Marin, as one of the leaders of the Wizard Sanctum, had never exactly hidden his distaste for all things Kirin Tor, Dalaran was still the magical jewel of the Seven Kingdoms. Even Marin couldn't help feeling proud knowing his daughter might finally be recognized by that place. But before the Molten Core, hearing this news might have made Jace as purely happy for Amy as Marin was. Now, after SI:7's string of suspicions, hearing the name "Archmage Vargus" made something catch in his throat. "Master, you must have heard about the Black Knight rumors in the north too." Jace said. "Amy and I are good friends — a lot of people have known that since Draenor. If you're writing back to her, Master, tell her I'm fine and not to worry. I don't want my problems to drag her into anything." "You should write to her yourself, Mr. Sesso." Marin pressed his hand on a sheet of paper. "Nothing would reassure her more than a letter in your own hand." "Well... all right." Jace nodded. After finishing the letter in Marin's study and handing it to his teacher, Jace felt a bit lighter as he stepped out. Marin was right — hesitating and overthinking everything would just trap him. He'd be better off facing it all openly, as long as his conscience was clear. "Your teacher's clean too, master." Morophil materialized beside him. "His study is spotless." Jace grunted in acknowledgment. Truth be told, by the time they finished talking, he'd completely forgotten about having Morophil scan his teacher. --- At the appointed time the next morning, Jace arrived at the alchemy lab. Ursula had already packed all the potion bottles, large and small. "Restorative Potion, Invisibility Potion, Shadow Protection Potion, Elixir of Greater Agility, Elixir of the Mongoose, Multi-Resistance Potion..." Ursula's finger swept across the bundled bottles before stopping at the four small ones in back. "And the most important — Destruction Potion." "I used some improved Dalaran formulas for the Invisibility Potion. The old version — once you entered the magical layer of invisibility, you couldn't see anything clearly except ghosts. Now at least you can detect living creatures in front of you through fluctuations in arcane energy." Ursula said. "I packed you a few extra bottles. Remember to test them before you actually need them — get used to the new potion's vision." "Got it," Jace said. "And don't use the Destruction Potion lightly," Ursula said. "Voidbloom isn't easy to cultivate. These four bottles are all we have right now. It'll take weeks to gather enough for another batch... by then it'll be too late to resupply you." "And keep them protected. Don't smash them all before you even reach the front lines. Even without the Destruction Potions, that lot costs several dozen gold. At that price, someone in the Eastern Weald would take a contract to assassinate Rend Blackhand in Blackrock Spire." "Give Grede Thunderfist a single gold coin and he'd assault Blackrock Spire. Anyone can brag." Jace snorted. "Be serious!" Ursula glared. "I know, I know." Jace cleared his throat and bent down to pretend to check the bottle count. "You're going after Black Dragons." Ursula asked. "Do you need any Fire or Shadow Protection Potions? I've been working on your order the last couple days — didn't have time to brew the various protection potions, or healing and mana potions." "The Wizard Sanctum and the king have those covered. Plus Shadowforge City..." Jace tugged the binding rope around the bottles. "No need to trouble yourself." "Shadowforge City, the Wizard Sanctum, the Kirin Tor..." Ursula tilted her head, squinting. "You sure have a lot of friends these days. And you know more high-level alchemists." "Huh?" "Not really," Jace said. "I haven't met anyone better than you." "When you get back from Blackrock Spire, come sign a contract with me." Ursula said. "Let Arkhana of the Mystic Star Alchemy become Court Mage Jace Sesso's personal apothecary. I need to seize this chance to make a name for myself." "Aren't you already?" Jace asked. "Nothing's in writing — who can say?" Ursula said. "I'll have to think it over," Jace said. "What's there to think about?" Ursula lowered her voice. "Some idiots think I'm full of it when I say I know Jace Sesso! Why don't you just leave me your Wizard Sanctum apprentice ring? It's not like you're using it for anything else." "Who says it's useless?" Jace opened his eyes wide. "Talk to me when I'm back from Blackrock Spire. I'm off — Khadgar's waiting for me to deliver the Destruction Potion in Lakeshire." Ursula nodded. Just as Jace was about to slip into the corridor, she called out again. "Hey, Jace." She hesitated for a moment before speaking. "I don't know how to say this, but... be careful. Whatever decisions you make, be cautious. Maybe it's from manipulating too much shadow fire over these past couple days — I've been hit with severe anxiety that even the potions can't fix. Just humor me and say you'll be careful, even if it's just to set my mind at ease." "I'll be careful," Jace said. "Good. That's all I needed." The alchemist rubbed her eyes hard. "Good luck."
