Chapter 1649: The Broodmother's Egg?

The passage near the broken section had been squeezed nearly out of shape by the mountain's shifting. It looked like it could collapse at any moment. If this were any other building, Jace would have said there was no point bothering to attack it. One good kick and it would probably fall down. But Blackrock Spire was nothing like that. The mountain itself supported the tower's main structure. Even if the lower section were cut in half, it would not collapse entirely. "I thought coming here meant I'd have to turn into an orc like you," Vereesa said, following Vaelastrasz. "I'd already braced myself for it." "If I could, I'd turn you into orcs too. It'd make moving around Blackrock Spire much easier." Vaelastrasz jumped onto a broken step. "But turning into an orc would not help much. There's still the risk of sudden exposure. Better to sneak in quietly. The place we're going, even orcs can't enter." "The Alliance army and the demons Jace summoned have the Horde forces below in chaos. They're preparing for a life-or-death battle. No one's going to look up. Just follow me. Your Lady Morofil is scouting ahead. There won't be any enemy patrols on this path." At "Your Lady Morofil," Vereesa shot a displeased sideways glance at Jace. Jace swallowed. "How do we free the whelps?" Vaelastrasz turned back. "I originally thought Nefarian and Onyxia were gathering whelps and young dragons from all over the world to experiment on their blood for chromatic dragons. They built a device long ago with the goblins' help that periodically releases toxic fumes to suppress the whelps' will to resist." "But those whelps aren't just suppressed — they flew out to attack the Alliance army," Vereesa said. "Exactly." Vaelastrasz said. "I only recently realized Nefarian has found a way to control those whelps' minds. Normally, once any chromatic whelp escaped the toxic fumes, it would try to flee immediately. If released from Blackrock Spire, they should fly straight for Grim Batol or further north." "Nefarian might be able to mentally dominate a few young dragons and whelps with shadow magic, but not dozens at once. So there's something wrong here. Combined with the necrotic plague..." "Is it demon magic?" Jace asked. Vaelastrasz glanced back at the warlock and shook his head. "No. It has to do with the black broodmother, Sintharia. Or, as you might know her better — Lady Sinestra." "I'm not familiar with either name," Needleflame said. "You'd better get familiar, because she is technically your mother." Vaelastrasz stared at her. "I told you, my father is Prince Sabellian!" Needleflame emphasized. "Fine, fine," the red dragon said impatiently. "Wait — you mean Sintharia is in the Spire?" Jace asked. "No. She wouldn't come to Blackrock Spire." Vaelastrasz said. "But Nefarian has one of Sintharia's treasures. It might have been left to him by Deathwing, or brought by Onyxia. Either way, it's an artifact that commands all whelps, whether or not they belong to the Blackwing brood. I heard about it from the lair's guardian, the mad Razorgore. Later, I found the thing in the deepest part of Hordemar — a shadow egg of the broodmother." A shadow egg of the broodmother? Jace had never heard of such a thing in the game. He did not remember any mechanic related to it in Blackwing Lair. In the game, the chromatic whelps were indeed suppressed by a device that sprayed a slowing mist, and they attacked players without mercy. But what did it have to do with this "shadow egg"? Why had Nefarian not used it in the game? Either way, this timeline was years before the Alliance and Horde would normally breach Blackrock Spire. Who knew what would happen in between? Right now, there were far more whelps in Blackrock Spire than in the game. And from what Vaelastrasz said, there were even young dragons of other colors in here? Maybe, in the original timeline, Vaelastrasz and other mercenaries who infiltrated Blackrock Spire had already destroyed the shadow egg, hindering Nefarian's chromatic dragon project. Since Jace's arrival had pushed the assault on Blackrock Spire years ahead, they would have to take care of this early too. "What is Hordemar?" Vereesa asked. "The Horde's garrison." Vaelastrasz explained. "Right beneath our feet. The orcs named their camp with some orcish word, and I've gotten used to calling it that. The corrupted egg is guarded by the dragonkin commander, Vim'salak. Taking him down won't be a problem — he's a strong and skilled warrior, but no match for the lot of you. The hard part is getting into that heavily guarded place without being noticed. Even orcs aren't allowed near it." "I'm worried that if they realize we're going to destroy that artifact, they'll move it somewhere else immediately. Let alone the fact it can command those whelps — when I saw it, I felt like I was back in the highland brood nest. So we need to destroy it quickly. Once it's gone, the imprisoned dragons of other colors inside Blackrock Spire won't just stop blocking the Alliance — they'll help us fight the Horde and the black dragons." Vaelastrasz led the three to a broken window and looked down. Below was a mess of rubble, broken tiles, and ancient ruins. Between the high walls, dim torchlight flickered in the black, deep gutters, illuminating dark red orc hide tents and damaged wooden crates. Crowds of orc soldiers hurried along narrow pathways piled with broken weapons. Harsh orcish and ogre shouts, too indistinct to make out, echoed back and forth. It fit Jace's stereotyped expectation of "every other race's city after the orcs occupied it." Another heavy shockwave came from outside, shaking everyone. Jace looked back the way they had come. "Sounds like Dagran Thaurissan's Dragon Breath Cannon fired another shot." Vaelastrasz blinked. "What?" "Red Dragon... Cannon," Jace replied. Needleflance gestured vaguely. "You stuff a fireball into its butt and it shoots out of its mouth. It's impressive." Vaelastrasz frowned and opened his mouth, but before he could ask anything or offer an opinion, Vereesa quickly waved her hand. "It just looks vaguely like that. Not as specific as she described, Vaelastrasz." The red dragon gave the young black dragon a skeptical look, then sighed. "Enough of that. The Alliance is about to breach the main gate. We need to hurry. Look down there — you see that bridge?" "Yes," Jace said. "North of the bridge is the ogre base camp." Vaelastrasz said. "After we destroy the shadow egg and while Hordemar is in chaos, we'll come back here and take out the Spirestone 'King.'" "You've figured it right. Even though the ogres obey Nefarian and Rend Blackhand, they still get to pick their own king. Even the Blackwing Prince and the Horde's Warchief have to accept that title. Kill that king, and the ogres will need time to recover from chaos and re-establish communication with the upper Spire. That buys your coalition enough time to wipe out their resistance." "Where is this shadow egg?" Vereesa asked. Vaelastrasz pointed toward a pile of collapsed rubble to the southeast. "Over there. Molten Core's collapse made a mess of the place, but it didn't destroy Vim'salak's territory. You'll see when we get there." Everyone nodded and continued along the wall. Jace followed the red dragon and asked, "Did you find out where Anduin Lothar's body is?" "Lord Lothar's body?" Vaelastrasz stopped to think. "Yes. I heard the orcs brought the bodies from the Burning Steppes into the upper Spire when they retreated. Do they include the former Alliance commander's remains?" "They were taken to the upper Spire?" Jace said. "I can't be a hundred percent sure. Didn't you check?" "Nefarian knows you're attacking Blackrock Spire." Vaelastrasz said. "He's sealed the entire top of the Spire with his dragon claw seal — what they call Blackwing Hall — to protect his precious eggs and chromatic dragon research, slowing the Alliance's advance." "I wanted to risk staying inside longer to investigate the necrotic and shadow magic. But I knew I couldn't break his dragon claw seal. Staying inside would be suicide. Nefarian would see through me sooner or later. So I slipped out early. At least I got the information about the shadow egg." "If even you can't break that seal, how will the Alliance?" Vereesa said. "The Dark Iron cannon can barely break the magical seals the orcs set up. I don't know if the Kirin Tor can handle it." "You have Onyxia's blood, Lady Vereesa!" Vaelastrasz lowered his voice. "The broodmother's blood can dissolve the dragon claw seal that blocks intruders. That's why I was never worried about you breaking into Blackrock Spire! Don't tell me you didn't bring it." "Ah, it's with Grede," Vereesa said. "Perfect." Vaelastrasz let out a breath. "Now let the Alliance army do their job, and we'll do ours. Nefarian won't escape this time. We just need to make sure this victory doesn't come at too heavy a cost."
