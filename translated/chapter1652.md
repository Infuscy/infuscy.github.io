## Chapter 1652: Source of Power?

As the black dragon passed, the dragonkin on patrol and guard duty bowed their heads in deference. Needleflame spread her wings, and the faint shadows cast by the braziers' light passed over these dragon descendants. None dared meet the young "mistress's" eyes.

Needleflame kept her gaze fixed on the banner-crowned gap ahead, descending slowly, not sparing them a single glance.

Even if she had never been treated as a "black dragon princess" growing up, Needleflame had still been born from Deathwing's lair and raised by an arrogant dragon prince like Sabellian.

When she got serious, her air clearly set her apart from those feral whelps rampaging between Tirisfal and Hillsbrad.

With everyone's attention drawn to Needleflame, Vaelastrasz, Jace, and Vereesa could move along the edges of the high stone walls and close in.

Needleflame was fast. They needed to keep up. And from the growing clamor in the distance, it sounded like the Dark Iron dwarves had already breached Blackrock Spire's gate. The Alliance might have already pushed inside. They had to hurry.

"You should return to Blackwing Hall, young mistress!" one of the gate-guarding dragonkin called out in accented draconic, head bowed. "The Prince has already issued a recall order, demanding all Blackwing progeny enter the Dragon Claw Seal as soon as possible. It may already be too late, but I believe General Drakkisath can make special arrangements for your arrival."

"They sense Needleflame isn't an ordinary whelp," Vaelastrasz murmured. "Dragonkin are extremely sensitive to rank among dragonkind. They know she is, by blood, Nefarian's sister."

"But isn't Needleflame Sabellian's daughter?" Vereesa whispered. "And Sabellian is Nefarian's brother."

"Those aren't mutually exclusive." Vaelastrasz looked at the ranger. "Needleflame simply refuses to acknowledge her bloodline. Prince Sabellian must despise his father greatly — that's why all the progeny he raised are determined to cut ties with Deathwing."

Not mutually exclusive? Vereesa mouthed silently and glanced at Jace. Jace could only shake his head helplessly. Discussing family ethics with dragons was asking for trouble.

"I need to see Vim'salak," Needleflame said in Draconic.

The dragonkin guard hesitated. "But General Vim'salak..."

"Silence!" Needleflame's command was almost a roar. Every dragonkin present flinched.

"I bring news of the human warlock Jace Sesso. It is vital to the Horde's defense! Before I see Prince Nefarian, Vim'salak must know. Open the curtain! Have him come out to meet me!"

"As you command, young mistress."

The dragonkin guard did not seem suspicious. He waved at a red-robed dragonkin mage behind him and said something too quiet to hear. The mage grunted and waddled toward the gap of shadow.

"It worked." Vereesa narrowed her eyes and raised her longbow. The flowers and grass on it were beginning to open slightly, ready to guide the Dreamshot arrows.

The dragonkin mage raised a hand and chanted something. The shadow curtain dimmed momentarily, and the shadow energy leaking from within grew even stronger.

Jace watched, feeling like he was standing on a rocky beach facing a surging storm tide. But this surge was not seawater — it was layers of deep regret and despair. Very different from the Shadowflame other black dragons breathed.

Then, a dragonkin commander far larger than the surrounding guards stepped out of the gap. His ornate armor, trimmed with gold and covered in crimson runic script, scraped against the stone walls. The black-brown scales visible through the gaps in his armor barely seemed to fit through the opening.

Though he was a dragonkin, his bulk nearly matched Needleflame hovering in mid-air. The blood-red spear in his hand was long enough that a single thrust forward would pierce her heart.

This was Vim'salak, no question. He looked thinner than his game counterpart, but no less imposing.

"You say you bring news of the human warlock Jace Sesso?" The dragonkin commander raised his spear tip. "I have never seen you before, little one. Not in Lordaeron, and not in Blackrock Spire. But the blood of the Prince flows in your veins. It's not hard to guess who sent you."

"Tell me — what is your relationship with Onyxia?"

"What?" Vaelastrasz was thrown by Vim'salak's reaction. Jace and Vereesa froze too.

They had considered that Needleflame might be exposed. But they never expected Vim'salak to suspect she was connected to Onyxia — and that he would be so hostile about it.

"Princess Onyxia?" Even Needleflame did not know how to respond. But before she could say anything else, Vim'salak did not hesitate. He raised his spear. "Kill her!"

"Yes!" the dragonkin shouted in unison.

One dragonkin guard hurled an enchanted net. Needleflame stopped pretending and spat a mouthful of molten fire back at it. But the blast that would have charred trolls and orcs could not damage the net — it only triggered layers of magical reaction shimmering across its surface.

After her breath attack, Needleflame tried to fly higher to dodge, but it was too late. The net caught her head and front claws, hooked one wing, and she lost her hover, tumbling down. The net seemed designed specifically to catch black dragons.

The surrounding dragonkin closed in on Needleflame. But several arrows flew down from above, coldly piercing the heads and bodies of several of them. Vim'salak roared and looked up toward the arrows' origin. One arrow struck his armored lower body and punched straight through as if the armor was not even there.

"Intruders have entered Hordemar!"

The dragonkin commander bellowed. Hot dragon blood sprayed from the hole melted through his armor. A burst of flame rolled up in front of him. Vaelastrasz stepped out of it, raising his ruby staff and slamming it into Vim'salak's helmet. The massive dragonkin commander staggered and fell to his knees from the blow.

"What about Onyxia? Confess what you're hiding, miserable slave!" the red dragon roared.

"Close the curtain! Maintain the shadow channel!" Vim'salak shouted with his last strength. Then he curled his tail, swept up his spear, and drove it through his own skull without a moment's hesitation. Even Vaelastrasz was startled.

"Don't think you can frighten me. I will serve the Prince forev—"

The dragonkin only got half the sentence out before his eyes rolled back and he died. Vaelastrasz cursed and looked toward the gap. Two dragonkin mages were already rushing to reseal the passage with magic as Vim'salak had ordered.

But they were not the only ones moving. While Vereesa's arrows had killed several dragonkin and suppressed the rest, Jace had already slipped to the gap using Shadowstep.

The two dragonkin mages had just begun to chant the curtain spell when one of them found his throat crushed by a spell of suffocation, unable to make a sound. The ritual faltered.

Seizing the moment as the shadow energy went out of control, Jace quickly channeled his emotions — regret and despair — and forcibly dismantled the half-raised shadow energy from the other mage's grasp.

Vaelastrasz followed. As he ran, he summoned a chain of fire from his palm that severed the upper body of the second dragonkin mage in a single blow. Five or six dragonkin guards rushed out of the gap to stop the red dragon, but they were met by a barrage of Dreamshot arrows, each one piercing through chests as they fell screaming.

Vereesa reached the gap and drew the Dragon's Call, easily cutting through the enchanted net binding the young black dragon. Those nets could not withstand the green dragonflight's dream power, which only confirmed they had been made specifically to hunt black dragons.

What was going on? It sounded like Onyxia was not only absent from Blackrock Spire but was seen as an enemy by Nefarian and his servants. What had happened after she caused chaos in Stormwind?

Jace knew Vaelastrasz, Vereesa, and Needleflame all had the same question. But now was not the time.

They charged into Vim'salak's hideout. Looking up, they saw a magical ritual maintained by three dragonkin mages. At the center, on a dragon claw altar, sat a black-purple dragon egg.

More dragonkin were converging from all sides, but these were lower-ranking attendants, far weaker than the guards they had just killed. Vereesa and Vaelastrasz handled them easily, shaking even the last mages maintaining the egg's power.

One mage tried to flee. Vereesa shot him dead with an arrow. The other two seemed to want to hold out, but their fate was no different. If the guards' armor and scales could not stop the dream power Belanur summoned, these much frailer mages stood no chance.

But what none of them expected was that, once the mages stopped channeling, the dragon egg, which had been brimming with black-purple energy, suddenly lost its color. The shadow energy quickly peeled away from the bumpy, reddish-brown horned surface of the egg.

The egg was indeed larger than most black dragon eggs Jace had seen. But compared to when the mages were channeling the ritual, it suddenly dimmed and weakened. That surging shadow power from moments ago quickly dissipated and dispersed.

Vaelastrasz was the first to transform into a streak of flame and reach the egg. He raised his staff and shattered the shell. A corrosive fluid filled with blood stench flowed out — but nothing more.

From the heights of Hordemar came the screeches of whelps. The red dragon looked up. "The whelps will soon realize they were being protected by a false and evil 'mother's love.' They'll understand who their claws and fangs should be pointed at."

"But this egg is not the source of the shadow, Vaelastrasz." Vereesa said. "These dragonkin mages weren't using the egg to cast spells. They were channeling shadow power from elsewhere in Blackrock Spire into it, making it appear as a broodmother's egg, tricking all the whelps and young dragons here."

Vaelastrasz did not speak. He just looked at the ranger. But Jace knew he had probably figured that out too.
