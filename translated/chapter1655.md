## Chapter 1655: Ancient Fragment

One of Khadgar's attendants left Blackrock Spire to contact the Draenei mage. The search and clearing operation inside the tower continued.

Varian walked over to Jace and the others. "The Silver Hand paladins captured a few warlocks from the Firebrand Legion. They say that to enter the upper Spire, we need something called the Seal of Ascension."

"After Nefarian learned the Alliance was attacking Blackrock Spire, he ordered the dragonkin to abandon the Seals," Vaelastrasz said. "In the past, every high-ranking officer in every legion had one, let alone the dragonkin. But now they're all trapped down here with us. The only thing that can open the passage seal now is the blood of Nefarian or his siblings."

"Stormwind still has some of Onyxia's blood," Varian said.

"Grede is carrying an artifact made from her blood, Varian," Jace said. "We brought it to use its magic against the fire elementals. Never thought it'd come in handy here. He'll be along soon. We'll give the Blackhand legion a surprise."

"But if there's been a rift between Nefarian and Onyxia," Vereesa said, "could Onyxia's blood fail to break Nefarian's seal?"

Jace and Vaelastrasz both turned to look at Vereesa, then exchanged glances. They had been discussing the possible reasons for the black dragon siblings' falling out, but had indeed overlooked this point.

"What's this about the two black dragons turning on each other?" Khadgar asked.

The red dragon explained what they had witnessed with Vim'salak. Khadgar frowned. "No matter. A lock is made to be opened. With Dagran Thaurissan's Dragon Breath Cannon saving the Kirin Tor a great deal of effort, we'll put it to good use."

"Remember, I crippled Deathwing with a simple trick back in Draenor. Using a similar spell to overturn his son's lair might just become a tale worth spreading across the Seven Kingdoms."

"You crippled Deathwing with a simple trick, Khadgar?" Vaelastrasz seemed utterly incredulous.

"More or less," Khadgar said, crossing his arms.

Alleria, standing by the cliff edge watching the street below, turned back with a smile. "If you ignore the part where his eyes were bloodshot, his hands shook for days, and he was screaming hysterically, sure, 'more or less' works."

"Minor details don't affect the epic grandeur of the story, Ranger-Captain." Khadgar raised a finger.

Just then, a Draenei mage hurried through the gate, fanning away the lingering cannon smoke. Even from a distance, Jace could feel his heavy heart. As he drew closer, his expression was grim.

Seeing the mage's mood, Jace felt he had already guessed the truth. A chill crept up his spine.

"Master Semid," Khadgar said. "Jace thinks you might be able to help us figure out what's so unusual about this tower."

"I heard from the orcs back in Draenor that when your ship arrived, it brought a treasure called the Ata'mal Crystal," Jace said.

"Strictly speaking, not a single Ata'mal Crystal." Semid said. "The complete crystal was shattered into several pieces by Kil'jaeden the Deceiver's magic over ten thousand years ago. But it was an ancient treasure bestowed upon Argus by the Naaru, containing extraordinarily pure power. Even shattered, each piece held its own unique strength, and they have given us irreplaceable aid ever since."

Semid looked up, his expression heavy. He sighed. "You're asking whether the power in this tower comes from an Ata'mal Crystal."

"Yes," Jace said.

"It feels very similar to what I remember." Semid closed his eyes. "We once guarded these fragments. But several were lost during the Orc War. Including one held even by the Prophet himself — the Soul Fragment."

That was the name.

The Soul Fragment was the darkest and most somber of the seven shards. Deep purple all over, staring into it was like staring into the Twisting Nether. Its power could amplify the wielder's consciousness and will to an absurd degree — something every spellcaster dreamed of.

Countless travelers in the Twisting Nether had sought it. In another timeline, Kael'thas Sunstrider, stationed in Farahlon, had planned to claim it for himself upon hearing of it, even hiring ethereals to help.

And the one rumored to hold the Soul Fragment was a demon lord — more precisely, a Nathrezim: Lord Kurthas. This demon believed he could rise above the Burning Legion with its power.

Though, in the game's timeline years later, he was slain by adventurers hired by the ethereals, and the fragment was recovered. But from how Nexus-Prince Haramad, Shattrath City, and the Naaru leader A'dal treated it, the thing's power was no joke.

Kurthas could use it to occupy a floating island in Netherstorm, answerable to neither the Burning Legion nor Illidan. So what about Nefarian?

More importantly, Jace really did not like connecting another clue to a dreadlord again, especially after the string of guesses and misunderstandings.

But the less he wanted it, the more things seemed to slide in that direction.

"How was the Soul Fragment lost?" he asked.

Master Semid shook his head. "When Draenor was torn apart by the Scepter of Sargeras, it was cataclysm. So many painful memories we'd rather not recall happened then, not to mention the ever-increasing demons that followed."

"I was at Prophet Velen's side the whole time. Perhaps it was lost in the chaos of the escape, or perhaps a stealthy demon stole it while we fought off the Burning Legion's encirclement. But the Soul Fragment was simply gone. In those days, many of my kin saw it as a clear sign that the Naaru had abandoned us entirely. The Aldor viewed it as proof that the Prophet had lost his leadership and could no longer represent the Naaru's will."

"We tried to find it, to use against the Burning Legion's demons." Khadgar turned to Jace. "But we found nothing. Do you think the Soul Fragment came to Azeroth, and fell into Nefarian's hands?"

"I admit, the energy here feels very much like the lost fragment." Semid conjured a ball of shadow energy in his palm, then flicked it apart. "But how did it come here from the Twisting Nether?"

"No one can answer that, Master." Khadgar said. "But if it really is the Soul Fragment, I can understand why Nefarian is so confident he can hold off the Alliance."

"I must inform the Prophet of this as soon as possible, Master Khadgar," the Draenei said respectfully.

"Of course," Khadgar said.

Jace realized he was almost hoping the thing in Nefarian's hands was actually Xal'atath instead. Even though that dark blade had spawned countless terrifying legends throughout history, at least in its current state, it was not strong enough to stop an entire Alliance army.

But if this truly was the Soul Fragment, it meant a great many things.

In the other timeline, no one knew how Kurthas found the Soul Fragment on Draenor. But from this Draenei's account, it might have been lost when Draenor was destroyed and Velen lost the shard, only to have Kurthas find it later in the Twisting Nether.

The soul whispers from orc warlocks in the mass grave. The spreading Black Knight rumors. The shadow of the dreadlords. And now this Ata'mal Crystal. Jace felt like pieces that had seemed unrelated were being pushed closer and closer together.

Had the Lich King Ner'zhul persuaded Kurthas, or had some demon brought the Soul Fragment to Nefarian?

"Don't worry too much, Jace." Khadgar said. "The Draenei lost several Ata'mal fragments. I know the orcs held two. One was called the Heart of Rage, which gave its wielder and followers unstoppable strength."

"But so what? The Draenei had all seven and still could not defeat the orcs and the Burning Legion. The orcs could not use that Heart of Rage to stop the Expeditionary Force either. They could not even hold their last stronghold at Karabor. Ner'zhul was forced to use the Scepter of Sargeras and the artifacts Deathwing stole from Dalaran to try to destroy everything with us. And now, even the Scepter of Sargeras has been destroyed by you."

Jace nodded. Khadgar was right. This crystal would not stop the Alliance. Even it could not save the crumbling Blackrock Spire.
