## Chapter 1656: The Dragon Blood Seal

To reach the entrance to the upper Spire from the lower section, one had to cross a wide bridge. The orcs had converted the bridge into living quarters, littered with hide tents and refuse. Urine, sweat, and blood stench had long since soaked into the whole space.

At the end of the bridge stood a massive, heavy stone door. Unlit braziers lined the steps before it. Jace recognized this spot — it was the entrance to the upper Spire from the game.

But in reality, it was far larger than in the game. The chamber before the door and the bridge itself were far more imposing. What he remembered as a passage for maybe four or five players could now accommodate fifteen or twenty people walking side by side. The overall impression was similar to the magnificent Dark Iron architecture below, to the point where Jace had crossed half the bridge before recalling the in-game counterpart.

After a brief rest, the Alliance regrouped before the door. The finest vanguards of the Silver Hand, Stromgarde, and Stormwind had gathered before the steps.

Occasional strange roars came from the upper Spire. Those were not orcs or ogres — ogres were not even allowed in the upper Spire. Those were the roars of Nefarian's children, who had surely been waiting for them.

"How do I use this thing to open the door?" Grede asked Vaelastrasz beside him. "Is there a spell, a password, a ritual?"

"No need, Lord Grede Thunderfist." Vaelastrasz said. "Just bring it to the door."

Grede climbed the steps, surrounded by many dwarven guards. Vereesa raised her bow, ready to loose an arrow the moment the door opened. Jace gripped his staff. He knew Grede was nowhere near the condition he had been in before entering Molten Core. If a group of elite dragonkin warriors burst out the moment the door opened, the dwarf could easily be hurt.

As the dwarf climbed the steps holding Onyxia's blood pendant, each brazier he passed ignited spontaneously, as if lit by the dragon blood itself.

Grede drew closer. Hoarse growls came from behind the door. The air grew hotter. Those behind Grede grew more tense, eyes fixed on the crack of the sealed door.

Varian drew his sword. Galen Trollbane even edged forward a little, standing beside his cousin, as if unwilling to let the younger Southern king outpace him.

Alleria glanced at Vereesa behind her. She, Nathanos, and the Farstriders had long been on edge.

Turalyon glanced down at Jace, weighed his warhammer, and exchanged a nod with the lord of Hearthglen beside him. The holy light sigils on both paladins' armor glowed faintly. Taelan Fordring watched the shimmering light, his eyes full of admiration and longing.

The braziers lit one after another, rising with Grede's steps as he neared the dark gate. Beside Jace, Sora took a deep breath, seeming nervous.

"What's wrong, Sora?" Jace asked.

"Prince Nefarian..." Sora sighed softly. "Never mind me, Jace. Every time I think about fighting my elders, I can't stay calm."

If even Sora felt this way, Vaelastrasz, standing not far away, could hardly be described as calm either. He had been maneuvering against Nefarian's children and servants in Blackrock Spire for years.

And Needleflame was already hiding beside Sora, taking deep breaths to steady her nerves. Dealing with the dragonkin servants in the lower Spire was one thing. Facing her own uncle was entirely different.

"You're also a female dragon, Sora," Jace whispered. "From your perspective, why do you think Onyxia would turn against her brother?"

"There's basically only one reason that would make a broodmother of her standing turn against blood kin, Jace." Sora murmured. "Something involving offspring."

"But Onyxia sent her own eggs to Blackrock Spire for Nefarian to experiment on, to create new dragon breeds. Countless whelps died, countless eggs were broken in the process." Jace said quietly. "She didn't betray him over that. She kept cooperating. I don't think..."

Sora was silent for a moment, then shook her head. "I don't know."

Jace had meant to make small talk and relax Sora. But Sora had answered seriously from her perspective, which only set Jace's mind racing.

By now, Grede had reached the door. All the braziers on the steps were lit. He held Onyxia's blood high. Purple-red dragon scale and claw patterns shimmered across the door's surface. Everyone present held their breath.

But nothing happened. Even the braziers all went out.

A faint "clacking" sound came from behind the door, like some monster or demon snickering.

"What's going on?" Grede's question echoed through the silent hall. Even Vaelastrasz did not know how to answer.

"They're mocking us!" Thaurissan shouted from above. "Victor Nefarius must have known you had Onyxia's blood, so he prepared in advance! Does the Kirin Tor have a way? Don't tell me that while the soldiers were hunting enemies in the orc camp below, you were all lying around sleeping, with no idea how to open this damned magic door!"

"It's not that we have no way at all," Khadgar answered loudly. But his tone lacked the confidence it had held before he and the mages witnessed how this seal had repelled Onyxia's blood.

"Can you feel it?" Vaelastrasz said. "The shadow energy in this seal feels just like the egg below. Maybe it's also powered by the Ata'mal Crystal fragment the Eredar mentioned."

"Another piece of good news," the archmage said dryly.

"If it comes to it, we could use that tablet," Jace said quietly. "The chromatic dragon blood tablet Master Krasus replicated. It could break Onyxia's magic, so it should break this seal too."

"Better save it for when we actually face Nefarian himself, Jace. Let's not expose and waste our secret weapon too early." Khadgar whispered. "And the Kirin Tor is no stranger to breaking black dragon seals. Master Krasus gained plenty of valuable experience during his two years dealing with Lord Daval Prestor."

"I... I've seen my father use a similar seal with the Scepter of Sargeras. To protect us and the eggs from demons."

Needleflame's faint voice came from behind. Everyone turned to look at the small figure.

"That's not surprising," Khadgar said. "Your father is Nefarian's brother."

Needleflame continued. "After Father was tricked into imprisonment, Lord Gelloril took over guarding the brood. He..."

"Gelloril?" Vaelastrasz frowned. "That windbag is still alive?"

"He helped us a great deal in leaving Draenor," Jace said. "Without him, the black dragons would never have cooperated with the Expedition from the start."

"That's not the point, Vieran." Khadgar stared at Needleflame. "How did he...?"

"He told us how to break the seal." Needleflame said. "He said he couldn't do it himself. Only father and the clutch of whelps born at the same time as us could."

Khadgar immediately raised his hand and shouted at the dwarves crowding the stairs. "Move! Move! Let the little one try!"

Needleflame walked up the steps. The dwarves watched her and murmured among themselves. "That's the black dragon who carried Lord Grede Thunderfist toward the Firelord!"

"She can turn into a human, like the old dragons from the legends. But she looks like she's still just a..."

"She betrayed her elders to help us..."

"Nefarian is the one who betrayed the black dragons!" Jace shouted. "Stay quiet and don't disturb her ritual, warriors of the Three Hammers!"

The dwarves fell silent. The young black dragon glanced back at Jace and quickened her pace, trotting to the top. But this time, the braziers on either side did not even light. Khadgar and Jace's expressions did not relax — this did not look like a good sign.

Needleflame pulled out her molten dagger, muttered something, and cut a gash in her wrist. Every brazier suddenly flared to life again, startling everyone on the steps.

She did not flinch. She sheathed the dagger, cupped the flowing red blood, lifted it, and spat a bright flame tinged with black smoke. When the flame struck the heavenly gate, the dragon scale and claw runes flared up, then hissed and dimmed from the center outward like magma plunging into seawater.

The great door groaned, then slowly swung open. Several heavily armored dragonkin inside turned around in shock.

"The door is open! Charge!"

Whether it was Galen, Taelan, or Varian who shouted, the Silver Hand knights reacted in an instant. They roared and threw themselves through the doorway, hammering the dragonkin to the ground in a few blows. Needleflame and Grede were swallowed up by the gleaming white armor. The enemies inside seemed completely taken by surprise — orders and war cries rang out in panic.

Jace raised his staff and followed the soldiers through the door. But he kept thinking about the stunned look on those dragonkin guards' faces. If Nefarian had prepared for Onyxia's blood, why did he not know about Needleflame?

Could it be that he still did not know the Alliance Expedition had brought his brother's daughter back from Draenor?
