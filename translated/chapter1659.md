## Chapter 1659: The Vanguard with Extreme Magic Resistance

"We were searching nearby for Marshal Anduin Lothar's body and the other knights the orcs took, on Lord Turalyon's orders," a priest said. "We soon sensed powerful necrotic energy in the area and found a group of dragonkin. They weren't trying to retreat — they were holding this place desperately. After we defeated them, we discovered this chamber."

"Why?" Needleflame asked. "Why would Prince Nefarian kill his own kind? Weren't these Blackwing dragons loyal to him?"

"Remember how Vim'salak treated you, Needleflame?" Vaelastrasz said. "He sensed Nefarian's blood in you, didn't recognize you, and his first instinct was to kill you. Not even capture and question you. Your uncle has lost his mind. Even his own sister knows this clearly. There's no point trying to understand his actions with reason."

The red dragon pointed at the pile of corpses inside. "I think these are the black dragons who sided with Onyxia in front of Nefarian. Most of them were born from her eggs, so they naturally defended their mother. They couldn't accept a madman who wanted to experiment on them as their father. Nefarian held a grudge and executed them all, including these unhatched eggs!"

"Nefarian is already more insane than his father. Deathwing went to enormous lengths to build a nest in Blade's Edge Mountains, even cooperating with orcs he saw as ants and arrogant, foolish warlocks like Teron Gorefiend — all to protect the precious dragon eggs stolen from Alexstrasza. And Nefarian? I can't imagine how furious Deathwing would be if he were still alive and saw this. He would tear his son apart himself, without any help from us!"

"Why did he abandon the bodies here?" Sora asked. "Maybe Prince Nefarian still has some... sanity left. He couldn't face the children he ordered killed, so he chose to avoid them?"

"I think he planned to leave them here for his dragonkin to use as plague weapons against us," Jace said. "These bodies are heavily corrupted. Even without any tampering, leaving them here wouldn't produce this much concentrated necrotic energy."

"I agree with Jace. All evidence points to that." Vaelastrasz looked at the green dragon. "You still call him 'Prince.' That's an insult to the Blackwing flight and the entire dragonflight, Somaliel. Even when we fought among ourselves in the past, we never sank to this level of madness."

Sora did not answer. She just turned her head away, as if unwilling to look inside any longer.

"Could the knights' bodies be in there, masters?" another priest asked. "We intend to search more thoroughly..."

"I don't think that's worth the effort, brother." Jace said. "The Horde went to great lengths to bring the Alliance heroes' bodies from the Burning Steppes into Blackrock Spire before we attacked. They certainly didn't bring them here to rot with ordinary corpses. Nefarian has other plans for them."

"Master Sesso, Master Vieran!" a Stormwind soldier ran over from another passage. "We found a dragon lair over there full of eggs, and similar... corpse piles. All dragons. Black dragons. Just like here."

"It sounds more and more like ammunition depots." Vaelastrasz's voice was barely containing his fury. "And this is just one level. Who knows what the upper floors hold? Or Nefarian's 'throne room'?"

"How should we handle these bodies?" a priest asked.

"I'll request instructions from King Varian Wrynn first," a paladin said. "For now, use the Holy Light to suppress the spread of the necrotic plague, brother. Then ask the Kirin Tor mages or dwarves to seal the door shut. Don't let the plague leak into the rest of this floor."

"Let me handle it," Vaelastrasz said. "I can purify their bodies with fire. The Blackwing dragons here may be corrupted by darkness, but most of them were still children. I don't want them left to rot in here, eaten by maggots. I'll be quick."

"Leave it to Master Vieran, everyone." Jace also said. "They're about to open the door to the upper level soon. We'll need all the hands we can get. Please head over and support them as fast as you can."

"Yes, Master Sesso." The clergy nodded.

"I'll catch up shortly," Vaelastrasz said.

Jace nodded and led Sora away. As Needleflame left, she could not help taking one last look at the dark, filthy corpse chamber. From the expression on her face as she turned back, she regretted that extra glance.

"Three... two... one... detonate!"

A Dark Iron officer's shrieking command was followed by another explosion from the upward passage, then the crash of falling rubble.

"It's blown! Iron Fury, attack!"

The red-bearded Dark Iron commander spun his battle-axe over his head and charged. The prepared Dark Iron dwarves shoved the Silver Hand knights aside and howled as they poured into the passage.

But before the paladins and the Stormwind and Stromgarde vanguard could follow the dwarves in, the Dark Iron soldiers who had been shouting with bloodlust suddenly fell silent. They came scrambling back out, screaming.

The dark passage, lit only by ruined braziers, suddenly flared brilliantly. Flames surged from deep within the tunnel, blasting every soldier who had not made it out of the entrance flying. They crashed to the ground, hit the walls, and one was even hurled over the railing into the rubble on the level below.

The paladins clustered near the entrance recoiled from the sudden firestorm. The dark tunnel mouth kept breathing flame like a fire-spewing monster. It took a long while before it subsided.

Seeing the fire pause, the bold Taelan Fordring stepped forward to test it. He walked into the passage, took a look, then his expression shifted and he turned to run back out. The fire chased his retreating form and erupted again, forcing the gathered soldiers to back off further.

"What is this?" Varian asked.

"It feels like a black dragonkin's breath, or a dragonkin mage's fire spell, Your Majesty," Sora said.

"Much stronger than the fire breath of the dragonkin we fought earlier," old Fordring said. "It seems we've truly reached the heart of the black dragons' and orcs' territory."

"Can they keep burning like this forever?" Varian asked.

"This is their tower, Your Majesty," Khadgar said. "I won't speculate about how many ways they have to replenish mana. We have no idea how many dragonkin guards capable of breathing fire like this are left at the top of this tower."

"Let me go in." Muradin Bronzebeard walked to the entrance with his axe and warhammer.

"You think you're the only one who can summon an aspect, Muradin?" Dagran Thaurissan called out in Dwarven. "You think I fear these flames? What if you or I charge through, and those conniving dragonkin and orc warlocks capture us with magic? Everyone else is still back there, waiting for us to come back victorious from the flames! It's not like before, when there were dozens of paladins and hundreds of elven rangers surrounding you, ready to break you out any time! We're not here to help you posture!"

The other Bronzebeard dwarves looked like they wanted to agree but also did not want to. Muradin paced at the entrance for a moment and then walked back, seeming reluctant to admit the Dark Iron was right but having no choice.

Needleflame had been eager to go too — she had even drawn her short sword and longsword. But hearing Emperor Thaurissan's words, she thought twice, looked at Sora and Vereesa, and quietly shuffled back.

"These black dragon descendants' fire is not so easily breached," Khadgar said. "Perhaps the Kirin Tor could try using Fire Protection barriers and Blink to break in first... buy a window of time for the follow-up assault."

"Your plan reminds me of the Battle of the Sacred Bastion sixteen hundred years ago, Master Khadgar." Danath said. "You know how well the mages of Stromgarde fared under the trolls' arrow rain."

"But Stromgarde still took the Sacred Bastion, thanks to the mages' sacrifice," Khadgar said.

"I have an idea that might work, and the cost is relatively low," Jace said.

"What is it?" Varian asked.

"Clear the way, everyone up front," Jace said.

As soon as Jace spoke, the scraping of claws on stone echoed from all the surrounding passages and stairs. Before long, packs of felhounds converged on the entrance from every direction.

The paladins and dwarves ahead did not need Jace's reminder to make way.

"Using the felhounds' extreme magic resistance to hunt those dragonkin mages!" Khadgar's eyebrows shot up. "Forgive my narrow thinking, Jace. I don't have much experience fighting alongside demons!"

Jace smiled and muttered in Demonic, "Send them in, Kar'lez. Kill every dragonkin and warlock."

A roar from Kar'lez answered in the distance. The felhounds responded from the entrance all the way down, scrambling over each other as they poured into the passage.

The sound of fire scorching the tunnel echoed again. But the felhounds charged through the flames without a care. Soon, muffled screams came from deep within the passage. The fire grew weaker and weaker...

"That's enough." Khadgar conjured a blast of ice and hurled it into the passage. The remaining embers and scorching heat were swept away by the cold magic.

Turalyon glanced at Jace and Khadgar and nodded. He raised his warhammer and shouted, "Careful not to attack these demon hounds! Follow me in, warriors of the Alliance!"
