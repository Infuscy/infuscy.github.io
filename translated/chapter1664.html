Chapter 1664: Bait?

After Rend Blackhand fell, the cheers of the Alliance soldiers and the silence of the Blackhand Legion quickly collided into yet another bloody battle. But one side fought with the joy of victory, the pride of watching their young king defeat a powerful foe, and the catharsis of a long-overdue reckoning. The other watched their chieftain fall -- old tribe, new tribe, orthodox or not, all of them had reached the end of the road.

The warlocks of the Shadow Council offered no resistance, slipping away through the rear passages. The remaining orc guards fought to the death, but it was less about fighting for the Horde, for the Blackhand Legion or the Blackrock Clan, and more about making a symbolic stand for the honor of "dying in battle."

This last group of chieftain's guards -- from their armor and cloaks, Jace could tell they were Rend Blackhand's own Kor'kron, the war chief's personal bodyguards and vanguard. But even the finest elite warriors and equipment couldn't compensate for the crushing morale disadvantage. Not to mention that the Sons of Lothar, who had crossed the Dark Portal, were in no way inferior to them in that regard.

The arena of Blackrock Spire was even more spacious than the Ring of Law in Shadowforge City. But it was still too cramped for over a thousand people to clash in bloody combat. The stench of blood and rot churned through echoing roars for a while, until the sounds of metal and flesh thudding against the ground faded, leaving only the pained groans of the aftermath.

Varian Wrynn sat in a corner of the arena floor, catching his breath, while two priests checked his injuries. It was clear he hadn't escaped the swift but brutal duel unscathed -- the armor plates and tunic across his chest were already soaked red.

But from his steady composure, firm gaze, and the way he still gripped his sword, the wounds probably weren't as severe as they looked.

"This orc broke his promise." Danath Trollbane looked down at Rend Blackhand's fallen body and wiped the orc blood from his blade. "Where did they hide Anduin Lothar's body?"

"Bait, Trollbane." Khadgar said. "Bait is always placed in the most obvious spot, right? I think he meant that if we keep going, we'll find it easily enough."

"But he doesn't want us to continue." Varian said quietly. "He called it 'advice.'"

"Advice from an orc?" Taelan Fordring said. "These orcs are full of lies, King Varian. They probably know Blackrock Spire can't hold out much longer, and they're trying to confuse us, make us hesitate."

"What do you think, Jace?" Khadgar said. "We all know a powerful warlock can tell if someone is speaking the truth."

Alleria added, "Speak your mind freely, Jace. You've killed more orcs on Draenor than most Quel'dorei have ever seen. No one here would think you're defending them."

Jace nodded. "I think he genuinely meant to advise us not to go after Lord Lothar's body. It's not just his emotions telling me that -- we all know the upper levels of Blackrock Spire are ruled by the dragonkin general Drakkisath. We haven't seen him yet, but there's not a single dragonkin left on this floor. Just Rend Blackhand and these Blackhand guards holding the last hall. I think Drakkisath has already taken his dragonkin back to the top of the Spire and abandoned the orcs here."

"So Rend Blackhand warned us because of that too," Khadgar said. "He realized that the Horde means nothing to the Black Dragon Prince."

"Look at these honor-obsessed orcs," Kurdran Wildhammer said from his high perch. "Taking orders from demons on Draenor, enslaved by black dragons in Blackrock Spire. A bunch of brainless idiots!"

The Dark Iron dwarves, who normally cursed the Blackrock orcs the loudest, were uncharacteristically quiet. Thaurissan was searching through the orc corpses for any warlocks who hadn't managed to escape, checking if they carried important magical items. Hard to tell if he hadn't heard the discussion or was pretending not to.

"So, what bait?" Muradin asked. "The black dragon has known we're attacking Blackrock Spire for a long time. He doesn't need to put Anduin Lothar's body here to lure or provoke us."

"He clearly has another purpose." Tirion Fordring said.

"Like how the trolls use their outriders' corpses to lure us into pre-laid traps and poisoned spikes?" a ranger asked.

"I don't think it's that simple." Nathanos said quietly. "That's Lord Lothar's body we're talking about."

Khadgar stared at the rune-bladed sword in the orc's hand for a long time without speaking. Jace didn't say anything either. Rend Blackhand's situation reminded him of something from his past life in the game -- the assault on Icecrown Citadel.

Six years after the Scourge ravaged the entire continent of Lordaeron, the Alliance and Horde organized a massive expeditionary force to attack the Lich King Arthas in Northrend. Even the Scarlet Crusade joined that great campaign.

In the end, the expedition reached Icecrown, beneath the Scourge's Citadel. They discovered that the Alliance leader who had fallen at the Wrath Gate -- the Regent of Stormwind, Bolvar Fordragon, who currently ruled the city for King Varian -- had been imprisoned by the Lich King atop the Frozen Throne, suffering endless torment. Tens of thousands of expedition soldiers, burning with rage, swore to shatter the glacier and rescue Lord Bolvar Fordragon.

But that wasn't so much a trap as it was the Lich King's mockery and humiliation of the Alliance and Horde. And Bolvar was still alive. Lord Lothar had truly died at the hands of Orgrim Doomhammer many years ago.

"Whether it's bait or not, we've reached the gates of the black dragon's lair." Danath Trollbane looked at the veterans. "We answered King Varian's call to come to Blackrock Spire. To end the orc wars, and to avenge King Llane Wrynn and Lord Anduin Lothar... We can't stop now just because of one orc chieftain's words."

"Black dragons excel at mind control. From Daval Prestor manipulating Lordaeron's court and deceiving Dalaran, to the chaos in Stormwind -- it's all been proven. And now the Black Dragon Prince holds the Eredar's greatest treasure, the Soul Song..." a Kirin Tor mage said. "I don't doubt that Rend Blackhand genuinely meant his warning to the King. But that sincerity itself might have been twisted by the black dragon's magic."

This Kirin Tor mage was also a member of the Sons of Lothar who had returned from Kirin'Var Village. Judging by the reactions of the surrounding soldiers, his argument carried more weight. Many nodded in agreement.

"I truly hope so," Alleria said quietly beside him.

Turalyon nodded as well, though it was hard to tell if he and Alleria actually agreed.

"What do you think, Lady Mor'fel?" Khadgar asked quietly. "I'd like a sayad's opinion on the mind control matter."

"Do you want the truth, or what the Sons of Lothar want to hear?" Mor'fel asked.

"Order her to tell me the truth, Jace," Khadgar said. "I know you have her true name. She can't lie to you."

"You're so tiresome, mage." Mor'fel shot Khadgar a glare.

"I want to know too, Mor'fel." Jace said.

"Fine. I don't sense any signs of mind control, master." Mor'fel said quietly. "After everything at Lakeshire, Stormwind, Princess Onyxia, and Prince Sabellian's performance at Auchindoun, I think I have a solid enough understanding of black dragon mental magic. Unless Prince Nefarian has come up with something new..."

"And," she raised her head, "the Prince is fully focused on channeling the dark curse that blankets all of Blackrock Spire. I doubt he has the energy to maintain a flawless mind control spell... Of course, with an Atal'ai crystal shard -- especially the Soul Song -- who knows what he's truly capable of."

"A pity. Sayads are authorities on mental magic, but not very persuasive when it comes to rallying Alliance soldiers." Khadgar rubbed the tip of his beard. "But Nefarian has his secret weapons, and we have ours."

"Make sure you have that tablet ready, Jace. Master Krasus explained to me what it's for. The moment we see Nefarian, don't waste words -- kill him. As for Anduin Lothar... don't dwell on it. The Sons of Lothar aren't fools. If the black dragon has resurrected him as a puppet death knight with Shadowflame, then he'll face a full purification. Turalyon won't hesitate."

"I've never forgotten." Jace said.

"I'm worried that tablet is no longer a secret to Nefarian either, archmage." Vereesa said from beside them.

"That changes nothing, ranger-lord." Khadgar said. "That tablet was designed to kill Nefarian. It wasn't even made for his sister. Whether he knows about it or not, if he intends to hold this tower, he won't escape it."
