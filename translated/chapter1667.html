Chapter 1667: Razorgore

One after another, whelps descended from the high ceiling above the battlefield. They carried black flames in their mouths, circling half-way around the high walls and towers built by the Dark Iron dwarves before diving at the allied formation.

"Watch out for Shadowflame!" Nathanos called out. "Above!"

The ranger-captain didn't need to warn them. The Kirin Tor wizards had already prepared. The first few whelps were caught by dense, powerful Slow spells, unable to flap their wings or even open their mouths.

Some had flames detonate right at their chests and necks, sending them crashing heavily against the high walls. Others lost balance entirely and fell into the allied ranks. Thick hide and sturdy scales were no match for a swarm of soldiers stabbing repeatedly with Light-touched spear-points.

One whelp was caught by the high elf knights' magic net and tumbled into the dragonkin, knocking over a large group who couldn't dodge in time. Another lunged straight down, only for Danath Trollbane to meet it mid-air, slicing a long gash under its ribs with his greatsword. Blood, organs, molten lava, and black smoke gushed out, ruining the prince's weapon. He grabbed another sword from an attendant and drove it into the whelp's eye, finishing it off.

A whelp with a golden shimmer around its neck flew high to meet them. When the other whelps saw her, their momentum faltered. Grede shouted at the top of his lungs, "Kill those whelps, Needleflame! I've got your back!"

In truth, the dwarf's crossbow bolts were essentially useless in this chaos. Vereesa, on the other hand, stood steady on top of a fallen dwarf statue, drawing her bow. Every single enchanted arrow she loosed was accompanied by a piercing scream of agony.

Occasionally, she'd glance dismissively at Grede, which infuriated the dwarf so much he wanted to grab a dragonkin, throw it aside, and charge at the enemy bare-handed. But each time, Vereesa's Thorn spell would casually block his path.

"Let me go, you damned pointy-ear! See if I can't tear a whelp's mouth apart with my bare hands without you getting in the way!" The dwarf bellowed.

"Sorry. Jace told me to watch you. Go complain to him."

Vereesa said it casually as she loosed another arrow at a whelp chasing Needleflame, blowing off its right wing. Grede could only grab at the vines and shout in frustration.

The dragonkin formation could no longer hold against the furious allied soldiers. The whelps coming to their aid from above couldn't withstand the Kirin Tor's spells and the Farstriders' arrows, let alone the little monster with the same blood as their master flowing in her veins, rampaging through their ranks with the brute force granted by the pendant tied to their dragon father.

The two sides had barely clashed before the disparity became clear. The black dragon's frenzy was suppressed, and the allied army's fury found its first release. Jace finally felt the shadow pressure from the entire Spire ease. He seized the moment to wipe the blood from his nose and mouth. A warm, soothing breeze wrapped around him, circling several times, and the pain from the shadow's oppression eased considerably.

That was Sora's magic. Even without looking for her, Jace knew she was healing him.

"We are at a disadvantage. Don't think you can save the entire kingdom so easily, my child." The Black Dragon Prince's voice rang from the depths again. He seemed to be speaking to his dragon-kin, but still used Common. His tone was still mocking. From the pained, suppressed look on Turalyon's face, the bastard was quoting Lord Lothar again.

"You walk a difficult path. But at least there are still those who can help you. And that is--"

At this point, Nefarian's tone turned playful. "Not me, not yet. Play with them a little. The smartest, clearest-sighted, freest guardian in this entire tower. Everyone's favorite -- Lord Razorgore! He has a big gift for you!"

A thick, dark figure ran out from deeper in the hall, behind the dragonkin formation. From the silhouette alone, Jace thought a Pit Lord had shown up.

But it wasn't a demon. It was a dragonkin -- far larger than Vimsalaak. Different from Vimsalaak and the others, this dragonkin's head wasn't the shape of a lizard or crocodile. It was the head of a full-grown true dragon. Horns, whiskers -- almost indistinguishable from an adult black dragon. Even its back had grown thick, real dragon wings!

Except for having two arms and four thick lower limbs, the overall body was still half-human, half-dragon -- otherwise it was nearly identical to a black dragon!

This was Razorgore. Jace remembered this rampaging beast. He held a blackrock orc leather war banner, its tip adorned with an enchanted whelp skull. He charged out, flapped his wings, leaped to an impossible height, and crashed straight into the allied ranks, sending several soldiers flying.

Tirion Fordring raised his warhammer and charged head-on, wrapped in the Holy Light like a burning golden fireball. But Razorgore swung his banner in a sweeping blow that knocked him down. The holy fire ignited the banner, making the towering monster look even more like a demon wielding a flaming warhammer.

Danath followed from the side, thrusting his sword at the dragonkin's hind leg. But this borrowed sword wasn't good enough -- it bent against the dragon scales. Razorgore whipped his tail around with a laugh, sending Trollbane flying into a pile of rubble. He looked seriously hurt.

The dragonkin seized the opening Razorgore had created and counterattacked. Turalyon, having lost Tirion and Danath as his powerful flanks, was immediately surrounded by several dragonkin guards and couldn't break free. Vereesa shot down another whelp, then immediately turned and loosed several arrows at Razorgore. Each one pierced the monster's hide, but Razorgore didn't even flinch. He only laughed more maniacally.

Jace noticed that the blood holes from the arrows weren't bleeding -- they were spewing black smoke and flames. Razorgore's face was also twisting, as if...

As if he was about to explode. Wait. Razorgore.

Looking at this scene, Jace suddenly remembered a mechanic from fighting Razorgore in Blackwing Lair back in the game -- if you killed him before using the orb to control his mind and destroy all the dragon eggs, Razorgore would trigger a massive explosion.

Did this Razorgore have a self-destruct too?

"He's planning to blow himself up to destroy our army, Khadgar!" Jace shouted at the mage up high. Khadgar glanced at him, immediately cast Polymorph to turn Razorgore into a turtle, then began casting a teleport spell.

He was trying to do the same trick -- toss the mad dragonkin outside the hall. But the Polymorph collapsed before the teleport finished, Razorgore reverting as flames burst from him. Khadgar's quick-teleport fizzled, sending nothing away.

Kurdran Wildhammer hurled a lightning-charged hammer that caught the restored Razorgore in the face, knocking his head sideways. But the blow made the dragonkin's chest and abdomen glow with fire. Razorgore's laughter grew even more uninhibited, and he started muttering to himself.

"The energy inside him is already too violent to control, High Thane!" Khadgar shouted. "Stop hitting him! Stop!"

"What?!" Kurdran couldn't believe his ears. Razorgore swung his banner down at the Wildhammer dwarf, but the Mountain King flashed in front of him and caught it with his bare hand. The two -- mismatched in size -- began struggling against each other, buying everyone a moment to think.

"How did you know, Jace?" Khadgar asked immediately.

"There's no time to explain!"

Razorgore was beyond reason. He couldn't be carrying out Nefarian's orders with such clear purpose. Nefarian was still channeling his shadow ritual. The orb must be in someone else's hands -- the Dragonmaw Clan, probably -- directing Razorgore to attack the allied army.

That meant if they could locate the orb and make Razorgore go back to the dragonkin side and blow up there...

But where was the orb? Jace looked up at the high walls and windows.

"Mind magic... Mor'fel!"

Jace called for the succubus. But even that moment of thought had nearly cost him his grip on the shadow pressing down on the entire hall.

Mor'fel had been thinking about taking a few swings at Razorgore to bring him down, but after hearing Khadgar's warning, she could only watch the Mountain King arm-wrestle the monster, unsure what to do. At Jace's call, she looked over.

"Mind magic. Can you feel the mind magic on him?" Mor'fel narrowed her eyes, then nodded.

"Take me to the source of that mind magic!"

"You want me to take you to Nefarian?"

"It's not that simple. Just do what I say!"

"Yes, master."

"Hold the line, Khadgar! Don't let the dragonkin win!"

"I'll try, I'll try!"

"Sora! I have to let go of the shadow suppression. Can you hold it down for a while? I know you have magic that can suppress anger!"

"I can, but I can't keep it going long, Jace!"

"It won't take long."

"Then..." The green dragon raised her staff of dominion with both hands. A clear chime rang through the air, and in that sound, the shadow energy in the hall was instantly suppressed.

Brilliant moonlight and stars surrounded every allied soldier... Even the wounded who had fallen found the strength to stand again.

Tranquility. This was Sora's trump card. But as she said, it wouldn't last long. Jace grabbed Mor'fel's hand without hesitation. The succubus spread her wings and dragged him into the air.

"Cover us, Vereesa! Shoot down any black dragons coming for us!"

Vereesa nodded and raised Belanore. Mor'fel dodged a whelp that Vereesa had shot down and flew straight toward a fire-lit window.

"Aren't you going to ask if I'm sure about the direction I'm flying, darling?"

"I trust your sensitivity to mind magic."

"What if I just wanted a little more alone time with you..."

"Damnit! Don't start that crap at a time like this!"

"Well, there's nothing else to do while flying... but fine, fine!"
