## Chapter 1669: The Orb of Command

This thought made Jace's scalp crawl. He had thought that if Nefarian was really using the Atal'ai crystal shard's power to drain the allied army with shadow, then by continuously channeling the shadow to relieve the pressure, he'd also be draining Nefarian and the Soul Song's power.

By the time they backed Nefarian into a corner with no retreat, the evil dragon's magic would be spent. Even if he had some way to deal with the tablet, he'd never be able to withstand Vaelastrasz and Jace and the rest ganging up on him.

But now, Jace couldn't help wondering what spell this really was.

Was it a massive Shadowflame capable of destroying all of Blackrock Spire? But all his subordinates and experiments were in this tower. If he was ready to abandon everything, why not just fly away from the start?

He was an ancient dragon, after all. Most of the people besieging his tower were mortals who wouldn't live more than a few decades. Even the high elves were few in number.

If he wanted to kill the Sons of Lothar, avenge the Horde or Blackwing, he could just take his whelps and experiments and leave, then outwait them all. If he wanted revenge on Jace for his father, he could assassinate him, find any number of methods. Was it worth destroying everything, even falling out with his own sister and mother -- his main source of dragon eggs?

Was he trying to gather all soul power into himself through the Atal'ai crystal's strength, taking a step toward his father's power?

Just looking at the directionless flow of shadow, Jace couldn't figure it out. The most important thing now was that the Invisibility Potion's effect would fade soon. Sora's Tranquility was also nearly spent. Shadow energy would soon surge back with Nefarian's blessing, pressing down on every allied soldier. Khadgar would have to waste energy controlling the shadow instead of using shadow magic to restrain Razorgore.

If they didn't find the source of that mind magic soon, the allied army would either have to retreat to avoid the wild energy building up inside Razorgore, or take it head-on. Neither was acceptable, let alone stopping Nefarian's ritual or killing him.

Jace knew his knowledge had limits. What he'd seen through the Invisibility Potion had to be shared with Khadgar and Vaelastrasz. They might have different insights.

The Invisibility Potion was wearing off. The world became clearer. But the orc had already led them into a dim chamber lit by shadow fire basins.

This place immediately reminded Jace of old Warlock Master Vorpil's room where he studied the Blood Scriptures. An old orc warlock with a white beard was intently guiding a golden orb on an altar the Dark Iron dwarves had once used to venerate Ragnaros. The reliefs around them still showed fire imps surrounding Executor Ekx'otus.

This old warlock was probably "Master Gritok," the Blackwing controller. He didn't react when Jace emerged from the Invisibility Potion, his entire focus on the orb. The warlocks and Blackwing orc guards protecting him froze for a moment, then roared in fury and called out for more guards. But it was too late.

Mor'fel cut down the guard she'd Charmed with one stroke, then swept through the orcs charging down from the altar with Apocalypse in hand, like a flame caught in the wind. Even armor made from whelp scales couldn't stop her blade.

Jace grabbed a fallen guard's corpse and tossed it at the doorway, igniting it with Shadowflame. Even so, the old orc warlock remained unperturbed. His emotions didn't even flicker.

"Even I'm getting curious now, master." Mor'fel walked up to the orb and looked at the white-haired warlock, then back at Jace. "It's like you knew from the start that the dragonkin general Razorgore was being controlled by orcs, not by Nefarian himself... Did you see this orb in the dream Deathwing wove for you?"

"Ask less. Do more." Jace pointed at the warlock. "Charm him into controlling the orb. We don't have time to learn how to use it."

"Yes, master." Mor'fel raised her hand to the old warlock's forehead. His previously calm face twitched, and he muttered, "I... I... what should I do... master?"

"Send your General Razorgore back to the dragonkin lines." Jace said in orcish. "As far away as possible. Then detonate him where the allied army won't be caught in the blast."

"Do as he says, old man." Mor'fel said.

"But that will cost me the chance for promotion... That would betray Lord Nefarius." The old warlock stammered.

"You don't want to betray me either, do you?" Mor'fel asked softly.

"Of course... I would never betray you, my noble master!" The old warlock nodded resolutely and turned his attention back to the orb, adjusting his gestures and muttering incantations. It seemed controlling Razorgore to do different things required different spells.

Jace stepped closer to examine the orb curiously. He saw figures and lights moving across it -- dragonkin, mostly, but also humans, elves, dwarves...

Was this Razorgore's vision?

The images were blurry, but he could still make out the expressions of people nearby. He could even sense the shadow energy pressing down, the fury, more and more fury...

"Protect Master Gritok!"

Harsh shouts came from outside. More orc soldiers were rushing over. But the Shadowflame Jace had ignited was already spreading from the corpse into the corridor. Many soldiers stopped at the entrance and started cursing.

The images on the orb flickered and shifted. A human face appeared, then a weapon. Suddenly a dwarf face flashed by -- Jace recognized Muradin Bronzebeard. The dwarf was trying hard to dodge a banner swinging at him from the main perspective. But from the way Razorgore swung the banner and the arcane energy the orb transmitted, it was being held back by the Kirin Tor mages' magic too.

"Don't waste time. No need to engage those soldiers. Go back to the dragonkin formation now!" Jace said.

The old warlock chanted without answering, but the tremor in his body showed he'd heard Jace's order.

The vision in the orb lurched without warning. Even Muradin Bronzebeard's expression froze for a moment. Razorgore had turned his head, and now only dragon soldiers filled his view.

A cold wind suddenly rushed into the corridor. Some orc conjurer had used frost magic to break through Jace's Shadowflame. Several orc soldiers pressed forward, weapons raised, charging into the room.

"Really? You water elementals -- I helped you beat Ragnaros, and you can't stop yourselves from being led around by arcane magic?"

Jace muttered in Elemental. To his surprise, the water elemental that had been growing and consuming the shadow fire weakened immediately, collapsing. The fire that had been suppressed by the cold surged back up. The two slower orcs were caught in the flames, screaming in terror and agony.

The ones fast enough to get inside didn't fare much better. Jace raised Ulthalesh, gathering the fury in the air and converting it into destructive will, unleashing a volley of Shadow Bolts at the orcs. The first three or four were hit and collapsed with screams. Those behind were severely corroded by the splashing shadow energy and fell too...

As the orc mage outside tried to re-summon a water elemental, Jace glanced back at the golden orb. The dragonkin were getting closer, Razorgore stepping right over them. Not a single allied soldier was in sight anymore.

At the same time, the shrieks of whelps and dragonkin rang out from outside. Jace couldn't tell if they were related.

Just as he turned back to prepare for the next wave of orc soldiers, the orb suddenly exploded. The old warlock let out an "aiyah" and sat down, dead.

"It's over... all over..."

"What?" Before Jace could ask, an explosion that swallowed every sound cut him off.

The whole hall shook. Fire basins, the orb's pedestal, orc bodies, shattered bricks and stones flew everywhere. The fire imp reliefs peeled off and smashed on the ground. The corridor outside was in chaos. The orcs stopped attacking. Shouts and screams faded into the distance.

Jace and Mor'fel ran back the way they came. They met no meaningful resistance along the way, until they reached the outermost window. Smoke had filled most of the Blackwing Hall. A whelp, only half its body left, clung to the nearby wall, screaming.

Razorgore had exploded. The force was more "wild" than Jace had imagined. Where the dragonkin had been massed up ahead, a massive crater had been blasted out. Countless dragonkin had been blown into the air. Those closest were too fragmented to count.

The once-imposing dragonkin army had been shattered in one blow. On the allied side, many paladins and dwarves who had been close had also been thrown back, but they'd mostly held formation. The dragonkin who'd been fighting the allied vanguard were intact physically -- but their spirits had taken an unimaginable blow.

It was hard to imagine what they felt, watching their leader inexplicably turn around and self-destruct, killing most of their own kind.

Cheers and dwarven laughter mocking the black dragons rose from the allied soldiers. Many probably had no idea what had just happened. Khadgar, surrounded by mages, looked up toward Jace's position, but the smoke was too thick to see clearly.

But there were still dragonkin to deal with. The melee, broken by the explosion, quickly reorganized. Jace had to get down and take over the shadow channeling. Sora's Tranquility had completely faded.

"Tell Vaelastrasz. He's down there purifying bodies. He should be catching up soon." Jace looked at Mor'fel. "Tell him about those souls. Quickly!"

"I understand, master." Mor'fel leaped over the railing, spread her wings, and vanished into the smoke.
