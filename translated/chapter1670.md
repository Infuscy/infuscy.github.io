## Chapter 1670: Regrouping

The shockwave from Razorgore's explosion forcefully shook the ancient Dark Iron hall. Several beams collapsed. Sections of the ceiling that had lost their support, along with stone bricks, came crashing down. The stone statues on both sides toppled over. Dragonfire smoke and dust obscured the view entirely.

Soldiers dealt with the remaining dragonkin while pulling the wounded from the explosion. The priests' and paladins' Holy Light was reduced to faint glimmers in the dust and smoke. The glow of the Blessing of Might from earlier had dimmed significantly from the impact.

"Jace Sesso!" Muradin Bronzebeard looked over. "In my two-hundred-odd years of experience, bad things tend to appear out of nowhere, but good things rarely fall on your head without reason! You found a way to make that dragonkin Razorgore blow up his own people!"

"I told you -- just buy me some time and I'd take care of the problem!" Jace shouted back.

A black whelp suddenly dove from above, Shadowflame in its mouth, aiming for Jace. Vereesa's rainbow-hued arrows streaked across the distance, punching through its shoulder and abdomen in rapid succession. The pain made the whelp writhe, spilling flames everywhere as it crashed into the Mountain King's direction. Muradin Bronzebeard's laughter vanished instantly. He scrambled to dodge but was sent flying anyway.

"The battle isn't over! Regroup! Don't panic, warriors!" Varian Wrynn shouted, his voice rock-steady in the chaos.

"Follow the King's command!" Jace heard Turalyon's voice, though he couldn't see him. "Lord Muradin, are you alright?"

"I'm fine! What about the others? This whelp took out two Dark Irons and a Bronzebeard! Go check on them, don't waste time on me! Where are the priests? The healers?"

"On our way, Lord Muradin!"

"Go check on Danath, Turalyon! He's almost a hundred years younger than me, but his beard's whiter than mine!"

"I'm here, still alive, High Thane Kurdran. But at least toss me a healing potion!"

"Jace!" Sora's voice came from beside him. In her green robes, surrounded by a faint halo of moonlight, she was easy to spot in the chaos.

"I'm sorry. The shadow energy here is too strong. I thought I could hold out longer..."

"I'll take it from here, Sora." Jace nodded.

"There are still whelps in the sky! They haven't all been killed!"

Some Stromgarde officer shouted. Jace looked up. Black shapes still circled in the sky, and the whelps' shrieks hadn't faded.

Alleria withdrew from nearby and glanced back at the warlock. "The Farstriders will cover your casting, Jace!"

Jace nodded. He raised Ulthalesh with both hands, straining to control the thick shadow that had surged back after Tranquility faded.

With the dragonkin army destroyed, the allied soldiers had won this engagement. After surviving a terrifying explosion, the fury Nefarian had stoked in their hearts had subsided considerably.

For Jace, controlling the dark energy in the hall became much easier.

But he could tell the power in the shadow had shifted from uncontrollable rage back to the complex mixture of emotions from before.

For Jace, readjusting his emotional "resonance" wasn't that hard. But this wasn't what he'd expected. He'd thought that by using his past-life knowledge to cleverly thwart Nefarian's plan of using Razorgore to cripple the allied army, the Black Dragon Prince would be furious -- and that would show through his channeled magic. But instead of the previous rage, the magic had shifted easily into other emotions.

"How pitiful, Razorgore. His life's ambition was to become one of us, a true Blackwing. My father and I did everything we could to make that dream come true for him. He served the Blackwing legion faithfully for centuries. But in the end, he wasn't born from an egg. Even with my father's help, reaching heights no one else had ever reached, he was ultimately driven mad by that obsession.

"At his core, he was just a mortal. His body couldn't bear such extraordinary power. His will couldn't carry the mission he was meant to fulfill. He wasn't yet worthy...

"And you? Cursed by demons, deceived by gods, a pack of self-righteous beasts. I'm curious how much farther you can go. You want to save this world, hmm? You're crazier than Razorgore ever was! Do you think your fate will be any better than his? Don't worry, you needn't fret. I will lead you down the same path. Continue, continue!"

With that, the voice from the depths of Blackrock Spire began laughing maniacally, until the sound mixed indistinctly with the crashing of rubble and stone.

Jace could tell this wasn't posturing from a humiliated foe. He knew black dragons were masters at concealing their emotions. Onyxia was. Sabellian was. And Nefarian, far stronger than both of them, certainly was too.

But he doubted that while channeling such a "grand" ritual, Nefarian had the spare energy to mask the emotions leaking through the shadow.

As Nefarian's voice faded, the whelps' shrieks above grew distant. The survivors had been recalled.

The explosion's dust settled. Jace reasserted control over the shadow energy. The pressure on the Silver Hand and the Church's clergy lightened, letting them heal the wounded and inspire the weary with the Holy Light more freely.

Danath Trollbane was hurt worse than he let on. He kept insisting he was fine, but the blood he coughed up told the truth. When Razorgore had charged out and hit him, he'd had no usable weapon and had taken the blow almost bare-handed. He clearly couldn't continue fighting.

Kurdran walked over and patted his shoulder. "Get some rest. If those Kirin Tor mages' healing potions are as good as they say, you and your cousin might still make it back to carry Nefarian's big head into Stormwind!"

"Stromgarde's soldiers have been at the front the whole time, and their commander took the worst wounds. When the Alliance wins, they deserve the title of 'dragon slayer' more than anyone else." Varian said quietly.

"Thank you, King Varian. I'll relay your words to Galen." Danath said ruefully. Clearly, missing the chance to face Nefarian hurt this veteran more than his wounds.

Khadgar and the Kirin Tor mages used arcane magic to remove the fallen rubble, dragon bones, and dragonkin bodies -- each one the size of a small kodo or thunder lizard -- clearing a path wide enough for the allied army to advance.

The whelps had all withdrawn. The remaining dragonkin had vanished. Under the commanders' orders and the prayers of paladins and priests, the various legions reformed their ranks. Rays of golden light pierced through the fine dust in the air, shining once more on the soldiers' helmets and their blue, red, or white tassels.

Though every face showed fatigue from fighting all the way from Burning Steppes, and every suit of armor and weapon was stained with the blood of orcs and dragonkin, Blackrock Spire was teetering on the edge of collapse. The Black Dragon Prince had few cards left. This was the moment to strike the final blow against this evil nest.

Whether to find Lord Lothar's body, avenge family and friends lost in the orc wars, or end the calamity that had begun with the Dark Portal, the soldiers' morale remained high.

"I apologize for doubting whether you could handle that walking bomb back there, Sesso." Khadgar looked at Jace as he caught up.

"There's something more important than backhanded compliments disguised as apologies, Khadgar." Jace stared at the demon skull atop his staff. "When I used the Invisibility Potion earlier, I saw countless ghosts from Blackrock Spire gathering toward the top. I don't think Nefarian's magic is just the shadow curse filling this place. That corrupting energy is probably just a side effect of channeling the spell. The real effect hasn't manifested yet!"

"Souls, shadow..." Khadgar frowned. "If it's not a wide-area shadow curse but a ritual that's been in preparation for a long time... I can't make out much. Probably because the spell hasn't fully formed yet."

He thought for a moment more, then lowered his voice. "Can Ulthalesh snatch those souls away?"

"If we get closer, I can try." Jace said.

"You know Ulthalesh's previous owner, right? The necromancer Sataiel, who was defeated by Guardian Scavell. He easily reaped all the troll souls in the Deadwind Pass with Ulthalesh." Khadgar glanced at him.

"I'm not Sataiel yet, Khadgar." Jace lowered his staff and took a breath. "But if you're okay with it, I can try."

"Let's get closer first." Khadgar said.
