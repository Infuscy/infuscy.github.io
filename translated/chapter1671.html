Chapter 1671: The Corrupted Will

The allied army pushed deeper. No dragonkin or orcs blocked their way. The empty dwarven fortress reeked of a nauseating stench, like walking into a corpse storage chamber that had been forgotten for untold ages. The Thaurissan noble officer leading the way cursed constantly. He seemed to have once lived on the top floor of Blackrock Spire -- the area they were approaching now. Seeing the black dragons and orcs had turned his home into this disgusting state, he was literally fuming. But this place no longer matched the Blackwing Lair Jace remembered from his past life. In the game, after defeating Razorgore and leaving the Dragonmaw-occupied hatchery -- the hall where the orb had been -- you entered a rather grand hall with a throne. That throne room had probably been where the overall commander of Blackrock Spire, or Dagran Thaurissan, received soldiers. It was also where Nefarian later poured Shadowflame into Vaelastrasz, fully corrupting him with shadow. In that room, players had to fight and kill the corrupted Vaelastrasz. But the entrance to that hall in the game was a moderately wide gate. It was fine for a huge dragonkin like Razorgore, but could that many whelps fly through? Jace had wondered about this when he saw the whelps retreating from the fight. Sure enough, when they reached the end of the hall, he found the walls and the gate had collapsed, revealing a massive hole. It wasn't just big enough for whelps -- Vaelastrasz could probably squeeze through too. The throne room was still there, but half-covered in semi-solidified lava that had flowed down. The temperature was so high it was unbearable for mortals, like being back in Shadowforge City before the Molten Core's destruction. This lava must have poured in through some crack high in Blackrock Spire when the Molten Core exploded. Ragnaros's defeat had changed all of Blackrock Mountain forever. Luckily, the Dark Iron dwarves knew other paths. Following stairs beside the chamber, through a cooled lava cave, into a stair tunnel, they emerged into an extremely wide circular hall. Broad, sturdy stone ramps lined the circular hall's walls. Stairways crossed and intertwined, extending all the way up into shadows the lava glow couldn't reach. The walls were carved with Dark Iron runes, mostly fire elemental script. Now that Ragnaros had been driven back to the Firelands, these runes had clearly lost all their enchantment. The hall felt dead, filled entirely with the stench of death and decay. Jace didn't remember a place like this in Blackwing Lair at all. After defeating the corrupted Vaelastrasz, you entered the hall of drakonid overseer Lashlayer, where various whelps were tormented. But Lashlayer was already dead at the Lighthouse. The path to that hall had collapsed with the Molten Core's destruction. Was this why Nefarian had thrown all the whelps to the lower levels, using them as cannon fodder under the shadow dragon egg's control? After passing Lashlayer's hall and the Blackwing alchemy lab, you reached the balcony overlooking Nefarian's lair. But the stairs the Dark Iron dwarves had led them up from the throne room made Jace feel like the allied army had climbed higher than that balcony... Was this... the Blackwing Descent? Blackwing Lair wasn't the very top of Blackrock Spire. The Blackwing Descent was actually the highest part. In the game, both the Dark Iron dwarves and the Blackwing forces had been conquered by the Alliance and Horde, leaving no one with the strength to occupy Blackrock Spire. This place had been left abandoned. It wasn't until Deathwing brought the Cataclysm that a resurrected Nefarian reclaimed the place. He'd regained control of some hidden servants, picked up parts of his old research, and even turned his dead sister Onyxia into an experimental subject -- fully cooperating with his father's plan, becoming even more evil and insane than during the Blackwing Lair era. Jace had no doubt the designers wanted to reskin old content and make everyone kill it again, but this part of Blackrock Spire was indeed real. He looked up at the circular hall. It reminded him of the oppressive depths of the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, but several times more grand. Ulthalesh murmured hungrily. The endless craving was nearly driving Jace past patience. He knew why the staff was so excited. They were getting closer to the peak of Blackrock Spire, where all the souls were gathering. Jace had no doubt this was what he'd been waiting for the whole journey. Four or five black whelps circled at the edge between shadow and light. Their exact numbers and sizes were hard to determine. They flew around something bound in chains. That thing was... "Lord Anduin Lothar!" Alleria gasped. Her voice echoed in the empty hall, growing into something like a banshee's wail. At her voice, everyone looked up and fixed their eyes on the shadow at the center of the chains. As the lava glow surged below, sometimes bright, sometimes dim, Jace couldn't see clearly. "That's definitely a human corpse, Jace." Vereesa said, looking up. "And he's wearing Stormwind armor." "Then your sister's right." Grede said. "I've seen Lord Anduin Lothar. Back in the Hillsbrad Foothills, he always wore that lion armor. Very similar to King Varian Wrynn's!" "Damnit!" Kurdran Wildhammer roared. Turalyon clenched his jaw in fury. "We have to bring him down. We can't let the black dragon keep desecrating his remains!" Taelan Fordring shouted. The allied soldiers boiled with rage. In an instant, their fury twisted together with the surging chaos filling the hall. Jace felt a heavy pressure closing in from every direction. Khadgar quickly pushed to the front of the allied formation, high on the steps. He raised his staff at the chained figure and chanted. A bolt of arcane energy shot toward it. He was trying to use arcane power to snatch Lord Lothar's body from this distance. But halfway there, the arcane energy was swallowed by a swirling vortex of shadow. Only a spine-tingling shriek echoed back. "Ghosts... endless ghosts." Sora said, looking up. "He's being guarded by countless spirits!" "Yes. And they're the dead of all Blackrock Mountain..." Khadgar said in dismay. He looked down at Jace. "Sesso." Jace raised Ulthalesh. The demonic staff let out a starving wail that made every nearby soldier wince. Blood seeped through the wrappings. The terrible dark magic tore the bindings open. But when Ulthalesh's claw reached up to tear at the clustered ghosts, it found no purchase. It couldn't overpower the shadow lurking there. Jace could feel it. Not only were the souls swirling around to protect what was inside -- an almost unshakable will within that thing was also protecting the souls around it, fending off Ulthalesh's extending claw. Ulthalesh felt it too. It howled furiously in Jace's mind, as if insulted. Jace could feel the demonic skeleton nearly breaking free of his control to lunge out and devour every soul -- dragonkin, orc, whelp, hundreds of whelps, and those who had perished in the Molten Core. "When I learned that a tiny fragment of that crystal could contend with the most terrifying soul reaper in the Twisting Nether, I could hardly believe it." Nefarian's low voice echoed through the hall. "But now I see -- it's not that it's too powerful. It's that the one holding that staff is an utter weakling." "It's hard to imagine that my father fell to someone like you." "Who gave you Atal'ai crystal shards, black dragon?!" Khadgar shouted. "A true guide. Someone I shouldn't have doubted." Nefarian laughed coldly. "The guests have all arrived. It's about time. Chromaggus, my child. Let's begin this new era together." Four eyes opened in the shadow above. Two thick purple dragon heads slowly extended into the firelight. A two-headed monster, larger even than a full-grown dragon, climbed down the high wall with its clawed limbs and chains, gradually revealing its entire body. A hideous purple horror, covered in broad scales, with dragon-horn-like spines covering its shoulders, head, and back -- looking like the peaks of Bladespire Mountains. Chromaggus. He was leaner than the monster in the game. Longer. More like an overly thick crocodile than a giant dragon-dog... Wait. No. There was something else different. When the monster fully revealed itself, a malformed giant wing sprouted from its back. Khadgar and the others didn't seem surprised. After defeating the chromatic dragonkin, they'd braced themselves for a final deformed experiment. But Jace was different. Chromaggus was supposed to be a failed experiment without wings. Why did it have a wing? But Jace wasn't the only one stunned. Grede had frozen solid too. "Jace..." Grede's voice trembled. "That wing... it's the female black dragon's. I'd recognize it anywhere. Because there's still a hammer-shaped wound on it from me!"
