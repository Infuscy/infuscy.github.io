Chapter 1672: Power of the Chromatic (Part 1)

"Princess Onyxia? You're serious?!"

Needleflame seemed frightened by Grede's words. Grede stared at her. "Why would I lie to you?"

Forget Needleflame -- even Jace was startled. If that really was Onyxia's wing, did that mean...?

Jace was reluctant to admit the faint sympathy for Onyxia stirring in his chest. She was undoubtedly Deathwing and Nefarian's most ruthless accomplice, a mother who would stop at nothing to ensure the black dragonflight's survival. But imagining her being brutally dismembered by her own brother -- and worse, having part of her body grafted onto this twisted monster -- still sent a chill down his spine.

From the look on Grede's face, he felt the same. Grede had been thoroughly beaten by Onyxia once. He couldn't have an ounce of goodwill toward the black dragon princess. But thinking about her fate now, there was no schadenfreude in his expression.

There was no time for sentiment, though. Chromaggus was scrambling down the wall like a lizard, flames in his mouths. Maybe "lizard" was too inadequate a comparison for his size -- if he were a lizard, this cylindrical hall would be no more than a slightly large pencil holder.

"Everyone, scatter!"

Varian Wrynn shouted, but his voice was weak against the scraping of Chromaggus's claws on the stone. The monster's scales and horns rose as his muscles bulged. Then he launched himself into the air, diving straight at the allied formation gathered at the entrance of the hall.

"Die, abomination!"

Muradin Bronzebeard faced the descending beast, wound up his arm, and hurled his lightning-charged hammer. But just before it struck one of Chromaggus's heads, the dragon wing deflected it!

The white flash from their collision lit up most of the circular hall for an instant. The Mountain King below squinted against the glare.

Most soldiers and commanders scrambled up or down the stairs, or dove through doorways. But the narrow ramps were too crowded. Not everyone made it. The monster slammed into the floor like an iron bomb, sitting the dwarf lord right under its bulk.

The beast's feet and horned heads smashed several large craters into the Dark Iron-built walls inscribed with massive flame runes, cracking them open. The thunder of shattering stone and tumbling debris nearly drowned out the soldiers' shouts and Chromaggus's roars.

Before the allied soldiers could shake off the concussion from the "bombing," Chromaggus swept a claw their way. Two dwarf soldiers who had just gotten up were swatted out of sight. His thick arm and the dark green bracers restraining it slammed into the wall, and the resulting spray of stone fragments blasted a Stromgarde red-cloaked knight who hadn't regained his footing into the central pit.

To the dwarves and humans on the ramps above and below, this monster was like a giant beetle that had fallen into an anthill. Even the slightest movement could knock over countless opponents. But after the initial chaos, the allied soldiers rallied, bravely turning their blades on the mighty foe, like worker ants and soldier ants defending their nest.

"For Lord Lothar!"

Turalyon shouted first, charging. The golden light from his long-handled warhammer blew away rubble and dust and even dispelled a mass of heavy shadow clinging to the soldiers. The paladin dashed forward and slammed his hammer into Chromaggus's jaw, making the entire beast shudder. But it immediately swung its claw back at him. Turalyon reacted fast, swinging his hammer to meet the claw, hitting the chain bracers and stopping the swing cold.

But the paladin's heavy blow didn't even leave a scratch on the bracers. A strange magical light flickered, and the bracers gleamed like new.

"Damnit, that's Arcanite!" a member of the Dark Iron Brotherhood screamed. "That treacherous black dragon stole our fiery treasures and used them to armor this freak!"

"That's the material used to craft Sulfuron, Jace." Grede said angrily. "If I still had the strength, if I weren't injured, I'd smash his armor with Sulfuras! Just like I did to Ragnaros!"

"He's not a summoned elemental. You don't need to break him down that way. Your main job right now is to protect yourself, Grede!" Vereesa shouted. "Stay out of his way! Aim for the monster's weak points!"

After the claw knocked Turalyon off balance, the beast raised its paw for a second strike. The paladin barely ducked under it. The claw struck a stone statue of a dwarf king, the violent shock nearly throwing Turalyon off his feet.

"Watch out, Turalyon!" Tirion Fordring bellowed, hoisting his shield and raising his silver-white warhammer, calling on the Holy Light as he leaped from the high steps. He brought the hammer down hard on one of the chromatic dragon's heads.

A dull thud. The chromatic dragon swayed sideways with a grunt. But before old Fordring could even steady himself on landing, the dragon lifted a foot and stomped down at him as if it hadn't been hit at all!

One of Chromaggus's heads was dazed, but the other was clear, driving the body to counter without hesitation. Fordring had to raise his warhammer and brace against the stomp. A brilliant golden radiance burst from his hammer and his entire body. This was undoubtedly the Holy Light's protection allowing him to match strength with a beast larger than a full-grown dragon.

"Farstriders! Loose!"

Alleria's Thalassian command rang from above. The rangers had already taken high positions on the stable parts of the staircase. Arrows enchanted with arcane power rained down on Chromaggus's back. Some bounced off scales and horns, but others clearly stung the monster, forcing him to raise that dragon wing as a shield. The remaining arrows bounced off the wing even with arcane enhancement. Grede's crossbow bolts were nothing but tickles.

That was Onyxia's original dragon wing. Her might was second only to her father's among ancient dragons. How could her wing membrane fear a few ranger arrows?

Only Vereesa's Dream Arrows could pierce the membrane and reach Chromaggus's body. But when her arrows -- strong enough to shake Ragnaros's hammer -- hit the chromatic dragon's scales, they were swallowed by a flash of light. Not deflected, not blocked -- swallowed, teleported to another world. Even Vereesa froze for a moment.

But the Farstriders' arrow harassment forced him to keep his shield-like wing raised, giving old Fordring a chance to break free from the pressure and dodge aside.

Lord Lothar's body hung high above. The rampaging Chromaggus, the fury of the entire allied army, and the hatred of the hovering dead for the living -- all of it had driven the shadow energy at the top of Blackrock Spire to a level Jace could barely contain.

"For Lord Lothar! For the Alliance!"

Many allied soldiers had recovered from Chromaggus's landing shock. They poured out from the stairs and entrances to surround the beast. But Chromaggus merely tangled with the two paladin leaders and the Silver Hand elites. An occasional movement was enough to knock nearby soldiers down or into the central pit. The soldiers' weapons couldn't meaningfully harm Nefarian's painstakingly crafted "failed product."

After all, Deathwing's original goal in creating chromatic dragons was to contend with the Dragon Aspects like Alexstrasza. Even a failure wasn't something mortals could easily handle.

It wasn't just the Farstriders' arrows or the soldiers' weapons. Even the mages' magic was useless. Everyone quickly realized Vereesa's arrows weren't the only thing being nullified. When fireballs hit the scales, the purple scales turned red and absorbed them. Arcane Missiles, Arcane Blasts, and Frostbolts -- the scales turned ice-blue to resist.

This chromatic monster, wielding the power of all five dragonflights, was virtually immune to all magical attacks. The Kirin Tor mages, who had faced dragonkin swarms without flinching, were now cursing in frustration. It seemed the paladins, soldiers, and dwarven warriors had a better chance. Vereesa had switched to the Farstriders' arrows, but with Belanore's magic enhancing them, she could at least pierce his scales and drive arrows into flesh.

Worse, many mages were hit by some kind of spell reflection. If struck, they'd not only be painfully interrupted mid-cast, losing their connection to the ley lines amid an arcane ring, but their bodies would burst into white arcane flames, forcing them to scramble on the ground to put out the fire. That was probably the blue dragon's trick too. Even Khadgar was struggling. He was busy dispelling the magical torment afflicting his colleagues while shouting with other mages about how to break Chromaggus's arcane defenses.

Through it all, the soldiers kept charging the monster wave after wave, heedless of casualties.

Jace hated to think it, but Lord Lothar's body, hanging in chains high above, had driven these elite veterans and Sons of Lothar past reason. And the shadow in Blackrock Spire was further inflaming their emotions. That was exactly what Nefarian wanted.

If he used a devastating shadow spell like Gul'dan's Grasp on Chromaggus now... the consequences would be unthinkable. And the chromatic dragon's magic resistance, comparable to a felhound's, meant one blow might not even kill it. Hard to say whether that would help the allied army or make Nefarian happier.

And Jace had no doubt that if he hit the two-headed dragon with shadow magic, its scales would turn brownish-black -- after all, this monster was infused with more black dragon power than any other.

Instead of wasting his breath telling everyone to calm down, he focused on controlling the shadow energy, reducing the spiritual and physical corrosion pressing down on the soldiers. That was a better bet for winning -- especially with Nefarian up above, smugly watching the gladiator show, waiting for them to come to his palace.
