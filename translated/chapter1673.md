## Chapter 1673: Power of the Chromatic (Part 2)

"Desecrate our holy relics, occupy our fortress, slaughter our people, and now make this ugly monster live in my palace? Victor Nefarius -- I haven't truly avenged us until I rape Deathwing's wife and mount your old dragon mother!"

Dagran Thaurissan shrieked as he leaped from above. He had transformed into a fire elemental, both hands gripping the Royal Scepter of Shadowforge City, which had also ignited. He dragged a trail of fire as he crashed down onto Chromaggus's back.

The blow snapped several of the dragon-spines on the monster's back and sent its entire body stumbling, half its bulk hitting the ground. Chromaggus tried to twist his neck to bite the Dark Iron emperor off his back, but his necks weren't long enough. All he could do was let Thaurissan methodically snap off the spines on his back one by one.

Muradin Bronzebeard had pried himself out of the floor by now. He couldn't find the warhammer he'd thrown, so he grabbed a battle axe and started hacking at the two-headed dragon's ankles and toes while cursing loudly.

The combined assault from paladins and dwarves seemed to enrage the monster. Chromaggus heaved himself up, both heads breathing out a pale pink mist. The Mountain King was closest and got caught in it. He crumpled to the floor as if struck by something, snoring loudly.

The nearby dwarves -- Dark Iron, Bronzebeard, and Wildhammer alike -- couldn't resist the sleeping magic. Human commanders and soldiers stumbled back, their steps growing heavier. But a shadow had already fallen over Chromaggus's head.

Sora, having prepared for the chromatic dragon's tricks, hadn't been caught by the mist. She puffed out her pink-purple cheeks and blew a hard gust, dispersing all the mist. Then she let out a shriek, and the dwarves' eyes snapped open.

"I was just asleep..."

"Can't I sleep a bit more? Who's calling!"

"Damnit, we're in Blackrock Spire! The black dragon and orc's Blackrock Spire! We're fighting, you idiots!"

"I almost forgot. Why isn't this monster dead yet?"

"Kill him!"

Seeing the green dragon easily dispel the sleeping mist, one of Chromaggus's heads roared in fury. It spat a fireball at her but she dodged easily. Meanwhile, the dwarves, charging fearlessly, kept battering his legs until they buckled, leaving him unable to deal with Sora.

Whelps up above began circling Sora, but Sora was much larger than them, and Needleflame wasn't to be trifled with. One whelp dove at Sora and was headbutted mid-flight, crashing heavily into the wall. The rangers shifted their aim upward, covering Sora and Needleflame as the two whelps fought.

Every whelp that fell was immediately surrounded and stabbed to death by soldiers. Unable to dent Chromaggus's defenses, they took out their frustration on the much weaker whelps. Jace had thought Nefarian was using the orcs, trolls, ogres, and even dragonkin as cannon fodder to slow the allied army's advance, wave after wave. But he hadn't expected even the fully grown whelps to be disposable pawns.

But if he'd mutilated his own sister, what were a few whelps in comparison?

This whole fight for Blackrock Spire felt less like Nefarian was trying to stop them and more like he was using the allied army to gradually wipe out his own forces. From the Horde to the Blackwing...

Had this guy genuinely lost his mind? Blackrock Spire had become a mountain of corpses and a sea of blood, most of it belonging to those who had once served him.

The allied soldiers had tried to move bodies out of the lower levels, not wanting corpses to create necrotic hazards or block the way. But now -- from dragonkin to whelps to orcs to the black dragon remains that needed urgent purification...

There simply weren't enough hands to handle the bodies. The priests could at most evacuate the fallen allied soldiers from the front line. Their remaining energy had to go to treating the wounded.

"Jace, hold on! We need you to resist Nefarian's dark magic!"

Turalyon had just been hit by Chromaggus's tail sweep. The paladin was covered in blood, breathing heavily, half his armor shattered. He seemed to sense Jace's increasing struggle, and Jace nodded, pushing all distractions aside. The most important thing now was helping the allied army kill this dragon-blooded monster.

Suddenly, one of Chromaggus's heads shrieked. His whole body flashed dark gold. The next second, the enormous, bloated monster vanished before everyone's eyes!

Muradin's axe swung through empty air. He nearly lost his balance. Everyone stared at each other in confusion. But just a few seconds later, the monster's shadow flickered behind Turalyon. Moving at an unimaginable speed, it bit the paladin and flung him onto the stone steps below, where he coughed painfully.

The dragon's speed was completely out of proportion to its size. It was like someone had hit fast-forward. The paladin couldn't react at all. Even an elven ranger might not have been fast enough.

Before anyone could react, Alleria had barely shouted her husband's name when several more paladins and groups of soldiers were sent flying at the same speed. Some were swallowed entirely.

Varian and a group of Stormwind knights were knocked flying by a sweep of the dragon wing. Some tumbled back into the entrance. Others rolled down the stairs, crashing into soldiers rushing to reinforce. Varian landed at the edge of a step, barely catching himself.

Walls and floor tiles were inexplicably crushed and shattered, as if battered by a violent storm. Following shadows flashed past old Fordring. The elderly paladin swung his hammer hurriedly, but hit nothing. His son beside him was struck by some invisible force and knocked unconscious against the wall. Chromaggus's massive form flickered and was somewhere else...

Jace realized it wasn't that the monster was too fast. It was that everyone else was too slow.

"He's manipulating time!" Sora shouted. "He's using the power of the Hourglass Guardians! Do something, quickly!"

Bronze dragon? So this was real time magic?

Jace had had that thought, but if Sora hadn't said it with such certainty, he wouldn't have dared believe it.

But how the hell were they supposed to stop it?

The thought had barely crossed Jace's mind when Chromaggus had already moved to the high cliff behind the Farstriders.

Needleflame noticed first and shrieked a warning. The elves scattered quickly, but against Chromaggus's time-accelerated speed, they were too slow. Many rangers were knocked off the steps, falling to the levels below or into the pit...

This monster was too fast. Only a few short incantations' worth of time had passed since he'd started using time magic!

Then Khadgar's voice rang from above. "Colleagues, if you make it back to Dalaran alive, feel free to report me to the Council!"

Before he'd even finished, the archmage had downed a potion. The rest of his words turned muddy.

Jace recognized it. Fel Might Potion. What was he planning?

Chromaggus's shadow flickered again, appearing behind a group of priests. Khadgar clenched his fist. Jace felt the very sound around him distort. Chromaggus's shadow grew clearer and clearer... slower and slower... until it stopped at normal speed. Then it slowed even more.

He lifted his foot slowly, as if about to kick all the priests into the pit. But the priests scattered immediately. He seemed unaware, still lowering his foot, his eyes still fixed hatefully on where the priests had been.

But this time, it wasn't that he'd slowed down. It was that everyone else had sped up! Was this Time Warp?!

The arcane glow in Khadgar's hands had turned bright gold, illuminating large stretches of wall. The mage's jaw was clenched. He stared at his palms and shouted, "Do it! Now!"

The rangers and mages immediately loosed arrows and spells. The soldiers sprinted forward. Jace saw that when the arcane-empowered arrows touched the chromatic dragon's scales, they couldn't change color fast enough to resist. Even the adaptive resistance scales' transformation speed had been slowed by Khadgar's time magic!

Vereesa realized it first. She drew and loosed several Dream Arrows in rapid succession, piercing the head Chromaggus had been using for time magic. The dragon's movements started speeding up again. Khadgar finally collapsed, sitting down hard. Chromaggus returned to normal speed, but that head had been completely shot dead by Vereesa, hanging limp against his chest.

Chromaggus stumbled back several steps until his rear hit the wall. For the first time, Jace tasted real fear in the mad monster's roar. He was finished.

"Kill him, warriors! Now!" Varian coughed and shouted.
