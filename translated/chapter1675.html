Chapter 1675: The Lord of Blackwing

Prince Nefarian's entire draconic hide armor flowed with the ghostly light of shadow and death energy, merging with the radiance of Soul Song at the heights of Blackrock Spire like a flickering fallen star.

Jace didn't know if he could win. Even though the Destruction Potion's effect hadn't worn off yet, in this nearly sealed-off hall, no one could come to help in time.

If Deathwing, in his haste to eliminate Alexstrasza, had overlooked Jace and his companions, and then lost his mind entirely when the Demon Soul shattered... If Onyxia, tangled up with Krasus and wanting nothing but a quick escape, couldn't bring herself to kill a friend who had fought demons alongside her and even adopted her daughter... If Sabellian, at the top of the Throne of Kil'jaeden, never managed to deliver the killing blow against someone who had stood with him against the Legion and welcomed his daughter into his home... then this Black Prince before him had none of his father or siblings' reservations.

"Die!"

A dragon-tongue shriek tore through the air. The shadow-figure suddenly exploded into a horrific mass of black-red lightning that dyed the entire dome of the circular hall, crashing down in the blink of an eye. Jace felt a wave of destructive fury intense enough to shatter anyone's mind on the spot explode before him, bearing down.

Nefarian didn't bother probing. He wanted only to pour everything he had into erasing this warlock — the one who had thwarted the Blackwing legion so many times — from spirit to flesh, here and now!

The bricks, stairs, and runes on the walls of the circular hall shattered into countless stones and even clouds of dust after the lightning flashed through, as if swept by a violent shockwave. Then the lightning slammed straight into a shimmering violet shadow-shield formed by circling ghosts.

Though the Soul Well ritual had severely drained Jace's energy, with the souls of all of Blackrock Spire seized through the Soul Well, with Ursalius in hand, and with his mastery of soul emotions, Jace now had nearly inexhaustible power at his disposal.

The souls weren't even consumed and converted into usable magic — they were simply twisted into part of his shadow barrier. And these souls, still retaining some will and spirit, proved far more effective than pure shadow at this moment.

They tore at the black dragon's Shadowflame energy, stripping away that nearly unstoppable power. Though Nefarian's magic burned through these souls like a firestorm sweeping through dead leaves, more souls obeyed Ursalius's command, surging up from the dark well to fuel the warlock's shadow shielding, giving the warlock at the center of this soul cyclone enough room to conduct his magical ritual.

Jace cut his palm with his sword blade, chanting the incantation to summon Morophil. A Twisting Nether rift opened immediately. But as it opened, he felt something wrong.

The blood-colored scepter in the high figure's hand emitted a strange magical light. The shadow in Jace's hand was twisted and transformed by a force he couldn't sense the shape or emotion of, into something completely unfamiliar. By the time he tried to close the rift, it was too late — the summoning magic was out of control.

Nefarian...

"Master! Master, look clearly!" Sazeno shrieked, his voice nearly drowned by the howling of souls.

"Endless souls... I felt them before I even stepped into this world, dear Jace Sesso!"

A strange unfamiliar Succubus, deep violet all over, leaped from the rift into the center of the soul storm amid the black dragon's wild laughter, wielding a fel-fire whip. She was clad in evil-iron armor engraved with the demonic runes of the Legion. Without hesitation or doubt, smiling, she snapped the fiery whip toward Jace. Frowning, he raised Ursalius and cut the whip cleanly in one stroke.

Her expression shifted slightly, but looking at this demon bone scepter, she showed little fear — as if she had accepted her fate before even answering the summons.

The Succubus decisively discarded her whip and spread her wings, trying to leap out of the soul cyclone to gain distance. But Jace locked her throat with a Grasp of the Blood Demon and yanked her in front of him. This time the Succubus finally let out an uncontrolled shriek of terror. A flash of blood light cut through her body and armor, ending the scream. The exposed half of her demon-heart spurted violet demon blood as it hit the ground. Her soul was instantly drawn into the dark shield, joining the spinning soul storm.

"Presumptuous mortal. You are in MY tower, in MY palace!"

Nefarian's eyes had shifted from the dimness of death to blood red. His triumphant shout was sharp enough to crack. The lightning he released blazed brighter, dyeing everything in sight a dark red. Ursalius, disturbed by the sudden Succubus, weakened its soul shielding by a hair — and the black dragon seized the moment, driving the lightning through like a spear...

For an instant, the lightning seared Jace's eyes. Every inch of his skin felt like it was being savagely cut by a thousand scissors. Blood sprayed from somewhere, only to be burned into black mist midair by the lightning.

Sazeno cast a Blood Pact sigil but was blown away by the massive magical backlash with a yelp. But the moment bought by that Blood Pact sigil was enough for Jace to raise Ursalius again and re-establish the soul storm to divert the lightning's burning assault.

Jace seized the chance to channel emotions, drawing power from the Soul Well beneath him. In the gap between the soul rotation, he unleashed a volley of Shadow Bolts toward the dark figure above. Empowered by both the Soul Well and the Destruction Potion, each shadow bolt was like a maddened black specter — fangs bared, eyes blazing with evil green fire, lunging at the black dragon above. These specters filled the entire circular hall like literal rain, even overwhelming the blood-red of the lightning...

But Nefarian didn't dodge. He didn't even make any particular move. The specter-like shadow bolts, upon nearing him, lost control one by one and scattered — some even arced mid-flight and rebounded!

If there had been any mortals present, they wouldn't have seen what Jace glimpsed: behind the Black Prince, several withered dragon shadows clawed and grappled like spiders, manipulating the chaotic energies throughout the air. They twisted the emotions within the energy — from destruction to despair, from depression to feverish fervor, from pain to distorted, even perverse pleasure... Jace felt every shadow spell he cast being twisted in various ways, most of them even turning back to bite at his soul shielding, seeping through to corrode his body and sear his spirit.

Was this Nefarian's power? Was this what Sabellian had warned him about?

Even if Nefarian was determined to break free of the Old Gods' control, he was still a black dragon who possessed a portion of an Old God's power. His mastery of shadow was beyond mortal comparison. Under the intense pain, Jace couldn't even feel any room to twist back against him.

The Black Prince's power seemed to have merged with the entire chaotic circular hall. Beyond the Soul Well beneath his feet, Jace could barely distinguish any other power. He could only see with his physical eyes — glimpse Nefarian's eyes, Soul Song, and the scepter in his hand, the legendary "Lok'amir il Romathis" — but he couldn't grasp the root of its power at all.

The soul vortex still tore at the black dragon's dark lightning suppression, but Jace was already feeling overwhelmed.

The Atama Crystal was not part of a naaru's body, not the same as the shards left by dead naaru. Its true origin was unknown even to the Eredar, who considered it their most precious holy relic. Its power exceeded anything ordinary beings could imagine.

Kil'jaeden had hunted Velen for tens of thousands of years just to reclaim the seven fragments of the Atama Crystal, to use their power in service of the Burning Legion.

Nefarian, having mastered Soul Song, was on an entirely different level from Sabellian. Without the Destruction Potion's effect and the empowerment of Blackrock Spire's entire Soul Well, Jace doubted he could have lasted this long even with Ursalius.

But the Destruction Potion wouldn't last much longer either.

Jace knew the power in his Soul Well had far exceeded what his will could control. When the potion wore off — whether Ursalius instantly claimed all the souls, or the Soul Well's chaotic energy exceeded his limits and fell back into Nefarian's grasp — neither outcome would end well.

He had to hold out as long as possible. Long enough for Khadgar and the others to return. But if he couldn't mount a counterattack and let Nefarian flay his spirit unchecked, even the Soul Well wouldn't hold...

What kind of magic couldn't Nefarian manipulate? What kind of energy couldn't this black dragon exploit?

Jace had studied Shadow Mimicry back in Karazhan. He understood Medivh's research on this power. He'd experimented with it himself. So he knew exactly what he was dealing with.

While the soul vortex could still withstand the Black Prince's suppression, he drew Azuresong and shouted in Elemental:

"Winds and thunder! Waves and storms! I, Jace Sesso, defeated the Firelord Ragnaros, freed Prince Thunderaan, and aided Duke Hydraxis of the Abyssal Council! Now is the time for you to repay me!"

For some reason, Jace had never been so certain that the wind and water elementals would help him — even at the very peak of Blackrock Mountain. And the elementals did not disappoint.

The temperature in the circular hall, which had been scorching hot under shadow and flame, plummeted. Even the flickering dark lightning grew feeble. Nefarian sensed something was wrong. He raised his scepter, Lok'amir il Romathis, and a shadowy fel-fire rose from his hand — but it was too late.

Jace raised Azuresong, pointing at the dark figure. On the stairs around the Soul Well, ice spikes suddenly grew, spreading upward at storm-like speed.

Nefarian tried to spread flame to melt the ice, but quickly realized he had underestimated the encroaching cold.

Souls could summon the powerful otherworldly elemental Murmur in Auchindoun, sustaining such a powerful entity's presence in Draenor. Of course, they could also amplify the power of ice elementals. This was, in fact, the very foundation of how shamans communicated with souls and called upon the elements for aid!

Jace had become familiar with this feeling back in Auchindoun. Though he didn't fully understand how souls empowered the elements, elemental power worked that way — as long as he provided soul energy, and the elements were willing to accept his summons, a powerful ally would throw itself at the enemy.

Ice walls and ice spikes covered half the circular hall in the blink of a few eye-blinks, sealing Nefarian entirely in an ice prison. The rampaging shadows and flames were all extinguished. For a moment, it seemed Jace had gained the upper hand.

But this was nothing more than a surprise attack. The ice elements whispered and lamented around Jace. They knew Nefarian could break free at any moment. Even with the Soul Well's empowerment, they couldn't hold Deathwing's proudest son forever. They urged Jace to seize the chance and flee, to stop persisting.

But Jace had already realized something. Nefarian could easily manipulate shadow, but under Ursalius's control, he had always struggled to seize souls. The Black Prince would never simply give up.

Jace tossed Ursalius into the air. The scythe-blade traced a blood-red arc at the height. One by one, specters surged out of the spinning soul storm, scrambling to grab this Reaper of the Twisting Nether...

"Fight for it! Fight for it!"

Jace murmured in demonic tongue, driving more and more ghosts from the Soul Well to fly upward, fighting each other for Ursalius, all the way up to the ice wall. At that moment, the Black Prince in the ice prison also understood exactly what Jace Sesso was planning.

The fury that had been burning in Nefarian's heart from being sealed by the elements suddenly turned to terror. His whole body tensed. As Ursalius suddenly split the ice wall before him, he swung Lok'amir!

The brilliant blood-dragon scepter was cleaved in two by the oncoming Reaper, erupting in blinding blood-light. Nefarian screamed in uncontrollable pain. He sprayed Shadowflame, instantly incinerating a swath of souls. But Ursalius was immediately snatched up by another group of souls and, once again at Jace's command, slashed down at the Black Prince in the center...

Nefarian knew exactly what Ursalius was. Having lost his Lok'amir scepter, he dared not confront it directly in any form — especially after it had devoured so many souls.

But a single slashing strike from a pack of souls couldn't occupy all of this Prince's attention. The clawing dragon shadows behind him had been spotted by Jace. And Jace wasn't putting all his focus into controlling those souls, either. At this moment, his eyes had turned blood red.

Suddenly, the spider-like dragon shadows pushed away the large swaths of souls surrounding the black dragon, even throwing Ursalius aside. As the multitude of souls lunged to grab the staff, Nefarian spun around sharply and pointed at Jace, his eyes blazing with purple-red fire.

When he saw the blood red in Jace's eyes, there was a flicker of surprise, then anger. But the emotions Jace sensed stopped there.

He had succeeded in mimicking the other's emotion, incantation, and spellcasting motion. Even though he had no idea what the spell was, based on his understanding of shadow, he dared to claim he had replicated at least eighty percent of it.

But the next second, his confidence wavered.

The sudden surge of shadow energy felt like it was seeping coldly into his brain. Part of his consciousness seemed to flee somewhere beyond his control, avoiding this energy. The dragon's roar, the souls' shrieks, everything shifted across the sky... His mind held only one thought: how the hell did HE learn Shadow Mimicry? Did that idiot Sabellian teach him back in Draenor?!

Wait — he should have been wondering what strange magic Nefarian had cast. Why had his own train of thought suddenly veered off?

No...

Jace forced his eyes open. What met his gaze was the deep circular hall, the black Soul Well at the very bottom, and standing on the platform above the well, looking up at him with equal bewilderment — Jace Sesso.

He lowered his head. There, embedded in his chest, was Soul Song. I'M Nefarian?!

Then Nefarian is... ME?!

So he had copied Nefarian's Mind Control?! Everything was backwards!

The "Jace Sesso" below seemed to realize the same problem. He immediately raised his hand, chanting some spell. The souls scrambling for Ursalius fell into chaos. Even the Soul Well began to show signs of collapse.

He was planning to use control of my body to dispel all the souls immediately! Well, I can't pass up this golden opportunity either.

"Nefarian" gripped the Soul Song embedded in his chest tightly. A wave of deep despair and regret seeped into his spiritual consciousness — that was the undertone of Soul Song's void power.

Just die already.

"Nefarian" yanked hard at Soul Song. The "Jace Sesso" struggling to dismantle the Soul Well let out an uncontrolled scream. Hearing his own voice from such a distance was a truly unprecedented experience.

Then "Jace Sesso" realized what "Nefarian" was about to do. He immediately drew Azuresong and stabbed it deep into his own abdomen. "Nefarian" felt a chill pierce his heart.

But what was a little wound like that to a body with red dragon blood? He'd just find Turalyon later for a quick Holy Light, and it'd be fine. What about you, Nefarian? What would you become after losing Soul Song?

Before "Jace Sesso" could make another move, "Nefarian" viciously tore at the Atama Crystal on his chest again. This time, "Jace Sesso" couldn't bear the pain. He stopped trying to self-destruct, since he didn't know any faster way for Jace to kill himself.

Then, "Nefarian" felt "Jace Sesso" attempt to break the Mind Control spell. The problem was, the real Jace knew nothing about black dragon mind control magic. If the other party wanted to give up, he couldn't maintain it either.

Seeing he couldn't pull Soul Song out, "Nefarian" kept pulling hard while chanting to drive the shadows.

"Savia... Avarrus."

Nefarian's mastery of shadow was so profound that even "Nefarian" felt envious. Especially with Soul Song's empowerment, he felt like he wasn't manipulating shadow — he WAS shadow itself, literally one with it. Which also explained why the Black Prince had lost so badly in their shadow duel earlier...

So the dark magic serpent in the sky gathered faster than he expected. But then the world spun again — Jace knew what was happening this time. The Mind Control was broken. His consciousness was being kicked back into his own body!

But the Hand of Gul'dan incantation was nearly complete. He only needed to seize back the half-cast spell and finish it. His many practice sessions with Ursula and Khadgar had made him very familiar with this spellcasting method. And Nefarian had no familiarity with the Hand of Gul'dan — as a black dragon, he looked down on these petty orc tricks. So he couldn't take it over, even though it was cast using his own body!

"Vilma!"

Jace hurled the dark meteor — flickering with Twisting Nether void-light in the sky — at the Black Prince. But he felt like he hadn't fully thrown it, as if a "part" remained suspended in the air.

No, it wasn't a part. It was a completely separate second dark meteor!

While Jace had been secretly casting the Hand of Gul'dan using Nefarian's body, Nefarian had used Jace's body to secretly cast a Shadow Mimicry as well! The Black Prince's eyes were blood red now, the dragon shadows behind him dancing like a struggling spider's withered limbs, laughing in triumph!

"Let's see who dies first this time! Jace Sesso!"

Two dark aggregates, each having drawn tremendous power from the Twisting Nether, hurtled toward the two of them from above and below simultaneously. Jace saw two massive black wings — larger than the towering stone statues of dwarven kings — spread in front of Nefarian. Jace could only use the Grasp of the Blood Demon to yank Ursalius back, swinging it up to raise an even fiercer soul storm to block the ever-growing dark meteor.

Two purple-red explosions erupted from within Blackrock Spire in succession. Stone pillars broke, massive walls shattered. Shadow fel-fire roared out from within. Even the magma erupting from the volcano at Blackrock Mountain's peak — shaken by the magical energy — was stained black. Every survivor inside and out, above and below, who witnessed the sight screamed and fled.

The circular hall hadn't collapsed yet. It was barely holding together. But it didn't seem like it would last much longer.

Grotesquely large chunks of stone kept falling. Nefarian looked down from above. The armor all over his body had exploded into pieces, but his form still stood towering. Until, in the smoky shadows he was staring at, evil green eyes slowly opened one by one.

Over a dozen tall Doomguards and Terrorguards surrounded him from all directions, roaring as they drew fel-energy blades and lunged at the black dragon, shouting in demonic tongue: "For Master Jace Sesso!"

Nefarian frowned. He looked down at the demonic rune Jace had somehow drawn in the smoke and dust below, cursed under his breath, and reached out a hand that had transformed into a dragon claw, ripping out the foremost Doomguard's demon heart. But a demon behind him swung a blade at his dragon wing, producing a piercing screech of metal and a shower of sparks.

Though the Black Prince had taken a Hand of Gul'dan strike, he could still casually kill these elite demons. But there were simply too many of them, and all were Eredar skilled in dark magic. For the moment, he couldn't shake them off.

The Destruction Potion's effect had already faded more than half. Jace could tell from how much slower he was at drawing the rune.

He had to seize this final moment. Deal a lethal blow to this twisted undead monster. Despair... regret...

Jace had been fighting against Soul Song's power from the bottom to the top of Blackrock Spire. He was intimately familiar with the undertone of this Atama Crystal fragment.

He stretched out his arms. As several Doomguards occupied Nefarian, he precisely clamped onto the power of Soul Song with his spirit.

Relying on the last traces of the Destruction Potion's enhancement, relying on the nearly limitless mana supplied by the Soul Well, he began twisting the crystal fragment's power with his own shadow energy. If he could destroy this thing, Nefarian wouldn't be able to resist anymore.

It was working. As Jace seized and tore at Soul Song, the black dragon above let out a roar of pain and fury. "Jace Sesso! JACE SESSO!"

The roar echoed through Blackrock Mountain. A bloody black wind suddenly poured in through the cracks in the circular hall's walls. Nefarian abruptly transformed into a black dragon too massive for the hall to contain, crashing into the demons surrounding him. And Jace felt his connection to Soul Song becoming unreal.

Most of Nefarian's body had turned into charred black bones, but the fiery red fel runes still marked what he had been in life.

He was already very weak. Bones were collapsing all over him. The Soul Song on his chest flickered like it was about to go out. But his expression remained calm.

"You've destroyed everything I had, Jace Sesso. I'll destroy yours too." What...?

Jace wanted to say something, but after taking a copied Hand of Gul'dan head-on, the Destruction Potion's power faded, draining the last of his spirit.

The undead prince laughed coldly. He raised a withered dragon claw, tearing open a purple-red rift, and began to step through. The Soul Well was also about to give out. Jace struggled to manipulate shadow to tear apart his teleportation, but it felt like beating against an iron wall.

Suddenly, a blinding white flash exploded from within the rift, shattering the portal in an instant. Nefarian howled as his whole body was knocked backward, crashing into the circular hall's wall and crushing a large section of the barely-standing stairs.

"Don't even think about escaping, Nefarian!"

Khadgar roared at the top of his lungs. Somehow, he had appeared again at the very bottom of the circular hall, the arcane glow on his staff still bright.

"You're not enough, little mage... Who can stop me?!"

Nefarian laughed coldly, dissolving into a cloud of dark dust that slipped through the rift. Above the tower, he re-formed into dragon shape and flew southwest at full speed.

Stormwind. He was going to Stormwind.

"Damn it!"

Khadgar shouted. The arcane energy gathering in his hand had to be dispersed. After continuously casting large-scale spells, his stamina was running low.

"NEFARIAN!"

Vaelastrasz's roar shook the hall. Flames blazed across the sky as a red dragon burst through, pursuing at far greater speed. More and more gryphon riders screamed as they chased after. Jace's heart pounded as he watched. He had to, he had to also...

"Jace!"

Sora's voice came closer. Vereesa was already on her back. "Look behind you, Jace!"

Before Jace could react, another pair of dragon claws scooped him up and tossed him onto a narrow brown-black dragon's back.

"Got you, Sesso. Let's go!" Needleflame said, tilting her head, then shot like a black arrow straight into the dark thick clouds above the Burning Steppes.
