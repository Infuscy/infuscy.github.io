Chapter 1676: Burning Sky

Nefarian and Vaelastrasz had already vanished from sight. Even with Sora and Needleflame flying at full speed, they could only watch the two dragons disappear between the distant peaks.

Amid the hoarse shouts of soldiers, waves of gryphons launched in emergency from the ground below. Spear-wielding dragonhawk riders streaked past — but not forward, backward. Neither gryphons nor dragonhawks could catch up to two young dragons, let alone the two full-grown dragons ahead of them. As for the Doomguards, though skilled in combat, they were far too slow in full flight compared to dragons and gryphons. There was no relying on them.

Jace gulped down mana potions, his mind racing for ways to stop the undead black dragon.

When Onyxia had been "ruling" Stormwind, if she had been watching him — as Nefarian claimed, just like their father — then she definitely knew where his parents lived.

The thought made anger surge in his chest, because there was nothing he could do about it.

Khadgar, realizing he couldn't stop Nefarian's escape, had already teleported away. He must have gone to Stormwind. He had to go alone — adding another person at this distance doubled the difficulty, and with his current mana, it was too much.

Rather than join this doomed chase, Jace wished he could get back to the city to warn everyone. This distance was nothing to a dragon; they'd arrive in no time.

If he couldn't stop Nefarian, as long as his parents could avoid the danger, that was enough. As for the house — lose it, whatever. He couldn't care less at this point.

Ahead, thunderclaps rang out continuously — the sounds of fire and magic. Along the way, collapsed cliffs, scorched dead forests, and watchtowers destroyed by terrible flames were everywhere.

The Storm Altar's Wizard Sanctum camp was completely burned, as if hit by a meteor shower. One of the three statues on the altar had collapsed, its fractured surface eroded into dark-purple molten rock. The remaining two were damaged to varying degrees.

Ahead, Stormwind wasn't far anymore. Past these endless mountain ranges, they'd reach Northshire. Jace had traveled this route north and south countless times, but this was the first time he wished the mountains separating Khaz Modan, Baradin Bay, and Elwynn could stretch just a little longer, a little farther...

"Nefarian! Your madness ends here!" "Little thing. You're nothing."

A furious dragon roar exploded in the distance again, followed by a rumbling like rocks tumbling down a cliff.

At the edge of the horizon, the shadows of two dragons wrestled in the black clouds. The flames and dark purple fel-wind they belched pushed back the dark clouds overhead.

Even wounded as Nefarian was, Vaelastrasz couldn't gain the upper hand. The smaller shadow was held fast by the larger, gaunter skeletal dragon, struggling weakly as it clawed at its wings and neck. The former could barely mount any effective resistance.

"Faster, Sora. Faster."

Vereesa rose steadily to kneel on Sora's spine. Wreathed in natural power, the green dragon flew fast and stable, giving the ranger on her back room to aim calmly, draw her bow, and loose.

Though the distance to where the two dragons tangled high above was so vast Jace couldn't even estimate it, arrows of dream flew out like rainbows, tracing beautiful trails of purple and white light under the black clouds, stabbing straight into the black fiend's body...

"Damned elf! Damned green dragon!"

Nefarian let out a piercing shriek, mixed with dragon-tongue curses Jace couldn't understand. Then he twisted his head and spat a massive spray of bright purple fel-flame toward Sora. But before he could even finish half the breath, Vaelastrasz grabbed his head and dragged him back into the clouds.

The green dragon carrying the ranger dropped altitude rapidly, accelerating past the spreading fel-flame's coverage and charging ahead. The gryphon riders behind shouted and dodged, some nearly crashing into the jagged cliffs beside them.

"Keep going, Vereesa!" Jace shouted.

Vereesa nodded slightly. With a sharp turn on the dragon's back, she re-aimed at the two dragons rising behind. She drew the bow horizontally, and the dream-light along it flared bright again!

Vaelastrasz and Nefarian burst from the cloud layer again, trailing black smoke as they angled downward.

The dream arrow loosed and followed, this time striking Nefarian squarely on a neck vertebra. A swath of burning marks extinguished. The black dragon, who had been gripping the red dragon's face to spit a gout of fel breath, coughed violently from the two hits. His claws trembled and nearly slipped. Vaelastrasz seized the moment, grabbing the black dragon's lower jaw and wrenching his head back as Nefarian erupted in profanity.

"Is this how you respect your elders, Vaelastrasz? Treating the Prince of Blackwing like a bad-tempered cat?"

"You pitiful ghoul deserves no respect. You don't deserve the name Blackwing!" "VAELASTRASZ!"

At this point, neither side could spare the attention to flap their wings. They fought lower and lower, crashing into cliffs and boulders repeatedly, slower and slower. One by one, the gryphon and dragonhawk riders caught up and joined the siege.

Burning javelins and lightning-wreathed war hammers struck the black dragon one after another. Nefarian cursed in at least six or seven languages and accents, running through the entire Alliance roster of elves, dwarves, humans, and gnomes.

Then Nefarian gave a powerful wingbeat, dragging Vaelastrasz sharply upward. The foul-smelling cyclone scattered the pursuing flying knights.

Sora climbed sharply to dodge the vortex. Vereesa angled herself to aim at Nefarian again, but the two dragons were fighting too fiercely — sometimes Vaelastrasz was between them and the shot, making it hard to loose.

"Extinguish, you annoying pests."

Nefarian's dead eye fixed coldly on their direction. Vereesa's face changed — she wanted to warn Sora, but it was too late.

The Black Prince extended a withered dragon claw toward them. A sheet of dark purple lightning, covering half the sky, erupted from his fingertip!

"Don't you dare harm anyone else, Nefarian!"

Vaelastrasz grabbed the black dragon's withered finger hard, but the lightning was already released. It passed through the red dragon's body, spreading like branching leaves up into the sky, followed by a deafening explosion and a piercing shadow-scream. The lightning wasn't like flame — there was barely any room to dodge. The foremost riders were struck instantly and fell into the deep valley.

As the lightning arced toward them, Jace immediately manipulated shadow energy to twist its direction, shielding as many of the pursuing knights behind him as he could.

But Jace could feel it — without the Destruction Potion's enhancement, even blocking this lightning was a struggle. Back in Blackrock Spire, he had endured Nefarian's full onslaught for dozens of seconds.

The lightning's aftereffects weren't over. The ensuing shockwave shattered the cliffside boulders, churning up the forest and river below. Needleflame nearly capsized as she dodged. High above, Sora lurched a few times, her wingbeat rhythm broken. Vereesa flattened herself and held on tight to avoid being thrown.

The black dragon, having just discharged his lightning, was viciously pinned by the furious red dragon into a distant valley. Then came an even more terrifying vortex of flame, rising high, and a blast like a battering ram hammering into a chest.

Lower down, shielded by the mountain, a group of gryphon and dragonhawk riders who hadn't been hit had already pursued ahead. Needleflame adjusted her flight posture and accelerated, even passing Sora. The place where the two dragons had fallen was consumed by blinding flames and black smoke. Shadowy blood-red swam within it, releasing swarms of vengeful spirits whose wails were audible even without necromantic talent.

"Lord Vaelastrasz! We're coming to help!" Sora roared in the Dragon Tongue from afar, but no response came from the depths of the thick smoke.

Except for the crackle of spreading flames, the indescribable sounds of shadow corrosion, and the howl of magic-stirred wind, this smoke-choked valley was terrifyingly silent. No Vaelastrasz. No Nefarian. Not even the beat of dragon wings.

"That lightning spell... Lord Vaelastrasz took the brunt of it," Vereesa said.

"I can still feel his power there, Vereesa! It has to be him, not the black dragon!" Sora shouted.

Jace said nothing. An uneasy thought had crossed his mind. Vaelastrasz... Nefarian... Last time he'd been in a situation like this was in his previous life — after defeating Razorgore, the guardian of the dragon's nest, and entering that Dark Iron throne room.

The two young dragons flew deeper into the valley. Two giant silhouettes became slightly clearer in the firelight and smoke.

Nefarian was leaning against a slope, watching them. His body — barely held together by bone and sinew — was covered in burning flames. Whether they were the red dragon's flames or his own was unclear. The fel-runes on his bones flickered like breathing, brightening and dimming with the passing flames.

The Atama Crystal on his chest, though tiny compared to his massive form, was barely visible. But Jace could still judge its weakness through his own perception and familiarity with its energy.

Across from him, the somewhat smaller red dragon Vaelastrasz had his back to them, crouched low, staring the black dragon down.

He didn't look good. Probably from taking that magical lightning. But even so, he was ready for a fight to the death.

However, as they drew closer, Jace sensed something off. "Hold on, Vaelastrasz!"

Vereesa shouted as she rose to stand steady on Sora's spine, summoning a dream arrow from Belanoor's bowstring aimed at the black dragon ahead. But Vaelastrasz suddenly turned his head.

His eyes were no longer the golden color of red dragons. They were the deep black of void-shadow.

"An unexpected bonus... Jace Sesso." Nefarian laughed, watching them.
