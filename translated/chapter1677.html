Chapter 1677: Doomsday Dream

"What's happening? What's going on?"

Several dwarven knights who had followed them panicked at the sight of the red dragon's condition. Vaelastrasz spread his wings, nearly blocking most of the view as he positioned himself between the new arrivals and the black dragon.

Arrow trajectories were blocked by Vaelastrasz's massive wings. High above, Vereesa couldn't bring herself to loose. Vaelastrasz muttered something. Suddenly, Jace felt the ground beneath him turn scorching hot. A second later, the heat became a devouring burn — a mass of flame engulfed his entire body!

He wasn't the only one. Both young dragons and their riders burst into flames at the same time. Even several gryphon riders in the distance caught fire, losing control and crashing into the forest.

Vereesa activated the Flowing Ice Crystal. Its cold healing power quickly extinguished the red dragon fire before it could fully consume her. Sora, beside her, also shook off the flames.

Olastrasz's Hide did its job. Any red dragon fire that touched it was quickly absorbed, leaving only a faint glow on the leather. Beneath him, Needleflame, as a black dragon, was extremely hard to ignite. What little fire caught between her scales died out quickly.

Free of the flames, Jace focused, trying to figure out what was going on. Nefarian had practically exhausted his mana. How could he have the strength left to mind-control Vaelastrasz?

But he didn't have much room to think. Vaelastrasz blasted flame across the sky. Needleflame dodged with difficulty. The slope and forest behind them were transformed into a hell of fire and black smoke.

"Lord Vaelastrasz! Snap out of it!"

Beyond the black smoke and spreading flames, though Sora's figure was nowhere to be seen, her voice remained steady.

Vaelastrasz wasn't one of the Dragonqueen's children or consorts, but he was still a powerful old dragon. How could he submit so easily, especially when his enemy was at the end of his rope, nearly defeated?

It shouldn't be a problem. Jace had just countered Nefarian's mind control. He had some understanding of how the black dragon's mind magic worked. He was confident he could sever their magical connection.

With that thought, he reached for his second bottle of Destruction Potion — but stopped with his hand on the stopper.

Because he couldn't sense any dark magic connection between the black dragon and the red dragon. At least not like the situation back in Blackrock Spire when he and Nefarian had swapped minds.

What... was going on?

"I AM... awake enough, Somniel! You haven't seen what I've seen! If you understood... words are useless. You're all on the wrong path! Every one of you!"

Vaelastrasz suddenly reared up, clawing at his chest and head. Large scales fell away. "You just don't understand."

"Master Vieran! We've taken Blackrock Spire! We're about to win! Wake up!" Voices of elven knights called from the sky. Hearing them, Vaelastrasz's roar grew even more pained.

It was at this moment that Jace sensed something strange within him — as if... two forces were wrestling. Was he resisting the black dragon's mind magic?

Wait. It wasn't the lightning that had affected his mind. It wasn't the black dragon's mind control.

More and more gryphon and dragonhawk riders gathered, trying to circle around the red dragon to hunt down the dying black dragon behind him. But Vaelastrasz, even as he fought himself, scorched the sky with flame, and seemed to be sacrificing his own life force, converting it into corrupted death energy, "transfusing" it to Nefarian.

In just this short time, several flying knights had been shot down into the valley, alive or dead unknown. To get rid of Nefarian, they'd have to deal with Vaelastrasz.

But if Vaelastrasz hadn't tangled with Nefarian, that demon dragon would probably have already flown into Stormwind. The city's strongest fighters were all gathered near Blackrock Spire. No one left in the city could stop his rampage.

So if not for the red dragon's desperate efforts to hold back the Black Prince, Jace could hardly imagine what his parents would be facing. And now they wanted Jace to kill HIM?

Nefarian laughed. Even if Blackrock Spire was destroyed, this delicious pain and dilemma rushing toward him was enough to cheer him up again, enough to mock freely.

"What do we do, Sesso? Sister Sora? What do we do?"

"Needleflame... we need to decide fast! Vereesa! Jace must be hurt! And Stormwind is right ahead! His parents are there!"

Vereesa was silent for a moment. Sora seemed at a loss too, her voice turning tearful.

"Sora! The Dream... make him sleep!" the ranger suddenly shouted. "Your sleep magic is stronger than mine!"

"No... Vereesa, I can't either. He is Queen Alexstrasza's emissary and most honored guardian. I don't have enough power," Sora answered.

"The Destruction Potion, Sora!" Jace shouted, grasping at straws.

"The Destruction Potion... I can try, Jace!" "Then catch this!"

He hurled the bottle of Destruction Potion hard toward the source of Sora's voice. Sora burst from the smoke and swallowed the bottle whole.

But her attention was entirely on the potion Jace had thrown. Caught off guard, she was struck hard across the midsection by Vaelastrasz's tail. With a cry of pain, she crashed into the forest.

"Sister Sora!"

"Vereesa! Sora!"

Needleflame, barely dodging the red dragon's sweeping tail, wheeled in the sky and dove back. The red dragon below fixed a vicious glare on the spot where the dragon and elf had fallen. Facing the hole torn in the trees by the dragon's wide wings, he already had flame in his mouth.

"I'm sorry... child of Ysera... I must—"

A pleasant sound rang through the forest — like a green dragon's call, or some kind of bell. Vaelastrasz's words were cut off mid-sentence, frozen in place.

The red dragon's figure began to blur. Not just him — the forest, the valley, the burning flames. Everything grew blurry.

"It worked! Vaelastrasz is asleep! Seize the chance to—"

Jace wanted to shout, but it felt like no sound came out. Like a wad of cotton was stuck in his throat. Then he realized — it wasn't just Vaelastrasz who had fallen asleep. He had too. Giving a Destruction Potion to a green dragon really wasn't the best idea.

A cold wind swirled through the forest. Jace stared at the eye of the wind and felt drowsy.

Maybe he was already asleep. Because a grass-green bird landed on his shoulder. An ordinary bird couldn't survive in a hellscape like this.

"Come with me, Jace." The bird pecked his ear. Right, this was a dream. Even birds could talk. "Quickly!"

It was still Vereesa's voice.

Jace felt like someone was leading him, as if sinking into water. The cold surrounded him. The flames and smoke vanished.

When he opened his eyes again, he was still in this valley. But the valley had no life — no beasts, no people, no flying gryphons, not even trees or grass. The sky was gray. The earth was gray. Like the Burning Steppes, but without any fire. Without a shred of vitality, or even warmth.

"What's going on?" Jace asked.

"Looks like someone's dream," Vereesa said. "Do you recognize this place?"

Jace couldn't say for sure, but it reminded him of another world. One he'd seen in a game in his previous life.

More precisely, it was another timeline. Not the game's timeline — one that never actually happened even in the game.

Was this his own dream? How did he wake up?

"Wait, Jace." The ranger frowned. "I think I hear something. Follow me."

Jace nodded and followed. The ranger ran fast ahead, but as he followed, something ahead seemed wrong.

A blurry shadow appeared in his vision. Extremely strange. He didn't even notice when Vereesa led him into a bone-chilling cave.

"Where is this now?" Jace asked.

"I feel like you know more than I do, Jace." Vereesa turned back.

"If I'm not wrong... the Hour of Twilight," Jace said. "This looks like the world the Twilight Lord Gestayn described — the world after the Old Gods' Black Empire descends again, after everything falls under the void's control."

"The world Nefarian wanted to avoid?" Vereesa asked. "The world everyone wants to avoid," Jace said.

Vereesa nodded silently and continued forward. She seemed to be chasing that strange shadow too. They hadn't walked far through the winding cave when voices came from ahead, indistinct.

"I know... rest assured, I will... do everything in my power... to change this... I will."

"Vaelastrasz!" Vereesa called out, speeding up. The murmuring voice suddenly seemed flustered.

"Who?! No — why would anyone else be here? Lady Vereesa Windrunner, is that you? Don't tell me Jace Sesso is here too..."

Jace followed Vereesa into a stone chamber. Vaelastrasz sat on a dragon-claw throne at the back, staring at them in surprise.

"Vaelastrasz! What's going on?" Jace demanded.

"You've seen all this, and you still don't understand?!" Vaelastrasz pointed toward the cave entrance. "I saw everything. HE showed me everything. I must... I must prevent this world from descending on Azeroth."

"You are a guardian of LIFE!" Vereesa stepped forward and shouted. "How is what you're doing any different from him?! You want to protect all life from becoming this, so you'd rather destroy them all first?!"

"Because I hadn't seen it before, Vereesa! Now you've seen it too!" Vaelastrasz leaned forward and roared. "Was stopping Nefarian a mistake? Will this world inevitably slide toward this outcome because of my actions? I don't know! If all life has to live in a world like this because of me, then what kind of guardian am I?!"

"Don't overthink this, Vaelastrasz!" Jace stepped up. "Think of your mother. Queen Alexstrasza. As her emissary and servant, you can't skip her to make a decision this important."

"Wake up, Vaelastrasz! Go ask Queen Alexstrasza what to do! Whatever the answer, it's NOT protecting Nefarian!"

"My mother... the great queen. Yes, that's right." Vaelastrasz seemed to be talking to himself. "I must obey my mother. Jace Sesso is right. He reminded me — this has been the premise of everything I've done since I was born... Even you must obey. This is simply—"

"Obey WHO? You're not talking about Nefarian?" Vereesa paused, looking back at the cave entrance, then around. "Who else has been here?"

"It's not Nefarian." Jace stared into Vaelastrasz's eyes, about to chant — when a mass of shadow suddenly shot out from atop the red dragon's head, flying straight out of the cave. Vaelastrasz let out a pained roar. The dream began to collapse. Light and shadow shifted. Everything disintegrated... Though the shadow was hidden like part of the nightmare's light and shadow, Jace still caught it sharply.

Because it was a demon. A Nathrezim.
