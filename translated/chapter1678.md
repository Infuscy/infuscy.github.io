## Chapter 1678: Listen to Your Mother

"What was that? Did you see it, Jace?"

"I saw it."

"What the hell was it—"

Vereesa didn't even finish before the whole cave began collapsing, the ground sinking along with it. Everything was falling apart except the stone seat where Vaelastrasz sat.

Was the dream ending?

But this was nothing like the way dreams dissolved when a dreamer woke up before. The ground shook violently beneath his feet. Jace felt his footing give way and his body lurch — he tried to activate Shadowstep to fly out of the way, but the shadow energy in this plane responded far less clearly than in reality. In the end, Vereesa lunged over and grabbed his hand, holding on tight.

"Don't let go, Jace!" she shouted. "I don't know what happens if you die in here, in the real world. This place isn't a normal dream!"

Jace nodded, stepped on the stone slab in front of him to climb out, and was about to escape the cave together when Vereesa noticed Vaelastrasz still sitting on his throne behind them, murmuring something.

"Vaelastrasz! Come with us, now!"

But the red dragon paid them no attention at all. It was as if he was deliberately staying there, intending to collapse along with the cave.

"Vereesa!" Jace grabbed her wrist. "The key right now is finding that shadow. If we don't get rid of him, Vaelastrasz won't wake up!"

Vereesa shot Jace a surprised look, but she didn't hesitate or doubt. She simply nodded and led Jace out of the chamber before it caved in completely, back into the narrow tunnel.

The passage was a bit more stable, but the violent shaking continued. The two hurried forward, faster and faster, neither knowing what the deathly still "Twilight Hour" outside had turned into now. Whatever it was, they needed to reach open ground first.

After a few steps, the tunnel walls seemed to stop crumbling and cracking. Vereesa asked, "Do you know what that was, Jace?"

Of course Jace knew. Though he wasn't supposed to.

A demon. A demon that could slip into dreams and control others' minds. A demon that turned into a cloud of black mist when fleeing, complete with the sound of beating wings. To Vereesa it might be baffling, but to Jace it was crystal clear.

"A Nathrezim."

"Are you sure? Really one of those demons of the shadows?"

"Pretty sure."

Jace said it as all the suspicion and doubt along the way settled into solid certainty at that moment.

In another timeline, Kael'thas Sunstrider, after failing at the Tempest Keep, chose to fully align himself with the Burning Legion. After returning from Outland, he planned to use the Sunwell's power to summon the Deceiver, Kil'jaeden, directly into Quel'Thalas.

To stop his mad plan, the Spell-Weaver sent two young blue dragon emissaries, Kalecgos and Madigos, to help the Shattered Sun offensive push into the Sunwell Plateau, already occupied by the demon legions under Brutallus's command.

But Kalecgos had barely arrived before he fell into a Nathrezim's trap. The Corruptor, Sathrovarr, had slipped directly into his consciousness, enslaved his mind, and turned him into a gatekeeper for the Sunwell Plateau. Unless someone entered Kalecgos's mind and destroyed Sathrovarr, Kalecgos could never wake up.

Jace figured this was more or less the same situation. Maybe for a dreadlord, disguising as a dragon or directly controlling their minds was just too difficult.

To control them and achieve maximum destruction, they had to enter the dreams and manipulate their consciousness directly.

But which dreadlord was this? Was it Kruzath, the dreadlord who controlled the Soul Song in another timeline? Or the Corruptor Sathrovarr himself?

None of that mattered right now. What mattered was finding that demon. After all, stuck in this chaotic world, the two of them had enough trouble just surviving.

"So Nefarian really was working with the Burning Legion?" Vereesa asked as she hurried forward.

"Looks that way," Jace said. "The demon probably offered to help the black dragon break free from the Old Gods' control, in exchange for his loyalty."

"Breaking free from the Old Gods... by becoming undead?" Vereesa shook her head. "And what did the Burning Legion want from him in return?"

"To invade Azeroth. Kil'jaeden's always wanted that, plain and simple." Jace leaned against the wall. "A powerful shadow manipulator like Nefarian would be a huge help — opening portals, connecting dimensional pathways. The usual."

"But even Sabellian couldn't tolerate demons sharing a world with him," Vereesa said. "Could his prouder, stronger brother accept it?"

"Black dragons are all the same," Jace said. "Deathwing thought he was using the Old Gods. Sabellian thought he could use that scepter to control the Dragonmaw, even the Horde. Nefarian must've figured he could use the Burning Legion to throw off the Old Gods, then stab the demons in the back."

Vereesa jumped nimbly over a cracked crevice. "Kil'jaeden definitely knows that too."

"Very likely." Jace paused. "Right now I don't even know whether I hope he knows or not."

Vereesa sighed. "When Kadzan portaled us out back there, Jace — the whole of Blackrock Spire was crawling with reanimated corpses. Even Karrelz's felhound legion couldn't handle it all at once. I don't know if you could really stop Nefarian's spell, but for a moment I almost lost hope... Was that the dreadlord's scheme?"

"Even Marshal Anduin Lothar — Kadzan said the marshal's will had been twisted by Nefarian to defend the tormented souls of Blackrock Spire, and even his magic couldn't break through. Was that also what the demon taught Nefarian? The plan to trap us all inside Blackrock Spire?"

"But he never expected your magic could break everything." The ranger looked back at Jace. "You defeated Nefarian, Jace."

"With some help from the Potion of Destruction," Jace said.

"He had the Atama Crystal too." Vereesa dodged a falling piece of rubble. "Seems like the Soul Song was the centerpiece of his whole plan."

"When Nefarian was cursing your name in despair, everyone racing to the top of Blackrock Spire heard it. Turalyon, King Varian, Kadzan... Alleria even cried, because she knew you'd won — but she hadn't dared to be that optimistic before. None of us expected he'd come straight for you, and you'd have to face him alone..."

As she finished, the whole tunnel shook again. The two had to stop talking and steady themselves. Jace looked up. "Why does this dream feel so... real?"

"You can thank Ursula Delrin for that," the ranger said. "In a way, she helped you defeat Nefarian, helped Sora cast that sleeping spell deep enough to trap Vaelastrasz so he couldn't escape... Wait. The exit's up ahead."

"Why does getting out feel so long?" Jace squinted at the faint light ahead.

"Maybe... things have changed. We need to be careful." Vereesa lowered her voice.

Jace sensed a shadow flickering ahead again, but much fuzzier than before. He knew it was the Demon Detection Sight at work, but in the dream, the shadow was chaotic and indistinct, as if it had spread across a huge area.

Still — didn't that at least mean the demon was waiting for them up there?

Vereesa crawled out of the tunnel first. The moment she emerged, she froze. Jace followed right behind.

The valley that had been a barren wasteland was now lush and green. But this was definitely not the valley where Nefarian and Vaelastrasz had fought. Towering redwood trees surrounded them, making the whole place look more like the highlands of Khaz Modan...

In a nearby valley surrounded by trees, countless fiery red dragon eggs lay everywhere. Flocks of young red whelps circled overhead. Several massive adult red dragons sat near the eggs, murmuring to each other — chatting about the whelps, or maybe idly discussing the weather.

Alexstrasza was among them. She was looking at Jace and Vereesa with a kindly expression, exactly as Jace remembered the Dragonqueen.

"Jace. You're here."

It wasn't the Queen who spoke, but another red dragon beside her. That was Vaelastrasz.

Vereesa whispered, "You told him to listen to his mother. Now mother's here."

"You're starting to sound more and more like Grede, Vereesa," Jace muttered.

Vereesa sighed. "So... is the Queen the demon in disguise?"

"Hard to say," Jace mumbled.
