## Chapter 1679: Dreadlord

"Your Majesty."

Vereesa greeted Queen Alexstrasza loudly. The Queen nodded approvingly. "It's good to see you, Vereesa Windrunner. Having you and Jace Sesso here at the Highlands makes me very happy. Though it hasn't been long since our last meeting, it feels like ages..."

"Likewise, Your Majesty," Vereesa said obediently.

Seeing Vereesa and Queen Alexstrasza exchange pleasantries, Vaelastrasz looked positively overjoyed. Jace felt like he was watching a classmate introduce his best friends to his parents — especially since those two classmates were exceptionally good kids.

But this was all a dream. The red dragon palace was fake. The Queen, the dragons beside her, the dragon eggs — all fake. Even Vaelastrasz himself was fake, at least his physical form. He'd only placed a consciousness in this dream. The moment he woke up, everything would vanish...

The demon knew they knew it was all fake, too. Back in that cave, attached to Vaelastrasz's consciousness, he'd heard everything. He knew this human and this elf had come into this place specifically to hunt him down and wake Vaelastrasz up.

So why had he replaced the End of Time vision he'd shown the red dragon with this dragon roost scene? And why bother putting on this act with them?

But Jace quickly realized the performance wasn't for them. It was for Vaelastrasz. He thought all of this was real. The whole show was staged for him.

A dragon's loyalty to the Dragon Queen was practically carved into their bones. There was no changing that. Until they figured out exactly where the demon was, the two of them would have to play along with Vaelastrasz and the demon's charade. Otherwise, messing up the order of things would make Vaelastrasz put them on the wrong side of the divide.

"Though I'm delighted to see you, I cannot ignore the chaos and darkness outside this highland." The red Dragon Queen spoke with concern. "Ancient corruption is rising again, and this world has not fully recovered from its last grievous wounds. How will we survive this trial... Vaelastrasz has brought some very dire news. Jace, Vereesa, if we are to defeat the ultimate evil that seeks to drag our entire world into endless shadow... some sacrifices will inevitably be necessary."

"The Queen speaks wisely. The threat to the Emerald Dream has shown us the terrible cost of underestimating our enemy—"

Even as he said it, a hint of loss flickered across Vaelastrasz's face.

The other red dragons echoed their agreement, discussing the matter further. They seemed to have forgotten all about Vereesa and Jace.

"Patience, Jace." Vereesa reminded him quietly. "If we choose wrong, the consequences could be catastrophic. This is Vaelastrasz's dream..."

She was right. The dream's owner's disposition mattered. They had to find the demon's disguise in one shot. But Jace couldn't see anything off about these red dragons. The shadow in his demon sight spread across the entire hall. He could be sure the Nathrezim was nearby — maybe among these whelps and adult dragons. But which one?

Then Jace remembered what he'd said to the disguised demon back in that cave — *"This is the premise of everything I've done since I was born. Even you must obey."*

"Krasus. It's Korialstrasz."

"Are you sure, Jace?" Vereesa asked. "Why?"

"Obviously, in Vaelastrasz's dream, the demon turned into a red dragon with enough authority to influence him — but not the Queen." Jace lowered his voice. "All red dragons unquestioningly obey the Queen. So who could make Vaelastrasz show that much respect, maybe even feel that they didn't have to obey? Only Korialstrasz."

The red dragons were discussing the distant darkness, the terrible curses beneath the earth, how great a price they could pay to prevent the world from withering forever. The topic grew twisted and bizarre, edging toward whether undead even counted as life. Vaelastrasz just listened, not even showing much resistance anymore.

"What if he switched identities?" Vereesa whispered.

"I think changing targets isn't easy for that demon. Otherwise, why wouldn't he have just turned into the Queen herself for more convincing power, instead of picking some other red dragon?" Jace said. "And either way, if he wanted to change form — even in a dream — he'd have to use demonic power."

"From earlier until now, I haven't been able to pinpoint his exact location or identity with my demon sight, but I haven't felt any demonic power spreading. Instead, it's gotten weaker and more drained. I think he knows about my power, knows I can see demons. So to avoid my detection, he kept his original disguise, and hid himself deep inside one of Vaelastrasz's memories."

"You've convinced me," Vereesa said.

"Nefarian is draining Vaelastrasz's life force every moment. Let's decide quickly. It's him." Jace said.

Vereesa nodded. Jace stepped past a few dragon eggs and called out, "I have a question for you, Master Krasus!"

The red dragons parted. Korialstrasz looked up and said quietly, "Speak."

"Tell me — are you an ugly demon?!" Vereesa shouted as she pulled a dream-magic arrow from behind Jace. The power of Bellanur blazed brilliantly in this dream, so bright it outshone even the sunlit red dragon roost!

Every red dragon was startled. Even Alexstrasza reared up in shock. Vaelastrasz roared, "What are you doing, Vereesa?!"

But none of it could stop the released dream-light. Korialstrasz's body was pierced straight through. With a twisted, high-pitched howl, the entire dragon roost warped, decomposed, and shattered. But the demonic shadows gathered around the red dragon consort.

"Well done, Jace Sesso!"

The demon-tongued shout exploded with a hoarse, low laugh. A pair of bat-like demonic wings spread, blackening the entire field of vision until it was as dark as a starless night.

"What's happening? What is this?! Your Majesty? Master Krasus?!"

Vaelastrasz seemed unable to process the change. But in an instant, every other red dragon around him — adults, whelps, even the Queen herself — began to swell like rotting corpses at visible speed, then burst, spraying blood mist and buzzing flies that filled the entire space.

Jace felt like he was instantly surrounded by these flies, these carrion-feeding beetles. They gnawed at his skin, his flesh, even his eyes.

But no matter how he tried to cast spells or summon aid from elements, nothing worked. All the shadow energy around him seemed to have been devoured by these tiny bugs. The shadow and emotion he had stored in his consciousness were eaten clean by the blood-sucking insects crawling all over him. He couldn't cast anything!

Jace pulled out Quel'Serrar. In dream or reality, Malygos's blade was always at his side. But even though the sword could easily cut through swaths of bugs with a single swing, there were too many of them, and they kept gathering.

"Feeling powerless? Welcome to my nightmare, Jace Sesso." The flying demon shadow boomed. "If they'd known you were this easy to manipulate, perhaps the Deceiver needn't have gone to so much trouble. He always overthinks things."

"Whose nightmare is this, Nathrezim?!"

Vereesa's shout came from not far away. The shadow faltered at the sound.

The sky echoed with spine-chilling bird cries. Wings flashed back and forth, stirring up whirlwind after whirlwind that swept through the corrupted forest.

The swarming insects tried to resist, but the wind grew stronger and never stopped. Soon they were blown clean away, leaving only a heap of severed wings and broken limbs on the ground.

"Gurrul... Quel'dorei! You can manipulate the red dragon's dream?!"

"You underestimated the dream's owner's own will to resist, demon."

With the insects dispersed, shadow energy surged back. Both his visual and conscious field of vision cleared. Jace locked onto the shadow trying to flee again and activated Blood Grasp.

Blood light shot out like iron chains, yanking the high-flying shadow down. Several dream-arrows flew over and pierced the entangled shadow. Whatever was hidden inside finally couldn't bear it anymore. It stumbled and fell to the ground, revealing its true form.

Its face was pale and gaunt like a vampire's. A pair of curved horns rose from its head. Its body was gaunt and tall. But its most striking features were the gray wings behind it — like bones, or spider legs — with tattered wing membranes.

A dreadlord. No question.

The demon staggered forward a couple of steps. Its black, cloven hooves trod on the piled insect corpses. Its demonic eyes, glowing with fel-green light, locked onto Vereesa Windrunner in the distance. The magnificent emerald armor it wore was already pierced by arrows, purple demon blood flowing out.

"Vita, tahodossi, milon, tahototani!"

Jace shouted the demon-enslaving incantation. A mark appeared on the demon's forehead. But the demon laughed twice, casually swiping the sigil away with its vulture-like claws.

"Enslaving a noble Nathrezim? Creative, mortal!" the demon sneered.

"Who are you?!" Jace gathered another spell of Suffocation in his hands.

"Me? I'm nobody." The dreadlord spoke in a very old, deep voice. "As you can see, I've basically completed my mission. The ending just wasn't perfect... Anyway, nothing after this concerns me. Good luck, Jace Sesso."

The moment the demon finished, the powerful Suffocation spell locked tight around its upper body and wings. But the Nathrezim didn't care at all. It leaped high, shrieking, and exploded in a flash of light — flesh and demon heart bursting apart. Only its armor clattered to the ground. The Suffocation had nothing left to hold.

"Trying to run?"

Jace raised both hands and chanted the Soul Drain incantation. A blue light in his palms lit up the surrounding woods. A distant demonic scream was dragged back...

"No, no... Lord Usar'ru is not in this dream!"

"Then I'll take you to meet him."
