## Chapter 1680: The Dragon's End

With the dreadlord destroyed, body and soul, the forest that had already fallen into darkness began to melt. This time it wasn't the earthquake-like collapse from before, but the whole field of vision twisting and warping in blurry light and distortion.

"Vaelastrasz is watching everything. I think he finally understands," Jace said.

"He's waking up, Jace!"

The harpies scattered and flew far away. Vereesa stood on a high branch, shouting, "Take a deep breath, Jace. Breathe."

Jace nodded. He sucked in a breath — then immediately started coughing.

When he opened his eyes again, he was in a misty forest. The air was acrid.

He was back. Back in this valley outside Northshire. But before he could even get up, a furious dragon roar ahead shook the smoke and dust between the trees, nearly knocking him back down. That was Vaelastrasz. He was finally awake.

"Sesso!"

Needlefang crawled over like a giant dark brown cat. She lowered her head. "You're awake. Sister Sora said you two might sleep a long time... She told me to stay by you."

"Thanks, Needlefang."

Jace glanced at the soul shard in his palm. This was the dreadlord's soul. He'd wanted to enslave it and interrogate it about what Kil'jaeden was planning. The demon clearly knew something.

But he hadn't counted on the Nathrezim having such strong mental magic resistance. On one hand, he'd underestimated the willpower of a demon specializing in mind magic. On the other, a dream was clearly more the dreadlord's home turf — it was harder to suppress him there.

One soul shard wasn't enough for what he needed. It was just a lump of energy — far more powerful than an ordinary soul shard since it came from a high-ranking demon — but it couldn't answer questions or resolve doubts.

Still, Jace didn't dwell on it. The chaos wasn't over.

Squadrons of gryphon riders swept overhead. Higher up, dragonhawk riders raised their arcane-glowing spears, aiming north.

"Nefarian! Have you sunk so low that you let demons enslave and command you?!"

Vaelastrasz's roar shook the earth. His rage was like real fire, scorching the forest. The shockwave sent the trees around them swaying back and forth.

"Get on, Sesso." Needlefang lowered her back. "Or did Sister Sora's spell hit too hard and leave you groggy?"

Jace nodded, jumped on, and grabbed her shoulders. The black dragon shot into the sky.

Before them, Vaelastrasz spread his wings, his whole body wreathed in golden flames. And in front of him, the much larger Nefarian seemed even weaker.

The undead dragon's bones and remaining flesh were all burning. The stench of scorched rot filled the whole valley. Now that Vaelastrasz had woken up, the life energy Nefarian had managed to drain was surging back as scorching life-flames, mercilessly burning his decaying body.

"I did this for my offspring, my kind, for this world, Vaelastrasz!" The black dragon's teeth ground audibly. "And you — you just stupidly follow the commands the gods gave you, running like some idiotic machine built by goblins and gnomes. You and every other red dragon... And the gods you worship and believe in have probably rotted away in the Pantheon by now. They don't care about life, don't care about the guardian mission you value above everything!"

With every struggle, every clash and bite, every breath of flame they traded, the black dragon prince seemed to shrink. Not to mention the lightning hammers and arcane magic constantly pounding into him.

He still looked savage and fierce, seemingly indifferent to the encircling Alliance riders. But his body clearly couldn't hold out anymore. The Atama Crystal shard had dimmed to the point you couldn't see it with your eyes, and even felt blurry to sense.

Needlefang carried Jace closer to the two entangled dragons. Nefarian caught sight of the black dragon overhead. Jace suddenly felt his emotions shift — the iron will that had seemed ready to fight to the death collapsed almost instantly.

The black dragon prince wrenched himself free from Vaelastrasz, turned and crawled backward. He spread his enormous black wings, like two rotting, burning banyan trees, and flapped them symbolically. But he no longer had the strength to fly.

Jace could tell — it wasn't his appearance that broke Nefarian. It was Needlefang.

The Nefarian before him now could barely be connected to the chaotic shadow that had appeared atop Blackrock Spire. He was like an ancient dragon on the verge of death, his bones and joints rotting and falling apart.

The slopes and forests he could have casually leveled now seemed like rows of upright razors. Every step forward cost him chunks of flesh and dried skin...

In that moment, Jace even saw the Atama Crystal shard glow again. But the light had nothing to do with Nefarian's magic anymore.

It brightened because of the sudden surge of intense despair and regret boiling up from Nefarian's own mind.

Finally, Nefarian collapsed onto a low hill. The rocks and soil cracked and slid under the weight of his massive head and body.

"I... envy you, little sister," the dragon murmured. The voice didn't sound like it came from his burning throat, but like an echo of dark magic.

Needlefang said nothing. She just watched the prince. She knew Nefarian wasn't referring to Onyxia.

"Tell me about the dreadlord, Prince Nefarian." Jace looked down at his tilted head. "Kil'jaeden was just using you. You don't need to protect those demons."

"True." The black dragon laughed hoarsely. "The demons did deceive me... But you won't get anything out of me either, Jace Sesso."

"Kil'jaeden will kill you. And I'll be waiting for you in Usar'ru. See you later, little one."

A violent purple flame burst from the black dragon's body. His mad laughter mixed with shadowy screams, as if they could drag anyone who heard them straight to hell.

The surrounding Alliance riders scattered in terror. Paladins and priests prayed to the Holy Light for protection as they retreated down the hillside. Even Vaelastrasz was forced back several steps by the blazing flames of corruption.

Soon, the enormous, horrifying black dragon corpse burned down to dry bones, cracked with purple light. Usar'ru had consumed Nefarian's soul. For a moment, even the deep red of the weapon wasn't quite pure — it flickered with a hint of purple light.

After devouring that dark soul, Jace, as the master and holder of Usar'ru, naturally received magical enhancement. So despite having just been through a fierce battle and the dreadlord's torment in the dream... he had to admit he hadn't felt this good since tearing up the *Book of Unholy Names*.

But this time, he wasn't as excited as when he'd gained power in Draenor. He could feel that Nefarian's cooperation with the dreadlords was far from simple. This was far from over.

Vaguely, cheers erupted across the sky and ground. Everyone was shouting "Long live the Alliance," "Long live Marshal Lothar," "Long live Azeroth." Many veteran soldiers were weeping.

They hugged each other, high-fived. Nearby soldiers ran and jumped, cheering toward Jace and Needlefang, shouting Jace's name.

The sky seemed darker than when Molten Core was still burning under Ragnaros's power. Maybe because Nefarian's terrible magic hadn't faded, maybe because night was falling.

But at least Blackrock Spire was safe.
