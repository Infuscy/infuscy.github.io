Chapter 1681: More Than One

The black dragon prince's corpse, sprawled on the hillside, was still flickering with flames. Jace and Needlefang — back in human form — stood by his teeth, looking up at his now-stilled head.

All around his body, countless soldiers were searching for fallen gryphons and their riders, tending to the wounded. Set against these mortal figures, the dragon's corpse seemed so enormous that it wasn't a dead creature at all, but some miraculously carved sculpture that had consumed immense labor and time — utterly unreal.

"He called me little sister," Needlefang said quietly.

"You're all Deathwing's children," Jace said.

Needlefang was silent for a moment. "Maybe I should hate him. But somehow I can't."

"When he was using magic against you from the top of Blackrock Spire, I wanted to tear through those walls and fight him to the death. But now — or at least since a while ago..."

"Because everything he did," Jace said, "whatever means he ultimately chose, was about breaking free from the Old Gods. It was about... you and your kind."

"Mmm," Needlefang murmured.

Jace sighed. "Breaking free from the Old Gods — even Deathwing couldn't manage that. For him to try was even harder. When people face impossible problems, a lot of them make terrible, irreversible choices. We all know that. But... not many actually pick the right option."

"If I ever have to make a choice like that," Needlefang said quietly, "I hope I pick right."

"I hope you never have to make such a terrible choice," Jace said.

"Jace!"

A hoarse dragon roar came from above. Sora descended, carrying Vereesa on her back. The ranger kept her eyes fixed on the dead dragon, as if he might suddenly revive and catch them all off guard.

"What did he say to you, Jace?" Vereesa called out. "Did he mention the Nathrezim?"

Sora landed, let Vereesa jump off, then shifted to human form and walked over. "Are you talking about... a demon?"

"Inside Vaelastrasz's dream, Sora." Vereesa said. "He was the one possessing the red dragon and influencing his mind — not Nefarian's magic."

Jace shook his head. "Nefarian definitely knew something. But he didn't want to tell me. It's clear Kil'jaeden wanted to use him to get rid of me, and he was happy to go along with it."

Vaelastrasz, now in human form, came over to Jace. Most dragons, when taking human form, tried not to let their wounds and defects show. But right now, even as a human, Vaelastrasz looked utterly exhausted — which said plenty about how badly he was hurt.

"My apologies, everyone." The red dragon bowed his head. "If not for my carelessness, there wouldn't have been so much extra trouble. Nefarian would probably have fallen by now."

"We have Sora's sleeping spell to thank for that," Jace said. "Otherwise we wouldn't have had any way to deal with you."

"Not entirely my doing, Jace." Sora glanced at the elf beside her. "I underestimated the Potion of Destruction's power. The sleeping mist I cast nearly put me under too... Besides Lord Vaelastrasz, a lot of gryphon and dragonhawk riders got caught. Lucky Vereesa seized the chance to take you into the dream. Otherwise I'd probably have had to face Prince Nefarian alone."

"Even that brief loss of control caused terrible harm," Vaelastrasz said. "I hurt many Alliance heroes. I'll request to stay in the Redridge Mountains from the Queen, to mend the wounds Nefarian and Ragnaros inflicted on nature here. Until everything is restored. I believe the Queen will grant me the opportunity, and I have the strength to complete this mission."

"I want to know," Jace said. "When did the demon enter your mind?"

"I can't be sure." Vaelastrasz shook his head. "Probably while I was inside Blackrock Spire. Lady Morofil said she smelled the scent of insect swarms driven by a Nathrezim... But at the time, the whole tower was filled with corpses and insects. Nefarian's magic had reanimated many of them. We had to immediately deal with the first wave of resurrected skeletal whelps and dragonkin, until you used soul magic to interrupt Nefarian's ritual."

"What about Morofil?" Vereesa asked.

"She should still be in Blackrock Spire," Vaelastrasz said. "She said Jace gave her the most important task — hunting the Nathrezim."

"But we killed that Nathrezim in your dream with our own hands, Vaelastrasz." Vereesa said. "I saw it myself. When it realized it couldn't escape Jace's magic, it destroyed its own body trying to flee back to the Twisting Nether. Then Jace caught its soul... Unless — there was more than one Nathrezim."

"What if one lured Morofil away, and another possessed the red dragon at the same time?" Needlefang asked.

At this, Jace couldn't help rubbing his forehead. But Kil'jaeden had sent more than one dreadlord to watch the Lich King in Northrend. Now he not only had to watch Ner'zhul, but also deal with Usar'ru's master. Sending extra help through various means made perfect sense.

He should have thought of this earlier. A dreadlord hiding in the Kirin Tor, and another here at Blackrock Spire... Even if a dreadlord could fly, disguise itself, and teleport between regions through the Twisting Nether, doing everything alone would be hard to conceal — especially carrying something like the Soul Song, a magical artifact so powerful its energy was impossible to hide. In a place like Dalaran or the Wizard Sanctum, it would have been nearly impossible not to get caught.

Vaelastrasz held out the Soul Song. "Take this to the draenei prophet — to its original owner. Let him examine it. If the Nathrezim really did bring this from Draenor to give to Nefarian, maybe he can sense something from it."

Jace took the Atama Crystal shard. "I was definitely planning to visit him."

"I think there's someone else who might know more about the demons, Jace," Sora said.

"Who?" Jace asked.

"Princess Onyxia," Sora said. "Now it makes more sense why she chose to break with Prince Nefarian... Prince Nefarian must have wanted to turn her undead too, then perform the ritual together. But Onyxia refused, and eventually they parted ways."

"I agree that's possible. The black dragons killed in the upper spire might have been on Onyxia's side," Vaelastrasz said. "Nefarian must have thought — since they wouldn't agree to the plan, kill them all and raise them as an undead legion."

"But you're forgetting about Chromaggus's wing," Jace said. "Grede was sure it was Onyxia's."

Hearing this, Sora seemed to recall the scene. She frowned uncomfortably. "Alright. I overlooked that."

"The wing?" Vaelastrasz asked, surprised. "I didn't look closely at that aberrant creature's corpse... But are you saying she's dead?"

"Very possibly," Vereesa said. "But we didn't actually see her body. At least not in Blackrock Spire."

Jace thought for a moment. "Remember what Nefarian said in Blackrock Spire? He said all the foolish, short-sighted, and cowardly kin would be left behind — including Onyxia."

"So if Princess Onyxia was killed by him," Vereesa said, "she should have been reanimated along with the rest in his ritual, turned into part of his new legion. Not 'left behind'!"

"That's what I was thinking," Jace said. "Nefarian thought he'd already won at that point. There was no need for him to lie."

"So Princess Onyxia might still be hiding in Kalimdor, guarding the last of the Blackflight's offspring and eggs," Sora said. "Could we pay her a visit and ask? She might have personally witnessed the demons' contact with Prince Nefarian."

"Pay her a visit... Sora, are you serious?" Vereesa frowned.

"I don't think it's impossible," Vaelastrasz said. "Onyxia values the Blackflight's future above everything. If demons urged Nefarian to turn all Blackflight offspring undead to fulfill Deathwing's goal of world domination, it's hard to say whether Onyxia would hate the demons more — or us."

Jace said, "We still need to get back to Blackrock Spire as soon as possible. See if there are any other clues about Onyxia. Prepare for every possibility. Also, we need to ask Morofil if she tracked anything down."
