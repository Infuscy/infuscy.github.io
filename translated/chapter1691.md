## Chapter 1691: How Can You Lose Without Gaining

"Prophet." Jace stepped forward and nodded.

"I am glad to see you well, Jace Sesso." Velen spoke in Common with an accent Jace could barely detect. "The victory at Blackrock Spire is significant not only for the Alliance, but also for me, for the Draenei. It means the Horde's bloody invasion of another world has been thoroughly defeated. Since Gul'dan used demonic magic to unite the orc clans, decades of suffering and slaughter have finally come to an end."

"The Draenei are honored to have taken part in this campaign. But I also know this cannot begin to make up for the enormous trouble the Soul's Song caused you. When Arcanist Semid brought word to Stonard that a lost fragment of the Ata'mal Crystal might be inside Blackrock Spire, I was shocked. In all the years it was lost, we never heard that it had been used by orcs or demons. I thought it had been lost in the Twisting Nether. I never expected it would come to Azeroth with us, and become a tool for a powerful necromancer."

"Today it returns to its rightful owner," Jace said.

Ever since he'd taken this soul fragment from Nefarian's body, Jace hadn't felt the Ata'mal Crystal grant its wielder any particular power. He'd assumed that Nefarian's final magical ritual and desperate resistance had drained the crystal's energy.

But it wasn't until this moment, when he was about to hand the crystal fragment over, that he felt a surge of overwhelming mana spreading from his palm up through his arm and throughout his entire body.

He found that deep down, he was almost hoping the prophet before him would reveal some greedy desire or possessiveness, giving him a reason to question whether this Draenei leader was truly worthy of holding this magical treasure.

But no. The prophet's heart was so calm that Jace couldn't sense anything at all. His aged, bark-like purple wrinkles were utterly still as he gazed at Jace's palm. The warlock straightened his arm fully and spread his fingers, exposing the Soul's Song to the prophet and everyone nearby.

Velen raised his hand. Without even reaching for the crystal fragment, the Soul's Song was lifted by an invisible, colorless arcane energy and floated slowly toward him.

The moment the Ata'mal Crystal fragment left Jace's control, he felt an immense relief. The wild thoughts that had been plaguing him melted away. His entire body relaxed, as if he'd just struggled free from a thick, black mud pit. But he was also exhausted to the core.

He took a deep breath, dispersing the remaining chaotic emotions in his chest, and spoke as calmly as he could. "I have many questions I'd like to ask you, Prophet. About this fragment. We have considerable evidence suggesting the Ata'mal Crystal fragment was likely brought to Nefarian by the Burning Legion, to create chaos inside Blackrock Spire and cripple the Alliance's army."

Velen nodded. Jace continued. "Now that we've recovered this fragment, is there any way to trace it back to the demons? Through sympathetic magic, perhaps?"

"The power contained within the Ata'mal Crystal far exceeds our imagination. The Soul's Song represents focus and spiritual strength. Magical residue cannot leave any discernible trace upon it," Velen said.

"So this fragment can't tell us anything?" Jace asked.

"In the past, the Soul's Song helped me communicate with the Naaru across the endless Twisting Nether." Velen said. "Now, I don't know if I still have the ability... or the right... to borrow its power again."

He stared at the fragment of energy rotating in midair, silent for a moment. Then he said, "But I am willing to try, Jace Sesso. Come with me."

Jace wasn't entirely sure what Velen intended to do. But seeing the giant, ancient tree-like figure of the Draenei turn and walk toward a hillside, he followed.

The prophet and the warlock walked along an inconspicuous path into a canyon beside Morgan's Post. Once inside, Jace could feel that this was a place where the ley energy was relatively rich. Velen cast the Soul's Song into the air a short distance away, suspending it over an empty space. Then he chanted a strange Eredar incantation.

Violet arcane mist immediately rose from the ground. Glowing magical runes wove together into a disc-like arcane array, spinning around the Soul's Song at its center. The Draenei's eyes blazed with dazzling magical light. The arcane sigils above his head lit up, vibrating with the roar of arcane power.

When the disc's rotation stabilized, the light in Velen's eyes dimmed somewhat, but his expression grew far more serious. His eyes began to tremble. He was clearly seeing something.

"Kurthasaz, the nathrezim lord sent by Kil'jaeden to hunt the Ata'mal Crystal fragment. I see his face." Velen murmured in Eredar. "He found the Soul's Song in the ruins of Gorgrond. From those warlocks... Then he died. Destroyed in the Twisting Nether."

"Why?" Jace asked.

Velen didn't respond, as if he hadn't heard Jace's question. After a moment, he continued. "The Soul's Song was given by Kil'jaeden to another nathrezim. I believe he brought it to Azeroth and delivered it to the black dragon."

"Who is he?" Jace asked.

"I cannot be certain, Jace Sesso. Perhaps I should see more clearly." Velen replied quietly.

Honestly, even if Velen couldn't see the whole picture, Jace could already guess part of it.

In another timeline, Kurthasaz found the Soul's Song and believed himself above the Burning Legion's rule. Even when Kil'jaeden's lieutenant, the Eredar lord Socrethar, demanded he hand over the Soul's Song, he didn't care. He planned to carve out his own domain in Farahlon and use the Soul's Song to build his own legion.

But he wasn't Nefarian. He overestimated himself. The ethereals hired mercenaries from the Alliance and Horde, joined forces to defeat him and his demon private army, took back the Soul's Song, and returned it to the Naaru in Shattrath City.

Clearly, in this timeline, he'd had the same idea. Except this time, Kil'jaeden had eliminated him early and given the Soul's Song to a more trustworthy dreadlord to carry out the next phase of the plan.

"I've heard Khadgar say that the Ata'mal Crystal is extremely important, both to the Burning Legion and to the Draenei." Jace said. "Why did Kil'jaeden, after getting the Soul's Song, not protect it? Why hand it over to a demon underling and risk bringing it to Azeroth?"

"Remember this well." Velen said quietly. "Kil'jaeden's purpose from beginning to end has been to destroy this world -- Azeroth. Our suffering, Draenor's fate, are merely steps in his plan. The Ata'mal Crystal fragments are powerful tools to achieve his goal."

As he said this, his eyes began to tremble again. The circular array beneath the Ata'mal Crystal seemed to ignite, releasing blinding magical light. The canyon walls and dead trees were bathed in a faint pinkish-purple hue.

Then, Velen's expression grew even more grave. The array's rotation became erratic, speeding up and slowing down. His eyes darted back and forth, their emitted light sweeping the heights like searchlights. Jace could feel a deep unease emanating from the aged prophet's heart, disturbing all the calm that had been there before.

"What did you see, Prophet?" Jace asked.

Velen didn't answer. He just maintained the ritual until it abruptly ended. All the arcane brilliance dispersed in an instant, leaving only lingering magical mist, like a group of Quel'Thalas sorcerers who'd just cast mass teleportation.

Jace couldn't help stepping forward again. "What did you see, Prophet?"

Velen grabbed the Soul's Song floating before him and held it tightly in his hands, but remained silent. Jace tried desperately to read through the shadow fluctuations what was worrying him. But Velen just looked at the warlock beside him. The turmoil in his heart quickly settled back into calm. The last trace of readable emotion vanished.

"You saw some kind of omen, didn't you?" Jace asked. "Not just what happened in the past. The future too."

The Draenei continued to be silent, as if he didn't know how to answer this human's question.

Jace sighed. "I know... Prophet. I know you're not sure which of those omens are prophecies of the future and which are chaotic illusions. But even if they're just illusions."

"Will I spend the rest of my life in a magical prison?"

"You will regain your freedom, Jace Sesso." Velen replied.

"Regain... freedom?"

Jace froze. On one hand, he hadn't expected Velen to answer so decisively. On the other hand, that answer...

Regain freedom?

Did that mean he'd have to be locked up first?

Just as Jace was trying to figure out how to process this answer, Velen seemed to relax, releasing the tension and gravity he'd been carrying.

But the next second, he held the Soul's Song out to Jace.

"Take it."

"Me?"

"You recovered this fragment from a pack of cunning nathrezim and Deathwing's most powerful child. I lost it under the attack of a rabble of demon-worshipping fanatics. You are more worthy of holding this relic than I am."

"I'm not so sure about that." Jace said. "You've protected it for thousands of years."

"That was only possible through the Naaru's guidance." Velen said. "If you find you cannot maintain the faith, you may return it to us. It will not be too late."

Jace accepted the Soul's Song. But this time, he didn't feel that invisible, crushing weight.

"Very well, then, Prophet."
