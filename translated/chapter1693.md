## Chapter 1693: Planning Ahead

Finally back in Elwynn Forest, Jace breathed in the cool, clean air -- fragrant with leaves and soil -- and almost hated to take more than a couple of breaths.

Though it had only been two months since he'd last passed through here, things were different. No death knight rumors this time. No pack of paladins chasing him. The black devil who'd had his eye on him from Blackrock Spire had lost his head, now riding behind him. Relaxed air and tense air tasted completely different.

"Actually, I've been thinking about where your statue should go," Varian Wrynn, riding beside him, suddenly said.

"What?" Jace blinked. "Isn't it supposed to be at Nethergarde Keep?"

"That statue was built by the soldiers guarding the Dark Portal to thank you. Now it's not just me -- the whole kingdom, even the Alliance of Lordaeron, wants this." Varian said. "So this is more of a notice than a request for your opinion... My initial idea is to place you and your horse right in the middle of the Trade District fountain. I'll need to discuss the specifics with Mister Edwin VanCleef further."

"We just fought a war, Your Majesty." Jace said quietly. "Putting up another statue there would cost a lot."

"Archmage Khadgar originally wanted the Dalaran wizards to handle it. But you wouldn't believe how enthusiastic the Stonemasons are about this." Varian said. "At this point, a lot of things can't be measured in gold coins. The Orc Wars are truly over. People need a symbol, something to commemorate all of this. To remember everything that happened, and then keep living, keep moving forward. Your image fits perfectly."

"I think I understand," Jace replied.

Hearing the king say this, Jace couldn't help starting to imagine what it would feel like to see himself every time he passed through the Trade District.

He'd practically never gone back to see that statue at Nethergarde Keep since the Dark Portal campaign. He could even ignore its existence. But this next one -- there was no way he could pretend it didn't exist.

He wondered what Mom and Dad would think when they saw it. Vic would definitely be grinning from ear to ear, while insisting on critiquing it and pointing out all the differences between the statue and the real Jace. He could almost imagine exactly what his brother would say.

He could barely suppress the smile tugging at his lips. But Varian was right there, so he couldn't look too pleased. That would make him look too much like a real evil warlock.

Up ahead, Alleria Windrunner riding her dark green hawkstrider looked back, tugged the reins, and slowed down until she was level with Jace's Midnight. Vereesa followed beside her sister.

"King Varian, we have something to discuss with Jace," Alleria said.

"Of course, Captain Alleria." Varian gave Jace an amused glance, then rode ahead, leaving Jace alone between the two elves.

"Blackrock Spire has fallen. The Sons of Lothar have achieved their goal. This continent has returned to peace." Alleria tilted her head. "I don't know how human stories continue from here. But according to the romantic legends of Quel'Thalas, this is where the prince and princess 'live happily ever after.'"

"Human stories are about the same, Captain Alleria." Jace said. "In any time. In any dimension."

"Perfect, then." Alleria lightly clapped her hands. "When you get back from Kalimdor, we'll get everything done. Turalyon and I are ready. And as for her second sister... I think she's been planning it for who knows how long."

"A Windrunner family wedding." She turned to look at Vereesa. "Can you imagine it, Little Moon? If Mother and Lirath knew, they'd be even happier and more excited than we are."

"I don't doubt that for a second, sister." Vereesa nodded. "When the time comes, Jace, I'll take you to Angelista's in Stratholme to pick out a proper suit of formal wear. You absolutely cannot show up at our wedding in that demon robe. It would be a total disaster!"

"No need to plan every detail so early. You should focus more on enjoying the long journey ahead of you two." Alleria said.

"What journey? We're going to Kalimdor on an important mission..." Vereesa emphasized. Then she switched to Thalassian. "And besides, sister, I'm tingling from the tips of my hair to the tips of my toes right now! How can I not start planning early?"

Jace listened to the two elves bickering back and forth, then looked over at Turalyon in the distance. The paladin could only give a helpless "what can I do?" shrug.

"After the wedding, let me take you, the dwarf, and the Sessos on a tour of Quel'Thalas." Vereesa said. "We'll have plenty of time this time. We can visit every corner thoroughly."

"I want to see it too, Vereesa." Sora said from behind. "I've never seen the Quel'dorei kingdom in reality."

"Of course, Sora," Vereesa said.

"Sun King and the Prince will be delighted by your visit, Lady Somallier." Alleria nodded.

"The honor is mine, Captain Alleria." Sora bowed her head slightly in return.

"I'm coming too." Needleflame had somehow appeared beside them.

"Of course you are," Vereesa said.

Alleria hesitated for a moment. "Sylvanas and I plan to invite many human and dwarf nobles and important guests. I'm sure a guardian of the Dream would be welcome too. But Needleflame... I mean, if she's a hero of the Blackrock Spire campaign, of course there's no problem. But..."

"She can't show up in Quel'Thalas as a black dragon," Vereesa said. "I know."

"Then I'll go to your homeland as a great human warrior." Needleflame lifted her chin. "I'm way better than those orc warriors anyway!"

"Nefarian is dead. Onyxia is in hiding. And Queen Alexstrasza has recognized Needleflame's identity." Sora said. "Isn't this the perfect opportunity... to reveal her existence to the world?"

Alleria said, "In Quel'Thalas, there are elders two to three thousand years old -- including the Sun King himself. Their ancestors, or even their fathers, personally experienced the destruction of the Well of Eternity. They witnessed Deathwing's transformation from leader of the Dragonflight to a traitor who wanted to destroy everything. Many of their loved ones died to the terrible power of the Demon Soul. Their memories are still vivid... I don't think they'll be able to accept this overnight. But it's precisely for this reason that they were willing to accept Jace's shadow magic so quickly -- because he truly defeated Deathwing."

"I'll change their minds back again someday." Needleflame said quietly.

"I'll do everything I can to help you," Jace said.

"You're not much better off than I am, Jace Sesso. Who's helping who?"

The black dragon flicked her reins and galloped ahead, leaving three elven women stifling laughter and one warlock standing there bewildered, wondering what he'd done to upset the little brat this time.

"I just remembered something, Jace." Sora said. "On this trip to Kalimdor, is there a chance we could visit Ashenvale and meet the Kaldorei High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind? Grede and I promised her we'd bring news to you and Vereesa."

"I remember," Jace said. "After we see Onyxia and Mosa, we'll go to Astranaar."

"Just thinking about meeting her makes me nervous," Vereesa said.

"Being nervous is only natural." Alleria said. "I know that after the War of the Ancients, our ancestors lived under her leadership on Mount Hyjal. And that was eight thousand years ago."

As she spoke, a cool breeze carrying the scent of earth blew through the forest. Vereesa raised a hand. "It's raining."

"It's fine. The column is almost to Goldshire," Alleria said.
