## Chapter 1709: A Brother

"What's happening? Is the wind talking?!"

Grede shouted, but in the intensifying sandstorm, Jace could barely tell which direction his voice was coming from.

"Careful, everyone. We might be surrounded by elementals!"

Jace drew the Song of Clear Skies as he shouted. Vereesa raised Belanore, the colored light on her bowstring flickering unsteadily in the furious storm as if the dream energy was disturbed by the gale. Solara pulled her cloak tight and drove her staff into the ground to keep from being blown away.

"What elementals?"

Needleflame had just drawn her Blade of Destruction, its searing lava-light burning fiercely through the dust for a moment, when Jace suddenly felt the storm shift direction, tearing violently toward Needleflame. He tried to shout a warning, but before he could, the little black dragon yelped, her figure vanished, and her voice flew off somewhere into the distance.

"Needleflame!"

Solara called out, her footing shifting as she nearly got blown sideways. Jace tried to move toward her and Grede, but a thick wall of wind and sand blocked his path.

When that wall hit him, stones and gravel slammed into his body. Even through the Hide of Orastraz, it felt like being shot by a Dark Iron dwarf's fragmentation rifle — knocking him back several steps.

When he focused forward again, he couldn't even see the others.

Thunderaan's voice was completely suppressed here. Jace could feel him trying to speak, but whatever sound he made was diluted by the howling gale. The cold emanating from him was weaker than ever.

Jace drove the Song of Clear Skies into a stone wall beside him, anchoring himself against the raging sandstorm. The storm was trying to split them apart, lose them in the chaos, using sand to block vision while elemental power dispelled all magical auras — from arcane to life.

And the strategy was working. Vereesa could barely stand in this gale, let alone cast spells, shoot arrows, or find her bearings.

The storm had hit so suddenly that Grede hadn't even had time to unwrap Sulfuras before he tumbled down a dune.

Solara and Needleflame were still in mortal form. Without Grede's weight to anchor them, they couldn't move at all. Shifting back to dragon form would be worse — their wings would catch the wind and make it even harder to stay grounded, let alone fly. Better to stay humanoid and huddle against a wall.

But whatever elemental was controlling this sandstorm had overlooked one thing: an energy that even gale-force winds couldn't dispel. The raw fury radiating from the elements themselves.

Jace closed his eyes, ignoring the wind whipping his cloak and robes. At the heart of the sandstorm, he reached out with his senses and grasped that surging elemental rage.

It had been a long time since he'd freely wielded this much shadow — not since Stormwind. This wasn't so much about dealing with the incoming wind elemental as it was a chance to release some long-suppressed cravings.

He chanted, entering shadowform, and raised his free left hand to channel darkness. He infused a Curse of Agony into the shadow source, amplifying the emotions he could manipulate, twisting the wind elemental's fury.

Soon he heard a sharp, whistling wind-sound. Whoever was controlling this sandstorm had fallen into confusion. The storm could feel his intrusion. He could sense that will searching everywhere for him, growing more agitated by the moment.

Jace used that swelling agitation to further twist the elemental's shadow energy, disturbing its emotions, interfering with its power over the elements. Even though he couldn't see exactly where the elemental was in the heart of the storm, the movement of shadow energy was enough for his senses to lock onto its location — and this opponent was already struggling.

Sure enough, as he deepened the shadow corruption, the wind weakened and visibility improved. Building shadows reappeared. In the distance, he heard Vereesa shouting — she seemed to be fighting something.

But just as Jace shifted his gaze to locate the others, a chaotic whirlwind charged at him. Gravel and debris spun with continuous explosions of lightning that detonated right in front of him!

But after Nefarian's shadow lightning, this thunder released by clashing winds was nothing to Jace.

He grabbed the Hide of Orastraz and threw it up, blocking the lightning strike without even using a shadow shield. The whirlwind in front of him let out a hoarse roar. Jace chanted, summoning flame to wrap around it, disrupting its elemental power, then infused shadow to corrupt the whirlwind directly, turning it into a spinning ball of shadowflame. The elemental bindings snapped on the spot, the wind-body dissolved, and it fell to the ground.

Just a wind elemental guard. Its interference hadn't even broken Jace's hold on the shadow energy. As the storm weakened, Grede and Vereesa easily defeated the few elemental guards attacking them. More importantly, the master of these smaller elementals was still out there.

He looked up. The sky had gone completely dark. A few streets away, a massive tornado connected heaven and earth at the city's center, as if the thick clouds themselves had descended to the ground. Countless building ruins beneath it had collapsed into rubble.

Lightning flickered continuously within the tornado, like blinking eyes and opening-and-closing mouths. As the sand spun, it sometimes deformed, sometimes reformed into an abstract human face glaring in their direction.

This was a wind elemental lord, still desperately controlling the sandstorm, trying to bury every living thing in this city under sand. But under Jace's contamination and infusion of emotion and will, this elemental lord was clearly not as composed as he looked.

"Your power is considerable, warlock." The thunder rumbled with growing rhythm, becoming distinguishable words. "The elemental remnant you carry has deceived you... I am Sarsalun, the herald of King Al'Akir. I am the master you should serve. Surrender the Windseeker's Brass Prison and go to Silithus. For bringing his cage back to Kalimdor, I may forgive your foolishness and even grant you the chance of elemental ascension!"

Go to Silithus for elemental ascension? This wind elemental lord thought they were mercenaries coming to join the Twilight's Hammer?

"I'm Jace Sesso. I imagine you've heard of me." Jace shouted.

"Jace Sesso..." The storm suddenly roared wildly: "Jace Sesso?!"

As he shouted, Jace seized the surge of rage, pulling at the wind elemental's will again. Seeing he couldn't maintain the sandstorm under the harassment of this shadow-wrapped darkness, the massive wind elemental lord simply pulled out a staff of earth and stone, held together by crackling lightning and as big as the troll watchtower outside Gadgetzan, and smashed it down toward the shadow!

The staff pushed aside vast amounts of sand and air as it fell. Just as Jace prepared to raise a shadow shield, a flaming long-handled hammer suddenly flew out from among the earthen buildings on the other side, spinning as it struck the staff's handle!

That was Sulfuras. Though it looked like nothing more than a tiny spark in comparison, it shattered the entire staff with irresistible force. The lightning connecting it exploded in a flash of white light as fragments of the staff crashed into the city like collapsing cliffs.

The flame continued spinning upward. Neither the sand nor the tornado's outer wind wall could stop its path. Instead, anything it touched immediately ignited. Before their eyes, the entire tornado transformed into a firestorm!

"I knew my Sulfuras was the real deal!" Grede roared with excitement.

"Hand of Ragnaros... You will regret angering the elemental lords, mortals. You will pay the price!"

The wind elemental lord was still talking tough, but his spinning form was completely chaotic. He looked like a panicked fire victim whose clothes had accidentally caught fire, desperately summoning sand to smother the flames consuming his body. But no matter how hard he tried, it was useless. Sulfuras' fire wasn't something you could just blow out.

He twisted and thrashed for a while, toppling who knew how many towers and statues, before the massive storm finally seemed ready to give out.

He released all the sand inside him, detached from the ground, and pulled himself up into the sky, fleeing through the dispersing clouds. Sulfuras' flames chased him all the way into the sky, only finally extinguishing when the thick clouds had completely cleared to reveal the blue sky.

Jace dropped shadowform and asked Thunderaan, "Who's Sarsalun?"

"In terms you can understand, he is one of my many brothers. A weak, self-important waste." Thunderaan replied quietly.

"And compared to you now, who's weaker?" Jace asked.

"Don't waste time mocking me, Jace Sesso. My father sent him — he must know we're already here. Find that temple quickly, or there will be more roadblocks!"

"Point is," Jace said, "our deal didn't include defeating a wind elemental lord in the desert. You'd better have a decent thank-you ready."

"If I cannot be reborn, there can be no thank-you." Thunderaan said in elemental. "Get over here and help, Jace Sesso! Needleflame is buried under a collapsed tower! She just landed and doesn't have the mana to shift back to dragon form!" Grede's shout came from the distance.
