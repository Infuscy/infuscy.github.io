Chapter 1714: At Least Fulfill the Dream First

Following Puzik into his workshop, Jace immediately noticed a drop in temperature. Either Puzik's cooling system was better than the mayor's office in central Gadgetzan, or Thunderaan's Rebirth Vial was stored in this very shed. At the very least, he could feel the Kirin Tor magic box on his arm beginning to stir — though none of the others or the goblin seemed to notice.

They looked around at the machinery everywhere: barrel-shaped metal shells, frames like beast skeletons, narrow chassis, and a pile of bombs sitting around doing who-knew-what.

"Where's your car?" Noggenfogger asked.

"Are you blind?" Puzik pointed at a silver giant barrel-shaped object sitting in an open space nearby. "That's it — my 'Frozen Passion'! Feast your eyes! A cross-era product designed entirely by me. No, it's a work of art!"

"What?" Grede said. "That's just a rocket on four wheels!"

Jace eyed the cockpit canopy glass that looked like a fighter plane's. "Strictly speaking, this is what you'd call a missile. It still needs a biological guidance system inside."

"Don't think I don't understand your sarcasm, smart guy!" Puzik glared at Jace, walked over to the 'Frozen Passion,' and pointed at its center. "The cooling system's right here. Touch it and you'll feel its frozen passion. I hired a mage in Booty Bay to make a cold energy extractor, drawing power from that bottle."

Jace walked over and put his hand on the missile's hull. Immediately he remembered the feeling of bringing the two halves of Thunderaan the Windseeker's Brass Prison together. Now he could even hear the cold elements in the air constantly calling out to him, even singing.

It wasn't just him. Grede heard it too, and his mocking expression turned serious.

"Name your price." Jace said.

"What?!" Puzik's face went cold. "Even if you left your magic sword here, I wouldn't trade. I've already fallen out with a bunch of crazy cultists over this thing. A lot of my employees died in the conflicts over the last two years. And now you want me to just hand it over for a few gold coins? I don't need gold!"

Jace looked at the ranger. Vereesa cleared her throat and said, "As far as I can tell from this 'cooling system' of yours, Quel'Thalas has plenty of magical items that could replace it — even outperform it."

"You seem to know a lot about blue dragons. You should know they're masters of cold magic. In Quel'Thalas, we have many gifts from the blue dragons, including plenty of magic stones imbued with cold magic. If you're willing to give us this thing, I can contact the Magisters and find you a more suitable—"

"If you're worried I'll go back on my word," she produced her Windrunner family crest, "this thing doesn't lie, Mr. Puzik."

"Ha!" Puzik laughed. "Don't think you can fool me. That mage from Booty Bay was an elf. And a member of the Magisters."

"He told me this thing's power is practically inexhaustible. There aren't many magic items in the world stronger than it. Even if this car blows up, it'll survive intact — of course, that'll never happen. My 'Frozen Passion' is way safer than any Steamwheedle Cartel product. My life is secondary, but my racing career and my great pursuit of ultimate speed are priceless!"

"But it's not meant for radiating magical power, Mr. Puzik." Vereesa said. "That mage — I don't know his name — but he should have told you the problem. This thing is meant to store power. Without the right conversion method, you're just wasting its potential. And arcane magic is not the right conversion method. That means an arcane caster could never unlock even a fraction of its full power... pessimistically, not even ten percent."

"Didn't know you were such an expert on race cars, little elf." Grede muttered.

"I don't know race cars. But I know magic, dwarf." Vereesa said.

Jace agreed with Vereesa. A mage might be able to forcibly extract elemental power using arcane energy, but only a true shaman could guide elemental energy with their soul. Facing the same elemental core, unless there was a massive gap in absolute power, the latter would always be far more efficient.

"Hmph." Puzik hesitated slightly. "So what? At least this thing gives me a cooling effect beyond my expectations. That's enough for me."

"And what about all the trouble it's brought you?" Jace said. "Trust me, if you keep holding onto it, you won't get to race your 'Frozen Passion' more than a few laps before an assassin takes you out. Your life is secondary, but your racing career and your great pursuit of ultimate speed are priceless."

"Don't underestimate me!" Puzik snapped. "Every goblin who settles in Gadgetzan has something going for them. And I'm no exception. Just because I can't handle you lot doesn't mean I can't beat a few costumed assassins with spiked clubs!"

"But your troubles aren't just Twilight's Hammer assassins." Jace said.

At that, Puzik visibly tensed. Jace seized on that nervousness immediately.

"Wind elementals are coming for you too." Jace said. "I noticed a sheet of iron torn off one of your factory roofs outside. Hopefully that wasn't taken by some wind elemental creature."

"I... can deal with elementals too." Puzik's confidence was noticeably fading.

"I don't know how much you and your former Twilight's Hammer allies know about this thing." Jace said. "But it's not just important to Twilight's Hammer — it means something to the wind elementals too. We've already encountered a wind elemental lord. It took a lot of effort to escape from him. His name was Sarsalun. His power could summon storms with a wave, destroy half a city in an instant, and connect the clouds above to the earth below."

With every sentence from Jace, Puzik twitched a little more. He must have heard of this Sarsalun before — maybe Twilight's Hammer or the wind elementals harassing the area had already threatened him with it.

"An enemy the five of us couldn't handle — do you think your racetrack security can take it?"

Puzik was silent for a long time before finally asking, "So what is this thing really? Can any of you give me a straight answer?"

"The Kirin Tor has asked us to keep it confidential." Jace showed the Kirin Tor signet ring. "But I can promise you this: if you hand this thing over to us, within a very short time, every wind elemental and Twilight's Hammer cultist will know it's no longer in your possession."

"The Kirin Tor..." Puzik plopped down on a small chair, propped his chin on his hand, and thought for a moment. Then he suddenly looked up. "No! I at least need to see what level of cooling system this Ranger-Lord can offer before I make a decision. You can't scare me otherwise!"

Vereesa sighed and took out the Flowing Ice Crystal. Jace gave her a look and leaned in to whisper, "Hey, why are you taking that out? What if Quel'Thalas doesn't have any better cooling option? That's Lady Aegwynn's..."

"This thing isn't specifically designed for cooling, Jace. Cooling is just a side effect." Vereesa said. "Don't worry, it's just cold magic... In enchanting, that's the simplest thing. These goblin technicians just haven't been to Quel'Thalas."

"Alright." Jace said.

Puzik eyed the blue crystal in Vereesa's hand with curiosity. At first his look was dismissive.

But when Vereesa's eyes glowed faintly, awakening the power within the Flowing Ice Crystal, the room grew cold again. Frost began forming at her feet. Now even Puzik stood up in surprise, muttering, "The elf kingdom really does have good stuff."

Then he shook his head, snapping out of it. "No. If you want to take it, you still have to agree to one more thing."

"What?" Vereesa asked.

"I heard Grim Batol has been reclaimed by the Alliance. The orcs were driven out, the red dragon captives freed, and you've become friends with the red dragons. Is that right?" Puzik asked.

"That's right." Grede said.

"Before this, I made a bet with the Coppershimmer brothers in Gnomeregan. I said my new race car could outrun a black dragon flying over the marsh. They didn't believe me." Puzik said. "Forget the black dragon — I get why the Coppershimmer brothers would be skeptical. So just help me invite a red dragon to race my 'Frozen Passion' in a straight-line drag race."

He ran to the door and pointed outside. "Right there on that track. Have Lady Vereesa Windrunner, Master Jace Sesso, High Thane Grede Thunderfist, Mayor Noggenfogger, and all the 'Scenery' track employees as witnesses. Win or lose, I'll give you that cooling system."

Jace glanced back at Needleflame, who was already licking her lips and cracking her knuckles, practically bouncing with eagerness.

"I'll get you a black dragon instead." He turned back and said.

"WHAT?!" Puzik and Noggenfogger screamed in unison.
