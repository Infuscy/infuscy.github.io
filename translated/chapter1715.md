## Chapter 1715: Mardi's Grand Straightaway

Early morning, the sky not yet fully light. A hot air balloon, pulled by a cable, floated in the sky.

Jace, Grede, Vereesa, Solara, and a few goblins sat in the basket below the balloon. As the sun crept up from behind the mountains, the Shimmering Flats were unveiled from shadow, revealing a truly spectacular racetrack.

Jace had never seen the full layout from this angle before. Even when he'd flown through the Thousand Needles on an airship, the rainstorm had blocked his view.

Now, looking down from above, every straight and curve was perfectly clear.

"Enjoy the view, my lords and lady, and the honorable Mayor Noggenfogger. This is our VIP observation point." A goblin in the basket said in a slightly hoarse, proud voice. "I'm Niclaze. Just call me Nick. I'm the announcer for today's race. Please direct your gaze to the far end, where you'll see a straight sprint track. That's the final stretch before the 'Scenery' circuit's finish line. We call it Mardi's Grand Straightaway, in memory of the late Trade Prince Mardi of the Bilgewater Cartel. May every racer making their final sprint on this track go faster than that business tycoon fell from his office window!"

The group exchanged an awkward look. Noggenfogger cleared his throat uncomfortably.

The announcer hastily added, "Of course, that building wasn't as tall as this track is long."

"So how long is it, Nick?" Grede asked.

"1.327 miles." The announcer replied.

"That's a very precise number." Vereesa murmured, looking into the distance.

"The engineers and technicians who built this track are the best from every cartel. And our boss Puzik is the cream of that crop." The announcer gesticulated excitedly. "He demands perfection in every detail!"

1.327 miles — probably about two kilometers. Not long, but not short either.

Jace studied it for a moment, then asked, "Has your boss's 'Frozen Passion' run this stretch before?"

"Of course." The announcer glanced at the pamphlet in his hand. "Best time was 28.11 seconds."

"28.11 seconds?" Jace blinked. "Is that a full-speed pass, or from a standing start to the finish?"

"Standing start, Master Sesso." The announcer replied. "The front section of the track isn't fully completed yet."

"What kind of time is that?" Grede whispered.

"I... I'm not sure either." Vereesa said.

Jace frowned. He felt he'd underestimated these goblins' engineering ability.

If you could cover about two kilometers in 28 seconds, that was a speed of roughly 250 kilometers per hour — and that included time accelerating from a standstill.

No wonder Puzik wanted a wind elemental lord's resurrection essence for a cooling system. Suddenly it didn't seem so ridiculous.

He hadn't been too worried about whether Needleflame could beat this missile. After all, black dragons could fly, and over a short burst without endurance being a factor, Needleflame wasn't that much slower than Solara.

But now even Solara's expression had turned serious. He knew she, as a green dragon sensitive to such things, had done the math and shared his concerns.

"By the way, where has the young, beautiful Miss Carolosa gone?" the announcer asked.

"She's... afraid of heights." Jace said.

"What a shame." The goblin sighed and looked into the distance. Then he suddenly snapped to attention. "Here they come! Mr. Puzik and his 'Frozen Passion' are taking position! Quickly, the balloon — head north! The highly anticipated straight-line drag race is about to begin!"

The basket jolted and started moving slowly toward Mardi's Grand Straightaway.

Noggenfogger looked up at the sky and said, "You're not really bringing a real black dragon, are you, Master Sesso? I don't know whether to hope you're joking or telling the truth."

"Mr. Puzik is already in position." Jace said. "I can't disappoint him."

Noggenfogger hesitated, then said, "I mean, our relationship with black dragons has never been great."

"If anything goes wrong, I'll take responsibility." Jace said.

"That sounds good." Noggenfogger said. "Maybe we should sign a contract or something first."

Jace glanced at him. The goblin immediately pretended nothing had happened and looked down again.

Both sides of the track were packed with people. Quite a few were track employees — Puzik's technicians, security, staff — but many had also come overnight from Gadgetzan to watch.

The Shimmering Flats' boss racing a black dragon? Even at risk of death, plenty of goblins wanted to see it. Puzik wasn't even charging tickets. The more witnesses to this race, the better.

"The silver-white 'Frozen Passion' is in position! She's as beautiful as my first love!" The announcer said excitedly. "And what about the other competitor? Hundreds, maybe thousands of spectators are waiting for her arrival!"

"Where's the black dragon?" Noggenfogger whispered, still sounding skeptical.

"Coming now." Jace shouted upward. "Ready!"

The noisy crowd on the track went quiet for a moment. On both sides of the two-kilometer white straightaway, the spectators fell silent. Everyone looked up, scanning the sky. Just as whispers started again, the sound of wings beating came from the distance, followed by a hoarse dragon roar!

"Bloody hell!" Noggenfogger ducked his head into the basket in fright. The announcer yelped and hid behind Grede. The balloon started shaking, the basket swaying.

A dark brown-black young dragon swept down from the distance, blood-red wings beating as she circled the track once. Every time her shadow passed over the stands, panicked screams erupted. Even the security guards and mercenaries were too scared to look up, let alone maintain order.

"That's a real black dragon! Dammit, a real black dragon!"

The announcer Niclaze was sitting on the floor of the basket, almost in tears. Noggenfogger was clutching the basket edge, barely peeking out with one eye.

But Puzik still sat calmly in his 'Frozen Passion,' no matter how chaotic things had gotten around him. Even when the black dragon slowly landed beside him and tilted her head to stare at him, and goblin technicians fled in all directions, Puzik just cursed them angrily.

"Are you ready, little one?" the black dragon asked.

"She's calling someone else 'little one.'" Grede snorted.

"I've been ready for a very long time, black dragon!" Even from the balloon, Jace could hear Puzik's shrieking voice cutting through the chaos of who knew how many spectators.

"Then let the race begin. I can't wait anymore." The black dragon looked up toward them. Noggenfogger immediately sat back down in the basket, avoiding her gaze and trembling.

"How does the start work, Mr. Niclaze?" Vereesa asked.

The goblin announcer managed to peek up and point at the starting line below. "There, there."

They had just looked that way when Needleflame lowered her head, spread her wings, and the 'Frozen Passion' roared to life behind her with a series of popping sounds like firecrackers, flames flashing, thick smoke billowing...

Soon a black device at the starting line flashed green. In that instant, the silver missile shot forward like it had been launched, and the black dragon's hind legs tensed, kicking off as she took flight.

"...like a black fish plunging into the low air! Truly elegant and terrifying!" The announcer Niclaze had somehow popped his head back up and was screaming at the top of his lungs. "No noticeable difference in reaction time, but the 'Frozen Passion' clearly has the advantage at the start!"

"But the black dragon has already increased her wingbeat frequency! Before her, Mr. Puzik's 'Frozen Passion' looks like a fleeing prey animal! I can see hunger in that black dragon's eyes! A thirst for victory!"

"Still accelerating! Still accelerating! The black dragon is catching up! Mardi's Grand Straightaway is long enough — only sustained acceleration can secure final victory!"

Jace watched as the 'Frozen Passion' spewed huge clouds of black smoke from all around it. From a distance it really looked like a rocket taking off from a launch pad, just horizontal instead of vertical.

Beside it, Needleflame's mouth was also starting to smoke, her muscles glowing with fiery red light. Jace had never seen her fly so frantically. If he was riding her right now, his backside would definitely be toast.

The twenty-odd seconds felt like they would never end. Even the once-chaotic stands had gone quiet. Every pair of eyes followed the black dragon and the missile tearing down the white track, straight toward the finish line.

Grede couldn't help jumping up and shouting, "Go! Go!"

The contest quickly reached the latter half. But from this angle, it was impossible to tell who was ahead. Noggenfogger was screaming for the balloon to move east for a better view, but it couldn't possibly be fast enough.

"The finish line is right ahead! Mardi's Grand Straightaway, in the presence of these two sprint maniacs, seems shorter than a Gnomeregan engineer's life! Finally, let's see—"

Niclaze was leaning further and further east as he spoke, as if that would sway the balloon enough to see who had won. Vereesa gripped the basket rope tightly. Jace was practically ready to fly over there with the Hide of Orastraz.

But black smoke had flooded the view. Soon, bells started ringing. At the finish line, goblins erupted into wild cheering.

"27.75 seconds! Boss Puzik has broken the record! First time under 28 seconds!"

A roar like an ogre's came from below. Grede whispered, "Did we lose?"

Before Solara or Vereesa could answer, the cheering suddenly turned into screams and shouts. After a series of explosions, they watched the 'Frozen Passion' tumble out of the track, rolling end over end, dragging flames across the Shimmering Flats until it suddenly exploded...

A fireball shot into the sky forming a small mushroom cloud. The shockwave and tremor reached the balloon, shaking everyone.

"The brakes must have failed again." Niclaze murmured. "The boss really went all in on this one."

"Does this mean Puzik is dead?" Noggenfogger asked.

"I'M THE CHAMPION!"

A parachute had somehow appeared high in the sky, with a goblin dangling from it, screaming at the top of his lungs. They could faintly hear him. "You think I'd die that easily?! I won! The 'Frozen Passion' beat a real black dragon!"

"What a shame." Noggenfogger clicked his tongue. The stands soon erupted in cheers and whistles. Streamers flew everywhere. Cannons fired...

"So he's fulfilled his dream." Jace said. "A deal's a deal."

"He can't wriggle out of it, Master Sesso. I'll be a witness." Noggenfogger said. "But only if that thing really survived that kind of accident, the way Puzik bragged."

Jace narrowed his eyes. The elemental essence capable of resurrecting the son of Al'Akir, the Wind Lord — it shouldn't be destroyed by a goblin bomb, should it?

Vereesa leaned in and said, "You should be less worried about that and more worried about how you're going to comfort her later."

Jace remembered. He looked at the dark shadow in the smoke and gulped.
