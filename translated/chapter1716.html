Chapter 1716: Silver Medalist

At the end of the track, the crowd had already erupted around the victorious racer Puzik. Never mind that his beloved car had exploded — he'd still crossed the finish line before Needleflame.

The flames from the wreckage in the distance were already dying down quickly, as if something had rapidly extinguished them. Thunderaan's Rebirth Vial seemed to be intact. Though who knew what the Wind Prince was feeling right now.

The balloon descended slowly. Noggenfogger couldn't help asking, "I still want to know how you commanded that black dragon, Master Sesso."

He looked at Vereesa, Solara, and Grede. "Can one of you command black dragons? What's going on here... Honestly, I only believed she was following your orders when she crossed the finish line, lost the race, and still didn't massacre the audience."

"She." Solara corrected. "That's a female dragon. She counts as our friend. This isn't about commands."

"Friend?" Noggenfogger said. "Nobody makes friends with black dragons, ma'am. Unless they've got a death wish or they're insane. Don't think goblins don't know. Look at what happened to every goblin who served Deathwing!"

Jace looked at the dark shadow slowly landing in the black smoke by the finish line up ahead. "Maybe things will be different in the future, Mr. Noggenfogger. This world is changing rapidly. Maybe we should all adapt to these changes and welcome a new era."

"That's certainly true, Master Sesso." Noggenfogger said. "But either way, this gives me more confidence that you can find those black dragon lairs in the marsh. Maybe the Steamwheedle Cartel can peacefully coexist with the black dragons in the marsh after all!"

With that, the goblin mayor laughed heartily, jumped out of the balloon basket, and turned back. "So, Master Sesso, when can you depart for the marsh?"

"Soon." Jace said. "But I can't guarantee we'll actually find where the black dragons are hiding."

"Just having Master Jace Sesso's name attached is enough to scare those black devils!" Noggenfogger laughed.

In the crowd, drums and gongs sounded. An ogre grinned stupidly as he carried Puzik out of the smoke, streamers flying everywhere.

The goblin already had a bunch of gold medals hanging around his neck and a big gold cup in his hands. In less than thirty seconds of racing, Jace felt like he'd won a world cup.

"Master Jace Sesso! Thank you! Thank you for giving me this chance! Thank that black dragon too! Thank you!"

Puzik's voice was hoarse, almost tearful. Jace and the others had been trying to get around the crowd to find Needleflame, but were blocked by the swarm of 'Scenery' team staff and technicians.

"I'm sorry your beloved car didn't survive." Jace offered politely.

"Didn't survive?" Puzik stared at Jace. "She just beat a black dragon! My god, is there a luckier race car in this world? Screw Coppershimmer! Screw Cobbercatch! I beat a black dragon!"

"But I haven't forgotten my promise, Master Sesso. I've already sent my technicians to recover the elemental bottle. Don't worry, it's perfectly safe in there. Just remember to spread the news across all seven kingdoms and Booty Bay that Puzik, team captain and owner of 'Scenery' Racing, drove his beloved 'Frozen Passion' to victory over a black dragon. That's my only request from you distinguished guests!"

"I'll keep that in mind..." Jace quickly agreed.

"Oh!" Just as the ogre was about to carry Puzik off on a "victory tour," Puzik spun around and tossed a silver medal over. "I wouldn't dare go near that black dragon myself, Master Sesso. Please give this silver medal to her on my behalf. Present it to her! I think you're the only one here brave enough to do that! You see, this is the silver medal from the Mardi's Grand Straightaway Insane Drag Race. It carries serious weight in every goblin cartel!"

"Victory! Glory!" Having delivered that final message, the goblin shouted, half-laughing, half-crying, "Long may it be!"

"This guy's lost it." Grede muttered.

"Those first few days after you defeated Ragnaros, you weren't much better, dwarf." Vereesa said.

"That was Ragnaros! The Fire Lord! An actual god! It's not the same!" Grede huffed.

Jace looked at the silver medal in his hand, inscribed in both goblin and Common with a smiling, falling goblin. He was speechless. When he looked up again, the dark shadow that had been there in the smoke was gone.

"Where'd she go?" Vereesa asked.

"She's not too embarrassed to show her face and ran north to find her aunt already, has she?" Grede said.

"Don't be stupid." Solara frowned. "She probably shifted back to mortal form to avoid trouble. Let's spread out and look."

They agreed and split up among the crowd. But for Jace, she wasn't hard to find. Dejection, disappointment — in this manic, cheering crowd, she stuck out like a sore thumb. At least to him.

Needleflame was sitting in an inconspicuous corner of the stands, chin in hand, staring into space. When Jace walked up, she glanced at him, then looked down again.

"I lost."

"Yeah." Jace sat down beside her, looking at the medal in his hand. "Congratulations. You earned the silver medal from the Mardi's Insane Drag Race. I'm here to present it to you."

"Something to remember." Needleflame snatched the medal. "What was my time?"

"27.94 seconds. Also under 28 seconds. Just a hair behind Puzik. Even faster than his personal best." Jace said.

"But I still lost." The little black dragon's eyebrows drooped.

"You lost to a rocket propelled by the essence of a wind lord's son that can't even last thirty seconds before exploding?" Jace said.

"He couldn't slow down and flipped out on his own. I didn't touch him." Needleflame said.

"I know." Jace took a deep breath and looked into the distance. "The way you chased that goblin rocket left a real impression on me, Needleflame. Your whole body burning with fire, giving it everything you had... It was beautiful. Like a true warrior, charging head-on at a powerful enemy."

"Yeah?" Needleflame bit back a smile. "I thought you couldn't see clearly from that balloon."

"Are you just saying nice things to cheer me up?"

"Well, was it nice to hear?" Jace asked.

"It was nice." Needleflame raised an eyebrow and tilted her head. "I'm beautiful anyway. I don't need you to tell me. My wings, my tail, my scales. You can't say we're not beautiful just because most black dragons are bad guys."

"I never thought that. I realized it even more after watching you fly that race." Jace said.

"Ah. So getting a true connoisseur of a black wyrm's elegance to recognize it — I guess this race wasn't a total loss." Needleflame clutched the silver medal and stood up, brushing the dust off her clothes and wiping the soot from her face. "Come on. Come look at that trophy with me, Sesso. You're just sitting around wasting time. What if one of those other goblins steals the bottle? Then I'd have done all that for nothing! And I'm not even hurt. You don't think I actually care that much about winning or losing, do you? It was just a little goblin with a piece of scrap metal!"

With that, the little black dragon vanished into the crowd. Solara was already waiting for her there.

That little one wasn't so hard to cheer up after all. Watching her go, Jace smiled to himself.
