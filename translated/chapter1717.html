Chapter 1717: Finally in Hand

The workshop was buzzing. Goblin technicians were cutting into the wreckage of the 'Frozen Passion' that had just been dragged back. Puzik, accepting congratulations from his underlings, passionately directed the workers as they searched for the cooling device's location.

Then he noticed Jace's group approaching — especially the shortest one, Needleflame. He trotted over, grabbed her right hand with both of his, pumped it up and down, and said excitedly, "I heard you missed my race, Lady Carolosa. What a shame. Master Jace Sesso tells me you're the daughter of a lord near the Alterac Mountains. Even if you didn't witness my victory in person, please help spread the news of this great triumph to Dalaran and the whole continent of Lordaeron..."

"I saw it." Needleflame said coldly. "From the finish line."

Solara, Grede, and Vereesa all glanced at her sideways, thinking, This little one isn't going to blow her cover and massacre everyone, is she?

They might all be well-acquainted by now, but she was still a genuine black dragon.

Puzik seemed to sense something was off. He gave a dry laugh. "Goodness! Then you had an even better view than Master Sesso and the others. I was worried for nothing!"

The goblin's cackling laughter cut off mid-stride, less than half a second after it started. The corner of his mouth twitched. He glanced down at his hand, still held by Needleflame's, swallowed hard, and yanked several times before finally pulling free. He grimaced in pain for a moment before forcing his smile back onto his face.

"You don't seem at all upset about losing the car, Puzik." Noggenfogger looked at him curiously. "So which insane insurance company agreed to cover this suicide machine of yours? Maybe you could recommend them to me."

"Huh?" Puzik rubbed his hand and tilted his head. "You think I'm this happy just because the car was a total loss? No, no. My old friend fulfilled her purpose. She accomplished a dream I used to think was an exaggeration. Now I'm moving on to a new chapter — combining the powerful magic of Quel'Thalas with the design expertise of Undermine's Elite Technician Union's senior mechanical engineers to build the next generation of super racing cars!"

With that, he glanced at Vereesa and, getting a noncommittal look, continued, "As for the gold spent along the way — it's just fuel and magic, consumables that keep me moving forward. Something a guy who's spent his whole life swimming in a sea of gold coins could never understand."

"Money always runs out eventually." Noggenfogger said impatiently. "We'll see what you use to fuel your dreams then."

"This world never runs out of things to burn, Noggenfogger." Puzik laughed.

A couple of clangs came from the 'Frozen Passion' wreckage. A magic box made of special pale green metal — probably thorium — fell out.

Several goblin technicians worked together to open it, revealing a crystal-like object, glowing faintly.

Jace had expected the entire workshop to turn freezing cold when it came out. But nothing happened.

"Has the energy inside been used up?" Grede asked.

"Don't joke like that, High Thane Thunderfist." Puzik explained. "The elven mage who made this device told me that if you released everything inside at once, it would be enough to create a storm off the coast of Stranglethorn that would last for weeks... probably with hail too. The elemental power extractor we built together was destroyed in the explosion. The bottle itself wasn't affected."

Jace relaxed at that explanation. Not that he'd underestimated the power needed to resurrect Thunderaan, but the mushroom cloud and shockwave from the 'Frozen Passion's explosion had been pretty scary.

Jace took the "Rebirth Vial" from the goblin. It felt slightly cool to the touch, like river water that hadn't yet been warmed by the summer morning sun. The crystal's surface was a little dusty, but showed no signs of damage. It was as perfect as if it had just been cut.

"Thinking back to when I first heard about this thing from those Twilight's Hammer people, and now I've used its power to build the fastest machine on earth — it's really like a dream, everyone." Puzik planted his hands on his hips, puffed out his chest, and looked around the workshop. "After this morning's race, I think we're friends now. If you ever need anything, come find me. Or just drop my name in Booty Bay."

"I'll remember that, Mr. Puzik." Jace said.

Back in Gadgetzan, the group gathered in the rest quarters Noggenfogger had prepared for them. Jace opened the Kirin Tor magic box and took out Thunderaan's Brass Prison, setting it on the table.

"Finally... I can see it again." Thunderaan said in Common. "I'm only one step away from rebirth, everyone. Despite the reckless desecration and abuse by countless arrogant mortals, this vial of wind essence has not been damaged. It can undoubtedly help me break the cage and return to the world."

"So where should we take you to resurrect?" Grede asked. "Back to that Sanderatiz temple?"

"No." Thunderaan said. "You cannot return to that temple. Take me to the Thousand Needles. Release me there."

"Fine. Your call." Jace said. "We'll set out at dawn tomorrow. Head for the Thousand Needles."

"I wouldn't want to cross that desert again either." Solara said.

"Is there anything we need to prepare for the ritual?" Vereesa asked. "Mana, incantations, or ritual materials? We're in Gadgetzan. It's convenient to stock up."

"I will guide you through the ritual when the time comes. With this crystal vial and my complete cage, that will be enough." Thunderaan said quietly. "But when we reach the Thousand Needles, we need to find the highest point with the strongest wind, and wait for the wind to be at its most powerful during the day. That will make the ritual easier."

"We have two dragons to fly us around. We won't need to climb any mountains. Should be simple enough." Vereesa said.

"I hope you still remember your promise, Prince of the Windseeker." Grede leaned in and warned. "All the extra trouble we went through — we're not settling for an ordinary reward."

"You'll see when the time comes." Thunderaan said.

Jace carefully loaded the Brass Prison back into the Kirin Tor box and wrapped it securely. The room warmed up again.

Grede flopped backward onto the soft leather sofa and let out a long sigh. "After all these trips around Gadgetzan, I'm starting to actually like these goblins. What's wrong with me?"

"Optimistic. Passionate. Energetic." Solara said. "Even black dragons who shut out all other races are willing to cooperate with them. It's normal to develop some fondness for the race."

"Unbelievable." Needleflame muttered.

"Don't let their weak appearance fool you either." Vereesa said, chin in hand. "The reason they're warm and generous with us is because we're famous on the other continent and have some power. In other words, we're worth befriending."

"And think about how Puzik got this elemental crystal." Jace held up the Rebirth Vial. "He said he found out about this treasure from Twilight's Hammer. But according to Noggenfogger, he'd been looking for a suitable cooling system for his new race car for years. That means no matter how his deal with Twilight's Hammer went, he was already planning to kill all the Twilight's Hammer partners and take the spoils for himself before the expedition team was even assembled."

"Ha!" Grede laughed. "Now I like him even more. Twilight's Hammer cultists dying is always a good thing!"

"I certainly agree." Jace said.
