## Chapter 1726: Owathanka

The palm trees at the edge of the oasis swayed back and forth under wave after wave of heat blasts. Dragon roars, monster bellows, and the thunderous crash of lightning filled the air.

Sora circled overhead. On the ground, the little black dragon was locked in close combat with a blood-red thunder lizard.

The grass had been scorched black by dragonfire. The thunder lizard's broadsword-like horns had been mostly broken off. Needleflame clung to its back like a berserk black lion on a rhino, tearing and biting like mad. By now, the thunder lizard was on its last legs.

Watching from the green dragon's back, nobody was surprised. No matter how tough a thunder lizard was, how could it beat a dragon whelp?

"Reminds me of hunting in the forest with my old man when I was a kid," Grede chuckled. "He kept a wild bear he'd caught in the Hillsbrad Foothills. Called it 'Khadgar.'"

"Khadgar, that Khadgar?" Jace asked.

"That very Khadgar," Grede said. "I told you, it's not some rare name. I know two Khadgars in the Firebrew clan alone."

"Anyway, that bear was something else. As long as it wasn't trolls or owlkin, it could handle most beasts on its own."

"Don't let Needleflame hear you comparing her to a dwarf's hunting bear, or she'll tear your mouth off," Vereesa warned.

"She threatens to tear my mouth off every day anyway. What's the difference?" Grede yawned.

Mossa chuckled softly, then looked back at the black dragon on the ground, as if still unable to believe this group could get along so well with a black dragon.

"Boring! Boring!"

Needleflame stood on the thunder lizard's corpse, calling up discontentedly.

"Dinner time! Finally get to taste some real game after all these days!" Grede raised his hands in excitement.

Sora landed next to the thunder lizard. Vultures circled in the nearly invisible sky, far off. Jace knew they'd noticed the two dragons, but seeing Needleflame puffing out her chest and strutting around, none dared come close. Sure, the saying went that a powerful dragon was no match for local snakes — but this dragon was a bit too powerful.

"Weak. Thunder lizard. Weak!" Needleflame crouched on the corpse, still griping in the Dragon Tongue. Sora was already chowing down on the other side, making the whole carcass wobble. All Jace could see from this side was her green tail swishing back and forth.

"Jace Sesso, is this the mighty beast you've been hyping up for so long?"

"This is just an ordinary thunder lizard, Lady Black Dragon." Mossa looked up. "Maybe we weren't lucky enough to run into a strong one today."

Needleflame lowered her head, looked at Mossa, and said nothing. A moment later, she turned away and muttered in the Dragon Tongue, "Weak lizard."

"Ignore her, Mossa." Grede was hacking at the thunder lizard's thick hide with his axe. "You've got to try Grede Thunderfist's specialty — Sulfuras barbecue! The only place in all of Azeroth you can find it!"

"Sulfuras barbecue?" Mossa was clearly having trouble processing this information. Grede, with immense pride, pulled back the enchanted cloth wrapping Sulfuras. The released flame was so intense it nearly dimmed the prairie sun.

Watching Grede and the tauren fuss over the carcass, Jace whispered to Vereesa, who was lying beside him, "There goes his rep — 'Sulfuras Barbecue Chef.'"

"Exactly." Vereesa stared at the white clouds in the sky, speaking softly. "No wonder he didn't dare tell Mossa about the High Thane business. He probably knows he's been squandering the glorious name the Anvilmar family worked so hard to build."

Before the elf could finish, a heavy rumble came from deep within the oasis, followed by an intense surge of anger. If Jace couldn't clearly feel the furious rage rising in whatever was making that sound — so strong he could sense it even from this distance — he would've assumed something had exploded. Maybe goblins conducting some secret demolition tests over here.

The carcass stopped shaking. Needleflame and Sora both raised their long necks, peering into the forest. Vereesa grabbed her bow and stood. Grede and Mossa came around the carcass to look.

"Is that it?" Jace asked. "Owathanka?"

"I... I don't know. I've never heard his voice, Jace."

Mossa shifted back from tauren to big cat in a few bounds, leaped to the top of the carcass, and tried to see further. But she quickly realized the move was unnecessary.

Trees in the oasis forest were being knocked down one after another, and whatever was doing it was getting closer. Whoever or whatever that was, it was charging straight at them.

Vereesa instinctively drew and aimed. But Jace raised a hand. "Don't leave Needleflame with childhood trauma, Ver."

"Oh. Right." Vereesa said. "But what if Needleflame gets hurt?"

"You're joking, right? The daughter of Deathwing getting run over by some random thunder lizard?" Jace asked.

Vereesa snorted and lowered her bow. Just then, a massive, crystal-blue monster burst through the palm trees.

This thing was so big Jace felt a bit surreal. Next to it, the thunder lizard they'd killed looked like a child next to an adult. Even Needleflame was reduced to looking like a scrawny little bat.

More importantly, this wasn't just a simple beast. It was a magical creature that could command the elements. Every scale on its body glowed with faint white lightning. Its eyes flickered constantly, giving it an airy, inflated look that made it seem even larger. For a moment, Jace wasn't even confident about his earlier question.

Based on Mossa's description and this ridiculous size, this had to be Owathanka.

"Bloody hell!"

Grede grabbed his hammer, wrapped it haphazardly, and ran for a slope. Sora took off immediately, climbing higher. Mossa glanced at the little black dragon still standing on the thunder lizard corpse, understood the others weren't planning to intervene, and retreated with the dwarf.

Owathanka burst out of the forest without slowing down, charging straight at Needleflame. It didn't even acknowledge the others on the slope. Was this thing pissed off by Needleflame's taunting roar?

Needleflame was stubborn too. Facing a beast several times her size, she actually lowered her head — was she planning to charge head-on?

Jace hesitated, wanting to tell Needleflame to dodge, but worried about ruining her fun. But at this point, was "fun" even the question? If she broke her neck, could Sora and Mossa's healing magic save her?

Needleflame stared as the thunder lizard's massive skull grew closer. The horn on its nose was bigger than her entire head. The ground shook. Thunder roared. Owathanka ran faster and faster — faster than something this size had any right to move. Like someone hit fast-forward. It reminded Jace of Chromaggus in Blackrock Spire, using time acceleration.

But he knew what this was. The wind howled. The tall grass waved. This was the speed granted by the Wind Prince's blessing. The tauren legends weren't made up after all.

Feeling the threat of this power, Vereesa couldn't help raising her bow again. Light gathered in her hand.

Sure enough, Needleflame wasn't completely crazy. At the last moment, she launched into the air, dodging the charge. The thunder lizard corpse under her feet was sent tumbling away, rolling several times. That heavy carcass was like a toy in front of Owathanka.

The black dragon cursed in the Dragon Tongue, circled half a turn, and dive-bombed the thunder beast as it turned around, drenching it from head to tail in a blast of breath. But the scales where Owathanka had been scorched only glowed brighter with electricity, as if the dragonfire had been half-blasted away. It had barely taken any damage.

Wasn't this a shaman's Lightning Shield? But did thunder lizards in another time line also cast electric shields?

Seeing her breath weapon barely scratch the giant beast, Needleflame grew anxious. Still relying on her aerial advantage, she was figuring out her next move when Owathanka suddenly, without warning, spat a thick chain of lightning that slammed into her chest!

The blinding white light nearly seared Jace's eyes. When his vision cleared, Needleflame had fallen, crashing into the grass. Owathanka was already charging toward her on thick legs, lowering its head for a second ram.

"Needleflame's injured," Sora said. "This isn't good."

"This stopped being fun," Grede said. "Little elf, you're fast. Go!"

Vereesa nodded and raised her bow. A dream arrow nocked, the string pulled taut. Needleflame's movements were too slow to dodge. Owathanka was getting faster and faster...

Suddenly, a heavy thud shook the ground. The lightning beast's body stiffened, grinding to a halt mid-charge. Its four legs didn't even have time for a braking motion before it toppled forward. The electricity all over its body dissipated into the air, leaving only a few crackling white sparks.

It was dead. So suddenly. The thunder element had abandoned it just as fast.

Grede, Sora, Mossa, and Jace had no idea what had happened. Even Vereesa froze — her arrow hadn't even left the string.

But Needleflame looked unsurprised. She pushed herself up off the ground, staring at the legendary beast, panting hard.

Grede walked down and circled Owathanka's body. He lifted the corpse with some effort — and a gush of blood and organs spilled out.

Its belly had been completely ripped open. The culprit was a thick stone spike that hadn't been there before, thrust up from underground and buried deep in Owathanka's abdomen. No wonder no one outside could tell what had happened.

"My enemy calls on the elements. I call on the elements too. This was a fair fight. And I won."

Needleflame was clearly proud of herself, even if she coughed a little mid-sentence.

"The earth listens to you?" Grede asked.

"The earth has no choice but to listen to her, dwarf..." Sora said.
