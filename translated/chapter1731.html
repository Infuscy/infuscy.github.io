Chapter 1731: Cooperating with a Black Dragon...

Though the Grimtotem tribe had never gotten along with other tauren tribes, and there had often been blood feuds — Mossa was one of the victims — they would still unite against external threats like centaurs or quilboar whenever possible.

So in another timeline, Grimtotem matriarch Magatha Grimtotem was also a respected elder in Mulgore.

But even when allied, the Grimtotem kept their distance from other tribes. Betraying the tribe was extremely rare, especially for the matriarch's own family. So what was this Nathra Grimtotem about? She was willing to be a mole for outsiders?

Still, since Mossa called her "friend," Jace spoke politely. "Please convey our greetings to this lady, Mossa."

"I can speak Common, human." The tauren nearby spoke with a heavy accent. "I am Nathra, niece of Grimtotem chieftain Magatha. I heard the great druid Mossa Woodbough fell in love with someone from another continent. I assume..."

"I'm not a great druid," Mossa said. "That's an honor only the most venerable druid masters can earn."

"The druidic way has been lost among our tribes for thousands of years, Mossa Woodbough." The Grimtotem observed the group with her black-pupil-dominated tauren eyes, speaking in quite fluent kaldorei. "Now that you've brought it back from the kaldorei in the north, you are undoubtedly the tribe's great druid."

Mossa frowned. Though Vereesa and Sora understood kaldorei, and Jace could follow most of it, Grede and Needle Flame couldn't. The dwarf looked between the two speakers, noticed Mossa's discomfort, and immediately stepped in. "If you can speak Common, how about saying it so everyone can understand?"

The Grimtotem's gaze dropped to the dwarf. "You must be the one. Grede Thunderfist, the Thane?"

"You know me." Grede said. "Yes, I'm Mossa Woodbough's fiance. Looks like my reputation travels faster than I do."

"Auntie always tells us to keep our eyes on the whole world. The Grimtotem can't focus only on this prairie." Nathra turned her gaze to Vereesa. "We know more about the other continent than you might think, Lady Windrunner."

"Good. Saves us the trouble of introductions," Vereesa said.

"Since you know what's happening on the other continent, why are you going to the black dragons?" Jace asked. "That's like hearing about the orcs' great defeat at Hillsbrad and then deciding to join Orgrim Doomhammer."

"Even a loser has value as an ally." Nathra took a breath. "On that point, I don't oppose my aunt's decision, Jace Sesso."

"So the Grimtotem really are going to ally with the black dragons?" Mossa asked.

"I don't think you have the right to criticize my aunt's decision, Mossa Woodbough." Nathra said. "I don't deny your efforts. You've studied the druidic way with the kaldorei. You're now more skilled than any of our shapeshifters. But that doesn't change the overall situation of the centaur war. It'll take ten years or more for your students to reach your level. Where will we be by then?"

"My efforts go beyond that. And there's already significant progress." Mossa sighed. "Regardless, I thought you understood how dangerous it is to ally with black dragons!"

After Mossa said this, the group glanced at Jace and Needle Flame. Actually... it wasn't that dangerous.

Of course, Needle Flame and Sabellian were one thing; Onyxia was another.

"Perhaps it was once," Nathra lowered her voice considerably, almost inaudible in this place.

"But as Jace Sesso said, the black dragons are on the decline. All of southern Kalimdor is eyeing their treasures, from the Thorny Ridges to Gadgetzan... So even the master of the black dragon lair has need of the Grimtotem. They help us fight centaurs; we help them guard the lair. Each gets what they need. My aunt thinks you and Chief Cairne Bloodhoof are both too weak and hesitant. We're at the edge of extinction now. This isn't the time for indecision."

"So you're increasingly agreeing with her decision." Mossa's voice turned cold. "Then why answer my summons and come to meet me?"

Nathra Grimtotem glanced at Sora and Needle Flame, who'd been silent. "Who are they?"

"My name is Sora Shadowmoon." Sora said. "I'm also from Quel'Thalas."

"I can see that," Nathra said.

Needle Flame said nothing, just eyed her sidelong. This Grimtotem had clearly been classified as "not normal tauren" in her mind.

"They can be trusted," Mossa said.

Nathra was silent for a long moment before making her decision and continuing. "The cooperation between my aunt and the black dragons... is indeed getting out of control, Mossa Woodbough."

Grede whispered to Jace, "Sounds like a smaller version of Blackrock Spire."

"Before deciding to cooperate with the master of the black dragon lair, you should have anticipated this situation," Mossa said.

Vereesa and Sora both looked at Jace. They didn't say anything, but Jace didn't feel like commenting either. When your race is on the verge of extinction, you grab whatever you can — it's only natural. Like when they were in Draenor — weren't they forced to cooperate with black dragons too?

Sure, things went sideways in the end, but without the Blackwing Legion's help, those thousands of expedition soldiers never would have escaped Kil'jaeden's grasp.

On the other hand, Jace didn't really care how these Grimtotem were faring with the black dragons. If the Grimtotem really were entangled with them, then the more important issue needed addressing.

"Can you take us to see the master of the dragon lair, Lady Nathra? Or tell us the exact location? We'd like to meet her."

"I... can't," Nathra said. "Even I can only go to the lair during sacrifices, and only under my aunt's guidance. Only she knows how to cross this fog to reach the palace's entrance."

"So without seeing your aunt, we can't find where the dragon is hiding?" Grede stared into the deeper fog forest beyond Nathra. "I don't believe it. If we just keep flying in, won't we find it eventually?"

"Only a Dragon Aspect like Queen Ysera could dispel Onyxia's fog, Grede," Sora said quietly. "Don't forget, she's hiding here precisely to avoid us, the Alliance army, and Nefarian."

"With Jace's shadow energy perception, maybe we don't need to break the fog to find her," Vereesa whispered. "Not to mention we have Needle Flame — she's family. Dragons can easily find their kin, like Sora did in that temple."

"Don't mention that troll shit-pit," Grede muttered darkly.

"But I can't sense anything right now," Needle Flame whispered.

"The problem is, we don't have much room for trial and error," Jace said. "Onyxia flew back here to escape Nefarian, and she might have killed a lot of the guards and kin here too. She's incredibly weak now. If she senses us sniffing around her doorstep, what would you do if you were her?"

"Run again," Vereesa said.

"Exactly." Grede folded his arms. "If we want to catch her off guard, we can't keep circling and trying random approaches."

"Then it looks like we have to meet that aunt," Vereesa said. "No better option."

Hearing their discussion, Mossa looked troubled, but eventually lifted her head and asked the young Grimtotem, "Can you take us to see Matriarch Magatha?"

Nathra laughed. "So you've finally revealed your true purpose, Mossa Woodbough."

"What do you mean?" Mossa asked.

"You have a blood feud with my aunt's deceased husband — my uncle. Don't think I don't know you hate her too. You want revenge." Nathra said. "You're not confident in your own strength, so you brought this group. You made up a grandiose excuse..."

"You think I want to assassinate Magatha? At a time like this?" Mossa stepped forward into the muddy stream, looking up at the Grimtotem on the rise. "We've known each other for years, Nathra! You should know what kind of person I am."

At this, the young Grimtotem's expression shifted slightly.

Jace could sense her probing Mossa's emotions through shadow energy changes, trying to detect deception. Such tricks were hard to hide from Jace now.

"It sounds like your knowledge of the other side isn't deep enough. If your tribe's cooperation with the black dragons is getting out of control, then we might be the last help you can find, Nathra Grimtotem," Jace said.
