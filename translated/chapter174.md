## Chapter 174: The Perfect Vessel

The city of Dalaran was roughly ring-shaped, but within the larger ring were nested smaller rings, and there weren't always direct roads connecting them — for outsiders, it could be quite confusing.

Jace left the corridor. Before long, the road ahead split into a junction. The rain was coming down hard, and the intermittent murmuring whispers maddened him, and all at once he didn't know which way to go.

Then he noticed a robed guard standing at the corner of the road on the right.

His back was to Jace — he seemed to be guarding that intersection. Had a death knight appeared here too?

"Sir!" Jace shouted as he walked over. "If I want to get to Horn Tree Alley, is this the right way?"

The guard turned around upon hearing the voice. When he saw Jace, he just stood there stunned, saying nothing.

Jace narrowed his eyes — in the curtain of rain, he couldn't make out the man's appearance at all. He shouted again, "Has a death knight appeared here? If one has…"

Halfway through, something felt off.

Compared to the robed guards he'd encountered in the main district earlier, this one was far too calm.

An arcane flare streaked across the sky, illuminating the street, and now he could see clearly: where the guard's battle-robe should have borne the eye of the Violet Citadel, there was now an enormously exaggerated, bloodied gash, as if clawed open by some kind of monster.

The guard began to move his feet toward Jace, gripping the guard's sword in both hands, walking faster and faster. The intestines and flesh hanging out of that huge gash shook with his footsteps, and it didn't hinder his movements in the slightest!

The other party let out a low growl and thrust forward with the sword held in both hands. Though he was fast and ferocious, his movements were chaotic and uncoordinated. Jace braced his staff against his chest and shoved hard, then swung with all his strength at his head, knocking him to the ground in one blow.

He jabbed the butt of his staff into the man's skull with force, putting an end to his twitching.

Was this… a resurrected guard?

Jace continued forward. As he walked, he heard another murmuring whisper, but this time it clearly.

"Truly… a perfect vessel."

What?

Jace almost thought he'd misheard. What did this mean?

"This is why my staff would answer to you, Jace Sesso… ah, so that's how it is."

Could it be that Gorefiend wanted to take his own body as a vessel for his soul?

But where was it? How was it even watching him?

Most critically — how did it know his name?!

Jace looked around and saw, on the roof of a building, a walking corpse staring straight at him.

The walking corpse was its eyes?

Was this guy out of his mind? Not going to steal the Eye of the Arcane, but coveting his body here?

But what if it wasn't the one stealing the Eye of the Arcane?

In another timeline's Dalaran, there were no Farstrider rangers stationed there when Teron Gorefiend paid his visit.

If Jace and Grede hadn't run into the black dragon in Southshore, they might still have been searching for the trail of that fleeing dragon in the Hillsbrad–Alterac area.

Facing these Farstriders, who excelled at tracking and hunting, would Gorefiend — or Deathwing — have changed their theft strategy?

Jace realized he'd already made this timeline deviate quite a bit.

In that case, right now he shouldn't keep heading toward where Grede lived.

If Gorefiend was really chasing him, then leading him to Grede's place would be getting him killed!

Right now Grede's injuries hadn't fully healed. Even if he came to help, it would be nothing but asking for trouble — and that was before you mentioned fighting a monster like the general.

Now he actually had to consider dealing with Teron Gorefiend — and it was all because of this staff in his hand, wasn't it? If not for it, the death knights probably wouldn't have taken notice of him in the first place.

He looked toward the southeast. If he kept going that way, he'd reach the place where the mages were fighting the Black Dragonflight. If he could reach the mages' location before Gorefiend found him, he should be able to escape this disaster.

"Run, run… run all you want… he who steals from others will pay the price in the end."

That low voice lingered constantly at his ear. Jace couldn't help but turn his head, but there was nothing to be seen.

He darted into an alley, and the surrounding space narrowed.

A clang of metal grew clearer by the second…

It was the sound of footsteps — a death knight's footsteps!

Where… where…

They were coming from inside the wall right beside him!

The wall at his side exploded without warning, and a spray of rubble struck Jace, knocking him flat.

Before Jace could even climb to his feet, he felt himself grabbed by a powerful, monstrous hand, lifted straight out of the pile of rubble and dust, and hoisted into the air!

"My property you have stolen, my soldiers you have killed — the sheer audacity…" As the dust gradually cleared, Teron Gorefiend's true form was revealed. He was draped in a yellowish-brown cloak, and beneath it, a suit of red armor was exposed by the streaks of arcane light flashing across the sky — a layer of metal severely corroded by shadow, like the carapace of some monstrous insect.

Though his soul occupied a human body, this body had been severely warped by his dark energies.

This monster was a rough estimate of at least two-point-three to two-point-four meters tall, his body so powerfully built it made one's hair stand on end.

Lit by an arcane lamp shrouded in dust and rain from the side, Jace could make out half of his blackened face.

The sickly dark skin was covered in wrinkles, broken teeth exposed from ruined lips, a jaw that was practically skin and bones — he looked like an old woman on the verge of suffocating to death.

In his other hand he held an exaggerated page-headed warhammer, the hammer's flanges burning with a purple-red ghostly fire around a round gem at its center, like a swaying demonic eye.

"A truly perfect vessel. What a pity to kill you just like that," Gorefiend said with a smile.

Jace grabbed the hand gripping his neck, feeling himself being strangled. And in this situation, forget speaking an incantation — he couldn't even get a word out!

"Sess!"

A shout from somewhere — Jace even thought he was hallucinating.

Almost at the same instant, the hand of Teron Gorefiend was suddenly covered by a layer of frost, so stiff it couldn't move.

His expression changed. He forced his hand open, and though he shattered the ice in one motion, Jace also seized the chance to break free!

"Run!"

Jace heard the shout. Amidst the chaos, he grabbed his staff and ran into the rubble torn open by Gorefiend. Gorefiend cursed at him in Orcish from behind.

"Teron Gorefiend… damn you, so it is you!"

Jace recognized the voice — it was Kael'far Brightwing!

He was the one who saved him?

"Arrogant Quel'dorei — you've doomed that human! I had no intention of killing him… but I'm not in the mood anymore. If all I need is a vessel, a dead one will do just as well!"

The moment those words ended, Jace, who was running away, suddenly felt a massive surge of shadow energy rush at him from behind, seizing his heart like an eagle's talon!

His heart instantly began beating as if seized by a spasm. A burning pain spread from his chest, and after a few steps Jace collapsed in agony into a pile of dust.
