## Chapter 1740: A Bronze Dragon's Treasure?

A burly dragonkin guard, clad in ornate golden enchanted armor, led Jace and his group through a side door into deeper passages. Grede kept eyeing the guard's absurdly complex magical protections, probably calculating whether one good swing of his hammer could shatter them.

Jace wasn't thinking about fighting. He was just curious where the massive, lava-filled hatching chamber from the game was located in this palace.

But when Onyxia had told them they could visit the so-called treasure vault, Jace initially thought it was through a different side door.

He glanced in that direction and noticed Onyxia's emotions suddenly spike -- tensing up as if yanked taut -- and her gaze snapped toward him.

Sensing the black dragon's reaction, he immediately dropped his curiosity and turned to follow the guard.

Jace guessed that direction led to the incubation chamber. If he'd taken one more step that way, the hard-won trust between them would have shattered on the spot.

"I never expected the legendary Princess Onyxia to be so reasonable," Mossa said quietly as they walked. "Honestly, I was prepared for a fight."

"When an excellent tauren hunter is badly wounded, even a gazelle can make them calm down," Jace said. "We both got what we came for. She's at the end of her rope, and we're stuck too. No point making things harder for each other."

Needleflame had been quiet the whole way. Jace looked at her and asked, "I thought you said you had some questions for Onyxia. Why didn't you say anything back there?"

"No questions anymore," Needleflame said, keeping her head down. Her mood seemed complicated.

Jace didn't press further. Let the young drake keep some privacy.

He wondered if it was because he'd spent too long in Blackrock Spire, or if underground monsters just shared similar tastes, but Onyxia's treasure vault looked to him like some hall in Shadowforge City.

Except the walls lacked reliefs worshipping the Firelord, or statues of Dark Iron heroes and kings.

Chests of all sizes and colors were piled in the corners, mixed with mounds of jewelry and coins from various eras and civilizations.

There were not only human gold coins but even plenty of troll currency -- some types Jace had never even seen before. Who knew how long they'd been sitting here.

Jace suddenly understood where all those legends about dragons hoarding treasure and gold came from. Probably from thieves and adventurers who'd snuck into Onyxia's lair and lived to tell exaggerated tales back in civilization.

Grede finally got excited. He ran right up and jumped into the scattered coins. It wasn't quite piled high enough to swim in like in those movies or cartoons... but there were several decent-sized mounds.

"She said we can only take one thing, Grede," Jace reminded him.

"So do these coins and gems count as 'one' each, or the whole lot as 'one'?" Grede asked.

"Did Sora hypnotize you before we came in? Dreaming bigger than in the Emerald Dream." Vereesa rolled her eyes at him. "Now you have a chance to pick anything from the most powerful black dragon's treasure vault in the world, and you're thinking about coins?"

"You say that every time!" The dwarf slapped his thigh. "Every time you say pick the most valuable thing. But did we ever actually sell any of the stuff we got our hands on for gold?"

"That's because *you* never wanted to sell." Jace pointed at the dwarf's waist. "If you'd sold that stone axe from Uldaman to Ironforge long ago, you could've bought a house in the Dwarf District by now."

"That axe has several notches in it. How much could it even sell for? Whatever. This time I'm definitely finding something I can cash in." Grede grumbled as he pried open a chest lid and pulled out a broadsword with shadow enchantment. "What's this?"

"Vis'kag the Bloodletter," the dragonkin guard said in a low voice. "An ancient assassin's blade. Lady Onyxia awards it to her most valiant champions."

"If this were ten years ago, I'd kill for this blade. But now..." Grede glanced at the hammer handle behind him. "I'm not short on good weapons anymore."

Vereesa examined an enchanted gem on the ground, then looked over at Grede. "I'm surprised you didn't just pull out your warhammer and show her some light when Onyxia was making those threats earlier."

Grede laughed dryly. "No need. Since we all agreed to sit down and talk, why spoil the atmosphere? Besides, she looked so weak -- what if I scared her to death showing it off?"

Jace could guess why Grede didn't like flaunting that warhammer in front of others. He loved Sulfuras, yes, but that hammer represented the position of High Thane. He just... still wasn't comfortable with that title, even if he didn't have to bear any of its responsibilities.

"If you're looking for something valuable..." Onyxia's voice came from behind. Jace didn't know when she'd slowly followed them in.

She held a long iron staff in one hand, the other hand hidden within her blood-red robes.

"I would recommend the treasure in that grass-green box at your feet, Jace Sesso."

Jace looked down and noticed the box. He opened it to reveal a golden leather vest inside. It looked heavy, but when he picked it up, it was thin and light -- thin enough to wear under a robe. It also carried an unusual magical aura.

He looked at Vereesa. She shook her head too. Neither of them could identify the enchantment.

"What is this?" Sora asked, frowning. "I feel like..."

"Your feeling is quite correct, Somnalir." The black dragon smiled knowingly. "This garment is made from the scales of a bronze dragon."

Jace was taken aback. So the magic on it was bronze dragon power? He'd never properly met a bronze dragon in his life, and now he was seeing one's relic.

"Can we even take this out?" Vereesa said. "Won't the bronze dragons come after us?"

"Your question surprises me, Miss Vereesa Windrunner." Onyxia's terrifying eyes fixed on the ranger. "You dare to bring my sinew into my lair and wave it around without a care... and now you're worried about carrying a few bronze dragon scales?"

"I... well..." Vereesa stammered twice, then lowered her head and shifted the bow on her back. "Sorry."

"Don't be so frightened. If I can't control something, it's technically not mine anymore." Onyxia smiled quickly, then lowered her head and coughed lightly.

She slowed her speech. "The enchantment on this scale armor is no ordinary one. Every seven days, its energy peaks, allowing the wearer to completely negate the next blade strike or any weapon attack, no matter how powerful."

"Well, I don't actually know its upper limit. Because it doesn't simply use shield magic to block blows. At the very least, Nefarian couldn't pierce it with his sword, Maladath."

"Think about how much human and elven kings and nobles would pay for such a treasure. Your Sun King, Anasterian Sunstrider, would surely be interested. After all, no matter how powerful a wizard is, there's no guarantee a blade from the shadows won't pierce his chest... and his life is priceless."

Jace knew the black dragon was referencing Medivh, whose heart was run through by Khadgar's blade. But coincidentally, in another timeline, King Anasterian himself died beneath the blade of the death knight Arthas's Frostmourne.

And considering this armor was made from bronze dragon scales... it was hard not to think too much about it.

"Did you personally kill this bronze dragon, Princess?" Sora couldn't help asking.

"What do you take me for, little girl?" Onyxia said in a mildly reproachful tone. "This is a treasure the dear little Chromie lost to me in a bet. No dragon blood was ever spilled for it. You needn't mourn some victim who never existed."

"You mean Lady Chronormu?" Sora asked in surprise.

"Who's Chronormu?" Grede asked.

"We'll take this one," Jace said.
