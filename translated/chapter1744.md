## Chapter 1744: Discussion Under the Tree

The Grimtotem hadn't allied with the Twilight's Hammer, but Jace knew that in another timeline, the Twilight's Hammer had never given up trying to recruit Magatha Grimtotem.

You could tell from the tauren who had joined the Twilight that Cho'gall and his followers had long noticed this population -- skilled at dealing with the elements and already exposed to shadow magic.

The threat of annihilation from the centaur also made them more likely to walk an unscrupulous path, which made them even more suitable for Twilight manipulation.

So in a way, for the tauren to cooperate with Onyxia -- who was focused on protecting her lair -- during this window before the tauren had truly risen, it was a temporary insulation from the Old Gods and their cultists.

Having a young witch who was looking for ways to learn shadow magic connect with him was far better than her searching all the way to Silithus.

After saying goodbye to Natira, the group rested briefly under a large tree on the plains toward evening. They planned to eat something, get some sleep, and prepare for the next leg of their journey tomorrow.

They'd barely slept the whole time in the swamps, tense from start to finish -- first on guard against the black dragon, then against the Grimtotem. Only now that they'd set foot on the plains did they finally relax.

"So the demon Morophil couldn't find -- that's Mephistroth, the one who kept advising Nefarian," Vereesa said. "He's also the one who planned to use plague insects as a last-ditch attack on the relaxed Alliance soldiers, hoping the expedition force would spread plague to every kingdom when they returned home. And he knew full well that people in those kingdoms would blame you."

"Why would humans think that?" Mossa asked. "Didn't Jace defeat Nefarian and help the human kingdoms' soldiers take Blackrock Spire? Then he goes and spreads plague to everyone?"

"Because of Ursales, Mossa." Vereesa said. "Because of the staff in his hand. Not many people know Jace holds Ursales right now, but the demons know. You heard the Black Dragon Princess -- those demons are lurking among humans, spreading rumors, smearing Jace's name."

"The plague the dreadlords control is the exact same thing as the undead plague caused by Ursales' dark energy leak back in Winterspring," Sora said grimly. "When Jace brought that bear-man potion recipe back to Kalimdor, he was trying to save the innocent farmers in Duskwood infected with necrotic poison. Now we have to worry about it becoming evidence of his crimes."

Mossa lowered her head. "It's all my fault. If I hadn't been chasing those ethereal legends for... help, if I hadn't been so blinded by revenge, I never would have taken everyone to Winterspring."

"We were going there anyway, Mossa." Vereesa put a hand on Mossa's wrist. "And if not for you, we wouldn't have met such a good helper in Sora."

"That said," Grede rested his chin on his hand, "Jace, I think we should head back east as soon as possible. And by soon, I mean tomorrow morning. Rest up, then set off from the east coast -- the same place we met that witch Aegwynn last time."

"Don't waste more time going to Ashenvale to meet the forest elves, or to Gadgetzan to collect payment from Noggenfogger. We grabbed a treasure from the black dragon that'll sell for plenty. Those goblins, when they see we didn't bring coordinates for the black dragon's lair, will definitely change their tune and refuse to pay the promised reward anyway. Going would just be a waste of effort."

"As for the agreement with Onyxia, telling the goblins a few months late won't matter. Noggenfogger hasn't made a move against the black dragon in all this time. He's not suddenly going to grow a spine in a few months."

"And our reasons for coming to Kalimdor were three: first, to send the Wind Prince's head back; second, to find Onyxia; third, to visit Mossa. All our goals are done!"

"If we wait a few more days..." The dwarf swallowed to wet his throat. "A few more days... those dreadlords might really take control of Dalaran. Khadgar can't see through the disguised demon. If the Council of Six turns against him, what influence will he have left? How can he still help you?"

"Morophil couldn't catch that Mephistroth. That schemer who knows so much about you got away. He'll definitely join up with the demon in Dalaran to keep plotting against you. So we should head to Dalaran now and tell Khadgar. Before too much time passes. Before that demon can get a real foothold in Dalaran. At least when we left, we hadn't heard of anything strange happening in Dalaran..."

"But you forgot what Khadgar warned Jace about, didn't you?" Vereesa looked at the dwarf. "Those rumors about the black knight's scythe are clearly a trap, waiting for Jace to go to Lordaeron and confirm them."

"Back then he didn't know a demon had already infiltrated the Kirin Tor council and become one of the six!" Grede's eyes went wide. "You said yourself that Vargus doesn't seem like a demon. Jace thinks so too. And our demon Morophil met him and didn't see through him. What if Jace is wrong and the demon turned into Antonidas instead? What if it's your boss, Prince Sunstrider? If one of those two is plotting against Jace, what does he do?"

"I hate to say it, but you're not wrong." Vereesa murmured. "If it's Antonidas, that's even harder. Think about Kel'Thuzad... He worked for the black dragon and the orcish Horde for a long time. Jace provided a ton of evidence just to get him thrown in prison. If the demon really is disguised as Archmage Antonidas... he doesn't even need to do it himself. When Jace gets suspected of creating the plague, that demon council member will certainly use that as an excuse to let Kel'Thuzad out. Don't forget, Kel'Thuzad was his favorite student."

"Yes, yes, that guy too." Grede cursed in Dwarven and said: "Why doesn't Dalaran have a straightforward death penalty? Isn't that traitor ready for the gallows?!"

"If Onyxia hadn't told me all this," Jace said, "a trip to Astranaar might not have been so important, Grede. But now, I think we *need* to go."

"Why?" Vereesa asked.

"Besides the Kirin Tor, the only ones on this world who've fought the Nathrezim are the Kaldorei," Jace said. "Most of the old mages in Dalaran are dead. But plenty of Kaldorei who witnessed the Well of Eternity's explosion are still alive today."

"You want their help?" Grede pointed at Mossa. "The tauren are nearly extinct. They wouldn't even agree to give them shelter, and the tauren's enemies are just a bunch of dumb centaur. And you're saying you want to go see that elven priestess you've never met, to ask her to help you fight a bunch of demon lords?"

"But don't forget, centaur are native to Kalimdor, Grede." Sora said. "The Kaldorei probably don't want to interfere too much in their neighbors' disputes. Sorry, Mossa. But if demons show up, they won't back down."

"What about the Dark Portal, then?" Grede said. "Orcs and demons have been invading for years. I didn't see any dark-skinned elves on the battlefield."

"Whatever their stance, I have to try," Jace said. "And heading out from the northern sea is more reliable. Going past the coast of Northrend, we won't end up flying for ages before finding an island to land on like last time. Sora might be fine now..."

"I'm fine too. Don't underestimate me," Needleflame cut in quickly. "Why does everyone always doubt a Blackwing's flying ability?"

"Nobody's doubting your flying ability. But following the coast is always more reliable," Vereesa said.

"And we can reach Dalaran faster from the north," Jace added.

"That's true," Grede grumbled. "Hopefully those forest elves still believe in those old legends. I'm not holding my breath."

Jace knew perfectly well that Vereesa and Grede were only worried about a few demons trying to ruin their reputation. But he was worried about what might follow -- the undead Scourge, even a demon invasion...

Things had already gone beyond what he and Khadgar had anticipated. He had to find help as soon as possible.

Since they were in Kalimdor now, and Tyrande Whisperwind had been wanting to meet him and Vereesa anyway, why not take the opportunity to ask for help?

After all, in another timeline, it was with the night elves' help that the Alliance and the newly formed Horde had made it through this catastrophe.

Either way, he had to inform the night elves of the potential crisis on their side. What they decided to do was beyond Jace's control.

"But I agree with the rest of what you said, Grede." Jace said. "No matter what, we need to speed up. Eat dinner, sleep tonight, set off at dawn tomorrow. I'll find the materials I need and communicate with Kalrez about all this."
