Chapter 1748: Moon Priestess

"Bless you, travelers from afar."

The Kaldorei leader called out in greeting, something that sounded like "Ishnu-alah," which immediately brought back memories of Jace's previous life. Maybe because he didn't see Night Elves often, or hear their language and accents, every time he encountered them and heard their greeting, it felt like being transported back through time all over again.

"An honor, High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind." Vereesa replied in Thalassian.

"Ranger-Lady Vereesa Windrunner of Quel'Thalas," the High Priestess said in a courteous tone, switching to the Common tongue, "and Master Jace Sesso, magical advisor to King Varian Wrynn. At last I have the chance to thank you in person."

"You rescued the spirit of Eranikus from the evil temple of the Gurubashi trolls. That not only helped him personally, but also helped the Emerald Dream close a vast source of corruption that had gone largely unnoticed."

Between Tyrande Whisperwind's silver moon ornaments and her jade-white robes, her exposed skin was like the surface of amethyst. Beneath her tied-back teal hair, a pair of moonstone eyes were as sharp as an eagle's. Though she was a High Priestess, her solid muscles and upright, soldierly bearing made it clear she was no mere servant praying before a shrine — she was an exceptional hunter and warrior.

If appearances weren't enough, Vereesa's expression said it all. The way she looked at the Moon Priestess carried not only respect but also a hint of wariness. Jace had only seen that look when Vereesa was sizing up the most dangerous opponents — and to catch Vereesa's attention like that meant something. Jace also knew that pure combat skill was the least of this priestess's many available powers.

As she spoke, the impossibly large white tiger beneath her kept sizing up the few people before her, especially Needleflame on the far right. This beast was no ordinary mount either — if the old legends were true, she had followed her mistress since the War of the Ancients, ten thousand years ago. Even appearance alone told the story. Other nightsabers generally had yellow or pale green glows of natural energy in their eyes. But this white tiger, two or three times the size of the others, looked as if blessed by the Moon Goddess herself — moonlight flowed through her eyes, just like when Sora chanted her spells calling on Elune's aid.

To the native inhabitants of Kalimdor, Tyrande Whisperwind was practically a living goddess. Even for Jace and his companions, who had been through all sorts of chaotic adventures, meeting the High Priestess face to face was hard not to be moved by. But on the other hand, her tone and attitude toward her guests was quite warm — at least more approachable than the nobles of Quel'Thalas.

"We're honored to have been of help, High Priestess." Jace bowed his head slightly. "But that trip to the Temple of Hakkar was mainly at Somaeliel's request. If she hadn't sensed something off about the green dragon's situation there, we probably never would have ventured into that nearly unknown pool in the depths of the Black Morass."

Grede cleared his throat, as if to say, "Who are you kidding, you crazy warlock — you went in to harvest troll souls for Usalleus."

Jace shot him a sideways glance, hoping the dwarf understood that look meant "You talk too much."

Sora glanced apologetically at Jace. "I'm already grateful you don't think I caused you trouble, Jace..."

"Just as Lady Somaeliel said... no need to be so modest, Master Jace Sesso." Tyrande said. "You informed the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza that the Emerald Dream had been severely corrupted by the satyr. If it were only the druids and Ysera's children, they might have struggled to fully cleanse this corruption. And we guardians outside the Dream barely realized what calamity had caused this long slumber."

"This not only saved the green dragons, but also saved me and my husband, Archdruid Malfurion Stormrage. I'm sorry he couldn't come to meet you — the Emerald Dream is still fragile after the Nightmare's corruption and needs further restoration. He can hardly get away. Your arrival was also quite sudden — only yesterday I heard from the ancient trees that you had entered Ashenvale, and I hadn't yet sent word to the Great Barrow."

"Many Kaldorei might think we humans don't know about the Emerald Dream," Jace said. "But in Dalaran, many scholars understand the close connection between the Dream and reality. The druids guarding the Dream are also guarding our world. Helping you is the same as helping humanity against this corruption."

Sora nodded earnestly. Tyrande looked at her and smiled slightly. "Indeed, Master Sesso."

When the Scourge plague and demon legions invade reality, hiding in the Dream won't help either, Jace thought. Hopefully, in times of crisis, everyone can selflessly help each other.

"Even with Nordrassil watching over it, the wind in Ashenvale has been growing colder these days." The High Priestess stroked the white tiger beneath her and pointed toward the bridge leading to Astranaar. "Please, enter the city, honored guests. The Kaldorei have prepared food and drink. We hope you can rest well here."

"Thank you kindly." Vereesa nodded and followed the sentinels with Sora, Mossa, and Needleflame.

Grede sidled up to Jace and muttered in Dwarven: "Food and drink... sounds nice. It's just a pile of raw fish, washed-clean shells, crabs, and the drink is herb-soaked water. Not much better than last time in Feralas. Don't get your hopes up."

"Shh," Jace whispered. "You think they can't understand?"

The nearby sentinels didn't seem to react, but the nightsaber Grede was riding let out a "rawr" that startled the dwarf. He lowered his eyes. "This one can't understand, can it..."

"Sit up straight," Jace said. "Don't lean sideways and give that cat scoliosis."

"Don't act like you're some feline expert," Grede grumbled as the trotting nightsaber carried him onto the bridge.

"I noticed you're all armed and armored. Your trip to Kalimdor must not be just a sightseeing tour of Ashenvale." Tyrande led Jace and the others through Astranaar's cobblestone streets. "If there's anything you need help with, feel free to ask. As long as it's within the Kaldorei's power, we're willing to assist."

"Originally we came on a commission from King Varian Wrynn to track down black dragons, High Priestess," Vereesa said. "But things haven't gone smoothly, so we've decided to set that aside for now. Still, thank you very much."

"Since Deathwing's death, the remaining black dragons who betrayed their kin have been avoiding you at all costs," the Priestess said. "If those cunning black dragons want to hide, hunting them is no easy task. In Ashenvale, the trees and beasts help sentinels find their hiding spots, but once outside northern Kalimdor, it's not so simple for us."

Hearing this, Mossa's expression grew complicated. Night Elves always think this way, Jace thought. If it's not about Ashenvale or Hyjal, it's not their concern. Mossa must be remembering when they refused to help the tauren fight the centaur incursions.

"But you did find a black dragon." Tyrande turned to look at Needleflame, who was staring back at her. "A young black whelp. What is her name?"

"Fydegonie." Needleflame lifted her head, her eyes glowing with pale yellow arcane light. "That is my name!"

Hearing that name, Tyrande frowned slightly. Even the white tiger beneath her stopped. The nearby sentinels halted too, murmuring among themselves, as if something was off about that name.

"You are Deathwing's child?" Tyrande asked.

"No..."

Before Needleflame could answer, Jace interrupted. "That's a long story, High Priestess. We can explain slowly later. But you can trust her completely — she's different from the black dragons most people know."

"I can sense that, Master Jace Sesso." Tyrande said quietly. "You may not believe it, but I once saw with my own eyes those guardians clad in black scales... who called themselves emissaries of earth and flame, daring to fight the darkest, most evil things in Kalimdor, protecting this world from corruption. But all that ended with the unforgettable betrayal ten thousand years ago."

"She reminds me of those greatest beings..." She looked toward Needleflame. "And of that age before the irreversible fall and destruction began."
