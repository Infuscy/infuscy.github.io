## Chapter 1756: Demon Hunter

Warden Maiev Shadowsong had seemed displeased when Illidan spoke wildly about Malfurion. Jace could feel her impulse to step in and stop her prisoner from rambling further. But once Illidan relented and expressed willingness to help, she steadied herself and held her tongue.

"Thank you very much for your willingness to help, Illidan Stormrage," Jace said. "And thank you as well, High Priestess."

"As long as it helps the people of the other continent escape the demons' plight..." Tyrande trailed off, glancing into the cage. "I believe Illidan feels the same way."

Illidan approached the cage bars. His movements suggested he wanted to reach out and touch them, but in the end his arm only lifted slightly before dropping back down. Only when he drew close did Jace realize Illidan wasn't much taller than he was. He hadn't yet absorbed the power of the Skull of Gul'dan. Strictly speaking, he was still a Night Elf — just partially "demonified" in flesh and spirit by Sargeras. If the other Night Elves who feared him knew what he would transform into after devouring that artifact's fel energy, they would never call him a demon again.

Of course, Jace's own height wasn't typical for a human male either. Among the Alliance, besides a few known for their size — like the paladin Dathrohan of Stratholme or Danath Trollbane of Stromgarde — few stood taller than him.

"Nathrezim disguises work in two ways," Illidan said. "First: shapeshifting magic. They're good at shape-changing disguises, but this kind of magic isn't very powerful and isn't that hard to break. Because they themselves are a bunch of fragile... blood-sucking flies who only know how to hide and run."

He continued, "The second is the truly dangerous one: possession. Occupying a mortal's body, suppressing their soul. When they take over someone's body, they also gain all their memories and personality. Interrogation and tracking to find clues from a possessed person is nearly impossible."

"Have you considered summoning an observer — a beholder?"

"A beholder can see through a possessed mortal?" Jace asked.

"Some of the most powerful beholder lords can." Illidan lowered his head. "Especially those who've dealt with Nathrezim for a long time in the Burning Legion..."

"So you're discussing summoning a demon here?" Maiev Shadowsong asked.

"I promise I won't summon any demon at the foot of Mount Hyjal, Warden," Jace said.

The Warden didn't reply. But Jace felt that ever since he'd spoken that first word of the demonic tongue, this Warden had been on guard against him.

"The problem is," Jace said, looking back at the cage, "even if I could summon a beholder lord, I couldn't take it into human settlements — let alone a city like that. I have reliable intelligence that a Nathrezim is hiding in Dalaran. That's a magical kingdom built by humans, with protective wards crafted by the most powerful mages in the world that prevent any demon from slipping in unnoticed. That Nathrezim either disguised itself as or possessed a member of the ruling council to get in. An high-ranking demon would be extremely conspicuous there, and would cause me enormous trouble in finding the Nathrezim."

"How many are there?" Illidan asked.

"Five. But not all of them are hiding in that city." Jace said. "On the other continent, we found one inside a red dragon's dream. I think that's the second type — it was trying to manipulate the dragon's dreams and take over its consciousness."

"How did you find that demon through a dream?" Illidan asked.

"A green dragon helped us enter the red dragon's dream," Jace said. "We caught him inside and drained his soul before he could return to the Twisting Nether. So now there should be four left."

"Drained his soul." Illidan said. "I hope you understand what that word means in Eredar. It means you now possess a Nathrezim soul shard."

"That's right," Jace said. "If our intelligence is correct, that demon was named Pittheron."

"Pittheron... the lieutenant of Curator Krusas." Illidan turned his head away. "See, Maiev? A wizard skilled in manipulating demons and dark magic is using his short, passionate life to hunt demons and protect this pitiful world. And you — the strongest, most elite guardian in Ashenvale, a female warrior blessed by the Moon Goddess Elune and the demigod Cenarius — have wasted ten thousand precious years of your life on me. You should be ashamed."

"Don't sell yourself short, Illidan," Maiev said quietly.

"Thank you for the compliment, Warden." The prisoner laughed twice, then turned back to Jace. "You have a Nathrezim soul shard, human. That makes everything much more convenient. Let's use that as our foundation to explore a solution."

"A beholder can't see through a Nathrezim's possession magic not because their magic eye isn't powerful enough, but because they haven't learned how to identify it. Having a Nathrezim's demon heart, having their soul shard — things that carry their most concentrated power — would be enough to let a beholder's eye pierce any disguise and see through to their true nature."

"You mean giving the soul to a beholder to absorb, letting it gain the ability to identify Nathrezim?" Jace asked.

"You forgot what you just told me, Jace Sesso — that magic city that doesn't allow demons in, yet let a Nathrezim slip through." Illidan said. "Besides, you'd need a soul contract, a true name, to be sure a demon servant won't defect when fear of the Nathrezim lord spreads. That's far too troublesome."

"Cut out its eye. You only need the most important part." Illidan clenched his fist. "Infuse a beholder's magic eye with the Nathrezim's soul. Then crystallize the demon's eyeball with arcane energy, ensuring the soul shard's power doesn't burn through that fragile thing. You'll have a magical artifact that can drive a possessed person into a frenzy when brought near them."

Jace had never heard of this method. He wasn't sure if it existed in the game, but at least if Khadgar had known about it, he would have mentioned it back when they were planning to find the Nathrezim.

But Illidan had once been an arcanist — a mage who served Queen Azshara. During that time in the now-sunken arcane empire, the demonic knowledge he had accumulated was probably immeasurable, all of it lost to later generations with the sinking of Zin-Azshari. And don't forget — using the soul energy of Auchindoun to create a portal back to Azeroth was a trick Jace had come up with inspired by another timeline's Illidan. He would have to be very bored indeed to doubt this Demon Hunter's demon-hunting skills.

Listening to their exchange, Maiev Shadowsong involuntarily turned to look at Tyrande. The Priestess had already moved off to the side, silently staring at the black wall. The Warden watched her for a moment, then turned her head back. Her dark green great helm hid her expression — impossible to tell if she was suppressing rage, resignation, or simply unwilling to interrupt anymore.

Infusing the power of a soul shard into a demonic organ through enchanting — that wasn't difficult for Jace anymore. Arcane crystallization should be doable with Khadgar or another trustworthy mage. So now all he needed was a beholder.
