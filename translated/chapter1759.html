Chapter 1759: Rumors Take Flight

After saying goodbye to the Sentinels, there wasn't time to return to Astranaar. They only stopped briefly at an elven outpost at the foot of Mount Hyjal to rest before Jace and his companions set off eastward. Even though they hadn't made it directly to the Well of Eternity or the foot of Nordrassil, Jace had at least managed to circle around Mount Hyjal before Archimonde's arrival, take in the scenery, and tick off a small wish he'd barely dared to dream of when he first arrived in Azeroth. Ancient Kaldorei ruins from ten thousand years ago or more were everywhere. Some had been taken over by the modern night elves and turned into settlements, camps, or watchtowers. But most were abandoned, blending into the trees, vines, and vegetation around them — reclaimed by nature. Vibrant life energy flowed in harmony with the arcane power radiating from the Well of Eternity. Even Jace, a mage not particularly sensitive to arcane energy, felt no jarring impact from the arcane ripples washing over him. It was unquestionably gentler than the Sunwell in Quel'Thalas, even though the Well of Eternity's power was far stronger than that magical font. Jace knew Vereesa would bristle if he said that out loud, but even she was clearly enjoying herself. She kept turning back to look at the forest as they flew away from Mount Hyjal, looking more reluctant to leave than Sora, who'd grown up under these very trees. Flying northeast from Hyjal toward the coast, the temperature dropped sharply once they left Nordrassil's shelter. Snow blanketed the entire field of view before they knew it — Winterspring. They decided to stop at Everlook to restock supplies for crossing the strait and the frozen coast of Northrend. The last time they'd returned, they'd been forced to land and beg walrus-men for food, and that embarrassment was still fresh in everyone's mind. Since they weren't exactly short on gold this time... at least not short enough to buy supplies from goblins... they could prepare in advance. Goblin cities had post offices, and mail traveled by zeppelin. If that was too slow, you could pay extra for a gyrocopter. Safety was questionable, but as the goblins always said — high risk, high reward. Either way, it beat Stormwind, where if you needed to urgently contact someone or send an express package, you had to run to the Mage Quarter and beg the mages to send it by portal. For someone like Jace these days, that wasn't a big deal — but for ordinary people it was nearly impossible. Getting mages to do anything for you took more than just money. "Welcome to the Everlook Post Office, human sir!" a goblin with a tuft of yellow hair on his head shouted. "Judging by your appearance, you seem to be a traveling mage. Don't worry about your mail being intercepted by the Kirin Tor or any other dirty-handed magical investigation agencies. Our business is backed by the mighty Steamwheedle Cartel, and customer privacy is our top priority — even more so since the Dark Portal opened!" "Good morning." "What business can I help you with? Sending something? If it's a magical item, we also offer professional runic mana-sealing services. Even shipping across the Great Sea only costs an extra twenty gold coins. Unless you can find an ancient Kaldorei or Kirin Tor archmage to seal it for you in person, we're definitely the safest option here, heh heh." "Registered mail," Jace said. "To Gadgetzan." "No problem at all, sir. This route is our specialty, esteemed guest." The goblin expertly pulled out paper and pen and started filling out forms. Jace handed over the letter he'd written in advance. It was to Marin Noggenfogger, telling him to put Dustwallow Marsh exploration plans on hold and not let any more goblins fly into that fog-shrouded, unexplored valley marsh. Considering that the best way to maintain peace with Onyxia was through face-to-face negotiation, Jace hadn't written about the specifics of his talk with her. He only said he'd made progress in communications with the black dragon and hoped Gadgetzan wouldn't make any unilateral moves that might break the negotiations. Once he'd dealt with the dreadlord problem over on the other side, he'd go to Gadgetzan to discuss the specific exchange terms, or negotiate appropriate compensation. Flying from here to Gadgetzan and back to the Great Sea, then on to Dalaran, would cost an extra half-month compared to flying directly from here. Under current circumstances, Jace and his group couldn't afford that kind of delay. Of course, he didn't mention dreadlords in the letter to the goblin magnate. "The recipient is..." "Marin Noggenfogger." "Noggenfogger himself? Uh, then we'll need not just the sender's name, but also a seal, sir. And a return receipt address... yes, we'll need all of that." "No problem." The goblin nodded as he recorded everything Jace said, carefully wrapping the letter before handing it over for Jace's signature and seal. But when he saw Jace's full name and the Ring of the Stormwind Court Mage, the guy's composure visibly tightened. Jace caught it easily. Jace handed the letter back, raising his eyes to study the goblin. The little bastard was putting on a brave face, but inside he was in turmoil. "You know me?" "Uh, of course... Master Sesso. Sorry I didn't recognize you right away. Everlook gets so many wild wizards passing through wearing fake dragonscale cloaks. Elves, humans, dwarves — most of them look pretty convincing." It wasn't that simple. Goblins, regardless of status, generally didn't have much special respect for outside big shots — unless you were incredibly rich and had close trade ties, or they really needed something from you. But this goblin's reaction to seeing his name was very different from other goblins who recognized him. More importantly, he was trying to hide something. That kind of emotion was child's play for Jace to read, especially in an ordinary person with almost no understanding of shadow energy. Jace tossed a gold coin onto the counter, looked around to make sure no one else was nearby, and lowered his voice. "Has anyone been asking about my whereabouts?" "Tell me the truth, sir. Since you know my name, you should know something about my abilities. Trust me — I can see right through anyone being honest with me. Even the best liars can't hide from my eyes. And if you lie to me..." The goblin stared at Jace's eyes as they gradually glowed with purple darkness, already sweating profusely from fear. His lips moved but nothing came out. "...if you lie to me, you don't get this coin," Jace finished. "Ah..." The goblin swallowed hard, glanced at the gold coin stamped with King Terenas II's likeness, and said, "No, nobody's been asking about you, Master Jace Sesso. I mean, at least nobody's come to me asking for news about you. As for other people or other places, I wouldn't know." "Then what is it? Something about me?" "Well, that would... that would cost another gold coin, Master Sesso. Since the last question was worth one gold coin." The goblin gathered all his courage and cracked a nervous grin. "Just this one," Jace said coldly. "No more." "Alright, alright, don't get angry." The goblin nodded vigorously and lowered his voice. "I'll come clean. It's just that some news came from the other continent..." He carefully tapped the portrait on the coin. "There's news — well, rumors — that King Terenas of Lordaeron has this strange illness he's never been able to fully cure. And that the illness is contagious. Even the mighty Kirin Tor mages don't know how to treat it, because it's... the curse of the warlock Jace Sesso." "What?" Jace frowned. "What does that have to do with me?" "See, that's not what I said, Master." The postal clerk immediately waved his hands. "They say it's related to the Golden Prince's coming-of-age ceremony. That Terenas II was cursed by Jace Sesso at the Golden Prince's ceremony, and that's how he got sick." Seeing Jace lost in thought, the goblin gathered his courage. "If you ask me, that's utter nonsense!" "Master, you're the royal magical adviser for Stormwind. That Arthas is clearly Lordaeron's prince. What would his ceremony have to do with you? Why would the King of Lordaeron travel all the way south to the Kingdom of Stormwind to hold his heir's important ceremony?" "Ha! Those idiots in risk assessment just make stuff up with no evidence. Total waste of the Cartel's emergency communication channels. They've always been like that — believing every rumor they hear. I certainly don't agree with them! You definitely didn't attend that prince's coming-of-age ceremony, right? Right...?"
