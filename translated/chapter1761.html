Chapter 1761: Path Dependency

"The Violet Eye is supposed to be investigating Karazhan. What right do they have to demand entry to Ilgala Tower? Are they even doing their own damn job before poking their noses into other people's business?"

Through the snow blowing across the white hill, Grede squinted and spat out the snow on his lips. "Has the Kirin Tor lost its mind? The goblin said there were only rumors on the other continent. Now even the archmages are coming after you? Isn't that overkill?"

"Didn't Archmage Khadgar say he'd help mediate on your behalf, Jace?" Sora asked. "Or is that Nathrezim in the Kirin Tor really so powerful that the whole organization jumps at his command?"

"Have you forgotten, Sora?" Vereesa said. "Khadgar went to Karazhan with Jace. Now Khadgar's gone silent and the Violet Eye shows up in Stormwind. It's hard not to connect the dots..."

"I think so too. The fact that Khadgar and I went to Karazhan might have been exposed," Jace said. "If that's out in the open, Khadgar can't say a word in my defense. Medivh, the Dark Portal, the demons... He was already suspected by a lot of people in Dalaran."

"You and Khadgar are the heroes of Blackrock Spire," Grede said, stepping through the thick snow to stand in front of Jace. "Not just Blackrock Spire — you crossed the Dark Portal, saved the Expedition, defeated Kil'jaeden's demon army! Unless they've all gone mad!"

"Once they're willing to believe that crossing the Dark Portal and taking Blackrock Spire was all just a scheme to summon demons and spread plague across the world... there's no such thing as a hero anymore," Jace said.

Grede faltered. He looked at Vereesa, Sora, and Needleflame, then turned back to the silent Mossa. "But... on what grounds?!"

"Don't go back, Jace." Vereesa said. "Stay here where we still have room to move, where we're still free. Right now we're in Kalimdor hunting Onyxia. King Varian can use that as a legitimate reason to block them from Ilgala Tower, block any investigation into you. But once you go back, you'll have to shoulder all that pressure yourself. You'll fall right into their trap, get pulled everywhich way, and have no energy left to deal with the hidden demons."

Vereesa was right. And Uzallus was still in his hands. No matter what, they'd need solid evidence to convict him. Right now, neither Ilgala Tower nor the Dagger Syndicate had any evidence linking him to Uzallus. The most suspicious thing was a handwritten book by Gul'dan.

That book was full of knowledge about demons and soul rituals — evil and dark, sure — but it contained nothing about spreading plague or anything connected to Uzallus. Even if Turalyon handed it over to the Kirin Tor, they couldn't use it as evidence that Jace poisoned King Terenas.

But if he stayed here, wasn't he just letting the dreadlords spread lies and plague uncontested on the other continent?

Right now, the only reliable way to identify dreadlords was in Jace's hands. On the other side, besides trying to summon a banshee hunter stronger than Morofil, there wasn't much else practical. And even if there were stronger banshee hunters in the Twisting Nether, ninety percent of them were on the Burning Legion's side. Hard to say whether that would help or make things worse.

"I know you're right, Vereesa. But I have to go back." Jace said. "I need to find Khadgar first — at least figure out what's happened to him. If he's not imprisoned yet, maybe there's still a chance. Rumors can spread fast. The Violet Eye can act fast. But Khadgar is the leader of the Sons of Lothar and a de facto leader of the Kirin Tor. They can't just throw him in the Violet Hold that easily."

"And you think the mages of Dalaran will let you see him?" Grede asked.

"The mages of Dalaran didn't let me go to Karazhan with Khadgar either." Jace said. "So we'll just sneak in. We've snuck into demon fortresses, orc strongholds, draenei tombs. Why not a city of mages?"

"Exactly!" Needleflame slapped Jace on the hip. "I second that!"

Vereesa looked at the little black dragon and let out a long sigh. "Honestly, I've imagined having to hide out in the sewers of Zul'Aman for months among rotting corpses and beast bones to avoid troll patrols on Farstrider missions... I never thought I'd have to 'sneak into' Dalaran."

She smiled. "But I have to say — the idea's kind of exciting."

"Nothing surprising there." Grede snorted. "We've always been exciting."

For some reason, standing alone on this snowy mountain among the pines, facing the demonic pressure bearing down on them, facing the incomprehension of who knew how many allies and even their own kind... these few couldn't help but look at each other and laugh.

"But since we're going to Dalaran..." The dwarf scratched his head. "We'll have to cross a huge forest — all Lordaeron territory. If someone spots dragons in the sky, any mage would connect it to us, right? Everyone knows we have two dragons now. Thanks for that, Miss Fidegonys — you just had to show off your size to everyone on the Stormwind docks."

"Sora did it too," Needleflame immediately said.

"If Vereesa is willing to lend me the Queen's pendant again, I can try to hide our forms and get us past Lordaeron's and Dalaran's watchposts," Sora said. "Like you said, Thunderfist — it's all forest there. Queen Ysera's power will be exceptionally strong."

Everyone looked at Sora. Grede said, "I thought you'd have objections to sneaking into our allies' city. Turns out you not only don't object, you've already figured out how to do it."

"I only stand with Jace. I have nothing to do with Dalaran or the mortals inside."

Sora said this with a cold detachment, as if she'd turned back into that icy figure who first walked into the Everlook inn, disconnected from the world.

"There's another important reason I have to go back." Jace took a deep breath. "My parents. I can't leave them there unprotected. Morofil is watching over them, but those mages — or at least the dreadlord — will definitely try to use them to make a breakthrough."

"Don't worry, Jace." Vereesa said. "When we get to Dalaran, I'll contact the Farstriders and tell Sylvanas to send someone to the Stormwind embassy to negotiate with the Violet Eye. Tell them to stay away from Mr. and Mrs. Sesso."

"What reason?" the dwarf asked.

"Soon enough, they'll be my parents too." Vereesa stared at the dwarf. "That means they're not just Sessos — they're Windrunners. Anything involving them isn't for the humans to decide alone. Simple as that."

"Alright." The dwarf planted his hands on his hips and blew out a big puff of white breath. "Then there's only one question left — what if we get caught?"

"We leave." Vereesa said. "If we can't deal with them, then fine. We come back to Kalimdor. Take Mr. and Mrs. Sesso to Gadgetzan or Everlook."

"Jace Sesso's mom and dad share his blood." Needleflame clasped her hands behind her back, staring at the gray sky. "It shouldn't be any different carrying them."

Jace and Sora laughed. Mossa said, "If you don't like the goblins, you can come live in our village, Jace. Our tribe will always welcome you."

"You could go to Princess Onyxia's place too," Needleflame said. "I guarantee those mages would never find you!"

"Cough." Jace couldn't help but sputter. "Let's just... no. Pass along my thanks to your new mom for the kind offer."

Everyone laughed again. Things suddenly seemed much simpler. Sneak in, find the target, have escape routes or battle plans ready...

"One more question." Grede raised a finger.

"Damn it, dwarf, you ask too many questions," Vereesa said testily.

"Last one." Grede said. "What if Khadgar really is in prison? Even if we make it into Dalaran and find out where he is, we can't see him... Are we really going to bust him out by force?"

Everyone looked at Jace. Jace cleared his throat and said:

"That'll cost extra."
