Chapter 1763: Tundra... Still a Tundra?

Riding a dragon solo was certainly more comfortable than squeezing onto Sora's back with Vereesa and Grede. No worrying about that boulder of a dwarf turning around and knocking you off the dragon, no agonizing over Vereesa's silver hair whipping into your face and eyes every time the wind caught it. None of that mattered much over the Kalimdor plains anyway. But once they left Winterspring with Northrend as their destination, things changed. Sora relied on natural energy to glide at high altitude — quiet and capable of shielding them from both the Burning Steppes' heat and the snowy tundra's freeze. Needleflame? That little dragon relied almost entirely on raw muscle and bone, powering through by sheer brute force. She had a belly full of lava and thick scales for warmth, but Jace's ass was getting burned on the back while his face was unconsciously layered with frost. Jace had complicated feelings about these superficial physical pains these days. Even though his nerves still transmitted warning signals like before, he was well aware that this level of assault could hardly do any real damage to his body anymore. What really worried him was that one day even these sensations would dull. That this body would gradually stop feeling like it was controlled by his original consciousness, and something else would start getting in the way. As for exactly what that something was, he didn't want to think too hard about it. The sea between Kalimdor and Northrend was notorious for its harsh weather, probably because of the constant arcane collision between the Well of Eternity and the Nexus. Everyone who'd been here — goblins and high elves alike — remembered the sea conditions vividly. The last time Sora had carried them back to Kalimdor, they'd run into a massive storm. But this time, luck was on their side. The weather stayed clear. After two days, they could see the Northrend coastline from far off — a gray-green arc. Maybe a little too lucky. The sky was a little too clear. As they got closer to Northrend, Jace actually felt it warming up. His first thought was that his internal shadow mana was out of balance, affecting his skin's senses. Was this the "dulling" he'd been worried about all along? But after spending some time carefully feeling things out, he didn't sense anything wrong with his consciousness or emotions. "Are you flying lower, Fidegonys?" "Don't call me that." The young dragon rumbled from her chest. "Oh, so I'm not worthy now? Back to the 'only true dragons can speak Draconic' routine with Jace Sesso?" "I'm not used to it! You call Sora 'Sora.' You call Vereesa by her nickname. Why do you have to use my full name?" "Then I call the dwarf 'Thunderfist' too. I'll call you whatever I want." "Hmph." "Fine, fine. Needleflame. Hey, did you and Sora lower altitude? It's hard to tell over the water." "No. Why would you think that?" "Don't you think... it's a little warm?" Jace asked. "I've never been here before," the black dragon said quietly. "Hey, Jace! What month is it now?" Grede suddenly shouted from up ahead. "June, probably! I don't tear off a calendar page every day!" Jace shouted back. "Don't you think Northrend feels warmer than last time? If it's June, warmer is normal, right?!" Grede shouted again. So he wasn't the only one feeling it. That dwarf felt it too. Jace relaxed a little. Then Vereesa's voice came from ahead. "Hey, you lot! Look at the shore!" Jace straightened up to get a better look. At first he couldn't make anything out, but as they got closer, the coastline stretching for dozens of miles was covered in green grass. Higher up, there were even patches of low forest. "Did Sora take us somewhere else? Is this still Northrend?" Jace muttered to himself. "What does Northrend normally look like?" Needleflame asked. Jace thought about it. If they'd been heading northeast from Winterspring, they should have hit the Borean Tundra. Most of the Borean Tundra's coast was barren tundra — exposed black mud with the occasional hardy patch of yellow grass. But there was no way they should be seeing these vast plains and low forests. If this was the Howling Fjord... first of all, this wasn't fjord terrain at all. And the two dragons' flight time and direction didn't match. Northrend wasn't an island — it was a northern continent that took half a month to fly across. Two and a half days wasn't nearly enough for Sora to reach the easternmost fjord. Dragonblight was even less likely. Dragonblight was a complete snow pit. Vereesa and Grede were arguing about something. From this distance, Jace could only catch fragments. Sora interjected once with "I was flying in the right direction!" Jace could guess — they were probably confused by the landscape too, which was completely different from last time. Could winter and summer really make that much difference? Needleflame pushed ahead to catch up with Sora. Vereesa looked at Jace and pointed at something messy on the shore below. "Kalu'ak village, Jace. We've made it to Northrend." "Tuskarr move. And there are tuskarr all along Northrend's southern coast." Grede shouted. "Isn't there anything else we can use to figure out where we are? Something memorable from last time?" "Why don't we just go down and ask those Kalu'ak?" Vereesa asked. "Remember last time? A lot of tuskarr abandoned their coastal villages and huts and moved elsewhere," Jace said. "Yeah." Vereesa said. "Because of that undead king. Because of those reborn spider-people." "Spider... people?!" Needleflame asked. "What the hell is that, Vereesa?" "Definitely not as big as that Terokkar tarantula, Needleflame. You can rest easy," Jace said. "What tarantula? What Terokkar tarantula?" Needleflame asked. Jace smiled. "But look — the Kalu'ak seem to have come back." "They have." Grede said. "There are tuskarr fishing below that village... Should we fly higher so we don't scare them?" "They've lived alongside blue dragons for hundreds, thousands of years, Grede. They've even learned Draconic. They're not that timid." Vereesa said. "But really — does this mean the 'undead king' can't affect this place anymore?" "Are these re-grown forests the work of Prince Korialstrasz?" Sora asked as she continued flying along the coastline. "Using powerful life energy to resist undead corruption — no one's better suited than the red dragons to come here and fight the underground undead." "Hey, is that the abandoned watchtower we saw before?" Grede pointed east. "If it is, then we can confirm this is the same tundra we visited... or is it just another Kalu'ak statue?" "I can't tell from here. What do you think, little elf?" Vereesa looked in that direction, then her eyes went wide. "That's not an abandoned watchtower, Grede. There's a Kirin Tor Violet Eye banner on it!" "Should we take cover?" Sora asked. "Absolutely!" Vereesa said. "Even though... damn it... I'm still not used to this." Sora and Needleflame immediately descended onto a cliff ledge, using the windblown tall shrubs that had grown up wild here as cover. Even closer than before, Jace still couldn't make out the tower's details. But he could vaguely see a dark banner on the outer wall. What emblem was on it was hard to say. But when it came to that, trusting Vereesa was always the right call. "Did they see us?" Jace asked. "Probably not." Vereesa stood outside, looking into the distance. "I don't see any unusual activity over there." "Are you sure that tower's manned?" Grede asked. "It is." Vereesa said. "There are two mage-looking people stopped at the base of the tower. I can't tell if they're talking or what." "The Kirin Tor has set up outposts and camps here." The dwarf turned back to look at Jace and the two dragons behind him. "Doesn't that mean Krasus is doing pretty well? He and the red dragons and Dalaran's mages have held back the spider legion that undead king was commanding. Even the tuskarr have come back to live here." He poked around at the surroundings with his finger. "Look at these forests. These trees... Last time we were here, there was nothing." Maybe that was really it, Jace thought. The Lich King hadn't yet risen on Arthas and all of Lordaeron's dead, plus the demon legion Kel'Thuzad had summoned. His power was far from what it would become later. Getting visited early by the red dragonflight, plus resistance from various places like the ice trolls of Zul'Drak — that might genuinely stretch him thin. "So he's doing a good job. Then why are they wasting time and energy going after me and Sesso?" Needleflame lowered her heavy head, grinding her sharp teeth as she muttered. "Maybe they see it as the same thing." Grede laughed, but when nobody else joined in, he cleared his throat theatrically and shut up.
