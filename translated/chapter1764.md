## Chapter 1764: Sneaking Back to the Homeland

Jace, the others, and the two dragons stayed on that cliff until nearly nightfall. Vereesa kept a close watch on the mages' movements.

Occasionally, mounted wizards came and went. Midway through, a wagon carrying who-knew-what came up the eastern road and entered the camp. Whatever it was, they didn't seem particularly wary of the undead that might lurk in the deep forest.

Jace guessed the Kirin Tor and the red dragonflight had already cleansed this land, pushing the underground nerubian occupied zone away from the coastline and even further north. This camp was probably already in a rear area, serving as nothing more than a lookout tower.

Looking at that purple banner on the tower wall, Jace almost felt grateful that the Violet Eye had moved so fast, going straight to Stormwind to petition King Varian for permission to investigate Ilgala Tower. It had let Mathias Shaw tip him off about the Kirin Tor's suspicions ahead of time.

Otherwise, they'd have barged right into that watchtower asking about the Scourge and walked straight into the Kirin Tor mages' hands without even knowing it.

So now they'd wait until nightfall, take off calmly, circle around the watchtower's line of sight, and continue east. Since the Kirin Tor might have set up multiple observation posts along Northrend's southern coast, Sora and Needleflame would have to fly further out. Good thing they'd stocked up on food in Everlook.

Flying along Northrend's southern coast, day and night, Jace, Vereesa, and Grede kept a careful watch on the sky and shoreline — looking for Kirin Tor watchtowers they couldn't quite make out, or unexpected gryphon riders.

Before they knew it, snow-covered pines and vast snowy cliffs had replaced the grasslands. No matter how the red dragons revived the decaying vegetation, they couldn't reverse this frozen wilderness.

"Fidegonys." Sora rarely used Needleflame's new name. She slowed down, bringing the two dragons closer, and spoke in Draconic. "Have you heard of the Wrathgate?"

"I... I've heard of it." Needleflame hesitated. "From Prince Nefarian and Vaelastrasz. But I don't know what it means."

"Prince Sabellian? He never told you about the Wrathgate?" Sora seemed surprised. She turned her head toward the endless snowfields to the north. "Then he really intends to stay in Draenor forever. Never coming back."

"The Wrathgate — and this entire snowfield — is our resting place. Aged dragons consider it a blessing to sleep here forever. A fortunate fate. Because not every one of our kind is so lucky."

"Those bronze dragons who died in the Tanaris Desert..." Needleflame said.

"They died in the War of the Shifting Sands against the Old Gods. May they find the same peace." Sora said.

The black dragon flew on, staring into the distance where white made it impossible to tell where the earth ended and the sky began. Her voice was low. "Maybe that's why I — even though I'm a black wyrm — love these snowfields so much. Because many of my elders and kin sleep here."

"Now that's an interesting explanation, Needleflame." Jace cut in.

"Don't eavesdrop on me and Sora talking!" Needleflame was so annoyed she forgot to switch back to Common.

"Eavesdrop?" Jace said. "You two are so loud that even Grede Thunderfist's snoring can't compete."

On the other side, even Vereesa on Sora's back couldn't help but snicker. Sora didn't show much expression in her natural form, but Jace could tell — even if she wanted to laugh, she couldn't muster much joy. This was Dragonblight.

"Hard to imagine Prince Sabellian being so decisive." Sora said. "Completely refusing to tell his most cherished descendants about our past."

"Probably because he wants to be a new Aspect." Jace said. "Completely cut ties with Deathwing. Rule the black wyrm and whatever other dragons might appear on Draenor — like the netherwing dragons, which you've all seen on the other side. To do that, he has to cut ties with 'old era' things."

"But the Wrathgate has nothing to do with Deathwing." Sora said. "Our black dragonfather has already completely abandoned us."

Jace thought about the other timeline... no, about a timeline that never truly happened even in another timeline. The Hour of Twilight. The future where the Old Gods and their servants finally succeeded.

In that timeline, Deathwing died at the top of the Wrathgate. That Titan spire pierced his chest like a spear, marking the end of the world. No matter why he died there, at least he returned to Dragonblight like every ancient dragon before him — returned to this sacred burial ground.

In a way, he was lucky. Like Sora said. Jace didn't know why he found himself envying a Deathwing who'd never really existed in any timeline. Not that he knew what he was envying.

"As a special descendant born on Draenor." Sora's tone carried a hint of provocation. "Lady Fidegonys, when your time comes... will you return to this sacred land, like the rest of us born in Azeroth's dragon nests?"

"Maybe." The black dragon said. "If you could all come with me. Accompany me back here."

"Wait for you to die of old age?" Jace raised an eyebrow. "I think helping Sabellian save Draenor would be easier, Miss Black Dragon."

"No!" Needleflame suddenly jerked her body.

Jace held on tight. "See? My riding skills... wait, that's not..."

"Ahh!" "Sorry! Stop!"

Dragonblight's snowfields gradually gave way to pine forests. The terrain grew rougher. The gentle coastline became unbroken steep cliffs — the Howling Fjord wasn't far. Time to change course southeast.

Of course, they couldn't head directly southeast. That would send them straight into the undeveloped mountains at the northwest corner of the Lordaeron continent — the legendary home of the "Titan's Hand."

That was where humanity's earliest faith in the Holy Light had begun. But after the Orc Wars and the Arathor Empire's establishment, this peninsula — half the size of the Wetlands, shaped vaguely like Brittany — had been largely forgotten. Only a few isolated villages remained. Just the occasional pious pilgrim passed through.

Cutting through these forests and mountains was fine. But after that, they'd have to cross one of the most densely populated regions of the human kingdoms — the skies over Lake Lordamere.

Even with the great forests of Tirisfal powering Vereesa's pendant for invisibility, Lordaeron's or Kirin Tor's wizards might still detect something unusual.

To avoid complications, Jace planned to have Sora fly south along the western coast, all the way to Pyrewood Village near southern Silverpine Forest — along the Gilnean border where the Greymane Wall was being built.

Then fly through the Fenris Isle that no one dared set foot on to Ambermill. By sea, it might not even take half a day. The only checkpoint they'd need to pass was Shadowfang Keep.

That fortress had originally been built to guard against Lordaeron's army. It had no capacity to defend against dragon magic. Especially green dragon magic — which few in Dalaran or Quel'Thalas had ever seen.

As a de facto satellite city of Dalaran, Ambermill would be much easier to slip into from the north. They could also scope out the town and its surroundings in advance...

Things like the black knight legends, the plague situation, Jace Sesso's reputation, Khadgar's current status — planning their next move.

After all, the goblin's information was too thin. And Mathias's message through Kalelez was far from clear.

As he pondered what might happen over the next few days, the cliffs of the Tirisfal mountains and the dark, fur-like forests on their peaks appeared on the horizon. Jace couldn't help but draw in a sharp breath of cold air.

Who would've thought he'd have to sneak back into his own homeland one day?
