Chapter 1770: Half a Step or Ten Thousand

The Angler led Jace back into the alley, but he didn't return to the main street. Instead, after circling through a maze of winding red-brick paths, they arrived in front of a row of delicate little houses. Jace glanced back the way they'd come. The little black dragon was nowhere to be seen. But given the skill Needleflame had shown back in Auchindoun and Shadowforge City, she shouldn't have had any trouble keeping up. The gate swung open as the mage approached, and after crossing the courtyard, the inner door unlocked with a slight gesture and a murmured word from him. "What is this place?" Jace asked. "You could consider it my house," Khadgar explained. "After Medivh picked off the members of the Order of Tirisfal one by one, a lot of unclaimed properties were left behind in Dalaran — both personal holdings and ones belonging to the Order itself." "These houses, mage towers, warehouses — regardless of location, they're all worth a fortune. But no one dares to claim them. Some of them, barely anyone even knows how to unlock. As it happens, I'm one of the few who does." "Here you can speak without worrying about being overheard. Even Antonidas couldn't easily break through the magical wards here." Khadgar settled into an old but well-kept carved leather chair and smiled slightly. "Unless Prince Kael'thas took a sudden whim to blast this entire district — and all its magical seals — to rubble." "How reassuring," Jace said. "So, how was the hunt, Jace? Where did you stash Onyxia's head?" Khadgar asked. "Still worried about Onyxia's head at a time like this?" Jace said. "Didn't Miss Morophil teach you any dark magic for extracting knowledge and information from someone's severed head?" Khadgar asked. "I know the latter is the real point." "She's no Gurubashi headhunter." Jace slumped into the chair across from him. "The Black Dragon princess was cooperative enough. As long as we promised not to destroy her lair and eggs, and helped broker a good relationship with the goblins, she told us everything about the demons." "You're serious?" Khadgar paused. "That black dragon was that easy to deal with?" "Don't forget we brought her daughter along," Jace said. "Wasn't it her niece? Or... sister? How'd she become her daughter?" Khadgar waved a hand. "Never mind, whatever. So what did she say?" "You probably already know," Jace said. "There's a Dreadlord who's taken the place of one of the Council of Six. We suspect it might be Archmage Vargus—" "A Dreadlord infiltrated the Council of Six?" Khadgar sat bolt upright. "Are you sure Onyxia wasn't just messing with you?" "Then how do you think you ended up arrested?" Jace countered. "Somehow word got out that we went to Karazhan." The mage shook his head. "Then add in the fact that Lordaeron's king was cured by that medicine you brought back from Kalimdor, plus some of the details of your earlier conflicts with Dar'Khan Drathir that Kael'thas Sunstrider's people dug up from the magisters... before long, some troublesome leads about Ulthalesh, the Deadwind Harvester started surfacing." "The Council of Six figured I must know your secrets. And as it turns out, they were damn right. It's a frustratingly reasonable deduction." "So..." Jace's eyes widened slightly. "Suspicion, still suspicion. I didn't sell you out that fast. But if you'd stayed away much longer, that might've been a different story." Khadgar said. "Right now, even the six of them can't say for sure." "Like I said, nobody knows what Ulthalesh actually looks like — not even Aegwynn. And this matter has been kept under tight lock. Right now, only the highest-ranking members of the Kirin Tor know about it. Antonidas believes that the possibility of you possessing Ulthalesh absolutely cannot be allowed to leak out. Otherwise..." "Otherwise it'd be complete chaos," Jace said. "Yes," Khadgar said. "Then everyone would be asking what Ulthalesh is, why the Kirin Tor didn't keep it safe, and those scholars who were supposed to keep the secret — faced with such a terrifying threat — wouldn't be able to stop themselves from revealing the truth. Your reputation and the Kirin Tor's would both be... very hard to salvage." "If one of the Council of Six is a Dreadlord, do you think he'd honestly help you and the Kirin Tor keep the secret? Shouldn't he be spreading the news everywhere and throwing everything into chaos? I don't know. Maybe that demon has other objectives. But I honestly hadn't considered that." Jace looked down, thinking for a moment, then said, "I heard people from the Violet Eye were sent by the Council of Six to investigate my tower." "Given the situation, Jace, even I've had to be kept under control," Khadgar said. "Everyone and everything connected to you is now swimming in suspicion — even King Varian. Not that I'm complaining, mind you. I'm the one who sent you to Karazhan, and you helped me a great deal by helping me regain control of the Karazhan tower. I just think..." "Who knew we went to Karazhan? And told the Kirin Tor?" Jace asked. "I don't know. That's what I've been wondering about all this time," Khadgar said. "Who could possibly have evidence that the two of us went to Karazhan? I even considered Miss Morophil, but as much of a demon as she is, she's not that demonic." "What evidence does the Kirin Tor have right now? Has Archmage Antonidas told you?" Jace asked. "They're not going to tell a suspect like me." Khadgar said. "But their evidence can't be complete, or I wouldn't still be living in a luxury suite in the Violet Citadel. I'd already be neighbors with Archmage Kel'Thuzad, and you wouldn't be getting away either. The matter's still under investigation, which is why the Violet Eye wants to search Ilgalar's Tower." "Have they met Midnight?" Jace asked. "You left Midnight in that mage tower?" Khadgar asked. "Yeah," Jace said. "Where else was I supposed to put him? In my parents' stable?" The mage shrugged and said, "Then I guess they haven't met him." "King Varian didn't give them permission to enter Ilgalar's Tower," Jace said. "At least, that's how it was the last time I contacted Kallrez." "They can't get in, but that doesn't mean they can't observe from outside." Khadgar stretched his stiff back a little. "I trust the soldiers at Stonewatch like you and are happy to help protect your tower. The Violet Eye wouldn't dare risk angering the House of Wrynn by sneaking in, especially with those absurdly powerful felhunters you've got there. But neither the lads nor the felhunters can stop Dalaran's greediest grave-robbers from using arcane means to spy from the forest outside..." "King Varian is throwing obstacles in their way at every turn, but they'll find ways to work within the rules to do everything they can. So we need to act fast too. You say Archmage Vargus might be a Dreadlord in disguise. Do you have solid proof?" "No." Jace shook his head. "Just speculation for now." And part of that speculation was based on memories from his past life — things that hadn't happened yet. That's what he was thinking. "Alright. But if I had to guess, I'd pick him too." Khadgar rested his chin on his hand. "He returns from Draenor out of nowhere, just recently joined the Kirin Tor Council, and his competitor suddenly dropped out of the race." "But right now we don't have the means to be certain. Never mind whether Miss Morophil could see through a Dreadlord's disguise with complete certainty — she can't even get past Archmage Antonidas's barrier. Even if we weren't under suspicion, it'd be hard to operate here with her. You know that barrier is specifically designed to keep out Succubi. Dalaran has suffered too many losses to Succubi over the past thousand years." "Of course I know," Jace said. "That's why I made some extra preparations. Back in Kalimdor, we found someone and learned a method to identify Nathrezim from him. But for this method to work, I need you." "What method?" Khadgar said. "Wait, Kalimdor... who did you find?" "Illidan Stormrage," Jace said. "Do you know him?" "Illidan Stormrage?" The archmage let out a bitter laugh. "Damned bastard... Even if you don't tell me what the method is, I know we've just taken a big step closer to the Violet Hold." "We've already taken half a step in anyway." Jace gestured with his fingers to show a distance. "What difference is there between the remaining half step and a full stride, or even ten thousand steps? So we need to make the most of that last half step." "A warlock sure knows how to take steps, Jace Sesso," Khadgar said quietly.
