Chapter 1773: A Luxury Suite Fit for Khadgar

"Correct. Weakening the barrier is just the prerequisite for everything that follows, ladies and gentlemen." Khadgar's Mirror Image moved to the center of the wall before the group. "In that brief ten-minute window, the Violet Citadel won't turn into a completely defenseless shell. There are still many complications waiting for us ahead."

"Currently, four of the six Council of Six members are in Dalaran: Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider, Archmage Antonidas, Master Modera, and Master Drenden. Master Krasus is in Northrend. As for Master Vargus — the primary suspect at this point — if I recall correctly, he should be in Southshore or Tarren Mill right now."

"What's he doing out there?" Jace asked.

"Hunting disguised Nathrezim." Khadgar said. "Before the Karazhan incident was exposed and the Kirin Tor put me under house arrest, Vargus had already been secretly deploying people around the Kingdom of Lordaeron and Dalaran to counter Nathrezim illusions and mind magic."

"None of that concerns us now." Vereesa said quietly. "Whether he's a demon or not, we should focus on the current operation."

"Well said, Ranger-General." The archmage's Mirror Image said.

"So do the other four all live inside the Violet Citadel?" Grede asked.

"Archmage Antonidas, without a doubt." Khadgar said. "Aside from going to the academy to teach, he spends most of his time in the castle. And when we make our move, he'll definitely be there — after all, he needs to ascend to the castle heights to perform the ritual. So we don't need to worry about him suddenly interfering."

"As for the other three: Modera, Drenden, Kael'thas... Kael'thas doesn't like that castle. He'll be at the academy or the Quel'Thalas embassy, so we don't have to worry about him either. That leaves Masters Modera and Drenden as the ones stationed and living in the castle. Modera generally likes to pace the castle halls and corridors while pondering Arcane magic and incantations — she's always believed that only by walking can she clear her mind."

"And the other one, Master Drenden?" Vereesa asked.

"You've hit the nail on the head, Ranger-General." Khadgar said. "The key to the suite — no, the luxury cell — where I'm being held is in his hands."

"Didn't you say the Violet Citadel's barrier defenses would be lowered during Antonidas's magical ritual?" Jace asked. "Now there's suddenly a key involved? And it's in the hands of one of the Six?"

"Sesso, Sesso..." The archmage's Mirror Image dropped its arms limp. "You didn't think a single shared barrier with the whole city would be enough to contain someone like me, did you? If we don't make the Eye of Dalaran 'blink' just that once, we won't even get a chance to reach my cell door, let alone figure out how to open it and get me out!"

"Alright, fair enough." Jace propped his chin on his hand. "Cell key. What other defenses does your suite have? Why don't you just spit it all out at once."

Khadgar shot him a sidelong glance. "A Life Ward, crafted by Quel'Thalas's senior botanist, Frevin."

"Master Frevin?" Vereesa said. "He's Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider's senior advisor. Is he in Dalaran too?"

"The decision to confine me was jointly issued by the Council of Six, and all of them are equally committed to enforcing it. See how valuable I am, everyone." Khadgar said with a helpless look. "This Life Ward monitors the life force in my body. If it drops below a certain threshold, it directly warns Antonidas or Vargus."

"Sounds like it's for yer own good." Grede said.

"But that also means the moment Khadgar leaves the cell, it triggers an alarm." Jace said.

"Exactly." Khadgar said. "They're the most senior mages in the entire human world. How could they not anticipate that I'd think of using a Mirror Image to replace my real body in jail? So they came up with this scheme that looks like it's ensuring my health, but is actually a precaution against me pulling a switcheroo."

"But I have a countermeasure for that too. It depends on Sora." Khadgar said.

"Inject life force into your magical Mirror Image." Sora said.

"Yes. Communicating with a Green Dragon lady who specializes in channeling natural and life energy certainly saves effort." Khadgar said. "The life force of a normal living person is enough to fool that ward. I've already tested it."

"How did you test it?" Jace asked.

"You don't need to worry about that." Khadgar said. "This has absolutely nothing to do with our operation plan anymore."

"But your Arcane Mirror Image doesn't have the ability to retain life force. Even if I inject life energy into it, it's still just a false life mass." Sora studied the archmage's Mirror Image. "It'll probably start deteriorating within two or three weeks... a month at most, and then completely dissipate."

"One month, Lady Sora." Khadgar said quietly. "If one month isn't enough time for us to catch that Nathrezim, then perhaps we never had the ability to save the world this time around. Fate is no longer on our side."

"Alright." Jace said. "So... at the same time you leave the cell, we send this Mirror Image of yours — infused with life force — back inside. That way the room maintains a balance of 'one mortal's life volume,' and the alarm won't trigger."

"It's not that simple." Khadgar said. "That cell's threshold also won't allow living things to pass through. Strictly speaking, it's not that it won't allow them — it's that when a living thing passes through... it immediately triggers the alarm. Even though its power will be weakened during Antonidas's ritual, likewise, it won't be fully lowered. Just the sensitivity will decrease."

"Can ye speak in plain Common?" Grede said. "I'm about ready to not follow a word of this."

"Half-dead." Jace said. "He means only something half-dead can pass through safely."

"At the same time, the room must maintain what you called, Jace, the balance of 'one mortal's life volume.'" Khadgar added. "Which means my Mirror Image can't be alive when it enters the cell door. Only after it's inside can we start injecting life force."

"So if the Kirin Tor decides to adjust the barrier's targeting at that point..." Sora said.

"Yes, you'll have to fly back to the Violet Citadel at top speed." Khadgar said. "The problem now is how to notify you from several dozen miles away. Jace's imp is still in Stormwind — it has to stay there to guard against any new developments in Stormwind..."

"Dalaran is full of greenery." Vereesa said. "They're all Sora's eyes and spies."

"Yes. When the time comes, you talk to the trees near the Violet Citadel... have them tell me through the outer forest that I should return." Sora said.

"I don't know if that'll work, but if you think it will, then that's what we'll do." Khadgar said. "The Mirror Image's life replenishment is sorted. Next up is controlling my life force."

"Why... is this so blasted complicated?" Grede furrowed his brow.

"Because they don't have a reason to throw me into the Violet Hold yet, but they wanted the same effect, so they racked their brains trying to figure out how to keep me contained!" Khadgar's voice carried a simmering anger. "You think I don't find this a headache, dwarf? If those old fossils put half this much effort into fighting demons, maybe even Argus would be liberated by now!"

"Ye want me to throw me axe at ye when ye step out, knock ye half-dead, mage?" Grede said. "Me axe guarantees it's got no life in it — safe passage through the threshold! And it's smart enough to fly in and 'control yer life force' for ye!"

"Your axe would only guarantee I immediately lose every bit of life I have." Khadgar glared at him. "Absolutely not! It has to be precise enough. After my Mirror Image starts injecting life force inside the room, my life force has to decrease correspondingly. Once it drops to a level the threshold can't detect, I personally leave the cell, and at the same time, my Mirror Image fills up to 'one mortal's life volume.'"

"Precision decrease. You mean precision-cut off a certain number of arms and legs, mage?" Needleflame asked.

"He's talking about me, Needleflame." Jace said flatly. "You want me to use Life Drain to control your life force. Isn't that right, Khadgar?"
