## Chapter 1793: Bathing in the Light

"Isillien, what are you saying?!"

Priest Voss stared in shock at the man beside him. The many priests surrounding Jace's group were all stunned as well.

But the next second — before even Khadgar or Vereesa could react — the high priest Isillien collapsed to his knees in agony, stiffly craning his head upward. From his eyes and every orifice on his face, dark violet magic light burst forth.

At the same moment, a piercing howl erupted, so intense even Jace could barely withstand it. Everyone present — before they could restrain him or help him up — was twisting their faces and covering their ears.

Some of the younger, less experienced priests collapsed on the spot, unconscious. Others fled in panic. Khadgar started to chant a spell but was cut off mid-incantation, left with one arm raised high as arcane energy gathered and then scattered.

The magic light spewed from Isillien's body, coalescing into a shadow mass that writhed and shot upward. Burning candles toppled to the floor. Books and scrolls were whipped into the air. Old but once-beautiful chandeliers swayed violently, as if about to fall at any moment. Pristine church paintings were torn. Ancient busts of saints toppled and shattered.

Voss gripped his staff, barely standing in the chaos, shouting something to the priests. But his voice was completely drowned by the devil's howl.

This demon was far more powerful than the one that had invaded Vaelastrasz's dreams. But it knew it had no chance of winning here. The shadow mass didn't linger in the air — it shot straight toward the exit from the library.

Jace tried to chant a spell of Suffocation to stop the demon, but halfway through, the monastery's Holy Light scorched his hands and his mind like fire, burning the flow of mana through his entire body. At the same time, the shadow mass's chaotic, untraceable emotions and its lack of any real flesh made it impossible for his shadow power to land on it.

Light. I don't expect you to save me in a crisis. But could you — at the very least — stop getting in my way?!

"Go after it... Sora, Needleflame!"

"No good, Khadgar — our bodies can't fit through those archways..."

"Damnation, my axe can't hit it either!"

In the chaos, the shadow mass about to flee suddenly burst with golden flames. Another even more hysterical, piercing shriek followed, and the howl that had filled the entire library corridor weakened.

From somewhere, the sound of prayers began to gain ground. Jace noticed Voss and some of the higher-ranking, older, more senior priests had steadied themselves, calmed their minds, and were praying in unison.

The corridor was now blazing with fire. Carpets, books, weeping young brothers, despair, shock, fury, and thick swarms of bloodthirsty flies — all of it filled the sacred hall in a chaotic mess. And at the center, most visible of all, was the spinning shadow, burning in the Holy Light's flames, filled with hatred and agony — crystal clear in Jace's eyes.

*Sirenita, naci, cansani, neyele, sepuhtitaienus!*

His cloak and coat had already burned to ash. His tangled beard and long hair were ablaze. Even his demon-resistant flesh and skin were reddened and blistering.

But still he raised both hands, shouting the incantation for Drain Soul. Amid the golden radiance and roaring fire, beneath the sigil of the Light watching over all mortals from the vaulted ceiling, a dark blue beam of fell magic struggled like a serpent making its last stand, wrapping tight around the burning shadow.

The shadow jerked like a kite whose strings had suddenly been pulled taut, careening out of control and slamming straight into a tall, burning bookshelf. The cloud of shadow mist that sprayed out instantly stained the surrounding flames with the same vile purple.

A twin-winged demon in vile green ornate armor stepped out from within. Every step of his cloven hooves seemed agonizing.

Because his entire body was already alight with the Holy Light. His spread black wings were riddled with burn holes. His eagle-like claws trembled as they clawed at the dark blue "serpent" wrapped around his chest. Gritting his bloodstained fangs, he ignored everything around him and fixed his gaze solely on the human — bare-chested, guiding dark magic, binding him so he could barely move.

"Jace Sesso, congratulations... you've scored another point." The devil spoke in Demonic. "But no matter how you struggle, you cannot escape your miserable fate. And that fate was set the moment you first tried to claim Uzallus's soul. It is an ending from which countless powerful demons, mortals, and even transcendents have failed to escape over thousands of years. You are no exception."

"Let that ending come," the warlock said in Demonic, his voice low. "I've waited for it a long time. I'm almost out of patience."

The devil laughed. But the laugh soon turned into violent coughing. His black skin began to curl. His armor melted under the golden flames, revealing purple flesh and even bare demon bone beneath. He stepped back, step by step, until he hit the marble-white wall.

But his enemy wasn't faring much better. The young warlock's beard was entirely burnt off. His hair was ragged and sparse. Large patches of skin were severely burned...

"Jace!"

Vereesa could no longer hold back. She'd already uncovered Bellonar's Bow, drawn the string, and nocked a dream arrow aimed at the demon's heart. But Grede grabbed her arm hard. "Are you out of your mind, elf? If you kill that demon now, he'll just escape back into the Twisting Nether!"

"Trust Jace, Vere... trust him!" Sora also shouted.

"But he's in pain, Sora... he..."

Vereesa's hands trembled. Her face was streaked with tears. But in the end, she couldn't loose the arrow.

Behind her, Needleflame gripped her enchanted blade tightly, her eyes trembling slightly as she watched the human she'd once known so well. He looked like a completely different person now.

"Light above... damn it," Khadgar muttered, staring at this hell of flames. He tried to raise his hand, but seemed to realize he wasn't much better off — there was nothing he could do.

A priest who could still stand picked up a sword and charged forward, shouting. The demon — already a ragged mess — caught him with one hand and lifted him high.

"You are not worthy to kill me, weak little filth — "

But the next second, the devil's triumphant grin twisted. He dropped the priest weakly and fell to his knees. Every ounce of his strength was drained by that dark blue serpent. Between Jace's hands, a bright purple crystal had formed, gleaming with an eerie light.

The demon stared at the crystal. His blood-red eyes grew more and more vacant. His final remains let out a low roar and then burned completely away, leaving behind nothing but a pile of ruined armor.

The library fell silent. The hall was plunged into darkness.

The raging flames dimmed as if smothered by the demonic wind kicked up by the devil's death. The mad swarms of giant insects that had been biting every living thing in sight fled and scattered. Only a few embers crackled faintly.

Through a broken window high above, a faint beam of morning light crept in. It cut through the dust, outlining the silhouette of a thin young man, his upper body covered in burn scars. He blinked instinctively, raising an arm to shield his eyes.

*Please don't let this be more Holy Light...* He clenched the soul shard in his hand, thinking. *One more burning and I can't take it.*

"Jace..."

Vereesa ran over and wrapped her cloak around him, frantically fumbling through her pack for healing potions.

In the hall, many of the priests lay scattered across the floor and against the walls, slumped beside bookshelves. Those still standing were either frozen in shock or just starting to move closer.

Sensing the nearby threat, the ranger drew a shortsword and pointed it at the nearest high priest.

"Get back!" she shouted in Thalassian. "Anyone who takes another step — "

"What more do you want?" Grede hefted his axe.

"This is Tirisfal Monastery, Lady Vereesa Windrunner, Lord Thunderfist." Voss looked at the group, then at the pile of demon armor debris on the floor, his tone humble. "All the best physicians in Lordaeron are here. We can treat Master Sesso's... burn wounds."
