Chapter 1796: The Only Innocent One

Inside the cathedral of the Tirisfal Abbey, between rows of ornate white stone pillars, the hall was cast in sharp contrasts of light and shadow by the chandeliers and candelabras lining the walls.

Jace tilted his head back, watching the incense smoke rise slowly, and couldn't shake the illusion that this place felt even wider and taller than the Cathedral of Light in Stormwind.

Take away the Scarlet Crusade's gold-on-red Holy Light insignia banners hanging from the high rafters, and this was basically identical to the Scarlet Cathedral from the game.

Which made sense, really. After the Scourge war, the Scarlet Crusade was barely holding on in the corners of Lordaeron. Just managing to produce all those banners, tabards, and carpets with their insignia was already an achievement.

Standing here, Jace felt like he'd been transported back to his past life, back to this cathedral he'd run through countless times. Facing waves of crimson-armored Scarlet warriors with swords drawn, watching the scarlet commander at the far end of the dais, wielding his copper-gold war hammer.

But here and now, the hall was nearly empty. Aside from the priests by the candelabras, a few pilgrims gathered at the front, and him — a solitary suspect sitting on a bench off to the side — there was practically no one else.

News of the demon disaster in the library a few days ago had spread. By the next morning, when Jace and his group were talking in their room, most of the pilgrims and travelers lodging there had already packed up and left the abbey in a hurry, as if the Holy Light's dwelling had become a nest of fiends overnight.

Not much faith from his Lordaeron countrymen, Jace thought. Then again, he wasn't sticking around because he had any particularly strong faith in the Holy Light either.

"Master Sesso."

The call came from behind. Quiet, but jarringly loud in the empty cathedral. At the sound of his name, a few priests up front glanced his way, and two or three pilgrims got up and quickly left.

Jace turned around. It was High Priest Voss. "Good morning, Priest Voss."

"Good morning." Voss sat down beside him. "You look much better today. Willing to come into the cathedral, even. Normally, someone burned by the Holy Light's power is advised not to re-enter a place like this — whether they were wounded by defying the Light or... pursuing it too zealously."

"Father Montor told me as much," Jace said. "But I still wanted to come and see. After all, it was with the Holy Light's help that I defeated that demon."

"You're different from many shadow magic practitioners I know — whether from Dalaran, or Wilder, or elsewhere," Voss said. "There are many rumors about you circulating in the abbey. Many young priests... even those who witnessed the chaos — they think the Holy Light descended because you called upon it, to stop that demon from escaping."

"That's ridiculous." Jace smiled faintly. "Then what about my burns? Did I pray to the Holy Light to burn myself to death?"

"Many believers are wounded or even die from their zealous pursuit of the Holy Light, as I just said," Voss replied. "Being harmed by the Holy Light doesn't mean you aren't favored by it."

"Having witnessed firsthand how you executed that demon, Master Sesso, I'm beginning to understand Archbishop Alonsus Faol's decision to recognize you as the Guardian of Tyr. Honestly, up until a few days ago, I didn't fully grasp it — just like many of the brothers here."

"There are disagreements within the Church about how to see me too?" Jace asked.

"Yes. From the very beginning until now. And it's not just within the Church — it's the same across all of Lordaeron," Voss replied. "Especially as the stories about plagues, demons, and the fallen black knight have spread further and wider. Since the Dark Portal opened, every time people think something is finally over and a new era of peace is coming, a new, more terrifying threat appears — again and again. By now, confusion, unease, suspicion, and anxiety have spread across all of Lordamere and Tirisfal, Master Sesso. It's hard to fault ordinary people who've barely survived nearly twenty years of this fear."

"I understand, Priest Voss," Jace said.

"By the way, how are Priest Isillien and Bishop Fairbanks?"

"Bishop Fairbanks has fallen gravely ill from the plague and remains unconscious," Voss answered. "He was bitten by the vile insects the demons summoned. The medicinal broth has kept him out of mortal danger for now, but he's still under isolation and observation."

He paused for a moment, then continued. "Priest Isillien passed away the day before yesterday. He couldn't hold on in the end. When that fiend fled his body, it nearly destroyed his mind and flesh entirely — even the Holy Light's blessing couldn't save him."

"Has Priest Isillien shown any unusual behavior in the past few months?" Jace asked.

Voss shook his head. "What kind of..."

"For instance, did he meet with people he wouldn't normally deal with — someone from the capital, or Stratholme, Dalaran, or even further south from Stormwind?"

Priest Voss thought for a moment. "He's rarely left the abbey in the past year."

"Did he ever mention anything about me to you?" Jace asked.

"He spoke more with Bishop Fairbanks. Perhaps you should ask the bishop once he wakes up," the priest said.

These Nathrezim hid so deep, played their roles so perfectly, even the Council of Six couldn't see through each other. A bishop like this was hopeless — he shouldn't have expected anything substantial from them anyway.

From what Fairbanks had said before, Jace could tell the bishop had taken all those criminal rumors about him as revelations from the Holy Light. In Bishop Fairbanks's eyes, Priest Isillien might not even be connected to Jace Sesso or the whole demon affair at all.

The bell tower outside suddenly rang. Priest Voss looked outward. "That should be the Kirin Tor arriving."

"Looks like those mages aren't giving me time to talk to Bishop Fairbanks," Jace said. "They couldn't even wait for my injuries to fully heal."

"Perhaps going to Dalaran would be better for your recovery," Voss said.

The warlock and the priest stepped outside the cathedral doors, where Khadgar had just climbed up the nearby stairs and was hurrying over.

"Jace, Priest Voss."

"Archmage Khadgar," Voss said. "I'll go greet the Kirin Tor's archmages."

Khadgar nodded. Once the priest had left, he leaned in. "You heard that. The Kirin Tor's here to take us away. Well, maybe not that dramatic, but pretty much. Damn it, these guys got here way faster than I expected. They must have ridden gryphons straight to Balnir Farmstead — afraid the Church might let us slip away."

He lowered his voice. "So have you figured out how to get the Eye to King Varian and brief him on the important details? Once we leave for Dalaran, up until the inquiry, every move we make will be under those mages' watch. A private meeting with your king is almost impossible. Let alone handing him a demonic artifact."

"The best way is to have Sora do it," Jace said.

"Why?" Khadgar asked.

"Because Sora wasn't part of the rescue operation," Jace said. "The three of us — me, Grede, and Vereesa — can't get out of it. And the black dragon that was flying around breathing fire everywhere at the time is also implicated, no way she's getting off either."

"But Sora wasn't. Nobody saw a green dragon flying in the sky — and if they did, well, it could have just been passing through." He continued explaining. "Sora was just someone we called in temporarily to help capture the Nathrezim at the abbey. As a green dragon who'd been sleeping in the Hinterlands, she knew nothing about you being captured in Dalaran or us breaking you out. We only brought her along to counter the Nathrezim's sleeping magic and those terrifying insect swarms — to make sure the demon couldn't escape."

"Alright, that checks out." Khadgar thought for a moment. "But those mages won't just let Sora go based on a few words."

"They wouldn't dare forcibly confine a child of the Aspect Ysera either," Jace said. "Sora can compromise with them — agree to stay in Dalaran temporarily and cooperate with the investigation. When Varian arrives, she can use the excuse of residing in Stormwind to meet with him and explain everything."

"That's... reasonable enough."

The mage let out a small sigh. "We'll just have to let her try it, then."
