## Chapter 1797: "Cooperating with the Investigation"

In the reception room off the garden, when Jace and his group arrived with Khadgar, a group of Kirin Tor wizards was already waiting for them.

The group was led by Modera. Aside from several Lordaeron white-robed mages, there were also a few elves from Quel'Thalas.

"Good afternoon, honored Master Jace Sesso, Ranger-General Vereesa Windrunner, High Thane Grede Thunderfist, Lady Somnilia, and..."

Modera stood up as they entered, but her gaze lingered on the black dragon, hesitating for a moment.

"Fidalgonia," Needleflame introduced herself.

The female mage was taken aback by the name at first, then nodded. "Lady Fidalgonia."

Everyone in the room — Church members and Kirin Tor alike — looked tense. Especially the mages behind Modera. Jace could feel it — the air in the room carried a faint scent of rusted iron.

These mages were all elite Kirin Tor members. You could tell from their violet-eye-emblazoned robes and the staves in their hands that this wasn't an ordinary delegation.

When Jace shifted his staff from one hand to tuck it under his arm, the mages all looked like they were mentally preparing to hurl whatever spell they could at a moment's notice. Panic, tension, even a hint of fear mixed with the growing iron tang in the air.

Khadgar, on the other hand, looked perfectly calm — inside and out — as if none of these mages could throw anything at him that posed any real threat.

Or maybe it was just his usual blind confidence. The kind where meeting his teacher's father face to face only to get turned into a cat and tossed out of the room didn't even make him blink when he was caught.

"I am Archmage Modera of the Kirin Tor, a member of the Council of Six." Modera's introduction was quite formal. "I believe we haven't formally met before — Archmage Khadgar excepted, of course."

"Good afternoon, Modera," Khadgar said casually.

"I didn't expect to find you here, Archmage Khadgar, let alone participating in this Nathrezim hunt." Modera glanced at the nearby priests and two Silver Hand knights. "I thought you were still in Dalaran."

Most of the Church members, like most people within the Alliance, had no idea the Kirin Tor had locked Khadgar in the Violet Hold. After all, everything involving Khadgar and Jace's actions at Karazhan — including the matter of Urtales — had supposedly never left Dalaran's walls. Especially Urtales. Even the abbey's bishops and high priests didn't seem to know about it.

"Allowing a Nathrezim to infiltrate the Tirisfal Abbey means the Kirin Tor shares the blame," Khadgar said. "So once I found solid leads, as a member of the Kirin Tor, I had to take responsibility."

Modera nodded. She observed the somewhat hostile expressions of Vereesa and Grede and others on Jace's side. "Archmage Antonidas learned of what happened here and urgently contacted the Kingdom of Lordaeron. The council decided to send me to the Tirisfal Abbey as quickly as possible to support the Church and all of you, in case there are other demons hiding in the shadows, waiting to cause further destruction and retaliation."

"However, it seems you've already handled everything properly, Master Sesso — and all of you. The Kirin Tor is deeply grateful."

"The Kingdom of Lordaeron feels the same, Master Sesso." A white-robed mage cleared his throat. "King Terenas entrusted me to convey his heartfelt gratitude. Allowing a demon to enter the most sacred ground of the entire kingdom at such a time — the kingdom's guardians and wardens share the blame. He is willing to cooperate with any future hunts for remaining threats."

"Thank King Terenas for me, mage," Jace said. "But this demon seemed to be acting alone at the abbey. We just can't rule out that more demons like him are lurking elsewhere."

"About this demon..." Modera said. "Did you obtain any detailed intelligence on him? What was his name?"

Jace shook his head. Khadgar said, "The demon tried to escape once exposed, so Jace Sesso destroyed his soul immediately to prevent him from fleeing back to the Twisting Nether. Throughout the entire process, he revealed nothing about his identity. However, from his demonic armor, it's clear this demon was no ordinary one."

"Priest Voss also mentioned the abandoned demonic armor," Modera said after a moment's thought. "We'll submit a request to King Terenas to have the armor brought back to Dalaran for further examination. It may help identify him."

Priest Voss nodded. The group exchanged glances, and Jace said, "If there's any result from the investigation, I hope you'll share it with us."

"Naturally, Master Sesso." Modera looked up, took a deep breath, glanced at Grede standing nearby, then quickly shifted her focus back to Khadgar, Jace, and Vereesa.

"Apart from the Nathrezim threat, there's another matter I've come for." Modera said. "Two weeks ago, Dalaran was hit by a rather destructive storm, occurring precisely during the window when the Eye of Dalaran's magical wards were at their most vulnerable. We have sufficient evidence to suspect that storm was no coincidence."

"Ah." Khadgar cleared his throat. "I recall the weather was rather unpleasant those days, Lady Modera."

"Indeed, Archmage Khadgar." Modera said quietly. "But when a powerful artifact of a wind elemental lord also appeared on the streets of Dalaran at the same time, it makes it rather difficult to separate the two."

"I understand you were resting and preparing in Dalaran during that time, Master Sesso, Ranger-General, before coming to the abbey to investigate the demon." The female mage continued. "Archmage Khadgar once mentioned that you saved a wind elemental lord's soul in the domain of Ragnaros the Firelord at Blackrock Mountain. You may have some insight into this unusual elemental disturbance. So I've also come here to invite you back to Dalaran to cooperate with the Kirin Tor's investigation."

Despite Modera's polite tone, her tension was affecting her speech. The elite mages behind her were even more tense — elves and Lordaeronians alike, as if they'd come up this mountain prepared to die.

Khadgar glanced back at Jace with an apologetic look that said, "My bad for talking too much."

Though to be fair, it wasn't entirely his fault. The part where they used the Wind Prince's power to shatter Ragnaros's protection runes at the last moment in Molten Core was something the Three Hammers and the Kirin Tor had all found out about eventually. The Kirin Tor and Quel'Thalas mages had been investigating those protection runes too. Chatting with Khadgar about the Bound Skull of Thunderaan didn't seem suspicious at the time. After all, who could have predicted they'd need the Wind-Kissed Blade for a little "jailbreak plan" later on?

Grede, meanwhile, stood there looking like it had nothing to do with him. He'd trimmed his beard a bit more since leaving Dalaran, but there wasn't much modification room left on what remained. At this point, even someone unfamiliar with dwarf appearances would probably think he and that Hominir Deepstone looked like brothers.

And from the way Modera glanced at him, she was well aware of it too. Even if, at first glance, that dwarf didn't look quite like Grede Thunderfist, by now everyone should have figured it out.

"Of course, Archmage Modera," Jace said flatly.

The moment those words left his mouth, the mages — and even some of the Church members — seemed to exhale slightly.

High Priest Voss, standing nearby, felt the same. The strange storm might have nothing to do with the Church, but with tensions running this high, any conflict would have spelled trouble for this poor abbey.
