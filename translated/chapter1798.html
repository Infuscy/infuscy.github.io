Chapter 1798: Frank and Open

There were quite a few mages from the Kirin Tor and Lordaeron at Balmind, and even some Red Mages of Stromgarde on the streets. They hadn't all followed Modera and Jace's group when they left, apparently intending to stay at the monastery to continue investigating the demons.

But what was the point of having so many mages here? Even Illidan Stormrage's fel artifact could only sense that a Nathrezim was nearby. These mages didn't even have Teron Gorefiend's demonic sight. At best, they were just going through the motions at the chapel.

After all, if the Kirin Tor couldn't even find the dreadlord right under their noses in Dalaran, Jace had no reason to expect them to accomplish anything useful.

It wasn't just about the dreadlords stirring up trouble before and during the Third War... Even years later, after the Burning Legion had invaded Outland and Azeroth multiple times, after battles with Kil'jaeden and Archimonde at the Sunwell and the Hellfire Citadel in another timeline, the Kirin Tor still couldn't identify Kasanathir possessing Vargoth.

And this was after that demon had been imprisoned at the bottom of Dalaran's prison for nearly two thousand years.

Khadgar must have realized this too. Even if he'd still held out hope for his colleagues when they returned from Blackrock Spire, that hope was surely running thin now.

Pitasseron was dead. A second unidentified dreadlord had been killed too. According to Onyxia, that left three — including one in the Kirin Tor's Council of Six.

Modera, who was flying in the lead, could already be ruled out. The eye that Sora was carrying hadn't reacted to her.

So that left two dreadlords whose locations and identities were still unknown.

If they could identify the dreadlord in the Council of Six, they might be able to find clues about the remaining two. After all, the dreadlords were clearly coordinating, with this council member spreading rumors and creating panic.

But that was hoping for the best. Isillien, during his time at the chapel, had appeared to behave himself. Yet so many members of the clergy — including Bishop Fairbanks — had taken his demonic whisperings as the Holy Light's guidance, even traveling to the capital to report these so-called revelations to the king.

Still, even if the outcome wasn't ideal, eliminating one dreadlord — possibly the most troublesome of them, maybe even Tichondrius himself — would still be a win. That's what Jace figured.

As their gryphons approached Dalaran, Jace could hear the clamor of voices from far away. Quite a few mages had gathered in Krasus's Square, and there weren't many dragonhawks or gryphons landing or taking off.

His nerves immediately tightened. He glanced at Modera up ahead. The female mage's face was expressionless as she stared at the landing spot ahead.

Were the Kirin Tor planning to arrest him right here?

He tightened his grip on Uthalesh and glanced back at Vereesa and the others, but soon realized the emotions radiating from below weren't the tension of an impending battle.

Antonidas stood at the center, surrounded by many high-ranking Kirin Tor members. The gryphons landed in the square, their wing downwash rattling the mages' robes and wide-brimmed hats. The old mage raised a hand to shield his eyes, steadying himself with his crystal staff as he stepped forward heavily.

Amid the rustling of the gryphons folding their wings and low grumbling, he announced loudly: "Welcome back, Khadgar. Welcome back, Master Modera."

"Master Antonidas." Modera dismounted and bowed slightly. Khadgar took a deep breath, stepped forward, and said, "Master Antonidas."

Undoubtedly, both the investigation into Khadgar and Jace's infiltration of Karazhan, as well as the suspicions regarding Uthalesh and the plague, were still in the evidence-gathering and investigative phase.

There was no conviction yet, and the Kirin Tor wouldn't dare to publicly execute the Son of Lothar and the Hero of Blackrock Spire in front of the entire city.

But the lineup of mages gathered in Krasus's Square at this moment was still quite impressive: elite battle mages in arcane crystal-barrier robes, wardens holding crystal staves, and several leaders of the defense department whom Jace had dealt with before.

It was hard to tell whether this was a heightened welcome for Jace, Khadgar, and the others, or preparations for a large-scale conflict that might break out at any moment.

Jace and his group dismounted from their gryphons. Antonidas looked their way and said, "Lady Vereesa, High Lord Grede, Lady Somaryll..."

After they exchanged greetings in turn, the old mage's gaze settled on the warlock at the far right.

"Jace Sesso." The old mage inclined his head slightly, then said, "I never expected you would visit Dalaran again."

"Dalaran is unavoidable, Master Antonidas," Jace said.

"A few days ago, I heard what happened at the Grand Monastery. I was deeply shocked," Antonidas said. "If the dreadlord at Blackrock Spire was merely clues and shadows, then the demon at the Tirisfal Grand Monastery has confirmed that the demons have launched a new invasion plan. The Kirin Tor must take responsibility."

Modera said, "In fact, the Kirin Tor has been strictly investigating all clues related to demon summoning and demon material trade within and beyond the Seven Kingdoms. If demon materials convenient for demonic and void research were once in a gray area, then since Blackrock Spire, we Alliance leaders have closed that last loophole. We just didn't expect it was already too late — that the Burning Legion's followers had summoned such a powerful demon and even infiltrated the Church."

"Going forward, the Kirin Tor will focus more on investigating existing demonic traces," Antonidas said. "We have already submitted a request to Archbishop Alonsus Faol, hoping to conduct a more comprehensive investigation at the Tirisfal Grand Monastery to aid in the search for hidden demons."

"I've been saying this all along, Master," Khadgar said. "The Kirin Tor has wasted too much energy on unnecessary matters. I'm not referring to Northrend."

"Start by pulling the Violet Eye operatives out of Stormwind," Jace said. "I think those elite investigators who've spent years researching in Karazhan are better suited to investigating residual demonic traces, Master Antonidas."

Modera glanced sideways at the Lord of Dalaran. Khadgar cleared his throat like someone watching a show.

"The investigation in the south is indeed no longer necessary," the old mage said, looking back at the few people staring coldly from the edge of Krasus's Square. "But the Kirin Tor still has some unresolved questions, Jace Sesso. Therefore, we hope that you, Lady Vereesa Windrunner, and High Lord Grede Thunderfist will remain in Dalaran for a time."

"I also want to resolve these questions as quickly as possible, Master Antonidas." Jace hugged his staff and said, "I know what the Kirin Tor suspects. From the day Magister Dal'kand of Quel'Thalas died in Raven Hill until today, you've never stopped your covert investigations and suspicions of me."

"In the past, I was unwilling to come to Dalaran, unwilling to confront you directly. But at this extremely dangerous juncture — especially with the Nathrezim appearing again in Lordaeron, possibly even entering the capital city, entering your Dalaran — it's time to clear the air between us and face the enemy together."

"You can invoke the Kirin Tor Council's right of inquiry and hold a public hearing before the entire Alliance. And I — His Majesty Varian's magical advisor, the Royal Mage of the Kingdom of Stormwind, an Alliance member, and a cooperator of the Kirin Tor — will answer all your questions candidly there."

The old mage was slightly taken aback, then looked at Khadgar beside him. Khadgar wore an expression that said, "This was all my doing."

"Then, your conditions?" Antonidas asked.

"My conditions," Jace said. "I need not just the six masters of the Council, but all Alliance leaders to be present as witnesses. And if I prove my innocence, the Kirin Tor must cooperate with me in investigating suspicious individuals within the Alliance. We can't repeat the tragedy of Priest Isillien."

Two members of the Council of Six exchanged glances. Antonidas's face shed the politeness he'd shown the crowd in the square, and his attitude turned serious. "This matter must be submitted to the Council for discussion. However, I believe that even with Master Krasus absent, we should be able to reach a consensus."

"Can Master Krasus return to Dalaran in time for this hearing?" Jace asked.

"I will immediately send two mages to the Kirin Tor's Northrend operations headquarters at Winterguard to notify him as soon as possible," Antonidas said. "But even I cannot guarantee that Master Krasus can return to Dalaran in time."

"If it concerns Jace Sesso," Khadgar said, "I believe there should be no problem, Master."

"I hope so," the old mage said.
