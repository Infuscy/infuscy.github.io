Chapter 180: The Most Unexpected Reward

He'd drunk half a vial of Sunwell water that night?!

No wonder he'd felt like he'd been pumped full of adrenaline, and his wound had healed so fast.

Jace of course knew exactly how powerful Sunwell water was, and he knew what something like that meant to an elf. He asked guiltily, "That… must have been incredibly precious…"

"Of course," Vereesa explained. "It can heal grievous magical injuries and ease physical damage as well. Even carrying it close by brings considerable benefits to those near you. It's just that the Sunwell's sanctum doesn't normally permit ordinary people inside, so obtaining a vial is very difficult."

"Then I'm… truly sorry."

Jace said, awkwardly. He figured he'd probably managed to offend her.

Thinking back to grabbing the bottle and knocking it back last night — that really hadn't been the proper way to treat something as precious as Sunwell water. Kaelfal, as a high elf, had — even with a mage's intense hunger for magic — only taken the smallest sip.

Jace had thought at the time it was because his injuries were too severe for his lips to move much.

Vereesa said, "Don't think of it that way. It healed you — that's fulfilling its true purpose, Mr. Sesso. Besides, you were injured that badly. If you'd missed the window for treatment and died, not even an entire jug of Sunwell water could have brought you back. I was only worried because I'd never seen an elf drink that much in one go… but if you're not feeling anything wrong now, there's nothing to worry about."

That actually left Jace at a loss.

"Speaking of which — you are a warlock, aren't you, from last night?" The elf lowered her voice and leaned in. "You know this city doesn't welcome warlocks, don't you? Even the shadow magic researchers in Quel'Thalas face the same. I'm sorry, but I've already told the Kirin Tor about what happened last night."

Jace's scalp prickled. "Huh? You told them everything?"

"I didn't describe the spells you used, and I didn't go into detail about the whole thing," she said, guilt creeping into her tone. "But they learned your name… Actually, the Kirin Tor were only asking about Professor Kaelfal's injuries. I was rushing to explain the full story behind the Teron Gorefiend incident, and only after I'd let it slip did I realize this could bring you enormous trouble."

"Then it's fine. I'll just say I did nothing at all," Jace said. "I was about to be killed by Teron Gorefiend, and then the two of you showed up and saved my life — that's the story, all right? You wouldn't want the Kirin Tor to haul me in, would you? If it came to that, your half-vial of Well water might really go to waste."

"Understood," Vereesa agreed quickly — but after a moment's hesitation she added, "But I know Gorefiend was actually driven to flee by you. By your magic."

Jace scratched his neck in mild frustration. If Vereesa hadn't said that, he might have been able to let it go.

"As long as Kaelfal is saved, that's what matters. I don't want anyone dying to save me. As for the rest… nobody knowing is a whole lot better than getting dragged away. Good thing I came to talk to you — if I'd been a little earlier or later and missed you, that would have been a disaster. Make sure you tell Kaelfal too — don't let him run his mouth."

"I understand," Vereesa nodded, then asked, "Actually, I've always been curious — how did you and your dwarf friend come to know him? He seemed quite concerned about you two. You may know he's not very fond of outsiders — I mean, those from outside Quel'Thalas."

"Uh…" Jace said, slightly hesitant. "In Duskwood — south of Elwynn Forest — we got into a fight. We really only got to know each other once we reached Dalaran. Ran into each other by chance and got to talking. Nothing complicated."

Just then he heard a familiar whistle from behind him, and said at once, "My friend's waiting for me, Miss Vereesa. A pleasure meeting you. Until next time. I really am grateful for that bottle… and I really am sorry."

"Wait," Vereesa called after Jace. "There's one more thing… I heard from Kaelfal that your friend is a gryphon rider who was hurt by bat venom and lost the use of one eye. Is that right?"

"Yes," Jace said. "Is there something you can do?"

"I know a senior who can treat that kind of disability. His name is Vinaire the Sunseeker — he's a master botanist." Vereesa said, "He has a good relationship with my family, and he practically watched me grow up. If I wrote him a letter, I'm certain he'd be willing to help."

Seriously? Jace never in his life imagined he'd end up connected to someone this remarkable. But the connection was there — now it was the logistics that were the problem, when he'd never expected the hardest part to be solved first.

"We can't go to Quel'Thalas, though, miss," Jace said helplessly.

"Ah, that's true. I'd hoped I could be of some help…" The light in Vereesa's blue eyes dimmed.

Jace ventured, "Could General Sylvanas do us a small favor…?"

Vereesa said, awkwardly, "My sister… she probably wouldn't want to see me right now, let alone do me any favors."

Their relationship was that bad right now?

Jace hesitated, then asked, "Is there a botanist living in Quel'Danel? We could try our luck there."

"You're heading to the Hinterlands?" Vereesa asked, as if something had just occurred to her. "Is your friend returning to Aerie Peak?"

"Yes, he's going back for Winterveil," Jace said.

"I think I could ask Master Vinaire to meet you in Quel'Danel," Vereesa offered.

"Wouldn't that… be too much trouble for him?"

Jace said that, but inside he was barely containing his excitement.

He glanced back at Grede. Grede was already getting impatient, his single eye blinking furiously, signaling at him to wrap it up.

"It's no trouble," Vereesa said. "He loves traveling by dragonhawk, and the Hinterlands aren't far from Quel'Thalas — well, not for dragonhawks, anyway."

"I don't know how to thank you or that botanist," Jace said.

"If he knew what happened last night, he'd be just as glad to help you. He hates death knights no less than I do," Vereesa said enthusiastically. "It's settled, then. I'll write the letter this afternoon and ask him to stay in Quel'Danel for a while during the dwarves' Winterveil."

And Jace was easily twice as excited as she was. This was a real chance to solve Grede's problem.

He couldn't wait to leave Dalaran and set out for the Hinterlands — he just wished time would move faster.

Come to think of it, everything he'd been through last night didn't hurt quite so much anymore.

If he hadn't met Vereesa, he'd probably have been thinking about heading to Stranglethorn Vale to see if there were any troll druids in the Gurubashi Arena… and whether they'd be willing to cure Grede's ailment for money.

And faced with a troll… Grede would very likely rather stay sick than accept their help. That was exactly why Jace had never dared bring it up with him.

But right now, there wasn't a single night elf druid he knew of anywhere on the entire Eastern Kingdoms. Even Broll Bearmantle — the night elf druid who'd later fight alongside Varian in the gladiator tournaments — wouldn't arrive here until after the Scourge invasion. But none of that was something he needed to worry about now.
