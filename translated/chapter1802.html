Chapter 1802: The Source of Everything

"As everyone knows," Antonidas declared, lifting his head toward the Church and Silver Hand knights in the distance, "Lord Jace Sesso is a dark magic and demonology scholar trusted by the Kirin Tor. He has long since proven his loyalty to the Alliance and his steadfast faith in the Holy Light through a series of catastrophic events that endangered the Alliance and indeed the entire physical world — Grim Batol being a prime example."

"At least for now, Lord Jace Sesso remains a dark magic and demonology researcher trusted by the Kirin Tor. The Council of Six's stance on this issue has not wavered."

"The Church of the Holy Light concurs," Alonsus Faol responded.

"The Silver Hand as well," Turalyon declared loudly.

At this, Jace caught sight of a golden-haired elven ranger among the Farstrider forces below, lifting her head toward Turalyon on the high dais. That must be Alleria. And the silver-haired elf in the light purple robe beside her should be Vereesa.

From this distance, pinpointing her position was no easy feat.

"Thank you very much." Antonidas continued. "Therefore, this hearing will not touch upon the use of dark magic or demon summoning. The focus will be strictly on the legacy of the Guardians of Tirisfal — specifically, Uthalesh, better known as the magical artifact, the Windreaver. I believe this is something Your Majesties and Lords were already informed of before coming to Dalaran."

"Uthalesh, the Windreaver. Even for many seasoned demonologists, it is a magical artifact that exists only in ancient texts and the folklore of the southern lands. But no one can deny that its very existence once forced the founders and early settlers of the Kingdom of Stormwind to abandon any attempt to enter the forests and deep valleys of southern Elwynn. The ancient and powerful Gurubashi troll empire also suffered heavy losses from it."

"Although, according to the records of the Order of Tirisfal, Uthalesh was destroyed a thousand years ago by the then-Guardian Skavel, the passage of time — especially since the Dark Portal — has seen a resurgence of rumors that may prove this matter is not as reassuring as the records suggest."

"At the center of this storm of rumors, Lord Jace Sesso — are you willing to swear that the answers you are about to give are truthful and reliable? Because whatever you say next will inevitably affect the entire Alliance, and even the whole world."

A tall, robust, golden-haired paladin carrying a heavy tome of scripture approached Jace. He was Uther the Lightbringer, whom Jace had seen once at the coming-of-age ceremony of Lordaeron's prince.

This paladin's face resembled a true marble statue. Even his pupils emitted a faint golden light, making it extremely difficult to meet his gaze directly. Jace had no doubt that if a Nathrezim demon appeared on the spot, Uther alone could smash it to pieces.

Jace placed his hand on the scripture and declared loudly, "By the Holy Light, I swear that I will speak to the best of my knowledge, that all I say is true, and that I will conceal nothing."

As the words fell, the runic letters on the scripture flickered with golden light one by one. Jace felt a slight burning sensation at his fingertips — reminding him of when he had held Azuresong atop the Throne of Kil'jaeden, that sudden, inexplicable agony.

Uther closed the scripture, turned to look at the Council of Six on high, and nodded. Once Antonidas had nodded in return, the Lightbringer turned back and said in a low voice, "May the Holy Light protect you, Lord Jace Sesso."

"The Holy Light protect us all," Jace replied. Uther descended the steps and left the center of the Council.

As if by prior arrangement, gusts of cool wind filled the silence that followed. Jace turned his head to look at the scripture in the paladin's hands behind him. To be honest, before coming to the Sky Council, he had been told there would be a swearing-in by the Holy Light. But he hadn't known the scripture would actually react.

In his previous life, every courtroom and inquiry had a swearing-in segment, but everyone knew it was just a formality. Here, though? If he intended to conceal something, or to lie — would the Holy Light punish him? He had no idea.

The six archmages on the ring exchanged glances. Antonidas looked toward Archmage Drenden on his right, who gave a slight nod and picked up a piece of parchment.

"Starting from the very source — the northernmost point of Kalimdor, Winterspring, Lord Jace Sesso." Drenden announced loudly. "We now know that Magister Dalk'an Drathir had been secretly searching for the rumored resting place of Uthalesh, and had traveled to the southernmost reaches of Winterspring — the snow valley forest where few ever tread. And you, Lady Vereesa Windrunner, and High Lord Grede Thunderfist traveled to the same location at nearly the same time — that is, Dark Portal Year Eleven — and brought back an ancient formula proven capable of curing the undead plague."

"Several Magisterium members who accompanied Magister Dalk'an Drathir to Winterspring, along with members of the Sacred Relics Society, have acknowledged meeting you in Winterspring. Do you confirm this?"

"Confirmed," Jace said.

"Then," Drenden asked, "what was the purpose — or motive — of your trip to Winterspring?"

"To find Uthalesh," Jace answered without hesitation.

The Council erupted in commotion. Antonidas had to call out "Order, please!" repeatedly in an attempt to restore the proceedings.

Jace noticed that Varian was already starting to glance down at his chest. This king didn't seem to care at all whether Jace actually had Uthalesh — he was just looking for a moment when nobody was paying attention to sneak that eye out and scan the entire venue.

"I must correct one point, Master Drenden." Jace raised his hand. "The source was not Winterspring, but Eldre'Thalas — known to the locals as Dire Maul — an ancient Kaldorei Empire ruin deep in the southern forests of Kalimdor."

"In Dark Portal Year Nine, commissioned by Quel'Thalas's Sacred Relics Society, I traveled to Kalimdor with Lady Vereesa Windrunner to search for the remains of the mysterious dark magic scholar known as Dasmola. In the end, we discovered that this so-called dark magic scholar was actually a shapeshifting demon hiding in those deep ruins. The reason Dasmola remained in Azeroth, refusing to return to the Twisting Nether, was because this demon was still carrying out the final mission the Demon Lord had left her — to find the relic of the Dark Titan, the Staff of Uthalesh, and bring it back to the Burning Legion."

"But I didn't know that at the time. Most of the scrolls Dasmola had left behind were written in a special demonic language I didn't understand — ancient Eredun. I only treated them as proof to collect my reward."

"It wasn't until I later began to master that language and realized that the materials Lady Vereesa Windrunner had handed back to the Relics Society likely contained clues about the legendary Uthalesh, that I understood: I might have inadvertently exposed a secret the Guardians had been trying to bury. So I resolved to make up for this mistake."

"Why didn't you report this to the Kirin Tor?" Drenden asked.

"Because I was still a 'dead man' at the time." Jace said. "And because I didn't trust the Kirin Tor back then."

"You sent me to Grim Batol, where I ultimately had to face Deathwing head-on. And what did I get in return?" He pulled a silver ring off his finger. "A pretty silver ring. The Ring of Kirin Tor Trust."

"I faced the Father of Black Dragons himself — the demon who had threatened this world for nearly ten thousand years — survived his grasp... and in exchange received a qualification to enter the inner circle of Dalaran's Grand Library, shared with hundreds of Kirin Tor members. Master Drenden."

"Worth noting: when I tried to use this precious qualification to enter that library and search for records about Uthalesh, I found nothing. You don't store truly important knowledge in what you call the 'inner circle.'"

"Think about it. If back then I had reported everything to the Kirin Tor — that I had summoned an Eredun demon, learned the demonic language, cracked the secret materials about Uthalesh — would I have lost the hard-won trust of the 'world of light'? Would I have been thrown into the Violet Hold? After all, I did summon and enslave a high-ranking member of the Burning Legion to do it."

"More importantly, how was I to know that some mage in the Kirin Tor wouldn't take the materials I submitted and go looking for Uthalesh themselves? Dalk'an Drathir's subsequent actions proved my concerns weren't baseless paranoia."

"Perhaps it was arrogance and conceit. Perhaps it was that spark of personal heroism that was still burning then but has since been extinguished. Perhaps it was all of it. Regardless, during what you mentioned as Dark Portal Year Eleven, soon after surviving Grim Batol, I decided to find — and destroy — that thing myself. To make up for a mistake I had carelessly made."

Krasus lowered his head slightly. The other archmages on the Council of Six exchanged glances. Murmurs of discussion rippled through the Council chamber, but compared to the sudden uproar from earlier, it had calmed considerably.

"If Uthalesh truly still exists, as you and Magister Dalk'an Drathir's records suggest," Drenden said, "that means even two Guardians — Master Skavel and his prized student Lady Aegwynn — spent centuries unable to destroy it. Why did you believe you could?"

"Now I know." Jace glanced at the Ring of Kirin Tor Trust in his hand. "If the qualification this ring granted me had allowed me to see those historical records related to the Order of Tirisfal, I might never have naively set out on this path, gotten myself into such a huge mess, and forced myself to face a hearing of this scale. Respected Master Drenden."
