## Chapter 1803: The Millennial Plague

After Jace finished speaking, the Sky Council buzzed with discussion once more. Kings and young royal descendants leaned together, speaking in low voices. From the distant Quel'Thalas magisters, there even came occasional, barely audible snickers.

"Your choice at the time is understandable, Lord Jace Sesso." Antonidas looked around at his nearby colleagues, then lowered his gaze back to the master of Il'garok Tower at the center of the Council. "But in any case, when encountering unknown magical threats — especially those involving demons and dark magic — it is best to report them first to the Kirin Tor, or to local arcane institutions. For example, our long-standing partner and ally, the Wizard Sanctum. Acting on your own is undoubtedly extremely reckless and dangerous."

"I understand that now, Master Antonidas." Jace looked up. "That is why I am willing to accept the Kirin Tor and the entire Alliance's inquiry — to resolve the long-standing mistrust between myself and the Kirin Tor, to mend these communication gaps as quickly as possible, and to prevent even more severe disasters caused by mutual suspicion and distrust in the face of the greater crisis to come."

"The Kirin Tor greatly appreciates your cooperation and support." After Antonidas finished, he looked again toward Archmage Drenden, signaling him to continue.

Drenden bowed his head slightly and continued. "Before the encounter in Winterspring, Lord Sesso, were you acquainted with Magister Dalk'an Drathir?"

"No." Jace shook his head, but then corrected himself. "Well, strictly speaking, we had crossed paths once before in Dalaran. But we only knew each other's names."

"Thank you for your candor, Lord Sesso." Drenden continued. "That would mean before that encounter, you and he had never discussed any clues about Uthalesh, nor any information about the demon Dasmola and the remains she had left behind."

"So then, was your meeting in Winterspring pure coincidence?"

No. It wasn't coincidence.

The reason Jace had been so eager to set out in search of Uthalesh was because Vereesa and Amy Marin had learned in Dalaran that Dalk'an was in contact with members of the Violet Eye.

He had immediately realized that Dalk'an couldn't find a way to translate ancient Eredun in Dalaran, so he might go to the Grand Library of Karazhan to search for knowledge — hence contacting the Violet Eye.

He had worried that Dalk'an might even be in touch with Medivh's treasure hunters — those mercenaries known as the Black Riders. Later events proved his concerns weren't unfounded.

Jace's peripheral vision caught a glimpse of the scripture behind him, still faintly glowing gold. "It was not a coincidence."

"Does this mean you knew that Magister Dalk'an was also searching for Uthalesh?" Drenden asked.

"I couldn't be one hundred percent certain," Jace said. "But I did know that there were people in Quel'Thalas and Dalaran investigating translation materials related to ancient Eredun. And given that I had not long ago submitted Dasmola's remains to the Sacred Relics Society in exchange for payment, it made me suspect that someone was already planning to go looking for Uthalesh. And they might even have more complete clues and information than I did. This was indeed an important factor that spurred me to begin my journey."

"What channels did you use to obtain this intelligence, Lord Jace Sesso?" Drenden asked.

"By now we all know," Jace said, raising his voice. "Magister Dalk'an Drathir of the Magisterium had close dealings with many warlocks and dark magic practitioners — including the Shadow Council, the corrupted spawn of Deathwing hidden within the Alliance, and even Master Kel'Thuzad of the Kirin Tor. As His Majesty King Varian Wrynn's dark magic advisor, if I didn't maintain some relevant channels, I would hardly be competent in this position. And if I recall correctly, this hearing does not cover topics of dark magic research and demon summoning unrelated to Uthalesh."

At this, even the magisters of Quel'Thalas in the distance stopped laughing. A group of high-ranking wizards on the Kirin Tor side fell into utter silence.

Prince Kael'thas, Krasus, and Antonidas exchanged words in voices only they could hear. After a brief discussion, Antonidas announced, "Lord Sesso's point is entirely reasonable. Please continue, Master Drenden."

Drenden seemed to have no intention of arguing either. He scanned the scroll in his hands. "At the time, you were not the only party searching for Uthalesh in Winterspring, Lord Sesso. There was also a group of shadowy necromancers. According to the Violet Eye's understanding, these individuals were likely themselves undead beings — the legendary Black Riders that had long been rumored around the town of Darrowshire."

"But these necromancers all died in the depths of the southernmost valley of Winterspring — the place suspected to be Guardian Skavel's hidden vault. This can be corroborated by Dalk'an Drathir and his collaborators and apprentices. Are you aware of this?"

Jace had to admit — the Kirin Tor's investigation had indeed gone deep. "Yes," Jace said.

Drenden nodded. "According to the records left by Dalk'an Drathir and the testimonies of his apprentices and collaborators, they first cooperated with these necromancers and exchanged information. Later, they planned to ambush them in Winterspring, seize everything they had obtained from the southern end of Winterspring — including Uthalesh, the Windreaver, should it resurface. But in the end, they came up empty-handed, only to find you."

"Those necromancers did clash with us and died by our hands," Jace admitted.

"At the same time, three groups, following the same clues, heading to the same place. One group left empty-handed. One group died violent deaths." Drenden said. "And in that forgotten underground palace believed to be the Guardian's vault, there was nothing left."

"Because that underground vault never contained Uthalesh, Master Drenden." Jace lifted his head and declared. "Guardian Skavel's vault was just a decoy. There was nothing there. And the undead and necromancers naturally assumed that we had taken Uthalesh, which they believed had been hidden inside — there was no way they were going to let us walk out of Winterspring."

He wasn't lying. That vault itself was indeed just a decoy.

The real hiding place for Uthalesh was a dream fragment created by Skavel. If Sora hadn't been with them, they could never have truly seized the bone staff from within the dream.

At this, the high-ranking mages on the Kirin Tor side lost their composure, breaking into heated debate. The kings and nobles on the upper tiers still seemed unclear on the details — after all, they weren't very familiar with the specifics of the Guardians, Uthalesh, and the Winterspring region of Kalimdor.

The six Council members exchanged glances. Vargus and Modera murmured something to each other. Kael'thas stood with arms tightly crossed, silent. Drenden exchanged looks with Antonidas and Krasus, scanned the scrolls a few times, his lips moving slightly as if formulating his next statement.

"Order, please, everyone," Antonidas called out loudly, but it still took a while for the chamber to settle down.

"But the undead plague is real, Lord Jace Sesso." Drenden stared at the scroll in his hand. "In the southernmost snowy valley of Winterspring, there also lived a group of non-human natives — furbolgs, who called themselves the Winterfall tribe."

"According to these residents, the plague had ravaged the valley for nearly a hundred years. They had managed to hold their homeland only thanks to a medicinal formula provided by the Timbermaw tribe — another group of furbolg allies from outside Winterspring who were skilled in alchemy and herbalism."

"That medicinal formula is the original version of the formula that has now alleviated the plague outbreak in Lordaeron and cured the illness contracted by His Majesty King Terenas Menethil II."

"With permission from the leaders of the Winterfall and Timbermaw tribes, Kirin Tor investigators examined the bodies of plague victims in and around Winterspring, and interviewed several furbolg healers who had witnessed the full course of the disease. Through years of comparison, researchers found that it was essentially identical to the symptoms of soldiers who had unfortunately died from the Blackrock Spire plague, and to the troll victims who had failed to escape the plague disaster in the Deadwind Pass centuries ago, as recorded by the Order of Tirisfal. And that specific healing formula largely corroborated this conclusion."

"After you and Dalk'an Drathir's party left Winterspring, when the last victim of the undead plague in the Winterfall tribe was cured, no further cases emerged in that valley. The furbolgs of the Winterfall tribe also believed that it was a human wizard and the foreign travelers he led who had taken away the curse and saved their valley."

"Lord Jace Sesso — what do you make of this connection?"
