Chapter 1804: The Final Question

No matter how many times Antonidas had called for order, the entire Council had never been this quiet as after this question was posed.

For a moment, the entire Alliance waited for the Lord of Il'garok to explain himself. It was hard to tell how many among them expected to see this most controversial hero the kingdoms had produced since the Alliance's founding... reveal his true nature.

The cold wind at this height cut through the steps, between the flagstones and stone pillars, sweeping across the center of the Council with a low moan. Jace turned to look at the assembled kings ahead, and the leaders of the Silver Hand and the Church behind him. Finally, his gaze settled on the faintly glowing scripture guarded by the Lightbringer, Uther, beside him.

"I cannot give an answer," Jace said.

Murmurs of doubt rose from the platforms near and far, but they didn't swell into a full-scale discussion. Archmage Drenden leaned forward slightly. "Are you saying you cannot explain these connections and coincidences for us, Lord Jace Sesso?"

"Since leaving the southern valley of Winterspring — the territory of the Winterfall furbolgs — I have never returned there, and I do not know how the furbolgs have fared in the six years since." Jace said. "Nor have I obtained the bodies of those Gurubashi jungle trolls who died violently in southern Elwynn a thousand years ago. There is no way for me to directly compare the causes of death."

"At first, I too believed that the undead plague ravaging the land was likely caused by Uthalesh. When I first stepped into the Winterfall Valley and saw the condition of the afflicted, I even felt a certain relief at having 'found the answer.' Because at that time, I was confident I could find Uthalesh, even destroy it, and put an end to the suffering of all the natives enduring this disease in Kalimdor. But when I actually approached that vault, I realized things might not be as I had imagined."

"The Kirin Tor's investigators may have also discovered this, Master Drenden. In that valley, there exist things corrupted by demonic magic."

"Yes." Drenden looked at the scroll in his hands and confirmed. "The remains of some magical creatures that died in the deep valley show clear signs of corruption by the undead plague."

"Before we went to Eldre'Thalas," Jace continued, "we first followed the clues provided by the Sacred Relics Society and located the most likely hiding place of Dasmola — Maraudon, the sacred tomb of the centaurs. A group of satyr demons had already occupied that place, declaring allegiance to the centaur's gods... attempting to use that elemental demigod's power to corrupt Kalimdor."

"It was from the leader of those satyr demons that we found information recording Dasmola's whereabouts. Many records indicated that these demons had once tried to search for Uthalesh together with Dasmola, and that Dasmola also believed that Guardian Skavel had likely used the power of Mount Hyjal to seal the artifact. Those traitorous Kaldorei — the satyr demons — had ways to breach the protection of that sacred Kaldorei mountain, helping her destroy Skavel's magical vault."

"Until we reached that vault, the satyrs had been continuously attempting to corrupt Mount Hyjal and the Emerald Dream. We even encountered a group of powerful satyrs at the Ruins of Keth'Zil in northern Winterspring, rescued a green dragon lady from their clutches, and afterward reported this to Queen Alexstrasza, resolving the crisis of the Dream."

"I can testify to that," Krasus said. "Because after Grim Batol, it wasn't difficult for Lord Jace Sesso — who had helped those red dragons — to gain an audience with them."

"Thank you, Master Krasus." Jace said. "I have sufficient reason to suspect that the plague suffered by the Winterfall furbolgs likely originated from the satyrs' and the Burning Legion's joint attempt to breach the vault. This includes their corruption of the Emerald Dream and Mount Hyjal. Because that valley lies at the foot of the sacred mountain. The corruption had already begun over a hundred years before we — Medivh's dark mercenaries, and Dalk'an Drathir's party — ever reached the place. Even after Dasmola was captured by the Kaldorei of Eldre'Thalas, the satyrs never gave up."

"When the Dream crisis was resolved, the satyr leader slain, and the corruption process interrupted, the inhabitants around Mount Hyjal naturally returned to peace."

The murmurs of debate grew louder. Krasus exchanged quiet words with Kael'thas. Antonidas, Modera, and Vargus discussed among themselves. Drenden nodded frequently as he listened to their exchange, then said, "But this cannot explain the similarities among the southern Elwynn disaster of a thousand years ago, the Blackrock Spire plague, and the Winterspring plague, Lord Jace Sesso."

"As far as I know, the Windreaver is not a magical artifact specialized in spreading plague," Jace said. "The reason it can spread plague is that the corruptive power released when the fallen demon soul within it reaps souls — that soul being Uthalesh, one of the progenitors of the Nathrezim, a demon lord who ruled the Nathrezim in ancient times."

"That is correct," Vargus confirmed. "The Guardians' records contain similar descriptions."

"And similar undead plagues are by no means unique to Uthalesh." Jace pointed downward as he addressed the mages. "Two thousand years ago, the Nathrezim demon Khasanathir attempted to use the waters of Lake Lordamere to spread a corrupt plague to Dalaran and all of Lordaeron. At Blackrock Spire, the Nathrezim released plague-spreading swarms to create chaos in order to escape the allied pursuit. Your Kirin Tor is hardly ignorant of the disasters these dreadlords have brought."

"Just because three different locations share similar plagues, can you definitively conclude that Uthalesh exists? Then I would like to ask the esteemed Archmages of the Kirin Tor Council a few questions."

"In the second year after we returned from Winterspring, the Black Dragon Princess Onyxia was lurking within Stormwind City. Her evil disguise was exposed, and she was forced to battle me, my friends, and Master Krasus in the palace of Stormwind. She was ultimately defeated and escaped. Why did no undead plague spread through Stormwind?"

"Many members of the Sons of Lothar are here. Many of you participated in the assault on Dragonmaw Fortress in Shadowmoon Valley. I was in that battle too — I killed several elite demons and Fel Reavers on the wall, and eventually entered the depths of Dragonmaw Fortress, where I, along with several lords and the Alliance vanguard, slew Warden Moggror and his guard, as well as Chieftain Zuluhed and the grotesque chimera he had created — the Netherwing Drake."

"Several paladins and Farstrider rangers participated in this final fierce battle. We all nearly died. So among any of them — inside or outside the fortress, on or off the walls — did anyone contract this undead plague?"

"When the Expedition finally retreated to Auchindoun, I, along with several Alliance allies, established a portal under siege by the Shadow Council. Before Master Khadgar and the Kirin Tor's allied forces arrived, I had to summon the souls of all of Auchindoun to empower the portal's opening."

"Every caster here knows very well that one end of the portal required the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh to sustain. What kind of power would be needed to match that on the other end? Even so, of everyone who participated in that final Battle of Auchindoun — from Expedition soldiers to the Kirin Tor and Stormwind forces and mages who arrived later, to Ironforge and Aerie Peak warriors, to the magisters of Quel'Thalas — did anyone contract a similar undead plague? Did anyone die from plague corruption?"

"At Blackrock Spire, I was forced to conduct a soul ritual to stop the Black Dragon Prince from using the souls of the dead to corrupt Blackrock Mountain and the entire Redridge range. And after that, at the very top of that tower, Nefarian seized the moment and forced me to face his assault alone. I ultimately defeated him."

"But this time, the undead plague finally began to spread? Are you completely forgetting the confirmed fact that the Black Dragon Prince had been cooperating with the Nathrezim — even overlooking that an adult red dragon who helped us assault Blackrock Spire had already had her mind infiltrated by a dreadlord?"

"If at Blackrock Spire my intention was to cooperate with the Burning Legion's demons to spread plague across the entire Alliance, why wouldn't I have accepted Kil'jaeden's terms from the start and taken up the Scepter of Sargeras, instead of destroying it?"

"Don't forget 'Aegwynn and the Dragon Hunt.' The power of the Scepter of Sargeras was enough to summon the Burning Legion's supreme master to Azeroth. If someone could have brought the Scepter to Azeroth for them, if someone could have directly summoned the demons' master to Dalaran — why waste energy sending a few high-ranking demons to this world, and go looking for black dragons and those necromancers of Northrend to be allies?"

"Please answer these questions, respected Council, respected Archmages!"

The Sky Council erupted. Many people even stood up. Drenden clutched the scroll in his hands, still reading, or perhaps talking with the archmages beside him. But it seemed they had no more questions to ask.

At this moment, all the noise felt like background hum that could be ignored. Jace stood at the very center of the Council, his mind buzzing.

Now that he had said his piece, he felt as though he had forgotten why he had come to the Sky Council, why he had participated in this hearing, why he was arguing with the Kirin Tor's mages.

But just then, a familiar young voice came from the upper tiers of lords.

"Lord Jace Sesso — I have a question for you too!"

"Sit down, Arthas."

"Please remain quiet for now, Prince Arthas. The hearing is not yet concluded. Everything must proceed in order."

"Do you possess the legendary Uthalesh, the Windreaver, Lord Jace Sesso? Under the witness of the Holy Light, answer this question!"

"Sit down, Arthas! How dare you..." "Prince Arthas!"

"Your Highness! Please calm yourself." "Arthas."

"If you insist on this, esteemed Prince Arthas, the Kirin Tor must ask you to leave the Sky Council to ensure the hearing proceeds properly."

"Do you possess that evil staff, Lord Jace Sesso? Do you possess Uthalesh? Answer... damn it, let go of me!"

"No."

Jace looked at the figure in the distance, gleaming in the sunlight under the blue sky. His voice was calm. "No. I do not possess Uthalesh. Are you satisfied with that answer, Your Highness?"

Hearing Jace's response, Arthas Menethil seemed to suddenly lose his conviction. He cast one last glance at the dim scripture on the altar beside the Lightbringer Uther, then finally stopped struggling and was dragged down the steps by the Kirin Tor guards.

And King Terenas had lost the vigor and composure he had shown just moments ago. He apologized to Antonidas with some explanation.

The Sky Council was now in an uproar. At the very center, the warlock turned and stared at that scripture.

You know very well. You know I have never truly possessed... Uthalesh.
