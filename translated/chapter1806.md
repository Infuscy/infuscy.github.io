## Chapter 1806: Defeat

At the Violet Citadel, Jace, Vereesa, Grede, and Khadgar gathered in the lounge. Before long, Sora and Needleflame pushed the door open, finally breaking the silence.

"So, Jace?" Sora asked anxiously.

Jace shook his head. "We came up empty. There was no Nathrezim in the Sky Council."

"At the end, I saw King Terenas say something to you. What did he say, Jace?" Vereesa asked.

"Just pleasantries," Jace said. "He apologized to me. Said he had believed some rumors spread by demons, blaming me for the plague he had contracted. He also apologized on behalf of his son."

"So this eye had no reaction at all the entire time?" Sora asked. "I made it very clear to King Varian — he had to fully remove it from the magic cloth to see as far as possible..."

"In the end, it wasn't even a matter of taking it out of the cloth. Plenty of people had already seen the eye, Lady Sora. So it wasn't a problem with King Varian's handling method." Khadgar raised his hand helplessly. "Maybe those demons knew about our plan. But where did it leak? Even the Church doesn't know we have an eye that can see Nathrezim..."

"Did I do something wrong..." Sora said quietly.

"No, Sora." Jace said. "It's more likely that... we were heading in the wrong direction."

"Damn it. We never should have trusted that black dragon," Grede said. "She's probably in league with those demons. One sentence from her and we got played for fools!"

"She left Blackrock Spire a long time ago!" Needleflame glared at the dwarf. "After that, how those demons and Prince Nefarian cooperated and schemed — she might not know all the details either! How can you be so quick to accuse her?!"

"Get real, black dragon." Grede said quietly. "Don't think that just because she's your mother now, everything she says is right. That she-dragon ran wild in Stormwind, wreaked havoc on who knows how many people, and almost killed me — almost killed Jace Sesso. And you think she suddenly turned into a nice old lady just because she was afraid we'd hurt her thousands of eggs? Today we wreck her nest, tomorrow she could lay just as big a batch!"

"Shut up!" Needleflame snarled.

"At least one thing she said was right," Khadgar said. "Before the hearing started, Master Krasus told me privately... they've found traces of nerubian necromancers in Northrend trying to summon a demon lord from the Twisting Nether, along with traces of a confirmed powerful Nathrezim presence."

"According to the surviving nerubians, this so-called 'undead king' has a powerful artifact for manipulating undead creatures. Based on what that black dragon said, that Nathrezim might be connected to that necromantic artifact. Right now, the Kirin Tor's most elite mages are all in Northrend, cooperating with red dragons and a group of Quel'Thalas magisters, including Aethas Sunreaver. They've already identified many magical characteristics of that Nathrezim. Krasus believes the Kirin Tor should be able to capture him eventually."

"Should we trust what the Kirin Tor says?" Vereesa asked. "Even setting aside that demon 'guarding the necromantic artifact,' the fact is that there were still three dreadlords running around Lordaeron doing whatever they pleased, and the first one we caught was by our own hands..."

"I'm inclined to trust Master Krasus. You know he's more than just Kirin Tor, Ranger Lord." Khadgar said quietly, though noticeably lacking in confidence.

So that meant, even in the most optimistic scenario, they still had to deal with at least two more.

But right now, they couldn't find even one — not even after gathering almost all the Alliance leaders here on this spot.

Were they really going to have to go to Quel'Thalas and present the eye before the Sun King?

Or were they still hiding in Dalaran? Or Stormwind? Had the demons already fooled Morofil and stretched their claws to his father and mother? Could it be the Wizard Sanctum? Could it be... Amy?

Jace's gaze shifted back to the eye in Sora's palm.

"Even that 'five' the demon-woman mentioned — I'd put a question mark on it," Grede grumbled. "Since she could be wrong about the Council of Six's intelligence, who's to say it's not seven or eight? Nine or ten?"

"High Lord Grede Thunderfist." Before Needleflame could explode again, Khadgar spoke up first. "Summoning a Nathrezim this powerful to Azeroth is no easy thing."

"Ten thousand years ago, it was the magic of the Highborne that summoned the demon legions, drawing Tichondrius himself. Over two thousand years ago, it was a planar catastrophe in Dalaran that created a breach for countless demons to sneak through, giving those demon summoners the opportunity to summon the dreadlord Khasanathir. And this many Nathrezim now — it's undoubtedly connected to the opening of the Dark Portal. In the thousands of years in between, the Kirin Tor and the Order of Tirisfal have no such records."

"This is exactly why the Kirin Tor could suspect your good buddy of having Uthalesh based on the plague characteristics the Nathrezim spread... If these demons could be summoned to any plane as easily as Eredun or Mo'arg, would the Kirin Tor still be this panicked?"

"Now that the power of the Dark Portal has been destroyed, to summon a Nathrezim, you'd need at least another Medivh — or someone like Jace Sesso — to lead the effort, and we'd have to know nothing about it. Do you think that's easy? Azeroth isn't unlucky enough to reach that point."

"Alright, alright. You know more than me, mage. You're right." Grede sighed.

It was then that Khadgar noticed Jace hadn't spoken for a while.

"Jace?"

Jace heard Khadgar's voice and looked up, realizing that everyone in the room was staring at him. And every one of them had a strange look in their eyes.

"Jace, what is it? What are you thinking?" Vereesa said. "Even earlier at the Sky Council, when you were facing all those mages and priests... I've never seen you like this."

"It's nothing, Vereesa. I was just... considering some other possibilities." Jace rubbed his face.

"Don't rush, Jace." Vereesa nodded. "At least the Kirin Tor is on our side now. As Master Antonidas promised, we can use all available resources to investigate. And this hearing has ruled out a lot of dangerous possibilities — namely, the leaders of all the nations. So it's not a complete failure. Remember? We anticipated this possibility before the hearing even started..."

"Could it be a clone spell?" Needleflame said. "Master Vargus is the Kirin Tor's greatest master of illusion magic. In Draenor, everyone said so. Khadgar, you were able to make a clone that fooled everyone. Is he even better at illusions than you?"

"I made a clone designed to fool random people on the street. That clone of mine didn't even dare to get close to Modera and Drenden, you little one." Khadgar stared at the little black dragon. "If that prison's isolation hadn't been so effective and the monitoring devices so inflexible, and if those masters had been willing to take time out of their schedules to visit me once — even Sora's life force would have been exposed immediately."

"What I'm saying is, no matter how skilled Vargus is at illusions, he couldn't possibly fool all five others at the same time. Especially Kael'thas Sunstrider — that guy's eyes might as well have been soaked in the Sunwell!"

"So you're still defending your mother, little black dragon." Grede crossed his arms. "Why can't you admit she played us all..."

At that, Vereesa frowned. She reached out to stop the dwarf and looked at the group. "What exactly did Onyxia say at the time?"

"The last demon wasn't responsible for Blackrock Spire." Sora repeated Onyxia's words, even imitating her tone. "He had infiltrated the Kirin Tor a long time ago. If nothing went wrong, he should have already made it into the Council of Six."

"Is there something wrong with that statement, Vereesa?" Jace asked.

"She really did leave Blackrock Spire before and stopped participating in Nefarian and the demons' plans," Vereesa said. "That means she wasn't certain that the demon had actually made it into the Council of Six. But she did know that person was heading for a seat on the Council. Right?"

Yes. That was right.

Jace froze slightly too. With this reminder cutting through the fog, impressions began to surface in his mind one after another...

"At first, his investigation traced the rumors to the Mage Quarter, so he focused on the Wizard Sanctum's mages — including your teacher, Malin. But soon the SI:7 agents realized it wasn't the Wizard Sanctum, Jace. It was the Kirin Tor."

"...must be brought back to Dalaran immediately, Master Khadgar. The Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh cannot remain in the Kingdom of Stormwind for too long!"

"After Master Khadgar announced he wouldn't compete, even though Master Runeval was a strong contender, because of his Expedition experience, Master Vargus had much higher support. You're the one who brought him back from Draenor, Jace. I believe he'll definitely be willing to help you."

"This is a letter from Master Antonidas. It's about the demon soul shard." "There's really no need, Mr. Sesso. You can come directly to me." "Better keep the letter, Master Vargus."

"He won a seat on the Council of Six. Anshlun Runeval chose to withdraw from the competition. But even if he hadn't, Runeval's chances wouldn't have been great. Master Vargus gained a lot of support through his legendary experiences in Draenor. Do you think Vargus is suspicious?"

"Is Master Anshlun Runeval attending the hearing?" the ranger asked the mage.

"It seems not," Khadgar said. "I don't remember him being there."

"Then where is he?"

"How should I know? I've been in the Violet Citadel for the last couple of days. If I had to guess — the Arcane Vault? Escorting the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh back to Dalaran was remarkable enough, but still not enough to match Vargus leading the mages of Kirin'Var Village across the Dark Portal twice. Though it did secure him the position of Arcane Vault Keeper. Wait — are you suspecting him?"

The Book of Medivh... damn it.

— Remember this firmly. Kil'jaeden's goal, from beginning to end, has been to destroy this world — Azeroth. Our suffering, Draenor's suffering — they were just steps in his plan.

Velen's voice seemed to echo in his ears.

Forgive me, Prophet. I really did completely push your words to the back of my mind.
