Chapter 1807: Returned in Full

Before dawn, the sky was still ink-blue. The streets of Dalaran were empty, but windows in the Violet Citadel were lighting up one after another.

Antonidas led a group of Kirin Tor mages — including Khadgar and Jace's group — hurrying through the corridors toward the descending staircase that led to the Arcane Vault.

"During the hearing, we received no alarms from the Arcane Vault... Jace Sesso." Antonidas asked as they walked. "What made you think it could be Anshlun?"

"It's difficult to explain the source of my intelligence in such a short time, Master Antonidas." Jace said. "But if there has been a misunderstanding, I will apologize to Master Runeval in person."

"The situation is tense now. Demons lurk across Lordaeron. Everyone's nerves are on edge. Some misunderstandings are only natural."

Antonidas said quietly as they arrived at the antechamber before the vault doors.

Several guards in purple robes, seeing the master of Dalaran and so many archmages — even Jace Sesso and his group — arriving all at once, tensed up immediately. After all, this was sudden, and they had received no prior notification.

"Were there any issues at the Arcane Vault during the hearing yesterday, everyone?" Antonidas asked.

"Everything was normal, Master." The leading battle mage in an enchanted armored robe bowed, his expression confused. "Has something happened?"

"Where is Master Runeval?" Khadgar asked.

The battle mage captain replied, "I'm not sure, Master Khadgar. Master Runeval hasn't been to the vault recently."

Antonidas raised his hand. "Please open the door."

"Yes." The battle mages chanted incantations. The vault door slowly opened, releasing a thick metallic rust smell.

Jace tried to detect any traces of demonic or shadow energy within, but unfortunately, under such powerful arcane radiation, even if a demon had snuck in and left some scent, it would have been burned clean away by the arcane energy.

Inside the Arcane Vault stretched corridors covered in magical runes and enchantment lines. There was almost no need for torches or lamps — the glow of these runes alone was enough to illuminate the entire space brightly.

There were still some delicate chandeliers and pillar lamps though, more decorative than functional. Regardless, everything was orderly — nothing like a place that had just been broken into.

Antonidas led the group to the deepest part of the vault, before a massive black box sealed by powerful magic. He turned to Jace. "The seal on the vault remains intact. The Book of Medivh, the Skull of Gul'dan, and other important artifacts are stored within."

"Can't you open it and verify?" Grede asked.

"Opening this vault requires the vault keeper — Master Runeval — two assistant administrators, Archmage Drenden and Archmage Otharos, and myself — four people performing an arcane ritual together. I have already sent someone to find Master Runeval. Master Otharos is here, and Master Drenden is in the Violet Citadel. He should be able to arrive soon. Please be patient, High Lord Thunderfist."

Jace very much hoped his suspicions were just his paranoia acting up again. But everything seemed to be heading in the direction he least wanted to guess.

The Book of Medivh. This was the prerequisite for summoning Archimonde and triggering the Third War.

In another timeline, the death knight Arthas had led the undead army to break through Dalaran precisely to seize this grimoire — to complete Kil'jaeden's ultimate goal for the Scourge: summoning the Burning Legion's most feared defiler directly into this world.

But at this very moment, the Kirin Tor's mages — even Khadgar — were completely unaware of this.

They only thought the obscure contents of the Book of Medivh might be related to the Twisting Nether and demon summoning... which, of course, was true. And Jace certainly shouldn't know any more than that.

But even understanding only this much — with Nathrezim ravaging Lordaeron and the threat of new demons being summoned at any time hanging over everything — it was enough to make the Kirin Tor spring into action and come to check the vault.

"Master Antonidas!"

A Kirin Tor mage ran into the vault, calling out. "Master Runeval left Dalaran several days ago. One of his assistants said he was heading to the Tyr's Hand Monastery to collect some remaining evidence about the Nathrezim..."

"What? I had no idea Master Runeval was involved in the monastery affair," Modera said.

No need to doubt any further, Jace thought.

Antonidas looked at the female mage beside him. "Initiate a full investigation into Anshlun Runeval immediately. Issue a wanted notice."

"Yes, Master." Modera nodded.

Watching Modera leave the vault gates with several Kirin Tor mages, Jace suddenly found the Kirin Tor's habit of coming down ruthlessly on anyone suspected of dark magic activity — without any due process — a little less objectionable.

"Is the magical power source of this vault the Eye of Dalaran, Master Antonidas?" Vereesa asked. "Could you forcibly remove this vault's magical seal by turning off the Eye of Dalaran?"

"No." Antonidas said. "Ever since the Eye of Dalaran was stolen by Deathwing and Kel'Thuzad and taken to Draenor, the vault's power source has been replaced. What it is now... is classified information that can only be disclosed after authorization by the Council of Six."

"But I can reveal this much, Ranger Lord — while I do have the ability to forcibly strip all the seal runes from this vault, it would still take at least a week to complete the casting. Because after that theft of the Eye of Dalaran, this vault's defenses were reinforced at least twice — to prevent any single member of the Council of Six from entering or exiting alone within a short time."

"And the second reinforcement was designed and led by Master Runeval himself, around Winter Veil," Archmage Otharos added.

"So if the Book of Medivh has been stolen, it could have been over half a year?" Jace asked. "But there was no inspection in that time?"

"The vault has not yet been opened, Master Jace Sesso. Nothing is certain yet." Otharos said.

"Then start today." Antonidas declared. "Prepare for an emergency magical disconnection. Since Archmage Anshlun Runeval has been placed on the wanted list, the defensive measures he designed and led can no longer be trusted."

"Yes, Master Antonidas." The battle mages guarding the vault replied in unison.

"But we can't just wait seven more days." Jace looked at the people behind him. "Runeval was already the head of the team escorting this grimoire back from Stormwind a year ago. He could have tampered with it during that process."

"I'll go back to the Quel'Thalas embassy immediately and have the Farstriders join the pursuit." Vereesa said. "A Nathrezim can't shake off a possessed mage easily. It wouldn't be silent. Either we find a dying Runeval, or we find a demon on the run."

"I think, Jace..." Khadgar pointed at the black magical vault. "Even if the Book of Medivh really isn't inside anymore, they don't have the room to perform a summoning ritual anytime soon."

"The entire Alliance is on high alert now. A single Nathrezim can't even get in — let alone use such a powerful artifact to contact the Twisting Nether. They can't possibly complete all of this in silence. We still have a chance to catch Runeval and figure out what happened."

Khadgar was right. In another timeline, the Book of Medivh alone couldn't bring Archimonde to Azeroth. It also required mobilizing the entire ley line network of Dalaran, and a sufficiently powerful summoner.

That summoner was Kel'Thuzad — the Kel'Thuzad who had corrupted and destroyed the Sunwell, drawing on its immense power to be reborn as a lich.

"Master Antonidas — may I visit Archmage Kel'Thuzad in the Violet Hold?"

Antonidas took a ring from among his many insignias and handed it to Jace. "Show this ring to Archmage Cassan. He will permit you to see that man."

"Thank you, Master." Jace took the ring.

"Don't thank me. This is the Kirin Tor's promise." Antonidas said. "Full cooperation in investigating the demonic infiltration within the Alliance."

Jace nodded. Khadgar immediately said, "I'm coming with you, Jace."

The sky had still not fully brightened. Thinking back to everything since returning from Draenor, Jace barely knew how he had made his way through the Violet Hold to see the warden, Archmage Cassan.

As he walked past the spinning Tal'arf Crystal on his way to the deepest cell where important Kirin Tor members were held, countless scenarios played through his mind — Kel'Thuzad had already escaped; Cassan might suddenly turn against him, with the elite battle mages on duty blocking his exit; even Khadgar behind him might be caught up in it...

But nothing happened. The city of Dalaran was as peaceful as any other morning.

After Cassan chanted the incantation, Jace stepped into what appeared to be a barrierless planar prison and saw the archmage in his gray-purple shirt sitting there calmly, looking no different from their last meeting — if anything, a bit plumper.

"You're back again, Jace Sesso. You've run into some trouble? And now I, your defeated opponent... can offer you some spiritual comfort?"

Jace's lips moved. But looking at the smile on Kel'Thuzad's face, he said nothing, turned, and immediately left the cell.

"Thank you very much for your assistance, Master Cassan."

"Just doing my duty, Master Sesso." Cassan replied.

"You didn't ask him anything, Jace?" Khadgar asked, bewildered, but ultimately had to bow to Archmage Cassan and quickly follow.

The Lich King had already been abandoned. That was why the Red Dragonflight and Kirin Tor's operations in Northrend had been so successful.

After all, if the Book of Medivh could simply be taken directly, what need was there to manufacture a plague, organize a Scourge army, and fight their way into Dalaran?

"...If someone could have brought the Scepter to Azeroth for them, if someone could have directly summoned the demons' master to Dalaran — why waste energy sending a few high-ranking demons to this world, and go looking for black dragons and those necromancers of Northrend to be allies?"

Walking through the dark prison corridors, recalling the words he had shouted to everyone at the Sky Council just yesterday morning, Jace felt nothing but a profound sense of complexity.

Make a feint in the east while attacking in the west. The whole trick, all over again. Were you really that petty, Kil'jaeden?
