Chapter 1817: The Meeting

The remaining human members of the Kirin Tor council, along with Khadgar and Sesso, assembled in the chamber. When Antonidas finally walked in, his expression was grave. The moment Jace saw him, he knew the past week of searching had turned up nothing. "Our mages," the Grand Magister's gaze paused on each person in turn, "and our allies from partner organizations have spent this time exploring nearly every gathering point and magical vault in the Eastern Kingdoms. Nothing. No traces of demonic summoning. If not for the theft of Medivh's Book, combined with the news of the Eye of Sargeras revealed by the Arcane aberrant left behind by Aegwynn, and her true intention to open a second Dark Portal... I fear no one in this world would have realized the horror approaching us." "Perhaps we should focus our efforts on Medivh's Book, honored colleagues," said Archmage Modera. "From the libraries of the Kingdom of Stormwind to King Perenolde's court magical advisors, to Ansirem Runeweaver's several assistants — they all maintain sympathetic links to Medivh's Book. In fact, Runeweaver himself used these links to bypass those checks..." "Tracking Medivh's Book?" Archmage Drenden looked at Modera. "The sympathetic links of that grimoire are notoriously deceptive, Master Modera. You didn't participate in the construction of the Arcane Vault. You haven't studied those fel artifacts. If you had deeper understanding of the Order of Tirisfal's past work, you would realize there is only one outcome to tracking Aegwynn or her son's magical creations..." "You and Othulos both participated in the inspection and improvement of the Vault, yet neither of you detected that Runeweaver had swapped Medivh's Book," Modera said. "I acknowledge the limitations of my knowledge and abilities, Master Modera," Drenden said. "But we have now confirmed that Aegwynn is indeed collaborating with those dreadlords. When a Guardian of this caliber participates in and even orchestrates the substitution of Medivh's Book, and her ally is a Nathrezim king more powerful than the ancient Kasanathiel, who found a way to penetrate the core of the Kirin Tor... there are very few people in this world who could guarantee anything under such a conspiracy." "What's done is done, both of you." Antonidas said. "The priority now is determining the direction of our continued search. As for our respective responsibilities, those can be settled once everything is resolved and the crisis has passed." "I think we still cannot ignore those powerful Arcane sources..." Vargos said. "The power of the Eye of Sargeras is formidable, but ultimately it is chaotic fel energy. It's difficult to replace Arcane as the most stable choice for energy and planar transference. The Sunwell, Mount Hyjal, Karazhan..." "Too late," Khadgar said. "Those were the exact words of Lady Aegwynn's shade when she spoke to us. That means she believed the summoning ritual had already begun — at the very least, Lady Aegwynn had found a suitable location and launched her plan. If it were at any of those sufficiently conspicuous magical sources, we would have heard news or felt the ley line tremors by now, Master Vargos." "What if that shade was merely a deliberately crafted simulacrum, Master Khadgar?" Vargos looked at Khadgar. "What if its purpose was to deceive us, misdirect our investigation, and deliberately make us overlook the most obvious clues..." "That was not a simple simulacrum spell, Master Vargos." Khadgar said. "That was... an energy aberrant split off from a powerful 'decision.' I can fully imagine what kind of unnatural struggle Lady Aegwynn endured before deciding to summon a demon. The mad demonic power her son left behind in Karazhan had probably already torn her consciousness apart. When I visited Karazhan before, I had already sensed that chaos she brought. The entire tower had gone mad, twisted by her divination ritual. That power had even affected Duskwood." At this, the assembled mages in the hall all fixed their gazes on Khadgar. Probably because the man had finally directly admitted to having visited Karazhan, even spilling quite a few details. But the danger everyone faced right now made this hard-won confession seem trivial. Jace could almost feel Khadgar mentally mouthing the words "Go ahead, throw me into the Violet Hold if you want." Khadgar adjusted his breathing and continued. "And that 'decision' must have been strong enough, firm enough, to force her to reject it in such an extreme manner. That discarded 'decision' wanted to tell us everything. You could even think of it as her last shred of sanity. If Master Othulos were here, he would know exactly what I'm talking about. He studied Karazhan for a long time. He must understand how a Guardian's power can make their 'thoughts' so difficult to deal with." "If we can't even believe that, then we must accept that the beloved Master Scavell, and the other masters who sacrificed nearly everything to fight the Burning Legion back then, were foolish enough to entrust power that could either protect or destroy all of Azeroth to a complete lunatic." "You're right, Master Khadgar," Vargos said quietly. "Mephistroth — that dead Nathrezim lord — was trying to assassinate her. To assassinate that shade." Jace said. "This might have been ordered by the Deceiver Kil'jaeden. But Lady Aegwynm must have told him the shade's exact location. That means she didn't want outsiders to find that shade either, or get clues from it." "Yes. That's also very important. Thank you for that addition, Master Sesso." Khadgar nodded. "So I think it's time to turn our attention to the secret facilities and meeting places the Order of Tirisfal left in Lordaeron, Master Antonidas." "The Order of Tirisfal never fully shared the locations of those secret facilities with the Kirin Tor, Master Khadgar," Antonidas said. "In fact, the Order has already deployed enough personnel to check every one according to the records we have." Khadgar sighed. He glanced at Jace, then looked at the others, frowning. "Can't we contact him, Master Antonidas? If he knew about all of this... he would surely be willing to help." "Master Meryl Felstorm." Modera shook her head. "You are probably the one here most likely to know the whereabouts of this founding member of the Order, Master Khadgar. You know this well. As for the rest of us, finding him might barely be easier than finding Aegwynn herself." Meryl Felstorm. Jace had never expected to hear that name from the lips of the Kirin Tor Six at this moment. One of the founders of the Order of Tirisfal had indeed survived to this day, but in a very... special form. He was practically an undead by now. But even in the game, until more than a decade later in another timeline, just before the Burning Legion's return, this ancient mage's whereabouts were almost a complete mystery. Even more so now, when the Scourge's disaster hadn't even begun. "Do our current allies have any new messages?" Archmage Drenden asked. "The Sun King has already replied. The leaders of Aerie Peak and Ironforge haven't left Dalaran yet — they answered our call early on." Modera responded. "Grim Batol also sent an envoy bearing the Red Dragon Queen's response..." "The Kaldorei from the other continent have also expressed their agreement, Master Antonidas." Jace said. "They sent word here through the Emerald Dream." "News from the Kaldorei is truly inspiring," the Grand Magister acknowledged. "What about the Nexus, then?" Vargos asked. "As it stands, the Nexus actually began investigating Aegwynn earlier than any of us. They just never shared their 'progress' with us." Khadgar said. "The blue dragon mage who went to Stormwind to investigate the ley line fluctuations earlier hasn't reported back at all." "Master Krasus has already indicated he will go to the Nexus, to meet with the guardian dragons of magic as their envoy." Modera looked at Khadgar. "He is the most senior dragon lore scholar among us. We need only wait quietly for his reply..." After the meeting, Khadgar hurried down the steps with a face like he desperately needed a toilet, muttering to Jace beside him. "Wait quietly? The Burning Legion's demons are about to jump on our heads!" "At least the Kirin Tor has eliminated countless wrong answers for us, Khadgar," Jace said. "I know. I know." Khadgar said quietly. "Holy Light... since when did I need Jace Sesso to tell me what the Kirin Tor is good for?" "But we can't just 'wait quietly' either. I know someone in Dalaran. The Kirin Tor can't find Meryl Felstorm right now, but this lady is almost as good. She just has her own problems. That's why you need to come with me too, Jace. I don't have the confidence to talk to her about these things without you. Honestly, if it weren't absolutely necessary, I wouldn't want to... disturb her." "Who?" "Let's talk outside."
