Chapter 1826: Eye of Sargeras

"Take us there, Master Alodi — if your soul can still see. Hopefully part of your spirit is bound to this statue, so you can watch your successors and see what they've made of this world." Khadgar might have been rambling, but he'd used arcane magic to hurl the massive mage statue beneath him and Jace into the storm, toward the blinding light. It was almost as if the Kirin Tor and Order of Tirisfal's shared founding father — the most powerful half-elf to ever exist — had actually manifested and was carrying them toward the mad Guardian. "Jace!" the mage shouted over the gale. "I'll draw Lady Aegwynn's attention. Find your opening and hit the portal with a strong enough Hand of Gul'dan. You don't need to destroy it completely — just damaging part of its structure will buy time for reinforcements to surround this place." "You don't need to remind me. I'm ready." Jace asked, "But can reinforcements actually break through and get in here? Does the Kirin Tor have a way to dismantle this dimensional barrier's arcane isolation ward?" Khadgar blinked rapidly, stunned for a second. Then he burst out cursing: "They'd better find a way! If they can't, then let this damn world go to hell!" It was hard to imagine that everyone's shining savior, pushed to this limit, would curse and tell everyone to "go screw themselves." Jace couldn't help but chuckle, which earned him another glare from the exhausted mage. But strangely, moments ago Jace had felt tangled in a mess of confusion and doubt. Now that he and Khadgar had decided to fly straight at Aegwynn and her giant portal, he felt lighter — as if all the burdens had been shed. The portal grew closer. From this distance it was hard to see clearly, but the outline was complete enough. The ring-shaped giant gate floating at the center of his vision looked like an abstract demonic maw, or a spiked iron ring — a collar. Inside surged severely twisted arcane energy, roiling like waves in the dark night, accumulating power. One command from its master and it could punch a path to any corner of the dark void. High above, countless chunks of debris tumbled in the abyss below the storm, becoming demonically corrupted as they flew toward the gate. The statue of Alodi that Jace and Khadgar stood on was just one of those pieces of rubble — albeit one of the bigger ones. "Where is she? Do you see her?" Jace shouted from the statue's shoulder. "I'm looking!" the mage yelled back from his perch on Alodi's head. But before he finished, a shock of arcane force that nearly shattered their chests blew open Alodi's chest, then snapped its neck. Khadgar clung to the head and went flying into the distance. The chest piece dragged Jace in a different direction. By the time he realized he needed to hold on tight, the mage was already gone. Everything spun. Space seemed to invert. With no discernible up or down, he fell through empty air like he'd dropped into a sky without gravity. Tumbling endlessly, the portal kept flashing past his vision. In his peripheral sight, a familiar yet unfamiliar figure seemed to fly straight through the chaotic magical gale toward Khadgar's position. She spoke no words, made no unnecessary movements. Merely glowing motes of starlight surrounded her... At this distance, Jace could glimpse a trace of the figure's inner emotions. She no longer hid her intent. No more hesitation. Remove all obstacles. Complete her goal. The chunk of Alodi's shoulder Jace was gripping shattered. He lost his hold and was sucked into the air again. Instinctively, he triggered Shadow Step toward a piece of debris, caught hold, and steadied himself. Looking toward the portal to find the two mages, he saw a massive spread of arcane "grids" scattered across the sky — like a vast net, or the countless crystalline facets of a giant diamond. These facets shifted and flickered — spreading apart, breaking, sometimes flashing brighter than the portal's own light. Like individual prisons, they reminded Jace of the dimensional prison that held Kel'Thuzad in the Violet Hold. None of this had existed a moment ago. Now it was generating in waves, being shattered, struck through by chain lightning, pierced by Arcane Missiles that either exploded or vanished — like fish struggling in a magical maelstrom. Khadgar and Aegwynn had already engaged. Jace couldn't waste time. He climbed onto his small floating island, raised his staff, and began chanting the Hand of Gul'dan. He didn't need extra soul shards anymore. Ulthalesh had already devoured countless souls from every source — so many that even Jace felt it was becoming an unbearable burden. Now he finally had a chance to unleash it. The howling magical wind gradually mixed with purple dark light. The ethereal arcane shrieks began to be penetrated by shadowy cries. Bloody tears streamed from Ulthalesh's eyes and scattered. Even the floating island beneath him corroded black, cracking and crumbling... Shadow serpents writhed through the air, converging at Jace's will... But just as they were about to form a dark star even Nefarian couldn't withstand, a rain of arcane arrows poured down from above. Some struck the shadow serpents directly; others spun and diverted their dark energy, weakening and scattering them. Jace stared at the arcane energy, the dark power he'd held slipping through his fingers like sand. A silver magic silhouette burst from the missiles and smashed straight into a floating wall fragment in front of him. A white-haired woman in a pale purple robe, ghostly fast — she raised her hands in a flash of light. Before Jace could even try to interrupt her casting with Silence, silver chains like mithril had locked his limbs and neck. The chains spread out, anchoring to several floating enchanted stones around him, restraining the warlock like a prisoner about to be drawn and quartered. "Please wait quietly... for the final outcome, Jace Sesso. I know this isn't your fault." The Guardian's voice echoed all around. Jace didn't bother to respond. The demonic mouth on his staff spat out a spray of blood-magic blades that severed the chain binding his right arm. He swung his scythe to cut the left-hand restraints, then easily shattered more chains. The ghostly female mage seemed surprised he'd broken free so easily. But in that moment's opening, Ulthalesh — drenched in dark blood — was yanked forward by Blood Grip, cleanly cut two illusions in half, and dissolved them into arcane mist that vanished into the storm. More arcane missiles rained from above — some were more illusions. Jace summoned a Dark Ward, and amid the arcane starlight, he moved like a black meteor weaving between spinning dark stones. He stabilized himself, reformed the scattered shadow serpents, chanted to release more of Ulthalesh's soul power to spawn more serpents, sending them to battle the meteor-like arcane arrows while gathering an ever-stronger Dark Star aimed at the portal growing ever closer. "Come on, Jace!" A distorted Khadgar shadow clung to a rock as it drifted by, its odd impromptu face shouting: "I'm about to be done for, Jace! Do it! Throw it!" The shadow was so fragile that shouting was enough to make it crumble. But Jace had already completed the final channeling. "Wilma." He spoke the final incantation. The Dark Star pushed through massive debris and arcane starlight, hurtling straight at the colossal portal. But as the star approached, its body began to crack and tear apart. Its shadow energy was stripped from the outside by an inconspicuous emerald object at the portal's center, bit by bit turning into soul fragments drawn into the gate — like a clump of mud dropped into a rushing stream. The last remnant of shadow energy struck the gate's frame and exploded in violet-red flames. But it didn't stop the portal from taking shape. The explosive shadow energy was also sucked into that object, becoming part of the whole ritual. The Eye of Sargeras? The core that had twisted all arcane energy and soul power — it was right there. The massive portal, built over months by Aegwynn using the Order of Tirisfal's runic ruins and connected to the Eye of Sargeras — its magical strength and structural integrity had reached absurd levels. "What? What?! How?!" Khadgar's phantom couldn't accept reality, but its voice faded quickly into the wind. Arcane missiles rained down, shattering Jace's Dark Ward. The explosion's shadow shockwave destroyed the stone beneath him and several nearby fragments. Jace lost his balance again, sucked toward the portal. Arcane missiles that had been chasing him were themselves caught by the portal's pull and shot past him even faster. Floating in the air, Jace stared at the object at the portal's center. With the Dark Star shattered, he could barely see it anymore — the emerald trace was completely buried by the blinding light. Devour it. Devour the Eye of Sargeras. If I consume its power, the portal will instantly lose its support and collapse. Aegwynn... You want to destroy Ulthalesh? Ah, yes... Jace Sesso... my master. Devour it.
