## Chapter 1828: Darkness Over Lordamere

"Fenris Keep?"

A small banquet was being held in the Menethil royal palace tonight. Those in attendance were either royal kin or members of the privy council currently in the capital.

Terenas II stood in a nearby corridor, glancing sternly at the brightly lit hall behind him, then turned his gaze to the expressionless Archmage Biso Ershad.

"Are you certain? Does the Kirin Tor believe Fenris Keep has been infiltrated by demons? What about Lord Garithos? Has he been possessed too? He attended the hearing with us. I saw nothing unusual about him. He never reported anything out of the ordinary, Master Ershad. This would be a very, very serious accusation."

"Archmaster Antonidas asked me not to disclose the source of this intelligence, Your Majesty." Biso Ershad lowered his voice. "As a member of the Kirin Tor, I should respect the Archmaster's wishes. But I am also a Lordaeron man, one of Your Majesty's most trusted magical advisors. So... the one who confirmed the castle might be under demonic control is actually Jace Sesso's so-called demon lover — that succubus."

The old king frowned, steadying himself against a doorframe. He let out a breath, his lips trembling slightly.

"So this is the wizards' verdict? Because of a single word from a demon servant, I'm supposed to agree to Antonidas's request to lock down Fenris Keep, evacuate the entire island, and then go in with the Kirin Tor for an investigation?"

"What is your view, Ershad? I value your opinion."

"Is this some sort of insult? Retaliation for Arthas's public challenge during the hearing? Or payback for the Tower of Ilgalar business? I don't want to provoke that young warlock any more than necessary, or Wrynn — but this has clearly crossed the line."

The archmage was silent for a long moment before speaking. "If I had to offer my judgment, Your Majesty... based on what I know of Jace Sesso, I don't believe he would act with such motives."

"What could you possibly know about that mystery man?" The king sighed. "So — is Sesso still at Fenris Keep?"

"He should be. Along with Archmage Khadgar," the mage replied.

"Ridiculous." Terenas narrowed his eyes. "Even if demons have taken control of Fenris Keep, even if they've possessed everyone including Othmar — what then? What are they going to do? Occupy a lake island and launch another Orc War from there?"

Before the king could finish, the ground began to shake violently. He grabbed the doorframe to keep from falling.

Beside him, the archmage immediately seized a pillar. Candlesticks swayed and toppled. Paintings fell from the walls. Inside the hall, exclamations and panicked murmurs rose. The candlelight flickered.

"An earthquake? What's happening?" Terenas shouted down the corridor.

Biso Ershad steadied himself in the shaking and stared at his palm. "The ley lines, Your Majesty. The ley lines are being disturbed..."

"What does that mean?" The king grabbed the mage's cloak. "Speak plainly, Biso!"

At that moment, a palace guard ran down from a nearby stairway, gripping the wall. "Your Majesty! Lake Lordamere..."

The old king said nothing. He pushed off the wall and stumbled forward, coughing violently as he passed the guard and climbed the stairs. Ershad had no choice but to follow.

Terenas fixed his eyes on the highest tower of the royal city, crossing the castle's rooftop walkway through the violent tremors toward the wall facing Lake Lordamere.

The city was filled with terrified screams. Fires had already broken out in several places. Roads were clogged with panicked horsemen, scraping carriages, and fallen citizens.

Below the walls, countless torchlights swarmed the lakeside docks. Boats with lanterns fled the center of the lake, crowding toward shore. Many didn't even bother docking — they ran their boats onto the beach and abandoned them.

Terenas gripped the battlements and leaned out toward the south. The normally pitch-black Lake Lordamere was flashing with strange magical light — silent lightning accompanied by eerie, ethereal ringing.

With each flash, the silhouette of Fenris Isle's castle was illuminated — and beyond it, the pine forests on the far shore. They looked like ranks of demons hidden in the dark, waiting to devour the world, staring at the helpless mortals on this shore.

Then an even stronger tremor struck. A chunk of the cliff beneath the wall collapsed into the lake. A section of the wall itself fell. Several royal guards lunged to catch the old king before he tumbled over.

"Your Majesty, we need to leave!" Biso Ershad shouted, holding his wind-whipped hood. "This wall won't hold!"

"Tell me what's happening!" Terenas seemed unwilling to leave. His hoarse voice boomed. "What in the blazes is going on at Fenris Keep?"

"Gone..." a guard supporting him murmured. "Fenris Keep... is gone, Your Majesty."

Terenas froze, gripping his cloak as he looked where the guard pointed. The cracking stones beneath his feet, the gryphon riders streaking overhead, the screams along the wall — all ceased to exist.

Because in the intensifying flashes, the outline of Fenris Keep had vanished. In its place stood a ring-shaped giant gate.

A foul, nauseating wind blew off the lake. Many soldiers retched on the spot. The only officers still at their posts without fear were veterans of the Dark Portal campaign — some had even returned from the other side.

"Demons," Ershad murmured, eyes hollow.

A gryphon's scream tore through the sky. Everyone on the wall looked up. A gryphon — half its body burned to bone by shadow energy — spiraled down, smashed into the wall, and tumbled off the cliff.

With the flap of leathery wings, a massive shadow landed on the wall. Its hooves crushed a section of battlements.

A hulking, dark brown monster with ram-like horns, a gaping maw, and cold, fel-green eyes grinned — an Eredar doomguard.

"Demons have reached the wall! Protect His Majesty!"

The archmage raised his staff and began to chant. The doomguard was immediately wrapped in layers of yellow magical light, cursing and struggling to tear through the Slow spell.

Several royal guards shouted "For Lordaeron!" and charged with spears. But their weapons couldn't pierce the monster's hide. One was grabbed by the chest and hurled over the wall, leaving only a fading scream.

Ershad calmly chanted a second attack spell, preparing to finish the doomguard — when a Shadow Bolt shot past him from behind, breaking through his Shadow Ward.

Both his hands were badly corroded. He stumbled back, barely able to hold his staff. Another doomguard landed on the far side, the shadow energy in its hands still dissipating.

The larger doomguard shook off the Slow spell, glanced at the soaring shadows overhead, then looked down at the humans surrounded by guards. Its raspy voice rumbled, "This world is at its end, mortals."

Terenas drew his ornate longsword. His lips trembled with barely-contained fury — or fear.

"This is my wall! This is my kingdom! Don't you dare defile it, demon!"

The archmage clutched his staff, ready for a final stand. The doomguard spat something contemptuous in Demonic and gathered a fistful of blinding green fire in its hand.

Ershad spun and threw up a sheet of frost, blocking the other doomguard's view. The ice shattered quickly, but it bought him time for a Counterspell. The first doomguard was struck by a burst of arcane energy at its skull. Its magic fire scattered into the air...

A strange, bright green flame swept across its arm. Half the demon's body was sheared off. A section of its demon heart gave a feeble pump before the remaining half of its body burned to ash.

"What..."

Biso Ershad thought his Counterspell had gone wrong — but a slender, winged demon landed between the humans and the remaining demon.

Compared to the hulking fel monster, this one — blood-red, hooves and wings that inspired terror, horns like a demon's crown — moved with the grace of an elven dancer.

The other demon crashed through the ice, only for the newly arrived female demon to flash in front of him, stab her hand through his chest, and grasp his heart. The massive body froze like a statue.

"I can devour your soul like a snack, Eredar." Morofil spoke in Demonic. "If you don't want that fate — tell me where Jace Sesso is."

"The Legion... will rule all!" the doomguard bellowed.

"I guess you never learned Eredan." Morofil narrowed her eyes. The demon crumpled like burned paper in her grip, screaming as it burned to nothing but blackened armor.

"Thank—"

Before the king could finish, the female demon whipped her head around a hundred and eighty degrees and fixed him with her gaze. In Common: "Is my master here?"

"If you mean Master Jace Sesso — I wish to see him too, madam," the king said, steadying himself.

"I only just informed His Majesty about Fenris Keep, Lady Morofil." Biso Ershad swallowed. "We know nothing of Master Sesso's whereabouts, or what happened at Fenris Keep..."

"This portal is not yet complete," the demon said. "The Burning Legion intends to finish it and summon the Defiler into this world once again. You know what that means, mage. You don't have much time."

"The Defiler..."

As the archmage stood frozen in shock, the demon spread her wings and vanished into the sky.

"Whatever that was." The old king drew a breath and shouted to all the soldiers and subordinates who could still move: "Rally everyone! Rally everyone... Defend the wall! Defend the capital! Answer Dalaran's call!"
