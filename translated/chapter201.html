Chapter 201: Griffin Drool

Jace was starving. He scanned the table, ready to grab whatever looked edible and stuff it down — surely there was something from that roast they'd brought earlier… wolf meat, boar, even bread, anything would do.

As his eyes swept the tabletop, a rhythmic scritch-scritch-scritch drifted over from beside him.

Only then did he notice the bearded dwarf sitting to his left, who was already thoroughly drunk. The dwarf squinted in Jace's direction, half-lidded, while propped on the table, his hand reaching out again and again to scratch the wood, his nails scraping out that scritch-scritch the whole time.

Jace had no idea what was wrong with him — he wondered if the poor bastard was sick — when a dwarf across the table cursed under his breath and shoved a plate of pear-like fruit right into the spot the drunk was clawing at. The dwarf's hand closed around one of the fruits with surprising steadiness, and he popped it into his mouth and started chewing. Only then did the maddening nail-on-wood noise finally stop.

"I…" the dwarf mumbled around the fruit, and to Jace's surprise he was speaking Common: "I'll tell you, human, there's another human too…"

"Huh?" Jace asked. "What do you mean? You're saying there are humans living on Aerie Peak?"

"Of course!" the dwarf declared — and then slumped under the table.

Aerie Peak was enormous. After the Orc Wars, it wouldn't be strange for people to be living up there — probably an emissary from one of the human kingdoms or something.

"Don't listen to his rambling!" Grede cut in from the side. "He doesn't even know what he's saying. Stop dithering, eat, eat — if you don't hurry there won't be anything left…" Jace nodded and was just reaching for the food when a hand like a chunk of stone clamped down on his arm.

He turned to find a dwarf glaring at him with pure venom.

The dwarf's other finger jabbed toward Jace, teeth grinding audibly, lips working — but no words came out for a long moment.

Jace tried to twist his head around to get Grede's help, but the dwarf grabbed his face and wrenched it back. If Jace hadn't quickly turned his head along with the motion, his neck might've been twisted clean off.

The dwarf pointed at his nose. His lips went from trembling to contorting — he was finally about to spit it out.

Just as Jace tried to reach for Grede, the dwarf suddenly rattled off a long string of Dwarvish, then started going "Huh? Huh?" at him — but Jace couldn't understand a word. Still, he got the distinct impression this guy absolutely hated him.

Had someone discovered he was a warlock? That was impossible — he'd hidden his warlock's bag and staff in Grede's little room. Had someone dug them out?

The dwarf kept pressing him. Finally, Grede seemed to catch on and leaned over.

"He's asking you… how the hell you got so tall."

"WHAT?!"

Jace looked down at Grede, who'd poked his head close — the dwarf seemed a little drunk himself.

What were these Wildhammer drinking, that they got wasted this fast? He glanced at the cups on the table. At some point, a full cup and a half-full cup had appeared right in front of him.

The dwarf in front of him flicked Jace right on the forehead — a proper thwack — nearly sending Jace to his grave on the spot. Then he unleashed another torrent of Dwarvish. Grede burped and translated: "He's asking if you've been stealing the griffins' rations."

"How am I supposed to answer that?"

"I'll… I'll talk to him!"

Grede grabbed the dwarf by the collar and punched him clean under the table.

"There, he knows now." Grede plopped his ass down on the floor, burped again, and pointed at the drink in front of Jace. "Hurry up, drink it — if you don't, it'll all be gone!"

"The drink's gonna get up and run away?"

Jace turned back — and sure enough, it had. There'd been a full cup a moment ago, and now there was only half a cup left.

He'd been a little worried the stuff might be too strong, but the inexplicable disappearance of a whole cup had sparked a bizarre possessive panic in his chest, a fear that it would be stolen again. So he grabbed the remaining cup and took a fierce gulp.

Holy hell — it nearly knocked Jace flat. What was this stuff?

Grede watched Jace's miserable face and burst out laughing. He slumped against Jace's leg, pointing at the cup. "Griffin drool…"

Hearing those words, Jace swore, leaped to his feet, and shoved Grede to the ground. He bolted all the way to the railing outside the hall and dry-heaved over the hundred-meter cliff — but nothing came up!

"No… no!" Grede shouted. "I mean griffins love this stuff too! They drool when they see it!"

"Bullshit!" Jace yelled. "You think a griffin can still fly after drinking this?!"

Grede chuckled. "Caught me!"

Jace was completely done. He dry-heaved again.

Still, thinking back on the taste of that cup — it hadn't had the rank stench of animal saliva at all. It really had been an intensely aromatic liquor.

A terrifying thought surfaced: could this be griffin mouth-brew?

His gaze swept across the Wildhammer city clinging to the cliffside. In the middle of the night, the torchlight all blurred into double visions — and that was from a single sip.

Why did I come here?

What am I even doing?

What kind of place is this?

Why… do I want another sip?

Jace turned back to look at the cup on the table.

At that moment, a dwarf swayed up onto the table and grabbed the cup.

"Let go!" Jace roared in Orcish.

The dwarf froze. His hand loosened.

Jace walked over step by step, picked up the cup, and took another gulp.

God, that was good…

He sat down on the stool and burped, then picked up a haunch of meat from some unidentifiable animal, tore off a strip, and stuffed it into his mouth, grease running down his chin.

To cut the greasiness, another sip of liquor.

To sober up, another bite of meat.

To cut the greasiness…

After a few rounds of this, Jace started feeling that something was very wrong.

The liquor was like soft, warm water, melting away every sharp edge in his awareness of the world around him. All the hard things he could see — stone, tables, armor, bones — suddenly became gentle and lovely, as if wrapped in halos.

The noisy, dancing dwarves all came smiling to his side, shouting "Great King Jace Sesso!", joining hands around him and breaking into an excited song…
