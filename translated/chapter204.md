## Chapter 204: What Did We Just Save?

"Judging by the shape of this cocoon, this doesn't look like one of your kin, Grede," Jace said in a low voice.

"I noticed. Damn it." Grede scratched his brow, thought for a moment, then said, "Then this elf is going to cost at least fifty silver. We'll drag him to Quel'Danis and let those folks pay the ransom."

"Isn't fifty silver too little? Those elves are loaded." Jace said. "We should squeeze them for at least two gold or something."

"Squeeze? That's a nasty way to put it — this is a commission." The dwarf felt along the wall, found an uneven handhold, and gripped it. "But first we need to unwrap him and see if he's good-looking. Elf or not, elves won't spend big money to ransom a companion who's grown ugly."

Jace swallowed uncomfortably. That was way too pragmatic.

Grede climbed up along the relief carvings on the wall and used his dagger to cut through the spider silk suspending the cocoon. It hit the ground with a *thump*, and a burst of excited *mm-mm-mm* came from inside — as if the person was overjoyed.

The dwarf jumped down, marveling. "This guy's still got plenty of spirit — he's been suffocating in there for god knows how long and he's still got this much energy."

"Well, he's an elf. With mana, he can survive."

As Jace spoke, he helped illuminate with the Light. The dwarf stepped forward and began carefully cutting away the outer layers of spider silk with his dagger — only to reveal a tuft of green hair after a few cuts…

Grede paused for a moment, then stood up and sighed. "Wasted trip."

He suddenly swung his hammer and smashed it hard into the wriggling head. The person inside didn't make a single sound — just went quiet. Bright red blood seeped out between the strands of spider silk.

Jace hadn't even reacted yet. He froze for a second. "This…"

"Ah, right, I forgot." Grede raised an eyebrow. "You said you wanted to bed a female troll — what a shame, I smashed her to death."

Jace looked at the coarse, weed-green hair. "That was way too fast, I didn't even have time to react… are you sure this is a troll? Couldn't it be an elf who dyed their hair?"

Grede shook his head. "Relax — elves would rather cut their own heads off than dye their hair the color of a forest troll. Though this does explain why the guy had so much energy. Trolls are always full of strength. Pity about my money, damn it."

"Open it up and let me see," Jace said curiously. "I've never seen a troll before."

Grede nodded and used his knife to cut away a few more layers of silk, revealing a bluish face with small tusks. Nostrils, eyes, and the corners of the mouth were all caked with blood. Roughly speaking, it had a human sort of look — but it also resembled a strange, small-nosed lioness.

"You're not going to get sentimental over a troll, are you?" Grede asked, watching Jace's furrowed brow.

"The death was a bit grim…" Jace muttered.

"If you'd seen those rows of spears with all kinds of skinned heads stuck on them, you wouldn't think she had it so bad," Grede said. "She was going to die anyway — I wasn't about to save her. In the end she would've been slowly eaten by that spider. A hammer blow from me was a mercy, don't you think?"

"That's… true enough," Jace said. "Mainly I was thinking you could've cut the silk open a little more."

Grede glared at him. "You really do need to find yourself a wife, Jace. Holding it in this long will twist your mind — I think you're already past the boundaries of what I can understand."

Jace said helplessly, "Are you out of your mind? Cut it open and see if there's any coin or valuables on him… even if I actually did want to bed a female troll, I wouldn't get worked up over a corpse! And even putting that aside — at the very least not now, and not here."

"Ah… right, right." Grede nodded as if struck by a revelation, and moved to keep cutting the silk.

Jace leaned in to take a look — when the dwarf suddenly stopped.

"What's wrong?"

Grede looked past Jace and shouted, "Behind you, look out!"

The words had barely landed when Jace turned around. Out of the shadows beyond the reach of the Light, a giant insect head the size of his upper body suddenly appeared, four pairs of eyes gleaming!

Thin, elongated legs stretched out from the darkness, clawing at the wall and floor with a *tap-tap-tap* as they crawled forward. Jace felt goosebumps erupt all over his body and instinctively pointed a finger at the colossal spider monster that stood taller than himself.

He shouted the incantation for Fear. The Light went out with it. The spider let out a piercing shriek and retreated back into the shadows.

"Out of the way!"

Grede darted out from behind Jace. In the pitch blackness, he leaped toward roughly the right position and swung his hammer in a crushing blow right between the spider's two rows of eyes.

Flashing lightning flickered once. A heavy, muffled *crack* — like a watermelon splitting open — and the spider gurgled and collapsed flat on the ground.

Its long, thick, hairy legs thrashed for a while. Grede ducked back to avoid a sweeping leg, retreating a few steps. Jace conjured another Light to illuminate the scene. The spider seemed to be done for — only its limbs were still twitching incessantly.

"A mother spider," Grede panted. "Nothing… nothing major, but her legs are still pretty nasty."

He made a jabbing motion with his hand and grinned. "One stab and she'd punch straight through your skull."

"Scared the hell out of me," Jace said.

"Got some progress this time — at least the Fear you cast didn't catch me instead." Grede said this as he gestured animatedly, clearly still riding the high of having just smashed a giant spider to death with one blow.

But Jace was feeling more and more that something was off. That dead spider behind the dwarf — hadn't it been twitching for a bit too long? Beyond the reach of the Light, there seemed to be a dark, pressing mass creeping closer. Jace said, "Forget the troll, we should go…"

Grede turned his head and just in time to see several palm-sized baby spiders crawling over the top of their mother's head, their chelicerae quivering frantically at Grede.

"Run!" Jace smacked Grede on the shoulder with his staff. "Move! You don't hear the spiders swarming over?!"

Grede yelped and took off running — but when they reached the passage ahead, they found the way out already covered with countless small spiders.

They poured down toward them, making a *chitter-chitter-chitter* racket. In the blink of an eye, the *tap-tapping* of spider legs on the walls had grown so dense that no other sound could be heard at all!

"Force my hand, will you…"

As he spoke, Grede's warhammer suddenly erupted with a chain of lightning that lit up the entire space, drowning out Jace's Light!

The flash of lightning, accompanied by a *crack*, instantly pierced through the whole passage. The smell of charred meat quickly filled the air.

The two of them climbed up over a carpet of dead spiders. Grede ran faster; Jace, holding his staff and the Light with focused concentration, was a bit slower. As he ran he kept watching the spiders behind him, ready to use the mass Fear enchantment on his staff as a last resort — but then, as he ran, he heard a sharp "Ah!"

Jace had just turned his head and hadn't even processed what was happening when he suddenly felt himself crash into something soft and yielding. His feet left the ground against his will. The Light went out instantly. He struggled, but it was no use… damn it, it was a spider web trap…
