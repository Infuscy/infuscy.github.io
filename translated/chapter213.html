Chapter 213: Uninvited Guest

Early the next morning, Jace set out for the Wizard Sanctum with the small box containing the phoenix whisker in hand.

He figured this time Amy Marin had better put up a proper show of gratitude — anything less would be an insult to the brilliant display of knowledge he'd put on in Dalaran.

Following the familiar path to Master Marin's study, he opened the door and saw a woman in a grass-green cloak standing by the desk.

Sunlight streaming in through the window cast sharply contrasting light and shadow across the room, and for a moment Jace couldn't make out the figure clearly.

"Amy, happy new year! Look what I brought you?"

The woman turned around. Wait… this was actually an elf?!

"Master Marin just stepped out, human. She'll be back shortly."

Jace found that arrogantly dismissive tone awfully familiar… He narrowed his eyes and looked again. Wait, wasn't this Vereesa?

What was Vereesa doing here?

Jace rubbed his neck, wondering if he'd slipped into some kind of bizarre dream again.

But just as he was doubting himself, the elf spoke again: "Jace Sesso? You're back?"

Right then someone pushed the door open from behind, cutting off their conversation. Jace turned — it was Amy Marin, walking in.

She was carrying a stack of documents and had been about to say something to the elf, but when she noticed Jace standing to the side, she froze for a second. Then her expression lit up with excitement.

"Jace! You! Did you get back yesterday or this morning? You went home and came back — how come you look even more tanned? Doesn't the north not get any sun?"

"If only you knew what I went through this trip…" Jace said with a frustrated sigh.

"You have to tell me everything," Amy said with a smile. "Oh — let me introduce you. This is Lady Aiselwyn of Quel'Thalas. Lady Aiselwyn, this is my eager underclassman, Jace Sesso."

"Jace, Lady Aiselwyn came here on the orders of the Ranger-General of Silvermoon to investigate the remnants of the Horde. Unfortunately my father is discussing Academy enrollment matters with Archmage Andormas and can't help right now, so he asked me to hand her his research materials on the orcs. And remember to go register at Archmage Andormas's office this afternoon."

"Got it," Jace said.

Aiselwyn?

Vereesa?

She'd reversed her name?

That "Aiselwyn" was staring at him with an expression especially stiff. Jace knew exactly what that meant — don't blow her cover.

"Hello, Lady Aiselwyn," Jace said. The female elf cleared her throat. "Hello, Jace Sesso. Thank you very much, Lady Marin. I'll take my leave then."

She took the stack of papers, nodded slightly, and left the room. Jace's gaze followed her all the way until the door closed.

"These elves are really something — they talk like they're ordering servants around, even their thanks sound strange," Amy whispered with a shake of her head, then noticed Jace's stare.

"What is it, Jace? Do you know her?"

"I'm… not sure I do," Jace said. "All elves look the same to me. I saw plenty of elves in Dalaran, and they really are all just like you say."

"Ha, what a trip to Dalaran!" Amy leaned against the desk. "So — did you remember the things you were supposed to?"

"How could I forget." Jace set the box on the desk. "Open it and take a look."

A trace of disappointment flickered across Amy's face — but the moment she opened it, her eyes went wide with shock.

"By the Holy Light, this is a phoenix feather!"

"Ah, er, you…" Jace said. "You actually recognized it?"

"Of course I recognized it," Amy said, her tone tinged with guilt. "I saw a Kirin Tor archmage use this as a signature ages ago. This is far too precious — I really didn't want you buying something this expensive."

"It didn't actually cost anything," Jace said. "I won it as a prize at a Winter Veil event in Dalaran. They didn't tell me how much it was worth — I just thought it was beautiful, and that it'd make a perfect thank-you gift for you."

"It's not like I did anything at all. Thank you so much, Jace! I can hardly imagine a bigger surprise this year!"

Amy came around the desk and hugged Jace tight, squeezing for a good long while before letting go, leaving Jace reeking of some unidentifiable perfume.

"Oh, I need to go put this in my bedroom first — if my father sees it he'll definitely find a way to snatch it! You have to tell me all about Dalaran when you have time. You have to!"

And with that she left the door, leaving Jace alone in the room.

Ah, a hug was still pretty warm and nice.

Master Marin was busy discussing things with Andormas, so it didn't look like he'd be coming here anytime soon. Jace figured he'd go ask about enrollment procedures at Andormas's office that afternoon instead.

This morning he might as well go buy a couple of decent outfits first… especially a mage's robe. That death knight had gone and ruined his even in death — just remembering the scene where it burned up twelve silver's worth made his heart ache.

But Vereesa?

Stepping out, Jace caught sight of that green figure leaning against the wall by the Alchemy Lab not far away, watching this direction. When she saw him come out, she straightened up.

"Lady Vereesa, what are you doing in Stormwind?" he asked as he walked over. "And with a fake name too?"

Vereesa glanced up at the Wizard Sanctum beyond the gardens. "The name is fake, Jace Sesso — but the reason I came to Stormwind isn't. I really am here to investigate matters concerning the Horde in the Kingdom of Stormwind."

Jace looked at the stack of documents in her arms. "Is it connected to the Dragonmaw orcs in the Wetlands? My teacher Master Marin has been tracking the Dragonmaw orcs' trail — I imagine he'd share his research as thoroughly as he could."

"No," Vereesa said. "I came here specifically to look for you."

"Me?"

Could it be because of that order?

So the Reliquary and the Farstriders were working together?

Vereesa nodded. "And precisely because we know each other, Sylvanas agreed to let me carry out this mission. As for these documents — it's only because I couldn't find you just now that I thought I'd see if there was some other way to resolve my current difficulty. Shall we find somewhere to talk slowly? It's a bit cold here, and from the look of it you're dressed pretty thinly."

Jace glanced down at his shirt. Wearing just this in winter was indeed a bit inappropriate — and this was only Stormwind. If this were in Lordaeron he'd have frozen to death on the street.

"Then how about a tavern? The Gilded Rose, or the Blue Heron?"

"What's the difference between the two?"

"The Gilded Rose has normal people in it. The Blue Heron is all mages."

"The Gilded Rose, then."
