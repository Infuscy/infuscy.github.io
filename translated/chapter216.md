## Chapter 216: What's Owed Must Be Repaid

For the rest of the afternoon, Jace queued almost the entire time at the top of the Wizard Sanctum. The tower, when it was first built, certainly never expected there'd be this many apprentices here one day.

Of course, it also had to do with how slow registration was. That many students and only two old mages handling it, and they were being especially long-winded about it.

The whole stretch of time had crowded out his chance to buy a robe. By the came out it was already evening and the clothes shops were closed.

He finished registration, got his course schedule — starting Tuesday the 22nd. That day was basically packed: Orcish, Dragon runes, and inscription. Wednesday morning there was one Orcish class; Wednesday afternoon and Thursday morning were Protection school courses.

This year his classes all seemed to be concentrated between Tuesday and Thursday, which meant four days a week were left for practice… or, put another way, free time.

He went home to grab some money to buy something to eat, only to find Grede right outside his door, skipping stones on the canal bank.

Jace called from a distance, "It's this dark and you can still see how many you skipped?"

"Why wouldn't I," Grede said. "Jace, come on, come on — let's see who can skip more. I just hit twelve. How about that?"

The dwarf handed Jace a shard of roof tile, pointed at the water, and said, "Go on, try."

Jace gave him a meaningful look. A technique he'd practiced God-knows-how-many times in his past life, and it still got to show off?

Dwarf, you asked for it.

He shifted his stance, dropped his hand low, adjusted to about twenty degrees off the water — and the familiar feeling came back. A textbook sidearm throw sent the tile skimming off the surface six, seven times, then it spun out a whole string of splashes too many to count before smacking into the opposite bank with a sharp *crack*. Even the old men fishing nearby let out a startled cry.

"How… many… was that?" Grede was so shocked he could barely get the words out.

"Does it matter?" Jace said. "The river's not wide enough, Grede. Let's have a proper match at the Nafeltari sometime."

"No, no more." Grede frowned. "I sometimes really suspect what you've been doing all these years… do you have forty-eight hours in a day?"

Jace glanced at him, thinking: *Should I tell you I went on Zhihu looking for stone-skipping techniques and practiced like a madman?*

The dwarf shook his head. "No fun at all, Jace Sesso. You're no fun. Right — forgot the important thing. The dinner we arranged. Why didn't you come find me?"

"Who arranged anything with you?"

"I said yesterday we'd drink today. You still owe me a drink. You think you can play dumb and slip out of it?"

"Weren't you saying tomorrow or the day after? Fine, fine, I owe you. Let's go, let's go…"

The two of them jostled their way to The Gilded Rose. Jace had barely sat down before he said, "This is my second time eating here today."

"Who was the first time with?" the dwarf asked.

"Swear on your beard you won't tell anyone, and I'll tell you."

"I swear on my beard I won't tell anyone. Besides, I've got no one else to tell… Jace, I barely have any friends in this city I can talk to like this."

"Then guess."

"I already swore and you still want me to guess? Varian Wrynn."

"No. Vereesa Windrunner."

"Oh, come on, stop messing around, just tell me."

"It's true," Jace said. "She came to Stormwind, and she came specifically to find me. So — Grede Thunderfist — are you jealous? Alleria Windrunner doesn't even know you exist, and her little sister's already hitting on me."

Grede frowned. "Really?"

Jace laughed twice, then dropped his smile. "No. Not hitting on me."

"Who cares if she's hitting on you. I mean is it really Vereesa?" Grede asked. "What's she doing here?"

"She says she's investigating that command — the one we sold off," Jace replied.

"Wasn't that letter already handed over to the elves? Oh, right… she *is* an elf. So Quel'Thalas is planning to dig something out of that Teron Gorefiend's underling. We're screwed then. I'd been thinking about that guy this whole time — wonder if he hoarded a bunch of orcish valuables while working for Gorefiend."

"True enough," Jace said, shaking his head. "Once a Windrunner's on your trail, there's nothing left for us."

Just then the waitress came over with a plate of crab legs and set it on the table, along with a dish of cut bread. Both of their eyes followed her as she walked away step by step.

"Don't give her any leads," Grede said, rapping the table and lowering his voice. "Don't tell her the important stuff. We find it first, beat her to it. Then we pick out what to give her so she can go back and report. Best of both worlds. You know Orcish — she doesn't!"

Jace said, "As if the two of us could just easily take him down. That's Teron Gorefiend's right-hand man!"

"Didn't you beat Gorefiend himself?" Grede said.

"Without Vereesa and Kafal, I couldn't have beaten him," Jace said. "And Grede — don't forget, she's the one who healed your eye. She's your benefactor, and you're scheming against her…"

"She only helped because she didn't want to owe you anything!" Grede said.

Jace picked up a crab leg. Hearing Grede's words, he thought for a moment, then said, "But I told her everything this morning!"

"She asks and you just tell her?" Grede said, displeased.

Jace tossed the crab leg down. "You didn't tell me you were planning to kill that death knight! I'm about to start school. I just want to study magic properly, become some great mage or something. Who thinks as much as you do?"

"At least talk it over with me first… And besides, if you don't dare kill that death knight, what kind of great mage are you going to be!"

As he spoke, Grede picked up the crab leg, cracked the shell straight open with his nail, dug out the meat, and ate it.

"It's not about him personally," Jace said, taking another one. "Even if we could beat that guy, he must have a lot of helpers — orcs, or something else. He was high up in the Horde. Would he suddenly be all alone? And I have this feeling…"

The waitress set two dark beers on the table. The two of them watched her leave again, and Jace continued: "Don't you think the Redridge gnoll trouble might have been stirred up by that death knight? The goal being to help the Horde retreat into the Dark Portal, split Stormwind's forces?"

"Not impossible, but that's baseless speculation too," Grede said. "So what's that death knight doing in Duskwood? Last time you said you thought the Duskwood werewolves were a death knight, but Duskwood hasn't had much gnoll activity since the Cataclysm. If you ask me, Raven Hill's a big cemetery. That death knight probably went there to raise reinforcements. Those Dalaran zombies were truly terrifying — scarier than Duskwood ghouls. The more human they look, the scarier they are."

"Could be," Jace said.

The dwarf asked again, "But if they can just raise the dead, why not kill one and raise one? Why run all the way to a cemetery?"

"Killing people draws attention, doesn't it?" Jace answered. "Pulling corpses straight out of their graves — the magistrate isn't going to come after you. Especially Raven Hill cemetery, which has been abandoned. Undead running all over the place. That's the perfect crime scene."

"That's true," Grede nodded.

But the more the two of them talked, the more Jace thought back to that night in Dalaran, and a thought suddenly flashed through his mind.

"What if that death knight is looking for a new body?"

## Flags

- **Title**: 欠下的必须补上 — rendered as "What's Owed Must Be Repaid" (the "debt" here is the drink Jace owes Grede, but the phrasing carries the broader sense of making up for lost time/effort).
- **Kafal** (凯法尔): the death knight Jace and Vereesa fought alongside; name kept as-is (OC, not in glossary).
- **Nafeltari River** (纳菲瑞提河): glossary's tentative "River Neltharion / Nafeltari River"; used Nafeltari River.
- **Zhihu** (知乎): Jace's past-life Q&A site reference; kept as Zhihu to preserve the modern-Chinese flavor of his inner monologue.
- **Grede Thunderfist** (格瑞德·雷拳): full name used here per glossary (Thunderfist = 雷拳).
- **Vereesa Windrunner** (温蕾萨·风行者): glossary has Alleria; Vereesa is her canonical younger sister — used Vereesa Windrunner.
