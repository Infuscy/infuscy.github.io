Chapter 222: We're All Mercenaries

"Useless little…" Grede muttered.

The imp immediately bellowed, "Useless?! I really did sense something, but it's faint! Maybe it's a not-very-strong spellcaster, or a human who doesn't know they have arcane talent! Or a well of power that those useless mages of the Kingdom of Stormwind haven't discovered yet!"

"All right, all right, we get it, stop yelling… just shut up for now," Jace said, and the imp immediately stopped its chatter.

Jace lowered his voice. "It has no reason to lie. Either way, we move forward carefully."

Grede muttered reluctantly, "Rather than walking around with this useless demon, we'd have been better off calling for Vereesa. At least a ranger would actually be useful right now."

"She's not here on vacation… and how would you even call her?" Jace stared at him and whispered. "Besides, do you want another person splitting the pot?"

"Some high elf noble, scrambling for our money?"

"You want her to do charity work?"

"She can't just help out? You've done so much for her already…"

Jace thought back to her behavior at the Gilded Rose the day before yesterday — nothing about it suggested she'd brought enough money. For all he knew, going without money might be one of the trials she'd set for herself.

"Honestly, I don't think she has much money either. But working for free? Absolutely not."

Grede said, "Her family's definitely richer than that elf who bought the letter, right?"

"Her family being rich doesn't mean she's rich…"

Jace hadn't even finished his sentence when the imp suddenly vanished in a flash of light.

"Where'd it go?" Grede asked.

Jace said, "Someone's here. Hide."

The two of them pressed themselves behind the trunk of a large oak tree. Once they'd gone quiet, voices drifted in from the distance.

Before long, six or seven people came walking through the woods, one behind the other. They seemed low on spirits. One of them kept cursing under his breath while another kept telling him to shut up.

From the occasional clear word that jumped out, Jace could tell they were talking about gnolls — it sounded like they'd just gotten into a fight with some. As their voices drew closer, he picked up more and more information about the gnolls.

This was probably another mercenary company hunting Hogger, just like them — one of the ones the sheriff had mentioned entering the forest these past few days.

Jace tried leaning out for a proper look. Most of them carried swords or blades, wearing simple leather armor or heavy coats. The bald one leading them had a single metal pauldron strapped to his shoulder.

At the back was a human in a cloak, carrying a light crossbow, following quietly and not joining in the argument.

If the imp had sensed a spellcaster approaching earlier, that was undoubtedly this person.

"Could it be these guys?" Grede leaned in and whispered.

"You mean they killed that man back there? I couldn't say for sure," Jace said. "Let them pass. There are seven of them."

The two of them stayed hidden behind the tree, keeping as quiet as possible.

But Jace's thoughts had only just drifted when he noticed, to his alarm, that the men seemed to be getting closer.

Grede's expression shifted too. He glanced at Jace and hardly dared to make a sound.

It seemed this group didn't want to cut through the forest either — they were trying to find the River Neltharion's channel and follow it along, which was why they kept heading in this direction.

Jace slowly shifted his footing to the side, waving for Grede to follow. The two of them tried to circle around the big tree to avoid the group's line of sight, but whether it was the carpet of dead leaves that gave away their footsteps, or the men had sensed something off—

"Who's there?!"

A loud shout came from the other side of the trunk.

"What did you hear?"

"You scared me, Chuck."

Jace and Grede were already pressed flat against the tree trunk, waiting for the men to dismiss the sound and move on.

But then one of them said, "I'll go check it out. Cover me. Might be a damn gnoll scout…"

The footsteps behind them grew closer. Grede looked at Jace. Jace pointed at the Shadow Protection Potion in his pack.

Grede downed the potion, swallowed hard with his eyes shut, then shot Jace a look that said the hell did you just make me drink.

"Don't attack!" he shouted. "We're here to kill gnolls too!"

"Come out!" the man behind the tree shouted. "Come out with your weapons down!"

Hearing that, Jace looked at Grede and gave him a hard stare. Grede hung his hammer at his belt and walked out with Jace.

The men quickly surrounded the two of them, leaving just one watching the wounded. One of them, seeing Jace emerge from behind the tree holding a staff, said, "Mage, drop your staff. Now!"

Jace obeyed immediately, throwing his staff down and raising his hands high.

"Take that hammer off and throw it aside too, dwarf." A bald man held a sword to the dwarf's nose.

The moment the man did that, Jace knew this wasn't going to end peacefully. He was going to die — no question. He didn't even need to look at Grede's face to know.

"You don't know what this hammer means to me, young man," Grede stared at him. "It's no less important to me than your own father is to you."

"Funny — I've never met my father," the bald man said. "Hurry up, stop talking. Throw down the hammer and take everything out of your pockets. I heard a bottle — healing potion, or whatever other potion… and coins. Quick."

Jace looked at the bald man. "No need to push this to the extreme, sir."

The bald man glanced back at the others and laughed.

The other soldiers nearby chuckled. The crossbowman not far off let out a cold laugh too.

"Fine, I'll unchain it." Grede unslung his war hammer. The bald man nodded. "That's more like it… that way we can make it quick for you."

Suddenly, Jace felt the air around the hammer warp and distort.

The men around them seemed to notice something off too. Grede appeared to be murmuring something under his breath. Every face shifted — from mocking grins to tension and rage.

"What are you doing?"

Someone shouted. Jace lowered his outstretched right hand, and the staff on the ground suddenly shot out a bolt of shadow magic. By the time his hand reached his waist, it had whipped up and was gripped tight. The man who'd come to grab the staff quickened his pace, but two steps weren't enough—

Jace raised the staff high and shouted the incantation for Fear. A strange, piercing sound rose and fell with his chanting. The bald man in front of him screamed, "Dark magic!"

The soldier nearest to him covered his eyes. The bald man's nostrils flared as if struck by some horrifying sight, his legs trembling. The men further away began to sob and wail…

Wails, screams — all of it was finally obliterated by a sudden flash of lightning…

When the white light faded, the bald man in front of him had his head smashed in, leaving only a charred body lying face-down on the ground. The nearest one, the axeman, had half his flesh blackened by the electricity. He howled and crawled sideways, but Grede snatched up a smoking broken sword and hurled it hard, driving it straight into his back.

In the distance, a man held a sword with both hands, trembling, pointed at Grede. His trousers were completely soaked, but he seemed to still have his senses. He screamed at Grede not to come closer. Grede strode toward him step by step, hammer already raised high.

Jace stood in place, but his legs felt weak.

What the…

He looked down and saw a crossbow bolt lodged in his lower abdomen, driven deep, with only half of it sticking out.

Translator Notes

Flags

- Title: 我们都是雇佣兵 — "We're All Mercenaries" — the bald mercenary's sneer makes it clear: out here, the line between hunter and hunted is razor-thin. - Vereesa (温蕾萨): Grede suggests calling for Vereesa Windrunner; Jace dismisses it — she's not on vacation, and another party member means splitting the reward. - Gilded Rose (镶金玫瑰): Jace recalls Vereesa's behavior at the inn, suspecting she may have deliberately come without money as a self-imposed trial. - Shadow Protection Potion (暗影防护药水): glossary term; Grede drinks it just before the ambush. - Chuck (查克): the jumpy mercenary who thinks he hears something. - Fear (恐惧术): Jace's first combat use of the warlock Fear spell — the incantation is implied rather than spelled out here, but the effect is devastating. - The crossbowman-mage: the cloaked figure carrying the light crossbow is implied to be a spellcaster (the imp sensed one approaching) — and he's the one who puts the bolt in Jace's gut. - Grede's lightning hammer: the dwarf's thunder-charged war hammer is the decisive first strike.
