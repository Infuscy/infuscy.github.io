## Chapter 226: Ambush

The four gnolls walked into the forest together, and the camp suddenly grew much quieter. Without the gnolls' whining and clamor, the remaining few fell silent, each going about their own business.

One of them, seeming bored, turned and walked into a tent. A chattering commotion came from inside — so there were more than just the six bastards on the surface; there were indeed some hiding in the tent.

Just then, a gnoll walked up to the campfire, holding a small burning torch, about to toss it into the fire...

With a boom, the fire ignited on its own!

The color of the flames was extremely unnatural, tinged with dark red, occasionally flickering with an ominous bright green, startling the three nearest gnolls.

"The party's started, you idiots! Look over here! There's something delicious right here!"

A bizarre shriek yanked every gnoll's attention over. A small burning flame zigzagged away from the grass. The nearest gnoll grabbed a crossbow from a small tent on its back and fired with a "shhh" — despite the quick reaction, the bolt still stuck in the mud far from that little ball of fire.

"Kill! Devil! Kill! Devil!"

Several gnolls started yelling. One wielding a long blade was the first to charge out. The gnoll ran with terrifying speed, its short legs churning as if trembling. The imp panicked, howling, "Help me, you two idiots! Come save me!"

A lightning-charged hammer smashed straight into the largest of the tents, blowing the entire structure apart with a thunderous crash. The gnolls inside, along with the one nearest to it who was picking up a spear, were sent to their deaths without even a cry.

The lightning howled as it flew back. Grede came charging in, caught the hammer, and smashed it down hard on a gnoll that had reacted in time — one blow shattering its shoulder and spine. The gnoll crumpled to the ground as if it had been snapped in half.

The gnoll archer up high quickly reloaded, taking aim at the dwarf who was charging after the gnoll swordsman — but was run through the throat from behind by a sword.

Jace kicked away the gnoll that was clutching its throat and struggling, drew his longsword, and ran it straight through another gnoll that had just picked up a spear.

Behind him, another gnoll swung an axe at him. Jace dodged it easily and kicked him to the side.

When the gnoll turned to swing again, its clothes and fur suddenly burst into flames. It panicked, yowling in confusion.

"Burn! Hahaha! What, you wanted to hit me? You wanted to kill me, you bastard!" came the imp's triumphant shout from afar.

Jace strode over in two steps. Seeing Jace running up, the burning gnoll tried to flee, but Jace cleaved his blade into the bastard's neck. To his surprise, the sword cleanly severed the gnoll's thick head from its body, cutting through the bone with remarkable ease!

He remembered how, back on Stonewatch, when he'd hacked at those gnolls several times his blade had gotten stuck in the bone. And the sword he'd taken from that mercenary wasn't much better than the standard-issue weapons at Stonewatch.

Having something to compare it against, Jace finally had a tangible sense of just how much his strength had increased.

Down the slope, the dwarf raised his lightning-wreathed war hammer against two gnolls fleeing southwest, slapping out the flames on their heads.

"Grede! Grede!"

Jace shouted at the top of his lungs, and Grede remembered what the plan was. The lightning slowly died down.

The dwarf turned around. "Sorry, almost forgot."

"Sazeno, get back here."

"Is the fight over?" the imp yelled as it ran back.

"It's over. Back in my pack."

"Ugh, finally I can talk freely! You two actually got hurt! I wanted to tell you earlier — those humans coming over had bad intentions! They smelled of blood — they've killed their own kind! But you wouldn't let me talk, damn it! Now I can finally say it — the one with the crossbow is the most experienced and the hardest to deal with, we should've killed him first! That crossbow guy even knows a little magic! And then the bald one, he's the leader! I think—"

"Shut up!"

"Mmph…"

Jace said, "Your latency is way too high. Why don't you start from Dalaran?"

The imp smacked its lips, glared at the dwarf, and climbed up Jace's cloak.

"Dared to call me an idiot," Grede said as he walked up the slope. "I was just thinking about letting the gnolls hack it to death before coming up."

Jace laughed twice and said, "Gotta admit, it's still pretty useful."

Grede nodded, looking at the gnoll on the ground still clutching its throat and writhing. "So now, we wait for their reinforcements? You think we should leave one alive here to yell and attract their attention? Otherwise if they're all dead here, those gnolls won't have any reason to come, right?"

"Kill them all," Jace said. "Don't forget — they can talk. If they come over and one of them suddenly yells 'ambush!' to warn the others, what then? So they all die. There's meat and weapons in this camp — just these things alone, they'd never give them up. Surviving in this forest, forty or fifty gnolls — food wouldn't be exactly plentiful."

"Will Hogger get scared off?" Grede asked. "I don't think that guy's the timid type."

"If he runs, fine. What I'm afraid of is him pointing out where we're hiding," Jace said. "I don't want to be dragged out from behind a tree for a second time."

He glanced at the gnoll on the ground that kept moaning, its throat already run through, and thought that if he'd learned Curse of Tongues, this problem would probably be solved.

Curse of Tongues was an extremely vicious curse — it could make the victim spew nothing but incomprehensible demonic, stripping them of the ability to channel magic through incantations. Applied to a gnoll, it would rob them of the ability to communicate, leaving them able to only yell incoherently.

So he really needed to visit the Slaughtered Lamb more often. He had to get his hands on every spell those bastards could dig up. The few spells Jace knew right now were just too limited.

Jace hefted his sword. "For now, just shut these gnolls up."

The two of them walked up the slope and stabbed each gnoll that could still move, one by one. Grede glanced around and asked, "How are we going to set up the ambush? The leaves on the trees are too sparse in winter — climbing up won't hide us."

Jace pointed to a fallen tree trunk to the north. "We'll hide behind that. Since those two gnolls ran southwest, their camp is probably by the riverbank over there. If we hide to the north we should be safe — they won't be able to come around behind us."

## Flags

- Jace's first real gnoll-hunting ambush — a coordinated three-way assault with Grede and Sazeno against a Riverpaw camp.
- Sazeno's combat utility confirmed: the imp's Fire spell draws aggro while Grede's lightning hammer delivers the killing blow.
- Jace notices a dramatic increase in his physical strength — a gnoll's head cleanly severed, something that would have been impossible back on Stonewatch.
- The gnolls speak Common (or a dialect of it) — a detail that unsettles Jace and raises questions about their intelligence.
- Sazeno's "high latency" — the imp notices the approaching humans' blood scent before the fight but is forbidden from speaking, a running gag about imp communication delays.
- Jace decides to kill all the gnolls rather than leave a lure, reasoning that gnolls can talk and might warn reinforcements — a tactical call that shows his growing pragmatism.
- Foreshadowing: Jace wants to learn Curse of Tongues from the Slaughtered Lamb warlocks to prevent enemies from coordinating.
