Chapter 227: Is This Supposed to Be Here?

On the slope above the clearing, the campfire was nearly spent. Without the flicker of shadow or fel energy, the flames danced ever more weakly.

The nights in Elwynn Forest were freezing. Jace leaned against the fallen log, waiting. He wasn't the least bit drowsy, nor did he feel the slightest hint of hunger — just listening intently to the sounds behind him, focused for a full one or two hours without relaxing.

He knew Grede was the same. Ever since that run-in with Hogger on the grain-wagon road, the dwarf had been itching for payback. Hogger, like that orc shaman, was on his death list — so the normally heavy sleeper kept his eyes wide open, peering through the cracks in the wood toward the camp.

Just as Jace was considering for the third time whether Hogger might not show up at all, Grede turned his head and glanced at him. One look at the dwarf's eyes and Jace knew.

A familiar, rumbling panting sound grew gradually clearer from the distance, drowning out all the white noise of the forest.

Jace still remembered that sound to this day — the day he'd run into that towering monster in the rain. That strength and that sense of overwhelming pressure had stunned him at the time.

Ever since then, he'd stopped wanting to think about that "Hogger the newbie killer" meme. This monster had brought far too much disaster to Elwynn Forest.

The soldiers garrisoning Westfall Keep, the residents, the travelers and merchants maimed or killed, the wounded he'd seen that day in Goldshire… and especially that Stormwind guard, the one with blood streaming from inside his helmet, the one who'd held the shield against Hogger and taken everything for Jace and Grede — Jace still remembered him.

What had become of him now? Dead? Paralyzed? Or was he still holding the line on Stormwind's front lines?

For some reason, Jace suddenly felt a strong urge to go see that man.

If not for the way he'd held that shield against Hogger for so long, the two of them might have ended their journey right there on the roadside.

Thinking this, he gripped his staff tighter.

Today, it ends.

"Damn it, quick, look," Grede whispered.

Jace found a gap and peered through. The first thing he saw was that towering monster who looked nothing like an ordinary gnoll.

Hogger… right there.

He stood like a staggering, lame giant, propped up by a ring of short gnoll guards. Jace made a rough estimate — he was probably quite a bit taller than an orc.

And his guards were armed with long blades, spears, and small hatchets, an assortment of weapons. What caught Jace's attention most was a small gnoll holding a crooked long staff, draped in ragged cloth robes — a caster?

But what about all this was worth Grede being so surprised?

Thinking that, Jace noticed that all the gnolls were looking in the same direction — the camp up on the slope.

In front of the dying campfire, a small gnoll was hunched over, doing something Jace couldn't make out. Two other gnolls were dragging the bodies in the camp into a pile in front of the small one.

Some kind of ritual?

No.

Jace felt an uncomfortable sensation permeating the air. It wasn't something he could smell with his nose — it was his keen sensitivity as a spellcaster to the energies around him.

The only thing he could associate that feeling with was one other time… that night, in Dalaran.

The streets had been saturated with necrotic energy. The shadows had seemed to be full of the living dead, everywhere!

That night's aura had been stronger, more direct. Tonight's was far more subtle, far more chaotic — but it made Jace's heart race all the more… because this was Elwynn Forest, not the great cemetery in Duskwood, or some other already-cursed place.

That gnoll was performing a necromantic ritual?!

Was it trying to raise those corpses?!

"You going or not?" Grede asked softly.

"Wait," Jace said.

He took a breath and calmly recited the Mage Armor incantation. After finishing it smoothly and steadily, a shimmering arcane glow enveloped him…

Once the spell was cast, he poked his head out again and glanced over, confirming that all the gnolls were focused on the necromancer and hadn't noticed the magical glow from his direction.

"You've been practicing on your own? Didn't blow a bunch of stuff up this time…" Grede marveled.

"Of course. I was wasting mana before," Jace said, gulping down a mana potion and finishing off a Shadow Protection Potion of his own. "Remember the potions, Grede. We take out the necromancer first — I'll hold off Hogger and his guards!"

"Got it."

The dwarf pulled out his last Shadow Protection Potion and downed it in one go.

"Sazeno," Jace said. "Cover me and Grede, just like that day on Stonewatch!"

The imp poked its head out from behind Jace. He could feel it clutching the edge of the warlock's pack, nearly tearing it apart.

The night wind seemed to sense something, surging and howling through the trees. Grede climbed up on the log and leaped out, charging through the underbrush at a terrifying speed for a short fellow.

Jace sprinted out too, aiming for the towering Hogger. Of course he couldn't match the dwarf's burst of speed — his cloak and robe billowed in the wind, his body's arcane glow blazing conspicuously in the night. Staff in hand, he immediately drew every gnoll's attention…

"Hogger… Hogger! Stop him! Stop him!"

Hogger roared in his gravelly voice, and the gnolls all turned to look at Jace.

Bring it, he thought. One Fear spell and this is over.

Grede was nearly on top of where the necromancer stood…

Wait — Hogger wasn't even looking at him!

The monster dragged a long spiked club and took two enormous strides in Grede's direction, seemingly trying to stop him from reaching the necromancer!

Just as Grede's war hammer was about to erupt with lightning, Hogger's massive club was already swinging in front of him!

Grede raised his shield and barely blocked the blow. With a tremendous clang, Grede was driven back more than two steps and slammed flat into the grass — but Jace had no time to worry about him…

Jace had intended to hit Hogger with a Curse of Weakness to slow him down, but the moment he raised his staff, his vision was blocked by several gnolls. The fastest one shrieked and closed in, its sharp spear tip already at Jace's face!

Jace simply slapped the Curse of Weakness straight onto the face of this charging lunatic. The bastard actually only stumbled two steps before lunging again — but Jace used his staff in his left hand to knock the spear away and drove his sword into its mouth with his right.

The thrust was clean and decisive, making the gnoll shudder and collapse on the spot — but the bastard had clamped down hard on the blade. Jace yanked, but couldn't pull it all the way out.

Behind him, a gnoll raised a spear and howled as it closed in, the long spear already thrusting forward!

Jace had no choice but to let go and dodge the thrust. The off-balance gnoll suddenly burst into flames all over its body. It tried to turn and thrust again, but panicked and howled — Jace drove his staff through him, sending him rolling on the ground.

In that gap, he glanced toward the other side.

Hogger was gripping his long spiked club in both hands and smashing it into the ground over and over. The tall grass obscured the view, but since the bastard was still wildly swinging, it meant Grede was at least still holding on.

"Sazeno, go help the dwarf! Quick! I don't need help!"

The imp yelled once and darted over.

The plan had completely fallen apart. Hogger had been drawn to that side, the necromancer was continuing its ritual undisturbed, and Jace was already surrounded by several gnoll guards.

Had to end this fast…

He extended his staff toward two more gnolls about to close in from the other side, backed up a few steps to create some distance, and prepared to cast a Fear spell to control the situation — when he suddenly heard a rushing wind behind him…

A burning heat grazed his ear. Jace instinctively tilted his head to the side as a fireball slammed straight into his back!

It felt like taking a bare-knuckle punch. Jace staggered two steps and nearly fell. The arcane glow all over his body surged and rolled wildly — but with the cloak and Mage Armor protecting him, he didn't even feel much pain!

Damn it… he'd forgotten about that short caster.

Seeing Jace knocked off balance by the fireball, one gnoll reacted with lightning speed, seizing the chance to charge in with a hatchet — but Jace turned and smashed his staff into its face, killing it on the spot.

Jace heard chanting not far away. Then let's see who's faster. He shouted the Fear incantation. As the shadow gem at the head of his staff flared with light, another gnoll hit by the Fear spell wasn't frightened or driven to flee — instead it shrieked and waved its spear, charging straight at him.

Jace dodged one swing, closed in, and smashed the staff into its stubby legs, then gripped the staff with both hands and brought it down hard on its head. He heard a crisp crack, and the gnoll vomited a stream of yellow fluid before collapsing face-first.

The other gnoll guards were either down or had fled who-knew-where. Only then did he notice the caster clutching a small short staff, sitting on the ground and wailing helplessly. Jace picked up a spear, charged forward, and ran it through the thing's chest with a running thrust. With the immediate threat dealt with, Jace yanked hard and pulled free the sword that had been clamped in the gnoll's teeth, then ran up the hill toward the necromancer.

He could see one of the piled-up bodies already standing up, charging toward where Grede and Hogger were locked in a death struggle — then a second one… if he didn't stop them, they'd have to deal with six or seven more undead gnolls!

He released a single-target Fear spell from more than ten meters away, trying to interrupt the ritual — but the necromancer was completely unfazed! What? Not enough distance? That's impossible…

Then the necromancer turned around. Its eyes glowed with a dull yellow necrotic light.

This thing was undead!
