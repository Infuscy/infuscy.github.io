## Chapter 228: More Tricks Up Its Sleeve?

"Fear? Fear delights me… mage! Hee hee…"

The words that came out of the gnoll's mouth were nothing like something a brainless monster could say. It let out a shrill, cackling laugh, and its crooked wooden staff began to gather shadow energy…

Shadow Bolt!

A beam of black shadow came streaking straight at him!

The bastard hadn't even spoken an incantation. Being undead, manipulating shadow was probably as instinctive as breathing.

At such close range, Jace barely managed to throw up his cloak as a shield. A cloud of shadow mist exploded across his skin and began to itch.

Without the triple protection of the Shadow Protection Potion, Mage Armor, and his cloak, what would that Shadow Bolt have felt like hitting him?

"Mage Armor — how long will that hold you up?"

The gnoll necromancer laughed even more wildly. It guided another Shadow Bolt with its staff, but this time Jace dodged it easily.

Seeing another Shadow Bolt streaking toward him, unable to dodge in time, he raised his staff and shattered it head-on. The shadow energy met Hakim's staff and was swallowed whole, vanishing into the tattered, dark-yellow wrappings.

That shadow gem… the necromancer was taken aback, and even Jace was a little surprised — but the other immediately began casting another spell. Jace heard it clearly and found it very familiar, something like Curse of Agony.

What did I brew that shadow potion for… if not for exactly this!

Finally it's being put to proper use!

Jace closed the distance in quick strides. The moment the other finished the incantation, he felt as if ants were crawling all over his body — but if the Shadow Protection Potion hadn't been working, it wouldn't have been as simple as a skin itch.

Even Sulenna, who never studied seriously, had been able to torment him half to death with her Curse of Agony — let alone this undead gnoll mage!

The curse didn't slow Jace down one bit. He charged right up to the gnoll and thrust his staff forward — but the bastard ducked its head with sharp reflexes and dodged!

It was still a gnoll, after all!

The necromancer pulled out a slender dagger and stabbed viciously at Jace's waist. Jace tried to dodge — the dagger's tip grazed his cloak, leaving a white slash across it… which felt even more critical-hit-worthy than having his gut opened up.

But Jace only flashed a wince through his mind — his reaction didn't lag one bit. As he dodged, his other hand drove his sword hard into the thing's cheek. Jace put his full strength into the thrust, nearly piercing clean through the gnoll's massive skull.

The necromancer shuddered twice, clutched the sword hilt sticking out of its head, and backed up two steps. "I am eternal… I am blessed…" Jace swung the staff with both hands and brought it down with full force, smashing its skull to pieces. The necromancer's skin rapidly turned black and toppled to the ground like a heap of black dung, giving off a foul stench that stirred up unpleasant memories.

"Come help, master! That dwarf's done for! I'm out of mana!"

Hearing the imp's shout, Jace shuddered and looked down. Hogger was gripping his long spiked club in one hand and attacking wildly — his other arm was nothing but a stump.

Beside Hogger, a reanimated gnoll was looking for an opening to attack the barely-standing Grede. On the ground were already piled three or four corpses, some burned, some smashed to a pulp.

Grede used his small shield to deflect the club again and again — that shield already looked deformed.

"Damn it… nobody told me I'd be fighting corpses! Hurry up and help, Jace! Damn it, you were supposed to hold Hogger off!" he complained as he backed up.

Coming, coming… Jace muttered in his mind, drawing his sword and running down the slope. As he ran, he raised his staff, preparing to cast Curse of Weakness on Hogger again…

Once it was done, he felt his shadow magic land on something soft and yielding — not the solid, sure sensation he got when cursing anyone else, like the summoned power had found its footing.

Sure enough, Hogger, who should have been weakened by the curse, only lifted his club slightly, adjusted his rhythm, and continued his frenzied assault.

Did this bastard have some kind of extra shadow resistance? Was it because that necromancer had cast some kind of Shadow Protection ward on him earlier?

The brief moment Hogger spent adjusting seemed to give Grede a rhythm. When the gnoll struck again, he found an opening and dodged to the side. The club smashed into the ground, leaving a dent — the gnoll leader had actually smashed his own club clean in half!

But Hogger wasn't flustered in the slightest. He drew a cleaver from behind his back with his remaining hand and pressed the attack. And because of the curse he'd just cast, the reanimated gnoll that had been helping Hogger attack Grede turned around, noticing Jace over here.

"Sss… ha!"

"The undead…"

The corpse gripped a long blade and a short blade and advanced at an angle, its eyes swaying with necrotic light like two floating will-o'-wisps.

Against this kind of reanimated lunatic, Curse of Weakness and Fear were obviously useless.

Liyousa!

A burst of arcane light flared between the undead and the warlock. The gnoll hesitated for a moment — and a longsword was already driven diagonally into its forehead.

This bastard definitely never fought a mage in life.

Jace pulled his sword free and pressed forward, raising his staff high to cast Curse of Weakness on Hogger once more!

This time it seemed to work. Hogger ground his teeth after being cursed, and his hind legs buckled slightly under his massive body — but he didn't fall. Grede caught his breath, and his war hammer seized the opportunity to gather lightning once more.

Jace could feel that even summoning lightning required energy to maintain. And although the elements answered Grede's call, Grede, who rarely trained his own willpower, was relying purely on his sheer determination to rouse the lightning again and again.

But even so, by now he was starting to struggle.

Still, Grede took advantage of Hogger's buckling legs and swung his hammer upward in a rising blow that smashed into Hogger's chest!

Hogger let out a muffled grunt and toppled backward, a massive gush of pus-blood pouring from his mouth — but in the end he still staggered a few steps and braced himself with his back legs.

The bastard really had no idea how he'd grown to be this tough. He just kept taking hits.

Jace ran up and drove his sword into the back of Hogger's neck, then pulled it outward diagonally with force, dragging out a huge gush of blood. Hogger struggled to dodge to the side, clutching the stab wound on his neck, muttering something.

"No one… can kill Hogger!"

Saying that, he suddenly hurled a black, round object over. The dwarf's eyes went wide and he bellowed at the top of his lungs: "Goddamn bomb! Down!"

The moment Jace heard it, he dodged to the side. Grede too scrambled and rolled away. A thunderous blast sent both of them flying. Jace rolled several times before coughing and climbing to his feet. The crystal-like Mage Armor on his body, after being blasted, displayed an especially vivid luster. Despite being blown back a considerable distance, Jace actually didn't feel much pain.

This really was high-level magic… the effect was way too obvious. It wasn't like the game where you just added a bit of resistance and reduced damage by eight or ten percent and called it a day.

He climbed to his feet and looked into the clearing smoke. "Grede?"

"The big one's running! The big one's running!" the imp shrieked from not far away.

"Where'd he get a bomb, damn it!" Grede muttered. "He absolutely cannot get away!"

Jace said, "Then we chase!"

## Flags

- The undead gnoll necromancer speaks with intelligence and mocks Jace — far from a mindless monster, it's a genuinely dangerous caster.
- Hakim's staff (the "Blood Demon's staff") reveals a new property: it absorbs shadow energy, shattering a Shadow Bolt on contact — both Jace and the necromancer are caught off guard.
- Shadow Protection Potion proves critical again — the necromancer's Curse of Agony is reduced to mild itching, validating Jace's potion-brewing efforts.
- Hogger shows unexpected shadow resistance — Curse of Weakness barely affects him, likely from a ward placed by the necromancer.
- Grede's shield is deformed from blocking Hogger's club — the dwarf is being pushed to his physical limits.
- Hogger fights with one arm and still smashes his own club through the ground, then calmly switches to a cleaver — his combat instincts are terrifying.
- Hogger throws a bomb — a shocking and desperate tactic that catches both Jace and Grede off guard.
- Mage Armor's real-world effectiveness is dramatically demonstrated: the explosion sends Jace rolling but causes almost nothing more than a bruise, far beyond game-mechanic expectations.
- Hogger flees into the dark forest — the hunt continues.
