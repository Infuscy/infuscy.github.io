Chapter 241: Trading What's Most Precious

Where had the demon gone?

The space before him was empty, quiet as though nothing had happened at all.

Jace poked under the bed with his staff, then got up and flipped back the covers, glanced at the cracks along the wall. Five square meters — where could she possibly hide?

Succubi could Phase Shift… he'd almost forgotten.

So was the succubus right in front of him right now, or… behind him?

Just as his gaze darted to the side, he felt a pair of arms wrap around his ribs from behind.

Jace was about to throw another elbow, but the succubus behind him whispered, "Don't hit me, darling. I don't want your soul. I just want you to… love me. Is that so hard?"

That humble, pleading hit Jace harder than any blow — it kept his elbow from coming down at all.

Since coming into this world, not a single girl had ever spoken to him like that.

But this was a real, bona fide demon.

"Let go."

"As you wish." The succubus slowly slid her arms away. Jace could feel her deliberately brush against his calf.

"Even just trying it would do. Just a little." She smiled.

Jace looked into the demon's arcane-infused eyes and said, "Falling in and out of love just like that out of nowhere — don't you find that boring?"

The succubus came around in front of him and looked at Jace. "Then what kind of love do you want?"

"Do you have a name? Let's start with your name."

"I am Morophil of the City of Boundless Desire," the succubus said. "If you'd like to give me a name, I don't mind. I wouldn't mind having one more."

So that's what "boundless" meant. An imp wouldn't possibly know a demonic word it didn't recognize, which meant whoever wrote that note had left a word out? But it really was some unknown corner of the Twisting Nether.

"I mean your true name," Jace said.

"Fine. Must I say it?"

Morophil asked, a hint of hesitation in her voice.

"That's the precondition for me to keep dealing with you. Without that, nothing doing."

"Then… let me think about it for two days."

"Think about it? My last minion wasn't this much trouble." Jace picked up the slip of paper with the banishment incantation and said, "If I send you back and summon another succubus, would it still be you?"

"The odds are slim," Morophil said.

"I don't like disobedient minions, Morophil. I'm sorry, but you've got to go!" Jace resolutely began chanting the spell — but Morophil suddenly pressed that small, cold hand of hers over his mouth.

"Don't. I'll tell you."

"Why the change of heart?"

"I think you're… not bad. And I like it here."

"You're missing the point," Jace said.

"Then let me say it again. I think you're exactly my type, how about that? I've chosen you, my master." Morophil said softly.

"Your true name," Jace reminded her coldly.

"Sigh." Morophil gave a light sigh and leaned close to Jace's ear. Finally, beneath the cloying sweet scent that filled the entire room, he caught a whiff of the fishy tang unique to demons.

"But my true name is everything I am. If you want it, you ought to give up something you treasure at least…"

As she spoke, Jace felt a small hand suddenly undo his belt. He shoved Morophil away hard, and the succubus gave a startled cry.

"I'm not in that much of a hurry to die," Jace said firmly. "Tell me your true name. Now."

At that, Morophil smiled and lowered her head. "Ah, so you thought being with me would kill you? You're quite cute." The question left Jace's mouth dry, his heart hammering as if it was about to burst out of his chest.

In that moment, he could no longer see the monster before him as just some demon from the Twisting Nether. Just a few exchanges and he felt his defenses about to collapse.

After all, in eighteen years of life he'd never so much as touched a woman. Right now, just the sheer flood of physical reactions all over his body was nearly strangling him.

And it wasn't just the physical reactions. The shadow power within him surged out of control, clawing at every inch of skin and organ, as if trying to transform him into a complete lunatic on the spot.

Jace took a deep breath and at least forced that shadow impulse down. He had to keep that much control.

In a daze, Morophil had already lowered herself in front of Jace.

She looked up and said, "I bet… you like my lips."

"Wait… slow, slow down."

Jace looked down and saw the two grisly broken bones jutting from her shoulders… but below them was a back and hips enough to make any mortal lose his mind.

He'd just lifted his eyes, trying not to look at her body, when he felt himself give a gentle push, leaning powerless against the wall. Then something wet and ice-cold enveloped his consciousness.

He tried to look out the window to calm himself, but the demon's swaying tail kept his gaze from focusing… He tried to grab his staff, as if it would give him a bit more control, but the feel of the staff's surface had become ice-cold and alien beyond all recognition, impossible to grip no matter what.

His hands tried to seize on anything they could, but it felt like being pulled into the water, losing every point of leverage. A sour, swelling sensation tugged at him in waves, over and over, making his head throb.

In the end, he closed his eyes.

If he died, he died… damn it.

In his panic, he managed to grab the succubus's swaying horns. He heard Morophil chuckle softly, and the next second he suddenly felt as if someone was ripping his soul right out of him.

Not that he had any idea what having your soul ripped out felt like…

Before that seed of worry could take shape, his consciousness felt as if it were being lifted bit by bit by an invisible dark-purple hook, pulled higher and higher.

Gradually, every thought and notion was stretched, disintegrated, and scattered by that pulling force. Any trace of reason became a luxury.

Soon, his groin grew hotter and hotter, then his whole body turned cold. His hands couldn't help but clench tight, his breathing coming in ragged gasps, gasps.

After a while, Jace opened his eyes again. Before him was the cold windowpane. He wasn't dead.

A strange thought crossed his mind: if this demon were to stab him through with those horns right now, it might almost be worth it.

Morophil braced her hands on his shoulders and crawled up bit by bit to his ear, murmuring softly.

"In exchange — Mollesphilissaritor. My true name. And a little secret: the only other living being in the entire Twisting Nether who knows that name is you."

When she spoke her true name, Jace felt her voice resonate like an ethereal chime from the void itself, searing itself deep into his mind.

Amid the reverberation of that true name, the demon gently bit his earlobe with her fangs, licked her lips, and said, almost as an exhale, "Ah… the master's taste. I feel like I came out ahead."
