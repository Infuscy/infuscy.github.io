Chapter 242: There Have to Be Rules

Jace leaned against the cold wall as Morophil licked her fingers, the back of her hand, and her lips without seeming to have her fill.

Once he'd calmed down, the monster pressed against him looked truly terrifying.

Her shoulders and tail were covered in spikes and barbs. Every time she opened her mouth, those two fangs showed. To think he'd actually been taken by a demon like this…

Then again, a demon's true name held real power. Speaking it was nothing like speaking a false name — it was like casting a spell, and it cost enormous energy, exactly as the succubus had just demonstrated.

So since the name was real, everything was fine.

He swallowed and asked, "That's it, then? You're my minion now?"

The succubus pressed against his neck. "I've told you everything. We've already forged the master-servant bond… what, you're not satisfied yet?"

"I thought we'd have to fight at least," Jace said, glancing at her sidelong. "And then I'd conquer you with my strength or something."

Morophil cocked the corner of her mouth. "I prefer other aspects of you to your strength… though if you really want to prove yourself physically, there's still a way."

"What?!"

"Ah… stop!" Jace felt as if his lower body had been hit with a jolt of lightning — the succubus's hand had suddenly squeezed—

He scrambled to block Morophil's movements, but the succubus pinned him to the wall with her arms, smiling as she kept her hand clenched tight, until Jace fought back with all his might, seized the succubus by the neck, and slammed her hard back onto the bed.

The succubus hit the mattress and lay on her back looking up at Jace, black hair splayed across the bed.

She showed a sliver of a smile. "Master, you're so strong. So the reason you didn't fight back earlier is that you were enjoying it too, right?"

"I'm warning you, don't do that…" Jace said, but his vision was already darkening, his whole body going soft, his lower half throbbing with pain in waves.

Then he felt a hot flush in his chest. He looked down and saw that in the earlier panic, the spikes on the succubus's body had scraped open the skin in several places. Blood was running from his arms, his waist, his chest — he looked as if he'd just gone a round with a wild cat.

But compared to the stimulation and pain of everything the succubus had just put him through… these hurts didn't even register.

Morophil dipped a finger in his blood and asked teasingly, "May I have a taste?"

"Whatever." Jace caught his breath for a while, looked over his wounds, confirmed they were all flesh cuts, and said, "Since you're my minion now, shouldn't we set some rules?"

The demon sucked on her finger, eyes narrowing. "Go ahead. I'm listening."

"Most basic one: stay in this room. You are not to step outside for any reason without my permission. If anyone comes near, you turn invisible… even if it's a human."

"Ah, the master wants to lock me in this little cage, enjoy me in private, and let no one know…" Morophil whispered.

Jace sighed. "This is my home, and outside are mages and priests who hunt demons without mercy. For your own sake — so you don't get tortured to death by the Holy Light — you'd better behave."

"Hunt? Hunt me?" The succubus chuckled. "Though being tortured by the Holy Light… that sounds rather interesting. Do the priests here enjoy tormenting people too? Can you summon the Holy Light as well, master?"

She gripped Jace's wrist. "I'd love to try."

Jace couldn't tell if she was being sarcastic or actually meant it. He just asked, "Are you trying to get me dead out of curiosity?"

"All right, stop threatening me with your own life. I'll agree to all of it." She locked eyes with Jace. "But in return… shouldn't you agree to some conditions of mine as well, as an exchange?"

Jace winced uncomfortably. "Uh, what?"

"For starters, address me as Master Morophil."

"Master Morophil."

"Good. Now, quickly, lick me…"

"Yes, Master Morophil." Jace pressed close to Morophil's chest and obediently licked her smooth skin.

"Lower…"

"Yes, Master Morophil."

"Lower, lower, quick, keep going… ha… ah… yes…"

Jace surged up, pinned the succubus's arms, and growled in a low voice: "The most important rule — no more charm spells on me!"

"Fine! Fine…" Morophil giggled for a while, then said, "Now that I think of it, you snap out of it awfully fast every time. Stronger than I expected. Is that the reason you were able to summon me here in the first place?"

"What do you mean?" Jace wiped his mouth. "Is there some special condition to summoning you?"

The demon's eyes slid toward the staff beside them, and suddenly her tail hooked it straight over.

"What are you doing?!" Jace snatched the staff out of the air. Waves of shadow light pulsed from the gem on the staff.

Morophil gave a soft cry of surprise, her eyes fixed on the gem until its glow dimmed.

Only now did Jace remember: he'd summoned this succubus using Taron's staff — no, his own staff. By rights, it should have been a stronger subspecies, just like the imp.

But the Morophil before him didn't seem any stronger except for the missing wings. Not only did she lack the wings a normal succubus had — she didn't have a whip either. Without a whip, how was she supposed to fight?

"You took the Eredar's long staff," Morophil said. "I knew it. I never would have been summoned directly by a weak human otherwise."

Jace set the staff back in its place and touched the scrape on his arm. "What, so this 'weak human' of mine is making you regret it?"

Morophil said, "Clearly, what I mean is that you're not weak at all… so one day I will win your heart, just as you promised me."

"You think I could develop feelings for a demon?"

"I can fall in love with a human. Why can't you fall in love with me?"

"You really in love with me?" Jace looked down at her. "That was just part of your charm spell a moment ago."

The succubus laughed. "Didn't you say you don't like straightforward, boring love? Then I'll give you something unfathomable. Do you like that?"

"Hah." Jace said, "Right now you're straightforwardly conning me."

Morophil asked, "If I had no feelings for you, why would I have given you my true name?"

Jace said, "Because that was part of the deal. But feel however you like — you think I care? You're mine now."

The succubus snorted and turned her head away. Seeing that she'd gone silent, Jace figured it was time to clean up the rest of the mess.

The lamb was still lying on the ground. If not for the cloying sweet scent coming off the succubus, the whole room would have been thick with the smell of blood by now. Good thing it wasn't an old goat — it didn't have much of a mutton stench yet.

He carried the goat out and found an old man fishing by the canal who was willing to take it home and dress it, and got a handful of coppers for it. Honestly, he didn't even care about the money anymore — as long as someone would take it away quickly.

By now Jace felt completely drained. Logically, a young lamb shouldn't have tired him out this much over a few hundred meters.

He didn't know if it was the strain of all that summoning and casting, or if the succubus had done something to him that he hadn't noticed yet.

Jace squatted by the canal, washing the blood from his wounds. After squatting until his legs went numb, he looked up and caught his breath.

Staring at the shadow of the Mage Quarter's tall towers in the distance, he thought about how he ought to explain this succubus-summoning incantation to the people at the Slaughtered Lamb.

Telling them within the next couple of days definitely wouldn't do. He'd have to wait at least a while — otherwise Galders would think he was some terrifying dark magic prodigy. And if it came too easily, nobody would take his results seriously.

Most of all, Jace really didn't want those orgy enthusiasts summoning another succubus of their own.

Letting another succubus into that basement — how chaotic would that get? Then again, no matter how chaotic it got, it couldn't be worse than his own rented room right now.

He got home, opened the door, and looked at Morophil. "I'm going to sleep!"

He lay down on the bed. The succubus leaned close to his ear. "So do you want a good dream, or a nightmare?"

"I don't want any dream at all."

"Then shall I help you fall asleep?"

"You… how?"
