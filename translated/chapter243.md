## Chapter 243: The Importance of Mana

"Jace Sesso?" Caro Dushan waved a hand in front of his face. "What's wrong? Did a death knight snag your soul in Dalaran?"

"Death knight?!"

Jace jolted back to his senses, remembering that he was in the middle of an Orcish lesson.

"No — I mean, I zoned out. I'm sorry."

Dushan nodded. "It's the first week of the new term. A lot of the younger students have the same problem. Try translating this word for me."

Jace looked at the Orcish rune Dushan had written. "Gromm. Giant."

"Good." Dushan's tone was flat. "I suppose Mr. Sesso didn't neglect his studies even over the break."

Jace breathed a quiet sigh of relief and stole a glance at the other students, making sure no one was giving him strange looks.

Only after class today had he learned that quite a few people here knew he'd been right there on the street during the death knight attack on Dalaran. He was basically a celebrity in the Wizard Sanctum now.

After causing that much trouble, it'd be stranger if he weren't famous, honestly.

But the reason he'd been zoning out wasn't because of all the attention — it was because of Morophil. Last night he hadn't reacted much at all, and then suddenly he'd fallen asleep, like he'd been put under by an anesthetist before surgery. What kind of sleeping spell was that?

Thankfully he'd woken up early. When he left the house, he'd checked the scent on his body several times and made sure there was no trace of that sweet smell before heading to the Mage Quarter.

Just thinking about having to face that succubus again tonight gave him a feeling he couldn't quite name.

The one feeling he could name was the one downstairs.

Reciting how, for that moment last night, Morophil had driven him half to death — like that little lamb, at someone's mercy, utterly powerless to fight back…

"Mr. Jace Sesso!"

"Uh, ma'am?"

The Draconic teacher pointed at the arcane glyph suspended in the air. "Sound this character out for me. Here's a hint — it's related to the element of life."

"I… I don't know."

"This is our very first introductory Draconic lesson, Mr. Sesso. I know the Dalaran incident may have affected you deeply, but now that you've returned to Stormwind, to your classes, shouldn't you be moving forward?"

"It has nothing to do with Dalaran, ma'am. I just zoned out. Sorry."

The female mage nodded. "This character is called Reithosotho. It generally appears in restorative magic or life magic. The Kirin Tor believes we can learn from the botanists of Quel'Thalas and push the application of magic further into agriculture. As a matter of fact, from what I know, before the war, some of the more radical measures had already been put into practice in the northern kingdoms — in places like Lordaeron and Arathi…"

That afternoon, during the Inscription class, Jace was stumped by the very first lesson — inscribing runes with enchanting force.

Because he'd discovered that he couldn't summon arcane energy at all. No matter how he recited the incantation the teacher gave him, no matter how he focused his mind, all that awaited him was the hollow feeling of being on the verge of burnout. When he pushed himself too hard, he even got that dizzy spell he'd had the time his Mage Armor had failed to cast…

He had to give up on forcing it. If he kept this up and passed out in class, the attention he'd draw would be far worse than underperforming right now.

After class, the Inscription professor called him outside. "Mr. Sesso, have you considered that you might not be well-suited for this course? In fact, a mage doesn't necessarily have to take part in activities that use the arcane frequently. Recording spells, maintaining arcane implements — these are all extremely important work."

"Absolutely not my normal level, Master," Jace said. "I'll improve a lot by next week. I'm truly sorry."

The professor nodded. "Since you have confidence, then I look forward to your performance going forward."

Watching the professor leave, Jace had a pretty good idea what was going on.

That damn succubus had definitely drained his mana last night, and he still hadn't recovered much of it. She'd probably sucked him dry right down to the bottom.

After class, Jace went straight to the Alchemist's Need and bought two mana potions. He was still planning to go outside the city tonight to reproduce that shadow fire he'd managed to conjure at Marin's place — he couldn't let a succubus derail his plans.

Wait — how about using her as a target?

If she was a demon from the Twisting Nether, her fire resistance ought to be pretty high, right?

When he got home in the evening, he saw the succubus sitting at the table writing something, her tail swaying back and forth.

Just seeing the outline of her back, Jace felt that familiar stirring again.

What a weak-willed bastard…

But what was she writing? She wasn't copying books for the imp, was she?

Wait — where was the imp?

Jace hadn't even introduced the imp to the succubus yet. How were they already helping each other out?

"What are you doing?"

The succubus turned her head and gave a wicked smile. "That unforgettable first-meeting experience last night absolutely had to be recorded properly."

"You keep a diary?" What kind of demon was this, seriously…

Jace leaned in and caught a glimpse of what she'd written. It was actually in Common. But he'd only read a line before his brain rang with a dull buzz.

"When the hell did I drink your pee?! Does a demon even have pee?!" He crumpled the paper into a ball, wanted to throw it straight out the window but didn't dare, and stuffed it into the stove. "These papers are useful. I bought them with money I scraped together. And you're using them to write some hardcore erotica?!"

Morophil looked at Jace innocently. "Why so angry? Can't I have a little fun when I'm bored?"

"Why am I angry? Did you drain me dry last night?" Jace demanded. "I couldn't cast a single spell in magic class today!"

"My survival requires mana to sustain," the succubus explained in a serious tone. "Without mana, I would die very quickly in this plane and return to the Twisting Nether to wander endlessly… Do you want to see me die?"

For a moment Jace couldn't even tell if she was being real or not.

He stared at the succubus for a while, then said, "Fine. Let me ask you — how long can you stay invisible?"

"That also depends on how much mana I have. Or rather… on how much mana you have." Morophil stared at Jace's lower half, her hoof tapping lightly against the table leg as she sat on the table.

Jace swallowed. "If you come out with me tonight, can you follow me invisible all the way outside the city? And back again?"

"You want me to go out with you?!" Morophil jumped down. "Then I… I can stay invisible until you die of wanting—"

"Can you be honest for once?" Jace asked.

"Really, Phase Shift is no burden to me at all, master." Morophil narrowed her eyes. "Have you ever summoned some useless… who couldn't even Phase Shift at will?"

"Ahem." Jace said. "Then turn invisible and show me how good you are."

The succubus vanished from before Jace's eyes in an instant. There wasn't even a visible arcane flash. If he were the version of himself from before he'd ever studied magic, he probably wouldn't have been able to catch anything at all.

Jace looked around and couldn't find a trace of the succubus. How was he supposed to confirm where she was?

Right — could the imp see her?

"Sazeno, come out and take a look."

"Is the master calling me?" The imp poked its head out. "Hey? Where did that demon go?"

"You know about her?"

"Of course I know! I could smell her stink from inside!" The imp curled its lip. "That kind of demon is ruthless to the extreme… you mustn't listen to a word she says, you mustn't believe her. But since she's not here, I'm guessing you sent her back to the Twisting Nether? Hah, that bitch ultimately couldn't seduce my great master. She simply doesn't understand—"

"So it was you." The succubus suddenly appeared behind Jace and reached out. The imp screamed and its body flashed with light, but before it could vanish, the whole of its tail was seized and dragged out.

"No wonder the room always felt so weird, hmm?"

"Ahh! I don't want to die… don't drag me away! Noble succubus, honest, beautiful, and elegant lady, spare me, I was just running my mouth—"

At that, the imp actually started convulsing from fear, babbling incoherently. Wasn't this little guy supposed to fear nothing?

"Master, why do you keep a thing like this?" Morophil clicked her tongue. "How about I help you dispose of it? I'm quite good at that…"

"No." Jace grabbed her arm and looked at the imp, which had twitched itself into unconsciousness. "It's helped me a lot. Put it back in the bag."

The succubus stared at Jace in disbelief. "You actually need its help? You have me — you still need this useless thing?"

"You see those papers?" Jace pointed at the stacks upon stacks of paper under the table.

"Uh." Morophil hesitated. "I see them. You use those papers to study demonic?"

"Those are all translations the imp copied out for me. There are still some it hasn't finished copying. You kill it, and you can do the work instead. I saw you're quite fond of creative writing, aren't you?"

The succubus immediately tucked the imp gently back into the bag and carefully pulled the drawstring tight. "Master, let's talk about going out instead, shall we?"

- **Caro Dushan:** 卡罗·杜尚 → Caro Dushan (glossary: Orcish-language teacher at the Wizard Sanctum).
- **Orcish word:** 格罗姆 = "Gromm" (Gromm Hellscream root; Jace translates it as "giant" — approximate).
- **Draconic character:** 雷伊索托 → "Reithosotho" (Kirin Tor Draconic; life/restoration element).
- **Marin's place:** 马林 → Marin (Master Marin / Arcanist Malin, glossary).
- **Alchemist's Need:** 炼金术士之需 → "the Alchemist's Need" (shop name).
- **Sazeno:** 撒厄诺 → Sazeno (glossary: imp minion, short name; true name Sazenilaz).
- **Imp's insult:** 嘲 — the imp calls Morophil a "嘲" (likely a slur; rendered as "bitch" in the imp's panicked voice).
- **Phase Shift:** 相位变换 → Phase Shift (glossary).
- **Mana drain:** Morophil admits she needs mana to survive in the material plane — she drained Jace's mana to sustain herself, leaving him unable to cast.
- **Tone:** Jace's internal voice stays dry and exasperated; Morophil is playful and possessive; the imp (Sazeno) is cowardly and sycophantic when caught.
