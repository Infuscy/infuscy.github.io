Chapter 246: Moon Plum Cake

Following Amy into the tavern, a wave of noisy chatter engulfed Jace.

The young students chatted eagerly, discussing the new term's courses, making small talk about the not-so-distant war — famous figures, heroes, and masters' performances in the war, as well as the rout of the Horde remnants at the Dark Portal and the officially launched Draenor Expedition that followed: the crossing through the Dark Portal.

In their mouths, that distant, unknown land seemed as if it would soon be incorporated into the Stormwind Kingdom's territory and become a colony.

Some even looked forward to the day they could travel to Draenor for pleasure, enjoying the joy of the great counteroffensive's victory.

If it had been a while ago at the Blue Heron, he would have quite enjoyed listening to these wild flights of fancy, but right now he felt he no longer belonged here — he was an outsider.

The Violet Prison was probably a more suitable place for him than Stormwind's jail.

Amy found a spot near the middle; Jace wasn't used to it, since when he came in with Grede they always headed straight for a corner — they didn't want anyone to hear what they were saying.

So the moment he sat down, he felt as if he were being judged by a crowd, the chatter around him turning into condemnation and abuse, making him extremely uncomfortable.

Was this a shadow hallucination?

So, until when did he plan to keep pushing the blame onto the shadow?

"What are you ordering?" The question from across the table snapped him back to his senses.

"I can pay for myself, really," Jace said quietly.

Amy patted the table lightly, displeased. "I finally get to treat you once, and you're pulling this? If you're like that, I'm not eating."

"Then I'll have the crab cake. Something sweet — I've got a magic class this afternoon," Jace said.

The female mage smiled. "Good choice. Archmage Antonidas believes that food and drink, especially sweets, are the healthiest way to replenish mana."

Jace unconsciously rubbed his right eye. Almost involuntarily, he thought of the least healthy way to replenish mana that he knew.

Amy called over the waiter. Besides the pastries and cakes for lunch, she also ordered two glasses of goat milk. By now, Jace had grown rather unaccustomed to the idea of going to a tavern to drink milk.

Jace held the delivered goat milk and said, "About the other day… I'm sorry, Amy."

"Did you think I dragged you here to judge you?" Amy raised an eyebrow. "Surprises and accidents like this are just part of learning magic — otherwise there'd be no reason for us to rush to learn protective magic. And I've caused far bigger disasters than this before. Nearly burned down the alchemy lab, and almost cost me and my dad our lives in the process. He's taught so many students; there have been plenty of accidents bigger than this."

"By the way, I also want to thank you for getting that Shadow Protection Potion to my father in time. The consequences could have been much worse — he might have had to take a long leave."

Hearing this, Jace said, "It was the least I could do."

"Not every student is like you. He thinks you really know how to take responsibility," Amy said. "He once told me, Jace, you're one of his favorite students. Though you can be impulsive and impatient at times, he's far more forgiving of youthful recklessness than you might think. Don't worry — being afflicted by shadow is dangerous, but as long as you're careful and recover well, you'll return to normal someday."

Jace licked his lips awkwardly. "Thanks."

"Thanks for what." Amy smiled. "Now hurry up and try this moon plum cake. It's my favorite thing in the whole Blue Heron. If it's too sweet, just have a taste — I've wanted to bring you here to try it for ages."

Jace nodded, picked up a small cut piece of the dark-red pastry, and took a bite. It was soft as cotton candy.

"Aiya!" Amy exclaimed. "Use the spoon… you've got jam all over your hands."

"Uh." Jace hurriedly licked his fingers.

Watching his distracted look, the smile on Amy's face gradually faded.

"I could tell from a distance today that something's off, Jace. I don't think it's just about my dad, do I? I'm a little worried about you. No, very worried."

"Is it that obvious?" Jace lifted his head.

"You were in the Park just now like a wounded kitten, licking your wounds all by yourself." Amy took a small spoonful of moon plum cake and put it in her mouth. "Tell your big sister about it, won't you? You can't always be the one comforting me, you know."

"I comforted you? When?"

"That very morning — you don't remember? I'm so hurt. Am I really that important…?" Amy sighed. "You told me, 'Never doubt your courage until you're face to face with danger.' It was a real wake-up call for me. Before that, I was always afraid I'd back down in the face of difficulty, that I'd let people down when they really needed me. But thinking about all the difficulties I've been through, I didn't run away every single time, did I? I figured, as long as I'm prepared, as long as I understand what I'm facing beforehand, I can handle trouble much more easily next time."

Jace stared at the female mage in front of him. For a moment, she seemed like a living angel, complete with a halo of Holy Light behind her.

"So? How about you — do you want to tell me about your troubles."

"I'm just… a little confused about what I'm doing right now," Jace said.

"Aren't you studying magic right now? The newly minted apprentice mage," Amy said with a hint of teasing.

Seeing Jace didn't react much, she seemed to realize something and nodded. "Ah, so you get lost too. I always thought you were the type who was always busy with something, all day long."

"How about this — tell me what you originally wanted to do. Let's say, when you first came to Stormwind."

"Buy a house," Jace said.

"Huh? You wanted to buy a house in Stormwind?" Amy covered her mouth and laughed softly. "I really didn't know that. Why not rent? Buying a house is so expensive. Not that I'm saying you definitely can't afford it, of course."

"For my parents. I wanted them to come to Stormwind," Jace admitted.

The moment he said it, he thought of his parents, and a sudden pang of misery drilled into his heart.

If Hennie found out what he'd done in Elwynn Forest, she'd probably grab the fork from the back wall and pin him to the old dirt wall of the Old Craftsmen's District herself, on public display.

"Isn't the capital of Lordaeron nice?" Amy looked at a piece of fruit on her spoon, squinting. "I heard it's the largest city in the human world, with everything you could want — all kinds of food, entertainment… Stormwind is like a small town compared to it."

"It's not that exaggerated," Jace said. "The biggest city, maybe. The most run-down and oldest city, I suppose…"

Amy listened attentively as Jace told stories about the ancient, grand walls and the bizarre tales and legends that had sprung up because they were so old. The more she listened, the more absorbed she became, stealing little laughs now and then and scolding him for making his hometown sound so bad.

As they chatted, without realizing it, Jace relaxed a lot.

Maybe he was still human, with a bit of humanity left, and hadn't completely lost his mind yet.

At least in Amy's eyes, he was still a normal person. He'd caused trouble and almost gotten her dad killed, but he was still a kind young man.

Even if he'd made mistakes, it was because of the black dragon's attack — nothing more than a "scar" left behind after a heroic battle.

He didn't deserve this trust.

Flags

- 月梅糕 — Translated as "moon plum cake." A fictional pastry; no canonical WoW equivalent. In WoW this is the Violet Hold beneath Dalaran; here it's used loosely as a high-security mage prison. Kept distinct from Stormwind's ordinary jail. - 蟹肉蛋糕 — Crab Cake, canonical WoW food, per glossary. - 羊奶 — Goat Milk, canonical WoW drink. - 安东尼达斯大师 — Archmage Antonidas, canonical Kirin Tor leader. - POV: third person throughout. Amy's dialogue keeps her warm, slightly teasing big-sister register. Jace's internal guilt over shadow corruption and his parents preserved.
