Chapter 247: As You Wish

What had he originally wanted to do, exactly? To earn money, to gain greater power, so he had to seize the opportunity to master shadow magic. If there was a chance to strengthen himself, he couldn't let it pass. So, had last night been a deviation from that path? Probably. In order to gain power… But could he blame the succubus for that? Morophil hadn't disobeyed his orders. She hadn't used her charm magic, hadn't let anyone know of her existence, hadn't harmed him. On the contrary, she'd been completely obedient, doing exactly as he said. And if he hadn't had her unleash her shadow power, she wouldn't have taken off her armor and walked up to him. At most she'd have sat there, mocking his little fire trick. When all was said and done, she'd just been trying to help him achieve his goal. He was the one who'd gone too far in pursuit of power. Before transmigrating, he'd never quite been able to grasp how difficult the choices faced by someone like Illidan Stormrage really were. Of course, he still didn't understand much now, because he'd already collapsed at a choice far simpler than anything Illidan had faced. He hadn't witnessed the fall of the entire Kaldorei empire, hadn't heard of the betrayal of a goddess-like, magnificent queen, hadn't experienced the apocalypse of his kin being slaughtered wholesale by demon armies, hadn't beheld the Dark Titan Sargeras personally present the gift of corruption, nor seen the temptation of the ancient power of the Well of Eternity itself. He hadn't faced a choice between the love of his life and his closest brother. A single fire spell had nearly destroyed his efforts to control the shadow. From beginning to end, the one who'd erred was himself. Morophil was nothing more than the target he wanted to shift the blame onto, the excuse for all that madness last night. Even that Bloodfang boar had been his order to the succubus to capture — simply because the burns had made him uncomfortable and he needed some life energy to ease the pain. The demon, half-dead from his berserk magic and barely able to walk straight, had still gotten gored by the boar — a beast driven mad by shadow torment — while capturing it alive, yet she'd still smiled and stroked the black blood overflowing from the corners of his eyes. That was one of the few clear images he still remembered from last night. Returning home, Jace pushed open the door. Morophil was sitting on the bed, looking at the Demonic text the imp had written. She seemed to be recovering well. "When are you planning to start learning Eredun, my master? Common is really so inconvenient. What kind of barbaric monster invented such a strange language?" "Master… please don't make me stay with her anymore, I beg you…" the imp said miserably. "Go back inside, Sazeno." Hearing this, the imp immediately dove into the bag. "Let's talk, Morophil," Jace said, sitting down on the table. The succubus looked up. "Sounds like you've decided to kill me?" "You know last night wasn't right," Jace said. "Were you happy, master?" the succubus said. "If you were, then that's enough." She seemed to sense Jace's mood. Her tone lost its playful, teasing edge from before, and even her tail had tightly wrapped around her leg. "I was," Jace said. "I learned how to control Shadowflame, and you gave me a feeling I'd never experienced before." "But you did want to kill me," Morophil said. "Why do you think that?" Jace asked. "And didn't you say you didn't care about dying? Like you said — you could escape this world, return to the Twisting Nether, and… who knows how long later, be reborn, find a new master, or just not bother finding one and go wherever you pleased, free and happy. Why do you care so much whether I want to kill you?" "But right now, I don't really want to leave you," Morophil said. "Being with you last night, I felt very happy." Jace frowned. "A demon saying these things sounds strange. Very, very fucking strange." "What's strange about it? Can't I pursue what I want?" The succubus leaned forward, propped her elbows on the table, and gazed into Jace's eyes. "I thought you liked releasing your true nature like that, instead of being constrained by your own kind in Stormwind. I thought you'd been waiting for an opportunity like last night for a long time… but it seems you weren't happy, and that wasn't your true nature either." "I'm guessing you're afraid of greater power, no… you're afraid that kind of power will change who you are now. So last night wasn't what you really wanted… was it?" "Tell me, my dear — what can I do to make you truly happy? I'd like to see you happy, not moping around, blaming yourself and miserable, and not spending every day thinking about how to send me back to the Twisting Nether." "Why?" Jace couldn't help but ask. "I want to win your heart, remember?" The succubus gave a light peck, then leaned in and whispered, "What fun is there in twisting a mortal's soul and making him submit to me? That's effortless, no challenge at all… don't you think?" Ah… damn it. This was still this succubus's plan all along. Jace felt like no matter which way he turned, he was moving toward this succubus's goal. But did he need to care about that right now? Seeing Jace say nothing, Morophil continued, "Set new rules for me — anything, my master. I'll remember them and try my best to follow them. If I make a mistake, please tell me? You can even punish me directly…" Jace let out a gentle breath. "You haven't made a mistake. And there's no need for other rules for now, Morophil. I'm not planning to do anything to you either." He was the one who couldn't control himself — neither his lust, nor his unchecked hunger for power. Jace knew that the succubus's appearance was yet another test of his resolve. She was the very embodiment of shadow itself. As his power gradually grew, the pull of corruption would only become more dangerous. If he fled from this corruption because he couldn't resist it, that would be the same as rejecting the possibility of growing stronger. Even arcane magic was maddeningly addictive. The path of a mage wasn't any easier than the road he was on now. Wherever he went, as Amy Marin had said — be prepared, understand what he was facing, and he could handle difficulties with much more ease. Jace felt he was still far from his limit. His will was enough to carry on; he shouldn't give up so easily. In the game, you could summon a succubus at level 20. Was he really not even as good as a level-20 little warlock? Thinking of this, he gently gripped one of the succubus's horns. "I'm going to sleep, Morophil. I didn't sleep a wink last night. I'm not you… as a human, I really can't hold on anymore. And I need to save enough energy for this afternoon's class. Keep an eye on that pocket watch — wake me up in two hours." "As you wish, my dear." The succubus narrowed her eyes.

Used the canonical term rather than the literal "Eredar" to match WoW usage. - 扭曲虚空 — Twisting Nether, canonical demonic dimension. - Chapter title 如你所愿 = "As You Wish," echoing the succubus's closing line. - POV: third person. Jace's self-lacerating internal monologue and the Illidan parallel kept intact. Morophil's seductive, manipulative register preserved.
