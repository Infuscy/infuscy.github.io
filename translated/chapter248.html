Chapter 248: The Little Alarm

That afternoon, Jace picked up his warlock's bag and headed for the Mage Quarter. Why? Wasn't it perfectly normal to bring his imp to the Slaughtered Lamb for frequent academic exchanges? Jace didn't go straight to class — the Protection school's lectures started quite late anyway. He went straight to the Slaughtered Lamb, exchanged a greeting with Tharle, and made a beeline for the alchemy lab. That afternoon, Erland and Sulenna had somehow found another unlucky bastard and were using him for practice in a room deep in the corridor. Strange yelps rang out from time to time, making the whole basement feel like a torture chamber. Ursula was still brewing medicine on the other side, so Jace headed straight for her area. "Beautiful and kind Lady Ursula, good afternoon." "Sweet talker, Jace. Have you gone to the Dark Iron dwarves?" "Tomorrow. I'll probably have the results for you by next week." "So eager? Then you didn't come here to scrounge for potions again, did you?" "Uh, not exactly… but do you still have any mana potions? For using in Redridge, just one bottle…" Ursula gave him a flat look. "Fine, I suppose. Who told you to be so cute?" "Heh." Jace smiled and took a small bottle of minor mana potion, tucking it into his bag. "So what are you here for?" Ursula asked. Jace asked, "Do you have a safe room here?" "Safe?" Ursula stared at him for a moment. "What are you planning? How safe do you need it? If that Andormas of yours is determined to break in, nowhere is safe." "It's not that bad… I'm planning to summon my imp and ask it some things," Jace said. "Sulenna and the others have taken the room over there, and I don't want to be too close to the door, so I wanted to borrow a room here." Ursula said, "Go to the back apothecary room. Don't let the imp set my herbs on fire, or I'll kill you with this ladle." "The smoke from the fire in there is already enough to choke you," Jace said. "Thanks!" Following the direction Ursula pointed, Jace arrived at the apothecary room. He hadn't expected it to be quite so large — rows upon rows of cabinets with all sorts of herbs in bags, the whole room reeking of a pungent herbal-medicine smell. Jace pressed his ear to the door and listened, confirming Ursula was still brewing medicine. Then he walked to the innermost corner of the room. "Sazeno, come out. Keep it down." The imp popped out and looked around. Jace asked, "You little thing — do you know why I brought you here?" Sazeno said smugly, "Heh, the master still trusts me more, doesn't he?" "Of course," Jace said. "Let me ask you — when you're in the bag, can you hear people talking outside?" The imp said helplessly, "It's really hard to hear clearly, master. When the voices outside get quiet, I can't make them out! This bag's quality is pretty good… unless you don't tie the opening tight, and I'm not invisible." Jace glanced at his pocket watch. "Let's keep this short — I still have to go to class later. Do you trust that succubus?" "Trust a Saya'de?!" the imp exclaimed. "Have I lost my mind?" "Isn't knowing her true name enough?" Jace asked. "She told you her true name?!" The imp's fiery eyes went wide. "Has she lost her mind?" "Didn't you tell me your true name too?" Jace asked. The imp coughed twice. "I… you clearly forced it out of me… But Saya'de don't think straight. You can't measure them by a normal demon's way of thinking. Tsk!" "Don't you demons serve your masters without telling them your true names?" Jace asked. The imp hopped up and down. "Most mortal masters that imps meet don't even know demons have true names! They don't have the chance to study demonology at your Kirin Tor, master. You know an awful lot!" Of course, Jace had learned about true names from the game. Whether it was the Outland quests or the dreadlord Razelikh quest in the Blasted Lands, knowing a true name meant grasping a demon's fatal weakness. You could weaken them, control them, and even eliminate them in cases of vast power disparity. That's why demons feared their true names being exposed. Illidan Stormrage's blood elf demon hunter disciple, Varedis VileSoul, had relied on forging a soul connection with the Demonicon — an artifact recording countless demon true names — to dominate Outland, a world overrun by demons. Based on this, Jace had concluded that he couldn't trust a demon without knowing its true name. It seemed warlocks here still didn't fully understand the importance of true names. It might take another decade or so for Azeroth's warlocks to mature before this kind of more stable bond could become common. "Alright," Jace said. "I can't fully trust the succubus yet, Sazeno, so I need you to help me. From now on, at home I won't tie the bag's opening tight — I'll leave a small gap for you. And you won't need to Phase Shift and disappear at home either. But you… you have to help me listen. If that succubus tries to set me up and you catch on, you warn me — and don't let her realize I'm onto her. Understood?" The imp hesitated for a moment. "Master, so how should I warn you?" Jace thought for a moment. "Right — why can I hear you clearly when I call your name? You can hear it across different planes, right? Every time I call you, you come out." "Because of the contract bond between us, master!" the imp said. "Then if I call your name?" Jace asked. "What's the master's name?" Jace was taken aback. "I've never told you my name?" "Never," the imp said. "But that stupid dwarf calls you Jace, right?" "My name is Jace Sesso," Jace said. "Remember it?" "Remembered!" the imp said. "Want to try it?" "Quick," Jace said. "Get in the bag, Phase Shift and go invisible, then call my name from the other plane." "Yes!" The imp dove into the bag. Jace tied the opening tight and saw the bag shrink — it must have Phase Shifted. At that moment, he suddenly felt his heart thump. — Jace Sesso! Then came a string of Demonic so unclear it didn't sound like the imp at all, but rather some demon lord in the void speaking. In the empty apothecary room, hearing this sound made the back of Jace's neck go cold… but at least it was unmistakably clear and obvious. "Come out, Sazeno!" The imp popped out immediately. "How was it, my master?" Jace said, "Clear enough. What does that Demonic phrase mean?" The imp said excitedly, "Jace Sesso, I call your name to get your attention — that's roughly the translation! Strictly speaking, it's also a spell!" Jace said, "Alright. If you catch that succubus lying to me, or deliberately hiding something to screw me over, you call my name — just like that. This is extremely important, Sazeno. Take it seriously." "Absolutely serious!" The imp patted its chest. "Are you bringing me to class today, master?" "Are you kidding? If I bring you into the Wizard Sanctum, the battle mage at the door would chop my head off. Get in." As Jace said this, he packed the imp away, went outside, and unbuttoned a few shirt buttons under his coat. "It's so hot in here!" "You know why I wear so little even in winter?" Ursula glanced at his chest. "And you had to go and talk to a little fire-breathing imp… Where is the imp? Did you kill it?" "What else? You think I'm going to move in with it?" Jace set the warlock's bag on the counter. "Big sis, watch this bag for me. It has the spell Galders tested on me earlier — I don't dare bring it into the Wizard Sanctum. I'll be back in two or three hours to pick it up." "Nothing dangerous in here, I hope?" Ursula patted the bag. "Just paper? I'm worried the moisture will get it wet — can it go in the cabinet?" "There are also a few quills," Jace said as he walked. "You can put it in the cabinet. Thanks so much!"
