Chapter 249: New Quest, New Gear

Abjuration lectures basically didn't teach spells — spells were generally learned directly from one's own master. Jace guessed it was because everyone's level was uneven; teaching them together would just make a mess.

Great. He'd thought he was going to embarrass himself in public.

The instructors in the lecture mostly explained magical history and some casting knowledge and techniques. But given the level of the courses Jace was taking, it was still quite elementary. Most of what was covered, Jace had already heard from Malin.

Regarding the issue of casting intensity that he cared most about, they only gave a brief introduction. Jace guessed this was also to give the new little apprentices some basic orientation, since he and the few other people his age were the oldest in this class.

Actually, by now, he'd already lost a lot of confidence in his ability to control casting intensity. The class mentioned that mastering spell intensity required a high sensitivity to the arcane.

Sensitivity to the arcane… Jace was still at the stage where he couldn't even hope to succeed at casting by rote. How was he supposed to be sensitive…

He'd memorized Mage Armor by heart for several months… thought he'd gotten pretty good at it, only to have the master say his casting wasn't quite right. Sigh.

Would he have a harder time advancing to journeyman mage and full mage than Amy? Amy was already considered talented compared to him… Was he really going to have to grind until he was thirty or forty? God.

Wasn't drinking half a bottle of Sunwell water supposed to help?

Considering that the Sunwell water had helped him heal his necrotic wounds, the puncture wound on his shoulder, and raised his mana bar a bit, he really couldn't expect much more from it.

He'd arranged a few days ago to get a letter from Malin today. After class, Jace picked up the bag from Ursula and hurried to Malin's study — by now he should have finished teaching the afternoon apprentices.

After going in, Malin was sitting inside writing something. He glanced at Jace and his expression seemed off.

"Master, how are you recovering…"

Malin said coldly, "What do you think? Do you need me to lie on the ground and scream a couple of times before you believe I'm sick? I'm not like those lazy, stupid bastards who stay in bed at home because they can't be bothered to attend class. I still have so many students to teach and so many things to do. And that idiot just now inflicted far worse trauma on me than your poison-tinged Scorch."

Jace listened to Malin curse up a breath and secretly felt relieved — at least it had nothing to do with him.

"Alright, enough small talk. I have things to do later." Malin picked up his glasses, holding the record paper in his hand. "The person you're looking for is named Henbury Ronmeis. The name is here. He's a mountain warden from Lakeshire. But he generally doesn't live in town — he lives in a hunting lodge up in the mountains. The exact location isn't very clear here, but there's a stone path leading up to it. You can ask around in Lakeshire. This Mr. Ronmeis has family living in Lakeshire."

"This is the Ronmeis who found the black dragon's scale?" Jace asked.

"At least he's the one who reported it." Malin took off his glasses. "Figuring this out is also your task, Mr. Sesso. I only learned of this intelligence recently. If you really can't find any leads, you can take a bit more time. If you don't come back, I'll help you take leave."

"Ah… Master," Jace said. "If I keep missing classes, will I never advance to a full mage in my whole life?"

"You can't even see hope for journeyman mage yet, and you're already thinking about full mage?" Malin asked.

"You don't have to be that blunt…" Jace said helplessly.

Malin glanced at his pocket watch. "By the way, Larson's shop is about to close — aren't you going to pick out a new robe?"

"Can I pick it myself?"

"One gold." Malin tapped the table. "This is the highest budget I can secure for you. It's Master Andromath's special thanks for your contribution to helping the Wizard's Sanctum handle the King's commission."

"That's too much!" Jace put away Malin's notes. "Then I'm off, Master."

Coming out of the study, he hurried all the way to Larson's shop. This time, even the shopkeeper recognized him.

"You're back, Mr. Sesso… Did the last robe fall apart after a few days?" Larson said, tidying the fabrics on the counter as he spoke.

"It didn't," Jace said. "But it needs some mending, so I need a replacement."

"You can have it mended here too," Larson said. "That robe of yours was also bought from me, wasn't it? If it's just minor damage, it can be fixed quickly."

"I'll bring it over in a couple of days, sir," Jace nodded.

In reality, that robe had been turned into a complete mess last night by fire, shadow, blood, and Morophil's demonic fluids. It had taken a thorough, long wash before it finally looked like something a normal person would wear.

But he'd have to wait for it to dry and inspect it again. If there were still problems, it would need another wash.

Fortunately, as Larson had said, that garment was very hard to ignite, so it hadn't been burned too badly. By and large, there was still a chance it could be mended. Otherwise, Jace would have planned to just destroy it and throw it away.

"The budget is quite generous this time," Larson said. "Master Andromath told me one gold. That can buy quite a lot of high-quality robes. Any particular requests this time, Mr. Sesso?"

"A short robe. Flame-resistant — that's the most important thing. I'm planning to study fire elemental magic, and I'd like to reduce the frequency of being sent to the Cathedral for healing."

"Don't you want a long robe this time?"

Jace shook his head. "It really doesn't suit me, sir. It's fine in the city, but I'm not used to it when traveling. I don't know if there's a high-quality short robe. If it goes over budget by a few silver, I can accept that. It has to be quality… and if it could also resist shadow corruption, that would be even better."

At that moment, he thought of that seven-gold robe at the other clothing shop. Even after being hit head-on by a red dragon's breath, it hadn't been completely destroyed — only the hem was partially damaged. He hadn't realized it at the time for lack of experience, but thinking about it now, it was just too powerful. Truly, you get what you pay for.

But after scraping by for months, Jace would still have to bankrupt himself to afford it.

Still, this kind of ultimate treasure that would shake his house-fund savings — even if he could afford it, he didn't want to consider it.

Not long after, Larson came out carrying a box. By Jace's experience, things carefully stored in a box like this were always good stuff.

He opened it, revealing a carefully folded purple-red garment inside.

Larson lifted the garment and said, "This silk-woven outer robe is a short robe made by the elves during the Second War, Mr. Jace Sesso. It was originally supplied to Stromgarde's mages to counter orc warlocks and demons. But because of the Horde's rout, the battles north of Alterac ended quickly, leaving behind many unused robes. Later, Stromgarde donated a batch of them to our kingdom to aid reconstruction efforts. This is one of them."

A purple-red outer robe might look a bit strange on an ordinary man, but in the Mage Quarter, where most people wore blue or purple outer robes, a purple-red one wouldn't stand out that much.

"Elf-made clothes… wouldn't they be expensive?" Jace asked.

Larson glanced at the sleeve. "This was made to human proportions and usage habits — purely battlefield mages' military supplies — so the cost isn't as high as you might think, sir. But since it is, after all, something made in Quel'Thalas, compared to other ordinary robes… it is indeed a bit pricey. Normally it would cost more than one gold and twenty silver, but considering we're providing robes for the Mage Quarter's students, and our cooperation with the Wizard's Sanctum, I'll give it to you for exactly one gold."

Jace put it on to try. He felt that elf-made clothes, so-called mass-produced goods or not, did look a bit elaborate in appearance — especially compared to his previous few garments.

"Are there many people wearing the same robe at the Wizard's Sanctum?"

"There were more last year; there are fewer this year, but no one will find it strange, Mr. Sesso. This is truly an exquisitely crafted robe, and it can be further enchanted. Elven items are very accommodating to all kinds of enchantments."

"Then this is the one," Jace said. "I'd also like a thicker pair of dark trousers, sir — preferably the kind you could wear straight across Stranglethorn Vale without coming back to you for repairs."

"What you need isn't trousers — it's a pair of mithril greaves enchanted with high protective magic."

"Then just enough to cross Elwynn Forest…"
