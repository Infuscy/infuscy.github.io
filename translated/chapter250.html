Chapter 250: How to Become a Demon Warlock

All the way home that evening, Jace kept thinking about tomorrow's trip to Redridge. Leave in the afternoon, arrive at the Eastvale waystation by evening, reach Lakeshire by the next morning to rest, then head up the mountain to find that warden. That should wrap up the day, one way or another. Head up to northern Redridge over the weekend. Even if he couldn't find any trace of the Dark Iron dwarves, at least he could scout the terrain and lay the groundwork for deeper exploration later. Arriving at his door, he looked at the unremarkable little building where his rented room was located and figured small had its advantages. The location was a bit out of the way, so rent was cheap. Plus, mages and priests rarely passed by, so it didn't draw much attention. If anyone suspected the existence of demons, they'd probably never imagine that so many demons could be hidden in such a cramped, run-down place. If he were to find a bigger place, considering he was only an apprentice right now… it might actually draw more suspicion. So for now, he'd just have to make do. He opened the door. Morophil was lying on the bed, twirling her tail in circles. "You're finally back. I was so bored without you. Come play with me." "I've got class tomorrow too, Morophil," Jace said. "I can't afford mana potions anymore, so stop tormenting me." "I won't secretly drain your mana anymore. Can't we just have fun, pure and simple?" "I don't believe you," Jace said, setting his bag on the ground. "When the time comes, you'll just do whatever you want anyway." "Ah!" Morophil scratched the wall. "So this is the decision you made earlier? You want me to be celibate forever?" "Forever? Fifty or sixty years at most. I might kick the bucket in a few days, and then you can find your next keeper!" "Then last night was the last time we'll ever be warm together in this life?" "What do you call that?" Jace looked up. "Your 'warm moment' nearly burned down that whole forest. So what does your passion look like? Blowing up all of Stormwind?" Morophil raised an eyebrow. "I don't care. I want it." "Think about something else," Jace said. "I need a good night's rest tonight." He opened his bag and checked its contents, giving a meaningful look at the imp sitting inside. "Don't bother me while I'm sleeping, Sazeno." "Got it, master." The imp patted its chest and narrowed its eyes slightly. It felt like a secret signal being passed. He hung up his coat, sat down on the table, and said, "I'm heading out of town tomorrow afternoon. Might be a few days before I'm back. You two behave yourselves at home — no fighting, no roughhousing, keep each other in check." "Yes!" the imp said. "A few days?" the succubus said angrily. "No!" "What a stark contrast, Morophil. Can't you learn from him?" Jace said. "And what good does your 'no' do? When I say no, it's no." The succubus's tail lashed out angrily. With a crack, the tip left a scratch on the wall, giving Jace a start. "If you wreck the wall I have to pay for it, you damn—" He rushed over to the bed, grabbed the succubus by the neck, and slammed her against the wall. "Do you want to die?" Caught like this, the succubus narrowed her eyes and strained her voice. "Keep going… master, just like that… choke me to death, quickly…" "You fucking freak…" Jace flung his hand away and ran to the bed to inspect the scratch on the wall closely, trying to rub it off with his finger. It wouldn't come off. Damn. It had carved a real deep gouge, not some shallow scratch you'd see everywhere. This wasteful bitch. Jace gritted his teeth. The succubus seemed to realize he was genuinely angry and unconsciously edged away. "Get lost! I'm going to sleep!" Morophil sat down on the table, glancing at him sideways, and rubbed her neck. "So what are you going out there to do?" "Just a run around. Helping someone with something." "Then I'm coming too." "What are you going for?" Jace lay down. "I'm riding a horse. You sit on a horse and you'll scare it half to death." "Are horses more complicated than people?" The succubus crossed her legs. "If I can charm a human, can't I charm a horse?" Jace's mouth twitched. "What are you charming a horse for?" Morophil smiled. "To make it obedient! What did you think? Ah… you don't think I'm going to… Rest assured, my dear, I only have you. I absolutely won't be with a second…" As she said this, she crawled over. Jace grabbed her horns and held her back. "Stop. Don't even think about disturbing my sleep tonight." The succubus shook her head, breaking free of Jace's grip. "Let me go out and protect you. As long as you agree, I'll stop pestering you. Deal?" Jace said, "Then keep pestering. I'm so tired I can fall asleep the moment I close my eyes." Jace pushed the succubus over and lay down on his side. Behind him, she gritted her teeth with a creaking sound. But with his legs right there, feeling the succubus rubbing against him from behind, Jace was honestly quite curious about just how strong she really was. Last night's fight with that wild boar honestly didn't count. For one thing, Morophil had no weapon. For another, he'd ordered her not to kill the boar. Third, she'd already been badly weakened by his own fire, so she hadn't shown her real fighting strength. Though there was no obvious difficulty in finding that warden, going after the Dark Iron dwarves would be quite dangerous. After all, northern Redridge wasn't just home to Dark Iron dwarves — there might also be orcs, plus the threat of the fearsome beasts that roamed the Burning Steppes. If it were just Jace alone, he probably wouldn't dare venture too deep toward the Burning Steppes. That would narrow his search area a lot. This trip had no monetary reward, so Grede wouldn't be particularly willing to come. Might as well bring these two demons along and be a proper demon warlock for once. If that were the case, he probably shouldn't stay at an inn in Lakeshire. After all, all kinds of people passed through Lakeshire from north and south — even elves — and it would be easy to notice something off. But if he brought two demons with him, he'd probably be the most evil presence in the whole Redridge forest. Even camping out in the wild wouldn't be dangerous, right? Add in the sinister aura radiating from the black dragon cloak… no beast would dare come near. He could just tie up the horse in Lakeshire. Right — the cloak's aura. Would the horse be afraid? He'd never actually tested it. If the succubus could charm a horse and keep it docile, that'd actually be quite convenient. "Alright, stop rubbing. You can come with me, Morophil." "Really?" The succubus crawled onto his arm. "Can you use that sword?" Jace asked. The succubus glanced at the sword hanging behind her. "This Nathrezim thing? Of course. I've always fought with short swords like this." Hearing this, Jace turned over with curiosity, and the succubus took the opportunity to sit on his waist. "You don't use a whip?" "Those who are good with whips are interrogators, master. I haven't been one for a long time. When I worked for other demons, I was specifically tasked with hunting down traitors." "You worked for other demons?" "A long time ago." "Is it related to your wings?" "Hmm… you're curious about that," Morophil said. "It's somewhat related." "Was it combat? Were your wings chopped off by some demon while hunting?" "Impossible," Morophil replied. "My wings were torn off by the Annihilan overlord I served. All demons who worked for him were forbidden from having demonic wings — whether Doomguards or Saya'de." "Why did he do that?" "Maybe because he lost his own in a duel tens of thousands of years ago, so he was jealous of us winged ones. How would I know? I don't care what he thinks." "Brutallus," Jace murmured. "You actually know his name!" The succubus threw herself onto Jace's chest. "Ah… such an ancient name coming from your lips — how marvelous." A sweet, fishy scent nearly suffocated Jace. He turned his face away. "The wingless pit lord of legend… it tends to leave an impression on demon scholars. But does he know your true name? I don't think he's dead yet, is he?" "He used a powerful arcane orb loyal only to him to control the demon souls of all his demon servants. Even death couldn't escape his grasp — they'd just be reformed in his demon nest and continue to be enslaved by him. So he had no need to know true names at all. Later, a demon lord named Kil'jaeden destroyed his castle, shattered his orb of power, and subdued him personally… which also counted as rescuing me. It's just that my wings could never grow back." "Even returning to the Twisting Nether to reform your body wouldn't make them grow back?" "I think he might have directly torn away a part of my soul. You want them to grow back?" The succubus twisted her body so Jace could see the stumps on her back. Jace suddenly felt a phantom pain in his own back. "You think they're ugly?" "Not ugly at all. Pretty cool — like a warrior who's been through many fierce battles!" "Really?" The succubus smiled. "I also think demonic wings are quite a nuisance, aren't they? Especially in this tiny house." "Don't you dare call my home small," Jace said. "I'm going to rest now. Get off — I can't sleep with you pressing down on me." "Then, before bed, at least give me a kiss, my master. I'll get off right away." The succubus lowered her eyes and pleaded. "I don't kiss demons." "I know you hate demons, so you won't be at a disadvantage. I'll trade you something good." "What?" Jace asked curiously. "An ability that'll be useful out there." The succubus's tone became unusually serious. "A benefit that that lowly lesser imp definitely couldn't give you. I want to increase your fondness for demons, just a little." "An ability?" Jace asked skeptically. "Tell me what it is first." "Have you heard of the Soul Link between a minion and its master?" the succubus explained. "Though I've never tried it with a human, I know other Saya'de have successfully linked souls with humans… it shouldn't be difficult." Soul Link? Was that the demonology warlock talent? The talent's effect was… transferring a portion of the damage the master took to the demon minion! This was a god-tier demon warlock PvP talent, a fearsome ability that had to be banned in many versions of dueling. But here, would the Soul Link have any other effects that weren't in the game? Like, if the minion got hurt, the master would also get hurt… Morophil wasn't hiding something, was she? Why hadn't Sazeno called his name? This was exactly when he needed it. It couldn't be that it didn't know about Soul Link… impossible… Jace muttered, "So if I get hurt…" "I'll bear the pain for you. So you already know about this — then I won't bother explaining!" The succubus wrapped her arms around Jace's neck. "The master's suffering, let me endure it — just thinking about it drives me crazy. I can't wait to establish the Soul Link with you and then stab you to test it…" Jace had been moved at first, but hearing the last part, he pushed her upright. "It's one thing for you to be a freak, but is there something wrong with your head too?" "Can't I even make a joke?" Morophil asked. "Just tell me — do you want it or not?" "Are you sure?" "If I weren't sure, why would I tell you?" "Alright then. How do we do it?" "First, love me wholeheartedly and with all your soul. After all, it's a soul link — we need to bring our souls close…" "Jace Sesso… Jace Sesso!" It was the imp's voice… finally, it was working. Jace had almost thought this alarm was broken. "Ha! Then there's no helping it." He turned over. "Alright, alright, I'm joking." The succubus wrapped his shoulder and rolled over. "When you take me out and we find a place with no one around, I'll tell you exactly how to do it. But right now, you have to kiss me first." "Wait — the process of establishing a demon link won't drain my mana again, will it?" Jace asked. "How could you have the strength to guide souls if your mana was drained?" the succubus asked. "So are you agreeing or not?" "No kiss. We'll talk after it's done." "Bastard!" "You said swearing means you're…" "Bastard!"
