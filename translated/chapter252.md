## Chapter 252: Soul Link

In the evening, riding along the road through Elwynn Forest, Jace tensed up a little every time he passed a patrol — especially the ones with mages in tow.

One mage coming from the opposite direction stared at him for a long while. Finally, Jace realized he was looking at the ring on his hand, not sensing anything off about him, and breathed a little easier.

Hopefully Morofil could, like she'd said, sense in advance when she might be discovered and slip away in time — because right now he had no idea where Morofil was.

Morofil's charm had made the horse docile as a puppy — no temper, no personality whatsoever, doing whatever it was told. Back in Lordaeron, Jace had never gotten along with horses at all, and now he was starting to feel downright uncomfortable about it.

He thought to himself that when he'd first summoned the succubus, this was probably what he'd looked like to everyone else.

Docile was docile, but after half a day's hard riding, by nightfall the dappled horse was clearly tired. Jace figured it ought to rest a while too.

He led the horse into the forest, walking slowly for a long way until he reached a low-lying hollow, far from any road, before finally stopping.

When you were traveling with demons, you could forget about resting by the roadside.

Elwynn Forest wasn't as cold as the north, but the winter forest was just as quiet. Even when Jace called Morofil's name softly, he felt his own voice was a little too harsh.

"Over here, master."

The succubus was sitting on a raised root up above, looking down. When he couldn't make out her face, Jace could finally, from the outline of her figure, objectively appreciate her build.

Jace hadn't learned many beautiful words in Common, and his Chinese vocabulary was pretty poor too — all he could come up with was "stunningly gorgeous."

"No one around here," he said, looking up. "Can we try the Soul Link?"

"Of course." The succubus leaped down and walked over.

"Sazeno, do you know about Soul Link?" Jace asked the imp.

"Of course I do," the imp said. "Soul Link is the closest bond between a demon and its servant…"

Morofil stepped closer. "We'll need a ritual circle too, master. I'm sure he can draw one."

"I can't!" the imp said immediately. "Don't look at me!"

"You dare say you can't?!" the succubus glared at him. The imp sighed. "Ugh, why is it always me who gets stuck with the dirty work…"

"Because I'm lazy," Morofil said.

With that, she grabbed the imp and tossed it toward a relatively flat patch of ground. The imp reluctantly kicked the dead leaves aside, looked around, and said, "Well, the blood can't come from me — look at this little body of mine. You could drain me dry and you wouldn't fill a bowl. And besides, she's the one who's going to suffer for you — my blood won't work anyway!"

Morofil glanced at Jace and said to the imp, "Hold on, I'm coming."

She drew her short sword and lightly sliced a long cut across her arm. The imp guided the flowing purple demon blood as it began drawing sigils on the ground. The sigil grew more and more complete, revealing an increasingly obvious faint purple glow.

Jace stared at the sigil — circle after circle of interlocking demonic runes and demon script. The complexity was something he could never hope to draw in his entire life.

Finally, the imp skillfully drew a small circle surrounded by sigil lines on one side of the ritual circle, completing it. It stood up and said, "There, now it's your turn to have a rough time, sayad."

The succubus stepped onto the small circle. "Come, master — step onto the large sigil!"

Jace walked to where she pointed and immediately felt uncomfortable all over, as if something had grabbed hold of him.

He felt the Void hovering all around, vaguely sensed something watching him — but when he looked at the succubus's arcane-lit burning eyes gradually being stained purple, the thing watching him seemed to shrink and gather onto her alone.

Jace had an instinctive feeling that what was watching him might be the succubus's soul. This ritual circle seemed to have, in some sense, already separated the demon's flesh from her spirit.

"Hurry and take my hand, master. You really are too slow. The last eredar grabbed my hand and enslaved my soul with a shout of will and incantation — yet you're standing here doing nothing. I'm finding it a little hard to get used to."

"You don't need to bring up your former masters anymore, Morofil." Jace grabbed the succubus's hand.

"Ah…" A whiff of jealousy. Morofil smiled.

The moment the words left her mouth, she closed her eyes and began chanting a Demonic incantation.

The ritual circle started emitting an ominous glow. Jace felt the eyes watching him pressing closer and closer. Gusts of wind picked up, dead leaves scattering in every direction.

From not too far off came the anxious whinnying of the dappled horse — Morofil's charm seemed to have worn off.

Jace couldn't help but turn his head that way. The imp immediately warned, "You'd better focus your attention now, master. I'll keep watch on our surroundings for you."

The imp's seriousness made Jace realize at once how important that reminder was. So he shut out everything happening outside and focused his attention on Morofil's mind and body.

The void soul watching him drew closer and closer. Jace didn't know why, but he felt as if the other party was reaching out too.

Jace wanted to reach out, but his hand was held by the succubus, and his body seemed unwilling to obey him. Still, he forced himself to reach out and touch that void soul — and somehow, impossibly, he did reach out… but this wasn't his current hand.

It was like making a movement from a world that didn't exist.

He felt that void soul getting closer and closer, finally embracing him. He hadn't expected the succubus's skin to be so cold, yet her soul was scorching — like a fel fire, burning him so badly he felt a violent searing pain through his whole body.

Suddenly, the succubus opened her eyes. The flames in her eyes instantly shifted from purple back to a ghostly blue. The entire ritual circle, as if caught by a violent gust of wind, dissipated all at once — utterly unrecognizable.

Jace was suddenly freed from the torment of that other world. He gasped a few breaths and asked, "Did it fail? What did I do wrong?"

The succubus said nothing. She stepped forward, steadied Jace's shoulders, and drew her black sword across the skin of his shoulder, leaving a bloody mark. Jace felt a sting, but the wound healed rapidly after the blade passed through the flesh.

And compared to a real sword cut, the pain felt dulled — like the anesthetic hadn't quite taken.

Then Morofil stuck out her long purple tongue and licked her lips. Jace noticed some bright red blood smeared on the skin beside her lips.

"Want to taste whose blood this is, master?"

## Flags

- Jace travels alone toward Redridge with Morofil (hidden) and Sazeno; he's nervous about patrols, especially mages, but the ring on his hand draws more attention than any demonic aura.
- Morofil has been charming Jace's horse to keep it docile — Jace finds the unnaturally obedient animal unsettling.
- Soul Link ritual performed: a warlock-demon bond (canon WoW Demonology talent) that shares damage between master and demon. Morofil guides Jace through it; Sazeno draws the ritual circle in demon blood.
- The ritual involves a void-soul encounter — Jace experiences the succubus's soul as a separate, scorching entity that embraces him, causing intense pain.
- After the ritual, Morofil tests the link by cutting Jace's shoulder — the wound heals fast and the pain is dulled, suggesting the damage-sharing is already active. She tastes his blood.
- Morofil refers to a previous eredar master who enslaved her soul by force — a rare glimpse of her past.
- "Sayad" (萨亚德) — Demonic term for a succubus-type demon; used by Sazeno here.
