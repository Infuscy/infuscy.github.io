Chapter 256: The Naked Hatred Between Peers

Jace hung the single Minor Mana Potion he had from his chest strap and crept forward under cover of the cliff.

At a distance that couldn't possibly get any closer, he caught sight of the dragonkin's appearance through the intermittent glow of shadow magic he kept releasing.

Just as Morophil had said, the dragonkin wasn't tall or large — barely any bigger than him.

But its body and tail were absurdly long, especially among that dead thicket. That twisted silhouette — a humanoid upper half fused with a lizard-like lower half — genuinely made Jace's scalp crawl.

Add in the dense, light-reflecting scales covering its body, and it looked like some grotesque horror stepped straight out of a cult film.

As Morophil had said, it wore a red robe over its upper half, but from this angle Jace couldn't make out its head.

Jace muttered the Mage Armor incantation.

The moment the spell completed and the arcane glow faded, the dragonkin suddenly whipped its head around!

Its yellow reptilian eyes were still sharp and clear in the darkness, its tongue flickering out in a quick taste of the air.

The dragonkin's upper body might have been humanoid, but its head was the real deal — a full-on reptile's skull! The moment the creature sensed the arcane energy flowing over Jace's body, its whole frame dropped low and it came barreling forward on all fours like a hunting giant lizard, charging straight at him!

Jace had guessed its speed might be frightening, but what he hadn't expected was for the dragonkin to sprint a couple of steps and then scramble up the face of the cliff — and come diving down from above…

Would Morophil be able to keep up with that speed?

Jace struck first with a Searing Curse. Shadowflame erupted in a vicious black-red explosion against the dragonkin's body. The blast sent the creature tumbling down in a roll, wreathed in fel fire that painted the surrounding trees and stones blood-red.

The dragonkin muttered something, staggered to its feet, spat a mouthful of black blood — equal parts fury and frustration — and then raised its staff to conjure a luminous lotus of light around its body… a Fire Protection Ward!

Even as it cast, the dragonkin broke into motion again, swinging the long staff in its hand — a staff set with a blood-red gem that gleamed with fel fire in the darkness.

It was a staff, sure, but it could just as easily be used as a polearm. Jace knew that better than anyone!

This was a battle mage too!

Jace had been expecting a long-range magical duel with this spellcaster, but he hadn't counted on the other side deciding it could get the better of him — a mage — in melee.

Then again, he had Mage Armor reinforcing him; the dragonkin had probably sensed that a pure spellfight wouldn't go its way, much the same way Jace was already annoyed by that Fire Protection Ward…

After taking that fire blast, the dragonkin's rhythm was clearly disrupted. Jace quickly followed up with a Curse of Weakness. The creature's front legs suddenly buckled, nearly face-planting into the dirt, but it stumbled a couple of steps and kept running.

Even with all the hindrances, those four legs covered ground terrifyingly fast.

Just as Jace was about to cast Fear to break its charge, the dragonkin vanished in a flash of light…

Blink, damn it!

His PvP experience from his past life told him that Blink never had an invisibility effect. If you couldn't see the caster in front of you, that meant they were already behind you!

Jace whirled and threw a Fear point-blank. The dragonkin, which had already raised its staff to strike, let out a shriek and staggered back several steps. Jace seized the opening and hit it with Shadowflame again. The flames slammed into the ward, but the shadow energy tore straight through into the dragonkin's scales.

Black dragons had considerable shadow resistance, but the creature still coughed up a fresh mouthful of purple blood in pain, trembling and growling low — and the Fire Protection Ward, after that single blast, had grown so faint it was barely visible.

Just as Jace was about to press the assault with more fire magic, the dragonkin suddenly roared and spewed a gout of flame from its mouth!

Jace raised his cloak to shield himself — but then the stream of fire suddenly veered skyward and skewed off to one side. When he looked again, Morophil had driven her dark sword deep into the dragonkin's neck and was riding its back, wrenching its face to the side.

The dragonkin swung its staff wildly overhead in fury. The succubus ducked low, ripped her blade free, and a spray of fire-lit blood arced through the air.

The demon landed lightly. The dragonkin's staff flared bright, and a fire whirlwind struck the spot where Morophil had been standing — but she was already darting out of the flames, leaping clear with nothing but smoke and sparks clinging to her skin.

Red runes flickered across her skin. The imp shouted from a distance, "Thank me for saving your life, Sayad! Hah!"

While the succubus kept it occupied, Jace began channeling Life Drain, aiming to further sap the dragonkin's ability to fight. The moment the Life Drain's power took hold, the creature's whole body shuddered — but it whipped its head around and started chanting.

Seeing the dragonkin begin to cast, the succubus, who had only just been battered by fire, shot him an urgent look and broke into a sprint back toward it — but she was a step too slow.

The dragonkin's fire-casting speed was terrifyingly fast. A fireball hurled from its palm, and Jace had to abandon the Life Drain channel, throwing up his cloak and diving aside.

The fireball struck the spot where he'd been standing. The explosion lit up half the ravine, and the heat washed over Jace. His cloak, collar, and hair whipped in the scorching wind, and the arcane brilliance flowing over his Mage Armor shimmered like a dazzling rainbow of drifting lights…

Despite how close it was, he barely felt any burn — just a hint of warmth that barely qualified as a threat.

The dragonkin mage had chosen melee for a reason. For Jace, fire magic of this level was no longer dangerous.

Even as the dragonkin cast, Morophil stepped on its hind leg and launched herself onto its back again, driving her dark blade straight down! The dragonkin shuddered from the stab, its hind leg buckled, and it crashed to the ground!

It was in so much pain it couldn't even grip its staff anymore. Its forearms clawed frantically at the succubus on its back. Jace kept channeling Life Drain, draining the creature's vitality until the dragonkin had no strength left to resist, lying on the ground twitching.

The succubus drove her sword into its long snout, ending what little life remained. Jace walked up and took a look at that hideous reptile head, letting out a long breath. "Fire Protection Ward… what a pain."

He was a little annoyed, honestly. He'd only just learned fire magic, and his first enemy turned out to be a battle mage who could ward against it.

Still, he touched the blood trickling from his nose, then the blood at the corner of his mouth — far less than the blood that had spilled over during the Life Drain.

This time he hadn't even thrown up.

Perhaps it was because the black dragon's vitality was more compatible with his own power, fusing more perfectly. Or perhaps Jace was simply getting more skilled at Life Drain. He could feel it.

"Master's magic reminds me of the Nathrezim… but you're far more charming than they are," Morophil said, leaning in close.

Jace smiled. "Is that supposed to be a compliment?"

"Of course." Morophil narrowed her eyes. "Countless succubus are madly infatuated with their beauty, you know."

Jace couldn't exactly deny that. The dreadlords held an extremely high standing within the Burning Legion, and even by the Legion's internal standards, they were considered rather "handsome."

Jace picked up the long staff the dragonkin had been carrying. It was a fire staff slightly longer than his own. Every time the dragonkin had cast a fire spell, he'd been able to see the round red gem at its tip glowing with the light of flame.

The books mentioned that when the Tirisfal Guardian Lady Aegwynn faced the Dark Titan's mortal avatar in Northrend, she had wielded two staves — and when the first one failed under the Dark Titan's terrible magic, she'd immediately drawn the second to keep fighting.

As a warlock who commanded both shadow and fire, wasn't having two staves of different affinities perfectly reasonable?

He tied the staff to his back, then bent down and tugged the dragonkin's red hood out from under the tattered cloak on that lizard head. He patted the soot off it and put it on. It still carried a thick, fishy reptile stench.

Jace pointed at the imp. "You two hide yourselves first. I'm going to check on that man's condition. Hopefully he's not dead yet."
