Chapter 257: The Chosen One

Deep in the ravine, Jace walked step by step toward the innermost part, gradually hearing the man's low, ragged howling.

The slightly unhinged quality of the sound made him realize the other party's mind might not be entirely stable anymore.

The man was bound to one of several gnarled trees deep in the valley. He thrashed and gasped without pause, still seeming somewhat unwilling to accept that he'd been trapped here.

Up close, Jace observed that the man wasn't much older than himself. This was probably young Ronmeis, who lived in Lakeshire.

Black veins stood out against his pale skin — Jace knew what that looked like. It was what happened when shadow torment ate away at a body from the inside.

Jace had been planning to cut him down, but the moment he got closer the young man's eyes snapped toward him, sharp and hungry as a savage beast's.

"Are you Mr. Ronmeis?" Jace asked.

The man lifted his head, stammering. "How do you know my… and who are you?"

Jace held out his hand. "I'm a mage from the Wizard Sanctum. This is my sigil — do you recognize it?"

"What good is a mage?" Ronmeis craned his neck forward, eyes wide. "Lakesire is going to be destroyed soon… and humanity is going to be destroyed too. Everyone's done for. I don't understand how humans, fragile as we are, can even survive in this world. Dwarves, demons, warlocks, the undead! Every monster and beast wants to kill us… only the Path of Ascension, only… following my mentor… no, you can't. You'd better run, mage!"

Ronmeis spoke in a continuous stream, muttering under his breath all the while, though Jace hadn't been expecting much in the way of mental stability from him anyway.

"Where's your father?"

"Hah!" Ronmeis roared, as if trying to scare Jace off, then shouted, "Who gave you the nerve to ask me a question like that? I am a Chosen One about to ascend — and what are you?"

A Chosen One about to ascend? So he wanted to become a dragonkin too?

If this man had been wandering around outside or helping the dragonkin fight, Jace might actually have believed he was some kind of black dragon minion.

But the fact that he was tied to a tree and being tormented meant the dragonflight didn't trust him enough yet. That magic just now had probably been an attempt to convert him into some sort of thrall.

Jace had no idea how scrambled the man's brain really was. All he could do was try. "The dragonkin has disturbed your mind, Ronmeis. Come to your senses. Your father could be in danger. If you don't tell me where he is, he could very well die. Can you understand what I'm saying right now?"

"My father? My mind? No… how do I know you're not part of the illusion?" he suddenly gritted out through his teeth. "Don't come any closer! Liar, scoundrel! I won't believe anyone ever again. I won't yield to illusions a second time!" With that, young Ronmeis squeezed his eyes shut, his teeth clacking loudly.

Jace looked around helplessly. "Morophil, seduce him for me."

As he spoke, he stepped aside. Morophil emerged from the shadows. Sensing something unusual about the aura here, Ronmeis opened his eyes and froze.

"You… you're beautiful, my dear lady. Is this an illusion? Have you come to save me?"

Morophil said icily, "That depends on your performance, human."

"Am I going to die?" Ronmeis actually started crying. "Do I really have to wait until the moment of my death to have an encounter this wonderful?"

"Well? That depends on your performance." The succubus twirled the short blade in her hand with a light laugh. "Tell me what I want to know, human, and then I'll consider how you should remember this encounter."

The evil blade in the succubus's hand radiated a faint purple glow. Even Jace couldn't help stealing a few extra glances. Ronmeis shuddered all the more and pressed himself back against the tree stump.

"What do you want to know… my lady?" he asked, his whole body trembling, eyes fixed on the succubus.

Jace said, "Ask him about that dragonkin just now."

"What did the dragonkin do to you?" Morophil asked.

"Dragonkin?" Ronmeis said. "What's a dragonkin…"

Jace frowned. "You don't know who was just tormenting you? Then what were you screaming about?"

"Hallucinations!" the young man howled. "Hallucinations, get out of here!"

Jace shot him a glare, but sighed and turned to the succubus. "You take over…"

The corner of Morophil's mouth couldn't help twitching. She tried again. "Who was the mage tormenting you just now?"

"Tormenting me?" young Ronmeis said. "She would never torment me. She's my mentor and my friend. But I think I've hopelessly fallen in love with this woman."

The guy was certainly honest enough. But did Ronmeis think that dragonkin was female? And human?

Dragonkin were indeed divided by sex, and most females were indeed mages. Only a handful of females could become champions or overseers, because although they were calmer by nature, their bodies were generally much smaller — not unlike the naga.

"His mind has already been affected by that dragonkin's magic, master. His brain's a mess," the imp said, popping his head out and lowering his voice. "Can't the master dispel his curse? Arcanists all learn how to dispel curses! You should know how too!"

"Shut up." Jace glared at the imp, then asked the succubus, "Is there any saving him?"

"Maybe letting this human sleep would do him some good. The magical influence doesn't seem to have gone that deep yet," Morophil said.

Jace said helplessly, "Don't let him sleep yet. Find out where his father is first. Ask him the name Henbury Ronmeis."

"I'd like to know where your father is," the succubus said.

"My father… my father…"

"Henbury Ronmeis. Does that name ring any bells?"

The moment he heard the name, Ronmeis's expression twisted violently.

"I don't have a father anymore! That man is a demon. He wanted to steal my beloved. He wanted to take that beautiful lady for himself. He wanted to kill me! He tried to kill me right inside that cave, and my beloved… she actually told me to forgive him. She's so beautiful and kind. She only wants to save humanity, to pull us free from the doom of the apocalypse. What fault is hers? I can't feel her anymore. She's left me…"

Ronmeis started raving frantically, and before long he was nearly crying.

Jace looked in the direction of the so-called cave he was pointing at. It seemed to be above the ravine, outside. There was nothing to be seen from here. He should probably go up and take a look.

"Let him sleep," Jace said.

Morophil nodded, stroked his forehead, and the young man collapsed on the spot.

Jace said, "You stay here and watch him, Sazeno. Don't let any strange beasts snack on him for a midnight roast. If anything comes near, set it on fire. Morophil and I are going to find that cave he mentioned — his father might still be inside. If you run into trouble you can't handle alone, don't try to tough it out. Hide and shout my name."

"Understood, master!"
