## Chapter 258: Mental Disruption

Jace and Morophil walked uphill for a while and indeed found a cave formed by a crack in the mountainside.

Although young Ronmeis's consciousness had been muddled, his directions had been pretty accurate.

Jace glanced into the cave — pitch black, nothing to be seen. He cast Light and probed inside. The tunnel was narrow, but not very deep. It was just that the further in he went, the hotter it became.

By the time he reached a complex of deep pits in the innermost chamber, Jace was already feeling dizzy from the heat.

Morophil, on the other hand, showed no reaction at all. She walked all the way forward until she passed Jace and took the lead. Watching her composed back, Jace deeply felt the gap in racial talent.

This was a lava cave. The deep pit inside had a light source — black dragonkin loved hiding in places like this.

Jace bit his steel and was about to walk straight in, but the exaggerated temperature inside stung his eyes shut. The lava cave was simply too hot. Jace couldn't even begin to gauge the temperature inside, since anything above forty or fifty degrees was already beyond his frame of reference.

All in all, he simply couldn't last a second in there. Had the Ronmeis father and son really come in here before? It was absurd…

"Morophil, you go in and look for anything suspicious. I'll keep watch at the entrance here."

"Master, will you be all right?"

"I'm fine. It's just the heat — I can't take it." Jace backed up a few steps toward the entrance. "I'd better go back a bit, or I'm going to pass out in here. If there's danger, don't try to fight alone — come back out first."

"As you say."

Morophil walked into the lava cave. Jace sat outside in the slightly cooler passage, panting heavily, and slowly recovered. Back in there he'd felt like he was about to collapse on the spot.

Just as Jace was starting to feel a little better, he suddenly heard a rapid burst of footsteps behind him.

Jace's scalp tightened. He turned to see a dark figure lunging at him with a dagger aimed straight at his face!

He threw up his arm to block, then grappled with the assailant for a while until he gained the upper hand in strength and shoved the man back into the wall.

But that sudden burst of exertion had nearly made Jace, already dizzy from the heat, lose his footing.

At that moment, a surge of heat traveled up his arm — the dagger had pierced clean through his forearm.

The man in front of him was human, but his two eyes gleamed with a strange purple-red light. Gripping the dagger, he lunged again!

Jace raised his staff. The man hesitated, unable to commit to pressing the attack. The two of them pressed against the narrow cave wall, locked in a standoff for a moment.

Jace figured that at this distance, casting a spell might not be faster than the man stabbing at him. The guy had plenty of strength too — clearly under the influence of some curse or magic.

Was this old Ronmeis?

Just as Jace was trying to figure out how to subdue the man, a sudden gust of wind rushed from behind. Morophil appeared in front of him with her dark blade flashing!

"Don't kill him!"

Hearing Jace's shout, the succubus switched her sword to her other hand and drove a hoof into the dark figure's face, sending the man tumbling several rolls before slamming into the wall. The man scrambled up and tried to swing his dagger again, but Morophil's tail whipped across his wrists, carving several bloody furrows and sending the dagger flying far away. The tip of her sword pressed against his neck an instant later.

"What exactly are you ordering, master?! I should kill him right now!"

The succubus stared at Jace and shrieked wildly, her voice as frenzied as a wrathful demon's — nothing like the gentle tone from before.

"Morophil… don't lose your head."

"But he dared hurt you!"

Jace conjured a Light spell to illuminate the passage. The man pinned to the ground and held down was a middle-aged fellow of about forty, his face covered in stubble. His eyes still gleamed with a crazed dark purple.

Compared to this man, the succubus above him was even more terrifying. Right now she looked exactly like a black-haired demoness that had leaped straight out of some abyss.

Jace stared at her burning demonic eyes, swallowed, and said, "He's very likely the person we're looking for. I'm still counting on getting his dragon scale to complete the mission. If you kill him, we might hit a dead end…"

Only then did he realize that the excruciating pain in his forearm hadn't arrived as expected. He glanced down and saw that where there ought to have been a puncture wound, there were now only two shallow cuts — one above and one below the arm.

And blood was trickling from the corner of the succubus's mouth.

Her hand holding the sword was still trembling. Jace raised his arm. "Morophil, listen to me… see? I'm fine. Your magic saved me."

At that, the succubus's ferocious expression softened considerably. Her exposed fangs slowly retreated behind her lips, and the muscles in her arms stopped clenching.

The dark blade in her hand had already opened a bloody gash in the man's neck. By now the man's breathing had grown heavier, and he was muttering something under his breath.

He stared at the succubus clutching him, his eyes growing more and more terrified. The purple glow didn't seem as vivid as before.

"He seems to be coming back to his senses. Disappear for now…"

The succubus obeyed and vanished before his eyes. Jace closed his eyes and endured the pain in his arm for a moment, then went over to check on the man.

The man lay on the ground, one side of his face swollen purple. There was almost no trace of the shadow energy that had gleamed in his eyes before.

But he stared straight at the ceiling of the cave, showing no reaction even when Jace passed an arcane light in front of his eyes.

Jace dragged him out of the cave, propped him against a rock, and slapped him hard across his unbruised cheek. Only then did the man finally gasp a huge breath, wailed a few times out of control, and his eyes began darting around wildly.

When he made out Jace in front of him, he actually scrambled away in terror, crawling on all fours…

"Demon! Demon!" he screamed as he fled.

"Ronmeis! Don't you want to see your son?!" Jace shouted.

The man heard the name, turned back, and asked in a choked voice, "My son? Who the hell are you?"

"I'm a mage from the Wizard Sanctum." Jace held up his arm. "Do you see clearly what I am now? I came from Stormwind to rescue you."

"I, I…" Henbury stopped in his tracks, rubbed his eyes vigorously, and said, "I felt like there was a demon in there… damn it…"

"You were cursed," Jace said. "Do you still remember who cast it? Do you have any recollection of what happened before, or have you forgotten everything?"

"Curse?" Henbury patted his forehead. "Right… yes. There was a woman in the cave…"

Jace understood — father and son had both been played by a single dragonkin mage. Just then, Henbury muttered to himself for a while, then suddenly said, "No, damn it, my son — my son is still in there! Mage!"

He spoke and tried to dart back into the cave. Jace ran over and grabbed him. "Your son is down below, in that ravine over there!"

He pointed in the direction of the ravine. "You see it? At the bottom of that slope. Your son was captured by a dragonkin. You really didn't know?"

"Dragonkin?" Henbury asked anxiously. "What's a dragonkin… how did it catch my son? I have to save him, mage. You have to help me."

Jace grabbed him by the collar and shook him hard. "Get a hold of yourself, damn it. I've already saved him. He's waiting for you down there right now."
