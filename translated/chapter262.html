Chapter 262: An Excellent Assistant

Jace deliberately chose the middle of the night to return to Stormwind.

Bringing two demons along, he didn't want to charge straight through the city gates while mages and Church of the Holy Light folk were still strolling about the streets.

All in all, the Redridge trip had gone fairly smoothly. They hadn't wasted much time, and they'd reached Stormwind on Sunday evening — no classes missed. Still, the damn troubles on the road had been endless.

And he hadn't expected to actually kill a dragonkin.

That night he finally got a proper sleep. The next morning, Jace carried the wooden box of bullets and the bag containing the dragonkin's head all the way to the Mage Quarter, returned the horse, and went to find Malin.

He pushed open the door to find Master Malin standing behind his desk, stretching his lower back.

Hearing the sound, the mage turned his head. "Mr. Sesso, did you return yesterday?"

"Yes, Master." Jace set the bag of things on the floor.

Malin asked, "What's this? The ranger didn't want to hand over the scales, or did you not offer a price he found satisfactory?"

"The scales are gone." Jace opened the bag. "This fellow destroyed them, or stashed them somewhere. Mr. Ronmeis doesn't know where they ended up either."

"What's this?" Malin came around the desk for a closer look. "A dragonkin's head? This is... a black dragonkin? You killed it?"

Jace cleared his throat. "Master, I suited up in a full set of fire-resistant gear, relied on Mage Armor and the King's Cloak, and dueled this fire mage. The results speak for themselves."

"A mage is always prepared," Malin muttered. "This is a dragonkin mage? I'd say you're fully qualified for apprentice mage status, Mr. Sesso. The School of Abjuration isn't just theory and incantations — practice matters enormously. Making targeted preparations before a fight is critical... by the Light, this is a dragonkin mage!"

Hearing his teacher say that, Jace felt a twinge of guilt. He honestly hadn't expected to run into a dragonkin fire mage either... When he'd been making plans at that mountain pass, he couldn't say he hadn't hesitated at all.

He'd just felt that all the money and effort he'd poured into acquiring various fire-resistant equipment and magic shouldn't go to waste.

"Honestly, Master," Jace said, "I feel like my current salary is starting to lag behind the dangers of this assistant job..."

"That's true — I hadn't anticipated difficulties of this magnitude either," Malin nodded. "At first I thought it was just a matter of meeting someone and getting some information. May I have this head? I'll pass it along to Master Andromath and speak with him about securing the reward you deserve. And you'll need to give me a full account of what happened, along with your thoughts."

"Of course," Jace said. He also deliberately rolled up his sleeve, showing off the bandage on his arm for his teacher.

"You've worked hard." Malin tied the bag back up and placed it on a cabinet behind the desk. Even after sitting back down, his eyes kept drifting toward it.

Jace gave a rough account of the whole affair. Aside from portraying the battle in the mountain pass as a desperate, wits-against-wits duel between himself and a dragonkin mage where he'd nearly lost his life, he was mostly straightforward about the rest.

When he'd finished, Jace came straight to the point: "Master, I think the black dragons want to extend their influence to Blackrock Mountain. They want to help the Blackrock orcs defeat the Dark Iron dwarves. This dragonkin alone is proof of their ambitions. Ronmeis said he was deceived in Lakeshire and lured into the cave where this dragonkin was hiding, and ended up cursed. I think the dragonkin wanted to cover up the discovery of the dragon scales and continue developing the black dragon cult. Lakeshire may already have been infiltrated by such a group."

"I agree with your speculation, but we shouldn't jump to conclusions," Malin said. "We need more evidence to prove it. But I think the information you've brought back is enough to convince the King to send intelligence operatives to investigate Lakeshire."

The so-called intelligence operatives were, of course, SI:7 agents.

In the game, they were also the agency primarily responsible for investigating black dragon conspiracies. If Jace's investigative findings could help SI:7 against the black dragons, then Onyxia and the Black Dragonflight's damage to Stormwind — and the entire kingdom — might be lessened compared to the other timeline.

Of course, considering the ultimate impact of what Jace had done in Dalaran, he couldn't say for certain how things would turn out in the end.

With his abilities, all he could do was help as much as he could.

Jace asked, "Master, about that apprentice mage business earlier... you weren't joking, were you?"

"Of course not." Malin said, "Considering you've already proven capable of slaying a dragonkin mage on your own, I can vouch for you... plenty of full-fledged mages have never even seen a dragonkin. I'd say some of them don't even believe dragonkin exist. In any case, I'll try to help you apply for an exemption from the magical aptitude exam."

"Still, you'll need to carefully study the School of Abjuration's magic, Mr. Sesso. If you want to take the exam sooner, you can't always follow along in the classroom — because the other students taking those classes are teenagers who plan to spend years earning their promotion to apprentice mage."

"I imagine the theoretical knowledge test shouldn't be a problem, given your memory."

"I understand." Jace immediately felt a surge of energy.

Malin said, "So prepare well, Mr. Sesso. Oh, one more thing — Master Caro Dushan is willing to act as your reference, and he's the one who looked after you after you were wounded by the black dragon's magic, correct?"

Jace nodded. "Yes, Master. I stayed in a house he rented while I was in Dalaran. I'm truly grateful for his help."

"Things are going smoothly." Malin nodded. "Besides, the letter to the Kirin Tor is already on its way. We should receive a reply from the Magical Defense Division in a couple of days. I don't think there'll be any issues."

Jace nodded. He hoped Kel'Thuzad was up to his neck in work and paid the reference letter no mind at all.

"Has there been a reply from Quel'Thalas?" Malin asked.

"Still looking for someone to deliver the letter," Jace said. "But I've already written it, and soon I can—"

"The elves I know aren't in Stormwind right now. Unfortunately I can't help you there." Malin lowered his eyes. "Er, what's inside this wooden box? Is it related to this mission?"

Jace looked at the wooden box at his feet, which he'd originally used for books and was now holding Dark Iron bullets. "Ah, just some souvenirs I brought back from Lakeshire, Master. For a friend."
