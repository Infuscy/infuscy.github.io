Chapter 264: Good Eye for People

After his morning class, Jace planned to go buy some more ink and paper, then have lunch at the Gilded Rose.

The moment he walked in, he felt the atmosphere in the tavern was off. A group of people at a table near the bar kept stealing glances toward a seat in the back.

He casually glanced that way and was surprised to find Vereesa sitting there. Vereesa had clearly spotted him too and waved him over.

"May I sit here, Miss A'selwyn?" Jace asked as he approached.

"Of course." Vereesa said. "Jace, it's been a while."

"It has. When did you get back?" Jace sat down.

Vereesa said wearily, "The day before yesterday."

"Didn't go well?"

"I don't know if I'd call it well or not." She ran a hand through her silver bangs. "I did find something. I think it's related to a death knight."

Jace said, "Then how is that not going well? I have something for you too. Earlier, in the Riverpaw gnolls' territory, I found some parchment that looked like necrotic knowledge a death knight might have carried. It looked deeply wrong, but you hadn't come back this whole time."

The elf let out a breath. "I was afraid coming back had been a waste of time."

"So why did you come back?" Jace asked. Vereesa's lips moved soundlessly for a moment. "I feel like I've hit a dead end everywhere. Wandering aimlessly through the forest didn't seem to be getting me anywhere, so I came back. And I ran out of money."

Somehow, Jace wasn't surprised in the slightest.

"You didn't bring money with you?"

"I did bring some," Vereesa said. "But clearly not enough. I'm not very familiar with the conditions here... the wilderness is quite troublesome. Right now I don't feel like a very competent farstrider at all."

"You haven't even graduated yet. You're just a student," Jace said. "I heard from Kafal you had the best grades in your batch."

"I should be the best, shouldn't I?" Vereesa asked. "I mean..."

She glanced at the people nearby and lowered her voice. "You know..."

"I know." Jace looked at Vereesa. "You haven't ordered yet? What do you want to eat?"

"Anything?" Vereesa asked.

"Try not to order anything too expensive..." Jace said in a low voice.

Vereesa nodded. "I'll have what you had last time, all right? Except for that glass of wine. By the way, do they serve fish here?"

"Stormwind has a port." Jace called the server. "Almond chocolate cake and a salt flatbread, one piece of sweet cheese, and a poached salmon. Anything to drink?"

"I'm fine!"

"Then I'll have a glass of Morning Dew wine."

After the server left, Jace said in a low voice, "I didn't just get casting materials from the gnolls' place. There's also a weapon I suspect belonged to a death knight. And most importantly, a corpse. You remember the husk that Teron Gorefiend left behind? It was almost identical to that thing."

At that, Vereesa leaned forward. Jace could practically hear the faint hum of arcane energy radiating from her eyes.

"You found these things in the gnolls' territory? Where is that?"

"You know Westfall Keep?"

"Ah, I know it. That's where the Riverpaw gnoll chief is — the one on the wanted poster. When I was short on cash I thought about trying my luck there, only to find the wanted criminal who hadn't been killed for months had died just these past few days. Ugh, it's like everything's working against me."

"Uh." Jace said, "That was us."

Vereesa blinked, and as the server set Jace's wine glass in front of her, she couldn't help but laugh. "I don't know whether to call that luck or bad luck. Can the death knight's corpse still be found? Can you take me there? I can head out right now."

"We burned it," Jace admitted. "That corpse seemed able to raise the dead into undead. The gnolls figured that out too, so to stop the necrotic spread we burned the whole place down. But everything we could pull out is right here with me — the things I wanted to show you."

Vereesa asked anxiously, "When can I see them?"

Jace had been planning to sell them for some money, but the high elf in front of him was clearly even broker than he was.

He cleared his throat, and Vereesa narrowed her eyes as if catching on.

"Let me guess — you want some form of payment, don't you?" she said. "Relax. When I get back to Quel'Thalas, if it really is something related to necrotic knowledge, I think the Reliquary will take it. Then I'll find a way to have someone send you the money. How does that sound?"

Jace nodded. "Then I'll leave it to you. I'm not selling that sword for money, but I'll definitely show you. So what did you find?"

"Some fabric." Vereesa said. "I think it's clothing a death knight wore — deteriorated over time and left behind as fabric. It clearly reeks of an orcish death knight."

"Where did you get it?"

"The Great Graveyard — Raven Hill's Great Graveyard."

"Did you go alone?"

"Yes."

"There are a lot of ghouls there." Vereesa popped an entire pastry into her mouth and spoke while chewing. "They are, but they're not much of a threat, Jace. They're not very sharp. If you went, you'd handle them easily too."

Jace watched Vereesa say this so casually, and recalled the scene of him and Grede being chased all over the place by a few ghouls that day. He could only manage a stiff nod.

The server brought over the still-steaming poached salmon. Jace looked at the cut sections of fish and thought for a moment. "So that corpse was brought by the gnolls from the Great Graveyard to the south bank of the Nafeltari River."

"You're sure it's the same death knight's corpse?"

Vereesa scooped a piece of fish into her mouth with a spoon, stuck out her tongue from the burn, and asked, "What if there are multiple death knights roaming the Kingdom of Stormwind?"

"Then I'm not staying here any longer. Time to move to Boralus Harbor early." Jace sighed. "But your suspicion makes sense. How about we take everything out and look? Can you sense the magical traces on these items?"

"You mean like the Reliquary mages do? Certainly not." Vereesa said confidently. "But maybe we won't need such complicated magical abilities to tell if they belong to the same person."

Jace suddenly wondered — that weapon had been carried by the succubus all the way around the Redridge Mountains. Would Vereesa be able to smell it on him?

But if he washed it with Purification Potion, there was a risk of washing away the death knight's aura too. Then again, since it was a demon's weapon, having a demonic scent on it shouldn't seem strange...
