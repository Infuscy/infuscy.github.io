Chapter 272: The Only Companion

"Master Thomas Waller, I'm an apprentice mage from Stormwind. I've come looking for herbs. I heard you're the finest alchemist and herbalist in Duskwood Town, so I was hoping I could ask you a few questions about herbalism."

After a long pause, the cabin door slowly opened a crack.

A man in his fifties or sixties peered through the gap at Jace, then at the elf behind him, and said, "You say you're a mage from Stormwind — let me see your ring."

Jace held out his ring. The man asked again, "And this elf — what's her role?"

"Bodyguard," Jace said. "I heard these forests have gotten dangerous. I needed to hire some help."

At that, Vereesa shot Jace a sidelong glance.

"I am Thomas Waller," Waller said. "What kind of potion are you looking to make?"

"Shadow Protection Potion," Jace said. "We're dealing with gnolls, sir. Quite a few gnoll warlocks have gotten hold of shadow magic, so we need a lot of Shadow Protection Potion to support the fight."

"What herbs does a Shadow Protection Potion require?" Waller asked. "Are there places in Duskwood where these herbs grow?"

"Grave Moss, Kingsblood, and Silverleaf all grow in Duskwood, but we searched for a long time... and couldn't find any Grave Moss," Jace said. "So I was hoping to ask you, sir. And, of course, the brewing process as well."

Waller glanced back into the house. "Let me look through my notes, young man."

He gave Jace's book a final once-over. "Come in. Don't touch anything."

Jace nodded and stepped inside. He swept his eyes over the room's furnishings — it felt like an ordinary scholar's dwelling.

"Sit!"

Waller went to his bookshelf, searching through it constantly, and finally pulled out a map. He put on his glasses and studied it. "Grave Moss is indeed hard to find. You can't go to Raven Hill anymore — that's gotten dangerous. But I know a few places where you can find Grave Moss without having to mess around with corpses."

"I don't plan to go out gathering herbs anymore, but these secret herb spots I've collected — I can't bear to let them be forgotten. That'd be too much of a waste."

"Let me tell you — south of the main road between Glenton and Raven Hill, if you keep heading south you'll reach the hills that were once occupied by trolls. There are vast stretches of Gurubashi Empire ruins there, burying many ancient jungle trolls. And the land there is as if bewitched by the trolls — Grave Moss always grows there. But the troll corpses aren't affected by dark power..."

"There's a place like that?" Jace asked. "Won't the trolls come back? Aren't there traps or something?"

Waller explained, "After the Gurubashi War, the trolls abandoned that area. As for traps, most of them have failed by now. So it's become a safe place to gather Grave Moss — and no one fights you over it, since nobody dares go deep in."

The old man grew more and more excited as he spoke, and Jace found himself getting drawn in.

If this seasoned herbalist and alchemist was telling the truth, could he find a chance to go dig up a ton?

"Just watch your step there. It's full of low slopes, short cliffs, and troll-built steps that don't match a human stride. Eliza broke her ankle there once when she came with me. Even with Wild Steelbloom it took two months to heal..."

At that, he hesitated. The excitement on his face suddenly drained away.

He put the map back, glanced at Jace, and coughed twice. "Sir, you'd better leave. I've already told you how to find Grave Moss."

Jace had barely been sitting for a few minutes — Waller's sudden dismissal caught him off guard.

He certainly wasn't leaving yet. "Mr. Waller, do you know what's been going on at Raven Hill lately? You seem to know a lot about the Great Graveyard. Would the Grave Moss from the Great Graveyard be different in quality from the Grave Moss at the place you described? The more dangerous the place, the better the herb quality, right?"

Waller's expression had already turned unpleasant.

"You really should leave, young man. What I know about the Great Graveyard is just hearsay..."

"Tom... are you calling me?"

A weak woman's voice suddenly came from the next room.

"Eliza, go back. It's nothing." Thomas Waller stood up, stumbling to the door, and said through it, "I was just mentioning you to the guest. You're not well yet... you should rest."

"Tom... are you calling me?"

"Thank you for caring, but you really should go back."

Vereesa glanced at Jace, mouthing the word — Zombie.

Jace nodded.

His wife was already dead. Perhaps that was why he'd moved out of Duskwood Town.

"Tom... are you calling me?"

Waller was silent for a moment. He stepped across the threshold toward them, then suddenly drew a knife from his belt!

Jace reached out, seized Waller's wrist, and slammed him against the wall. Behind him, Vereesa had already gripped the long knife at her waist.

"I'm sorry! You have to die!"

Waller screamed wildly, struggling with all his strength, but he was far too weak and old. Pinned by Jace — a young man tainted by black dragon blood — he had virtually no resistance. The knife was easily snatched away and thrown onto the table.

Vereesa stepped through the doorway. Watching her figure flash past, Jace felt a chill run through his mind.

"Don't kill her yet!"

A frenzied snarl came from inside. Vereesa frowned and backed out. A deathly-white woman lunged out — the elf sidestepped, snatched up the knife from the table, and hurled it in one motion. It punched straight through the woman's arm bone and pinned her to the wall. The zombie couldn't pull it free.

Jace didn't know whether to be more stunned by the accuracy and force of Vereesa's throw, or by the zombie's appearance.

Eliza was clearly already dead, yet her skin was smooth, her pale complexion that of a woman in her thirties — not the same age as Waller, in his fifties or sixties.

And judging by her features, she was a remarkably beautiful woman.

"Eliza!" Waller screamed, nearly mad. "I'll kill you! I'll turn you all into undead servants to tend to my wife for all eternity!"

Vereesa glanced at Jace. "What do you want to do?"

Jace didn't answer right away. He gripped Waller's neck and said, "Do you want your wife to die again, sir? I can have this elf smash her pretty face in this instant!"

Waller shouted, "Go to where the dead belong, you fraud! Shadow curse you!"

"Vereesa — on three, two, one, you blow that bitch's brains out!" Jace roared. "Three!"

"I'll kill you! I'll kill you!"

"Two! One!"

"No! No! No!" Waller burst into tears. "Don't... no... whatever you want, just don't touch her... I have nothing but her!"
