## Chapter 276: Contraband

On the road back to Stormwind, Jace kept turning over his plan to head for the Blasted Lands.

He had never made any real preparations for dealing with orcs. Even though he did not intend to actually fight this time, he had to be ready for the possibility of a chance encounter.

The Shattered Hand clan was a bunch of vicious, battle-hungry lunatics. Even a routed remnant that had been scattered across the Black Swamp was not to be taken lightly.

With Sylvanas's combat strength and his own magic, Jace figured he could handle a patrol of three or four. But if they were spotted by a large force, there were no guarantees.

To guarantee safety, he needed the ability to pull out at any moment from a danger he could not handle.

That meant invisibility potions.

Since he had started studying alchemy, Jace had never looked into invisibility potions. He had glanced at the entry in an alchemy book once, seen a line about the high difficulty and cost of production, and never looked back.

Riding through the night, he did not reach Stormwind until late Monday evening. Jace barely managed to get Morophil settled and asleep before he woke again at three or four in the morning.

He could not rest easy until he went to ask Ursula.

The cold of the pre-dawn hours, the sky still pitch black — the Mage Quarter already had apprentices coming and going. The Wizard Sanctum had no shortage of diligent overachievers.

Jace arrived at the still-shut tavern door, knocked, then pounded on it hard until it cracked open a sliver.

Tharle peered out. Without those white eyes, Jace could barely make out that there was anyone in the gap at all.

"I thought it was Galders. What are you doing here so early?"

"Is Ursula Deline here?"

"She's here. You two hooking up too?"

"All you ever think about is hooking up. I need her for something urgent."

Tharle sighed and pulled the door open. Jace hurried inside.

The warlocks had all gone home in the middle of the night. Only Ursula was still tinkering in the basement.

Hearing footsteps behind her, Ursula glanced back and said in surprise, "What's going on? Why are you here so late?"

Jace looked at his pocket watch. "You should be asking why I'm here so early, Ursula. I wanted to ask you about invisibility potions."

"Invisibility potions?" Ursula flicked her hair back. "What do you want those for? You know how insane Stormwind's controls on invisibility potions are — you'd be investigated just buying the materials from an herbalist."

"Relax," Jace said. "I'm not going to use them in Stormwind. I've got a commission that involves orcs. I need invisibility potions to keep my hide intact."

Ursula nodded. "I can make them, but like I just said, I can't get the materials to make them for you."

"What do you need?" Jace said. "I'll do my best to find them."

"There are two recipes," Ursula said. "They last different lengths of one. One needs Fadeleaf and Wild Steelbloom. The other needs Ghost Mushroom…"

"I'm guessing the one with Ghost Mushroom lasts longer, right?" Jace asked.

Ursula nodded. "Not only that, it's more stable. But the difficulty of gathering Ghost Mushroom — well, actually, Fadeleaf is hard to come by too, Jace. None of these materials grow anywhere in the Kingdom of Stormwind. If you go buying Wild Steelbloom or Fadeleaf, you'll almost certainly get noticed by the mages or by SI:7."

"If it were me, I might buy some Wild Steelbloom, since it's a relatively common herb and cheaper. Fadeleaf… every drug related to Fadeleaf has to do with light and vision, and it's expensive. You might have to go gather it yourself in Stranglethorn."

Jace sighed. "So there's still no way around going there."

"You could try your luck in the Black Swamp — I hear there's more Fadeleaf there," Ursula said. "But I hear there's still fighting there, plenty of orc stragglers. Of course, even if the orc invasion had never happened, that place would be dangerous enough."

"Forget it, then," Jace said. "Can you tell me where Fadeleaf is usually found? If it's in Stranglethorn."

"Swampland." Ursula glanced at the bookshelf behind her. "It grows in the drier patches of the swamps. Watch out for fin-fish. I can draw you a map — I should have a diagram of Fadeleaf here somewhere…"

Jace stopped her. "No rush. Since I can't get my hands on it right away, I'll have to think harder about whether to go to Stranglethorn. And I should be able to find an herb diagram in the Wizard Sanctum library. Have you drunk an invisibility potion before?"

Ursula said, "Of course I have. Otherwise how would I dare claim I can make them?"

"How effective are they?" Jace said. "Or any tips for using them, things to watch out for, that sort of thing."

"Invisibility potions are dangerous, Jace," Ursula said. "So the demands on the alchemist are very high, and the production cost is very expensive. But since I promised to make your potions for free, I won't ask for much more from you."

"They convert your own mana to produce a light-bending effect. That effect takes time to work — meaning you don't turn invisible the moment you drink it, you fade gradually. And this invisibility works not only on your enemies, but on you as well. While the potion is active, you'll feel like a blind man who can only see a dim, hazy light."

"But the enemies can't see me at all, right?"

"Of course. As long as I'm the one who made it…"

"So if I memorize the terrain around me before drinking it, I can run, right?"

"You can do a lot of things." Ursula counted on her fingers. "Escape, steal, even attack. But no casting — mana fluctuations interfere with the invisibility. Speaking of which, are you planning to assassinate some orc officer?"

Jace said, "I'm planning to get close to an orc camp and scout out the situation. I'm worried I'll be spotted by orcs, and my legs can't outrun them, so I want some invisibility potions as a lifeline."

"Did the old men at the Wizard Sanctum send you?" Ursula said with a light laugh. "If they had any conscience, they'd just give you invisibility potions outright, or teach you an Invisibility spell or something. Though for a young mage like you, Invisibility isn't an easy spell to learn — it's even more dangerous than the potion."

Jace said, "If I have to deal with an orc warlock, besides a Shadow Protection Potion, what other potions would you recommend? Any suggestions?"

Ursula said, "Anti-Magic Potion? A common choice for dealing with spellcasters. If you're dealing with demon minions, Demonslayer Potion. If that warlock uses fire magic, Fire Protection or the like. If your budget is high there are even more excessive options, but you clearly don't look like someone with a generous budget."

"Is there a potion that can cure Necrotic Poison?" Jace asked.

"You're not going to fight a death knight, are you?" Ursula asked in surprise.

"Maybe," Jace said. "At least I need to be prepared for it." Ursula said, "You'd better just bring a priest along with you."

"That's not an option, and I'd need to prepare more invisibility potions on top of that, wouldn't I?" Jace shook his head. "I'll start by figuring out the Fadeleaf."

"Oh, right, Jace." Ursula carefully took down a bottle of grayish potion from the top of the cabinet. "Take a look at this first."

- **Contraband:** 违禁品 → "Contraband" (chapter title; refers to invisibility potions as controlled substances).
- **Shattered Hand clan:** 碎手氏族 → Shattered Hand clan (glossary).
- **Ursula Deline:** 厄苏拉·德林 → Ursula Deline (glossary: Ursula, alchemist/warlock at the Slaughtered Lamb).
- **Tharle Mor:** 札雷·莫尔 → Tharle Mor (glossary; bartender at the Slaughtered Lamb). His white eyes are a distinguishing feature.
- **Galders:** 加尔德斯 → Galders (glossary; warlock in the Slaughtered Lamb circle).
- **Fadeleaf / Wild Steelbloom / Ghost Mushroom:** 枯叶草 / 野钢花 / 幽灵菇 → WoW herbs, rendered by canonical name.
- **Fin-fish:** 鳍鱼 → "fin-fish" (Ursula's colloquial term for murlocs; not in glossary, kept flavorful).
- **Invisibility potion mechanics:** Gradual fade, works on user too (near-blindness during effect), mana fluctuations break it — all kept per source.
- **Anti-Magic Potion / Demonslayer Potion / Fire Protection:** 抗魔药 / 屠魔药 / 火焰防护 → WoW potion names.
- **Necrotic Poison:** 亡灵腐毒 → Necrotic Poison (glossary; also called Corpse Poison).
- **Mana Essence:** 魔精 → Mana Essence (troll-derived alchemical material).
- **Gurubashi ruins:** 古拉巴什废墟 → Gurubashi ruins (between Duskwood and Stranglethorn).
