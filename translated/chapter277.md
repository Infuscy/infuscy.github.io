## Chapter 277: Flame Amplification

"What is this?" Jace asked.

Ursula said with a showy air, "Remember that new potion I told you I was always experimenting with? I've now used the fire-element residue you extracted from the gunpowder you gave me to achieve the effect I wanted. If the recipe is right, this potion can raise the user's level of resonance with the fire element and further strengthen fire-magic power. Just think what a price this potion could fetch if sold to the fire mages at the Wizard Sanctum."

"Do you know fire magic, Jace? You're the only mage I know right now. I remember most mages start by learning fire magic after they begin their studies — if I didn't know you, I honestly wouldn't have had the confidence to find someone to test it on."

Jace said, "I do. You want to test it now? There isn't any danger, is there?"

Ursula turned in a circle. "I already drank it myself. See? Still alive."

"But I don't know any magic, so even if I drink it I can't test out its real power, right?"

"Fair enough." Jace nodded. "Let's go take a look in the practice room."

"I knew you'd be willing to help." Ursula took out two Basic Mana Potions and some discarded old clothes from the cabinet and followed Jace to the practice room.

She hung up a piece of clothing in front of them. "Try it without drinking first. Let me get a sense of what your fire magic feels like in its normal state. Don't control your magic — just cast in the most standard way."

"You're overestimating me," Jace said. "Being able to cast arcane magic in a standard state is already pretty good for me."

"Then that's even better," Ursula said. "That way the effect will be easier to see."

Jace shot her an unhappy look, readied his casting stance, and fixed his eyes on the garment.

He murmured the incantation for Scorch. A sudden blast of fiery wind blew the sleeve clean off, and the whole garment went up in flames at once.

The pitch-black practice room was lit bright as day by the firelight. Ursula's eyes went wide in shock.

"Fire magic… that's incredible!"

"Stop messing with me. I don't know how many times I've practiced to reach this half-baked level," Jace said. "Give me the potion!"

Ursula handed over the potion. "Galders says your shadow magic is terrifyingly strong. I figured a person can't be especially good at everything, right?"

"Hah, that's true enough." Jace held up the bottle. "How much do I drink?"

"Just a small sip."

Jace took a light sip and immediately felt as if he'd been slammed with some terrible liquor.

He barely managed to swallow it before a scorched, burnt taste shot straight through his throat. He held it in for a long while before he could choke out a few coughs. "This… this really isn't going to kill anyone, is it?"

"I never said it tasted good." Ursula was already changing into another garment. "Quick, try again and see the effect."

Jace frowned hard, nodded, fixed his eyes on the clothing, and began chanting the Scorch incantation again. Mid-cast, something already felt off.

As the incantation finished and the casting gesture came to an end, a violently disproportionate burst of flame suddenly exploded from the garment! The fire swallowed half the room in an instant!

"Go!" Jace had felt something was wrong well before, so his reaction was lightning-fast. He hurled Ursula out of the room in one motion, dove into the hallway himself, and slammed the door shut behind him.

The blast of heat that followed blew the door open again. The metal door crashed into the wall with several loud booms, the noise echoing back and forth through the entire basement.

Jace sat on the ground, glanced through the door crack at the still-burning scraps of cloth, then looked back at the stunned alchemist behind him. "Isn't that a bit too strong?"

Ursula swallowed. "Jace, you scared me half to death just now… I really didn't expect it to turn out like that."

"Can you give me the rest of this bottle?" Jace asked.

"I'll make you a whole new bottle, Jace. Thank you for helping me test it. That effect… far exceeded my expectations!"

"But do you still have any of the Dark Iron dwarves' gunpowder?" Jace asked.

"Now that it's proven to work, I can try contacting people to buy it directly." Ursula caught her breath. "If this batch of potion can be sold to mages at a good price, Gajin might agree to connect me with the Dark Iron dwarves. Before I knew whether it would work, asking him to arrange something like that once — if it failed… it would be too much wasted effort."

"Fine," Jace said. "I'll also need some Shadow Protection Potions, Ursula. This time I want the strong kind — the sort that can only eat half a shadow spell, I don't think that's enough anymore."

"How many do you want?"

"Six bottles," Jace said.

Ursula said, "The cost of making six Strong Shadow Protection Potions is no small sum. Dreamfoil is even harder to gather than Ghost Mushroom. If you buy it in the Mage Quarter, a single stalk might run you sixty or seventy silver. Making six bottles would need at least two stalks."

"Where's the nearest place that has Dreamfoil?"

"There's some in Duskwood," Ursula said. "You know that dead volcano in southern Elwynn?"

Jace asked, "You mean that raised hill between Raven Hill and Duskwood — the middle of it is a dead volcano?"

"So the legend goes," Ursula said. "A lot of people say the forest inside that crater is like a dreamland — even the creeping darkness hasn't managed to swallow it completely."

"That's where Dreamfoil grows. But it's also an extremely dangerous place. In my view, so-called dreamlands are usually tied to imperceptible hallucinogenic components. I suspect… there might be creatures living there that cause hallucinations."

Terrifyingly powerful creatures… could there already be green dragons lurking in there?

"Do you have any… Dreamfoil?" Jace asked in a low voice. "Cheaper — scratch the six bottles, I'll take two or three, how about that?"

"How could I have something like that?" Ursula said. "Even if I did, I'd have sold it long ago to fund my other potions. Frankly, that kind of Shadow Protection Potion only suits mages with more money than they know what to do with — the cost-performance ratio is far too poor. I still have four or five regular Shadow Protection Potions. A few days ago Gajin gave me a bunch of materials specifically to make a batch for an experiment. I can give you what's left over."

"Then let's do that. Thanks."

Jace also felt that, given his current strength, it was still too early for him to set foot in a place like that.

"So, are you planning to go to Stranglethorn?" Ursula asked.

Jace said, "I'm not sure I have the time for such a long trip. Maybe I'll go back to Duskwood and see if anyone's selling Fadeleaf. Last time I was in Duskwood I noticed quite a few people selling things from Stranglethorn — furs, herbs, and the like."

"That's a shame. I was hoping you could bring me something back from Stranglethorn." Ursula leaned against the wall and sighed.

"Bring what?"

"Mana Essence." Hearing the strange word, Jace's head buzzed.

"You want me to go fight trolls?!"

He nearly shouted it. His throat, still burning from that potion, made him break into a fit of coughing.

Ursula looked at his expression and laughed. "So you actually know what Mana Essence is! You don't necessarily have to snatch it from trolls. You know those Gurubashi ruins between Duskwood and Stranglethorn… there are plenty of things the trolls stored away in there, and there might well be Mana Essence among them. Helping me won't leave you empty-handed either, Jace. Look at this bottle… Ignition Cocktail — I'm calling it that for now. How powerful is that potion? Just imagine if that ball of fire had exploded on an orc."

Jace listened while shaking his head. "Whatever it is, let's put the troll business aside for now."
