## Chapter 278: Friend of the Elves

After the morning Orcish class, Jace brought Sylvanas's letter of guarantee to Master Marin's study.

He had wanted to peek at the contents, but the letter was carefully sealed, the wax stamped with Sylvanas's Windrunner family crest — meant to give the impression it had made the long journey from Quel'Thalas to Elwynn.

Entering the door, Marin was reciting something from memory. He frowned when he came in, still muttering a passage under his breath.

"Master, the elven letter has arrived."

Jace set Sylvanas's letter on the desk as he spoke. Marin picked it up and looked it over. "That was fast. The wax looks brand new. The Kirin Tor's letter hasn't even arrived yet."

"An elf mage helped deliver it. I heard they were quite enthusiastic once they learned it was a letter from Lady Sylvanas." Jace covered smoothly.

"Sounds like you have quite the connections in Quel'Thalas." Marin broke the seal, drew out the letter, adjusted his glasses, and read aloud, "Elven Thalassian script is truly beautiful… is this Lady Sylvanas's own handwriting…"

So it was in the elven language, Jace thought. He could not read it even if he looked. He ought to learn Thalassian at some point.

As he watched, Marin's expression grew more and more solemn, until his gaze came to rest on one spot and did not move again.

"An-lun, a-nu, nor… bei-luo… a-lun…" he murmured. "If my several decades of Thalassian haven't been wasted, this sentence should mean: you drank the water of the Sunwell."

"Huh?" Jace blinked. "Why would it mention that?"

"Lady Windrunner writes here that she used half a vial of Sunwell water to cleanse the death energy that Teron Gorefiend inflicted upon you, and so she has confirmed you will not carry any necrotic aura harmful to others. Is this true?"

"Yes, Teron Gorefiend—"

"I mean the Sunwell. You drank Sunwell water?"

"Uh…"

"Why didn't you mention this at all before?"

Jace scratched his head. "I did drink a little Sunwell water. I just felt that after drinking it, learning arcane incantations this slowly seemed a bit too…"

Marin said, "Not necessarily, Mr. Sesso. After all, it healed the injuries you received. Had you been struck by Teron Gorefiend and not had it, you might not have recovered so quickly — you could still be recovering even now. Don't treat a little Sunwell water as some sort of panacea. But still, ah, if only I could have a taste myself."

He shook his head, then swept his gaze across the letter once more and suddenly said, "I think I know why this letter was delivered so quickly. Although most of the wording is quite formal, there are many places where it's clear you and Lady Windrunner are on familiar terms. She must consider you her friend to have used these particular forms of address."

"So I think, in that battle, you weren't simply being hunted by Teron Gorefiend, were you?"

"I resisted a bit," Jace said. "I couldn't just wait to die, after all…"

The old mage nodded. "No matter how weak you are by comparison, when faced with a powerful evil, to dare to strike with everything you have — that is an extraordinary quality for any race. Even the habitually proud elven nobles would genuinely admire it."

He pinched the letter, let out a long sigh, and said, "Centuries ago, when the Tirisfal Guardian faced the Demon King, it was precisely because he possessed this courage to meet the charge head-on that he was able to achieve what no mortal otherwise could. And it is precisely because heroes like this continually emerge that we have been able to carry on through disaster after disaster to this very day."

Jace nodded.

"That said, you still need to work hard at your theory exam, my good student." Marin fixed his gaze on him. "Don't capsize in the apprentice-mage theory exam after going through so many dangers — even killing a drake-mage in a duel, Mr. Sesso. That would be too much to explain away."

Jace asked, "When is the next apprentice-mage exam?"

"You didn't go ask that yourself?"

"I… didn't think I'd be taking it this soon."

"It should be March twenty-second," Marin said. "A little over a month. Do you think you can master the material in that time? I can sign you up."

"I don't know," Jace said. "But I can give it a try."

"Very well," Marin said. "Speaking of which, if you pass in one go, you'd break Mr. Skraun's record and become the fastest of all my apprentices to reach the apprentice-mage stage."

"Anything else? It's almost noon now. I'm about to go eat."

"There is." Jace said, "I have a question about necromancy I'd like to report."

"Necromancy?" Marin asked. "Related to Teron Gorefiend?"

"Not my problem," Jace said. "There's a retired doctor in Gran Town named Thomas Waller. I suspect he's been researching necromancy in order to bring his dead wife back to life."

"What evidence do you have?" Marin asked. "That's a rather serious accusation, especially now, when Duskwood is in urgent need of all manner of doctors and herbalists."

Jace said, "His room carries the aura of necromancy. As a mage who has fought a death knight, I believe I'm capable of distinguishing between a shadow aura and a necrotic aura. I suspect he may have already begun attempting necromancy on his wife. I hope the Wizard Sanctum will send someone to investigate the situation as soon as possible and place him under surveillance — especially given that the situation in southern Elwynn has been worsening year after year…"

"It is not my responsibility to monitor necromantic activity within the kingdom, Mr. Sesso," Marin said. "However, Andormas mentioned last time that Lord Bolvar Fordragon has already asked the Wizard Sanctum to pay closer attention to the spread of dark power in southern Elwynn. I believe this should fall within that scope. Rest assured — this afternoon, when I go up to the tower, I'll inform Master Magino Dumas, who is currently overseeing this matter."

"Oh, right. Regarding the drake's head — Master Andormas intends to have it slightly processed for storage, so you'll certainly be paid a certain amount of compensation. Would you prefer coin, or something else?"

"Could you…" Jace tugged at his short robe. "Have an enchanter put an enchantment on this robe for me?"

"Of course," Marin said. "What would you like?"

Jace said, "Something that can improve casting efficiency — like with mana, or…"

Marin asked, puzzled, "There's an enchantment that works by keeping the wearer calm and focused, indirectly improving casting efficiency a little. Of course, if you're a manic patient, this sort of magic won't suddenly turn you into a scholar — it can only exert a slight influence."

"Then that one, Master," Jace said firmly.

## Flags

- **Friend of the Elves:** 精灵之友 → "Friend of the Elves" (chapter title; refers to Sylvanas considering Jace a friend).
- **Windrunner family crest:** 温蕾萨的风行者家族徽记 → Windrunner family crest (used to seal the letter, lending it false authenticity).
- **Thalassian script:** 萨拉斯文字 → Thalassian script (glossary: 萨拉斯语 = Thalassian, the elven language).
- **Sunwell water:** 太阳之井的井水 → water of the Sunwell (glossary: Sunwell). Marin's envy at Jace having drunk it is played for both humor and lore weight.
- **Teron Gorefiend:** 塔隆·血魔 → Teron Gorefiend (glossary; first death knight).
- **Tirisfal Guardian:** 提瑞斯法守护者 → Tirisfal Guardian (glossary: Order of Tirisfal).
- **Mr. Skraun:** 斯克劳恩先生 → Mr. Skraun (Marin's former record-holding apprentice).
- **Gran Town:** 格兰镇 → Gran Town (town in Duskwood; location of Thomas Waller).
- **Thomas Waller:** 托马斯·沃勒 → Thomas Waller (retired doctor suspected of necromancy).
- **Andormas:** 安多玛斯 → Andormas (glossary; senior mage of the Wizard Sanctum).
- **Bolvar Fordragon:** 伯瓦尔·弗塔根 → Bolvar Fordragon (glossary; Duke/regent of Stormwind).
- **Magino Dumas:** 玛吉诺·仲马 → Magino Dumas (Master currently overseeing dark-power monitoring in southern Elwynn).
- **Enchantment request:** Jace asks for a calm/focus enchantment on his robe to indirectly improve casting efficiency — Marin notes it won't cure mania, only gently influence.
- **Tone:** Marin's mix of scholarly envy, genuine warmth, and pedantic scolding kept intact.
