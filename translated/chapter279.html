Chapter 279: Enchanted Arrow

That evening, Jace had arranged to meet Sylvanas at the Gilded Rose. When he arrived at the inn, he found the elf already waiting at the entrance.

"Aren't you going in?" Jace said.

"No, Jace." Sylvanas carried her little bag up to him. "Shall we walk along the canal? I bought some pastries — we can eat as we walk."

"You don't like it in there?" Jace asked.

"Too many people stare at me. It's uncomfortable no matter what we talk about." Sylvanas pulled out a flaky pastry and bit into it. "I thought I could adapt to life in a human city, but it seems I can't, at least not yet."

"Give me one." Jace took the pastry Sylvanas handed him and stuffed it into his mouth, chewing as he spoke. "When we were in Duskwood I thought you didn't care anymore."

"That was me trying to toughen myself up," the elf said. "But this should count as a rest period between missions, right? I'd rather not make myself miserable."

Jace nodded. "There still aren't enough elves in Stormwind."

Sylvanas wrapped her cloak tighter. "Now I don't know whether wearing a hood draws more attention or not wearing one does. Lots of people wear hats outside — that's fine — but wearing one in here, I look like a thief!"

The two of them talked as they walked, eventually finding a quiet spot by the embankment to sit down.

"How did it go?" Sylvanas asked. "Did the letter work?"

"It worked wonderfully. Thank you very much."

The elf smiled. "Good. You didn't forget what we discussed earlier today, did you?"

"How could I forget," Jace said. "This morning I was thinking — since we're going to scout the situation, stealth tools are indispensable. The Shattered Hand orcs are formidable killers, and they're in exile, so they're bound to be very sensitive to their surroundings. I'm planning to get some invisibility potions to keep my hide intact."

Sylvanas pulled an apple from her bag. "Invisibility potions — I know those. Where are you getting them? Doesn't Stormwind strictly control that sort of thing?"

"It does, but there's an alchemist who can make them for me," Jace said. "It's just that she's a bit short on materials. I'm thinking of finding time to head back to Duskwood and see if anyone's selling Fadeleaf or the like. She told me there's some in Stranglethorn."

"I know Fadeleaf. I can go gather it in Stranglethorn, Jace."

"Haven't you never gathered herbs before?"

"I took herbalism courses — it's an important part of Farstrider training too. I've just never put it into practice. Let me have a try."

"Don't," Jace said. "I'll figure out the invisibility potions myself. Otherwise I'd feel too guilty taking your payment."

"Then what am I supposed to do in Stormwind these days?" Sylvanas clenched the apple core in her hand. "You still have classes, right? I get bored sitting around here. Just don't make me deal with humans — I'm better at communicating with the jungle. I'll treat it as a stroll."

"You sure?"

"Of course!"

"All right. Stay away from those Gurubashi ruins," Jace said. "Stranglethorn used to be jungle troll territory. They don't want conflict with humans right now, but we can't let our guard down."

Sylvanas said, "Fadeleaf usually appears in swamps, right? Looking at the map, most of the rivers and wetlands in Stranglethorn are far from Zul'Gurub up in the mountains. It should be fine."

"There are also troll villages and cities along the coast, so remember to keep your distance from the shore too," Jace cautioned. "And watch out for the predators there — raptors, tigers…"

The more Jace described it, the more absurd Stranglethorn seemed. No wonder anything brought out of there was so expensive.

Sylvanas said, "Who's the predator remains to be seen. I'll get ready and set out tomorrow."

"Need money?"

"How about ten silver?"

"Let me give you more." Jace set his entire coin pouch on the table.

"Thanks, this is enough."

Sylvanas lifted the pouch as she said it. Watching her, Jace could no longer connect this image with the Windrunner noble who had so generously handed him half a vial of Sunwell water to drink.

That vial, if put up in the Mage Quarter, might have been traded straight for a house.

Of course, that was just Jace's own guess. Sunwell water certainly wouldn't be brought out by the elves and sold to humans.

But there weren't many things that could surprise an archmage like Marin. Even when Jace told him he'd traded a few blows with a black dragon, Marin had only said a few words. But when it came to the Sunwell water… from the look on his face, it was as if he'd begun to curse the shortness of his own life.

Still, no one truly knew the value of Sunwell water. But how much food these silver coins could buy in Stormwind — Sylvanas had certainly come to know that very clearly over this time.

Jace pulled an apple from his bag and bit into it. "From what you said before, we're going to capture that death knight, not kill him, right? I think that's quite difficult. Take Gorefiend, for example — in the end we drove him into a corner, didn't we? He couldn't kill you, and he couldn't shake you off to deal with me… but he could still abandon his body and flee."

"I think if that death knight really is being protected by the Shattered Hand legion, even if we found a way to defeat him, we couldn't stop him from escaping the same way his superior did. Then finding his soul again would take considerable effort, let alone capturing him alive."

Sylvanas smiled. "I have a way to deal with death knights."

As she spoke, the elf produced a small bottle of golden liquid from behind her waist. "This is called Holy Warlock Oil. Applied to enchanted arrows, it wounds a death knight's physical form and prevents his soul from escaping. When the death knights invaded Quel'Thalas, this was verified many times over."

"Before, we simply didn't expect to encounter a death knight inside Dalaran, so we didn't prepare in advance — it's just that this oil is difficult to make, and the demand isn't high. Otherwise, I believe Teron wouldn't have escaped so easily."

"When the time comes, I'll coat my arrows with this oil to prevent him from escaping, then cut off his head once he's defeated — that's the part that traps that filthy orc soul. And that will be a wrap. That's what the alchemists told me."

"So that's how it works," Jace nodded. "If you have a way, then that settles it."

"So you're still planning to help me all the way through to capturing him, right?" Sylvanas leaned in.

Jace hurled the apple core straight into the river, then said, "Honestly, just finding him and getting paid as generously as that — I'd feel I hadn't earned it."
