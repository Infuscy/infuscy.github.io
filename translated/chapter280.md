## Chapter 280: Old Town Shadows

After Sylvanas set out, Jace began going from herbalist to herbalist and alchemy shop to alchemy shop, buying up Wild Steelbloom and lead vials for making invisibility potions. He bought extra, as well, to make Basic Anti-Magic Potions.

Just as Ursula had said, Wild Steelbloom was not expensive — about the same as Briarthorn. To Jace's current finances, it was nothing to speak of.

He bought enough materials for ten Lesser Invisibility Potions, plus two Basic Anti-Magic Potions on top, spending a little over seventy silver.

If he could turn out these invisibility potions, there ought to be some left over even after fulfilling Sylvanas's commission, so it counted as an investment in the future too.

In the days that followed, guided by Dushan's pointers, he dug up a great deal of intelligence on the Blasted Lands and the Shattered Hand outposts.

Although the Kingdom of Stormwind didn't know much about that region, through information exchange with Ironforge it was possible to confirm that Jace's guess was more or less correct: a number of Shattered Hand orcs had indeed settled in that area.

Whether they were at the Kargath outpost, though, was still impossible to confirm.

The rest of his time, aside from classes, was spent organizing the portion of the dictionary the imp had translated.

The Eredar language was extraordinarily difficult for a human to pronounce. Some of the distinctions between different sounds Jace simply could not hear at all, which made the imp so furious he was steaming but didn't dare show it. Still, after several days of careful training and discrimination, Jace had vaguely grasped the threshold — at least when it came to pronunciation, he more or less had the hang of it.

Sylvanas had been gone to Stranglethorn for nearly a week. Considering the travel from here to Duskwood and then into Stranglethorn, just the journey back and forth took over a week on its own, so Jace was in no particular rush.

That night, lying in bed reading through his demonic-materials notes, he suddenly heard someone shouting his name from outside.

"Jace… Jace Sesso!"

A woman, and she was screaming hysterically. There was something familiar about the voice.

He shoved his demonic notes under the bed, went to the window, and looked down. A familiar figure was standing by the canal, looking up at his place.

Not many people knew where he lived. Aside from Grede, it was just the folk from the Slaughtered Lamb.

He went out, ran down the stairs, and came to the door. The moment Sulenna saw Jace she ran over. "Jace… Gajin's in trouble."

"What happened to him? Where's Erland?"

"He… I don't have time to find him right now, Jace. Only you can help. I think someone's watching his house, Jace — I can feel it… bring your things!"

Sulenna's lips were trembling from nerves. It was already very late, and there was no one by the canal. Jace said in a low voice, "Wait here." He went back inside, picked up his short sword, and hid it under his coat. The imp and the succubus were both staring at him.

Morophil asked softly, "Do you need help, master?"

"In the city, don't you show your face."

Leaving the house, the faint sense of foreboding in his mind grew heavier and heavier. He vaguely felt that whatever had happened to Gajin might well be connected to him.

Had his demon-summoning ritual drawn the attention of SI:7 or the Wizard Sanctum?

And if so, how was he supposed to fight them?

He turned over in his mind the right way to deal with it while scanning the streets in every direction for anyone suspicious.

Jace still remembered where Gajin's house was: Old Town, opposite the fur shop, that black-tiled, single-household run-down building.

The last time he'd come here, Gajin had been arguing with Erland. You could even hear the commotion through the windows. But this time the windows were shut tight, and not a sliver of light leaked out.

The surroundings were quiet and still, with no sign of anyone approaching.

Walking up to the window, Jace pressed close and listened for a while.

No sound. That was to be expected.

"Sulenna, did you go in just now?"

Sulenna shook her head. "Something felt off. I didn't dare go in."

"What felt off?"

"I… I can't explain it."

Jace knew that although Sulenna had learned a few shadow incantations, she hadn't learned any of them solidly. She basically had no combat ability to speak of, and in an encounter she might just get in the way.

"Then wait here."

"Careful, Jace!"

Jace nodded, walked into the building, and came to Gajin's door. Just as he raised his hand to knock, he suddenly caught a whiff of a cloying, greasy sweetness.

The scent made every hair on Jace's body stand on end, and a flash of an image burst into his mind — Morophil throwing her arms around his neck, frantically demanding kisses, licking his lips and cheeks like a madwoman.

But that strange thought only flashed for half a second before the sensation of something watching him from the dark crawled up the back of his neck.

Jace took two steps toward where he was looking, raised his sword, and stabbed hard into the shadows. A shrill shriek rang out. A pale-red-skinned demon materialized from the darkness, one hand gripping his sword blade, her palm already sliced open and streaming purple blood.

Jace had just drawn his sword back when he was suddenly grabbed by the hair by the agile succubus. Her evil eye locked onto Jace, those blue-tinged-with-purple pupils staring straight into his eyes.

"Listen to me, human. There's been some misunderstanding between us…"

Her voice was so gentle that any normal person who heard it would have felt their bones turn to mush. But Jace was not normal.

He drove his sword straight into her eye. The succubus shrieked, struggling backward and crashing into the wall. Purple demon blood flew everywhere. She flapped her two narrow wings and, like a disemboweled wasp, bounded deeper into the corridor.

"Madman! You must be insane not to listen to me!"

"Tell me — what happened to Gajin?!" Jace roared, chasing after her.

"He loves me! Gajin… he loves me!"

Jace caught up in a few strides, kicked her to the ground, drove his sword into her back, and wrenched her down by her black hair.

"You know him, don't you? Tell me — what did I do wrong?" The succubus, her face covered in demon blood, twisted her head two hundred and seventy degrees to stare at him, repeating over and over, "I just wanted to make him happy. This is what he wanted…"

Jace stabbed his sword into her chest, piercing her demon heart. The succubus's head lolled to one side. Her eyes went dark. At last she stopped breathing. Her body twitched and curled into a ball.

Jace could feel her continuously, ceaselessly trying to bewitch him with charm magic. He could keep his head clear now, but he couldn't guarantee he'd still be sane a second from now.

Looking at this monster, her face twisted by rage, struggle, and fear, he couldn't help but mutter a curse in his native tongue.

Sulenna seemed to have heard the commotion inside and came running in. When she saw the dead demon, she sucked in a breath. Her face went deathly pale. She could only lean against the wall, giving one ragged, broken gasp after another.

"Wh-what is this?"

"A demon." Jace kept his voice level through the fury. "Did you know Gajin had summoned a demon?"

"I didn't know he'd actually succeeded," Sulenna said, trembling.

"So you already knew it was possible a demon had gotten in, didn't you?" Jace asked coldly.

Sulenna was speechless. Jace glared at her, then looked around.

Fortunately, this broken-down little house in the corner was so shabby and old that Gajin was the only one living here. God knows what kind of disaster it might have caused otherwise. Then again, this guy lived alone precisely to keep things from getting out of hand if a situation like this ever happened.
