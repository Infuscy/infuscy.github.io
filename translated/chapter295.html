Chapter 295: Embers Among the Ruins

The moment the dragon had swept over their heads, everything went quiet — a terrible silence. But the wind from the beating of vast wings could still hear in the distance, circling.

Jace used the opening to cast Arcane Intellect, then continued chanting the Mage Armor incantation.

Then, Vereesa suddenly looked up to the west.

"Here it comes."

The wind of rushing wings drew close, followed by a rasping screech. Jace had barely finished his chant and picked up the mana potion bottle — he hadn't even pulled the cork — when Vereesa leaped lightly onto a nearby wall, took several running steps upward, and in the same motion drew her bow to full tautness at the sky!

The black dragon's shadow suddenly emerged from the dust, a blast of flame belching down beneath the wall where the elf stood. Vereesa nimbly leaped clear; the flame smashed into the ground and sent debris flying in every direction.

Jace barely dodged the direct blast, but the shockwave sent him reeling. The mana potion in his hand was shattered in the blast.

He turned to look at the black dragon skimming past along the ground. Through the smoke and fire came the cold, sharp hiss of two arrows in quick succession. The beast took the hits on the neck and swayed — then beat its wings and climbed again. Amid the waves of heat, Jace braced himself against the wall, leveled his staff at the passing black dragon, and rapidly spoke the Curse of Weakness. The dragon shrieked in midair and vanished once more.

It hit. Jace could feel the magic seep solidly into the creature's body — but he had no idea how much a Curse of Weakness would actually affect a beast this size.

"We should get out of here," he said.

Vereesa nodded and pointed to another street. Jace left the wall and followed her.

Sprinting down the mountainside in bursts — one moment calm, the next suddenly surging forward — had left his raw throat burning and a coppery taste of blood in his mouth.

He gasped and felt for the potions at his side, only to find the ones in the bandolier had all been blown apart. As he ran he dug around inside his bag, pulled out a mana potion, and gulped it down. The uncanny sound of air churning overhead continued — the black dragon was still circling somewhere close.

The two of them darted into another street. A few seconds later, Vereesa suddenly came to a stop — Jace felt it too. The black dragon was right ahead, somewhere in the dust.

The shadow suddenly darkened and solidified. Flames wreathed the black dragon's already burning maw as it lunged straight at them. The two of them bolted to opposite sides of the street.

Jace ducked into the gap between buildings and felt a terrifying wave of heat crash into his back. Even with Mage Armor's protection, even with the cloak shielding him, it was almost unbearable.

Faint, dark-purple shadows rolled through the black smoke rising in the streets. The waves of heat toppled wall after wall nearby — dust and the rumble of collapse were everywhere.

Jace raised his staff to the sky, waiting for the black dragon to reappear — but the sound of wings had vanished.

Just as he scanned the heights, a thunderous crash erupted from behind the walls!

The dragon was behind him!

Jace leaped to one side. A shadow surged up from behind. The wind from the creature's wings sent his cloak and robe flapping wildly. Jace felt that alone the wind nearly knocked him off his feet — a moment slower and the whelp would have crushed him outright.

The ground shook with each footfall. The black dragon charged back into the street, wings flaring, as if trying to take off again.

Jace charged out over the still-burning flames, pointed at the black dragon's back, and roared at the top of his lungs: "K'BIIMOFLOSAAS!"

He wasn't even sure he'd pronounced it correctly — but just as the dragon's body lurched upward, its shoulder erupted in a burst of crimson flame, like blood suddenly spraying forth.

The dragon bellowed and veered — whipping its head around in midair.

Jace found himself staring into its yellow, serpentine eyes.

"Oh shit!"

He dove into a nearby wall and scrambled several more steps in. A chunk of magma, like a shooting star, blew the wall he'd just been hiding behind to smithereens. The blast wave sent him flying through the air, slamming into a wall before he crashed to the ground.

Jace coughed himself upright, his whole body so weak he could barely summon strength. He coughed and spat a little blood — he couldn't tell if it was internal bleeding or if a tooth had been shaken loose.

After a blast like that, the Mage Armor shimmering across his robes had begun glowing like a cluster of arcane crystals — far brighter than before. According to the spellbook's description, this was the result of absorbing an excess of magical damage. In other words, on its current form, the spell was close to breaking apart.

Mage Armor wasn't a spell shield or a barrier, but that didn't mean it could endure unlimited punishment without failing.

Once he'd finally recovered his strength, the pain hit in waves. Behind him, the black dragon screeched again and again in the sky.

He turned to look. Thin blue lines stabbed hard into the shadow wobbling across the sky. Several times the black dragon tried to veer to the side — not a single one dodged. All it could do was roar and spew flame at the ground.

It was attacking Vereesa down there!

"Sazeno… get out here!"

"Master… why do you want me to come out now?! I don't wanna come out!" the imp shrieked.

"Less talk, more help!"

Jace shouted and scrambled to his feet, running over. As he ran, he watched gouts of flame rain down from the sky — each blast shattering a stretch of rubble and billowing black smoke — but before long, another thin blue line would fly up from somewhere else and pierce right in again.

Every hit made the black dragon bellow like a mad thing.

A few rounds later, the beast angrily folded its wings and dove straight into the street in an explosion of stone and dust!

"My magic can't hurt that dragon either, Master…!" the imp wailed from somewhere behind him.

Before Jace could respond, the moment the creature landed it unleashed a horizontal blast of flame — like a military-issue flamethrower — sweeping toward him. He dropped flat, dodged it, pushed himself up, and pushed forward, charging through the fires burning everywhere to the black dragon's rear end.

By now the black dragon had reared up. One swipe of its foreclaw shattered a wall of stone in front of it. From within the shattered wall flashed a silver shadow — the elf had used the force of the black dragon's own strike to leap nimbly, seizing its arm and landing squarely on its lower neck!

The black dragon snarled in fury, raising its wings again, trying to whip up a gale and take off. Jace once again unleashed his shadow-corroded Immolate. The blast tore through the dragon's wing membrane — this time when it tried to flap, it only managed half a beat before howling in pain and dropping its wing. It turned its head back toward Jace, seething.

Before it could spew fire in his direction, Vereesa braced herself, drew a blade, and slashed a long gash through the scales and skin on its neck. The scrape of steel against dragonhide rang out shrill and clear. The black dragon threw its head back in a howl. The flames in its mouth turned into a cloud of churning black smoke. The beast began whipping its serpent-like neck back and forth in a frenzy, trying to shake Vereesa off. The elf nimbly leaped onto its back. Before she could steady herself, the creature immediately whipped its head around, trying to reach around and bite her from behind!

Jace struck the black dragon in the face with another Immolate. Crimson flame erupted at its mouth, blowing out a spray of purple-red blood. The dragon tilted its head, panting — dazed, as if stunned.

Jace leveled his staff and, without hesitation, began draining the black dragon's life force. The dragon felt the tearing of the magic and howled in agony. Its head finally couldn't keep pace with Vereesa's movements.

But it only caught its breath for a moment before its long tail rose like a war hammer behind him.

Its target wasn't him — it was Vereesa!

The tip of the tail swept diagonally across the elf's back like an iron spike. Vereesa flipped up and dodged the blow. The black dragon wheeled and swung a claw at the now-grounded Vereesa. She barely avoided the enormously wide sweep and nearly lost her footing.

The beast roared and shrieked violently — half cough, half gloating cackle. It felt like it had the upper hand, and as if emboldened by that, it screeched and lunged in pursuit.

Jace continued to drain the black dragon's life force without relent. The injuries he'd sustained from the fireball were gradually mending, his vitality surging back.

But this black dragon, even as he drained with all its might, only grew sluggish in its movements — its temper, if anything, only more savage. It shrieked madly as it pursued Vereesa, attacking without pause. The creature had gone berserk, snapping and swiping. Though Vereesa dodged every strike, it was driving her further and further back. The black dragon charged after her into the dust, and soon Jace's magic couldn't reach it anymore.

The black dragon roared, heaving up chunks of ruined wall after chunk of ruined wall. Vereesa could barely avoid the brunt of its fury — but it was clear she'd nearly been caught several times over.

Jace shouted after it in Orcish as he ran: "Black monster! Over here! Here!"

At the shouts, the black dragon suddenly turned its head.

"Wretched… whelp!"

Jace stumbled to a halt. Could it actually be working?

The black dragon drew its whole body back a great distance, then swung its long tail high and brought it crashing down. Jace threw himself to the side with everything he had, hit the ground, rolled, and scrambled up. The dragon's tail hammer smashed precisely where he'd been standing, blasting flagstones into the air and gouging a crater into the stone road.

If that had hit him, it probably would have pulped him on the spot.

For a whelp, this seemed to cost it no effort at all. It raised its tail and struck again. Jace staggered back a few steps and barely dodged. Even the rubble kicked up by the impact struck him hard enough on the forehead to nearly knock him senseless.

At last, the young dragon drew a step back and swept its tail horizontally. Jace ducked low and dodged — and heard the imp shrieking from somewhere: "You idiot, look out! You're done for!"

Jace looked up. The young dragon's eyes had ignited.

"Damn it—" The flames lit up the whole stretch of ruins as they bore down on Jace. He desperately clawed at a chunk of broken wall and dove to one side. He felt the space behind him warp — a blunt force kicked him into the sky. He tumbled through the air a few times and crashed heavily to the ground.

The Mage Armor on him was completely finished now. Inside his cloth bag came a clinking of glass — no telling how many potion bottles had shattered.

But he had no time to check. Chunks of magma and sparks landed and exploded all around him. Quite a few splashed onto him.

They didn't ignite right away, but some of the fragments that flew against his neck still made him cry out in pain.

At least he was still alive. The burning pain meant his nerves were still working. Red sigils had already begun floating across his skin — so the imp had thrown a Blood Pact ward over him to save his life.

"I'm spent, Master… you… you're on your own!" the imp wailed from somewhere.

You son of a… Jace cursed inwardly, and couldn't help but vomit a mouthful of blood.

The trouble was far from over. The black dragon beat its wings and lunged straight at him. Jace felt the massive shape growing closer behind him with every beat, and could only squeeze himself into a gap in the wall. The building was smashed apart by the black dragon's enormous body!

A chain of explosions and impacts. Jace felt like he was going deaf. The stench of the black dragon's breath was right on top of him — but he couldn't hear the roar at all. He could only rely on instinct to find a way to dodge the black dragon's attacks, searching for walls to take cover behind. Still, he could feel it — the black dragon's speed had slowed a lot compared to when it first landed. After smashing through several walls, it had to catch its breath several times before mustering the strength to resume the chase.

Jace used one of the breathing openings to glance back. The black dragon shoved a wall aside, revealing its savage face. Molten, lava-like drool was already flowing from its mouth again. Its chest swelled and glowed.

Suddenly, a shining arrow shot into its mouth from the side. The black dragon lurched and swayed. Before it could look to its side, another arrow pierced straight into its eye.

Vereesa stood anchored steadily on a distant wall, calmly drawing and aiming. The black dragon opened its mouth to gather another ball of flame — but the Farstrider didn't dodge or flinch. An arrow flew true from its mouth straight down the black dragon's throat. This one made the black dragon heave in a bout of vomiting, a great mass of glowing, molten lava spilling from its mouth.

The black dragon finally sensed something was wrong. It whipped its head and shrieked, backing several steps to one side, using its wings to shield its body from further arrows.

Vereesa was no longer shooting with the same decisiveness and speed as before. She seemed to be running low on arrows — each shot was far more careful now. Seeing the creature shield itself with its wings, she held an arrow that glimmered with arcane light, her fingers reluctant to release.

The beast continued to back away, its tail furiously leveling a vast stretch of ruins — but it seemed to have trouble making out its surroundings. It only smashed through the rubble until it reached an open space, and when it sensed the two people behind it weren't following, it decisively raised its wings.

Bailing? The black dragon flapped its wings, trying to take off. When it reached halfway into the air, Vereesa stared into the black dragon's eyes and loosed her arrow. The shaft flew a slight arc and struck the neck dead-center. The black dragon howled and nearly plummeted — tucking its body as it furiously beat its wings, trying to climb higher.

By now, Jace had sprinted to the area nearby. He caught sight of what looked like a firework rocket — a flame-tipped arrow — flying up from somewhere. It hadn't even touched the black dragon before it fizzled out.

Alright, I know you did your best…

He leaped onto a broken wall, getting as high as he could. Before he could steady himself, he hit the black dragon — still struggling to gain altitude — with an Immolate.

Crimson flames erupted at the base of the dragon's tail. Shattered scales and purple blood rained down like a storm.

The black dragon let out a few short, sharp roars and dropped a great deal in height. Jace shouted the Immolate incantation again. This time the burst of blood-light exploded from the black dragon's chest. The beast beat its wings a few more times — and finally couldn't hold on. Deprived of strength, it plummeted, crashing into a distant stretch of broken walls.

Vereesa had already rushed over. Jace leaped off the wall and followed. When the two of them drew near, a cloud of black-red smoke rose up from ahead…

This was different from all the flames before!

And Jace knew it all too well.

"Vereesa! Don't go over there! Run! Run now!"
