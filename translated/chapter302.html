Chapter 302: Whose Side Is Fate On?

Even though this was a human, and wearing the familiar armor of a Stormwind cavalryman, his appearance did nothing to put Jace at ease.

This was by no means a friendly place, and ghosts were far from comforting existences. Though the other party hadn't shown any hostility, Jace was prepared to destroy him on the spot.

"Who are you?"

"You mean my name, mage?" the soldier said, lowering his head. "I don't remember… it's right on the tip of my tongue, but I've never been able to recall it. I need my energy to remember more important things. My name is clearly not among them."

"At least you can tell I'm a mage," Jace said. This time he wasn't wearing the sigil of the Wizard Sanctum.

"You're holding a staff," the soldier said. "You look like a young scholar. Easy to tell."

Just then, Sylvanas heard the voice and walked over. The weapon she carried gleamed with a golden light from the holy wolfsbane oil, but the soldier's ghost didn't seem afraid in the slightest.

"And an elven lady…" the wraith said. "Why have you come here? To this place of the dead? Are you fleeing here?"

"No," Sylvanas said coldly. "Rather, you should tell us why a human died in a dwarven city."

"Died here? No," the soldier said. "I followed Sir Lothar in pursuit of Orgrim Doomhammer's forces, but near the City of Thaurissan we were ambushed by Blackrock troops. That was a truly bloody battle."

"I thought I could kill that orc scout who was fleeing in panic, but in the end I discovered he was nothing but a bait to lure me. By the time I realized my grave mistake, it was already too late."

"My mount was savagely cut in two by those orcs. One orc slit my neck. An orc warlock seared my skin with dark magic. After I fell from my horse, they snatched my war hammer and smashed it into my skull… I'm certain that was no dream, but the truth I witnessed."

Jace fell silent for a moment. Just hearing the description, he couldn't imagine what this knight had been through.

"Did we lose?" the soldier asked?

"The whole war?" Sylvanas said. "No, we won. But there are still many orcs scattered about. But we won. Soon our army will enter the Dark Portal, strike back at their homeland, and let these damned monsters know that some actions carry a price."

"We won?" The wraith's form seemed to grow a little clearer, then dimmed again.

"But since we won… why do I feel I still have an unfinished mission? I must save Marshal Anduin Lothar. He is in danger."

"Marshal Lothar has already fallen," Jace said. "Orgrim defeated him in single combat."

The soul fell silent for a moment, then said, "I'm dead too, aren't I?"

Sylvanas frowned. "What do you mean?"

"Death does not mean the end," the soul said, lowering his head. "Even the Marshal's death does not mean he has found eternal peace. What on earth is going on…"

"Forgive my forgetfulness, both of you… wait, I remember now. Marshal Lothar's body is still on that battlefield."

"Someone has been trying to find his body… I must protect his remains from defilement by evil. I remember it all, I remember everything!"

Hearing the soul's words, Jace felt as though he'd been struck by lightning.

The battlefield before Blackrock Spire had been in such chaos that after the battle, many bodies couldn't be recovered — including Marshal Anduin Lothar himself. So in order to commemorate him, people had later erected a statue in the center of the Burning Steppes, which was in truth also a kind of tombstone.

Could it be that the death knight had come to this area precisely to find Lothar's corpse? And the body in the Raven Hill cemetery had merely been a transitional vessel he'd sought after being injured…

After coming to Blackrock Mountain, he had attempted to seek Blackhand's aid. Blackhand may have guessed his purpose, and in order not to let him obtain too great a power from Lothar's remains, had refused him.

So that was why he had turned instead to the Shattered Hand clan in the Blasted Lands?

"How do you know this?" Jace asked.

"I've seen that man," the wraith said.

Jace and Sylvanas exchanged a glance. The wraith continued: "He moves like a reaper — unpredictable. His soul is more evil than the most terrible orc I've ever seen… You are a mage of Stormwind, aren't you? If you, like me, have sworn fealty to the king, then you must help me find Lothar… Marshal Lothar's body, before that evil spirit does!"

"Do you know where Marshal Lothar's body is?" Jace asked.

"Leave this city and head west, mage," the wraith said. "There is an orc mass grave there. Many human and orc corpses have been piled up there. Even from here I can hear the howls of the vengeful spirits, but compared to the beginning… it's grown much quieter."

"But how are we supposed to leave here?" Sylvanas said. "The place is full of dwarf wraiths. Every time we've tried to get out, we've been blocked."

"I can lead you out of here," the wraith said firmly.

"You can leave this temple?" Jace said. "Then why didn't you leave before?"

"My existence depends on an altar in this temple," the wraith said softly. "It took me a long time here to figure out why I couldn't go to the Shadowlands with the other spirits… At first I thought it was because my faith in the Holy Light wasn't firm enough."

"But now, with your help, I think I can leave here with peace of mind. My soul is enough to guide you out of this dwarven city. After that, it'll be up to you. I've grown weary of this endless darkness."

"Let me serve Marshal Lothar one last time. Let me protect him from the erosion of evil. Let me make my final contribution to the defense of the Kingdom of Stormwind. Let me find eternal rest at Marshal Lothar's side."

As he spoke these words, the wraith's form seemed to grow much clearer all at once. Jace could even make out his features.

He was a plainly featured man with a mustache, no different from the older veterans on the streets. He gave off a familiar feeling, like being back in Stormwind.

But something was off, no matter how he looked at it.

This seemed too convenient.

Learning of the death knight's scheme, knowing the way out of this dwarven city, a noble soul willing to sacrifice himself to help them, knowing everything…

Especially when connected to this temple that set one's teeth on edge, and the altar the soul had mentioned.

A temple that worshipped the Firelord, or perhaps even the Old Gods — what kind of normal altar could it have?

No… Jace realized he'd been thinking too far in the direction of the Old Gods. This abandoned temple had given him too many misleading hints.

Reassembling these familiar elements: an ominous altar, a soul, a death knight, a corpse…

Jace had the feeling that all of this was familiar, though he couldn't say where he'd encountered it before.

Was this deja vu?

Wait — wasn't this the quest with the ancient orc spirit in the Shadowmoon Valley?

An ancient orc who had you collect the remains of Teron Gorefiend for a divination ritual, only to discover in the end that he was…

Teron Gorefiend himself.

Damn.

Jace felt a chill run through his entire body. The wraith before him was no Stormwind knight at all!

The other party had occupied this shadow temple and used the power here to maintain his existence. He was the demon who wanted to seize Lothar's corpse!

Jace glanced at Sylvanas. The elf didn't seem to have noticed anything.

She wasn't sensitive to wraiths — that was normal. And the wraith before them wasn't giving off any of the presence a wraith should have.

Even if it were him, if he hadn't done that quest in his past life, what would have occurred to him?

Because this was no ordinary ghost at all, but the soul of a death lord who excelled at disguise and deception! Could an ordinary human wraith do this? Could he so perfectly conceal the foul, dark nature of an undead being? This time, he absolutely could not let him escape.

"Then lead us out of here, sir," Jace said in a low voice.

"Gladly." The nod. The soul passed between Sylvanas and Jace and headed toward the hall.

Jace tightened his grip on the blade coated in holy wolfsbane oil and glared at Sylvanas.

Sylvanas noticed the abnormality in Jace's eyes, then saw the knife he gripped tightly, then looked at the soul ahead. Her lips moved slightly.

Jace's pace grew faster and faster, gradually falling in behind the wraith. Sylvanas also caught on and quickened her steps to follow.

Both raised their blades at the same time. The wraith sensed the disturbance behind him and turned around — only to take two direct stabs from daggers imbued with holy power!

Golden light exploded. The wraith let out a hysterical shriek, but the sound quickly turned from piercing to dull and terrifying. The darkness around them dropped sharply, even swallowing the golden light!

The wraith's color suddenly turned purple and then swelled violently. The two of them found that the spirit power where they had stabbed scattered in an instant. A hollow, ghoulish coughing echoed throughout the hall.

Jace realized it was about to flee again. He immediately hurled a ball of fire toward the temple entrance. The flames burst into gold and spread along the doorframe to the ceiling. A terrifying howl erupted in the hall!

In the light of the flames, they could see a ferocious demon climbing from the entrance up to the deep ceiling, roaming back and forth throughout the temple.

"All right, all right, this game is ending faster than I thought. Fate does not seem to be on my side — it never was from the start. But I'm not surprised, because I know who I am. From the very beginning, I never planned to choose the easy path! But that doesn't mean I won't punish those who betray me, who obstruct me…"

"Him," Sylvanas said coldly, gazing into the depths of the temple.

The soul roared: "The traitor thought he could take advantage of my weakness, but his ambition met with a shameful failure — because I am Teron Gorefiend, and he is nothing but an insignificant pawn. He will die. He will die in unspeakable agony, and in the end his soul will not escape my grasp."

"And you… I must admit that fate hasn't completely abandoned me, after all — it sent the two of you to me! You are the same as that traitor. Only you will pay the due price right here, right now!"

Flags

- 温蕾萨 (Sylvanas): Rendered as "Sylvanas" per the established translation convention in this work (温蕾萨 = Sylvanas, the second Windrunner sister). - 塔隆·血魔 (Teron Gorefiend): Canonical. The first death knight. The "Stormwind knight" wraith is revealed to be Teron Gorefiend in disguise — a direct nod to the Shadowmoon Valley questline, where an "ancient orc spirit" tasks the player with gathering Gorefiend's relics for a divination ritual, only to be revealed as Gorefiend himself. - 安度因·洛萨 (Anduin Lothar): Canonical. Grand Marshal of the Alliance. Gorefiend (as the fake knight) claims he must protect Lothar's corpse from defilement — the real scheme is for Gorefiend himself to seize Lothar's body. - 奥格瑞姆·毁灭之锤 (Orgrim Doomhammer): Canonical. Orc Warchief who defeated and killed Lothar in single combat. - 黑手 (Blackhand): Canonical. Orc Warchief — Jace deduces Gorefiend came to Blackrock seeking Blackhand's aid to find Lothar's corpse, and was refused. - 碎手氏族 (Shattered Hand clan): Canonical. Orc clan — Jace speculates Gorefiend then turned to the Shattered Hand in the Blasted Lands. - 影月谷 (Shadowmoon Valley): Canonical. The zone where the in-game quest paralleling this deception takes place. - 神圣巫师之油 (Holy Wolfsbane Oil): The holy oil coating Jace's blade — used to expose and wound Gorefiend's true form. - 索瑞森城 (City of Thaurissan): The ruined Dark Iron capital where the battle against Gorefiend takes place. - 暴风王国狮头 (Kingdom of Stormwind lion head): The emblem on the fake knight's armor — Gorefiend's disguise as a Stormwind cavalryman. - 影之国 (the Shadowlands): The afterlife — the fake knight claims he cannot pass on with the other spirits. - 圣光 (Holy Light): Referenced by the fake knight as the faith he thought he lacked. - 达拉然 (Dalaran): Canonical. Referenced by the fake knight in his confusion — "Do you think you were impressive in Dalaran?" (carried over into Chapter 304 as Tharon'go's challenge).
