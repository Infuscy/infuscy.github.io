## Chapter 303: Gorefiend's Shadow

The pitch-black hall had no second exit. This was now a fight with one's back to the wall.

Jace took out the gray potion Ursula had brewed and gulped it down in one swallow. Right now he was in no mood to savor its horrid flavor, which was enough to sear one's throat raw.

Vereesa warily fixed her gaze on the distance. The short blade in her hand seemed to glow even brighter, as if influenced by her battle-forged will.

The deep darkness spread over them. Even the Light spell couldn't illuminate far. Ethereal howls and laughter echoed throughout the hall, impossible to tell where they started.

The two of them stood back to back, watching the surroundings. Suddenly Jace felt a surge of violet magical force break through the darkness and rush straight at them. They dodged in unison. A demon's face suddenly thrust itself between him and Vereesa, and shadow energy rapidly gathered toward this wraithlike form!

Gorefiend's shriek was enough to knock a person unconscious. At the same time, he released all the gathered energy in a shadow nova. Though Jace reacted extremely fast and retreated several steps, he still couldn't avoid it.

Every patch of exposed skin on the side the blast had caught was seared by the shadow power. His arms and neck burned and itched fiercely. All at once, his legs and feet felt as though they no longer obeyed him.

Vereesa?

The demon spun in mid-air, leaving layers of indistinct afterimages as it dove toward the darkness. The red light it released illuminated every wall it passed until it revealed Vereesa's figure in the distance. Several red claws flashed to life around the demon, then madly slashed and raked ahead like a frenzied demonic whirlwind. Vereesa zigzagged back and forth like a hare evading a wolf, then stepped off the wall and leaped high to avoid the final sweeping strike.

The demon suddenly turned and reached a blood-red hand toward Vereesa in mid-air, but Vereesa slashed out with her blade almost simultaneously, cutting it off with a stroke of golden arcane light. The deep-red blood-shadow splashed into the dim shadows.

A piercing wail followed. The shadow demon vanished, then reappeared in the blink of an eye a few feet away. Crimson energy gathered around its body once more as if preparing for another shadow nova. Its scarlet demon-eyes had already locked onto this direction.

Jace pointed at the monstrous shadow and incanted Scorch. Raging fire exploded in a series around its body, blowing apart and collapsing the nearby pillars. The blood-shadow shrieked and suddenly vanished into the flames.

Just when the two thought the enemy had temporarily withdrawn, Jace felt another wave of shadow surge push through the chaos!

"Shadow magic!"

The darkness that had swallowed the firelight was again pushed back by a violet shadow wave. Vereesa nimbly leaped up along the adjacent wall and vaulted over this shadow surge. Jace had a much harder time of it — he scrambled up a pillar and jumped to the high ground, barely avoiding it. He nearly had his legs devoured by that wave of shadow energy.

The red shadow retreated deeper inside. The two pressed forward. Gorefiend laughed wildly and hurled a bolt of shadow that spun and burned with violet flames. Vereesa easily avoided the direct hit of this Shadow Bolt, but when the energy exploded behind her, the shadow nova it released sent her stumbling.

The moment she lost her balance, the red shadow suddenly tightened like a blood arrow and shot between the two of them. Shadow energy gathered once more.

"Jace! He's heading for you…"

A howl of agony erupted as the shadow energy coalesced, drowning out the elf's shout. But this time Jace chose not to dodge. Instead, he directly released a burst of golden fire at the shadow demon that was gathering its energy!

The fire exploded directly beneath the shadow demon's feet. The energy it had gathered was blown apart by the flames carrying holy power. A crimson skull shrieked and broke free of the flames, rising to the ceiling. After a spin, it scattered its shadowy form and condensed into several slashing, clawing demons!

Two of the blood-red wraiths charged toward Jace; the rest chased after Vereesa in the distance. Jace had no attention to spare for what was happening over there — he could only raise his long blade and hack at one of the wraiths.

The wraith before him nimbly dodged his slash. Another wraith charged in from the side and grabbed Jace's chest. Jace only felt a sensation — impossible to tell if it was burning hot or ice cold — spread from his heart.

He mustered every ounce of his strength to regain his senses and slashed back with his blade. The demon that was struck instantly dissipated, while the shadow demon before him extended its claws and lunged. Jace barely dodged the lunge, then thrust his blade straight into the skull atop the wraith's form, piercing the demon so its soul scattered with one strike. A wraith's shriek came from the distance — Vereesa seemed to have been locked in a struggle too. But the darkness swallowed everything again, and Jace could see nothing.

Teron Gorefiend had charged between the two of them again and again to split them apart, and this time he had succeeded.

Jace ran back along the corridor, following the sounds. Deep in the corridor, flashes of blood-red light flickered incessantly. He ran closer and closer, until at the end of the passage he turned a corner and saw, in the narrow hall before him, a massive demon glowing with blood-red light, lowering its head to stare at him and sneer.

"Well, well, if it isn't Jace Sesso!"

The demon laughed coldly, then suddenly lunged forward and wrapped itself entirely around Jace. The speed was so fast that Jace had no chance to dodge at all.

He felt himself lifted high into the air by a deep, bloody grasping force. He had no room to resist whatsoever. Just then, a flash of golden light swept past his eyes. The demon's gaze flickered and it suddenly dissipated — Vereesa had reached him!

Just when Jace thought he was saved, the demon suddenly reformed several yards away. Countless blood claws seized the elf who had charged right in front of him!

In mid-air, Vereesa had nothing to push off from to change direction. She was snatched away helplessly, just like that.

Jace crashed heavily to the ground. He immediately rose and began incanting. Golden fire instantly swallowed the entire hall. The heat raised the temperature to an unimaginable height in the blink of an eye — he felt his own skin was about to blister. The shadow of Gorefiend, roasted by the golden power, trembled all over and could barely hold together, unable to bind anything any longer…

The falling elf grabbed Jace and dragged him out of the room that was being rapidly consumed by flames. The two of them stumbled out. By the firelight, Vereesa was covered in wounds. She frowned in pain and could barely stand, half-crawling on the ground.

Jace also felt as though his body was no longer his own. He had no idea what a pitiful state he must look like to Vereesa. And he had nearly drained his mana in one continuous stretch — he was already starting to feel dizzy.

But when he looked back, he saw the shadow of Teron Gorefiend, like a storm of blood-red wind wrapped in smoke and sparks, charging at them once again!

"Ver… he's coming again."

Gorefiend's savage howl drowned out all other sounds. His form, save for the grinning skull at the leading edge, could barely hold its shape any longer — like a crimson wave struggling to keep up with the skull's speed. But that wave still unleashed a devastating shadow wave!

Facing what was nearly the final strike, the elf gritted her teeth and dove into the darkness. Jace grabbed a nearby pillar and used the last of his strength to pull himself behind it. The stone pillar absorbed most of the shadow energy, but he still felt as though he'd been struck hard by a surging tide of darkness. After a dizzying spell that nearly made him lose consciousness came stinging and itching all over his body.

Before he could recover, the wraith of Teron Gorefiend rounded the pillar and reached out a blood hand to grab his neck. Almost simultaneously, a flash of golden light suddenly shot out from the darkness. The Sunblade, gleaming, spun straight through Gorefiend's skull. The trembling, ghostly skull at the center of the wraith was torn open by the blade.

"No… no…"

The blood hands of the skull clawed at its own face as if trying to seal the crack. But after only two or three seconds, it stopped moving and fixed its gaze on Jace.

"Even without… Lothar, yours will do just fine, Jace Sesso. After all, I don't have many choices…"

The demon's eyes blazed with violent violet fire. The entire blood-red shadow plunged into Jace's eye sockets. Jace only felt the whole world instantly turn a deep, dark red. The last sound he could hear was Vereesa's nearly voice-breaking shout.
