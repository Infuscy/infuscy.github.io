Chapter 307: Proof

"What do we do next?" Vereesa asked.

Jace leaned against the pillar and turned his head to look at her. "Do you still want to keep heading north? To find that Angore the Deadwish? Teron Gorefiend wanted to seek his help before, Vereesa. I saw that man in his memory visions."

"Is he the traitor Teron mentioned?" Vereesa asked.

Jace recalled the vision of that black wraith. It had been inside a vast, pitch-black pavilion built from the massive bones and hides of great beasts.

Outside the door was an endless desert. The death knight with the blackened face was sneering directly at them — or rather, at Teron Gorefiend.

The death knight wore the plate armor of a Stormwind footman, though it had been badly corroded by shadow and necromantic energy. The style was still recognizable.

This had to be Angore. It matched the description from Thomas Waller — Abercrombie — that the death knight had taken over the body of an officer he'd once treated, and must have claimed the buried armor along with it.

Angore was Teron's subordinate. The fact that he dared to smirk so triumphantly meant Teron could no longer do anything to him.

"I think so," Jace replied. "After we defeated Teron in Dalaran, he fled south. I think the black dragon abandoned him and didn't take him through the Dark Portal, so he could only seek refuge with his own subordinates. Clearly, his subordinates weren't exactly loyal either. In his memories, I heard them discussing Anduin Lothar. I think Angore probably wanted to find Lothar's body too — and betrayed his weakened master for the sake of that corpse. I imagine that's why Teron cursed him so furiously."

Vereesa asked, "Can you be certain it's him?"

Jace said, "I could see sand, and an orcish camp. Combined with what we'd deduced before — that Angore is somewhere in the wastelands to the north, not far northeast of Blackrock Mountain — the death knight in those memory visions has to be him. If it is him, he's been there for the last month or two, since we defeated Teron Gorefiend in Dalaran only about two months ago. If he still intends to seize Lothar's body, he won't leave easily — after all, he's already betrayed his master for that corpse."

Vereesa said, "I'd say that pins down his location, then."

"I'd say so," Jace said. "I think we should head back to Stormwind first — at least back to Lakeshire. We don't have many potions left, you're out of arrows, and I... I still don't know what kind of effect Teron Gorefiend had on me. From then until now, I've felt like the necromantic energy hasn't fully drained out. I want to go back to the Cathedral of Light and get checked. The priests there told me necrotic poison isn't something you can afford to delay."

"Then I need to return as well. I'll take the Farstriders north to hunt that death knight and put a period to this whole affair." Vereesa nodded. "This trip had too many surprises. That black dragon... and the spirits in this ruined city. When I first saw those Shattered Hand orcs, I thought we were lucky. Turned out it was just the start of a string of bad luck." After a moment, she said distantly, "Still, we killed Teron Gorefiend. When I sit quietly and think about it, I can hardly believe it's real."

"After all, he's the one who killed my brother, Jace." She looked over. "Maybe not by his own hand, but it was his order either way. Those death knights — Teron Gorefiend's loyal killers — they entered Windrunner Village and slaughtered so many of my people..."

"But Lirath shouldn't have been there. He... he loved playing the lute, loved music and melodies. His favorite thing back then was playing 'Maiden of Silverlight Lake' alone. I know that was his favorite..."

At that, Vereesa lowered her head and fell silent for a moment.

Jace asked, "Is that the one you played in Eastvale?"

The elf nodded faintly.

"He just never forgot he was a Windrunner. He begged Sylvanas to teach him how to be a warrior, how to become a ranger — because he knew Sylvanas doted on him most. In her heart... her brother mattered more than the Sunwell."

"But Sylvanas wanted him to pursue his own strengths — like becoming a court musician in the Sunstrider's court. We all knew he could do it. He was actually on the verge of achieving it. Even Prince Kael'thas adored his playing. That would have been a remarkable accomplishment — you imagine how exacting the Sunstriders are about art and beauty."

"So he came to me. I was just an apprentice myself back then. I just felt I should do my duty as an older sister and help my little brother fulfill his wish — or maybe I just thought it was fun. I don't know..."

"But he was a true Windrunner. Though frail in body and young in age, he learned quickly."

By now, tears were streaming down Vereesa's face.

"He just wanted to do his part to protect his family," she said softly.

Jace nodded. He didn't know what to say, and he knew he probably shouldn't say anything about this.

The two of them sat in silence for a while, until Vereesa gradually composed herself.

"The third one is the Staff of Sargeras," Jace said.

"Long before Teron Gorefiend went to Dalaran, his death knight subordinates had already headed to the Tomb of Sargeras to seize that holy artifact. If everything had gone according to plan for Teron in Dalaran, he was supposed to rendezvous with those death knights near here and head to the Dark Portal together."

"But now the black dragon has clearly completed that task in his place. You can tell General Sylvanas she won't need to waste any more effort searching for the third artifact."

"All of this... you saw all of this in Teron Gorefiend's memories?" Vereesa asked, surprised.

"Of course," Jace said. "How else would I know?"

"You see, Vereesa, we defeated Teron Gorefiend — that's why I could see what was in his memories. If not for that, who knows how long Quel'Thalas would have kept searching."

Vereesa wiped away her tears and said quietly, "But how am I supposed to prove it... after all, his soul has already been scattered to the winds."

Jace pried the shadow gem from the staff setting and handed it to her. "Take this back as evidence. Don't forget to return it to me — this isn't a gift. And don't sell it to the Reliquary."

"I'll definitely return it to you." Vereesa clutched the gem to her chest and nodded. "Thank you, Jace."

Jace let out a short laugh. "Teron Gorefiend's wraith, the black dragon, this stone as evidence, plus all this valuable intelligence your sister would treasure — by my count, that dagger of mine doesn't even come close to covering it."

"It doesn't," Vereesa said.
