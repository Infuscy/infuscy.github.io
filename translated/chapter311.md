## Chapter 311: Shadow Protection

After ordering his clothes, Jace kept thinking about the height thing all the way home until noon.

A sudden growth spurt could have many causes. Maybe the black dragon. Or maybe it had something to do with Teron Gorefiend's soul?

After all, when a death knight possessed a body, the frenzied necromantic energy would make the original corpse grow a good deal taller. That was why every death knight's build was so freakishly large.

Never mind Teron Gorefiend — even the servant of Teron that Jace had intercepted halfway through Dalaran had been terrifyingly tall.

In any case, Jace couldn't think of any way to verify exactly whose power had affected his physique.

He pushed open the door. Morophil was sitting in the very back, staring at him.

"Master, so how are you now? Planning to become some kind of ascetic? How long has it been since we had some fun together? When was the last time?"

"You're an immortal monster. How can you be so impatient?"

Jace muttered the words, his face expressionless, as he opened the box and took out the scrolls stored inside. He looked at the dense notes he'd written — the incantations left behind by Gorefiend.

Remembering the day he'd recorded all this in that Dark Iron temple already felt like a lifetime ago.

Never mind Andromath not understanding how he'd managed to kill Gorefiend — even trying to imagine himself from a month ago felt unbelievable to him now.

"Are you starting to suspect me because of that demon summoner business?" Morophil asked, tilting her head.

"A little," Jace said. "You have to admit, that scene was a bit terrifying for a simple young lad like me."

Morophil narrowed her eyes and stared at Jace for a moment, then said, "My simple master, have you fallen in love with someone else and you're just using this as an excuse?"

"Let me be honest." Jace looked at the succubus. "I summoned you to help me, not to cause me trouble, Lady Morophil. You've helped me plenty, I mean it — but don't let the trouble you cause me outweigh the help you give me."

"Fine!" Morophil sat on the bed, swinging her legs back and forth. "At least while you were away, I protected your few possessions quite well, especially this magic sword... doesn't that count as helping?"

As she spoke, she suddenly pulled out the bag containing Azuresong straight from the box. Jace snatched it away. "Damn it, don't treat this like a toy."

Morophil said coldly, "A toy? This is clearly some kind of love token, isn't it? I bet when this thing first landed in your hands, it still reeked of the stink of a mortal in heat. That damned elf, I'll..."

Jace laughed. "You don't know her at all. She killed Teron Gorefiend, Morophil. If you had the ability to finish her off, I wouldn't have to stay here. You and I and your fellow demon could go out and become treasure hunters or something. I say you help me wipe out Blackrock Spire first — Rend Blackhand has who knows how many Horde treasures hidden away up there."

The succubus shook her head and asked, "So who exactly is Teron Gorefiend? A necromancer?"

"I didn't go over it with you in detail?" Jace said. "He's a monster created by Kil'jaeden's apprentice. You know Kil'jaeden, right?"

Hearing Kil'jaeden's name, the succubus's eyes dimmed. "Never mind, then."

She glanced sideways at Jace and asked, "So then, master, have you fallen in love with that elf?"

"Did you smell the stink of a mortal in heat on me, succubus?" Jace asked.

Morophil chuckled softly. "Right now any smell is covered up by the weird stench that comes off a mortal after they rot to death — though it's better than when you first got back here."

"Then I'll be heading to the Cathedral of Light again later." He spread the scroll out on the table. "Sazeno, come out first and help me look at this."

The imp climbed onto the table, stepping on the scroll. "What is it?"

"Get your paws off." Jace pushed the imp away. "If you burn this scroll, I'll punch you to death."

"Ouch, ouch." The imp shuffled two steps to the side. "This kind of paper doesn't catch fire that easily!"

Hearing the imp's words, Jace nodded. "True, it's expensive after all. But don't scorch it anywhere either. All right, my Demonic isn't good enough to read these yet. Help me sort through this first — see which ones might be incantations and which ones are probably just useless words."

"Morophil, you help look too," Jace said to the succubus.

"I don't understand Demonic," the succubus said.

Jace shot her an annoyed glance. Morophil said, "Oh dear, I've been so disobedient. Master, punish me quickly!"

"Help me look. If you find something, I might just reward you."

"Really?" Morophil smiled and obediently sat down at the table, joining the imp in looking over the scrolls.

"Master, what kind of stuff have you written here!" the imp sighed. "Why is it all such a mess? I... ah... I can more or less understand this line."

"I was just going by pronunciation. I couldn't understand what they were actually saying," Jace said. "The fuzzy parts have Common phonetic notes too. If you don't recognize it, think about whether there's a Demonic word with a similar pronunciation."

Morophil murmured one of the lines, repeating it under her breath continuously. After a while she said, "I know this line, master. This should be a shadow protection spell. I've heard the Nathrezim chant similar incantations many times. I think I'm right."

"A shadow protection barrier?" Jace asked.

"No, the incantation for a shadow protection barrier isn't like this, and it's not a spell the Nathrezim created either," Morophil said. "If I remember correctly, this is a shadow shell that protects oneself. It can block spells and also stop weapons from penetrating, so the Nathrezim are very fond of it."

"Are you sure this is the complete incantation?" Jace asked.

Sazeno said, "The succubus is right. It has a beginning and an end — this is at least a complete sentence! And it's long enough, master. Don't you think it takes a bit too long to chant for a protection spell?"

"Tomthokas, Veholisha, Vasdhaung..." Jace murmured the incantation. He felt shadow power stir, but there was no actual effect.

This meant the incantation wasn't so easy to activate.

But since it was a protection spell, if it wasn't destructive, it should be safe to test out in the near future. Still, he'd go to the Cathedral of Light first and have the priests double-check it.

"I'm going out again," Jace said. "Be good at home. You've both been performing well lately — verbal commendation."

"What about the reward?" Morophil grabbed Jace's sleeve. "I want a reward. You promised!"

"But I'm going to test this incantation later and I'll need mana, Morophil." Jace said this as his hand reached for the door. Morophil darted like a hunting cat to Jace's other side, pressing her body against the already-open door and slamming it shut.

She looked up into Jace's eyes. "If you feel even the tiniest bit of mana drain from you, then I'll never pester you again. How about that? I only want you."

"You said it, not me."

Jace scooped Morophil up in one motion and tossed her back onto the bed. The imp shrieked and leaped off the table.
