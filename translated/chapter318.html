Chapter 318: Stormwind Keep

Today was the day he'd be meeting the king.

Jace arrived at Stormwind Keep early and followed the guards inside. No sooner had he caught sight of the long ramp leading straight up to the throne hall than the palace attendants steered him aside into a side chamber before he could take a single step forward.

He knew the drill — he was going to get the whole etiquette briefing on how to appear before the king all over again.

Jace sat in a handsome dark-red chair while an attendant he'd never seen before droned on about the procedures he'd need to follow. Before the man had been talking for long, a clamor suddenly broke out outside. The attendants who'd been listening all wore slightly hesitant expressions.

Jace looked toward the door just as a powerfully built man strode in with long, brisk strides.

He recognized the fellow. He stood up, watching as the man swept his gaze across everyone in the room and said, "Gentlemen, I don't know which is more terrifying — these tedious courtesies or the death knights. Though I do appreciate your efforts to maintain a solemn atmosphere here in Stormwind Keep."

"Yes, Your Majesty!"

The attendants filed out at the command. Two guardsmen holding spears at the door exchanged a glance. The man looked at them. "I'll ask the two of you to leave as well. My thanks for your service."

The guards nodded and left, closing the door behind them, leaving Jace standing there alone.

Compared to their last meeting, Jace could clearly sense that Varian had grown more confident. His skin had even lightened a little. The one thing that hadn't changed was that absurdly solid wall of muscle.

Of course, he still wasn't wearing a crown. His black hair was simply tied back, stray locks at his temples scattered casually beside his cheeks, and he wore nothing that befitted a king's station.

A plain blue outer tunic, a pair of brown trousers, and a pair of tooled leather boots — he looked far more like a young knight who'd only just come off a battlefield, barely out of his armor and still catching his breath, than the ruler of this kingdom.

"Your Majesty!"

Jace bowed slightly.

"Mr. Sesso." Varian nodded. "When did we last meet? Half a year ago, it seems? So much has happened these past six months it feels like half a lifetime."

"It has been about half a year!"

"Sit down." Varian gestured toward the stool behind him with one hand while casually pulling over another stool with the other and sitting. He sighed. "Lord Bolvar wanted me to wait for you up there, but I find that chair absolutely unbearable, and I'd run out of patience waiting."

"Out there I always defer to them. In here I ought to get to make my own calls, don't you think? Tell me — Jace, may I call you Jace?"

"Of course, Your Majesty," Jace said as he sat. Compared to the somewhat reserved Varian he'd encountered at Stonewatch, this version was so uninhibited it took Jace a little off guard.

Then again, Stormwind Keep was his home. Stonewatch was full of warriors who would lay down their lives for him and his family without a second thought. Even Jace would've been at a loss what to do with his hands and feet facing a crowd like that.

"Speaking of which — how's that cloak been treating you, anyway?" the king suddenly asked.

Of all the things to bring up...

"I'm sorry," Jace admitted. "Burned to ruin."

"The court mages said that cloak was fire-resistant. How could it possibly have been burned to ruin?"

"By a black dragon."

"Black dragon?" Varian rubbed the back of his hand across his neck. "When did you run into a black dragon?!"

"Did Your Majesty not know about the black dragons north of Redridge?"

"I received reports that there were dragonkin up there, and that they'd been trying to harass Lakeshire," Varian said. "So you ran into an actual dragon on the Burning Steppes? But the letter from the Silvermoon Ranger-General didn't mention this."

Jace said, "Quel'Thalas suffered an assault by the Red Dragonflight during the war. Perhaps to the Ranger-General, compared with encountering Teron Gorefiend, running into a young dragon might not count as a significant enough event to mention..."

"Be that as it may," Varian said, "even a young dragon is enough to destroy a warship, Jace. I've been itching to fight a dragon myself — but I'm the king now. They'd never let me come face to face with one, not even a dragonkin warrior."

"Dragonkin are dangerous enough in their own right," Jace said. "The dragonkin I encountered at Lakeshire was a female mage, Your Majesty. She had a mind-control magic that could make a victim mistake her for a human woman and let their guard down."

Varian nodded. "The mages here in the palace mentioned that as well. And they noted that the arrival of dragonkin means the black dragons are reaching their claws in this direction. The gnoll troubles have only just eased off, and now the black dragons are stirring again. One problem after another, without end."

"But can dragonkin really wield that kind of mind-affecting magic? I've read introductions to these so-called dragon-blooded hybrid beasts in books. I'd always assumed they were just creatures with brains about as functional as a gnoll's — only far more powerful."

Jace said, "I saw it with my own eyes. And dragonkin are transformed from mortals. The dragonflight remakes them into more loyal and powerful magical beings. There's no reason that would make them stupider."

"So their masters — the black dragons — must command even more complex and terrifying magic?" Varian asked.

"Adult black dragons don't just deceive. They can even transform directly into mortals, and even the most powerful mages can barely see through their disguises," Jace said.

"I thought I'd read quite a lot of books, Jace." Varian sighed. "But just these couple of sentences chatting with you make me feel like I'm nowhere near ready to face the black dragon threat."

Only then did Jace realize he'd let himself say a bit too much.

At this point in time, the dragonflight hadn't yet involved itself deeply in the mortal world. Only the most senior scholars and mages knew anything about the dragons' shape-shifting abilities.

But the elves already knew the dragonflight could assume human form, because the blue dragons — who maintained magical order — had considerable dealings with Quel'Thalas on account of the Sunwell.

Since he was on fairly familiar terms with the elves, it wouldn't be too strange for him to know this much.

The king rested his chin on his hand, gazing up at the portraits of his ancestors high above. "If there's a black dragon who's turned into a human, as you say — if he slipped into Stormwind looking to cause trouble, what could we do to counter him?"

Jace fell silent for a moment. "Frankly, I don't know either, Your Majesty. But a black dragon would always need to meet with his own kind."

Varian's eyes snapped back to Jace, and he nodded. "You're right. Even if we can't see through the disguise, he can't possibly leave not a single crack showing... Still, this isn't a problem the two of us can solve by chatting here for a few minutes, is it? I originally came to thank you for your tremendous contribution to safeguarding the kingdom's safety, and it's turned into a consultation..."

"That is a mage's duty, Your Majesty," Jace said.
