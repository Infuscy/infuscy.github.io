## Chapter 329: Wandering Hunter

Sylia hadn't lied to him. The rainforest took a single morning to walk through, and when Jace heard the sound of waves he felt he'd been saved.

Just as he'd assumed this was an uninhabited corner of the coast, he spotted a rather large ship sitting on the water at the far southern edge of his vision — a pirate vessel, or something else, he couldn't tell.

Standing on the sand, he felt an itch on his leg and couldn't help scratching it — only to feel a swollen lump!

Damn it... the spot where that bug had bitten him was already swollen to the size of a fist!

Just looking at the bump made Jace feel a little dizzy.

But stopping now meant he might not reach the place the troll witch doctor had described before sundown.

Following the instructions, Jace walked north along the coast, keeping his eyes on the tree line the whole time, searching for that "shaded jungle path half-hidden from view."

After walking for he didn't know how long and poking around in several gloomy spots, the sun slowly sank and the light slanted in sideways. Jace felt his eyes were about to spin.

Just as he noticed what seemed like another shady spot up ahead and was going to check it out, the succubus whispered from behind, "Look at the shore ahead, Master."

Jace looked toward the coast and saw a tall, thin figure standing in the shallows holding a long spear, apparently fishing.

He rubbed his eyes, crawled forward for a closer look. The figure wasn't much taller than a human, with light-blue skin and golden hair swept high...

Wasn't this exactly the troll Sylia had described?

Jace was weighing whether to ambush her outright or try talking first — but would she even speak Common? She wasn't a troll who lived in Booty Bay. They might already be fairly close to Gurubashi-controlled territory.

Just as he was settling on an approach, the figure suddenly glanced this way.

What the hell — Jace hadn't moved a muscle.

With just that one look, the female troll suddenly bolted for the shore. Damn it — she must have caught their scent.

Jace scrambled to his feet and gave chase, but the troll ran with a speed that could've matched an Olympic sprinter, vanishing into the tree line in the blink of an eye.

He bit down on his teeth and forced himself to ignore the pain in his leg, sprinting after her. The race gap between species hit him all over again — more visceral even than the gap between him and Vereesa Windrunner in arcane talent.

Back in Stormwind, there probably weren't many young men left who could outrun him. And now this troll had just disappeared, fast enough to make a man despair.

Jace hurdled a few vines, doing his best to follow the rustling and snapping of branches up ahead.

"Careful, Master!" the imp suddenly shouted. No sooner had he spoken than a hatchet came spinning out of the jungle straight toward Jace's face. Forewarned, Jace instinctively jerked his head aside. The hatchet whirred past and buried itself in a tree trunk.

His heart nearly stopped from the shock. If that had connected square with his face, Soul Link wouldn't have saved him.

But this wasn't the time to dwell on it.

He kept chasing, and before long he heard Morophil shout something in Demonic — her voice already twenty or thirty meters ahead.

When had that demon gotten so far in front?

"Morophil! Be careful!"

He pushed through the foliage as he ran, and thrusting aside a curtain of green leaves, he saw the troll in a forest clearing up ahead, both hands gripping her spear and thrusting straight at the succubus. Morophil swayed lightly to the side to dodge, then whipped around a small tree and tried to lunge with her sword — but the distance fell short and she had to parry a jab from the spear tip, forcing her to take a step back and look for another opening.

The troll was practically naked, draped in a few unknown ornaments and a couple of hatchets. Her light-blue skin had flushed a faint red, and she was chittering something under her breath — probably having entered the so-called berserk state.

Noticing another spellcaster with a staff appear, the troll took several steps back and set herself to receive them.

She was remarkably tall — even crouched low, she was about Jace's height. Her face was a snarl, teeth bared, her eyes darting between Morophil and Jace like a beast worked into a fury, muttering continuously in the troll tongue. Unfortunately, Jace couldn't understand a word.

The succubus hadn't even tried to mesmerize her — perhaps because against a troll in this state, mental magic no longer worked.

"She knows she can't beat us, Master," the imp said. "She's using that ferocious expression to hide her fear. If you give the word, I can set her mohawk on fire — heh heh heh!"

"Hold on."

Jace lowered the tip of his staff, which had been trained on the troll, and looked at her. "Sylia."

He spoke the name, then traced a shape on his right hand with his left — the outline of a long-nosed mask.

Hearing the name and seeing Jace's gesture, the troll seemed to understand something. Her frenzy cooled a little.

She pulled a blue-green mask from behind her back — so she'd been carrying it on her all along.

Jace noted that the mask did indeed depict a creature with closed eyes and a long nose, but it was quite stylized — impossible to tell whether it was meant to be a troll's face.

The troll tossed the mask to the ground and turned to run. Morophil moved to give chase, but Jace stopped her. "Forget it. We don't have the advantage in the jungle, Morophil. We've got what we came for. Don't let her lure us into an ambush set by other trolls."

Morophil nodded. "As you say, Master."

No sooner had she spoken than a hatchet came flying out of the jungle and struck the mask dead-on, shearing a chunk clean off — nose and half an eye chopped away in one stroke.

"Goddamn it."

Jace looked back into the jungle. By now he couldn't even hear the troll's footsteps anymore.

The succubus stood to the side, smiling, and pointed behind her. "No catching her now, Master. Not even me. If she hadn't run into her cave and back out again, I wouldn't have had the chance to cut her off either."

Jace picked up the two halves of the mask with a sigh, then looked in the direction Morophil had pointed — the dark mouth of a cave, barely visible.

"Where's my cloak?" he asked, looking at the bare-skinned succubus.

"Dropped it on the beach." Morophil stuck out her tongue. "It was getting in the way."

"Go back for it later..." Jace said, exasperated. "For now, check the cave — see if she left anything inside. Take whatever's worth taking."

"Nothing's going to be more valuable than this mask," Morophil said. "My guess is she ran into the cave specifically to grab the mask and flee."

"So what *does* this mask do, anyway?" Jace turned the mask over in his hands, pieced the halves back together, and put it on his face. Nothing happened.

## Flags

- **Chapter title**: 流浪猎手 — "Wandering Hunter" (lit. "Roving/Roaming Hunter").
- **Key terms**: 莫洛菲尔 = Morophil (succubus); 撒厄诺 = Sazeno (imp); 灵魂链接 = Soul Link (warlock spell); 恶魔语 = Demonic (Eredun, the demon language); 巨魔语 = the troll tongue / Zandalari; 通用语 = Common; 西尔娅 = Sylia; 温蕾萨 = Vereesa Windrunner (canon); 碧空之歌 = Song of Azure (referenced in Ch 328).
- **Lore note**: The troll's "berserk state" references the canonical troll racial trait (Berserker Stance / berserking). Jace notes that mental magic (mesmerism/Charm) doesn't work on a berserking troll.
- **Note on the mask**: Described as blue-green (青色), closed eyes, long nose — a voodoo/shamanic mask of deep personal significance to Sylia. Its function is not yet revealed here; Jace tries wearing it and senses nothing.
