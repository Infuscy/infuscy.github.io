## Chapter 330: The Witch Doctor's Dwelling

The troll's cave reeked of urine, but Jace was beyond being bothered by a simple urine stink at this point — he'd weathered worse.

The cave was full of all sorts of shells, trinkets, and cloth dolls hanging on the earthen walls. The more he looked, the more unsettling it became.

Considering this hunter didn't appear to be a skilled practitioner of voodoo, one couldn't help but question her mental state.

As for coins, Jace couldn't find a single one — only two square Gurubashi coins, just like that trinket shop owner had described. These coins couldn't be used as currency to buy anything anymore.

But given that the mask had already been hacked apart, Jace figured he might as well recoup whatever he could. He pocketed both coins.

By now he was starting to feel disappointed. Only the innermost corner of the cave remained — a junk pile of a space.

This seemed to be where the troll slept. Jace lifted the filthy blankets inside, sifted through the foul-smelling clutter — and suddenly turned up a single gold tooth.

It was quite a handsome gold tooth, pointed, about the size of a large canine.

What was this troll keeping this tooth for — planning to swap it for one of her own?

In any case, the trip hadn't been a total loss.

"Sazeno," he said. "Help me find a purple liquid stored in a bottle — in this cave, and around it... see if this hunter stashed anything like that. Trolls use that stuff for voodoo rituals. It's definitely magical."

The imp perched on Jace's shoulder. "Master, I very much doubt we'll find the kind of liquid you're describing in here! Because I can barely sense any magical presence at all in this place. Although if I *had* to point to something magical, there's just the... off-kilter life force wafting out of the whole forest... Ugh! Let's get out of here!"

Jace had much the same feeling. This hunter seemed to be simply hiding out here. There were no ritual implements or other valuables in the cave.

That made him even more suspicious about what this mask actually did — it looked like nothing more than a plain wooden object.

After sweeping the area around the cave several times with nothing to show for it, Jace rested on the beach for the night. By the time he returned to the troll witch doctor the next day, it was already afternoon.

Holding the mask broken clean in two, Jace figured the troll was going to be furious.

But given how flimsy its quality was, it probably wasn't anything especially precious either.

He knocked on the door with one of the larger pieces. "I've got your thing back, Sylia."

After a moment, the little window slid open again, the skull mask pressed to the door.

"That hunter wasn't much of a challenge," Jace said, holding the larger half of the mask up to the window. "But the mask is broken."

Sylia suddenly wrenched the door open and unleashed a long string of trollish. Jace couldn't understand it, but he knew it wasn't anything pleasant.

She snatched the half-mask from Jace's hand, then looked at the other half. Her hands trembled for a moment. She said something in trollish, then seemed to realize the human in front of her couldn't understand, and switched to Common: "That troll — did you kill her?"

"No," Jace said. "You said to kill her if she wouldn't hand it over. She handed it over. Why would I kill her?"

"But my thing was *ruined*!" the troll roared.

"At least I brought it back," Jace said.

The troll tried to slam the door shut. Jace grabbed it and held firm. "Trying to cheat me?"

"I'm going inside to get your thing. Have your gold ready!" The troll yanked hard — and the door barely moved.

She looked at Jace in surprise, bared her tusks, and said, "Let go, brute!"

"I'm coming in too," Jace said. "So you don't lock the door behind you and try to skip out on the deal. I'd hate to waste mana blasting your door open."

Sylia stared at Jace for a moment, then let go. "Fine. I warn you — don't touch any of the materials."

She led Jace deeper inside. Only now did Jace notice that the witch doctor wore her hair long and red, swept back and tied behind her head by the mask's cord.

She was about his height, dressed in a dark red cloth robe. Even though she'd said she wasn't skilled in close combat, the muscular outline of her entire frame was visible from her back.

Though a troll's arms were freakishly long, her figure was... passable. It was only ruined by those two enormous feet.

Granted, those two thick-toed feet helped trolls move nimbly through the forest's complex terrain — even climb trees with ease — but they were so wide that they made her walk with a slight wobble, which had a strange sort of appeal all its own.

The inside of the cave was far more tidy than he'd imagined. Though it was dug out, the walls and ceiling were smooth, supported by pillars and looking quite stable.

This place must have already existed before the troll arrived. She'd simply repurited it.

The witch doctor kept an assortment of materials arranged neatly on several shelves — unidentified animal palms, unidentified creature scales, and a few dried animal specimens.

And of course, several shriveled skulls with their craniums and eyeballs removed, eyelids and lips sewn shut.

At a glance, these things looked less like alchemical or voodoo materials and more like some kind of exhibition.

Sylia carefully placed the mask into a box, then walked into an inner room. Jace, stifling, pulled off his cloak and leaned against the wall far from those things, sitting down in a rattan-woven lounge chair beside him with a deep sigh.

He rolled up his pant leg and glanced at where that bug had bitten him — the swelling had mostly gone down.

Lucky. It hadn't been some vicious venomous insect after all.

Still, after that rainforest trek, he couldn't bring himself to let his guard down. So he took off his boots and checked his feet, then felt around the rest of his body, confirming there were no more bug bites, no blisters or festering wounds, before he finally breathed easy.

By now the troll came back out, glanced at his calf, and held out a cloth pouch. "Mushroom. Take it."

Jace took the mushroom and pulled a gold coin from his elven cloth pouch to hand to her.

"What was that mask for?"

Sylia stared at the box holding the mask. "It's been destroyed. Its magic is gone. So now it's a piece of junk — meaningless beyond a little sentiment, human."

"Sorry. Just curious." Jace said. "I have some other things I wanted to ask about. Regarding fel essence."

"Hmph. Fel essence." The troll nodded. "Every knowledge-hungry guest who meets a troll they can actually have a conversation with — the first thing they all want to ask about is fel essence."

"What exactly *is* fel essence? Troll blood, or something else?" Jace asked.

"That's a hard question to answer." Sylia smiled. "You should just buy a bottle and research it yourself. I don't want to discuss this with a human."

"You have fel essence?"

"Of course I do. And the highest quality kind."

"How much?"

"A bottle, eighty silver." Sylia held up two fingers. "This size. You won't find better fel essence in all of Booty Bay."
