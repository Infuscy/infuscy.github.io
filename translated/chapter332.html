Chapter 332: Delivery

That night, sitting on a lone plank way down low in Booty Bay, staring at the four moons out on the water, Jace closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and opened them again.

Still four.

He rubbed his eyes.

He'd originally planned to go find the goblin tonight and deliver the three mushroom caps, but his legs were so shaky he literally couldn't climb up there. He'd rest here for the night first and recover before doing anything else.

Molophil sat beside him, wearing a smile that carried a constant hint of mockery.

"What are you smiling at?" Jace asked.

"Because she snuck in to watch, master!" Sazeno shouted. "I told her not to go in — told her she'd expose master — but she wouldn't listen to me at all!"

"I'm completely unsurprised," Jace said, looking at the succubus.

"You knew I slipped in?" the succubus asked.

"Of course. We have a soul link. You don't think I'd be so far gone I couldn't feel anything, do you?" Jace gave her a sidelong look. "I could even feel you standing right there watching me. So fess up — you didn't cast any weird spells on me, did you?"

"Oh, master ordered me not to cast anything on you, so of course I didn't dare do a thing." Molophil tilted her head. "But I think I found master's button. You like being watched, don't you?"

"Yeah, right. Like I invited you in."

After he said that, Jace sniffed the musk on himself and muttered, "I can't believe I actually fucked a troll."

"I'm so jealous of her," the succubus said. "When can we have a go like that?"

Jace glanced at her. "Forget it. I don't want to end up like that dried-up specimen in the cave."

Molophil couldn't help but laugh. "I'm not that bad."

The imp shouted in displeasure, "This demon is way too cocky, way too full of herself — she does whatever she wants! I can't stand it anymore! I demand that master strictly regulate her every move! Hey — what are you — aah!"

Molophil grabbed the imp and hurled it away. Jace's eyes went wide as he watched Sazeno fly into the air, shrink into an indistinct little green flame, and drop into the sea without so much as a ripple.

"Can… it get back?" Jace stood up.

"Would be best if it didn't. Pity…" Molophil lay down. "But at least I'll have some peace for a while."

Jace glanced down and saw a faint little flame dart up onto the planks, weaving through the crowd as it bounced its way back up toward him. Only then did he relax a little.

He picked up the clear bottle on the ground containing the purple fel essence, muttering to himself about what the hell this stuff even was… Whatever. Ursula wanted it, and he'd gotten it for her. That was what mattered most.

The next morning, Jace climbed the stairs toward the top of Booty Bay. Looking out at the sea again, he found the sunlight a bit glaring — probably because the seaside air was so clean.

From up high, gazing out at the water, Jace realized Stormwind was a seaside city too, and the air wasn't bad either.

Enough making excuses. Yesterday afternoon he really had overdone it a bit.

Even with all of Molophil's "training," satisfying that troll hadn't been easy.

The more he thought about it, the more he felt a deep wrongness, spiritually and physically — trolls were still a little too far ahead of his time, as far as species went. So he didn't want to dwell on the details either. The past was the past.

He arrived at Misersheld's alchemy lab. The goblin was still wearing his spectacles, staring off into the distance.

"Sunbathing all day — aren't you afraid of skin disease?" Jace asked.

"Getting a noble, lustrous green tan is far more important than health." The goblin sat up. "What took you so long? I thought that troll had boiled you down to nothing. I was about to come looking for you."

"Finding the troll's place cost me some time. She lives pretty hidden, I'll say that." Jace held up three bags. "But the mushroom I mentioned — she really did have it."

"Hah." The goblin took the three bags and opened them one by one, his eyes growing wider and wider. "My heavens, it's my first time on this continent seeing new fungi to rival Kalimdor's many strange mushrooms. Truly extraordinary. You've got some skills, human! You know an awful lot!"

"Honestly, it's not a new species," Jace said. "The Amani trolls have been living with them for years. They even gave it a rather formal-sounding name — Lukongkani."

"Hah, you've studied troll culture too? But good things discovered by trolls might as well not be discovered at all! They don't understand the value of good things — they just fumble by feel!" The goblin gave a confident grin. "Now witness the miracle. Once I grind them to powder, the experiments begin!"

"How long will it take?" Jace asked.

The goblin turned his head. "If it goes smoothly — maybe this afternoon, maybe midnight, maybe tomorrow. I've gotten so practiced at brewing this potion I could do it with my eyes closed. If it doesn't go smoothly… then there's a problem with the materials."

Jace thought: The materials definitely won't be the problem. If there's a problem, it's you.

"I'll find a place to stay," he said. "By the way — these materials cost me nine gold. How are you planning to pay me back?"

The goblin exclaimed, "That expensive?! You're sure that troll didn't cheat you? Forest trolls are full of lies — can't be trusted! I was swindled by her, and that's how I ended up in debt!"

"Considering the cost of getting to the Hinterlands, I think it's reasonable," Jace said. "But if you succeed in making the potion, I'll knock a bit off."

"Oh, should I thank you?" The goblin laughed. "But once my formula succeeds, nine gold won't mean a thing! And when this potion goes into mass production, once I've worked things out with the Trade Cartel fellows, I'll definitely set aside a share for you. Then you'll know how ridiculous it was to haggle over a few gold with a master alchemist!"

"Let's hope so."

It sounded nice, but Jace had zero faith that these goblins would ever agree on a reasonable profit split.

After all — it was a goblin's potion, a goblin's formula, a goblin's town, a goblin's Trade Cartel.

Since the Gadgetzan tycoon Noggenfogger was willing to share his formula with this Baxes Misersheld, Misersheld must be an important member of the Trade Cartel.

Which meant that in the entire operation, aside from him — the human — everyone else was in the same camp.

So Jace just hoped the potion would actually work soon, and free him from the Death Shadow sooner rather than later.

"I'm starting experiments now. You can rest in the study behind me, human — or take a stroll through town. Careful not to lose your little life, or I'll be missing an important witness!" The goblin finished and ducked into his room.
