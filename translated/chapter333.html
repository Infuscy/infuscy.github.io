Chapter 333: A Taste of Death

"Wake up, human! Get out here!"

In the morning, hearing the goblin shrieking outside, Jace pushed open the door and looked out. The sun was shining in off the sea, and there stood Misersheld in front of him.

"Come try it, quick…" Misersheld beckoned. "Come here, let me show you my results! That Lu-whatever mushroom and Noggenfogger's old formula are a perfect match. I can't believe a human like you came up with this. Is that alchemist woman you know secretly a goblin? Did she secretly coach you to come help me break through? A fan of mine? Or a secret admirer? That would actually make a lot of sense!"

"I thought you said you didn't care about pretty girls?" Jace asked.

"Not caring means not letting them interfere with my research!" Misersheld held up a finger for emphasis. "If they can help my experiments, why would I refuse? Am I that rigid?"

As he spoke, he led Jace toward a large room behind the alchemy lab that looked like a laboratory. Misersheld pushed the door open, and Jace caught a strange, fishy stink mixed with the smell of medicine. He looked inside. In the dim light, iron cages were lined up one after another.

What is this… an animal testing room?

Wait — these aren't animals. Jace's eyes swept over them and spotted three murlocs in one cage, a short female naga in another, and in the distance, a black-furred gnoll!

Jace instinctively took a step back at the sight of these bizarre creatures. Misersheld waved a hand. "Don't worry, human. They all qualify under the Trade Cartel's long list of acceptable targets. Testing on them in Booty Bay doesn't violate any regulations! There was a white gorilla too, but it accidentally died last week."

Jace observed these various intelligent or semi-intelligent creatures. They seemed to be in a drowsy, half-asleep state — not collapsed, but not awake either, paying no attention to the goblin and human entering.

Then he noticed a yellow-furred boar in the farthest cage, tilting its head against the bars.

At first he'd thought it was a tall Bloodthirster boar serving as an actual animal test subject, but as he got closer he realized — this was a boar standing upright!

A quilboar… this was actually a quilboar! Shipped over from Kalimdor?

"Never seen that before, have you, lad?" the goblin said proudly.

"Why don't they try to escape? Or attack you?" Jace asked.

"I have my own methods," the goblin said. "That's a trade secret."

Jace guessed the little bastard had probably used a gnomish mind-control ray on them one by one, or some goblin equivalent. Anyway, in Azeroth there were plenty of strange ways to make people behave.

"Except for this murloc." The goblin pointed to the smallest murloc with red scales. "Every other test subject took the new potion I brewed last night."

"They all began turning into undead within ten minutes of taking it… and gradually recovered between twenty minutes and two hours. Not a single case of coma. The transformation was extremely natural. And most importantly — their memories were continuous. Before and after the test, they were all equally stupid, and they all remembered that they had to obey the orders of Master Alchemist Misersheld!"

"The last test subject — was myself." The goblin spread his arms. "Look at me. No — look at this marvelous new potion. Damn it, this is what success tastes like!"

"You drank it yourself?"

Jace looked the goblin up and down. Indeed, no visible changes.

"Of course!" Misersheld said. "If I don't dare drink it myself, how could I dare serve it to distinguished customers?"

"What about the feigning?" Jace asked. "The most important part is that it has to produce a convincing false death…"

"No problem." Misersheld said. "According to all the data, they all underwent a death that was nearly impossible to distinguish from the real thing. It fooled even my life-signs monitoring instruments! Ugh, last night was three hours of nightmare. If they'd all actually died, I'd have been ruined! And gained one more damn… no, dear creditor!"

Jace felt his heart pound twice in his chest. This trip hadn't been for nothing after all.

The goblin grabbed Jace's trouser leg and shook it. "Right — how many more of those mushrooms does that troll have?!"

"According to the witch doctor, the three I gave you were the last of them," Jace said. "She wasn't planning to sell them to me in the first place."

"What?!" Misersheld's eyes went wide. "Then it's time to figure out a way into the Hinterlands. I still know a few people at Razdunk's Venture Company. I need to let them know the potential profits in this potion…"

"I don't care how you're going to make your money, Misersheld." Jace held out his hand. "Now you owe me my bottle."

"Of course. This way!"

Misersheld went back into his alchemy lab, took a bottle of the pale green potion from the several on the table, and handed it to Jace behind him. "Drink it!"

"Right — don't drink it all in one go. Just about a third of it. I didn't have a bottle the right size, so I made do with a large one."

Jace nodded, pulled the cork, and a bitter, indescribable smell drifted out.

After drinking a third of it, he gently closed his eyes.

The space around him seemed to shift and move. He felt his body spinning, but when he tried to open his eyes, he couldn't control his eyelids.

Then his body felt suspended — but not fully. His senses, his limbs, none of them felt like his own. His body was more like a cage locking him inside.

Was this some kind of near-death experience? No… this went a step beyond near-death. With his vision gone, even what he heard seemed to drift from one distant place to another, rather than into his own body.

Jace tried to grab onto something, but there was nothing to hold. A wave of insecurity washed over him — until he heard Molophil's voice.

"Don't panic… master. You've felt this before."

Right, yes. The soul link. Jace remembered now. The soul link.

"Don't let me die for no reason…"

He murmured it, wanting to walk toward the demon. He'd barely taken a step when everything in front of him suddenly shook.

"Hey! Can you hear?! Can you hear me?!" A muffled voice roared from beside him.

"Don't move! You definitely don't want to injure yourself in this state, human! The consequences would be unthinkable! Damn it, I shouldn't have let you take the potion in here full of bottles and jars — outside! Follow me outside! Move!"

Jace nodded and reluctantly followed a vague pull down the steps. Every step felt like he wasn't stepping on the ground, but on a stiff chunk of meat. Only after his senses returned somewhat did he realize these were the increasingly withered soles of his own feet.

Gradually, images began to appear in his field of vision — but everything had lost its color, reduced to black, white, and gray.

Then he noticed a slender figure in front of him, also looking at him. The build was thin, clothes hanging off him like they were on a clothes hanger. Wait — why was he wearing the same thing as Jace?

It was a mirror!

The goblin poked his head out from behind the mirror. "Well? Isn't it terrifying? If you went to that human pumpkin festival looking like that, you'd be the most spectacular guy there, guaranteed!"

Jace tried to speak, to ask when he'd return to normal, but his throat felt like a tangled ball of lint. He opened his mouth for a long time and only managed a few clicks and croaks.

"I know how you feel, but don't talk. Dead men don't talk! While you're at it, enjoy the feeling of eternal life! You might not be able to afford to try it again later! Hahahaha!"

Flags - POV: Third person, Jace. - Names: 米瑟谢尔德 → Misersheld. 莫洛菲尔 → Molophil (succubus). 撒厄诺 → Sazeno (imp). 诺格弗格 → Noggenfogger (canon). - Lore terms: soul link (灵魂链接), feign death (假死), Trade Cartel (热砂财团), Venture Company (风险投资公司), Razdunk (拉兹敦克), quilboar (野猪人), naga (纳迦), gnoll (豺狼人), murloc (鱼人), Bloodthirster boar (血牙野猪), Kalimdor (卡利姆多), the Hinterlands (辛特兰). - Tone note: Misersheld's manic, self-aggrandizing scientist energy is preserved; Jace's out-of-body potion trip is rendered with disorienting, sensory language. - Translation choice: "lad" used for 小子 to give the goblin a colloquial, slightly condescending flavor. "勉强" rendered as "reluctantly / with effort" — here "barely managed to follow."
