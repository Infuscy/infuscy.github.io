## Chapter 340: The One Who Got Chased by a Black Dragon

That afternoon, after parting with Grede, Jace brought the fel essence to the Slaughtered Lamb.

In the alchemy room, Ursula was sitting in a chair reading. Bubbles rose from the jars by themselves. Seeing Jace, she straightened up at once.

"Jace… don't tell me bad news. I'm already about to get crushed flat."

"What's wrong?" Jace asked, sitting down nearby.

"What else?" Ursula said. "Worried sick that you'd have a reaction to some goblin medicine."

"Not at all — you saved my life, Ursula," Jace said.

"Did it work?" Ursula asked.

"Not as smooth as I'd imagined," Jace said. "But in the end the result wasn't bad."

Ursula held back a laugh, then said, "How'd it go, Jace Sesso — in the end you still had to rely on my information?"

"Of course. I met a goblin alchemist in Booty Bay," Jace said. "He took one look at the invisibility potion you made and declared you a formidable alchemist."

"I need some goblin to judge me?" Ursula said. "I'm an alchemist who studied potion-making. Those little green-skinned things only make explosives!"

Jace said, "So I'm curious… where exactly did you learn alchemy?"

"Can't I keep it a secret?" Ursula glanced over and asked.

"Of course you can," Jace said. "Just asking casually. Everyone has their secrets."

As he spoke, he pulled a bottle of fel essence from his bag. "Take a look at this."

"What is this?"

"Don't you recognize it?" Jace shook the bottle. "It's fel essence. Isn't this what you wanted me to find?"

Ursula took the bottle, shook it back and forth, stared at it a moment. "Are you messing with me? How can fel essence be this thick? And the color's this dark. I've seen fel essence before — it's a light purple, potion-like. All right, a bit thick, sure, but compared to this… this is practically paste."

"Where'd you get this from?"

Jace cleared his throat lightly. "A troll witch doctor sold it to me. She said it was her finest fel essence."

"Oh my." Ursula sighed softly and shook the bottle again, her hand moving toward the stopper as if to open it — then hesitating.

"I… I'll open it when I actually need to use it, Jace. This thing is too rare a find. I can't believe you got it from a troll. I thought the rumors about trolls living in Booty Bay were just rumors. Honestly, I never really expected you to actually bring fel essence back for me — especially one this potent. Even though I can't sense the magic inside the way a mage can, especially through the bottle, even so, just by looking I can tell it's extraordinary."

"I thought that troll was exaggerating on purpose," Jace said.

As he spoke, he took out the restorative potion as well. "And this, Ursa. The trip went quickly, so there's still more than half left. I know this potion uses Elemental Earth — it must be extremely precious — so I'm giving it back to you."

Ursula took the bottle of potion and hugged it to her chest. "You didn't need to be this frugal."

"I don't want to owe you too much," Jace said with a smile. "So — is there anything else you need me to do? In a while I may be heading north of Redridge. Might be gone for a long time, exactly how long I'm not sure. If you need anything from the Burning Steppes or around Loch Modan, I can look into it for you along the way."

"I haven't thought that far ahead," Ursula said. "But — did the alchemist in Booty Bay tell you the specific formula for that potion?"

"How could he?" Jace said. "From what I gathered from their words, these goblins treat formulas that can make them money like their own lives. Let alone an outsider like me — they don't even tell their own kind."

"What a pity," Ursula said, disappointed. "A potion that can create a false death is such a rare thing."

"All right, I at least remember a few of the ingredients," Jace said. "One is a mushroom from the Hinterlands — the trolls call it rukongkani — extremely hard to gather. Then there's a plant called the mogranmos ghost grass. And… the dew gland from a swamp beast in an oasis in the southern desert of Kalimdor."

The dew gland of the Tanaris oasis swamp beast was, of course, contraband he'd smuggled over from the memories of his past life — part of the Noggenfogger potion quest requirements.

Since the strange mushroom from the Hinterlands had such a good effect, the other half of the quest should be fairly credible too.

"Desert oasis… swamp beast… let me see if there's anything that can serve as a substitute." Ursula's expression grew more complicated the more she listened, but she still said, "Can you tell me about the sensations you experienced after taking that potion? All of them…"

"I'll do my best." Jace described, from start to finish, everything he'd felt after drinking the potion. Ursula took notes as best she could, but after finishing she still sighed. "With just these clues, it's probably very difficult to reverse-engineer what formula they used."

"They used ghost fungus before," Jace said. "But that formula with ghost fungus caused the fainting you mentioned — even a chance of memory loss. It wasn't until they swapped the ghost fungus for the Hinterlands mushroom that these two side effects were resolved."

"That's an important clue too," Ursula said with a nod. "I'm not counting on recreating the goblin potion here, but having some reference is good."

"Anything else?"

"For now… there shouldn't be. If I think of something I'll come tell you."

Ursula nodded. "If you need any more potions, remember to come find me."

"Oh right," Jace said. "Do you have a reliable potion pouch — something that can hold them in place? Last time I was out, I got chased by a black dragon and smashed quite a few bottles."

"Ah," Ursula said. "I have one."

She handed a small leather pouch to Jace and smiled. "This pouch is small, but it has eight potion slots that hold them in place. Next time you get chased by a black dragon, they shouldn't get knocked around and broken."

"I don't want to get chased by a dragon again," Jace said with a smile. "Thanks."

Ursula paused a beat. "You're not joking? You really ran into a black dragon?"

"A whelp," Jace said. "But it was plenty troublesome."

"I'm thinking — next time you come, eat and drink well beforehand. I want to draw a little of your blood and take a look," Ursula said, staring at Jace's wrist. "See what exactly you are."

Blood draw? Jace glanced at all the strange equipment in the alchemy room, and his mind started pulling up everything he'd ever heard about the barbaric surgeries of early humanity.

He forced a smile. "You're joking."

Ursula asked, "You mean the joke about you getting chased by a black dragon?"

Jace's smile faded bit by bit. He swallowed.
