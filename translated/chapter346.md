## Chapter 346: What Demons Fear

The succubus's command didn't send the room into immediate action — perhaps because she'd spread her influence over too many minds at once and come up short on power.

"That man… is no outsider." Ursula murmured under her breath. "Jace, why—"

"She's scrambling our brains. Damn demon, I'll kill… I'll…" Erland, standing beside Galders, pressed a hand to his forehead and mumbled.

Sulenna brandished a bottle she'd found God-knows-where and charged screaming forward — Jace drove a kick into her ribs and she dropped to the floor with a pained groan.

Jace forced his mind into focus, fighting off the seduction. This succubus was no weakling; if she got her hooks into him, everyone in the room was done for. And right now, her attention was squarely fixed on him.

Sensing the threat before her, the succubus lunged and locked her fingers around Galders' throat. Galders seized her wrist but hadn't the strength to pry her off.

"Let him go," Jace said.

"You're interesting, human." The succubus smiled. "Maybe I should be serving you instead of him. Interested?"

"You're not worthy." Jace snorted. "Now. Let him go, or—"

"Or what, you'll kill me?" the succubus whispered. "You think that means a damn thing to a demon? Go ahead. Maybe I can't beat you, but one word from me and these people start tearing each other apart. You think you can stop them?"

Watching Jace's eyes flick toward the others, the succubus said, "Ah, I was right — you do care about them. I love that. No matter how powerful a mortal is, they always have something they—"

"You have something you fear too, Sayaad."

Jace spoke in Demonic. His command of the language was rough, but he got the meaning across clearly enough.

"So you can do more than just recite incantations in Eredun, warlock." The succubus answered in the same tongue. "Who taught you?"

"A banshee huntress," Jace said. "She'll be paying you a visit."

"Hah, you're lying—" The succubus had just started when something seemed to click. Her expression suddenly went panicked. "No… that's not—"

In that instant Galders lunged and seized her by the throat!

Realizing her magic had failed, the succubus thrashed wildly, trying to wrench free of Galders' grip.

"Hold her down!" Jace shouted.

Erland snapped back to his senses and rushed over. Together with Jace he helped Galders pin the demon down. In the chaos Galders drove a dagger into the succubus's heart, and at last her frenzy stopped.

"Ah, fuck." Erland stared at the dagger. "There goes the resale value, Galders…"

"You were still thinking about selling her?!" Jace snapped. "Were you not just in that room?"

"Jace… nothing happened to me, did it? I resisted the demon's magic!" Erland protested quickly.

"That's one way to put it… fine, I suppose it counts." Jace had to admit — at least Erland had kept his wits about him.

Galders stared at the succubus's corpse and let out a few ragged breaths. "Seems I wasn't ready… Jace, thank you."

Jace drew a long breath and let it out in a rush. "Next time, before you summon, figure out a handle on the succubus seduction magic first."

"The fact that she could hit all of us at once…" Ursula held her forehead. "I wonder if a Shadow Protection Potion would even help. Or maybe I should look into potions that strengthen mental fortitude."

"It's not like I didn't know succubi could pull something like this." Galders leaned against the wall. "I thought I could grit my teeth and hit her back with a Fear spell or something, but I didn't expect the act of summoning itself to take such a toll on me."

"I'm sorry, Jace." Sulenna clutched her chest, voice low. "I… I don't know what I was thinking."

"Don't dwell on it, Sulenna." Jace helped her to her feet.

"Thanks, everyone. Let's call it for today." Galders inspected the claw marks across his ribs. "I need to patch myself up. Erland, are you hurt?"

Erland gave himself a once-over and waved it off. "I'm fine."

"So what about the body?"

"Leave that to me!"

Erland hoisted the corpse and hauled it toward the storage room. Sulenna called after him to put it in that empty room no one ever used, and for God's sake not to get demon blood all over the storage room's things.

"Jace, really, you should have been the one summoning first. You didn't seem bothered by her magic at all…" Galders began.

Jace glanced over, and before he could speak Galders seemed to catch on to something. "You've already summoned a succubus yourself, haven't you? That's how you knew her weakness — just a few words and she bent the knee…"

Jace nodded.

"So when did you summon her?"

"Before Jagin died," Jace said. "I'm sorry. I'd planned to master the spell before saying anything, but then what happened to Jagin…"

"Now I understand your hesitation." Galders rubbed the scratch marks the succubus had left on his neck and sighed. "But how did you manage to enslave the one you summoned?"

"A demon's true name," Jace said.

Galders thought it over. "So… that fellow Mosh'aru was telling the truth. I'd figured it was just a story circulating among demon researchers — that controlling a demon's true name meant holding their leash. But getting a demon to give up their secret can't be easy, can it?"

"Either make them unable to refuse, or offer them something they want in exchange," Jace said.

Galders stared at the pool of demon blood spreading across the floor. "These demons don't fear death. They claim it's nothing but an ordinary cycle — so how do you make them hand over their names? I've half a mind not to go ask those Dark Iron dwarves after all. Those arrogant bastards… they're only willing to part with the simplest of spells. I'm sure they know the secret of demon names, but they never breathed a word of it to Jagin or me."

"Jace, it's true these demons don't fear death. But once we summon them, we can find a way to trap them here — then have my succubus torture them until they break and hand over their names."

"That actually sounds feasible." Galders asked, "So is that succubus only afraid of you because she's scared you'll do exactly that?"

"Succubi are experts at torment. They know better than anything what it feels like." Jace looked at him. "I'll find a way to bring my succubus into the Mage Quarter, to the Slaughtered Lamb. She can help us summon other demons."

Galders nodded. "But I'll need some time to recover before trying again. The summoning took a lot out of me, on top of that succubus's magic…"

"I'll walk you back to your room," Jace said.

"Thanks!"
