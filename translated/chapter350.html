Chapter 350: The succubus's First Day of Work

By evening, the streets and gardens of the Mage Quarter were nearly empty.

Walking along the roadside, Jace still felt as though wool was growing all over him — deeply uncomfortable. He had a feeling that after being turned into a sheep by Malin during the day, he'd probably dream of running across some vast grassland tonight.

At night, even under the influence of Sense Demons, Morophil's silhouette was hard to make out.

She advanced carefully along the edge of the street, while the passersby standing and chatting in the distance paid her no mind at all.

In the end, the apprentice mages and student mages who relaxed here at night were simply too insensitive to demons — or perhaps the more cautious Morophil's ability to conceal herself was simply too strong for them.

In any case, once they'd left the Wizard's Sanctum's district behind, no one sensed anything amiss. When they reached the quiet street where the Slaughtered Lamb stood, Jace relaxed considerably.

He entered the tavern and went down to the basement. Zardeth, Surena, and Erlan were already there waiting for him, while Ursula sat off to the side reading a book.

"Sorry I'm late," Jace said.

"Jace." Erlan's eyes were fixed unblinkingly on the passage leading into the basement. "You said you were bringing a succubus, right? So where is she — or should I say, he?"

"Succubi know Invisibility, Erlan," Surena reminded him. "Have you forgotten what I told you about Gakin? And if she can't turn invisible, how would you even get her into the Mage Quarter?"

"Right." Erlan cleared his throat, a little nervously, and swept his eyes around the room. "So that means your succubus is already among us?"

"Morophil, you can come out," Jace said. "It's safe here. Everyone's one of us."

"Morophil…" Surena murmured the name under her breath.

The succubus stepped out of the shadows. She swept her gaze across everyone present, then looked back at Jace. "So this is where I'll be working?"

"You don't like it?" Jace asked.

Morophil sized up the fire burning in the center of the hall. "If it's what you want, then I can like it."

"You won't enchant us, will you?" Surena said anxiously. "Miss Morophil — forgive me, I'm still not quite used to dealing with a demon as… beautiful as you."

Morophil smiled. "Master told me not to enchant you, so of course I won't."

Zardeth had been studying the succubus's back, seemingly curious about Morophil's broken wings.

He clearly wasn't the only one interested. Erlan asked, "Maybe I shouldn't ask, Jace, but other succubi have a pair of demon wings on their backs — and this lady's back…"

At the question, Morophil's eyes cut sideways and locked onto him, leaving the tall man so unsure of where to put his hands that he awkwardly shifted his stance.

"That doesn't matter, Erlan," Jace said. "All you need to know is that she's a succubus too. I trust she has the ability to help us subdue the demons we summon."

"I was just asking — not doubting her abilities or anything," Erlan said, raising both hands.

"All right, it's already late." Zardeth stood. "Now that Jace is here, shall we begin?"

"Ready whenever you are." Erlan picked up a long-handled fork that had been prepared beside him. "This time we need to be even more careful than last time."

Everyone gathered their potions and spell materials and headed for the practice room. Jace walked at the rear, and he could feel that although everyone was doing their best not to pay too much attention to the succubus — looking away, trying to act natural — they were anything but natural.

Ursula gradually drifted closer to Jace as they walked. She glanced at Morophil, who was trailing behind Jace, and said in a low voice, "You never introduced me to this friend of yours, Jace."

"We all have our secrets, don't we?" Jace said, half-joking.

The alchemist knew he was alluding to her reluctance to talk about where she'd learned alchemy, but she still said, "That's not the same thing, Jace. After all, summoning a demon like this means a great deal to us."

Jace nodded. "I admit I shouldn't have hidden it. It was what happened with Gakin that made me hesitate — and I've already explained it to Zardeth."

"All right, I understand how you feel," Ursula said with a nod.

They entered the practice room. As before, the soul shard and the ring were set out. Morophil kept staring at the ring. Jace noticed her gaze. "What's wrong?"

"It looks quite nice. I can sense what's stored inside," Morophil said. "But it won't attract a very powerful sayaad, Master. You'd better not get your hopes up."

With that, she tilted her head and glanced sideways at him.

Jace knew what she meant. After all, she was here for fun, not to get any real work done.

But right now, it was clear she felt she couldn't even find the fun.

"This is just the beginning," Jace said, comforting her. "Later, there will be things you like."

Zardeth nodded. "For now, we can only run preliminary tests."

As he spoke, he drank down a Shadow Protection Potion, double-checked the incantation, and asked, "Is everyone ready? Potions and so on."

Everyone nodded. Their expressions visibly tightened — especially Erlan.

He took the half-bottle of Shadow Protection Potion Surena handed him and drank it down, then immediately hefted the iron tongs, looking as though he was ready to rush forward the moment a demon appeared.

But Jace was far more at ease than last time.

After Zardeth received everyone's confirmation, he began the summoning ritual. The soul shard dimmed as before. Jace noticed Morophil's gaze drift to one side, and his own eyes followed.

The room seemed to be shrouded by some shadow, and the burning lamps flickered.

"Where the hell have I ended up?!"

The room lit up again. An angry voice roared from the corner. Everyone present couldn't help but flinch — even Jace felt the shout prickle across his scalp.

A completely naked succubus flapped her wings and flew to the far side of the room, pressing herself against the wall. She glared at Morophil and shouted a stream of something in Demonic.

Erlan raised the iron tongs and was about to smash them down when the succubus shrieked at him, forcing him to clutch his head and stagger back a step in pain.

Zardeth covered his ears and said quickly, "Hold on… Erlan, see what she has to say."

"Common, damn it," the succubus said, fixing her eyes on her kin nearby. "Why are you with a bunch of humans?"

She glanced at Erlan beside her. "Are you her servant? Sworn to a skin-flaying devil?"

"Her servant?" Erlan cursed. "What the fuck are you talking about?!"

Morophil didn't move or speak much. She simply stared at her, expressionless, watching her press flat against the wall, trembling all over with anger… or perhaps with fear. The newly summoned succubus gripped her whip tightly, sweeping her gaze quickly around the room, but in the end she kept her eyes locked on Morophil.

"Tell me — is this some kind of ridiculous prank trap?"

"It's not a trap," Zardeth said. "It's only a summoning ritual, demon. You were summoned here by me. You answer to me. Speak your true name."

"Speak my true name?" Even as she asked it, the succubus's eyes never left Morophil. One hand braced against the wall, she held a tense, ready-to-fight stance.

She hesitated a moment, then asked, "Just that? You want me to reveal my true name to my summoner? To obey?"

"Dear Master… what do we do? Once she confesses, is that it?" Morophil asked Jace, a look of clear disappointment on her face.

Jace raised an eyebrow and looked to Zardeth. "Zardeth, she's yours. It's your call."

Zardeth looked at the succubus. "Yes. As long as she's willing to speak her true name and tell me."

The succubus spoke at once. "I'll say it — just keep that hag away from me… I'll tell you everything, my new Master…"

Everyone in the room except Jace turned to look at Morophil with complicated expressions. Morophil, for her part, was staring at the door leading out of the practice room, making no effort at all to hide her desire to go home immediately.
