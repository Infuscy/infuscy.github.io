Chapter 351: The Trail of the Demon Scholar

In the basement of the Slaughtered Lamb, Zardeth was in his room talking with the succubus about something. To prevent another incident like Gakin's, the others hadn't left just yet.

They sat around the cold fire pit, and for a long time nobody spoke. Morophil was leaning against the wall behind Jace, tossing her shortsword into the air and catching it over and over — she looked thoroughly bored.

In the end it was Surena who looked up first. She glanced at the succubus by the wall, then at Jace. "Skinning… you mean skinning a demon, right?"

"That demon was just trying to scare you," Jace said, propping his chin on his hand from the armrest, his gaze fixed on the doorway to Zardeth's room. "Don't worry so much…"

"Honestly, I've never skinned a human before," Morophil said.

"Then keep it that way, lady," Erlan said quickly.

Just then, a dull thud came from Zardeth's room. Erlan turned his head; Jace sat up straighter.

"I'll go have a look."

Erlan walked into the passageway. Surena watched nervously.

A moment later, Erlan came back with a wicked grin and looked at Surena. "Relax — Zardeth is alive. And living quite passionately, I'd say."

"They're already at it?" Surena asked, surprised.

"A grown man and a naked sex fiend alone in one room — what do you think is happening? They've probably gone a few rounds already," Erlan said. "Though I certainly understand the man."

Surena peered down the passageway, then looked at Erlan. "So what are we supposed to do?"

"Seize the moment and have a round of our own, of course!"

Erlan scooped Surena up and charged down the corridor toward the practice room. Surena let out a yelp. They'd clearly scurried off to their little dark room for some exercise.

Jace coughed and turned to look at Ursula, who was sitting on a stool with a straight face. "I'd already considered what bringing a succubus in here would turn this place into…"

"It wasn't any different before, Jace. Now there's just one more weirdo joining this chaotic sporting event," Ursula said with a shrug.

"What about us, master?" Morophil had somehow already pressed up behind him.

"In the Mage Quarter?" Jace glanced toward the exit. "No."

"Ah, so you and your succubus have done it too?" Ursula asked, giving him a sidelong look.

"You should give it a try, human," Morophil said to Ursula, stroking Jace's neck. "The master really knows what he's doing."

The alchemist took a deep breath, stood up, and said with a wicked grin, "Looks like I'm in the way here!"

"Ursula, she's doing this on purpose…" Jace said helplessly.

Ursula grabbed her coat, glanced back at the two of them, raised one eyebrow, and walked off down the corridor.

"Master, now it's just the two of us," Morophil said, pushing Jace down onto the chair and sitting in his lap. "You don't have to lift a finger — I'll do all the work…"

Jace caught her hands. "This place puts me on edge. That has nothing to do with whether we're alone. Tonight, keep an eye on that succubus and make sure nothing goes wrong. I can't afford to lose another person."

Morophil said, "She already told that warlock her true name. What are you still worried about?"

"Better safe than sorry. You stay here and watch that end. I'm going to the alchemy room to ask her something."

"So you're just going to leave me here, at a time like this?"

Even as she said it, a series of soft moans was already drifting from the practice room.

"We'll talk about it later. Just make sure nothing goes wrong tonight," Jace said.

The succubus hopped lightly to the side, used her tail to flick the shortsword up into the air, and caught it. "Fine. I'll do whatever you say!"

Jace nodded and walked into the alchemy room. Ursula was tidying things on the shelf. She turned at the sound of his footsteps.

"Already done?" she asked.

Jace raised both hands. "I didn't do anything!"

Ursula smiled. "You mean you didn't do anything just now, or that things between you and your succubus are clean?"

"Not clean at all," Jace confessed.

Ursula leaned against the cabinet and clicked her tongue. "Wouldn't have guessed it."

"Stop messing with me, Ursula," Jace said. "Don't take what the demon said to heart."

"I know what succubi are, Jace," Ursula waved a hand. "I'm not as bothered by that stuff as you think. Though I'm finding it harder and harder to see you as a kid!"

"You send kids into troll ruins and Dark Iron dwarf camps?" Jace said, sitting down nearby.

"The young need the training more," Ursula said with a smile. "But you didn't come here just to apologize, did you?"

"I did come to apologize," Jace said. "But there is something I wanted to ask you. How much do you know about that Mor'zul fellow?"

"I wasn't expecting you to ask about him." She glanced sideways at the nearby cauldron, thinking for a moment. "Mor'zul… all right. He's a little older than me, on the thin side, looks down on everyone, talks like a jerk, and he always reeks of all sorts of strange magical ingredients — and not the pleasant kind. Pretty much the exact opposite of Zardeth. But when it comes to shadow magic, especially demonology… that man is an absolute genius, Jace. He was never satisfied with just summoning an imp or whatever. I don't think he even cared much about succubi. He wanted to step into the Void himself and capture a demon with his own hands — can you believe that? Ever since he got here, he was dead set on achieving that goal. I thought it was a long shot, but a lot of what we know about demons, we learned from him. I think at first Mor'zul wanted us to help him, but after a while he decided we were all just holding him back."

Jace asked, "Is that why he left?"

"Probably," Ursula said. "I didn't know him that well, but Gakin was fond of him. And like Zardeth said, I don't think Gakin's cut ties with him."

"Do you have any idea where he might have gone?"

"If I had to guess? Most likely Moonbrook," Ursula said. "Where else? He'd need other people interested in demons as helpers to achieve his goal. Outside of Moonbrook, there's no larger gathering of warlocks. Are you planning to go looking for him?"

"No plans to," Jace said. "Just curious, the way you all talk him up."

But even as he said it, he was genuinely entertaining the idea of tracking this man down — to see just how much there was to learn from him.
