## Chapter 366: The Disturbed Earth

For a while now, every stone in the ravine seemed to have grown feverishly hot, as if on the verge of boiling.

Before his madness, Deathwing had been known as the Earth-Warder, and his offspring were elemental masters capable of manipulating lava and scorched earth. The black dragon that had just swept past was already far away, but the insane power it had brought had clearly thrown these elements into disorder too.

The freshly assembled earth elemental immediately stretched its body and lunged toward the two dwarves. Grede swung his hammer, shattering a chunk of stone and deflecting the elemental's follow-up blow; fragments of rock exploded into the air in every direction.

The earth elemental let out an angry, gurgling roar — like wind howling through stone — and the scorching, smoldering air spinning around its body actually drew in a fresh mass of rubble, condensing it into a new fist. The other fist lashed out swiftly at Daggett beside it.

In the span of this exchange, Jace quickly incanted a Curse of Weakness. Just as the earth elemental was bearing down on Daggett, a large scatter of stones fell away from its body as the curse took hold, its forward momentum thrown into disarray.

The elemental felt its searing power being dragged down by the shadow magic striking from behind and was about to swing at Jace when Grede's hammer smashed into its torso!

The shattered boulder let out a tremendous crash, and the whole earth elemental tilted and collapsed like a demolished stone hut.

The first earth elemental to rise had been toppled in a few blows, but now several more of varying sizes were pulling themselves up all around them.

Jace felt a blast of hot wind at his back. He turned to see several chunks of earth and stone gathering amid dancing flames, flinging a mass of rock at him — and it hadn't even fully formed when it launched the attack!

The half-formed earth elemental wielded its stones with tremendous force, but its sheer ferocity telegraphed its intent too early.

Jace easily dodged the strike and instinctively spat an Immolate incantation at one of its "arms," but the blast of flame didn't do much against the already-burning stones. Though it shattered a few chunks, the fire seemed to draw even more stones in, making the elemental before him grow an entire size larger.

A roar from Grede came from behind, followed by the sound of a warhammer crushing stone — clearly, they'd been tangled up by other earth elementals too.

"Kuuu... kah!"

The earth elemental before him, beneath its two flickering flame-like eyes, suddenly spat a burst of rubble. Jace dodged to the side; the scorching stones clattered across the ground, only to be swept up again by a burning whirlwind and drawn back around the elemental.

After pulling the rubble back together, the elemental seemed to have grown a notch taller.

Jace used Fel Grasp to casually tear a chunk of rubble from its chest. The earth elemental's body swayed, but it quickly filled the gap with several large stones and scraped in another mass of rock from the side to patch the void, all while emitting a strange whooshing sound. The rubble beneath it suddenly rolled toward Jace with accelerated speed, charging up to him like a battering ram, both arms raised high. Jace reacted instantly, incanting: "Thomsogas, Veholi—"

The twin arms of condensed boulders crashed down together. Jace sidestepped the blow, glanced at the cracked, parched earth where it had struck, and continued: "...Thasia, Vasdaron!"

The earth elemental, enraged at having missed, swept its fist horizontally — and it slammed straight into the Shadow Ward!

The Shadow Ward held firm against the blow, and the dark-hued barrier of shadow flickered and burst apart. The entire body of the earth elemental was blown backward, its arm shattering into the air in a plume of dust.

A faint purple dark energy gnawed at the stones of its torso and head, slowing its ability to gather its shattered stone body back together.

Jace raised his staff at the half-shattered earth elemental, focused his will, and intoned Immolate. A burst of shadowflame devoured the lingering fires on its broken body, triggering an even more violent dark explosion.

The force of the blast didn't just shatter the stones — the shadow energy within, merging with the elemental's own heat, tore through and carved a dark elemental "fissure" amid flame and smoke, as if it had directly infected and destroyed the very force binding the rubble together.

The already-grievously-wounded earth elemental collapsed at once. The great stones that had floated on searing power fell one by one, turning dim and black, rolling a short distance before lying still forever.

Jace caught his breath. The scattered chunks of earth and stone on the ground had lost all vitality. Looking over, he saw Grede had just smashed an earth elemental to pieces with his warhammer and was hurling his hammer at another that had Daggett tangled up.

Daggett parried the elemental's assault with a long staff — he seemed a nimble enough fellow, but under the elemental's relentless blows he was starting to look a little rough.

Grede's warhammer, crackling with lightning, smashed squarely into the elemental's back. His Lightning Hammer Throw was devastatingly effective against these elementals — a chain of detonating current blasted it into a shower of stone, freeing Daggett from the relentless hammering.

He reeled his crackling hammer back in, nodded to Daggett, who lowered his head, caught his breath, and raised a hand. "Thanks, Grede brother, I'm alright!"

Grede rolled his shoulders. "I was hoping a few more of the buggers would show up — let me work off some of the anger from being chased by a pack of beasts half the night."

Daggett looked at the two of them. "You two are even more impressive than I thought. If there'd been folk like you on that expedition, things might not have ended in such a mess."

He looked at the rubble on the ground, rummaged around, and walked over to a large cracked, still-smoking boulder. He pulled out a chisel, struck it, and split it open — a puff of black smoke and fire billowed out.

"What is that?" Grede asked.

"Watch this." Daggett pulled a thick, oven-mitt-like glove from his backpack, put it on, picked up a chunk of the split stone that still held a trace of fire, and said, "This is an elemental core fragment of an earth elemental. An experienced alchemist can refine true earth from it... I mean pure Elemental Earth."

So that was Elemental Earth... In fact, during the fight with the earth elementals just now, Jace had completely forgotten about that.

"Do the other earth elemental creatures have them too?" he asked. "The ones over there?"

"These don't come around often," Daggett said. "Because the force that drives earth elementals vanishes when they collapse. Only by good fortune does a still-warm elemental core like this get left behind — all the other stones have gone cold."

With that, Daggett cradled the stone in his gloved hands and handed it to Jace. "Find a knowledgeable alchemist, pay him twenty or thirty silver for labor and materials, and he'll be happy to refine Elemental Earth out of this core for you, mage."

"You're the one who found this special stone. You don't want it?" Grede asked.

"All I want is to go back in one more time. The rest is unnecessary. You two are the ones who defeated these hostile elementals — they should belong to you." Daggett said, "Let's keep moving."
