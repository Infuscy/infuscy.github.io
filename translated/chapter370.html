Chapter 370: An Anomaly on the Stone

At the corner of the passage, just as Daggett had said, there was a fearsome crack in the cave — as if something had burrowed straight through it, claw marks gouged all over the walls.

Grede took point, with Jace and Daggett behind him as the three of them squeezed inside. In the game, this was where three dwarf mercenary gunmen had holed up — no idea how long they'd been hiding here, but it definitely couldn't have been a decade or more.

And if there'd been three Bronzebeards hiding in there, these troggs would've long since found some way to seal up this cave entrance, rather than leaving it wide open.

Sure enough, once they were inside, Jace could already hear the troggs' nonsensical babbling.

Daggett kept looking ahead, nearly pulling past Grede, who'd been walking in front the whole time. Whatever was in there clearly meant a great deal to him — even after leaving this place all this time, he still couldn't let it go and had come all this way back for one more look.

As the winding passage reached its end, Jace saw a trogg at the lit cave opening up ahead, its back to them, staring into the cave.

Grede gripped his hammer and looked at Jace, making a pulling-back motion. Jace immediately understood what he meant.

Jace reached out, and a bolt of crimson magical energy shot forward and seized the trogg. The creature barely made a sound before it was yanked over. Grede smashed his hammer into its spine with a crack, flattening it against the wall, where it slid down limply.

Silence inside for a moment. Before long, a set of footsteps approached, and a trogg poked its head in to look. Grede hurled his hammer straight at it and blew its skull apart.

That set the cave abuzz. Several troggs chattered and shrieked. Grede charged straight in, swinging his hammer and sending a trogg beside the cave flying. Jace followed behind him, raising his staff and knocking a trogg charging headlong at him to the ground. He watched as a tattered-cloth-wearing trogg raised a short club and began muttering something at him.

Jace shouted an Immolate incantation straight at him. A detonation burst before his eyes. The trogg shaman howled and stumbled back several steps but didn't take much damage.

He watched as a strange little totem, lashed together from bone and wood, was wreathed in a blaze of fire and shadow, then turned to powder and vanished into a cloud of smoke.

What the hell was that... a Grounding Totem?

In the game, a Grounding Totem was a very fragile little thing that could absorb a single spell attack. Jace hadn't expected to run into one of these in front of a trogg shaman!

Jace tried to use Immolate to attack again, but the shaman had already ducked into an earthen trench.

"There's a shaman, Grede!"

"Saw him!" Grede punched the face of the trogg that had grabbed his hammer-wielding right hand, wrenching its head sideways, then pulled his hand free and smashed his hammer through its skull, chasing after the shaman.

Daggett pulled his short blade from a trogg's forehead and, together with Jace, jumped into the trench and ran after Grede. Grede braced himself against the narrow earthen trench, pushing forward — when suddenly a flash of light appeared before his eyes!

A bolt of lightning streaked straight along the earthen wall. Grede immediately let go of the wall he'd been bracing against, and Jace pressed himself to the other side as the bolt blasted the wall apart in a shower of stone chips and dust.

"Nature Protection Potion."

Jace instructed Daggett, drank his own, and pressed forward to follow.

"Hah!"

Grede roared and raised his crackling hammer, about to bring it down. Lightning lit up the inside of the trench, and the trogg shaman had summoned a sheet of lightning as well. The two bolts met — and actually sent Grede staggering back a step!

"You dare... stand against me in my own domain?!"

The trogg howled as it sent a bolt of lightning crashing toward Grede. The lightning detonated in the narrow space, bouncing and bursting back and forth, blinding and deafening. Grede dodged the first strike but still got hit by the chain of lightning that followed, coughing and dodging to the side.

The two of them on this side recovered quickly after drinking the potion. Seizing the opening, the shaman pulled a bone club from behind its back and drove it into the wall, still muttering incantations — another totem!

Jace had no idea what kind of totem it was, but the first order of business was to destroy it. He used Fel Grasp to grab the club and yank it over, snapping it in midair.

The shaman, seeing its totum destroyed, immediately intanted a spell. Jace had planned to finish it with Immolate and blow its skull apart, but instead the whole earthen trench began to shake. Jace felt the ground give way beneath his foot — one of his legs sank straight into a crack that had split open in the earth!

"Careful!" Daggett grabbed him with one hand and clutched a rock with the other. Jace braced himself against the nearby earth, pointed at the shaman, and finished his Immolate incantation. A burst of blackened flame shattered half its body, finally stopping the shaking of the ground.

"Grede!"

Jace pulled his foot out of the sinkhole and walked over to Grede's side. Grede coughed, sat up, and brushed the sand off his body. "This bastard can actually summon a Lightning Shield to protect itself! How does he know how everything..."

"Did you drink the Nature Protection Potion?" Jace asked.

"It was so urgent back then, when did I have time to..." Grede felt himself all over — his skin, his clothes were covered in black scorch marks — before finally pulling the potion out.

"Drink a Healing Potion." Jace pulled one out. "Quickly."

Grede nodded and drank it, catching his breath. Jace looked over at Daggett — and found that he'd already run ahead to rifle through the trogg shaman's corpse.

"Daggett?"

"I know — it's got to be on him!" Daggett whispered.

"What the hell is it?" Grede hobbled over and asked.

Daggett didn't answer, just kept searching. After a while, he pulled a necklace from a patch of flesh charred and rotted by shadow.

"This is it..." Daggett murmured under his breath, then held it up to Grede. "Look — what do you think of this thing, Wildhammer brother?"

Grede frowned and glanced over impatiently — but the moment he saw what was in Daggett's hand, his expression softened and his eyebrows rose. "That's... actually quite pretty."

"What the hell." Jace craned his neck curiously and looked at the strange object in Daggett's hand.

It was a stone carved with the delicate face of a woman, set on a rather handsome dark stone and strung into a necklace.

As for how beautiful this woman was... Jace could only say she was reasonably attractive by human standards. But why would a Bronzebeard dwarf be so taken with this thing? Was this his wife?

But the moment he glanced at it, he couldn't help but look again. Wait — this stone contained an extremely potent shadow energy. With that thought, he snapped back to his senses.

Could it be that the shadow power within this stone had been tugging at Daggett's heart all this time, leaving him unable to forget it for over a decade and forcing him to come all the way here to put this obsession to rest?

"It's finally back in my hands." Daggett lowered his head. "This is mine... no one can take it from me."

"If it really is that important, the troggs outside might come looking for it, Daggett," Jace said. "We need to leave this cave now, before they get curious about what happened in here."

Daggett nodded. "Right, you're right. My goal's been accomplished now, Grede — I'll take you deeper inside."

Grede stared at Jace and tilted his eyes toward Daggett.

Jace knew Grede was feeling that something was off too.

Jace recognizes this as a phantom created by the Old God Yogg-Saron, imprisoned in Ulduar in the far north. The reference is to the in-game Yogg-Saron raid encounter, where Yogg-Saron appears as a seemingly benevolent maiden named Sara to deceive players; "Sara" is also the name associated with the Yogg-Saron achievement progression. The stone is likely forged from ore condensed from Yogg-Saron's black blood that pervades Northrend. - Game mechanic note: Jace explicitly references the Grounding Totem mechanic from the game ("absorbs a single spell attack") and his own hundreds of repeats of Yogg-Saron's first phase for achievement progress — kept as an in-universe gamer reference in his internal monologue. - Tone note: The trogg shaman fight is rendered as a chaotic, close-quarters brawl in a narrow trench. The reveal of the necklace and Daggett's unsettling attachment to it shifts the tone toward quiet dread; Jace's growing wariness is kept understated but clear.
