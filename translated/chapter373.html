Chapter 373: Underground Siege

At the sudden death of their elder, the troggs erupted like punctured blisters, waving their weapons and surging up the steps in a swarm. The mass of dark creatures under Skysong's light was like a surging tide, their eyes like the lurking deep-sea fish within it.

Jace clenched his fist and chanted calmly, loosing a bolt of dark-red Incinerate at the nearest trogg. The flames, empowered by Skysong, burst open like a blooming lotus on the creature's body, simultaneously blasting back four or five nearby troggs. Their falls tripped up even more dark shapes surging up from behind, momentarily disrupting the tide of the monster horde.

Even so, more and more creatures kept climbing over the bodies of those ahead and pressing forward.

When the troggs closed in on the steps, Jace raised his staff and sang out a Fear spell. The creatures that had been madly charging up the steps froze in their tracks and tried to retreat, even pushing those behind them backward. A few of the braver ones were still trying to push up — Jace detonated another Incinerate at their feet, blowing the fastest ones to pieces.

Daggart pressed himself against the stone slab, not daring to move. He'd clearly been affected by the Fear as well, his eyes darting sideways at the troggs charging up from below.

Just then, a bolt of lightning flashed from a distance. Jace felt a tingling wash over his whole body, followed by unbearable, searing pain — but he only shook his head, gritted his teeth through the pain, and pinpointed the source of the lightning.

Mage Armor had given him enough protection to keep him from taking serious damage. He looked over and saw a shaman had climbed atop a nearby raised stone and was already casting his next spell at Jace. The troggs below the steps were still in chaos from those who'd fallen and those who'd been frightened, colliding into each other. Jace pointed at the trogg shaman and immediately channeled Life Drain — but before he'd drawn even half, the shaman's legs gave out from weakness and he slipped off the stone, causing Jace to lose his target and the spell to snap.

But even that single pull was enough to recover from the Lightning Bolt he'd just taken.

By now, a trogg wielding a stone hammer had finally broken through the chaotic mass and charged up the steps. Jace struck it across the skull with his staff, cracking its head open with one blow. It convulsed and collapsed on the steps.

He brandished his sword and roared down at them, "Come on, then! Try melee with a mage!"

The shock value of that blow was effective. The troggs below who'd still been trying to rush up suddenly realized that the mage up high was also a brawny fighter. The one-two punch threw the charging crowd into even greater chaos.

Seizing the opportunity, Jace cast another Incinerate into the packed crowd of troggs. He had no idea how many it hit this time — whole groups of troggs had flames bursting from their clothes, their arms and bodies blasted with purple blood tainted by shadow, howling and trying to flee backward. But they were too packed to move, so they could only struggle back and forth on the spot.

Then a trogg as tall as a Kul Tiran, wielding a war hammer, shoved through his companions and charged up the steps. This one wasn't some lackey or minion — he was a true warrior, possibly even a minor chieftain. Before he even reached Jace, he'd already swung his long-handled stone hammer!

Jace shoved Daggart aside and chanted rapidly, "Tomethos, Veholisha, Vasdaron..." A shadow shield, indistinct amid the interplay of light and dark, rose and surrounded Jace. The tall trogg warrior brought his hammer down squarely on the dark shield — it bounced off as though he'd struck something soft and yielding, and then the whole body was thrown back by a wave of shadow force, his feet slipping on the steps, nearly toppling over.

Its face and body had already been blasted open by shadow, covered in corroded wounds — and yet it still straightened itself and raised its stone hammer once more.

Just as it was about to swing again, Jace drove his sword into its chest. Its eyes went blank and it toppled backward.

Jace wrenched his sword free. The arcane light from Skysong tore through the trogg's ribcage, and he swung straight into the face of another creature that had already charged up, shearing off half its head. He swung again at another trogg rushing him with a Dark Iron cleaver — one stroke cleaved the blade in two and took off its arm. As it howled in pain, Jace kicked it back down the steps.

The troggs trampled over their companions' corpses and lunged up again, only to be cut down and run through by Jace one after another. Daggart fought beside him with his bronze rod, again and again stabbing back the troggs that tried to climb up from the sides of the steps.

Then a trogg at the base of the steps tried to hurl a rock upward. Jace smashed the face of a nearby trogg wielding a small chisel with his staff, then turned to cast Incinerate at the rock-thrower — when suddenly he felt as though he'd been punched, and he coughed. The fire elements he'd been gathering scattered at once.

Another shaman had appeared from somewhere in the distance, a totem planted beside him that continuously emitted a pale green glow, as though performing some kind of healing — though who it was healing was anyone's guess.

Jace thrust his staff forward and channeled Blood Grasp. The shaman was wrapped in a blood-colored light, his arm and ear torn apart, and his whole body flew forward uncontrollably.

Jace had meant to meet him with his sword and run him through — but the shaman turned out to be a bit heavy. He slammed bodily against the edge of the steps with a series of cracking bones, then tumbled back down.

Although he'd taken care of the shaman, having his spell interrupted had left his already badly drained energy even more exhausted.

He casually thrust his raised sword into the head of another trogg charging at him — but even with its skull pierced, the creature still managed a hammer blow into Jace's flank.

The hit wasn't serious enough to be crippling, but it threw him slightly off balance and he staggered.

He lowered his eyes. Over a dozen corpses were already piled on the steps. To maintain his high-ground advantage and avoid being overwhelmed and killed by the monsters, he'd had no choice but to stand on the corpses and keep fighting the troggs charging up at him.

Uneven footing underfoot, mana draining fast, stamina fading too — and before his eyes was still a mass of dark, surging bodies pushing and shoving each other as they climbed upward. Daggart was laboriously stabbing them back down one by one, and the dwarf who'd been roaring encouragement to himself just moments ago had grown somewhat despondent as well.

What's more, the creatures seemed to realize that Jace's stamina was running out, and they grew even more excited and emboldened. Their howls thundered for a moment, all manner of strange shrieks echoing endlessly through the cave.

Just then, the stone door behind him shuddered.

The troggs that had been charging upward all froze for an instant.

Jace seized the chance to immediately run through a nearby trogg and shove its body down the steps, then backed up to the door — which was slowly beginning to open.

Suddenly he didn't know whether to deal with the monsters on the steps or the problem behind the door.

While the creatures hesitated, unsure whether to charge, he glanced inside — and saw a "dwarf" about his own height walking out.

This dwarf was bare-chested, wearing only a belt that was half broken and hanging loose, and tattered leather pants that had been burst at the seams and barely qualified as clothing.

His skin was ashen white, his shoulders and chest covered in scratches and chisel marks. His eyes were no longer eyeballs — they radiated arcane light.

He carried a battle hammer in one hand and a stone axe he'd gotten from God-knows-where in the other, walking step by step toward Jace, each footfall accompanied by the clack of stone on stone.

"Jace." The dwarf barely managed to squeeze out the name, as though even one more word would cost him enormous effort.

Jace nodded, leaning against the doorframe to catch his breath. "Deal with the ones below. Quick..."

Grede stared at the troggs below for a moment, then walked down the steps with his two weapons. The troggs in front hesitated and backed away.

A tall trogg wielding a long staff roared and charged, swinging hard — the staff smashed into Grede's head with a clang and snapped clean in two, while Grede didn't even budge. He was like a statue mounted with a base on the steps.

A flash of lightning suddenly flickered across his battle hammer, and with one strike he smashed the trogg into the stone wall. All the troggs seemed to snap awake at once, wailing and fleeing in panic. After a chorus of heaven-piercing howls, all that remained in the cave were corpses strewn across the ground.

Translator Notes

Flags

- 灼烧咒: Rendered as "Incinerate" (fire spell; distinct from 献祭/Immolate in the glossary). - 恐惧术: Fear (glossary). - 闪电箭: Lightning Bolt (glossary). - 生命吸取: Life Drain (glossary). - 血魔之握: "Blood Grasp" — not in glossary; literal rendering of the named warlock spell. - 暗影之盾: "Shadow Shield" — not in glossary; the incantation "托姆索加斯，维霍利西亚，瓦斯达昂" is treated as flavor. - 库尔提拉斯: Kul Tiras (glossary) — used here as a height comparison. - 萨满 / 图腾: shaman / totem (glossary). - 碧空之歌: Skysong. - 黑铁砍刀: "Dark Iron cleaver" — Dark Iron (glossary) + cleaver (glossary 劈骨刀/劈肉刀). - 石腭怪: trogg. - 达葛特: Daggart. - 格瑞德: Grede.
