Chapter 387: Whose Contribution Was Greater?

By the time Jace returned to Stormwind with the summoning-spell records Mosh'aru had handed him, it was already afternoon. He returned Alna to the Mage Quarter stables, and by the time he got home the sky had darkened.

Arriving at his doorstep and climbing the stairs, he could already hear a chaotic commotion inside — something seemed to be scratching at the door.

He pushed the door open, ready to yell at the two demons for not keeping it down and saving him some trouble, when Sazeno suddenly leaped up and grabbed him by the collar. "Master, I heard your footsteps a long time ago!"

"What are you so worked up about?" Jace asked.

"Honestly, I can't tell whether you're a minion or a pet," Melfiel said from the bed, smiling.

"Shut up, toy!" the imp shrieked.

Melfiel parted her lips, baring her fangs, and the imp instantly darted behind Jace, tugging at his collar. "I need to discuss something important with the master — rein it in! You don't want the master getting hurt, do you!"

"Then stop wasting time," the succubus murmured.

"I'll tell you right now!" The imp pressed close to Jace's ear. "Master, we cracked the incantation! I can feel it this time — it's definitely right. These words… I can feel their power!"

"Let's not forget my contribution, imp," Melfiel said from the bed. "Don't forget who reminded you about the Eredar idioms in that word…"

"I didn't forget! You obviously know far more about the Eredar than I do." The imp glanced at the succubus. "Master, this really is an incantation created by the Eredar — extremely powerful. It requires immense, immense energy. Eredar warlocks never cared about energy consumption when they crafted their incantations!"

"Besides, just reciting it made me feel the power it gathers! But there's no way I could ever cast it with my own energy — nowhere near enough!"

"You tried to cast shadow magic inside my quarters?!" Jace asked, alarmed.

"I was watching, Master — nothing would have gone wrong," Melfiel said. "And this imp couldn't cause much trouble anyway. Aside from being rather skilled at conjuring a little flame with the fel energy all over its body, there much else it could manage."

"So it'll have to be the Master who tries it. Just reciting this incantation gives me a headache — I can't stand it!" The imp pointed at the stack of papers on the table, densely covered in copied-out runes. "It's the top one! That's where I wrote it."

Jace picked up the top sheet and looked at the transcribed incantation. He had to admit, this language for communicating with the shadow was quite different from the demonic tongue used for conversation. He could barely make out what it meant, only sensing that it seemed to have something to do with summoning.

"Is this a summoning incantation?"

"Not the kind you understand it to be, Master. This is a remarkably potent incantation. When I recited it, I could even feel the arrogance, the impulse, the hunger for slaughter the Eredar held when they created it!" The imp waved its arms. "Ah, it's magnificent! The power of the shadow! Master, you should give me more incantations like this to crack! I've always been devoted to making you stronger and stronger!"

"If I hadn't pushed you, little thing," Melfiel said coldly, "would you even have remembered the task the Master gave you?"

The words on the scroll were, in the end, all incantations that Teron Gorefiend had memorized.

If they were connected to the Eredar, then what could they be?

Could this be Gorefiend's Shadowfury?

Of course he wanted to learn such a powerful dark shockwave as soon as possible. If he'd mastered it earlier, when he'd faced that horde of berserk troggs deep beneath Uldaman, he might have been able to quell the scene in an instant.

Naturally, Jace also knew he shouldn't get his expectations up about this incantation. He had to account for every possible scenario.

"You're sure this isn't a demon-summoning incantation, Sazeno?"

"No," Sazeno said. "Demon-summoning incantations are much easier to understand! After all, you're talking to demons in the Twisting Nether, not asking the shadow to do something for you. Am I right, Master?"

"This isn't any demon-summoning incantation I've heard before, Master," Melfiel added. "And if it's one I haven't heard… that would mean it might require a massive amount of soul energy to open a channel to communicate with the other side. Simply reciting the incantation would absolutely never work — the shadow power contained in a single mortal body couldn't sustain a connection like that."

"Of course, I'm not disparaging your power…" At this, the succubus blinked and smiled.

Jace knew exactly what she meant. After all, he'd only just returned from Moonbrook a few days ago, after hearing what Mosh'aru had to say. And he believed that an incantation that had left such a deep impression on Teron Gorefiend surely wasn't something for summoning a mere voidwalker, felhound, or Gan'arg servant — some weak demon like that.

"I need to find a place to test this incantation," Jace said. "Prepare potions in advance… Shadow Protection Potions and the like. You're both coming with me to help, in case something gets out of control."

"I want to see what this thing really is…" Sazeno said excitedly. "After all, I'm the one who discovered it! If the Master can't think of a name for it, I hope we can call it… Sazeno's Shadow!"

Jace, peeling off the dirty shirt he'd worn all the way back and hanging it on the window, said, "You don't even know what this spell is and you're already naming it? Besides, Teron Gorefiend probably already gave it a name."

"But he's dead, ha!" Sazeno said.

"That's true enough."

Jace gathered up the scroll, along with the dense stacks of rune-covered scrap paper the two demons had filled while researching what this incantation was, rolled them up, stuffed them into a worn bag, and shoved it under the bed.

"You did well, Sazeno. Remember what I promised you last time?" Jace sat down on a stool and pulled a soul shard from his trouser pocket. "Take a look at this."

"A soul stone pulsing with arcane energy!" The imp took the soul shard, hopped about in delight, then said, "I'll treasure it carefully! It's a gift from the Master! Ha!"

With that, Sazeno darted into the bag in a flash.

"Your neck, Master." Melfiel pointed at the spot beside her ankle, displeased. "That's a wound left by a felhound's tendril. I don't understand why you'd take a risk like that without bringing me along."

Jace touched the scar where the tendril's spike had cut him. "Don't worry about it — I'm completely fine, aren't I?"

Melfiel narrowed her eyes and pinched the spearhead-like tip of her tail between two fingers. "Only I am permitted to taste your mana, Master. Anyone else… no matter who tries, they must die."
