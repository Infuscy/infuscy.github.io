Chapter 390: The Basement Guard

Erland and Sulenna arrived at the basement together. Jace just in time to see Erland carrying a cloth bundle — and inside the bundle was a blurry, indistinct shadow.

An imp?

He was just about to marvel that Erland had actually summoned an imp, when he remembered that the matter of his possessing demon-detection ability probably wasn't something he should reveal to the other members of the Dagger Society right away.

After all, it wasn't some incantation, still less a skill that could be taught to others. Speaking up might invite suspicion instead.

But before he could say anything, Erland eagerly called out to those below: "Come take a look — the demon I've bound!"

"Gerkik, appear!"

A pale green imp leaped out of Erland's bundle, looked around, and shouted, "I am Gerkik! Heh heh!"

"I was wondering what you'd summoned…" Sulenna rolled her eyes at him.

Ursula laughed. "But now you're the only member here without a familiar, Sulenna."

"Fine." Sulenna reluctantly conceded.

"Congratulations, Erland," Jace smiled. "I knew you could do it."

Erland immediately asked, "So when are you helping me summon a succubus?"

"At least get the felhound business sorted first, Erland…" Galders said. "Right now we need to hurry and help Ursula complete the felhound summoning ritual."

Everyone filed into the practice room. Galders's succubus and Jace's Melfiel revealed themselves from invisibility. When that succubus saw Melfiel appear at Jace's side, her eyes locked straight onto them.

"We meet again," Melfiel said. "I don't know your name yet."

"Senlora," the succubus answered. "Miss Melfiel…"

"Don't call me by my name, Miss Senlora," Melfiel said. "It makes me uncomfortable."

Jace rubbed the back of Melfiel's neck. "We're here to deal with a felhound, Melfiel — she's not an enemy."

"As long as she stops staring at you," Melfiel smiled.

"I'm certain Senlora isn't staring at Jace at all — she's worried about you," Galders said.

"Forget it," Melfiel said. "After all, asking her to resist the Master's charm would be too much."

Senlora said coldly, "In any case, I am loyal to one master alone — Lord Blackclaw Galders."

Melfiel gave a disdainful smile. Jace turned his head and coughed, trying to steer the topic back.

"So, Ursula — how well have you got the incantation down?"

"This definitely won't be the hardest step for me," Ursula said. "I think I'm ready. Soul shard, summoning incantation, and the carving incantation — I've got them pretty much memorized."

"I knew you'd get them down quickly, Ursula," Galders said. "You just need to complete the summoning boldly. There are plenty of comrades here to help you, plus two demon partners — you'll be fine."

Ursula gave a soft "mm" and walked step by step to the center of the practice room.

The practice room had never held this many people before — especially with several demons added to the mix. Still, Ursula was quite nervous, given that the thing they summoning was a beast none of them had ever dealt with.

She set the papers aside, pulled a bottle of murky, dimly glowing potion from her pocket, and drank it.

After a moment, she closed her eyes and recited loudly: "Mo'anan, Suojia, Tuluokiduosi, Luokeruini."

She pronounced it remarkably standard and practiced. Her casting gestures followed Mosh'aru's records to the letter. Jace knew she would succeed on the first try.

Sure enough, the moment she finished the last phrase of the incantation and gently lowered her hands, the light in the brazier dimmed. The air in the center of the group tore open like a patch of black fog that had been ripped apart. A panting, snarling hellhound thrust out its eyeless, noseless gray-white head from the darkness. Like a newborn being birthed, it delivered itself from the void, hit the ground, and the moment it scrambled to its feet it snarled at everyone around it.

The felhound was about the size of a large dog — not quite as absurdly massive as the one Jace had summoned at Mosh'aru's place, that ridiculous fat-pig-like monstrosity, but roughly on par with the one Mosh'aru had summoned.

When it noticed Ursula was its summoner, its tentacles shuddered violently. Ursula had just been about to speak the naming incantation when its sudden shriek startled her.

The felhound suddenly lunged toward Ursula, but before its tentrils could coil around her, Melfiel moved even faster, seizing them. Its tail whipped around the felhound's head, and with both hands and tail exerting force at once, the felhound was flung to the side in one motion, tumbling across the floor several times.

"Hong'Aike…"

Erland tried to subdue the hound with a Fear incantation, but before he'd finished the first syllable, the demon let out an even more piercing shriek. Erland's eyes rolled back on the spot, and he slumped against the wall, nearly unconscious.

"How dare you attack my master!" Erland's imp Gerkik shrieked in rage, raising its hands to conjure a ball of flame — only for the felhound to swallow it whole in one gulp!

"Stop casting, you fools!" Melfiel shouted. Galders, who also looked mid-incantation, hesitated at the yell.

Jace stepped to Ursula's side, shielding her. Killing a felhound wouldn't be hard for Jace, but subduing one? He truly had no experience with that at all.

The other succubus reacted quickly too, lashing out with her whip and wrapping it around the tail of the felhound as it tried to charge at Melfiel again, dragging it to the side. Jace shouted, "Recite the incantation now, Ursula — make it know its name!"

Ursula nodded and immediately spoke the naming incantation.

When the incantation was complete, the felhound shrank back as if something had seized its head, struggling for a while before finally going still — even coughing up the imp whole.

Senlora reeled her whip back in. The felhound broke free once more, but this time it didn't charge at Ursula again.

"Does it… obey me now?" Ursula asked, looking at Galders and Jace.

"If the incantation worked, it should obey," Jace said. "It says here that once this step is complete, the felhound will submit to its new master. Try giving it a command."

"Come to my side," Ursula said, looking at the hound.

But the felhound showed no reaction whatsoever. Though it no longer bore hostility toward Ursula, it still regarded the others around it with wariness, looking back and forth restlessly.

"Try repeating after me, Miss Ursula," Melfiel murmured in the demon tongue. "Follow me."

Jace more or less understood it, but the others were completely lost.

"Is that an incantation?" Ursula asked.

"No — it's Eredar, what you call the demon tongue," Melfiel said. "Say that to the dog."

Ursula nodded and repeated what Melfiel had said. The felhound seemed to be suddenly jolted awake — it looked over, then obediently came to Ursula's side.

"It really works!" Sulenna exclaimed. "Don't tell me if we're going to summon felhounds like this, we'll have to learn an entire demon language?"

"Actually, if you want to be a proper shadow warlock, you should be learning the demon tongue," Galders said. "From now on, everyone will be learning this new language, Sulenna."

Listening to Galders, Jace thought to himself that he should contribute those demon-language materials and lead everyone in making progress together.
