## Chapter 396: A New Approach

No demon had been drawn by the fragile soul fragment. Perhaps his mana wasn't enough to tear open a wide enough passage between the Twisting Nether and Azeroth for a demon's soul to pass through. Perhaps the demon was too fragile and, upon arriving in this world, had perished in the barrage of shadow.

He should probably say… he still had some way to go before he could make this spell a complete success.

He looked at the shadow scorch marks everywhere in front of him. Forget Stormwind — he absolutely couldn't replicate this even in Elwynn Forest. His decision to switch practice locations after familiarizing himself with the incantation had been the right call.

---

On Monday, Jace returned to Goldshire, rested a bit at the inn, and brought the two demons back to Stormwind in the evening.

The next few days were busy with classes, but he finally found time to help Marin clean up his room, organize the articles and books of the apprentice and novice mages, and settle down to writing his report on the death knights.

Still, he kept thinking back to the emotions he'd channeled when using the Hand of Gul'dan in Duskwood.

Since those feelings he'd brewed up were enough to affect the smooth casting of shadow magic, he'd started to get some ideas about that shadow shield of his.

Maybe he just hadn't found the right emotion to harmonize with the shadow power, which was why that shield had ended up coming out like some kind of spell reflection.

Previously, while he'd tried hard to restrain the emotional influence of shadow on himself, he'd also noticed that it was easier to cast powerful shadow magic under extreme emotions. But he'd never actively tried using emotions to attempt new spells.

If a specific emotion… could really extend the duration of this shadow shield.

In any case, in terms of casting gestures or the standards of incantation recitation, it was very difficult to continue developing this spell.

That evening after dinner, he went home and stared at Morophil, thinking he should try this magic tonight. No class tomorrow — sleeping in a bit wouldn't matter.

"You can feel the emotions in shadow magic, can't you, Morophil."

Morophil said, "That isn't difficult for any succubus, master. Devouring others' souls is my specialty."

"Then, do you still remember that shadow shield you told me about last time? Come feel what emotions are present in this spell."

"You're trying to adjust your mindset like you did in the forest last time, to affect the power of shadow magic?" the succubus asked.

"If it worked for that spell, why not try it on others?" Jace said.

The succubus nodded. "I'm really not sure how to help a mortal learn and master shadow magic."

"Then let's start here." Jace said this while glancing around. This single room was really too small. If the shield accidentally triggered and caused an explosion, it might blow the windows to pieces. He had to go to the Slaughtered Lamb basement — and now, not tomorrow.

If he didn't try this, Jace probably wouldn't even be able to sleep.

In the middle of the night, he went out with the succubus, all the way to the Mage Quarter. There were still quite a few people here at this hour, of course none of them could detect the succubus's presence.

Arriving at the Slaughtered Lamb tavern, it was still fairly lively. Tharle was lying on the counter. When he saw Jace come in, he didn't react much, just said, "You lot need to do something about that damn dog."

"He's not interested in you," Jace said.

"Just standing there is scary enough," Tharle said helplessly.

Jace couldn't help but laugh. He came down to the basement and pushed open the door. Sure enough, that demon was crouching at the far end, staring this way. If he hadn't had the ability to detect demons, he'd probably only see a faint shadow squirming in the darkness, because that thing didn't even have eyes — nothing to reflect light.

Morophil walked out from behind Jace. When the hound saw her, its presence clearly diminished a few notches. It backed up a few steps warily, staring at the two of them.

Jace said in Demonic, "Basadim, I'm your master's friend. Don't you remember me?"

The succubus stared at the hellhound and said in Demonic, "Master, shall we speak in Eredun from now on? After all, your Eredun…"

She muttered a word Jace completely didn't understand. Jace asked, "What did you say?"

"Fine." Morophil switched back to Common. "In Common it translates to — 'melodic.' Looks like we'd better stick with your language for a while."

The basement was quiet at the moment. Jace glanced into the alchemy lab — Ursula was sleeping on a bench.

He brought the succubus to the practice room, closed the door and said, "I'll cast, you feel what emotions are in this spell… I don't know how to describe it exactly, but you know what I mean, Morophil."

"Understood." Morophil nodded.

Jace recited the incantation and skillfully summoned the shield. The dark shimmer lasted a few seconds and then instantly dissipated. The succubus blinked, then stared at Jace in a daze, hesitating before asking, "That's… it?"

"So what's wrong?" Jace asked.

"That was fast," Morophil laughed.

Jace glanced at her. "I don't need your commentary, just feel what emotions are in this spell. Help me improve it."

Morophil put away her smile. "This is a defensive spell. I can't feel much outward force, and its manipulation of shadow power isn't nearly as strong as the one you cast in the forest. I need to feel deeper."

"How deep?"

"I need to try to trigger its reflection. In other words, attack your spell."

"You're sure? You're not afraid of getting hurt?"

Morophil said, "Shadow power — I've built up considerable resistance from long battles against all sorts of strange things in the Twisting Nether. I don't believe a defensive spell from someone who's learned barely half of it could seriously hurt me. Besides, if I'm really badly injured, you can contribute a bit of your energy, master. I'll recover very quickly."

"Fine." Jace walked to the side and warned, "Then react quickly."

"Of course," the succubus said. "My reactions are always fast."

Jace acknowledged this and recited the incantation again. When the shield expanded, the succubus suddenly struck, slamming hard into the shield. When her hand hit the surface, it seemed to be yanked backward by some force, and immediately after, a shockwave of shadow energy rippled outward.

The shield shattered. The succubus tried to block with her other arm but was still blasted back two steps.

She let out a low groan and slumped against the wall, hanging her arm limply. "Master… your shadow power is beyond imagination. I need immediate healing… quickly…"

Jace looked at her sideways. "Alright, you can stop the act."

Morophil looked up, gritted her teeth, and stood up. "No fun when there's no one else here."

"Don't waste time… tell me, what did you feel this time?" Jace asked.

"Nathrezim dark magic is always ice-cold," Morophil reminded him. "I guess you should try to calm yourself down, master. Become cold, for instance. Despise your enemies."

Jace agreed. "Fits the nature of those demons."

Recalling the moment in front of the stone hall gate of Uldaman, facing the swarm of earthen, had Jace felt something similar?

Perhaps. After repelling several waves of attacks, forcing the earthen at the front to tangle with their charging companions and tumble continuously down the steps, his heart had indeed slowly developed a trace of contempt from the initial nervousness.

Then, how to capture this emotion now? Jace felt that although he was already quite good at suppressing the negative feelings brought by the corrosion and contamination of shadow energy, he wasn't very good at summoning emotions that didn't originally exist in him.

A dismissive attitude might be hard to reproduce, but the calm Morophil had mentioned — being as cold as possible — he could probably manage to imitate.

That meant emptying all the feelings he cared about at the moment, draining all emotions.

Under the repeated torment of shadow corrosion, he'd already become very good at this.

When he'd completely calmed down, Jace recited the shadow shield incantation again. The black magic shield expanded once more…

One second…

Two seconds…

Three seconds…

Four seconds…

Five seconds…

This had already lasted longer than every time he'd used the shield spell before!

When the excitement suddenly welled up in Jace's mind, the shield vanished. Morophil narrowed her eyes. "This spell seems to require the master to find a way to maintain it."

"Seems that way," he said. "But I think this is already a very rare improvement. I almost thought this magic was just like that, Morophil."

## Flags

- **萨亚德** (sà yǎ dé): rendered as "succubus" — the demon race term Morophil uses for herself.
- **艾瑞达语**: Eredun, the Eredar language. Morophil mocks Jace's pronunciation.
- **柏萨迪姆**: Basadim, the hellhound lurking in the Slaughtered Lamb basement.
- **纳斯雷兹姆**: Nathrezim. Morophil uses their cold, contemptuous nature as the emotional template for the shadow shield.
- **石腭怪**: earthen — the Uldaman creatures Jace recalls fighting.
- **情绪-as-casting-tool**: the chapter's core concept — Jace discovers that a specific emotional state (cold contempt) stabilizes and prolongs the shadow shield, a departure from his earlier purely technical approach to spellcraft.
