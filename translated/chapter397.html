Chapter 397: Prelude to the Fire Festival

The Midsummer Fire Festival was only two days away. That evening, Jace came up from the basement and walked near the mage tower, seeing the decorations fluttering like sunset clouds in front of the shops all around, and only then realized the summer festival was approaching.

In the two weeks since returning from Duskwood, he'd been busy between classes and the basement. The Hand of Gul'dan couldn't be practiced, but using the shadow shield incantation to improve his emotional control wasn't bad.

Though he could hardly say he'd found some trick, by now he could maintain the incantation for nearly ten seconds.

But because he'd been so focused on practicing, he'd even forgotten the passage of time.

The Midsummer Fire Festival started on June 21st and lasted two weeks. The festival had a long history, and people took it quite seriously.

Many races believed that fire could dispel ancient evil. Long ago, they would even select a Flame Keeper to preside over a grand sacred flame ritual to bless the peace of the coming year.

Of course, the Flame Keeper, like Old Winterfather during the Feast of Winter Veil, had become part of the celebration, no longer carrying the strong religious significance of hundreds of years ago.

Even though the Fire Festival hadn't officially started, the festive atmosphere was already present.

Passing through the Park, Jace saw a mage who had turned himself entirely into a man of fire dancing beneath the tower. Quite a few young mages were watching. Some marveled at his mastery of fire elements, others at his powerful movements and rhythmic dance, made even more fervent against the flames.

For the moment, the entire Park had become much livelier because of this impromptu performance.

Jace had to admit he quite envied this guy's fire mastery. This flame was definitely no sleight-of-hand trick by some street magician. Jace had noticed that although the mage was performing on the stone path, the weeds growing in the cracks beneath his feet had already been scorched yellow by the flames coming off him.

This guy was probably channeling an Immolate spell onto himself while using a powerful fire barrier to protect himself. Jace could only guess simply like that. In any case, given his own skill at manipulating fire magic, he couldn't figure out at all how this guy did it.

There were many novice mages in the crowd, even full mages. It was clear everyone was amazed.

Not long after, Jace smelled something off. The smell wasn't very obvious, but it kept drifting past his senses from time to time. An illusion from practicing the shadow shield?

No, this wasn't the smell of shadow.

He gradually began to forget the performance in front of him, focusing on this aura. Specifically, it was a smell of burning.

Not the smell of clothes or hair being singed, but a… very awkward stench. The stench of sulfur. The smell of demons!

Demons in the Mage Quarter?

He hadn't brought Morophil or Sazeno here, and when they were seriously hiding, they wouldn't give off this smell.

Jace looked around, focusing his mind and using his Detect Demon ability to scan back and forth. Had someone else from the Slaughtered Lamb brought an imp they'd summoned here?

But there were no demons around. He couldn't see any suspicious shadow.

Then where was this stench coming from?

The mage in front had paused his performance at the moment, apparently preparing for the next dance, but the flames were still burning on him.

Couldn't anyone here smell it? Only he could feel it?

"Ah, wait… help!"

The mage suddenly shouted. He started patting the flames on his body. The spell seemed completely out of control!

At this moment, an older mage in the crowd shouted an incantation. A small blizzard immediately engulfed the burning mage, and the flames dimmed at once. Just as Jace slightly relaxed, the flame suddenly expanded again, and when it expanded, it was no longer an ordinary flame — it had become a ball of green fel fire!

Although this ball of fel fire had flared up for an instant before being swallowed and extinguished by the blizzard, it still startled everyone present. What was going on?!

The older mage helped the injured, soaking wet fire mage to his feet. It seemed that because the rescue had been timely and the fire barrier hadn't been completely destroyed, there didn't appear to be any particularly serious burns. The fire mage just said quietly, "Master Zonma… I don't know why…"

"Make way for the injured!" Magino Zonma looked at the crowd and said. "The Wizard Sanctum will immediately investigate this matter."

Why had fel fire appeared? And such pure green fel fire. Jace had only occasionally seen it on top of imps' heads, and even the flames they ignited with fel energy weren't much greener than ordinary flames — they still looked like normal fire.

Jace hadn't noticed any demons nearby, but there really was something off in the air.

The crowd dispersed under the urging of the battle-mage guards. He came to Marin's study and knocked. Marin wasn't there.

Looking back in the direction he'd come from, he felt this probably had nothing to do with the warlocks in the Slaughtered Lamb. After all, the chances of them producing such a ball of pure fel fire weren't high.

Leaving the Mage Quarter and heading home, by the canal, he saw in the distance that a plume of black smoke seemed to be rising from Old Town.

He remembered that last year during the Fire Festival, there had been occasional fires in the city.

But accompanied by this strange smell he could catch from time to time, Jace always felt this plume of black smoke wasn't right.

And it wasn't even the Fire Festival yet — why was the whole city getting so worked up?

Some Stormwind guards hurried down the street, rushing toward the Trade District gate. Many passersby watched them go with curiosity.

The city seemed to be getting more and more sweltering at the moment, and the strange smell he'd caught in the Mage Quarter earlier seemed less distinct now.

He pushed open the door and went home. Sazeno was actually crouching on the window, staring outside. Jace immediately snapped in a low voice, "Are you crazy? Get down, quickly. What if someone sees you?"

"Master, don't you feel it?" The imp turned around, jumped off the table and said. "Both Morophil and I felt it."

"You mean this demon smell," Jace said. "I smelled it. What do you think? Has a demon entered Stormwind to cause trouble?"

Morophil shook her head. "I can't sense any demon's trail. This fel fire aura is too dispersed. It seems to be everywhere, yet too thin to be the deliberate act of any specific demon."

"In any case, the Wizard Sanctum is already investigating. I'll ask around in the Mage Quarter tomorrow," Jace said.
