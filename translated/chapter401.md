## Chapter 401: Everyone Has Their Use

Marin and the knight captain got a fuller picture of the situation. While the fiercest fighting was still concentrated around the outposts and strongpoints between Nethergarde Keep and the Dark Portal, a large number of virtually uncontrolled demons had already pushed through to the rear of Nethergarde — into the vicinity of Stonard.

By the knight captain's estimate, demons may have already penetrated deep into the Black Swamp.

Though Stonard's garrison was not large, five or six enormous Alterac gryphons and a number of smaller Hinterlands gryphons were stationed there.

The camp was already short-handed, yet quite a few people were still busy around those gryphons — hauling feed or tending to their moods. After all, this place was far closer to the Dark Portal than Stormwind City; the demon stench that permeated the air had the gryphons extremely agitated.

But this agitation wasn't fear or terror — it was anger and resentment. Every so often those beasts would fix their eyes on the sky, or peer over Stonard's low walls, as if they were about to leap out and fight those demons then and there if their handlers weren't holding them back.

Just then, a Wildhammer dwarf strapped on a pair of goggles and, with the help of a few other Wildhammer dwarves, climbed atop a tall, gray Alterac gryphon. Once it spread its wings, the creature was downright terrifying — like a juvenile dragon.

Receiving the order to take off, it happily launched itself from the rookery, kicking up a cloud of straw and debris. It circled around triumphantly, showing off to its companions who were still being held in their nests, while the dwarf gryphon handlers kept shouting commands in Dwarvish: "Calm down!" "Easy, now!"

"Nethergarde front line needs battle-mage support!"

A Kirin Tor mage strode over, shouting at the mages who had just stepped out of the portal: "We need qualified full mages, or apprentice mages who've mastered Fireball, Arcane Missiles, or other offensive capabilities. The pressure up front is enormous — the demons could bypass our outposts and lines at any moment and strike Nethergarde's walls directly! Veterans of the Orc Wars would be best!"

Hearing the Kirin Tor mage's call, the leader of the mages who had already arrived immediately began selecting those who met the criteria. Marin also came over to Jace's group and started calling out names one by one: "Mr. Gorse! Mr. Bilde! Miss Christian! Mr. Sadre!"

Although Jace wanted to head straight to the Nethergarde front line, he clearly wasn't the best candidate — he didn't meet a single one of the Kirin Tor mage's requirements.

He'd passed on learning Fireball earlier because it suited battlefield combat more than skirmishes, and now he was paying for it. Knowing only a single Scorch incantation was absolutely not suited for the front line.

I wonder if it's too late to start learning now…

Arcane Missiles was out of the question, given his grasp of arcane magic, but maybe Fireball could still be salvaged — after all, the usual next step after learning Scorch was to move on to Fireball.

But by now the mages Marin had called out were already excitedly stepping forward. Together with the Arathi red-robed mages who'd arrived through the other portals, they headed toward the rookery under the Kirin Tor mage's lead.

In other words, nearly every mage in the group who knew Fireball had left.

That enormous gray gryphon had carried three mages in a single trip. Just then, another massive Alterac gryphon carrying wounded soldiers swept over the stone camp and touched down on the nearby flat ground, kicking up a cloud of dust.

It shook out its wings after landing, and a cloud of black smoke billowed up, carrying the thick stench of scorched fel. Had it just been fighting demons?

"Mr. Barry Frede."

Marin called out to another unfamiliar-looking mage in his forties, though he wore the Wizard Sanctum's full-mage ring.

"That is your name, isn't it? You're a student of Master Cannon?"

"That's me, Master Marin."

"I'm heading to Nethergarde to assist with its defense. You'll stay here and lead these apprentice mages, ensuring the safety of the portal and Stonard. Be ready to answer Captain Mash Selbury's call at any time."

"Yes, Master, I understand." This Barry Frede acknowledged.

After Marin left, Frede stepped before the mages and said: "Stonard isn't safe either, my colleagues and fellows. The mages staying behind should get into fighting shape right now. Put the spells you learned at the Wizard Sanctum to good use — check your spell scrolls, enchant weapons, conjure water, assist the priests in treating the wounded, and craft mana gems for the mages maintaining the portal!"

"This is a big swamp — water elementals are absolutely plentiful here. Don't hoard your mana! The supply line between Stonard and Duskwood is under constant threat from demon stragglers, and clean drinking water is in short supply. You heard what the knight captain said!"

"Also, be ready to fight at any moment, gentlemen. The demons could attack here too — after all, this is currently the only teleportation point. The Kirin Tor is working to find another safe teleportation point as quickly as possible, but if that doesn't go smoothly, the demons will be here soon enough."

Jace nodded. He wasn't good at any of the abilities mentioned earlier, but at least he knew how to fight.

Just then, a warrior in a blue shirt carrying a long-handled war hammer came over from the Arathi mages' direction. He looked at Barry Frede and asked: "Are you the leader of the Stormwind mages, Master?"

"For the moment. Archmage Marin has already departed for Nethergarde."

"We need a mage who can track demons, Master, right now."

"Only full mages know how to track demons, sir," Frede said. "A new batch of mages will arrive from the Wizard Sanctum tomorrow — they'll be able to help you then."

"What about you?" the warrior asked.

"I need to oversee things here, and I'm not adept at tracking demons either. I'm better at enchanting and dispelling magic," Frede said.

"Bad luck." The warrior sighed and glanced over at the Stromgarde red-robed mages, pinching the bridge of his nose in frustration.

"I can track demons," Jace spoke up.

Frede glanced at Jace's ring and said: "Are you one of Master Marin's students? But you're just an apprentice mage — the Wizard Sanctum's apprentice curriculum doesn't cover tracking demons or shadow creatures…"

"But I have experience fighting demons," Jace said. He looked at the warrior with the war hammer and continued: "If you don't trust me, you can wait for tomorrow's mages. But if you need someone right now, I think I can handle the task."

"We do need someone urgently, young sir. The demons captured a group of patrols — we're organizing a team to get them back." The warrior looked at Jace and said: "Tell you what — if you can tell me why the demons are capturing people alive instead of just killing them, I'll trust that you can actually help us."

"They need living souls to summon more demons," Jace said.

"Then come with us, mage," the warrior said.
