Chapter 402: Phantom of the Marsh

Black Swamp, living up to its name — this was a genuine dark quagmire. The dense canopy high above blotted out the sky; the dark green ground, covered in all manner of plants, was split by winding channels. No — not channels, but rivulets of mud and shallow streams full of filth.

This horrifying swamp, already choked with muck, had been poisoned for eight years by the fel energy pouring from the Dark Portal to the south. What lurked in its dark waters now was anyone's guess.

But at the moment, at least Jace didn't need to worry about swamp beasts or forest predators.

Because this party numbered over a dozen, ten of whom were fully armed soldiers — veterans who had come here with the Expedition. Every one of them wore heavy armor, carried shields and longswords, and moved through the swamp like they owned the place.

The captain leading from the front was the very one who'd recruited Jace into this squad. His name was Quenton — Jace could tell from his accent that he was a Lordaeron man too.

This fellow carried a war hammer that looked awfully heavy, yet his steps were remarkably brisk. He had to be a formidable fighter.

At the very front and rear of the column were two elven rangers, a man and a woman. Both were Farstriders, subordinates of the Expedition's ranger commander, Alleria Windrunner.

These two elves had originally been stationed at Nethergarde to assist the Quel'Thalas mages and soldiers in moving through the Dark Portal to reinforce the Sons of Lothar. But after the demons appeared at the Dark Portal a few days ago, the Farstriders' leader at Nethergarde had dispatched them to Stonard to help the Alliance soldiers here guard the portal.

In short, nearly everyone in this group had been stationed here for a year or two. But even so, they still needed Jace to track the demons — just like the Farstriders, these people all lacked experience fighting demons.

Although the scent of fel fire could be felt throughout the swamp, Jace — who was familiar with demons — could still distinguish where the demonic presence ran thicker and where it likely ran thinner.

The Burning Legion's demons disdained to hide their scent the way a lurking succubus would, which made tracking them somewhat easier, too.

Of course, he first had to filter out the fel radiation emanating from the Dark Portal to the south.

"Mage, are we still heading the right way?" The captain at the front gazed toward the deeper marshland to the northeast and asked.

"That's right," Jace said. "I can smell the demons' stench getting stronger up ahead, Captain. And the shadow energy, too. I think the presence of shadow energy might be even more significant for us — it means there's a caster involved."

"The presence of a caster means a demonic summoning ritual," Captain Quenton agreed.

Jace walked to the front of the column and focused his attention on the surrounding forest once more. Through a dense patch of low swamp shrubbery, he caught what looked like an indistinct shadow flickering past.

In this gloomy swamp, light and shadow were chaotic everywhere — he couldn't be sure whether he'd actually seen a demon's silhouette.

The soldiers moved quickly. A few minutes later, he glanced idly to the right, then swept his gaze toward the back of the column. This time, he spotted a similar shadow trailing behind another one.

He looked in yet another direction — there seemed to be another shadow closing in from that way, too.

Now he was certain: demons were tracking this party!

The female elf at the rear seemed to sense that something was off. She stared intently at Jace, as if seeking confirmation. Jace looked back at her. The elf in front was still leading the captain forward, apparently having noticed nothing.

The Farstriders' scouting experience was clearly inadequate against demons capable of hiding themselves. After all, up to now they'd been dealing with orcs and trolls; the only demons they'd encountered were mostly minions summoned by orcs. Facing the demon legion in earnest was only a few days old for them.

Just as Jace was about to walk to the front and inform the captain, he suddenly felt a demon's shadow close in abruptly — charging toward the female elf at the rear of the column. Jace spun around and shouted: "Look out, Farstrider!"

The female elf at the rear immediately drew an arrow from her back — but before she could nock it, a completely naked female demon materialized from the shadows, brandishing a sharp longblade, and lunged at her with a shriek!

Jace thrust out his staff and spoke the Scorch incantation. A burst of flame detonated against the demon, blowing off one of its wings and half its chest in a single strike. It howled and crashed into the water. But one after another, several more succubi leaped out of the shadows, their piercing shrieks coming from every direction.

Thanks to Jace's warning, the soldiers reacted with extreme speed — they weren't caught off guard by the sudden appearance of these succubi. The first succubus that attacked the soldiers was surrounded by several soldiers and run through with swords. Another succubus slipped through a gap between soldiers, only to be shot in the shoulder by the Farstrider at the front of the column.

She tore the arrow out without flinching, laughed maniacally a few times, and just as a soldier charged in to run her through, the wounded succubus was still faster. She lashed out with the barbed whip in her hand, cracking it hard across his body.

The soldier raised his shield and pulled his sword back to block the blow, but the tip of the whip curled around the shield and struck the arm behind it. He grunted in pain and stepped back. Before he could counterattack with his sword, the succubus had already vanished into thin air.

In the span of a few heartbeats, aside from one succubus that Captain Quenton had smashed in the back with his hammer and left crumpled at his feet, plus the first one that had been run through, all the others had disappeared.

The area around the party, which had just been surrounded by demons, was suddenly empty. Jace focused his attention on the surroundings and warned: "They haven't left!"

"Cowardly bastards," a soldier muttered behind him.

"We're the cowards?"

A seductive question came from somewhere. The soldier immediately stammered: "No, no… not cowards!"

The captain smacked the soldier's helmet and said: "Snap out of it, soldier! Don't let these female demons bewitch you!"

Unexpectedly, that one smack immediately brought the soldier back to his senses. He coughed and said: "Sorry, Captain Quenton!"

A shriek sounded not far off. Several demon shadows closed in again — but Jace immediately noticed one succubus shadow that had climbed into the trees above the soldiers at some point, and now dropped from above, trying to attack the captain directly!

Jace spoke the incantation straight at that shadow. A ball of searing flame burst in midair. The succubus was blasted out of the shadow-form and sent crashing to the ground. The captain charged over and crushed its chest with a single hammer blow.

After this succubus died violently, the other demon shadows immediately stopped closing in. They seemed to have realized that Jace could detect their invisibility, and scattered in every direction, abandoning the attack.

"I think they've left," Jace said.

The female elf walked up to the front and said: "We should pick up the pace, human. These female demons may go alert their companions — if that happens, we'll be the ones caught in a passive position."
