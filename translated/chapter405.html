Chapter 405: No Ordinary Mage

The soldiers made their way behind the murloc village. There were roughly twenty people there, bound tightly with rope. Most hadn't eaten anything in the past few days and were so starved they could barely speak. Even seeing the rescuers arrive, only two or three still had the strength to cry out.

"At least we didn't arrive too late," the male Farstrider in front said as he cut through the ropes. "If we'd waited until tomorrow, God knows how many more would have died. The consequences of a completed ritual are something we can't afford either."

"Thanks to this Mr. Sesso here, we made it in time," Captain Quenton said, helping up a human captive who couldn't sit up on his own. "I heard most of the mages went to support the fighting at Nethergarde — honestly, I wasn't expecting to find a mage as capable as you."

"It's been my pleasure to help, everyone," Jace nodded.

After untying everyone, they fed them some simple rations and water, then counted heads. Four people in the group had died; the rest were extremely weak from the torture of shadow magic.

The soldiers fed the captives the herbs and healing potions they'd brought, according to the severity of their condition. That paladin, Captain Quenton, also helped feed them medicine one by one — it didn't look like he was particularly skilled at using the Light to heal others' wounds either.

The Order of the Silver Hand these days — most of its members were warriors who had only recently learned how to call upon the Light to aid themselves. It was perfectly normal that they couldn't heal their allies the way a priest could. Jace was injured too. It was only after the battle ended that he realized the spot where the felhound's tentacles had stabbed him at his waist was quite serious — several deep, bloody holes, the pain making the whole side of his abdomen throb.

He took some anonymous herbs another soldier had brought and applied them to the wound. It was about half an hour before it felt slightly better.

After helping arrange the wounded and the bodies, Jace sat down beside a tree and watched the soldiers finally getting a moment to rest and eat. He wondered what kind of demons they'd be facing in the near future.

"Thanks, mage."

The female elf came over and handed Jace a biscuit. Jace took it and said: "It's nothing, Farstrider. Weren't you the one who was planning to hold off those demons for me anyway?"

"I thought you couldn't handle them — after all, those were felhounds built for hunting mages," the elf said. "But you're more capable than I imagined. So why didn't you go to Nethergarde?"

"Maybe my teacher decided I'd be more useful helping out here," Jace said, embarrassed. "Or maybe I spent too much time brawling and didn't pay enough attention to my magic lessons."

The elf laughed and said: "Sounds like those felhounds picked the wrong target."

A moment later, she put away her smile and said: "When the demons first appeared a few days ago, I thought that given the Alliance of Lordaeron's efficiency, it might take a month or longer for reinforcements to arrive. I didn't expect the Kingdom of Wind to respond so quickly, honestly. I'd already made peace with the idea of dying at Stonard."

Jace took a bite of the biscuit and said: "After all, this concerns the Expedition… On the other side of the Dark Portal, there are too many people's loved ones and friends. As far as Lordaeron, as close as Stromgarde, as near as Stormwind City."

"And Quel'Thalas," the ranger picked up his thread. "I know the Ranger-General will be arriving at Nethergarde soon."

"The Ranger-General of Silvermoon?" Jace asked. "She's coming to Nethergarde?"

"Of course," the elf said. "Alleria Windrunner is her older sister — how could she ignore the crisis here? Though I'm not too familiar with the current troll situation in Quel'Thalas, I imagine after the Orc Wars, Zul'jin and his tribe were badly hit and won't be making any moves anytime soon."

I wonder if Vereesa Windrunner is coming too.

Jace thought about it and gripped the sword at his waist. Ever since receiving this sword, he'd been wanting to find a chance to thank Vereesa in person — after all, if not for this sword, he'd probably have died in the vaults of Uldaman already.

Though he'd thought about writing a letter of thanks, it never felt serious or formal enough, so he'd never put pen to paper.

"Your sword." The elf seemed to notice his movement. "It carries a powerful arcane energy. I noticed it earlier — may I take a look?"

Jace nodded and drew the longsword, handing it to her. The Farstrider held the lightweight magical sword and examined it this way and that. It wasn't long before her gaze went unfocused with awe.

"This is truly the most beautiful sword I've ever seen, mage," she murmured. "Does it have a name?"

"Azure Song," Jace said.

Hearing the name, the elf stared at Jace in surprise: "I know there's an Azure Song in the Windrunner family treasury as well. It's said to be a treasured sword bestowed by the blue lady Matzressa upon the Windrunner family, in gratitude for their protection of Eversong Woods and the Sunwell."

Matzressa — Jace still remembered that name. Together with another blue dragon, Kalecgos, who later became the Blue Dragon Aspect, she had been tasked with observing the Sunwell and all of Quel'Thalas. As guardians of magic, the blue dragons' duty was to protect and monitor Azeroth's sources of magic.

In the game, when the Sunwell was occupied by demons, the blue dragon Matzressa stepped forward to do battle with Brutallus, the Burning Legion's vanguard — the wingless demon lord who had torn off Molfir's wings — but was ultimately defeated and died at Brutallus's hands.

"This is that very one, ranger," Jace said. "If there's only one Azure Song."

"There's only one…" the ranger examined the sword again, then asked: "Why do you have it? Wait… are you the human mage who killed Teron Gorefiend alongside Lady Vereesa Windrunner?"

Jace nodded. "That's me."

"So that's how it is. I heard the news at Nethergarde, too — Teron Gorefiend's death has been an inspiration to all the Farstriders… Watching you casually cut down those felhounds, I knew you were no ordinary apprentice mage."

The elf ran a finger along the blade, which gleamed with arcane light. And the sword, as if responding to a child of the Sunwell, radiated a soft blue glow that curled around her finger.

"I had a very close uncle who died at the hands of a death knight as well," she said. "You and Lady Vereesa are both benefactors of Eversong Woods. This sword belongs in your hands more than anyone's. I can feel it — more than anything, it wants to purify the foul blood of demons and orcs. No matter how exquisite the Windrunner family treasury may be, that's no place for it."

"May the Sun continue to guide your path, human."

Translator Notes

Flags

- 碧空之歌 = Azure Song (legendary-style magical sword; name rendered as "Azure Song"). - 玛蒂苟萨 = Matzressa (blue dragon, consort of Malygos; fought Brutallus at the Sunwell). - 卡雷苟斯 = Kalecgos (Blue Dragon Aspect). - 布鲁塔卢斯 = Brutallus (Burning Legion fel lord). - 莫洛菲尔 = Molfir (unclear lore reference; translated literally — possibly a confused reference in the source). - 塔隆·血魔 = Teron Gorefiend (first death knight). - 温蕾萨·风行者 = Vereesa Windrunner (youngest Windrunner sister). - 永歌森林 = Eversong Woods; 太阳之井 = Sunwell. - Source text says 奥蕾莉亚·风行者的姐姐 ("Alleria Windrunner's older sister") describing the Ranger-General of Silvermoon — grammatically inverted; Alleria is the eldest Windrunner, so the intended meaning is that the Ranger-General (Sylvanas) is Alleria's younger sister. Rendered as "Alleria Windrunner is her older sister" (i.e., Alleria is older than the Ranger-General), which is unambiguous and lore-correct. - 游侠将军 of Silvermoon = Sylvanas Windrunner (implied by context).
