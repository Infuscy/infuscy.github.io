## Chapter 416: Bitter Reluctance

If they let this she-demon leave the spacious cavern and enter the mine tunnels, using the shadows and her incredible speed to duck and weave while fighting a guerrilla war, then this group's advantage in numbers would be completely wiped out.

Sanen reacted with lightning speed, sealing the cave entrance with a wall of ice. The shivarra slammed straight into it, materializing on impact, and swung her longblade repeatedly at the ice wall, shearing off several layers of frost — but then a volley of crossbow bolts came whistling in, one of them striking her square in the back.

The she-demon tore the bolt out with one arm and turned, only to find several soldiers closing in around her. Just as she raised her blade to strike at the enemy before her, Amy unleashed a Slow that snared her arm with pinpoint accuracy.

The shivarra shot a glance toward Amy's position. Another mage followed up with a Slow of his own. The shivarra began chanting an incantation on the spot, as if trying to break free of the layered arcane magic — but Master Sanen cut her off with a Counterspell.

By then, Amy's Frostbolt was already away, slamming full-force into the shivarra's armor and freezing several of her arms in place. The fireball Jace had been nursing for so long followed right on its heels, detonating against her chest and making her cough — one of her arms shredded by ice shrapnel, blood streaming from the wounds.

The shivarra roared in fury and Blinked straight toward them again. This time she meant it!

Compared to the she-demon's speed, Jace's retreat was painfully slow. He could only start chanting a Shadow Shield to save his life…

But the thought had barely formed in his mind, before he could even get the words out, the shivarra Blinked again, closing several meters in a blink to appear right behind him. Didn't this woman have a cooldown on her Blink?!

The shivarra slashed in the same motion, but Jace was quick too — he swung Skysong to parry, and the blow snapped the she-demon's longblade clean in two.

He threw himself back a step, dodging the second arm's downward chop, but couldn't avoid the third arm's thrust. It punched into his shoulder and chest.

Jace felt a burst of numbness across his shoulder, as if the air had been let out of him. His body went soft, and he barely managed to stagger back a few steps.

After landing those three strikes, the she-demon let out a triumphant laugh — a sound so hollow and layered it was as if several shivarra were laughing at once.

She adjusted her stance, about to launch a second round of attacks, when suddenly a mass of ice spikes erupted from the ground upward, engulfing her lower half and freezing her in place.

"Annoying wench of a mage…"

The shivarra wrenched free of a chunk of ice and hauled a leg out, but the momentary loss of speed was all the soldiers needed to swarm in.

She slashed at one of them with her blade — the blow caught on a shield. Another soldier stepped in and severed her raised arm with a sword. The shivarra cried out, only to have another soldier hack off one of her legs. A soldier with a polearm drove her clean to the ground with a brutal thrust…

After a frenzy of stabs and chops, the she-demon who'd been so savage and rampant just moments ago went still. The fel fire atop her crown flickered and died.

The shivarra had been ferocious, but against three and a half mages and twenty or so soldiers, she'd ultimately been outmatched. But if there had been a few succubi mixed in to support her back then, the situation probably wouldn't have been so clean.

The soldier who'd been run through the back had already died. The other wounded soldiers varied in the severity of their injuries, but at least they could still howl in pain.

Captain Mackler led the soldiers who'd come to help, along with the rest, to carry the wounded out. Once they reached the outside, even the wind blowing from the Dark Portal — carrying its stench of fel — felt like a relief.

Jace glanced at the wound on his shoulder. It wasn't light, and the pain was making his ears throb. But compared to the others, it was nothing worth mentioning.

Recalling the Shadowflame he'd cast earlier, he stole a glance at the other mages' reactions.

In truth, he'd only resorted to shadow magic because the shivarra seemed on the verge of breaking free. And with the cavern saturated in shadow energy, he'd figured his mana wouldn't be so easily noticed.

And indeed, the mages hadn't paid him any particular attention. The one who'd entered the leftmost tunnel alongside him and Amy even came over to ask about his wound.

He found himself wondering: if he coordinated with Morofil and Sazeno and went all-out with shadow magic, could they have taken down this shivarra?

Maybe use a Shadow Ward that reflected damage to block her attacks while Morofil struck from the shadows. If the shivarra went after her, between Morofil's agility and the imp's Blood Pact, they could definitely hold her off for a while — and that's when he'd use Blood Grasp to rip the demon's arms apart… The more he thought about it, the more he realized a stubborn, unwilling frustration was creeping in, precisely because the she-demon had made such a fool of him.

But this was a battlefield. Under the watch of so many mages and soldiers, not to mention paladins and priests, at most he could only slip in some inconspicuous shadow magic when no one was looking — a Curse of Weakness here and there, something like that.

Using Blood Grasp or a Shadow Ward with such flashy, overt effects? Forget it.

So he cut the train of thought short and cleared the mess from his head.

"How's your wound, Jace?" Amy came over. "Let me see?"

Jace pulled his robe tighter. "It's nothing. Just a stab. Not deep."

Amy still produced one of her own healing potions and held it out. "Drink this, Jace. Better safe than sorry. I won't be needing potions for a while."

Jace nodded and drank it down. After the other mages followed the team down to help sweep the battlefield, he turned to the mage beside him. "Amy, you really should be promoted to a full-fledged mage right now!"

He meant it from the depths of his heart. The Amy who always seemed a little delicate had, in the face of that she-demon, been as calm as a battle-hardened warrior. Every incantation, every spell choice — utterly unflappable.

Amy gazed at the she-demon's corpse being dragged away. "I felt like I was terrified, honestly… fumbling all over the place… I've never seen a demon like that. Her speed was just too much. I kept Blink on the tip of my tongue the whole time, but she could Blink too… It was way too close."

"If that's your version of terrified, then I hope I get terrified like that every time I run into trouble like this," Jace said. "If you hadn't frozen that she-demon with your magic the moment you did, I'd have been hacked to pieces!"

"How could that be?" Amy said. "You held your own against that demon for several exchanges with nothing but that sword. The others couldn't even survive a single blow. Sometimes I think you'd be better off joining the Silver Hand. Have you seen the knights of the Silver Hand fight? You remind me of those swordsmen blessed by the Holy Light."

Jace sighed. "Unfortunately, the Church doesn't take in guys like me who aren't devout enough. Besides, the whole reason I went to your dad in the first place was to make money. Come to think of it, June's salary should have been settled by now!"

"You still have the nerve to ask for your salary?" Amy said, incredulous. "Sure, they hired someone else for the daily cleaning, but as his assistant you're still supposed to help my dad organize his articles, right? All the way up until we came to Nethergarde, that was my job!"

"Ouch, no — my shoulder's starting to hurt!" Jace said quickly. "I need a priest…"

Amy pinched his cheek hard. "Then let me help you take your mind off it…"
