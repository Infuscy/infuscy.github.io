Chapter 418: The Decision on the Other Side

Ever since Grede had arrived at Nethergarde, the past couple of days had been a lot more relaxed. Though Grede, as a gryphon rider, was always flying out on scouting missions, whenever he had downtime he'd come over to the mage tower to find Jace and chat — and sometimes, when he ran into Amy, he'd even swap a few words with her about Jace's adventures, leaving Amy green with envy.

But for all Grede's easy chatter, the news he brought back wasn't always good.

The Alliance watchtowers near the Dark Portal were being destroyed one by one. The pressure around Dreadmaul Hold was mounting. And Grede had flown close to the Altar of Storms again on a scouting run — coming back with a face like a sheet of iron. When Jace couldn't help but ask what he'd seen, Grede would only say he'd never seen so many demons in his life.

The soldiers had guessed right: demons kept pouring out of the Dark Portal. If this continued, even if elven reinforcements arrived, they'd probably have a hard time reversing the Alliance's fortunes.

That afternoon, Nethergarde was unusually quiet for once — no missions out, no soldiers rushing back and forth, and Jace hadn't heard any word of mages being deployed. But his mood hadn't relaxed in the slightest because of the calm. Then a horn sounded again.

This time the horn blared without stop, as if calling everyone to assemble in the square.

Jace left the tower with the other mages and came to the center of Nethergarde. Two men walked up onto the wooden platform at the back of the square. One was a Kirin Tor archmage — Jace didn't recognize him, but he was certainly one of Nethergarde's leaders. The other was a powerfully built human general: golden hair, blue armor, a white eagle-emblem cloak draped over his shoulders, both hands resting on a greatsword of silver chased with gold, its blade engraved with the runic crest of the Kingdom of Lordaeron.

Jace knew this man: Alexandros Mograine, High Commander of the Knights of the Silver Hand and supreme commander of Nethergarde.

He'd followed Grand Marshal Anduin Lothar into the Battle of Blackrock Spire, and after Marshal Turalyon crossed the Dark Portal, he was now the Alliance commander of highest prestige and authority here.

"Comrades of the Alliance."

He looked out at every person who'd come to the square and spoke in a deep, resonant voice: "Just this morning I received a message — a message from the other side of the Dark Portal. And I believe this message should be shared with every soul in Nethergarde before it's sent to any king, minister, or noble. You have the right to know it before anyone else on Azeroth."

"The expeditionary force is winning. Before long, the demons will lose their grip on the Dark Portal."

An eruption of cheers swept through the crowd. Jace stared into Mograine's eyes — excitement surging through him, yet something felt off.

If the expeditionary force really had the power to gather all the soldiers and Sons of Lothar from across Draenor and rally them beneath the Dark Portal in less than two weeks, then in the other timeline, why had Archmage Khadgar chosen to destroy the Dark Portal to protect Azeroth?

Mograine continued: "The demons' true purpose… is to scatter as many of their minions across Azeroth as possible, to poison our land and devour our world. The Sons of Lothar are doing everything in their power to stop them — and our perseverance has strengthened their resolve!"

"So, before the expedition achieves its objective, we must hold these last dark hours before dawn!"

"The days ahead will grow harder — the demons will fight more ferociously — but these are merely the final throes of a dying beast. The supply line from here to the Kingdom of Stormwind is stable. The army of Quel'Thalas is on its way. Soon the entire Alliance — human, elf, and dwarf alike — will stand on the same line of defense… No enemy can break this will."

"Our one and only goal is to hold the demons here. To protect the world behind us. To protect our homes. And the Light will stand with us."

These words did something to lift the gloom of the past few days and all the rumors that had come with it.

Listening to General Mograine's disposition of the battles to come, the defensive focus had shifted back to Dreadmaul Hold — and perhaps before long, the demons would launch an even more terrible assault on it.

But Jace still harbored doubts about what he'd said — about the message from the other side of the Dark Portal.

Perhaps word really had leaked through. But the word wasn't of victory — it was that the expeditionary force was already planning to destroy the Dark Portal. Khadgar must have made up his mind: to put a permanent end to Draenor's collapse affecting Azeroth, and incidentally seal off the demons' passage into this world.

Under the current circumstances, it was indeed the most direct and effective choice.

But the countless people here had come to save the brothers, sisters, and friends trapped in the expeditionary force. General Mograine certainly couldn't tell everyone here that the Dark Portal was about to collapse — that the expedition might never come back.

It was just that the demons were growing more numerous and more ferocious by the day — a fact clear to anyone.

These past two days, morale at Nethergarde had been shaken too. After all, the fighting was getting harder, and news of outposts and watchtowers falling came one after another.

Mograine had to find a way to keep the garrison holding on a little longer — until the Dark Portal was destroyed.

Recalling what the general had said earlier, he'd never actually claimed that the expedition had defeated the demons, or that the expedition was about to seize the Dark Portal.

What the general kept saying was that the demons were about to lose control of the Dark Portal, and that the expedition would achieve its objective and carry out its plan…

Of course, this was all just Jace's speculation. He had no idea what was really happening on the other side of the Dark Portal.

By evening, Nethergarde's tavern was packed to the rafters. Jace and Grede couldn't squeeze in at all, so they ended up beneath the mage tower. The mage tower didn't forbid other soldiers from entering, but aside from mages, not many people were keen on coming into a place that made them uncomfortable.

Grede, though, was used to it — back in Stormwind, he'd occasionally tagged along with Jace to the Blue Heron, so he didn't mind much.

Once seated, the dwarf took a sip of the ice water a mage had conjured and asked, "So what do you make of what that human general said?"

"I don't know," Jace said.

Grede said bluntly, "I don't believe for a second that a gryphon could fly out of that Dark Portal without getting shot down. When I got close to the Altar of Storms, I had a look — it was black with demons, and a lot of those demons could fly. How many gryphon riders would you need to organize to get a message out?"

Jace didn't know how to argue. He'd been turning the same question over in his mind. But he didn't think Alexandros Mograine would fabricate all of this out of thin air.

"Whatever happens, we have to keep fighting the demons," Jace said. "If we fail here, then the whole world really will have to fight another Orc War."

Grede pursed his lips. "Sure. I just think it's a little strange."

"Me too," Jace nodded.
