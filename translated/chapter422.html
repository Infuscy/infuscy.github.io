Chapter 422: The Calamity Summoned

The first lightning hammer had smashed into the Doomguard and covered him in green gore. One arm had been blown clean off, leaving nothing but a skeleton stained with vile green blood. Yet he still snarled a grin — or rather, the creature's natural expression already resembled a triumphant, wicked leer.

And it wasn't just here. The griffin riders plunged into this swarm of burning demon eyes. Lightning hammers and flying thunderbolts scattered in every direction, illuminating the forest below and all the surrounding space. In the distance, a Doomguard was struck by several lightning hammers at once and instantly exploded into a cloud of fel-green blood mist, shattered demon wings and limbs flying everywhere.

But the Doomguard facing Grede's side was clearly larger. Just as a griffin rider dove from above, raising his lightning hammer to strike, the creature suddenly spat a ball of black-smoking fire from its mouth, caught it in its hand, and hurled it at the oncoming griffin.

The griffin rider had no choice but to abandon his throw, pulling his hammer back to smash it against the fireball — only to be engulfed by the fel flames that burst outward. Man and griffin alike caught fire, screaming as they plunged into the forest below.

Grede's warhammer was already gathering a charge of lightning. Jace felt that demon amassing a powerful mass of shadow energy. He immediately began chanting the Fear incantation — but it was like touching a blazing fire. His consciousness was suddenly seared, and the shadow energy he'd painstakingly gathered was instantly devoured by the fel fire.

"What the…"

"Grede, watch out for shadow magic!"

A Shadow Bolt came flying toward them right after Jace's shout. Grede hurled his warhammer and detonated the Shadow Bolt midair, the one that had been streaking straight at them.

After shattering the attack, the dwarf immediately barked a command. The griffin beneath them suddenly dropped a great deal. The sudden force nearly flung Jace off the seat, but the straps held him fast.

A moment later, another Shadow Bolt howled past overhead. The shadow power grazed by close, letting out a piercing shriek. But in dodging it, they had already flown past the Doomguard.

"Damn it!" Grede roared in fury. The griffin beneath him let out an irritated growl.

The griffin surged forward twenty or thirty meters, coming around behind the Doomguard. Grede reached over and grabbed Jace by the robe. "Use your dark magic and blow that bastard to pieces!"

Jace grunted an acknowledgment. He looked back down at the Doomguard staring up at him, then at the griffins all around weaving through shadow magic and fel fireballs. He gripped his staff, pointed it at the demon, and chanted the Immolate incantation!

A blaze of crimson flame erupted above the Doomguard's head. The demon's horns were blown off, the entire scalp peeled back in a layer. Shadow fire raced along the fuzz atop his head and instantly engulfed his whole body. He howled, beating at the black flames spreading over him. Then a griffin rider below seized the chance, swinging his warhammer as he flew in and slamming it straight into the demon's chest, cracking his body apart with a single blow. The moment he was struck, the demon went still. Tumbling through the wind, he spiraled down into the forest, his body torn in two by the airstream in the process.

Almost simultaneously, a Doomguard wielding a war blade suddenly appeared above and to the side of Gorjet. It laughed savagely and swung its sword. Jace blocked it with his dark staff — a tremendous clang, and the whole griffin was driven downward by the impact. Jace felt half his body go numb from the shock.

Had it been an ordinary staff, the demon would have sliced through both the caster and the rod in one stroke.

After the Doomguard was deflected, Grede reversed his grip and swung his hammer around, striking the demon's shoulder and tearing away a chunk of his black armor.

The demon steadied itself in the air, beat its black wings twice, and charged back. By now Grede's warhammer was crackling with lightning. As it raised its blade and lunged again, Grede swung and struck it square in the chest, smashing it down into a dark ditch on the ground.

Relying on their speed and the element of surprise, the griffin rider legion quickly seized the advantage. By now they had already killed several Doomguards; fewer than ten remained in the distance. Grede's group banked and charged that way. By this point, twenty or thirty griffin riders of all sizes were already launching their assault.

The mages' spells were faster than the griffins' flight. Arcane Missiles and ice lances danced through the air from every direction, ceaselessly striking at the flying demons that could barely hold their ground. Then the dwarf rider captain shouted, "New enemies incoming, mages — time for you to really make a difference!"

Jace looked toward the captain, and sure enough, another mass of dark, swarming monsters was appearing in his field of vision from afar.

The shrill shrieking could already be heard from here. That's right — the fel bats Amy had told him about! The Burning Legion's reinforcements had arrived too!

The griffin riders had no choice but to rouse the lightning in their warhammers to meet the swooping tide of fel bats. The nearest griffin rider swung a glowing warhammer into the mass of dark monsters. Wherever the hammer passed, it released a wide burst of lightning, striking who knew how many bat demons — but he still couldn't dodge them all and was swallowed up by still more bats. He struggled for a moment, then vanished from sight.

The remaining Doomguards seemed to take heart as well, since the griffin riders could no longer focus on them entirely. With their small, nimble bodies and overwhelming numbers, the fel bats quickly encircled the battlefield, striking from every direction. By now several griffin riders, exhausted from fighting the suddenly surrounding imps, were struck by Doomguard Shadow Bolts and fell screaming into the forest.

Grede guided his griffin past another griffin that was gnawing furiously at fel bats, the dwarf rider on its back nowhere to be seen.

Then a fel bat dove from above, going for Gorjet's head with its claws. Grede smashed it to a pulp with one swing of his hammer. The dwarf then hurled his lightning-charged hammer and blasted apart three fel bats that had clustered together, attempting to ambush another griffin rider.

"This is a waste of time. We focus on that Doomguard, Jace — take him down first!" Grede pointed at a demon below as he said it.

Jace acknowledged. Then suddenly a fel bat dove at them from above. Jace swung his staff and blew its head apart. The fel bat's remains spun and tumbled into the dark canopy below.

Lightning flashed in all directions, tearing at swarms of fel bats. Gorjet carried the two of them through the middle of it. Grede raised his warhammer high again. Jace lowered his staff, chanting, and with his other hand began gathering a sphere of shadow flame at the staff's tip.

Their target was flapping its wings in pursuit of another griffin rider beset by fel bats. Gorjet was silent, but its speed grew ever faster, shaking off several fel bats that tried to follow as it charged straight at the Doomguard clad in green armor.

Grede took aim as they closed in. His warhammer erupted with lightning as they drew near, then he hurled it with all his might!

The warhammer spun, releasing a shower of electric sparks. But the Doomguard seemed to sense the lightning suddenly striking from behind. It swung its blade around and deflected Grede's hammer.

After a burst of electricity, the Doomguard laughed loudly and raised its blade to meet the charging griffin. The war blade in its hand was thick and massive. If it came down on Gorjet, it would cleave him clean in two!

Grede yanked the reins hard, but Gorjet was moving too fast — a collision seemed inevitable… Jace's Fireball incantation was already complete. Just as the Doomguard was about to swing its blade at Gorjet, he plucked the blazing mass from his staff and smacked it straight into the Doomguard's face!

The griffin had done its best to dodge, but the shockwave from the violent shadow explosion still sent all three of them reeling. Still, compared to the Doomguard, they were in far better shape. Jace managed to glance back down. The Doomguard had been blown down to half a body, tumbling into the forest. At some point, he felt, his magical killing power had grown to a level he'd never imagined.

He didn't have to think hard to know it was surely connected to the fierce combat on this battlefield, to the intense emotions born from hating these demons.

"That's it, Jace!" Grede laughed as he reeled his warhammer back in.

Looking into the distance again, the griffin riders had already retaken the initiative. The demons were being steadily suppressed. Now only a single Doomguard remained in the surrounding sky, barely holding on — but it was already badly wounded, not long for the world…

"Awaken! The brand of Kil'jaeden has descended upon this world!"

A deep, earth-shaking roar rolled in from the southwest. Quite a few griffins were startled by the sound and turned to look. Even more absurdly, even the fel bats fell into disarray.

Jace immediately used Detect Demons to observe the southwest, but he couldn't see a thing.

"I, Azgoth, am his herald, his messenger!"

"Go forth, go forth, and proclaim the coming of Azgoth to this world!"

The voice was coming from the Storm Altar. Had the Burning Legion used the Storm Altar to summon a demon lord?

From the direction of the western fortress, Jace saw countless Infernals crashing into the walls. On the ground lit by the demonic fires, dense masses of demon soldiers surged forward in unison toward Grimhammer Hold.

More and more Doomguards appeared on the horizon. The griffin rider captain shouted the order to withdraw. A sense of foreboding slowly spread through Jace's heart.

Grimhammer Hold seemed unable to hold.
