Chapter 424: Battle Mage

The next noon, Jace was eating dried mushrooms in the mage tower when a mage in Dalaran robes entered. He looked at a mage nearby who was tidying a desk and asked, "Is Mr. Jace Sesso in the tower, by any chance?"

"I'm Jace Sesso," Jace raised a hand.

The mage came up to Jace. "Mr. Sesso, you've been inducted into the battle mages!"

Jace was taken aback. He knew that even wanting to join the Wizard Sanctum's battle mages required a series of assessments and training. Why had he been inducted directly?

Just as he was wondering if he might be some kind of cannon-fodder role like a "battle mage apprentice" or "battle mage assistant," the mage produced a ring from his robe and handed it to Jace. "Safeguard this Violet Battle Mage Insignia, Mr. Jace Sesso."

Jace took the ring and placed it beside his Wizard Sanctum apprentice mage ring, nodding blankly.

The Dalaran mage glanced at the tattered robe Jace was still wearing and said, "Go to the supply depot near the alchemy lab outside and find a Kirin Tor officer to collect your gear. And starting tonight, Mr. Sesso, you'll also begin taking wall-watch duty — from dusk until the first horn at midnight."

With that, he said his goodbyes and hurried out of the mage tower, apparently off to find the next mage to be inducted into the same unit.

Arriving at the quartermaster, Jace still hadn't quite come back to his senses.

He showed his battle mage ring to an officer wearing an Eye of the Arcane battle robe and received a heavy package.

Back in his room in the mage tower, he opened it to find a fairly new blue-purple battle mage robe — and even a battle mage helmet with lining.

He tried it on. He could tell the robe had been enchanted, though he didn't know how long ago. There were also signs of damage on the robe — it had simply been roughly repaired before being handed to him.

In any case, he'd rather not think too hard about what had happened to this outfit's previous owner.

Over the following days, he attended several battle mage training sessions. It seemed the mages here were indeed becoming more and more scarce. And most importantly, fewer and fewer mages could be summoned to Nethergarde Keep.

And from the conversations among his fellow battle mages, Jace could sense that everyone knew a decisive battle was coming to this place — and that it wasn't far off.

That evening, Jace stood on the wall gazing into the distance. Green fire was burning in the black forest below where he'd fought alongside the griffin riders. The flames lit the entire wall of Nethergarde Keep in green, and his cheeks glowed green in the reflection.

The soldiers on the wall coughed frequently — either choked by the endless dust or more or less contaminated by fel. It was the same for everyone else in Nethergarde Keep. Humans especially. Dwarves and elves fared much better, but most of them still felt some discomfort.

"Mage, is there any way we can stop those green meteors?"

A sentinel beside Jace suddenly asked.

"When the time comes, the Kirin Tor will lead the mages to establish a magical barrier, sir," Jace said. "I believe those meteors won't be able to break through the archmages' magic."

"Then why… can't that magical barrier block this damned dust right now?" The sentinel coughed twice.

Jace looked up at the magic crystal atop the mage tower. It had dimmed to the point where it could barely emit any light. Was it being suppressed by fel? Or was it conserving its strength…

He didn't know exactly what would happen either. But he knew that he and this sentinel would soon see the answer with their own eyes.

Just then, a company of elven rangers hurried past. One of the rangers stopped and glanced down below the wall.

"Farstrider… excuse me," Jace said, stepping over.

The ranger at the rear of the company, who seemed to be the captain, stopped. "What is it?"

"Could I ask — where is Lady Vereesa Windrunner right now?"

The elf glanced at his two rings, then said, "She's with the Ranger-Lord's company carrying out a cleansing mission, mage."

"What exactly is the mission?" Jace asked.

The elf glanced south and said, "We found a demon mage at Mandel Outpost in the south. You should know what a cleansing mission means."

"Thank you very much," Jace said. "I understand!"

The elf gave Jace a curious look. "Do you have an urgent matter to report? Or were you actually asking about General Sylvanas Windrunner?"

"No, it's Lady Vereesa I'm asking about," Jace said. "Sorry to hold you up."

The elf nodded and turned to hurry after his company.

Jace leaned over the wall, gazing toward the shadow of collapsed ruins on a distant height far to the south. That was Mandel Outpost, a place that guarded the Dark Portal. It was burning with demon fire too — otherwise it would be impossible to make out at this distance.

He wondered how the rangers' mission was going.

An Eredar mage… even killing just one would weaken the demons' assault on Nethergarde Keep by a fraction.

The relief horn sounded. Jace met the mage relieving him and returned to the mage tower. Dust was everywhere — in the hall, on the stairs.

The rooms, being swept clean, were much better than outside. But the mages lying there coughed incessantly. Nearly half of them were in too much pain to sleep.

Lying in bed, Jace tried his best not to overthink things and forced himself to get some sleep.

Just as he was finally drifting off, about to fall asleep, a gloomy, deep laugh sounded from outside.

"Do you hear the footsteps of doom, mortals?"

The voice seemed to come from an unknowable distance — as if resounding from the sky, and as if trembling through the earth.

Jace looked around and found that most people were already awake, staring wide-eyed toward the window by the door.

Then one of the mages began to cry, stifling the sound, sobbing uncontrollably.

"You are but one of many worlds that have been destroyed. None can stand against Kil'jaeden. None can stand against the Burning Legion…"

The demon's voice plunged the room back into silence.

Before long, an unfamiliar woman's singing drifted in from outside.

"The dream-dew flowers in the overgrown garden do not bloom from tears…"

"Look — the tender moonlight kisses the banks of the Nafeltari, my love. Tonight, at least, you may rest in my arms…"

"At least you…" Jace murmured the lyric to himself, and without realizing it, drifted off to sleep.

Jace asks after her specifically — the elf assumes he means Sylvanas. - 曼德尔哨站 → Mandel Outpost: Southern outpost guarding the Dark Portal; tentative rendering of 曼德尔. - 纳菲瑞提河 → Nafeltari River: Per glossary (River Neltharion / Nafeltari River). The lullaby lyric references its banks. - 恶魔法师 → demon mage: Generic term for a Legion spellcaster, here an Eredar. - The demon voice and the woman's lullaby are atmospheric — the lullaby lets Jace (and the mages) sleep through the terror. Left the song's imagery loose and dreamlike.
