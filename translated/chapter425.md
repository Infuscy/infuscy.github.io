## Chapter 425: The Day Has Finally Come

Unexpectedly, he'd had a peaceful night's sleep that night.

When he got up in the morning, the sky was unusually bright. He stepped out onto the mage tower's terrace and looked down. Outside the castle lay a scene of bleak desolation. This version of the Blighted Lands made him deeply uneasy.

Of course, compared to Elwynn Forest or the Redridge Mountains, it was gray enough. But compared to the previous few days, you could even describe it as bathed in sunlight.

The thick clouds overhead had dispersed quite a bit. He could make out the position of the sun behind the clouds — a spread of white halo. Just the sight of it was immensely comforting.

Dry weather, a hazy but at least bright sky, a faint scent of scorching in the air that wasn't particularly malevolent. Was this some kind of dream?

He pinched his own face. Honestly, it had been a very, very long time since Jace had had a dream he couldn't distinguish from reality. This shouldn't be a dream.

This was reality, because the Dark Portal was still there, standing motionless.

It was just that under the sunlight, that green curtain was no longer so conspicuous. But it still stood beyond clear view, revealing a sliver of light, watching this side like a half-open demon eye.

Then, the sound of hurried footsteps came from within the mage tower.

"All battle mages, assemble in the square."

So the moment had still arrived. When he reached the square, even as the battle mages were still assembling, crowds of soldiers had already been leaving the gates for positions beyond the walls.

Dwarves holding shields marched past, singing songs he couldn't understand. Swarms of elves split into two groups — one group mounting the walls, the other following the human soldiers and dwarf legions out the gate ahead of them.

Jace's unit followed a company of elven strider cavalry out of the city. The moment they passed through the gate, he saw that the slope outside Nethergarde Keep was already packed with company after company of soldiers.

They held aloft banners of every color, standing in neat rows and ranks, all facing the direction of the Dark Portal. The battle mages took their position behind a group of brawny dwarf axemen.

Jace looked at the battle mages beside him. Most of them were pale but calm, gripping their swords tightly as they stared toward the chaos ahead.

Then, the crystal atop the mage tower suddenly released a dazzling radiance. The light spread like a burst of ripples, driving away all the gloom and thick clouds, brightening the area around Nethergarde Keep considerably.

The area protected by this magical shield was far greater than Nethergarde Keep's walls. Perhaps when the black dragons had besieged Dalaran back in the day, the Kirin Tor mages had used just such a barrier to protect the city from the black dragons' magic bombardment.

Nethergarde Keep's soldiers had to sortie and meet the enemy to seize the advantage. If they stayed on the walls, they would eventually exhaust the crystal and the mages' strength entirely, and in the end they could only be bombarded into exhaustion by endless Infernals and fel fireballs until the city fell and the men died.

General Alexandros Mograine appeared at the very front of the army. He held Lordaeron's Marshal's Sword — a blade so enormous it was exaggerated, especially viewed from a distance. Jace couldn't help but worry about the great warhorse beneath him, which was already bearing heavy armor and barding.

He seemed to be haranguing the troops loudly, and the soldiers up front were shouting back in response. But from where Jace was, aside from catching the occasional high note, he couldn't make out a thing.

He didn't know how long they stood in silence. Just as Jace felt his legs getting sore from standing and was desperate to move around, a sudden commotion rippled through the ranks.

He saw figures slowly emerging from the black woods in the distant lowlands.

On the heights farther still, groups of shadows appeared. In gullies, on distant roads, countless surging heads emerged. Green fire burned on their bodies. They raised all manner of long weapons high and gradually converged, advancing bit by bit toward the high ground where Nethergarde Keep stood.

"At last…" Jace heard a female mage beside him whisper.

Yes. The oppression of these past days had brought everyone to the verge of breaking. Everyone knew what was going to happen, but no one knew when.

Better to end this quickly than to watch the sword hanging overhead and wait for it to fall. At least here, the blade in his own hand could still make a difference.

The demon army drew closer and closer. Jace noticed that at the very front of the demon host walked several extremely tall, red-skinned demons.

They wore dark golden armor and strode with arrogant steps far ahead of the other demons. By now they had drawn close enough to the Alliance's front line that their features could be made out with the naked eye.

These demons walked on hooves. They were tall and powerfully built. Upright bony plates crowned their heads, with horns. Strange tentacles writhed about their chins. Their eyes were small but glowed with an eerie fel light.

A group of Eredar.

At last, these cunning sorcerers had finally shown their true faces.

"Attack!"

General Mograine roared the word, and the other generals of Quel'Thalas and Ironforge followed with commands in every language.

"Advance!"

A mage beside him shouted, and Jace moved forward with the battle mages around him. Everyone drew their longswords. Jace pulled Azure Song from his cloak as well.

Even though he tried his best to keep the blade from shining too brightly and turning himself into a target on the battlefield, quite a few mages and even elves glanced over in surprise. But when the mages began chanting Mage Armor, Jace realized his worry had been entirely unnecessary.

As the Alliance army began to push forward, the Eredar at the front gave a soft, contemptuous laugh, then raised his hands and began channeling a fel summoning. The Eredar around him immediately began making the same casting gestures.

As the Eredar cast, the demon army began to advance amid waves of howling, their pace growing ever faster. They surged past the Eredar sorcerers' casting formation to the front. At the same time, swarms upon swarms of fel bats appeared in the sky — a dark mass sweeping across the bleak heavens. Their sharp screeching and the beating of their wings nearly drowned out every other sound in that moment.

Just as Jace thought the Alliance vanguard was about to suffer aerial harassment from these fel bats, several flights of griffin riders dove in from different directions and scattered the leading fel bat swarms at once. Then a flock of dragonhawks shrieked forward from overhead, flying higher than the sky where the griffin riders and fel bats were locked in combat, charging straight at the Eredar sorcerers!

The sky began to rumble. Malevolent green lights appeared in the clouds… just as he'd expected, the Eredar sorcerers were channeling Infernals!

Golden light burst from the Alliance vanguard. The paladins' shouted prayers could be heard clearly even from here. Rank after rank of fel guards, upon encountering the Holy Light's radiance and assault, immediately hesitated in their steps. That moment of hesitation was instantly devoured by the Light of the Order of the Silver Hand. In the distance, a company of knights in white and blue charged down from the heights into the dark mass of demons, piercing through the demon host like a spear thrust clean through. Coordinating with the legion vanguard led by the paladins to squeeze from both sides, the demon front line had already begun to waver!
