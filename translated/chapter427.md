## Chapter 427: Overlord

The Dark Portal's curtain flickered ceaselessly. The earth trembled. The stench of fel fire filled every breath. Flames of crimson and green streaked across the dark sky, crossing paths as they flew.

The battle cries of Alliance soldiers and demons alike thundered across the field — impossible to tell apart whether they were spells, curses, or shouted declarations.

A dragonhawk, struck by some fel magic, crashed burning into a mass of demons. It struggled to its feet again, beating its flame-colored great wings at the demons trying to close in — but a towering Felguard behind it hacked through its neck with one axe blow.

Jace watched it happen. Then a Mo'arg demon suddenly charged at him from the side, and he had to dodge aside at once. The bloated vanguard drove a spiked mace into the ground where he'd been standing, wrenched it out with a clump of blackened earth, and stared at him with a vacant grin, weighing his weapon in his hand.

"Come on!" Jace shouted, gripping Azure Song.

But before the crimson-skinned monster could finish its roar and raise its hammer-head, a bolt of lightning howled down from above — a warhammer came flying from somewhere and blew the demon's arm and shoulder to pieces.

The demon howled and toppled. Jace rushed forward and drove his blade deep into its heart, and the massive bulk of blubber sagged and collapsed on the spot.

A gryphon rider swept overhead with a whoop of "Yah-hah!" and soared toward the distant fray, the warhammer flying back to his hand with his cry, then spinning off toward the next target.

"We shall prevail!"

General Mograine's voice rang out with startling clarity not far away. The moment he finished shouting, he raised that dazzling greatsword, wheeled his mount, and charged a Felguard.

Rank upon rank of blue, white, and red cloaks and battle garb lined the field. Paladins raised their gleaming longswords and warhammers high. The Alliance soldiers were pushing forward, step by step.

The sky darkened. The arcane crystal atop the mage tower blazed like a second sun, illuminating the battlefield. Every time it flared, it seemed to drive back the surging, frenzied black tide before the soldiers a little further.

An Eredar warlock three or four meters tall was surrounded by human soldiers with spears. He gritted his teeth and swung his staff — each swing sent a soldier flying — but his body was riddled with bloody holes, and the twin fires that had burned so fiercely in his eyes when he first appeared were long gone. He crashed to the ground heavily.

The soldiers trampled his corpse and pressed on. The other Eredar warlocks glared at the advancing line, casting spells as they retreated. The mage tower's mana shield seemed even stronger than before. More and more priests and paladins called upon the Holy Light, and a brilliant gold had filled Jace's entire field of vision…

Jace stared ahead and felt his body go soft.

He didn't know how long he'd been fighting here. His energy was nearly spent, his hands and barely obeyed him.

He was already carrying several wounds — one seemed to have sliced across his leg, and it burned fiercely, keeping him from moving forward.

By comparison, the wound on his back from the Felhunter's tentacle bite earlier didn't count for anything at all.

Demons' corpses lay everywhere now. Occasionally he could spot a human soldier buried beneath demon flesh. He felt like he couldn't match the paladins' boundless spirit anymore.

Jace swallowed. His mouth was bone-dry. He could only drink his last mana potion.

The elves around him advanced with the line, their steps firm and their spirits high. Even after fighting for so long, aside from the damp golden hair clinging to their faces and the nearly emptied quivers at their sides, they seemed as full of fight as the moment they'd arrived on the field.

By comparison, he stood there panting like a beaten straggler. Honestly, by this point, under the endless harassment of Felhunters, the battle mages' formation had already fallen apart — only a handful of Kirin Tor battle mages were still holding the line up front.

Just then, he felt that brilliant gold light suddenly dim. A soldier beside him faltered and stopped his charge, looking up into the distance.

Jace followed his gaze, a little dazed. Between the grey mountain ridges, an enormous dark shadow had appeared at some point — so massive that the fortress walls seemed almost diminutive by comparison. Jace felt his heart start pounding hard again.

"Weep! Tremble! Witness the coming of Azgus — your meaningless resistance ends now!"

The roar that followed was nearly shattered by its own force, and the entire vast mountainside trembled with it. Jace noticed the shadow suddenly erupt in blazing green fire.

Its form and features snapped into clarity with those flames.

It was an unspeakably ugly demon. It didn't have the dull simplicity of a Mo'arg, the beastial ferocity of an Ered'ruin, the bewitching, bizarre beauty of a Shivarra, or the bone-chilling cold of a Nathrezim.

Its face seemed born for no other purpose than to inspire fear and revulsion in mortals — born to make anyone who saw it physically recoil, on the verge of vomiting.

Its teeth interlocked in layers like rows of razors. Two great fangs jutted upward from the corners of its mouth like savage ivory tusks. On its fat snout, its eyes had been squeezed by folds of demonic flesh and fat into two flickering little flames.

When it raised the ridiculous fleshy wings on its back and shifted its obese, sauropod-like bulk, the iron chains draped across its body clanged against its fel-fire-burnished armor with a grating assault on every senses on the battlefield.

A pit lord… a true Annihilan overlord.

Jace couldn't even estimate just how large the demon lord was from where he stood. Dragging that obese, bloated body out of the shadows in the distance and hurtling toward the battle line, it was like a moving hill. Countless demons were crushed to death beneath its tread, and the demons' wails on the field even drowned out the war cries.

"Why…"
"What is that?"

He heard the soldiers beside him murmur.

A horse whinnied behind him. Jace noticed General Mograine hauling on his mount's reins, struggling to keep it from bolting in terror — but it was no use.

The moment Azgus appeared on the battlefield, every warhorse lost its composure. And not just the horses — even the gryphons in the sky suddenly felt the overwhelming dread, and under the counterattacking fel bats and flying Doomguard, they were forced to pull back for the moment.

Jace watched several gryphons get shot down from the sky in that span, tumbling into the black tide of demons without a sound.

The demons grew visibly more frenzied, but the Alliance line was clearly beginning to waver…

Azgus shoved aside a mass of soldiers and demons with his enormous weapon and crushing bulk, plunging straight into the depths of the Alliance line — but he had no interest in lingering. He only relied on his matchless strength to keep pushing forward.

The mage tower!

His objective was utterly clear. Like a battering ram several stories tall, he charged straight for the mage tower, laughing and cursing, sweeping his massive blade back and forth across the ground at Alliance soldiers at random. Nothing seemed able to stop his power…

If the mage tower fell, the Eredar's Infernals would destroy everything they'd fought for…

Wait — Amy and old Malin were still up in the tower. The Wizard Sanctum's mages were still at the top of the mage tower!
