Chapter 428: The Last Effort

No… wait.

Jace felt his scalp tingle. He broke into a run toward the path the pit lord was barreling down. On that vast battlefield, not a single demon, not a single person paid any mind to why an apprentice mage had started running sideways.

Beneath the demons charging forward, a paladin's golden light was suddenly snuffed out as the pit lord's foot came down on him. Another knight drove his screaming, madly neighing warhorse in a death charge at the demon lord — and was swallowed in an instant by the fel fire that burned in the pit lord's wake.

The Farstriders had expended vast quantities of arrows in the long battle before this, and the shafts they could aim at this pit lord now were sparse and scattered — but even the ones that struck home were useless.

The monster's demon-hide was covered in thick scales and tough carapace. Unless you precisely struck the weak points that were nearly impossible to even see, it wouldn't even count as a scratch!

The mage tower began hurling Arcane Missiles down one after another — only for the pit lord charging up to open his maw wide and swallow them all. He laughed wildly and kept striding forward, followed by masses of soldiers who couldn't match his pace, able only to watch as he raised that absurdly massive blade high…

His target was the arcane crystal blazing with magical light atop the tower.

The demon roared and swung his giant blade at the top of the mage tower — but his blade was caught by a dark force of blood, throwing the strike off-target so that it hit the base of the tower instead, shearing away a thick wall of rubble.

The tower shuddered. The arcane crystal shuddered and flickered with it, and the entire mana shield instantly destabilized.

The demon looked in the direction the strange force had come from, puzzled — a feeble figure kneeling not far away.

Jace stared at the rumbling colossus before him, feeling the blood in his chest surging without end. He coughed, choking on a mouthful of black blood, and forced himself to swallow back what hadn't come out.

He'd cast Blood Demon's Grasp — that single tug had instantly burned through every last scrap of energy he had left, and churned the blood inside him into chaos.

The pit lord had noticed him. It was hard to read any expression on that hideous face, but Jace knew he'd been provoked.

Such a puny insect — what right did it have to impede his advance?

In his blurred, reddening field of vision, the demon that blotted out the sky with its massive body and wings laughed and raised its weapon high. Jace felt he had no strength left to dodge anything. He'd lost all his energy, and he had no idea what kind of damage that single yank had wreaked inside his body. All he knew was that this was his last effort — the last he had in him.

A black shadow swept across the sky. A gryphon screamed. The sound of countless feathers beating the air echoed in his ears.

He stared at the mage tower behind the demon. Had his teacher gotten out? Had Amy gotten out?

"Jace!"

He didn't know who was calling him — someone familiar, it seemed.

"Jace Sesso!"

The demon's longblade swung down — and halted with a crack of thunder!

Jace stared at the stone-statue-like monster in front of him, holding the pit lord's weapon aloft mid-swing with two weapons braced against it.

"Jace Sesso! You goddamn—"

With a cascade of stone cracking sounds, Grede Thunderfist swung his warhammer, lightning crackling along its head, and smashed it against the pit lord's giant blade, knocking it aside!

The pit lord muttered in surprise, then locked his gaze on the tiny stone figure that had gone mad and charged right to his feet.

He tried to stomp it — but the dwarf dodged easily, then hacked an axe into the demon's hind leg. That only made the demon more hysterical. It cackled and cursed, and the demonic fire that burned from the crown of its head to the tip of its fat tail erupted again.

It raised its massive arm, gathering a wave of fel flame, and as its right hand lifted, a shockwave of fel fire erupted from beneath it and exploded outward in every direction!

Jace was blasted by the shockwave, thrown to the ground and sent rolling among a field of spent Infernal rubble. The crushing impact and dizziness nearly wiped out what little consciousness he had left.

Using Blood Demon's Grasp to tug at that weapon… that was genuinely the dumbest thing he could've done.

"Grede…"

He struggled to push himself up. The pit lord was frenziedly slashing at everyone who surrounded him — paladins, spearmen, that stone-statue-like dwarf…

But the battle line up ahead had been torn open.

Demons surged forward, shoving through the soldiers' attempts to hold them back. A Felguard with a poleaxe was chopping down at him. Jace gripped Azure Song at his waist — and realized he didn't even have the strength to draw the blade.

Just when he thought he was about to be cleaved in two, a flash of arcane light suddenly tore open the Felguard's chest before his eyes. An arrow wreathed in arcane energy drove deep into the ground, and the Felguard dropped to its knees in front of him and collapsed, trembling.

Vereesa Windrunner's figure was already in front of him, standing guard. She drew two long knives and lopped the head off a Mo'arg soldier that had lunged at them with a single stroke.

She shouted something in completely unintelligible Thalassian to the Farstriders and elves in the distance, nimbly dodged a vertical slash from another Felguard, and at the same time drove her sword straight through the Felguard's helmet, plunged the other blade into his chest, stepped off his thigh, and in the same motion hacked through another Felguard's neck.

In the span of that short while, she must have slaughtered countless enemies. In his peripheral vision, her swaying figure was like a spinning blade storm, sweeping in every demon that tried to rush at Jace — leaving only a blur of limp limbs and severed heads tumbling out.

Jace forced himself to sit up and look into the distance. The pit lord had nearly finished off all the soldiers surrounding him. Everywhere was shattered, gore-smeared wreckage that was impossible to identify… except for a stone statue, drenched in the pit lord's green blood, ceaselessly hacking and smashing at that nearly immovable demon with its weapons.

Jace watched the demon's longblade nearly catch the dwarf several times — but the latter never retreated an inch, only laughing louder and louder.

"I won't let you die, Jace." Vereesa drove her blade into a demon's chest even as she murmured the words, her voice barely more than panting, not really speech.

"Jace Sesso!" the stone statue in the distance bellowed in a rough, muffled voice. "This is what I call a real battle — a true battle for the blood! Are you watching?! Are you watching?! Are you dead?!"

"I'm watching, you… I'm not dead yet."

Jace muttered it through gritted teeth. Then he bit his lip. He had an idea.

He didn't know what he cared about, but he didn't want to lose anyone. He didn't want to lose Grede. He didn't want to lose Amy. He didn't want to lose his teacher. He didn't want to lose Vereesa. He didn't want to lose… anyone here.

He'd never been a smart man. He'd always been the type to act on impulse, a hotheaded fool.

Maybe a world like this wasn't meant for fools like him?

"Luhrawenkta, Lu'alakipta…"
