## Chapter 429: Black Destruction!

Blighted Land…

Endless shadow — shadow from the Void, shadow from the Dark Portal — all of it drawn here as if called, converging from every direction.

Jace reached out his hand. He watched his skin darken bit by bit.

His vision snapped into clarity. Jace felt as if he'd regained boundless energy — but when his senses returned, what followed was nearly unbearable agony. His skin felt like it was being stung by swarms of venomous insects, his innards churned as if something was grinding them to paste…

But his reason told him: the mana was still far from enough.

He needed to convert more.

"Luhrawenkta, Lu'alakipta…" Jace repeated the incantation until his hands trembled, until he could hear nearly every soldier's shout and wail on the battlefield, every demon's hiss, every order being given.

"Come on, demons. Come on, demons. Let me see how much fight you've left, you goddamn—"

"We are Azeroth's last line of defense, soldiers! Stop that demon! Don't let him bring down the mage tower!"

"Don't let him destroy the arcane crystal!"

"General Zygoni, we—"

"Lorima! Hold the demon back! I'm going to kill that—"

"Kill that demon! Kill that demon!"

"You've lost, mongrels! You've lost, mongrels! You've lost, lost, mongrels…"

Gradually, Jace couldn't make out any of those sounds anymore. They were all drowned out by the howling of shadow.

It was as if the world had been emptied, leaving only him.

And, of course, the demon before him.

Because he had to kill him.

This far, and no further.

"Savia…"

Jace raised Azure Song and the Blood Demon's staff high. The glinting shadow gem and the blue dragonblade came together. He felt as if his motion was pulling at shadow power potent enough to destroy him outright.

"Avarus…"

He forced the incantation out. Many of the soldiers, rangers, and priests nearby turned to look, watching serpents of shadow slither from his body and merge with the purple phantoms roaming the sky.

The sky was already obscured by wavering shadow-stars. Even the Eredar warlocks in the distance stared over in a daze.

"Antia, Minuerlle…"

Jace clenched his teeth and chanted. He had no energy left to spare on anything around him.

He couldn't even tell if his casting was succeeding. He could only focus on completing the spell step by step, the way he'd practiced — no time to check whether the shadow serpents were gathering, let alone worry about soul shards…

In fact, he didn't need soul shards at all.

Too many souls drifted across this battlefield, unwilling to depart. Elven souls, dwarven souls, human souls…

After several Life Taps, he could almost see countless souls jostling toward him the moment they sensed his casting, shouting at him.

"Kill him!"

"Kill… that demon…"

"Take my soul… If I leave here it's all for nothing. I'm a soldier — I can't leave the battlefield!"

"My soul will help you kill him… You understand me, don't you? You understand, right?"

"I shouldn't have died… Curse that demon! Let me help you kill him!"

"Hurry up, you—"

"Are you even alive?! You want that demon to march straight into Stormwind?"

"No problem." Jace answered them, his eyes locked on that swaying, enormous, obese demonic body, murmuring under his breath.

"No problem… die, die, die, demon…"

Countless meteors spiraled upward, converging into a shadow-star that let out an eerie, wailing howl. Several fel bats that didn't escape in time were caught in the shadow-star and vanished without a sound. Soldiers and demons on the ground alike looked up at the bizarre magic.

"Jace…"

Vereesa turned back at that moment, stunned nearly senseless.

"Die…" Jace clenched his teeth, still unable to keep from vomiting a mouthful of black, purulent blood — but his gaze never left the demon.

"Die, you son of a bitch!"

He swung the staff with all its might. The wrappings on the staff burned away utterly by the terrible shadow power, blazing with abyssal purple light.

Jace put so nearly all his strength into it that he almost toppled himself. The shadow-star, driven by his force… howled as it hurtled toward the pit lord. The demon, locked in combat with the soldiers, only noticed the strange object suddenly flying in from above.

Azgus's eyes slid toward it — and he couldn't help but open that abyssal maw dripping with green lava.

The soldiers beneath his feet scattered in every direction. The demon tried to raise his fel blade to block — but it was too late.

The shadow-star struck his squarely on the arm. Purple demonic fire swallowed his entire body in an instant. A violent explosion billowed outward, and the streaking shadow-flame shook the earth…

Even the arcane crystal spinning atop the mage tower dimmed in that thunderous burst of shadow, struggling to reignite only after a long moment.

Jace's legs gave way beneath him and he sat down on the ground. Then he laughed — as purely happy as when he'd watched the fireworks he'd set off as a kid.

But the smile faded from his face almost immediately.

As the black fire dissipated, the pit lord wasn't dead yet. He was still laughing. Though he'd lost an arm and a chunk of his chest, though shadow had torn him open with hideous gashes, that enormous fel heart lay exposed outside his body — and it still beat without cease.

Those thick vessels kept spraying fel lava-like blood dozens of feet into the air, reaching dozens of yards away.

Jace suddenly remembered what happened when a pit lord died.

"Jace… you…"

"Vereesa, tell them to run… tell everyone… to run! Now!"

Nearly every demon on the slope had abandoned any will to fight and was fleeing in a frenzy. Vereesa understood what Jace meant.

"Now!" Jace grabbed Vereesa's collar and shoved her forward with force, roaring with his last strength: "Tell everyone — run!"

Vereesa nodded and shouted with all her might into the distance — but Jace could no longer hear her voice.

Countless soldiers sprinted with all their might away from the demon. Jace felt like he was done for. The demon kept staring at him, laughing without stop. He tried to turn and run, but his legs wouldn't move…

A Stormwind knight came galloping from the distance, bent down, and hauled Jace's broken body up onto his horse's back. Not long after, as a flash of white light suddenly blazed forth and swallowed everything, Jace blinked — and finally couldn't hold on anymore.
