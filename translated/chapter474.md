## Chapter 474: Preparing for the Scavenging Run

That evening Jace came to the Slaughtered Lamb, and just as Jarel had said, Erlan and Surena were in the main hall drinking and laughing about something. A thick, indescribable rich fragrance filled the entire hall, mingling with the scent of expensive wine.

Then he noticed the figure sitting beside Surena — that incubus.

Erlan glanced over, then his eyes widened. "Jace? Is that you? You're really alive. I never quite believed what those guys kept telling me until I saw you with my own eyes. I'm not drunk, am I? Ever since I heard about you... damn, I really wanted to see you again."

"What were you doing in Booty Bay? We've been waiting for you to get back so we could set out again. In a while, those demon corpses are really going to be carved up by every force under the sun — the Kirin Tor, mages from other kingdoms, orcs, gnolls... even the Venture Company, can you believe it? I don't think the Black Morass has been this lively in tens of thousands of years..."

"Sorry about the delay," Jace said.

Erlan might have been fine, but Surena was clearly a little drunk. She was hugging the incubus. "Jace, you're back! Come meet my new partner, Gakres."

"Hello, I am Gakres, Mr. Jace Sesso." The incubus set down his glass and stared fixedly at Jace — stared enough to make Jace uncomfortable. All he could see was the fire in his eyes flickering with his words.

Then, right before Jace's eyes, the lower half of his waist slowly rose, propped up by the thing beneath to an unimaginable height.

What the hell was this guy trying to do?

Jace immediately understood why Ursula looked at this incubus with such distaste. When this guy got going, he had no shame whatsoever — far more glaring than a female succubus.

"Clearly, he likes you, Jace," Surena said with a smile. "I told you already, Gakres — our Jace is quite popular with demons."

"Mr. Jace Sesso's body truly possesses a unique charm that's hard to resist, my master. I was captivated right away," Gakres said, then gently kissed Surena's cheek, drawing a light laugh from her.

"I say this, master — you're not upset, are you?"

Surena's smile faded slightly. "You think I don't know your kind, succubus?"

Jace said, "Tell your good friend not to touch me one bit, Surena. Otherwise, I won't even need to lift a finger — Morophil will cut that thing of his off at once."

"Who's Morophil?" Gakres raised his chin, curious. "Is she Mr. Sesso's companion?"

Erlan laughed. "This guy hasn't met your Miss Morophil yet. Don't blame him, Jace."

"Let's talk about the scavenging, Erlan," Jace said. "You sure you're not drunk?"

Erlan slapped the table. "Of course not. We've been waiting for you to get back these past few days. We're going into the Black Morass with a scavenging party from Lakeshire, Jace. Rest easy — the leader is a friend of mine, and after this last trip, Surena and I have gotten pretty close to them. All reliable people."

"They can handle demon companions?" Jace asked.

"Of course," Erlan said. "There's a dwarf warlock in there too, and he's got an invisible imp. I heard from Zardeth — you'll go by Yurk, right?"

"Right," Jace said.

Erlan rubbed Surena's ear. "Remember, Surena — don't let it slip and expose Jace's identity when the time comes."

"You've reminded me several times already," Surena said impatiently.

"So how much will I get paid?" Jace asked.

"At least ten gold for this run, Jace," Erlan said. "The day before yesterday I already talked it over with my friend at the Pig and Whistle. I told them you're a master at killing succubi — kills them like flies — and the succubus you can summon kills other succubi even more easily! So you won't need to do any other dirty work. You just need to keep an eye out for any demons that might show up for us."

That incubus Gakres, hearing Erlan say this, kept staring at Jace with a cold expression, as if he took issue with the description.

But Jace didn't care about this guy one bit. He only cared about that ten gold. Once it was in his hands, that'd be another big step toward buying a house.

"It really is a lot," Jace said.

Erlan patted his belly. "I'll tell you, the leader makes even more. But he knows where the battles took place in the Black Morass — meaning, where there are dead demons... Without his intel, we'd go in and find nothing."

"Shame we can't keep making money like this forever," Surena said with a light sigh.

"Half a year of this wouldn't be bad either," Erlan said. "A few runs like this and we'd be comfortable for years after. Isn't that worth it?"

"What do you think, Jace?"

"Not bad at all," Jace said. "So, when's the next departure?"

Erlan said excitedly, "Then I'll head to Old Town tomorrow morning to find that guy and tell him our muscle is ready and we can prepare to set out. How about we head to Lakeshire tomorrow afternoon and wait for them there? While we're at it, we can try the food there again. Remember that raptor meat, Surena? Expensive, sure, but damn it was good."

"Remember to book a bigger room at the inn this time, Erlan — Jace is with us now," Surena reminded him with a smile.

"Uh, I won't be sharing a room with you two," Jace said immediately.

"Huh?" Erlan said. "You and Miss Morophil aren't going to join in the fun with us?"

"No," Jace said flatly.

Gakres smiled slightly. "Mr. Sesso really is quite special."

"Maybe you two are the special ones?" Jace said. "I'll get my own room."

Erlan lay back across Surena's lap. "What a shame..."

"Enjoy yourselves. I'm going to find Ursula and ask about potions."

With that, Jace walked into the side passage. He couldn't stand the sight of that incubus for one more second.

When he arrived at the alchemy lab, Ursula was busy brewing. Jace could catch a whiff of it from the smell — Shadow Protection Potion.

"Ursula, is this the medicine you're preparing for Erlan and the others for the Black Morass?"

"Oh, Jace, you're back," Ursula glanced over. "Yes, they brought back a lot of herbs this time. I'm helping them whip up some healing potions and Shadow Protection Potions."

As she spoke, she pulled out a small box. "And of course, mana potions for you."

"Thanks," Jace took the small box and asked, "Can you make Blessed Wizard Oil?"

"Blessed Wizard Oil?!" Ursula shook her head. "No, can't make that here at least, Jace. What are you planning to use that for?"

Jace said, "A goblin in Booty Bay told me a way to find Essence of Undeath — using some device of theirs to extract Essence of Undeath from ghosts. I was thinking of preparing something to deal with those spirits after I get back from the Black Morass."

"Just go ask the church for holy water," Ursula said. "That stuff might not hit as hard as Blessed Wizard Oil, but against ordinary ghosts..."

"Ah... right." Jace slapped his forehead. "I'll go ask around later, Ursula. You really jogged my memory."
