## Chapter 475: Curse of Recklessness

After arranging with Erland and Sulenna to meet the next afternoon at Erland's home in Goldshire, Jace left The Slaughtered Lamb in the evening and returned to his old rental to gather the remaining things and collect his two demons.

Arriving at the new place, Morophil entered and carefully sniffed around. "This place doesn't have your scent. It's really hard to get used to."

"What scent? Sweat?" Jace shot her a look. "It's more spacious than before, isn't it, Morophil? The two of you can live a little more comfortably."

"I have no requirements for space, master," Morophil said.

"This room costs a fortune in rent. Say something nice, for fuck's sake," Jace complained.

"What higher praise is there than your love?" the succubus narrowed her eyes. "Anything I say is redundant, dear."

Jace cleared his throat, a little awkwardly. "All right, that's enough. I do like it. I do have requirements for space, after all."

He sat down on the bed and lay back. "It's been a long time since I've been able to stretch out like this. After that weird growth spurt, my feet kept hitting the wall on the old bed, knees bent the whole time. Miserable as hell."

He stretched out an arm. "Sazeno, bring me that Curse of Recklessness. I didn't practice a single incantation this whole trip to Booty Bay. Feels like I wasted all that time."

"Here, here, master." Sazeno hopped onto the bed and spread Senluola's transcription of Gajin's manual — the page on Curse of Recklessness — across Jace's chest. Jace picked it up and glanced at it, murmuring the incantation.

Uruul, Gaatuo, Yaadus.

"Your Eredar is getting more and more like a real Eredar, master!" the imp said happily.

"So you fooled around for several days and only fixed this one incantation?" Jace asked.

Sazeno lowered his voice. "It's short, but... but... it was a lot of work to find."

Jace flicked the imp hard on the head, sending him flying off the bed. He muttered, "Slacking off on me."

"Master!" Sazeno climbed back onto the bed. "I fixed it really carefully!"

Jace glanced at the succubus. "I thought you knew the Curse of Recklessness spell, Morophil?"

"My Curse of Recklessness is channeled using our kind's incantation, master. Only succubus can channel it that way — just as only Nathrezim can transform into black bats and fly away..." The succubus lay down beside Jace. "I don't deny that some human mages can also turn into bats, but the way they do it and the way Nathrezim turn into bats are definitely not the same thing."

"I more or less get it," Jace said. "Then I'll try this incantation. Curse of Recklessness... shouldn't be too destructive, right? I'll test it on you two."

"Test it on me, master." Morophil crawled onto Jace's chest and licked her lips. "Please!"

Jace looked at her already-blazing demonic eyes. It seemed she was already getting reckless without any spell.

"Forget it. I'd like to live a couple more years." Jace turned his head to look at the imp, who was preparing to watch the show. "I'll test it on you."

Sazeno froze, pointing at himself. "Me? Why..."

"This is for slacking off..."

Jace glanced at the incantation and quickly recited: "Uruul, Gaatuo, Yaadus."

As he channeled the incantation, he felt a surge of heat rush to his head. He quickly guided the power to wrap around the Sazeno in front of him, making a gesture similar to Curse of Weakness.

Then, right before his eyes, the little flame on top of Sazeno's head suddenly burst open. The imp let out a yelp and said, "Why are you testing it on me?! What can I do?! What can I do?!"

"Master wants to punish me?! Why this kind of punishment?!"

The imp screamed and jumped off the bed, running around the room. Demonic fire blazed off his body, leaving a trail of sparks with every footprint. Jace watched a spark ignite the edge of the bedsheet and, startled, immediately pinched the flame out with his fingers. "Grab it, Morophil!"

Morophil flicked her tail and, in one motion, scooped up the fire-wreathed, room-storming imp with her tail. The flames scorched her tail, making her wince in pain, but Sazeno seemed far from finished, roaring, "Don't stop me! Don't stop me! I'll destroy every fool in master's way! I'll destroy this pathetic world that's trapped master! I'll punish everyone in this world."

Jace watched it howling, hands gathering two balls of fire. What was it trying to do — set the whole building on fire?!

The first day he'd moved in!

Right across from the Stormwind Stockade!

"Lidstawening!"

Jace acted without hesitation, hitting Sazeno with a Counterspell. The imp let out a loud cry, tilted its head, and passed out. The flames on its head and in its hands were extinguished at once, leaving it dangling in midair from the succubus's tail like a dead yellow rat.

Morophil flicked her tail and tossed it onto the table, rubbing the tip of her tail gently. "This little thing's got quite the firepower."

"Almost burned the house down," Jace sighed with relief.

"If you need it to help in a fight, give it a Curse of Recklessness, master," Morophil said, leaning against the wall. "Make it a bit stronger — it'll be just the help we need!"

"Then it'd draw way too much attention," Jace said. "This guy's too fragile. He'll get killed in no time. Better to keep him calm and low-key, waiting for the right moment to set enemies on fire, or give us a Blood Pact to save our lives. Frontline combat isn't his role."

"Master cares about this little thing quite a bit," Morophil said, displeased. "I'm getting jealous."

"You too, Morophil," Jace said.

"I could use a Curse of Recklessness too." The succubus put on a shy expression and smiled. "I'm not that fragile..."

"Better be careful anyway," Jace said. "But I'm thinking — wouldn't casting Curse of Recklessness on an enemy just make them more dangerous?"

Morophil said, "Making your enemy fly into a rage isn't a bad approach either. Especially for mages — the more irritable they get, the more it disrupts their plans, makes them impulsive, restless... and harder to cast effectively. It's the same for mages as it is for warlocks. Don't forget, master — how important emotions are to casting."

"What about warriors?" Jace asked.

Morophil said, "Even for warriors — keeping them from facing their opponents calmly makes it easier for their foes to find openings. And I happen to be very good at finding openings."
