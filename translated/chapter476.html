Chapter 476: The Pleasure-Demon

The next day, Jace spent the morning getting familiar with the area around his new rented room, grabbed a midday meal, and headed to Goldshire in the afternoon with his two demons in tow.

He cast Sense Demons and swept it across the whole town, picking up the faintest trace at the top floor of a two-story building on a street corner. Probably too far away to be sure, but according to the address Erlan Drudgemoor had given him, that was his place — which meant that faint trace must be his imp.

Late-autumn Goldshire was crisp with cool wind. Jace looked up at the deep blue cloudless sky — the last traces of the tearing force that had surged through the Dark Portal had vanished completely. The whole forest had returned to its old quiet peace; the stench of rampaging fel energy and the Void's fluctuations were nowhere to be found.

The streets were crowded with people too — far livelier than when he'd first come back from the Dark Portal.

Following the agreed address, Jace walked up to the small building and knocked on the door. Drudgemoor opened it, glanced down the hallway, and said, "Where are your demons?"

"Right here," Jace said, stepping inside.

Drudgemoor shut the door. "No need to hide yourselves at my place at all…"

"Come on out, Morophil." Jace sat down in a chair beside him and caught his breath — the walk from Stormwind to here was no joke.

The succubus materialized from behind Jace, and the imp poked its head out of his pocket. Erlan stood up, facing the succubus. "Every time I see Miss Morophil, my motivation to learn the succubus-summoning incantation gets another boost… When I summon my own succubus, Jace, you've got to be there in person. Maybe I can borrow your mana and attract one nearly as powerful as Miss Morophil!"

Morophil gave him a flat look. Jace chuckled. "Using me as your engagement ring?"

"You saw how Surena's succubus looked at you too, Jace." Erlan grabbed the arm of Jace's chair. "Gakres was practically bewitched."

"Gakres?" Morophil glanced sidelong at Erlan. "What the hell is that?"

Erlan cleared his throat. "Surena will be here soon enough. You'll find out then, Miss Morophil."

Jace got up to pour himself a cup of flower tea. Footsteps sounded outside the door.

"Sorry I'm late, you two." Surena pushed the door open. "How are the preparations coming?"

Jace took a sip of tea. "I'm ready to go whenever."

"Hold on a moment — let me count the packages we're bringing." Erlan's voice came from the room at the end of the hallway. "You all have a seat and wait a bit…"

At that moment, Gakres appeared from behind Surena. The moment he materialized, he fixed his gaze on Morophil — with none of the fear or nervousness other succubi showed around her.

"This is Morophil," Surena said. "We're all going to be friends from now on, Gakres."

"Lady Morophil." Gakres smiled warmly. "Both you and your master are utterly captivating."

"I'm well aware," Morophil smiled.

"Not going to compliment me, Morophil?" Surena leaned over eagerly.

Morophil said, "Oh, Lady Surena Caledon, you must be quite something yourself, to have attracted a pleasure-demon all the way out here."

Hearing that, Jace glanced at the incubus.

So he was an unusually high-ranking succubus too? Clearly.

A pleasure-demon — Jace wasn't quite sure what sort of demon that was in the Twisting Nether — but if Surena had summoned him… well, it wasn't exactly surprising.

Jace recalled the incubus he'd chased through Old Town, the one Gakin had summoned. That one had been built like a tank; this one was leaner and taller — but in presence, he was every bit the equal.

Gakres's twin horns curled high, and even folded tight against his back, his wings billowed like a cloak — never mind what they'd look like spread wide.

But why would a succubus of this caliber have been subdued by Surena without any trouble at all?

Neither Erlan nor Ursula had mentioned Surena running into any danger while summoning him. Could it really have been the felhound Basadim's doing?

Then again, Morophil hadn't put up much of a fight when she'd first appeared before Jace either. Probably only the lower-ranking succubi instinctively resisted their summoners.

It was the only explanation he could come up with.

Gakres seemed to sense Jace sizing him up. He spoke softly. "Mr. Jace Sesso, have you changed your opinion of me today?"

"Stay away from my master," Morophil said evenly.

"I believe that's for your master to decide, not you," Gakres said.

"I already gave you my answer," Jace said impatiently. "Gakres, stop pushing."

"You'll change your mind, Mr. Sesso." Gakres gave him that captivating yet unsettling smile again, then looked at Surena beside him. "It's your wish too, isn't it, mistress?"

"My wish?" Surena frowned. "What are you talking about, Gakres?"

"Having Mr. Sesso and Lady Morophil join our little gathering." Gakres's eyes, glinting with arcane light, flicked between Jace and Morophil.

Jace didn't feel him attempting to seduce — but the danger in that gaze was no less than a psychic disruption spell.

Not to mention the fact that his lower half had been standing at full attention the whole time, like some kind of deliberate provocation.

"He doesn't seem to listen to you much, Surena," Jace warned. "Hasn't he even told you his true name?"

"Of course I know his true name," Surena said. "But you've never forced Lady Morophil to obey by speaking her true name either, have you? Zardeth said the relationship between a master and their demon servant works best when there's some harmony…"

"Don't cause trouble, succubus," Morophil cut in coldly.

"Miss Morophil, what's going on?" Erlan came into the main room carrying packages, only to find an incubus and a succubus facing off. Morophil's expression was icy, while Gakres still wore that bewitching smile.

"Gakres, I never said you had to — damn it…" Surena grabbed Gakres by the arm. "Listen to me for once, and stop doing whatever you please!"

"Can't you feel the powerful desire your master is suppressing deep inside, Lady Morophil?" Gakres said. "He's constantly holding back that fierce urge to break free of its chains and let loose — and you, his servant, not only fail to help him, you're either oblivious or deliberately ignoring it. Right now, in this whole room, you're the only succubus standing against me. Isn't that hilarious? Were your wings broken because you defied your master's will?"

Jace had been about to tell Morophil not to start a fight here, but after that little speech, he felt his own temper rise.

Between him and Surena, who was the master here, exactly?

If Jace was only mildly annoyed, the Soul Link had already told him that Morophil beside him was practically seething.
