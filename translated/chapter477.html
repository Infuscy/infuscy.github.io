Chapter 477: Completing the Summoning Ritual

Morophil glanced at Jace. He said nothing, so she spoke. "It seems you haven't fully submitted to your master yet, Gakres. I suppose the final step of this summoning ritual will have to fall to me — the taming."

"Taming?" A barbed scepter had somehow appeared in Gakres's hand. He spoke coldly. "Certainly not by you. You probably don't even know what you're dealing with, huntress — my former master was Matron Delika. Did you think I was just some—"

"Never heard of her."

The words had barely left Morophil's mouth before she vanished in a flash of light. The pleasure-demon's smile disappeared instantly, his skin shifting from pale pink to furious red in an instant.

The situation shifted in the blink of an eye. Jace had just gripped the sword at his hip when Gakres suddenly beat his wings and swept a vast cloud of pink mist across the entire room!

Sazeno shrieked and dove into the bag. Erlan quickly pulled the scarf from his neck up over his nose and retreated into the hallway. But Surena, standing closest, took the mist full in the face — her eyes rolled back as if she'd fainted, and she began muttering incoherently.

Jace instinctively covered his mouth and nose, but his head still swam. He barely managed to cling to consciousness — then the Flowing Ice Crystal in the bag at his waist began to release its power. Feeling the pure arcane energy flowing from it, Jace finally felt a little relief.

Through the pink haze, he watched a shadow suddenly pierce the mist and lunge toward Gakres. A low snarl, the crash of furniture being smashed to pieces — and then the room fell silent.

He pulled the Flowing Ice Crystal from the bag. Its crystalline glow began to slowly drive back the pink mist, and before long the room returned to normal.

Before him, Morophil sat on the table swinging her hooves. Gakres had been run clean through the neck by the black Tothrezim blade and pinned firmly to the wall, his whole body convulsing, able only to let out muffled whines as dark purple demon blood poured from his mouth.

Morophil glanced at the limp, dead-serpent-like thing hanging below Gakres, then looked at Jace. "Want me to chop this off, master? At least then he'd behave himself for a while."

"Hold on — he's Surena's servant," Jace said.

Erlan coughed and came out from behind the door, slapping Surena's face. "Hey, Surena Caledon! What the hell is going on?!"

"Ah… ah… uh……"

After several slaps, Surena snapped back to her senses, gasping for air.

"I'm sorry, Jace… I……"

Then she noticed Gakres, pinned to the wall, his hands and legs trembling.

"He attacked me?! He actually… attacked his own master?!"

"He was aiming for me — you just got caught in the crossfire. On the whole, at least he didn't seduce you. So he's not completely beyond saving, Lady Surena Caledon." Morophil smiled. "I think he's just a bit too full of himself. He needs a lesson. Want me to cut that thing off so he can think more clearly?"

"No… don't." Surena said immediately.

"It'll grow back," Morophil said. "He's a succubus, not Erlan Drudgemoor."

Erlan winced and instinctively covered his crotch. "Don't use me as the example."

"Don't do it, Lady Morophil," Surena said. "Give me a chance. I'll control him with his true name. I've really learned my lesson this time."

Morophil looked to Jace. He nodded. Only then did she pull the black blade, slick with purple succubus blood, free.

Gakres collapsed to the ground, his wide blank eyes staring at nothing, clutching at the pierced wound in his neck as he convulsed.

Morophil said, "Don't ever let me see you raise that thing in front of me or my master again. I can cut it off faster than my master can speak my true name — and my name is quite long, Gakres."

Gakres nodded vigorously, spat out a mouthful of pus-blood, and rasped, "I understand… my lady."

It took Erlan a good while to set the house back in order. Once he'd confirmed the commotion hadn't drawn the attention of the town watch or the neighbors, the three of them — with their demons in tow — boarded the carriage Erlan had prepared and set off from Goldshire toward Lakeshire in the evening.

Despite having his neck run through, Gakres clearly still had the strength to keep himself hidden. Through Sense Demons, Jace could see his distinctly weakened shadow huddled motionless in a corner of the carriage.

"I'm really sorry, Jace," Surena said sheepishly. "This really wasn't my intention. He's made decisions on my behalf before, but I figured — he's a demon, and when we're out hunting demon corpses, he ought to know more than I do. So I kind of got used to him taking charge…"

"Don't let the demon end up controlling you, Surena." Jace sighed. "Then again, it's your first time dealing with a succubus. I lost myself for a while too when I first summoned Morophil, so I can hardly blame you. We can only take it one step at a time."

"Was Miss Morophil that disobedient at the beginning too?" Erlan, driving up front, looked back with curiosity.

"I wouldn't call it disobedient," Jace said. "That was my own problem."

Erlan stared at the complicated expression on Jace's face and didn't know what to say for a moment. After a while, he spoke up. "Even I'm getting a little worried, honestly. After all, I'm the only one left who hasn't summoned a succubus."

He sniffed his fingers after running them through his hair, then looked down. "Still reeks even now. When we get to Eastvale, I need to wash off in the river. It's unbearable."

"There's also Jalane Ayrole," Jace said.

"Ah, right — but that woman's clearly talented," Erlan said. "I reckon she could summon a succubus or a felhound in no time if she wanted. Zardeth just doesn't want her rushing into it."

"When you all went to the Black Morass before, didn't you notice anything off about Gakres?" Jace asked.

"That dwarf warlock thought Gakres was impressive, and he envied me for it," Surena said. "So I was thrilled to have summoned such a companion… And he genuinely was a lot of help along the way — and I don't just mean in that department… So I let my guard down, Jace."

"That's succubi for you — competitive! Jealous!" Sazeno suddenly poked his head out of the bag. "He definitely saw Morophil as a threat and wanted to show off! Ha! Who knew this vicious, savage bitch of a succubus was even more competitive than he was!"

Jace sensed the demon-shadow beside him bristling with irritation. "Get back in the bag and stay there," he said flatly.

"There's nobody around, master!" The imp glanced about. "But I'll obey, anyway — after all, there's already one poor bastard on this carriage who got beaten black and blue for disobeying!"

Surena covered her face. "What was I thinking!"

Around midnight, nearing Eastvale, Erlan didn't drive the carriage all the way to the logging camp. The three of them got off by the road and walked along the river for a while, washing their clothes in a calm stretch of the stream and giving themselves a quick rinse.

Soaked in the ice-cold late-autumn river water, none of them were in the mood for conversation. Jace watched Morophil, who was staring straight at him from the riverbank, and Gakres in the distance, huddled beneath a tree. At least the problem had been dealt with ahead of time, he thought. If this had come to light in a real crisis, someone could've actually died.
