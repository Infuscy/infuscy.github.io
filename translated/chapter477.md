## Chapter 477: Completing the Summoning Ritual

Morfell glanced at Jace. He said nothing, so she spoke. "It seems you haven't fully submitted to your master yet, Gaxulis. I suppose the final step of this summoning ritual will have to fall to me — the taming."

"Taming?" A barbed scepter had somehow appeared in Gaxulis's hand. He spoke coldly. "Certainly not by you. You probably don't even know what you're dealing with — I'm a banshee huntress, and my former master was Matron Delika. Did you think I was just some—"

"Never heard of her."

The words had barely left Morfell's mouth before she vanished in a flash of light. The pleasure-demon's smile disappeared instantly, his skin shifting from pale pink to furious red in an instant.

The situation shifted in the blink of an eye. Jace had just gripped the sword at his waist when Gaxulis suddenly beat his wings and swept a vast cloud of pink mist across the entire room!

Sazeno shrieked and dove into the bag. Erland quickly pulled the scarf from his neck up over his nose and retreated into the hallway. But Sulenna, standing closest, took the mist full in the face — her eyes rolled back as if she'd fainted, and she began muttering incoherently.

Jace instinctively covered his mouth and nose, but his head still swam. He barely managed to cling to consciousness — then the Frostflow Crystal in the bag at his waist began to release its power. Feeling the pure arcane energy flowing from it, Jace finally felt a little relief.

Through the pink haze, he watched a shadow suddenly pierce the mist and lunge toward Gaxulis. A low snarl, the crash of furniture being smashed to pieces — and then the room fell silent.

He pulled the Frostflow Crystal from the bag. Its crystalline glow began to slowly drive back the pink mist, and before long the room returned to normal.

Before him, Morfell sat on the table swinging her hooves. Gaxulis had been run clean through the neck by the black Thorzrezim blade and pinned firmly to the wall, his whole body convulsing, able only to let out muffled whines as dark purple demon blood poured from his mouth.

Morfell glanced at the limp, dead-serpent-like thing hanging below Gaxulis, then looked at Jace. "Want me to chop this off, master? At least then he'd behave himself for a while."

"Hold on — he's Sulenna's servant," Jace said.

Erland coughed and came out from behind the door, slapping Sulenna's face. "Hey, Sulenna Kayldon! What the hell is going on?!"

"Ah… ah… uh……"

After several slaps, Sulenna snapped back to her senses, gasping for air.

"I'm sorry, Jace… I……"

Then she noticed Gaxulis, pinned to the wall, his hands and legs trembling.

"He attacked me?! He actually… attacked his own master?!"

"He was aiming for me — you just got caught in the crossfire. On the whole, at least he didn't seduce you. So he's not completely beyond saving, Lady Sulenna Kayldon." Morfell smiled. "I think he's just a bit too full of himself. He needs a lesson. Want me to cut that thing off so he can think more clearly?"

"No… don't." Sulenna said immediately.

"It'll grow back," Morfell said. "He's a succubus, not Erland Dedemore."

Erland winced and instinctively covered his crotch. "Don't use me as the example."

"Don't do it, Lady Morfell," Sulenna said. "Give me a chance. I'll control him with his true name. I've really learned my lesson this time."

Morfell looked to Jace. He nodded. Only then did she pull the black blade, slick with purple succubus blood, free.

Gaxulis collapsed to the ground, his wide blank eyes staring at nothing, clutching at the pierced wound in his neck as he convulsed.

Morfell said, "Don't ever let me see you raise that thing in front of me or my master again. I can cut it off faster than my master can speak my true name — and my name is quite long, Gaxulis."

Gaxulis nodded vigorously, spat out a mouthful of pus-blood, and rasped, "I understand… my lady."

It took Erland a good while to set the house back in order. Once he'd confirmed the commotion hadn't drawn the attention of the town watch or the neighbors, the three of them — with their demons in tow — boarded the carriage Erland had prepared and set off from Goldshire toward Lakeshire in the evening.

Despite having his neck run through, Gaxulis clearly still had the strength to keep himself hidden. Through Detect Demon, Jace could see his distinctly weakened shadow huddled motionless in a corner of the carriage.

"I'm really sorry, Jace," Sulenna said sheepishly. "This really wasn't my intention. He's made decisions on my behalf before, but I figured — he's a demon, and when we're out hunting demon corpses, he ought to know more than I do. So I kind of got used to him taking charge…"

"Don't let the demon end up controlling you, Sulenna." Jace sighed. "Then again, it's your first time dealing with a succubus. I lost myself for a while too when I first summoned Morfell, so I can hardly blame you. We can only take it one step at a time."

"Was Miss Morfell that disobedient at the beginning too?" Erland, driving up front, looked back with curiosity.

"I wouldn't call it disobedient," Jace said. "That was my own problem."

Erland stared at the complicated expression on Jace's face and didn't know what to say for a moment. After a while, he spoke up. "Even I'm getting a little worried, honestly. After all, I'm the only one left who hasn't summoned a succubus."

He sniffed his fingers after running them through his hair, then looked down. "Still reeks even now. When we get to Eastvale, I need to wash off in the river. It's unbearable."

"There's also Kayleen Eiro," Jace said.

"Ah, right — but that woman's clearly talented," Erland said. "I reckon she could summon a succubus or a hellhound in no time if she wanted. Galders just doesn't want her rushing into it."

"When you all went to the Black Swamp before, didn't you notice anything off about Gaxulis?" Jace asked.

"That dwarf warlock thought Gaxulis was impressive, and he envied me for it," Sulenna said. "So I was thrilled to have summoned such a companion… And he genuinely was a lot of help along the way — and I don't just mean in that department… So I let my guard down, Jace."

"That's succubi for you — competitive! Jealous!" Sazeno suddenly poked his head out of the bag. "He definitely saw Morfell as a threat and wanted to show off! Ha! Who knew this vicious, savage bitch of a succubus was even more competitive than he was!"

Jace sensed the demon-shadow beside him bristling with irritation. "Get back in the bag and stay there," he said flatly.

"There's nobody around, master!" The imp glanced about. "But I'll obey, anyway — after all, there's already one poor bastard on this carriage who got beaten black and blue for disobeying!"

Sulenna covered her face. "What was I thinking!"

Around midnight, nearing Eastvale, Erland didn't drive the carriage all the way to the logging camp. The three of them got off by the road and walked along the river for a while, washing their clothes in a calm stretch of the stream and giving themselves a quick rinse.

Soaked in the ice-cold late-autumn river water, none of them were in the mood for conversation. Jace watched Morfell, who was staring straight at him from the riverbank, and Gaxulis in the distance, huddled beneath a tree. At least the problem had been dealt with ahead of time, he thought. If this had come to light in a real crisis, someone could've actually died.
