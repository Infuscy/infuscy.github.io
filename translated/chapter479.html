Chapter 479: The Leader

That evening, Jace drank and ate his fill of meat along with the other five, then headed upstairs toward his room, still turning over the strange priest in his mind.

By all rights, a strong priest who was adept with weapons and could afford a suit of armor should have been able to join the Order of the Silver Hand and become a proper paladin.

Yet he still called himself a priest and said he was from the Monastery of the Holy Light — which meant he hadn't joined the Silver Hand.

Maybe it was precisely because he spent all his time running around with warlocks like them, dealing in demon-related business, that he couldn't pass the Silver Hand's vetting.

In the distance, Erland, Sulenna, and the female dwarf Volis walked toward the end of the hallway laughing and clinging to each other, while Gaxulis had even materialized and was following behind them in plain sight.

After a night's rest, that fellow seemed to have recovered considerably from his injuries — he was back to his lively, vigorous self.

Probably had something to do with Sulenna. Last night when they'd rested at Eastvale, they'd apparently gone at it again in the tent — most likely "replenishing his mana" again at that time.

Arriving in his room, Morfell emerged from the shadows and plopped herself down on the bed.

Jace asked curiously, "Why doesn't Gaxulis get jealous that Sulenna sleeps with other men?"

"That's just how pleasure-demons are…" Morfell narrowed her eyes, displeased. "Don't tell me my master wants a servant like that?"

"What would I want with a monster like that," Jace said.

Morfell giggled. "I don't mind you seeking your pleasures, master. I just don't want you cheated by other succubi. That Gaxulis has clearly set his sights on your shadow power and wants to drain strength from you. I will never allow it."

"But…" She leaned in with curiosity. "Is it because you're afraid I'd be upset that you don't join their gatherings?"

Jace said, "I can't stomach being mixed in with men like that, and I'm not feeding Sulenna Kayldon some made-up excuse either. And most importantly — same as you — I can't trust other succubi."

"Hmm… trust." Morfell said. "Does this mean I've finally earned my master's trust? What an arduous journey it's been."

"You could say that." Jace lay down on the bed. "Long day. We've been on the road since morning. Let's sleep early, Morfell — and I've had quite a bit to drink. I'm a little dizzy."

"No." Morfell said. "I can already hear them at it… I don't want to just lie here doing nothing while listening to that all night, master!"

"So you want to join them?" Jace raised an eyebrow.

"Absolutely not." The succubus smiled and leaned against Jace. "I belong to my master, and no one else…"

"That's a pretty speech," Jace said. "We definitely won't reach the Black Swamp tomorrow, based on my earlier estimate of this area…"

The succubus hooked Jace's waistband. "So my master shouldn't need to deal with any trouble tomorrow — which means he won't need his mana?"

Before Jace could even respond, the succubus quickly yanked down her master's trousers and was about to bury her head between his legs. Jace grabbed her by the horns and hauled her head back up. "Whether I need my mana is my call."

Morfell locked eyes with Jace. Her long tongue suddenly darted out, hooked down, and flicked across him in a flash. A jolt of surprise shot through Jace's body before he could brace for it. He watched helplessly as she pulled her tongue back, rolled it around in her mouth several times, and narrowed her eyes in bliss.

"You wait just a minute…"

"Well then, my master's the one who decides. Will you need your mana tomorrow?" she asked softly.

"Probably…" Jace swallowed. "Probably not…"

The moment he let go of her horns, Morfell lowered her head and took him in whole…

"I'm done for tonight…"

The thought had barely formed in his mind when, accompanied by the sounds of ravenous swallowing, Jace felt as though half his life had been drained out of him. He nearly blacked out, and could only grip the edge of the bed tightly to reassure himself that he was at least still alive…

Early that morning, Jace sat on the Lake Everstill bridge watching the river flow, his mind completely blank.

Morfell had really been too brutal — she'd nearly drained him to the point of passing out, definitely working off some long-overdue revenge when she'd gone down on him. If not for the Frostflow Crystal in his hand cushioning him through the night, he'd truly have been wrung dry by now.

But even now, clutching that pouch and feeling the arcane energy slowly flowing into his body, it still felt like he'd never quite recover.

The Morfell beside him was as energized as a mana bomb about to go off, and even staying invisible, she insisted on pressing close to his side.

At last, he spotted two familiar silhouettes emerging from the inn — the two men he'd met yesterday, the priest and the hunter.

Jace stood up, brushed the dust off his backside, and walked over. The priest Hubouri saw him and waved. "Light be praised — at least there's one sober soul here."

"Where are the others?" Jace asked.

The hunter Dunn said, "What do you think? It's the last blowout before we set out — they're definitely planning to have a good time to the end. At least Obrik hasn't arrived yet. We'll just wait for them here!"

The three of them sat outside the inn and hadn't waited long when Dunn suddenly looked up. A man in a rather handsome pale-blue fitted coat was waving and walking toward them.

Jace noticed that his coat was actually a short robe, and coupled with the ring on his finger…

When the man came closer, Jace was surprised to notice that he was wearing the official mage's ring of the Wizard Sanctum!

He was actually a mage. From how Erland had described him, Jace had assumed this fellow was a dwarf.

Even though they both belonged to the Wizard Sanctum, Jace had never seen this man before, and he didn't seem to recognize Jace either. After greeting Dunn and Hubouri, he spotted Jace and asked, "You must be Mr. Urk?"

"That's me," Jace said.

"Erland Dedemore speaks highly of your abilities," Obrik said. "Since I'm planning to push a little further south this time, into the hills closer to Stonard, to continue our explorations."

He lowered his voice and continued. "So I need more and stronger help — more warlocks. By the way, did Erland go over the payment with you?"

Jace nodded. "He did."

"I hope you can live up to what that price demands, Mr. Urk," Obrik said.

"I'd rather we ran into nothing at all," the priest said. "Like the first time we came, before Erland and Sulenna joined."

"But we didn't find much that time either — far less than the second time, at least," Dunn said.

Obrik said, "Now more and more factions are coming to hunt for demon corpses. Which means if we don't go to the dangerous places, our haul will only get smaller."
