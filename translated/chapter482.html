Chapter 482: The Fel-Eye

Dunn looked at Morfell. "You're sure what you're smelling isn't just the stench of dead demons here?"

"The two scents are quite different, Dunn," Jace said. "Even I can tell the difference between a dead demon and a living one. What did you smell, Morfell?"

Morfell said, "I don't know what kind of demon it is, mistress. It doesn't feel very powerful — I can't make out the details."

Dunn climbed a nearby tree to see farther. Jace also used Detect Demon to scan in that direction. His detection vision couldn't reach very far; if Morfell couldn't get a clear sense of it, there was almost no chance he'd be able to see it.

"Is it not very powerful, or is it just far away?" he asked.

"I should say… extremely weak," Morfell said. "I've never sensed such a faint demonic presence. It's like…"

From his perch high up, Dunn suddenly pointed north, lowering his voice. "Look… over there…"

Jace scrambled up the tree too and looked in the direction Dunn was pointing, following his line of sight. The gloomy dark of night made it impossible to judge distance, but with Dunn's guidance it didn't take long for him to spot it — a faint, flickering light weaving between the trees.

It was a slowly advancing green pinpoint. A rough estimate put it several hundred meters away, though he couldn't be sure… after all, there was no way to know the actual size of whatever it was.

"It's a Fel-Eye!" Sazeno grabbed Jace's collar. "Get down, master, quick — don't let it spot you! It sees much farther than you do!"

"A Fel-Eye?"

Dunn heard the imp's warning and immediately slid down the tree. Jace jumped down too and ducked behind a low slope below.

An Eye of Kilrogg?

Gulkrik, the imp beside Erland, stared at Sazeno and said, "A demon's eye summoned by fel magic — a favorite trick of the watchers! I know it! I know it!"

"Shut up," Sazeno said with disdain. "What's there to be proud of, knowing something like that?"

"There are watchers here?" Morfell asked. "In a forest held by humans? Would the Burning Legion send watchers to patrol here?"

"Watchers only appear near the Legion's strongholds…" the male succubus Gaxulis said.

Erland said, "I've seen orcs summon these eyes when they held Moonbrook. Some warlocks used them to patrol the perimeter."

"Did it see us?" Sulenna asked.

"Even if it hasn't yet, it probably will soon," Jace said. "Get the people below out of there."

Erland nodded and ran to the edge of the pit, shouting, "Obrik, we've spotted demon tracks — drop what you're doing and get out of there!"

The moment his words fell, a wolf's howl echoed from deep in the forest to the northeast. This low, rumbling howl was no ordinary forest wolf — it was an orcish warg!

"Damn it, it's orcs. That eye must have noticed us." Dunn pointed south. "We go that way!"

Everyone immediately packed up and fled, but each of them had drawn their weapons and readied themselves for a fight. Even Sulenna, who usually panicked at the sight of combat, was much calmer than before — perhaps because Gaxulis's protection had emboldened her.

They retreated deeper into the forest. As they ran, Volis asked, "Why would there be a Fel-Eye summoned by orcs patrolling here? Could there be an orc settlement nearby?"

"When the Dark Portal closed, a group of orcs was stranded in the Swamp of Sorrows," Jace explained. "They call themselves the Bleeding Hollow clan. Their leader is Kilrogg Deadeye — that's why the orcs call this eye spell the Eye of Kilrogg."

"A freakish, cursed thing," Dunn cursed, glancing back as he ran — and his expression suddenly changed.

"Warg riders… warg riders!"

Jace turned to look. A ferocious black warg was weaving between the trees in pursuit from behind, and on its back stood an orc half-rising in the saddle, brandishing a cleaver overhead with savage fury.

When the orc noticed the people ahead looking at him, he roared in rage. As he and his warg charged into a clearing no longer blocked by the treetops, the dark blood-colored tattoos on his green skin were clearly visible in the moonlight! Bleeding Hollow orcs, for certain!

Though wargs couldn't run as fast as horses, they were far quicker than traveling on foot. Obrik shouted, "Dunn, put that damned beast down!"

Without waiting for the order, Dunn had already leveled his crossbow and stood his ground, taking aim. The warg charged straight at him. He pulled the trigger and the bolt whistled out, striking the warg full in the cheek!

The warg tumbled to the ground, dragging the orc on its back in a roll — yet the orc wasn't thrown clear. He roared savagely and seized the warg's mane, and when the beast scrambled back to its feet and turned toward them, the orc had somehow steadied himself in the saddle again!

Dunn was clearly caught off guard by the warg rider's tenacity. His movements grew flustered as he hurried to reload. Priest Hubouri had already stepped in front of him. Sulenna was just beginning to chant a Curse of Weakness — but a Shadow Bolt streaked past everyone's flank and slammed into the warg rider's face.

The Shadow Bolt didn't produce a violent explosion on impact, but a sinister shadow spread across the orc amid a piercing, shriek-like magical wail. The warg howled and collapsed on its side. The orc rider pitched forward onto the ground, clutching his face and roaring in agony for a while before toppling over and going still.

Everyone looked back at Jace. Jace took a few sharp breaths. This was his first time using a Shadow Bolt in real combat, and the spell was every bit as terrible as he'd imagined when he'd first encountered the incantation.

The orc was no longer moving. His upper body had been corroded by shadow beyond recognition, looking like a corpse that had been rotting for ages.

Beneath him, the warg still writhed in pain, struggling again and again to stand, only to collapse each time. Its black fur fell away in patches, revealing the reddened skin beneath. The blood it vomited had already turned purple.

At that moment, two more warg riders appeared in the forest. They didn't charge straight in, but watched from a distance — seemingly stunned by this unexpected blast of dark magic.

Beyond these two riders, seven orcs armed with cleavers or axes were slowly closing in around them. Among them rode an orc in dark-yellow robes astride a warg, gripping a staff studded with horned skulls. Jace knew those were likely Draenei skulls.

Was this Kilrogg Deadeye?

No — the Bleeding Hollow leader should have returned through the Dark Portal long ago, and Kilrogg Deadeye had one blind eye. This orc's eyes were both intact.

He was probably a minor chieftain among the Bleeding Hollow clan stranded on this side of the Dark Portal. Jace had no way to identify him.

By now, Obrik had finished an incantation and cast an Ice Barrier on himself. Dunn had reloaded his crossbow. The two succubi had vanished. Jace cast a Protection-from-Evil version of Mage Armor on himself.

Hubouri murmured a quiet prayer, and a bright light enveloped his whole body, forming a soft glow. Jace couldn't help but glance at him.

Was that Power Word: Shield?

Flags

- Fel-Eye / Eye of Kilrogg: The text uses 魔眼 (demon eye) generically and 基尔罗格之眼 (Eye of Kilrogg) as the specific spell name. I render the common reference as "Fel-Eye" and the spell name as "Eye of Kilrogg" to match the glossary (基尔罗格之眼 = Eye of Kilrogg). - Bleeding Hollow: 血环部族 = the Bleeding Hollow clan (canonical WoW clan stranded in the Swamp of Sorrows after the Dark Portal closed). Kilrogg·死眼 = Kilrogg Deadeye. - Warg riders: 狼骑兵 = warg riders; 座狼 = warg (orcish wolf mount). - Spells: Detect Demon (侦测恶魔), Shadow Bolt (暗影箭), Curse of Weakness (虚弱诅咒), Ice Barrier (寒冰结界), Mage Armor (法师护甲), Protection from Evil (防护邪恶), Power Word: Shield (真言术：盾 — Jace's guess). - Creatures: imp (小鬼 — Sazeno, Gulkrik), succubus (魅魔 — Morfell, Gaxulis). - Location: Moonbrook (月溪镇), Swamp of Sorrows (黑色沼泽). - Draenei skulls: 德莱尼的头骨 — Jace's inference about the staff ornaments.
