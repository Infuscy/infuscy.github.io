## Chapter 483: In the Horde's Way

This was the first time Jace had seen a Holy Light blessing spell cast with such clarity. And Hubouri — a priest who worked alongside warlocks and demons — prayed to the Holy Light for this magic with an ease that put the apprentices of the Cathedral of Light to shame.

Though the orcs didn't have the numbers, every one of them was powerfully built — not a single one shorter than two meters tall. Some wore armor; others wore leather jerkins made from the thick hides of creatures Jace couldn't identify. Two of them carried heavy shields that were clearly brought over from Draenor.

But they didn't launch an immediate assault. The orcs looked at one another, seemingly only just realizing that the party before them was no easy prey.

One orc stared hard at the warg rider who'd died horribly. By now the rider's chest had been stripped down to bare bone.

The orc warlock in the yellow robes was clearly the leader of this band. He coughed and dismounted from his warg, stepping to the front. Obrik spoke to him in Orcish: "We're just scavengers, not Alliance soldiers. There's no need to fight to the death."

Jace wasn't surprised in the least that a formal mage spoke Orcish — but the warlock spat and replied in Orcish: "For the safety of my clan, you cannot leave this place…"

"You don't have the advantage," Obrik warned in Orcish. "You think I've never killed an orc before, warlock?"

Erland murmured, "Stop wasting breath on them, you damned beasts…"

"I don't think these orcs can beat us," Volis said. "Let them try!"

"There's no need to get hurt," Obrik said. "We're here to earn money. Can't you see? These orcs want to survive too. You followed me out here, and I intend to see every one of you walk back home whole with your pay. Don't be rash — they're clearly hesitant too…"

"Mak'gora," Jace said. "I challenge them to Mak'gora!"

"What's Mak'gora?" Obrik asked.

"Mak'gora," Jace said.

"What… you mean that…" Obrik frowned. "You want to issue a Mak'gora to him? But that's…"

While Obrik was still hesitating, Jace stepped up to the warlock and said in Orcish, "I call for Mak'gora!"

Hearing a human invoke the orcs' sacred duel, the orcs were visibly astonished. One orc warrior even couldn't help but laugh out loud.

He looked at the two warg riders beside him. "I'm the one who killed your comrade. I demand that your ancestors judge the right and wrong of this!"

"Only orcs may call for Mak'gora!" one of the warg riders shouted. "Only orcs have the right to call for Mak'gora!"

"But Mak'gora was originally an ogre ritual," Jace said to him in Orcish. "You ought to know that better than I do."

"That's true…"

"That's so," the orc warlock said. "No one has ever said that only orcs may take part in Mak'gora… no one has the right to lay down such a rule for Mak'gora!"

"Then will you be the one to accept the duel?" Jace asked.

"What is Urk planning?"

Dunn asked in confusion — he couldn't understand the exchange between the orcs and Jace in Orcish.

"It's a duel ritual the orcs revere," Obrik explained. "In their clans, this kind of duel settles many matters in place of laws and rules…"

"But Urk, you don't need to do this."

"In this group, this is this one's responsibility. Just remember not to short my commission when the time comes, sir," Jace said, glancing at Obrik.

"However, since the participants don't have to be orcs…" The warlock chuckled, then began chanting a spell. A dark-purple void circle appeared beneath his feet. Jace noticed the pouch at his waist beginning to emit a flickering dark light — the glow of a Soulstone being consumed!

He was summoning a demon!

The warlock tore open a void gate, and a towering shadow stepped out.

It was… a Felguard!

"My companion accepts the Mak'gora," the warlock said with a smile.

This Felguard was extraordinarily tall — even taller than the Burning Legion vanguard Jace had seen on the battlefield, definitely over three meters. Chains draped across its body, and even its heavy hooves were inlaid with armor plates.

What drew the eye even more was the red greatsword in its hands, its blade crossed by straight metal grain lines, as though sealing within it a sinister, blood-soaked power.

A Cataclysm Blade?

This Felguard was wielding a Cataclysm Blade — the very weapon carried by the lieutenants of the Defiler, Archimonde?

Jace had killed Felguards on the battlefield before, but always in the midst of army formations, with other soldiers holding the front line and battle mages providing cover.

And this Felguard seemed somehow different. But at this moment, he would have to face it alone.

"It appears this sacred duel must be fought by me." Hubouri hefted his war hammer and stepped in front of Jace. "In the name of the Holy Light, I shall unleash my wrath and pass judgment on this evil."

"I called for the duel, priest," Jace said, gripping his shoulder. "You're too important to this group. If you get hurt, it'll be hard for us to go on far from here."

"I am a servant of the Light, Urk," Hubouri said. "You can't ask me to shrink back at a moment like this."

"You don't need to take this risk," Jace said. "Trust me — even if I get hurt, I recover quickly. And this Mak'gora was my call."

"But what if you die, master?" Morfell appeared at his side at some point, smiling as she looped an arm around Jace's neck. "If you die, you won't be able to Life Drain yourself back again."

"The duel is one-on-one, Morfell. Otherwise these orcs won't be convinced," Jace said. "Let me take on this Felguard. Trust me — I've dealt with their kind before the Dark Portal. It's nothing to worry about."

"So you think you've killed more Felguards than I have?" Morfell asked softly. "No offense, master… but I'm certain everyone here falls short of me. I know every step, every movement of these lumbering Felguards. Don't forget what I am, dear."

"Besides, wouldn't it be more fitting for me to deal with a summoned demon servant like this?"

"Most importantly, I cannot stand by and watch you fight a demon single-handedly and do nothing. This battle will be mine." She twirled the black razor in her hand and pointed it at the Felguard, speaking in Eredun: "Rest easy — this fool won't live past thirty seconds."

The Felguard boomed with laughter, its chest twisting the sound into something as ugly as banging on an iron pipe.

"Morfell…"

The succubus said, "I couldn't protect you at the Dark Portal. Give me this chance now."

"Then use this." Jace gripped the hilt of Skysong.

"The arcanists who forged this sword never intended it for a demon's hand," Morfell said with a smile. "Wielding it would only get me killed in a most ugly fashion, master."

## Flags

- **Mak'gora**: 玛克戈拉 — rendered as Mak'gora (canonical WoW orcish honor-duel ritual). Jace's point that it originated as an ogre ritual is kept as lore flavor.
- **Felguard**: 恶魔卫士 — Felguard (canonical WoW warlock summon / Legion infantry). Distinguished from generic "demon guard."
- **Cataclysm Blade**: 灾变之刃 — "Cataclysm Blade" (the weapon of Archimonde's lieutenants, the Defilers / 污染者). Jace's guess "Archimonde" = 阿克蒙德 (the Defiler).
- **Spells**: Soulstone (灵魂石), Ice Barrier (寒冰结界), Mage Armor (法师护甲), Life Drain (生命吸取).
- **Language tags**: Orcish (兽人语), Eredun (恶魔语 — the demons' tongue, rendered per glossary).
- **Skysong**: 碧空之歌 — Skysong (a named sword Jace carries).
- **Hubouri's magic**: 真言术：盾 = Power Word: Shield (Jace's guess, confirmed as canonical priest spell).
- **Character note**: Gulkrik (格尔基克) is Erland's imp; Sazeno (撒厄诺) is Jace's imp.
