## Chapter 484: Demon Killer

Morfell stepped up to the Felguard. "I issue the challenge, big fellow — their thing, whatever it was. Mak'gora."

She finished with a giggle. The Felguard looked down at the succubus and muttered in Eredun: "A succubus without wings."

Morfell lifted her chin. "Funny — neither of us has wings anymore, so that makes things a bit more even. So when you die and reform in the Twisting Nether, don't go making excuses for your failure."

"Very well, very well." The warlock glanced at Jace, coughed a few times, then said in Orcish: "Then let the duel begin…"

Though all the orcs present murmured among themselves, none seemed inclined to object to their leader's decision.

"That demon is too strong. Can Lady Morfell really handle it?" Sulenna asked worriedly.

Jace didn't answer — he didn't know how. Facing a Felguard, a succubus truly looked as pitiful as a frail child, to say nothing of the red greatsword in the demon's hand. It looked as though a single careless brush against Morfell would cut her clean in two.

The Felguard took several steps forward, and with each step Jace felt the ground tremble slightly.

Then it suddenly lunged a great stride forward and swung the blood-red gleaming greatsword at Morfell. The succubus vanished in a flash of light. Jace knew invisibility wasn't true disappearance — even while invisible, one could still be struck.

But this swing clearly cut only empty air. The Felguard immediately slashed behind it, as though it knew exactly where the succubus had gone. Jace was forced to concentrate, using Demon Detection to track Morfell's position, but he found that even under Demon Detection, her shadow had grown blurry and indistinct. She was completely serious now.

The Felguard kept hacking at the succubus's shadow. Jace was certain the creature could sense her presence — just not with great precision.

Gradually, he realized he'd become extremely concerned about whether Morfell lived or died. Even knowing a demon would reform its body in the Twisting Nether after death…

He couldn't help worrying. Every swing of the blade made his heart lurch.

After all, they'd spent so long together day and night. Jace was no unfeeling stone.

By now, the Felguard had lightly thrust at a spot, then immediately sensed Morfell circling behind it. It spun and brought a vicious chop straight down into the earth. Morfelt seemed to sense an opening and suddenly reappeared from invisibility, leaping up and driving a sword straight into the Felguard's chest.

The strike was precise and vicious, clearly aimed at the Felguard's heart. But taking the blow, the Felguard showed no sign of being shaken. It turned and reached for the succubus, but Morfell nimbly slipped away and melted back into the shadows.

The demon's thick hide and flesh were too hard to pierce, and the rest of its chest was guarded by armor and chains, leaving no easy targets… In the attempts that followed, Morfell seemed unable to break through the demon's defenses. Though several gashes now marked the Felguard's chest, it still stood firm — its movements hadn't slowed in the least. If anything, it grew more frenzied in its assault. And Jace knew that if Morfell took a single hit, she was done for.

The succubus's dark skin had even begun to flush red, the demon-fire in her eyes burning brighter and brighter. Jace's heart pounded faster and faster, and the onlookers grew more and more uneasy.

They were even less at ease than Jace — after all, they couldn't see Morfell's shadow darting back and forth through the darkness…

God only knew how many thirty-second spans had already passed.

Suddenly, the Felguard swung its blade straight in the direction the shadow was dodging. Jace knew this strike would land, and Morfell had no time to evade…

The greatsword cleaved straight into that shadow. With a harsh screech of metal and a flash of sparks, the black razor was sent flying from her hand. Morfell let out a pained groan and pulled to the side.

She didn't appear to be injured… but she'd lost her weapon…

The orcs were visibly emboldened. A smile surfaced on the orc warlock's face — it seemed to them the outcome was already decided.

Now the succubus no longer melted into the shadows. It seemed repeated invisibility had strained her mana. But if she'd nearly been cut down by the Felguard several times even while invisible, wouldn't being weaponless in plain sight be even more dangerous? Several times Morfell tried to retrieve her weapon, but each time the Felguard anticipated it and swung its blade over, forcing her to dodge back. In the end, the Felguard even kicked the sword away to who-knew-where.

Morfell bared her teeth. Jace frowned, already considering what he could do…

No. If he stepped in, there'd be no climbing down from this — the two sides would be fully at war, with no telling who'd live and who'd die. What would have been the point of initiating this challenge in the first place?

Morfell… damn it, you…

He was frantic with anger inside — when Morfell suddenly braked to a stop right behind the Felguard.

The Felguard grew more savage and triumphant by the turn. It spun and heaved a vicious chop. The blow was fierce and fast, but Morfell didn't dodge or evade…

Just as the blade was about to hack into her neck, her eyes suddenly erupted in flame. With an agility almost beyond human comprehension, she stepped forward to slip past the blade, then raised her hoof and kicked savagely into the Felguard's incoming wrist — faster than the Felguard's own chop, the kick landing squarely on the wrist it had thrust forward!

The Felguard hadn't expected this succubus to be so fast and hit so hard. Taking such a fierce kick, it failed to keep its grip on the sword's hilt.

Clearly, after such a prolonged fight, its strength wasn't infinite either… it had only barely kept up the pace!

But Morfell gave it not the slightest margin for error. The moment she caught the Cataclysm Blade — now falling free — she gripped it in both hands and pivoted into a sweeping horizontal slash that ripped open the Felguard's belly!

The Felguard roared in fury, trying to wrest its own blade back, throwing itself forward without regard for the fel blood gushing from its stomach!

Morfell gave a cold laugh and, in one clean stroke, cleaved through every chain and plate of armor, driving the blade straight through the Felguard's chest. The monster lurched forward several more steps against the sword, then ground to a complete halt. The demon-fire in its eyes flickered out, and it toppled to the ground.

Morfell wrenched the red greatsword free from the demon's chest and planted it in the earth, looking at the warlock and saying in Eredun: "He's gone where he ought to go. Declare the result now!"

The orc warlock stared at the succubus expressionlessly for a moment, then walked up to the Felguard's body. With a stern face he chanted a spell, and the Felguard's corpse immediately burst into flames laced with shadow energy.

"The Mak'gora is over. You have won," he said in Common. "Leave this forest!"

Several members of the scavenging party couldn't contain their excitement. Obrik let out a long breath of relief.

Jace said in Orcish: "Did you burn those demon corpses?"

"Yes." The warlock explained in Orcish: "Although we have gradually thrown off the control of the demon blood, that doesn't mean no one misses the feeling. Many in the clan still want to seek out demon blood and drink it, to reclaim that power lost to chaos. So I must burn every demon corpse, to cut off their means of obtaining fel blood. The Horde should command demons — not be commanded by them."

"I can accept that outcome. Your handmaiden is indeed a powerful warrior, human — like a Shattered Hand."

## Flags

- **Mak'gora**: kept as Mak'gora (canonical). The warlock accepts the succubus as the duel's participant.
- **Felguard vs. succubus**: 恶魔卫士 = Felguard; 魅魔/萨亚德 = succubus. The Felguard's insult "succubus without wings" (没有翅膀的萨亚德) is rendered in Eredun.
- **Cataclysm Blade**: 灾变之刃 = Cataclysm Blade (carried by the Defiler Archimonde's lieutenants).
- **Twisting Nether**: 扭曲虚空 = Twisting Nether (canonical demonic dimension; demons reform there after death).
- **Demon Detection**: 恶魔侦测 = Demon Detection (Jace's tracking method).
- **Shattered Hand**: 碎手 = the Shattered Hand clan (canonical orc clan) — the warlock's comparison.
- **Language**: Eredun (恶魔语) used for demon-to-demon speech; Orcish (兽人语) for orc dialogue; Common (通用语) for the warlock's final declaration.
- **Pacing note**: The duel is rendered as a single sustained action sequence matching the original's tension.
