## Chapter 488: The Corrupted Presence

"What movement?" Sulenna's voice dropped to a half the moment she heard Dunn. "I didn't hear a thing."

Not just Sulenna — Jace hadn't heard anything either. There was only the inexplicable sound of water now and again from the chill depths of the bog. Morfell, leaning on her sword, gazed into the mist-filled valley in the direction Dunn was looking, but there was little conviction in her eyes either.

The Felhound skirted the dragonkin's corpse and pressed deeper in. The dwarf Volis immediately shouted a command in Demonic: "Stop!"

The demon halted on the spot, though it still craned hungrily toward the interior.

The sky was already late, and the swamp — already gloomy — had churned everything into a murky, indistinct mass.

"Hsssh," Dunn warned Volis. "Keep your voice down, dwarf."

"So is it a magic treasure, or a demon corpse?" Jace asked quietly.

"There's only the one staff here," Obrik said, taking the staff from Dunn. "We haven't found the Eredar's body yet, and I refuse to believe an Eredar mage would carry nothing but a single staff."

"Listen carefully, all of you," Dunn said to the others. "Stop talking for a moment. Just be silent and listen to whatever that is."

The group fell quiet. After a moment, Hubouri nodded. "I hear it too. It's..."

"The sound of chewing."

Jace heard it as well. It sounded like some beast gnawing on something inside.

"I think we should leave," Erland said, looking at Obrik. "That dragonkin out front — it might be a guardian of some sort, watching over whatever's in there..."

"The troll's evil god?" Sulenna asked.

"Don't be ridiculous," Volis said.

"Whatever it is..." Obrik hesitated, then nodded, glancing around at the group. "I'm satisfied with this staff alone. You know Dalaran is always willing to pay a high price for demon staves."

"Or we could sell it to the goblins in Booty Bay, or the elves in Quel'Thalas," Erland added. "There's no need for us to go in there and take the risk."

Dunn cast one last uncertain look into the depths of the ravine, then nodded. "So... should we pull back?"

Jace took a step backward. His boot dragged through the muck, the wet sound scraping across the ground — but when his foot stopped, the slick, squelching noise of parting mud didn't stop with it.

He turned his head. A small mushroom was growing at visibly speed right behind his heel, pushing aside the murky bog water.

"Mushroom..." he murmured.

"What mushroom?" The nearest Hubouri glanced back. He flicked his eyes to the mushroom under Jace's foot — but then his gaze lifted.

Jace caught the shift in his expression and looked where he was looking.

The forest behind them had already sprouted mushrooms of every size — on the trees, in the earth, spreading in a frenzied tide.

Every one of them reeked of dense shadow power, and judging by the pale yellow mist wafting off them, they were likely poisonous to boot!

Mushrooms. Green dragons. They brought to mind a deeply ominous name.

In the game, the corrupted green dragon Emeriss was one of the most powerful in Queen Ysera's green dragonflight, and the most distinctive mark of her corruption was the virulently toxic, degenerate fungi that spread across any ground she walked.

But how could Emeriss possibly be here? She hadn't been sent to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar to disrupt the evil ritual.

But it didn't matter anymore. Even if it wasn't Emeriss... it had to be connected to her!

"Get out of here."

He'd barely raised the warning when he turned to see a strange, luminous mushroom sprout right beside Obrik. Jace shouted: "Get away! Get away, Obrik!"

The mage vanished in a flash of light at Jace's warning. The luminous mushroom promptly exploded, spewing a great cloud of dark-yellow poison mist. Fortunately Obrik was already more than ten yards away, his face pale with shock.

"Leave! Run now!"

Jace shouted and everyone bolted into the trees. The mushrooms were multiplying by the second — some damming the waterways, some blocking the path. And then Jace saw a ghastly black shadow gradually materializing from deep within the ravine.

A round, bulbous thing suddenly arced out of the darkness and rolled to a stop at Erland's feet.

A skull. A skull with a bony plate across its brow. An Eredar skull!

"Delicious... more deliciousness has come..." a muffled, aged voice snarled. "And... more... demons."

The group plunged into the deep forest. The exhaustion from the journey was instantly forgotten. Volis, with the shortest legs, was somehow the fastest, shrieking the Felhound's name as she fled like a madwoman — even the long-legged, agile Dunn couldn't keep up.

Jace didn't dare look back either. Clutching Sazeno's pack, he dodged the poison-mist mushrooms and the luminous ones about to explode, doing his best to run on solid ground. He couldn't spare the focus to use Detect Demons to check Morfell's position, but the Soul Link was still there — she was probably running even faster than him...

This was the moment his years of long-distance running training and the inexplicable black dragon blood in his veins were truly going to pay off!

He dodged a mushroom venting poison mist from a hollow and was forced to pull his mask up, but breathing became a struggle. Behind him came the sound of trees snapping. By now Jace could hear the enormous footsteps closing in from behind...

Those footsteps — could it be an adult dragon?!

"Ghost! Run!" Erland shrieked hysterically from behind him. At that sound, Jace felt as though he'd been jolted with lightning — his whole body surged with fresh strength.

Dunn shouted: "Damn it, dump all the food and water! Keep only the most valuable loot!"

Jace hastily untied his rations. Nearby he saw Hubouri throw away the bag of Felhound horns he'd been carrying — though he kept the Doomguard's fel horn.

Obrik Blinked a good distance ahead in a flash. Jace noticed he'd thrown away his entire pouch of mana potions, carrying nothing but two staffs as he ran. The man must have been traveling the world for years — he had the stamina of anything but some bookish mage who spent all day cooped up in a tower.

Wait... Sulenna...

Sulenna was unquestionably the weakest of the party physically.

Jace glanced aside as best he could. Erland was pulling up alongside him — the man was fast, nearly catching up to Jace himself.

But he wasn't carrying Sulenna... A beat of wings swept past his ear. Gaxulis, beating his demon wings, was carrying Sulenna from trunk to trunk, and in a single bound he'd already overtaken Jace and Erland, reaching Obrik's side.

Gaxulis was remarkably strong. Even on the ground he could keep pace with Obrik ahead while carrying Sulenna. Jace immediately understood why Sulenna trusted this succubus so deeply.
