## Chapter 492: The Emissary from Quel'Thalas

Once they'd set aside the materials Jalen needed for the robe and handed them over to Tharle for safekeeping, Erland packed up the remainder and took them away, planning to make trips to Moonbrook and Booty Bay over the next few days to see how best to offload them.

According to Erland, it would probably be another month or two before they saw all their cut of the proceeds — he'd have to wait until he got back from Booty Bay. But Obrik had already advanced Jace his commission of six gold, naturally minus the four gold that had gone to the hunter and the dwarven warlock woman.

Back home, sitting on his bed counting those six gold coins, Jace felt that his generous promise to split the money with the others had perhaps been a bit of a loss.

Then again, there was that terrifyingly large Cataclysm Blade leaning in the corner — so of course it was worth every copper.

"Morfell." He looked to the succubus sitting beside the crimson greatsword. "I feel like you've gotten taller. Is that my imagination?"

"It's certainly not your imagination. After reshaping my body, my strength became far weaker than before. It takes time to grow back," Morfell said.

"Does it have anything to do with me?" Jace asked. "I mean…"

"I'd very much like to say it's purely from the energy my master provides me…" Morfell leaned closer. "But that would be a lie. Azeroth is a place brimming with arcane energy — simply staying here allows a demon to draw upon it. And as the master I'm bound to grows stronger little by little, my power rises as well."

"Of course, that other thing is also very important…" she added, lowering her eyes.

Jace nodded and asked, "So the master's strength affects the demon servant as well. That's why Sazeno keeps pushing me to get stronger — but then why doesn't it seem to have gotten more powerful?"

"I clearly have gotten more powerful," the imp said, with uncharacteristic seriousness.

"Even if a booger doubled in strength, it'd still just be two boogers," Morfell said dismissively.

"Bah!" the imp shrieked.

"Let's not pay it any mind." Morfell wrapped her arms around Jace's neck and murmured, "You didn't touch me once this whole trip, and I went to no small amount of trouble, master. How are you going to make it up to me…"

Jace caught Morfell's arm and pushed her down onto the bed. He gazed at the sight of her laughing as she fell back onto the blankets, and his mind flashed back to the way she'd glared furiously at the Felguard, raised that massive sword, and driven it clean through his armored chest in a single stroke…

"What is it, master?" Morfell asked, running a finger across her lips.

Jace lifted her hoof. He could see her hips already writhing — she could barely hold on.

"Morfell, honestly — if the two of us dueled, could I beat you? Don't lie to me."

"Honestly." The seductive look on Morfell's face faded somewhat. She lowered her voice. "I don't know."

"Maybe the two of us should find somewhere and give it a try someday," Jace said.

Morfell couldn't help but laugh. "I'd be happy to. I look forward to any kind of contest with my master. But right now, you'd better… hurry…"

"Mm…"

"Ah… that was too fast… ah…"

The next morning, when Jace dragged himself out of bed, the winter sun was already high.

It was nearly noon. He'd been planning to head over to Marin's today to ask about the Holy Reliquary's reply.

Jace climbed out of bed, quickly dressed under the watchful eyes of his two demons, stretched, rolled his shoulders, wrapped himself in his warm mountain-lion-skin cloak, and bolted out the door.

He had to admit, although this place cost considerably more than his old rental, it was closer to the Mage Quarter. More importantly, getting to The Slaughtered Lamb from here didn't require passing the entire Wizard Sanctum tower and the gardens below it — bringing demons in and out was far more discreet than before. Tharle had probably taken that into account.

And given Jace's current income, covering the rent here was no challenge at all. In less than a month running around the Black Morass, he'd already pocketed six gold, with nearly another ten gold in profit shares to come. That was enough to keep him in this rental for a decade.

He hurried all the way to Marin's quarters and arrived just in time to see Marin saying a few words to several students at his study door, then descending the steps and crossing the garden in the direction of the Blue Heron.

Jace cut through the garden and caught up in a low voice. "Master… Master Marin."

Hearing Jace's name, Marin raised his eyebrows and lowered his voice. "Mr. Sesso, you've come at just the right time. Three days ago, an emissary from the Holy Reliquary in Quel'Thalas asked to see you — asked specifically for Mr. Urk."

"An emissary?" Jace asked. "I thought there was meant to be a letter for me?"

"There was no letter," Marin said. "At least, none that was passed along to me. Though, have you been making contact with Quel'Thalas again under the name Urk? You really do get along well with the elves over there."

Jace explained, "It's just that every now and then I come across something good that I need to sell for money. Wouldn't giving it to the Holy Reliquary be a much better deal than handing it over to Dalaran?"

Marin nodded, and Jace asked again, "Where is that elf now?"

"Remember that residence I mentioned to you before, the one where the elf is staying? He's resting there. His name is Lam Flameweaver."

"Lam Flameweaver. Got it. I'll head over at once."

Jace turned and made his way toward the small building where the elf was lodged. This time, with the ring, he could simply have the guard call the elf out.

Lam Flameweaver was wearing a long robe of fiery red, making him stand out considerably among the elven mages in their pale green and golden-yellow robes who occasionally came and went. Paired with his dark golden hair, if not for those eyes still shimmering with a faint arcane glow, Jace could have sworn he was looking at a genuine blood elf.

Then again, blood elves and High Elves had no fundamental difference apart from their style of dress. Some blood elves who had drained less arcane energy didn't even have green eyes.

"So you must be Mr. Urk," Lam Flameweaver said. "It seems Master Marin has already introduced me. I am the special emissary of the Holy Reliquary of Quel'Thalas, Lam Flameweaver."

As he spoke, he took a small box from his pack and said, "Could we find somewhere a bit quieter to talk?"

"Of course," Jace said.

Lam Flameweaver led Jace to a shaded corner of the residence garden, screened by overhanging trees. He came straight to the point. "You are Jace Sesso, am I right?"

Jace nodded. "Yes, that's me. I never intended to hide it from the Holy Reliquary."

"Then it wasn't hard to figure out," Lam said. "Actually, our examiner in Booty Bay had already caught on. After all, even in Quel'Thalas there are very few people who could have gotten hold of Lady Vereesa Windrunner's family token…"

He smiled. "Of course, the Holy Reliquary didn't send me just because you're Jace Sesso. It's also because of the importance of this dagger. It belongs to an ancient Kaldorei noble warlock — a Highborne, as you call them, Mr. Sesso."

"We don't know her exact name, but our investigators sent to Kalimdor to seek out Highborne magical relics have found several Highborne artifacts connected to the same individual, all clearly tainted by shadow and fel energy."

"We had assumed this warlock might have perished in the millennia since leaving the Well of Eternity. It was only because of the magical wards on these artifacts preserving her arcane traces that we were able to identify them with certainty hundreds or even thousands of years later."

"But clearly, this dagger was crafted by arcane means less than a hundred years ago… which means this Highborne may still be alive to this day. That is an extraordinarily significant discovery."

## Flags

- **Lam Flameweaver (拉姆·焰心)** — OC, special emissary of the Holy Reliquary from Quel'Thalas. Name is a phonetic rendering; "Flameweaver" for 焰心 (lit. "flame heart/core") — coined term, flag for approval.
- **Holy Reliquary (神圣遗物学会)** — the Quel'Thalas-based organization tracking Highborne relics (per chapter 471, "Holy Reliquary").
- **Mr. Urk (尤尔克)** — Jace's alias, used by the Reliquary emissary.
- **Vereesa Windrunner (温蕾萨·风行者)** — canonical Windrunner sister; her family token is the clue that exposed Jace's identity.
- **Kaldorei / Highborne (卡多雷 / 上层精灵)** — the dagger belongs to an ancient Kaldorei noble warlock (a Highborne); the Reliquary has been tracking her artifacts in Kalimdor.
- **Well of Eternity (永恒之井)** — the ancient source of the Highborne's power; the Reliquary assumed the warlock perished millennia after its loss.
- **Black Morass (黑色沼泽)** — the zone where Jace recently scavenged demons; referenced as the source of his recent income.
- **Mountain-lion-skin cloak (山狮皮斗篷)** — Jace's winter garment; not in glossary.
- **Blood elf vs. High Elf distinction** — Jace notes the two differ mainly in dress; some blood elves who drained less arcane energy lack the green eyes.
- **Note**: The Reliquary's examiner in Booty Bay deduced Jace's real identity from his possession of Vereesa's family token — a plot thread linking chapters 471 and 492.
