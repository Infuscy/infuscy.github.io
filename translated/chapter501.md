## Chapter 501: The Stalker

"Grede, don't you feel like something's off…" Jace asked.

The dwarf walked along, glanced back, and twitched his nose. "I don't know. What are you picking up on?"

Sazeno also looked behind them, then suddenly grabbed Jace by the collar and whispered, "Master… Master, there's a spellcaster back there! His power is really faint, but that's because he's doing everything he can to hide it…"

"An ogre mage?" Grede asked.

"No!" the imp pointed toward a thicket of low scrub behind them. "Right there, right there."

"We keep walking," Jace said.

The two of them entered a patch of woodland, slowed their pace, and slipped behind a few trees.

Before long, Jace noticed a humanoid shape in the depths of the silent forest behind them. A bald head, a purplish outer robe — and on the robe, just barely visible, a blurred eye sigil. The Violet Eye of the Kirin Tor.

Was he a member of the Karazhan investigation corps, the Violet Eye?

By now the figure seemed to sense that something was wrong too, stopping some distance away. Grede and Jace exchanged a glance. Jace tilted his eyes outward, and Grede nodded.

Grede's hand was already on the war hammer at his belt. Jace gripped his staff. They stepped out of the trees and moved toward the figure. The man had clearly spotted them too, but he didn't retreat or make any other move — just stood there, waiting.

"Master?" Jace called softly.

"The Flow of Ice Crystal… it's real — it's the real Flow of Ice Crystal!"

The stranger suddenly shrieked, his voice hoarse and strange. A few black, misshapen birds took flight at the startled outburst, lending this godforsaken dead wood the faintest whiff of life.

The mage tottered forward a few steps, and now Jace could see clearly that, while he still had a human shape overall, his face was covered in wounds and his eyes carried the sickly yellow glow of undeath.

"Undead!" Jace had barely shouted the word when a mass of white frost already swirled around the mage's body.

It was Frost Armor — and on a completely different level from the one Obryck had used against the orcs earlier. The frost gathering around him even shimmered with a cold light. The glare obscured the undead mage's form, and suddenly a frigid bolt of ice came screaming out of it. Jace was already prepared. A film of dark shadow energy, its surface shimmering with iridescent light, snapped open and took the ice bolt head-on…

As the ice bolt struck the shield, a ring of black ripples spread outward, blasting the weeds on the ground to splinters.

At the same same instant, Grede's war hammer erupted with lightning. The flash of it even drove back the black ripples spreading from Jace's shield.

The mage saw this and immediately tried to fall back, but the dwarf was faster. The electrified hammer dragged a trail of light across the dark earth and smashed straight into the mage's protective magic.

The glowing ice shattered like a windshield taking a direct hit, fragments scattering in every direction. With a single blow, the mage's Frost Armor was completely destroyed.

But when the ice mist cleared, the undead was gone.

Grede hauled his hammer back and looked around. "Did he run?"

"No!" Sazeno shouted. "He's still nearby!"

A mocking incantation rang out from their flank. Grede had barely turned toward the voice when, in a puff of arcane dust, he transformed into a black sheep tumbling on the ground, scrabbling with its hooves but unable to right itself.

The undead mage mumbled something in a raspy mutter, letting out a low, chuckling laugh. He seemed to have no mind of his own anymore — just a corpse running on residual instinct, attacking anything that came near.

Unlike death knights, this kind of undead horror had long since lost its own will. Fear was useless against it.

Jace was about to break the Polymorph on Grede when a Frostbolt came screaming from the mage. He knew this one couldn't pierce his Shadow Shield, so he turned the staff toward the shadowy figure channeling the frost power and fired a Shadow Bolt in reply.

The Frostbolt slammed into the shield and was swallowed by the shadow energy rippling outward. Sazeno clung to Jace's hair to keep from being swept away by the magical crossfire, but no frost power broke through the barrier to harm either him or Jace.

Jace watched his Shadow Bolt arc through the air, chasing the target — only to be scraped away by a dense lattice of branches, losing much of its force. It seemed to strike some kind of faint mana shield. Seizing the opening, Jace snatched up a rock and hurled it at the sheep's face.

A puff of arcane mist burst, and Grede scrambled to his feet with a string of curses. Without hesitation he hurled his hammer toward the spot where the mana shield had flared. The electrified hammer blasted a whole stretch of woods apart.

The undead shrieked. In the electric light bursting through the felled trees, Jace caught sight of a stumbling figure struggling to rise. He quickly spoke the words of a Curse of Weakness.

Jace felt the curse take hold — and yet it still couldn't slow the undead's feet. The mage Blinked backward, vanishing from their sight once more.

"Missed the bastard… that son of a bitch," Grede panted.

"Where is that mage?" Jace asked, smacking the imp on his shoulder.

Sazeno didn't answer right away. He looked this way, then that, and said hesitantly, "He's… everywhere…"

"What?!" Grede called his hammer back and demanded.

Another Frostbolt came flying from between a few fallen trunks on the other side. Grede sensed the arcane disturbance the spell brought with it, spun on his heel, and swung his hammer — a bolt of lightning shattered the fragile ice bolt. But that was only a feint…

A razor-sharp Ice Lance screamed from yet another direction, straight at Grede's back. Jace shouted a warning. Grede immediately threw himself a big step to the side — but that ice lance, influenced by arcane force, actually curved slightly in mid-flight. It clipped the dwarf's broad helmet with a clang and bounced into the sky, leaving behind a smear of frost.

Grede slapped the snow off his helmet. "Goddamn thing."

The two of them stood back to back, staring into the pitch-black forest. Jace said, "This bastard probably knows Mirror Magic."

"Mirror Magic?" Grede asked.

"It creates a copy of yourself to fool the enemy… like that Frostbolt you just smashed." Jace scanned the forest in every direction. "I should've brought Morophil."

"I can do it too! I can do it too!" Sazeno jumped up and down. "Just give me a little time…"

"Wait." Jace frowned. "Get me a soul shard from the bag."

"But you said you'd give me one…" the imp wailed.

"It's an emergency," Jace said. "Quick. He's after the Flow of Ice Crystal. He can't beat us head-on, but there's no way he's letting us go easily. He's in the dark, we're in the open — if we keep tangling like this, he'll find his opening sooner or later. He's an undead monster. He doesn't get tired!"

"Protect me in a bit, Grede. Don't let him interrupt my casting…"

Grede turned back and said, "You're not about to… You don't even even know where that mage is. Even if you called down a meteor, could you hit anything?"

"What do you think I'm doing?!" Jace immediately spoke an incantation, reinforcing his Shadow Shield once more. He looked at Grede and said, "Stay a little further away from me. Don't let the shadow energy touch you. I'm summoning a doomhound."

## Flags

- 跟踪者 = "The Stalker" — the undead mage trailing Jace and Grede through the Duskwood-like forest of the Narrow Pass.
- 镜像魔法 = Mirror Magic — the mage's ability to create illusory duplicates; Jace recognizes the tactic from game knowledge.
- 紫罗兰之眼 = the Violet Eye — the Kirin Tor's Karazhan investigation corps; the bald mage's robe bears their sigil.
- 寒冰护体 = Frost Armor; 寒冰箭 = Frostbolt; 冰枪 = Ice Lance — the undead mage's frost spells.
- 灼烧咒 = Scorch (Jace's counter-spell, rendered as "灼烧咒" in the Chinese).
- 虚弱诅咒 = Curse of Weakness — Jace's DoT curse, applied but barely slowing the undead.
- 莫洛菲尔 = Morophil — Jace's demon, left behind; he regrets not bringing it.
- 撒厄诺 = Sazeno — the imp minion.
- 安尼赫兰语 = Eredun (the demonic language) — referenced in the next chapter's summoning ritual.
- 地狱犬 = Doomhound / Hellhound — Jace's solution to the mirror-image problem; hellhounds can detect the real body.
- 闪现术 = Blink — the undead mage's escape tool.
- 暗影之盾 = Shadow Shield — Jace's defensive spell.
- 暗影箭 = Shadow Bolt — Jace's offensive shadow spell.
- 变形术 = Polymorph — the mage turns Grede into a sheep (black sheep, specifically).
- 肯瑞托 = Kirin Tor — the mage's original affiliation, implied by the Violet Eye sigil.
- 流冰之晶 = the Flow of Ice Crystal — the artifact the undead mage is drawn to.
