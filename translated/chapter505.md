## Chapter 505: An Alley Aberration

Of the three and a half bottles of Essence of Undeath, Jace kept one to give to Jalane Ayrole. The rest he planned to take to Booty Bay on the way to Kalimdor to trade to the goblins for coin, and then settle up with Grede.

He returned to Stormwind in the afternoon. Without wasting any time, he went home, dropped off his things, and headed straight for the Slaughtered Lamb.

When he arrived at the tavern, the winter business was fairly good — there were quite a few people inside. Jace saw the dark-skinned tavern keeper bringing food to the customers and waved him over.

"Is Jalane Ayrole downstairs?" Jace asked as Jarel Moor walked over.

"No." Jarel wiped his hands with a damp cloth. "She might come by this evening. I'm not sure."

"I've got Essence of Undeath," Jace said. "Should I leave it in her room, or leave it with you for safekeeping?"

Jarel frowned. "Don't put that ghostly crap in my warehouse, Jace. That's where the food for normal people goes. Go put it in her room, or put it in Ursula Deline's alchemy lab — whatever works."

"Fine. By the way, is Ursula here?" Jace asked.

"Oh, right." Jarel glanced toward the cellar door. "Ursula hasn't been around for several days. Do you have a key to her alchemy lab?"

"Several days?"

"Three or four days, maybe…"

"Has she ever stayed away from the cellar for this long before?"

"Has she… I can't quite remember. But now that you mention it, it really is rare for her to stay away from the cellar this long."

"Did she say where she was going?"

"No. She's been very quiet this whole time."

"Didn't you ask her?"

"Heh. You people, always this thing or that thing. How am I supposed to ask? Even you don't know there's something wrong with Ursula — why would she tell me?"

Hearing Jarel say that, Jace's expression darkened.

He hurried down to the cellar, left the Essence of Undeath on the bookshelf in Jalane's little room, then rushed back up, ready to go look for Ursula — only to come face-to-face with a felhound blocking his path.

Basadim just stared at him for a long while without moving. Jace's mind had been full of Ursula, and having a demon suddenly block his way gave him a start.

But seeing that this felhound was still here, he actually felt considerably reassured.

That meant Ursula… was at the very least still alive.

He went back to the tavern entrance, got Ursula's address from Jarel, and immediately headed for the Trade District. By the time he reached the alley where Ursula lived, it was already evening. There were few people about. Jace lifted his eyes and glanced at the dark blue sky, streaked with faint clouds. After returning from Deadwind Pass, he was even somewhat afraid that when morning came, the sky would never brighten again.

He didn't know why he was connecting this place to that valley at Karazhan. Perhaps because…

He caught a faint whiff of shadow.

Could it be because of Ursula?

Was she secretly brewing some kind of shadow-related potion?

Or had he stayed near Karazhan for too long, and was already hallucinating?

In any case, he warily swept his Sense Demons ahead of him. Nothing.

Ursula lived in a three-story building. Jace entered the front door and climbed the stairs. A sickening smell of rot and age hit him.

This alley had existed before the Orc Wars. Most of the buildings in the Trade District had been damaged — or occupied — by orcs and ogres during the war. The condition of the structures that had survived the war was generally poor, so even in the Trade District, rents weren't high. Perhaps that was exactly what Ursula had her eye on.

Ursula lived on the top floor. Jace climbed the stairs, and suddenly someone came toward him from above. Jace looked up and saw, to his surprise, that it was an elf.

No — a half-elf. Not the kind of half-elf like Ursula, who barely had any elven blood left, but one who still possessed considerable elven features.

"Good evening, sir," the half-elf said casually, continuing downward. But Jace couldn't ignore this fellow's presence.

He even had the impression that the entire building's aura of decay… seemed to be radiating from this man.

Was this a warlock?

Ursula was a half-elf warlock, and she seemed to have run into some kind of trouble recently. Coming here, Jace had been nursing a bad feeling all along… He really couldn't help but suspect whether this half-elf in front of him had some connection to Ursula.

He focused his mind and cast Sense Demons toward the half-elf's shoulder, wanting to see if any imp was following him. But what Jace saw made his heart skip a beat.

There were no demons following him — because he himself was a demon!

This man's entire being was a demon's shadow!

"Wait, sir," he tried to call him back, but the half-elf had already rounded the stairwell corner.

Jace ran after him in a sprint, but had already lost sight of him.

Damn it.

Jace focused his senses on the area around him. He suddenly felt a shadow appear behind him. He turned to see a dagger lunging straight at his chest. Jace reached out and caught the attacker's wrist!

The dark figure in front of him let out a low growl. In the moonlight filtering through the window, Jace saw a hideous, goat-like face right before his eyes. A pair of demon horns stood tall on its head, its eyes glowing with a dim yellow demon-light, and its body was covered in fur that gave off a nauseating stench.

A satyr!

This was a satyr demon that could disguise itself!

Jace had always thought satyrs only existed in Kalimdor — demons that night elves had been twisted into, with horns and hooves. He'd never imagined they existed in this part of the world too.

Then again, Karazhan itself had been hiding plenty of satyrs all along. He really shouldn't have been so surprised…

By now, Jace realized he was completely overpowered. He could feel the dagger's tip about to pierce the skin of his shoulder…

"Fekt!"

Under the influence of Fear, the satyr's demon-eyes flickered and its grip loosened slightly. It let out a bellowing, bull-like roar, as if in utter fury, as if fighting against the terror itself.

Jace used the opening to shove it away, drew Azuresong, and thrust hard — but the satyr suddenly vanished in a cloud of black mist, and Jace's strike hit nothing but air.

This monster could truly disappear!

This wasn't some trick of light and shadow, not an invisibility spell — it genuinely vanished into thin air! Jace looked back down the stairwell, sweeping it warily with Sense Demons, then glanced up. The dark shadow dropped straight down from above!

He immediately scrambled up two steps to dodge the demon's thrust, turned, and drove a sword-stroke at the satyr — but the creature parried with its dagger. Its strength was terrifying. That single parry sent Jace tumbling down the stairs.

The creature clearly realized Jace wasn't easy to deal with either. Seizing the chance while Jace was down, it chanted an incantation. The deformed black dagger in its hand gave off a wisp of shadowy black mist. Jace immediately felt a stabbing pain radiating from his chest outward. The first wave nearly made him vomit.

Damn it, Curse of Agony…

Under a frenzy of ringing in his ears, Jace was on the verge of losing all sensation. He could only crawl up a few steps. The sound of cloven hooves clattering on the floorboards chased after him. Jace immediately spoke the incantation for Shadow Shield!

Black shadow energy swelled from his palm. The savagery of the satyr's backstab struck the face of the shadow shield. The shadow energy immediately turned on the attacker, blasting the whole satyr away. It howled and tumbled several times before slamming into the wall at the corner of the stairwell.

Jace gritted his teeth against the pain and pursued with his sword. Wounded by that last shadow blast, the satyr barely managed to stand. It gripped its dagger in both hands and thrust viciously once more — only to be deflected again by Jace's still-persisting black shield. The shadow exploded once more, slamming the entire satyr against the wall. A large section of plaster was blasted into fragments by the shockwave. Even the small window at the stairwell corner was blown to pieces. The satyr demon trembled, steadied itself, and vomited a mouthful of blood. Jace followed up and drove his sword through the creature's heart.

He pulled the sword free, and the satyr collapsed powerless to the ground, its goat-like yellow eyes fixed on Jace.

"Who are you?" the satyr asked in Eredun.

Jace didn't answer him. He watched as the other's eyes slowly dimmed and the shadow energy in his body rapidly dissipated. Soon, it grew as cold and eerie as a black sheep's carcass.

Only then did he feel a stabbing pain in his shoulder.

The satyr's second thrust had pierced through his Shadow Shield… Which was why, even after taking damage from the shield's backlash, it had still tried to press the attack. It must have sensed that it had the ability to break through Jace's defenses.

The result was that most of the damage had been blocked — but the demon's dagger had still opened a small wound. By now, the shadow poison had darkened the flesh around the wound somewhat.

Jace could tell the wound wasn't serious. But considering he was about to set out for Kalimdor, he didn't want to travel with an injury.

The Flowing Ice Crystal hadn't recovered enough to be activated again. He needed to find a Shadow Protection Potion, and fast.

He hoped Ursula was home.

Jace still had many questions for this demon, but he didn't have the leisure to think about them any further either.

He hitched the demon's corpse onto his shoulder, carried it up to the third floor, and arrived at the door number Jarel had recorded. He tossed the corpse to the ground, sighed, and knocked.

No response from inside.

"Ursula — Ursula Deline… damn it, open the door!" After shouting twice, the door was suddenly pulled open. Jace looked at the familiar silhouette backlit from within, and finally felt himself breathe again.
