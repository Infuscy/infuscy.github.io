Chapter 507: New Medicinal Herbs from a Mysterious Continent

That evening, Jace and Ursula scrubbed the hallway with a purification potion that cleared away shadow and demon blood. Once they'd gotten it more or less clean and Jace could barely smell it anymore, he went back to his room.

"What about the broken windows?" Jace asked.

"I'll find someone to fix them. Any excuse will do." Ursula said, "Not many people care about these old buildings anymore, as long as the smell of shadow and demon blood doesn't leak out and get noticed by the Wizard Sanctum."

"And this demon corpse?" Jace said. "Get Erland?"

"That's the only option," Ursula said.

"Then I'm heading to Goldshire." Jace stood up.

Ursula asked, "Now?"

"With this demon here, I won't be able to sleep." Jace threw on his cloak. "Be careful, Ursula. But... there shouldn't be any other trouble, right?"

"There won't be, Jace," Ursula said.

Jace nodded, wound through the alleyways for a while to make sure no other demons were hiding around, then rushed through the night to Erland's home in Goldshire.

After briefing Erland on the situation, the two of them drove a wagon back through the night to Stormwind. They got interrogated by the gate guards and barely talked their way in with some excuse — by then it was past midnight.

After all that running around, Erland was still heavy with sleep, but the moment he saw the demon's corpse, he was wide awake.

"What the fuck is this thing? I've never seen anything like it."

"Go to the Wizard Sanctum library and copy me some material and you'll know," Jace said. "Anyway, it's definitely a demon."

"I can smell that it's a demon," Erland frowned at Ursula. "So who summoned this time — you or Jace?"

"Would you call it summoning?" Ursula said with a bitter smile. "Someone summoned a demon to come kill me."

Erland said in surprise, "Then whoever you've pissed off is really something!"

"I know," Ursula said. "So after this I'll need your help moving anything important from my place to the Slaughtered Lamb, Doldemore. I can't live here anymore."

"No problem, but one thing at a time, Madam Dlin," Erland said. "Right now the most important thing is getting rid of this corpse. You two help me too."

Jace nodded. The three of them cut apart the demon corpse, which had already begun to shrivel now that its fel energy was gone, stuffed it into the bags Erland had brought under cover of darkness, and loaded them onto the wagon.

Once the chunks had been mostly dealt with and the residual demon blood cleaned up, dawn was already breaking.

Fortunately there was no one on the streets. Jace looked out at the desolate morning road as Erland drove the wagon toward the hazy sun just peeking over the horizon, and felt his heart still pounding.

Right then he realized — he still couldn't set everything aside and calmly be a warlock. Or a villain, for that matter.

The creak of the wagon wheels faded bit by bit. Ursula leaned in and whispered, "Don't think that what happened last night... was to get you to agree to go to Kalimdor."

Jace nodded. "How could you even think that? You'd better have my reward ready, Ursula."

"Of course," Ursula said. "But you'd better hear what I want you to get first."

"Go on," Jace said.

"In the south of Kalimdor there lives a species of strange insect," Ursula said. "I've got a goblin travelogue here that describes their appearance, habits, distribution, and so on. I'll sort out the important info for you, and you can search for them according to that. Broadly speaking, I want a specimen — the more complete the better — so I can see what medicinal use their organs or body parts might have."

Those strange insects Ursula was talking about — Jace knew exactly what they were.

Those creatures that lived deep in the desert, spreading across thousands of miles through underground insect burrows that extended even beyond the desert, blanketing all of southern Kalimdor — the silithid. They were actually remnant species of the ancient Black Empire created by the Old Gods.

After the Old Gods were defeated and imprisoned by the Titans, these insectoids had, over the course of tens of thousands of years, gradually broken free of the Old Gods' control and evolved into all manner of giant insect species.

But as the seal corroded by time slowly lost its potency, many of the insectoids once again came under the vague, intermittent influence of the ancient god will buried beneath the earth...

"I've heard those insects are huge," he said. "How am I supposed to carry one back for you?"

"If you know about them, even better," Ursula said. "Find a way to get me a smaller one... or even just bring back part of one. Hardly any goblins think those insects are worth the trouble, so they're rarely brought over from that side. I've almost never heard of any alchemist getting hold of a sample. So I'll have to count on you."

"If I run into them, I'll try to get some," Jace said. "Though maybe I shouldn't ask... Ursula, are you still planning to get revenge on that Stratholme merchant?"

Ursula said, "After all, he's the one who killed my father — I can't let him off easy. But... maybe I should first figure out exactly what grudge he had against my father. Before... I was probably too impulsive, too impatient. For now I'll keep my head down for a while — let him think I'm dead, or disappeared, or fled."

Jace thought for a moment. "That letter you asked me to deliver — don't you think you should pay a little more attention to that person?"

Ursula said, "I know. But he's my father's cousin, and his best friend. If it weren't for him in the first place, I might never have escaped Lordaeron. But I probably can't contact him again either. I don't want to suspect him, but you're right — I have to consider every possibility... And even if he means me no harm, contacting him might put him in danger."

"As long as you've got it figured out, I'm relieved," Jace said. "I just don't want the master alchemist I went to all that trouble to save to vanish the next time I look around."

Ursula smiled. "You saved my life, but don't underestimate me either, Jace. Speaking of which — did you manage to get any necrotic essence on your last trip?"

"I got some," Jace said. "I can't get into your alchemy lab, so I left it with Kaylen."

Ursula nodded. "Galders brought back some flame essence last week. He's still putting a lot of effort into that Shadow Council robe. The base of the robe I already helped Kaylen finish — it's with her now. The only thing left is to use the power of the flame essence and necrotic essence for enchantment. I think we can start the final process?"

"Then let's set up a meeting with Galders and get this done," Jace said.
