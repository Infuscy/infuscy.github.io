Chapter 509: The Void Robe

Two days later, Jace arrived at the basement at the time he'd arranged with Galders.

He'd been at the elves' residence that morning, so he came a bit early. There were no humans in the basement yet — only Jace's two minions and Ursula's dog.

On one hand, he'd deliver the reply to Vereesa, and on the other, he'd inform the envoy Ram Flamheart that he intended to take the Kalimdor mission.

In the letter he'd made it clear that the companions he'd be traveling with might be hard to accept. Of course, he couldn't mention the word "succubus" in a letter that other elves might see — he shouldn't even openly mention "demon" — but since Vereesa had already hinted at warlock minions before, she should understand exactly what he meant.

Before, when Ram Flamheart had told him Vereesa might not be able to come because of interference from the Sunstrider royal house, he hadn't thought much of it — just that the elves of Quel'Thalas had enough bullshit to deal with.

But now, sitting in the corner of the basement with its chilly air, Jace found himself unable to stop thinking about how Vereesa might react.

Not long after, Kaylen arrived carrying the robe, and the others trickled in one by one. Galders came down the stairs carrying a black jar.

So this was the vessel holding the flame essence?

Kaylen spread the robe out on the table. Jace ran his hand over it gently — he could almost feel the care she'd put into making it. After wearing enough robes at different price points, he couldn't articulate the finer distinctions, but he could tell the difference in quality.

Dyed with special pigments that held a certain magical power, the whole garment had a grayish-purple that wasn't particularly pleasing to the eye. But Jace knew it was because the robe over-prioritized magical resistance and adaptability to shadow power — there was no room left to worry about looks.

The robe was woven from quite good silk and spider silk from God-knows-where. Jace wasn't the only one who could tell — Erland had been staring at the robe with glazed eyes too.

"Tell me, Airo — this robe uses spider silk... Where'd you get your spider silk?"

"Northrend," Kaylen said.

"Northrend spider silk." Erland nodded. "No wonder. I've heard that to get truly high-quality spider silk, you have to go into ancient ruins in Northrend that have stood for tens of thousands of years..."

"You know quite a lot, Mr. Doldemore," Kaylen smiled.

"Hah, of course," Erland said.

"Did you use the demon cloth we found earlier, Kaylen?" Sulenna asked.

"Of course," Kaylen said. "I stitched the demon cloth in exactly as the recipe Jace translated instructed... This cloth from the Twisting Nether, taken from demons, reinforces its affinity with magic and its durability. And most importantly, it can bear the power of runes."

"Then the next step is enchanting the robe with demonic runes," Galders said.

"Right."

Kaylen and Galders set the vials of necrotic essence and the black jar of flame essence to the side. Jace and Erland laid out the demon skins bearing the corresponding runes on the table, noticing that the inside of the robe had already been stitched by Kaylen with arcane sigils identical to the rune patterns, along with a string of demonic incantations specified in the recipe to activate those sigils.

According to what Sazeno had said before, all that was left now was to complete the power-transfer ritual!

Kaylen said, "Jace, you said your imp minion Mr. Sazeno knows the ritual to transfer this power. I know some myself, but I'm not very confident — I've only ever transferred some very weak rune power before. I'm not confident about a doomguard's powerful runes at all. So to guarantee success, let your imp do it."

"Don't be so formal, Madam Kaylen Airo!" Sazeno said excitedly. "I'd be happy to help!"

"You sure, Sazeno?" Jace asked.

"Absolutely no problem!" Sazeno said. "A doomguard... hah! Not too rare as demon idiots go! But I'll need two vials of lesser mana potion poured into a bowl next to me. And have Besadim stay away from this bowl, Madam Ursula!"

"No problem."

Ursula poured the mana potion into a bowl and set it on the table. Sazeno hopped up and sniffed the bowl, nodding in satisfaction. Right now it was like he'd become the center of the Slaughtered Lamb.

"Being able to craft a robe like this will put us ahead of most warlock circles, Mr. Sazeno!" Galders' imp minion Zij said happily. "Then our master will be able to hold his head high in front of those damned dwarves — those bastards could never pull this off!"

"Quiet, Zij," Galders said. "Let Sazeno do his work!"

Sazeno said, "That's right, quiet down... Now, open the vessels holding the element essences."

Ursula, wearing heavy gloves, opened the two containers. When the black jar was opened, Jace clearly felt a wave of heat blast out — the whole room seemed to warm up.

Galders glanced nervously at the bonfire nearby, even a little worried the flame essence might set it alight.

Sazeno raised his hands high and began chanting an extremely long, hard-to-remember incantation. Jace noticed that the vials holding the two essences had started to tremble.

It guided the flame and necrotic elements into the demon skin to reactivate them, while simultaneously channeling them into the runes on the robe. The identical runes on both sides began to glow at the same time, linked by elemental power...

Sazeno kept his eyes locked on the elemental energy on the robe's side. The other warlocks watched tensely too. Jace didn't know about the others, but he was a little worried the power of the flame essence might burn the robe.

An imp's face made it hard to tell whether he was nervous, strained, or frightened — but at least he was quite excited, tears like molten fel fire streaming from his eyes, the volume of his chanting rising and falling. By now more than half the mana potion in the bowl had evaporated.

Ursula leaned close to Jace and whispered, "Should I go get some more mana potion?"

"Right now!" Sazeno switched to Common and shouted. "Witness my power!"

The demonic runes suddenly ignited in a blaze of purple-green fel fire, turning to ash in an instant. At the same moment, the runes on the robe flared brilliantly — even Kaylen took two steps back in shock.

The vial of necrotic essence was completely empty. The black jar belched rolling green smoke. The bowl holding the mana potion erupted in chaotic green flames, shattering to pieces. The imp let out a cry and collapsed on the ground — even the flames on his head had gone out.

Jace scooped him up. "What the hell happened? Sazeno!"

"He's asleep, my master," Molophir said, looking at Jace with disdain.

"Well... all right." Jace, hearing the succubus say so, then looked up to find everyone staring at him — being this concerned about an imp minion did seem a little odd.

But hearing the succubus confirm the little demon wasn't dead, Ursula relaxed a bit too.

"Nothing went wrong, fortunately. Try on the robe, Jace," Kaylen said.

Jace nodded and draped the robe over his shoulders. When he wrapped it tight, the runes that had already dimmed... flickered once more, arcane light radiating from his collar and cuffs, then slowly faded again.

Just by appearance, this garment looked much like an ordinary robe of decent craftsmanship that didn't bother much with looks. The demonic runes weren't conspicuous when they weren't glowing, after all — the runes had been stitched on the inside.

Its biggest drawback was that it might be a bit hot to wear in summer.

"A robe this valuable... if it gets accidentally slashed, it'd really be the end," Jace said.

"This is probably the most cut-resistant robe I've ever seen, Jace," Kaylen said. "You have no idea how much effort it took me to cut that demon cloth... Not to mention it's been through several rounds of enchantment. It's on a completely different level from the few-gold pieces from Larson's or Duncan's. But don't keep testing its limits, Jace..."

"I know," Jace said with a nod. "Thank you, Kaylen. Thanks to everyone for the help."

"You've earned it, Jace," Galders said. "This robe doesn't have any special name, but the orcs kept emphasizing in the recipe that a garment made this way holds power from the Void. So let's just call it — the Void Robe."

The Void Robe... Jace thought. So this is the Void Robe?
