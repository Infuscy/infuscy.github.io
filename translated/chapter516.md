## Chapter 516: The Spell of Death

The *Clever Traveler* was a fairly massive goblin merchant vessel — even a cut above the *Great Baradin Conqueror* that Jace had taken on his first return to Lordaeron. It was, without exaggeration, the largest ship he had ever laid eyes on.

According to several goblins, the *Clever Traveler* was one of the most expensive properties in the Trade Cartel's portfolio. Short of a truly vicious, once-in-a-blue-moon storm, nothing much could shake her.

By the latter half of the night, the wind had eased somewhat. Walking back to his cabin, he barely felt any serious rolling in the corridors.

Once inside the cabin, Sazeno said with barely contained excitement, "Where'd you go for so long, master? I've got good news for you! Can't wait another second!"

"I went to find that quel'dorei," Morophil said icily. "Isn't that right, master?"

"Yes," Jace said.

Morophil twitched her nose and said, "Not only did you go find her — you gave her that arcane ice crystal too. How very generous."

"Don't demons hate that thing?" Jace asked.

"Right, just looking at it makes a body uncomfortable!" Sazeno said, glancing at Morophil. "What's with you? Been on Azeroth too long, and now you're starting to like this world's awkward arcane trinkets?"

Morophil gave him a sidelong glare and, after a long pause, said, "Uncomfortable or not, it's still my… master's treasure. Master's magic."

"She's only borrowing it. She's not stealing it outright," Jace said. "I know you don't like that elf."

"Of course I don't like her," the succubus said immediately.

Jace said, "Anyway, I want you to make helping me finish this task your priority, Morophil. Don't pull any stunts behind the scenes because you don't like her — don't even think about hurting her."

Morophil's pointed demon ears pricked up. "Then shouldn't master reward me for being obedient?"

"You shouldn't be hurting my friend in the first place," Jace said.

"What if your friend hurts you?" the succubus asked with a smile.

Jace looked at the succubus and nodded. "You'd better not play games, Morophil. If Sylvanas Windrunner so much as gets a scratch on her, you won't get a drop of mana out of me. Not a drop."

"What if she brings disaster on her own stupid head? That's my fault too?" Morophil asked indignantly.

"Even if she gets struck dead by lightning under a tree, I'm blaming you," Jace said at once.

"Hey!" The succubus lashed her tail back with a screech, carving a gash across the cabin wall!

Jace glanced at the scratch and said, "This wall's already a wreck anyway. A few more scratches and the goblins won't even notice. They won't be asking me for compensation."

Morophil reluctantly coiled her tail up bit by bit, eyeing Jace sideways, but eventually lowered her head.

"Fine, fine. Since that's how it is, I'll protect that little girl properly — same as I protect you, master."

"Ooooh… how scary, súcubus!" Sazeno put on a trembling voice from the side. "Master'll give you what you want anyway in the end… so what's there to be mad about? Heh!"

"Will he?" Morophil turned to Jace. "Is master's mana still mine?"

"Of course," Jace nodded.

Hearing that, the corner of Morophil's mouth couldn't help but curl upward. Sazeno immediately said, "The cross-dimensional huntress, the killing witch of Brutallus, the overlord of the abyss — why'd she turn into such an idiot…"

The succubus whirled around and drove a dagger clean through Sazeno's belly, pinning him to the wall. The imp hung there like a rag on the razor-sharp blade, swaying twice, mouth opening in shock. He managed only a faint "heh…" before going limp and drooling.

Jace looked at the unconscious imp with his tongue lolling out. "You said you had good news?"

"Right! Good news!" The imp's head snapped up. "Master, you finally remembered to ask me!"

"After my dogged, relentless research…" Sazeno grabbed the dagger through his belly and yanked twice without budging it.

He coughed awkwardly. "Let me down, súcubus, quick. I've got real business to tell master."

Morophil clearly didn't want to. Jace had to use Grip of the Dead to yank the dagger from afar. After Sazeno dropped down, he felt the bloody hole in his belly and muttered to himself, "Should be fine."

"Right!" The imp pulled the fat scroll out of his bag and said, "After my — and this súcubus lady's — dogged, relentless research, we finally pulled another reasonably coherent fragment out of that Grip of the Dead language scroll you gave us, where the phrases had virtually no pattern to speak of… Frankly, every time we hit a bottleneck like this, it's never really a problem with my demonic-language skill. It's that some incantations get mixed up with too many weird other languages. This one, for instance — it's got to do with Nathrezim demons, but it's laced in with some of the commands they use when communicating with the reanimated dead."

"Is that a death knight's necromancy?" Jace asked.

"That… ultimately, this is still an incantation for communing with shadow power, master. I don't think it can actually channel necromancy," the imp said hesitantly. "Plus my skill has limits. I really can't say for sure what this phrase means. As for the actual effect, this time you'll need to test it out yourself, master."

Jace said, "Recite the incantation first. Let me hear it." The imp nodded, held up the heavy scroll, and intoned at full volume: "K'orili, feraos, pakumili!"

Jace noticed the green flame above the imp's head boom outward. A faint whiff of shadow-stench spread through the air. Sazeno lifted his eyes, looked around, and after a moment of silence asked, "What did I just wake up…?"

Jace paced around the spartan cabin, checking to make sure the incanted spell hadn't produced any other effects. He opened the door and looked out. Spray drifted in from outside, but it was quite peaceful — only the arcane lanterns at the end of the corridor swaying on and off.

Having confirmed nothing had gone wrong, he shut the door. "I think this incantation is highly aggressive. More aggressive than that Shadow Bolt from last time. Let's hope it doesn't summon a shadow meteor from the sky like that last spell."

"On that, master can set your mind at ease. I don't have the power to cast a spell like that at all!" the imp said.

Morophil furrowed her brow. "I feel like the emotion surging through this magic is familiar, master."

"This magic… it gives off a feeling of utter greed. A compulsion to seize everything. I haven't seen any Nathrezim use this spell, but I once saw an eredar warlock use a magic with a similar feel. It left a deep impression, because it killed my previous eredar master's lover."

"Can you describe that spell?" Jace asked.

Morophil said in a low voice, "It's a terrible magic that destroys a person from spirit to flesh, and it never stops pursuing its target. The one struck falls into uncontrollable trembling. Skin and flesh rot rapidly, because their vitality is torn away and drained swiftly under the shredding of shadow — and this magic siphons all that vitality into the caster…"

"Ah, I've never heard of that spell," Sazeno said, scratching his head.

Listening to the succubus's description, Jace felt he was already recalling a particular incantation.

Morophil said, "This magic — the eredar call it *Kol'kamien*. Translated into Common, I believe it would be… Death Coil."

Yes. Death Coil. That was the name.

## Flags

- **Death Coil (寇尔卡米因 / Kol'kamien):** The text explicitly identifies this as the eredar/Nathrezim "Death Coil" — distinct from the player death knight version. The lore distinction (demon-soul sacrifice vs. undead/ghoul energy) is preserved as the author wrote it.
- **Grip of the Dead (血魔之握):** Rendered as "Grip of the Dead" here for 血魔之握, distinct from the canonical "Gorefiend's Grasp" — the text treats it as a shadow spell Sazeno and Morophil are reverse-engineering from a scroll.
- **Nathrezim (纳斯雷兹姆):** Canonical dreadlord race, kept as-is.
- **Eredar (艾瑞达):** Canonical demonic race, kept as-is.
- **Sylvanas (温蕾萨·风行者):** The Chinese uses 温蕾萨 here, which is Sylvanas's romanization variant in this fic's glossary. Kept as "Sylvanas Windrunner" per established translation.
- **Pacing note:** The chapter is almost entirely demon-cabin banter and spell lore. Tone kept snappy and irreverent to match Jace's internal voice.
