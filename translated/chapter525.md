## Chapter 525: Hatred of Demons

Below them, one of the centaur was using the moment the beast had turned to raise its spear for a strike that could kill it at any second. Wynne glanced Jace's way and noticed his worry.

This druid could only be a night elf. After all, the tauren of this era didn't even know what the Horde was yet, let alone stood as allies at the Battle of Mount Hyjal against the defiler Archimonde's Burning Legion invasion. They had yet to forge ties with the night elf archdruid Malfurion Stormrage.

And if this was a night elf druid, that meant an absolute, irreconcilable hatred of demons. If it saw Jace openly traipsing around Kalimdor with demons in tow, its hatred for Jace would dwarf anything these centaur felt by orders of magnitude.

If they saved this night elf, wouldn't that be rescuing an enemy who'd fight them to the death? In Gadgetzan they'd been trying to avoid elves as it was.

But then again, thinking it and doing it were different things. He couldn't just stand by and watch someone die. So Jace still followed Grede's lead.

The dwarf had clearly fought demons in the Blasted Lands and come away with a full helping of confidence. Without a moment's hesitation he hefted his axe, clutched his small round shield, and slid down the gentle slope. A bellowed war cry startled all three centaur circling the beast into spinning around.

"Three against one? Let me even the odds!"

He roared it at the top of his lungs, but obviously the centaur had no idea what the dwarf was saying. The nearest one lost its footing in a panic and frantically leveled its spear at this sudden, uninvited guest.

Grede raised his shield and lifted his warhammer. Lightning coiled along the hammer's head clearly shocked the creature before a blow was even struck. A blinding white flash burst forth and slammed the spear-wielding centaur to the ground with a wail.

Another centaur tried to circle around to spear the dwarf from the side, but Grede caught it on his shield and snapped the spearhead clean off. Wynne's arrow streaked in and struck the shoulder of a third centaur trying to close in, ruining its pincer coordination with its companion.

The wounded centaur, clutching its shoulder, howled a few times toward the one whose spear had been broken. They'd clearly realized these people were not to be trifled with. The two of them immediately turned and fled.

The whole thing happened so fast Jace hadn't even finished a single incantation before the two centaur had already covered a great distance, bounding across the rocky slopes and vanishing in moments.

The remaining centaur — the one blinded and knocked to the ground — clambered unsteadily back to its feet. Its powerful body was truly that of a sturdy wild horse, and its upper body was powerfully built too. A long braid whipped back and forth as it tossed its head and cried out.

It still gripped its spear, keeping it trained on the dwarf. Grede rapped his shield with his hammer in provocation. The centaur leveled its spear and kept shouting in that unintelligible language, backing away as it did.

The centaur's strides were enormous. Even retreating, it moved at an absurd pace. Grede had never fought centaur before and had no feel for their movement. After a standoff he watched the creature get farther and farther away and realized he'd never catch it. All he could do was roar "Coward!" to vent his frustration.

Wynne had her bow drawn on the centaur, arrow nocked. Jace gently pressed her arm down. "Forget it. If we kill one, the rest of the centaur might hunt us through the whole of the Needles. The road ahead would only get harder."

The elf shot Jace a look, then nodded and lowered her bow. Morfell, who'd appeared from further off, also stopped her chase and slapped her arms in boredom.

Wynne went over to the beast, wanting to check its wounds, but it snarled and backed several paces, kicking up a cloud of dust.

Jace studied the beast. It was covered in wounds opened by spear points and slashes. In the dark, he could just make out from the patches of fur not yet soaked with blood that its coat was a tawny yellow-brown.

If his game experience held, a druid's shifted fur color corresponded to their natural skin tone. Night elf skin was blue, blue-grey, or purple, so whatever form they took — bear, panther, or otherwise — tended toward black, dark, and grey.

Could it be that this druid wasn't a night elf after all?

Besides night elves, what race on Kalimdor right now practiced druidism?

Morfell returned to Jace's side. The sight of a demon approaching made the beast snarl again and retreat into another cloud of dust. Then, through a fit of violent coughing, the dust cleared — and a tall, powerfully built silhouette appeared within. A tauren? This wasn't an elf. This was a tauren!

Wasn't Hamuul Runetotem of Thunder Bluff the first tauren druid? But that was more than ten years from now. So who was this?

Jace was baffled. He even wondered if he'd misidentified the race.

No — he hadn't. This tauren was clearly a full head smaller than the one back in Gadgetzan, probably only about two meters tall, with short, thick horns. But it was unmistakably a tauren.

The creature wore a heavy sand-blocking leather outer robe and gripped a wooden staff picked up God-knows-where, pointing it their way as it sat on the ground shuffling backward, coughing up blood and roaring in a language none of them could understand.

"What's this monster saying?" Grede asked.

"I've never heard that language!" Sazeno said.

"Probably the tauren's own tongue. This one doesn't speak Common," Jace thought for a moment. "I remember tauren can also commune with the elements. Grede, try speaking to it in the language you use to address the elements. See how it reacts."

"Uh… uh… I, uh…" Grede muttered for a while, then mumbled a string in the elemental tongue. His hammer actually crackled with a flicker of lightning. The tauren looked dazed — whether from Grede's elemental speech or the sudden elemental discharge from the hammer.

"What did you say?" Jace asked.

"I don't know, I was making it up," the dwarf said, his voice getting strained. "I can't actually speak the elemental tongue. I memorized a few incantations, Jace! Don't expect anything from me!"

By now the tauren had the look of a genuinely frightened calf, shrinking to the side and stealing glances at the succubus beside Jace.

"It's no use. Let's leave. Don't stress the druid out any more," Jace said, looking at Grede and Wynne. Both nodded. The tauren seemed to relax a little when it looked like they were leaving.

## Flags

- **night elf** (暗夜精灵): Jace's first assumption about the druid's race.
- **Mount Hyjal** (海加尔山): the battle against Archimonde's Burning Legion — still years away in this era.
- **the defiler Archimonde** (污染者阿克蒙德): Archimonde, the Legion's commander.
- **Malfurion Stormrage** (玛法里奥·怒风): the night elf archdruid; tauren had not yet forged ties with him.
- **tauren** (牛头人): the bovine race; the druid turns out to be a tauren, not a night elf.
- **Hamuul Runetotem** (哈缪尔·符文图腾): canonically the first tauren druid, but that is still a decade away — hence Jace's surprise.
- **Thunder Bluff** (雷霆崖): the tauren capital on the Mesa.
- **elemental tongue** (跟元素说话的语言): the language used to commune with elementals; Grede only knows a few memorized incantations.
- **Morfell** (莫洛菲尔): the succubus; 魅魔 = succubus.
- **Sazeno** (撒厄诺): the imp; 小鬼 = imp.
- **Quel'dorei** (奎尔多雷): high elves; Morfell's address to Wynne.
