Chapter 536: A Dangerous Idea

Vereesa Windrunner said, "I'm wondering — is it possible to create a portal that we can pass through, but demons cannot? One that connects Azeroth and Draenor?"

"I wouldn't say it's absolutely impossible," Jace said. "But demons can slip through any crack between planes. I'd bet there are plenty of portals only demons can use — ones no mortal could ever step through."

Vereesa frowned. "Then what about a small portal? One that only lets a few people through... a little gate that no powerful demon, or demonic legion, could pass through. At least then, people on this side could see what's actually going on over there."

"You'd be better off asking the masters at the Kirin Tor about that, Vereesa," Jace said. "You know what my level of arcane magic is."

"You might not be as skilled with the arcane as they are, but you understand demons," Vereesa said. "Especially since you have real demons here willing to help you. The Kirin Tor fears a second Dark Portal, so they refuse to touch anything related to the Sons of Lothar."

"A real demon is right here, and of course small portals are possible!" Sazeno suddenly poked his head out of the warlock's pack. "Lady Vereesa Windrunner, cross-planar minor passages are absolutely feasible — otherwise, how would demons respond to summoning rituals? The Burning Legion has definitely snuck countless demons into Azeroth this way already, and you didn't even know it!"

"Of course I know," Vereesa said, looking at the demon. "Why else would the elves and humans have founded the Order of Tirisfal?"

The imp went on: "Whether you know it or not, the problem is this: a portal across the Twisting Nether requires a mage who understands the Void to maintain it — and we don't have a mage of that caliber here! I'm afraid even among the Kirin Tor you worship, there's no one like that... after all, communing with the arcane and commanding the power of the Void are two completely different things."

"You're right," Vereesa said. "An archmage I know said something similar — he said unless Medivh came back to life, no mage in the Alliance could do it."

"Sorry, I can't help you with this," Jace said.

"No one can help with something like this," Vereesa said softly. "I'm just talking nonsense — I'm already happy to have someone to listen to my crazy talk."

The imp climbed onto Jace's shoulder. "Morfil has seen countless demons! Demons of the Burning Legion... demons who betrayed the Legion, demons who haven't joined the Legion yet! Master can summon her and let Lady Vereesa ask her opinion — after all, she knows so much!"

"No," Vereesa said almost immediately. She glanced at Jace, then added with a somewhat unnatural explanation, "I'm just not quite used to interacting with that succubus yet. Let's leave it at that."

At that moment, she tied up her hair and said, "There are many aggressive raptors in this oasis — their coloring is different from the raptors in Stranglethorn. These ones are all cyan, and their fangs and claws are terrifying. They don't listen to me very well either. Whether it's the influence of some sinister force or not, the animals here are far more aggressive than those outside. I don't want to spend the night here. Let's pack up and head out as soon as the others get back."

Not only did Vereesa feel this way — after Musa and Grede circled the perimeter, they too realized how aggressive the animals in the oasis were. So the group hurried onto the road at dusk, leaving the oasis behind.

A short distance from the oasis, on the edge of a hill, there was a small centaur village. A centaur spotted the party from far away, but he just stood there staring intently, without raising an alarm or alerting the others.

Musa said this centaur wasn't a resident of this village — his coat pattern was different. He was probably a scout from another tribe who'd come here to scout the area.

There were conflicts and struggles within the centaur as well — that wasn't particularly surprising.

In the game, just north of the oasis was "the Crossroads."

The Crossroads wasn't just a literal crossroads — it was the name of the largest village and settlement in the Barrens. From here, heading north you could walk all the way to Ashenvale Forest, where the night elves had lived for ten thousand years. Heading east all the way to the coast would bring you to Ratchet, near the wastes of Durotar. Heading south was the direction Jace and the others had come from — Agama'gor, home of the quilboar. And heading west was the direction Jace's party was traveling — the Stonetalon Mountains.

But at this point, the Crossroads was nothing more than a crossroads. Jace saw several massive fallen totem poles here, so decayed that the patterns carved into them were no longer visible.

Musa didn't need to tell him — Jace already knew that tauren might once have lived here, but under the expansion of the centaur, they'd gradually been unable to hold their ground.

For all he knew, there might have been a larger town here than the Crossroads in the game — after all, why else would anyone erect such massive totem poles if there wasn't some kind of settlement?

The tauren's houses had probably been torn down by the centaur to build their own villages.

Continuing west from the Crossroads, the party didn't take the main road — instead, following Musa, they traveled along the edge of a range of hills.

According to her, the main roads were full of centaur.

After four days of walking, one morning after climbing a hill, Jace gazed out in the early morning light and finally saw a sight beyond the hills, the badlands, and the grassland.

It was a range of red mountains — darker and more brownish than the Redridge Mountains. Musa said beside him in Common: "There are tauren houses here."

"Tauren houses? Where?" Grede looked around.

Musa had been learning Common from Grede and Vereesa these past few weeks. Though she hadn't learned much, she was quite proactive about using it. Seeing Grede completely lost, she realized she still couldn't express herself clearly, so she explained to Vereesa in Thalassian.

Vereesa said: "She means there's a tauren village ahead."

"Really?" Jace asked. "Not some ruined wreckage from before, right?"

"On the mountain," Musa said. "Safe."

"I understand," Jace said. "They built their homes high up the mountain, where centaur can't easily attack — that's why it's survived."

Wait — a tauren village in the Stonetalon Mountains.

Could it be Sun Rock Reach? Had Sun Rock Reach originally been a tauren settlement that predated the founding of the Horde?

Musa turned and said something to Jace in Thalassian. Vereesa said: "Musa is reminding you to keep your demons hidden. The other tauren won't forgive having demons appear near their village."

Jace nodded immediately. It seemed that after two weeks of traveling together, she'd come to at least somewhat trust these two demons.
