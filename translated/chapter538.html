Chapter 538: Sun Rock Reach

The situation at Sun Rock Reach was far more horrific than Jace had imagined. Thick smoke billowed into the sky, blackening several nearby cliffs. The sky lit by firelight was full of harpy shadows. The place seemed to have been overrun by harpies. The tauren had probably been forced inside to defend themselves — which was why the harpies had dared to snatch whatever they wanted and leave. In their eyes, the remaining tauren were likely incapable of any resistance.

Then a burst of flame suddenly shot up from Sun Rock Reach into the sky. With a savage roar, the flame seized a harpy's body, then tumbled and slammed into a mountainside, falling together.

Was that a wyvern tamed by the tauren?

A shriek suddenly came from the sky. Jace had just looked up when Vereesa loosed an arrow, hitting the harpy that had raised the alarm. The harpy howled and crashed into the pine treetops. Her cries had already drawn the attention of a group of companions not far away.

"Damn it," Grede cursed. "These bloody things can all fly! Nowhere to hide!"

"Then we kill as many as we can," Vereesa said.

The dwarf laughed. "What a ruthless elf — exactly what I'd expect! Come on, Jace Sesso, show Musa your true colors!"

Jace shot him a look, then turned to the harpies flying down from above. He channeled a shadow bolt with his staff and hurled it into the night. The black sphere of arcane energy was nearly invisible in the darkness. The harpy didn't even know what had hit her. She shrieked, flapped her wings a few times, but could only watch as all her feathers fell out, her skin blackened and rotted, and she tumbled down the slope.

The harpies quickly spotted the group. At the cry of a blue-feathered harpy, they broke off from the high ground above the village and flew toward them.

"That blue harpy is their leader, Vereesa!" Jace shouted.

Vereesa nodded and drew and loosed in one motion, impossibly fast. Before the command to attack could even finish sounding, the blue harpy was pierced through the chest. It flew weakly with the arrow for a distance before falling into the forest.

But the harpies rushing at them didn't give up just because their leader had fallen. They shrieked and dove into the forest. One, in its frenzy, clipped the pine branches with its wings and crashed to the ground — then got up, dragging a broken wing, and charged at Jace. Before it could reach him, a flying battle axe smashed its head to pieces.

Looking in the direction the axe had come from, Jace saw a harpy diving at Grede. The dwarf hadn't had time to retrieve his hammer. He swung his stone axe, but the harpy flew higher and dodged. Jace used Gorefiend's Grasp to yank the harpy to his side, and Morfil perfectly cooperated by splitting open its chest with a single sword stroke.

Grede pulled his hammer back and immediately hurled it at several harpies flying toward Musa. This time the hammer trailed lightning and set off a chain of explosions. Jace was about to check on Vereesa when he heard a whistle of something cutting close. He tried to dodge but it was too late — an arrow had struck him in the ribs on his back.

Jace felt a sharp pain in his back, but red fel runes had already appeared on his skin. The succubus, who had been staring upward, suddenly turned her head.

"Master?"

She must have felt the pain through the Soul Link. Had the arrow hit his kidney? With the imp's Blood Pact and the succubus's Soul Link... he should be fine, right?

Jace hadn't expected harpies to have archers. He looked in the direction the arrow had come from. The harpy was flapping her wings — she was holding a bow with her talons!

She flapped hard, gained some altitude, and used the moment of hang time to quickly nock another arrow and aim. But before she could loose, an arrow suddenly pierced her skull. The harpy's body went rigid and dropped into the forest.

Jace touched his back but felt no blood.

Sazeno glanced behind him and said, "Master, that arrow is just hanging there — it pierced through your outer hide cloak but didn't penetrate your robe. You're not hurt!"

So this was the power of a Fel Cloth robe?

Surprised as he was, Jace also remembered Jaline's words — don't push the robe's defenses just because it's much tougher than ordinary robes...

Being able to block a harpy's arrow probably didn't count as particularly impressive defense.

"Jace, are you all right?" Vereesa called over.

Jace tossed the arrow to the ground. "I'm fine."

The elf nodded hesitantly. Jace cast a Shadow Shield on himself. The moment the incantation ended, a harpy dropped from a tree, trying to ambush Musa from behind as she channeled nature magic. Jace fired a shadow bolt straight into her. The harpy shuddered and dropped to her knees, quickly becoming a rotting, broken corpse.

Compared to Fireball, casting a shadow bolt was not only simpler for Jace but easier to control. It could even shift direction slightly with his will — much like how he manipulated the gatherings of Gul'dan's Hand.

After casually blasting two harpies to death, Jace was starting to feel a bit addicted.

The group wiped out the band of harpy warriors summoned by the blue-feathered harpy and continued climbing toward the village. Suddenly a fierce wind blew up, kicking up sand and pine needles that immediately blinded Jace.

The wind wasn't natural — it was magically directed. But in the chaos, Jace couldn't tell where the caster was.

Compared to Jace, Vereesa's senses were far clearer in the chaos. She steadied herself, raised her bow, and loosed an arrow in a specific direction. The arrow, wreathed in arcane energy, cut straight through the wind and sand... and hit its target dead-on!

A scream nearly drowned out by the wind sounded, and the sandstorm died down by half.

As the sand and gravel cleared, a white-feathered harpy appeared, circling up high. She had an arrow in her chest, but she was still chanting loudly, raising her hands high. A dry thunderclap sounded in the night sky, and two lightning bolts struck her hands. The harpess was channeling element magic high above!

Vereesa raised her hand and loosed another arrow, piercing the harpy's skull mid-air. The gathered electrical energy immediately scattered, blowing the harpy mage to pieces. She fell into the forest like a rag.

"Too slow," the elf muttered, looking into the distance. She loosed another arrow, dropping a harpy that was drawing a bow to shoot at them.

Jace watched her pick off enemies with effortless, single-shot precision and couldn't help but imagine, for a split second, what a duel against her would look like — his spell barely two words in before she shot his mouth shut. Grim.

The sand and wind slowed their advance into the village, giving the enemy time to gather another group of unknown size to surround them. For a while, the treetops were full of flapping wings, dark shadows darting back and forth under the moonlight, and the harpies' piercing voices rising and falling. Even without understanding their language, Jace could feel their furious curses.

A harpy broke from the circling flock above and dove screaming at Musa. Vereesa tried to draw an arrow to intercept, but felt her quiver and realized she'd already loosed half the arrows she'd brought.

The rest of the road... was still long.

Of course, Musa was no fragile caster. She grabbed a stick she'd found somewhere and struck the harpy to the ground in one blow, then crushed its chest under a hoof.

But the numerous harpies overhead were still circling, waiting for more of their kin to gather for a larger assault. They'd clearly realized how tough this group of uninvited guests was.

"Keep moving forward, get away from me," Jace said, gripping his staff.

Grede immediately understood what Jace was about to do. He urged Vereesa to move quickly. Musa only hesitated for a moment before following the elf and the dwarf.

Seeing the three suddenly advance, some of the harpies circling in the treetops moved to pursue. Jace raised his blood magic staff and shouted the incantation for Fear. A wave of darkness engulfed the pine forest...

In an instant, countless terrifying harpy shrieks came from all directions. The sky suddenly cleared considerably. The chaotic shadows, the sound of wings scraping branches... all of it vanished.

Grede stopped and looked up. Vereesa's face had gone pale. Musa gripped a nearby tree trunk, staring back at Jace without daring to look away.
