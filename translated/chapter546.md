## Chapter 546: The Maraudine Priestess

At the entrance to the valley, several powerfully-built centaur stood posted, scanning the other centaur herds coming and going.

Armor plating covered their chests, shoulders, and hooves; each one carried a proper spear tipped with a metal point, rather than the sharpened sticks most centaur patrols dragged along behind them.

In the game, the valley where Maraudon sat was called Spearholder Valley — and looking at it now, the name was well earned.

Jace guessed these must be the guardians of the sacred grounds. If they wanted to enter Maraudon, their best bet was to start by talking to this lot.

"How do we go up there?" Grede asked quietly at his side.

"I think we just walk right up," Wynne said. "If we sneak around, they'll only get more suspicious."

Jace said to the hidden Morfell, "If those centaur try to make a move, kill one of their leaders however you like — throw them off rhythm and buy us a chance to escape."

"No problem, Master," the succubus said. "I'm half hoping those lesser creatures make the first move..."

"I've got to admit," Grede said, clearing his throat and looking at the succubus, "I was thinking the same thing."

Morfell flashed a smile. Jace looked at the dwarf. "Let's try not to get hurt. Healing potions are limited, we've got a long road ahead, and right now Mosa can't exactly patch us up properly in this dead land."

Grede nodded. "Just kidding. Let's go."

Wynne murmured an acknowledgment and stepped forward. Jace pulled up his hood, Grede tightened his cloak, gave the strange-looking cat wearing a Felguard iron helmet beside him a pat, and whistled softly.

A few centaur hauling fish baskets toward the sacred valley glanced over at the whistling, noticed the figures approaching, and came to a stop to watch.

Then the sanctuary guardians farther off caught sight of the commotion too. One of the tall centaur guards hefted his spear and strode toward them with a haughty gait, closing the distance while leveling his spear tip their way.

Wynne spread her arms wide and called out in the centaur tongue, telling them they needed a priestess who spoke Kaldorei. Hearing their own language from an elf's lips, the centaur hesitated — and slowly lowered his spear.

Jace and Grede exchanged a glance. This route was going to work, it seemed. These centaur weren't nearly as hostile toward outsiders as they'd imagined — at least not toward the Kaldorei.

Then again, Jace had seen this coming.

The centaur weren't a monolith. Desolace was home to numerous mutually hostile centaur tribes — the Gelkis, the Magram, and the Maraudine among them. In the game, players doing the quests here generally had to pick a side between the Gelkis and the Magram and fight the other two tribes.

That said, his plan wasn't without risk.

Guarding the Maraudon sacred grounds was the Maraudine tribe — the most powerful of them all, and the one least willing to cooperate with outsiders.

But it looked like "least willing" was only relative.

The Maraudine had a problem they couldn't solve on their own — the demons in Maraudon. Jace just didn't understand yet how the centaur outside had detected those demons so deep inside the tomb.

A centaur horn sounded from within the valley. The sanctuary guardians bellowed toward the heights, and answering calls echoed back from the cliffs above.

Grede glanced up at the centaur archers perched on the cliffs, then caught Wynne's eye. The two of them traded a look.

At that moment, Mosa stretched and let out a rumbling purr. She'd noticed the tight guard too.

Just as Wynne had predicted, this valley was essentially a centaur city, tightly defended by the Maraudine tribe — on the valley floor and among the ridges alike. Sneaking in unnoticed would be anything but easy.

Jace had to wonder how those goblin explorers had ever gotten in. Some strange alchemy potion or cloaking device, maybe.

In any case, Wynne's centaur had gotten the message across.

At the valley entrance, a centaur in a headscarf came out.

She was a female centaur — and not only a good deal smaller than the sanctuary guards, she stood shorter even than the other Maraudine male commoners hauling food supplies.

Yet her status was in no way beneath these males. Her steps were measured and her bearing elegant. She was draped in rune-carved stone pendants and bone chains, and the long staff she carried was capped with a peculiar gemstone that clearly brimmed with the power of nature.

She came toward the four of them with no guards at all, and with pure confidence. She stopped, looked Wynne over from above, then spoke softly.

Jace could tell it was elven — the pronunciation was a little off, but a couple of the words were ones he knew quite well.

Wynne answered openly. She came right out and said her name: Wynne.

When they heard the surname Wynne, the female centaur priestess actually smiled. She said something to the sanctuary guards behind her, and the male centaur immediately didn't bother hiding their feelings — they all started laughing too.

One of the most muscular centaur laughed so hard he doubled over, coughing. He wore armor, a headpiece denoting his status, and even silver adornments on his wrist-guards — clearly the leader of these guards.

Jace still had no idea what they found so funny. Wynne glanced at them, showed no particular reaction, and kept conversing in Thalassian.

It was obvious that Kaldorei and Thalassian differed at least somewhat — the centaur priestess couldn't make out every word precisely, and the exchange moved slowly.

But when Wynne mentioned demons and Maraudon, Jace could sense a subtle shift in the centaur's attitude.

The priestess glanced back at the sanctuary guards again, then turned and said something to Wynne in a contemptible tone.

The completely unintelligible back-and-forth was already wearing on Grede's patience, but rather than derail what still seemed like a productive conversation, he just drew a deep breath. Jace noticed his fingers drumming anxiously at his belt.

Wynne seemed to sense the impatience behind her too. She glanced back at Jace and said, "These centaur don't trust us. They think we're probably thieves using this as an excuse to go in and steal centaur relics."

"Only natural," Jace said. "Ask them how we can prove ourselves."

## Flags

- **Chapter title**: 玛洛迪祭司 — "The Maraudine Priestess" (玛洛迪 = Maraudine, the centaur clan guarding Maraudon).
- **Spearholder Valley**: 长矛谷 — the canonical name for the valley containing Maraudon in the game.
- **Centaur tribes**: The Gelkis (吉尔吉斯), Magram (玛格拉姆), and Maraudine (玛洛迪) clans — canonical WoW centaur factions in Desolace.
- **Morfell**: 莫洛菲尔 — Jace's succubus, hidden nearby.
- **Mosa's disguise**: The great cat wearing Grede's Felguard helmet — a running visual gag.
- **Wynne's name**: The centaur laugh at the surname "Windrunner" — likely because it relates to wind, which the centaur worship (hinted at by Mosa earlier).
- **Thalassian vs. Kaldorei language**: The priestess speaks Thalassian with difficulty; the ancient Kaldorei language differs from modern Thalassian (the high elves' language).
- **The priestess**: A female centaur shaman/priestess, smaller than the male warriors but clearly of high status — indicated by her rune ornaments, bone jewelry, and nature-infused gem staff.
- **Chapter cliffhanger**: The centaur don't trust the group and want proof of their strength — setting up the duel in the next chapter (第547章).
- **Naming note**: This chapter was updated to match the project's established naming conventions (Wynne, Morfell, Maraudine, Spearholder Valley, Felguard) for consistency with chapters 542-545 and later chapters.
