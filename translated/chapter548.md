## Chapter 548: The Ancestor of Maraudon

A centaur sanctuary guard led the three humans and one big cat deeper into Spearholder Valley. They passed through earthen walls one after another, watchtowers rammed together from stone and soil, and row upon row of cheval-de-frise made of blackened wooden spikes — all defensive works, most likely built to fend off other centaur tribes.

This really was a centaur city. Hide tents and stone dwellings lined both sides of the path, and centaur children hid inside them, looking for all the world like a herd of foals.

As far as Jace knew from the game, this was the only place in all of Desolace — perhaps in all of Kalimdor — that the centaur had built anything worthy of being called a city.

But this city was occupied solely by the Maraudine tribe. Were the Maraudine truly powerful enough to build a city that the other tribes couldn't even conceive of?

He guessed that a long, long time ago, the centaur must have been united. This was probably the ancient capital of all centaurkind — it had only later fractured and been seized exclusively by the Maraudine tribe.

"Hey, elf, did you talk about payment?" Grede asked quietly as he caught up to the elf's pace.

"Of course," Wynne said. "If I hadn't brought up payment, you and him would've gone and fought me over it, wouldn't you?"

"Hah, not exactly," the dwarf said with a chuckle. "Can they pay in gold? Silver? Or some other kind of object?"

"If we resolve the demon problem, they're willing to give us a gemstone ornament," Wynne said. "She wasn't clear about exactly what it is — maybe I didn't understand. But she did make it clear that a few years back, a goblin offered six gold coins for this thing, because it had been brought out from deep within the sacred tomb."

"Six gold coins," Jace said. "If a goblin was willing to pay that much, it's definitely worth far more than that."

"The centaur must know that too, which is why they never even thought about selling it," Wynne said.

"Turns out you were right after all, Jace," Grede said. "Even six gold coins is no small sum. Why would the centaur be willing to put up such a high price to put a bounty on the demons in the tomb?"

Wynne said, "I don't know either. That centaur didn't tell me much beyond that. Once I learned about the reward and the way to prove our strength, I didn't press her further."

Jace said, "I'm guessing these demons aren't just hiding in there. They must be disturbing the souls in the centaur sacred tomb."

Wynne asked, "You mean the demons are using the souls of the centaur's ancient warriors for dark rituals?"

Jace said, "Souls represent far too many important things to demons. Powerful souls that a centaur tribe holds in reverence would naturally be even more valuable. Of course, that's just a guess on my part."

"Powerful, revered souls… demons attempting dark rituals… you're probably right," Wynne murmured. Then she asked, "By the way, did your two lackeys follow us in?"

Jace said, "They're right here beside us."

"I hope she really is as sharp as you say," Wynne said, glancing warily to the side — except the direction she was looking had no demon in it at all. Morfell was walking on the other side.

As they pushed deeper into Spearholder Valley and rounded a corner, Jace was adjusting his hood and smoothing his hair when Grede looked up toward the high slope and suddenly sucked in a sharp breath.

"What is it?" Jace asked, and immediately looked up in that direction — just in time to see a terrifying spear-wielding monster crouching on the high slope, a pair of blood-red eyes staring off into the distance…

It was a stone statue. Its eyes were a pair of enormous red crystals of unknown origin — crystals that were distributed quite widely deep within Maraudon. The goblins had risked the depths of Maraudon specifically to search for these crystals.

Cradled in the statue's arms was a stone gate. Several powerfully built centaur warriors paced back and forth around it. That was the entrance to the Maraudon sacred tomb.

Jace couldn't quite tell what kind of creature the statue was supposed to be. It crouched low, and in posture it looked more like a demon — yet its body was engraved with centaur runes.

Given that the sacred tomb had been built by centaur, this was most likely the image of an ancestor or deity as the centaur imagined it.

In truth, the centaur of Desolace were all children of the earth elementalist princess Theradras.

When Theradras drained the life energy of the entire region of Maanghe, the sudden loss of life power startled Cenarius far to the north in Ashenvale Forest.

Cenarius was a demigod — half man, half stag. He was the teacher of the archdruid Malfurion Stormrage, and the teacher of all the night elf druids, a forest guardian who had watched over all of Kalimdor for tens of thousands of years.

He sent his son Zaetar to Maanghe to find out what had happened here. He never expected that after Zaetar discovered the culprit — the earth elementalist princess Theradras — he would quickly be drawn in by the boundless life power she commanded.

It was hard to say whether Zaetar was attracted to Theradras, deceived by her, or bewitched by the hidden power of an Old God. In any case, legend held that the union of the two gave birth to the twisted and savage centaur. Even Zaetar himself came to dislike these murderous children. After a long passage of time, a gradually sobering Zaetar began to reflect on it all, even coming to see himself as a sinner.

Perhaps because of their chaotic nature, perhaps because of the hatred born of being scorned by their father, the ancient centaur ancestors eventually banded together and killed their own father. Theradras furiously drove out these patricidal children — and yet the centaur still regarded this cavern as sacred ground.

Perhaps the statue at this gate was what the later generations of centaur imagined their father looked like.

Then again, this was only Jace's guess. After all, the monster at this gate looked nothing at all like Cenarius and his children. Cenarius and his kin — the forest guardians — actually had the faces and upper bodies of aged night elves.

Thinking of this, Jace suddenly realized that in the game, the cavern deep within Maraudon where Princess Theradras dwelled seemed to be open to the sky.

Which meant that if they could slip past the centaur surveillance and climb over these valleys, they might be able to reach the earth elementalist princess's lair directly — just drop in from above. For all he knew, the goblins had gotten into the deepest natural caverns by exactly this method, bypassing the centaur tomb's main gate entirely.

In the game, of course, you couldn't do that. Models, invisible walls, instance phasing — all of it stopped you.

But here, it wasn't an impossible task. Just a far more dangerous approach than going in through the front gate.

The natural caverns deep within Maraudon had complex terrain, and they were teeming with all manner of elementals in the service of Princess Theradras, plus every kind of savage beast — hydra monsters, burrowing worm swarms, and more. Dropping straight in would probably mean dying without a burial.

Better to go through the front gate, advance steadily, and explore step by step. That was the reliable way.

The stone gate slowly opened. The guardians at the sacred tomb entrance stepped aside, and the centaur soldier guiding them mounted the steps. Maraudon lay just ahead.

## Flags

- **centaur** (半人马): half-human, half-horse race; the Maraudine (玛洛迪部族) guard the sacred valley.
- **Maraudon** (玛拉顿): the dungeon in Desolace; Jace's destination.
- **Spearholder Valley** (长矛谷): the valley housing Maraudon's entrance.
- **sacred tomb / sacred grounds** (圣墓 / 圣地): the centaur holy site within Maraudon.
- **Theradras** (瑟莱德丝): the earth elementalist princess; mother of the centaur; dwells in the deepest part of Maraudon. (Glossry uses 瑟莱德丝; canonical spelling Theradras.)
- **Maanghe / Mangan** (马珊希): the region whose life force Theradras drained, giving birth to the centaur. (Name tentative — canonical lore uses "Maraudon" region; the novel gives 马珊希.)
- **Cenarius** (塞纳留斯): the half-man, half-stag demigod; forest guardian of Kalimdor.
- **Malfurion Stormrage** (玛法里奥·怒风): the archdruid; Cenarius's student. (Glossary gives 玛法里奥; canonical full name Malfurion Stormrage.)
- **Zaetar** (扎尔塔): Cenarius's son; father of the centaur.
- **Old God** (上古之神): the hidden power implied to have influenced Zaetar and Theradras's union.
- **red crystals** (红水晶): the crystals deep within Maraudon that goblins mine; form the statue's eyes.
- **Morfell** (莫洛菲尔): Jace's succubus, traveling with the party.
- **Mosa** (莫萨): the tauren druid traveling with the party.
- **soul link** (灵魂链接): the warlock bond through which Jace senses Morfell's emotions.
