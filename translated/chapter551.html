Chapter 551: Enlisting Every Potential Ally

Jace ducked behind a stalagmite beside a rock wall, but a long moment passed and nothing seemed to stir. Had he misread Morophil's warning? But that couldn't be… the signal had been clear and urgent enough. Jace cast Sense Demons and peered in that direction. In the darkness, a shadow stood stock-still in the middle of the river not far ahead. The thing was actually invisible to the naked eye in the dark — some kind of invisibility magic? The shadow kept advancing step by step, its movements cautious, as if it had noticed something amiss over here. When it passed a purple crystal, the light illuminated its goat-like, hairy face, a pair of demon horns curving high. This was definitely a satyr. One more step and it would spot Grede hiding behind a low boulder. Windrunner drew and loosed in one clean motion. Jace was certain no human could have moved that fast, but the instant the bowstring snapped taut, the satyr before them simply vanished. The arrow was true — but it struck nothing, smashing into the opposite rock face and shattering. Windrunner froze. The satyr had already appeared behind her! Jace snapped off a spell without hesitation. The satyr was seized by Fear, hesitating for a beat and glancing this way. Windrunner had just registered the demon at her back when Morophil materialized behind the satyr and drove a blade straight through its heart. The satyr coughed, staggered a few steps forward, and collapsed into the river, its body withering rapidly. The demon's corpse tumbled downstream in the current, its mane buffeted by the rushing water, swaying back and forth. Shadow energy poured from the stab wound on its back, staining the water a faint blackish-purple. Windrunner glanced at the succubus wiping demon blood off her dark blade beside her, then at the demon's corpse disappearing into the depths of the water cave. "What kind of magic was that just now?" "A demon's Shadowstep, elf," Morophil said with a slight curl of her lips. "These demons can slip between shadows using Shadow. Be careful." Windrunner nodded slowly, her expression somewhat strained. Her throat bobbed — whether from the aftershock of the attack that had nearly caught her from behind, or from the sheer strangeness of being warned by a demon, it was hard to tell. "I've never actually fought a satyr before," she murmured. "What in the hell… what even is this, I haven't even processed it yet," Grede said from the water. "So the demons here aren't like the ones at the Dark Portal, Grede," Jace said, looking at him. "From here on out, we need to be extremely careful." The dwarf nodded. The cocky confidence that had filled his face moments ago had faded considerably. "Are there more of these demons inside? Satyr demons." "Did you sense any up ahead?" Jace asked the succubus. "The stench of satyrs fills this cave, master," Morophil said. "I can feel at least ten, maybe twenty satyrs lying in ambush in there, and they're not all the same strength." "Twenty whole demons that can use that Shadow magic?" Grede sighed. "Look for an opening. Pick them off one by one when they're not together," Windrunner said. "Let's keep moving. If their numbers are small, they'll soon realize one of their own is missing and grow wary — that'll make things harder for us." With that, everyone nodded and pressed on, following Morophil's lead. They were close to the water cave's exit now. Climbing up, they entered another vast chamber in one go. Morophil slipped in first to scout, confirming no demons or other creatures lay in ambush, and the rest of them filed out of the water cave and into the space. Here, Jace finally felt a flicker of recognition. This was another ancient temple — it didn't look like something centaurs had built. It was more like an elf's shrine. The style of the cave mouths, the walls, the stone pillars was strikingly like the ancient Kaldorei ruins they'd seen in Desolace. They entered a corridor, its walls even carved all over with various runes… But these runes weren't in Kaldorei or Quel'Thalas script. They were in demonic. At the end of the corridor stood a pair of enormous braziers, each taller than a man. No fire burned in them, but they reeked of concentrated Shadow — this place had likely burned Shadowfire once. The passage beyond the corridor glowed with a blue-purple light, probably lit by the Shadowfire from braziers like these. The cave was already thick with the reek of Shadow corruption; Jace could barely tell where the Shadow energy was stronger anymore and where it was weaker. They were just about to leave the corridor when a strange sound drifted in from the passage outside. It was strange not because it was unfamiliar, but because it had no business being in an underground cave — a sound like wooden branches scraping across the ground, reminding Jace of a broom sweeping a floor. The sound drew closer. The group crouched beneath the massive stone braziers, eyes fixed on the entrance. Before long, a shadow over two meters tall shuffled into view. It was a thick, walking tree root — a small treant. The treant had been corrupted by Shadow, its bark covered in black scorch marks. It swayed along on several thick roots extending from its base, a pair of golden eyes flecked with flickers of purple chaotic energy scanning left and right. When it reached the corridor entrance it stopped, peering into the corridor. Everyone held their breath — for all they knew, their voices or scent might give them away. But clearly, it had already sensed something amiss here. The treant had barely stepped inside when Musa, who had shifted back into her tauren form at some point, walked straight out from behind the brazier and up to the treant. Grede and Jace both widened their eyes, wondering what in the world she was doing. But Musa said something to the treant, and the treant just listened patiently. Instead of attacking, it turned and left the corridor, continuing on its way. No one dared speak. They exchanged glances, and only when the treant's footsteps had nearly faded did Musa say something to Windrunner. Windrunner said, "These are the ancient guardians of this cave. The demons have used Shadow magic to cloud their minds, tricking them into guarding the grounds for the demons. But at least they still remember who they truly are. Musa will help us persuade these guardians to look the other way." "It'd be even better if Musa could talk them into helping us," Grede muttered. Windrunner looked at the dwarf, then said something to Musa. Musa blinked in surprise, then said in Common, "I will try." "I told you," the imp whispered. "This place doesn't welcome demons at all! Those ill-intentioned Burning Legion lackeys are in for it!" "You sound mighty pleased, as if you weren't a demon yourself," Grede grumbled.
