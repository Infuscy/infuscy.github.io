Chapter 553: Vyletongue

"This way," Grede said. "That satyr was clearly running to report in — so the direction it came from is likely where their leader is!"

The others nodded. Everyone was thinking the same thing.

Morophil went invisible and took point. The rest followed her lead as they pressed on. All along the way were treants, snapped and toppled, and several satyrs with their bodies torn open, their heads ripped off, and their chests split apart, lying on the ground.

There weren't many forks ahead. Following the Shadowflame braziers straight forward, on a high platform near a sacrificial altar, a treant was savagely tearing at another treant. Their battle was ferocious — one could even call it insane.

One of the treants had already had an arm torn off, yet it clung to the other treant like a beast, refusing to let go. The two were ripped from one side of the path to the other, then staggered back again. One of them had little Shadow left in its eyes, but the other's eyes blazed like kindled Shadowflame.

Not all the treants had been roused from demonic servitude. They still served the satyr leader here and, following his orders, moved to eliminate every treant affected by Mosa's influence.

The group slipped along the wall beneath the platform, descended a long flight of stairs, and suddenly the space opened up before them.

They quickly ducked behind a stone railing for cover. Jace lifted his head and looked out — this was a hall built like a temple. Jace could hardly imagine a temple like this had been built by the centaur driven from the depths of Maraudon.

Interlocking stone stairways with finely wrought railings led upward. The pool below, though it reeked by now, still showed signs that this had once been a garden-like place.

The temple proper up above was ancient and solemn — pure Kaldorei in style. But the Shadowflame braziers everywhere, with their purple fel light, lent the temple's beauty a sinister edge.

Jace was certain that either the satyrs had fashioned this place, or it was an ancient Kaldorei temple long ago corrupted.

They had entered this temple from the upper side. The main entrance meant crossing the pool below. Just then, a treant covered in jagged wounds staggered in through the front, into the pool. It threw its head back and let out a hollow roar — and was immediately struck by a black arrow soaring in from above. Where the black arrow hit, a terrifying Shadow corrosion spread. The treant quickly rotted into a mass of black, festering rubble and collapsed into the water.

The satyr who'd loomed above and fired the shot stood a full two heads taller than the satyrs around him. His black horns curved backward, and his mane was a bright purple — completely different from the withered dark-yellow, deep-brown, or black-purple of the other satyrs. He was not only far more heavily built; more importantly, his body was covered in runes inscribed with demonic power.

As soon as this treant fell, a pack of bounding imps rushed in, scrambling up the stairs toward the satyrs atop the temple, reporting on the situation outside in Demonic as they ran.

Jace remembered that in Ashenvale and other places where the Kaldorei lived, there were indeed forest creatures that looked just like these imps. The Kaldorei called them grell, and they had a close connection to the satyrs — these must be the hornless little imps.

"Master, be careful!"

"Master! Master Vyletongue… Master's palace…"

"Everything will return to normal at once, hah!"

"The culprit has already been killed by the powerful satyr masters — truly, too powerful!"

"The Princess's peace will not be disturbed…"

"Absolutely not!"

"Idiot. The intruders are already close." The bow-wielding satyr lord roared in a heavy voice that echoed without end through the temple.

"Worthless wretches — can you not sense the magical aura the intruders are giving off? Get out! Keep searching!"

"Yes, master!"

"We will do our utmost…"

The little demons chattered as they chased each other back down the stairs, crossed the shallow pool, and left the temple. The bow-wielding satyr must be Vyletongue himself, the satyr lord of Maraudon.

"I expect it's just another Quel'dorei arcanist, Master Vyletongue," another satyr said then. "Or a daydreaming slave eager to curry favor with Remulos, come here to die."

Vyletongue said nothing — whether he couldn't be bothered to answer or agreed with the assessment, there was no telling. But from what the imps had said, it was clear the chaos the treants were causing outside wouldn't last long. If they wanted to settle matters here, they had to move fast.

By now the temple hall was deathly quiet. Jace worried that the faintest whisper would be heard by the satyrs outside. He drew a simple map of the palace in the damp mud piled against the nearby wall, and marked Vyletongue's position in the center of the temple.

He pointed at Grede, then at himself, and tapped the pool on the little mud map. Vereesa pressed a finger straight onto Vyletongue, then pointed to herself.

Jace nodded, then pointed at Morophil, signaling for the two of them to go together.

He was confident that in a fair one-on-one fight, this Vyletongue shouldn't be Vereesa's match. But considering the satyr might have demonic assassins lurking in the shadows to protect him, the succubus would need to help as much as possible.

Vereesa slid her eyes toward Morophil but raised no objection — in the water cave earlier, she'd already seen just how dangerous the Shadow power of these satyrs could be.

After that, Jace, Grede, and Mosa would handle the frontal assault, drawing the attention of the other satyrs.

Jace pointed at Mosa, then clapped Grede on the shoulder — keep the dwarf safe. Mosa nodded at once.

Grede lifted the helmet off Mosa's head and clapped it onto his own. He shot Jace an "all good" gesture, then hefted his hammer.

The dwarf glanced up toward Vyletongue — and lightning suddenly exploded across the head of his war hammer.

They'd been in the dark cave far too long. The only light to speak of was the kind brought by dark power. The group had long since grown used to the gloomy, half-lit environment. The sudden burst of electrical energy flashed across Jace's vision and left him seeing white — for a moment he could barely see a thing.

That violent flash of lightning naturally drew the attention of every satyr in the temple in an instant. But the next moment, the war hammer was hurled straight at Vyletongue. The blinding light swept across the pool and the shadowed walls, lighting up the entire enormous cavern as bright as midday. Before any of the demons could react, it smashed through the railing and stone wall ahead with a satisfying crash. The electrical energy blew the nearest satyr clean off its feet, and the elemental force from the blast knocked the satyrs beside it off balance.

"What is this… what IS this?!" Vyletongue roared in Demonic between coughs. "To barge straight into my temple? To disturb Princess Theradras's peace so brazenly — to march right up to me and defy my rule?!"
