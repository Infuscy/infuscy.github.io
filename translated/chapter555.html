Chapter 555: Clash

Though Grede was itching to roar and chase down the demon and fight him to the death, he and Musa had in fact burned through most of their strength on the stairs beside the pool. Jace knew how difficult it was to summon even a scrap of usable Life Force for healing in a place choked with demonic power and Shadow breath, where the Life Elementals were running rampant.

Just then, another satyr suddenly darted out of the shadows behind them. Jace could even feel the Shadow power enchanting its dagger about to stab into the back of his neck.

He turned to raise his staff to block — but the satyr took a flying axe buried in its shoulder, howled, and lurched forward a few steps. Jace drew his sword and ran it through the satyr's heart. The satyr growled, seized Jace's wrist, and wrenched him aside — but once the sword was pulled free, the demonic blood pumping from its heart sent it crumpling to the ground. It fell, no strength left for a second assault.

Grede ran out and wrenched his axe free. Jace stared at the satyr's corpse. Its build and appearance weren't much different from the satyrs that had been mobbing Grede earlier — and its strength was no less than Jace's, even a bit greater. It drove home for Jace why Grede had needed to Avatar up and go toe-to-toe with those satyrs.

The demons hiding in this cave definitely had some real muscle.

"Probably the demons that were fighting the treants back in those caves, come after us, Jace," Grede said. "Get up front and help — you've still still got the legs to keep running. I'll watch our backs and catch up quick. Got an eight-foot tauren backing me up — nothing to worry about!"

With that, the dwarf tossed his axe to the tauren and unslung the small round shield from his back. Jace tossed a mana potion to Grede. "Give it to Musa. Take care of her, and let Musa take care of you."

"Got it. Go help those two!" Grede shouted.

Jace nodded. He couldn't let up — he still had strength left. He pushed himself to the limit, chasing the crisp ring of clashing blades from up ahead.

There were two satyr assassin corpses along the way. As he ran, Jace used Sense Demons to scan the shadows in the tunnel for any satyrs lying in wait. The furious roars of demons echoed without end from the deep of the cave. He ducked through a narrow doorway into a darkened chamber — and saw the tall satyr lord, two longswords in hand, going toe-to-toe with Windrunner and Morophil. And for the moment, he wasn't falling behind at all!

"It's you… the human. No matter — a few more won't matter either! I have existed in this perfect form on this world for an age beyond your comprehension, you lowly mongrels. Your ancestors trembled in the dust before the might of the Legion — and you think you can stand against me now? Think you can kill me so easily?!"

Vile'tharn didn't just speak Common — he spoke it fluently. He roared his fury, swinging his blades wildly to hold off both opponents at once. Jace immediately raised his staff and began chanting a Curse of Weakness — but one of Vile'tharn's eyes suddenly slid over and locked onto him.

"What?! Shadow magic?! I AM Shadow!!"

Vile'tharn slipped past a thrust from Windrunner and suddenly vanished into Shadow. Where he disappeared, a mass of Shadow energy like poisonous mist rapidly spread. The succubus and the elf had no choice but to back up a few steps to avoid it.

But the next moment the satyr reappeared — a dozen meters away, right behind Jace!

The shadows hadn't even cleared before the demon's two blades cleaved out of the dark mist in a slashing strike. Jace felt the blood in his body surge searingly all at once, and with a fierce, hot burst of strength he threw himself to one side, dodging the slash.

Was that Sazeno's Blood Pact? Jace had no time to think — because the moment that blow was dodged, the demon's thrust came almost instantly. Jace barely deflected it with his staff, nearly failing to knock it aside. The creature's strength was still terrifyingly immense.

And not only strength — the satyr lord's Shadow resistance was absurdly high. Though he'd been hit with the Curse of Weakness, the effect was barely noticeable. The satyr lord was clearly a little surprised that this warlock before him could still block his attack. He stepped forward and swung his blade again — but it bit into the Shadow Shield and was thrown back, the spreading Shadow forcing him a step backward.

Jace had used that opening to summon another Shadow Shield around himself. This time, Vile'tharn's expression changed.

"Nathre…"

Seizing the chance, Jace unleashed Immolate straight at his chest. Fierce Shadow fire exploded from within Vile'tharn's torso, and the satyr's mane burst into flames.

The Shadow magic hadn't done any significant damage to Vile'tharn at all — it was the fire that hit harder. No wonder he'd felt so strained fighting this demon chieftain this whole time, every spell seeming to barely take hold, while the demon himself was acutely sensitive to how Jace's Shadow magic took effect — even able to easily dodge and predict it!

"Use your fire magic, Sazeno — wherever you are!"

"Master, aiya, at a time like this do you think I've got mana to spare?! You'd best be careful — because the next Blood Pact's got no juice left either!"

The imp shouted from some hidden corner. The demon, now ablaze, roared and shook off the flames, raising both blades to charge again—

With a crisp clang, Windrunner had already reached the front and parried his attack cleanly!

The satyr, deflected to the side, slashed again. Their blades met and locked. The satyr growled low, "Savage bitch — you haven't inherited a shred of our ancestors' combat technique. You're no different from a rabid beast snapping wild!"

"You vile creature covered in beast fur — how dare you judge true lineage?!" Windrunner shoved the satyr's twin blades back in fury, driving her blade into him in a rapid thrust. The demon fell back steadily, raising both swords to block Windrunner's assault. The two exchanged blows too fast to follow.

By now Morophil had caught up as well, circling behind and charging at the demon. Vile'tharn realized he was being attacked from both front and back, and shifted into a mass of Shadow, moving some distance to dodge Windrunner's blade. His eyes erupted in purple flame, and energy. The succubus chased faster, overtaking the elf, and Vile'tharn immediately swung a blade in a ferocious frontal strike. Morophil had no choice but to dodge — but the satyr pressed on with consecutive attacks, and within two or three blows Morophil was being suppressed with no room to counter at all.

Had Windrunner not pressed in from the side in time, forcing Vile'tharn back step by step, she very well might have been hit before she could get away.

This was the first time Jace had seen Morophil get the worse of it in a fight at this tempo. In truth, the attack speed of all three parties was astonishingly fast.

Against an enemy this agile, the best tool to use, of course, was…

Since Shadow power was doing real difficulty against this demon chieftain, Jace picked up the Blue-Sky Song. The moment Vile'tharn was too preoccupied to watch him, Jace spoke the incantation for Conjure Water — and in the time it took to cast, the satyr, the elf, and the succubus had already exchanged five or six rounds!

The incantation complete, a sheet of frost immediately froze over Vile'tharn's hooves, the ice spreading rapidly.

His feet frozen in place in one move, he wrenched at them — only to step onto the ice beside them and slip. In that one stumbling step, Morophil's blade was already thrusting at his heart!

Vile'tharn staggered back a few steps and barely deflected the succubus's attack. Windrunner drove both her swords straight through his chest from behind, the blades punching out through his front. The demon's body snapped rigid all at once. The pair of eyes glowing with Shadow light flickered out, and he murmured a few words in the high elves' tongue.

One step's delay, and the demon's life was forfeit.

Windrunner pulled her twin swords free. Black-purple blood poured from the satyr lord's back. His lips trembled as he took a few steps forward, then his powerful body crashed to the ground, heavy as a massive buck.
