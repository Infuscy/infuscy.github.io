## Chapter 556: No Way We're Wasting the Loot

The moment the satyr hit the ground, Vereesa backed up against a broken wall to catch her breath. A little while later, the clatter of footsteps and the clop of hooves came from outside as Grede and Mosa hurried in.

The dwarf was already pulling the black bow off the demon's corpse. "Now that this demon's dead — any chance the elf woman we're looking for is hiding in here too? Any of you see anyone else?"

"I didn't see a thing when I came in," Jace said. He looked to Vereesa, and she said, "Aside from this demon chieftain… there's no one else."

She glanced at the succubus beside them. "Did you notice anything?"

"Of course not," Morophil said. "If I'd noticed any other enemy, I wouldn't have stayed silent about it, master."

The ranger nodded. Jace turned to the imp hopping around beside them. "Can you sense any other casters nearby?"

"Where?" Sazeno looked around, twisting his head this way and that. "From the very beginning… I haven't sensed any mages in here, master — except for this Quel'dorei right in front of me. She's a genuine ball of arcane energy… blinding!"

"Quit the chatter," Jace said. "If there's nothing, then you're useless to me for now."

"I think so too," Vereesa said. "She probably was never here to begin with. If she really were, she wouldn't have sat in here and watched us defeat all these demons without lifting a finger. And if she were actually here, the magical traces would be far stronger than this, Jace."

Jace said, "Morophil, do you remember what those satyrs were saying when we closed in?"

The succubus answered, "They thought the commotion we were making was caused by yet another Quel'dorei mage."

"You actually understood what those satyrs were saying?" Grede's eyes went wide. Then he caught himself and scratched his head. "Damn it, for a second there I'd almost forgotten this woman's a demon."

Vereesa said, "You mean they've encountered a Quel'dorei mage before? And how do you know they were saying 'Darkeye' and not just talking about the corpses we ran into in the water cave?"

"Too early to say for sure," Jace said. "Maybe if we search this temple top to bottom, we'll turn up some clues. By the way — are you hurt, Vereesa?"

"I'm fine." Vereesa asked, "And you?"

"No," Jace said. "I'm all right. That was a close one."

Jace glanced at Morophil. He could tell the succubus hadn't taken any real damage, and he relaxed.

Vereesa looked over at the succubus, hesitated a moment, then said, "You're impressive, sayaad. Thank you for throwing yourself in front of me several times to block those satyr guards' Shadow attacks."

Morophil said offhandedly, "Throw myself in front? Keeping you alive is just the master's order, that's all."

Jace glared at Morophil. "Repeat after me: 'You're welcome, it was the least I could do' — let me teach you a handy little Common phrase you'll use all the time, Morophil."

Morophil clutched a soul shard radiating a ghastly purple light, leaned in, and gently tucked it into Jace's robe. Reluctantly, she said, "Fine, master… it was the least I could do."

Vereesa was clearly displeased. She shot a glare at the demon leaning against Jace. "My mistake. Why would I thank a warlock's demon servant."

Jace was about to explain when Grede immediately stepped forward. "Come on, Vereesa. We just defeated a demon chieftain who'd been hiding underground for God knows how long — this is a time to be happy! I'll grant you, Miss Morophil is a demon through and through, but at least she's on our side! Without her we wouldn't have won so easily, right?"

"I'm warning you — don't trust a demon just because you trust Jace Sesso, dwarf." Vereesa took a deep breath. "Fine. I'm going back into the temple to look for other clues. There might be something related to our target… no, I'm certain there will be. I can already feel it in here."

"It's only temporary trust…" The dwarf watched Vereesa walk away, then glanced at Mosa, who was leaving alongside her, deep in conversation, and let out a quiet sigh.

The imp stood off to the side watching the two groups, hopping back and forth with a look like he was barely holding in a laugh. The exhaustion and weariness he'd complained of during the fight had vanished without a trace.

"I bloody well gave it everything I had, Jace," Grede said, turning back. "Never in my wildest dreams did I think the day would come when Grede Thunderfist would stick up for a demon."

"There's nothing strange about speaking up for a sayaad," Morophil said, leaning against the wall.

"Right — I must've been charmed. That explains it." Grede shot her a look, then said to Jace, "From now on I can't look this demon straight in the eyes anymore."

Jace sat down and said to Grede, "This is my fault. I didn't think this situation through."

He looked at Morophil. "Don't talk to Vereesa like that anymore, Morophil. I know you understand what I mean, and I won't explain it further — can't you just be a little friendlier? Even pretending would do."

Morophil raised her eyes. "The master really does care about that Quel'dorei's feelings. Well, if the master says so…"

"You might as well just order this succubus not to talk to Vereesa at all — might improve things a lot," Grede grumbled. But then he made a face. "Still, straightforward communication does seem better. If Vereesa talks to her again and she just keeps her mouth shut, wouldn't that seem even stranger? Ah, I don't know what to do either."

"Let's just leave it for now," Jace said, standing up. He eyed Vyletongue's corpse sprawled on the ground. "Now — help me skin this demon. I want his horns and his hide."

"You want to flay him?" Grede asked.

"You mean you don't know demon hide is worth a fortune?" Jace said. "We came here to make money, Grede, so don't look at me like that. We can't carry stuff off all the other satyrs, but we're taking the tattoos off this demon lord's skin."

Hearing there was money in it, Grede rubbed his hands together. "So that's the play. All right, how do we do it?"

"The two of us lop his head off to take back to the centaur. The rest of him is ours." Jace looked at Morophil. "Come on, Morophil. Drop that patently fake jealous act you're putting on for me. You do the skinning — you're the professional. Careful with it… don't damage the tattoos."
