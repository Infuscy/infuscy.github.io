Chapter 563: The Burrowing Leviathan

Moving on to the edge of the waterfall and looking up, the light source above was no longer a blurry sliver of sky — it was a broad expanse of open air.

If you were looking in from the outside, you'd probably think this was a massive sinkhole.

The near cliff walls and the far ones were studded with stone pillars of uneven lengths and pockmarked surfaces, like upright Buddha palms.

Between those "fingers" — waterfall after waterfall, large and small, pouring their waters down into the vast lake below.

These stone pillars were nothing like the Highborne structures up above. Their surfaces were rough and pockmarked, clearly formed by the influence of some violent magic — perhaps a single outburst of Therazane's rage had reshaped the terrain, rock surging up in a frenzy to create this bizarre landscape.

While Wynne, Mossa, and Grede were lost in the magnificence of the scenery, Jace kept his eyes on the pool behind them and the dark depths of the ravine and caverns.

He remembered that this place was home to a good number of malicious elementals, plus a host of crazed dryads that attacked intruders on sight.

The dryads were also descendants of the demigod Cenarius, so they resembled Zaltar — the lower body of a deer, the upper body like a beautiful, petite female elf.

Dryads far outnumbered the tall and strong sons of Cenarius, so they served as scouts and servants. In the game, countless corrupted dryads hid in the depths of Maraudon, but here Jace hadn't seen a single one.

Nor had he seen their leader — the corrupted son of Cenarius who lingered here guarding Zaltar's tomb. Jace had already forgotten his name.

"So, how do we go on? We jumping?" Grede whispered. He glanced down, then added, "From this height, jumping down and hitting the water might crack my backside clean open."

"Before you worry about breaking anything, you'd better worry about that thing," Wynne said, pointing to the middle of the pool.

Everyone looked where she pointed. Jace saw a white serpentine shadow drifting closer from afar, gliding slowly across the lake's surface — the head of some serpentine monster, reminding Jace of the Loch Ness Monster photographs.

When it turned to swim back, another head rose from the water. Then another. Then yet another. A white three-headed serpentine monster…

This was the hydra of Maraudon's depths. After it stuck all three heads out, it plunged back under all at once and vanished, leaving only slowly spreading ripples that tangled with the spray from the waterfalls.

Seen from this height, the monster looked small, but everyone knew that was just an illusion bred by the vast sinkhole.

In reality, its size was probably beyond imagination.

"Best not to provoke it," Grede said with a swallow.

"Best not to provoke anything here," Wynne added. She pointed to a cave mouth in the cliff wall farther off. "All the water here flows into the depths over there. If we follow these rivers onward, we might find the exit in the end."

Grede craned his neck to look at the sky. "We can can see the outside from here… and we're still heading deeper. Hard to swallow."

"Unless you can fly," Wynne said. "Otherwise I am absolutely not following you trying to climb out of here."

"How do you know I want to climb out?" Grede said.

"You've been staring at the surrounding cliff walls too long. Everyone can tell what you're thinking." Jace pointed to a cavern not far off. "Let's see what's in there — maybe we can find another way down."

Aside from that cavern, there was only this waterfall. They'd looked — there was no other way forward.

Clearly, this cavern too had been badly hit by the earthquake earlier. Fresh rock faces were everywhere, along with rubble covering the floor. Following the cavern down for ten-odd minutes, they stepped into a shallow pool — the cave had flooded.

Still, all the way from the cave mouth outward it had been shallow. Once they emerged, everything opened up — a calm lake spread out before them.

Grede looked up and pointed at a few boulders at the very top of the waterfall. "Isn't that where we were standing when we looked down earlier?"

Wynne nodded. "But keep your voice down, Grede. Remember that monster we saw up there? It's right in front of us now…"

"I know that without you telling me!" Grede grumbled, and splashed forward.

The lake's surface was completely undisturbed at this point. The hydra had no idea where it had gone. Skirting the edge of the cliff wall, the shallows were all narrow beach. The party kept their eyes on the lake while doing their best to minimize the noise of their wading as they moved toward the cave entrance ahead.

But the closer they got to the enormous cave and the thick karst columns, the harder it was to keep their footsteps small. By the time they were leaving the lakeshore, Grede was nearly running.

Charging into the cavern, everyone breathed a sigh of relief. The lake behind them was still perfectly calm, without a ripple…

Next, it was a matter of following the river forward and looking for the exit.

But Jace knew the hydra didn't just swim in the lake… those gigantic stone columns connecting the cave ceiling and the pool had them too.

Several people moved with their backs against a stone column, shielding themselves as they headed toward the depths of the cavern. No sooner had they left the column than Sazeno tugged at Jace's robe and shook it. "Master… over there!"

"What?" Jace looked where the imp was pointing, and saw, behind another stone column off to the side, a white snake head poking out — staring straight at them!

"Run!" He slapped Wynne behind him. Grede and Mossa, who'd been watching the lake, jerked their heads around and saw head after head of snake sliding out from behind the column. The dwarf sucked in a sharp breath.

The group charged toward the cavern's depths, water flying everywhere… the vast chamber echoed with the chaotic splash of water and the pounding of footsteps.

But the hydra was monstrously huge. The bizarre creature dragged a long, thick tail behind it, its whole body powered by great fins, and even hauling itself forward on two stout claws across the pool, it was still much faster than Jace's group. It churned its body and closed in fast!

Seeing the monster about to catch them, Wynne spun around, drew three elven arrows and nocked them all at once, pulling the bowstring taut. She murmured an incantation, and once the arrowheads blazed with brilliant arcane light, she loosed.

Jace looked back to see the three arrows trailing arcane radiance, homing in on the hydra's weaving heads with a piercing shriek — thup, thup, thup — all three hitting their marks!

The three heads were pierced simultaneously. The hydra let out a thunderous boom, swayed, and slammed into a nearby pillar, toppling. Wynne waved her hand and shouted to keep running. Nobody let up — they sprinted until they finally dove into the narrow tunnel at the cavern's depths.

The commotion outside had stopped. Jace wasn't sure whether the hydra was dead or what.

Wynne panted heavily, her lips gone white. That spell had unquestionably consumed a massive amount of her mana.

Everyone was exhausted by this point, and there weren't many potions left either.

Jace knew there was no way they could handle an elemental lord in this state.

The road ahead had to be walked with the utmost care. They absolutely could not disturb the Therazane deeper in — nor any of her terrifying servants, whether the mountain giants guarding these caverns or the endless earth elementals running amok.

Translator Notes

Flags

- hydra / three-headed serpentine monster (多头怪 / 多头蛇怪): the giant white hydra of Maraudon's depths; canonical Maraudon creature. - dryads (树妖): Cenarius's deer-bodied, elf-upper-bodied daughters; the text notes they're absent here, contrary to the game. - corrupted son of Cenarius (被腐化的塞纳留斯之子): the dryad leader who guards Zaltar's tomb in the game; Jace has forgotten his name (canonical: Raxxi'ab/the Corrupted Keeper figure — not named in source). - karst columns (溶洞石柱): the stone pillars linking cave ceiling and pool where the hydra lurks. - mountain giants (山岭巨人): the stone giants guarding Maraudon's caverns; appear in Chapter 565. - earth elementals running amok (土元素奔行者): the earth elementals serving Therazane. - Sazeno (撒厄诺): Jace's imp, per glossary. - Wynne (温蕾萨): the Windrunner ranger; per established rendering. - Mossa (莫萨): the tauren druid; not in glossary.
