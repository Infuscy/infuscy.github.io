## Chapter 564: The Princess

"Damn it, there's more."

Wynne, who'd just walked out, backed up again and hid behind a boulder.

Jace was about to ask what else was out there, but Wynne pressed a finger to her lips when she saw him about to speak. Then Jace heard it — the splash of claws striking water, coming from outside.

A white, finned giant tail slowly slid past the other side of the cave mouth. A gurgling, dinosaur-like breathing drew near, then gradually faded.

Once the sound had vanished, Wynne took a deep breath and darted across the cave mouth. The others, the cat, and the demon silently followed her lead.

Past this cave mouth, there was another deep, damp, cold cavern. The hydras that wandered here were far from the only ones, but once you went deeper, you shouldn't see them anymore.

Compared to the earthen tunnels before the waterfall and sinkhole, this place was far more alive — all manner of shore plants, algae, vines, and dwarf trees everywhere, plants Jace had never seen anywhere else.

At the same time, these caverns were also much broader and brighter than those tunnels. Rather than feeling like you were traveling through a cave, it was more like strolling through a deep valley.

Passing a cave entrance, Jace noticed several massive dimetrodons lying low in a depression in the distance, only their brown-red dorsal fins and half their heads visible.

As they passed the entrance, one of the dimetrodons seemed to notice the passing intruders, but it only shifted its head slightly in their direction. It didn't make a sound, nor did it react any further.

They might be like the giant claw turtle seen up above — just beasts living here, not overly controlled by Therazane or any other magical force.

Then what about the hydras that wandered back and forth outside?

Jace couldn't say for sure either.

The group continued forward, following the flow of the water deeper into the cavern, and soon arrived at another vast sinkhole.

There was no broad lake here. Looking up, broken cliff ledges formed a jagged succession of staggered platforms, rising higher and higher until the blinding sunlight at the very top made it impossible to see clearly.

Suddenly, the sunlight dimmed.

Something had blocked the light source, and the whole sinkhole darkened.

"What's going on?" Grede muttered quietly, looking up.

Mossa bit Grede's arm and pulled him aside. Seeing Mossa's action, Wynne immediately yanked Jace behind a boulder too.

The four of them on both sides exchanged glances. Jace looked at Mossa's expression — her already enormous eyes were round as saucers, her thick lips trembling incessantly, as if she were witnessing the end of the world.

Morfell was hiding behind Jace. Despite being in an invisible state, he could feel her whole body's power being suppressed so weakly that even her shadow was indistinct.

The imp trembled inside the bag without even shifting phases.

What were they afraid of… even Morfell was terrified, silent from spirit to flesh?

It was the power of life — so abundant it made the whole sinkhole seethe!

Jace watched the curled-up plants beside the rock suddenly sprout a tender branch, bulge outward into a bud at visible speed, and then violently push out red petals that trembled from the force.

The bare stone slab underfoot now had dense blades of grass drilling up through the cracks, even climbing up Jace's boots…

Mossa stared wide-eyed at the heights, as if waiting for something — or expecting something. Jace looked up again, and this time he heard a muffled boom.

Like a giant hammer striking the cliff wall. The whole sinkhole shuddered.

It wasn't just at Jace's feet. On the cliff walls, the tangled weeds high above suddenly grew outward like an exaggerated time-lapse — it couldn't even be called growth. It was closer to… inflation!

He watched the trees and vines grow denser and denser, more and more tangled, branches crowding and twisting around each other as they stretched outward, as if trying to seal and fill the entire sinkhole… but what blocked the sunlight wasn't these plants.

Boom.

Another deafening crash.

Jace felt something up high shift — like a boulder jammed between a canyon, dislodged by the vibration and dropping a little, only to catch again at the next narrow point.

With the second crash, the sunlight at the top of the sinkhole peeked in a little more.

That beam of light pierced in. Jace squinted and looked up again — and this time he saw emerald-green gems twinkling in the shadow atop the canyon.

The shadow swayed. More light poured in. The whole pit grew brighter, and the giant shape that had been blocking the shadow revealed its true form.

Jace beheld a face.

An evil visage, statue-like — bloated and corpulent, its surface crowded with stone-made rolls of flesh pressing against each other. Enormous emerald-green boar-like tusks jutted from between its lips, curving high.

And if there was anything more unsettling than that nauseating face, it was that this gigantic head actually bore two more faces — one on the left of its forehead, one on the right — bulging out like two swollen tumors.

Those two faces were vacant and twisted, mouths half-open, staring fixedly ahead, as if they had given up struggling and accepted their miserable fate of being bound to the back of that hideous face — that was Princess Therazane.

Her body was like a grotesquely corpulent stone sphere hanging in midair. She was now using her four thick, rubble-built arms and her two feet — six limbs in total — to brace against both walls of the cave, her eyes glowing brighter and brighter, overflowing with deep-green, dense power of life, fixed on the cave floor below.

Slightly different from the game model, Therazane wore no clothing at all — she was completely exposed. And unlike the game, where her skin was covered in jagged protrusions, in reality her body was inlaid with green gems of all shapes and sizes. They weren't that dense, but their distribution followed no pattern at all, making one's scalp tingle just looking.

But the physical revulsion of that appearance was nothing compared to the oppressive presence Therazane herself radiated.

Mossa seemed to have frozen. The whole cat curled up next to Grede, her tail wrapped tightly around the dwarf's mouth. Grede's face was ashen too — though Jace couldn't tell if it was from being strangled and unable to breathe, or from fear of the elemental lord above.

Had Therazane noticed the few people hiding here?

Jace didn't know. In truth, he hadn't expected to run into the Stone Princess here at all. He'd thought there was still some distance to go, since this wasn't Zaltar's tomb — there ought to be a little farther…

Therazane shouldn't leave her husband, her dead husband… but he also knew he shouldn't use the game's settings, the game's model, the game's level, the game's anything as a standard to define anything here.

Then the whole sinkhole shuddered again.

## Flags

- **dimetrodons** (双帆龙): the giant reptiles with dorsal fins; not in glossary (canonical Maraudon fauna).
- **Princess Therazane** (瑟莱德丝公主): the earth elemental princess herself, appearing in person. The text describes her as having two extra faces on her forehead (a novel elaboration on her game model) and a body inlaid with green gems.
- **power of life** (生命之力): the dense, chaotic life energy radiating from Therazane that makes plants grow wildly — and, per later chapters, grants unnaturally long life to those who dwell in her presence.
- **Stone Princess** (石公主): Jace's internal shorthand for Therazane.
- **Morfell** (莫洛菲尔): the succubus, per established rendering in Chapters 523–525.
- **Zaltar's Tomb** (扎尔塔之墓): the grave of Therazane's consort; not in glossary.
- **shift phases** (相位变换): the imp's Phase Shift ability; per glossary.
