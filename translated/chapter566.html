Chapter 66: Lord of the Elements

"Die? Die your fat stone ass!"

Grede roared, then suddenly whirled around and hurled his war hammer at the mountain giant's arm blocking the cave mouth. A burst of white light made the mountain giant flinch — and the explosion that followed shattered the massive boulder!

"Go!"

Grede bellowed, hefted the stone axe in his hands, and leaped up to swing hard at the mountain giant's body, cracking its chest wide open.

The mountain giant stumbled a couple of steps as if about to topple backward. Seizing the chance, everyone scrambled over the fallen boulder and dove into the cave, sprinting forward. The mountain giants perched up high hadn't even reacted yet.

Behind them came the rumbling voice of Theradras. Jace couldn't make out what she was saying, but it seemed as though the entire world was answering her summons.

The screech of insect swarms, the low hiss of twin-sailed drakes, the mad rolling of boulders, the roar of hydras — a chaotic cacophony of every kind of sound echoed through the deep caves, hammering at their eardrums.

"Run, run, run…"

Grede muttered under his breath as he charged ahead, even though the cave had grown so dark they could barely see anything. But they all knew that stopping meant death.

From the crevices in the rock on either side, yard-long rock worms kept lunging at them, along with shrieking carnivorous flowers that screamed as they struck. The dwarf hacked left and right with his axe, chopping them to pieces, clearing the path ahead without hesitation.

Jace glanced back and saw a flood of elementals surging toward them. He ran while chanting an incantation for Shadow Bolt, gathering a shrieking mass of black power at the tip of his staff and hurling it behind him. The Shadow Bolt detonated a chain of shadow lightning in the narrow cave, corrupting and twisting all the life energy around it.

Every elemental that passed through was tainted into crumbling black rubble, crashing to the ground — quickly falling behind the group's pace.

But Jace knew that shadow energy wouldn't last more than a few seconds. Theradras's life energy would soon swallow and purify everything once more. There was no room to let up.

Before long, the sound of a waterfall echoed from ahead. Jace shouted, "There's water up ahead — follow it down!"

"That's exactly what I was thinking!" Grede yelled back, running even faster. Wynne explained to Musa, and Musa nodded.

The group burst out of the cave, and their vision suddenly opened up completely, as if they'd thrown themselves into the sky.

But none of them were in any mood to take in the scenery. They came to the edge of a high cliff, with a waterfall plunging down right beside them. Jace could even feel the icy mist from the crashing water spraying against his face.

Grede walked to the cliff's edge, about to jump, when he suddenly shouted and slammed on the brakes.

Jace crashed into his back, nearly sending him over the edge, but managed to stop himself and sit down. Wynne and Musa came up beside them and looked down.

It turned out this place was even higher than the waterfall where they'd encountered the hydra — far, far higher. Jumping straight down from here and hitting the water would mean instant death.

In the game, so long as there was water below, you could jump from any height and take no damage. But how could that possibly work here?

Only then did Jace have the time to lift his head and gaze into the distance. Through the mist, he saw a spectacular stone bridge extending from the side, stretching all the way to a broad platform at the edge of his vision.

That high platform blazed with brilliant flowers of every color — a perfect garden.

Clean water trickled down the surrounding stone walls, nourishing the plants atop the platform. At the center, a raised garden was bathed in golden sunlight streaming from above, sacred and solemn beyond words.

That was Zaelta's tomb — the thing Princess Theradras wished to guard forever. Her lover, and the father of all the centaur of Kalimdor.

Jace lowered his head. He suddenly remembered there should be another ledge between here and the water's surface.

He tried peering downward, then ran onto the bridge and looked over toward the cliff face, finally spotting the lower ledge and stone bridge below.

Jumping straight down from here would probably smash them to pieces — but the plants growing on the cliff face beside them would be more than enough to let them climb down to their destination.

"We climb down the cliff face," Jace said, pointing at the rock wall, then shifting his finger to the stone bridge at the lower ledge. "Get over there, and then we'll see if there's anywhere to jump."

The group immediately agreed — after all, there was no other way down. They couldn't exactly go waltzing into Zaelta's Tomb, could they?

Wynne immediately leaped out and grabbed a tree root on the cliff face. Light and agile, she handled the situation with ease.

Musa also leaped out lightly, sinking her claws into the rock wall. Jace had the feeling she didn't even need to grab onto those plants — she could use her big-cat skills to grip the uneven surface and slide down quickly and safely to the bottom.

Seeing the two women already descending, Grede wiped his hands and charged straight at the wall. Halfway down he grabbed several branches, but his weight snapped them all. He tumbled and slid four or five yards before finally catching a sturdy one, cursing up a storm in Dwarvish.

Jace also leaped out and grabbed a solid branch. Though he wasn't as nimble as Wynne, he wasn't as heavy either — it was considerably easier.

By now the plants on the rock wall were growing thicker and more lush. What that meant went without saying.

Wynne had already climbed far down, and before long she leaped onto the lower stone bridge, Musa right behind her.

Jace and Grede soon jumped down onto the platform one after another. Looking down again, the rock wall near the water's surface had few plants left to climb on. The wall itself was quite smooth, covered in moss and the like. From here on, they'd have to jump.

Grede glanced down, then over at Jace. Jace said nothing.

This height probably wouldn't kill them — but whether they could avoid injury, nobody could say. Under the circumstances, they could only grit their teeth and jump.

Jace recited the Water Breathing spell on each of his companions one by one, then said, "I don't know how long it'll last. If you start feeling like it's getting harder to breathe, look at me and point to your nose. I'll try to recast it."

Everyone nodded and began strapping down their luggage, weapons, and everything else they carried. Wynne explained Jace's warning to Musa. Jace drank a mana potion and made his preparations.

Then he saw it — high above, bathed in sunlight, a giant silhouette on the broad bridge leading to the platform, staring straight at them. Theradras had caught up.

The other mountain giants were watching from various positions, but none of them moved.

The plants beneath their feet began growing wildly. The dwarf's boots were suddenly wrapped tight by a thorny vine that had appeared from nowhere. He yanked hard to pull his leg free.

"Never mind her — we jump," Jace said.

Wynne nodded and leaped down first. Jace followed right behind her. Grede landed butt-first in the water. Musa clawed at the rock wall under the bridge a few times, then plunged in. The group was carried by the current toward a dark, deep cave in the distance. If all went well, this would be the last waterway out of Maraudon.

Just when Jace thought they'd finally escaped with their lives, wind suddenly howled around them.

At first it was just a breeze — but within the blink of an eye, the air current grew violently fierce. Jace watched gravel from the cave walls around the underground lake get ripped up and sent flying high into the air, even the spray getting sucked away…

The plants on the cave walls were uprooted. The entire rock face hummed and roared under the escalating sandstorm.

High above, Theradras's eyes blazed with life energy as bright as a lighthouse, clearly visible through the flying gravel and water mist. The ancient monster was opening her pitch-black maw wide, swallowing everything she pulled toward her…

Jace felt his body starting to lift.

He was being sucked right off the water's surface!

"Watch it, Jace!"

Beside him, Wynne suddenly tumbled through the air with her pack, sucked toward the sky. Jace felt his mind go white. He lunged out of the water but failed to grab the hand she'd reached out—

Death Grip?

Death Grip's own damage was enough to tear apart any limb it touched — but there was no time to think!

Just as he was about to channel his dark power, a shadow suddenly materialized in midair. Morfell grabbed one of Wynne's legs and dragged her down a good distance!

Jace had no time to wonder how the succubus had figured out a way to launch herself up there — but the demon's weight still wasn't enough to drag them back into the water!

As the gale whipped up by Theradras's inhalation grew fiercer, Jace couldn't even reach for the demon — he himself had already left the water's surface. Fortunately, Musa had already shifted back into her tauren form. She stretched out an arm and grabbed Jace by the cloak, pulling him back into the water.

In the distance, Grede looked up at the succubus high above and shouted, "Grab this, demon!"

He flung his war hammer into the air. Morfell caught it with her long tail mid-flight. Grede summoned the hammer directly. The weapon flashed with a burst of lightning in midair, dragging Wynne and Morfell, churning against the spray, hurtling back toward the water's surface…

But just as the hammer was about to fly back into Grede's hand, its speed grew slower and slower. Grede reached and reached — and then he himself was dragged off the water's surface!

The dwarf grabbed the hammer in midair, transforming into a stone statue amid a crackle of lightning. Only then was he heavy enough to plunge back into the water, dragging the demon and the elf down with him.

But the storm didn't relent — it only grew wilder.

The entire cave was shrouded in spray, gravel, dust, and shattered plants. Musa clung tightly to him from behind. Jace felt like he was about to be torn apart by the current.

"No, no…" the tauren warned in Common. "No good, Jace!"

Musa was about to lose her grip too. Heavy as a tauren was, she couldn't compare to a dwarf in Avatar form! By now half her body had left the water — she was no longer floating, she was being lifted!

"Grab me, Jace!" Grede noticed the situation too. He bellowed in a thunderous voice: "Stop hesitating, grab me, damn it! You think you can hurt me?"

Jace understood immediately.

He decisively channeled the shadow power within him and released Gorefiend's Grasp. The blood demon's claw lashed out on a distant blood line and wrapped precisely around Grede's arm!

Grede roared and heaved. Both sides pulled toward each other, finally crashing together amid the surging waves.

Musa threw her arms tight around Grede, her broad limbs binding everyone together. Inside the huddle, Grede gripped Morfell's and Jace's arms with all his might. Wynne clung tightly to Jace's neck. The demon wrapped her tail around Jace's thigh. Human and demon clung to each other for dear life — and the wild storm whipped up by Theradras's inhalation could no longer budge them an inch.

"Gonna crush us… gonna die…"

The imp muttered weakly from the middle, his voice coming from the warlock's pack, growing fainter and fainter.

The surging current pushed the group — locked together in a tight ball — into a completely submerged water tunnel. Even driven into the deep water, all of them refused to let go. Besides the gurgle of bubbles in their ears, all that remained was the Stone Princess's furious roar from the surface.

Jace had no idea how long they'd been submerged, swept along by the current. He constantly had the illusion that the Water Breathing spell was about to wear off — after all, in this chaos, casting with incantations and gestures was nearly impossible. If it really did fail, he had no idea what to do.

But before he truly felt suffocated, the group finally broke the surface. Another vast cavern stretched before them. Now, aside from the muffled rumbling and the violent crashing of the water, nothing else could be heard.

"You've got a real cruel streak, Jace Sesso."

Grede spat out a mouthful of water. Jace noticed that the dwarf's stone arm had been pulled with deep cracks by his own grip.

If that grab had landed on Wynne, it might have torn off her arm or leg on the spot…

Only Grede, after his transformation, could have withstood a hit like that.

"Next time, even if you let me be eaten by that female monster, I'm not letting you use that damn magic on me again. I mean that from the bottom of my heart." The dwarf gasped, complaining.

"Who the hell wants to use that magic to drag you around?" Jace muttered.

Wynne smiled. "Thank you, Grede."

"What's there to thank me for, little elf?" Grede said. The lightning in his eyes had already begun to fade. He seemed to have no strength left to maintain his powerful form. His body shrank smaller and smaller, and the stone color receded from his skin.

Wynne glanced at the Morfell beside her, hesitated for a moment, then finally said, "And thank you for your master's order, demon."

The diminutive Morfell lifted her head, shaking off the water that had nearly covered her lips, and said in a strange tone: "At your service, Miss Windrunner."

The ranger coughed unnaturally a couple of times — as if she'd choked on water, or as if she was completely speechless.

Though it seemed they'd escaped danger, Jace couldn't shake the feeling that… there was still something in the underground lake beneath Zaelta's Tomb.

He looked back in the direction they'd been swept from — but saw nothing.

Could he have remembered wrong?

Jace pulled his arm free from between everyone's chests and backs. Regardless, it was time to refresh the Water Breathing spell. He had no idea how much longer they'd be drifting, or whether they'd encounter another completely submerged cave.

Translator Notes

Flags

- Theradras (瑟莱德丝): Princess Theradras, the Stone Princess; an earth elemental lord. - Zaelta (扎尔塔): Zaelta, Theradras's lover and father of the centaur; his tomb lies deep in Maraudon. - mountain giant (山岭巨人): stone giants serving Theradras. - rock worm (岩虫): giant worm creatures in the caves. - hydra (多头怪): the multi-headed beast they encountered earlier. - twin-sailed drake (双帆龙): reptilian creatures in the caves. - Shadow Bolt (暗影箭): Jace's warlock spell. - Water Breathing (魔息术): the spell allowing the group to breathe underwater. - Death Grip / Gorefiend's Grasp (死亡之握 / 血魔之握): death knight / warlock abilities to pull targets. - Avatar / Stoneform (天神下凡 / 石像): Grede's dwarf transformation into living stone. - Morfell (莫洛菲尔): the succubus; 魅魔 = succubus. - Musa (莫萨): the tauren companion. - Wynne (温蕾萨): the high elf ranger. - Maraudon (玛拉顿): the dungeon they are fleeing. - Miss Windrunner (风行者小姐): Morfell's address to Wynne, referencing the Windrunner lineage.
