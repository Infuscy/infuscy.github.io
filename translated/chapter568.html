Chapter 568: The Journey Ahead

"Lorgrim Deeper — a giant crocodile lurking in the deep underground rivers beneath the Taramurk mountains," Grede said mysteriously. "Legend has it that it's been living here for hundreds, even thousands of years. At this point, you might as well call it a crocodile god. When I was little, I had no idea where Taramurk was. I asked my father and he told me it might be in Stranglethorn Vale — but later I learned there's no such place in Stranglethorn at all. Slowly I forgot about the whole thing."

"But Nessingway always hoped to find the location of Taramurk, so he could hunt the legendary giant crocodile Lorgrim Deeper with his own hands. Look at this, Jace — I'm certain the Taramurk in that story is this very place. Beneath the mountains, deep underground rivers, a distant and mysterious continent — it's a perfect match!"

Grede stood up excitedly and slapped the rough stone pillar beside him. Wynne asked, "If Taramurk is real, does that mean the giant crocodile Lorgrim Deeper is real too?"

"That doesn't matter," Jace said. "So long as this place is real, what matters is—"

"Sell the location to Nessingway!" he and the dwarf said in unison.

Wynne gave them both a sidelong look. "I thought you two were determined to hunt this so-called crocodile god with your own hands. If so, I might actually have been able to help."

"That'd take too much time," Jace said. "All I want right now is to get out of here fast."

Grede nodded. "I learned hunting skills to survive in the forests of the Hinterlands, not to challenge some scariest beast in the world. I even hope that crocodile is completely made up. I don't want to run straight into it in a place like this! After all, Grede of the Thunderfist clan has already defeated a general of the Burning Legion. I don't need a crocodile to prove my strength."

"I was the one who actually won that fight," Jace said from his armrest.

Wynne laughed. Beside them Grede began snoring loudly. Jace glanced over and said, "Fell asleep fast."

After resting for a while and letting their clothes dry more or less, the group followed the edge of the underground river downward.

Their outer robes and cloaks were both thick and made of complex materials. In the damp underground cavern there was no way to start a fire, and burning flames with mana alone was far too wasteful — so the clothes had only been dried halfway, barely wearable. The road ahead was going to be extremely uncomfortable.

Jace took out his pocket watch and looked at it. It had stopped running — probably from water damage.

In the underground cave, there was no telling day from night. Figuring out how long they'd been walking, or whether it was night or day outside, was now impossible.

Wynne, who'd been walking at the front the whole time, turned back. "Should we go to Eldre'Thalas?"

Actually, ever since they'd stopped to rest, Jace had been wanting to bring up this very question. After all, the clues were already pointing there — not going to have a look would feel like leaving a job half done.

But after the perilous journey through Maraudon, fighting an elementalist lord, wrestling in the stream for so long, exhausted and starving, Jace really didn't have the heart to ask his companions to go to another even more uncertain place.

Yet Wynne seemed far more eager than he was.

Grede looked at her. "We've already found a lot of clues in these caves. By our original agreement, we should get at least seventy or eighty gold from the Reliquary. Are you really planning to go to that terrifying place, elf? I mean, that high elf might not even be hiding in there permanently. Look — she left so many things here, and the Reliquary found traces of her in many other places in Kalimdor. This guy is clearly living a nomadic life…"

"I know," Wynne said. "Originally, just a dagger wouldn't have been enough to motivate me to go there. But after seeing all the information on Eldre'Thalas the satyrs collected…"

"Maybe it's just a coincidence," Grede said. "The satyrs were preparing to sneak into Eldre'Thalas to find treasures of the ancient elves, and the demon statue connected to Darzha Mora was just something interesting they found somewhere else. These two clues might be completely unrelated…"

"You have a point," Wynne said. "But only because you can't read Kaldorei, Grede. If you'd read those books about Eldre'Thalas, you wouldn't think so."

"That's right — some of the books mention the outer defenses of the city, the internal energy systems… But an exaggerated proportion of the books, documents, and materials talk about the internal structure, especially the demon seals deep within Eldre'Thalas, the incantations for the demon prisons, the sealing methods, the energy sources, and so on…"

Hearing this, Jace couldn't help but look up. When he'd been helping Wynne look through those books, his focus had been entirely on the satyrs inside Eldre'Thalas. He'd paid no attention at all to whatever else those messy elven letters mentioned — and of course, he hadn't had the ability to read them anyway.

The elf continued, "I can't explain it to you clearly in just a few words. Put simply — Vilethorn and his men weren't focused on how to break through Eldre'Thalas's outer defenses at all. It was more like they were trying to escape from the inside. More accurately, trying to escape from the prisons within. I don't know if you understand what I mean."

"I don't," Grede said. "Are you saying these satyrs have companions locked inside that city, and they want to break in to help them escape?"

"Skip the 'break in' part — that would be more accurate," Wynne said. "I have a theory. They want to use summoning incantations to call their companions out of the city, because their companions are sealed by Kaldorei magic prisons. It was Morfell that reminded me of this. Remember, demon? You said that within that complex incantation, there was a passage used to summon a target under very difficult conditions."

"That's right, Quel'dorei," Morfell said. "That incantation can be used to break through a summoned demon's seal — provided the summoner's mana is powerful enough, or rather, the energy they use is strong enough."

"Would those centaur hero soulstones be enough?" Wynne asked.

Morfell smiled. "That depends on how strong the seal is. Clearly not enough, I think — if it were, they would have attempted the summoning long ago, wouldn't they? There must be all sorts of reasons they haven't done it."

"This is getting messier and messier," Grede said, looking at the demon and the elf discussing animatedly. "So this sealed demon — he should at least be a demon, right? What does he have to do with the Darzha Mora we're looking for?"

"Our target is a warlock, fathead!" Sazeno reminded him. "This demon is very likely her minion. Don't forget that demon statue closely connected to her — have you lost the ability to connect the dots?"

Flags

- Eldre'Thalas (埃雷萨拉斯): the ancient high elf city, later known as Dire Maul. - Reliquary (遗物学会): the Reliquary, a scholarly organization seeking artifacts. - Darzha Mora (达斯莫拉·暗眼): the warlock they are hunting. - Vilethorn (维利塔恩): the satyr leader in Maraudon. - Kaldorei (卡多雷): the night elves' name for themselves; also the language. - Quel'dorei (奎尔多雷): high elves; Morfell's address to Wynne. - centaur hero soulstones (半人马英雄灵魂石): powerful centaur artifacts. - Morfell (莫洛菲尔): the succubus. - Sazeno (撒厄诺): the imp. - Hinterlands (辛特兰): zone in the eastern Kingdoms. - Thunderfist (雷拳): Grede's dwarven family name. - Burning Legion (燃烧军团): the demonic army.
