## Chapter 582: The Missing Shen'dralar

"The Shen'dralar are a group of nobles," Shandris explained. "Eldre'Thalas was originally built as a fortress to defend against Zandalari troll incursions. After the Zandalari were defeated and fractured, the city was designated by Azshara as the southern capital of the empire and, at the same time, as a repository for gathering and safeguarding the empire's secrets. The nobles charged with guarding this repository were called the Shen'dralar, and they eventually became the city's rulers."

"All the Shen'dralar were Quel'dorei — though not your kind of Quel'dorei." She looked at Vereesa. "Those who came after called them the Highborne, which is a truly fitting name. In those days they lived in towering palaces, walling themselves off from the forests and wooden huts with ramparts of silver and purple... But of course you've heard all this."

Vereesa nodded.

"Dathmora was most likely one of the Shen'dralar. According to our investigations, she had lived in Eldre'Thalas since at least the era of the War of the Ancients. But she fled the city afterward, because everyone within Eldre'Thalas had been tainted by demonic energy... They had summoned a terrible void demon to replace the Well of Eternity and sate their hunger for magic. So we could not allow those who escaped to roam freely across Kalimdor, making trouble at will — we had to monitor their movements."

"So where is she now?" Grede asked.

"We lost track of her roughly a hundred and twenty years ago," Shandris Feathermoon said. "We tried to find her again, but to no avail."

Hearing this, the group couldn't help but feel a flicker of disappointment. They had hoped they might find their target without needing to enter Eldre'Thalas at all.

"Could she have gone to our side?" Grede couldn't help asking.

"Impossible," Vereesa said. "If she were on the other side, the Reliquary would have dug her up long ago. She couldn't leave behind the barest trace of magic — no way, Grede."

"We found some clues among the satyr in Maraudon, General Shandris," Jace said. "Those satyr intended to summon a demon into Maraudon, one likely tied closely to Dathmora, to achieve some purpose. The demon should be a doomguard — or, an Ered'ruin."

"I know that kind of demon," Shandris nodded.

"We suspect that demon may be Dathmora's minion, imprisoned in the dungeons of Eldre'Thalas," Vereesa added.

"So it would seem these satyr also want to find Dathmora?" Shandris asked.

"We haven't yet figured out their exact plan," Jace said.

"What do you make of the satyr's purpose, General?" Vereesa asked.

"The satyr have long been collecting magical relics left behind by Kaldorei nobles," Shandris said. "They want to amass enough power to summon mightier demons into this world. If I had to guess, Dathmora must know a great many relevant secrets — the locations of artifacts, and so on — after all, she may be one of the Shen'dralar... Remember what I said earlier? The Shen'dralar were the keepers of Eldre'Thalas's imperial repository."

"Does that repository still exist?" Jace asked.

"The repository still exists, but fewer than one percent of its treasures remain," Shandris said. "Over ten thousand years, many artifacts were carried away by those who fled, and the rest were largely drained of all magical power by the nobles within, reduced to mere decorations. Those treasures helped the vast majority of the Kaldorei survive the most difficult thousand years between the Sundering and the summoning of the demon."

"There's one thing I haven't understood through all this, General Shandris," Grede said. "Why do you talk about this city as if it's a prison? Is it so hard to leave? And if it's so terrible that they must leave, why are there still so many elves willing to stay inside?"

Shandris Feathermoon hesitated before speaking. "For the inhabitants of Eldre'Thalas, leaving the city means giving up their immortality, dwarf — that much is easy to understand. But the power of that demon is not infinite, and now there are more fugitives than before, so its rulers have begun executing unimportant citizens of Eldre'Thalas in a planned fashion, in order to delay the exhaustion of magic and preserve the city's survival."

After hearing this, Grede sat down on a nearby railing and muttered, "That's a real bitch of a choice."

"If you have no other questions, the hour is growing late, friends," Shandris said. "If you truly intend to head to Eldre'Thalas next, then rest well for the night first."

Jace glanced at Vereesa beside him. She seemed lost in thought, a little downcast. Grede shook his head too, and of course Mosa had nothing to say. So Jace spoke: "We have no further questions. Thank you, General Shandris."

Shandris gave Jace a slight bow and turned to leave. Jace felt that as the military leader of an entire people, Shandris Feathermoon truly had no airs about her — she had been remarkably courteous even to a foreign mercenary like him.

After retrieving their luggage, Jace and the others followed the sentinels to a wing of the quarters. In a pavilion overlooking a small pond outside, a night elf woman presented them with a wooden tray of food wrapped in leaves. The moment the night elf left, Grede couldn't resist reaching out and unwrapping every leaf one by one.

"Pickles, salted veggies, apples? Radishes... Fish! By the Light, is this fish, Jace Sesso?"

He whispered the exclamation, pinching a morsel of plump, tender fish between his fingers and popping it into his mouth, sucking in a sharp breath. Then he looked at Vereesa beside him. "Come on, girl, dig in!"

Vereesa nodded, but seemed to lack enthusiasm.

Jace shot her a curious glance, though he didn't dwell on it — he was starving himself, after all.

He quickly unwrapped a leaf near him and found a chunk of giant clam meat inside — raw, however.

He picked up a piece and slurped it into his mouth. There was no unpleasant fishy taste at all — it was remarkably fresh, tender, and smooth. Even after chewing for a while, no disagreeable flavor came back. He had no idea how the night elves had found this kind of clam, or whether they had treated it with some special method.

The night elves' dwellings, food, weapons, and tools — all of it gave Jace the same impression. On the surface they seemed rough and simple, yet every single piece was perfectly judged, so that being in their spaces or using the things they provided felt effortlessly comfortable.

"More meat, more meat!" Grede spotted the clam meat before Jace and reached over. "Let me try some of that, Jace — here, have some of mine too..."

The two of them went to work on the few varieties of meat on the table. Vereesa hesitated at first, but seeing the meat about to run out and Mosa close to polishing off all the vegetables, she too joined the feasting. The group soon swept the table completely clean.

## Flags

- **Shen'dralar**: Canonical WoW name for 辛德拉. The glossary does not list this term, but it is firmly established lore — the Highborne faction residing in Dire Maul / Eldre'Thalas.
- **Highborne**: Canonical translation for 上层精灵 (the ancient night elf nobility). The text itself makes this etymological link explicit.
- **Ered'ruin**: Canonical demon classification for 埃雷杜因 (doomguard). Used here as the satyr's target summon.
- **Reliquary**: Canonical name for 遗物学会 — the blood elf archaeological organization, counterpart to the Explorers' League.
- **"By the Light"**: Grede's exclamation — kept as a natural dwarven oath; he is not a Light-wielder, just using it as a generic swear.
- **"Jace Sesso"**: Grede uses the full name here in his excitement, consistent with his familiar, rough-edged register.
- **"girl"**: Grede calls Vereesa "小姑娘" — rendered as "girl" in his bluff dwarven manner, not meant condescendingly.
- **Giant clam / raw**: The text emphasizes the raw clam meat has no fishy taste — a small detail highlighting night elf food preparation.
- **POV**: Third person throughout.
