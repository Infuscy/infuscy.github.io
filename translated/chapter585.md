## Chapter 585: The Shifting Sands Crisis

The voice came from the terrace, where a night elf priestess in a white robe, tidying a storage rack, was speaking. "Long ago I saw a druid wear it while shapeshifting. It could even adapt to the form of a great bear, protecting the druid's chest and ribs. It is made from the carapace of a silithid leader, and only so perfect a carapace could possess such magic."

"Really?" Grede asked her.

"You never know what's in the stone until you swing the pick," Jace said, reciting a simple dwarf proverb. He leaned against a nearby wooden post. "Just put it on and grow — you'll find out. I don't believe some precious ancient armor would be so easily ruined by the likes of you."

"That's one thing — whether it can be ruined is another matter. What about whether it strangles me?" Grede asked cautiously.

He glanced at Vereesa, then at Jace. Neither of them said a word.

"If I don't even have this much guts, what kind of Mountain King am I?!"

Grede pulled the armor out and pulled it on, muttering all the while. "It's definitely heavy... and isn't it a bit big..."

After all, this was armor meant for a night elf to wear. Putting it on a dwarf was clearly not the right fit, but before he could even finish the complaint, the armor seemed to come alive, contracting rapidly.

Grede thought the armor was actually going to strangle him and hastily tried to grab at the collar, but once the armor had conformed to its wearer's form it stopped changing.

"Interesting, Jace."

He slapped his chest, scowled, and his body suddenly grew a size larger. His skin began to harden and turn gray, and his eyes released a burst of lightning — a thunderous sound that rang out rather loudly in the quiet of Feathermoon Stronghold.

The insectoid armor expanded just as one would imagine, but it didn't infinitely scale up with the original form. It simply adapted to the new body, protecting the vitals — heart, shoulders, and other key points — while the plates were linked by what looked like sinew, soft-looking but in fact remarkably tough.

Grede soon returned to his original form, after all maintaining the Avatar was extremely draining. The insectoid armor once again contracted into its previous dense plate shape.

"By the Light, it really is as you said." He looked at the night elf woman who'd been tidying the storage rack. "Thank you, miss — if you hadn't said anything, we never would have known!"

But the woman had already been stunned into a daze. She blinked and only then nodded, clearly astonished by the dwarf's Earthen form.

"So, do we make the trade?" Grede's tone was already thoroughly excited.

"I agree," Vereesa said with a smile. "Besides, what would we do with that satyr head? You don't think those Mala'dari centaur would offer us anything better than this armor, do you?"

"I agree too," Jace said. "And I'd say this already exceeds the price."

Mosa nodded. "Pretty thing."

Since everyone agreed, after lunch Jace carried the bundle containing Vilethane's head to the wooden house where Shandris Feathermoon was quartered, set it down, and said, "We agree, General. We hope this head serves your purpose."

"Thank you very much." Shandris lifted the cloth and glanced at the half-hidden demon face within. "I'm curious — how did you know Vilethane was hiding deep within Maraudon?"

"Actually, we had no idea at all," Jace said. "We only went in tracking demons, following the trail. The centaur said there were demons hiding inside... Yesterday Vereesa Windrunner already gave you a detailed account, didn't she?"

Shandris nodded. "After all, we hunted for a long time without figuring out where this satyr leader was hiding. Perhaps we should make contact with the centaur after all — it would help us track the movements of the other satyr as well."

"I have one more question, General," Jace said. "I'd like to know where to find those giant silithids."

"Leave Feathermoon Stronghold, head back into the jungle, and keep walking south. You won't need to search for them — they'll come to you," Shandris Feathermoon said.

"I thought you had to go into the Silithus desert to find those insects," Jace said.

"Perhaps this world's misfortune has made you lucky," Shandris Feathermoon said.

"What do you mean?" Jace asked.

The sentinel general looked at Jace in silence for a moment, then said, "At this very moment, the insectoid activity in Silithus is higher than at any time in the past thousand years. So you can find countless burrows dug by silithids that have fled the desert, and the eggs they've left behind, even outside of Silithus. Neither the Cenarion Circle nor the Sentinel Corps has the power to stop these lesser creatures from escaping anymore, and the bronze dragons have not answered our calls for a long time now."

"I think perhaps you could try asking the tauren for help, General," Jace said. "If a second War of the Shifting Sands is unavoidable, they are the allies you can count on most."

Shandris's expression did not change, and she offered no response — probably reading a hint of sarcasm into those words.

But Jace had no intention of being sarcastic. He just hoped to say something that might help the tauren out of their predicament. If the night elves were willing to offer the exiled tauren some shelter within their forests, rather than stubbornly refusing them entry, the tauren's situation would be a great deal better.

Jace gave a simple nod of the head. "We've already decided to leave Feathermoon Stronghold. We're very grateful for your hospitality, General. Until we meet again."

"In any case, thank you for the suggestion." Shandris Feathermoon nodded. "Farewell, human. May time eventually dissolve your sorrows."

"May time eventually dissolve your sorrows" was a common night elf farewell. Jace was quite surprised that Shandris was willing to say it in Common.

Perhaps she was saying it to herself as well. The bronze dragons were the watchers of time — if there was anyone who could help the Kaldorei escape the predicament of Silithus at this moment, it was the bronze dragons.

As for why the bronze dragons said nothing about the crisis facing Kalimdor, Jace surmised that the War of the Shifting Sands a thousand years ago had inflicted devastating casualties on the bronze dragonflight. Not to mention that the Bronze Aspect Nozdormu and the other Aspects' powers were still sealed within the Demon Soul forged by Deathwing, leaving them unable to step forward and personally halt the Qiraji's second expansion.

The Dragonmaw orcs in Grim Batol held the Demon Soul, enslaving the Dragonqueen. So long as it remained unbroken, the Aspects' powers — sealed ever since the War of the Ancients — would remain locked away for one more day.

In the afternoon, the group boarded a wooden boat leaving Feathermoon Stronghold, heading back to the Feralas jungle. A voyage about as dull as the one out — much the same empty fog and indistinct sea — but already lacking some of the unknown, which dissolved a great deal of worry and unease.

When they returned to that beach the next day, the sentinels departed one after another. Only a lone night elf guard remained on the pier. Jace looked at him, then turned toward the forest that was nothing but a blur of shadow in the distance.

The next leg of the journey was already before his eyes.

## Flags

- **Vilethane**: 维利塔恩 — satyr leader. See Chapter 584 flag. The text confirms his head is being traded to the sentinels.
- **Mala'dari**: Canonical name for 玛洛迪 — the centaur tribe guarding Maraudon (the "Maraudon centaur").
- **Silithid / Qiraji**: Canonical insectoid race terminology. The text distinguishes between the lesser silithids (异种虫) and the greater Qiraji (其拉虫人).
- **Avatar**: Canonical dwarven warrior ability (天神下凡) — the stone-form transformation. Grede demonstrates it here.
- **Earthen form**: 土灵形态 — the night elf's term for the dwarf's stone-body transformation, linking to the ancient Earthen (土灵) lore.
- **Cenarion Circle**: Canonical druidic organization (塞纳里奥议会).
- **Bronze dragons / Nozdormu**: Canonical dragonflight and Bronze Aspect (青铜龙 / 诺教多姆). The glossary lists "青铜龙族" as the Bronze Dragonflight.
- **Demon Soul**: Canonical artifact (恶魔之魂) — forged by Deathwing to enslave the Aspects. Glossary lists it.
- **Grim Batol**: Canonical location (格瑞姆巴托) — where the Dragonmaw orcs held the Demon Soul and enslaved Alexstrasza.
- **Dragonqueen**: Canonical title for Alexstrasza (红龙女王).
- **"You never know what's in the stone until you swing the pick"**: Dwarf proverb — rendered naturally in English; Jace uses it to goad Grede into trying the armor.
- **"By the Light"**: Grede's exclamation of amazement — kept as a dwarven oath.
- **Feralas**: Canonical zone name (菲拉斯).
- **Tauren exile subplot**: Jace's suggestion to ask the tauren for help, and Shandris's cold reaction — reflects the strained night elf / tauren relationship in the story (Mosa's earlier coldness toward the night elves).
- **"May time eventually dissolve your sorrows"**: Night elf farewell — rendered poetically; Jace notes it's unusual for Shandris to say it in Common rather than Elvish.
- **POV**: Third person throughout.
