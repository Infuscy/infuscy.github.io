Chapter 607: Ominous Fury

"Don't you know any control magic? Binding spells and the like?" Jace asked.

"Charm magic, but it's hard to affect targets with too strong a willpower, and it doesn't do much against things with no brains either," Morfell admitted.

"So it's basically useless, then, succubus," the imp said from the side, barely containing his laughter.

Morfell shot the imp a glance and said softly, "Remember those foolish words of yours well."

Jace recalled his head-on clash with Das'mora. She'd quickly unleashed a dreadful dark magic that seized his throat, leaving him unable to breathe. If he hadn't relied on Fel Breath to barely scrape by, he might have lost consciousness then and there — or simply choked to death.

What kind of spell was that? Was it the Death Knight's Asphyxiate from the game?

If only he could learn that control spell. The problem was, he couldn't remember the incantation Das'mora had chanted. She'd spoken it quickly, then pointed lightly and cast the spell successfully. The next second, Jace had felt as though something had seized his neck — there'd been absolutely no room to memorize the incantation.

Could he reconstruct that moment in his mind under the influence of Arcane Intellect?

Still, it was best not to cast Arcane Intellect on himself again for the time being. He couldn't ignore Marin's warning. Better to focus on the magic he was about to practice.

"Stop arguing," Jace said. "Figure out how to stall that hellhound. Try not to kill it — I can't cast summoning magic too many times in a day without consequences. Look at these collapsed stones and pillars, Morfell. Use them to dodge the hellhound. In any case, give me as many attempts at enslavement as possible."

"Other than obeying orders, I have no other choice," the succubus said, leaning against the wall and flicking her tail.

Jace burned a soul shard and chanted the hellhound-summoning incantation. He didn't wield the Blood Demon's Staff — instead he channeled shadow magic with his bare hands to tear open a void rift. A hellhound summoned this way would be weaker.

The incantation complete, a normal-sized hellhound squeezed through the crack and tumbled to the ground, unleashing a stream of strange "tut-tut-tut" demon cries at its surroundings.

Morfell picked up a chunk of rubble and hurled it, striking the hellhound. The creature had been staring at Jace, but it whipped around at the impact and immediately snarled ferociously, lunging at the succubus.

Come.

He didn't just want to enslave this hellhound. He wanted to utterly destroy this demon — no, destroy everything here. The garden outside that was keeping them trapped. The high elves hiding behind the walls who refused to show their faces or help them. The centaur blocking their path back to Gadgetzan…

In the span of a single breath, he suddenly realized his emotions were spiraling out of control.

He even felt an impulse to immediately chant Immolate and incinerate the hellhound — his lips actually trembled as if about to speak the incantation. But he didn't chant anything. The fist he'd clenched for the enslavement spell suddenly erupted in flames…

As his emotions surged, the dark chain sigil that hadn't yet stabilized began to disintegrate and burn, devoured by fel fire. The hellhound let out an even more piercing shriek. Its head was quickly scorched by the blazing fel flames and reduced to ash; its entire chest was burned through…

The whole thing happened in the blink of an eye. Jace quickly drew on his long-honed ability to regulate his emotions and subdued the near-riot within him.

But the hellhound was already dead. He hadn't even felt its soul flee back to the Twisting Nether — it had been utterly consumed in the fel fire he'd unleashed.

Morfell's expression was extremely grave, whether from witnessing the sudden annihilation of a weak demon's soul or something else entirely. She stared over and said, "Master, you've been too heavily influenced by that demon's blood."

"You don't like the way master looks now?" the imp asked with a grin.

"I don't like seeing master unhappy. Clearly, master is extremely unhappy right now," Morfell glared at the imp angrily. "And I'm extremely unhappy too, because you should have been reduced to ashes long ago, just like that hellhound."

The succubus seemed genuinely angry. The imp awkwardly shrank to the side and kept his mouth firmly shut.

"Jace."

Wynne's voice came from behind. Jace turned to see the elf standing at the entrance to the passage, holding half a roasted frog in her hand.

"Are you alright?"

Translator Notes

Flags

- Fel Breath (魔息术): the spell Jace used to survive Das'mora's choking magic. - Asphyxiate (窒息术): Jace wonders whether Das'mora's throat-grabbing dark magic is the Death Knight ability Asphyxiate. - Arcane Intellect (奥术智慧): Jace considers using it to reconstruct the memory of Das'mora's incantation, but holds off due to Marin's warning. - Immolate (灼烧咒): the warlock spell Jace nearly instinctively cast on the hellhound. - Fel fire (邪火): the fel flames that erupted from Jace's clenched fist, consuming the incomplete enslavement sigil and incinerating the hellhound. - Demon blood influence (魔血影响): Morfell warns that the demon blood Jace absorbed is affecting his emotions and control. - Morfell (莫洛菲尔): the succubus; 魅魔 = succubus. - Sazeno (撒厄诺): the imp; 小鬼 = imp. - Wynne (温蕾萨): the high elf ranger.
