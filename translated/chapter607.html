Chapter 607: Ominous Fury

“Don’t you know any control magic? Binding spells and the like?” Jace asked.

“Charm magic, but it’s hard to affect targets with too strong a willpower, and it doesn’t do much against things with no brains either,” Morophil admitted.

“So it’s basically useless, then, succubus,” the imp said from the side, barely containing his laughter.

Morophil shot the imp a glance and said softly, “Remember those foolish words of yours well.”

Jace recalled his head-on clash with Dasmora. She’d quickly unleashed a dreadful dark magic that seized his throat, leaving him unable to breathe. If he hadn’t relied on Unending Breath to barely scrape by, he might have lost consciousness then and there — or simply choked to death.

What kind of spell was that? Was it the death knight’s Asphyxiate from the game?

If only he could learn that control spell. The problem was, he couldn’t remember the incantation Dasmora had chanted. She’d spoken it quickly, then pointed lightly and cast the spell successfully. The next second, Jace had felt as though something had seized his neck — there’d been absolutely no room to memorize the incantation.

Could he reconstruct that moment in his mind under the influence of Arcane Intellect?

Still, it was best not to cast Arcane Intellect on himself again for the time being. He couldn’t ignore Malin’s warning. Better to focus on the magic he was about to practice.

“Stop arguing,” Jace said. “Figure out how to stall that felhound. Try not to kill it — I can’t cast summoning magic too many times in a day without consequences. Look at these collapsed stones and pillars, Morophil. Use them to dodge the felhound. In any case, give me as many attempts at enslavement as possible.”

“Other than obeying orders, I have no other choice,” the succubus said, leaning against the wall and flicking her tail.

Jace burned a soul shard and chanted the felhound-summoning incantation. He didn’t wield Gorefiend’s staff — instead he channeled shadow magic with his bare hands to tear open a void rift. A felhound summoned this way would be weaker.

The incantation complete, a normal-sized felhound squeezed through the crack and tumbled to the ground, unleashing a stream of strange “tut-tut-tut” demon cries at its surroundings.

Morophil picked up a chunk of rubble and hurled it, striking the felhound. The creature had been staring at Jace, but it whipped around at the impact and immediately snarled ferociously, lunging at the succubus.

Come.

He didn’t just want to enslave this felhound. He wanted to utterly destroy this demon — no, destroy everything here. The garden outside that was keeping them trapped. The Highborne hiding behind the walls who refused to show their faces or help them. The centaur blocking their path back to Gadgetzan…

In the span of a single breath, he suddenly realized his emotions were spiraling out of control.

He even felt an impulse to immediately chant Scorch and incinerate the felhound — his lips actually trembled as if about to speak the incantation. But he didn’t chant anything. The fist he’d clenched for the enslavement spell suddenly erupted in flames…

As his emotions surged, the dark chain sigil that hadn’t yet stabilized began to disintegrate and burn, devoured by fel fire. The felhound let out an even more piercing shriek. Its head was quickly scorched by the blazing fel flames and reduced to ash; its entire chest was burned through…

The whole thing happened in the blink of an eye. Jace quickly drew on his long-honed ability to regulate his emotions and subdued the near-riot within him.

But the felhound was already dead. He hadn’t even felt its soul flee back to the Twisting Nether — it had been utterly consumed in the fel fire he’d unleashed.

Morophil’s expression was extremely grave, whether from witnessing the sudden annihilation of a weak demon’s soul or something else entirely. She stared over and said, “Master, you’ve been too heavily influenced by that demon’s blood.”

“You don’t like the way master looks now?” the imp asked with a grin.

“I don’t like seeing master unhappy. Clearly, master is extremely unhappy right now,” Morophil glared at the imp angrily. “And I’m extremely unhappy too, because you should have been reduced to ashes long ago, just like that felhound.”

The succubus seemed genuinely angry. The imp awkwardly shrank to the side and kept his mouth firmly shut.

“Jace.”

Vereesa’s voice came from behind. Jace turned to see the elf standing at the entrance to the passage, holding half a roasted frog in her hand.

“Are you alright?”
