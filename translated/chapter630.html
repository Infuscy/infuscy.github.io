Chapter 630: The Red Dragon

Jace certainly remembered him.

Not only did he remember — he knew why this man had come.

"Looking for you alone? Trouble?" Grede murmured, eyeing the mage not far off.

"It's fine, Grede." Jace turned his head. "Go on ahead — it's getting late. I'll treat you another day."

"That's what you said." The dwarf nodded and left the street corner, looking back every few steps.

The high elf mage watched him go, then approached Jace. "I apologize — when we met before, I didn't tell you who I was, because I'm not accustomed to revealing my identity to others. My name is Krasus. I've come to meet you on behalf of the Kirin Tor."

"I've heard your name, Master." Jace nodded slightly.

Krasus glanced up at the house behind Jace. "You probably think I've come to stop you from meeting your parents, like Biso Ershad — but in fact, the opposite is true."

Jace said nothing. Krasus asked, "You've been helping Stormwind investigate the connection between the orcs and the dragons, isn't that right?"

"Yes," Jace said. "I'm a student of Master Marin. I've been assisting him with this work as an assistant." Krasus said, "Then you must know where the key to solving the Dragonmaw orc problem lies."

"Find the Dragonqueen," Jace said.

The red-robed mage nodded. "Yes. Find the Dragonqueen."

Jace looked into Krasus's eyes. The hidden red dragon ultimately couldn't completely suppress his emotions — when the Queen was mentioned, Jace could feel the scorching flames in his heart churning uncontrollably.

So it was indeed about this matter.

Saving the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza. Should this be his task?

In another timeline, Rhonin, who had been imprisoned for accidentally killing a comrade during the war, had accepted this suicide mission. After a series of harrowing adventures, he'd helped the Dragonqueen throw off the Dragonmaw orcs' control and even defeated Deathwing…

Jace couldn't help but furrow his brows. His mind was in chaos. Krasus raised his eyes and caught his expression. "I know you don't want to go, Mr. Sesso. But after completing this mission, you can be vindicated before the entire Alliance, step back into the light — and then you'll be able to smoothly bring your parents to this residence you've poured your heart into earning."

Jace said nothing.

Krasus continued, "If I were you, I'd seize it with both hands, because you have the ability. Kel'Thuzad told me you're fluent in Orcish, and you also demonstrated your talent for dark magic at the Dark Portal, so I determined long ago that you are the key candidate I've been searching for."

Hearing this, Jace started. "Kel'Thuzad?"

"Yes," Krasus said. "He, like me, strongly recommended you for this role. At the Council of Six meeting, he helped me persuade the other four — including Antonidas — to send you to Khaz Modan to investigate the orcish traces in the ancient dwarven city of Grim Batol."

Jace ran his tongue over his front tooth. So this was what you were waiting for, you damned old bastard, Kel'Thuzad.

Krasus seemed to notice a shift in Jace's expression, but he didn't dwell on it. He continued, "The Kirin Tor always believed that the closing of the Dark Portal trapped Deathwing and his offspring in the other world — Draenor. And for some time, many mages even believed the Dragonlord might already be dead. But in reality, more and more signs indicate that many black dragons still remain on Azeroth — not the juveniles that harass watchtowers and raid villages, but true adult black dragons, and possibly… Deathwing himself."

"I must find Deathwing. Not just me — the entire Kirin Tor is working to track his trail, along with the other adult black dragons. In this process, I need a reliable person to go to Grim Batol and rescue the Dragonqueen before the black dragons catch on. Time is already extremely tight."

"You cannot tell anyone about this. It must be kept secret. Any third party besides you and me could jeopardize the entire plan — including those closest to you."

Jace knew that Krasus feared Deathwing's spies might be spread across Azeroth — whether races loyal to the black dragons, or the members of the Cult of the Dragon hiding within the Alliance. In short, once an outsider learned of it, Deathwing would quickly learn of the plan.

But Jace felt that Deathwing already knew.

Without a doubt, this was a suicide mission. Krasus knew this clearly, and Kel'Thuzad knew it too.

Since Kel'Thuzad had strongly recommended him for this mission, it very likely meant that Kel'Thuzad wanted to use this matter to eliminate him and remove the risk of his own collusion with Deathwing being exposed.

If Kel'Thuzad really was in contact with Deathwing, then when Krasus told the Council of Six about this, Deathwing would certainly have learned of it already.

But could he tell Krasus all his suspicions?

This red dragon before him seemed to still trust Kel'Thuzad quite a bit. And what was Jace? A "man not in the light" — a warlock who kept company with demons.

What's more, regarding Kel'Thuzad's collusion with Deathwing, Jace's suspicions were just that — suspicions. He'd never had any evidence.

Putting all that aside, Kel'Thuzad recommending him was perfectly reasonable. A fellow student who'd attended the Orcish language council, with an exceptional understanding of dark magic — at least among those the Kirin Tor could find, he counted as one of the best.

And the biggest trouble on this Grim Batol expedition — one was orcs, the other was dark magic. For him, it was practically his specialty.

Jace couldn't help but look back over the past few years. It seemed that everything he'd done since entering the Wizard Sanctum had been building toward this decision to go die today.

The mage raised his head. "I know this is a difficult choice. You need time to consider it. But we don't have much time. Every day the Dragonqueen remains in the orcs' hands, we face a graver threat. The black dragons may also reach there before us. I'll be waiting for your decision in the Mage Quarter, Mr. Sesso. Don't make me wait too long."

For some reason, he was facing an even grimmer situation than Rhonin had. In another timeline, Krasus had told Rhonin that an elf would be waiting for him at Menethil Harbor to escort him into the heart of the Wetlands on the mission — and that was Vereesa.

But now there seemed to be no such elf at all. Krasus hadn't mentioned an escort to him. Why?

Did this member of the Kirin Tor Council of Six think he was already powerful enough — no need for a helper, no need for any assistance… At some point the mage had already left, before Jace could even say anything.

Of course, he didn't know what he would've said anyway.
