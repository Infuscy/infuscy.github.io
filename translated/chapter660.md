## Chapter 660: The Disaster of Grim Batol

The orcs charged into the gate, pouring in without end. The tall drakonid at the entrance was already bristling with arrows, yet still brandished its spear, blocking every soldier trying to storm the prison.

The snarling of whelps echoed throughout Grim Batol. Nekros had lost control of the Demon Soul. No matter what, their queen was no longer under duress. It was time for vengeance!

But Jace hadn't finished his task.

He had no more time to search for a way to destroy the Demon Soul. Deathwing could descend on Grim Batol at any moment—he knew it, in his bones he knew…

But the mission Krasus had given him remained unfulfilled.

He tossed the disc to Morfil and pointed at Nekros. "Make him chant the spell—release the Dragonqueen!"

"Yes, Master."

Morfil placed the disc in Nekros's hands. Nekros, kneeling on the ground, gritted his teeth and nodded. His will had been utterly crushed.

"As you command, my queen… my beloved… my all."

Watching Nekros raise the disc high and begin to chant, Jace returned the cracked Skysong to his scabbard and held his staff before him.

An orc was trying to circle behind Grede. Jace casually cast Curse of Agony on him. The orc immediately howled and dropped to his knees, clutching his head, clutching his chest, thrashing in agony. Jace then pointed his staff at a high vantage point where an orc crossbowman was taking aim at Vereesa from behind. The bolt in the crossbow suddenly flew out of control, and the crossbowman himself clutched his eyes and collapsed backward.

Jace unleashed Curse of Agony on every orc still fighting within his line of sight, one after another, and finished them off with Life Drain, until the entire hall was filled with wails and weeping, until black, festering blood poured from every orifice of the orcs' bodies…

He didn't know how long he'd been fighting. Jace felt as alert as if he'd just woken up. His robe, his arms—everywhere was blood that had poured from his nostrils and eyes.

In the heat of battle, when he'd nearly lost himself in the frenzy, a tremendous clatter of iron chains being wrenched apart rang out from behind. The Dragonqueen's forelimbs had already raised her body. Those chains that had seemed utterly unbreakable were being snapped and torn apart like brittle branches, crashing to the ground with a deafening metallic roar…

By now, the Dragonmaw orcs charging in had seen the Dragonqueen rise to her feet. They hesitated and stopped at the gate.

The Dragonqueen's neck flushed a deep red. The temperature in the prison hall spiked. Jace roared to Grede, who was still grappling with several orcs up ahead, "Run, Grede! Run!"

Grede turned his head and saw the Dragonqueen looking straight at where he stood. His eyes went wide and he leaped aside. Alexstrasza opened her enormous maw and unleashed a torrent of flame that blasted out of the gate, incinerating everything in its path.

Jace took the Demon Soul from Morfil, stood up, and looked outside. Hundreds of orcs, fallen dragon corpses, even the dragon eggs—all burned to nothing in that single breath. The hall outside had become a blazing inferno.

"The dragon eggs…" Vereesa said, dazed.

"Deathwing is coming," Alexstrasza said. "I will not leave him anything he can use."

"Deathwing," Vereesa said. "I knew it."

The Dragonqueen said softly, "I am grateful, brave warriors. But I regret to say that we are about to face the most deranged devil in this world…"

"Can you summon the other Aspects here?" Jace asked. "Let them aid us together—whichever ones can come, as many as possible."

Alexstrasza lowered her head. "It is too late now. Summoning the other Aspects requires abundant magical energy and time… and at present, I have neither."

Jace was not surprised by the Dragonqueen's words. In truth, he'd already steeled himself for the likelihood that no other Aspects would come to help.

"Frankly, this is a rather unfair fight," Grede said, looking at the Dragonqueen. "The six of us against him."

"Far more than six, dwarf," Alexstrasza's voice carried a hint of amusement.

With that, the Dragonqueen let out a low cry. Dragon roars echoed from every corner of Grim Batol—the snarls of whelps, the shrieks of hatchlings, and even… a heavy sound Jace had never heard before. Was that the sound of an adult dragon?

"Taelan, my beloved—I do not sense Korialstrasz drawing near. Perhaps he has encountered difficulties and cannot come to our aid. Now we can only rely on ourselves, these three warriors, and our two guests from the Twisting Nether. Do you know what we are about to face?"

"An old friend." A deep, hoarse voice echoed from deep within the citadel. "I have long awaited this battle. I will fight for the Queen to my last breath."

"This moment has come sooner than you think, Tyranastrasz? Old fellow, I'll make sure to give you a quick end!"

A shattering roar instantly drowned out every sound in Grim Batol. Jace was nearly bowled over by the force of that roar. Even Vereesa and Grede couldn't help but cover their ears.

How… he was here? So soon?!

A storm of manic laughter swept through the mountains, reverberating through the corridors and halls of this colossal city carved into the mountain. Almost simultaneously, everything around them began to burn. Stone, walls—black flames whirled like a cyclone across everyone's surroundings…

"So this is…"

Grede, surrounded by flames, could barely stand firm against the raging winds. "What you've been waiting for all along, Grede," Vereesa said through gritted teeth. "Deathwing!"

"Don't forget to drink your potions, you two!"

Jace reminded them as he quickly cast Mage Armor, then raised a Shadow Shield to try to withstand the suddenly surging dark flames.

Hearing the reminder, Grede and Vereesa each drank the Fire Protection Potions they'd prepared in advance. Grede even downed a Strong Shadow Protection Potion, but Jace highly doubted how much good that would do against the magic of the Black Dragonfather.

The Dragonqueen raised her head. Jace followed her gaze upward—and then witnessed something he could scarcely comprehend…

The vaulted ceiling of Grim Batol was torn apart in a single stroke by a wave of darkness. All the bricks, statues, stone pillars, and massive stone slabs were swept into the air by a terrible violet-black flame.

This all-consuming fire rapidly devoured every part of the stone hall—until the Dragonqueen spread her enormous wings to shield them all. The hurricane she conjured blocked the devouring Shadowflame, but it also sent Jace hurtling who-knows-where…

——Alexstrasza! Come… come!

The shadow descending from the sky was like a storm and a cloud of fire pressing down, blotting out the sun, swallowing the entire field of vision. Crimson eyes like two dark suns hanging in the sky gazed down upon the mortal world…

The world spun. Boulders flew through the air in every direction. Jace kicked and grabbed at the air mid-flight, but it was all in vain. The violent cyclone flung him one way and then the other. One sudden spin, and everything went black. He lost consciousness…

- **POV:** Third person throughout.
- **Names:** 克拉苏斯 → Krasus (canonical dragon name, Jace's mission-giver); 考雷斯特拉兹 → Korialstrasz (Alexstrasza's mate, canonical red dragon); 泰兰 / 泰兰尼斯特拉兹 → Taelan / Tyranastrasz (Taelan is Alexstrasza's familiar address to the elder red dragon Tyranastrasz); 死亡之翼 → Deathwing (canonical).
- **Magic:** 法师护甲 → Mage Armor (glossary); 黑暗护盾 → Shadow Shield; 火焰防护药水 → Fire Protection Potion; 强效暗影防护药水 → Strong Shadow Protection Potion; 痛苦诅咒 → Curse of Agony (glossary); 生命吸取 → Life Drain (glossary); 暗影烈焰 → Shadowflame.
- **Key beats:** Jace orders the enslaved Nekros to release Alexstrasza. She breaks free, incinerates the hatchery (including the dragon eggs) to deny Deathwing resources. Deathwing arrives almost immediately and shatters Grim Batol's dome with Shadowflame; the Dragonqueen shields the party but the blast scatters them. Chapter ends with Jace knocked unconscious mid-air.
- **Dragon lore:** Alexstrasza cannot summon the other Aspects (she lacks the power). The red dragons of Grim Batol rally to her—whelps, hatchlings, and at least one adult (Tyranastrasz) answer her call.
- **Tone:** Escalates from desperate last stand to apocalyptic; ends on a cliffhanger as Deathwing's presence obliterates the battlefield.
