## Chapter 670: A Total Loss

By afternoon they had reached the entrance of the road leading into Thelsamar — about time to say goodbye.

Jace looked out at the rolling hills and pine forests ahead, then glanced at the elf beside him. "When's your Ranger Lord promotion ceremony? If I head back to Stormwind now, will I miss it?"

Vereesa rested her elbow on Jace's shoulder and leaned in at an angle. "At the very least, they have to tell me the promotion's been approved first, then set the date for the ceremony, Jace. I'll write you in time — might even catch up to you when you come with me to Quel'Thalas."

"Can I come?" Grede asked. "Not that I'm trying to intrude on anything — I just really want to see the inside of Quel'Thalas, little elf."

"Of course you can," Vereesa said with a smile. "We were just talking about this — you absolutely have to come if you can."

"Ha, nice!" Grede nodded.

"How are you two planning to get back to Stormwind?" Vereesa asked.

"There's a roost in Stormwind now," Grede said. "I know the nearest gryphon roost on the Loch Modan side is at the outpost over in the Valley of Kings. Want me to take you back on a gryphon, Jace?"

"That'll cost a lot, won't it…" Jace said.

Grede said, "The biggest expense is the rider's fee. Now you've got a professional rider right here — I'm not charging you a copper. You just have to pay for the gryphon's feed. Flying from here to Stormwind should only take two or three days. You can't tell me you can't afford two or three days' worth of gryphon feed, can you? I'm sure it's cheaper than a ship ticket from Menethil Harbor to Stormwind! What's more, you're not going to want to go all the long way around the Wetlands again, are you?"

"Then I'm counting on you, Grede," Jace said.

"No problem — just follow me," Grede said. "Have Morophil follow quietly… I'll pick a big gryphon. Your secret's safe with me."

Jace looked around and gave a slight nod. "Then what about you, Vere?"

"I'll head to the Farstrider Lodge to the east, Jace," Vereesa said. "They'll help me contact a dragonhawk rider to get back to Quel'Thalas."

"Then Dalaran it is," Jace said.

"Dalaran it is." Vereesa leaned in and kissed Jace. "I'll miss you."

"Me too," Jace said.

With that, Vereesa smiled, turned, and ran off down the slope. Grede watched her retreating back and said, "Jace, I didn't want to say this in front of her, but there might be a lot of trouble waiting in Quel'Thalas. Not every elf is as friendly as Vereesa Windrunner."

"I know," Jace said. "Honestly, I don't really care."

"Ah, of course not — after all, you're the one who killed Deathwing!" Grede said with a laugh.

"Strictly speaking, I didn't kill him," Jace said. "His own mind went wrong, but I at least saved the Dragonqueen — that's absolutely true."

"If we're being that strict, then the Dragonqueen wasn't saved by you either — she was saved by that orc warlord."

"Morophil charmed him."

"Then Morophil saved her."

"Morophil is my servant…"

"Then the little elf saved her — looks like you're about to become her servant."

"Bullshit."

Getting from Loch Modan back to Stormwind wasn't so simple as just cutting straight across the mountains from north to south — you had to go around Blackrock Mountain…

…After all, black dragons were already appearing around Blackrock Mountain. If you ran into one, a gryphon would have almost no chance of escaping.

Even detouring through the Badlands into Redridge was safer, but not entirely safe. Jace kept a close eye on the sky the whole way for any sign of a dark shadow. Fortunately, nothing happened all the way until they entered the Elwynn region.

The gryphon landed in the Valley of Heroes. Gazing up at the grand statue of Turalyon, Jace felt something different this time.

Honestly, when he'd faced Deathwing in Grim Batol, his mind had been full of nothing but how to destroy the Demon Soul, how to help the Dragonqueen drive back the black dragon, how to survive — almost no fear, doubt, or hesitation at all…

Right up until the very end, until the moment he saw Deathwing swallow the Aspects' power, he'd still been thinking there might be a chance… Only when he realized there truly was nothing to be done did he slowly accept it all.

But now, wave after wave of after-the-fact terror came surging up.

He felt that if he had to do it all over again, he didn't know if he could manage it.

He thought of Turalyon defeating Orgrim Doomhammer at Blackrock Mountain, barely winning the victory in the Orc Wars, then immediately deciding alongside Khadgar and the others to lead the army into the Dark Portal — to face the root of the Horde's threat head-on, heading into another unknown land…

That was courage beyond description.

The whole Alliance would worship a general like that. No wonder Alleria Windrunner could fall in love with a hero like him.

He touched the burn scar on his arm. By now it no longer looked anything like a dragon's claw — the scar from Azuresong had broken the continuity of the pattern.

Right now all he wanted was to hurry home and sleep properly, forget everything about Grim Batol, eat some familiar food, see some familiar faces, then head to Lordaeron — and if possible, throw himself into his mother's arms… The torment, pressure, threats, and the near-inescapable terror he'd endured on this journey had kept his nerves wound tight to the point of madness.

Not much of a hero, he thought with self-derision.

When the gryphon landed on the wall-top roost, the dwarf gryphon keeper hurried over to help get the gryphon under control. He glanced at Grede and said, "Grede? You're back? Gorjet was sent back earlier — no need to worry about him."

"Oh, I wasn't worried about that guy at all," Grede said.

"Who's this, Grede?" the keeper asked. "How do you have so many mage friends?"

"Not so many, this is… uh." Grede started to speak, then hesitated.

Jace straightened his luggage and robe. "Jace Sesso. Pleased to meet you, sir."

The gryphon keeper pushed his helmet up and looked closely. "So you're Jace Sesso?! Why didn't you say so, Grede! I was at Nethergarde Keep too, Mr. Sesso. Damn — my apologies, I really thought you were dead."

"Close enough," Grede said. "You have no idea how dangerous it was, right, Jace?"

"Extremely dangerous," Jace said. "Took a lot to recover afterward. Thank you for remembering me, Mr. Keeper."

The gryphon keeper seemed to have a thousand more questions he wanted to ask. Grede grabbed him and started explaining. Jace looked toward the city and took a deep breath of the not-exactly-fresh air within the walls.

He gazed at the bustling Trade District behind the walls, the smoke rising from Old Town, the white Cathedral of Light, Stormwind Keep resting on the hillside in the distance, and the magic tower in the Mage Quarter surrounded by tall trees. A month ago he'd thought he'd never come back — that Jace Sesso would never come back.

Then he caught sight of the faintly visible red eaves in the Park, and something felt off.

Off? What exactly…

Right — damn it, with Deathwing dead there would be no Cataclysm! The Park was safe?!

Then didn't he buy that house for nothing?! Wasn't that a total loss?!
