Chapter 672: A Northerner's Trouble

"Could I take a look?" Jace asked.

"I'd advise against it," Tharle said. "Galders won't let anyone open that door. You'd best not go down there alone, Jace. But that demon's been quiet too — it was summoned almost a week ago now, and there hasn't been a peep out of that room since. If you're curious about the specifics, you can go ask Ursula downstairs. I don't understand any of that spell and magic business, and I'm afraid of saying something wrong and misleading you."

"Ursula's downstairs? All right. Thanks, Tharle," Jace said.

Tharle opened the door for him. "No problem. I'm glad to see you're all right."

Jace glanced at him. He knew Tharle Mor was just being polite, but the words still stirred something in him. Descending to the basement and entering the main hall, he was frozen in place by the scene before him.

On a large rug on the floor, Erland was on all fours, driving into a female succubus without pause, while Sulenna, sitting bare naked beside them, swapped saliva with him…

Sulenna noticed Jace first and stopped what she was doing with her mouth. Erland looked over as well. The three of them shared an awkward stare for a moment. Sulenna asked, somewhat hesitantly, "Do you… want in?"

"Thanks, but… I'll pass," Jace said.

The unfamiliar succubus lifted her head and, seeing Jace, immediately narrowed her eyes. "Who is he, master?"

Erland said, "Jace Sesso — the most powerful warlock we've got here. You ought to get to know him. Jace, you're never around…"

"…and when you are back you never give us a word. How about we all go for a drink sometime?"

"Next time. You're a bit busy right now," Jace said. "I'm looking for Ursula. Is she in there?"

"She's in the back, reading," Erland said.

"Right," Jace said. "Carry on, don't mind me."

Erland and Sulenna both nodded. Jace cleared his throat and walked down the hall, the sounds from the main hall behind him starting up again in waves.

Heading toward the alchemy lab, before he'd even reached the door Ursula poked her head out.

"Jace, I heard you talking… At least this time you haven't vanished for half a year before coming back."

"Just up north. It's not like I was in Kalimdor," Jace said with a smile.

Entering the alchemy lab, Ursula brewed a cup of herbal tea and handed it to Jace. "But it feels like you were gone a month, almost…" Jace held the warm tea. "What?"

Ursula leaned against the desk and took a sip, her eyes flicking over Jace's. "You seem like you've got your spirit back. You're not all skin and bones and run-down like before. I'm glad to see you've recovered from whatever happened at the Dark Portal. Actually, I think you're even better than before — you've grown so tall, after all. You're turning into quite the handsome young man."

"I don't know if that's true, but it's nice to hear," Jace said.

"I'm telling the truth," Ursula said. Then she asked, "How about it — how many of my potions did you drink?"

"All of them," Jace said.

"All of them?!" Ursula said. "Did you get thirsty out in the wilds and drink them like water, or did you actually take them to Dalaran and sell them?"

"I ran into some really troublesome trouble. I had to make use of everything I could," Jace said.

"And how were my potions? Did they work?" Ursula asked, a hint of a proud smile creeping onto her face.

"More effective than you could imagine," Jace said. "I mean it — I'm planning to do you a free favor for it. Come on, what do you need right now? Materials, or…"

Ursula wagged her finger. "You brought me an exotic insect head from Kalimdor that's impossible to get around here, Jace, and you gave me all that money on top of it. I really can't bring myself to trouble you any more, especially since that concoction set you back a bit as it is."

Your potions helped me kill Deathwing, Jace thought to himself.

He knew that without the dual effects of the Refined Brilliance Elixir and the Restorative Potion, he never could have suppressed the demonic form that Nekarius transformed into. And without the elemental protection potions, Windrunner and Grede very likely wouldn't have survived that apocalyptic storm of flame and Shadow magic intertwined.

More than that — without the invisibility potion, the original plan for the three of them to infiltrate Grim Batol wouldn't have been possible in the first place.

Without Ursula, Jace never could have gotten his hands on all those potions in two days' time and put them to use. He didn't even know who could have brewed him an invinvisibility potion or a Refined Brilliance Elixir — concoctions that were either banned or absurdly difficult to make.

"It's fine, Ursula." Jace set the cloth bundle holding the void robe down on the table. "I'm going to have Gaylene mend this robe for me. I'm heading over to Lordaeron in a little while — if there's anything you need, just say the word."

"Lordaeron?" Ursula tucked the bundle into the cabinet, scratched her chin, and thought for a moment. "Actually, no, there's nothing… oh, wait…"

"What?" Jace asked.

"Forget it." The alchemist waved her hand. "There's really nothing I need you to do. Just knowing you're alive is enough." Jace said, "Say it. If I can't get it, then I can't — but if I can, I'll find a way to help you."

"That serious?"

"That serious."

"All right, Jace," Ursula said. "There is a troublesome problem right now, but I honestly don't know if I should trouble you with it. Do you still remember what I told you about that merchant last time?"

Jace blinked. "Of course I remember. But I'm not helping you stab anyone."

"Of course not," Ursula said. "I wouldn't let Galders or Jagin do it, let alone you. But help me kill a demon — that I can ask? Summoning demons is a crime in Lordaeron anyway, and plenty of mages are always hunting demons that show up… you'd be maintaining public order, more or less?"

"That's fair. What kind of demon?" Jace asked.

"A succubus," Ursula said. "I know those Weilde people hired a Dark Iron dwarf warlock who can summon a succubus to act as a go-between for them and the warlocks carrying out their assassination jobs. That satyr who came after me last time was probably contacted through that succubus."

"She can turn invisible, and that little town of Andorhal doesn't have any real demon-hunting capability. That warlock often lets her move around on her own without a worry — and that gives us our opening, Jace. I want to use that succubus's death to send a warning to that dwarf. Let him know what's what, and stop mucking about in these waters."

Having said that, Ursula thought it over and continued, "Galders told me that succubus is in Andorhal right now. Ah, that place seems… if you're heading to the royal city, that's a bit out of the way, isn't it? In any case, isn't this request a little too much?"

Jace said, "I can make a trip. Are there a lot of demons in Andorhal?"
