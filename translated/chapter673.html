Chapter 673: The Fire Elemental's Enthusiasm

"Couldn't be more than Stormwind," Ursula said, pulling a potion bottle from the small cabinet behind her. "They know I'm an apothecary. Take this purification potion with you. Once you've taken care of that succubus, pour it over the demon's corpse. If he's got any brains at all, he'll know immediately that it's me sending him a warning."

Jace took the potion. "Dark Iron dwarf, succubus — fairly distinctive features. I've more or less got the picture. I'll go take a look when the time comes."

Even though he'd be going up against a succubus, Jace wasn't planning on bringing Morophil along this time. Between Dalaran, visiting his parents, and Quel'Thalas… there was nowhere that wouldn't be a needless risk for a succubus to show up.

If he still couldn't handle a mere summoned succubus belonging to a Dark Iron dwarf assassin by now, then he could forget about calling himself the hero who'd saved the Dragonqueen.

"There's one more thing. I'm going to the practice room to test a spell, Ursula," Jace said. "I want to summon a hellhound to use as a practice target. Could you bring Mr. Basadem along and help keep an eye on things? You know hellhounds are built to hunt spellcasters — I'm afraid something might go wrong by accident."

"Ah… right. There's a demon locked in one of the practice rooms right now," Ursula said. "We'll use the other one."

"Tharle told me about it earlier," Jace said. "I heard it's a voidwalker, is it?"

"Voidwalker," Ursula raised her eyebrows. "Seems to be that sort of demon. On the whole it's a mass of shadow — terrifying, like a ghost. When it first came through it didn't attack us right away, thanks to your binding circle, Jace. But we don't have any good way of hurting that voidwalker either. Erland's even thinking of finding a trustworthy priest to deal with it."

"That's dangerous for a priest too," Jace said. "Did Galders summon this demon?"

"Of course," Ursula said. "The others can't make the incantation work at all. Sulenna tried summoning it first — she couldn't get the spell to take effect no matter what. Galders only needed that necklace and a few tries before he succeeded… he really is gifted."

"Necklace? What necklace?" Jace asked.

Ursula explained, "A necklace with a blood-red stone. Erland found it on an orc corpse in the Black Morass. The Dark Iron dwarf said that kind of necklace can serve as a casting reagent to summon a small Void demon. With your circle in place, Galders decided to test the demon first as practice for summoning a felguard. But none of us expected that we wouldn't even be able to enslave that Void demon, let alone figure out how to forge a master-servant pact with it."

"In principle you'd need to subdue that Void demon first, but…" Jace said. "I'll go ask my demon minions when I get a chance. Maybe they'll have an answer."

"Galders said you'd probably have a solution too," Ursula finished, then spoke in the Demonic tongue: "Basadem, come — help your master."

Her Demonic tongue was remarkably fluent. Jace was a little surprised, but then he thought about it — he hadn't stayed at the Slaughtered Lamb for the better part of a year now. Everyone here must have gotten awfully proficient at the Demonic tongue in order to learn dark magic and summon more powerful demons.

At times like this, it carried a bit of that feeling from childhood — coming back after a long sick leave and finding that all your classmates had moved on to unfamiliar material. "Come on, Jace," Ursula said, walking out of the alchemy lab. "What spell do you want to test?"

Jace followed her into the hall. "Fire."

"Fire again?" Ursula raised her eyebrows. "Last time you helped me test that potion — the combustion potion — remember? Do you want a bottle of that to help out? I've still got two left in my cabinet."

"Do you have a steady supply of Dark Iron dwarf gunpowder now?" Jace asked.

Ursula shook her head. "Galders had originally helped me get in contact, but because that merchant's detective keeps giving me trouble… I can't produce it reliably anymore. Good thing I've still got a little stockpile."

"All right, I won't need it for now."

As he said it, Jace thought to himself that the Dragonqueen had already told him to be careful when casting fire magic. If he added that combustion potion — the one that had nearly blown him and Ursula up together in that room — who knew what might go wrong.

Passing through the main hall, the two of them did their utmost not to look at the group's exhibition, and made their way to the practice room on the other side.

The room he'd always used for spell practice was, as expected, sealed shut now, covered all over with various demonic runes and magic circle lines.

Jace took out the small soul shard he'd prepared and tossed it to the ground, fluently chanting the hellhound-summoning incantation. Soon a rift tore open at the far end of the practice room, and a hellhound was smoothly pulled through from the other world, landing on the floor.

This hellhound was indeed a lot smaller than the one he'd gotten with Teron Gorefiend's staff in hand, but as a target for practicing attack magic it was perfect. If he summoned one of those massive creatures like last time he'd practiced demon enslavement, without Morophil by his side, one slip-up with a countered spell and the thing could wipe out the two spellcasters in the practice room plus the little hellhound all in one go.

"Protect Jace, Basadem."

Ursula said it, but she was clearly a little unnerved by the hellhound Jace had summoned too.

Jace didn't hesitate for a second and immediately began chanting Scorch. He'd been wanting to test the power of the Dragonqueen's essence all the way here — he'd held back long enough…

The moment the first syllable of the incantation left his lips, he felt the blood in his veins boil over. The hellhound, fresh on the ground, immediately sensed the aggression radiating from the spellcaster before it, and the elemental power the man was gathering…

The hellhound lunged without warning, its tentacles greedily lashing outward. Its speed was terrifying — there was no way he'd finish the incantation… But the hellhound had barely taken a single step before a brilliant burst of flame engulfed the entire demon in one stroke!

Jace and Ursula were both blasted backward by the heat wave from that explosive burst. The demon before their eyes was burning like a ball of pure fire!

The flames didn't just devour the demon in the blink of an eye — they even seemed to hunger for more, splashing and spreading toward the surrounding walls. The two of them were forced to press themselves against the base of the wall, yanking the door open and ready to flee at any moment.

There wasn't a single trace of Shadow mixed into that fire. Jace had deliberately restrained his Shadow power, keeping it from coming into play. And yet the hellhound had been reduced to a lump of black cinders in seconds — there was no telling what it had originally looked like at all.

He stared at that lump of cinders for a while. Ursula suddenly let out a shriek and began frantically swatting Jace's shoulder, and only then did Jace realize that his clothes had caught fire.

The two of them fussed for a while and managed to pat out the flames on Jace's hair and clothes. Ursula caught her breath. "You're not burned, are you?" Jace touched his neck and scalp. "Seems like I'm all right. Lucky."

"Good thing you didn't drink that potion," Ursula said. "That fire was terrifying, Jace. Your magical power really is immense, but…"

"…you might want to rein it in a little."

Jace nodded blankly. "You're not the first person to tell me that."
