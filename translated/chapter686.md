## Chapter 686: The Red Dragon Emissary

"This certainly warrants careful consideration." Antonidas nodded, rose to his feet, and walked to the bookshelf. "Should you make your decision, you may come to the Violet Citadel and find me at any time."

"However, regardless of whether you choose to enter Dalaran, I have persuaded the other members of the Kirin Tor Council to open Dalaran's Great Library to you — including those records pertaining to the Order of Tirisfal — as thanks for your actions on this matter."

"Across the three-thousand-year history of the Order of Tirisfal, there have been many great figures who sought to use demons' magic and power against demons themselves. Among them were no shortage of masters who achieved astonishing accomplishments, Mr. Sesso. I imagine this should be of great help to you." As he spoke, he took down a small purple box, opened it, and withdrew a silver ring set with an amethyst, which he presented to Jace.

Within the amethyst's center there seemed to lie a faint-pink Eye of the Arcane, carved from within by arcane energy itself. "Bearing this Kirin Tor Ring of Trust, you may freely access the Order of Tirisfal section of the Great Library."

Jace rose and accepted the ring. Regarding the mysterious Order of Tirisfal, even in his past life he had known little — only a rough outline. To be able to enter Dalaran's Great Library and examine the knowledge those old predecessors had gathered on demonic and shadow magic was naturally a rare opportunity.

Still, he could hear what Antonidas was really saying between the lines. That "no shortage" of dark magic masters had achieved success meant that even more had failed spectacularly — otherwise the Great Library wouldn't be so tight-lipped about that particular section.

"Thank you, Archmage Antonidas. This is far too precious to me."

"Given the sacrifices and contributions you have made, this is truly nothing." Antonidas said. "Archmage, may I ask where Master Krasus is?" Jace said. "I would like to pay him a visit."

"He is not in Dalaran at the moment," Antonidas said.

Jace had assumed that the so-called Red Dragon Emissary was Krasus himself, but only now did he realize that aside from the dragons and his own companions, no one else knew what Krasus's true identity was — and of course they couldn't possibly consider him an emissary of the red dragons.

"Has he returned since Grim Batol?"

"Not to my knowledge," Antonidas said. "But Master Krasus's movements have always been mysterious, and he almost never reveals his plans to outsiders, so this is quite normal."

If Krasus wasn't in Dalaran, could he be recuperating at the Dragonqueen's side? Or was he still trapped among the black dragons, unable to break free?

If only he could find a red dragon to ask. That would be for the best.

Jace said, "May I meet with that Red Dragon Emissary, Archmage?"

"Of course," Antonidas said. "He is in a guest room on the third floor right now. You may go see him whenever you wish."

"One more thing, Archmage," Jace said. "When Master Krasus sent me to Grim Batol, he specifically instructed me not to reveal this mission to anyone. Until I met my two friends at Menethil Harbor, I didn't breathe a single word of what I was about to do to anyone."

"But clearly, the black dragons knew Master Krasus's movements inside and out. Before I even left Stormwind, he assaulted my mind with a magical vision, attempting to stop me from saving the Dragonqueen. In the dream, he appeared as a middle-aged man wearing a dark-brown noble's robe..."

"Lord Daval Prestor," Antonidas said. "While you were en route to Grim Batol, the Kirin Tor and Master Krasus had been investigating the Black Dragonfather's whereabouts. Afterward, we discovered that he had actually been hiding within the court of Lordaeron all along, disguised as an Alterac nobleman — Lord Daval Prestor. The Alliance is still investigating his disappearance. So what you mean to say is that someone within the Kirin Tor leaked information to him."

Jace didn't respond immediately. Antonidas continued, "Deathwing is the foremost dark magic master in the world. We cannot possibly predict how he obtains the information he desires, nor can we know how complex a vision he can weave with his magical arts. To suspect a member of the Kirin Tor Council of Six on that basis alone..."

Hearing him say that, Jace thought to himself: Antonidas is not an ally.

"That's not what I meant," he said quickly. "Archmage, it's just that the Blackfather nearly killed me with his dark magic before I even reached Grim Batol. It makes me... uneasy, looking back."

"That is only natural," Antonidas said. "You are still very young. Possessing such a powerful will in the face of such hardship has already filled me with boundless wonder and admiration. No one would not waver when confronting a dark fortress like Grim Batol — to say nothing of Deathwing. Even the mighty dragons tremble before him."

"Thank you and the Kirin Tor for your precious gift, Archmage Antonidas." Jace slipped the ring onto his finger and stood. "I intend to pay a visit to that Red Dragon Emissary."

Antonidas nodded. Jace left the room and, led by an attendant, made his way toward the Red Dragon Emissary's quarters. The decorations along the way, the elegant style of the buildings' interiors — he had no mood left to appreciate any of it.

Vereesa had been right. The Kirin Tor would not stand on his side.

To put it bluntly, in the eyes of these old mages, he was still an unstable element who dabbled in dark magic — even if he had saved Grim Batol.

"Master Vielan?" the attendant knocked on the door. "Master Jace Sesso of the Kingdom of Stormwind has come." Master Jace Sesso of the Kingdom of Stormwind.

Jace repeated the title in his mind. Ever since arriving in Dalaran, he had heard the various administrators, attendants, and mages here call him a man from Stormwind, or from the Kingdom of Stormwind.

They were right. He didn't belong here.

The deep-purple door before him was flung open all at once. Inside stood a tall man with a small brown beard. He acknowledged the attendant with a nod; the attendant left the corridor, and the man turned his gaze onto Jace.

"Jace Sesso."

The man addressed him by name. Jace felt that this man's appearance and name were somehow familiar, but he couldn't recall right away where he had seen or heard them before.

Vielan stepped aside. "Please, come in."

Jace walked in as he asked, "You... you're a red dragon, aren't you, Master Vielan?"

The Red Dragonflight's emissary could be a dragonkin; high-ranking dragonkin mages could also assume human form.

Vielan said, "Did Prince Korialstrasz not introduce me to you? Ah... I am Vaelastrasz. Does that name sound a little more familiar?"

## Flags

- **Vielan / Vaelastrasz**: The red dragon emissary's mortal name is Vielan (维埃兰); his true draconic name is Vaelastrasz (瓦拉斯塔兹) — the same Vaelastrasz who becomes the second boss of Blackwing Lair ("Corrupted Vaelastrasz") after Nefarian enslaves his will. At this point in the story he is still uncorrupted.
- **Korialstrasz** (考雷斯特拉兹): Krasus's true dragon name. Canonical.
- **Daval Prestor** (达瓦尔·普瑞斯托): Deathwing's human alias — an Alterac nobleman he impersonated to infiltrate the Lordaeron court. Canonical.
- **Kirin Tor Ring of Trust** (肯瑞托信任之戒): Grants access to the Order of Tirisfal section of Dalaran's Great Library.
- **Eye of the Arcane** (紫罗兰之眼): The Kirin Tor's symbol, set into the ring's amethyst as an arcane-carved glyph.
- **POV note**: Original Chinese is first-person; rendered in third person per style guide. Jace's internal monologue ("Antonidas is not an ally") preserved as free indirect thought.
