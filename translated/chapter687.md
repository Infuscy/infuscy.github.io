## Chapter 687: Real or Fake?

He was the second boss of the raid instance Blackwing Lair — the corrupted Vaelastrasz.

Of course, before the black dragon prince Nefarian had captured him and enslaved his will, he had been a kind-hearted red dragon who desired nothing but to destroy the black dragons' plans — meaning, he hadn't yet been "corrupted."

Jace felt that the fact he even remembered his original name was pretty good. There was no way he'd remember his mortal form's name, especially one spoken in Common.

"Did you make that orb that turned me into an orc?" Jace asked.

Vielan asked, somewhat surprised, "You could actually tell I made it? I deliberately masked every last trace of red dragon magic emanating from that orb, just so you could infiltrate Grim Batol smoothly..."

"I didn't figure it out," Jace said. "Krasus said the orb was made by a friend of his, and I naturally made that guess."

"So that's how it was," Vielan said. "The Grim Batol incident happened so quickly. At the time, I was tracking black dragon movements in the wastes south of Loch Modan, so I didn't respond to the Queen's summons in time. By the time I arrived, everything was already over."

He pulled over a chair. "Sit down, Jace. Actually, I've been wanting to meet you too. Is there anything I can help you with?"

"I was wondering where Master Krasus is now," Jace said.

Vielan shook his head. "I don't know either."

"Didn't he go to Grim Batol?" Jace asked.

"No," Vielan said. "He hasn't shown himself since Grim Batol. In fact, a few days before Grim Batol, I had already stopped hearing his voice. But that's not strange — there was once a span of several decades during which I heard not a single word about him. During that time, the Kirin Tor even expelled him from the Council of Six. And of course, this isn't the first time that's happened."

"But I believe he's imprisoned by Deathwing and still can't break free," Jace said.

Vielan pondered for a moment, then asked, "Did you see this in the nightmare Deathwing gave you? The Queen mentioned that Deathwing has been trying to control your mind with magic."

"Yes."

"You should also know that Deathwing loves weaving illusions to deceive and frighten."

"I know."

"But still, describe what you saw," Vielan said. "Deathwing is arrogant and conceited. If he truly captured Prince Korialstrasz, using dreams to display his power to you would be the most natural thing in the world. We cannot ignore that possibility." Jace nodded.

Frankly, he could barely recall what he had seen in those dreams anymore. He felt that after the Grim Batol affair had settled, he had deliberately tried to forget everything Deathwing had left on him.

It seemed that merely recalling those dreams, recalling what Deathwing had said to him and shown him, would cause him to be eroded by shadow power. But this was no time to flee from those nightmares. Krasus wasn't in Dalaran, and he wasn't with the red dragons either. He was very likely in danger.

"A prison," Jace said. "Braziers... Krasus's human form trapped inside. I remember..."

"The Prestor family dungeons? I don't think those could hold a prince."

Vielan narrowed his eyes as he spoke. For a moment, Jace could even see his pupils gradually elongate into vertical slits, like a reptile's... which immediately reminded him of Deathwing's eyes from the dream, and he drifted for a moment — the images in his memory becoming more vivid.

"Black walls, very tall and vast. Pitch-black corridors. Black metal chains," Jace said. "Those chains bound him, as if enchanted."

"Blackrock Mountain?" Vielan asked. Blackrock Spire...

Yes, that was right. Jace recalled the appearance of Blackrock Spire from the game — it was almost exactly like that.

It was just that in the dream, he had focused too much of his attention on Krasus and Deathwing. And... he had also half-convinced himself that it was nothing more than a deceptive illusion.

"Does it look like Dark Iron dwarven architecture?" Vielan pressed. "Roughly similar to Grim Batol, but built entirely from black stone bricks and metal..."

"It does look like dwarven construction," Jace said.

"Then I can't sit around here doing nothing any longer. I should go take a look at Blackrock Spire." Vielan stood. "I cannot ignore the possibility that he's really there. Thank you for the lead. I know recalling Deathwing's visions must be painful for you."

"Before I set out, I have a personal gift for you, Jace." He turned and took a large dark-brown cloak from a wardrobe. "Please accept this. It's my thanks for rescuing my mother from Grim Batol."

"This is..." Jace touched the cloak and felt that the black patterns on it didn't seem like cloth — more like metal threads, enough to make his hand feel like it might be cut just by touching them.

"This is a cloak I've treasured for a long time, made from the skin and scales of a powerful black drake I defeated long ago," Vielan explained. "After making that orb, I specifically had this cloak enchanted so that its wearer could transform into an orc. I think you'll like this cloak. The orcs possess a great deal of knowledge about demonic and shadow magic that none of us know about. Don't you want to ask them directly?"

"Good heavens," Jace said. "This thing is incredible..."

Vielan said, "This cloak is far more stable than that orb. Most importantly, it can't be easily destroyed by magic."

"How do I trigger its power?" Jace asked.

"Put on the cloak," Vielan said. "Then speak a simple draconic incantation — Vaelastraszanoth — and that's it. Your body flows with the Queen's essence, so triggering this effect should be simple enough."

Jace wrapped the cloak around himself. "Can I try it here?"

"Of course." Vielan stepped aside.

Jace tried to speak it: "Vaelastraszanoth..."

With a boom, wreathed in a blaze of flame, Jace once again felt something similar to when he had activated that orb — a searing energy causing his form to rapidly expand...

He transformed into an orc once more. But compared to the last time, this one was taller and more solid — less a wretched, scheming orc warlock and more a warrior.

Vielan looked him over and nodded. "Although this transformation can resist magical disruption, it doesn't last as long as that orb, Jace. At most two or three days, and it requires a week or two to recover its power. So don't think you can go live in an orcish camp."

"I'll remember."

"Then, I suppose I should have that orb back."

"Uh, I lost it. At Grim Batol."

"Ah, so it really... went where it was meant to go." Vielan sighed. "No matter. It's time to say goodbye, Jace."

Jace held up the cloak and cautioned, "If Krasus is trapped in Blackrock Spire, that means the black dragons have already taken control of it."

"I'm well aware." Vielan threw on his red traveling robe. "Don't worry, Jace. I have ways to slip past the black dragons' detection. I'm only going to see the situation and confirm that your dream isn't one of Deathwing's magical illusions. Even if the Prince really is trapped in there, I'll go notify the Queen first."

## Flags

- **Blackwing Lair boss reference**: Jace recognizes Vaelastrasz as the second boss of Blackwing Lair — "Corrupted Vaelastrasz" — whom Nefarian enslaves. At this point the corruption hasn't happened yet.
- **Vaelastraszanoth** (瓦拉斯塔扎尔诺斯): The draconic incantation that triggers the cloak's transformation effect. Constructed from Vaelastrasz's name + a draconic suffix.
- **The cloak**: Made from the skin and scales of a black drake Vielan defeated long ago, then enchanted to allow the wearer to assume orc form. More stable than the orb, but shorter duration (2–3 days) and longer cooldown (1–2 weeks).
- **Blackrock Spire** (黑石塔): Vielan identifies the dream-prison as matching Blackrock Spire's architecture — black stone, black metal chains, Dark Iron dwarven style.
- **Prestor family dungeons**: Vielan initially suggests the Prestor (普瑞斯托) family dungeons as a possible location, but dismisses them as insufficient to hold a dragon prince.
- **Game mechanics reference**: Jace's internal commentary about "remembering his original name" is a gamer nod — he knows Vaelastrasz from the raid but not his mortal alias.
