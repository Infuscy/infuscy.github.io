Chapter 689: The Magister in the Library

The next morning, after bidding farewell to Grede, Jace and Vereesa arrived at the port on Lake Lordamere. Seeing the endless stream of travelers preparing to depart for the Lordaeron capital, both of them had an ominous feeling.

Sure enough, with Winter Veil approaching, there were far too many travelers on the lake. The only seats they could get were on an evening ship. "So what do we do now?" Jace asked. "Go somewhere and have some fun?"

Vereesa glanced at the pine groves on a distant island on the lake. "I don't know, Jace. Best not to go too far."

"Of course," Jace said. "If we miss the boat back, that'll waste another whole day."

"No." Vereesa gave him a sidelong look. "I'm about to be too tired to walk, you maniac."

Jace laughed. "How about the Great Library? Archmage Antonidas gave me a Kirin Tor ring that lets me access some of the Order of Tirisfal's content that isn't open to outsiders. I've been wanting to take a look. Since we can't leave right now, let's go check it out."

"Then let's go. I've never been to Dalaran's library," Vereesa said.

The two walked toward the Great Library. Jace said, "King Varian Wrynn plans to take back a mage tower from the gnolls at Stonewatch next spring."

"Ilgalar Tower," Vereesa said.

"You actually know about it?" Jace asked.

"When I was tracking that death knight, I did some research on the Redridge Mountains, Jace. I just thought that tower was still in the Alliance's hands," Vereesa said.

"It isn't anymore," Jace said. "The King said that if I help take that tower, he'll give it to me. So I also want to look up some things about mage towers in Dalaran's Great Library... after all, I know nothing about mage towers, let alone how to manage one... honestly, it's a little embarrassing."

"He's going to give you that tower?! King Varian really treats you well," Vereesa said, surprised.

"Dealing with a pack of gnolls shouldn't require my help, right? I remember you and Grede could charge into the Westfall forest alone and steal my bounty a long time ago."

"You don't have to hold a grudge for that long..." Jace muttered.

Dalaran's Great Library wasn't a single building — it was a district composed of many buildings together. The largest one was practically comparable in scale to the Violet Citadel itself. Many mages came and went, all wearing the purple robes of the Kirin Tor or Dalaran. Jace and Vereesa, a pair wearing ordinary clothes, stood out somewhat.

Arriving at the library's entrance, the two most eye-catching individuals naturally drew the attention of the Eye of the Arcane guards.

Several purple-clad guards looked over. One of them stepped forward. "The Great Library of Dalaran allows all residents and travelers of Dalaran to enter, but identification is still required. You two."

"Jace Sesso." Jace presented his two rings as he said it.

Vereesa took out the Windrunner family crest. "Vereesa Windrunner of Quel'Thalas."

"Please enter. Apologies, you two." The guard bowed slightly.

"Sir," Jace asked, "I'd like to know where the Order of Tirisfal-related books are."

"I can take you there, Master Sesso," the purple-clad guard said. "That area isn't easy to find."

"I appreciate it, sir." Jace said.

"Happy to be of service," the guard said.

Following the purple-clad guard into the Great Library, stepping on the purple carpet, Jace felt as though he had entered a fortress built out of books used as bricks.

Even Vereesa looked up in astonishment at those impossibly tall bookshelves, which mages telekinetically retrieved or returned books with arcane spells. The entire hall was therefore filled with a metallic scent emanating from the arcane energy, overpowering even the fragrance of the books.

"The mages of Dalaran must have restrained themselves from the tenet of curbing magical abuse," Vereesa said softly.

"Shh." Jace raised a finger to his lips. Vereesa smiled.

"Just ahead, you two," the guard said. "Once you pass through this corridor, you'll enter the section with Order of Tirisfal-related records. Please do not transcribe the books inside, and do not attempt to take anything out. That is a grave crime under the Kirin Tor. Also, Lady Vereesa Windrunner... forgive me, but you are not permitted to enter. Only those wearing a Kirin Tor ring may consult the books within."

"I understand. It's no problem." Vereesa nodded slightly. The guard bowed and left.

"I'll be out soon, Wen," Jace said. "I'm just seeing if there's anything I want. I won't dig too deeply."

"I'll go look at other books. Let's see how the Kirin Tor mages describe our Quel'dorei." Vereesa folded her arms. "Still, I'm curious about what you're planning to look for in there."

"Knowledge about the Ered'ruin," Jace admitted. "Doomguard invading Azeroth isn't exactly rare, and there are even demons like Dathremar who've been lurking here all along. I'm sure the Order of Tirisfal must have extensive records on this kind of demon."

"If they've even figured out the Ered'ruin language... then I won't have to go through the trouble of specifically summoning a doomguard to help me decipher those scrolls, right?"

"Makes sense." Vereesa nodded. "By the way, when exactly are you planning to start learning Thalassian? Now you're about to start learning another demon language right before my eyes..."

"Starting from this trip to Quel'Thalas, Vereesa," Jace said. "I promise to start learning, from the first Thalassian character, bit by bit..."

Just as the two of them were chatting happily, a rather magnetic male voice speaking a string of Thalassian suddenly came from the corridor.

It was a very slender high elf with red hair, wearing a light-gray robe, with a pale-blue magic gem mounted on his head. He also wore a Kirin Tor Ring of Trust on his hand, but his robe wasn't of the Kirin Tor or Dalaran.

The elf glanced at Jace, then bowed to Vereesa and continued speaking in elvish. Vereesa, however, replied in Common: "I'm just here to browse with Jace. There's nothing in particular I want to see, Mr. Dar'shil."

"So this is Mr. Jace Sesso." The elf nodded slightly. "I've heard your name several times already. It's a great honor to meet you. Then I won't disturb your date. Farewell."

The elf left in a hurry. Dar'shil... Jace felt he'd heard this name somewhere.

"Who is he?" Jace asked.

"Dar'Khan Drathir," Vereesa said. "A magister, and a scholar devoted to studying the Twisting Nether, Jace. I'm not surprised at all to see him here."

Translator Notes

Flags

- Ilgalar Tower (伊尔加拉之塔): A mage tower in the Redridge Mountains (Stonewatch area) currently held by gnolls. King Varian promises it to Jace if he helps retake it. Canonical location. - Eye of the Arcane (紫罗兰之眼): The Kirin Tor's symbol; the guards at the Great Library are its wardens. - Ered'ruin (埃雷杜因): The demon race — Doomguard. Jace is researching their language to decipher scrolls obtained from Dathremar without having to summon a doomguard. - Dathremar (达斯莫拉): A demon (likely Nathrezim/dreadlord-type) who has been lurking on Azeroth. Jace possesses scrolls with Ered'ruin script that he needs translated. Name rendering tentative (OC). - Dar'Khan Drathir (达尔坎·德拉希尔): Canonical — a Quel'Thalas magister who later betrays the Sin'dorei to the Scourge, helping Arthas breach the kingdom's defenses. Vereesa knows him as a scholar of the Twisting Nether. - Quel'dorei (奎尔多雷): "High elves" in Thalassian — the term Vereesa uses for her people. - Thalassian (萨拉斯语): The elven language. Jace promises to start learning it on their trip to Quel'Thalas. - Windrunner family crest: Vereesa presents this as identification — the Windrunner family sigil. - Stonewatch (石堡): The Redridge border fort where Ilgalar Tower is located.
