## Chapter 690: A Future Traitor

Dar'Khan — Jace could never forget this guy.

In Quel'Thalas, he had long been a frustrated magister. His research into the Twisting Nether and dark magic grew increasingly extreme. Decades later, when the Scourge invaded Quel'Thalas, he defected to the Lich King in order to gain powerful necromantic power, helping the death knight who led the undead armies — Arthas — breach Quel'Thalas's defenses, leading to the fall of the Sunwell and the elven kingdom.

Jace stared at his retreating back, hesitating for a moment. Vereesa asked, "Have you heard of him?"

"A magister of fairly high standing. I may have heard of him, but I don't recall," Jace said.

"Then, Jace, I'll wander elsewhere for a bit," Vereesa said.

Jace nodded and walked into the corridor of the library's inner district.

He really couldn't ignore Dar'Khan. If not for him, the elves' defenses wouldn't have been so easily breached by the Scourge's undead armies. Remember that during the Orc Wars, even the Red Dragonflight hadn't been able to completely destroy Ban'dinoriel's magic to help the Horde enter Silvermoon.

Come to think of it, what had Dar'Khan come in here to look up?

And he'd come and gone in such a hurry, walking so fast, as if he'd accomplished his goal and was rushing to carry out the next step.

Considering this guy had always been connected to the Twisting Nether or shadow magic, Jace even felt a sense of being outpaced. But for now, he had no good way to find out what the other party was doing.

Compared to the outside of the Great Library, this district was not only darker and narrower, but also had few people around. After all, not everyone had the right to come here and consult the knowledge and records left behind by the Order of Tirisfal.

Jace casually looked through several bookshelves and cabinets, scanning the books on them, and realized that most of what was recorded here was knowledge and records the Order of Tirisfal had gathered from all over the world — even from the Twisting Nether — concerning demons, the Burning Legion, or shadow creatures. There was no dark magic, including spells... methods of casting, and so on.

Of course there couldn't be such things. If the contents here weren't good for being open to the outside, then these spells and magic would basically count as absolute taboos.

It was also possible that the Order of Tirisfal simply hadn't been able to preserve these things properly.

Jace walked back and forth and found the bookcase on demon languages. The records of demon languages here were far more detailed than those in the Wizard Sanctum. Perhaps he could really dig up something valuable.

However, after searching through the records related to the Ered'ruin, much of his initial optimism was dampened. In the end, he only found a few books describing the linguistic characteristics of doomguard. There were no specific dictionary translations or such content at all — only a few fragments, even far fewer records than the ancient Nathrezim language.

In other words, even the Order of Tirisfal... knew very little about the language of the Ered'ruin race.

Jace guessed this was because most of the Ered'ruin the Order of Tirisfal had encountered communicated in demonic — that is, the Eredar language — and rarely used their own original language to communicate.

Perhaps only when high-ranking Ered'ruin demons conversed with each other would they occasionally use their own nearly-forgotten language.

If his guess was correct, the so-called superior officer of the Burning Legion who had communicated with Dathremar should also have been an Ered'ruin doomguard. That was why the spells recorded by Dathremar were covered in Ered'ruin characters.

If that was the case, then those scrolls he'd found at Dathremar's place were extremely valuable — this was important linguistic material that even the Order of Tirisfal lacked... no wonder the Reliquary was willing to pay such a high price as a reward.

The Reliquary...

Jace suddenly thought — didn't Vereesa know how to trace magical residue?

Could she trace this Dar'Khan and see exactly what he'd been reading in this library?

After wandering around for a few circles, he glanced at his pocket watch. It was almost noon. Time to go out and get something to eat. Vereesa must be getting anxious from hunger by now.

Coming out of the corridor, he saw the elf sitting at a nearby table reading a book.

"Wen." He sat down beside her. "If I asked you to trace that magister's magical residue, could you manage it?"

"Trace a magister's magical residue in Dalaran?" Vereesa shook her head. "I'm not a mage, Jace. That's very difficult to do. Why are you suddenly so interested in that person? Did his attitude just now displease you? Many magisters are like that. They're not just like that with you — they're the same with the Farstriders."

Jace said, "You said he's a scholar who studies the Twisting Nether. I'm just a bit curious about what he's planning to look up. If it can't be done, then forget it."

Vereesa raised an eyebrow. "Performing a sympathetic working in a place like Dalaran where arcane energy converges to find a magister's magical residue requires many conditions, Jace. First, you need to have come into contact with something of that magister's magic — his staff, for example. Then, the thing you're looking for — the books he might have read, for instance — also needs him to have concentrated on them for a long enough time to leave detectable magical residue."

"Then, you need a gifted, well-trained mage. Clearly, I'm not one. Remember in Eldre'Thalas? Once the magic got chaotic, I couldn't find anything at all — to say nothing of these extremely subtle traces of a magister's compared to demons."

"Finally, tracing a magister without any reason is illegal in both Quel'Thalas and Dalaran. So whoever you get to help you with this must be trustworthy — someone who won't report you."

A gifted, well-trained mage, Jace thought. And trustworthy.

Damn it, wasn't there one right here in Dalaran? Amy Marin!

Jace scratched his head. He couldn't believe he hadn't thought of Amy until now. He should have come to visit her in Dalaran in the first place.

Maybe it was because the Violet Citadel and the Krasus business had occupied his thoughts too much, and then he'd run into Vereesa so early.

"Do you know any professors currently in Dalaran? Preferably from the School of Evocation... I think a frost mage... I want to find a Kirin Tor mage's residence. Amy Marin. You should know her."

Vereesa said, "Of course I remember her. I'll go ask at the Quel'Thalas embassy district. It's already noon — if we're going, we need to hurry, Jace."

## Flags

- **Dar'Khan Drathir** (达尔坎·德拉希尔): Canonical traitor of Quel'Thalas. Jace knows from future lore that he defects to the Lich King during the Scourge invasion, helping Arthas (阿尔萨斯) breach Quel'Thalas's defenses and destroy the Sunwell (太阳之井). This is Jace's internal knowledge — he can't reveal it openly.
- **Arthas** (阿尔萨斯): Canonical — the death knight who leads the Scourge armies against Quel'Thalas. Jace knows him as Prince Arthas Menethil.
- **Ban'dinoriel** (班蒂诺雷尔): The magical barrier protecting Quel'Thalas. Canonical — during the Orc Wars, the Horde (with Red Dragonflight help) couldn't fully destroy it; only Dar'Khan's betrayal lets the Scourge through later.
- **Ered'ruin** (埃雷杜因): The Doomguard demon race. The Order of Tirisfal's records on their language are sparse — most Ered'ruin communicate in Eredar (艾瑞达/demonic) rather than their native tongue.
- **Nathrezim** (纳斯雷兹姆): Canonical dreadlord race. Their ancient language is better documented than Ered'ruin.
- **Dathremar** (达斯莫拉): The demon whose scrolls Jace possesses — written in Ered'ruin script. Jace deduces Dathremar's Burning Legion superior was also an Ered'ruin doomguard.
- **The Reliquary** (神圣遗物学会): Canonical — the Blood Elf organization of magisters and researchers. They're paying Jace for the Ered'ruin linguistic material.
- **Amy Marin** (艾米·马林): From the glossary — apprentice mage, daughter of Master Marin. Jace realizes she's in Dalaran and could help trace Dar'Khan's magical residue.
- **School of Evocation / frost mage**: Jace specifies he needs an Evocation-specialist (塑能系), likely a frost mage (冰霜法师), to perform the sympathetic tracing working.
- **Eldre'Thalas** (埃雷萨拉斯): Vereesa references a past incident where she failed to trace magical residue in chaotic conditions — explaining why she can't do it herself.
- **Farstriders** (远行者): Vereesa notes that magisters are condescending not just to Jace but to Farstriders in general.
