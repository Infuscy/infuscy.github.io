## Chapter 705: Farstrider Retreat

Farstrider Retreat lay to the southeast of Silvermoon, already inside the runestones. When Jace set out from the Windrunner residence he left the imp and his demon pack back in Windrunner Village, then rode hawkstriders to the retreat together with Grede, Vereesa, and a company of Farstriders. They rode hard the whole day before arriving.

The closer they got to the Sunwell, the more the scenery of Quel'Thalas became dreamlike. By the time they reached this point, both trees and grass seemed alive, brimming with radiant energy. Numbers of elves simply sat on the meadows in conversation, several to a group playing music together, the sound of talk and laughter rising and falling.

By comparison, their little party of riders pushing hard along the road stood out like a sore thumb.

As they passed a group of elves chatting while sitting on a wooden fence by the roadside, the elves looked up at Jace and Grede and said something in the elven tongue, surprised. Vereesa shot them a withering look; the group clapped hands over their mouths, lowered their heads, and shrank away. Jace guessed it certainly hadn't been any flattering term.

Farstrider Retreat was a sizable domed sanctuary, its outer walls wrapped in vivid red and gold streaks like living flame. A spiraling staircase ran up the outer wall to a second-floor terrace, reminding Jace of the design of the Wizard Sanctum's mage tower — at the very least in this respect, human mages had certainly learned from the elves.

It sat on a high slope near a junction. Dismounting from the hawkstriders and looking northwest, Jace could already see, past the lush forest, the rising towers of Silvermoon in the distance. He wondered whether he'd get a chance to actually go inside the city this time.

When they saw Vereesa arrive, many Farstriders came forward to offer their congratulations and wishes in the elven tongue. Vereesa happily thanked each of them in turn. Jace noticed that the Ranger-Lord he'd met at Nethergarde Keep — Lor'themar Theron — was here too.

After speaking with Vereesa, Lor'themar Theron looked over and lowered his head slightly. Jace lowered his head in return.

The Farstriders weren't the only ones who'd arrived. Plenty of magisters had come as well, and in the distance there were even Light-worshippers and priests — a turnout far larger and grander than Jace had imagined. He even spotted several squads of Silvermoon Guardians carrying long shields and warblades advancing up the main road from the direction of Silvermoon.

"I've never seen so many elves in my whole life," Grede muttered beside him.

"Neither have I," Jace said, watching the elven priests approaching from over there.

The dwarf unconsciously swallowed. "I'm getting more and more uncomfortable, Jace. Hiding out here is worse than being cooped up in Grim Batol."

Before long the priests also came up to Vereesa to express their blessings. Some of them, after leaving, went to chat with the magisters or Farstriders. Others whispered to each other while looking over at the two outsiders.

Truly more agony by the minute.

Jace rolled his shoulders — when suddenly someone slapped him and Grede from behind. The two of them turned around. It was… "Kael'fas?"

"You two." Kael'fas Sunwing stood with his hands on his hips. "Why aren't you going over to chat with those folks?"

He looked at Jace. "You're a warlock — you ought to have plenty to talk about with those mages who study the Twisting Nether, don't you think?"

"Neither of us speaks the elven tongue," Grede said. "What would we even talk about?"

"You can't be serious." Kael'fas frowned. "Jace Sesso, you've already gotten the Windrunner little princess, and you still haven't learned Thalassian?"

"Busy…" Jace said, guilty.

"Look at Nathanos." Kael'fas pointed at a ranger off in the distance wearing a brown cloak and green leathers. "That man's Thalassian is already indistinguishable from a Quel'dorei's."

"Is Lord Nathanos here?" Jace looked over in surprise. Sure enough, the ranger's build did look sturdier than those around him — it was just that he'd been chatting away so naturally with the other elves that Jace hadn't even noticed him a moment ago.

"I'm surprised that human ranger came too," Grede said.

Kael'fas said, "Practically all the Ranger-Lords of Quel'Thalas have gathered. Of course he had to be here too — after all, he is a Ranger-Lord himself."

"You don't approve of him becoming a Ranger-Lord?" Jace asked.

Kael'fas answered, "In terms of the leadership and combat ability he showed during the Orc Wars, as a human he was genuinely remarkable. But there are many Quel'dorei Farstriders who made enormous contributions during the Troll Wars who still haven't been promoted… especially compared to Lor'themar Theron, who became Ranger-Lord before him. By that measure he's less than fully convincing, Jace."

Just then Vereesa finally freed herself and came back over to the group. "Master Kael'fas, I'm truly glad you came back from Lordaeron for my promotion ceremony."

"An order from General Sylvanas Windrunner — I had no choice but to attend." Kael'fas smiled. "But congratulations on finally getting what you wished for, Lady Vereesa."

"Don't call me 'lady,' Master," Vereesa said. "You will always be my teacher."

Kael'fas said, "If you'd spoken to me this politely a few years back, my teaching might have gone a good deal more smoothly."

Vereesa gave an embarrassed laugh. Just then, a horn sounded from the heights of Farstrider Retreat. Even without understanding Thalassian, Jace knew — the ceremony was about to begin.

The guards coming from the direction of Silvermoon had already taken up positions guarding every approach to the retreat and were standing at attention at key points. Watching their stern demeanor, Jace had the distinct feeling this ceremony wouldn't be quite as simple as it looked.

Several more priestesses then came over to Vereesa to express their blessings, but the area around the retreat had already grown much quieter than before.

"Will Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider be coming?" Grede couldn't help asking. "I've never seen that fellow."

Kael'fas gave him a look and said softly, "Grede, what are you thinking?"

"What do you mean?" Jace asked.

"Of course he'll be coming," Kael'fas said, as though it were the most obvious thing in the world. "This is Vereesa Windrunner, after all."

Kael'thas is coming?

Holy hell — finally, a chance to meet him.

Jace's eyes locked onto the road leading toward Silvermoon, where hawkstrider knights and shield-bearing guards were still approaching in a steady stream.

Then a flock of white hawkstriders, surrounded by red and gold ones, arrived at Farstrider Retreat.

At their head rode an elven leader clad in full golden armor, draped in sun-bright golden pauldrons. When he appeared, every mage, Farstrider, and priest present bowed toward him. The honorific they called out in the elven tongue was one even Jace could understand.

He was the Sun King — Anasterian.
