## Chapter 716: Shadowsteps in the Woods

After retrieving the robe Jialein had mended from Ursula, Jace spent that morning putting on his robe, throwing on his cloak, lacing up a clean pair of boots, and combing his hair into something presentable before heading for the palace.

The guards at Stormwind Keep had grown quite familiar with him. Even without showing the ring on his finger, without giving his name or stating his business at the palace, the moment they laid eyes on him the guards would call out "Master Jace Sesso" and clear the way.

Being treated with that kind of deference in the palace still sat uncomfortably with him. Fortunately, this wasn't the time for any ceremony or important rite — the keep's inner sloping passages didn't have that many guards stationed along them, only patrols moving up and down, so he didn't have to be welcomed like that the whole way.

When he was about halfway there, Lord Bolvar Fordragon came out of a side passage. He wasn't wearing armor, just a pale yellow silk coat that looked a bit thin for winter, and he met Jace head-on as Jace came up the main path.

"Master Jace Sesso."

"Lord Bolvar."

Bolvar nodded slightly and pointed to a passage not far behind Jace on the far side. "Still not quite familiar with this keep, are you? I'll admit there are times even I get turned around in here. The court mages' council chamber and the library are that way."

Jace inclined his head slightly. "I was planning to see His Majesty, my lord."

"Unfortunately, the king is not at Stormwind Keep," Bolvar said. "I heard you went back to Lordaeron to visit your parents, so you may not know. His Majesty traveled to Ironforge some time ago for King Magni's Winter Veil banquet — by now he should be on his way back. If you need to see him, you can wait a few more days."

"I see," Jace said. "Actually, I wanted to ask about the timing of the operation to retake Iragal's Tower. The king told me before that the kingdom planned to launch an offensive against the gnolls in spring."

"From what I know, Stonewatch's commander Tordman intends to launch the offensive in early February, after Stormwind's reinforcement column arrives at the fort," Bolvar said. "He's already received word that you'll be taking part in the operation. As a member of the court mages, there's no need for you to travel with the reinforcements, but you'd best arrive at Stonewatch within two to three weeks."

"Understood, my lord," Jace said. "There's one more thing I wanted to know — that horse the king gave me, Arna. Where is she now?"

"She's been boarded in the Mage Quarter this whole time. Haven't you gone to see her?" Bolvar asked.

Hearing that, Jace thanked the lord at once and left the palace for the stables outside the Mage Quarter walls. Sure enough, Arna was still there.

But compared to a year ago, she seemed to have grown quite a bit thinner. Had the king not been taking proper care of her?

Jace hadn't ridden her to all that many places, but she was still the first mount he'd ever owned, and he could feel that Arna had grown quite attached to him too. Seeing her this thin tugged at his heart.

Then again, Arna had always been a sturdy, handsome mare. Even grown thinner, she wasn't any weaker than the other horses in the Mage Quarter stables.

When she caught sight of him, Arna let out an excited whinny in the pen, craning her neck outward uncontrollably. Jace couldn't help asking the stablemaster walking alongside him, "Has she been let out very little these past months?"

"At the same frequency as the other horses, Master Sesso. We've followed the king's instructions to the letter in caring for Lady Arna — but she wants to go out and roam more than the others do. We're short-handed here at the stable, so some of her needs may have been overlooked. Truly, our apologies."

"Still, thank you for helping look after her," Jace said. "Is she fit to go out now?"

"I'd say she can't wait any longer," the stablemaster said.

Jace nodded. In truth, the reason he'd gone to the palace to find Arna was so he wouldn't need to hire a carriage when he headed to Moonbrook. Before going to Redridge, he planned to look in on Musul and see if he could get his help summoning a doomguard.

The summoning incantation for Das'mora they'd obtained from the satyr in Maul — even Sazeno couldn't use it to summon other ered'ruin demons — so Musul was now the biggest breakthrough.

Trying to summon a doomguard at the Slaughtered Lamb with those warlocks who barely understood demons could spell real trouble, given that they themselves could barely manage to summon a felhunter.

Thinking it over, he felt he could partly understand why a demonologist like Musul had left the Slaughtered Lamb. And of course, as part of the bargain, he ought to agree to help Musul summon that greater demon.

If that greater demon happened to be an ered'ruin, all the better — but if Musul wouldn't tell him, how could he possibly know what exactly they were summoning before the ritual?

Musul didn't seem inclined to tell him directly — maybe he didn't know either. "Master Sesso?" the stablemaster asked. "Do you plan to take her out for a walk right now?"

"Ah…" Jace snapped out of his thoughts. "Yes — actually, I was planning to take her on a long trip." He'd been about to ask whether he could lead Arna back to the stable at his new place north of the Trade District and look after her himself.

But the words died on his lips. He glanced at the stables, then at the stablemaster, then at the trough and the hay inside, then turned to look at the paddock — and swallowed what he was about to say.

For now, better to let the king keep boarding her a while longer.

Leading Arna back to his rented room, Jace called Morpheus out, packed his demon bag, and left the city gates with Sazeno.

Last time he'd gone to Moonbrook without his two demon servants, he'd gotten ambushed by a hellhound in an alley and ended up feeling cornered at Musul's. This time he had to bring Morpheus for protection.

They stopped for the night at Westfall Keep. Jace didn't rest near the keep itself — he found a spot under the trees by the river across the main road, somewhere he couldn't see the Westfall camp, on account of the two demons he had in tow.

Plenty of people knew by now that he practiced dark magic and even summoned demons — even Quel'Thalas and the Kirin Tor had come to accept it — but that didn't mean ordinary folk wouldn't be terrified at the sight of a demon.

To avoid causing trouble for Stormwind's town watch, he still needed to be mindful of the impression he made when traveling. More importantly, coming out into the wilds this night, he wanted to practice Shadow Step again.

Following what Morpheus had said before, and recalling his experience from the last attempt, Jace sat beneath a tree, closed his eyes, and called to the shadow power in Demonic.

Whether it was because the forest at night gathered more shadow power, or because his call was answered by the darkness of Duskwood farther off, Jace felt that summoning the dark power came far more easily this time. Shadows drifted all around him, deepening his silhouette further and further, until it even reminded him of converting life into shadow mana with Life Tap.

Within minutes, Jace had almost completely dissolved into a mass of shadow. Last time, the sudden clench of darkness had caught him off guard, but now he tried to hold himself in this state, doing his best to grow accustomed to being enveloped by shadow…

But then — how was he supposed to use shadow power to move quickly?

He'd found the fulcrum for summoning shadow, but he still lacked a fulcrum at the destination. "Master, try to close the distance to me," Morpheus said.

Jace looked up at the succubus, and the shadow power around him rapidly faded by more than half. "What do you mean?" Jace asked.

"Make me the target you want to pursue," Morpheus explained. "We have a soul link — it's far easier for you to sense my presence with your will. Try to let the shadow carry you to my side, and express your intent in Demonic. At least, that's how demons do it, even though they don't need Demonic to manipulate shadow power…"

"Let me try."

Jace stood, called shadow to him once more, and let himself be completely shrouded in darkness. This time it came even faster than before.

He focused his attention on Morpheus and tried to use Demonic to guide the shadow power to carry him to the succubus's side.

Morpheus was right — with the soul link, he sensed her presence far more clearly than the trees around them, or the distant crowds, or whatever animals might be nearby. The shadow seemed to seize its target all at once, and Jace felt himself hurled toward Morpheus as if thrown, swept up in a sudden surge of shadow power. But just as he was about to make contact with Morpheus's body, the succubus before his eyes vanished in an instant amid a burst of dark shadow.

"Master, she went that way!" Sazeno poked his head out of the bag and pointed in the other direction.

Jace caught his breath, turned, and found Morpheus standing where he himself had been standing a moment ago, looking back at him. He'd suddenly shifted five or six meters.

Then a wave of violent nausea hit him. He stumbled to a tree, leaned against it, and vomited several times over, nearly emptying himself of the bread and dried mushrooms he'd eaten for dinner, and still it wouldn't stop.

But there was nothing shadow-blackened in his vomit — meaning this was just his body failing to adapt after being jolted by a savage surge of shadow power.

"That's it? Come and get me, Master," Morpheus said with a smile. "What the hell kind of stupid game is this," Jace said, wiping his mouth.

"Don't you want to take this chance to practice more?" Morpheus said, leaning against a tree. "You've got mana to spare, I can tell."

Jace stared into her eyes burning with golden-red flames and nodded. Once more he called shadow in Morpheus's direction in Demonic. This time he appeared at her side in an instant, but when he reached for her arm she slipped away easily again and he missed. The misstep threw him off balance, and another wave of dizziness hit him. He dropped to the ground and retched — this time there truly was nothing left to throw up.

"It seems Master's tolerance for shadow isn't all that impressive after all," Morpheus said, swaying her tail.

"Verbal mental disruption still counts as mental disruption — don't you dare use reverse psychology on me," Jace said, catching his breath while propping himself against a sapling. "This genuinely sucks."

"At least it's a little better than last time," Morpheus said."

"At least…"

Jace hadn't finished speaking when he suddenly summoned shadow!

This Shadow Step was blisteringly fast. After those few attempts, Jace could now rouse shadow with pure will and let it carry him forward without needing to speak a word of Demonic.

He appeared at Morpheus's side in a flash and reached for her throat — only to find the blade of Endless Dark pressed against his neck. His outstretched hand froze midway, and in the end he never laid a finger on the succubus's skin.

"Last lesson of the day, Master: don't use Shadow Step against a target whose close-combat ability far surpasses your own," Morpheus said, that strange smile still on her face.

Jace took several deep breaths, fought down the urge to vomit, steadied himself, and lowered his eyes to glance at the flat of the blade.

He'd abandoned Grede's swordplay lessons far too long ago. How hard he'd struggled against that satyr in Maul had made him realize that for the sake of self-preservation, he needed to pick them back up.
