Chapter 720: Making His Own Preparations

"I'll do it, Musul," Jace said. "I'll attend your gathering. Tell me the location."

Musul said, "Our previous meeting point was in an abandoned mine to the south. But now that we're preparing to go to the Blackrock orcs' great altar, I'll notify the other warlocks to head to one of our old gathering places — an abandoned orc shelter about several dozen kilometers northwest of Redridge, less than two days' walk from the mountains where the great altar sits. That place once hid a Blackrock orc force being pursued by Anduin Lothar's army. The position is extremely well-hidden. Later, after Alliance forces withdrew from Blackrock Mountain, they abandoned that place and followed the Blackrock chieftain into Blackrock Mountain."

"Sounds good," Jace said. "But I'm not sure I can find it that quickly."

"I'll send someone to fetch you, Jace — I guarantee you won't get lost," Musul said with an enigmatic smile. "Tell me where you live. Just a general idea — the street…"

"North canal, opposite the Stormwind Stockade," Jace said.

"I know those apartments. That's enough," Musul nodded. "Then I need a personal item of yours — something not too valuable, but that you've been carrying on your person all along — as a token."

Jace felt around himself and finally handed over the naga bead bracelet from his wrist. Musul said, "Wait for my word, Jace. Welcome to our group."

Jace said, "I don't know who among you are, and I don't know what your organization has been doing all this time. It's too early to talk about joining."

"At least you're planning to join the ritual," Musul said. Jace couldn't deny it.

He did some rough math on the journey. Even if he returned to Stormwind right from here, then rode straight to Lakeshire and crossed the mountains to the Burning Steppes to find that watchtower — though he was already very familiar with the road — it would still be hard to get there within a week.

If the other warlocks all lived in Lakeshire or nearby, or if they were orcs living in the Burning Steppes, it might be faster.

After returning to Stormwind, Jace sent Arna back to the Mage Quarter and headed straight for the Slaughtered Lamb. Even though Musul said these warlocks would definitely be able to control the demon they summoned, he absolutely couldn't let his guard down — so he needed to stock up on potions to the max.

Arriving at the alchemy lab, Ursula was trying on a new robe. Hearing footsteps outside, she looked over and asked, "Jace?"

"This is…" Jace looked at the new robe. "A robe Jialein made for you?"

"Strictly speaking, this isn't a robe," Ursula answered. It's a 'protective robe' specially made for alchemists. Jialein enchanted it specifically so it can block the dark power that leaks out when a potion related to dark magic is brewed imperfectly, preventing it from harming me. It's also quite breathable… though for an alchemist of my level, the chances of something like that happening are extremely small, right?"

"It only takes once for it still to be dangerous. This robe seems very important to me, Ursula," Jace said.

"I remember once when making an antidote, I was corroded by the fumes and couldn't see clearly for several days," Ursula sighed. "But soaking in water vapor in such a fine garment is still a bit uncomfortable. Anyway, Jace — what did you come to me for this time?"

"I need to deal with a demon," Jace said.

"You're not going to the Black Morass again, are you?" Ursula said. "Sulenna just came back from Booty Bay some time ago. She told me that except for around the Dark Portal, there aren't many places left where you can find demons."

"No," Jace admitted. "I'm going to attend one of Musul's demon gatherings. Don't tell the others in the Dagger Circle about this."

"Musul?" the alchemist said. "Galders would probably be furious to hear that. Of course I won't go looking for trouble by telling others — so you're planning to summon a felguard?"

"Even more than that," Jace said. "In any case, I need the best shadow protection potions possible. Flame protection potions… I don't think I'll need them, but prepare one just in case. Also, I don't know if there are any potions that work particularly well against demons — for example, a demon-slaying potion…"

Ursula nodded. "You're in luck, Jace. The Dark Portal incident made the ingredients for demon-slaying potions much easier to get. I can make you one — free of charge. The shadow protection potion too, of course. But I don't have the extra materials to make the top-tier potent potion right now — I can only give you the next grade down. Still, it's much stronger than those small vials."

"By the way — you didn't only brew one Elixir of Intellect last time, did you?" Jace asked.

Ursula raised her eyebrows helplessly. "Of course not just one. But I can't give you this one for free — I'm currently looking for customers."

"Name your price," Jace said.

"You really are rich these days," Ursula said. "Eight gold. That's already a friend's price, Jace."

"Eight gold, and make an extra demon-slaying potion," Jace said.

Ursula sighed. "Fine, fine — who can I blame for having no temper around you, kid? Come get your potions tomorrow noon."

"Thanks," Jace said with a smile. "If I only drink half an Elixir of Intellect, does it reduce the potency, or the duration of the effect?"

"Both," Ursula said with certainty. She flipped through her bookshelf, then turned back and asked, "Still — are you planning to summon a demon just to kill it?"

"I hope it won't come to a situation where I need to use this kind of potion," Jace said. "But I need to be prepared for every possibility. I don't think Musul and his people are very reliable."

"If Musul isn't reliable when it comes to summoning demons, then there are no reliable demon scholars in this world," Ursula said.

"Maybe there are no reliable demon scholars in this world to begin with," Jace said. "If you'd visited the Order of Tirisfal section of the Great Library in Dalaran, you'd know — even those legendary demon hunters keep capsizing in the gutter on questions like this."

"Capsizing in the gutter?" Ursula turned around with a puzzled look. "Why do these mages… ha, I see what you mean. Is some Brill saying that?"

"It should be a saying from around here," Jace said.

Jace was quite certain he'd heard a saying similar to this in his past life here — he just couldn't remember who'd said it exactly.

"Still, this does sound like something Musul would do," Ursula said. "Actually, I can understand it too. It's like alchemy — if I were afraid of being harmed by potions, it would be hard to make great progress. If you're not willing to take risks, how can you learn the secrets of the Twisting Nether? The Twisting Nether itself is extremely dangerous. The Wizard Sanctum has it written — 'do not seek to know the matters of the Twisting Nether'…"

"That's true," Jace said, lifting his cloak. "Then I'll head back. I'll bring the money to find you tomorrow."

"No problem," the alchemist said.
