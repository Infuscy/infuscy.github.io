Chapter 722: A Suspicious Name

"I need you to help me summon my minion," he said immediately.

The goblin summoner with the dark red ponytail in the crowd said, "You can summon whatever demon you need right here. Don't tell me you still can't summon a minion on your own."

Mosh'aru said, "Our Mr. Sesso, like me, is a warlock who values building a relationship with his demon minions. Gorsuch, he doesn't casually kill his demons, nor does he summon them carelessly and drain his own energy, damaging the bond with his familiar. So let's take a little more time and help him summon his demon companion."

Hearing Mosh'aru's words, a few of the summoners over there burst into laughter. The way these people related to demons reminded Jace of Gajin — maybe Gajin had originally been one of them.

"Did he fall in love with the succubus he summoned?" a summoner in a dark blue robe murmured. "I wouldn't have guessed the legendary Jace Sesso was such a sentimental warlock."

A female warlock beside him said, "He still looks quite young... it's not that strange. Have you forgotten what happened in Moonbrook last time?"

"Enough chit-chat, Rona. Finish the summoning ritual quickly and let's get everyone assembled." The older summoner in front of him looked at Jace and pointed to the side of the circle. "I'll channel the summoning magic. Jace Sesso, go to that position in the circle — between those three runes — and recite the incantation, channeling shadow energy. Once the portal is activated, you'll call out your demon minion's name and have him answer your call. Hope you can keep him under control and know where he is right now. During this, you must maintain the circle's activation, or your minion won't hear your call."

Mosh'aru handed Jace a slip of paper with the incantation written in Demon Tongue. "No need for me to repeat how this incantation is pronounced, right, Jace?" Jace glanced at it and said, "No need."

The older summoner began channeling the magic, reciting a lengthy incantation. The ritual circle lit up bit by bit. The female summoner on the other side of the circle also began reciting, and Jace followed by reading the incantation off the paper. With the three of them working together, the circle was successfully activated — simpler than Jace had imagined.

Following the summoner's instructions, Jace thought about the location of his rented room, and then he felt that mental pressure.

The process of directing his thoughts was much harder than before, like swinging a heavy object. The images in his mind moved through Stormwind with great difficulty, as if he were flying through the sky and had to flap his wings laboriously just to shift position.

Mana drained bit by bit. Jace finally located his alley. He tried speaking in Demon Tongue: "Answer my call, Molophil... Accept my summons — and don't forget to bring my things!"

That last addition drained more than half his mana all at once. He nearly collapsed right there on the circle. Several of the summoners watching the ritual changed expressions, but Jace managed to steady himself and maintain the circle's activation. A rift in the void appeared between the three summoners, and a familiar figure stepped out of it.

Molophil stood a full head or two taller than the succubi hiding behind the other summoners. She pushed out her chest, which had grown considerably fuller since she'd first met Jace, and held her black dagger in one hand as she surveyed everyone around her with a cold gaze.

Wherever her eyes landed, the summoners or demons instinctively avoided her gaze. After all, the bright fel markings covering her entire body and her eyes blazing with red dragonfire made her look like a female judge who'd descended from the Burning Legion to punish these backwater sorcerers — stern and imposing without even trying.

Finally her gaze landed on Jace, and she immediately smiled. "Ah, my dear master, there you are." She handed the purple demon bag to Jace. "Here are the things you asked me to bring... and an imp."

"What a powerful sathayar." The goblin named Gorsuch couldn't help but say. "How did you manage to summon such a demon minion, Mr. Sesso?"

"It's my master's power that nourishes me." Molophil wrapped her arm around Jace's neck. "Isn't that right, master?"

"You don't need to be that direct," Jace said.

"I work just as hard as you do, so why isn't mine this strong?" a human warlock nearby said even more bluntly. "I bet it's your problem," a gnome beside him said.

The human was about to blow his top, but his succubus immediately comforted him: "I'll grow stronger, worthy of my master's love..."

The human warlock snorted, hugging his succubus, and looked at the gnome. "Your demon minion is as weak as if it got crippled by something while crossing the Twisting Nether, Mr. Fizzlebang. That kind of barbed comment can bounce back and hit you."

The gnome immediately fired back with a few sarcastic remarks, but hearing his name, Jace was taken aback. Fizzlebang?

Was that gnome Wilfred Fizzlebang?

Jace couldn't possibly not know Wilfred Fizzlebang's great reputation in the Alliance — though in this world, he might not be that famous yet.

In the future, during the Alliance and Horde's joint campaign against the Scourge in Northrend, warlocks had more or less cleared their name and earned the trust of the coalition forces. At a pre-battle training assembly, Wilfred Fizzlebang intended to summon a powerful demon to provide the coalition soldiers with a trial opportunity. Although the coalition leaders, King Varian Wrynn and Garrosh Hellscream, weren't particularly in favor, they ultimately didn't stop his summoning.

His mana was indeed powerful — he actually managed to single-handedly summon a powerful eredar lord, Lord Galacus. But the eredar killed the poor gnome the moment he appeared, and brought considerable chaos to the assembly...

It was only after this incident that the reputation of warlocks was once again cast under a shadow across Azeroth. It could be said that at this summoning ritual, the last name Jace wanted to hear was this gnome's.

But in Jace's memory, Wilfred seemed to be a small old man with white hair. This gnome had deep purple hair. Had his hair turned white later?

Still, he tentatively asked, "Mr. Fizzlebang, what's your relation to Wilfred Fizzlebang?"

"Ah, that's my brother, Jace Sesso," the purple-haired gnome said. "I'm Albert Fizzlebang. If Wil knew that a demon scholar like you knew his name, he'd be thrilled."

Well, at least it wasn't Wilfred himself. A little better — but not by much.
