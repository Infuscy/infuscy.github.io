## Chapter 724: Dath'morar's Master

The next morning, Jace woke up and crawled out of the orc tent. Many warlocks were already busy on the altar.

They used Azugoth's blood to inscribe the circle and runes atop the original altar, overlapping with the stone-carved summoning circle already on the altar, yet seemingly not conflicting — as if they were interweaving to form a new, more harmonious circle.

Mosh'aru and Ely held scrolls and supervised every stroke. The other warlocks without tasks stood at the feet of those giant statues observing from the middle. Looking down from above to the center revealed nothing, but truly stepping onto the altar, one could feel its enormity — the warlocks were only about as tall as these statues' toes.

Jace gazed into the darkness within the hood of the statue across from him, feeling as if it might open its eyes at any moment.

After the circle was completed came the stage of laying out casting materials. Everyone, including Jace, had to contribute their soul fragments for use when needed.

Soul stones were placed among the circle. Jace could feel that many of these soul fragments were human souls, or at least humanoid — like orcs or dwarves — containing terrifying hatred and fury, far more ferocious and violent than the souls Jace had brought out, which were already dead and harvested by satyrs into soul fragments.

But for casting, these souls carrying hatred and anger were far more powerful. Of course, Jace hadn't contributed Dath'morar's soul fragment.

By afternoon, all the circles were nearly complete — not only the binding circle but also the demon-weakening circle. Judging by the runes on the circles, they were orcish work, mixed with quite a few characters in the orcish language.

Of course, using orcish circles at the Storm Altar, a facility built by orcs, was only fitting. Jace noticed that Kagel Shadowfist had been staring at the three giant statues ever since completing the circle's inscription, his eyes full of reverence and nostalgia for the past.

After Gul'dan fell from power and fled to the Tomb of Sargeras, the new Horde Warchief Orgrim Doomhammer carried out a purge of Gul'dan's subordinates. Many orc warlocks fled into the wilderness during this time — Kagel was probably one of them.

And when the Storm Altar was built, it was precisely when the Shadow Council ruled the Horde — those few glorious years for orc warlocks.

"Everyone, take your previously assigned positions. This is not our first attempt at summoning, everyone!" Mosh'aru shouted loudly. "The power of Azugoth's blood is extremely chaotic. The circle has just been completed — this is the moment it's easiest to control. Seize this opportunity... don't waste any more time!"

The warlocks responded to Mosh'aru's call and headed to their respective positions. Jace also stood up and came to the edge of the innermost ring of the circle, standing opposite Mosh'aru and Ely.

The three of them faced the three giant statues directly across from them. At this moment, the statue across from Jace was staring down at him. Ely said, "There must be a reason this altar was built this way, and we came here at exactly the right time. We should have come here to attempt the summoning long ago, Mosh'aru."

Mosh'aru glanced at the distant deep valley and said, "Well, we're here now."

"Let's begin," he shouted. "Begin the summoning ritual!"

The warlocks nodded. The six maintaining the binding circle simultaneously began reciting incantations. Jace felt the lava around the altar begin to rumble, and the entire valley grew hotter. The circle written in Azugoth's blood also grew slightly brighter from its dark green color. An overwhelmingly oppressive evil, burnt stench became increasingly distinct.

Ely raised his hands high, closed his eyes, and recited the incantation. Soon Mosh'aru also raised his hands and began reciting. Then Jace followed their lead, going through the practiced motions.

Close his eyes and recite the incantation as written on the scroll.

Ulit, Totolit, Kaskayajad...

As the incantation was spoken character by character, Jace felt as if someone was responding in sequence to his words. The power buried deep underground seemed to be crying out, answering his recitation.

This power, as the endless, almost interminable incantation was continuously recited, was also continuously replenished and completed, eventually transforming into a complete dark surging force that gradually spread upward, locking onto the three people at the center of the circle — especially himself.

Jace felt his feet become extremely heavy, or rather, firmly fixed to the ground by the power channeled through the altar.

In that instant, he felt as if he had merged with all of Blackrock Mountain, communicating with the Twisting Nether through the mountain's foundation. An uncontainable arrogance born from this dark mountain surged from the depths of his heart. Jace knew this was the terrifying shadow energy infecting his mind in return. He had to restrain this power to avoid losing control, while also focusing on the progress of the summoning ritual.

The lava outside the altar churned even more violently. The ritual of Azugoth's blood actually ignited. Dark green fel fire spread rapidly along the circle's patterns. The six within the binding circle remained calmly reciting their incantations, channeling the altar's power. The warlocks on the outside had clearly grown somewhat uneasy due to the altar's agitation.

It could only be said that Mosh'aru's selection of people was well-founded.

Between the three of them, a pitch-black void gate roughly seven or eight meters tall slowly unfolded. Jace felt his body as if it had been torn open halfway, the power erupting from within spinning to tear him apart. He knew this wasn't a real sensation, but rather his consciousness and physical body becoming indistinguishable under the pressure of various forces.

The summoning portal seemed to have been established. Mosh'aru couldn't help but open his eyes and glance at this gate — and then he could no longer contain his excitement.

"Success?"

"Never this successful before!" a warlock in the distance shouted. But... where was the demon?

Just as Jace was growing doubtful, he suddenly heard a distant voice coming from within the gate.

"I am carrying out the Great One's mission. Who dares disturb my steps at this time, you insignificant worms... report your names."

The rumbling sound seemed to startle the warlocks present awake all at once. Mosh'aru immediately shouted, "We are Dath'morar's loyal servants, great lord! We carry out Dath'morar's orders, calling for your arrival according to the will of Dath'morar's master!"

"I've told her countless times not to complain to me anymore, not to speak those meaningless words to me. And now she's actually found you lot..."

The voice grew closer and closer, until finally Jace felt as if the demon in the Twisting Nether were speaking directly into his ear.

Suddenly, a giant claw covered with brown mane grabbed onto the void gate before him. A pair of massive black horns, bound with ring after ring of demon-rune-inscribed iron chains, poked out from within.

Jace felt his heart pounding. Mosh'aru looked back and forth at the people in the binding circle, confirming they were still maintaining the magic, then quickly locked his gaze back.

The demon poked out its head — an extremely twisted and hideous Eredruin head. A pair of tiny green eyes looked around at the environment before them. It opened its mouth to say something, and beneath that evil mouth full of fangs, there were even dark, beard-like thick tentacles sniffing back and forth.

Not only on its chin — this creature's cheeks also grew two longer, thicker brown tentacles, also bound with rings of black metal rune rings. These two tentacles poked into the void gate along with its head, curiously feeling out the surroundings of the Storm Altar, and then it extended its entire upper body in...

This monster's upper body alone was already about four meters tall. Dark metal armor inscribed with fel fire runes covered its arms, shoulders, and chest. A pair of broad pauldrons, like layers of stacked spiked shields, covered the sides of this deformed head. Even without counting those two absurdly long black horns, its head was about the size of a pony.

Jace began to doubt whether these warlocks' binding circle could actually hold this demon lord. At this point, he wasn't the only one doubting this summoning ritual — he wasn't even the only one among the fifteen warlocks and their demon minions present...

The demon lord suddenly froze. Those small eyes and thick tentacles seemed to have noticed something amiss. "Cunning maggots! What are you planning?! This is a binding circle!"

The demon roared furiously and suddenly raised its arm, seemingly wanting to retreat back into the void gate. Mosh'aru suddenly shouted, "Hold him!"

The six warlocks in the outer ring simultaneously began casting, reciting various curses. Jace immediately heard the recitation of Curse of Weakness, along with various other curse magics he didn't recognize at all. From the meaning in Demon Tongue, he could roughly make out various hindrances, obstructions, and even impairments to thinking ability. The demon lord was hit by an unknown number of curses all at once. His movements slowed for a moment, and he coughed a few times.

Seizing this opening, Mosh'aru shouted at the summoner in charge of the main summoning: "End the summoning incantation, Ely! He's trying to escape!" Ely paused for a moment, immediately ending the summoning magic. The edges of the void gate rapidly disintegrated!

The demon lord roared. No longer retreating, he gripped the cracking rift and forced his way in before it could tear him apart, spreading his massive wings and landing on the ground... causing the entire altar to rumble and shake!

He'd suffered severe damage from the void gate's closure. Even his body had shrunk somewhat. Cracks appeared layer by layer in his armor. Turbid demon blood continuously seeped from the gaps, covering his legs and black giant hooves.

"He's fallen for it! Maintain the binding circle! Maintain the circle!"

Mosh'aru's roar carried wild joy. But at this moment, one of the warlocks maintaining the circle had blood streaming from his nose, and the churning lava even splashed onto the altar...

Jace couldn't help but grip the sword hilt beneath his cloak. It seemed he wasn't the only one who thought a fierce battle was coming. He noticed the Dark Iron dwarf Fezias on the outside also seemed to be reaching for something in his bag.
