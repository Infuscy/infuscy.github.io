Chapter 725: As Expected…

“Maintain the binding circle, don’t concern yourselves with anything else!”

Mor’zul shouted at the top of his lungs. The demon lord before them was being pressed to the ground by the binding circle, green demon blood oozing from the cracks all over his body.

The power of the Altar of Storms had dragged this monster here from some distant void. The sudden rupture of the void portal had dealt severe damage to both his body and soul. This massive Ered’ruin was half-kneeling on the ground, gritting his teeth and glaring at every warlock before him, but under the power of the binding circle, he couldn’t move at all.

Compared to when he’d first poked his head through the void gate, his body had shrunk considerably. Not only had the void gate’s closure injured him — being so absurdly brought from the Twisting Nether to Azeroth had already weakened his power by a considerable degree.

He was clearly well aware of this. The fury of losing power made his already hideous face even more deformed.

Seeing the demon lord in this state, Mor’zul couldn’t help but smile. He looked toward Jace and shouted, “Things are going even more smoothly than I imagined, Jace Sesso! A lord of the Burning Legion, hmm? See the power of us insignificant mortals! Continue the binding circle, complete our ritual, trap him here forever!”

He finished with a laugh, but by the time this laughter reached Jace, it had become quite muffled.

After all, even in the inner ring, the sheer size of the place and the noise on the altar made it incredibly chaotic. The demon lord was actually trapped — on the surface, nothing seemed to have gone wrong.

Although the warlocks maintaining the binding circle looked extremely strained, the demon lord before them looked to be in even more pain. After landing and entering the circle, he couldn’t even speak — his sharp upper and lower teeth were being ground together with a clatter.

As long as they could control this demon lord, they should be able to learn Ulthalesh’s whereabouts.

As a high-ranking Ered’ruin demon, he would certainly know the ancient Ered’ruin language that even the Order of Tirisfal couldn’t figure out — this was even more reliable than summoning a doomguard.

After all, doomguards weren’t difficult for the Order’s mages to control. If the mages couldn’t obtain information about the Ered’ruin language from a doomguard, it meant that after Eredun became widespread in the Burning Legion, ordinary doomguards had probably forgotten their own language.

Not to mention, as Dasmora’s superior, he was very likely the Burning Legion officer who had assigned Dasmora to find Ulthalesh. He probably knew more about Ulthalesh than Jace could imagine.

Ulthalesh — a thing that could truly bring disaster to this world. In fact, it had already brought disaster to Azeroth.

It was just that centuries had passed since the matter, and very few people now remembered how it had swept mercilessly across piece after piece of land in the hands of demons, harvesting the souls of the residents upon them.

If they couldn’t stop someone like Dar’Khan from seizing that artifact…

Jace’s hand slowly released the hilt of Azuresong, but he couldn’t ignore that Dark Iron dwarf in the distance.

The Dark Iron dwarf Fezias had pulled a scroll from inside his robe. He stared at the demon in the middle while constantly reading from the scroll, looking like he was weakening the demon with magic just like the other warlocks, assisting the binding circle in completing the final imprisonment.

But compared to the other outside warlocks excitedly reciting incantations to weaken the demon’s power, he looked tense. Most importantly, his emotions were also off.

He didn’t look like he was trying hard to weaken the demon — rather, as if… he had some dangerous intention. What incantation was he reciting?

“That Dark Iron dwarf is acting suspicious, Mor’zul!” Jace pointed at Fezias and shouted toward Mor’zul across from him. “What?” Mor’zul looked in Jace’s direction.

The Dark Iron dwarf seemed to notice that the warlocks in the inner ring had noticed him, and his emotions grew increasingly urgent.

“I am your master!” He then simply shouted the incantation toward the demon in the middle. This time Jace heard: “Vita! Tahotusqi! Mi…”

This was a demon-enslaving incantation. Had Mor’zul told him to attempt an enslavement?

Jace shouted loudly at Mor’zul: “He’s reciting an enslavement incantation, Mor’zul! Is this your idea? The binding circle hasn’t stabilized yet — we’re not out of danger. And you’re trying to enslave this demon now?”

“No, no!” Mor’zul immediately shouted back. “He has no idea what he’s doing! Stop him, quickly!”

Jace wanted to hit him with a Fear spell to shut him up, but this distance was too far for Fear. Jace was standing in the innermost ring, ready at any moment to support Ely in executing the banishment magic, so he couldn’t leave his position. And Morophil was outside the altar with the succubi guarding against possible intruders — even farther away!

Mor’zul completely lost his temper. With all his strength, he practically roared: “People nearby, someone deal with Fezias! That idiot’s gone mad! Don’t let him interfere with the ritual!”

Jace noticed that the moment he shouted, the Dark Iron dwarf’s eyes had erupted with fel fire. His voice, which had been loudly reciting the incantation, had also turned into whimpering.

The doom lord let out a cold laugh from between his pressed-together teeth. The demon’s will had overwhelmed that overreaching dwarf. The dwarf’s enslavement incantation had backfired…

Hearing Mor’zul’s voice, Albert Fizzlebang reacted extremely quickly. He moved his lips, pointed his finger, and instantly turned Fezias into a black-furred sheep. He was actually a mage who had mastered a certain amount of arcane magic!

But this sheep, once dropped, didn’t stand still or wander in circles. Its whole body was trembling, its teeth chattering. As the doom lord’s laughter grew more manic, the sheep suddenly charged straight toward the binding circle!

The fel fire erupting from the sheep’s eyes ignited its entire body in the blink of an eye. A sudden green flash startled the warlocks all around.

The gnome warlock Spackle shrieked the incantation of Fear and cast it at the sheep, but this sheep, completely robbed of reason under the demon’s will, no longer understood what fear was.

It let out rasping bleats as it charged toward the middle. Another warlock cast Curse of Agony on the sheep, but still couldn’t stop its advance!

The polymorph on the sheep’s body rapidly faded under the distortion of various shadow corruptions, but didn’t fully fade — instead turning the dwarf into a half-human, half-sheep short demon monster covered in fel fire.

The monster howled and charged into the binding circle, knocking “Short Staff” Gladys, who was nearest and maintaining the binding circle, to the ground!

Gladys, forcibly interrupted in his casting, fell to his knees and coughed up a mouthful of blood. Losing this one support point, the binding circle visibly dimmed…

This “half-sheep dwarf” was shot in the back by an arrow and fell to the ground. Under the influence of curses and Corruption from several warlocks, it quickly stopped moving and breathed its last. But the circle had already weakened. The doom lord laughed loudly and forcefully spread his wings!

The moment he did this, all the warlocks maintaining the circle staggered. Seeing that Gladys had already fallen unconscious, the orc Kagel roared: “Someone else! Quick!”

“I’ll take over!”

The female warlock Martha Stein, expelled from Dalaran, immediately ran to Gladys’s position and maintained the binding circle. The circle lit up again. The doom lord’s laughter was suppressed into silence, replaced by the clicking sound of his teeth gnashing against each other.

The oppressive force on him grew stronger again. His body shrank once more. The cracks all over his body oozed even more demon blood…
