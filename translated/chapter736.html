Chapter 736: All Mine

The ground floor had been cleared. The crossbowmen who'd fallen down, the warlocks, the gnolls who'd survived the explosion of Jace's Dark Shield — all of them had been finished off by the soldiers who followed up.

Jace drove Azure Skysong through the skull of a gnoll still wailing as shadow tormented it, then looked upward. There were still three more floors above.

He and the soldiers chased after the hellhound's footsteps up the stairs, when a gnoll's shriek from above was followed by a burst of fiery flame plummeting down from overhead and exploding against the middle of the wooden staircase. The hellhound nimbly dodged the fire magic and pressed on, but the spreading embers landed on the lower floors, forcing the soldiers behind to scatter away from the center.

Jace stared at the burning staircase and suddenly realized — he was going to have to repair all this damage afterward!

He immediately raised Azure Skysong and chanted a Conjure Water incantation, laying a layer of ice over the flames to smother them. At the same time, the soldiers raised their crossbows and fired at the gnoll crossbowmen and warlocks peering down from above. Some bolts struck the walls; others hit the windows and shattered the glass…

Listening to the clanging and crashing, every blow felt like it was landing straight on his heart.

Dazarm had already scrambled higher up, let out a roar, and pounced on a warlock trying to haul something further upward. It drained the warlock's mana dry, then snatched up the weakened body, shook its head, and hurled it over the side.

The poor gnoll warlock crashed down, smashing a gap into a wooden ladder before hitting the ground in a twisted heap. Jace stared fixedly at the broken gap, the damaged windows and supports, the wooden pillars… it made him want to grind his back teeth to dust.

But he couldn't say anything to the soldiers that might hinder the fight — there were still plenty of gnolls holed up above, and the soldiers had to press the attack with everything they had. After all, a tower could be repaired; a life, once gone, was truly gone.

"The gnolls outside are surrounding us, commander!" a soldier shouted from the ground floor.

This meant the Darkhide tribe had realized this tower was the Stonewatch soldiers' main point of attack. They had to speed things up.

Colonel Tordman was clearly getting anxious. He charged upward with his shield, shoving a gnoll warrior who was trying to pursue the hellhound back down the stairs. The gnoll tumbled to the floor below and was immediately run through by the surrounding soldiers.

Jace was even more anxious than him — after all, the longer the fight dragged on, the worse the damage to the tower.

By now, the melee on the stairs had already blasted several gaps through the walkways, and no small number of windows had been shattered by fire magic and arrows. The already dilapidated Darrowshire Tower, which had weathered several fierce battles, clearly still had considerable room for further destruction.

By this point, the soldiers had pushed to within striking distance of the top floor. Jace felt the tower's energy rapidly draining away, and the shadowy radiance from the highest level had vanished as well.

Could it be that the fierce fighting had already exhausted the shadow mana the defending gnoll warlocks had been drawing from the tower?

He followed the hellhound up another flight. Ahead, several gnolls had the hellhound surrounded, hacking and slashing at it. Jace quickly chanted a Scorch incantation that blasted the gnoll warriors surrounding it apart. One of them tumbled back down the stairs with a string of crashes as wooden planks shattered in its path.

A gnoll pulled itself back together from the Scorch's blast, stood up again, flames still clinging to its body, and charged at Jace swinging a club. Jace swung Azure Skysong and cleaved through the gnoll, its armor, and its club in a single stroke, sending it crashing to the ground.

He was about to strike back at another gnoll when a piercing tension suddenly pressed into his chest… he didn't know why, but Jace suddenly thought of Vereesa — thought of countless zombies and skeletons surrounding the lone elven ranger… as the image flashed through his mind, he immediately realized a warlock was casting Fear on him.

And the mana behind this Fear was remarkably strong.

In that instant of hesitation, the gnoll warrior bearing down on him smashed his club hard into Jace's face. Jace felt his head go blank, stumbled a step, and spat out a mouthful of blood — but the gnoll himself was the one who'd been startled.

He'd never expected that a full-force swing, landing square on this human's face, would only push the man back a single step.

Jace gave him no chance to swing again. He extended his hand and used Life Drain to suck the gnoll dry into a wiry husk, then kicked it aside. The laceration torn into his face by the spiked club healed at what could only be described as a pathological speed.

A soldier behind him had already been stunned by the terrifying sight. Jace caught his breath — that Life Drain had at least eased some of the hatred the vision had brought on.

A large force of gnolls was now assaulting the tower gate. The soldiers outside had withdrawn into the tower and were organizing a defense under Tordman's command. The tide of battle had turned.

Jace understood that this was the moment for him, the court mage advisor, to hold steady under the pressure.

He looked upward, tracing the source of the Fear. On the top floor, an old gnoll in a tattered black robe was staring straight at him. The creature held a rotten wooden staff in its hand, shadow power still coiling around it, and its mouth was already murmuring another incantation.

Dazarm was covered in wounds and missing limbs, but it was still pushing up the stairs on Jace's orders.

Here he didn't dare use Shadowstep to chase down the enemy — he still couldn't precisely control the distance of his shadow teleportation. If he lunged and missed, falling to his death, that would be the end of everything.

So he chanted a Shadow Bolt and fired it straight at the old gnoll up above — but at that moment a dark figure appeared on the stairs above, drew the Shadow Bolt flying through the air toward itself, and devoured it entirely.

It was a voidwalker… a voidwalker wearing dark, ethereal armor!

Jace understood now why the tower had lost its energy in an instant. To stop him, the old gnoll warlock had released the tower's power source, intending to have this demon fight Jace personally.

"Face the emissary of the Void, Hukugash, human!" the old gnoll shrieked at the top of his lungs. "This tower is MINE!" The voidwalker let out a piercing cry that no flesh-and-blood creature could have produced.

"I… must devour, I must hunt, the shadow-blessed mortal… the Void… releases… me…"

Its speech gradually shifted into something that was neither Demonic nor Common — an arcane chant. As it intoned, waves of shadow power cascaded down the stairs and engulfed the hellhound. Even a hellhound with formidable mana resistance collapsed under the onslaught of shadow power and could not move again.

At the same time, the old gnoll chanted Fear once more, trying to crush Jace's will. Jace fired back with a rapid Fear of his own and instead scared the old gnoll into sitting down hard, nearly losing his footing and tumbling off.

But Dazarm finally buckled under the dual torment of the warlock's magic and the voidwalker's assault, collapsing to the ground. Jace felt the bond between himself and his minion snap.

The voidwalker before him flickered its flame-like eyes, then turned to Jace and reached out a thick, shadow-wreathed arm. Jace swung his sword and severed the shadow power cleanly. The magic of Azure Skysong made the voidwalker's entire shadow shudder.

Very soon, this tower would no longer be yours — and neither would this voidwalker.

Thinking this in his heart, Jace spoke the Enslave Demon incantation. Though the voidwalker bared its fangs and claws and tried to unleash yet more shadow power to engulf the human before it, it felt its mana being rapidly forced back by a stronger force of will. The shadow energy it had shaped quickly lost its structure and dissipated, flowing away in all directions.

The enslavement incantation completed. A shadow rune appeared directly between the voidwalker's eyes, hovering above them. The voidwalker's ever-expanding form abruptly shrank under the crushing pressure.

The old gnoll warlock up above seemed to sense that he'd lost his connection to his minion. He frantically kept issuing orders, commanding the voidwalker to attack — but the chaotic demon only circled in place, murmuring in Demonic in a low voice: "Struggle… torment… madness…"

The old warlock realized he could no longer control the voidwalker at all. He turned to flee on the spot. Jace vaulted past the voidwalker and ran up the stairs into the great hall on the top floor, where he caught sight of the gnoll trying to climb out the window!

This warlock's power was considerable — after all, he'd been able to summon such a powerful voidwalker. Jace could feel that even enslaving it had taken considerable effort, and he was wearing the Chain of Will! If he let this gnoll escape into the gnoll-infested forest, there was no telling what kind of disaster he might bring to Stonewatch in the future.

The gnoll leaped out the window and vanished. From outside came a chaotic scrabbling of claws scraping against the tower wall. Old as the creature was, it was still a gnoll — far more agile than an elderly human.

Jace closed his eyes and locked onto the warlock's energy, while at the same time channeling mana from the voidwalker he'd already enslaved behind him.

"Kaurili, Feraos, Pakumili!"

The voidwalker wailed and dissolved into a streak of flying dark soul. Armor and binding metal rings clattered to the ground. The dark soul shot straight out the window in pursuit of the old warlock, then curved downward. Jace ran to the window and looked down in time to see the demonic wraith seize the gnoll, who was clambering down the stone wall.

The already haggard, withered old gnoll withered further under the dark soul's erosion, turning into a desiccated bag of skin and bones amid helpless wails, then fell. It hit the base of the tower, twitched, curled up slightly, and moved no more.

Seeing their master killed, a human head appeared at the top of the tower. The mass of gnolls still pressing their assault from below immediately scattered in all directions.

A chorus of soldiers' cheers rose from the base of the tower. They shouted "For the Alliance!" and "Sesso!"

Jace returned to the stairwell and looked down. He didn't see any Stonewatch soldiers' corpses — at least there was that relief.

But then he looked again at the gaps and cracks blasted by magic and arrows, the broken staircases… it genuinely hurt worse than if the blows had landed on his own body.

The only silver lining was that he hadn't let Grede come.

If he'd charged in swinging that war hammer of his, the repair work would probably have been harder than tearing the whole thing down and rebuilding from scratch.
