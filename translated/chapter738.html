Chapter 738: Unexpected News

In the days that followed, to guard against the possibility that the gnolls might suddenly counterattack before Darrowshire Tower's defenses were properly secured, Jace traveled to the tower every morning. At the same time, he had to clear out the waste, timber, and rubble inside.

Blessed by the red dragon, Jace felt that his physical stamina had improved by who-knows-how-much compared to when he'd been working in Marin's study — no matter how much he cleaned, he felt no fatigue.

After a morning of intensive work across the floors and alongside the damaged bookshelves, Jace leaned on the railing and glanced up at the areas he'd already swept, then looked down at the floors he hadn't gotten around to cleaning yet. His body wasn't tired, but his heart already was.

He figured he should find a mage, chant a spell, conjure five or six magical brooms, and summon a few arcane servants to do the rest of the work — though he wondered whether Marin would be willing to help.

But that was impossible. Marin would rather spend silver hiring young lads like him to work in his study than waste mana on a spell to tidy it up. Why on earth would he help Jace clean this tower?

He wondered if Grede would be willing to come help. How much would it cost to hire a "Mountain King" to do cleaning? Jace rubbed the stubble on his chin, wondering whether ten silver would be enough.

At that moment, a Stonewatch soldier walked into the ground floor of the mage tower and shouted upward: "Master Sesso, there's a mage here to see you!"

"A Wizard Sanctum mage?" Jace asked.

"No — he said his name is Vielan, but he's not wearing Dalaran robes either." The soldier craned his neck. "From the look of him, he's probably from Stromgarde. He's wearing a red robe."

Vaelastrasz.

Looking at the soldier, Jace felt a measure of relief at the news — and, admittedly, a measure of annoyance. The relief was that at least Vaelastrasz hadn't been trapped on this trip to Blackrock Mountain.

The annoyance was: why did these red dragons always show up to make trouble for him at the most relaxed moment? Was this one here to send him on a dangerous rescue mission again?

The last time, it had just been the handover of that house. This time, he hadn't even spent a full week in this tower yet.

As he walked down the stairs, he thought to himself that even if Krasus really were trapped in Blackrock Spire, he wasn't about to agree to Vaelastrasz's request for a wild goose chase.

If the Red Dragonflight was powerful enough, if Alexstrasza truly loved her consort so much, then they should find a way to rescue him themselves. It hadn't even been half a year since Grim Batol — three or four months, more or less — and Jace felt he'd only just shaken off the shadow Deathwing had left behind. He deserved a period of "leave."

Still, he was only thinking this out of petty displeasure.

The sight of Krasus being trapped by Deathwing in that dark prison was still fresh in his mind. If he truly hadn't escaped danger, then even setting aside the matter of asking him for help against Kel'Thuzad, Jace could hardly look the other way.

Outside the tower gate, the red-robed mage stood on the edge of the slope gazing out at the distant forest. He turned at Jace's approach and smiled. "I've finally found you, Jace. Does that cloak fit all right? Have you had a chance to test its 'special feature'?"

Contrary to what Jace had expected, this dragon seemed to be in surprisingly good spirits. "I feel like I'll have the chance to test it soon, isn't that so?" Jace said. "What do you mean?" Vielan asked.

"Weren't you looking for Master Krasus? Is he in Blackrock Mountain? Is that why you're here — to ask me to deal with the Blackrock orcs?"

Even as he said it, Jace could barely conceal his displeasure.

Vielan shook his head. "I've come to thank you, Jace. If you hadn't been willing to tell me about Deathwing's dream, Prince Korialstrasz might have suffered unimaginable torment, and I might still have no idea where he was."

"So he really is in Blackrock Mountain." Jace said.

Vielan said, "Yes. I scouted Blackrock Mountain for several days before I found his exact location. He's imprisoned in a deep cell within Blackrock Spire, sealed off by the black dragons and guarded by a powerful mixed force of dragonkin and orcs led by a dragonkin commander. I had no room to attempt a rescue on my own. After confirming his location, I went to Dalaran looking for you, and then heard you'd already left for Quel'Thalas. So I informed Antonidas, and he dispatched an archmage to accompany me deep into Blackrock Spire — that's how we managed to rescue the Prince."

Jace was taken aback for a moment, then asked, "An archmage of the Kirin Tor… who?"

"A mage who made mistakes during the Orc Wars. His name is Rhonin." Vielan said. "He'd been imprisoned in Dalaran — this was Antonidas giving him a chance to redeem himself. The Kirin Tor Council has now permitted him to return to the Kirin Tor… I suppose heroes are never truly buried. He proved himself to be an extraordinarily powerful mage. Without him, the Prince and I might both have been locked away in Blackrock Spire with no way out ever again."

"It was also during this rescue that I discovered the black dragons have already taken complete control of Blackrock Spire. I've reported this to the Queen, and next I'll continue infiltrating it to investigate exactly what they're plotting with the orcs."

Hearing this, Jace still felt a little dazed.

Still, he asked, "When did all this happen? I haven't heard a word about it."

"More than ten days ago." Vielan said. "Actually, that mage came looking for you in Stormwind before he set out — I'd thought of having him borrow that black dragon cloak of yours to infiltrate Blackrock Spire more easily. But in the end he couldn't find you; neither the Wizard Sanctum nor the king knew where you'd gone, so I could only produce a bead I'd just finished crafting at the time to help him disguise himself."

So while Jace had been in Moonbrook and at the Stormwind Altar discussing the demon summoning with those warlocks, Rhonin had followed Vaelastrasz into Blackrock Spire and rescued Krasus.

"I was out at the time," Jace said. "Sorry I couldn't be of help."

Vielan shook his head. "You are a benefactor to the entire Red Dragonflight, Jace. We're the ones who should apologize. The Queen has always felt immense guilt for dragging you into our feud with the black dragons. And things didn't go smoothly for us in Blackrock Spire either — in that extreme situation, the black dragon cloak might actually have been less convenient than a bead that could sustain the magic more easily. The several of us were trapped in Blackrock Spire for days before we finally escaped. If we'd been wearing the cloaks, we might have been exposed long ago, and things would have become several times more troublesome."

"Where is Master Krasus now?" Jace asked.

Vielan said, "He's in Grim Batol. The Queen is tending to him. His condition is not good, Jace. He could barely fight in Blackrock Spire — he only managed to escape thanks to me and Rhonin's care, and the torment along the way… Is there something you wanted to ask him?"

Jace glanced around. "Something to do with the Kirin Tor and Deathwing. I'm certain someone in the Kirin Tor sold him out — I'm sure he was captured by Deathwing because of that person."

"The Prince thinks so too." Vielan said. "And he's almost certain who it is. The Prince will recover quickly under the Queen's care, and when he does, he'll return to the Kirin Tor, Jace. Wait for his good news."

Let's hope so.

All of this sounded far more straightforward than Jace had imagined.

Whether it was Rhonin and Vaelastrasz rescuing Krasus, or Krasus already having considered the matter of the Kirin Tor traitor.

Perhaps there was no need for him to live in endless anxiety either — after all, at present, the danger of the Ursuulas scythe was trouble enough to keep him busy.

The good news was that they'd found the breakthrough for locating this scythe: the demon was on his side, not in the hands of any other warlock. Thinking about it that way, Rhonin had really done him a tremendous favor.

Vaelastrasz was right — heroes are never truly buried; they only need to wait for an opportunity.

Thanks to Rhonin, Jace could also relax for a while and stop worrying so much about the Kirin Tor. From now on, he only needed to focus his energy on the mage tower and the matters related to Ursuulas.

None of these were things that could be resolved in the short term — there was no use rushing.

"Oh, right." The red dragon produced a dark-colored jewelry box. "I nearly forgot the most important reason I came here."

"What is this?" Jace asked.

"After you left Grim Batol, the Queen had been helping you search for your staff, and finally there's a result." Vielan said. "But unfortunately, Teron Gorefiend's staff has been burned to nothing by Deathwing's magic — only this shadow gem survived."

Jace took the box. "That staff was forged from a special metal of Draenor. Sturdy as it was, it couldn't possibly withstand Deathwing's magic — I expected as much."

Vielan glanced at the box. "I'm glad you won't take it too hard. The Queen was rather upset about the staff being destroyed. Well then — is there anything else I can help with?"

"Are you heading to Blackrock Spire immediately?" Jace asked.

"Not yet." Vielan said. "I and Rhonin have been raising havoc in Blackrock Spire these past days — we'll need to wait some time for those orcs and black dragons to calm down and forget about the incident. After that, I'll come up with a completely different identity to infiltrate again and investigate the situation inside further."

"Then help me clean this tower." Jace turned and looked up at the tall tower behind him. "Er, this…" the red dragon asked. "Darrowshire Tower?"

"Right." Jace said, craning his neck. "If all goes as expected, the king intends to grant this tower to me, so I'll be responsible for its upkeep and renovation. A few days ago I fought a battle here against a pack of gnoll warlocks with the Stonewatch soldiers. Now, though it's been roughly cleaned up, there are still some stubborn shadow corruptions and even tainted blood in the upper levels and in the odd corners. My abilities are limited working alone — I'd like to find a helper. So…"

"Then let me give it a try." Vielan straightened his collar. "No fire-breathing," Jace warned.

"Understood." The other nodded.
