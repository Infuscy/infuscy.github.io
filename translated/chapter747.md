## Chapter 747: The Voice of Souls

These stones burning with green firelight rolled down like so many lumps of coal, and the imps each picked out their rightful share from within, according to the number of soul shards their master had provided.

Jace picked up two of the stones, tossed one to Sazeno, and asked, "Do imps actually grow from this kind of thing?"

"What else?" Sazeno opened his mouth and swallowed the entire stone in one gulp. His bulging belly glowed with firelight, then slowly dimmed.

"Master, give me the other one too…"

The imp raised his skinny hands high, as if he couldn't wait to eat another. Jace had been wanting to keep one to try for himself, but it was rare to see Sazeno so eager for anything, so he tossed it over to him too.

After swallowing them both, even Sazeno's climb up onto Jace's shoulder became much clumsier. Gripping Jace's cloak tightly, he whispered, "Open the pack, Master, let me go in and sleep… tomorrow let me try and see if I can cast a Language Curse on you, how about that?"

"Good to see you still remember why I had you eat those stones," Jace said as he opened the pack.

"Of course." The imp hopped right in as he said it.

The warlocks reactivated the binding circle, and Kallrez, cursing and swearing, was forced back down to the ground.

Leaving the altar and returning to the tent, what mattered next was up to Sazeno. Hopefully he really could do as he'd said — that eating a Healing Stone would make him a bit stronger, giving him the power to cast more spells.

Though Jace figured the imp was probably using this as an excuse to trick him into having that demon convert Healing Stones for him to eat. But in the end, there was no harm in making Sazeno's fel power a bit stronger. Plus, being able to obtain access to Healing Stones through some channel was itself a step forward for researching demons or warlock magic — no harm in it.

Of course, this time they'd also used the Healing Stones as an opportunity to get everyone to agree to release this doom lord for a bit.

Though Jace had been a little worried during the process, it seemed this big fellow hadn't tried to make any trouble either. Just as Sazeno had said, these powerful demons still valued their lives quite a bit. Having their true name mastered meant having their lifeline clutched in someone's hand.

At dinner, Jace took a bite of the salted dried fish he'd brought from Lakeshire, chased it with some breadcrumbs, wiped his hands, and took out the paper on which Sazeno had recorded the Eredun language, comparing it word by word against Dasmora's report.

He hadn't handed every word on the report to Kallrez to translate, so he could only get the general idea. But even reading it this way, he'd picked up something.

The voice of wailing souls, a weapon hidden by a mage, elven traces, magical foes, a great dark-green wall.

The weapon hidden by a mage — Jace actually knew about that. He remembered that Skavir, the Guardian of the Order of Tirisfal at the time, also Aegwynn's teacher, had hunted down and defeated the previous owner of Ursaal, the eredar necromancer Sethir.

He hadn't been able to destroy the weapon, so to keep it from continuing to bring calamity upon the world, he'd chosen to hide it away in a place no one knew. That was the place Dasmora and Medivh's treasure hunters were searching for.

Since the report mentioned the mage who'd hidden the weapon, that meant this report indeed recorded Dasmora's progress in her search. What was the great dark-green wall? An actual wall? Could it be the Greymane Wall in Silverpine Forest?

No — the Greymane Wall hadn't been fully constructed yet at this time. Why would Dasmora mention it here? Could it be a titan relic in Northrend? But where in Northrend would there be a great dark-green wall…

Magical foes… human mages, or perhaps elves? After all, elven traces had been mentioned earlier too.

Elves, a dark-green wall, magical foes — Jace felt like he was just barely grasping the edge of something. He took a deep breath, picked out the key words he hadn't figured out, shuffled them, and prepared to go ask the doom lord again the next day.

In the morning, arriving at the Stormwind Altar, the moment Kallrez saw Jace he immediately tried to force himself upright, only to be held down tightly by the magical bindings. Staring at Jace, he said, "Release me! I haven't violated your 'rules,' I've carried out your orders — exactly how long are you planning to trap me in this circle?!"

"If you serve the Burning Legion, you should know what kind of place Azeroth is," Jace said. "It's packed with Holy Light faithful who want to wipe you demons out. If it were a succubus minion, she could turn invisible, but if—"

"Let me out and let me lean against this altar to recover my strength. I'll kill them all," Kallrez growled.

"I can't be bothered to explain," Jace said, taking out another word list he'd organized the night last night. "Help me translate these. Just a few words this time."

"I can't for the life of me figure out what you're doing with all these words — I'm losing my patience!" Kallrez said irritably. "If you're learning our language to read the writings the doomguards recorded, you might as well just tell me the original text directly and I can tell you straight up what they're saying. Don't waste my time here! Do you have scrolls from other Eredun lords, or did you find something from Dasmora's hiding place?"

"A demon even more impatient than a mortal," Jace said coldly. "Carry out my orders and stop running your mouth."

Kallrez immediately said, "Of course you're patient, you slick-skinned little pink meat! You can still hop about here, while I'm locked in this open-air prison without being able to move a muscle!"

"Best get used to it sooner rather than later," Jace said, glanced at the words on the paper, and asked, "Vurosk. What does that mean?"

"You might as well just show me the characters directly — your ridiculous mouthparts are insulting our ancient language," Kallrez said with displeasure.

"I know you understand me," Jace snapped. "I haven't even started mocking how your Common sounds exactly like a boar farting nonstop, producing a stench worse than the dried meat in my pack. I suggest we drop this pointless back-and-forth and you tell me right now — what does Vurosk mean?!"

Standing nearby, Molophil shifted her neck uncomfortably at Jace's outburst. She glanced over that way, then looked at the doom lord before her and said, "My master rarely loses his temper, Lord Kallrez. I'd advise you to restrain yourself. You are under his control at present — know your place."

Kallrez glanced at this shivarra, then down at the circle beneath his feet, and said, "The voice of souls. Whether in demonic or your Common… there is no single word that can describe that sound. It is the powerless… feeble noise emitted by souls as they are devoured, forced to accept the miserable fate long since destined for them. Heh."

"Vurosk?" Jace asked.

"Yes," Kallrez confirmed.
