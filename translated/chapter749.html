Chapter 749: Cripple

That night, as Jace was turning the report over in his mind and wondering if he'd missed anything, he suddenly remembered that there was something he hadn't done. He slapped the demon pack and said, "How's it going, Sazeno?" "Ah, Master!" Sazeno poked his head out of the pack. "I'm awake. What is it?" Jace stared at him and said, "Don't forget what we agreed on — the Language Curse. Don't tell me you're trying to stall again? What, is casting one spell really that hard?" "It's not that I don't want to cast it, Master. I finally managed to build up some strength, and now I have to turn right around and spend it…" the imp said in a somewhat complaining tone. "I still want to let this power brew a bit longer. The soul shards converted by the doom lord Kallrez — good heavens, back where I used to be, I couldn't even dream of tasting something like that. All I could do was suck in a mouthful of the fel energy drifting out of the furnace…" "Casting one Language Curse is that much trouble?" Molophil shook her head. "I might as well learn the incastation myself and help my master try it. No wonder Kallrez can't hear a word you're saying — not only were you weak to begin with, you're genuinely getting more and more useless." "Who says he can't hear me?!" the imp shrieked. "Didn't I help him record all those words Master asked to translate?! I'll do it right now — since it's Master's order, I'll attempt to cast the Language Curse!" Molophil gave a soft laugh. Jace set the scroll in his hand aside and said, "Go on, then." The imp's thin black lips trembled a few times, his hands gathered a ball of fel fire, and he leaped up and pointed at Jace. Jace hadn't even clearly made out what incantation he was chanting before he felt his throat begin to burn faintly. Though it was the familiar sensation of being hit by a Language Curse, it really didn't have the intense pain in the throat that the orc Carohl's casting had produced — it was more like his neck was slowly heating up. "I did it! I did it!" The imp excitedly ran back and forth across the ground twice, then hopped in front of Molophil and said, "How about that, how about that?! It was that simple! I told you I could do it… I'm a real demon!" "A demon using demon magic — nothing remarkable," Molophil muttered, then turned to look at Jace. "Ingru… Yinka… Yin'karash… Yineksindallas… Yin'k…" At this point, Jace tried to pronounce the incantation for Cripple. The demon's voice rumbled continuously inside the tent. Though each time it was scrambled into gibberish by the power of the Language Curse, he also discovered that resisting the imp's Language Curse wasn't all that difficult — he was quickly able to produce the opening of the Cripple incantation. He coughed, tried to swallow, and felt the degree of burning in his throat. Could it be that because the imp's mana was weak, the power of the Language Curse was much weaker than the orc's, making it easier to resist and produce the sounds he wanted? Jace gathered his breath and forcefully pushed back against the power of the curse, blurting out in one go: "Yin, ka, ke, wei, la, li, ka, jia, de!" The entire incantation was spoken completely in a demonic voice. The demonic power stirred up by this voice drowned out all the light in the tent in one fell swoop, and balls of fel energy's green glow cast a green hue over the gloomy space. Because although he'd tried to summon the power of Cripple, with no suitable target, the gathered fel energy quickly dissipated. Molophil slowly lay on her side and said with a smile in demonic, "Master, your voice is truly captivating. I think in the future we really will be able to converse in demonic, don't you think?" "But… my… voice…" Jace caught his breath and continued in demonic. "Is only produced… because of the curse…" As the effects of the Language Curse gradually faded, he also came to realize the problem with the imp's Language Curse. Although the imp's Language Curse was easier to resist, its duration was also shorter. If he wanted to turn the Language Curse into a longer-lasting state that provided a massive boost to his demon magic, he'd probably need the imp to possess stronger mana and unleash a more powerful Language Curse. At that point, it could probably be considered a controllable "fel corruption." Jace said, "Sazeno, come with me. I'm going to go find some beast or other to test Cripple on. There are some spiders in the gorge by the Stormwind Altar — just right for target practice." "You can use me directly," Molophil suggested. "You sure?" Jace asked. Molophil said, "No beast can tell you exactly what level your magic has reached. I also want to test whether I, as I am now, can resist the Eredun Cripple." Jace felt as if there were something different in the burning eyes of the succubus. But since a test subject who could talk had volunteered, that wasn't bad either. He picked up the demon pack containing the imp and said, "Then let's go practice in that gully behind here." Walking out of the tent in the middle of the night, passing between the orcs' hide tents, one could already hear pants and gasps coming from who-knew-where. The two of them left the warlocks' dwelling area and came to an uninhabited gully with bare black stone walls on either side. Molophil's fel hooves trod on the scattered black ash and soil as she walked to the distance and said, "Come, Master." Her body's fel runes blazed with crimson flames, and her eyes were illuminated by the blaze. In the black shadows of the mountain valley, she was like a leaping fel-flame sprite. Jace thought that if he could control a demon like this with Cripple, then his magic could be considered truly mastered. "Language Curse, Sazeno," he commanded. "The Language Curse has already been cast once — I don't have enough mana!" the imp whispered. "If only I could have a soul shard…" "Drink a mana potion," Jace said. "There's one right in the pack." "Agh…" The imp shook his head, opened one, and gulped it down, then muttered, "What a weird aftertaste!" After drinking it, the imp quietly murmured the Language Curse incantation and cast it at Jace. Once again Jace felt the burning sensation in his throat… "Yin ka ke wei la li ka jia de!" This time Jace gathered his strength and will, forcefully suppressing the power of the curse and shouting the incantation at full volume, pointing straight in Molophil's direction. A wave of demonic fel energy spread out and descended upon the succubus, pressing the fel flames all over her body until they were extinguished! Yet Molophil wasn't completely suppressed. She suddenly vanished into a patch of Shadow, then reappeared behind Jace. Already familiar with the power of Shadowstep, Jace instinctively dodged to the side. The Nathrezim longblade swept past his neck, nearly taking his head off… "What are you—" "Dodge, Sesso!" Before he could finish speaking, Molophil's Blade of Endless Darkness was already stabbing before his eyes! A thick Shadow force held the blade's edge firmly in front of his eyes — the Dark Shield had already sprung up. Jace reached out to grab Molophil's arm, which was bound by Cripple, trying to disarm her, but she easily pulled her hand back and dodged. Her movements were a bit slower than when she hunted other demons. Jace thought that if it weren't for the Cripple, either his neck or his eyes would have been torn open or pierced through by now. Following this, Molophil swung her blade in a horizontal chop that struck the black halo of the shield. The shield immediately burst. The succubus vanished into the shadows again to avoid the shockwave. Her demonic shadow, trailed by the green glow left behind by Cripple, flashed several meters in the blink of an eye, attempting to close in from another direction. This time Jace directly spoke the words of Curse of Weakness in a demonic voice. Molophil gave a low moan and emerged from the shadows, nearly falling to the ground. Seizing her stumble, Jace fixed his eyes on the Blade of Endless Darkness in her hand. A bright red thread of energy lashed out — but the fel runes all over Molophil's body flashed with fel light. With a shout, she forcefully retreated and tore free from Jace's Blood Grip, gripping her twin blades and charging over again. But before she'd taken a few steps, she was bound by a purple Shadow around her throat and hauled up hard. Jace had already used this opportunity to cast Asphyxiate, locking her throat tightly! Hit by Curse of Weakness, the succubus was also bound by Cripple and Asphyxiate. This triple suppression had left her completely unable to move. Jace clenched his fist and said in demonic, "How about it, shivarra? Still want to continue? Challenging your master's authority?" Molophil gave a pained laugh, swung her arm, and hurled the Blade of Endless Darkness at Jace. The dagger struck the Dark Shield and slowed rapidly, as if it had been fired into a thick bog, then landed on the ground with a clang. Looking at the dagger, and at the messy boot prints beneath his feet, he noticed that he hadn't moved from his spot even a little. At this moment, he suddenly felt the demon he was controlling with Asphyxiate becoming harder and harder to grasp. Molophil's power was growing stronger and stronger, and the flames all over her body were burning once more. The effects of Cripple were fading… Seizing this opportunity, Molophil roared and shattered all of Jace's control magic in an instant. The fel runes all over her body — even her fel horns — blazed with flames. The short blade in her remaining hand also erupted in fire. The demonic power binding her had been completely thrown off. Jace could feel that the Shadow resistance granted by her fel runes was no weaker than that of the Satyr Lord Vilethorn in Maraudon! After all, those had been copied directly from Vilethorn's hide… It was just that Jace was still unfamiliar with the Cripple incantation. Seeing this frenzied scene, he wanted to chant it again but it was already too late. Molophil charged through a blaze that burned away the Shadow power, and Jace had no choice but to channel Shadow and attempt to escape using Shadowstep himself. After his whole being fell into the darkness and he fled some distance in who-knew-what direction, when he opened his eyes again, the blade was already pressed against his chest. By this point, his hand had only just reached the hilt of Skyshiver at his waist. "Shadowstep still needs work, Master…" the succubus whispered by Jace's ear. Jace sat on a dark-colored stone to the side and said, "Indeed. I concede." Molophil put away her weapon and wagged her finger. "Concede? You held back. You didn't use a single spell that could actually hurt me. Not to mention, if Master had been willing to draw that sword, I'd have been sent straight back to the Twisting Nether in one stroke. What's more, the one who made me this strong was your—" She licked her long, pointed eyes and lowered her eyes slightly. Though Molophil had said some flattering things, Jace knew that his Cripple — both in strength and duration — was far from adequate. Especially compared to the one Kallrez had cast personally, and that had been when he'd just suffered heavy injuries from the portal. "It scared the hell out of me." At this point the imp poked his head out from behind a stone. "I thought you were done for, shivarra!" The succubus paid it no mind. Instead, she straddled Jace's legs, propped his shoulders, and said, "You tortured me with so many dreadful spells, Master. You're really not planning to compensate me a little?" Molophil's height was already nearly 1.7 meters at this point. When she leaned in this close, pressing down on Jace's shoulders and looking down at him, Jace suddenly felt… as if she'd become a different demon altogether. "But this fight just about burned through all my energy too." Molophil hooked a mana potion out of the pack with her tail and brought it before Jace's eyes. "Then drink this." "I'd better hide again," the imp said, and ducked behind the stone. Jace took the potion and drank it. After drinking, he caught his breath — his nostrils were filled with the unsuppressed fel fragrance emanating from between Molophil's bright red cleavage. The succubus slowly raised her body, then gradually lowered it bit by bit. She slowly closed her eyes and panted, "It's been a long time since I've had such a satisfying… dear." "Has it been that long?" Jace asked. "I mean fighting," Molophil opened her eyes and stared at him. "Fighting a caster like you is a pleasure no lesser than physical ecstasy." "Ah, so from now on we could use fighting practice to replace… this, how about it?" Jace asked. "If that's not an order, I refuse," Molophil said softly. "I want both."
