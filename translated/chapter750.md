## Chapter 750: Magic Demonstration

"Master Sesso?"

Outside the tent was the voice of Demitri — it seemed she'd returned from Lakeshire.

Hearing the voice, Jace reluctantly opened his eyes, sat up, and braced himself against the wooden pillar to catch his breath, not knowing how long Molophil had tortured him last night.

He rubbed his face, threw on his robe, glanced at the succubus lying on her side in the corner peering at him with narrowed eyes, and then said, "Come in."

Demitri ducked in and looked at the Jace and the succubus before him. Any demon summoner could tell what had happened last night just by smelling the air in this tent.

She took a scroll out of her pack and said, "The Demon Empowerment curse — it's yours now, Master Sesso."

After Jace opened it, he saw that its contents were recorded in demonic, carefully annotated by someone in Common, with quite a few orcish symbols added for supplementation — one could say it was described in considerable detail.

Demitri asked, "This spell… how much coin could it fetch, Master?"

"That depends on precisely how powerful it is, Mrs. Clauss," Jace said. "I need to learn it, see what this magic can actually do, and then consider its value. But I'd say it's worth at least five gold — I place great value on the strength of my demon minion."

Demitri was clearly a bit hesitant. She clenched her fist and asked, "Could you let me witness the magic first, Master?"

"Two soul shards," Jace said, holding out his hand.

"No problem." Demitri took a deep breath, trembling as she took the soul shards from her pocket and placed them in Jace's hand.

Jace rubbed the smooth surfaces of the two shards, then took out a Greater Mana Potion and drank it in one gulp before saying, "Follow me."

At the Stormwind Altar, quite a few warlocks had gathered around Kallrez. The rumbling of Blackrock Mountain's rolling lava made it impossible to hear what they were saying at all.

Kallrez responded from time to time, and lost his temper from time to time, startling all the warlocks into taking a step back. But by now those warlocks also understood that Jace had enslaved this demon and given him orders not to harm anyone, so approaching him wasn't dangerous.

Jace wasn't worried about the warlocks being able to coax anything out of Kallrez — after all, even communication between him and his own master was this difficult. How much easier could theirs possibly be?

And it was obvious that although Kallrez, as a doom lord, commanded many demon spells, the most powerful among them all required roaring out the incantations with demonic power in order to be successfully cast — like Cripple.

If those warlocks really had the ability to use a Language Curse to overcome the curse's interference and roar out the incantation the way he did, then letting them learn it wouldn't matter either.

After all, the warlocks had helped him summon this demon. Although the demon now being enslaved by him was an accident, he was unwilling to obstruct the communication and learning that had already been planned — not with this group of people who walked on the dark edge.

Jace led Demitri to the gully behind the mountain with practiced ease, looked around to confirm no one was about, and then said to Demitri, who was standing in the distance watching: "I'll only cast once, Mrs. Clauss."

"I understand," Demitri said.

Jace tossed the soul shards to the ground and lightly murmured the incantation with practiced ease, slowly guiding the Shadow energy around him to rapidly gather in the air above his head.

When those dark serpents converged, Demitri had still been trying to make out what Jace's lips were saying. But when she saw those black demon-serpents biting each other and coiling together to form an evil demon star, she forgot everything she'd originally planned to do, leaving only a blank stare fixed on the sky…

It had been a long time since Jace had cast Gul'dan's Hand. He tried to guide the demon star in the sky and found he was far more proficient than he'd been at Nethergarde Keep, or when he'd guided this magic to bombard Dasmora at Eldre'Thalas…

And even without the aid of the souls from the Blasted Lands, he'd guided a rather terrifying demon star coiling overhead.

Mana burned furiously, and Jace even felt he was nearly losing his grip on it.

Because the two emotions required to cast Gul'dan's Hand — loneliness… and hatred — he'd experienced them both to the fullest during the Grim Batol expedition.

Thinking back to that stretch of the journey, thinking back to when he'd set out from Stormwind for Menethil Harbor, gazing at the waves of the Great Sea, pondering his path ahead… that sense of solitary travel that had nearly choked him to death, interwoven with his loneliness as a transmigrator stranded alone in this world, growing colder and colder.

And hatred… it was also at that moment that his urge to kill Deathwing had nearly gotten the better of him.

The dark demon star shone violently. The dark purple fel fire burst into flame with a boom, startling Demitri into bracing herself against the jumbled rocks.

Jace also felt the magic he was guiding had already exceeded his control. Though it couldn't compare to the terrifying result at Nethergarde that had combined the advantages of "terrain" and "allies," it was frightening enough.

But at Nethergarde Keep, the pit lord Azgoth had completely absorbed all the Shadow damage. At Eldre'Thalas, Dasmora had withstood the impact with a shield powered by the city's mana array. But here, there was nothing that could take such an explosion.

Jace still remembered the destruction caused when he'd first successfully cast Gul'dan's Hand in Elwynn Forest — even he himself had nearly been unable to withstand it.

He interrupted the spell and disintegrated the demon star in midair. If he'd let it fall… there was no telling what would happen.

Watching those black demon-serpents tear apart their own bodies and scatter through the air, dyeing the entire gully dark purple, it was a long time before things returned to normal.

Jace stared blankly at his own hands. These spells most strongly influenced by emotion were also the most likely to spiral out of control because of it.

If he'd lost his composure for a moment just now, this strike might have buried both himself and Demitri Clauss in this nameless place.

"Letting it fall is premium content, Mrs. Clauss," Jace said, looking up at the black clouds in the firmament. "Once you've scraped together enough money, I'll show you the whole thing."

"This is more than enough. Perhaps I'll never be able to forget that Shadow star just now, Master Sesso," Demitri said, her voice trembling. "No matter the price, I want to learn it. My earlier proposal… could you please consider it carefully?"

"I've already told you," Jace said, turning his head. "Did you think I refused you back then because Molophil was present?"

"Miss Molophil is a powerful huntress, many warlocks know that. But…" Demitri cautiously paused, then continued, "And I… my minion has shapeshifting magic. You've been to 'Short Staff' Gladys's hiding place and must have seen Gorsich's minion. Mine is far stronger than his. The two of us could serve you together. Whatever form you want her to take, she could become that. A certain lady you've been thinking of, or even a certain noble — say, Queen Tiffin, or the princess of Lordaeron, or a high elf… until you're satisfied."

Jace said, "Much obliged for the kind offer, Mrs. Clauss. It does sound quite appealing. But try your best to scrape together the money — I'm shorter on coin right now."
