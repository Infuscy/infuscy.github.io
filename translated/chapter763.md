## Chapter 763: SI:7 and Dark Magic

Darkwhisper Gorge.

Jace gave the poorly rendered forest on the map another look.

Since SI:7 agents had already been near that place, there had to be a reason they'd painted a forest there — meaning, before Darkwhisper Gorge was overrun by demons retreating from Mount Hyjal, the ravine should have been covered in woodland.

Green forest, rugged ravine… was this what the Green Wall and the Maze had been hinting at?

Jace very much doubted a demon lieutenant like Dasmora would call an ordinary stretch of woods and ravines "high walls" and a "maze." After all, she was a demon who had lived in Eldre'Thalas — when it came to high walls and mazes, what could possibly compare to Eldre'Thalas?

But the other clues — an unapproachable foot of a mountain, the murmuring of ghosts — had all been confirmed by this agent's account. As Jace followed the agent further into the building, he felt there was probably no place in this world that came closer.

Just as he was considering whether he should write a letter to Vereesa right away, Denton Het led him to a small room where a man of about thirty was bent over searching through a bookshelf. Seeing the two arrive, he looked up.

"Master Sesso." The man raised his head. "I'm Mathias Shaw. I'm sorry to call you here at this hour, and by rights we should have come to you in person. But in the end we decided to invite you here."

Mathias Shaw — the future leader of SI:7. In Jace's impression he was a bearded middle-aged man, but here he had a ruddy complexion, only a hint of stubble, and looked youthful and sharp overall.

"My home isn't a good place for a conversation," Jace said. "It's cramped enough for one person, let alone fitting two."

Mathias nodded, gestured to the seat beside him for Jace to sit, and said, "It's late, Master Sesso, and I don't want to take up too much of your time, so I'll get straight to the point. Actually, SI:7's investigation into the black dragons didn't start with Daval Prestor's disappearance — we've been deep into it for a long time now. And it even has something to do with you."

"Because of the Wyrmcult?" Jace asked, sitting down.

"Exactly," Mathias said. "There were rumors of black dragon activity in the Redridge area. We ran several investigations and found some hidden dragon scales and claws, but never discovered an actual dragon. At one point we even suspected it was veterans who'd faced red dragons during the war and were scared out of their minds, chasing shadows. But then we heard that a black dragon's head had turned up at the Wizard's Sanctum."

"Following that lead, we wiped out a Wyrmcult cell operating in the Redridge and Duskwood area, and killed a dragonkin leading the group. But the gains from those operations didn't let us uncover the conspiracy hiding behind Lord Daval Prestor in advance, nor did they help us figure out how to identify a disguised dragonkin — let alone a real dragon. We asked the Wizard's Sanctum for help too, but Master Andromath was powerless as well. Though the king never said it outright, I could tell he was very dissatisfied with our work."

"So how did you find that dragonkin?" Jace asked.

"Nothing special," Mathias said. "One of the group was willing to talk, and we followed his words to the dragonkin. But that kind of efficiency is far too low."

"I have to be honest with you, Mr. Shaw," Jace said. "Although I survived my encounter with Deathwing, I've never had the chance to face a black dragon disguising itself in front of me, so I don't know if I have the ability to identify one. But since His Majesty has given me this task to assist SI:7, and I'm already a resident of Stormwind, I'll do my best to find a way to identify black dragons."

Mathias lowered his gaze and thought for a moment, then said, "Actually, we've captured a few people, Master. We suspect there are dragonkin among them… or based on the existing leads, we can basically confirm there are dragonkin among them. But neither the Church priests' Holy Light nor the Wizard's Sanctum's alchemical potions can force these people to reveal their true forms. I don't suppose you could take a look?"

"Of course," Jace said, somewhat surprised.

"But," he asked, "if there are non-dragonkin among this group, how did you catch them? Are all of these people Wyrmcult members?"

Mathias said, "They're all warlocks who can use dark magic." Jace nodded. "Take me to see them."

Mathias Shaw's smile faded. He glanced at Denton Het standing to the side, signaling him to lead the way.

The three of them came to the basement of the building, and only then did Jace realize this was an underground complex far larger than the cellar beneath the Slaughtered Lamb.

Of course, if the SI:7 headquarters' basement had been nothing more than an ordinary cellar, that would have been the real surprise.

Unlike a typical terrifying dungeon, there were no sudden screams here, no sounds of whips and flaying — only the drip of water echoing from somewhere.

They walked through the dim passage to the sound of heavy footsteps. Mathias said, "Master Maginor Dumas of the Wizard's Sanctum has long believed that SI:7 is using the hunt for these warlocks as an excuse to secretly learn dark magic. So he's been pushing through various channels to have these people transferred to the Wizard's Sanctum's magic cells, or to have us throw them in the Stockade. That's also why I wanted you to come take a look soon — in a while, there may not be another chance."

Thinking of the rogues' Cloak of Shadows, Shadowstep and similar abilities in the game — clearly shadow-magic-type powers — Jace felt Maginor Dumas's concern wasn't entirely unfounded.

Of course, as Stormwind's number-one dark magic criminal at present, he was in no position to say anything on the matter.

They reached a cell door on the lowest level. Mathias blocked the entrance, turned back to Jace, and said, "My grandmother actually didn't want me to bring you to see these people, Master Sesso. Though I had some hesitation in my heart, I decided to trust you."

"You're worried I might know someone among them and cover for these warlocks," Jace said.

"That's right," Mathias said with a rare smile. "But I also figured you wouldn't want someone you know to actually be a black dragon, would you?"

"Of course I wouldn't," Jace said. "Though Deathwing wasn't killed by me, he did die because of me. Every black dragon probably hates me to the bone."

Mathias nodded and stepped aside.
