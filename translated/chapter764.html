Chapter 764: A Special Cooperation

This small block of cells was quite unusual. Jace could tell magic had gone into their construction — meaning that although the Wizard Sanctum wanted SI:7 to hand over these dark magic users, they had actually helped renovate this place, specifically to hold spellcasters.

There were four separated prisoners in the cells — three men and one woman. Though they were all warlocks, Jace didn't recognize a single one of them. The number of dark magic circles within the Kingdom of Stormwind was far greater than he'd imagined.

Jace couldn't tell whether it was because these people had been imprisoned so long they'd burned through their energy, or because of the dampening effect of the magic cells, but he could barely sense any dark magic power from them at all. He'd originally thought that since black dragons were a race that wielded fire and shadow magic, he could rule out any warlocks who'd dabbled in fel.

But now he couldn't detect shadow, fel, or any other mana at all. As he examined the four of them, he felt increasingly out of his depth.

He glanced back at Mathias, wondering — was this a test? Did they already know which of them was a dragonkin, or was there no dragonkin at all, and they just wanted to see if he had any real ability?

But even though he was someone acutely sensitive to shadow energy, he still couldn't sense any difference between these people.

"Do these cells have the ability to weaken magic?" he asked.

"Yes," Mathias nodded. "To prevent them from suddenly using dark magic to attack the people outside the cells."

"Then I need to go in and look," Jace said.

"That's dangerous, Master," Mathias warned.

"Trust me, Mathias — I'm not the one in danger. They are." Jace fixed his gaze on a gaunt young man among them. Mathias smiled slightly, nodded, and signaled the guard to open each cell door in turn so Jace could go in and examine them one by one.

But after he'd seen all four, nothing had improved. Though he could sense they'd indeed used shadow magic, and could roughly gauge their strength from experience, it wasn't enough to let him judge which one had black dragon blood running through his veins.

In the end, Jace could only shake his head and say, "There's no way. This is beyond my ability, Mr. Shaw. Dragonkin disguises aren't some simple arcane Polymorph or sleight of hand — it's a true transformation."

"If we used magic to attack them, would it force them to reveal their original form?" Denton asked.

Mathias fell silent. Jace said, "Perhaps. Dragonkin are more resistant to fire and shadow magic than ordinary mortal warlocks, so if you hit them with the same level of magic, the one who breaks and dies first would most likely be the mortal warlock. But are you going to execute every warlock you've captured just to identify a dragonkin? What if these dragonkin have turned into nobles, or one of your own?"

Denton nodded. Jace swept his gaze over the four again. They were all watching him or the two agents beside him. The woman lowered her head and sighed. Jace wondered if he could detect something off through their emotions.

"I want to look at them again."

"Of course." Mathias signaled the guard to open the doors once more.

This time, Jace fixed his target on a middle-aged man whose emotional fluctuations were off. It wasn't that his emotions were especially intense — it was that they were different.

The others were mostly resigned, weary, or hopeful for something — probably still hoping to get out soon. In short, the normal thoughts of someone locked up.

But that middle-aged man was confused. Even a little afraid.

He kept forcing his emotions down, not letting them show — but the harder he suppressed them, the easier they were for Jace to pick up. Because Jace had never judged emotions by outward expression. He judged the characteristics of emotion by the shadow energy they radiated.

What was this middle-aged man afraid of? What was he confused about? Was it because Jace had killed Deathwing that he was afraid?

"Do these people know about Grim Batol?" Jace asked.

"No. They've been isolated here for nearly a year. No one's told them anything that's happened outside," Denton replied.

Then why? Jace looked at the middle-aged man for a while and asked, "Do you know me?"

The middle-aged man only stared into his eyes and said nothing. Jace let out a resigned breath, and just as he was thinking he might be overthinking this, the man suddenly glanced at Jace's cloak.

Though he quickly shifted his gaze back, Jace still caught the emotion that flickered when the man looked at his cloak…

That's right — this middle-aged man didn't know him, didn't know what he'd done, but he was afraid of the dark cloak Jace was wearing — this black dragon-leather cloak that Vaelastrasz had given him!

"Do you know what material this cloak of mine is made of, Mr. Shaw?" Jace asked.

"Does that have anything to do with what we're doing right now?" Mathias asked.

"It does," Jace said.

Mathias reached out and touched it, frowning. "I don't know, but the material of this cloak is strange — it's like some kind of soft metal. Isn't it heavy to wear?"

"A bit heavier than an ordinary cloak," Jace said. "Since you, as an investigator of black dragons, don't even know what this cloak is made of — can't even tell by touching it — then the fact that this man knows is very suspicious, don't you think? This is woven from the scales and hide of a black dragon."

Hearing that, the fury on the middle-aged man's face was almost impossible to contain. But he kept his mouth shut and refused to say a word. Jace spoke in Draconic: "Deathwing has already fallen. How much longer can his underlings survive?"

"Utter nonsense!" the middle-aged man cursed in Draconic. "You're dead, little mage!"

The man's pupils narrowed to vertical slits. His clothes burst apart as his body swelled up. His form expanded, brown-black scales filling Jace's vision. He raised his claws high — and Jace simply spoke the incantation for Fear, making the dragonkin shrink back on the spot, wailing, arms and claws curling in, dropping to his knees, throwing himself uncontrollably at the bottom of the cell, clawing frantically at the walls as if he wanted to dig through and escape from the other side.

Jace calmly closed the cell door and walked outside. The guard immediately locked it. Mathias and Denton had just drawn their daggers and longswords, staring at the dragonkin howling madly inside the cell.

The other three warlocks had dropped to their knees, prostrating themselves before the dragonkin, begging for mercy, while hurling all manner of vicious curses at Jace. Jace only gave them a glance and said nothing.

Mathias gripped his sword tightly and asked, "How could you tell he recognized your cloak?"

"I picked up on something off about his emotions," Jace said. "It's not a reproducible method, sir — not much reference value. After all, we can't count on every dragonkin losing control of their emotions the way he did in this situation. We still need something more reliable."

The group came outside the cell block. Mathias glanced back inside and said, "Now that we've found a disguised dragonkin, Master Sesso — can we use his characteristics to search for other disguised dragonkin?"

"We'd need a dog whose nose can sniff out magic," Denton joked.

But that offhand remark was the thing that jolted Jace awake.

He looked at Mathias, then at Denton, and said, "The real question is — if there really were such a dog, would you be willing to cooperate with these special dog trainers?"

Flags

Jace baits him with Draconic speech, forcing him to reveal his true form. - Fear spell used: Jace subdues the transformed dragonkin with a single Fear incantation, demonstrating his power. - Other prisoners are Cultists: The three remaining warlocks prostrate themselves before the dragonkin and curse Jace — confirming them as Cult of the Dragon members. - SI:7's identification problem: Mathias wants a reliable method to identify disguised dragonkin. Denton's joke about a "magic-sniffing dog" gives Jace the idea to propose using a fel hound. - Prisoners held in magic-dampening cells: The Wizard Sanctum helped SI:7 construct these cells to suppress spellcasting, which is why Jace couldn't sense magic from outside.
