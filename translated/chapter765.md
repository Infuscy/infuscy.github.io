## Chapter 765: Safe and Controllable

The hour was already late. In a spacious room on the second floor of SI:7, Mathias Shaw sat in the corner, his fingers tapping restlessly on the tabletop. Jace sat quietly beside him, watching. After a long while, Mathias said, "A lot of people think SI:7 might have ties to dark magic. But, Jace Sesso — I'm not especially keen on turning SI:7 into a genuine shadow nest."

"I know what you're talking about. Fel hounds, right? This isn't the first time SI:7 has dealt with those things. When we raided a warlock hideout in Lakeshire, one of our agents was swallowed alive by one of those horrible creatures. And now you want me to cooperate with those things?"

"I guarantee it as Court Mage," Jace said. "As long as the fel hound's master doesn't die, the fel hound won't go out of control — provided you cooperate with the people I name."

"Then would they be willing to cooperate with us?" Mathias asked with a frown. "Those warlocks? I can understand your motive for working for the Alliance, Master Sesso. You want to settle your parents in Stormwind. You want to protect yourself, your family, and your friends. I know people who've turned to dark magic for those reasons — you're not unique. But the vast majority of warlocks are doing things that bring ruin to themselves and others. The vast majority."

Jace said, "My friends all hope to be able to use dark magic legally for legitimate purposes. I'd like to help them win that kind of exemption too — meaning, with SI:7's permission, using dark magic within Stormwind without harming others, whether for experimentation or to assist you in hunting black dragons. Isn't that a compelling motive?"

"Fair enough," Mathias said, rubbing his forehead. "But I need to discuss this with my grandmother."

"I think Lady Poesonia Shaw values your decision more at this point," Jace said.

Mathias looked up with an expression that clearly said, *how do you know that?*

Jace said, "She was the one who initially disagreed with you taking me to see those prisoners, but when you stated your view, she agreed. So I'm guessing the successor Lady Shaw mentioned in front of the king is probably you — which is why she wants you to start making certain decisions ahead of time."

"She mentioned the successor matter in front of the king?" Mathias asked. Jace nodded. Mathias frowned tightly.

"Whether the decision is mine or hers, I need to meet one of those demon dogs first and confirm they're 'safe and controllable.'" His tone on "safe and controllable" carried a distinct edge of sarcasm.

Jace said, "I can let you meet one right now."

"Now? Here?"

"That right."

"Don't summoning those hellhounds require some kind of complicated ritual?"

"No. All you need is an open patch of ground."

"Then follow me."

Mathias led Jace to the backyard, shaded by dense trees, with the thick wall of Stormwind rising behind. A single moon hung in the treetops; the other was hidden behind the wall. The stillness was the kind that made a person feel safe. That sense of safety, however, was about to be torn apart by the power of the Void.

Jace walked to the flagstone clearing in the middle and tossed a soul shard onto the ground. He noticed the SI:7 agent staring at the shard with a grave expression, as if it were a time bomb.

He spoke the incantation with practiced ease. When the rift to the Twisting Nether tore open out of thin air, Mathias raised his eyes and fixed them on the rift, his arms crossing unconsciously over his chest as he drew a sharp breath.

When the fel hound tumbled out of the rift, hit the ground, and rolled to its feet, Jace could clearly sense that Mathias nearly wanted to draw his dagger and fight this Void demon to the death.

This fel hound was about the same size as the smaller one Jace had summoned before. The pact with Kael'zhul replacing magical enslavement must have made a difference — otherwise the fel hound he'd summoned this time might have been even smaller.

It crouched on the ground, tentacles quivering, facing its summoner. Before it could react, Jace had already completed the naming ritual.

He looked at the tense man on the other side and said, "Meet Dathalmut IV." Mathias muttered skeptically, "How obedient is it?"

Jace spoke in Demonic: "Sit."

Dathalmut IV immediately sat down on the ground. Though its head was too large to lower the way a normal dog's would, and with its head tilted half-upward staring at the top of the villa after sitting, it looked like a dim-witted dog with a crooked head — at least it had obeyed the command to sit.

The agent said nothing. Jace said, "Now let's take it to see those warlocks and find out whether it can pick out the dragonkin's magical signature."

The two returned to the cells. Jace ordered the fel hound to wave its tentacles over the warlocks and the dragonkin in turn. When it approached the dragonkin, the dragonkin roared furiously at the little demon — even the guard was shaken by the sudden furious bellow — but the fel hound was completely unfazed, continuing to sniff about on its own.

Soon, Dathalmut IV ran back to its master as if it had gotten a result. Jace asked in Demonic, "Did you memorize the dragonkin's signature?"

Though the fel hound responded with a pleased "bub-bub-bub" sound, there was still a problem.

This dragonkin, though very good at concealing his aura, had now fully transformed into a dragonkin and refused to change back — giving SI:7 and Jace no chance to identify distinguishing features.

Fel hounds were skilled at sniffing out differences in magic, in ways more detailed than humans imagined — but that didn't mean they could be counted on to pick a disguised dragonkin out of a crowd of mortals just yet.

"Do you have any other prisoners who might be dragonkin?" Jace asked.

"There are some in Goldshire and Lakeshire," Mathias said. "I understand your intent. Next I'll notify them to transfer the prisoners to Stormwind as soon as possible and see what this demon can actually do."

"Good. Then I'll wait for your word," Jace said. "Can this dog stay at SI:7 for now? You can keep it in a magic cell like this one. I can't very well parade it through Stormwind in broad daylight — not even in the middle of the night. You saw its size — it's not easy to hide."

"Fine." Mathias glanced at the guard beside him. "Find an open spot for this demon."

The guard nodded. Jace said to Dathalmut IV in Demonic, "Stay in this place. Don't try to escape. Don't try to attack anyone."

The fel hound responded with a low, hoarse rumble. Mathias gave it a look, then said, "That was the demons' language, wasn't it? Giving orders to such a terrifying demon so casually must be deeply satisfying. I wonder — if you told it to die, would it obey?"

"Guess why it's called Dathalmut IV," Jace said.

"Because three fel hounds have already died for you?" Mathias asked.

"Because I only remember three," Jace said offhandedly.

Mathias nodded meaningfully, then said, "I'll carefully consider the matter of further cooperation. I just have a feeling — this really is making a deal with demons."

"It's a deal with the demons' master," Jace corrected.
