## Chapter 838: The Druid's Goal

"What happened to Mosa?" Grede asked urgently. "Where is she?"

"Easy, Grede," Jace said quietly.

The blond, green-clothed elf's expression was hard to read — perhaps some displeasure at Grede's sudden rudeness, or some inexplicable anger or anxiety. Jace found it difficult to read this elf's emotions, even when he focused.

"It's hard to explain in a sentence or two," the elf said, lowering her gaze to meet Jace's eyes. "I want to know how you know Mosa. Aren't you mercenaries from another continent?"

"We…" Jace hadn't even gotten the words out when Grede grabbed her cloak impatiently. "You answer my question first — starting with who you are!"

The strange elf lowered her brows and glanced at the sleeve he'd grabbed, then stared straight at Grede until he squirmed, realized he was genuinely out of line, and had no choice but to let go.

"My name is Sola Shade, a botanist from Quel'Thalas."

Beside them, Vereesa's eyebrows trembled slightly upon hearing this — she probably didn't know this person.

But at least this explained why she didn't dress like a Farstrider at all and didn't carry any weapons. Botanists from Quel'Thalas were typically quite accomplished mages and wouldn't need weapons to handle self-defense in the wild.

"Now tell me how you came to know Mosa Woodmane," Grede said.

The elf who called herself Sola Shade looked at the dwarf expressionlessly. "I thought all I needed to do was offer payment."

"Damn it, this woman's like a rock," Grede sighed.

Jace stared at the cold-faced woman, still harboring doubts.

"If you're from Quel'Thalas, then do you know her?" he asked, pointing at Vereesa. Sola glanced at Vereesa and shook her head. "I'm not familiar with the Farstrider corps."

Grede glanced at the patrons at a distant table and lowered his voice. "How am I supposed to believe you? You say you're from Quel'Thalas, but you don't even—"

Vereesa frowned and cut him off before he could spill everything to a complete stranger who had appeared out of nowhere. The dwarf swallowed and choked back the rest of his sentence.

Jace also agreed they shouldn't lay everything out so plainly. This elf didn't look like a bad person, but who knew — she might have ties to Dar'Khan Drathir.

Every high elf showing up in Winterspring at this time deserved suspicion.

Actually, Jace had a feeling too — this woman wasn't a high elf. She was a dragon. A green dragon. Given the green tattoos on her face, the habitual green clothing, the far more indifferent demeanor compared to a high elf… But this guess had its problems too. Green dragons' hatred of demons was no lighter than a night elf's.

Yet here was Morophil, a succubus, sitting right in front of her, watching her with what appeared to be a rather smug expression, and she was completely unbothered.

If she wasn't a mage who'd practiced a great deal of evil magic, or a mercenary with many warlock and shadow mage friends, then probably only the high elves of Quel'Thalas could be this tolerant.

And if she were a green dragon, wouldn't she recognize him?

Even if he hadn't personally slain Deathwing, the name Jace Sesso must have spread among the various dragonflights by all rights — not to mention that Vereesa was wearing Ysera's earring.

Then again, that last point was hard to say too. Maybe there was some wild green dragon that hadn't been in contact with Ysera or even the Emerald Dream in ages and was out of the loop.

And even Neltharion's necklace — Grede needed to use earth magic to unleash the necklace's power before black dragons could detect it. The last time Vereesa had used that necklace was about a month ago. Besides, the earring was meant to conceal its wearer's aura in the first place — how could it release enough magical power to draw attention?

His mind churned through all of this in a few seconds, but beyond raising more questions, it did no good. Better to ask a few more questions.

Jace exchanged a glance with Vereesa, then said, "Since we've accepted this job, we need to know as much information as possible. Lady Sola Shade, I'd like you to tell us everything — starting with how you met Mosa."

Sola thought for a moment, then said, "I came to Winterspring to study the growth of plants in the frigid lands around Hyjal Summit, as well as the strength and range of the Well of Eternity's magical energy radiation. It's all related to the way arcane and nature powers nourish each other — part of my research. Mosa Woodmane was a tauren guide I found on the southern grasslands. She was an excellent nature expert, and she was willing to help me with my research…"

"What a load of nonsense," Grede quickly lost his patience. He stood up and said, "Mosa came here from the south with her own goal — she wasn't out looking for plants or studying nature or whatever. Do you take us for fools? What danger is she in right now? Did you get her into it, and now you want to trick us into walking to our deaths?!"

Hearing Grede say this, Sola's expression finally shifted. She said with a trace of anger, "How presumptuous. I have never deceived anyone…"

"You're deceiving us right now," Jace cut her off. "I…" Sola stared at them, at a loss.

In the end, she let out a sigh and said, "Very well. Mosa was indeed trapped by some evil because of me."

"What evil?" Vereesa asked.

"I don't know…" Sola said helplessly.

"You don't know?" Grede looked like he wanted to wring Sola's neck. He said angrily, "So it comes back to you getting her into it after all!"

"But she is unquestionably alive!" Sola said, keeping her voice low. "I can feel it… I can feel that she's still alive. Perhaps she's hiding somewhere, just trapped and unable to show herself, waiting for me to rescue her."

"What good is your feeling?" Grede said angrily. "I can't feel a thing!"

"What is she to you?" Sola asked in return.

"I'm her… I'm her…" Grede gritted his teeth but couldn't find the right word for the relationship.

He mulled it over for a moment, then said in a low voice, "I, Grede Thunderfist, am her fiancé, Lady Sola Shade. If she dies, I'll twist your little head off this thin neck bit by bit, take it back to the Hinterlands, and hang it in my house at Aerie Peak as a decoration!"

"Grede…" Vereesa sighed. The fool still couldn't help but blurt out who he was.

"You are Grede Thunderfist?" Sola Shade said, eyes wide, then looked at Vereesa. "You are Vereesa Windrunner."

Finally, she looked at Jace. "You are Jace Sesso." Jace nodded and watched for her reaction.

If she revealed herself to be a high elf sent by Dar'Khan Drathir, things would get ugly.

The group fell silent for a moment. Sola said, "I did lie to you. My real name is not Sola Shade — that's just a false identity I use while traveling."

"Couldn't tell," Vereesa said with a frown. "The 'Highborne' you spoke of are our ancestors. The tone you used when talking about them made me very uncomfortable. Now tell us your true identity, and we'll consider whether to let you walk out of here."

"I apologize, Quel'dorei," Sola said calmly. "My true name is Somali'er. I am one of Ysera's children — what you call the Green Dragonflight."

Grede was completely stunned. Vereesa's expression changed dramatically too. The noise of the tavern's clamor suddenly drowned out the group's hushed argument.

Only Jace looked calmly at the elf in front of him — he'd already had his suspicions. But if the other party was a green dragon, then his questions only multiplied.

The green dragons were guardians of the Emerald Dream. Most of them existed in a state of slumber, including Ysera herself. Even a red dragon of Krasus's stature would need to enter the Emerald Dream to see Ysera.

The green dragons who were awake outside the Dream were mostly there because Ysera had given them important missions — like the Dreaming Lord Ilanicus and his children in the sunken temple deep in the Swamp of Sorrows. Or those who wandered outside the Dream for some reason and were unwilling to return — like the dream guardian Ysondre, who had broken free of the Nightmare.

If this one in front of him really was a green dragon, which kind was she?

"Did you come to Everlook through Timbermaw Hold?" Jace asked. "I heard someone say a Quel'dorei in green passed through Timbermaw Hold with a leopard. Was that you?"

"Yes," Sola answered.

"Was the leopard Mosa?" Vereesa asked. "But she doesn't have horns on her head…"

"Mosa's cat form doesn't have horns to begin with," Sola said. "Her shapeshifting ability is stronger than many Druids of the Claw — she's already able to avoid revealing too many of her original features."

So that was it. Mosa was more skilled at transformation magic, so she'd hidden her horns. Such a simple possibility — Jace was a little surprised he hadn't considered it at all.

Vereesa asked, "Then why didn't you fly over Mount Hyjal directly instead of taking the long way through furbolg territory?"

Sola seemed reluctant to answer this question. Her gaze flicked to Jace's waist and said, "Before I answer that, I'd like to see that sword, Jace Sesso."

Jace glanced at the two sitting across from him and nodded. He partially drew Azurewrath, and Sola's pale face was immediately bathed in arcane light, turning a cyan-blue.

She narrowed her eyes slightly, watched Jace sheathe the sword again, and then said, "Mosa's and my movements cannot be discovered by the Kaldorei, nor by their allies — whether furbolgs or the ancients. If I traveled in my true form, the Kaldorei would detect us quickly, and our original objective would be completely exposed. Then everything would fall apart…"

"What are you looking for? You can tell us that much, right?" Grede asked.

The green dragon glanced at the dwarf. "We are searching for the lost Celadilann'dorei."

"Could you say that in Common? Or Dwarvish," Grede said.

Jace blinked guiltily — he didn't understand this word either, which seemed to be elven, but Vereesa already looked stunned. "The Fang of the Fang King," Vereesa said. "That staff from Kaldorei legend that can summon werewolves?"

Jace's eyes went wide. Could she be talking about the Scythe of Elune?!
