Chapter 845: Origin of the Relic

"Did you capture Musa?!" Grede shouted, his voice echoing through the cavern. The felguard didn't answer. Sazeno shouted, "Definitely not him, fatso — he's not smart enough for that!"

"Then damn you, die!"

Grede slung his shield onto his back, raised his axe, and charged with a war cry — only to be struck on his axe by the felguard's blade, sent tumbling to the side and slamming into the wall.

He groaned in pain and muttered, "How the hell is he this strong?"

"My master commands you to stop."

The felguard muttered in demonic tongue. Flames burst from his eyes, and the entire cavern was bathed in the crimson light of the sigils glowing across his body. Jace could tell this fellow was far more powerful than any felguard they'd encountered at the Dark Portal or the Black Morass.

This was no cannon-fodder vanguard pouring in batches through the Dark Portal. And whoever had summoned it here was definitely no ordinary orc warlock.

After bursting into flame, the felguard suddenly took a step forward, his iron boots clanging against the ground. Jace cast Curse of Weakness on him, but it barely hindered his strength. The felguard charged toward the nearby Vereesa like a red locomotive.

Was this also an empowered felguard? Jace could clearly sense the fel blood ignited in the fellow's chest.

Vereesa loosed an arrow that struck him square in the chest, the arrowhead poking out his back. The felguard didn't care in the least, charging straight up to her and raising his blade.

The towering demon's first strike was dodged by the ranger as she ducked aside. His second swing was caught by a red blood-light and couldn't come down. The demon roared and looked toward the source of the red light. With one hand, Jace gripped the demon's weapon with Gorefiend's Grasp; with the other, he hurled a Shadowfire ball at him.

Vereesa shifted to the side. The fireball exploded at the felguard's midsection. Purple demon blood splattered amid the blooming blood-red flames, and shattered armor scattered everywhere.

However, though the felguard's abdomen had been blown open into a charred gash, he seemed utterly unfazed. With a roar, he raised his polearm and leaped through the air toward Jace — Vereesa put an arcane-enchanted arrow through his shoulder mid-air, but it still didn't slow his movements.

This was indeed a demon minion strengthened by Demon Empowerment! Only after learning this incantation had he gained the ability to recognize it.

Jace watched the massive felguard scrape across a mass of tree roots on the ceiling as he raised his blade and leaped down from mid-air. He immediately melded into shadow and pulled himself some distance in the opposite direction — but no sooner had his feet steadied than the demon drove the hilt of his blade hard into Jace's chest, sending him toppling to the ground.

"Master!"

Morophil drove a fierce kick into the felguard's arm, blocking his second slash. The felguard reached out to grab her, but she melded into shadow and evaded. The felguard's gaze landed almost instantly on the spot where the succubus reappeared.

Jace remembered that the felguard in the Black Morass had been able to predict roughly where Morophil would reappear after each Shadowstep. His own third-rate Shadowstep could never fool a Burning Legion warrior of this caliber.

The felguard raised both hands on his blade and swung in a wide arc, as if trying to cut down both Jace and Morophil beside him in one stroke. Morophil kicked backward and dodged the slash. Jace barely managed to step back, nearly having his neck opened.

Clearly, Morophil's movements enraged the felguard even more. Under the frenzy of Demon Empowerment, he had completely lost his reason, single-mindedly chasing and hacking at the more provocative succubus.

After the succubus dodged several slashes, the tree roots above his head suddenly began to grow, dropping down like tentacles to wrap around his shoulders and neck. Sola was channeling the tree roots to hinder his movements.

As the felguard struggled to break free of the vines, Jace shouted, "Sazeno, Language Curse!"

"Y-yes, Master!"

Jace felt his throat grow hot, burning, and hoarse, then immediately recited the Cripple incantation. A coil of green light wrapped around the felguard. His movements immediately became weak and sluggish, making it hard for him to break free of the tree roots' control. Added to this, arcane arrows pierced his chest one after another. Demon blood had soaked through the armor covering his entire lower body.

At that moment, Grede, the Earthen wreathed in lightning, leaped out of the shadows with a roar and crashed into the felguard. A giant three or four meters tall was toppled by the impact.

Grede hacked off his head with a single axe blow. But even headless, the felguard bound by Cripple still struggled with all his might. Blood like molten lava gushed from the severed neck, roaring and burning. Blood splattered onto Grede's face, burning him so badly he roared several times and shook his head.

After shoving Grede away, the headless demon hurled his polearm hard toward Jace's direction. Jace, channeling Shadow Bolts, barely dodged to the side. Looking again, he saw the demon striding forward, stumbling and limping — but arcane-glowing arrows pierced his chest one after another, and it was clear he could barely walk now.

Finally, just as the felguard was about to reach Jace, he knelt down, collapsed to the ground covered in arrows, the sigils across his body finally dimming. The flames slowly died out.

"Damn it… damn it!"

Grede pulled off his helmet and tossed it aside, continuously wiping the demon blood from his face with his cloak. The blood had probably already left his skin stinging and itching.

Vereesa limped over to lean against the earth wall on one side. She managed a faint smile at Jace, then lowered her head and swallowed. Her breathing rhythm had already been thrown off.

Jace also smiled weakly. After that massive pack of felhounds just now, plus bringing down this powerful felguard, he had basically run out of strength too. He slowly clutched his ribs, which had just been viciously struck, backed up a few steps, and sat down on a stone jutting out of the ground.

Sola came to Vereesa's side and helped her examine the wounds on her flank and thigh, gathering natural energy with her hands to aid her recovery. The demon blood and the places burned by fel fire were all dispelled by the green dragon's power, and the wounds healed at a visibly rapid pace. Vereesa frowned, but still softly said thank you. Sola only shook her head.

"Are you hurt, Grede?" the green dragon looked over.

The dwarf waved his hand as he walked toward Jace and said, "I'm completely fine, Sola." He came up and said, "Lend me your sword, Jace."

Jace handed Azurewrath to the dwarf. The dwarf raised the sword, and its faint blue glow lit the cavern somewhat, replacing the bloody red from before. That suffocating sense of pressure instantly vanished by more than half.

Grede searched back and forth through the cavern, but this was the deepest point of the cave — there were no other passages or exits.

The ground here was covered in all kinds of runes. Jace could tell that some of them were for communicating with other demons. There was a damaged but extremely powerful summoning circle. The ground was littered with the mangled limbs of unknown beasts — some of the shredded flesh and bones were likely owlbeast.

Had the original occupant of this place sacrificed owlbeast souls to summon such a felguard?

In any case, these circles hadn't been used for some time. Jace couldn't sense much of the residual power.

Jace said, "Musa's clearly not here, Grede. And the one who commanded these demons to hold us back just now wasn't this felguard either."

"Damn it!" Grede, furious, hurled the axe in his hand aside, plopped down onto the ground, and sighed while cradling Azurewrath.

Jace could tell he'd known this for a while too — he just hadn't been willing to accept it.

"Do you have any leads, Morophil?" Jace asked.

Morophil looked around, searching as far as the entrance, and said, "I can sense the aura of a special Shadow caster, Master, but he's already fled. Since this is a dead end, he most likely slipped away by turning invisible while we were fighting just now. I can think of anyone besides a satyr or a succubus who could pull that off."

Jace said, "But there's no scent of another succubus here. If that succubus had been staying here long-term, the scent would be obvious, right, Morophil?"

"Yes, Master," Morophil said.

Jace looked at Sola and said, "Is there anything else you know that we don't?"

"I don't know… but satyrs shouldn't have returned to the vicinity of Mount Hyjal." Sola said gloomily. "We guard this place together with the Kaldorei…"

"But clearly all of Kalimdor is riddled with holes, Miss Sola." Jace took the blue dragon sword Grede handed back to him and sheathed it. "Satyrs have been working to gather treasures from all across Kalimdor, and they're also trying to make contact with the Burning Legion. Even the goblins of Gadgetzan probably know this. Denying it doesn't make it go away. Look at this felguard — think about how powerful the satyr who could summon him here must be. The demon who fled is most likely the chief culprit corrupting these owlbeasts. Even the most sacred beasts around Mount Hyjal, the ones most devoted to nature and your beloved Elune, have gone mad. Draw the connection yourself — you should realize how serious things have become."

Sola nodded in silence.

Jace wearily stood up and said, "In any case, there's no Moon Goddess relic here, and no Musa. We'll have to start the search from scratch."

"Look at this, Jace."

Vereesa picked something up from a distant corner and looked over.

Everyone gathered around. Vereesa held up what she'd found under a gentle Light — a slightly pale stone fragment, like a translucent, faintly green crystal, about the size of a single finger joint.

Its green wasn't fel — it was the stone's own color. Though it was just a small fragment, Jace could tell it must be a fallen piece of something that had been carefully polished. Finding such a thing in a cave dug out by owlbeasts and occupied by demons was definitely unusual.

"What is this, Sola — is it a green dragon scale?" Grede asked.

Sola shook her head, took a green object from her bosom shaped like a small oval bronze mirror, and said, "This is our scale." Grede looked at the scale and swallowed. Jace also glanced at the scale.

He could clearly sense the abundant natural energy within it, and its surface was as smooth and warm as turquoise. It was indeed completely different from what Vereesa had found.

Vereesa said, "I have seen something quite similar, actually."

"What?" Jace asked.

Vereesa said, "The Reliquary of Quel'Thalas preserves a gem fragment — said to be a shard of an ancient artifact destroyed in a terrible magical catastrophe. Although that shard still contained extremely powerful magic and was far larger than this, I could guess what it would look like once its magic was exhausted. It would probably look exactly like this — in terms of both internal structure and material characteristics…"

"What are you talking about…" Grede waved his hand. "A gem fragment collected in Quel'Thalas across the sea — the same thing as this little fragment we found in this cave? What a joke. You must be mistaken. What are you, some kind of expert in identifying ancient treasures?"

"Joking around is fine normally, Grede — but don't actually underestimate my powers of observation." Vereesa held the stone fragment and said, "And the reason I dare be certain is that that fragment was also found by Quel'dorei mages in Winterspring. They paid a great price for it."

When Vereesa said this, Jace immediately remembered a quest he'd done in his past life. "What was that gem called?" he asked.

"Shimmrath," Vereesa said. "If I remember correctly."

Yes, the Shimmrath crystal — that Highborne treasure. The quest chain connected to it wasn't particularly long, but it was a leg-breaker…

At that, the ranger pressed her forehead again. "Damn it, I should have asked more about this thing when I was at the Faltherine Academy. I've forgotten where this gem was found."

"Then let's start the search from the Highborne ruins," Jace said. "A magical catastrophe, an ancient artifact — sounds exactly like something the Highborne would do."

Vereesa raised an eyebrow and nodded. "I have to admit, that's exactly right."

"At least the trip wasn't a complete waste," Sola said. "I don't know what I would have done if I hadn't run into you all."

Grede coughed and said, "If you really want to thank us, then after we rescue Musa, how about just giving us that one too…"

"Grede…" Vereesa reminded him, exasperated.

Grede waved his hand. "Fine, fine. What I actually wanted to ask was… how many Highborne ruins are there in Winterspring, Sola?"

Sola clutched the scale in her hand and thought for a moment. "Many. Before the Sundering, this was a place brimming with magic, so many Kaldorei lived here — the ruins of Elodor, the ruins of Maisoreil, the destroyed Temple of Eldretharr, ancient Caer Darrow…"

"Caer Darrow!" Vereesa said excitedly. "I remember now — it's Caer Darrow. That's the name!"

Jace looked at Vereesa and thought — having a properly educated high elf in the group really was something. No need to keep passing the buck to the library.
