Chapter 855: Cut Off from Home

"J-Jace... how are you... doing...?"

"Almost there... Vere."

Jace forced the words out, watching the powerful, scar-crossed elven back before him rise and fall.

He drew a deep breath and closed his eyes. Vereesa slowly lowered herself, shifting to a position where she could put more strength into it and quickened the pace. Jace felt himself getting closer and closer to the edge; he ground his teeth, tried to lift his head and look at the ceiling to distract himself — he did not want it to end this quickly.

Bang, bang, bang — someone was knocking hastily on the door. "Oh... hell..."

Vereesa shuddered all over, moved aside, panting, and pulled her cloak over herself.

Jace sat up and pulled the elf into his arms, skin slick against skin with sweat. He looked at her damp silver hair, felt the warm wetness below, breathed in her scent and a hint of sungrass, and his heart was still pounding.

Whoever was outside, he honestly wanted to kick them in the face. "Who is it?"

"Master?"

Molofier's voice?

Jace said, "What are you knocking for at this hour?! On purpose?"

"Of course not." Molofier pushed the door open with a finger, glanced at the two of them, and wore the smile of someone enjoying the show.

"Hey!" Vereesa bit her lip, pulling the cloak up to her neck, caught her breath and said, "Do not come in, demon! Are you insane?!"

"Not yet, at any rate." Molofier glanced at the window. "Keep your voice down. Go look outside, Master. I would hate for you to miss something."

Jace looked back outside. It was the middle of the night and snowing hard — what could possibly be out there.

He kissed Vereesa's ear lightly, got out of bed, and felt his way to the window, slowly and carefully poking his head out.

Standing in the snow outside the inn were two figures. One was Sola, unmistakably. The other was a night elf woman, considerably taller.

This night elf stood a head taller than the average sentinel. She wore a pale yellow robe and had long emerald-green hair. Her expression as she spoke to Sola was somewhat stern, while Sola kept her head lowered, eyes seemingly brimming with tears.

Who was that woman?

Jace had a suspicion, but he was not sure.

Just then, the night elf woman turned and walked toward Everlook's main gate. Sola stood where she was, head down, dazed for a long time before finally turning back toward the inn.

"Who was that?" Vereesa had the same question.

He looked back at her. "Should we go ask her?" Vereesa nodded.

Jace pulled on his shirt and a fresh jacket, wrapped it tight, and went out onto the staircase with Vereesa — right as Sola was coming up.

She looked up and saw the two of them, and asked in surprise, "Why are you two going out so late?"

"Is everything all right, Sola?" Jace asked.

"You saw," Sola said quietly.

"Who was that?" Vereesa said. "She did not seem very friendly, Sola. Was that a druid? Or was she connected to those two druids we found earlier?"

"No." Sola shook her head. "That was Ysondre." Jace's guess had been right — it really was Ysondre.

Even though she was not wearing the same green outer robe as in the game, everything about her — her looks, her height, her adornments, her hairstyle, the way she spoke to Sola, the subtle dynamic between the two of them — made it obvious.

But was not Ysondre already corrupted by the Nightmare? Why had she suddenly shown up here, and looking perfectly fine? So Jace asked, "She came here? I thought you said you could not reach the other green dragons?"

Sola avoided Jace's eyes. "These past few days I have been trying to call out to Ysondre, and she responded quickly. I think the reason they were not responding to me before was not because they were plagued by the Nightmare — it was because my own mind had been corrupted by the Nightmare, and I had lost the ability to communicate with the rest of my kind."

Jace and Vereesa looked at each other. Neither of them knew what to say.

So it had been Sola's own corruption that had cut her off from the other green dragons — not the other way around.

No wonder Sola had said before that "the other green dragons wanted to capture her" and "if a druid saw her, they would report her to the green dragons at once."

Jace had thought it odd at the time, but given the danger of the Scythe of Elune, it made sense that the Dream's guardians would be extremely vigilant — so he had not questioned it further.

If Ysondre was still sane, that meant the Nightmare's corruption might not be as severe as he had feared. The four Dream Guardians beneath Ysera — Ysondre, Taerar, Lethon, and Emeriss — should all still be in their right minds.

Then again, that made sense. The Kaldorei druids drew on the power of Nordrassil to stay connected to the Emerald Dream — they nourished and protected one another.

Nordrassil still stood. Corroding the Dream deeply would not be that easy.

It was probably only after the Battle of Mount Hyjal, when Archimonde's destruction left Nordrassil at death's door, that the satyrs' Nightmare corruption had the chance to spread and swell so rapidly.

But judging by what they had seen inside the Nightmare — the fog-shrouded, blood-red forest, the countless twisted monsters already corrupted — even eroding the Dream from the edges, going deep into it was already horrifying enough.

"Did you tell her what happened to us in Kesselir?" Jace asked.

"I did," Sola said. "I summoned her here specifically to tell her about what Mossa endured and witnessed regarding the satyrs' crimes. But..."

Seeing her hesitate, Vereesa asked, "What is it?"

Sola hesitated a long time before speaking. "She said she does not want me to return to the Dream. The green dragons and druids are trying to purify the corruption that I and the other tainted wyrmlings caused. If I go back, I would only interfere with the purification. Queen Ysera has not said this explicitly, but I can tell she thinks the same."

"A green dragon, barred from returning to the Dream." Vereesa let out a slow sigh. "I really cannot imagine how you feel, Sola."

"I have been out here a long time. I am more or less used to it," Sola said.

"Well," Vereesa said, "our group is not exactly short on strange characters, is it? A ranger who is not all that good at tracking, a tauren hunter who turns into a cat, a Mountain King who does not like mountains — and the one in front of you, a warlock who is always trying to save people with black magic."

Jace murmured, "A tauren who likes dwarves and a dwarf who likes tauren — that is strange enough already."

"Next to being entangled with a demon from the Twisting Nether, it is nothing," Vereesa said coolly.

Jace cleared his throat, guilty. "What I mean is — at least you are welcome here, Sola. We are in desperate need of a..."

"Travel expert," Vereesa finished for him. "Not wrong," Jace said.

Sola nodded, but a shadow still seemed to linger in her heart.

Molofier's interruption preserves her sardonic voice.
