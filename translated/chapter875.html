Chapter 875: Everlook and Companions

"Skull-iron ring with skull engraving — the craftsmanship isn't bad, the material... is iron. Eight silver — what do you think?"

The goblin looked up at Grede over his spectacles. Vereesa said with a trace of impatience, "There's an Arcane Intellect enchantment on this. Take a proper look, or fetch someone who knows how to appraise magical items."

The goblin glared at her. "And who are you? Let me tell you, elf, not everyone who comes from Quel'Thalas is an enchanment master. Don't try to bluff me!"

Vereesa pulled the silver ring off her finger and slapped it on the counter. "Take a good look at what this is, you green rat!"

The goblin seemed completely unbothered by Vereesa's insults. His gaze immediately locked onto the insignia.

He picked it up and felt the eagle engraving on it, murmuring under his breath, "Windrunner family insignia? This should be worth... I don't know, at least two hundred gold. Let me ask... ah, ah, I see. You mean you're of the Windrunner family? My apologies, my apologies, Lady Windrunner, I understand now."

Vereesa snatched the ring back from his hand and slipped it back onto her finger. "Now then. How much for this Skull Ring?"

The goblin nodded. "Yes, yes, my lady. There is indeed an arcane enchantment of intellect on this — looks like it could maintain its faint power for at least a few decades. I imagine thirty silver shouldn't be a problem."

Hearing "Lady Windrunner," quite a few of the humans nearby looked over. Noticing these gazes, Jace looked back and sized them up.

These people saw the unknown object wrapped in black cloth on Jace's back and guiltily lowered their heads, avoiding eye contact.

Relying on Vereesa's knowledge of enchantments and cursed items, the several weapons, plus the broken black knight blades and the bloodied old bracers, gauntlets, and the like, had managed to sell for twenty-three gold — Jace was a little surprised.

As for that Arathi royal cloak, the goblin was willing to pay fifteen gold to acquire it. Jace wanted to bring it back to Stormwind or even Dalaran to see if any collectors would be willing to pay for it — he felt the goblin probably didn't value these antiques highly, especially the antiques of a human kingdom.

But neither Grede nor Vereesa particularly agreed, after all this cloak was indeed the largest item among the spoils. Heading to Eldaras next, they might encounter naga — there was no town like Everlook there to store things. Carrying this big thing around would definitely slow them down.

So after the few of them discussed it and haggled a bit with the goblin, they sold it for eighteen gold in the end.

As for the vial of poison, Vereesa planned to keep it for herself. Morophil disdained using this kind of poison to enhance her lethality. Vereesa had studied poisoning arrows and weapons back when she was with the Farstriders.

As for Sola's scales — before she'd decided to join the group, Jace hadn't thought much of it. Now that Sola had become a member of the party, selling her scales felt weird, so he had Vereesa keep them for now.

In any case, by the end, including the potions and so on, these miscellaneous items that Grede called "filthy goods" had sold for a total of forty-four gold. This already exceeded Jace's expectations — he absolutely hadn't expected to rake in this much money from those black knights.

This way, he was one step closer to renovating his mage tower.

Bringing the money back to the inn that evening, Jace spread the gold coins on the table and looked at the tauren, the green dragon, the elf, the dwarf, the succubus lounging lazily in the corner of the stone platform with her demonic flame markings retracted and half-naked, and the imp sitting on her flat little belly scratching its ears.

"The demons don't get a share. The rest..."

"I'm happy you at least considered me a little, master," Morophil said with a smile.

"You're welcome," Jace said.

"I wasn't involved in your operation at all, Jace," Mosa shook her head.

"Just give my share to Mosa, Jace," Grede said, lightly tapping the table with his index finger.

The tauren touched Grede's hair. "I don't need money, my Thunderfist. You need to live well in Stormwind — I know everything costs gold there."

"You can live well on silver too," Grede said, holding the tauren's large hand. "Use the gold to buy some fun things from the goblins — bombs and such, something useful against centaurs. I insist you take it."

The tauren sighed. "All right."

"I don't need any either," the green dragon said. "I don't see anywhere I'd need money right now."

"Keep it for now, Sola," Vereesa said. "If you decide to leave Kalimdor for the other side, there will be plenty of places that require money..."

"Like where?" Sola asked.

"Eating, drinking, fun, rent, clothes, ale." Grede counted on his fingers, then hesitated. "Er, it seems you don't really need any of those. Although I don't know what dragons usually like to eat..."

Jace said, "Let's keep it for now, Sola. Vereesa's right. We'll figure it out when the time comes — after all, you've done us a great service, and besides..."

"Flying us everywhere," Vereesa said with a smile. Sola nodded and didn't refuse the money either.

"You keep my share, Jace," Vereesa said. "Consider it me funding your renovation of Ilgathar Tower. It's useful to me too."

"Then I won't stand on ceremony." Jace smiled and swept half of it to his side, then split the remaining twenty-two gold into two piles and pushed them toward Mosa and Sola, then divided up the remaining silver evenly.

"Good, that should do it. It's getting late. Let's each head back to our rooms..." He said this while looking at Sola. "Oh right — do you have a room, Sola?"

"No. I didn't get a room," Sola shook her head.

Grede rested his chin on his hand. "Tomorrow we're heading to Timbermaw Hold to report back to the furbolgs, then south toward the Rage River. You need to rest well — not like last time where you stood outside all night. Anyone else here can sleep poorly, but not you."

"Fine," Sola said.

Jace looked at the demon reclining to the side. "Morophil, help keep watch tonight. Don't let thieves in to steal our things..."

"Got it, master. I'll keep an eye on things. I guarantee no one will disturb your nice time with Lady Windrunner," Morophil said in a low voice.

Grede couldn't help but laugh. Vereesa glared at him until his smile vanished, then stared at Jace — a look that clearly said, "Why did you provoke her."

"Lady Morophil and Mr. Sazeno can stay in my room," Sola said.

"Mr. Sazeno!" Hearing someone address him this way, Sazeno immediately excitedly leaped from the succubus onto the table, making Morophil frown from the stomping.

Everyone at the table was stunned. Jace asked, "You're willing to room with demons?"

"I've realized that although demons have already begun to encroach upon the Nightmare, I know nothing about demons whatsoever — that really shouldn't be," Sola said. "They're both experts on demons. I'd like to learn more about demons from them."

"Ah, kind Miss Sola." Morophil raised her head. "Truly moving. I'll hold nothing back..."

"Me too, me too!" the imp jumped about.

"Then... that's settled, then," Jace said.
