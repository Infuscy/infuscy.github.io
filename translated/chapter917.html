Chapter 917: The Qualifications a Proper Warlock Should Have

This mill occupied by the warlocks looked ordinary inside and out. Like warlocks elsewhere, even this "Guide's" outside contact point was hidden in the cellar — but the tunnel was unusually large for a farm cellar.

Jace followed the fat man named Kloven through the narrow earthen passage. After a while, there were even root tendrils poking through overhead.

Passing through several roughly installed iron doors guarded by sentries, they reached a dim chamber lit by fel-green witchfire. The whole space reeked of an overwhelming, utterly unapologetic stench of demon and the fishy tang of Shadow.

Of all things, though, what Jace found even harder to endure was the cloying perfume of multiple succubi mixed with human sweat.

The cellar's conditions were appalling. He couldn't imagine a normal person surviving underground in an environment like this for long. This had to be a burrow the warlocks had the felhounds dig after seizing the abandoned farm.

In the chamber stood an eredar and two figures in black-gray cloaks bearing warhammers — presumably the main security detail for this cellar.

The demon, well over two meters tall, stood hunched in a corner watching Jace, a long-hafted battle axe in hand. It looked as though if it raised its head even slightly, the spikes on its helmet would gouge the ceiling.

As for the other two, Jace suspected from their powerful builds that they might be "physical warlocks" as well. "Lady Kraus said that to meet the Guide, I'd have to pass a test," Jace said.

"Yes, Mister Jurik." Kloven leaned his hatchet against the wall, caught his breath, and looked up as he spoke in Eredun. "Come with me this way."

Jace nodded and glanced at Demiseet beside him. She looked back at him somewhat nervously — her eyes saying, "If you blow my cover, I'm done for too."

Pushing through the iron door and crossing a short corridor, they entered a smaller, darker chamber. Arrays of runes for different purposes covered the floor and walls. A single sweep of Jace's eyes was enough to make out a demon-binding circle, a Shadow-enhancement circle, and a circle that strengthened communion with the Twisting Nether. This was probably a place for summoning demons — the eredar in the other room might well have been summoned here.

When Kloven and his succubus brought Jace and Demiseet inside, two people were already waiting in the room. A moment later another warlock came in from behind, closing the door and fastening the chain.

Now this small chamber, sealed shut with six people and a demon inside, felt positively cramped — stuffy, damp, and layered with disagreeable smells from multiple sources, including the foul reek of the pigments used to draw the circles. The crude room had been turned into something resembling a purpose-built torture cell, and nobody's expression looked good.

Jace studied the warlocks in turn. All unfamiliar faces. Only one of them wore a robe, but each carried a pack of varying size. Using his demon-detecting sight, Jace could tell that one of them, like himself, had stashed an imp in his bag.

Not hard to guess — these were all warlocks, very likely the ones responsible for performing summoning rituals here.

"Prove that you've truly come to learn demon magic, and not some drifter who gathered here on the strength of a few rumors." Kloven looked at the other warlocks. "Summon a succubus here, on the spot, to show you're a qualified warlock."

"Summon a succubus?" Jace said. "Summoning a succubus has become the standard for a qualified warlock?"

"Jace Sesso had barely made a name for himself in Stormwind when he successfully summoned a succubus," Kloven said, staring at Jace.

Hearing his own name, Jace's lips twitched uncomfortably. Demiseet drew a deep breath and folded her arms.

A light-brown-haired male warlock beside them said, "After many people's attempts and verification, summoning a succubus from the Twisting Nether here is no longer some difficult, profound secret art. Is that a problem for you? Then you'd be better off going back to your homeland and finding a few warlocks to start learning imp-summoning. No need to go to the lighthouse..."

"The problem is, I already have a succubus," Jace said with a smile. "I could summon a different demon — an imp, say, or a felhound. How about that?"

"Where's your succubus, then?" Kloven asked.

Jace had Morophil stay outside, ready to support him at any moment. Through the soul link, he could sense she seemed to be somewhere nearby, above ground.

And Morophil, as a powerful huntress, was far too intimidating to ordinary succubi. Jace was reluctant to bring her inside and draw too much attention — this was hardly the time to show off his strength.

He also hadn't expected these people to demand he summon a succubus on the spot as proof.

"Don't waste time," a bald warlock said, looking at Demiseet. "Before you brought your friend here, did you not explain our rules to him clearly?"

"When I came, you didn't demand I summon a succubus on the spot," Demiseet said to the bald man. "You know how dangerous it is for a warlock to summon two succubi."

She argued anxiously, and only when the other warlocks' attention shifted from Jace to her did she ease up slightly.

"It's fine," Jace said. "I can try summoning a succubus for you to see." The light-brown-haired warlock said in Eredun, "Good. I look forward to your performance."

Kloven exhaled impatiently and leaned against the wall, eyes locked on Jace.

Jace moved to the center of the dark chamber under everyone's watch. He pulled a soul shard from his bag and tossed it to the ground.

Truth be told, Jace wasn't all that confident. Because even though he was the younger generation of the Kingdom of Stormwind's first to figure out the succubus-summoning incantation, even though he'd gone through multiple succubus summonings in the cellar of the Slaughtered Lamb — when all was said and done, he'd only ever summoned Morophil.

Of course, the people here would never believe that the legendary Jace Sesso had only ever summoned a single succubus in his life.

He closed his eyes and familiarized himself with the incantation. The bald man said, somewhat arrogantly, "I think you've forgotten the most important casting material for summoning a succubus, Mister Jurik."

"I think you've forgotten the most important quality for watching a summoning ritual — staying quiet," Jace said. Then he familiarized himself with the incantation once more and opened his eyes.

The other warlocks now wore expressions tinged with anger, confusion, or the unmistakable look of someone settling in to watch a show.

Among them, only Demiseet kept her anxious eyes fixed on Jace — she alone was deeply convinced that Jace could summon a succubus directly, without a love token.

Jace raised his hands and chanted the incantation, establishing contact with the Twisting Nether. At once he felt quite a few presences in the Twisting Nether responding to his voice, which slightly scattered his focus.

Right now his energy was maintaining his link with Morophil, the two felhounds guarding the wizard tower, and the doomguard Lord Kaltz. Even a measly imp... took up a small portion.

Under these circumstances, summoning even a felhound left Jace feeling exhausted, let alone a more intelligent and combat-capable shivarra.

But he still gathered his focus and pushed through to complete the incantation. When the void gate opened, the smug looks on the warlocks' faces were swept away as they concentrated to see what would emerge.

Because it meant the incantation had taken effect even without a token.

At that moment, a succubus clad in full armor materialized from within. Tall and slender, her long horns curving high, her massive wings spreading confidently behind her as she turned her back — Jace found the sight especially dazzling.

She looked down at the people around her. "Who's my summoner, hmm? I'm quite sure it's not you lot... what is this, some ridiculous gathering?"

"I've completed the summoning as you required," Jace said. "Good enough now?"

Hearing Jace's voice, the succubus whipped around and smiled. "It was you."

Her appearance was entirely unlike Morophil's — she radiated a mature, angular beauty, her gaze as arrogant as a venomous spider about to devour someone the next moment.

Her strength, while clearly no match for Morophil's, had exceeded Jace's expectations.

He'd thought that without Teron Gorefiend's staff, on top of already controlling so many demons, his remaining energy wouldn't be enough to summon a particularly powerful being.

Of course, he was now utterly exhausted. This summoning ritual had cost him far more effort than summoning a felhound. "Tarukzun," the bald man said in a low voice, his voice even beginning to tremble.

A heavy thudding came from outside — the eredar, having heard the call.

It squeezed through the passage into the dark chamber, lowering its head to stare at the succubus. The succubus locked onto it in return.

Every warlock present had gone pale with tension — Demiseet included. They all knew the succubus before them was no ordinary one. Judging by her armor, she was a fearsome killer who specialized in combat.

The male succubus also shed his disguise in an instant, gripping a scimitar as he faced the combat succubus and stepped in front of his master Kloven, assuming a fighting stance.

Very quickly, the succubus Jace had summoned gave further proof for their fears, a long sword wreathed in green fel flame suddenly igniting in her hand.

She swept her gaze across those present, then smiled at the eredar. "You'll die. So will the rest of you — if you dare take one more step..."

Jace immediately began chanting the banishment incantation. Hearing her chant, the fire in the succubus's eyes dimmed. The sword in her hand extinguished and turned into something like a chunk of black scrap iron. She no longer cared about the eredar or the other warlocks.

She looked over and murmured in disappointment, "Am I not to your satisfaction, my future master? I heard your voice in the Twisting Nether and felt your magic — I knew we would forge a successful bond. I simply didn't expect this beauty to be so fleeting..."

Jace didn't respond and continued chanting. Soon the succubus accepted her fate. She threw her head back and vanished in a blaze of fel fire and Shadow flame intertwined, leaving behind only fragments of shattered armor clattering to the ground.

The air, which had already reeked foully just moments before, now carried the acrid tang of chaotic flame on top of it — an even more complex bouquet. Even more complex were the expressions on the surrounding warlocks' faces.

"Why didn't you keep her, Mister Jurik? That was a powerful succubus. She could help us fight the Wizard Sanctum's hunts," Kloven said.

"I told you — I won't control a second succubus," Jace said evenly.

"Remarkable," the light-brown-haired warlock said. "How are you able to summon a succubus successfully without a token — and such a powerful individual at that?"

"A token is merely one element to attract a succubus," Jace explained. "If your mana is sufficient, you can summon one successfully even without a token."

The light-brown-haired warlock said to the bald man beside him, "I've seen similar records in other books, but I assumed it was the sort of exaggeration common in demonologists' writings — because I've never personally witnessed such a summoning ritual."

"The Guide will value you highly," the bald man nodded. "I look forward to meeting him soon as well," Jace said.

Kloven said respectfully, "Please give me an object that holds meaning to you. I'll pass it to our courier to deliver to the lighthouse. Then the summoners on the island can use it for a summoning ritual, and you'll be able to meet our Guide, Mister Jurik."

"No problem." Jace handed Kloven the quill he used to take notes from his bag. "Will this do?"

"Close enough to you, and the connection is strong enough." Kloven held the quill between his fingers.

"You can rest here for the time being, until the lighthouse sends word. Actually, I have many questions I'd like to ask you about summoning a succubus, Mister Jurik..." the light-brown-haired warlock said.

"All right," Jace replied. The male succubus approached Jace and nodded respectfully. Kloven said, "He'll take you to your resting quarters, Mister Jurik."
