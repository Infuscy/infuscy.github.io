Chapter 947: The King's Tacit Understanding

Arriving at the SI:7 headquarters, the place was as quiet as ever, with only the occasional person of unknown identity passing in and out in silence. When they saw Jace, they would nod slightly and call out "Master," but Jace didn't recognize a single one of them.

"Master Sesso."

The familiar young man appeared at the entrance. He gestured toward the hall and said, "Please, come in. I was starting to think you weren't going to show up."

"Mathias." Jace nodded politely and followed him into the room.

Mathias Shur fiddled around in a side room for a moment, then hauled out a clay jar and set it on the table. "I'm glad you didn't treat the agreement you proposed that day as mere politeness. As it happens, I have some wine that was sent up from the north not long ago — would you like to try it?"

"I'm not much of a connoisseur," Jace said.

Mathias smiled as he poured. "Just drink it like any ordinary wine, then. It's even a touch sweeter, and the flavor fuller — don't you think?"

Jace nodded, picked up his glass, and took a sip, immediately sensing an unusual chill within. "Brewed by Dalaran's mages."

Mathias raised his eyebrows in mild surprise. "And you said you don't know wine?"

"I don't know wine, but I know a bit about elemental mana. I can tell this was frozen and stored with ice water elementals conjured by arcane magic, though the enchantment has mostly faded," Jace said.

Mathias nodded with a hint of admiration, took a sip, and said, "Sometimes I really feel how powerless our agents are when facing mages, Master Sesso. They possess all manner of strange abilities we can't begin to fathom — magical tracking, magical sensing, invisibility; powerful mages can even alter the weather. And not all of us can tell whether a change is natural or whether someone's messing around with magic."

"It's like that with mages — never mind warlocks. SI:7 has at least had decades of dealings with mages since its founding, but warlocks... that's an entirely new frontier."

Jace set down his glass. "I'm guessing you didn't ask me here to talk about this, Mathias."

The young man glanced toward the door. "Of course we're here to talk about the hottest topic of late. That Alterac princess's arrival has set off a wave of interest in the history of the Kingdom of Alterac within the city walls. Before this, I'd wager the vast majority of Stormwind's citizens couldn't even pronounce the word 'Alterac' smoothly."

Jace shook his head. "These days, every single person I meet starts by lecturing me about Alterac, Katrana, Prestor — I'm getting a headache from it."

"Hah, tell me about it," Mathias said. "Still, I'm quite curious — what do you think of that princess? I've never seen a woman so beautiful, so poised, so widely read... having learned so much in just over twenty years — was her father pushing her too hard?"

"She's not human," Jace said.

The other man studied Jace for a moment, then asked tentatively, "Are you serious, or is that just a figure of speech?"

"What do you think?" Jace asked.

Mathias laughed. "I knew it, Sesso. I knew it. You're the only one who sees it the same way I do. Everyone else in the palace sees that woman as if they've been bewitched."

Jace laughed along, then asked, "Have there been any new dragonkin appearing in Stormwind lately?"

Mathias replied, "No. Redridge, Goldshire, Moonbrook — these past two weeks, no new dragonkin have been encountered. I've re-established contact with Erland, as well as Ursula Dring and Sulenna Keldom, two ladies skilled at commanding demon minions, but truly nothing came of it... Do you think that woman is a dragonkin as well?"

"Dragonkin? If she's a dragonkin, disguising herself to this degree wouldn't be simple," Jace said.

"I hear she wants you to help find Lord Daval Prestor. How about it, Master Sesso — I imagine you've already agreed to her?" Mathias asked.

Jace could tell the other man seemed to have already figured everything out. "It sounds like you already know."

Mathias nodded. "It's not as though I was having you followed. The king asked me to assign someone to protect this distinguished guest from Alterac and to find out everyone she's been in contact with. If she intends to leave the city, her movements are to be watched. Do you think our king shares our suspicions as well?"

Hearing Mathias say this, Jace felt considerably more at ease.

Although King Wrynn had shown Katrana every welcome on the surface and was fully intent on leveraging this northern noblewoman to further elevate the Kingdom of Stormwind's standing within the Alliance, he hadn't lost all reason because of it.

A few years ago, when Jace had first received an audience from this young king, he'd asked him how to deal with black dragons that might have infiltrated the palace. Back then, Jace had told him that a black dragon disguised within the city would certainly have to maintain contact with the outside — whether returning to its lair or meeting with the black dragon's secret emissaries, either could expose itself.

He hoped the king had arranged things with Mathias this way because he'd remembered his advice. That way, even if Jace left the city to head to Shadowforge City in search of the spell to expose the black dragon princess's secret, he could rest easy knowing no major chaos would erupt in Stormwind.

The greatest advantage of the current situation compared to the other timeline was that the entire city of Stormwind, from top to bottom, was already on high alert against black dragons — rather than waiting until Onyxia abducted the king before considering the black dragon threat. This was exactly the result Jace had been working toward for so long.

Jace was also slowly coming to understand that perhaps this was precisely why Katrana Prestor's method of appearing and her persona were completely different from the other world.

She wasn't as aggressive, lacked the typical upper-noble overbearingness, and relied only on a turbulent past and a sorrowful beauty to win sympathy — avoiding the arrogant, overbearing stereotype of a black dragon infiltrator that would trigger associations across the whole city. It was tailored to the current situation, after all.

Jace said, "I hope the king made these arrangements with vigilance in his heart. I'm just worried that SI:7's surveillance might put that princess on guard."

Mathias snorted coldly. "She'd best be on guard. Otherwise, let her think everyone in the Kingdom of Stormwind is like that Leskowa family — the moment they see some 'heir to the throne,' they fawn all over him like dogs. But don't you worry — for now, aside from myself, no second person within SI:7 knows of our suspicions. The protective measures the king ordered me to take are all within normal bounds."

Jace nodded. "That puts me at ease, then. Soon I'll be leaving the city to conduct an investigation. Suspicion is one thing, but the investigation has to happen regardless."

"Be careful in all things, Master Sesso," Mathias said, refilling his own glass. He looked up and set the jar down. "Though I hate to say it, if she really is a dragonkin in disguise, she'll very likely try to set a trap for you."

"I've considered that. Don't worry," Jace said, picking up his own glass again.

Mathias gave a bitter laugh. "Besides 'don't worry,' there's not much else I can do."

"There is, actually, one more thing," Jace said. "I heard from the king that SI:7 is currently cooperating with the army to scout the movements of the orcish forces north of Redridge?"

Mathias nodded. "Not just orcs — Dark Iron dwarves as well. It's just that the Dark Iron dwarves have suffered defeat after defeat as of late. Most of their outposts, even their fortresses, have already been seized by the Blackrock orcs. Of course, the orcs have also taken considerable losses — after all, they were never great in number to begin with, and for now they can only scale back their offensives."

Jace said, "I'd like to know the distribution of orcs and Dark Iron dwarves on the Burning Steppes, in as much detail as possible. My investigation will involve that area next, and I want to prepare as well as I can. Ah — I don't suppose this is classified?"

"Classified?" Mathias laughed. "I don't suppose there's anything left in this kingdom that needs to be kept from the king's dark magic advisor, Master Sesso. Go to Lakeshire and find one of our collaborators — a member of the Morgan family. He'll brief you on the situation on the Burning Steppes and help you cross it. Wait a moment and I'll write down the detailed address for you."

"Much appreciated." Jace nodded. "Oh, and there's one more small favor I'd like to ask. I'd like to visit those Dark Iron dwarf prisoners in the jail — and the orc prisoners. I don't suppose you have a way to arrange that?"

Mathias gave Jace a slightly odd look, then said, "Seeing the Dark Iron dwarf prisoners isn't difficult, but... orcs? I'm not entirely sure Stormwind has any orc prisoners, because the previous ones were all transferred to Lordaeron. The orcish-language scholars at the university even protested the matter, so it left quite an impression on me."

"I heard from outside that some Blackrock orc stragglers have been captured recently," Jace said.

"I heard that too, but I'll need to confirm further with the prison staff," Mathias said. He thought for a moment, then raised his eyebrows. "But may I ask — what do you want to see them for?"

Jace explained, "I have a mage friend who's writing a paper on the conflict between dwarves and orcs, but she's never even seen a real orc..."

Mathias burst out laughing. "So that's the caliber of the mages in your Wizard Sanctum?"

Jace took a sip of wine. "She's an apprentice mage. What more do you expect? And this is just to get through her language teacher's exam, not to write a report for the king. No need to take it so seriously."

"I know, I know..." Mathias waved a hand. "No problem, Master Sesso. This is a trifling matter."
